# catix in Telegram

## What is catix's Telegram bot?

The bot is https://t.me/catlxbot.

## How do I get notifications about schedule changes?

- Open the bot's private chat and press `/start`, then set your study group on the site.
- catix finds reschedules, room changes and cancellations in the study chats and sends them to the users of the chat's group. Changes from common chats go to everyone.
- Each notification has a button to add the change to your calendar.

## What happens if I block the bot?

Notifications stop until you press `/start` again.

## What does the bot not do?

It does not answer questions: not in the private chat, not by mention, reply or inline. Ask on the site instead.

## Which chats does catix read?

Chats chosen by the admins, in Telegram and MAX. Messages of your group and the common chats feed search answers. Some large chats are searched live and not stored.

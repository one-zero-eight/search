# What catix can do

## Where do I ask a question?

The catix assistant lives on the site at https://innohassle.ru/search/ask. Sign in with InNoHassle. The Telegram bot does not answer questions.

## What does catix answer?

- University rules and documents, EduWiki, and the sites of the university and Innopolis, with numbered sources.
- Messages of the study chats catix reads: your study group's chat and the common chats.
- Clubs, events, the map of rooms, group schedules.
- Free rooms and music room windows, sport trainings.
- Your Moodle course materials, when you send them through the browser extension (see that guide).

## What actions can catix take?

- Book a room or a music room, sign you up for a sport training, add, change or remove events in the AI calendar.
- catix always asks for confirmation before changing anything.
- Buttons under an answer: "Add to my calendar" and booking choices.

## Can I attach files?

Yes: up to 3 PDF, TXT or image files per message, up to 10 MiB each.

## How do I search without the assistant?

- Start a message with `/search` or `/поиск` to get plain search result cards.
- When the assistant is temporarily unavailable, questions are answered by search automatically and a notice says so.

## How do I set my study group?

You set your study group on the site. Chat search covers your group and the common chats.

## How do I export the AI calendar to an external app?

In the calendar settings on the site you can create a secret subscription link for an external calendar app and revoke it. It is read-only and is not two-way sync.

## How do I rate an answer?

Use 👍 or 👎 under an answer, with an optional comment.

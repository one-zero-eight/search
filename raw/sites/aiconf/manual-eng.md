Instructions for applying discounts on hotels of the Sviyazhsky Hills Group of Companies
How to apply the promo code?
1.
In the hotels of the resort town "Sviyaga hills" (3 km from Innopolis University) you can book a room online using promo code: AIIN2023
![](https://static.tildacdn.com/tild3661-6234-4533-b066-363836323761/1.png)
Go to the website [https://sviyaga-hills.com](https://sviyaga-hills.com), on the homepage you will see a box to enter details for booking:
2.
Participants of our conference have two accommodation options: Hotel "Deja vu" and Hotel "Mayak". To book a room, you need to:
- Choose one of the options
- Enter the dates of your stay
- Specify the number of guests
- After that the site will show you the following message: There are no rooms available for the dates you have chosen
![](https://static.tildacdn.com/tild3438-6463-4861-a361-313862376264/2.png)
3.
To see the accommodation options, you need to enter the promo code AIIN2023 and click "Apply". After that you will be able to choose a room:

Printers and Scanners
MFP (multifunctional device) - allows performing multiple operations. MFPs can print documents from various sources, scan them, and also make copies.
Printer - peripheral equipment designed for outputting information onto paper.
Scanner - used for transferring text and images from paper to a computer in the form of a file.

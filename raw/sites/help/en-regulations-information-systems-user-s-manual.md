Information systems USER’S MANUAL
Main topics of the User’s manual:
- basic rights and obligations of the employee when working on computer equipment and with information systems of the University,
- general password requirements,
- guidelines for using passwords,
- antivirus protection regulations,
- rules for working on the Internet,
- rules for working with email,
- responsibility for illegal actions,
- procedure for obtaining administrator rights to information systems and computer equipment.

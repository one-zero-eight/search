Terms, definitions and abbreviations

INFOSEC (information security) — the practice of preventing unauthorized
access, usage, disclosure, distortion, alteration, search, recording or destruction
of information.

CT (computer technology) is a set of software and technical elements of data
processing systems capable of functioning independently or as part of other
systems.

IS (information system) — a system designed for storing, searching and
processing information, and the corresponding organizational resources (human,
technical, financial, etc.) that provide and disseminate information (i.e., e-mail,
corporate portal, Internet).

ITO — Information Technology Office, Department of Administrative Policy
and IT Support, ANO HE “Innopolis University”.

External storage media — any type of media intended for recording or
reading information from a computer or any similar device.

Учетная запись — запись, содержащая сведения для идентификации
пользователя при подключении к информационным системам, сведения для
авторизации и учета (логин).

Personal account — a record containing data for identifying a user when
connecting to information systems, information for authorization and accounting
(login).

1. GENERAL PROVISIONS

1.1. The present ANO HE “Innopolis University” information systems user’s
manual (hereinafter referred to as the User’s manual) defines the general rules for
working with information systems and in networks of ANO HE “Innopolis University”
(hereinafter referred to as the Organization).

1.2. This User’s manual is developed in accordance with the requirements of
the Information security policy of ANO HE “Innopolis University” (hereinafter — the
INFOSEC Policy), instructions and other internal regulatory documents of the
Organization. The terms, definitions and abbreviations used in this User’s manual
are in accordance with the INFOSEC Policy.

1.3. IS users are all employees of the Organization and third parties
participating in data processing and having access to information resources and
information systems (computing and network equipment, hardware, software, data
and means of their protection) of the Organization.

1.4. The users in their work are guided by the regulatory legal acts of the
Russian Federation governing the processing of data in information systems, the
Charter of the Organization, the INFOSEC Policy, this User’s manual, local regulatory
legal acts of the subdivision in which they work, their job descriptions, and other
documents of the Organization that regulate data processing in information
systems, the terms of contracts and agreements.

1.5. Information generated in the course of labor and other activities of the
user is the property of the Organization and is not subject to use for the personal
purposes of the user or other persons and organizations.

2. USER RESPONSIBILITIES
A user has responsibility to:

2.1. Know and comply with the regulatory legal acts of the Russian Federation,
the terms of contracts and agreements governing the observance of copyright and
related rights to intellectual property in the field of software, as well as in relation
to scientific and other works, papers and materials, other objects of intellectual
property.

2.2. Know and adhere to the requirements of the legislation of the Russian

Federation, this User’s manual and other internal documents of the Organization
regulating work in information systems.

2.3. Perform at one’s workplace using computer technology (hereinafter
referred to as CT) only data processing procedures determined by one’s job
description, official or contractual obligations.

2.4. Use the information resources of the Organization and the technical
means of storing, processing and transmitting information transferred to the user’s
disposal only to perform the assigned work, and official or contractual obligations.

2.5. Take appropriate measures to protect internal (service) information on
external storage media, lock the screen (work session) before leaving one’s
workplace.

2.6. Upon completion or termination of work (labor relations), all physical
storage media containing service information (USB drives, magnetic and optical
disks, printouts, drafts, blueprints, film and photographic materials, models and
mockups, samples, etc.) must be transferred to the user’s direct supervisor or to the
ITO.

3. USER RIGHTS
The user has the right to:

3.1. Send to his (her) direct supervisor substantiated proposals for the
purchase and installation of new software and hardware.

3.2. Send to his (her) direct supervisor substantiated proposals for upgrade or
modernization (replacement with new analogues) of the software and hardware
used.

3.3. Receive from his (her) direct supervisor or ITO staff advice on rules and
regulations for working with information resources, systems, network services and
databases of the Organization.

3.4. Request the rights of an administrator of an information system or a CT
provided prior approval of his (her) direct supervisor by a memo in the form
approved by the annex to this User’s manual, using an application form on the Bitrix
corporate portal addressed to the head of ITO with justification of the need and
indicating the required validity period, in cases when an information system or a CT
are mandatory for fulfillment of the user’s official duties.

3.5. Request help and advice to resolve issues related to the use of IS from IOT
by e-mail: it@innopolis.university, via Telegram: @iuithelp, tel: 8 (843) 203-92-53
(ext. 200) or in the ITSM requests management system -
https://it.innopolis.university/.

4. RULES AND RESTRICTIONS
The user is prohibited to:

4.1. Violate the rules for working in information systems and networks
established in the Organization.

4.2. Receive (bring, download), store, install and use unlicensed software.

4.3. Violate copyright and related intellectual property rights in relation to
software, scientific, literary, artistic, cinematographic, musical and other works,
papers and materials, other objects of intellectual property.

4.4. Use software and hardware of the Organization for non-business
(personal) purposes.

4.5. Change the composition and configuration of the used software and
hardware, install and modify software and hardware without the consent of the
user’s direct supervisor and ITO.

4.6. Perform actions aimed at obtaining unauthorized access to information
systems, computers, servers and networks of the Organization, as well as to
Internet resources.

4.7. Disable, change settings of information protection tools (including firewall
and antivirus protection applications and settings).

4.8. Use unregulated (not related to work, not authorized by the head of ITO)
programs and resources that create an excessive load on the network (games, P2P
clients), designed to distribute or receive information in violation of copyright and
related rights, as well as remote administration tools, etc.

4.9. Without the consent of ITO, create shared network resources (folders and
shared files), change the content of network resources or access rights to them
without the permission of their owners, grant rights to any resources in the form of
“full access for everyone”, allow unauthorized (anonymous, guest) access to
network resources with the right to write (modify) content.

4.10. Eliminate independently malfunctions in the computing or network
equipment of the Organization.

4.11. To prevent officials and responsible employees of the Organization from
conducting inspections and official investigations related to ensuring information
security, inventory or audit.

4.12. Delete, alter or distort programs and files with service data.

4.13. Use undocumented properties and errors in software or in protection
settings that can lead to any incident.

4.14. Use accounts with administrator rights, except in cases when such rights
are required to perform the user’s official duties.

4.15. Use accounts with administrator rights without a password or with an
empty password.

4.16. Install and use software that is not required for the job.

4.17. Connect personal computer equipment, mobile and network devices to
the networks of the Organization, as well as change IP addresses, MAC addresses
and other network settings of any equipment without the consent of the direct
supervisor and ITO.

4.18. Distribute without permission confidential information containing
personal data, official or commercial secrets, as well as information about the
essence of an invention, research, development, model or industrial design prior to
the official publication of information about them.

4.19. Distribute and receive materials that contradict the laws of the Russian

Federation and the internal rules of the Organization. Publish information of
extremist, racist and offensive content.

5. PASSWORDS
5.1. General requirements for passwords.

5.1.1. The minimum password length is eight characters.

5.1.2. The minimum requirement for a password is to contain characters from
three of the four categories listed below:
 capital letters;
 lowercase letters;
 numbers (from 0 to 9);
 characters other than letters and numbers (for example,!, $, #,%).

5.1.3. The new password must not be the same as the old one.

5.1.4. The password should not coincide with the account name (login), contain
parts of the username longer than two adjacent characters and contain easily
guessed words and numbers (names, dates of birth, company names, etc.), common
abbreviations, document numbers, etc. other information about the user available
to third parties.

5.1.5. It is not allowed to use one character repeatedly or a repeating
combination of several characters as a password.

5.1.6. It is not allowed to use a combination of characters typed in a regular
order on the keyboard (123456, qwerty, qazwsxedc, etc.) as a password.

5.1.7. User password replacement period: once every six months.
5.2. Rules for the use of passwords.
5.2.1. The user is prohibited to:

• disclose his (her) password to third parties;

• give other employees of the Organization and third parties access to CT
and information systems under their own account and password;

• write down and store passwords in easily accessible places: in a desk
drawer, on a monitor, on sheets of paper, on the back of the keyboard, in a shared
folder, etc.;

• store the password on personal electronic devices;

• use the accounts of other employees in the performance of official duties.

5.2.2. The user is obliged to:

• change his (her) password at the request of the operating system,
information system or ITO;

• when entering a password, exclude the possibility of reading it by an
unauthorized persons or by technical means;

• immediately change the password and inform his (her) direct supervisor
and ITO in case of loss, leakage, unauthorized change of the password.

5.2.3. Unscheduled replacement or deletion of the user's password is
performed in the following cases:

• in case of suspicion of compromise (disclosure, leakage) of the password;

• when a password expires;

• upon termination of authorities (dismissal, change of duties);

• at the direction of ITO.

5.2.4. Upon dismissal or change of duties of a user who, in addition to his
account, has access to other resources (network equipment, servers, administrative
accounts, etc.), an unscheduled change of passwords to these resources is also
performed.

6. ANTIVIRUS PROTECTION

6.1. The user is obliged to perform antivirus scanning of all files received by him
(her) in any way and from any sender.

6.2. In case of suspicion of a computer virus (atypical operation of programs,
the appearance of graphic and sound effects, distortion and disappearance of data,
frequent error messages, etc.), the user should not take any action with the CT (do
not turn off, do not restart), but immediately report it to his (her) direct supervisor
and ITO.

7. WORK WITH INFORMATION SYSTEMS, ON THE INTERNET, WITH E-MAIL

7.1. General provisions for work with IS.

7.1.1. The access to information systems (e-mail of the Organization, the
Internet) is provided only if it does not contradict the requirements of the INFOSEC
Policy and other regulatory documents of the Organization.

7.1.2. The technical capability to gain access to information resources or
services does not guarantee that the requested resource or service is allowed to the
user by the policies and rules of the Organization.

7.1.3. The basis for user access to information systems is a request sent to
it@innopolis.university with an indication of the required resource and type of
access rights to it.

7.1.4. After receiving the request, ITO provides the user's access to the
specified information resources in accordance with the Regulation on providing
access to an IS.

7.1.5. ITO exercises control over the use of information systems in the
Organization.

7.1.6. The user's CT can be disconnected from the information resources of
the Organization and the network on the basis of:

• violation by the user of this User’s manual, the INFOSEC Policy and other
information security regulations of the Organization;

• violation by the user of the current information technology legislation, as
well as of copyright and related rights;

• dismissal of the user or change of his (her) duties;

• detection and investigation of unauthorized access attempts, virus and
other attacks;

• carrying out maintenance and technical work.
7.2. Regulations of work on the Internet.

7.2.1. The use of Internet services in the Organization is carried out for the
performance of official duties.

7.2.2. Information about Internet resources visited by users is recorded and
can be provided to the management for analysis and action.

7.2.3. When using Internet resources, it is prohibited to:

• provide third parties with access to the Internet from their CT, including by
software and hardware methods;

• get access to the Internet on a CT in any way other than provided by the
Organization (unauthorized 3G/4G modems, Wi-Fi devices, etc.).

7.3. Regulations of work with e-mail.

7.3.1. The Organization's e-mail is intended for the fulfillment of duties.

7.3.2. When working with the Organization's e-mail, it is prohibited:

• send mail messages to multiple addresses simultaneously, except for
service announcements;

• use a different return address when sending mail;

• send messages with unethical or illegal content;

• use offensive language in messages;

• send unsolicited or unverified information;

• use a work email address to subscribe to unofficial mailing lists

(commercial, entertainment, etc.), as well as to register on third-party sites (forums,
clubs, etc.);

• send and open executable or system files upon receipt (in particular, with
the extensions bas, bat, bin, cab, cat, cmd, com, cpl, csh, dat, dll, dpl, drv, exe, inf,
ins, inx, ipa, isu, jar, job, js, jse, ksh, lib, lnk, mdz, msc, msi, msp, mst, msu, nls, olb,
osx, out, paf, pif, prg, pwz, reg, rgs, rom, run, scr, sct, sh, shb, shs, sys, tlb, vb, vbe,
vbs, vbscript, vxd, workflow, ws, wsf, wsh), including as part of archive files;

• open any attached files without first checking it with antivirus tools;

• open attachments and links sent from unknown senders or without prior
request.

7.3.3. When working with e-mail, it is recommended to create a notification
for the period of a long absence, in which you indicate the period of absence and
contact information of the substitute employee.
8. FINAL LIABILITIES

8.1. The user bears personal (disciplinary, material, civil, administrative,
criminal) responsibility for his (her) actions or inaction that will or may result in
disclosure or loss of confidential (official, commercial, personal and other) data, as
well as malfunction of information systems, information and telecommunications
network of the Organization or its individual components, unauthorized access to
information or violation of copyright and related rights in accordance with the
regulations of the Organization and the legislation of the Russian Federation.

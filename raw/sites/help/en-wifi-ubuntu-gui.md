## ¶ Connection for guests
Click the network icon and select "UniversityGuest" from the network list
![](https://help.university.innopolis.ru/images_en/image_1699440642523.png)
Click "Connect" button
![](https://help.university.innopolis.ru/images_en/image_1699440647612.png)
Wait for lobby page in your browser (Edge, Chrome, Mozilla Firefox). Type your mobile phone number and click on "Получить код" (Get the code) button. Next, enter a code from the SMS and click "Ввести код" (Confirm). Connection is complete.
![](https://help.university.innopolis.ru/images_en/image_1699440652832.png)
## ¶ Connection for employees
For connect to the network you must download and install the security certificate. It can be downloaded via Wi-Fi "UniversityGuest" or via a wired network. Open [http://pki.university.innopolis.ru](http://pki.university.innopolis.ru) and download "iu_RCA.crt" certificate file
![](https://help.university.innopolis.ru/images_en/image_1699440657862.png)
Click the network icon and select "UniversityStaff" from the list of networks and click "Connect".
![](https://help.university.innopolis.ru/images_en/image_1699440661113.png)
In the "Authentication" field select "Protected EAP (PEAP)" from the drop-down menu. In the field «Domain» type «innopolis.university» In the field "Certificate of CA" select the previously downloaded certificate "iu_RCA.crt". In the field "Username" enter the user name of your lnnopolis University domain account, in the field "Password" enter the password of the user account and click "Connect"
![](https://help.university.innopolis.ru/images_en/image_1699440665427.png)
Connection is complete
![](https://help.university.innopolis.ru/images_en/image_1699440670546.png)

Click the network icon and select "UniversityStudent" from the networks list and click "Connect".

In "Authentication" field select "Protected EAP (PEAP)" from the drop-down menu. In «Domain» field enter «innopolis.university» In "Certificate of CA" field select the previously downloaded certificate "iu_RCA.crt". In "Username" field enter the username of your lnnopolis University domain account, in "Password" field enter the password of the user account and click "Connect". Connection is complete.

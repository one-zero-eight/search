Access for non-employees of the IU
To obtain access for non-employees of the IU, you must fill out a form (NDA) and provide the signed form to the IT department. The form can be found on the IU portal in the Local Regulations section.
[Regulations for granting access rights to information resources of ANO VO "Innopolis University" to persons who are not employees of the organization](https://portal.university.innopolis.ru/lna/733242/)
For questions regarding the regulations, please contact Evgeniy Lysov (Data Protection Officer)

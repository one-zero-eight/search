How to create a Teams Meeting in Outlook
There are two ways to create a Teams meeting in Outlook:
- Open Microsoft Outlook application. At the top of the Outlook application, go to the Home tab and select New Items, then Teams Meeting.
![](https://help.university.innopolis.ru/images_en/image_1699457230658.png)
- To schedule a meeting, open Outlook application and switch to the calendar view. Click New Teams Meeting at the top of the view.
![](https://help.university.innopolis.ru/images_en/image_1699457250598.png)
You need to fill in the required ones:
- Add your meeting subject
- Add your invitees to the Required
- Add your meeting location, start time, and end time
- A link in the body of the request will be generated automatically.
![](https://help.university.innopolis.ru/images_en/image_1699457277297.png)
Click the Send button, all meeting attendees will receive an email with a link to join the Microsoft Teams meeting.
You can accept the meeting, or refuse, or propose new time.
![](https://help.university.innopolis.ru/images_en/image_1699457302291.png)
The meeting organizer will receive a response letter with your answer.
![](https://help.university.innopolis.ru/images_en/image_1699457316120.png)
To join the meeting go to your calendar in MS Outlook. Select the meeting and click Join Teams meeting.
![](https://help.university.innopolis.ru/images_en/image_1699457344091.png)
You can also view the "Meeting" options in MS Outlook by double-clicking on the meeting in the calendar.
![](https://help.university.innopolis.ru/images_en/image_1699457363791.png)
For join to the meeting you must open invitation-letter, open link, and click button “Join To” at the lobby window of Microsoft Teams.
![](https://help.university.innopolis.ru/images_en/image_1699457384200.png)
Click button “Join Now” at the lobby window of Microsoft Teams. In the end of the meeting please click button “Hang up”.
![](https://help.university.innopolis.ru/images_en/image_1699457413325.png)

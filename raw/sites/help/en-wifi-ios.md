## ¶ Connection for guests
Select "UniversityGuest" from the networks list.
![](https://help.university.innopolis.ru/images_en/image_1699447321842.png)
Wait for the opening of your web browser window (eg, Edge, Chrome, Mozilla Firefox). In this window, enter your mobile phone number and click "Получить код" (Get the code) button. Next, enter in the code from the SMS and click "Ввести код" (Confirm). Connection is complete
![](https://help.university.innopolis.ru/images_en/image_1699447350060.png)
## ¶ Connection for employees
Select "UniversityStaff" from the networks list.
![](https://help.university.innopolis.ru/images_en/image_1699447405972.png)
In "Username" and "Password" fields enter name of your domain account and University lnnopolis password, click "Join". Click "Trust" in the next appeared window. Connection is complete
![](https://help.university.innopolis.ru/images_en/image_1699447430780.png)
## ¶ Connection for students
Select "UniversityStudent" from the networks list.
![](https://help.university.innopolis.ru/images_en/image_1699447456890.png)
In "Username" and "Password" fields enter name of your domain account and University lnnopolis password, click "Join". Click "Trust" in the next appeared window. Connection is complete.
![](https://help.university.innopolis.ru/images_en/image_1699447478716.png)

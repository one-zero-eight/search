## Development of a hardware-software complex for ensuring movement on smooth and rough terrain of anthropomorphic robotic systems with variable stiffness drives
![](https://robotics.innopolis.university/wp-content/uploads/2019/05/Klimchik-Aleksandr-300x300.jpg)
Alexandr Klimchick
project lead
| Partners: | Androidnaya Technika SPA |
| --- | --- |
| Financing: | FTP |
| Laboratory: | [Laboratory of mechatronics, control and prototyping](https://robotics.innopolis.university/en/?post_type=labs&p=98) |
| Status: | Завершен |
The scientific research objectives: 1. Development of a universal modular cross-platform software and hardware system for ensuring smooth and rough terrain anthropomorphic robotic systems with variable stiffness drives. 2. Development of a hardware-software complex for an interactive anthropomorphic robotic platform and its interaction with humans. 3. Development of a software package for the autonomous movement of an anthropomorphic robotic platform along a previously known and unknown dynamically changing rugged terrain.

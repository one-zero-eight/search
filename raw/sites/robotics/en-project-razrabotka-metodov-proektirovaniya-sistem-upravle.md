## Development of design methods for airship control systems, unified to their technical characteristics and executive mechanisms
![](https://robotics.innopolis.university/wp-content/uploads/2019/05/Roman-F.-278x300.jpg)
Roman Fedorenko
project lead
| Financing: | The Russian Federation President Grant |
| --- | --- |
| Laboratory: | [Autonomous Transportation Systems Lab](https://robotics.innopolis.university/en/?post_type=labs&p=96) |
| Status: | Завершен |
The purpose of the scientific research is to develop design methods for airship control systems that are unified to the technical characteristics and design of actuators, allowing to expand the scope of airship control systems and increase the reliability of their operation due to automatic reconfiguration in emergency conditions.

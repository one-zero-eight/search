## A series of public lectures will be presented at Innopolis University by Evgeniy Briskin
Evgeniy Briskin, Doctor of Physics and Mathematics, Professor, Head of the Department of Theoretical Mechanics (Volgograd State Technical University).
16.10.2019
Lecture 1
The dynamics of the controlled movement of mobile robots with cyclic walking motors
The problems of the program motion formation (by the example of the mobile robot «Octopus»)
Date/Time: October 17, 2:00 pm
Location: Innopolis University, room 321
Lecture 2
Oscillations of Mechanical systems (Part I)
Small oscillations of deformable solids as a part of various robotic systems. Methods for compiling differential equations of motion, methods for their analysis
Date/Time: October 18, 1:00 pm
Location: Innopolis University, room 321
Lecture 3
Oscillations of Mechanical systems (Part II)
A second part of the lecture on Deformable Solids Oscillations
Date/Time: October 21, 2:00 pm
Location: Innopolis University, room 300
Lecture 4
Oscillations of Mechanical systems (Part III)
A third part of the lecture on Deformable Solids Oscillations
Date/Time: October 22, 2:00 pm
Location: Innopolis University, room 321
Language of lectures – Russian

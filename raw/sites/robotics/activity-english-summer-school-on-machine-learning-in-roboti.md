## Summer School on Machine Learning in Robotics
August 3 — 16, 2020.
A unique opportunity to explore the fundamentals and practical applications of Machine Learning methods in Robotics
26.11.2019
The International Summer School on Machine learning in Robotics will highlight the powerful combination of Robotics and Machine learning which is opening
the door to entirely new automation possibilities.
When? August 3 — 16, 2020
Where? Innopolis, Russia
The keynote sessions of the school program aim to give a clear overview of ML methods in Robotics with a particular emphasis on
- Reinforcement Learning
- Deep Learning
- ML in Neuroscience
![](https://robotics.innopolis.university/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/IMG_7278-min-768x512.jpg)
One of the main goals of the Summer School on ML in Robotics is gaining practical experience to put ML theory into practice and make it more relevant for all participants.
We invite students, researchers and professionals to exchange ideas and to be a part of fascinating conversations on ML in Neuroscience and Robotics.
Summer school on ML lasts for two weeks but the knowledge you gain will last forever! Go ahead and press [Submit](http://events.innopolis.university/summerschool#rec128298167)!
If you have any questions, please contact our school organizer Alfiia Khabibulina, a.khabibulina@innopolis.ru
You can visit the page of the last International double summer school [«Robot’s cognition, perception and control»](https://university.innopolis.ru/en/research/summer-school-2019/),
which was held in June 2019

## Creation of a milling complex based on an industrial robotic arm FANUC
![](https://robotics.innopolis.university/wp-content/uploads/2019/05/Artur-SHimanovskij-1-300x300.jpg)
Artur Shimanovskiy
project lead
| Partners: | Fanuc |
| --- | --- |
| Laboratory: | [Industrial Robotics Lab](https://robotics.innopolis.university/en/labs/laboratoriya-promyshlennoj-robototehniki-zablokirovana-laboratoriya-promyshlennoj-robototehniki/) |
| Status: | Выполняется |
Project goal: creating a case, the use of which will make it possible to ensure the accuracy of an industrial robot comparable to the accuracy of modern CNC machines. Tasks:
- Provision of an automatic cycle for milling parts of complex prof;A
- Automated milling of the following materials: polystyrene, MDF polystyrene, wood, plastics, aluminum glass and carbon fiber. All materials whose hardness is lower than aluminum.
Scopes of a milling complex:
- Model sections of foundries
- Production of fiberglass parts
- Prototyping
- Production of equipment for vacuum molding of plastics
- Furniture production
- Art objects

Технологии робототехники

The diagram illustrates the interdisciplinary nature of robotics technology through a Venn diagram of four overlapping circles:

  • Механика (Mechanics) - Top circle (pinkish-red)
  • Электроника (Electronics) - Right circle (light green)
  • Управление (Control) - Bottom circle (light blue)
  • Программы (Programming) - Left circle (yellow)

The central intersection of all four circles is labeled Робототехника и мехатроника (Robotics and Mechatronics).

Associated technologies are indicated by lines pointing to specific regions:

  • Моделирование (Modeling) - Points to the intersection of Mechanics and Programming.
  • CAD/CAM системы (CAD/CAM systems) - Points to the intersection of Mechanics and Programming.
  • Электромеханика (Electromechanics) - Points to the intersection of Mechanics and Electronics.
  • Сенсорные системы и сервоприводы (Sensory systems and servomotors) - Points to the intersection of Mechanics and Electronics.
  • Симуляторы (Simulators) - Points to the intersection of Programming and Control.
  • Цифровое управление (Digital control) - Points to the intersection of Programming and Control.
  • Системы управления (Control systems) - Points to the intersection of Control and Electronics.
  • Микроконтроллеры (Microcontrollers) - Points to the intersection of Control and Electronics.

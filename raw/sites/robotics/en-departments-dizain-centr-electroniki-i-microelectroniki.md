## Department of the organization of educational and olympiad activities
Preparation and conduct of international and Russian competitions, schools and other educational activities in robotics. Maintenance of educational programs of a bachelor’s and master’s degree, postgraduate study. Specialists develop programs of additional education and retraining in robotics. Development of new educational programs.

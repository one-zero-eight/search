## Center for technologies in robotics and mechatronics components
The activities of the Center are aimed at conducting [scientific research](https://robotics.innopolis.university/science), developing [educational programs](https://robotics.innopolis.university/learn) in priority areas of robotics, and implementing industrial [projects](https://robotics.innopolis.university/projects) together with a technological [consortium](https://robotics.innopolis.university/consortium)

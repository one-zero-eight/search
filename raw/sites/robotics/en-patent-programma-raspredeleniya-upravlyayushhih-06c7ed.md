## The program of distribution of control actions on the executive mechanisms of the airship by the control method with forecasting
21.05.2019
The program is designed to calculate the distribution of airship controls of various designs using the forecasting control method and can be used as part of airship control systems. The program distributes the control actions by minimizing the generated functional, including an element that estimates the energy consumed and an element that estimates the error between the set and the achieved generalized force by the forecasting control method.

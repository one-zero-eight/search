## Career
Join our team. We create new solutions for complex technological problems, develop methods and algorithms for various fundamental and applied problems of robots application.
[Research Assistant / Research Engineer](https://robotics.innopolis.university/en/career/nauchnyj-sotrudnik-inzhener-issledovatel-tsentra-razvitiya-robototehniki/)
[Requirements: Higher engineering, physics or mathematics education Working knowledge in the fields of robotics, mechanics and control Good knowledge of physics and mathematics Good knowledge of algorithms, data structures, willingness to participate in the development of complex algorithms at the highest technical levels Ability to analyze algorithms’ performance on real hardware and their modification to (…)](https://robotics.innopolis.university/en/career/nauchnyj-sotrudnik-inzhener-issledovatel-tsentra-razvitiya-robototehniki/)
Requirements:
- Higher engineering, physics or mathematics education
- Working knowledge in the fields of robotics, mechanics and control
- Good knowledge of physics and mathematics
- Good knowledge of algorithms, data structures, willingness to participate in the development of complex algorithms at the highest technical levels
- Ability to analyze algorithms’ performance on real hardware and their modification to improve efficiency by taking into account the physical processes
- Programming skills in C ++ / Matlab / Python
- Knowledge of modern numerical methods
- Technical English skills
- Experience in writing technical texts in Russian and English
- Analytical mindset, ability to conduct research independently and as part of a team.
The following skills will boost your candidacy:
- Practical experience with robotic platforms (including drones and autonomous vehicles)
- Experience with robot control
- Post-graduate degree
- Knowledge of ROS
- Matlab / Simulink knowledge
- CAD experience
- Experience in control systems development for complex objects
- Knowledge of the fundamentals of robotics
- Control systems understanding and design
- Experience with machine vision
- Experience with microcontroller programming
- Experience in the development of software and user interfaces
- Development and writing of engineering and technical documentation
- Experience with writing technical and academic papers in English.
Working conditions and benefits
- Challenging research tasks and work with a team of ambitious young professionals
- Collaboration with highly qualified peers and colleagues in a variety of projects
- The organization’s commitment to invest in staff training and development
- Opportunities for personal and professional growth (the operational scale and scope of the Center will be continuously expanding as the current research objectives are met)
- Competitive salary and bonuses
- Registration under the Labor Code of the Russian Federation.
- Work in a modern and comfortable environment at the Innopolis University
- Young and friendly team
- Rich program of corporate events
- Unlimited tea and coffee!
If you have any questions regarding this position or you want to join is, but have not found suitable vacancy please attach your CV to

Main Responsibilities
- Conducting research in selected area
- Annual and future projects planning
- Formation of laboratory’s research objectives and expected results
- Academic mentorship in the framework of the laboratory research plan
- Monitoring and quality control of work conducted by the employees and research partners
- Ensuring practical implementation of research results, supervision and technical assistance
- Work with patent applications and licensing of scientific and technical inventions and ensuring the registration of inventions and innovation proposals
- Ensuring the efficiency of the unit and rational use of work force.
Qualifications
- PhD degree (Russian equivalent: Candidate or doctor of science)
- Strong publication record
- A minimum of 5 years work experience in research and management
- Excellent knowledge of the subject area
- Contract negotiations and management skills when collaborating with other institutions
- Experience with patent applications and technical documentation
- Upper-intermediate English level.
Working conditions and benefits
- Challenging research tasks and work with a team of ambitious young professionals
- Environment created with the aim to make an impact on robotics worldwide
- Collaboration with highly qualified peers and colleagues in a variety of projects
- The organization’s commitment to invest in staff training and development
- Opportunities for personal and professional growth
- Competitive salary and bonuses
- Modern comfortable office in the Innopolis University
- Corporate discount for the sports center
- Private Medical Insurance coverage
- Registration under the Labor Code of the Russian Federation.

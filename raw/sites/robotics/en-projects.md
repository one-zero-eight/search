## Research and development
The Center implements federal and commercial projects in the technology development for robotics, digitalization of production, health care and education. Solutions are based on the technologies of anthropomorphic and industrial robotics, mixed and augmented reality, unmanned ground and aerial vehicles, artificial intelligence.

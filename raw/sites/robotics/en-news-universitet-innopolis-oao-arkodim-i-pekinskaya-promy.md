## Innopolis University, ARKODIM OJSC and Beijing Industrial and IT Technology Transfer Company signed a Memorandum of Understanding
During a visit to China as a part of the First Sino-Russian Innovation and Entrepreneurship Competition, Innopolis University, ARKODIM OJSC and the Beijing Entech Industrialization and Information Technology Transfer Company signed the Memorandum of Understanding.
30.09.2019
The Chinese side was represented by the General Manager of the Beijing Entech Industrialization and Information Technology Transfer Company Qiang Wang, the Russian side was represented by Head of the Center for Technologies in Robotics and Mechatronics Components (Innopolis University) Alexander Klimchik. The document execution took place in Beijing in the “House of Friendship”.
![](https://robotics.innopolis.university/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/Kitaj-768x576.jpg)
“The signed agreement with the Beijing Industrial and IT- Technology transfer company allows us to start building relations with [China](https://robotics.innopolis.university/news/sovmestnyj-proekt-tsentra-tehnologij-komponentov-robototehniki-i-mehatroniki-i-ooo-arkodim-stal-finalistom-konkursa-criec/) and more closely engage in the search for investors and end consumers for our products. We got an invitation from our new partners to an exhibition in the Celestial Empire, where we will get the abilities to have meetings with companies, investors and government organizations, we are looking for consolidation of strengthening of our contractual relationship”, told Professor Klimchik.

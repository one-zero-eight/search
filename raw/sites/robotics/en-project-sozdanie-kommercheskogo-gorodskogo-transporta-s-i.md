## Creation of commercial urban transport with the City Pilot intelligent driver assistance system
![](https://robotics.innopolis.university/wp-content/uploads/2019/05/Klimchik-Aleksandr-300x300.jpg)
Alexandr Klimchick
project lead
| Partners: | KAMAZ |
| --- | --- |
| Financing: | FTP |
| Laboratory: | [Autonomous Transportation Systems Lab](https://robotics.innopolis.university/en/?post_type=labs&p=96) |
| Status: | Завершен |
The aim of the project is to create a family of electric urban smart commercial vehicles with the City Pilot driver assistance system to reduce emissions of harmful substances into the atmosphere in an urban environment and to reduce the number of accidents and the severity of the consequences of accidents. The uniqueness of this project lies in the design methodology of both the carrier system of the car and the driver assistance systems ADAS.

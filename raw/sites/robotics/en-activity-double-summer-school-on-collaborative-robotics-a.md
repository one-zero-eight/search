## Double summer school on Collaborative Robotics and Soft Engineering
The summer school aims to get together ambition Master and PhD students, PostDocs and young researchers with a background in Robotics or Software Engineering.
06.08.2018
The summer school aims to get together ambition Master and PhD students, PostDocs and young researchers with a background in Robotics or Software Engineering. The working language of the Summer School is English.
[▶ video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_lNjekTbDIE)
Representatives from Italy, Great Britain, Spain, Israel, Pakistan, India, Syria, Sri Lanka and Russia took part in the Double Summer School 2018.

## Teaching staff
Education is facilitated by world-class experts and practitioners participating in a plethora of national and international projects in the fields of robotics and mechatronics. This way, the practical experience of solving advanced scientific and applied challenges is incorporated into educational programs and approaches. Over 15 researchers of the Center are teaching 30+ courses.

## Development of a system for optimizing warehouse processes based on mixed reality
![](https://robotics.innopolis.university/wp-content/uploads/2019/05/Mihail-Ostanin-2-300x300.jpg)
Mikhail Ostanin
project lead
| Financing: | Innovation promotion fund |
| --- | --- |
| Laboratory: | [Industrial Robotics Lab](https://robotics.innopolis.university/en/labs/laboratoriya-promyshlennoj-robototehniki-zablokirovana-laboratoriya-promyshlennoj-robototehniki/) |
| Status: | Выполняется |
The purpose of the research work is the development of a system for assisting warehouse workers based on mixed reality technologies to increase the efficiency of logistics processes. Areas of application: receiving and placing goods, assembling goods in a warehouse and packing them in a parcel, and verifying goods. This system will be in demand at the warehouses of logistics companies, online stores, as well as large factories.

## Club League
Compete, grow, and earn rewards for your club's achievements
## Club League Aims
**1.** To determine the most active, initiative and successful student societies at Innopolis University, as well as to reward them
**2.** To promote the IU by contributing to the development of the educational institution and the formation of a strong student community
**3.** To integrate competition element into student clubs' life in order to stimulate such clubs to grow and set targets
**4.** To raise student clubs' prestige and promote them within student contingent and in outer environment, as well as to address strategic importance of non-formal education at Innopolis University
**5.** To teach student club leaders to run reports
## Competition Principles
The main competition principle lies in sportsmanship, where each competitor fairly assesses its correspondence to each criteria, respects opponents and crediting decision-making.
Report form will be published to Club Leaders telegram group every month, according to which points will be credited.
## Priority Order in Crediting Decision Making
- Criteria from the official table
- In controversial situations decision is made by [@T_Konovalov](https://telegram.me/T_Konovalov)
- In difficult situations decision is made by group vote in Club Leaders telegram group

## Important Rules
- •Budget must be spent before the end of the semester
- •Clubs get points only for activities related to their field (except criterion 16)
- •Every criterion requires proof
- •Unspent points from last semester expire
## Club Purchases
## What Clubs Can Purchase
- Equipment
- Workshops, lectures and other related services
- Merch of the club
- Other materials related to the activities of the club
Example: Badminton club can buy shuttlecocks, but cannot buy a flower pot.
Buying food for events is prohibited now.
## Purchase Process
**1.** Purchases are made only 2 times per semester
**2.** The club manager collects orders from the club leaders in advance, and later makes a purchase in the form of an official order
**3.** If the purchase is non-standard and doesn't correspond to the activities of the club, you have to write a justification for the purchase and send it to [@T_Konovalov](https://telegram.me/T_Konovalov)

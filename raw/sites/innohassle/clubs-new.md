## Create Your Own Community
Have an idea for a club? Start your own community and make a difference at Innopolis University!
## Why Create a Club?
## Learn More About Yourself
Joining a student organization contributes to your self-awareness and presents opportunities to learn more about yourself, your goals, and your strengths.
## Develop Soft Skills
Learn and improve essential people skills like communication, teamwork, and work ethic through real-world experience.
## Networking Opportunities
Meet new students, make connections, and build relationships that will help you down the line when looking for a job.
## Gain Practical Experience
Get hands-on experience in your field of study in a safe environment where making mistakes is OK.
## Engage with Diverse Groups
Learn how different people respond to situations and develop your skills in presenting and implementing ideas.
## Expand Your CV
Show employers that you're ambitious, broad-minded, and an active person who takes advantage of opportunities.
## Give Back to the University
Set standards and build future legacy by being an active member of the campus community.
## Have Fun!
Meet new people, make friends, and participate in activities that make your university experience memorable.
## Club Leader Benefits
### Innopoints Rewards
Apply for innopoints at the end of every semester via the Innopoints Website.
### Regular Schedule & Resources
Request rooms, projectors, sports halls, and other resources for your club's meetings.
### Guest Passes
Get access passes for club members who need entry to IU main building.
### Club League
Compete in the Club League to earn higher positions and more benefits.
### Semestral Budget
Get budget allocation based on your Club League position.
### Higher Scholarship
Top 20 clubs earn points for higher scholarship. Top 3 can nominate 3 active members.
## Club Types
When registering your club, you'll need to choose one of the following categories. Each type has specific criteria in the Club League.
## Technical
Clubs focused on technology, programming, engineering, robotics, and other technical disciplines.
Examples: Programming Languages, Robotics, Web Development, AI/ML
## Sport
Clubs centered around physical activities, sports, and fitness. Can give sport hours for trainings if accredited on InnoSport.
Examples: Football, Basketball, Volleyball, Badminton, Yoga
## Art & Culture
Clubs dedicated to artistic expression, cultural activities, and creative pursuits.
Examples: Music, Theater, Photography, Painting, Dance
## Special Interest
Clubs focused on hobbies, special interests, and community activities that don't fit other categories.
Examples: Board Games, Languages, Debate, Movies
## How to Register Your Club
Step 1: Find Your Team
You need at least two responsible clubmates to set up a club. Think about positions like communications officer, marketing assistant, treasurer, etc.
Step 2: Create Telegram Group
Set up a Telegram group for your club and invite all your friends who are interested in joining!
Step 3: Define Goals & Mission
Think about your aims and goals to keep your club going purposefully. Consider writing mission and vision statements to clarify your club's purpose.
Step 4: Prepare Club Materials
Create an avatar or logo for your club.
Step 5: Fill Registration Form
Complete the official registration form with all required information about your club. You'll need to provide:
- Full and short official names
- Club description and goals
- Logo/avatar file
- Telegram group link
- Leader's contact information

## Innopolis University: federal boot camp training in Olympiad Robotics is now finished
26 августа 2015
The list of teams approved for World Robot Olympiad is available
![](https://media.innopolis.university/upload/iblock/386/_MG_3490.JPG)
Recently, the official closing ceremony of the Federal Boot Camp Training in Olympiad Robotics took place in a new Russian city, Innopolis. The event was marked with summing up of results of the boot camp training carried out during 10 days in modern Educational & Research Complex of Innopolis University, the first Russian university focused on modern information technologies. Sergey Masyagin, Vice-Provost for Pre-University Education of Innopolis University gave a commencement speech wishing the participants honorable presentation of their projects at the World Olympiad and getting golden award. After that, the jury announced selection results for Russian national team to participate in World Robot Olympiad that will take place in Doha, Qatar.
During the boot camp, more than 150 Robotic experts from 18 regions of the Russian Federation from Krasnoyarsk Krai to Murmansk Oblast had been training under supervision of the best Russian teachers.

## Assistant Professor Ilya Afanasyev took part in the International Conference on Advanced Concepts for Intelligent Vision System
29 октября 2015
<p>The conference was focused on techniques for building adaptive, intelligent, safe and secure imaging systems.</p>
![](https://media.innopolis.university/upload/iblock/8fa/photo_2015-11-12_09-39-57.jpg)
[International Conference on Advanced Concepts for Intelligent Vision Systems](http://acivs.org/acivs2015/) (ACIVS) was held in Museo Diocesano in Catania, Italy, 26-29 October 2015. The event brought together leading researchers and engineers, which work in the field of computer vision and video processing. Techniques for building adaptive, intelligent, safe and secure imaging systems were the key issues that were discussed at the conference.
Members of Intelligent Robotic Systems Laboratory Ilya Afanasyev, Artur Sagitov and Evgeni Magid prepared a research paper «[ROS-based SLAM for a Gazebo-simulated mobile robot in image-based 3D model of indoor environment](http://acivs.org/acivs2015/AbstractList.php#186)», which was presented at the Conference by Assistant Professor Ilya Afanasyev. The proceedings of ACIVS 2015 will be published by Springer in the [Lecture Notes in Computer Science](http://www.springeronline.com/lncs) series and the manuscript abstract is available [here](http://acivs.org/acivs2015/AbstractList.php#186).
“While the title of the conference is not a pure robotics and automation systems conference, at least a quarter of the reports described computer vision related issues, which are crucial for robotics: vision for mobile ground robots in simultaneous localization and mapping (SLAM), vision for drones in video surveillance systems, vision in manufacturing scenarios and in banking equipment, etc. Most of the reports presented new methods of image and video processing for different applications, such as motion tracking, object recognition and classification, 3D and depth analysis issues, biometrics, etc. Another interesting feature of the conference was the geography of the participants: while the majority came from Italy, France and Belgium, I was pleased to attend the reports of Russian speakers, e.g. of the Moscow State University representatives”, - shared his impressions Ilya Afanasyev.

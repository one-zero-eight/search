## Innopolis University Master’s students will develop projects for the leading national IT-companies
17 декабря 2015
Graduates of Innopolis University will develop products and solutions for Ak Bars Bank, I-Teco, Dnevnik.ru, Runa Capital, SKB-Kontur, Visiology and Acronis.
![](https://media.innopolis.university/upload/iblock/7eb/IMG_0392.jpg)
An industrial project serves as a basis of the MSIT-SE Master`s degree program. During two semesters (from January until July) students will work on projects suggested by IT-companies. Tasks will include the full software development lifecycle, engineering of architectural solutions, development of system technical design and various tests. The main goal of a training project is not to create a software program, but to apply the knowledge and skills gained at Innopolis University in practice. Besides, all students of Innopolis University MSIT-SE program already have a work experience in the IT-sector. They will be divided into seven teams according to the requirements of each project. Teams will work under supervision of mentors assigned by the industrial partner that submitted the project, and representatives of the international faculty of Innopolis University. Presentation of project results is scheduled for the end of summer 2016 instead of the common graduate project defense procedure.
It is noteworthy that topics of training projects were subject to strict requirements: only one third of 30 proposals from different companies was presented to students. Recently industrial partners of Innopolis University had a meeting where they presented eleven projects in detail. The companies told about main goals, important aspects and expected work results. It shall be noted that company projects belong to different fields.
Thus, a mobile application for easy money transfer between bank cards will be developed for Ak Bars Bank. Innopolis Master’s degree students will also work on further evolution and refinement of the software and hardware complex “EC2015-Terem” for the I-Teco company. In addition, they will develop the Swift API component for the Acronis Cloud Storage system as requested by Acronis, a provider of backup and data recovery services. For the global educational network Dnevnik.ru students will deliver a user-friendly mobile app for the offline work. In the framework of the SKB-Kontur project, students will design a web-service for supporting documentation and record-keeping. In the context of cooperation with Visiology, students will design a tool that enables loading and conversion of data received from an enterprise accounting system and transfers them to the analytic data warehouse. Speaking of the project by the Runa Capital venture capital firm, students will build a platform enabling data search regarding the best international startups. This platform will make an intelligent analysis based on the rules established by the company.
Alesh Zhivkovich, Head of the Master`s degree program in Software Engineering (MSIT-SE) of Innopolis University said: “In order to assess success of this learning concept, we need to understand its goals that are twofold and concern business and education.”
Professor of the IT-University emphasized: “The business goals are: relatively inexpensive workforce, access to well-trained software engineering professionals, insight into current best practices in software development, opportunity to test different solutions and try various development methods ”
Thereafter, the researcher described academic goals of the program as “learning on real problems, interacting with industry clients, integration with the MSIT-SE core courses, opportunity to try different approaches and reflect on the experience, opportunity to fail and learn from the experience.” He also pointed out that the mentioned goals can be achieved in every project only partially. In most cases, success depends on the right combination of the project, student team and mentor.
Dmitry Varenov, Chief of the Department of Software Solutions for Computing Complexes at the I-Teco company said:
«We are very pleased that students got interested in our industrial project. In cooperation with young experts we are planning to develop a new management interface for a corporate virtualization platform and Linux Servers lifecycle management. The platform called “ES2015-Terem” was designed by the “I-Teco” company”. We expect young experts to take a fresh look at the product development and introduce new ideas and solutions. From our side, we will try to give our young teammates a free hand to create and choose enabling technologies.”
It`s good to know that a new generation of young IT-experts, who study at the leading domestic universities, pay attention to projects aimed at the development of open, free software, designed as part of an open community of software developers. We hope for a fruitful cooperation with our team and we are likely to continue working together even after the industrial project is completed.”
Ivan Vakhmyanin, CEO of Visiology:
“Visiology develops software for data visualization and analysis. It’s a modern and dynamic field in a desperate need for experts. For us cooperation with Innopolis University, first of all, is an opportunity to find promising young employees and new ideas. We suggested students to solve a practice-oriented problem i.e. to implement a new concept of the ETL-model (data load tool) for the Visiology analytical platform. For sure, it will be a simplified model. However, we are eager to understand the developers’ mode of thinking and learn what new creative solutions they can find. I will consider it as a great victory on the part of students if at least some of solutions can be put into practice.
Speaking of students, they got a chance to try their hand in data storage and analysis which is one of the most in-demand areas on the IT market. In future, this experience will help them to deal with corporate information systems like ERP, BI, EPM etc. Besides, we will give them an opportunity to deploy the designed system in a test mode as part of a real project along with current industrial solution.
At the test deployment stage, students will work under supervision of our developers. I believe that for young professionals this communication is the best way get a real teamwork experience. As for the company, it’s a chance to see their potential”.

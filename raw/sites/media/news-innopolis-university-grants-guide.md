### «Innopraktika», Joint institute for nuclear research (JINR, Dubna), European Organization for Nuclear Research (CERN, Geneva)
[International School on IT and GRID Technologies for young scientists](http://innopraktika.ru/news/481/?sphrase_id=2747)
- Selection for participation in the international school is conducted in two stages:
- You need to send a [completed application](https://yadi.sk/i/v_9eB7XSgMQA7) form in English to grants manager of Innopolis University by e-mail: a.pavlov@innopolis.ru until 10 June, 2015.
- According to the results of internal selection the composition of potential participants in international school will be determined, which until 15 June, 2015 will take part in the selection organized by "Innopraktika"
- Target group: students, graduate students and young scientists
- Venue: Tivat, Montenegro
- Date of the event: September 28 - October 2, 2015

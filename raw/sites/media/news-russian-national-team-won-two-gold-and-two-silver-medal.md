## Russian national team won two gold and two silver medals at the World Robot Olympiad
9 ноября 2015
The main competition in robotics hosted by the capital of Qatar is now completed.
![](https://media.innopolis.university/upload/iblock/9cb/_MG_1128.JPG)
Russian teams won four sets of awards i.e. two gold ones in the Regular Junior and in the Senior Open Category, and two silver medals in the Elementary Open Category and the University Challenge at the 12th World Robot Olympiad — WRO, held in Qatar on November 6 – 8. Having lost the competition for the Third place in the Gen II Football to the fellow players from Hong Kong, the Russian team finished the fourth. Besides, Russian schoolchildren took positions from the sixth to the eighth in the Open Category, and settled on the eighth position in the Regular Junior group. A project of the team from Saint Petersburg was marked with a special Creativity Award.
Results of the Russian teams are as follows.
1st place:
- Regular Category (Junior High): “Treasure Hunt”, the Dzeta, Moscow, coach — Sergey Mustafin;
- Open Category (Senior): the BURITOS, Saint Petersburg, coach — Igor Lositsky.
2nd place:
- Open Category (Elementary): the Robofriends, Saint Petersburg, coach — Sergey Fillipov;
- University Challenge (“Bowling”): the PML30-students, Saint Petersburg, coaches — Dmitry and Ekaterina Luzins
Creativity Award is a special prize for technical complexity of the project:
- The DRL, Saint Petersburg, coach — Sergey Fillipov
4th place:
- Gen II Football: the Hexagon, Moscow, coach — Sergey Mustafin.
“Congratulations to our national team that has shown great results at the major international robot competitions, — said Nikolay Nikiforov the Minister of Telecom and Mass Communication of Russia. — This is an advanced field of scientific and technical art, and I am glad that Russian young people have proved again how skillfully they can tackle most challenging engineering tasks. The number of awards and scores is not crucial. It is important that our Olympians develop in the fields of radio electronics and programming, graduate from Russian universities and become leaders of domestic ICT companies. They represent the national elite and our golden personnel reserve. We shall train as many highly skilled experts in the main areas of ICT as possible, because the central point of each project is a human, an engineer”.
Our country was represented by the winners of the [Russian](http://minsvyaz.ru/ru/events/33423/) stage of the World Robot Olympiad 2015, which welcomed more than 800 children from 43 regions of Russia and was held at Innopolis University in the Republic of Tatarstan.
Source – [Ministry of Telecom and Mass Communication of the Russian Federation](http://www.minsvyaz.ru/ru/events/34281/)
Photo: Sergey Mustafin
The World Robot Olympiad was launched in 2004. Every year more than 20 thousand teams from over 50 countries of the world take part in the WRO competitions. In 2014 the WRO finals were first held in Russia, in Sochi. Innopolis University is the operator of the Russian stage of the World Robot Olympiad. Since 2014 it coordinates regional stages that welcome over eight thousand of school- and university students. The University provides training for the judge committee, develops and adjusts the international Olympiad rules and manages the Russian stage. For the two years in a row the teams showing the top results at the Russian stage of the World Robot Olympiad undergo the intensive training and additional selection for the Federal Training Boot Camp held at Innopolis University. The national team of the Russian Federation is formed considering these results.

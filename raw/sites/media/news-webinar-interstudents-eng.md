## Webinar for international candidates: how to enter Innopolis University
17 ноября 2020
Ekaterina Protsko, the Head of Admission Office in Russian IT-University will hold 5 webinars and tell about admission campaign for international candidates. At the second part of the webinar listeners will be able to ask their questions. Language: English.
![](https://media.innopolis.university/upload/iblock/5e7/1764213C-C438-4C52-BF75-E5D42B4FE719.jpeg)
“Webinar is the opportunity for our team to talk with applicants. Besides a short presentation about conditions, scholarship and benefits of the University, I will explain that the enrollment to Innopolis is real, you just need to go through several steps, which we will discuss in detail. We received 12 896 applications from 161 countries this year. It’s clear that due to the pandemic situation most of those, who would like to participate in Scholarship Contest, didn’t apply as it’s difficult to decide to study abroad. We will discuss it is too: how to study in the mixed format, how to take exams, how to get a Scholarship from your home country”, - explains Ekaterina Protsko.

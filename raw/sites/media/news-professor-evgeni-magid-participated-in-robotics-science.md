## Professor Evgeni Magid participated in “Robotics: Science and Systems 2015” Conference
20 июля 2015
The event took place at the Sapienza University of Rome
![](https://media.innopolis.university/upload/iblock/d92/111.jpg)
Professor Evgeni Magid took part in [Robotics: Science and Systems (RSS) 2015 Conference](http://www.roboticsconference.org/), [Principles of Multi-Robot Systems Workshop](http://mrs-rss2015.sciencesconf.org/) and [Human­Robot Hand-Over Workshop](http://how.sciencesconf.org/resource/page/id/3) at the [Sapienza University of Rome](http://en.uniroma1.it/) on July 12–17, 2015. The event brought together researchers working on algorithmic and mathematical foundations of robotics, robotics applications, and analysis of robotic systems. The program included invited talks as well as oral and poster presentations of refereed papers.
Professor Magid had meetings with the Head of [Autonomous Navigation and Perception Laboratory (ANPL)](http://vindelman.net.technion.ac.il/) Assistant Professor Vadim Indelman ([Technion](http://www.technion.ac.il/), Israel), and the representatives of [PAL Robotics](http://pal-robotics.com/en) (Spain) Luca Marchionni and Hilario Tome where the opportunities of extending mutually beneficial collaborations were discussed.
Photo: Evgeni Magid and Vadim Indelman at Principles of Multi-Robot Systems Workshop

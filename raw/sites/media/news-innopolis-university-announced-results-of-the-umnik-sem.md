## Innopolis University announced results of the UMNIK semi-finals
29 октября 2015
24 IT projects were presented at the event.
![](https://media.innopolis.university/upload/iblock/b96/image24088955.jpg)
The [“UMNIK”](http://umnik.fasie.ru/) program of the Fund for Assistance to Small Innovative Enterprises provides a two-year funding in the amount of 400,000 rubles for the most promising development projects in five major areas: Information Technology, Future Medicine, Modern Materials, New Appliances and Hardware Complexes, and Biotechnologies. The aim of the Program is to identify and encourage young scholars willing to fulfil their potential in the sphere of science and technology.
The jury selected 10 projects to participate in the finals on November 18 at [КNIAT](http://www.xn--80apfm1a.xn--p1ai/) High-Technology Industrial Park.

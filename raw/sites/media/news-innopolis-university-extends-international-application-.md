## Innopolis University extends international application deadline till May, 13
30 апреля 2019
Apply to participate in the scholarship competition and win a scholarship that covers up to 100 % of the tuition fees and covers monthly allowance for Bachelor’s and Master’s degree programs at the Russian IT University.
![](https://media.innopolis.university/upload/iblock/e5c/deadline_extended.jpg)
## International scholarship application deadline (Fall-2019 entry) has been extended till May, 13.
Innopolis University offers five Computer Science programs: one Bachelor’s and four Master’s: [Data Science, Software Engineering, Robotics, Secure System and Network Engineering](https://apply.innopolis.ru/en/master/). The programs descriptions and entry requirements are available at [https://apply.Innopolis.ru/en/it-degree/](https://apply.innopolis.ru/en/it-degree/).
## Scholarship competition steps
Step 1: register your application through Innopolis university application portal [https://apply.Innopolis.ru/en/get-in/](https://apply.innopolis.ru/en/get-in/) . Fill in the application form, prepare CV, motivation letter, project description (required) and letters of recommendation (optional), pass online tests and submit your application.
Step 2: get invitation to the Skype interviews with IU professor and HR expert.
Step 3: pass IU English language test or provide a certificate of proficiency in English (IELTS Academic, TOEFL certificates, etc.).
Step 4: receive the interview results and the scholarship offer.
Step 5: provide your enrollment and visa documents and come to Innopolis in mid-August.
For your convenience we have prepared an extended [Guidelines section](https://apply.innopolis.ru/en/get-in/guidelines/) on your online application.
You are very welcome to join IU official social media pages on [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/InnopolisU/), [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/innopolisu/), [Youtube](https://www.youtube.com/user/InnopolisU), [Twitter](https://twitter.com/InnopolisU/status/974558530815500288) and [Linkedin](https://www.linkedin.com/company/innopolis-university/) to learn more about student's daily life, persons and events.
Should you have any questions please feel free to contact us at admissions@Innopolis.ru or via chat room on your application page.

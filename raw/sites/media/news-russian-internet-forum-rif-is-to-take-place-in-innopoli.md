## Russian Internet Forum (RIF) is to take place in Innopolis.
29 мая 2015
A new Russian IT city will host a regional stage of RIF.
![](https://media.innopolis.university/upload/iblock/92d/unnamed.png)
The regional stage of RIF is to take place in Innopolis city on June 9-10, 2015. The participants of the event will discuss the new digital economy and trends of the Russian Web (RuNet). The conference is a significant part of RIF.Region, a project that is entirely devoted to the internet industry and digital economy.
The conference will consist of three parts. The first one is the Session of the Committee on Modernization of the Russian economy where participants will discuss the current state of the internet industry in Russia and share ideas on its development. The second part will constitute the main RIF proceedings. Major topics of the conference are:
· RuNet today: events, numbers, facts, trends
· RuNet’s economy
· infrastructure of the RuNet: Overcoming the digital divide
· E-Business and development of RuNet’s ecosystems: e-commerce, advertising, remunerated services, web-development, mobile technologies, digital marketing etc.
· The law of the Internet: current state and new trends
· Investment and start-ups, young entrepreneurs
· Training of IT-specialists
The last past is OS DAY 2015 - Operating System Research and Development Conference.
The conference will be held at a new campus of Innopolis University. RIF will be one of the key events in the opening of the new Russian university. The organizers plan to conduct RIF.Innopolis annually.
Registration for the conference: [http://region.rif.ru/events/innopolis/](http://region.rif.ru/events/innopolis/)

## Innopolis University announced the four new academic exchange partners
19 октября 2015
The University has established cooperation with the universities from Hong Kong, Italy, Turkey and Spain.
![](https://media.innopolis.university/upload/iblock/7e4/_MG_6971.jpg)
In the near future, starting from the academic year 2016/2017, Russian students will be able to get a unique experience of studies at one of the top universities of the world. The University signed agreements on academic exchange with the Middle East Technical University (Ankara, Turkey), Polytechnic University of Milan (Milan, Italy), Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Hong Kong) and Polytechnic University of Catalonia (Barcelona, Spain).
Organization of academic exchange programs for students in cooperation with partner universities is one of the major tasks of the international strategy of Innopolis University. “Spending one or two semesters abroad has many advantages: acquisition of new knowledge and experience due to foreign academic environment, introduction to global tendencies and approaches, cross-cultural exchange and consolidation of international relations along with creation of international ambience”, - as Elmira Khalitova, Head of International Relations of Innopolis University. She is sure that students of the partner universities will also find interesting opportunities for their personal and professional growth at the Russian IT University.
Head of International Relations mentioned the current international agreements and assured there will be more partners in future. “Expanding horizons of our students is a key to successful development of a world-class university”, - summed up Elmira Khalitova.
## Reference Information
### [1. Middle East Technical University](http://www.metu.edu.tr/)
Over 1 500 students from 80 countries of the world come to study at this university every year.
English is the official language of instruction.
Department of Computer Engineering of the university received the following awards for supporting the international educational standards: the Silver Badge of Honor awarded by UNESCO in 1977 and the Meritorious Achievement Award in Accreditation Activities issued by the Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers (IEEE).
The country code top-level domain (".tr") is administered by the Middle East Technical University.
[METU-Technopolis](http://www.odtuteknokent.com.tr/tr) is the first high-technology park in Turkey and a project of the Middle East Technical University. It hosts, in particular, over 150 IT companies.
Prominent alumni: Ahmet Bozer – President of The Coca Cola Company; Kadir Semih Incedayi - Chief Executive Officer of Turkcell Technology (the leading mobile operator of Turkey); Erdal Inonu - former Prime-Minister of Turkey.
[Times Higher Education World University Ranking 2015 – #85](https://www.timeshighereducation.co.uk/world-university-rankings/middle-east-technical-university?ranking-dataset=1083)
[Times Higher Education, Engineering &amp; Technology 2015 – #99](https://www.timeshighereducation.co.uk/world-university-rankings/middle-east-technical-university?ranking-dataset=1084)
### [2. Polytechnic University of Milan](http://www.metu.edu.tr/)
The largest technical university of Italy. All Master’s degree programs are taught in English.
Professors of the university earned various awards. The most prominent among them is Giulio Natta, the Nobel laureate in Chemistry (1963).
The first Italian nuclear reactor was launched and used by PoliMI.
According to the opinion poll held in 2007, 80% of alumni are employed within 3 months after graduation. Over 95% of young specialists find jobs within 6 months after receiving diplomas.
Every fifth Master student comes from abroad.
[QS Computer Science and Information Systems 2015 – #43](http://www.topuniversities.com/university-rankings/university-subject-rankings/2015/computer-science-information-systems#sorting=rank+region=+country=+faculty=+stars=false+search=di%20mila)
[Times Higher Education, Engineering and Technology – #86](https://www.timeshighereducation.com/world-university-rankings/polytechnic-university-of-milan?ranking-dataset=1084)
### [3. Hong Kong University of Science and Technology](http://www.metu.edu.tr/)
Established in 1991, the university is the youngest higher educational institution in Hong Kong. During 2011 – 2013, QS University Rankings, one of the most influential rating agencies, recognized HKUST as the top higher educational institution of Asia. In 2015, QS University Rankings included HKUST in the list of the top 10 universities in the field of Computer Science and Information Systems. 100% of the faculty members have РhD degrees obtained, in most cases, from Oxford, MIT, Carnegie Mellon, Stanford, Princeton, Harvard, Yale and other highly authoritative universities of the world.
[QS World University Ranking 2014/2015 – #28](http://www.topuniversities.com/university-rankings/world-university-rankings/2015#sorting=rank+region=+country=+faculty=+stars=false+search=hong%20kong%20uni)
[QS Computer Science and Information Systems 2015 – #8](http://www.topuniversities.com/university-rankings/university-subject-rankings/2015/computer-science-information-systems#sorting=rank+region=+country=+faculty=+stars=false+search=hong%20kong%20uni)
[QS University Rankings: Asia 2015 – #2](http://www.topuniversities.com/university-rankings/asian-university-rankings/2015#sorting=rank+region=+country=+faculty=+stars=false+search=hong%20kong%20uni)
### [4. Polytechnic University of Catalonia](http://www.metu.edu.tr/)
The largest technical university in Catalonia, Spain.
Internationalization is one of the key priorities of the university development. Thus, for instance, the vast majority of the international post-graduate and Master’s students in Spain study at this university. The overall number of international students amounts to 28%. Though Polytechnic University was founded not long ago, it is widely recognized.

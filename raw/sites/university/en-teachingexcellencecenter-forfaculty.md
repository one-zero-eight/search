## Education Process FAQ
Where do I get room keys?
Reception ground floor, near Cava.
Where do I get stationery?
310 room
What are the possible events of the course?
- lectures
- tutorials
- labs (seminars)
- office hours
- exams
- retakes
How do I print materials?
There are two printers for professors on the 4th floor across from 470. Inquiry with IT to get set up (contacts)
What are the course materials?
- for lectures: slides
- for tutorials: slides, code examples
- for labs: slides, handouts, problem sets, assignments description, reference solutions for the problems
- for homeworks: problem sets, reference solutions, for the problems, additional reference materials
- for exams: problem sets, reference solutions for the problems
What should I do in case of cheating in my course?
Please refer to the certain chapter in Teaching Regulation
What is Moodle?
- It is a learning management system that has to be maintained for each course. Moodle is good for:
- Publishing lecture slides and assignments
- Releasing grades
- Calculating course grades
- - Reading students' feedback on the course
- - Track students' attendance
LMS Moodle quick start-up links
Who can help with Moodle?
Please refer to useful contacts
Who can help with IT equipment?
Please refer to useful contacts
Who provides the material for the course lectures and labs?
PI is responsible for everything. Many times courses come with a legacy material that has to be adapted from year to year. TAs may help with labs and tutorials preparation.
How many lectures are there in a regular course?
Semester long course : 15 weeks = 15 lectures + 15 labs or/and tutorials + exams
Half-semester block : 8-9 weeks = 15 lectures + 15 labs or/and tutorials + exams
What is the grading duration policy?
1 week is a great target to release notes for an assignment
Is there a Fall/Spring break?
Please refer to academic calendar
Where do I find information about the academic year, including exams?
Please refer to Academic Calendar
What options for teleconference solutions are provided by IU?
MS Teams, Google Meet, LMS Moodle

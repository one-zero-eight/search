## Ramazan Bolat
International Information Technology University, Kazakhstan
Fall Semester 2021/22
## Знакомство с Иннополисом
I have picked Innopolis because it was recommended to me by my brother who studies there. As expected, the University is advanced and the studies are challenging.
The learning process was quite unusual for me personally, since at our university classes are divided into days, while in Innopolis almost all classes in one course are held in one day. In the course of the program, I passed 3 exams, 1 test, 1 project presentation and 1 written exam. In general, I am satisfied by the results of the exams.

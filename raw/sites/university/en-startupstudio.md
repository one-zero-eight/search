## INDUSTRIAL INTERUNIVERSITY ACCELERATOR «STARTUPHOUSE»
186 participants
35 universities and colleges
We invited 50 student startup ideas from all over Russia and pumped them on an offline accelerator. As a result of the intensive, 13 projects were selected and presented to investors.
The key goal of the Accelerator is to test hypotheses and package the product to attract investment. We have assembled a cool team of curators, experts and investors, added an educational block and informal communication with specialists from different companies.

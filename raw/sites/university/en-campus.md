## LIVING AND STUDYING AT THE UNIVERSITY
RESIDENTIAL VENUE SPORTS VENUE HEALTH-CARE CENTER EDUCATIONAL AND LABORATORY BUILDING CATERING
![](https://innopolis.university/upload/landing/3f5/photo_2021-10-25_15-27-41_2_@2x.jpg)
The Innopolis University students live in the comfortable rooms of a modern campus. Each room is equipped with all necessary household appliances and furniture and has a separated kitchen and bathroom. Free WiFi is available on the entire campus territory, there are also cleaning service and a laundry for students, and a canteen.
Residential venue buildings are equipped under the Accessibility Program. All 4 buildings are connected by a passageway that segues to the University. Due to this warm covered way, it is convenient to move around the compound whatever the weather is. We pay a great attention to the security of our students; therefore, access control is arranged in the residential compound.
![](https://innopolis.university/upload/landing/ea4/_MG_8931@2x.jpg)
Sports and fitness club Innopolis helps residents, students and staff of the Innopolis-located companies keep a healthy lifestyle. Total area of the complex is 8,500 square meters. A multipurpose gym for team sports, a workout facility, a Finnish sauna, a hammam (Turkish bath), and a swimming pool are at the students’ disposal. For the Innopolis University students, admission to the sports and fitness center is free.
Working hours: daily from 7:00 a.m. till 11:00 p.m.
Contact us: +7(937)296-86-85
![](https://innopolis.university/upload/landing/9b4/Медицинский_центр@2x.jpg)
Health-Care Center of Innopolis is a structural unit of Kazan City Hospital No. 7. This center is a far cry from classical outpatient clinics. This is a day-stay hospital with a clinical diagnostic laboratory and a physiotherapy unit, a convalescence home, a consultative and diagnostic center, and an emergency suite — all in a single building. Here you can consult with a specialized physician, get screened with the advanced medical facilities, and undergo treatment.
A physician is available to the Innopolis students and residents in Terminal 2 in the 24/7 mode.
Working hours: daily from 7:00 a.m. till 11:00 p.m.
Contact us: +7(937)296-86-85
![](https://innopolis.university/upload/landing/21e/Аудитория_1@2x.jpg)
The advanced training and laboratory building of the Innopolis University was designed to ensure as much comfort to the students, teachers, and employees as possible. Laboratories and lecture halls are equipped with all necessary facilities for conducting training and research, there are also special rooms for independent and team activities. Within the premises, there are also a library, a coffee house, recreation areas, and film-watching spaces.
31 158 sq.m. 27 lecture rooms for 1 140 people, 17 meeting rooms, library area of 2 390 sq.m, 610 seats in the cafeteria, 309 parking slots, 96 bicycle parking slots
![](https://innopolis.university/upload/landing/e37/IMG_6246@2x.jpg)
University students choose themselves where to eat. You can cook your meals yourself or go to canteens or cafes. In the Campus buildings No. 1 and No. 2, there are canteens where you can get a good nutrition for your meals. You may pay in cash or with a card. The W&G cafe and two coffee houses, Irte kofe and CAVA, operate in the Innopolis University. There are also many cafes, pizzerias, and coffee houses in the town. For more information, please visit the Innopolis website.
Each room in the residential building, whether double or a room for five, is equipped with a small kitchen where students can cook their meals themselves.
## ACCOMMODATION OPTIONS
Double Room
One room, 21 m², with 2 single beds, work area and kitchenette. Designed to accommodate two students.
In the room:
• 2 single beds
• 2 bedside tables
• wardrobe with a large mirror
• desktop
• hinged shelves
• refrigerator
• kitchen (without dishes)
• air conditioning
In bathroom:
• sink
• toilet bowl
• shower
• mirror
Quintuple room
Three rooms, 65 m², with two bedrooms, a living room, an entrance hall, 5 single beds, a TV and a kitchenette. Designed to accommodate five guests.
In the room:
• 5 single beds
• bedside tables
• sofa
• two wardrobes with a large mirror
• hinged shelves
• refrigerator
• kitchenette (without dishes)
• TV
• air conditioning
In bathroom:
• sink
• toilet bowl
• shower
• mirror

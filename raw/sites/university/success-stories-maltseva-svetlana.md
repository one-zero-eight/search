## Maltseva Svetlana
Summer School 2025 Institut Teknologi Sepuluh Nopember (ITS) Surabaya, Indonesia
## Знакомство с Иннополисом
Two weeks in Indonesia flew by unnoticed. I was at the Summer School at CommTech Camp Highlight 2025 on Stream 1: Innovations in Engineering. Insanely cool summer school, very eventful, every day we were given interesting lectures, showed the university laboratories, told about projects that ITS students create.
To my surprise, many participants in the summer school were already masters and graduate students, and some were already lecturers at their universities. I was the youngest member of the summer school, but nevertheless it was unbelievably interesting. As a first-year undergraduate student, I received a lot of advice and ideas for the development of my project. It's amazingly cool that I had such an opportunity to communicate with people who have gone through such a long, but interesting path in the field of engineering.
In addition to lectures and classes, we also had various activities, games, songs, dances, but the most memorable one was a tour to the Bromo volcano. At first, we took the bus for 2 hours, then we moved to jeeps and drove another 30 minutes along the serpentine, after that there was a hiking 40 minutes and we were at the highest point where you can see the volcano, the beautiful dawn and just amazing views. It's incredible! Nature is insanely beautiful. But our tour did not end there, we mounted our horses and went to the crater of the volcano. Extremely beautiful views!
I was able to meet children from completely different countries, different cultures, for me this is an excellent experience of communicating with foreigners, this is an excellent practice of English. The summer school had guys from the Philippines, Canada, India, Vietnam, Malaysia, China and Taiwan. In our free time, we walked along Surabai, traveled to neighboring cities. Indonesia is a very cheap country where you can be a millionaire)))
I express my deep gratitude to the Innopolis University and the Student Exchanges team for such a wonderful opportunity! I definitely recommend this summer school to all students!

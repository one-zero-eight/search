## Bakhtiyar Bauyrzhan
International Information Technology University, Kazakhstan
Spring Semester 2022
## Знакомство с Иннополисом
My supervisor helped me adjust to university life. I had some problems with understanding the subjects and further studying them. However, the teachers were loyal to the situation and provided me with the necessary teaching materials.
I have chosen to study three courses: Software Quality and Reliability, Applied Software Architecture and Introduction to Game Programming. At Software Quality and Reliability my team and I created the architecture of a theoretical project, which was positively evaluated by the teacher. While studying Applied Software Architecture we created a mobile application, which was presented at the hackathon and won one of the prizes. During Introduction to Game Programming we created a mobile game based on Unity Engine.
In general, living in Innopolis was a positive experience, also Kazan, which is not far away, is a very beautiful city.

## Shavel Viktoryia
Belarusian State University of Informatics and Radioelectronics, Belarus
Spring Semester 2024
## Знакомство с Иннополисом
This year, for the spring semester, I had the amazing opportunity to become a participant in an exchange program and study at Innopolis University.
To say that I am pleased with this experience is an understatement. Every day spent at this university was filled with discoveries and inspiration. In the less than six months that I studied here, I finally understood what I want to do in life. I realized with full confidence that IT is my calling. Immersing myself in the learning process, I derived immense pleasure from every minute of the classes and from the professors who are truly passionate about their work.
But Innopolis is not just about studying. I actively made new acquaintances here, visited interesting places, and enjoyed the wonderful sports complex. Looking back at these months, I can confidently say: the people here are incredible! The memories of friends and the emotions they gave me will stay with me forever. And I hope that sooner or later, but we will meet again.
Innopolis gave me an experience that will remain in my heart for a lifetime. I am immensely grateful for this and very much hope to return here again. It would be great to become a student again, but I would be happy to return in any capacity.
This period has been a real gift of fate for me, and I sincerely believe that there are many more exciting meetings and achievements ahead of us. Innopolis will forever remain a special place in my life, and I am eagerly looking forward to the new opportunities it can offer me.

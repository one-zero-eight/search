## Intersectoral Technology transfer centre
This is a structural subdivision of Innopolis University, that aims to commercialize and promote the developments of educational and scientific organizations and to develop a national technology transfer network.
Head of the Center — Abdrakhmanova Marina Vasilievna
[m.abdrakhmanova@innopolis.ru](https://innopolis.university/en/m.abdrakhmanova@innopolis.ru)
[Information on the progress of the ITTC programme in 2021](https://innopolis.university/filespublic/%D0%9E%D1%82%D1%87%D0%B5%D1%82_%D0%9C%D0%A6%D0%A2%D0%A2_%D0%AD%D1%82%D0%B0%D0%BF_1.pdf) [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCn4aXydqRl2kwofFelM_4lQ/featured)
## The main areas of activity
![картинка](https://innopolis.university/upload/landing/e7b/блок_Услуги@2x.jpg)
— registration of IP both in Russia and abroad;
— conclusion of IP rights agreements (license agreement, agreement on alienation of exclusive rights, copyright license agreement);
— conducting a patent and information search;
— preparation of patent landscapes;
— conducting marketing and PR-campaigns to promote IP on the market;
— registration in the register of Russian Software (Domestic Software);
— audit and consulting on issues of technology transfer and IP protection, management and commercialization;
— educational activities to train IP specialists;
— support for spin-off projects: assistance in selecting directions and teams, transferring IP rights and other results of scientific and technical activities, and assessing risks related to IP;
— support for transactions related to the transfer of IP rights.
## Consortium
![картинка](https://innopolis.university/upload/landing/d0c/блок_Консорциум@2x.jpg)
The Intersectoral technology transfer centre (hereinafter referred to as ITTC) invites higher education institutions and scientific organizations to become a Member of the ITTC Consortium in order to promote the developments (technology transfer) of higher education institutions and scientific organizations in the priority sectors of the Russian and world economy and to create a unified network of technology transfer centres.
The ITTC, acting as the Head Organization of the Consortium, based on its own experience and on successful practices of leading technology transfer centres, will help to effectively manage intellectual property rights, identify weaknesses in intellectual property management and improve the competencies of specialists involved in technology transfer.
To join the Consortium, you need:
1. Read the [Agreement](https://innopolis.university/filespublic/1%20%D0%A1%D0%BE%D0%B3%D0%BB%D0%B0%D1%88%D0%B5%D0%BD%D0%B8%D0%B5_%D0%BE_%D0%9A%D0%BE%D0%BD%D1%81%D0%BE%D1%80%D1%86%D0%B8%D1%83%D0%BC%D0%B5_%D0%BD%D0%B0%D1%83%D1%87%D0%BD%D1%8B%D1%85_%D0%B8_%D0%BE%D0%B1%D1%80%D0%B0%D0%B7%D0%BE%D0%B2%D0%B0%D1%82%D0%B5%D0%BB%D1%8C%D0%BD%D1%8B%D1%85_%D0%BE%D1%80%D0%B3%D0%B0%D0%BD%D0%B8%D0%B7%D0%B0%D1%86%D0%B8%D0%B9_%D0%BD%D0%B0.pdf) and [Regulations](https://innopolis.university/filespublic/%D0%9F%D0%BE%D0%BB%D0%BE%D0%B6%D0%B5%D0%BD%D0%B8%D0%B5_%D0%BE_%D0%9A%D0%BE%D0%BD%D1%81%D0%BE%D1%80%D1%86%D0%B8%D1%83%D0%BC%D0%B5_%D0%BD%D0%B0%D1%83%D1%87%D0%BD%D1%8B%D1%85_%D0%B8_%D0%BE%D0%B1%D1%80%D0%B0%D0%B7%D0%BE%D0%B2%D0%B0%D1%82%D0%B5%D0%BB%D1%8C%D0%BD%D1%8B%D1%85_%D0%BE%D1%80%D0%B3%D0%B0%D0%BD%D0%B8%D0%B7%D0%B0%D1%86%D0%B8%D0%B9_%D0%BD%D0%B0.pdf) of the Consortium

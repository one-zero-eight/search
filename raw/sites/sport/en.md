## "INNOPOLIS" SPORTS COMPLEX
Sports and fitness club Innopolis helps residents, students and staff of the Innopolis-located companies keep a healthy lifestyle. Total area of the complex is 8,500 square meters. A multipurpose gym for team sports, a workout facility, a Finnish sauna, a hammam (Turkish bath), and a swimming pool are at the club members' disposal.
Working hours:
Daily from 7:00 a.m. till 11:00 p.m.

Outdoor sports
In Innopolis, there are open facilities for team sports. Football, volleyball, and basketball grounds are covered with rubber crumbs with polyurethane binder. Beach volleyball ground is covered with coarse-grained quartz sand, while tennis court is covered with Hard Cushion.
You can book a facility by phoning us or using our Telegram group. Three hours before you are going to start a game, please come to the reception of the sports complex. They will give you a key of the relevant facility.

"Innopolis" Sports Complex
The Innopolis sports complex is an important part of the Innopolis city ecosystem, focused on promoting a sports and healthy lifestyle among students of Innopolis University, residents of Innopolis, employees of resident companies. The sports complex is also open to guests of the city - good access roads and ample parking allow residents of neighboring cottage villages and cities to visit the complex.
The 3-storey sports complex of the European level provides an opportunity to provide a wide range of modern fitness services.
The unique interior design, the warm atmosphere of the club, the friendly staff, will undoubtedly delight the customers and guests of the club with their hospitality. The total area of the complex is 8,500 sq. m
Working hours are from 7.00 to 23.00

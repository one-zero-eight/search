Accommodation and accommodation rules
in a residential complex
ANO HE "University Innopolis"

Innopolis
2021

1. General Provisions

1.1. These Rules of accommodation and residence in the residential complex of ANO HE
"University Innopolis" (hereinafter referred to as the Rules) determine the procedure for accommodation
and residence in the residential complex of ANO HE "University Innopolis" (hereinafter referred to as
the University), located at the address: Republic of Tatarstan, Verkhneuslonsky municipal district,
Innopolis, st. Universitetskaya, 1, buildings 1, 2, 3, 4 (hereinafter referred to as the Complex) and are
mandatory for all categories of residents (hereinafter referred to as residents).

1.2. Accommodation in the Complex is carried out on the basis of an agreement for the
provision of accommodation services or on the basis of an accession agreement for the provision of
accommodation services by signing an accession consent - a registration card (hereinafter referred to as
the Agreement).

1.3. In order to implement the statutory activities of the University, these Rules establish the
following order of accommodation for Residents in the Complex:

- first of all, students are accommodated at the University (hereinafter - Students). The agreement
with the Students is concluded for one academic year, excluding summer holidays (until May 31 of the
corresponding year). The term for the provision of services can be extended for the period of summer
holidays upon admission from a student 14 (fourteen) days before the expiration of the Agreement of a
document confirming the need to extend the Agreement and if there are vacant places in the Complex.

Stay in the Complex can be:

• preferential categories of Students: orphans left without parental care; disabled people of
I and II groups; victims of the accident at the Chernobyl nuclear power plant;

• students who pass the summer test-examination session or final certification (according
to the schedule);

• students who receive a diploma;

• students who are sent to industrial practice in Innopolis.
For the indicated reasons, the Agreements are extended until the appropriate date (end of the
session, practice, diploma delivery) by filling in a new registration card.

- in the second place, persons who have graduated from the University (hereinafter - Graduates)
are accommodated, provided that the Students are fully provided with places for accommodation. The
agreement with the Graduates is concluded for one calendar year from the date of receiving the diploma;

- in the third place, participants of socially significant and other events held at the University are
accommodated in accordance with the calendar of events;

- in the fourth place i University employees are accomodated;

- all other categories of persons are accommodated in the Complex subject to availability.

1.4. Residents are provided with services for temporary accommodation in residential
premises of the Complex (hereinafter referred to as the Rooms) that meet sanitary requirements, strictly
upon presentation of the original passport of the state of which he is a citizen, a minor Living under the
age of 14 upon presentation of the original birth certificate, as well as other documents specified in the
order "On the approval of the forms of documents for placement in the residential complex ANO HE
"University Innopolis ".

1.5. Placement is made on a reimbursable basis. The University has the right to establish a
differentiated cost of accommodation for various groups of Residents (students, participants in the events
of the University, etc.). The cost of accommodation is determined by the prices approved by the relevant
order for the University in force at the time of the conclusion of the Agreement (prices are posted at the
administration desk in building № 3 of the Complex and on the website
https://innopolis.university/campus/).

1.6. The University is not responsible for the personal belongings of the Residents, stored in
the Room, as well as those left unattended on the territory of the Complex.

1.7. Accommodation of the Residents in the Rooms is carried out by the administration of the
Complex (hereinafter in the text - the structural unit of the University responsible for organizing
accommodation). Each resident is assigned a specific Room number or bed. Unauthorized relocation of
Residents from Room to Room is prohibited.

1.8. These Rules are approved by the order of the Director of the University. The current
version of the Rules is published on the Internet on the website of the Complex at:
https://hotel.innopolis.university (hereinafter referred to as the Site). A paper version of these Rules is
available at the administration desk of the Complex in building №3 of the Complex.

1.9. In case of changes and additions to these Rules, the University notifies the Residents
within 3 (three) days from the date of approval of such changes and additions by e-mail of the Resident.
2. The procedure for the provision of services and the procedure for settlements

2.1. Billing period: from 14:00 of the current day - check-in time - until 12:00 of the next day -
check-out time. Working hours of the administration of the Complex: from 9:00 to 20:00 daily, place
for registration of Residents: building No. 3, floor 1, department of accommodation organization.

2.2. The start date for the provision of services is determined in accordance with the Agreement
or the registration card.

2.3. Payment for accommodation services is carried out within the terms established by the
relevant Agreement.

3. Procedure for access to the Complex

3.1. For access to the Complex, Residents are issued with passes (access cards) of the sample
established by the University. It is forbidden to transfer the pass to third parties, as well as to copy it in
any way. Residents are responsible for the transfer of the pass, as provided for in these Rules.

3.2. Guests of the Residents can visit the Complex accompanied by the Resident after registering
in the visitors' log at the security post on the basis of an identity document.

3.3. When bringing personal belongings into the building of the Complex, Residents and their
guests provide personal belongings for inspection by scanning them through an introscope. In case of
refusal from voluntary inspection, things that have not passed inspection, or persons who have refused
inspection, are not allowed into the building of the Complex.

3.4. The requirements of the security personnel, aimed at ensuring the safety of people and the
safety of inventory items, are mandatory for all persons on the territory of the Complex. A complete list
of requirements is presented in the Rules of access and stay in the Educational and laboratory building
and the residential complex of ANO HE "University Innopolis" (hereinafter referred to as the Access
Rules). The current version of the Access Rules is published on the Website, the paper version is
available at the administration desk in building No. 3 of the Complex.

3.5. The entry / removal of material values by Residents is possible only with a material pass

(hereinafter referred to as MP) issued by the administration of the Complex. A complete list of material
assets subject to mandatory registration of the MP when entering / taking out is specified in the Access
Rules.

4. Rights and obligations of the Residents of the Complex
4.1. Rights of Residents in the Complex

4.1.1. To eliminate technical malfunctions in the Room, issue temporary registration and receive
other services, Residents have the right to apply for service through the Site or by personal appeal to a
representative of the University (administration of the Complex).

4.1.2. Residents have the right to refuse to carry out scheduled cleaning in their Room upon
presentation of documents to the University employee, which are the basis for removing the Room from
cleaning cleaning (medical certificates for allergic, infectious diseases, etc.). In this case the Resident
is responsible for the authenticity of the documents provided and for the sanitary condition of the Room.

4.1.3. Residents have the right to gain access to educational and household premises using their
pass (access card) and use inventory, furniture and equipment without taking them outside these
premises.

4.1.4. Residents have the right to use the room for storing drinking water and take one bottle of
drinking water into the Room.

4.1.5. Residents have the right to contact the administration of the Complex to coordinate the
operation of electrical equipment with a capacity of more than 3 kW by submitting an application on the
Site.

4.1.6. Residents have the right to invite visitors during the daytime from 7:00 to 23:30, observing
the access procedure specified in clause 3.2 of these Rules, while the Resident is responsible for the
timely departure of the invited persons and their compliance with these Rules.

4.1.7. In order to avoid violation of paragraphs 4.2.22 and 4.2.23 of these Rules Residents have
the right to use cells for temporary storage of personal belongings located in the lobby of building No.
3 of the Complex.

4.2. Responsibilities of residents of the Complex

4.2.1. At the time of settling in the Complex, the Resident is obliged to accept the Room from
the administration of the Complex in accordance with the terms of the Agreement.

4.2.2. Residents are obliged to carefully use furniture and equipment accepted for use upon
arrival.

4.2.3. Residents are prohibited from independently repairing furniture, equipment, painting or
engaging third parties to carry out these works.

4.2.4. In the event of the expiration of the Agreement, the Resident is obliged to hand over the
Administration Room of the Complex in accordance with the inspection certificate, which is an integral
part of the Agreement.

4.2.5. Residents of the Complex are required to comply with the orders, regulations, rules and
procedures adopted at the University, the current versions of which can be found on the website
https://innopolis.university/

4.2.6. In the absence of the Resident (categories Students and Graduates) for more than 10 (ten)
days, the Resident is obliged to notify the administration of the Complex by signing a written application
indicating the reason for the absence.

4.2.7. Residents are obliged to use the premises for their direct functional purpose. It is prohibited
to conduct any commercial activity on the territory of the Complex, engage in all types of production
activities (including mining) for personal or commercial purposes.

4.2.8. Residents are prohibited from moving furniture and equipment between the Rooms and
the public areas in which they are installed.

4.2.9. Residents are obliged to maintain cleanliness and order in the Rooms and public areas
assigned to them, including independently taking out garbage into designated containers in equipped
places.

4.2.10. Residents are obliged to provide access to the authorized representatives of the Complex
to the living quarters provided to them to perform maintenance of the Room or to check compliance with
these Rules and the Instructions for observing fire safety measures in the residential complex ANO HE
"University Innopolis" (hereinafter referred to as the Fire Safety Instructions).

4.2.11. Residents are obliged to use water and energy efficiently. When leaving the Room, close
water taps, windows, turn off lights and other electrical appliances.

4.2.12. In order to avoid overloading the network, Residents are allowed to operate only
serviceable electrical appliances. It is forbidden to use several electrical appliances at the same time, the
total power of which exceeds 3 kW per outlet and 5 kW per room.

4.2.13. Residents are prohibited from making interior and design changes, including mounting
(dismantling) wall shelves, hooks, transferring sockets, replacing larvae in locks, installing additional
locks, etc. It is forbidden to place any objects on the walls, the operation or removal of which may
damage the integrity of the wall covering.

4.2.14. Residents are forbidden to transfer the key to the Room to third parties, to unauthorizedly
move citizens - family members or temporary residents - into the occupied living quarters.

4.2.15. Residents are obliged to immediately inform the administration of the Complex about
equipment malfunctions in residential premises and public areas that may lead to a fire, flood or other
emergency. Residents are obliged to inform the administration of the Complex about all known
violations of these Rules by the staff or other Residents.

4.2.16. Residents are prohibited from keeping personal belongings in common areas, blocking
corridors and emergency exits.

4.2.17. Residents are prohibited from taking actions that may result in damage to the equipment
of automatic fire protection systems, deactivating (shutdown, closing, etc.) voice alarms of the warning
system and evacuation of people in case of fire, fire detectors (manual and smoke), fire alarm systems.

4.2.18. Residents are prohibited from bringing into the building of the Complex and storing in
the Rooms large items, the size of which in the sum of three dimensions (length, width and height)
exceeds 150 cm, including sports equipment, bicycles, scooters and baby carriages, with the exception
of suitcases, easel backpacks, skis and snowboards (in covers).

4.2.19. Residents are prohibited from performing work or other actions on the territory of the
Complex that create increased noise and vibration that violate normal living conditions. From 22:00 to
07:00 the use of audio equipment and other sound-reproducing devices is allowed only if the volume is
reduced to a degree that does not disturb the rest of other Residents.

4.2.20. Residents are obliged to maintain an atmosphere of goodwill, cooperation and mutual
respect, observe generally accepted moral and ethical standards, avoid conflict situations, not use
profanity, not speak rudely towards the staff of the Complex and the University, as well as to other
persons living or located on the territory Complex, both in person and in the official chats of the
University. It is forbidden to wear or display signs or other symbols aimed at inciting racial, social,
national and religious hatred, to place posters, announcements containing national or political overtones
on the territory of the Complex.

4.2.21. Residents are prohibited, without the consent of the administration of the Complex, to
take actions to place and distribute information content products (announcements, posters, leaflets, etc.)
on the territory of the public areas of the Complex, including on the entrance doors of the Rooms.

4.2.22. Residents are prohibited from bringing, storing and consuming alcoholic beverages on
the territory of the Complex.

4.2.23. Residents are prohibited from bringing in, storing and using hookahs on the territory of
the Complex.

4.2.24. It is prohibited to bring and store narcotic substances, firearms and edged weapons
(including axes, hunting knives, etc.), explosive, flammable and toxic substances.

4.2.25. Residents are prohibited from smoking on the territory of the Complex, including
electronic cigarettes, hookahs and other products. Smoking is allowed only in specially designated areas
on the adjacent territory, marked with signs "Smoking area".

4.2.26. Residents are prohibited from bringing and keeping animals in the Complex: dogs, cats,
rabbits, parrots, fish, etc.

4.2.27. In order to avoid accidents, Residents are obliged to strictly observe safety precautions.
It is forbidden to move in the buildings of the Complex on scooters, rollers and other means of
transportation, run on staircases, sit on window sills, lean out of windows, walk on window slopes and
roofs (including the roofs of building crossings) in order to avoid injuries and accidents, as well as throw
or hang anything outside the windows, store personal belongings and products on the slopes of the
windows.

4.2.28. In case of unsatisfactory health, the Resident is obliged to consult a doctor for a diagnosis.
In the event that the Resident is assigned to the isolation regime, he must inform the administration of
the Complex about this, presenting a confirming medical document, in order to provide him with the
possibility of single placement during the illness in order to prevent the spread of infectious diseases.

4.2.29. When concluding an Agreement for a period of more than 90 (ninety) days, Residents are
required to register at the place of temporary stay, guided by the order “On approval of the Procedure
for issuing a package of documents for temporary registration of citizens of the Russian Federation at
the place of stay in the residential complex ANO HE “ University Innopolis ”.

5. Rights and obligations of the University (administration of the Complex)
5.1. University rights

5.1.1. The University has the right to instruct Residents on compliance with these Rules, Fire
Safety Instructions, labor protection, as well as conduct training alarms for evacuation from the Rooms.

5.1.2. The University has the right to conduct rounds to check the operability of the fire warning
system, to take inventory of inventory items located in the Rooms, and to inspect the Rooms for
compliance with these Rules.

5.1.3. In order to implement the statutory activities, aimed, among other things, at ensuring the
necessary conditions (social, cultural, sports and recreational and recreational infrastructure) for
learning, creative development and preserving the health of students, as well as to provide the student
with protection from all forms of physical and mental violence , insults to the person, protection of life
and health, the University has the right to refuse placement to a person in a state of alcoholic and drug
intoxication. In this case, the confirmation of the specified state can be the use of visual inspection and
sense organs by the administrator.

5.1.4. The University has the right to terminate the Agreement with the Resident in the cases
established by these Rules and / or concluded Agreements and the current legislation of the Russian
Federation.

5.1.5. If the Residents refuse to provide access to the Room or it is impossible to contact them to
obtain the specified access, the administration of the Complex has the right to use a duplicate key and
inspect the Room, guided by the requirements specified in the order "On the Procedure for access to the
rooms of the ANO HE "University Innopolis" for representatives administration and employees of
service organizations ".

5.1.6. The University has the right to conduct an independent assessment and demand
compensation for damage caused to the Complex by the actions of the Residents or by persons for whose
actions on the territory of the Complex Residents are responsible.

5.1.7. The University has the right to ask Residents for documentation on the electrical appliances
they use. In the absence of documentation or refusal to provide it, the University has the right to demand
the removal of this electrical equipment from the Complex.

5.1.8. If an unsatisfactory sanitary condition of the Room is revealed, the University has the right
to carry out unscheduled cleaning of the Room and demand payment in accordance with the order "On
approval of the list and cost of additional services in the residential complex ANO HE "University
Innopolis" within 2 (two) days from the moment this fact is revealed.

5.1.9. If foreign things are found in public areas and common areas, the University has the right
to consider them forgotten and act in accordance with the order “On approval of the Procedure for
registration, storage and return of forgotten things in the residential complex of ANO HE “University
Innopolis”.

5.1.10. The University has the right to refuse to approve the distribution of information content
products (announcements, posters, leaflets, etc.) without explaining the reasons.

5.1.11. The University has the right to call the ambulance service upon detection of external signs
of unsatisfactory health of the Resident.

5.1.12. The University has the right to relocate a Resident in case of illness - to a separate isolated
Room, in other cases - to another bed, if his joint stay with other Residents creates a threat to their health
or discomfort.

5.1.13. In case of a request by the Residents of a separate Room for permanent residence for
health reasons, while maintaining the established differentiated cost of accommodation, the University
has the right to request documentary evidence of the disease (medical certificate, extract from sick leave,
etc.). In this case, the Resident is responsible for the authenticity of the documents provided.

5.1.14. In case of incomplete settlement of the Room, where payment is made for one bed by
each Resident, the University has the right to relocate Residents to other Rooms in accordance with the
order "On approval of the procedure for relocation of residents of ANO HE "Innopolis University"
relocation from the side of the Resident University has the right to demand payment of a free bed at the
cost approved by the order "On approval of the cost of accommodation and catering services in the
residential complex ANO HE "University Innopolis "

5.2. Duties of the University

5.2.1. The University is obliged to provide accommodation for Residents in accordance with the
requirements of sanitary standards and the terms of the Agreement in accordance with these Rules,
subject to availability.

5.2.2. To ensure compliance with sanitary standards, the University is obliged to organize daily
cleaning of common areas and cleaning of Rooms with replacement of bed linen in accordance with the
frequency specified in the order “On approval of the List of consumables and soft inventory for
completing rooms during accommodation, terms of replacement of components and cleaning services in
residential complex ANO HE "University Innopolis", except for the cases provided for in paragraph

4.1.2 of these Rules.

5.2.3. To ensure the safety of the Residents and their guests, the University is obliged to organize
access control and round-the-clock security posts at the entrance to the Complex.

5.2.4. In order to meet the household needs of the Residents, the University is obliged to maintain
the equipment of the laundry rooms and living rooms in good condition.

5.2.5. The University is obliged to accept for consideration applications for services received
through the Site or with a personal appeal of Residents to a representative of the University.

5.2.6. In case of termination of the Agreement on the initiative of the University, in the cases
provided for by the Agreement, these Rules or the current legislation unilaterally, the University is
obliged to notify the Resident of this by e-mail indicated in the registration card, by sending the mail, or
inform personally 5 (five) calendar days before the date of termination of the Agreement.

5.2.7. Upon receipt of a request for temporary registration from Residents who have entered into
an Agreement for a period of more than 90 (ninety) days, the University is obliged to prepare a package
of necessary documents for it to be submitted to the Migration Department of the Ministry of Internal
Affairs of the Russian Federation.

6. Responsibility for violation of these Rules

6.1. On the facts of non-fulfillment by the Residents of these Rules, the University (the
administration of the Complex) draws up an act on violation of the rules of residence in the residential
complex ANO HE "University Innopolis" (hereinafter - the Act).

The act is drawn up by a commission of at least 3 (three) employees from the University
(hereinafter - the Commission). On the initiative of the head of the Complex, employees of other
departments of the University or Students may additionally be invited to the Commission. In case of
violation by Residents of fire safety rules, an employee of the University responsible for ensuring fire
safety may be invited to the Commission. If the Resident refuses to familiarize himself with the Act and
/ or does not sign it, the Commission makes a corresponding entry in the Act. When drawing up 3 (three)
or more Acts in respect of the Resident during the term of the Agreement, the University has the right
to terminate the Agreement with him.

6.2. In the event of a single gross violation by Residents of these Rules, the University has the
right to unilaterally terminate the Agreement. Violations of the provisions specified in paragraphs. 6,
4.2.7, 4.2.20, 4.2.22, 4.2.24, 4.2.29 of these Rules.

6.3. In case of violation of clause 4.2.4 of these Rules, the administration of the Complex has the
right to register the things left in the Room by the Residents as forgotten, in accordance with the
Procedure for Forgotten Things.

6.4. In the event of material damage to the Complex or the creation of conditions for its infliction
by third parties, the Resident is obliged to compensate it in full based on the amount of damage specified
in the Act, within 5 (five) banking days from the date of drawing up the Act or within the period specified
in the Act. Refusal of the Resident from compensation for damage may result in termination of the
Agreement and recovery of damage in court.

6.5. For violation of clause 4.2.17, as well as the Fire Safety Instructions, the University has the
right to demand payment of a fine in the amount of 3,000 (three thousand) rubles for each violation as
compensation for the University's losses. Reimbursement is carried out within 5 (five) banking days
from the date of drawing up the Act or within the period specified in the Act.

6.6. In accordance with clause 4.1.4 of these Rules, for the storage of two or more cylinders in
the Room, the University has the right to collect payment in the amount of 300 (three hundred) rubles
for each container.

6.7. In case of losing the key to the Room, the Resident is obliged to reimburse the cost of
restoring the key in the amount of 200 (two hundred) rubles for each unit.

6.8. In case of unauthorized check-in in a free Room or unauthorized check-in by third parties

Living in the Room, the University has the right to demand payment of reimbursement of expenses,
based on the calculation of the cost of the corresponding room category for each day of stay in the
amount of two times the cost specified in the Order on approval of the cost (clause 1.5 of these Rules).

7. Conditions associated with the spread of the new coronavirus infection COVID-19

7.1. Residents with a confirmed diagnosis of a new coronavirus infection COVID-19 (hereinafter
referred to as COVID-19) or with signs of an acute respiratory illness (temperature over 37 °, dry cough,
chest tightness, weakness, etc.) must inform the University administration about their state of health in
a messenger Telegram @hoteluni or e-mail hoteluni@innopolis.ru , immediately contact a medical
institution, and in a serious condition, call an ambulance team and stay in the room in which they live
until recommendations are received from the administration of the Complex.

7.2. Residents who have contacted persons with COVID-19 within 2 (two) weeks prior to the
confirmation of the disease must inform the University of this fact in the Telegram messenger @hoteluni
or by e-mail hoteluni@innopolis.ru and stay in the room , in which they are posted until the receipt of
recommendations from the administration of the Complex, specified in clause 7.7 of these Rules.

7.3. Students and other persons arriving (returning) to the Complex from foreign countries are
obliged to follow the recommendations and requirements for preventing the spread of COVID-19,
specified in the official documents of the state authorities of the Russian Federation and the Republic of
Tatarstan, including the requirements for mandatory self-isolation and passing the test on COVID-19.

7.4. Residents and guests of the Complex are obliged to use protective masks and treat their hands
with skin antiseptics using installed contactless dispensers when they are in the corridors, common areas
and dining rooms of the Complex. In the Complex antiseptics are located in the main halls of the first
floors of each building, in the dining rooms of the Complex, in bathrooms.

7.5. The administration of the Complex once a week disinfects the Rooms of residents and daily
disinfects the surfaces of public areas.

7.6. After notifying the representatives of the University about the fact of COVID-19 disease or
its signs at the Resident, the administration of the Complex takes a number of measures related to
preventing COVID-19:

• isolates a Resident with a confirmed diagnosis of COVID-19 or signs of an acute respiratory
illness in a separate room until complete recovery or a negative COVID-19 test result;

• informs residents who have been in contact with a COVID-19 patient about the need to control
body temperature every 4 (four) hours for 14 (fourteen) days and inform the University administration
about an increase in body temperature (over 37 °) in the Telegram messenger @hoteluni or by e-mail
hoteluni@innopolis.ru , in case of receiving information about an elevated body temperature, records
the data received in a special journal;

• upon request, provides the necessary bedding, excluding personal contact with an isolated
resident;

• organizes cleaning and disinfection of the Room in which the resident was until the detection
of signs of the disease;

• on request, provides drinking water, excluding personal contact with an isolated Resident;

• on request, helps in organizing meals;

• organizes garbage removal upon request, excluding personal contact with an isolated Resident.
7.7. Residents are recommended:

• stay in their Rooms, except for the need to move for educational activities and meals;

• restrict visits to places with large numbers of people, travel to other cities, including Kazan;

• regularly carry out wet cleaning in the Room, ventilate the room;

• Dispose of used disposable masks (change of disposable masks at least 1 (one) time in 3 (three)
hours, reusable masks in accordance with the instructions) by placing them in a separate plastic bag,
which must be sealed and thrown into the trash bin;

• in case of contacting a medical institution, strictly follow the doctor's prescriptions.

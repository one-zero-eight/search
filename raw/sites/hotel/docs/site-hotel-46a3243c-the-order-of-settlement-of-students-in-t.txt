The order of settlement of students in
the residential complex of
ANO HE "Innopolis University"

1. General provisions
1.1 This Procedure defines the actions of students studying at the ANO HE
"Innopolis University" and the administration of the residential complex of the ANO HE
"Innopolis University"(hereinafter referred to as the Complex) when settling in the Complex
mandatory for all parties.

2. Submitting an application for placement
2.1. The student submits an application for settlement through the Complex's website in
the section
"Student accommodation - Application for accommodation" or by email hoteluni@innopolis.ru.
In the application specify the necessary data for booking: full name, date and time of arrival,
contact details (phone, alias in Telegram).

2.2. After receiving the application the administrator responsible for the accommodation
of students contacts the author of the application, confirms the possibility of accommodation in
the Complex and begins the procedure for preparing a free place in the room.

2.3. The administrator reserves a place for placement in the 1C program:The hotel. At the
same time the number of the building and the category of the room are provided in accordance
with the available number fund.

3. Check-in of students who previously lived in the Complex

3.1. A student who arrives at the Complex during the working hours of the administration
(daily from 9:00 to 18:00), applies to the administrator in building №2 or building №3 for
accommodation. To conclude a contract for accommodation services, the student provides the
original passport of the state of which he is a citizen.

3.2. A student who arrives at the Complex outside of the administration's working hours
and has previously booked a place in the Complex, applies to the security post in building №3 for
accommodation. To conclude a contract for accommodation services, the student applies to the
nearest working day of the administration (daily from 9:00 to 18:00) in building № 2 or building
№ 3, providing the original passport of the state of which he is a citizen.

3.3. To students who arrive at the Complex during non-working hours of the
administration and have not previously booked a place, the administration of the Complex does
not guarantee the availability of a prepared place.

4. Settlement of first-year students

4.1. A student who arrives at the Complex during the working hours of the administration
(daily from 9:00 to 18:00), applies to the administrator in building №2 or building №3 for
accommodation. To conclude a contract for accommodation services, the student provides the
original passport of the state of which he is a citizen.

4.2. A student who arrives at the Complex during non-working hours of the
administration applies to the security post in building №3 for accommodation and provides an
identification document. Placement takes place according to the list of first-year students. To
conclude a contract for accommodation services, the student applies to the nearest working day of
the administration (daily from 9:00 to 18:00) in building № 2 or building № 3, providing the
original passport of the state of which he is a citizen.

5. Student accommodation conditions

5.1. Students can be accommodated only in double-bed one-room and five-bed three-room suites.

5.2. Accommodation of students with a spouse is possible upon submission of a marriage
registration certificate. Payment of the cost of living of the spouse in the Complex is made in
accordance with the current order on the cost of accommodation services in the residential complex
of ANO HE "Innopolis University" for certain categories of persons.

5.3. Accommodation of students with other persons is possible. Payment of the cost of
accommodation for other persons in the Complex is made in accordance with the current order on
the cost of accommodation services in the residential complex of ANO HE "Innopolis University".

5.4. Single accommodation of students in a one-bed double room is possible when paying
for a second bed, according to the current order on the cost of accommodation services in the
residential complex of ANO HE "Innopolis University".

5.5. Foreign students are placed together with students who are citizens of the Russian
Federation. At the same time the administration recommends that at least two foreign students be
accommodated in a five-bed three-room suite.

5.6. The absence of a signed agreement on the provision of accommodation services in
the Complex may be the basis for the administration to collect the cost of accommodation in the
Complex from the student, according to the current order on the cost of accommodation services
in the residential complex of ANO HE "Innopolis University".

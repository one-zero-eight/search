Rules for using the study room

1. Access to the study room - round the clock!

2. You can get into the room using your access card.

3. Use the study room for its intended purpose and do not forget that it is
a public place.

4. It is forbidden to use the study room for other purposes, such as
spending the night in it and eating food.

5. The room must be kept clean and tidy. Store the trash in the designated
bins.

6. There is a daily maintenance cleaning service in the study room.

7. The study room is equipped with furniture, electrical and Internet
outlets, it is forbidden to take out furniture and equipment from the room.

8. For troubleshooting and resolving issues regarding the use of the
training room, you can contact the administrator personally or leave a request
on the campus Website using a QR code.

9. From 22:00 to 7:00 the use of audio equipment and other sound-producing devices is allowed only if reduced to a degree that does not disturb
the peace of the residents of the campus.

10. The campus administration is not responsible for personal items
left unattended in the room.

11. We remind you that upon non-compliance with these rules,
the campus administration has the right to draw up a violation report
(p. 4. 2. 9 of the Rules for settlement and accommodation in the
residential complex of ANO HE "Innopolis University", the current
version of which can be found on the Website).

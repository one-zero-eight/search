2. The price of accommodation services includes: maintenance cleaning of rooms, use
of a refrigerator, TV, microwave and kettle (if available in the room), air conditioning in
summer, Wi-Fi-zone, Parking, 24-hour laundry room, as well as provision of a set of bed linen
and towels.

3. A child under the age of 7 (seven) years of age inclusive is provided with free
accommodation when sharing accommodation with a parent without providing a separate
place.

4. Categories of persons who receive a discount when providing accommodation
services:

- for students of ANO HE "Innopolis University" a discount of 90 % of the cost of bed-type accommodation services for the period of study is provided;

- for those who have completed their studies at ANO HE "Innopolis University"
(graduates), a discount of 72 % from the cost of services is provided for bed-type
accommodation during the first year after graduation;

- for employees of ANO HE "University Innopolis" when staying in a double room, a
discount is set for each bed in the amount of 90 % of the cost of accommodation services,
subject to the conditions specified in the Order of approval on the corporate portal" Bitrix24 "
of applications for temporary accommodation of employees in the residential complex of ANO
HE "University Innopolis" during the waiting period for rental housing or as part of official
necessity, as well as invited persons;

- for students and employees of ANO HE "Innopolis University", when staying together
with their spouse in the room, a discount for the second bed for the spouse is established in
the amount of 80 % of the cost of accommodation services;

-students and employees of ANO HE "Innopolis University", when staying together with
their spouse and children in a room, receive a 70% discount for a bed for the spouse, while
children are placed together with their parents in additional places in the room of the
appropriate capacity;

- for employees of Innopolis AC, when staying in a double room, a discount is set for
each bed in the amount of 90 % of the cost of accommodation services for the duration of the
employment contract.

5. During the academic leave period, students of the Autonomous Non-profit
Organization HE "Innopolis University", specified in paragraph 4 of this Appendix, will not
receive a discount on accommodation services.

## August Education Meeting Held in Innopolis
22 August 2025
![](https://innopolis.com/uploads/thumbs/obr2398-52b08d7446-229f404a9810ebc30e9390e0a913562f.jpeg)
The Republican August Education Meeting took place in Innopolis with the participation of Rais of Tatarstan Rustam Minnikhanov.
The event brought together educators, school and university leaders, and representatives of the scientific community. Participants reviewed the results of the past academic year and discussed priorities for the future.
![](https://innopolis.com/uploads/thumbs/obr2289-7e795708f0-43443f5ff86232497e17c8f530d4ac44.jpeg)
![](https://innopolis.com/uploads/thumbs/obr2344-23c30f5c5b-d0e9520e710bd4b65f466f376927182a.jpeg)
The program opened with an exhibition of IT solutions for the education system. Innopolis representatives also showcased their projects at the Popov Technopark: Sergey Ezov, Director of Innopolis School, Arman Kostanyan, Director of Innopolis Lyceum, and Ulyana, a preschool student, who shared her experience participating in the “First Movement” project.
![](https://innopolis.com/uploads/thumbs/obr2176-86efe749b9-229f404a9810ebc30e9390e0a913562f.jpeg)
Deputy Prime Minister of Tatarstan Roman Shaikhutdinov and Innopolis Mayor Dmitry Vandyukov presented the city’s strategy for developing educational infrastructure.
![](https://innopolis.com/uploads/thumbs/obr1999-956cb60f7e-229f404a9810ebc30e9390e0a913562f.jpeg)
The event concluded with a plenary session on the development of education in the republic. As part of the meeting, new school buses were handed over to municipalities in Tatarstan, including Innopolis School and Lyceum.
![](https://innopolis.com/uploads/thumbs/obr2569-4df52c1e0e-43443f5ff86232497e17c8f530d4ac44.jpeg)
![](https://innopolis.com/uploads/thumbs/obr1839-ad69dde046-d0e9520e710bd4b65f466f376927182a.jpeg)
After the official program, the “PhysMat Breakthrough” festival began at Innopolis University.
![](https://innopolis.com/uploads/thumbs/obr2683-3839bb95ea-43443f5ff86232497e17c8f530d4ac44.jpeg)
![](https://innopolis.com/uploads/thumbs/obr2859-9a53f82e72-d0e9520e710bd4b65f466f376927182a.jpeg)

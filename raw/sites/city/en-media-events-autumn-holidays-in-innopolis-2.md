## Autumn holidays in Innopolis
![](https://innopolis.com/uploads/thumbs/frame-72-20-63da87e772-815b55499fa4958c76ccfa4219de7d46.jpeg)
When
29 October — 08 November 2026
Where
Innopolis
## Technology Leaders
The final of the II Inventive Problem Solving Championship will be held at the Innopolis Lyceum. Students of grades 5-9 will choose a direction, solve a technological case and present their solution to the experts
October 31 — November 2
[Register](https://forms.yandex.ru/cloud/6ace2c1d5057b279fb3f8fc1)
## Master class on painting a feeding trough
'Spellican Academy' will hold a lesson for elementary school students
October 31st at 16:00
Sportivnaya Str. 130
[Buy a ticket](https://t.me/Spellican_Academy/3033)
## UAS Festival
Innopolis School will host the fest 'Creating an unprecedented flight', dedicated to drones and quadrocopters
October 30th
[Broadcast](https://vkvideo.ru/video-102747373_456239448)
## Culinary shift
For children 6-10 years old 'Spellican Academy' will host a camp shift dedicated to cooking
November 2-6
Sportivnaya Str. 130
[Buy a ticket](https://t.me/Spellican_Academy/3037)
## The Autumn Club
'White Fox' children's center organizes club for young residents of Innopolis to improve sewing, cooking, first aid, financial literacy and solving everyday problems
November 2-6
Sportivnaya Str. 130
[Buy a ticket](https://t.me/whitefoxinno)

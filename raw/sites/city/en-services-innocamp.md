## First steps in IT
INNOCAMP is an IT-camp for anyone from 10 to 17 y.o.
Kids have an educational programme during the day, and participate in sports or creative activities in the evening. The camp provides five meals a day and the area is guarded 24 hours a day.
At the end of the camp, the children present their project and the best team has a chance to gain an internship at an IT company.
![](https://innopolis.com/uploads/thumbs/photo2022-09-04-233013-c48dc09169-4adde261d297182ae92bbccd318eae46.jpeg)
![](https://innopolis.com/uploads/thumbs/img0586-ca1ad77e0f-4563069ceed50322b6d03a65754aadf5.jpeg)
## ​Directions
- Java, C#m Python;
- Computer programming in Minecraft, Scratch, App Inventor (10–13 yo);
- Design of the interface;
- Digital Marketing;
- IT management.

## How to take part
The camps are organised within the summer holidays and last for 2 weeks. The price starts from 75,000 roubles per camp.

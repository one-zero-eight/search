## Suburban comfort
Zion is a residential complex surrounded by its unique community. Minimalist houses are painted with warm and natural tones. Many trees are planted nearby to create an atmosphere and shield you from the wind. The courtyards have a mown lawn with automatic irrigation and space for a barbecue area.
There are standard and duplex flats in townhouses. They come unfurnished, finished and furnished. The courtyards are safe and full of greenery
![](https://innopolis.com/uploads/thumbs/img9011-0f5d8a1389-52c65139a756c7e6b3e5fc79299626af.jpeg)

## Zion -2
The second phase of townhouses features the addition of high-tech and the same Swiss or Scandinavian vibes. New residents are already settling in. In total, 35 houses will be built. The cost is 160,000 roubles per square metre.

The Popov Technopark has an IT and service center for corporate and private clients.
What services can be obtained:
- service center: hardware diagnostics, component repair of laptops, PCs, servers and regular maintenance of office equipment
- copy center and polygraphy: printing (including large format), scanning, binding and lamination of documents
- PC assembly and B2B supplies: selection of components, professional assembly of workstations for specific tasks and supply of corporate equipment
- system integration: design and installation of low-current networks (SCS), video surveillance systems and server facilities
- DevOps and IT outsourcing: process automation (CI/CD), monitoring, containerization and comprehensive technical support
- AI infrastructure: configuring GPU clusters, deploying sovereign (local) LLM and private RAG systems for secure work with trade secrets
- import substitution: IT audit and seamless transition of corporate infrastructure to domestic software
- development and automation: rapid creation of MVPs, analytical dashboards, Telegram bots and web integrations for business
- information security: basic infrastructure audit and configuration of secure network perimeters

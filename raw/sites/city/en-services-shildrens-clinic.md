## Сhildren’s clinic
![](https://innopolis.com/uploads/thumbs/img2994-51bb21f110-0736364e51264d8571d59436a58fa6da.jpeg)
Useful links
[Make an appointment](https://uslugi.tatarstan.ru/)
[Schedule of specialists](https://t.me/innochildhealthcare)
Contacts
Address:Sportivnaya Str. 301
Call a doctor at home:+7 843 221-94-00
Administrator:+7 843 221-94-02
Working hours
Monday, Tuesday, Thursday 8:00–12:00
Wednesday, Friday 12:00–16:00
The clinic is situated in the medical centre. Pediatricians work on weekdays. Specialists and a mobile laboratory also regularly visit the clinic. You can check the timetable in the [telegram channel](https://t.me/innochildhealthcare).
The clinic runs a free vaccination programme.

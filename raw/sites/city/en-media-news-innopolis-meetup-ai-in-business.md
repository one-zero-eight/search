## Innopolis Meetup: AI in Business
22 May 2026
![](https://innopolis.com/uploads/thumbs/meetup-site-2-523fa3ffde-067a3dfb2d1662f4e1bb725f308fd963.jpeg)
## Innopolis will host the small-scale meetup
The Innopolis Meetup will bring together founders, CTOs, product leaders, and researchers. Speakers will share their experiences implementing AI in business. The focus will be on creating AI employees, LLM prototypes, ROI from AI agents, and AI in industry, finance, and marketing.
Speakers:
- Vladimir Makarov, CTO and co-founder of AgenticLab, former Mckinsey;
- Ilina Nigmatullina, Chief Product Officer (CPO) at Algatop, lecturer at HSE and Innopolis University;
- Albert Nasybullin, Lead Developer at the RnD MWS AI Center;
- Valeria Zhdanova, Head of Generative AI Research at Sberbank;
- Yaroslav Shuvaev, CEO of Panteo.ai, led the implementation of AI in fintech.
Following the presentations, there will be a networking event: meet founders, product leaders, developers, and researchers.
Offline registration: Participation is free with advance registration: https://innopolis.timepad.ru/event/3979175/.
Link to register for the online broadcast: [https://innopolis.timepad.ru/event/3994188/](https://innopolis.timepad.ru/event/3994188/)
When: June 4, 5:00 PM
Where: Innopolis, Lobachevsky Technopark, Sfera coworking space

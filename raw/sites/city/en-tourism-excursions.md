## Excursion «Innopolis. Area code»
### Discover the secrets of Russia's city of the future
This is the city's official guided tour, created by those who know and love Innopolis best—our own community. We've woven together the official story with urban legends from its first residents, and we'll even give you the inside scoop on when to expect the A-242 robot
## What to expect on your tour:
• A look inside Innopolis University: explore the academic heart of the city
• Visit the IT development center: step into the innovative Innopolis Special Economic Zone (SEZ)
• Architecture & Neighbourhoods: discover the unique design and residential areas
## Tour details
An individual group of up to 5 people will last 1.5–2 hours

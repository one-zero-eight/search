## Second and third phase of the townhouses ‘Zion’
This is an urban extension of an elite residential area in Innopolis. A school, the IT lyceum, Innopolis University, company offices, a park, cafes and restaurants, and a yoga studio are all within walking distance.
![](https://innopolis.com/uploads/thumbs/ptica-bab6b05e75-ba4966aefabbd56ab10699b6fde4d0cb.jpeg)
![](https://innopolis.com/uploads/thumbs/trg-3-b83e737b5b-3cff98b033da5b8e0861abed5594f2dc.jpeg)
## New technoparks
Two boomerang-shaped buildings are under construction. Residents of the SEZ 'Innopolis' will be offered the opportunity to rent offices on favourable terms. The technoparks will be launched in 2025 and will provide 4,000 jobs.
![](https://innopolis.com/uploads/thumbs/tehnoparki-d75ffd231f-b5766db449b57070a534de9cb0e96f1b.jpeg)
## Coliving

There will be 975 apartments in the colivings.

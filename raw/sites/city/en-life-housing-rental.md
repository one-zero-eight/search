## Move in and settle
We’ve already taken cared of you and provided all the essential furniture in appartments. Smart intercom and fingerprint door lock are installed, so don’t worry about forgetting the keys.
Safe and spacious courtyards are equipped with playgrounds, gazebos and book crossings. Also there is a ground for pets placed just 3 minutes away from you.
Underground parking for 2000 rub a month.

## Rental cost
Starting from November 15th, 2025, the rent will be:
One-bedroom flat - from 17 000 rub a month
Two-bedroom flat - from 21 000 rub a month
Three-bedroom flat - from 25 000 rub a month

## Accommodation in
You can also rent an apartment in the nearest point of Kazan to Innopolis. You can get from the residential area to the city by shuttle
Starting from November 15th, 2025, the rent will be:
Two-bedroom flat - from 18 000 rub a month
Three-bedroom flat - from 22 000 rub a month

## Courses
At Innopolis University, you get to learn frontend, backend, management, testing or game development right from scratch. Courses are available for beginners without any training and school students from the 8th grade onwards.
Some courses are free while others will cost you a fee, but it is possible to receive a grant for tuition.
[Find a suitable course](https://stc.innopolis.university/?utm_source=innopolis-com&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=resident-education).

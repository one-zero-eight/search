To Innopolis by bus
You can easily take a bus from Kazan to Innopolis. There are two shuttle-buses operating on the route from “Kombinat Zdorovya” station and from bus station “Vostochny”.
If you pay via card, the fare is 270 rub. Cash payment is 300 rub. Discounted tickets for children under 7 years old are 110 rub (please show the original birth certificate of the child). For INNO ID card holders the fare is 250 rubles or 200 rubles when buying a monthly subscription. [More info about the card.](http://www.winwinrides.ru)
[Check the bus schedule](https://innopolis.com/en/city/bus-schedule)
[Live map of buses](https://t.me/InnoHelpBot)
[Buy INNO ID](http://www.winwinrides.ru/)
![](https://innopolis.com/uploads/transport/map-auto-bf3dddccb9.png)
Driving to Innopolis
It takes 40-50 minutes to reach Innopolis from Kazan. The route is mapped through the M-7 “Volga” highway. You travel through a beautiful scenery of two rivers flowing into one another, fluffy forests and the bridge. There will be a series of signposts to help you find the way to the city.
[Route in Yandex Maps](https://yandex.ru/maps/geo/innopolis/769768365/?ll=48.727998%2C55.752060&z=13.52)
[Fellow travellers’ chat](https://t.me/joinchat/BZaU2UDW8zpUizpiMvDRQA)
![](https://innopolis.com/uploads/transport/map-carsharing-b0eba539ee.png)
To Innopolis by carsharing
Carsharing enables you to press the button at the app, find a car, open the door and set off the road. There’s a carsharing service called ‘Delimobil' in Innopolis.
There's just a few easy steps to log in to the service within the app: send documents and wait until the access is granted
[Download ‘Delimobil’ app](https://go.onelink.me/Jlks/e055cc89)

## The meeting of BRICS Communications Ministers was held in Innopolis
28 September 2024
![](https://innopolis.com/uploads/thumbs/photo2024-10-29-093428-3e75898895-11fdc94bf43941ac9bd82646136d5af3.jpeg)
## Digital cooperation of the BRICS countries
On September 26-27, a meeting of BRICS communications ministers and a Digital Forum were held in Innopolis.
The main event was the plenary session, which resulted in the adoption of a [Declaration](https://cdn.brics-russia2024.ru/upload/docs/Declaration_of_the_10th_BRICS_Communications_Ministers_Meeting.pdf?1727446983143395) on Digital Cooperation. The document highlights the importance of partnership and an inclusive approach to modern technologies, including joint efforts in the fields of 5G and space projects.

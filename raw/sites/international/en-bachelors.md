## Admission fall 2025
### APPLY FOR GRANT
Register an admissions portal account, fill in an application form with all the mandatory fields, take the tests (English, Computer Science and Math) and click “SUBMIT” to complete the application.
[Apply](https://profile.innopolis.university/auth/register)
GO THROUGH THE CONTEST
Successful applications are selected for further consideration. The next selection round includes:
— Profile testing in Computer Science and Math
— Programming test
— English language testing
— Interview with HR expert
NB: The Grant Contest goes online.
[Learn more](https://new.innopolis.university/faq/)
### GET THE OFFER
Get the offer by email and confirm it. Provide all enrollment and visa documents, pay tuition fee (for Partial Grant candidates) and come to Innopolis in mid-August.
[Apply](https://profile.innopolis.university/auth/register)
### How to apply for the Bachelor's program
### **1.** Submit an application on the website
[Apply](https://profile.innopolis.university/)
### **2.** Take an online test
Sections for testing will appear in your personal account of the applicant after reviewing the questionnaires by the admissions manager
### **3.** Pass the test
Take a profile test, an English test, a contest and an interview with HR and an industry representative
### **4.** Receive a grant offer
The information will appear in your personal account, and you will also receive an email
### **5.** Take the internal entrance exams
The results of internal entrance exams are uploaded to your personal account on the website
### **6.** Admission letter
The information will appear in your personal account, and you will also receive an email
### **7.** Submit the original documents for enrollment
Come to Innopolis University at 1 Universitetskaya Street
### **8.** Get a letter of enrollment and come to study with us!

You have questions about what a grant competition is and how it goes?
Let's see how it works …

So, our Grant is an opportunity to study at a paid place at the expense of the university fully or
partly. That means, if you get a Grant approval and pass the IET (internal entrance tests)
successfully, you can get a discount on tuition from 50% to 100%, it depends on the results of
the passing competitions. The full tuition fee without a discount is 800,000 rubles per year.

n order to receive the grant, it is necessary to take part in the Grant competition, which runs from
December to May. The procedure of the Grant competition will be available from December, 1.
You can register here https://profile.innopolis.university/. To apply, you will need to register, add
a CV and a motivation letter in English. We advise you not to delay with this competition in order
to find out your results as early as possible.

The grant competition consists of two stages with competitive tests.
At the first stage, you need to pass the English test.

Next, if you successfully complete the first stage, you will be invited to the second online stage.
Here you will pass advanced profile tests in computer science and mathematics.
Online interviews with a Professor (selected earlier from the list presented on the website) and
HR expert are also included at this stage.
Based on the results of these tests, the Grant Commission makes the final decision on the
approval of the grant.

The final result will be sent to the email address you specified in the registration form. If the
grant is approved, you have to take the entrance tests, wait for their results and expect
enrollment lists in August.
It's also important to consider that you can participate in the grant competition only once per
academic year.

Participation in the grant competition does not oblige you to do anything, it gives you more
opportunities instead. Therefore, we recommend everyone to pass the grant competition and try
to get the grant to study at Innopolis University!

Possibility № 2 - the Russian Government offers the state scholarship for international students
which includes tuition fee coverage and monthly allowance (for the entire duration of study
regardless of the student's success). We advise you to apply for it.
Register at https://education-in-russia.com/ and fill in the application. You will be able to choose
6 Russian Universities. Please put Innopolis University on the 1st place in the priority list so that
we can receive your application first. Otherwise, you may be accepted to another university.

We are waiting for your application to participate in the grant competition and wish you good
luck!

International Fest 2023
Explore the variety of cultures at Innopolis University!
Fest Schedule, 3-5 March
![](https://static.tildacdn.com/tild3361-6631-4930-b365-616433376661/photo_2022-03-21_18-.jpg)
Round Table Discussion
This event is largely aimed at international students. Address your questions and concerns related to your life at Innopolis University and Russia. We will do our best to provide answers to all your questions. Register [here](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/1WdXZuGn95knXxs9zfG9wHL1fa2vNMGwTqVfNqUwIXMM/edit) to ask your question and attend.
3 March
18:00 – 19:30
Room 425
![](https://static.tildacdn.com/tild3861-3632-4131-b430-383839613534/noroot.png)
Quiz
Register your team to take part in the International Festival Quiz prepared by the [IU Quiz Club](https://t.me/quiz_iu) and their friends from outside the university!
Topics:Culture, Culinary, Places, Famous Persons, Music..
Max of 6 people per team, 8 teams max.
Register [here](https://forms.gle/h4xiYEt3Q2XHRQqP9) by 28 February 23:59.
If you don't have a team, you can be drawn to one.

Grand Concert
Show a creative performance (song, dance, poem or presentation) showcasing the culture and traditions of your home country or a country you love.
Fill in the form [here](https://forms.yandex.ru/u/63f36cb5f47e73bef38c802a/) to take part.
And of course, you are very welcome to come as a spectator to the event!

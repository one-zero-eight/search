our mission
The main purpose of our club is to unite people through the greatest game of football. We want to give an opportunity for students to enjoy playing football and improve their skills. Regular trainings, tactics discussions, experience exchange, different physical exercises and friendly atmosphere - things that we preach here.

Our projects
At the end of the Spring 2022 our club launched one more 8x8 championship between five teams. According to the traditions, first games were dedicated to the group stage, after that four best teams were promoted to the knock-out stage. The winner was decided in the final game. In the main game Phoenix defeated Goal Diggers.
Futsal Cup 2018
In Spring 2018 Innopolis Futsal Cup became the first ever tournament, organized by students. We had five teams, filled with staff members, professors and, of course, students.
Community Training
In November 2018 our club reached out in the community and held a training dedicated to futsal basics. This training was designed for kids who want to become future football stars!
Tasma 8x8 Championship
Our team has participated in the Kazan 8x8 Football Championship "Tasma". It was a useful experience for the young team which mainly consisted of first and second year students. Guys have shown their own playing style with dignity.
Innopolis Football League 18'
After a successful start SAFI club stepped up on the next level and launched the first ever Innopolis 11x11 Football League! The first season collected three teams, which played 2 rounds in fall 2018. The winner was decided in the final game: Phoenix.
All-Star Game
Exclusive event organized by Innopolis University Football Club. Three teams were represented by respective profs: Manuel Mazzara, Giancarlo Succi, Victor Rivera. Teams were formed by draft and each team was given a special uniform sponsored by professors - team captains.
Innopolis Football Cup 2021
After the organization of several successful events our club started to launch regular Innopolis 8x8 tournaments. In 2021 four teams participated. Firstly, the group stage was launched. Then there were knock-out games. The winner was decided in the final game, where team Pivara won against their opponents: FC Neugomon.
Innopolis Futsal League 2022
In the beginning of Spring 2022 we organized a regular futsal championship in which eight teams participated. We divide teams into two groups by four teams in each. Then two best teams from each group qualified into the knock-out stage. The winner was decided in the final game, in which Phoenix played against Pivara and won the game.
Collaboration with clubs
Also, we don't forget about collaborating with other clubs. In the March, right after the futsal tournament, we exchange our experiences in format of open trainings with participants of social dance club.
Rubin FC trip to Kazan
We are more than just a club, so in addition to playing football ourselves we visit games of the most successful football club of our republic - "Rubin Kazan". In May 2022 we gathered to support "Rubin" in its game against "Ufa" in "Ak Bars Arena". Despite the result, all of us gained much positive emotions and enjoyed a wonderful game in a good company.

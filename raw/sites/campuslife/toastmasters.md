What is Toastasters?
Inno Toastmasters teaches public speaking and leadership skills through a worldwide network of clubs. Inno Toastmasters has already started to help people from diverse backgrounds making them more confident speakers, communicators, and leaders.
Why attend our club?
Do you want to practice public speaking, improve your communication and build leadership skills? With Toastmasters, you can break barriers, not your budget.
Develop and practice communication and leadership skills in the safe, supportive environment of our club, working through projects and assignments that enable you to build real–world skills in a variety of disciplines.
Experienced Evaluators
Experienced speakers from the university encourage, guide and support you in your goals and help you to achieve more than you thought possible.
Experimental Learning
By giving speeches and fulfilling leadership roles, you practice and improve with the pace that you prefer using proven curriculum and materials.
Roles of your choice
Choose the role that you are most comfortable to speak with, master the area of speaking that you prefer to either it relates to leadership skill or public speaking.

Who are we?
Student Union helps students to maintain effective communication with university administration, as well as aids students to make university life more interesting, productive and fun. Join our [Telegram Channel](https://t.me/suiu_news) for news!

How to join Student Union?
Apply for internships at the beginning of the Fall, Spring, and Summer semesters. All the related information is posted in our [Telegram Channel](https://t.me/suiu_news).
![](https://static.tildacdn.com/tild3064-3161-4639-b230-333438613336/photo_2026-07-16_16-.jpg)
The Union consists of 3 main structures
![](https://static.tildacdn.com/tild3336-3539-4032-b162-313366633262/su_Edited.png)
Core
Those who gather opinions, brainstorm, and represent the student community in social, financial, and administrative questions.
Skills: Critical thinking, decision making, dedication, high degree of responsibility, public speaking, teamwork.
![](https://static.tildacdn.com/tild3336-3539-4032-b162-313366633262/su_Edited.png)
Active
Those involved in organising events, creating decorations, script writing, working with volunteers, and hosting at events at the University.
Skills: Event management, creativity, teamwork, excellent communication skills, and leadership.
![](https://static.tildacdn.com/tild3336-3539-4032-b162-313366633262/su_Edited.png)
Media
Those involved in photography, design, and social media management to cover events at the University and the activities of the Student Union.
Skills: Photography, video, and design skills, a trained eye, a strong sense of style.

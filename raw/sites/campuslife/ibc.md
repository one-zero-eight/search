Innopolis University Summer School for Bachelors
About Summer School
Summer School at Innopolis University is designed to help and prepare students for their life at the campus. Summer school consists of three main blocks: professional courses, personal career development sessions (PCD) and English lessons. It is an intense program which gives a taste of academic, and also extracurricular life in Innopolis.

Workshops / Student Clubs
All participants will be able to attend exlusive sessions run by specialized teachers and local student clubs, which proudly form active student community at Innopolis University. Find description below.
[Register for Workshops / Student Clubs Sessions](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1AqLlzjixYKED2u14OL-5WLvO7t6CflEXIDrCKH74taQ/edit#gid=0) See Student Clubs of #IBC2018
Academic English
Boost your English with experienced international teachers. You will cover the following topics: Logic and Rhetoric, Idioms, Phrasal Verbs, etc.
Science Workshops
Four amazingly interesting classes: Fractals and Manifolds, The Big Bang Metaphor, Bounded Infinity and Black Holes, Scalar Modulation.
Career Development session
Tatyana Yurkina, Head of Resource Centre, will give you an insight on your career perspectives and will try to answer topical questions.
Mind, communication and relaxation workshops
Our psychologist, Elya, is happy to deliver three workshops: Mind Sharpener to solve irregular life tasks, Effective Communication to help your further academic performace and social life, and Art-Therapy to help you to express your feelings through art.
Student Clubs & Societies session
Andrejs Blakunovs, Student Clubs Advisor, will talk about the importance of student clubs & societies at IU, and how they complement contemporary higher education.

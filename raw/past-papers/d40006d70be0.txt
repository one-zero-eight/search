The first grading midterm (written & oral) on Mathematical Analysis
(Innopolis University, Fall semester 2024, BS24)

Description
Overall cost of the assignment is 30 points. This assignment is designed to evaluate how do we
understand each other during study on weeks 7-12 the following topics:
• limits and continuity of real functions,
• derivative and differentiation (including applications),
• basic properties of differentiation,
• analysis of function’ graphs,
and how we are ready to continue our studies of (of theory of integration).
The Assignments consist of the following.
1. Build a table of size 12x2 where for each of 12 exercises from the list below and indicate in
the table your belief
a. whether you don’t understand the exercise’ task/question (by putting 0),
b. or understand the task/question but not sure how to solve/answer it (by putting 1),
c. or you believe that you understand and can solve/answer the task/question (by
putting 2).
2. Provide solutions/answers for 3 (not more than three) exercises (preferably from different
sub-topics) that you think that you understand and can solve as proof/evidence/justification
for your belief (in the filled-in table).
a. the first of provided solution/answer should be for any of exercises 1-6,
b. the second one – for any of exercises 7-11,
c. the last one is obligatory – for the exercise 12.

Submission and grading rules
Please write your table and solutions on a paper in a readable way, make photos/scan, and
upload as a single pdf-document to Moodle by midnight from Sunday November 17 to Monday
November 18, 2024. File-name convention: submitted file should be named by student first name
and surname (for example NikolayShilov.pdf).

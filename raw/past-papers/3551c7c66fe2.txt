The first grading midterm (written & oral) on Mathematical Analysis
(Innopolis University, Fall semester 2024, B24)

Description
Overall cost of the assignment is 30 points. This assignment is designed to evaluate how do we understand
each other during study on weeks 1-6 the following topics:
• proof techniques,
• basics of Naïve Set Theory,
• set-theoretic representation of the natural numbers,
• real sequences, their convergence and limits in reals and infinities.
The Assignments consist of the following.
1. Build a table of size 12x2 where for each of 12 exercises from the list below and indicate in the table
your belief
a. whether you don’t understand the exercise’ task/question (by putting 0),
b. or understand the task/question but not sure how to solve/answer it (by putting 1),
c. or you believe that you understand and can solve/answer the task/question (by putting 2).
2. Provide solutions/answers for 2 (not more than 2!) exercises (preferably from different sub-topics)
that you think that you understand and can solve as a proof/evidence/justification for your belief (in
the filled-in table); one of provided solution/answer should be for any of exercises 1-6, another one –
for any of exercises 7-12.

Submission and grading rules
Please write your table and solutions on a paper in readable way, make photos/scan, and upload as a
single pdf-document to Moodle by the midnight from Sunday October 6 to Monday October 7, 2024. Filename convention: submitted file should be named by student first name and surname (for example
NikolayShilov.pdf).

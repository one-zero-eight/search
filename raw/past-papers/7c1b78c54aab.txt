The first grading assignment (oral poll) on Mathematical Analysis
(Innopolis University, Fall semester 2023, BS-I)

Description
Overall cost of the assignment is 15 points. This assignment is designed to evaluate how do we understand
each other during the first 2 weeks of our studies of
• proof techniques,
• basics of Naïve Set Theory and
• set-theoretic representation of the natural numbers
and how we are ready to continue our studies of the foundations of Mathematical.
The Assignments task consist in
1. building a table of size 9x2 where for each of 9 exercises from the list below just indicate your belief
whether you understand (by putting 1) or not (by putting 0) the exercise' formulation, and (by putting
2) whether you think that you can or cannot solve the exercise,
2. provide solutions for 2 (not more than 2!) exercises that you think that you understand and can solve
as a proof/evidence/justification for your belief (in the filled-in table).

Submission and grading rules
Please write your table and solutions on a paper in readable way, make photos/scan, and upload as a
single pdf-document to Moodle by the midnight from Sunday September 10 to Monday September 11,
2023. File-name convention: submitted file should be named by student first name and surname (for
example NikolayShilov.pdf).

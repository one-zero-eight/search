The final assignment (final overall examination) on Mathematical Analysis
(Innopolis University, Fall semester 2023, BS-I)

Assignment Description
Overall cost of the assignment is 40 points. This assignment is designed to evaluate how do we understand
topics of the course and each other during the Fall semester of Academic Year 2023/24. It covers the
following topics:
• Varieties of number systems (axiomatic and set-theoretic approaches)
• Theory of limits and continuity
• Differentiation, derivatives, and differential
• Indefinite and definite (Reimann) integral

Assignment Specification
1. It comprises the Consent Question and 12 tasks (3 for each topic listed above). The question is about
students’ consent to skip solving tasks (to get the final grade on base of 4 assignments during the
semester) or (exclusive) to attempt tasks (claiming to include this assignment grade and all bonus
points earned during the semester to the final grade).
2. Student attempting the tasks must build a table of size 12x2 where for each of 12 exercises from the
list below and indicate in the table his/her belief
a. whether he/she doesn’t understand the task (by putting 0),
b. or he/she understands the task but not sure how to solve the task (by putting 1),
c. or he/she can solve the task (by putting 2).
3. The table must be accompanied by solutions for 2 (not more than 2!) tasks (each from different topics)
that you think that you understand and can solve as a proof/evidence/justification for your belief (in
the filled-in table).

Submission and grading rules
Please write your table and solutions on a paper in readable way, make photos/scan, and upload as a
single pdf-document to Moodle by the midnight from Sunday December 17 to Monday December 18,
2023. File-name convention: submitted file should be named by student first name and surname (for
example NikolayShilov.pdf).

The examination on Discrete Mathematics & Logic
(Innopolis University, December 14, 2020)

Task 1 (5 points)
(Variant 1) What is the number of ways of choosing r objects from n types of objects (with
repetitions)? Prove this. Give non-trivial examples.
(Variant 2) Prove Binomial Theorem. What is the coefficient of (x − 2)11 at x6 ?
(Variant 3) What are the number of k permutations of a set with n distinct objects (without
repetitions) and the number of r-combinations of n object (without repetitions)? Prove the both!
Give one example for each.

Task 2 (5 points)
(Variant 1) What is the algorithm for solving a linear recursive sequence? Give and prove the solution
for Fibonacci numbers.
(Variant 2) What are the Stirling numbers? Give and prove the recursive formula for them.
(Variant 3) What are multinomial coefficients? Explain your answer. Give examples.

Task 3 (5 points)
Let G = (V , E ) be a graph. Find all spanning trees. What of them are pairwise non-isomorphic?
Where V = {1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6} and
(Variant 1) E = {{1, 2}, {2, 3}, {2, 4}, {2, 5}, {3, 6}, {4, 5}, {5, 6}}
(Variant 2) E = {{1, 3}, {1, 5}, {1, 6}, {2, 5}, {3, 4}, {4, 5}, {5, 6}}
(Variant 3) E = {{1, 3}, {1, 6}, {2, 5}, {2, 6}, {3, 5}, {3, 6}, {4, 6}}

Task 4 (5 points)
Give with explanations an example of
(Variant 1) a 4-colourable graph such that it is Eulerian, but is not Hamiltonian.
(Variant 2) a 4-colourable graph such that it is Hamiltonian, but is not Eulerian.
(Variant 3) a 4-colourable graph such that it is neither Hamiltonian, nor Eulerian.

Task 5 (5 points)
(Variant 1) Suppose that you know only Euler’s formula. Find all n, m such that K n,m is planar.
Explain your answer.
(Variant 2) Suppose that you know only Euler’s formula. Find all n such that K n is planar. Explain
your answer.
(Variant 3) Suppose that you know only Euler’s formula. Prove that if G is a planar graph and
v G ≥ 3 then eG ≤ 3v G − 6 .

Question 5. (2 points) Answer the following questions regarding the TDM data-link layer protocol.
Answer:
a) What is the key purpose of the TDM protocol?
To provide access to multiple network nodes over a shared communication channel.
b) What is its major drawback?
Every time slot is reserved for a specific node to send data. Even in case that specific node does not
send data, other nodes must wait for their time slots before sending data, what drastically decreases
the network channel utilization efficiency.

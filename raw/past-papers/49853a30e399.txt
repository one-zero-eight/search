The second grading assignment (oral poll) on Mathematical Analysis
(Innopolis University, Fall semester 2023, BS-I)

Description
Overall cost of the assignment is 15 points. This assignment is designed to evaluate how do we understand
each other during study weeks 5-6 of
• real sequences, their convergence and limits,
• limits of real functions at real and infinite points, and
• basic theorems about sequence’ and function’ convergence,
and how we are ready to continue our studies of the continuity of functions.
The Assignments consist of the following.
1. Build a table of size 9x2 where for each of 9 exercises from the list below and indicate in the table
your belief
a. whether you don’t understand the exercise’ task/question (by putting 0),
b. or understand the task/question but not sure how to solve/answer it (by putting 1),
c. or you believe that you understand and can solve/answer the task/question (by putting 2).
2. Provide solutions for 2 (not more than 2!) exercises (preferably from different sub-topics) that you
think that you understand and can solve as a proof/evidence/justification for your belief (in the filledin table).

Submission and grading rules
Please write your table and solutions on a paper in readable way, make photos/scan, and upload as a
single pdf-document to Moodle by the midnight from Sunday October 15 to Monday October 16,
2023. File-name convention: submitted file should be named by student first name and surname (for
example NikolayShilov.pdf).

Consider the following table:
Faculty (facName, dept, office, rank, dateHired)

Assume that no faculty member within a single department has the same name. Each faculty member is
placed in one office (faculty members do not share offices), considering FACNAME as primary key.
Which of the following statements is true about the faculty table?
a. Not in 3NF, not in BCNF
b. In 3NF, in BCNF
c. Not in 3NF, in BCNF
d. In 3NF, not in BCNF

The table is in 3NF, because every non prime attributes is fully functionally dependent on the primary key,
which is facName, and it is not in BCNF because the functionally dependency office → dept shows that a
non-prime attribute (dept) is dependent on something other than a superkey, since office is not a candidate
key or part of one, as BCNF requires that for every functional dependency, the left-hand side should be a
superkey, which is not the case for office.
The correct answer is:
In 3NF, not in BCNF

The final assignment (final overall examination) on Mathematical Analysis
(Innopolis University, Fall semester 2024, BS-I)

Assignment Description
Overall cost of the assignment is 40 points. This assignment is designed to evaluate how do we understand
topics of the course and each other during the Fall semester of Academic Year 2024/25. It covers the
following topics:
• Varieties of number systems (axiomatic and set-theoretic approaches)
• Theory of limits and continuity
• Differentiation, derivatives, and differential
• Antiderivative and indefinite integral

Assignment Specification
1. It comprises 13 tasks (questions/exercises), 9 tasks for the first 3 topics and 4 tasks for the last one.
2. Student attempting the tasks must build a table of size 13x2 where for each of 13 tasks from the list
below and indicate in the table his/her belief
a. whether he/she doesn’t understand the task (by putting 0),
b. or he/she understands the task but not sure how to solve the task (by putting 1),
c. or he/she can solve the task (by putting 2).
3. Provide solutions/answers for 3 (not more than three) tasks that you think that you understand and
can solve as proof/evidence/justification for your belief (in the filled-in table).
a. two of provided solution/answer should be for any of tasks 1-9 (but for different topics would
be better),
b. the third of provided solution/answer should be for any of tasks 10-13.

Submission and grading rules

single pdf-document to Moodle by midnight from Thursday December 12 to Friday December 13,

The first grading midterm (written & oral) on Mathematical Analysis
(Innopolis University, Fall semester 2024, BS24)

Description
Overall cost of the assignment is 30 points. This assignment is designed to evaluate how do we
understand each other during study in weeks 1-5 (Spring semester 2024/25) of the following
topics:
• Numeric and power series,
• Definite and improper integrals with applications.
The Assignments consist of the following.
1. Build a self-assessment table of size 12x2 where for each of 12 exercises from the list
below and indicate in the table your belief
a. whether you don’t understand the exercise’ task/question (by putting 0),
b. or understand the task/question but not sure how to solve/answer it (by putting 1),
c. or you believe that you understand and can solve/answer the task/question (by
putting 2).
2. Provide solutions/answers for 3 (not more than three) exercises (preferably from different
sub-topics) that you think that you understand and can solve as proof/evidence/justification
for your belief (in the filled-in table).
a. the first of provided solution/answer should be for any of exercises in 1-5,
b. the second one – for any of exercises in 6-12,
c. the last one is obligatory – for any of exercises in 1-12.

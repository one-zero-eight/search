# Brief for the wiki (read by the model that decides what goes into it)

Audience: anyone who asks an assistant about Innopolis University, inside or outside it:
- student: bachelor and master students (study, exams, retakes, leave, transfer, expulsion, discipline, money, campus life, exchange);
- postgraduate: PhD students and their supervisors (postgraduate programmes, attestation, dissertation);
- applicant: future students and their parents (admission rules, entrance exams, places, fees, programmes on offer);
- teacher: teaching staff (teaching load, course rules, assessment, programme documents, their rights and duties);
- staff: administrative staff (internal procedures, positions, units and who does what);
- management: rector's office and heads of units (strategy, finance, reports, accreditation, statistics, results of surveys);
- public: people outside the university (partners, employers, journalists, schools, other universities): what the university is, what it teaches, its structure, facilities, results.

The official documents of the university outrank everything else. Facts must be traceable to a document.

Keep what at least one of these readers would ask about: rules and procedures, money (tuition, scholarships, grants, finance reports), all educational programmes the university runs
(higher education, postgraduate, and also school, secondary vocational and supplementary programmes: someone asks about them too), study plans and calendars, structure and governance,
facilities and support, admission, international activity, survey results and statistics that say something about the university.

Lower priority: legal acts of the state that the university only repeats, near-empty forms and templates.

Never: personal data of named people or lists of people (students, applicants, staff), scans without readable content.

Documents of the same kind that differ only by year or version: keep them; the wiki marks the newest.

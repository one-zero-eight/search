# Brief for the wiki (read by the model that decides what goes into it)

Audience: current bachelor and master students of Innopolis University who ask an assistant practical questions.
They need: rules and procedures (study, exams, retakes, leave, transfer, expulsion, discipline), money (tuition, scholarships, material aid),
the curriculum (study plans, calendars, programme documents), campus life and support (dormitory, support offices, who to ask), international programmes and exchange.

The official documents of the university outrank everything else. Facts must be traceable to a document.

Lower priority: applicants only (entrance exams, admission lists), documents about staff, finance reports, accreditation paperwork, legal acts of the state
that the university only repeats, internal rules of units that students never deal with.

Never: personal data of named students or lists of people, surveys and statistics tables, scans without readable content, forms with nothing to read.

Programmes: only higher education (bachelor, master, postgraduate) matters. School programmes, secondary vocational (SPO) programmes, supplementary and short professional training
programmes (DPO, professional training) are not for these students.
Documents of the same kind that differ only by year or version: keep them; the wiki marks the newest.

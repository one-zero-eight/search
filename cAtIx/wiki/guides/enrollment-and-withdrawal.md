# Enrollment Contract and Voluntary Withdrawal

## Enrollment Contract Contents

The enrollment contract must specify the type, level, and direction of the educational program (or part of a program of a certain level, type, and/or direction) [1]. It must include the main characteristics of the provided education (educational service) [1]. The contract must state the form of study and the terms for mastering the educational program (duration of study) [1]. It must indicate the type of document issued to the student after successful completion of the corresponding educational program (or part of the program) [1]. The procedure for changing and terminating the contract must be outlined [1]. Other necessary information related to the specifics of the paid educational services provided must also be included [1].

## Voluntary Withdrawal Procedures

Educational relations may be terminated early at the own desire of the student or the parents (legal representatives) of a minor student, including in the case of the student's transfer to another organization carrying out educational activities [2].

To initiate early termination of educational relations, the student or the parents (legal representatives) of a minor student must apply to the University administration [3]. The application must include the following documents:
*   A personal application to the director for dismissal at the student's own desire [3].
*   A clearance sheet in the form established by the University [3].
*   A receipt for payment for education for the corresponding period of study (if applicable) [3].
*   A student ID card and grade book (if they were issued to the student) [3].

Dismissal of the student at the initiative of the student or the parents (legal representatives) of a minor student is carried out within a period of no more than one month from the moment of submitting the corresponding application [3].

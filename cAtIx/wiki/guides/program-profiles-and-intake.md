# Available Program Profiles and Admission Years

## Specializations and Admission Years
Students and applicants, the official document lists the following specializations and admission years for the requested programs [1][2][3][4][5].
- Informatics and Computational Engineering (Bachelor's): AI360: Data Engineering, 2024 admission year [1].
- Mechatronics and Robotics (Master's): Robotics, 2024 admission year [2].
- Informatics and Computational Engineering (Master's): Computer Game Development Management, 2024 admission year [3].
- Business Informatics (Postgraduate): Informational, Technological, Entrepreneurship, 2023 admission year [4].
- Informatics and Computational Engineering (Postgraduate): Mathematical modeling, numerical methods and software complexes, 2021 admission year [5].
- The same postgraduate clause also lists 2022, 2023, and 2024 admission years for master's programs, but does not name the profiles for those master's programs [5].
- The document directs you to the university's official website for information on all implemented educational programs, specifically in the "Information about the educational organization" section under the "Education" subsection [5].

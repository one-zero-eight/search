# Mandatory vs Optional Course Status

## Mandatory vs Optional Status
Facultative disciplines (modules) are optional for you to study [1]. They are not included in the annual volume of your educational program [1]. Elective disciplines (modules) are mandatory for you to study in bachelor's, master's, and postgraduate programs developed in accordance with FGOS VO [2]. Elective disciplines (modules) are also mandatory for postgraduates studying under programs developed in accordance with FGT, if the University includes them in your postgraduate program [2].

## How Requirements Differ
You must select elective disciplines (modules) from alternative options in your study plan [3]. Each block must contain at least two disciplines (modules) [3]. You may choose no more than one discipline from each block [3]. If a discipline (module) appears in two or more blocks of your study plan, you may choose it for study no more than once [3]. Facultative disciplines do not carry these selection limits or mandatory requirements [1].

## Gaps and caveats
The evidence does not specify deadlines for choosing electives or facultative courses [1][2][3]. It does not explain the procedure for making selections or where to submit them [1][2][3]. It does not define what constitutes the annual volume of an educational program [1]. It does not state what happens if you fail to choose a mandatory elective [2]. It does not provide details on how course pages list these disciplines or how they differ from the regulation [1][2][3]. The regulation is silent on registration steps, grading, or credit allocation for facultative courses [1]. No course pages are provided to compare against the regulation, but the regulation takes precedence if any course page differs or goes beyond it [1][2][3].

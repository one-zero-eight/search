# Mandatory vs Optional Course Status

## Mandatory vs Optional Course Status

Elective modules are mandatory for you if you are enrolled in bachelor's, master's, or postgraduate programs [3]. You must choose no more than one module from each block of alternatives, where each block contains at least two modules [4]. If a module appears in multiple blocks, you may study it only once [4]. If you do not choose an elective module by the deadline, the university assigns you to a study group for a specific module [6]. You cannot change your elective module selection after the course begins [5].

Facultative modules are optional for you [1]. You may change your list of planned facultative modules or refuse to study them entirely [1]. Any changes require approval from the university department responsible for organizing the educational process, considering the objective possibility of organizing the educational process [1]. You cannot change your facultative module selection after the course begins [1]. If you do not choose a facultative module by the deadline, you are exempt from studying it [2].

## Gaps and caveats

The evidence does not specify the exact deadlines for choosing modules [2][6]. The evidence does not define the specific criteria for "objective possibility" when approving changes to facultative modules [1]. The evidence does not state how the university decides which module to assign you to if you fail to choose an elective module [6]. The evidence does not detail the procedure for the intermediate certification of facultative modules beyond stating it follows local acts [2]. The evidence does not specify the consequences of failing the intermediate certification for elective modules [3]. The evidence does not clarify if the exemption from facultative modules affects your overall academic progress [2].

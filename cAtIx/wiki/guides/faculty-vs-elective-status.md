# Mandatory vs Optional Course Status

## Mandatory vs Optional Course Status

Faculty courses are not mandatory for students and are not included in the annual volume of the educational program [1].

Elective disciplines (modules) are mandatory for students in bachelor’s, master’s, and graduate programs [2]. For graduate students in programs developed in accordance with FGT, electives are mandatory only if the University has included them in the graduate program [2].

In study plans, elective disciplines (modules) are presented alternatively [3]. Each block contains at least two disciplines (modules) [3]. Students may select no more than one discipline from a block [3]. If a discipline (module) is presented in two or more blocks, a student may choose to study it no more than once [3].

## Gaps and caveats

The evidence does not specify the total number of elective blocks or the specific number of credits required for electives. The evidence does not define what constitutes a "block" beyond the alternative selection rule. The evidence does not address consequences for failing to select an elective. The evidence does not specify procedures for resolving conflicts if a student wishes to take a discipline offered in multiple blocks beyond the one-time limit.

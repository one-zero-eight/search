# Admission Rankings and Enrollment Procedure

## Ranked Admission Lists
The ranked admission lists are published on the university's official website no later than August 18, 2026 [1].

## Submitting Original Documents and Enrollment
You must submit your original education document and/or your education and qualification document [2]. If you possess a document confirming priority or special admission rights, you must also submit it [2]. You must complete this submission by August 21, 2026 [2].
If you submitted your application using the state services portals, you confirm your consent to enrollment and submit your original education document through the portal's functionality [3]. You must do this by August 21, 2026, and if vacancies remain, by November 25, 2026 [3].
After the deadlines for submitting original documents pass, the university issues an enrollment order for applicants recommended by the admissions committee who submitted their original documents [4]. For applicants who used the state services portals, the order is issued based on their portal-confirmed consent and the electronic duplicate of their education document [4]. The order includes a name list of these individuals [4]. The order and its list are posted on the next working day after issuance on the admissions committee's information board and on the university's official website [4].
The enrollment order is issued only after you provide the original education documents, sign a contract for paid educational services, and pay for your tuition [5]. You must provide the tuition payment receipt personally to the university or send it to college@innopolis.ru [5]. The order will be issued no later than August 25, 2026 [5].

## Gaps and caveats

# Final state attestation (graduation)

## Forms and structure
The FSC applies uniformly to Bachelor’s and Master’s Degree programs [1]. The schedule is set independently by the University [4]. The scope, structure, and content are established by the University in line with Educational Standards [5]. Specific forms are determined independently by the University for each Educational Program according to FSES HE requirements [6]. The typical form for all enrolled students is a defense of a Final Qualification Work [7]. The FSC Program, which contains the requirements, completion procedure, and assessment criteria, is approved by the Academic Council and communicated to students no later than six months before the FSC begins [8]. The FSC Program forms part of the Educational Program [8]. Students with disabilities form a distinct group that shares common accommodations, including joint examination rooms when feasible, dedicated technical assistants, permission for technical aids, and unimpeded facility access [10]. All local regulations for this group are provided in accessible formats [11].

## Commissions
State Examination Committees are established to conduct the FSC [12]. Appeal Committees are established to handle appeals regarding FSC results [12]. Both committee types operate for a period of one calendar year [12]. The University independently establishes the rules of procedure for the committees [12]. Committees are grouped by academic scope, covering individual fields of study, individual programs, or aggregated groups of fields and programs [13]. Leadership roles are split into two groups: State Examination Committee chairs, who are external experts with doctoral or professorial credentials or employer representation approved by the Ministry [14], and Appeal Committee chairs, who are internal university appointees [14]. The Ministry approval for State Examination Committee chairs must occur no later than December 31 of the year preceding the FSC year [14].

## Thesis requirements
The Final Qualification Work demonstrates the graduate's level of preparedness for independent professional activity [15]. The type, requirements, completion procedure, and assessment criteria are established independently by the University in accordance with Educational Standards and the Regulations [16]. Works are grouped by degree level into Bachelor's Theses and Master's Theses [8]. Collaborative works form a subgroup where joint authorship is permitted within a single field of study, provided individual contributions are explicitly delineated [17]. A supervisor's review form for a Bachelor's Thesis is provided in both Russian and English [23].

## Appeals
Students have a uniform right to appeal the results of the FSC [18]. The procedure for filing and considering appeals is communicated to students no later than six months before the commencement of the FSC [19]. Appeals form a single procedural track requiring written submissions to the Appeal Committee regarding alleged violations of the established certification procedure [20]. The application form for this track is located in Appendix 19 to the Regulations [20].

# Final state attestation (graduation)

## Forms and structure of the attestation

The Final State Certification (FSC) is conducted by State Qualifying Committees to determine whether learning outcomes comply with Educational Standards [3]. The schedule for conducting the FSC is determined independently by the University [4]. The scope, structure, and content of the FSC for each Educational Program are established by the University in accordance with the requirements of the Educational Standards [5]. Specific forms of the FSC are determined independently by the University for each Educational Program, in accordance with the requirements set by the FSES HE [6]. For students enrolled in Educational Programs at the University, the FSC is conducted in the form of a defense of a Final Qualification Work [7].

## Thesis requirements

The University has established the following types of Final Qualification Works corresponding to the levels of higher education: for the Bachelor's level – a Bachelor’s Thesis; for the Master's level – a Master’s Thesis [8]. The Final State Certification Program, which includes the requirements for Final Qualification Works and the procedure for their completion, as well as the criteria for assessing the results of the FQW defense approved by the University, shall be communicated to students no later than six months prior to the commencement of the FSC [8]. The FSC Program constitutes a part of the Educational Program and is approved by the Academic Council of the University [8].

The Final Qualification Work is a piece of work completed by a student (or jointly by several students), demonstrating the graduate's level of preparedness for independent professional activity [15]. The type of the Final Qualification Work, the requirements for it, the procedure for its completion, and the criteria for its assessment are established independently by the University in accordance with the requirements set by the Educational Standards (where such requirements exist) and these Regulations [16]. The Final Qualification Work may be completed jointly by several students within the same field of study of an educational program [17]. In a Final Qualification Work completed jointly by several students, the individual contribution of each student must be clearly identified [17].

## Commissions

State Examination Committees are established at the University to conduct the FSC [12]. Appeal Committees are established at the University to consider appeals regarding FSC results [12]. The State Examination and Appeal Committees are valid for a period of one calendar year [12]. The University independently establishes the rules of procedure for the Committees [12]. Committees are established at the University for each field of study, or for each Educational Program, or for a group of fields of study, or for a group of Educational Programs [13].

The Chairperson of a State Examination Committee is approved by the Ministry of Science and Higher Education of the Russian Federation, upon nomination by the University [14]. The nominee must not be employed by the University, must hold a Doctor of Sciences degree and/or the academic title of Professor, or be a leading specialist representing employers or their associations in the relevant professional field [14]. Approval must occur no later than December 31 of the year preceding the year in which the FSC is conducted [14]. The Chairperson of an Appeal Committee is appointed by the Director of the University or another authorised representative [14].

## Accessibility and accommodations

For students with disabilities, the FSC shall be conducted at the University taking into account their psychophysical development characteristics, individual capabilities, and state of health (hereinafter referred to as "Individual Characteristics") [9]. During the conduct of the FSC, the following general requirements shall be ensured: conducting the FSC for persons with disabilities in the same room as students without disabilities, provided this does not create difficulties for the persons with disabilities or other students during the FSC; presence of an assistant (assistants) in the room who provides the necessary technical assistance to students with disabilities, considering their Individual Characteristics (e.g., taking their seat, moving around, reading and completing assignments, communicating with the chair and members of the State Qualifying Committee); permission for students with disabilities to use the necessary technical aids during the FSC, considering their Individual Characteristics; ensuring unimpeded access for students with disabilities to the examination rooms, restrooms, and other premises, as [...the clause continues; do not state what it leaves out] [10]. All local regulations of the University concerning the conduct of the FSC shall be communicated to students with disabilities in an accessible format [11].

## Appeals

A student has the right to appeal the results of the FSC [18]. Students should be informed about the procedure for filing and considering appeals no later than 6 months before the commencement of the FSC [19]. A student has the right to submit a written appeal to the Appeal Committee concerning an alleged violation of the established procedure for conducting the state certification [20]. The application form is provided in Appendix 19 to these Regulations [20].

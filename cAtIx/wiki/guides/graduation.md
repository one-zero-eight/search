# Final state attestation (graduation)

## Forms of the Final State Attestation
The Final State Certification applies to students completing state-accredited Bachelor’s and Master’s Degree programs at Innopolis University [1]. The schedule for conducting the certification is determined independently by the University [4]. The scope, structure, and content of the certification for each educational program are established by the University in accordance with the Educational Standards [5]. Specific forms of the certification are determined independently by the University for each program in line with the Federal State Educational Standards [6]. For enrolled students, the certification is conducted as a defense of a Final Qualification Work [7]. The certification program containing the requirements and assessment criteria is approved by the Academic Council and forms part of the educational program [8].

## State Examination and Appeal Commissions
State Examination Committees and Appeal Committees are established at the University to conduct the certification and handle appeals, respectively [12]. These committees operate for a period of one calendar year [12]. The University independently establishes their rules of procedure [12]. Committees are grouped by field of study, educational program, or combined groups of fields or programs [13]. The State Examination Committee Chair is nominated by the University and must be approved by the Ministry of Science and Higher Education of the Russian Federation by December 31 of the preceding year [14]. The chair must not be employed by the University and must hold a Doctor of Sciences degree and or the academic title of Professor, or serve as a leading specialist representing employers in the relevant field [14]. The Appeal Committee chair is appointed directly by the University director or an authorized representative [14].

## Final Qualification Work Requirements
The Final Qualification Work demonstrates a graduate’s preparedness for independent professional activity [15]. At the Bachelor’s level the work is a Bachelor’s Thesis, while at the Master’s level it is a Master’s Thesis [8]. The type, requirements, completion procedure, and assessment criteria are established independently by the University in accordance with the Educational Standards and these regulations [16]. Students may complete the work jointly within the same field of study, provided each student’s individual contribution is clearly identified [17]. The certification program detailing these requirements is communicated to students no later than six months before the certification begins [8].

## Appeals Process
Students have the right to appeal certification results [18]. The procedure for filing and considering appeals is communicated to students no later than six months before the certification commences [19]. Appeals must be submitted in writing to the Appeal Committee regarding alleged violations of the established certification procedure [20]. The required application form is located in Appendix 19 of the regulations [20].

## Accessibility Provisions
The certification is conducted for students with disabilities while accounting for their psychophysical development, individual capabilities, and health status [9]. Students with disabilities are examined in the same room as other students, provided this does not create difficulties for them or others [10]. An assistant is present to provide technical assistance tailored to individual characteristics, such as seating, movement, reading, or communicating with the committee [10]. Students with disabilities are permitted to use necessary technical aids during the certification [10]. The University ensures unimpeded access to examination rooms, restrooms, and other premises [10]. All local regulations concerning the certification are provided to students with disabilities in an accessible format [11].

# Teacher and curator responsibilities in distance learning DPO programs

## Rights

You have the right to control the learning process by assigning control works and test tasks that are provided for in the educational program [1]. You have the right to organize forums and online consultations [1]. You have the right not to allow a student who has not mastered the previous module of the educational program to study the next module in the case of sequential learning [1].

## Obligations

You are obliged to assist in processing information and consult students if necessary [2]. You must help students document the results of their independent activities through communication via the internet or any method convenient for you and the student [2]. You must provide feedback during the execution of tasks [2]. This feedback includes clarifying the degree of task completion, answering questions, diagnosing the causes of difficulties, giving recommendations, and helping to determine ways to solve problems [2]. You are obliged to design and carry out high-quality teaching of academic disciplines conducted in a distance learning format [2]. You must timely replenish and edit educational and methodological complexes [2]. You must answer student questions in a timely manner [2]. You must systematically improve your professional qualifications, including in the field of mastering specific tools that allow you to conduct distance learning [2].

## Tutoring Duties

As a teacher or curator using distance learning technologies, you also perform the duties of a tutor [3]. Your duties as a tutor include organizing the process of individual work with students [4]. You must assist students in overcoming difficulties in the process of self-education and in mastering new technologies, including those related to the field of distance learning [4]. You must monitor all actions of students [4]. You must support the cognitive interest of the student [4]. You must organize individual and group consultations [4].

# Перевод с одной образовательной программы на другую

## Procedure for Transfer

To transfer from one study program, specialization, or form of study to another, you must follow the procedure established by the University's local regulatory acts governing student transfers [1].

**Eligibility and Timing**
You may apply for a transfer no earlier than after completing the first intermediate assessment, provided you have no academic debts [2]. Your application must be submitted to the structural unit responsible for organizing educational activities [3]. The application must be addressed to the University Director and must follow the form prescribed by the local regulatory act governing transfers [3]. You must submit this application no later than seven working days before the start of the semester [3].

**Review Process**
Upon receiving your application, the University organizes an attestation commission to consider the possibility of your transfer [3]. The attestation commission reviews the submitted documents and determines the list of disciplines (modules), other components, including practices, scientific research, and scientific (research) activities that will be credited upon transfer, in accordance with the University's local regulatory act [5]. The commission then makes a decision on the possibility of transferring you to another specialization, program, or form of study [5].

**Conditions for Transfer**
A transfer is possible if there is established correspondence between the list, workload, and periods of study of disciplines (modules), other components (including practices), and the calendar academic schedule of your current program and the target program [4]. If these conditions are met, you may be transferred to study under the basic academic plan [4].

**Decision and Notification**
After making its decision, the attestation commission sets the date of your transfer to the new specialization, program, or form of study [5]. The commission's decision is communicated to you in writing under your personal signature and/or in the form of an electronic document using the University's electronic information and educational environment within ten working days after the decision is made [5].

**Finalization**
Following the attestation commission's decision, the University Director or another person authorized by the Director issues an order regarding your transfer [6]. An individual academic plan is approved for you [6].

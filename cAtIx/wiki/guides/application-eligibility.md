# Application Rights and Foreign Citizen Employment Rules

## Eligibility by Academic Position
Applicants, teachers, staff, and the public should review the qualification requirements for each academic position below. [2][3][4][5]
To apply for a professor position, you must hold a Doctor of Sciences degree and at least 5 years of scientific-pedagogical experience, or hold a professor academic title. [2]
To apply for an associate professor position, you must have higher professional education, a Candidate or Doctor of Sciences degree, and at least 3 years of scientific-pedagogical experience, or hold a docent or senior researcher academic title. [3]
To apply for a senior lecturer position, you must have higher professional education and at least 3 years of scientific-pedagogical experience, or at least 1 year of experience if you hold a Candidate of Sciences degree. [4]
To apply for a lecturer position, you must have higher professional education and at least 1 year of work experience in an educational institution, or no experience requirement if you have postgraduate education or a Candidate of Sciences degree. [4]
To apply for an assistant position, you must have higher professional education and at least 1 year of work experience in an educational institution, or no experience requirement if you have postgraduate education or a Candidate of Sciences degree. [5]
For senior lecturer, lecturer, and assistant positions, candidates with a Candidate of Sciences degree receive priority in the competitive selection. [4][5]

## Foreign Education Recognition
Qualifications and education obtained in a foreign state are considered if they are recognized in the Russian Federation according to international treaties or Russian legislation. [6]

## Requirements and Procedures for Foreign Citizens
If you are a foreign citizen who passes the competition, the Department of Attraction and Support of Teaching Staff prepares and sends you a job offer. [1]
If you accept the job offer, the Department handles the administrative support for your invitation and admission, which includes preparing a visa invitation and a work permit, including for highly qualified specialists. [1]
Upon employment, you must present the following documents to the Department of Attraction and Support of Teaching Staff or the Personnel Accounting and Movement Department:
- A passport. [7]
- A diploma, including foreign diplomas that are legalized and recognized in the Russian Federation. [7]
- A diploma supplement, if available, including foreign supplements that are legalized and recognized in the Russian Federation. [7]
- A document confirming a scientific degree or academic title, including foreign documents that are legalized and recognized in the Russian Federation. [7]
- Documents confirming your pedagogical, scientific, or scientific-pedagogical experience. [7]
- A certificate of criminal record or absence thereof, or a certificate of termination of criminal prosecution on rehabilitating grounds, legalized for the Russian Federation. [7]
- A marriage certificate, legalized for the Russian Federation. [7]
- Birth certificates for children under 18, if applicable, legalized for the Russian Federation. [7]
- Notarized Russian translations of all documents if they are in a foreign language, prepared according to Russian legislation. [7]
- A medical book and conclusions from medical and psychiatric examinations conducted based on directions issued by the Department. [7]

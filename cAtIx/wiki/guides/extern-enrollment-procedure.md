# How to Enroll as an Extern

## Application deadline
You must submit your application and all attached documents no later than one month before the University's established start date of the academic year [5].

## Where and how to submit
Submit a personal application addressed to the head of the University to the structural unit responsible for admissions [1]. When you submit the application, you must present an identity document [2]. You must also complete a consent form for the processing of personal data in accordance with Federal Law No. 152-FZ of 27.07.2006 [3]. Based on this consent, the University may enter your information into federal, regional, and municipal information systems [3].

## Required documents
Attach the following to your application [1]:
- A copy of your education document or education and qualification document [1].
- A copy of a certificate (or certificates) of your study period confirming successful completion of previous intermediate certifications at other educational organizations, if you are studying in the form of self-education [1].
- A 3x4 cm photograph [1].
- Any other documents that serve your interests as a person wishing to be enrolled as an extern [1].

## Review and decision
The University's examination commission reviews your submitted documents to decide whether you can be enrolled as an extern [4]. The commission's decision is formalized in a protocol [4].

## Enrollment completion
Based on the examination commission's protocol, the University concludes a contract for paid educational services with you as an extern [6]. The University then issues an order enrolling you as an extern to pass intermediate and final state certification [6].

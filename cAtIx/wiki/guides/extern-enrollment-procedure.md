# How to Enroll as an Extern

## Deadline
You must submit your application and all attached documents no later than one month before the university's established start date of the academic year [5].

## Submission and required documents
You submit a personal application addressed to the director of the university to the university structural unit responsible for admissions [1]. When you submit the application, you must present a document that verifies your identity [2]. You must also complete a consent form for the processing of your personal data in accordance with Federal Law No. 152-FZ dated 27.07.2006 "On Personal Data" [3]. The university uses this consent to enter your information into federal, regional, and municipal information systems [3]. Your application must include the following documents [1]:
- A copy of your education document or your education and qualification document [1].
- A copy of a certificate (or copies of certificates) of your study period, confirming successful completion of previous interim certifications at other educational organizations, if you are studying in the form of self-education [1].
- A 3x4 cm photograph [1].
- Any other documents whose submission serves your interests as a person wishing to be enrolled as an extern [1].

## Review and decision
The university's examination commission reviews your submitted documents and makes a decision on your possibility of enrollment [4]. The commission's decision is formalized in a protocol [4].

## Enrollment and contract
Based on the examination commission's protocol, the university signs a contract for paid educational services with you as an extern [6]. The university then issues an order enrolling you as an extern to pass the interim and state final certification [6].

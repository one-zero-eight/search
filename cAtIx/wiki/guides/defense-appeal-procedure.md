# How to appeal your thesis defense results

## Deadline to submit
You must submit your appeal no later than the next working day after the state final certification results are announced [3].

## How to file the appeal
You have the right to appeal the results of your thesis defense [1]. You may submit a written appeal to the appellate commission regarding a violation of the established procedure for conducting the state final certification [2]. You must send a scanned copy of the appeal application, which must include your signature and a transcription of your full name, to the email address education@innopolis.ru [3].

## What happens after submission
After you submit your appeal, the ГЭК secretary forwards the case materials to the appellate commission via email [4]. The forwarded materials include a scanned copy of the ГЭК meeting protocol, a scanned copy of the ГЭК chairman’s conclusion on procedural compliance, the defense video recording, your thesis, and the review(s) if necessary [4]. The chairman of the appellate commission must conduct an online meeting to review your appeal no later than 2 working days after your application is submitted [5]. The meeting is conducted via video conference using Microsoft Teams, Zoom, or similar software [5]. The secretary of the appellate commission keeps a protocol of the meeting, and the meeting is recorded [5]. On the day of the meeting, the chairman sends the results to the Methodological Department by emailing education@innopolis.ru with a scanned copy of the protocol [6]. The Methodological Department then notifies you of the results via your corporate email no later than the next working day after the appellate commission meeting [7].

## Finality and further steps
The decision of the appellate commission is final and cannot be revised [8]. If necessary, a repeated thesis defense may be conducted by the ГЭК in the presence of one appellate commission member according to the requirements of clause 2 of the regulation [9]. An appeal against a repeated state final certification is not accepted [10].

## Gaps and caveats
The evidence does not specify what constitutes a violation of the established procedure that justifies an appeal [1][2]. It does not state what happens if the 2-working-day deadline for the commission meeting is missed [5]. It does not provide details on the requirements of clause 2 mentioned for a repeated defense [9]. It does not specify the format or content requirements for the written appeal beyond the signature and full name transcription [3]. It does not state whether you can attend the online commission meeting or present your case [5]. It does not clarify how the Methodological Department determines your corporate email address [7].

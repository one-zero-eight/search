# Financial Aid, Scholarships, and Grants

## Eligibility for Grants

To participate in the competition for a grant for higher education programs (bachelor’s and master’s), you must be a citizen of a country not belonging to the Commonwealth of Independent States (CIS) [1]. You must be a student in the final year of secondary general education, a graduate of secondary general education, a graduate of secondary vocational education (for bachelor’s programs), or a current student or graduate of higher education (for master’s programs) [1]. Your educational documents must be legalized according to Russian legislation and translated into Russian, unless an international treaty provides otherwise [1]. You cannot participate if you are or have been a student under certain conditions that are not fully specified in the available text [1]. You may participate in the competition no more than once per year [8].

## Application Procedure for Grants

The competition is held annually from October 1 of the year preceding admission to August 10 of the admission year [2].

The first stage includes registration and online testing on the Official Website, with dates determined by the Organizer [3]. To participate in the first stage, you must:
*   Register on the Official Website as a Contestant [4].
*   Pass online testing [4].
*   Provide the following information and documents:
    *   An application form filled out on the Official Website, including full name, date of birth, citizenship, city of residence, and details of the educational organization, course, and program [4].
    *   A motivation letter in English justifying your desire to receive the Grant and study at the University [4].
    *   A portfolio in English showing your achievements during your studies [4].
    *   Copies of your certificate of secondary (full) general education or diploma of secondary vocational education (for bachelor’s applicants) or diploma of higher education (for master’s applicants) [4].
    *   A certificate confirming English language knowledge (if available) [4].
    *   Consent to the processing of personal data in the form established by the University, including via simple electronic signature (checkbox) on the website [4].
    *   Any other documents and information requested by the Organizer [4].

The passing score for the first stage is:
*   45% correct answers for the English language test (for bachelor’s) and 50% for the English language test (for master’s) [5].
*   45% correct answers for the profile test [5].

Participants who meet all conditions of clause 2.2 and achieve the passing test score are invited to the second stage via email [6]. You must confirm your participation in the second stage within 7 calendar days of receiving the invitation [6]. Failure to confirm results in withdrawal from the competition [6]. The date of receipt is considered the date the message was sent by the Organizer [6].

Winners must:
*   Conclude an education contract with the University before the date of the admission order [7].
*   Conclude a Grant agreement with the University before the start of the academic year following the completion of the current year’s competition [7].
*   Participate in the "Summer School" organized by the University (if held), on a voluntary basis [7].

## Material Aid Eligibility and Application

To qualify for material aid, your cumulative grade point average (CGPA) from the previous semester must be less than 4.33 for Russian students and less than 4.37 for international students [9]. Your per capita family income (including parents and minor siblings) must be below the subsistence minimum in your region of residence (for Russian students) or below the subsistence minimum in your country of residence (for international students) [9].

You must submit the following documents to the Student Support and Development Department:
*   A certificate of family composition [10].
*   A certificate of parents' income [10].

International students may provide documents in English or their native language [11]. The Department can assist with translation, but the student is responsible for the translation [11]. Documents and the application can be submitted on any working day, including during holidays [12].

## Material Aid Payment and Retention

Material aid can be received as a one-time payment or monthly for the entire semester until the next interim assessment according to the academic calendar [13]. Payments are made on the nearest scholarship payment day if the application is submitted at least one week prior; otherwise, it is paid on the scholarship payment day of the following month [13].

To retain material aid throughout the semester, you must attend at least 70% of classes [15]. Failure to meet this requirement results in the cessation of material aid [15]. If your semester GPA does not improve compared to the previous semester, the University will refuse material aid for the next semester [16]. The final decision on granting material aid is made by the University Director or authorized person based on the Scholarship Commission's petition and is approved by order [14].

## Scholarships

University and state scholarships are appointed based on academic performance results from interim assessments, starting from the first day of the month following the assessment's end, no less than twice a year, by order of the University Director or authorized person based on the Commission's protocol [17].

**State Academic Scholarship**
This is appointed for full-time students on funded places using federal budget funds [18]. You must have no "satisfactory" grades in interim assessments and no academic debts [19].

**Increased State Academic Scholarship**
This is appointed for special achievements in one or more areas (academic, research, public, cultural-creative, or sports) [20].

**State Social Scholarship**
This is appointed for full-time students on funded places using federal budget funds to:
*   Orphans and children left without parental care, including those who lost both parents or a single parent during studies [21].
*   Children with disabilities, disabled persons of groups I and II, and disabled persons from childhood [21].
*   Persons exposed to radiation from the Chernobyl disaster or other radiation accidents, or from nuclear tests at the Semipalatinsk polygon [21].
*   Disabled persons due to military injury or disease during military service, and veterans of combat actions [21].
*   Recipients of state social assistance [21].
*   Citizens who served at least three years under contract in the Armed Forces, Internal Troops of the Ministry of Internal Affairs, engineering-technical, road-building military formations, rescue formations, foreign intelligence, security services, state protection, and federal services [21].

**Increased University Scholarship**
This is appointed for special achievements in one or more areas (academic, research, public, cultural-creative, or sports) [22].

**Increased Academic University Scholarship**
This is appointed to bachelor’s students who received "excellent" grades for the last two study periods preceding the appointment and have no academic debts [23]. It may also be appointed to students who have concluded a Grant agreement with the University that provides for the payment of University scholarships [24].

**Scholarships from Legal and Physical Persons**
The appointment order and payment terms for scholarships from legal and physical persons, including those who sent students for study, are established by the founders of the respective scholarships [25].

**Named University Scholarships**
These are appointed to students upon recommendation from a University structural unit [26]. The appointment order, amounts, and payment terms are established by the University's local normative acts [26].

# Financial Aid, Scholarships, and Grants

## Grants for Tuition
You must hold citizenship of a country outside the Commonwealth of Independent States to participate in the competition [1]. You must be in the final year of secondary education, a graduate of secondary vocational education (for bachelor's programs), or a current student or graduate of higher education (for master's programs) [1]. Your education documents must be legalized according to Russian law and translated into Russian unless an international treaty states otherwise [1]. You cannot participate if you were or are a student in [1]. The competition runs annually from 1 October of the year before admission to 10 August of the admission year [2]. Grants cover tuition starting from the academic year following the competition [2]. You may participate only once per year [8]. Stage one requires you to register on the Official Website and pass an online test [3]. You must submit a completed questionnaire, a motivation letter in English, a portfolio in English, copies of your education certificates or diplomas, an English proficiency certificate if available, and a signed consent to data processing [4]. The passing score is 45% correct answers for the English test for bachelor's programs or 50% for master's programs, and 45% for the profile test [5]. If you pass stage one, you will receive an invitation to your registered email [6]. You must confirm participation within 7 calendar days of the email's sending date, or you will be withdrawn from the competition [6]. Winners must sign an education contract by the admission order date, sign a grant contract by the start of the academic year, and attend the Summer School if organized [7].

## Material Aid
Your GPA from the previous semester must be below 4.33 for Russian students or below 4.37 for foreign students [9]. Your per-capita family income must be below the regional minimum subsistence level for Russian students or the country's minimum subsistence level for foreign students [9]. You can submit your application and documents any working day, including holidays [12]. You must provide a family composition certificate and a parents' income certificate [10]. Foreign students may submit documents in English or their home country's language, and you are responsible for translation even if the department assists [11]. You may receive aid once or monthly until the next interim assessment [13]. Payments occur on the nearest scholarship day if submitted at least one week prior, otherwise on the next month's scholarship day [13]. The director or authorized person decides based on the scholarship commission's petition [14]. You must attend at least 70% of classes to keep support, and failure stops payments [15]. If your semester GPA does not improve, you will be denied aid the following semester [16]. The commission decides on one-time aid based on your application and need documents [22]. Full-time students receive a maternity benefit from federal budget funds for 70 days before birth, 84 days for multiples, 70 days after birth, 86 days for complicated births, or 100 days for two or more children [23].

## Scholarships
You must have no "satisfactory" grade at interim assessment and no academic debt to receive a state scholarship [17]. Full-time students who are orphans, disabled, Chernobyl or radiation victims, military injury or veteran recipients, social aid recipients, or contract military personnel may receive a state social scholarship [18]. This scholarship is awarded from document submission until the basis expires, or until studies end if the basis is lifelong, except for social aid recipients [19]. First or second year students with excellent or good grades meeting the social categories, or students under 20 with one disabled parent, may receive an enhanced state social scholarship [20]. This enhanced scholarship follows the same duration rules as the standard social scholarship [21]. Students admitted from 2024 onward must have no "satisfactory" grade and no academic debt to receive university scholarships [24]. You must have a signed grant contract providing for university scholarships to receive them [25]. Bachelor's students with excellent grades for the last two periods and no academic debt may receive an enhanced university academic scholarship [26].

# Financial Aid, Scholarships, and Grants

## Eligibility and Application for Grants

The grant competition is open annually from October 1 of the year preceding admission to August 10 of the admission year [2]. It is for foreign citizens from countries not belonging to the Commonwealth of Independent States (CIS) who are in their final year of secondary education, have graduated from secondary education, or are students/graduates of secondary vocational education (for bachelor's programs) or higher education (for master's programs) [1]. Educational documents must be legalized according to Russian legislation and translated into Russian unless an international treaty provides otherwise [1]. Individuals who are or were enrolled in certain programs are not admitted to the competition [1]. Participants may enter the competition no more than once per year [8].

The first stage involves online registration and testing on the Official Website during dates set by the Organizer [3]. To participate, a candidate must register on the Official Website, pass online testing, and provide specific documents and information [4]. Required submissions include: a completed online questionnaire with personal details, education history, and program information; a motivation letter in English; an English-language portfolio of achievements; copies of the secondary education certificate or vocational diploma (for bachelor's applicants); copies of the higher education diploma (for master's applicants, if available); an English language proficiency certificate (if available); and a signed consent for personal data processing [4].

The passing score for the first stage is 45% correct answers for the English language test (for bachelor's) and 50% for the master's program, and 45% for the profile test [5]. Participants who meet all conditions of clause 2.2 and achieve the passing score are invited to the second stage via email [6]. The participant must confirm participation within 7 calendar days of receiving the invitation; failure to confirm results in withdrawal from the competition [6]. The date of receipt is the date the message is sent by the Organizer [6].

Winners must sign an education contract with the University before the date of the enrollment order and a grant agreement before the start of the academic year following the competition [7]. Winners must also participate in the University's "Summer School" if organized, on a voluntary basis [7].

## Eligibility and Application for Material Aid

To qualify for material aid, a student's Grade Point Average (GPA) from the previous semester must be less than 4.33 for Russian students and less than 4.37 for foreign students [9]. The income per family member (including parents and minor siblings) must be below the subsistence minimum in the region of residence for Russian students, or below the subsistence minimum in the student's country of residence for foreign students [9].

Applications and documents can be submitted to the Student Support and Development Department on any working day, including during holidays [12]. Foreign students may submit documents in English or their native language; the Department may assist with translation, but the student bears the responsibility for the translation [11]. Required documents include a family composition certificate and a parents' income certificate [10].

Material aid can be received as a one-time payment or monthly for the entire semester until the next interim assessment [13]. Payments occur on the nearest scholarship payment day if the application is submitted at least one week prior; otherwise, payment occurs on the scholarship payment day of the following month [13]. The final decision is made by the University Director or authorized person based on the scholarship commission's petition, confirmed by an order [14].

To maintain material aid throughout the semester, a student must attend at least 70% of classes; failure to meet this requirement stops the aid [15]. If the student's GPA for the semester in which they received aid does not improve, the University denies aid for the following semester [16].

## Eligibility and Application for Scholarships

Students receiving a state scholarship must have no "satisfactory" grades in the interim assessment and no academic debts [17]. Students admitted from 2024 onwards must also have no "satisfactory" grades and no academic debts to receive University scholarships and increased University scholarships [24].

A state social scholarship is assigned to full-time students funded by federal budget allocations who belong to specific categories: orphans, children left without parental care, persons who lost both parents or a sole parent during studies, children with disabilities, persons with disabilities of groups I and II, persons with childhood disabilities, victims of the Chernobyl disaster and other radiation catastrophes, nuclear tests at the Semipalatinsk polygon, military injury or disease veterans, recipients of state social assistance, and citizens who served at least three years in the Armed Forces, Internal Troops, engineering/technical/road construction military formations, rescue formations, foreign intelligence, security services, state guard, and mobilization preparation bodies [18]. The scholarship is assigned by order from the date of providing the confirming document until the end of the basis for appointment, except for those receiving state social assistance [19]. If the confirming document is indefinite, the scholarship is assigned until the end of studies [19].

An increased state social scholarship is assigned to first and second-year students with "excellent" or "good" grades who belong to the categories in clause 6.1, or to students under 20 years old with only one parent who is a disability group I holder [20]. It is assigned by order after the interim assessment from the date of providing the confirming document until the end of the basis for appointment, except for those receiving state social assistance [21].

One-time material aid is decided by the Commission based on the student's application and documents confirming need [22]. Maternity leave benefits are paid to full-time students from federal budget allocations for 70 days before birth (84 days for multiple births) and 70 days after birth (86 days for complicated births, 100 days for two or more children) [23].

A University scholarship is assigned to students who have signed a grant agreement with the University providing for University scholarships [25]. An increased academic University scholarship is assigned to bachelor's students who received "excellent" grades for the last two learning periods preceding the assignment and have no academic debts [26].

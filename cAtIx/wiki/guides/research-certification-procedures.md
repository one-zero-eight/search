# Research Certification and Practice Structure for Graduate Programs

## Intermediate Research Assessments
You will complete an intermediate assessment by stages of scientific research [1]. This assessment is linked to the educational component [1]. You will also complete an intermediate assessment by disciplines (modules) and practice [3].

## Practice Types and Execution
You will complete practice [2]. The type and methods of conducting practice are defined [2].

## Final Certifications
You will complete a final assessment [3].

## Gaps and caveats
The official document does not specify the evaluation criteria, grading scales, or deadlines for the intermediate research assessments, practice, or final certification [1][2][3]. It does not list the specific types of practice or the exact stages of scientific research [1][2]. The official document confirms the presence of these components but leaves the detailed structure and evaluation methods open [1][2][3]. Course pages may provide further details, but the official document does not address them [1][2][3].

# Rules for using the University’s name and trademarks

## Using the University’s name and logos
Students, postgraduates, teachers, and staff may use the University’s means of individualization to denote a link between an event, project, publication, product, work, or service and the University only after obtaining prior written consent from the official authorized to make decisions on intellectual property [1]. To request this permission, you must send a corresponding application to the MCTT [1]. Authorized officials may grant permission for a specific category of cases, such as publications, editions, or projects [8].

You do not need special permission to use the University’s means of individualization in the following cases:
- Using official University document letterheads in the manner established by local regulatory acts [2].
- Using them in official and periodic University publications, on the corporate Bitrix portal, and on other University websites [2].
- Denoting events, publications, products, works, or services conducted or performed within University projects and/or in accordance with the University’s contractual obligations [2].

## Using a structural subdivision’s name
Students, postgraduates, teachers, and staff may use the name of a structural subdivision to denote a link between an event, project, publication, product, work, or service and that subdivision only after obtaining prior written consent from the head of that subdivision (or another official authorized by the head) and prior written consent from the official authorized to make intellectual property decisions [5]. Additional prior written consent from the intellectual property official is required if the subdivision name is used in paid relations with third parties or in the interests of third parties, including advertising their events, projects, goods, works, or services [5].

You do not need special permission to use a subdivision’s name in the following cases:
- Using official letterheads of the subdivision (if the subdivision’s regulation provides for such letterheads) in the manner established by local regulatory acts [6].
- Using it in official and periodic University publications, on the corporate portal, and on other University websites [6].
- Using it in other cases provided by decisions of the subdivision head [6].

## Specific communications and publications
If you use the University’s means of individualization in mass media or audiovisual works, including when responding to requests from media or audiovisual producers, you must coordinate this use with the University’s Public Relations Department according to the procedure set by organizational and administrative acts [3].

When preparing and publishing works where copyright belongs to the University, you must include a copyright notice in the form “© ANO VO “Universitet Innopolis”, year of publication” [4]. This notice must be placed on the title page or second page of the publication [4]. This notice must be placed on the cover and material carriers of software or databases, as well as on the visual image appearing on a computer screen when running or accessing them [4]. This notice must be placed on the cover, material carriers, and credits of audiovisual works [4].

You may use the University’s or a subdivision’s name to denote your position or the educational organization where you are studying, provided you follow internal rules and local acts on affiliation [7]. This use must not allow the conclusion that the University or subdivision is responsible for your events, projects, products, works, or services [7]. You may also include links to University-supported research projects in your publications, where the content of the link is determined by local regulatory acts and University contracts with the author governing the project [7].

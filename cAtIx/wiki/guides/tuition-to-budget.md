# Moving from paid to state-funded study

## Eligibility and Conditions

To move from paid study to state-funded study, you must be a student enrolled under a contract for paid educational services [5]. You must have no academic debts, disciplinary sanctions, or unpaid tuition fees at the time of submitting your application [5].

You must meet at least one of the following conditions [5]:
- You passed the intermediate certification in the last study period preceding the application with grades of "excellent", or "excellent" and "good", or "good" [5].
- You belong to one of the following citizen categories (excluding foreign citizens, unless an international treaty of the Russian Federation provides otherwise) [5]:
  - Children who are orphans or left without parental care, as well as persons from among children who are orphans or left without parental care [5].
  - Citizens under the age of twenty who have only one parent who is a disabled person of Group I, if the per capita income of the family is below the subsistence minimum established in the corresponding subject of the Russian Federation [5].
  - Women who gave birth to a child during their studies [5].
  - Children of persons participating or who participated in the special military operation in the territories of Ukraine, the Donetsk People's Republic, the Lugansk People's Republic, the Zaporozhye region, and the Kherson region [5].
  - Loss by the Student during the study period of one or [...the clause continues; do not state what it leaves out] [5].

Participants of the special military operation who have no academic debts, disciplinary sanctions, or unpaid tuition fees are transferred to vacant budget places redistributed in accordance with the Rules for redistribution of vacant places [6]. Such participants must submit an application to the Director of the University and a document confirming participation in the special military operation (if this document is not already in the student's personal file) [6].

## Vacancies and Information

Vacant budget places are funded from budget appropriations or from the University's own funds [1].

The number of vacant budget places is determined by the University as the difference between the control figures for the corresponding admission year (the number of places for the first year of study) and the actual number of students enrolled at the University for the corresponding educational program, specialty, field of study, scientific specialty, and form of study for the corresponding course, at least twice a year (upon completion of the study periods allocated within the courses) [2].

The number of vacant places funded from the University's own funds is determined by an order of the Director of the University or an authorized person, taking into account the volume of budget funds allocated to educational activities for the corresponding educational programs [2]. If the University lacks the financial capacity, vacant places funded from the University's own funds are not allocated [2].

The University ensures openness of information about the number of vacant budget places, the number of vacant places funded from the University's own funds, and the deadlines for submitting applications by posting this information in information and telecommunication networks, including on the official website of the University on the Internet [4].

## Application Procedure

The deadlines for submitting applications for transition are established by the University after a vacant place appears [3].

To apply, you must submit an Application for Transition to the Responsible Structural Division, addressed to the Director of the University, according to the form specified in the appendix to the Regulation [7].

You must attach the following documents to the Application for Transition [7]:
- Documents confirming your belonging to the categories of citizens specified in sub-clauses "b" and "v" of clause 9 of the Regulation (if these documents are not already in your personal file) [7].
- Documents confirming special achievements in academic, research, public, cultural-creative, and sports activities of the University (if any) [7].

## Decision Making

The Commission makes decisions with the presence of at least 2/3 of its composition [8]. Decisions are made by open voting [8]. A decision is considered adopted if more than half (50% + 1 vote) of those present at the meeting voted for it [8]. The Commission's decision is recorded in the form of a protocol [8].

Within 10 calendar days from the date of receipt of the Application for Transition by the Commission, the Commission considers the application, attached documents, and information, and makes one of the following decisions [9]:
- About the transition of the Student from paid study to study at a vacant budget place [9].
- About the transition of the Student from paid study to study at the expense of the University's own funds [9].
- About refusal to transition the Student from paid study to study at the expense of budget appropriations or the University's own funds [9].

The decision to transition a Student from paid study to study at the expense of budget appropriations is made by the Commission taking into account the number of vacant budget places and priorities established by the Commission in accordance with clauses 13 and 14 of the Regulation [10].

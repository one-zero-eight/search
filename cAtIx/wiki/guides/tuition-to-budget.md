# Moving from paid to state-funded study

## Eligibility and Conditions

You are eligible to apply for a transition from paid study to state-funded study if you are currently enrolled at the University under a contract for paid educational services [4]. You must not have any academic debts, disciplinary sanctions, or outstanding tuition fees at the time of submitting your application [4]. Additionally, you must meet at least one of the following specific conditions [4]:

*   You passed the intermediate certification in the last academic period preceding the application with grades of "excellent" only, or a combination of "excellent" and "good", or "good" only [4].
*   You belong to one of the following categories of citizens (excluding foreign citizens unless an international treaty of the Russian Federation provides otherwise) [4]:
    *   Orphans and children left without parental care, or persons from among orphans and children left without parental care [4].
    *   Citizens under the age of twenty who have only one parent with a disability of Group I, if the per capita income of the family is below the subsistence minimum established in the corresponding subject of the Russian Federation [4].
    *   Women who gave birth to a child during their studies [4].
    *   Children of persons participating or who have participated in the Special Military Operation in the territories of Ukraine, the Donetsk People's Republic, the Luhansk People's Republic, the Zaporizhzhia region, and the Kherson region [4].
    *   You have lost one or more [the clause continues...] [4].

Participants of the Special Military Operation who do not have academic debts, disciplinary sanctions, or outstanding tuition fees at the time of application are transferred to vacant state-funded places [5].

## Vacancies and Information

Transitions are carried out only if there are vacant state-funded places financed from budget appropriations for the corresponding educational program, specialty, field of study, scientific specialty, and form of study at the corresponding course, or if there are places financed from the University's own funds [1].

The University ensures openness of information regarding the number of vacant state-funded places, the number of vacant places financed from the University's own funds, and the deadlines for submitting applications by posting this information in information and telecommunication networks, including on the official website of the University on the Internet [3].

The deadlines for submitting applications are established by the University after a vacant place appears [2].

## Priority Rules

Priority for transition is established as follows [6]:

1.  First priority is given to students who meet the condition specified in sub-item "a" of clause 9 (passing certification with "excellent" or "good" grades) [6].
2.  Second priority is given to students who meet the conditions specified in sub-items "b" and "c" of clause 9 (belonging to specific citizen categories) [6].

If a vacant state-funded place arises due to the dismissal of a person who studied under a target training contract, priority is given to students who have concluded a target training contract for the corresponding specialty of secondary vocational education, field of study of higher education, or scientific specialty [6].

If there are two or more candidates of the same queue for one vacant place, priority is given to students who have special achievements in academic, research, public, cultural-creative, and sports activities of the University [7]. The special status of the achievement is determined by the Commission [7].

## Application Procedure

To apply, you must submit an Application for Transition to the Responsible Structural Division [8]. The application must be addressed to the Director of the University and follow the form specified in the appendix to the Regulation [8].

You must attach the following documents to your application [8]:

*   Documents confirming your belonging to the categories of citizens specified in sub-items "b" and "c" of clause 9 (if these documents are not already in your personal file) [8].
*   Documents confirming special achievements in academic, research, public, cultural-creative, and sports activities of the University (if any) [8].

Participants of the Special Military Operation must submit the Application for Transition to the Director of the University along with a document confirming participation in the Special Military Operation (if this document is not already in the student's personal file) [5].

## Decision Making

A Commission reviews your application, attached documents, and relevant information within a period of no more than 10 (ten) calendar days from the date the application is received by the Commission [9].

The Commission makes one of the following decisions [9]:

*   About the transition of the student from paid study to study at a vacant state-funded place [9].
*   About the transition of the student from paid study to study at the University's own funds [9].
*   About refusal to transition the student from paid study to study at the University's own funds or state-funded places [9].

The decision to transition a student from paid study to study at state-funded places is made by the Commission taking into account the number of vacant state-funded places and the priorities established by the Commission in accordance with clauses 13 and 14 of the Regulation [10].

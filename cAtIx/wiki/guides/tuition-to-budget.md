# Moving from paid to state-funded study

## Eligibility and Conditions

Students enrolled under a paid education contract may apply if they have no academic debts, disciplinary sanctions, or tuition fee arrears at the time of submitting the application [5].

Applicants must meet at least one of the following conditions:
*   They passed the intermediate certification in the last academic period preceding the application with grades of "excellent", or "excellent" and "good", or "good" [5].
*   They belong to specific citizen categories, excluding foreign citizens unless an international treaty of the Russian Federation provides otherwise [5]:
    *   Orphans and children left without parental care, including persons from among orphans and children left without parental care [5].
    *   Citizens under the age of twenty who have only one parent with disability group I, if the per capita income of the family is below the subsistence minimum established in the corresponding subject of the Russian Federation [5].
    *   Women who gave birth to a child during their studies [5].
    *   Children of persons participating or who participated in the special military operation on the territories of Ukraine, the Donetsk People's Republic, the Luhansk People's Republic, the Zaporozhye region, and the Kherson region [5].
    *   Other categories defined in clause 9, subsection "v" of the regulation [5].

Participants of the special military operation are transferred to vacant budget places redistributed according to the Rules of redistribution of vacant places approved by the Decree of the Government of the Russian Federation of August 8, 2023 No. 1292 [6]. These participants must submit an application to the University Director and a document confirming participation in the special military operation, unless such a document is already in their personal file [6].

## Vacancies and Information

Vacant budget places are financed from budget appropriations or from the University's own funds [1].

The number of vacant budget places financed from budget appropriations is determined by the University as the difference between the control figures for the corresponding admission year (places for the first year of study) and the actual number of students enrolled at the University for the corresponding educational program, specialty, direction of training, scientific specialty, and form of study for the corresponding course [2]. This determination occurs no less than twice a year, at the end of the study periods allocated within courses [2].

The number of vacant places financed from the University's own funds is determined by an order of the University Director or an authorized person, taking into account the volume of budget funds allocated to educational activities for the corresponding educational programs [2]. If the University lacks financial capacity, vacant places financed from the University's own funds are not allocated [2].

The University ensures openness of information regarding the number of vacant budget places, the number of vacant places financed from the University's own funds, and the deadlines for submitting applications by placing this information in information and telecommunication networks, including on the official website of the University on the Internet [4].

## Application Process

The deadlines for submitting applications for transition are established by the University after a vacant place appears [3].

An applicant wishing to transition to a vacant budget place or to study at the University's own expense submits an application to the responsible structural subdivision addressed to the University Director, according to the form specified in the appendix to the regulation [7].

The application must be accompanied by documents:
*   Confirming the applicant's belonging to the categories of citizens specified in subsections "b" and "v" of clause 9 of the regulation, if such documents are not in the applicant's personal file [7].
*   Confirming special achievements in academic, research, public, cultural-creative, and sports activities of the University, if available [7].

## Decision Making

The Commission makes decisions with the presence of at least 2/3 of its composition [8]. Decisions are made by open voting [8]. A decision is considered adopted if more than half (50% + 1 vote) of those present at the meeting voted for it [8]. The Commission's decision is recorded in the form of a protocol [8].

Within a period of no more than 10 calendar days from the date of receipt of the application by the Commission, the Commission considers the application, attached documents, and information, and makes one of the following decisions:
*   To transition the student from paid study to study at a vacant budget place [9].
*   To transition the student from paid study to study at the University's own expense [9].
*   To refuse the transition from paid study to study at budget appropriations or at the University's own expense [9].

The decision to transition a student from paid study to study at budget appropriations is made by the Commission taking into account the number of vacant budget places and priorities established by the Commission in accordance with clauses 13 and 14 of the regulation [10].

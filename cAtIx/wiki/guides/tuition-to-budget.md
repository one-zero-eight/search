# Moving from paid to state-funded study

## Eligibility and Conditions

A student enrolled under a paid education services contract may apply to move to state-funded study or study funded by the University's own funds [5]. The applicant must not have academic debts, disciplinary sanctions, or tuition fee arrears at the time of submitting the application [5][6].

The applicant must meet at least one of the following conditions [5]:
- Pass intermediate certification in the last academic period preceding the application with grades of "excellent", or "excellent" and "good", or "good" [5].
- Belong to one of the following categories (excluding foreign citizens unless an international treaty provides otherwise) [5]:
  - Orphans and children left without parental care, or persons from among orphans and children left without parental care [5].
  - Citizens under the age of twenty who have only one parent with disability group I, if the family's per capita income is below the subsistence minimum established in the relevant subject of the Russian Federation [5].
  - Women who gave birth to a child during their studies [5].
  - Children of persons participating or who participated in the special military operation in the territories of Ukraine, the Donetsk People's Republic, the Lugansk People's Republic, the Zaporozhye region, and the Kherson region [5].
  - Loss of the student during the study period of one or [...the clause continues; do not state what it leaves out] [5].

Participants of the special military operation who do not have academic debts, disciplinary sanctions, or tuition fee arrears are transferred to vacant budget places redistributed according to the Rules of redistribution of vacant places [6]. They must submit an application to the University Director and a document confirming participation in the special military operation (if not already in the student's personal file) [6].

## Vacancies and Information

Transitions are carried out only if there are vacant budget places funded by budget appropriations or by the University's own funds [1].

Vacant budget places funded by budget appropriations are determined by the University as the difference between the control figures for the corresponding admission year and the actual number of students enrolled on a budget basis for the corresponding educational program and course [2]. This count is performed no less than twice a year, at the end of the academic periods allocated within courses [2].

The number of vacant places funded by the University's own funds is determined by the order of the University Director or authorized person, taking into account the budget volume allocated for educational activities for the corresponding programs [2]. If the University lacks financial capacity, no such places are allocated [2].

The University ensures openness of information regarding the number of vacant budget places, the number of vacant places funded by the University's own funds, and the deadlines for submitting applications by posting this information in information and telecommunication networks, including the official University website [4].

## Application Procedure

The deadlines for submitting applications are established by the University after a vacant place appears [3].

The student submits an application for transition to the responsible structural division addressed to the University Director, according to the form specified in the appendix to the Regulation [7].

The application must be accompanied by documents confirming the student's belonging to the categories specified in subpoints "b" and "v" of clause 9 (if not already in the personal file) and documents confirming special achievements in academic, research, public, cultural-creative, or sports activities (if any) [7].

## Decision Making

The Commission makes decisions with a quorum of at least 2/3 of its members [8]. Decisions are made by open voting and are considered adopted if a majority (50% + 1 vote) of those present vote in favor [8]. The decision is recorded in a protocol [8].

The Commission considers the application, attached documents, and information within a period of no more than 10 calendar days from the date of receipt of the application [9].

The Commission issues one of three decisions: transition to a vacant budget place, transition to study funded by the University's own funds, or refusal of transition [9].

The decision to transition to a vacant budget place is made by the Commission taking into account the number of vacant budget places and priorities established by the Commission in accordance with clauses 13 and 14 of the Regulation [10].

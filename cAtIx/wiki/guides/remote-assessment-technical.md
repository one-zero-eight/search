# Technical Requirements for Remote Assessments

To take a remote exam or test, you need computing devices such as a desktop computer, laptop, or tablet [1]. You need communication equipment that ensures access to the university's electronic educational environment through local networks and the Internet [1]. You must have the corresponding software required for this access [1].

## Video Conferencing Mode
If your assessment uses video conferencing mode, you need the following:
- a webcam with a resolution of at least 640x480 (0.3 MP) [2]
- a monitor or screen with a resolution of at least 1280x1024 [2]
- a microphone with speakers or a computer headset for communication [2]
- configured video conferencing software such as Microsoft Teams, Zoom, or a similar tool that allows remote audio-visual contact [2]
- an internet connection speed of at least 2 Mbps, or 1024 Kbps per workstation [2]
- the Google Chrome browser, version 81.0 or higher [2]

## Proctoring System
If your assessment uses a proctoring system, you need the following:
- the Google Chrome browser, version 72 or higher [3]
- an operating system of at least Windows 7, macOS 10.12 Sierra, or Linux [3]
- a webcam with a resolution of at least 640x480 (0.3 MP) [3]
- a microphone with speakers or a computer headset to communicate with the proctor [3]
- an internet connection speed of at least 512 Kbps [3]
- a browser plugin if required [3]

## Online Testing
If your assessment is an online test, you need the following:
- the Google Chrome browser, version 72 or higher [4]
- an operating system of at least Windows 7, macOS 10.12 Sierra, or Linux [4]
- an internet connection speed of at least 512 Kbps [4]

## Other Formats
If your assessment uses a format chosen independently by the teaching staff, the technical and software requirements and recommendations will be communicated to you [5]. You will receive this information no later than 3 working days before the day of the assessment [5]. The university will send it to your corporate email addresses and/or use other information and communication technologies [5].

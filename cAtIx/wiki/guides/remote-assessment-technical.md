# Technical Requirements for Remote Assessments

## General Requirements
You need computing devices such as a desktop computer, laptop, or tablet to take a remote assessment [1]. You must have communication equipment that provides access to the university's electronic educational environment through local networks and the Internet [1]. You must also have the corresponding software installed [1]. If a course page specifies technical requirements that differ from or go beyond this official document, the official document takes precedence [5].

## Video Conferencing Mode
If your assessment uses video conferencing, you should use a webcam with a resolution of at least 640x480 (0.3 MP) [2]. You should use a monitor or screen with a resolution of at least 1280x1024 [2]. You need a microphone with speakers or a computer headset for communication [2]. You need configured video conferencing software such as Microsoft Teams, Zoom, or a similar program that allows remote audio-visual contact [2]. Your internet connection speed must be at least 2 Mbps, or 1024 Kbps per workstation [2]. You must use the Google Chrome browser, version 81.0 or higher [2].

## Proctoring System
For assessments using a proctoring system, you should use the Google Chrome browser, version 72 or higher [3]. Your operating system must be at least Windows 7, macOS 10.12 Sierra, or Linux [3]. You should use a webcam with a resolution of at least 640x480 (0.3 MP) [3]. You need a microphone with speakers or a computer headset to communicate with the proctor [3]. Your internet connection speed must be at least 512 Kbps [3]. A browser plugin may be required if necessary [3].

## Online Testing
For online testing, you should use the Google Chrome browser, version 72 or higher [4]. Your operating system must be at least Windows 7, macOS 10.12 Sierra, or Linux [4]. Your internet connection speed must be at least 512 Kbps [4].

## Other Assessment Formats
If your assessment uses a format chosen independently by the instructor, the instructor sets the technical and software requirements and recommendations [5]. You must receive these requirements no later than 3 working days before the assessment day [5]. The notification is sent via corporate email addresses and/or other information and communication technologies [5].

## Gaps and caveats
The evidence does not specify which software is required for general access to the electronic educational environment [1]. The evidence does not list operating system requirements for the general setup or for video conferencing mode [2]. The evidence does not state what happens if a student's equipment does not meet the recommended specifications [2][3][4]. The evidence does not define what constitutes other information and communication technologies for notifications [5]. The evidence does not specify which browser plugins are needed for proctoring beyond stating they may be required [3]. Course pages may list different or additional technical requirements; if they do, the official document takes precedence [5]. The evidence leaves open whether the recommended specifications are mandatory or optional for each format [2][3][4].

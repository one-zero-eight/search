# When to Choose Optional and Elective Courses

You must complete your selection for optional and elective courses before the semester in which they will be taught begins [2][3].

## Optional courses
You must select optional courses in the semester that precedes the semester in which they will be implemented [1]. You register for them through your student account before the start of the semester in which you will study them, according to the academic calendar [2]. The University sets the specific deadlines for this selection and communicates them through the electronic information and educational environment and corporate email [2]. You may change your list of selected optional courses or withdraw from them before the start of the study period, but all changes require approval from the College considering the objective feasibility of organizing the educational process [2]. You cannot make changes to your planned optional courses after the study begins according to the schedule [2].

## Elective courses
You register for elective courses through your student account before the start of the semester in which you will study them, according to the academic calendar [3]. The College sets the specific deadlines for this registration and communicates them through the electronic information and educational environment and corporate email [3]. You cannot make changes to your planned elective courses after the study begins according to the schedule [3].

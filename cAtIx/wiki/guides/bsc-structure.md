# How the BSc programme is organized

## Duration and structure

The Bachelor of Science in Computer Science and the Bachelor of Science in Computer Engineering are organized in 4 years of instruction performed mostly in English [1][2]. The degree requires the student to acquire 240 credits organized over these 4 years, with a pace of 60 credits per year [2][6]. One credit of study approximately corresponds to 36 academic hours of effort per student [6].

## Tracks and streams

The first two years contain fundamental courses in mathematics, physics, and computer science and engineering [1][5]. In the next two years, students select one of four streams: Software Engineering, Data Science, Robotics, or Security-Blockchain [5][12]. The Software Engineering stream combines system theory, computer science, and software engineering [12]. The Data Science stream integrates intelligent systems, computer science, and data science [12]. The Robotics stream provides fundamental knowledge in mechanics, mechatronics, electrical engineering, control theory, and robotics [12]. The Security-Blockchain stream focuses on information security and distributed ledgers technologies [12].

## Course load by year

Overall, a student takes 45 courses [2]. The yearly course distribution is the same for the Software Engineering, Data Science, and Security-Blockchain streams, but slightly different for the Robotics stream [2].

For the Software Engineering, Data Science, and Security-Blockchain streams, the course distribution is:
*   First year: 12 core courses, 0 elective courses [3].
*   Second year: 12 core courses, 0 elective courses [3].
*   Third year: 10 core courses, 1 elective course [3].
*   Fourth year: 6 core courses, 4 elective courses [3].
*   Total: 40 core courses and 5 elective courses [3].

For the Robotics stream, the course distribution is:
*   First year: 12 core courses, 0 elective courses [4].
*   Second year: 12 core courses, 0 elective courses [4].
*   Third year: 11 core courses, 0 elective courses [4].
*   Fourth year: 8 core courses, 2 elective courses [4].
*   Total: 43 core courses and 2 elective courses [4].

The body of knowledge in the first two years consists of Math, Physics, Algorithms and Complexity, Programming Languages, Software Development Fundamentals, Architecture and organization, Operating Systems, Computational Science, information management, and Networking and Communications [5]. The body of knowledge in the next two years mainly consists of Graphics and Visualization, Information Assurance and Security, Intelligent Systems, Parallel and Distributed Computing, Software Engineering, Systems Fundamentals, Human-Computer Interaction, Finance, Mechanical Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Mathematical Physics, Robotics, and Control Engineering [5]. The curriculum also includes instruction in humanities (history and philosophy) and life and safety and sport, which complete the educational program with 10 credits [5].

## Internships

Every year, students have a mandatory internship during the summer break [8]. There are three types of internships: Industrial, Scientific, and Administrative [8]. Industrial internships involve working in Innopolis University partner IT companies, primarily located within the city of Innopolis [8]. Scientific internships involve working in Innopolis University labs on ongoing research and development tasks [8]. Administrative internships involve working in different Innopolis University departments on IT analysis, design, and implementation projects [8]. It is also possible to have internships that combine these three types [8]. The internship usually starts once the exam session for the spring semester is completed [8].

The credits for internships are:
*   First year: 14 credits [5].
*   Second year: 10 credits [5].
*   Third year: 10 credits [5].
*   Fourth year: 24 credits [5].
*   Total: 58 credits [5].

An internship of 8 credits requires 288 hours of effort, which is about 7 weeks [6].

## Language requirements

A graduate from the program is expected to know English at least at the level of IELTS 7 [9]. At admission, the student must have a knowledge of English at least at level of IELTS 5 [9]. At the end of the first year of instruction, the student must have a knowledge of English equivalent to at least the level 5.5 of IELTS [9]. At the end of the second year of instruction, the student must have a knowledge of English equivalent to at least the level 6 of IELTS [9]. At the end of the third year of instruction, the student must have a knowledge of English equivalent to at least the level 6.5 of IELTS [9]. At the end of the fourth year of instruction, the student must have a knowledge of English equivalent to at least the level 7 of IELTS [9]. The university will make available to the student the fundamental resources to achieve the required levels, but it is the responsibility of the student to achieve them [9].

## Multidisciplinary exam

At the end of the second year of study, the student has to pass a multidisciplinary exam aimed at evaluating their comprehensive understanding of the discipline beyond the borders of an individual subject [10]. This ensures that students who progress to years three and four have the ability to engage in system-oriented thinking and have the capacity to solve broadly-based IT problems [10].

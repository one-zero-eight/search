# Academic leave

## Who can get it and on what grounds

Academic leave is available to students and postgraduate students enrolled in the University’s main professional educational programs [1]. You may receive it if you are temporarily unable to complete your program due to:

*   Conscription for military service or signing a contract for military service [1].
*   Medical indications [1].
*   Other exceptional cases, including natural disasters or family circumstances [1].

## How long it lasts

The leave is granted for a single period not exceeding 12 calendar months [2]. However, if the leave is due to military service or medical indications, the period may not exceed two years [2]. You may be granted academic leave an unlimited number of times during your studies [2].

## How to apply

To apply, you must submit a personal application to the University Director [3]. The application must use the form specified in Appendix 1 to the Regulation [3].

You must also provide supporting documents depending on your reason for leave:

*   **Medical indications:** A medical conclusion issued by a healthcare organization within the state or municipal health system [3].
*   **Military service:** A mobilization notice, order, or summons from the military commissariat containing the time and place of dispatch to the place of military service, and a copy of the contract for military service in the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation, certified in the manner established by Russian legislation [3].
*   **Other cases:** Other documents confirming the grounds for the leave, if available [3].

## Decision and timeline

The University Director or an authorized official makes the decision on granting the leave [9]. This decision must be made within 10 days from the day the University receives your application and attached documents [9].

The granting of the leave is formalized by an order issued by the University Head no later than 5 working days from the day the decision to grant the leave is made [4].

## What happens during the leave

Your educational relations are suspended while you are on academic leave [6]. You are released from obligations related to mastering the educational program and are not admitted to the educational process until the leave ends [10].

If you study under a contract, tuition fees are not charged during the academic leave [10].

## Returning to studies

The leave ends when the granted period expires [5]. You may also end it early by submitting a personal application [5].

If you return early, you are admitted to studies based on an order from the University Head [5]. If the leave ends naturally, you are admitted to studies based on an order from the University Director or an authorized official [10].

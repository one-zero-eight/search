# Academic leave

## Who can get it and on what grounds

You can get academic leave if you are a student or postgraduate enrolled in the University’s main professional educational programs [1]. The leave is granted when you are temporarily unable to study for the following reasons [1]:

*   Conscription into military service or signing a contract for military service [1].
*   Medical indications [1].
*   Other exceptional cases, including natural disasters or family circumstances [1].

## How long it lasts

The standard duration of academic leave is up to 12 calendar months at one time [2]. However, if the leave is for military service or medical reasons, the period can be up to two years [2]. You can take academic leave an unlimited number of times during your studies [2]. The total period of leave cannot exceed two years [7].

## How to apply

To apply, you must submit a personal application to the University Director [1]. The application form is specified in Appendix 1 to the Regulation [3]. You must also provide supporting documents depending on your reason for leave [3]:

*   **For medical reasons:** A medical conclusion issued by a healthcare organization within the state or municipal health system [3].
*   **For military service:** A mobilization notice, order, or summons from the military commissariat containing the time and place of dispatch to the place of military service, and a copy of the contract for military service in the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation, certified in the manner prescribed by law [3].
*   **For other reasons:** Other documents confirming the grounds for leave, if available [3].

## The decision and the order

The University Director or an authorized official reviews your application and supporting documents within 10 days of receipt [9]. The decision is made within this 10-day period [9]. The leave is formalized by an order issued by the University Director no later than 5 working days from the day the decision to grant the leave is made [4].

## What happens during the leave

While you are on academic leave, your educational relations are suspended [6]. You are released from obligations related to studying at the University and are not allowed to participate in the educational process until the leave ends [10]. If you study under a contract, you are not charged tuition fees during the academic leave [10].

## Returning to studies

Academic leave ends when the granted period expires [5]. You may also end it early by submitting a personal application [5]. If you return early, you are admitted to studies based on an order from the University Director [5]. If the leave ends naturally, you are admitted to studies based on an order from the University Director or an authorized official [10].

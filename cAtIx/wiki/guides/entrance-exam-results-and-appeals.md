# Entrance Exam Results and Appeal Procedure

## Publication of Exam Results
As an applicant, your exam results will be published on the official website within 3 working days after the day of the exam [1]. The results will be published no later than 1 day before the publication of the competitive lists [1].

## Filing an Appeal
As an applicant, you must submit your appeal on the day the results are announced or within the next working day [3]. If your appeal concerns a violation of the established procedure for conducting the exam, you may also submit it on the day of the exam [3]. You can submit the appeal in person or by sending it to the email address `айт!5510п5 @ ппоро!!5.ги` [2].

## Review and Decision
The appeal will be reviewed no later than the next working day after the day you submit it [4]. As an applicant, you have the right to be present during the review of your appeal [5]. After the review, the appeal commission will decide to change your exam score or leave it unchanged [6]. The commission's decision, formalized in a protocol, will be communicated to you [6]. Your acknowledgment of the decision is recorded by your signature if you are present in person, or by an email sent to your personal email address from `аат\!$5510п5@ ппоро!!5.ги. \!` [6]. The procedure also references lists of applicants who filed appeals and competitive lists [6].

# Getting a Repeated Grant After Academic Expulsion

## Eligibility and timing
You may apply for a repeated or partial Grant if you were previously expelled from the University for failing to fulfill your duties to study and complete the curriculum [2][5][6]. You are eligible if you previously studied at the University using Grant funds that covered your tuition, or if you previously studied using federal, regional, or local budget funds and received a Grant that provided a University scholarship [2]. You are also eligible if you were previously admitted under a target training contract within the target admission quota [6]. You may submit your application within five years after your expulsion date, but you can receive a repeated Grant no more than once [2][5][6]. The Grant is intended to cover tuition after you complete the restoration procedure [2][6].

## Application and review process
The decision to provide a repeated or partial Grant is based on your personal application [1]. Your application is reviewed by a Competition Commission that is created and operates according to the Regulation [1]. The Competition Commission may conduct separate selection tests for you as a restoring student who applied for a repeated Grant [7].

## Grant amounts and coverage limits
The volume of your repeated Grant is determined by the Competition Commission [3][6]. Your repeated Grant cannot exceed the percentage of tuition that was covered by your Grant before expulsion [3]. If your previous Grant covered 71% to 100% of tuition, your repeated Grant can be no more than 70% [3]. If your previous Grant covered 61% to 75%, your repeated Grant can be no more than 60% [3]. If your previous Grant covered 51% to 60%, your repeated Grant can be no more than 50% [3]. If your previous Grant covered 26% to 50%, your repeated Grant can be no more than 25% [3]. If your previous Grant covered 25% or less, you will not be provided with a repeated Grant that covers part of your tuition [4]. If you previously studied using federal, regional, or local budget funds and received a Grant providing a University scholarship, your repeated Grant can be no more than 75% of tuition [5]. Based on a separate University decision, you may be provided with a Grant that provides a University scholarship if you are enrolled through restoration to budget-funded places [5]. For persons admitted under a target training contract, the Competition Commission determines the volume of the partial Grant [6].

## Payment and contract requirements
You must pay the portion of tuition that is not covered by your repeated Grant in the trimester you are restored [5]. The remaining balance is paid according to the education contract you sign when you are restored [5]. The cost of tuition for persons admitted under a target training contract is determined according to clause 6.3 of the Regulation [6].

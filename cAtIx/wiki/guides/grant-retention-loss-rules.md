# Rules for Retaining or Losing the Scholarship

## Loss of Scholarship Eligibility

A winner loses the right to receive the grant if they do not meet the requirements specified in clause 3.2 of the Regulation [1]. Grants that are not claimed by the start of the academic year following the completion of the current year’s competition are not issued by the Organizer and are not carried over to the next year [1]. This includes cases where the winner fails to perform the actions established in clause 3.3 of the Regulation [1].

The Organizer may refuse to issue the grant to a participant if the participant is caught committing fraud, deception, or violating the terms of the Regulation [3]. This also applies if the participant provided unreliable information [3].

The Organizer has the right to unilaterally prohibit any person from further participation in the competition if that person falsifies documents, derives benefit from submitting fake documents, violates the Regulation, acts destructively, or intends to harass, insult, threaten, or cause trouble to any person connected with the competition [2]. This right also applies if the person violates conduct rules at the competition venue, including public order [2].

## Repayment and Early Termination

In any other cases of early termination of the Agreement for reasons not specified in clause 5.4 of the Contract, the Grant Recipient is obliged to return the portion of the grant provided under the Contract [4]. The repayment amount is 20% of the sum equal to the cost of education, calculated proportionally to the period of actual study by the Grant Recipient [4]. This period runs from the date of the order on admission to the date of the order on dismissal [4].

If a Grant Recipient is dismissed before the end of the first year (for bachelors) or the first trimester (for masters) of study under the Agreement, the Grantor may decide not to demand the return of the provided grant amount [5]. The parties conclude an additional agreement to the Contract regarding this decision [5]. The Grantor’s decision is based on information about the Grant Recipient’s current academic performance and attendance, as well as other information indicating the Grant Recipient’s good faith in mastering the educational program [5].

## Academic Performance Conditions

The grant is affected if there are more than two satisfactory (C) grades after the first (autumn) trimester (semester) [6]. It is also affected if there are more than four satisfactory (C) grades after the first (spring) and/or first (summer) trimester (semester) [6]. Results of repeated interim assessments for the corresponding subjects are not taken into account in these cases [6].

The grant is affected if there is one or more unsatisfactory () grades after the corresponding trimester [7]. Results of repeated interim assessments for the corresponding subjects are not taken into account in this case [7].

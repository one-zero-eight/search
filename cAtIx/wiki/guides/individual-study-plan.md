# Индивидуальный учебный план

## How to apply

You submit an application for transfer to an individual study plan to the Director of the University using the form specified in the appendix to the Regulation [1]. First-year students enrolled in their first semester or trimester must submit the application no later than fourteen calendar days after the start of their studies [1]. All other students must submit the application no later than fourteen calendar days before the start of the semester [1].

## Approval process

The University organizes an attestation commission to consider the possibility of transferring you to an individual study plan based on your application [2]. The attestation commission can decide to recommend the transfer or to refuse it [3].

The commission must refuse the transfer if:
*   You violated the deadlines for submitting the application [4].
*   The annual volume of the Educational Program is exceeded [4].
*   There is a violation of the logical sequence and content-methodological interconnection of disciplines (modules), other components, including practices of the corresponding Educational Program [4].
*   You have academic debt [4].
*   There are other cases decided by the attestation commission [4].

The transfer to an individual study plan and the approval of the individual study plan are carried out by an order of the Director of the University or another person authorized by the Director, based on the decision of the attestation commission [5].

## Academic schedule

For bachelor’s and master’s programs, the academic calendar is approved as part of the individual study plan or separately by an order of the Director of the University or another person authorized by the Director [6]. For graduate programs (aspirantura), the academic calendar is approved by an order of the Director of the University or another person authorized by the Director [7].

You attend classes and undergo intermediate assessment during the periods established by the academic calendar included in the individual study plan or approved by the order of the Director of the University or another person authorized by the Director [8].

## Assessment

The forms, frequency, and procedure for conducting current performance control, intermediate assessment, the grading system for intermediate assessment results, the procedure and deadlines for clearing academic debt, and the forms and procedure for passing the state final assessment for students on an individual study plan are regulated by the local normative acts of the University [9].

If you fail to clear academic debt within the established deadlines, you are expelled from the University for failing to fulfill the duties of conscientious study of the Educational Program and execution of the individual study plan [10].

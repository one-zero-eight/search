# Индивидуальный учебный план

## Application and deadlines

You submit an application for transfer to an individual study plan in the form per the app [1]. You address the application to the Director of the University [1]. You must submit the application no later than fourteen calendar days before the start of the semester [1]. If you are enrolled in the first year, you submit the application no later than fourteen calendar days after the start of your studies [1].

## Approval and rejection

The Attestation Commission reviews your application and may recommend transfer to an individual study plan or refuse the transfer [2]. The Director of the University or another person authorized by the Director issues an order to transfer you to the individual study plan and approve the plan based on the Attestation Commission's decision [4]. The individual study plan is approved by the order of the Director of the University or another person authorized by the Director [5].

The Attestation Commission may refuse your transfer to an individual study plan if you violate the application submission deadlines [3]. The Commission may refuse if you exceed the annual volume of the Educational Program [3]. The Commission may refuse if you violate the logical sequence and substantive-methodological interconnection of disciplines (modules), other components, including practices of the corresponding Educational Program [3]. The Commission may refuse if you have academic debt [3]. The Commission may refuse in other cases decided by the Attestation Commission [3].

## Academic schedule and assessment

The calendar academic schedule for study under an individual study plan for bachelor's and master's programs is approved as part of the individual study plan or separately by the order of the Director of the University or another person authorized by the Director [6]. You attend classes and undergo intermediate assessment within the deadlines established by the calendar academic schedule included in the individual study plan or approved by the order of the Director of the University or another person authorized by the Director [7].

You are transferred to the next course if you do not have academic debt in disciplines (modules), other components, including practices, scientific research, scientific (research) activity, and have fully completed the individual study plan of the current course [8]. If you do not eliminate academic debt within the established deadlines, you are expelled from the University as having failed to fulfill the duties of conscientious study of the Educational Program and execution of the individual study plan [9].

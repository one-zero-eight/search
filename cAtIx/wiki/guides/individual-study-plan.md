# Индивидуальный учебный план

## Application and Approval

To apply for an individual study plan, you must submit an application for transfer to the Director of the University using the form specified in the appendix to the Regulation [1]. First-year students must submit this application no later than fourteen calendar days after the start of their studies [1]. All other students must submit the application no later than fourteen calendar days before the start of the semester [1].

The University organizes an attestation commission to consider the possibility of transferring to an individual study plan based on the application [2]. The attestation commission can decide to recommend the transfer or refuse the transfer [3].

The commission must refuse the transfer if:
*   You violated the deadlines for submitting the application [4].
*   The annual volume of the Educational Program is exceeded [4].
*   There is a violation of the logical sequence and content-methodological interconnection of disciplines (modules), other components, including practices of the corresponding Educational Program [4].
*   You have academic debt [4].
*   There are other cases decided by the attestation commission [4].

If the commission recommends the transfer, the Director of the University or another person authorized by the Director issues an order to transfer you to the individual study plan and approve the plan based on the commission's decision [5].

## Academic Schedule and Assessment

The academic calendar for bachelor's and master's programs is approved either as part of the individual study plan or separately by an order of the Director of the University or another person authorized by the Director [6]. For doctoral studies (aspirantura), the academic calendar is approved by an order of the Director of the University or another person authorized by the Director [7].

You must attend classes and undergo intermediate assessment during the periods established by the academic calendar included in the individual study plan or approved by the Director's order [8].

The forms, frequency, and procedure for conducting current performance control, intermediate assessment, the grading system for intermediate assessment results, the procedure and deadlines for clearing academic debt, and the forms and procedures for passing the state final assessment for students on an individual study plan are regulated by the University's local normative acts [9].

If you fail to clear your academic debt within the established deadlines, you are expelled from the University for failing to fulfill the duties of conscientious study of the Educational Program and execution of the individual study plan [10].

# History of Russia (S26)

**• Course name:** History of Russia (Russian science in the History of Russia)

**• Course number:** S26

1. **Course characteristics**

<blockquote>The course involves studying the history of science and technology in Russia and their role in the development of the country in various periods of history, as well as the role of Russian science in global scientific and technological progress. The relevance of the course is determined by the insufficient reflection of this topic in the school course of Russian history, as well as the importance of studying these issues for the formation of a full-fledged scientific worldview in students.
</blockquote>
**Key concepts of the class**

<blockquote>• Origins of scientific cognition in Russian culture and different influences on it

• The development system of Russian education in connection with the development of science

• The problem of implementing technologies in Russia

• The role of Russian science in the World scientific progress

**What is the purpose of this course?**

To form the pattern of the history of Russian scientific knowledge, contributing to the formation of students' scientific outlook and their professional competencies.
</blockquote>
**Course Objectives Based on Bloom’s Taxonomy**

**What should a student remember at the end of the course?** *(5-7 points for bachelors and 3-5 for masters)*

By the end of the course, the students should be able to remember and recognize:

• The main milestones in the development of the Russian education system

• Key scientific discoveries of Russian scientists in connection with the development of world science

• Technological inventions of Russian engineers

• Main stages of the development of Russian science

**What should a student be able to understand at the end of the course? (*5-7 points for bachelors and 4-5 for masters*)**

• Features of Russian scientific development at different stages

• Relationship of scientific and technological progress in the history of Russia

• Social factors influencing the development of Russian science and technology

• Role of Russian science in the history of world science and technology

**What should a student be able to apply at the end of the course?** (3-5 points for bachelors and 4-6 for masters)

By the end of the course, the students should be able to:

• Use some methods of the humanitarian studies and their professional field

• Find and critically analyze information relevant to professional development

• Evaluate the possible prospects of a particular scientific and technical product from a historical point of view

• Argue personal position on the development of Russian science and technology

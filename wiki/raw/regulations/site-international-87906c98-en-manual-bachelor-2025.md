# BACHELOR'S PROGRAM — Download the admission manual

Hello, dear applicant!

You have questions about what a grant competition is and how it goes? Let's see how it works …

So, our Grant is an opportunity to study at a paid place at the expense of the university fully or partly. That means, if you get a Grant approval, you can get a discount on tuition from 20% to 100%, it depends on the results of the passing competitions. In order to receive the grant, it is necessary to take part in the Grant competition, which runs from December to May. The procedure of the Grant competition will be available from December, 1. You can register here https://profile.innopolis.university/. You need to attach a portfolio with your achievements to the application. We advise you not to delay with this competition in order to find out your results as early as possible.

The grant competition consists of two stages with competitive tests. They differ depending on the educational program you choose and the language of studies.

At the first stage, you need to take tests in mathematics and computer science. If you have chosen a program with English as the language of studies, you will also pass the English test. Next, if you successfully complete the first stage, you will be invited to the second online stage. Here you will pass advanced profile tests in computer science, mathematics, physics and programming. If you choose an English-language program, you also take part in listening and reading tests in English. An additional profile test is given in Russian-language programs. Online interviews with HR experts are also included at this stage. Based on the results of these tests, the Grant Commission makes the final decision on the approval of the grant.

The final result will be sent to the email address you specified in the registration form. If the grant is approved, all you have to do is submit your documents online and take the entrance tests.

It's important to pay attention that the program of your grant can be changed. If the grant is approved for an English-language program, you can choose any of them and use it, but for a Russian-language program, you will be able to use it only for a program in Russian. It's also important to consider that you can participate in the grant competition only once per academic year. Participation in the grant competition does not oblige you to do anything, it gives you more opportunities instead. Therefore, we recommend everyone to pass the grant competition and try to get the grant to study at Innopolis University! Possibility № 2 - the Russian Government offers the state scholarship for international students which includes tuition fee coverage and monthly allowance (for the entire duration of study regardless of the student's success). We advise you to apply for it. Register at https://education-in-russia.com/ and fill in the application. You will be able to choose 6 Russian Universities. Please put Innopolis University on the 1st place in the priority list so that we can receive your application first. Otherwise, you may be accepted to another university.

We are waiting for your application to participate in the grant competition and wish you good luck!

# System of fines

List of fines for violations of the Rules of accommodation in a residential complex "Innopolis University"

| No. p/p | List of fines | The amount (rubles) of the fine for each violation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. | Transfer of a pass (admission card) to third parties (clause 3.1 of the Rules) | 5 000 (Five thousand) |
| 2. | Lost key and pass | 300 (Three hundred) |
| 3. | Damage to the electronic lock | 1 000 (One thousand) |
| 4. | Keeping two or more bottles of potable water in an occupied residential area (hereinafter referred to as a Room) (clause 4.1.4 of the Rules) | 400 (Four hundred) for each container unit |
| 5. | Violation of sanitary standards of living, cleanliness and order in Rooms and public areas (including, but not limited to, the presence of the following violations: dirt in the room, garbage, the presence of personal items scattered in the Rooms, food, furniture surfaces and walls of rooms contain significant contamination, garbage removal in violation of the established rules, as well as other violations of sanitary standards established by the Rules and current legislation of the Russian Federation) | Double Room – 1 195 (One thousand one hundred and ninety-five) Four-bed room – 2 000 (Two thousand) Five-bed room – 1 650 (One thousand six hundred and fifty) |
| 6. | Violation of sanitary standards of accommodation, cleanliness and order in the Rooms identified when renting a Room to Residents at checkout | Five-bed room – 10 000 (Ten thousand) |
| 6. | Violation of sanitary standards of accommodation, cleanliness and order in the Rooms identified when renting a Room to Residents at checkout | Quadruple room – 5 200 (Five thousand two hundred) |
| 6. | Violation of sanitary standards of accommodation, cleanliness and order in the Rooms identified when renting a Room to Residents at checkout | Double room – 4 300 (Four thousand three hundred) |
| 7. | Storage of garbage in the Room (two or more bags up to 30 liters or one bag from 60 liters), including food containers and boxes | 600 (Six hundred) |
| 8. | The presence of ice on the inner walls of the refrigerator or freezer, the presence of dirt in the refrigerator, a broken freezer door | 2 250 (Two thousand two hundred and fifty) |
| 9. | Damage to the equipment of automatic fire protection installations, disabling (disabling, closing, etc.): - voice sounders of the warning system and management of evacuation of people in case of fire - fire detectors (manual and smoke) - fire alarm systems | 10 000 (Ten thousand) |
| 10. | Damage to the floor covering in the Room and public places | 2 500 (Two thousand five hundred) per sq. m |

| 11. | Damage to the wall covering in the Room (the amount of the fine is calculated based on the area of the entire wall, the coating of which was damaged) and public places | 1 000 (One thousand) per sq. m |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 12. | Unauthorized occupancy in the Room; unauthorized relocation from Room to Room; unauthorized entry into the occupied Room of citizens-family members or other temporary residents | Payment for each day of stay in the amount of twice the cost of accommodation in accordance with the rates approved by the relevant order of ANO HE "Innopolis University" |
| 13. | Carrying, storing and using on the territory of a residential complex: - alcoholic beverages, - hookahs, - narcotic substances - firearms and cold weapons - explosive, flammable and toxic substances | 3 000 (Three thousand) |
| 14. | Smoking on the territory of the residential complex, including: - electronic cigarettes, - hookahs, - other tobacco products. At the same time, the fact of smoking can be confirmed by a commission consisting of two or more administrators and Committee members not only during the violation, but also after, using visual inspection and sensory organs | 3 000 (Three thousand) |
| 15. | Furniture breakage (in case of possible repairs) | 3,000 (Three thousand) per unit |

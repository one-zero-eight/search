# The cost of accommodation services in the residential complex for privileged residents

The cost of accommodation services in the Residential complex of Innopolis University from 20 October, 2025

Cost of accommodation services by room category:

| n / | a Room category (type of accommodation) | Cost of a bed per day, rubles |
| --- | --- | --- |
| of Building No. 1-4 |  |  |
| 1. | Double room (a bed-place) | 1 800 |
| 2. | Double two-room suite (a bed-place) | 2 200 |
| 3. | Four-bed two-room suite (a bed-place) | 1 800 |
| 4. | Five-bed three-room suite (a bed-place) | 1 800 |
| Of Building No. 5-7 |  |  |
| 1. | Double single room (a bed-place) | 2 100 |
| 2. | Triple room (bed/second tier) | 1000 |
| 3. | Triple room (bed/first tier) | 1400 |
| 4. | Triple Room (a bed-place) | 1800 |

| 6. | Five-bed two-room suite (bed in a triple block/second tier) | 1000 |
| 7. | Five-bed two-room suite (bed in a triple block/first tier) | 1400 |
| 8. | Five-bed two-room suite (bed in a triple block) | 1600 |
| 9. | Five-bed two-room suite (bed in a double block) | 2000 |

**2.** The price of accommodation services includes: maintenance cleaning of rooms, use of a refrigerator, TV, microwave and kettle (if available in the room), air conditioning in summer, Wi-Fi-zone, Parking, 24-hour laundry room, as well as provision of a set of bed linen and towels.

**3.** A child under the age of 7 (seven) years of age inclusive is provided with free accommodation when sharing accommodation with a parent without providing a separate place.

**4.** Categories of persons who receive a discount when providing accommodation services:

- - for students of ANO HE "Innopolis University" a discount of 90 % of the cost of bed-type accommodation services for the period of study is provided;

- - for those who have completed their studies at ANO HE "Innopolis University"

(graduates), a discount of 72 % from the cost of services is provided for bed-type accommodation during the first year after graduation;

- - for employees of ANO HE "University Innopolis" when staying in a double room, a

discount is set for each bed in the amount of 90 % of the cost of accommodation services, subject to the conditions specified in the Order of approval on the corporate portal" Bitrix24 " of applications for temporary accommodation of employees in the residential complex of ANO HE "University Innopolis" during the waiting period for rental housing or as part of official necessity, as well as invited persons;

- - for students and employees of ANO HE "Innopolis University", when staying together

with their spouse in the room, a discount for the second bed for the spouse is established in the amount of 80 % of the cost of accommodation services;

-students and employees of ANO HE "Innopolis University", when staying together with their spouse and children in a room, receive a 70% discount for a bed for the spouse, while children are placed together with their parents in additional places in the room of the appropriate capacity;

- - for employees of Innopolis AC, when staying in a double room, a discount is set for

each bed in the amount of 90 % of the cost of accommodation services for the duration of the employment contract.

**5.** During the academic leave period, students of the Autonomous Non-profit Organization HE "Innopolis University", specified in paragraph 4 of this Appendix, will not receive a discount on accommodation services.

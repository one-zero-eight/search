# Rules of the bicycle rooms use

RULES FOR USING THE BICYCLE STORAGE ROOM

For the convenience of storing bicycles of students of the ANO HE "Innopolis University", a bicycle storage room is organized at the address: Innopolis, Universitetskaya str., 1, building № 2, ground floor.

## 1. General provisions:

**1.1** Access to the bicycle storage room is only available to students of the ANO HE "Innopolis University".

**1.2** Only bicycle storage is allowed in this room.

**1.3** There is no charge for the use of the bike room. ANO HE "Innopolis University" does not accept bicycles for storage and is not responsible for the safety of bicycles and other things left in this room.

**1.4** ANO HE "Innopolis University" has the right to make adjustments to these Rules.

## 2. Access to the bicycle storage room:

**2.1** Key collection is carried out at the security of building № 2 upon presentation of a pass, which remains in the deposit until the keys are handed over. It is forbidden to take the keys outside the case, make copies, change the lock.

**2.2** The bike must be firmly installed or suspended in a free compartment. If necessary, it is allowed to fasten the bike with a padlock.

**2.3** A sticker with the owner's name and contact information (residential address, contact phone number) must be affixed to the bike.

**2.4** Leaving your bike you need to remember about others, respect other people's bikes, put your own carefully, without interfering with others and not blocking the passage.

## 3. It is forbidden in the bicycle storage room:

**3.1** Store other property (tires, personal belongings). Such things will be disposed of!

**3.2** Penetration into other ground floor compartments.

**3.3** Smoking. Remember for violation of fire safety rules, you are subject to administrative and criminal liability in accordance with the current legislation!

**3.4** Damage other people's equipment installed in the bike room.

**3.5** Store bicycles that are in a technically defective (dilapidated) condition. In case of violation of the Rules, this user will be permanently denied the right to access the bike room!

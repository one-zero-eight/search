# Master's degree — Download the admission manual

Hello, dear applicant!

You have questions about what a grant competition is and how it goes? Let's see how it works …

So, our Grant is an opportunity to study at a paid place at the expense of the university fully or partly. That means, if you get a Grant approval and pass the IET (internal entrance tests) successfully, you can get a discount on tuition from 50% to 100%, it depends on the results of the passing competitions. The full tuition fee without a discount is 800,000 rubles per year. Depending on the possible discount, you will pay the following amount:

Grant amount Tuition fee per year, R.

100% 70% 60% 50% 0%

240 000 320 000 400 000 800 000

In order to receive the grant, it is necessary to take part in the Grant competition, which runs from December to May. The procedure of the Grant competition will be available from December, 1. You can register here https://profile.innopolis.university/. To apply, you will need to register, add a CV and a motivation letter in English. We advise you not to delay with this competition in order to find out your results as early as possible.

The grant competition consists of two stages with competitive tests. They differ depending on the educational program you choose and the language of studies.

At the first stage, you need to pass a profile test (the exception is the program "Technological Entrepreneurship"). If you have chosen a program with English as the language of studies, you will also pass the English test.

Next, if you successfully complete the first stage, you will be invited to the second online stage. Here you will pass advanced profile tests. If you choose an English-language program, you also take part in listening and reading tests in English. An additional profile test is given in Russian-language programs. Online interviews with HR experts and industry representatives (for Advanced Engineering School programs – Artificial Intelligence and Data engineering, Software Engineering, Security and network Engineering) are also included at this stage. After passing the tests of the 2nd stage, you will have the programming contest. It is attended by the applicants of the Artificial Intelligence and Data engineering, Software Engineering, Robotics and computer vision programs. The test date is set separately. For English-language programs, it is also necessary to have an online interview with the professor in English. For Russian-language programs, the interview with the professor is conducted in Russian.

Based on the results of these tests, the Grant Commission makes the final decision on the approval of the grant. As a result, you will get the grant from 50% to 100%, depending on the number of the points scored during the competitive tests, or you may be denied the grant if the final test score did not reach the minimum passing score.

The final result will be sent to the email address you specified in the registration form. It's important to pay attention that you can apply for only one program. It will be possible to use the approved grant only for the declared educational program. If the grant is approved, all you have to do is take the entrance tests, wait for their results and expect enrollment lists in August. It's also important to consider that you can participate in the grant competition only once per academic year. Participation in the grant competition does not oblige you to do anything, it gives you more opportunities instead. Therefore, we recommend everyone to pass the grant competition and try to get the grant to study at Innopolis University! Possibility № 2 - the Russian Government offers the state scholarship for international students which includes tuition fee coverage and monthly allowance (for the entire duration of study regardless of the student's success). We advise you to apply for it. Register at https://education-in-russia.com/ and fill in the application. You will be able to choose 6 Russian Universities. Please put Innopolis University on the 1st place in the priority list so that we can receive your application first. Otherwise, you may be accepted to another university.

We are waiting for your application to participate in the grant competition and wish you good luck!

# DOCUMENTS — Rules of the water room use

Rules for using the drinking water storage room

Access to the exchange room-round the clock!

**1.** Residents can enter the room using a pass (access card).

**2.** The room is equipped with shelves with cells, each of which contains a full cylinder. The water bottle can be taken by putting a replacement container in this place. Attention! It is not allowed to take two full bottles of water into the room at once!

**3.** If necessary, residents can use a trolley to transport the balloon to their room and return it to its place.

**4.** It is forbidden to use the room for other purposes, for example, as a warehouse of things.

**5.** The administration is constantly monitoring the condition of the room, monitoring the total number of cylinders.

**6.** Residents are allowed to keep one bottle of drinking water in the room. In accordance with paragraph 6.6 of the rules of accommodation for the storage of two or more cylinders in the room, the administration has the right to charge a fee of 300 rubles for each unit of packaging.

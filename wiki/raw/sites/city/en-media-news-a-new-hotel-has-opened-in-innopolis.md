## KRAFT HOTEL INNOPOLIS has started accepting bookings

A new four-star hotel with 44 rooms, including standard, superior, suites and options for disabled guests, has opened in the city.

The infrastructure includes a NANO lobby bar, an 86-seat PHOTON restaurant in the all day dining concept, a conference room, meeting rooms and a gym. A restaurant with a brewery will open in 2025.

The interior design was developed by the Moscow bureau MAD Architects with an emphasis on functionality and adaptability.

You can book a room on the [website](https://kravt-hotel.ru/)

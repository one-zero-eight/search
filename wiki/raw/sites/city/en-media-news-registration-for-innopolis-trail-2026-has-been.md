# Registration for Innopolis Trail 2026 has been opened

[Рус](/raw/sites/city/ru-media-news-registration-for-innopolis-trail-2026-has-been.md)

[Тат](https://innopolis.com/tt/media/news/registration-for-innopolis-trail-2026-has-been-opened)

11 February 2026

![](https://innopolis.com/uploads/thumbs/frame-1-1-429adfcc3d-a888ec0a0e57275239f9363917fec464.png)

The TIMERMAN team has opened registration for Innopolis Trail 2026, the main running event in Russia's youngest city.

The trail will take place on July 18, 2026 along the route of the Volga Trail of the Republic of Tatarstan, a 350 km long pedestrian highway along the Volga River. The participants will find picturesque forest trails with views of the Sviyaga River.

The race program will begin with TIMERKIDS children's starts at distances of 300 and 600 meters. Next, the athletes will have trails with a length of 3 km, 10 km, 20 km and 30 km — for any level of training.

Registration is open until July 13, 2026. All details and registration are available on the [TIMERMAN website](http://timerman.org/event/innotrail2026).

The trail race format has been held in Innopolis since 2023 and traditionally gathers hundreds of participants from different regions of Russia.

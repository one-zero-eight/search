Glamping ‘SVIYAGA glamp’ has found its home just three kilometres away from Innopolis. It is a place of serenity, tranquillity and harmony with nature.

Spend your best days at the glamping site, which has 10 warm homes, bath complex, event venue and a campfire area. Open all year round.

All lodges are equipped with film projectors, and there is a barbecue area on the patio.

## The creative development platform

The urban space ArtSpace covers the cultural life of the city. Here you may attend gigs, plays, parties and exhibitions. Additionally, you could explore a sound studio, a workshop, and creative classes for kids and adults

## Cinema&Theatre

‘Cinema&Theathre’ has taken place within the ArtSpace since 2021. There are festival and popular films shown in Russian or in their original dubbing. The film programme is devised by the Saint-Petersburg’s ‘Cinema&Theatre’ located in Angleterre.

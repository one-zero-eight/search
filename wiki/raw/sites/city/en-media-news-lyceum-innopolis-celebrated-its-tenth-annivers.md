# Lyceum Innopolis celebrated its tenth anniversary

[Рус](/raw/sites/city/ru-media-news-lyceum-innopolis-celebrated-its-tenth-annivers.md)

[Тат](https://innopolis.com/tt/media/news/lyceum-innopolis-celebrated-its-tenth-anniversary)

12 January 2026

![](https://innopolis.com/uploads/thumbs/frame-1-9718cff1ac-0df237a8ec9594fed504dc9406337e56.png)

In honor of the anniversary, the Innopolis Lyceum gathered students, teachers and graduates. Together we recalled the history of the Lyceum's creation, walked through the exhibition of students' projects and watched a concert created by lyceum students and a guest orchestra. A special note to the celebration was given by the emotional parting word of the lyceum director Arman Kostanyan, addressed to the students.

The honored guests of the celebration congratulated the lyceum on an important date. Among them are Roman Shaikhutdinov, Deputy Prime Minister of the Republic of Tatarstan, Minzaliya Zakirova, Deputy Minister of Education and Science of the Republic of Tatarstan, Ildar Khuzzyatov, Mayor of the city, and Ilgiz Khanbikov, head of the Innopolis Executive Committee.

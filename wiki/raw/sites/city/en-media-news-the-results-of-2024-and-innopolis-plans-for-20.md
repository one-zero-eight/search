## How did Russia's most technologically advanced city develop in 2024 and what awaits it in 2025?

A reporting meeting of the City Council was held in Innopolis.

## Economy and standard of living

Innopolis's tax deductions increased by 22% over the year, and 7.7 billion rubles of investments were attracted for infrastructure development. 45 new companies (669 in total) and more than 1,000 jobs have appeared in the city. The average salary increased by 8% and amounted to 154,631 rubles.

7846 people are in Innopolis every day, the residents' happiness index is 82 points. The city remains one of the safest: there are more than 500 surveillance cameras operating here.

In 2024, the renewal of the shuttle fleet was completed: the average age of intercity buses is now 1.4 years old, and the number of deviations from the schedule has been reduced by three times.

## Main events — 2024

A technopark with 2,000 jobs and the largest data center in the Volga region have been opened in Innopolis. New houses have been built in the residential complexes "Kvartal Yu" and "Zion-2", a water treatment plant and an automatic modular boiler house have been modernized. An important event of the year was the opening of the Ulyanovsk interchange.

In 2025, it is planned to open a new technopark, complete the Zion-3 residential complex and update the city's general plan.

## Urban environment

For the sixth time, Innopolis took the first place in the urban environment quality index among small towns in Russia. He won the All-Russian competition of the best projects for a comfortable urban environment with the reconstruction project of Quantum Boulevard. The photo exhibition "Innopolis through the eyes of a Neuroartist" opened in the underground passages.

In 2025, the reconstruction of Quantum Boulevard and the construction of a mini-football field will begin.

![](https://innopolis.com/uploads/thumbs/vid-na-prud-5dfc8f855f-1ca2c368bc1d90ed1a935d13c09c73a3.jpeg)

## Technology in the city

Innopolis continues to test advanced technologies. In 2024, the Russian government approved an experimental legal regime for the operation of unmanned aircraft systems in the city.

## Education and medicine

In 2024, the Innopolis School joined the UNESCO Association, lyceum students became Olympic medalists and showed high results on the Unified State Exam. The university has attracted 1,528 students from 32 countries and has become a leader in training AI specialists. In 2025, the School will celebrate its 10th anniversary, the Lyceum will strengthen cooperation with Sirius and the universities of Hong Kong, and the University will open the first college enrollment.

In 2025, Innopolis plans to develop medicine for children and adults by increasing the number of specialized specialists.

## Events and tourism

The city has become a venue for major events, including BRICS and the Games of the Future. More than 140,000 tourists visited the city, most of them in organized groups. In 2025, it is planned to open a tourist information center and hold the 10th anniversary of Innopolis.

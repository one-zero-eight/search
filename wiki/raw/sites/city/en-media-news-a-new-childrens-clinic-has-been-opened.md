## The opening of a children's polyclinic after major repairs took place in Innopolis. It has doubled in size, and the number of classrooms has tripled.

The renovated polyclinic has two offices for pediatricians, two offices for highly specialized specialists (surgeon, orthopedist, ENT, neurologist, cardiologist, ophthalmologist), an ultrasound and ECG room, a treatment room and a dairy food dispensary.

"The development of medicine is one of the key priorities of the city. And the renovation of the children's polyclinic is part of the systematic work. The repair will improve the diagnostic process, expand capabilities, and reduce waiting times. It is important for us that every family can receive medical care in the city itself," said the head of Innopolis, Ildar Khuzzyatov.

The expansion was carried out within the framework of an order of the Cabinet of Ministers of the Republic of Tatarstan on behalf of the Prime Minister of the Republic of Tatarstan Alexei Pesoshin.

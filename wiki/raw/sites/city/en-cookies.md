In case of refusal of personal data processing metric programs I am informed about the need to stop using the site or disable cookies in your browser settings.

* A "cookie" is understood to be a small piece (file) of data sent by the web server hosting the website and subsequently stored on the respective device using which the website is accessed. This piece of data stores information about the settings used when you visit the website.

Device information includes the model and type of device, IP address, and software such as web browser, operating system and other software used to access the site, the date and time of access to the site, and the location of the device.

Processing of the above mentioned data means actions related to collection, systematization, accumulation, storage, specification (updating, changing), use, distribution (including transfer or providing access to third parties which are Russian entities, in particular Yandex LLC), depersonalization, blocking and destruction.

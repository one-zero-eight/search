## 10 Innopolis University students received a grant to implement their ideas

The winning projects make life more convenient through technology—from sports equipment rental and clothing selection to fraud protection, drone control, and the creation of 3D home models.

Innopolis University submitted 46 applications to the Foundation for Assistance to Small Innovative Enterprises (FASIE) competition. 36 of these advanced to the on-site round, and 10 won, receiving 1 million rubles each to implement their ideas.

Tatarstan ranked second after Moscow in the number of projects supported, accounting for 13% of all competition winners.

The university's startup studio assisted the students with their applications. Over the four years of participation in the program, Innopolis students have already raised 57 million rubles to develop their projects.

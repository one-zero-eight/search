## About the school

The school follows the regular curriculum with an in-depth study of particular subjects. The school is committed to build an individual learning curve for every student.

Classes are arranged in a two-shift system. No admission tests are required.

![](https://innopolis.com/uploads/thumbs/pol9318-10e775c55e-bb901e85cd0460697d750d8a842c8118.jpeg)

## Extra classes

There are clubs in programming, robotics, financial and computer literacy and various sports.

![](https://innopolis.com/uploads/thumbs/pol9302-9fd065beb8-bb901e85cd0460697d750d8a842c8118.jpeg)

## How to enrol

For admission, apply through the [Gosuslugi RT](https://uslugi.tatarstan.ru/education/school) website.

In case you switch from another school, you need to have a personal file, a medical card and a report card.

## IT education

Education at the university is conducted in English and Russian. The programme is taught by 135 lecturers and researchers from 17 countries who have experience working for the world's leading IT companies. 40% of the programme is made up of practice and team-based projects

Master's programmes:

- Software engineering;

- Security and network engineering;

- Artificial intelligence and data engineering;

- Robotics and computer vision;

- Information technology entrepreneurship.

Postgraduate study:

- Mathematical modeling, numerical methods and software packages.

## How to apply

Admission requires an entry test in mathematics, programming and an interview with a teacher. The tuition fee is 400,000 roubles a semester. It is possible to get a grant covering up to 100% of your tuition fees

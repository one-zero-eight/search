## 10 IT-startups from Tatarstan will present their developments to an expert council

On October 30, 10 IT startups from Tatarstan will present their projects at a pitch session before an expert panel, which includes venture investors, IT business leaders, and representatives from Innopolis. The panel will select which startups will be granted resident status in the Innopolis Special Economic Zone (SEZ).

Following the pitch session, there will be a screening of "Business to Be!" ("Бизнесу быть!"), a film about entrepreneurship in Russia, presented by Tochka Bank. Afterward, guests can take part in a public discussion on the role of AI in business, featuring entrepreneurs from Kazan and experts from the Innopolis SEZ.

When: October 30, 16:00–21:00

Where: A.S. Popov Technopark, Innopolis

Transport: A free shuttle from Kazan departs at 14:30 from the "Kombinat Zdorovye" bus stop. Additionally, a shuttle service runs hourly between Kazan and Innopolis—view the schedule here.

## ​The Startup Village 2026 program from Skolkovo has become known The Skolkovo Foundation has opened registration for Startup Village 2026

In 2026, the Startup Village program is built around technology requests from corporations. Partners of the Skolkovo Foundation publicly formulate tasks and allocate budgets for implementation.

Tech startups offer solutions ready for piloting and scaling. Meetings are held offline: negotiations, pitch sessions, signing agreements.

Participants:

Startups and small tech companies

Corporations and venture funds

International partners

Registration is open on the [official conference website](https://startupvillage.ru/).

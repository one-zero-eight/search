# Information signs about robots on the roads appeared in Innopolis

![](https://innopolis.com/uploads/thumbs/tablicka-v-innopolise-2-a03d3c4b73-90bda2ff2ab21bc39070f26c7ce914b4.jpeg)

Innopolis, together with the Yandex autonomous transport division, installed the first signs in Russia warning drivers about the movement of robots. These signs tell you where to find self-driving cars or robot couriers.

For locals, robots on the roads are a familiar phenomenon, but visitors to the city may get confused when they meet a delivery robot at a pedestrian crossing. The new signs will help drivers better navigate the peculiarities of local traffic.

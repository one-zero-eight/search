# Robotaxi will continue to be tested in Innopolis until 2028

[Рус](/raw/sites/city/ru-media-news-robotaxi-will-continue-to-be-tested-in-innopol.md)

[Тат](https://innopolis.com/tt/media/news/robotaxi-will-continue-to-be-tested-in-innopolis-until-2028)

28 March 2025

![](https://innopolis.com/uploads/thumbs/robotaksi-1-5c6b77adcf-84917f53a4d4a43339e9f3bfdfd44fab.jpeg)

The Ministry of Economic Development [extended](https://www.rbc.ru/rbcfreenews/67e622519a794743edf3a484?from=newsfeed) the experiment for another three years.

The first robotaxis were launched in Innopolis in 2018. The experiment yielded significant results — almost 100 thousand trips and 1 million km of total mileage in the city. Automated driverless control technology was also tested.

Innopolis plans to continue testing unmanned vehicles in an urban environment.

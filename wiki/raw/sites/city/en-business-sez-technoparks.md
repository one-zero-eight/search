## A.S. Popov Technopark

Companies are welcome to rent a fully furnished office in an open-space, or have it redecorated to suit their needs. The rent ranges from 735 rub per square metre a month.

Several co-working spaces are available for start-ups, providing a workspace which costs 1,000 rub a month.

You can find a canteen, bank branches, notary, post office and pick-up point on the ground floor. There is also a conference room and a press centre.

## N.I. Lobachevsky Technopark

You can also rent offices ranging from 80 m² to 1,100 m² on a turnkey basis.

The Sphere, the AR-enabled co-working space with 150 workstations, is open for start-ups. There is a food court on the ground floor and underground parking for 215 cars.

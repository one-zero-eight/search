## The city award

For the third time, residents chose the best initiatives in urbanism, science and business that have improved life in the city of the future.

Voting took place in 16 nominations. 125 nominees received 50,000 votes. Among them are top managers, leaders of science and IT, as well as residents who develop the city through projects and blogs. A special nomination was given for robots that have become part of the daily life of Innopolis.

Voting ended on December 18, and the results were announced at a gala evening on December 20 in the Artspace, where all the nominees and winners gathered, including Rover A-242, Innopolisyan's favorite.

You can view the results on the [website](/raw/sites/innopolislife-ru/home.md).

The award was organized by the Innopolis marketing, brand and communications team with the support of the City Development Fund and the Mayor's Office.

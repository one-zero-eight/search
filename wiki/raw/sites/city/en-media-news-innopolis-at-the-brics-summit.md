## The best IT developments in Tatarstan

The BRICS Summit is taking place in Kazan from October 22 to 24. At the Made in Tatarstan exhibition, Innopolis will present the InnoVtol-3s unmanned aircraft for cargo monitoring and delivery, smartphones running on a domestic operating system, IT infrastructure monitoring systems, smart access control systems and a smart gate with biometric identification.

All these developments were made at Innopolis University or the Innopolis Special Economic Zone.

## Move in and settle

We’ve already taken cared of you and provided all the essential furniture in appartments. Smart intercom and fingerprint door lock are installed, so don’t worry about forgetting the keys.

Safe and spacious courtyards are equipped with playgrounds, gazebos and book crossings. Also there is a ground for pets placed just 3 minutes away from you.

Underground parking for 2000 rub a month.

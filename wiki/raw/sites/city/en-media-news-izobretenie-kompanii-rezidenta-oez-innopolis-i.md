## An invention by resident company of the Innopolis SEZ has been included in the registry of the Russian Ministry of Industry and Trade

A computer mouse developed by ICL, a resident company of the Innopolis Special Economic Zone (SEZ), has been included in the Unified Register of Russian Radio-Electronic Products.

The device is a wired optical mouse designed for use in offices and educational institutions. Sales are set to begin in the fourth quarter of this year.

An ICL keyboard had previously been added to the Ministry of Industry and Trade’s register. Customers now have access to a complete set: a Russian-made mouse and keyboard.

## About the school

There are two areas of study at the Children’s school of art - Music Art and Fine and Applied Art.

You are welcome to do vocals or play accordion, accordion, guitar, clarinet, saxophone, flute and piano.

## How to apply

You can make an appointment by telephone. Bring your child's birth certificate and National Insurance Number (СНИЛС).

Tuition is free, and there are auditions in the music department.

## Skate and workout grounds

![](https://innopolis.com/uploads/thumbs/img4262-5a068a1453-71cb59f2e2639cd5fd935545233fe421.jpeg)

The skate and workout grounds are located in the city park. Come and do sports at your spare time.

The skatepark is equipped with ramps, skate-boxes, flat banks and quarter-pipes. They are well suited for beginners and expert skaters.

Workout area is composed of different horizontal bars, huge cross-fit tyre, Swedish wall, parallel bars and a mini climbing wall.

## The City Hall

Comfortable environment is the key tenet of life in Innopolis. Everything that is created in the city is done for people, and the main KPI is the happiness index of our residents.

The city administration actively implements different ideas and projects dedicated to shaping the urban environment and organising various events alongside numerous marketing projects, while also being responsible to tackle common issues concerning water supply and landscaping.

## Appointment with the mayor

Every resident and guest of the city can make an appointment for a personal meeting with the mayor of the city, Ildar Ravilevich Khuzzyatov.

Appointments take place on Tuesdays from 2 pm until 5 pm. The City Hall is located at the Sportivnaya Str. 114. Please make an appointment in advance via concierge service, or contacts us directly: +78002222287

Information needed for an arrangement:

— Full name

— Organisation, current occupation

— Address, phone number and email

— Summary of the questions you’d like to raise

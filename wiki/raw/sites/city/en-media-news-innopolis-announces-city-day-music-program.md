## Innopolis will celebrate its birthday with a major summer festival

Innopolis invites you to the INNO birthday festival. Guests can expect a musical program, activities for children and adults, a food court, and the first party at University Square.

This year's INNO music program is built on contrasts: from new indie stars STRIO to Russian rock legend Nick Borzov.

Residents of the city will also take the stage with the Kazan project Kruzhok Chora.

The celebration will conclude with an open-air party at Innopolis University Square. Kazan musician DJ BubbleGum B. will perform at the afterparty.

Program:

5:00 PM — Festival Start

5:20 PM — Children's Bubble Show

6:00 PM — Performance by a local band from Innopolis (selection ongoing)

6:40 PM — "Choir Circle"

7:00 PM — STRIO band

8:00 PM — Nick Borzov

9:00 PM — Party with DJ BubbleGum B.

Innopolis, University Square.

June 11, 5:00 PM–11:00 PM.

Free admission

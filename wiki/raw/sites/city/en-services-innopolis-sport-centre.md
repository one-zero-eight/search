## About the sports centre

You'll find a 25-metre swimming pool with a hot tub, a Finnish and a Roman-Turkish sauna. You can also work out in the gym with a coach or in a group.

Sports grounds can also be rented. Sports facilities are free for club card holders, the football field is not included. Check the rates on the website.

# New housing in Innopolis will be available for purchase with a social mortgage

> This page is from 2025: what it says may be out of date.

[Рус](/raw/sites/city/ru-media-news-new-housing-in-innopolis-will-be-available-for.md)

[Тат](https://innopolis.com/tt/media/news/new-housing-in-innopolis-will-be-available-for-purchase-with-a-social-mortgage)

25 December 2025

![](https://innopolis.com/uploads/thumbs/frame-1-1-66a508fb9d-f80427239f9247a56ce02a6d16c78a64.png)

Innopolis was included in the [program](https://pravo.tatarstan.ru/npa_kabmin/post/?npa_id=1758364) for the construction of new housing on a social mortgage. This is an opportunity to purchase an apartment in an innovative city on preferential terms — 7% per annum and from 10% down payment. The program is aimed at specialists working in the city of Innopolis.

If you want to participate in the program, collect the necessary documents and submit an application at the mayor's office. The [application](https://pravo.tatarstan.ru/gorod-innopolis.htm/?npa_id=1742844) will be considered by the housing commission on the basis of the regulations.

Detailed terms of participation are available on the [official channel of the program](https://t.me/+MMljfnd3wsBjNzJi).

## Vocal classes for children

You can take up singing lessons without any prior training.

## Vocal. Adults

Vocal training for adults does not differ from the usual vocal course. In contrast, once a person reaches adulthood, their voice is finally shaped and they can work on it to get it into perfect pitch.

Teacher - [Yana Cepkova](https://t.me/yana_tsepkova)

# Innopolis Turns 10: How the City Celebrated Its Anniversary Festival

## The birthday celebration brought together over 5,000 people

On June 9, Innopolis marked its 10th anniversary with the 1NN0 birthday festival.

The event kicked off with a performance by the street theater Nebesnaya Karusel from Saint Petersburg. The artists presented a story about a descendant of Gaetano Ciniselli and his circus.

![](https://innopolis.com/uploads/thumbs/img2887-521e12ddfb-e1b9a10d4400ddc61ebc62885bcf8458.jpeg)

![](https://innopolis.com/uploads/thumbs/img2789-cbdacee263-ced70177f5358a9f6aa1758937a7b1f9.jpeg)

Later, the main stage featured performances by local band ASAP, Kazan-based musicians Beku and Hopeless, the group Beautiful Boys, and the evening’s headliner — PIZZA.

![](https://innopolis.com/uploads/thumbs/049a4668-610fc87535-d311ae15532057e3c7eb1dac79d032d1.jpeg)

![](https://innopolis.com/uploads/thumbs/img6211-71ab349c35-92b0ef950bcaf1594900ad0045ff71c9.jpeg)

Next to the stage, a food festival took place with over 30 vendors, accompanied by electronic sets from DJ Bubblegum. A limited-edition merch collection, created specially for the anniversary, was also on display.

![](https://innopolis.com/uploads/thumbs/img5179-c0e63aca36-d32e51833c76b1b62e31f0a273ee86ce.jpeg)

![](https://innopolis.com/uploads/thumbs/049a0678-ac8abdc474-f4c30cf8d4b320333d9e892dabf6d41a.jpeg)

Deputy Prime Minister of Tatarstan Roman Shaykhutdinov and Mayor of Innopolis Dmitry Vandyukov spoke at the festival. Shaykhutdinov thanked the people who laid the foundation for the city: “Greetings to Rustam Minnikhanov and Nikolay Nikiforov — the visionaries behind this project.” Mayor Vandyukov greeted residents in Tatar: “Хәерле кич, кадерле дуслар!” (“Good evening, dear friends!”), noting that despite its young age, Innopolis is already recognized beyond the borders of Tatarstan and Russia.

![](https://innopolis.com/uploads/thumbs/img5938-a9f63bb2a4-fe12e8a2b86ff6d7c6206e50f0cd20a8.jpeg)

![](https://innopolis.com/uploads/thumbs/img6015-45f7d87e80-74fff7fa000a16c597c8d7f67042584c.jpeg)

The festival was supported by key partners — SEZ Innopolis, Innopolis University, and JSC Innopolis. The general partner of the event was Ozon.

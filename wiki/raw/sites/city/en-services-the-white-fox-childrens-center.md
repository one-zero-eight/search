## About the children's center

They accept children from 10 months old. There is a full-day group with five meals a day. They also conduct classes in the "mom and baby" format.

## Extra classes

There are many individual and group classes. There is a speech therapist, classes are held on the development of children's emotional intelligence. They also conduct creative classes in art, speed reading, English and music.

On September 18 at the Kazan Digital Week International Forum, Innopolis and JSC “KAMA” signed a cooperation agreement. The company is developing the Russian electric car Atom, and Innopolis will serve as a testing ground for the vehicle’s future technologies.

“Innopolis is a unique platform for bringing cutting-edge transport solutions to life. Our partnership with a domestic developer will help accelerate the advancement of these technologies and their integration into the urban environment,” said Roman Shaykhutdinov, Deputy Prime Minister of the Republic of Tatarstan.

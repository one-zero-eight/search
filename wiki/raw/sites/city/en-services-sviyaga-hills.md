Sviyaga Hills is a city-resort located 10 minutes away from Innopolis.

In winter you can go snowboarding and skiing, and in summer you can play golf.

There are several hotels and chalets where you are welcome to stay. Saunas, swimming pools, hammams, sports facilities and restaurants are also available in the area.

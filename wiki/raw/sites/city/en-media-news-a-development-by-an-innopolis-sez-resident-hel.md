## NtechLab, a resident company of the SEZ «Innopolis», developed an AI-based facial recognition system

NtechLab, a resident company of the Innopolis Special Economic Zone, has helped locate 250 missing children in the Novosibirsk region in less than a year and a half using its AI-powered video analytics.

Since April 2025, the FindFace Multi system has been operating in the region as part of the "Safe City" integrated security complex. The algorithm analyzes video feeds from city cameras—including those on public transport and in streets, public squares, and government buildings. Recognition accuracy exceeds 99.9%, with analysis taking mere fractions of a second. This enables law enforcement agencies to quickly locate lost children.

In addition, NtechLab’s video analytics help identify wanted criminals; over 3,000 offenders were detected with the system's help in 2025. The company’s solutions are also deployed in the Nizhny Novgorod and Samara regions, Krasnodar Krai, the Yamalo-Nenets Autonomous Okrug, the North Caucasian Federal District, and the Kaluga region.

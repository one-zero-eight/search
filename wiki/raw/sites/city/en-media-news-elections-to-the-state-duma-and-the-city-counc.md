# Elections to the State Duma and the City Council of Deputies have begun in Innopolis

[Рус](/raw/sites/city/ru-media-news-elections-to-the-state-duma-and-the-city-counc.md)

[Тат](https://innopolis.com/tt/media/news/elections-to-the-state-duma-and-the-city-council-of-deputies-have-begun-in-innopolis)

18 September 2026

![](https://innopolis.com/uploads/thumbs/frame-72-11-2824e8a2a1-2ab11d38935d5023b3f757c0169d0a09.jpeg)

From today until Sunday, you can cast your vote in the elections to the State Duma, and residents of Innopolis with permanent residence can vote for deputies to the City Council.

Together with the residents, Roman Shaikhutdinov, Deputy Prime Minister of the Republic of Tatarstan, curator of Innopolis, was one of the first to vote at the polling station in Innopolis.

The elections are held from September 18 to 20 at polling station No. 1259 in Artspace from 8:00 to 20:00.

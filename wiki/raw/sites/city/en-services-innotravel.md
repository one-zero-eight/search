## InnoTravel

Travel Agency

![](https://innopolis.com/uploads/thumbs/img6317-919d137498-9d49870ca383130df46a99d778bc050d.jpeg)

Useful links

[Website](https://www.innopolistravel.com)

The first tour desk in Innopolis. Over 10 years, we have already hosted more than a million guests. A team of professional guides organizes interesting programs and helps you learn more about the city.

All guides are regularly trained and have city trust cards from the mayor's office, which guarantees the accuracy of the information. Guided tours are conducted individually and for groups.

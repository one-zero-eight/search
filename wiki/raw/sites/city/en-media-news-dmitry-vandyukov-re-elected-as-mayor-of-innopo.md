## On September 19, the first meeting of the Innopolis City Council of the second convocation was held

The elected deputies officially took office and chose the city’s mayor. Once again, Dmitry Vandyukov was elected.

“Innopolis is home to talented people who are ready to make the world a better place. My main task is to create all the conditions for your growth and a comfortable life,” said the Mayor of Innopolis.

![](https://innopolis.com/uploads/thumbs/photo2025-09-22-131609-8396b3ec91-faddd6f4fdfa08270fe370a5455166f7.jpeg)

![](https://innopolis.com/uploads/thumbs/photo2025-09-22-131612-f509cdecea-899e8e568c2ccc8a1850bde388a37c05.jpeg)

The meeting was also attended by Roman Shaykhutdinov, who was reappointed today as Deputy Prime Minister of Tatarstan and will continue to oversee Innopolis.

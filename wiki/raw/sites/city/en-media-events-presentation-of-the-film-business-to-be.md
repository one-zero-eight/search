## Presentation of the film 'Business to be!'

![](https://innopolis.com/uploads/thumbs/anonsdlasajta-0556e9a05b-49c343a31760e603450605123dd17d4a.jpeg)

When

30 October, 16:00

Where

Popov Technopark, conference hall

'Tochka Bank' will show its documentary about building a business in Russia in Innopolis. The film tells about the phenomenon of entrepreneurship in Russia and the people who chose this path.

After the screening, we will discuss the film with the experts, and the startups will present their projects for obtaining the status of a SEZ startup

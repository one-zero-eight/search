## Innopolis traditionally co-organizes the Trail with the TIMERMAN team

The race routes are laid out through the Volga Trail, a specially protected natural area. This location allows for a combination of exercise and time outdoors.

Since 2017, the total length of routes covered by participants has exceeded 50,000 kilometers. Last year, the race attracted approximately 1,000 athletes from across Russia.

This year, there will be three adult categories (3, 10, and 20 km) and children's races (300 and 600 meters). A separate start will be held for dogs.

When: July 18, 2026 (Saturday), 9:30 AM - 8:00 PM. Where: Innopolis Sports Complex (107 Sportivnaya Street).

Registration is open until July 13th.

Detailed information on the route map, participation rules, and registration requirements is available on the official TIMERMAN team website.

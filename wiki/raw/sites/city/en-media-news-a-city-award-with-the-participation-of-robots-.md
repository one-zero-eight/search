# A city award with the participation of robots was held in Innopolis

> This page is from 2025: what it says may be out of date.

[Рус](/raw/sites/city/ru-media-news-a-city-award-with-the-participation-of-robots-.md)

[Тат](https://innopolis.com/tt/media/news/a-city-award-with-the-participation-of-robots-was-held-in-innopolis)

19 December 2025

![](https://innopolis.com/uploads/thumbs/frame-1-23de8f50a9-125f6e21a452da8d1a8f15b0412aab45.png)

The results of the "Innopolis 2025 City Award" were announced in the IT capital. For the fourth time, residents chose the best initiatives and projects of the city. There were 17 nominations in total, among which the most notable events, people and developments of Innopolis were noted.

A special feature of this year was the selection of the highlights of the decade in special anniversary nominations. The Innopolis University team became the best team of the decade. And the AI platform for creating medicines was chosen as the main innovation of the year. A special award for his contribution to the development of the project was presented to the Deputy Prime Minister of the Republic of Tatarstan Roman Shaikhutdinov. He received his statuette "from the hands" of the robot drone "Innospector".

An exhibition of historical artifacts was organized in honor of the 10th anniversary.The exhibition combines the memories of the founders and the first inhabitants of Innopolis. Visitors were able to see a capsule with a message to future generations, laid in 2012 by the founders of the city. As well as the first robot that appeared in the city already in 2016.

160 nominees received 40,000 votes. Voting ended on December 16, and the results were announced at a gala evening on December 18 at the A.S. Popov Technopark.

All the voting results are announced on the [website](/raw/sites/innopolislife-ru/home.md).

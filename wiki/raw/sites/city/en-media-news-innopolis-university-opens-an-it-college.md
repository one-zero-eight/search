# Innopolis University opens an IT college

[Рус](/raw/sites/city/ru-media-news-innopolis-university-opens-an-it-college.md)

[Тат](https://innopolis.com/tt/media/news/innopolis-university-opens-an-it-college)

13 January 2025

![](https://innopolis.com/uploads/thumbs/2016h663-1-81fbbfef22-69a6f4aca24181b0bb74c55af130e4dc.jpeg)

An IT college will open in Innopolis in the 2025/2026 academic year. 9th grade graduates will be able to enroll in full-time training in programming and robotics.

75 students are expected to be recruited this year. The training will last 3 years and 10 months and will be conducted in Russian.

You can [apply](/raw/sites/college/home.md) until August 20th.

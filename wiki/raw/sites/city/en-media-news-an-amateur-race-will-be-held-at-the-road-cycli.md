## Innopolis will host the Russian Road Cycling Championships on the 1st of July

This is the main race of the season, where more than 250 of the strongest riders from all over Russia will compete for the champion title. City residents will also have the opportunity to ride the champions' track.

Two disciplines will unfold on the roads of Innopolis:

July 1st – Individual Time Trial (women – 26.6 km, men – 36 km).

July 2nd – Team Mixed Relay (25 km).

But most importantly, amateurs will also be able to take part. On July 1st at 3:00 PM, a mass race will be held for Innopolis residents and visitors. This is your chance to ride the very track where the professionals will compete for medals the day before.

The distance will be 5 km, starting from the Popov Technopark bus stop. Anyone over 10 years old is welcome to participate.

You only need to bring a bike and helmet. A short safety briefing is required before the start.

Get your bike ready – see you at the start!

Registration: [https://innopolis.timepad.ru/event/4006135/](https://innopolis.timepad.ru/event/4006135/)

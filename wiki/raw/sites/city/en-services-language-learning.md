## English for school children

English learning by playing games, songs and drama. The programme targets the four skills such as reading, writing, listening, enunciation and speaking.

## English course. Adults

Course for beginners. You will reach the A1 level of English after five months. It is intended to help you understand and use daily expressions and simple phrases.

Teacher - [Galina Manannikova](https://t.me/galinamanannikova).

## ​Tatar lessons

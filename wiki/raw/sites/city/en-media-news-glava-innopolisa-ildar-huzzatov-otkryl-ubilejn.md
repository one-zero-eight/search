## Today a presentation of the popular science route took place in Innopolis. This route became the anniversary - the hundredth - for all of Russia

The new route included Innopolis University, technology parks, city infrastructure and a master class on artificial intelligence. Guests got acquainted with robotaxis and delivery robots, learned how the youngest IT city in the country works, and looked at modern technologies in real life.

Today the route was presented to representatives of the media, the scientific community and the tourism industry.

In Innopolis, innovation is part of everyday life. Every third city resident is an IT specialist and scientist. Therefore, our main task as a tourist location is to popularize science and technology. By the way, many come to Innopolis on an excursion, and then return as students, company employees or residents of the city, - noted the head of the city, Ildar Khuzziatov.

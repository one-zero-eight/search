DID 2024 & Innopolis AI Conference

The international forum "Digital Innopolis Days 2024" (henceforth referred to as the Forum), which will focus on the Digital Transformation of Education, Business, and Public Administration and be held in Innopolis (Russian Federation, Republic of Tatarstan) from October 02–October 04, 2024. The conference is being organized by Innopolis University, a Russian university that specializes in teaching, research, and development in the fields of information technology, robotics, and artificial intelligence.

The biggest artificial intelligence technology companies, heads of digital transformation for the BRICS, EAEU, and Latin American nations, and executives from IT businesses will all be present at the conference. Prominent international and Russian speakers, along with representatives of academic institutions and large corporations are scheduled to talk at the Forum sites.

October 2-4, 2024

![](https://static.tildacdn.com/tild3134-6661-4336-a336-333637306431/Frame_15.png)

DID online 2024

The annual DID Online Conference will be held for the third time. Every year, it brings together teams from the country's best universities to promote the spreading of new educational ideas, concepts, and practices. "Innovative Educational Spaces" is the conference's 2024 theme.

Innovative educational spaces are environments created for learning and development. Methods and approaches using the latest technologies are implemented there. Leading universities will present participants with real-life case studies at DID Online 2024. They will demonstrate how to stimulate active and interactive learning, employ alternative methods, foster the creative thinking of their students, and assist them in successfully adapting to a quickly changing world.

The Сonference is organized by Innopolis University, a Russian university that focused on education, development, and research in the fields of information technology and robotics.

April 27, 2024

![](https://static.tildacdn.com/tild6461-3837-4035-b334-346537383130/Frame_16.png)

DID 2023

The third annual Digital Independence Forum in Russia, Digital Innopolis Days in Innopolis (Republic of Tatarstan). The main topics of the event include issues of information security, the development of the video game industry, and the human resources potential of Russia.

Participants in the forum include representatives from the IT business, science, government bodies, and educational organizations: Huawei, VK, Yandex, the Boris Shchukin Theatre Institute, Skolkovo, the ANO "Digital Economy", leading Russian universities, and industry ministries.

April 27, 2024

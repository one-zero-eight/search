# FAQ

## [Writing hub](/raw/sites/university/en-writinghubhome.md)

## FREQUENTLY

ASKED

QUESTIONS

1. Do I need to sign up?

You do if you need a face-to-face consultation to discuss a writing-related issue with our tutor. Read the detailed description of the process [here](https://t.me/Inno_Uni_Writing_Hub/19).

However, if you have a text that you want to improve, it might be better to [write to us](/raw/sites/university/en-writinghubhome-contactus.md), and a free tutor will help you to make your text better.

2. Can I email my paper to you and receive written comments from a tutor?

You can email your paper to g.gelvanovsky@innopolis.ru , and you will be assigned a tutor to improve your paper.

3. How much does it cost to use the Writing Hub?

Our help is absolutely free if you are an IU student, faculty, or employee.

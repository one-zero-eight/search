## Zhakubayev Azat

International Information Technology University, Kazakhstan

Fall Semester 2022

## Знакомство с Иннополисом

I would like to start with the infrastructure of the city of Innopolis and the University. The city has everything you need: shops, cafes, bus stops, marketplaces with fast delivery, like Kazan Express. The Sports Center has everything you need: halls for practicing various sports, modern fitness equipment, a swimming pool with a jacuzzi, a hammam and a sauna. The city is surrounded by picturesque forests and the air here is very clean.

As for the University building, I was happy to find the reading hall and separate meeting rooms where you can study and prepare for classes. I liked the European-style dormitory, where everyone feels at home. The rooms have a study area, high-speed internet, a kitchen, a shower room and a TV.

The learning process was very interesting, each professor has their own individual approach to teaching. I liked the mutual help between students - there is a Study&Chat group in the Telegram, where you can ask questions, and everyone is ready to help.

![3_Azat.jpg](https://innopolis.university/upload/medialibrary/d85/akqn3pburbf6ohqvzggbbpn7qbij0ob2/3_Azat.jpg)

I studied Operating systems, Linux in particular, which is one of the popular and convenient operating systems for programming. I liked the format of the exams, as well as the fact that after each exam you can come to the appeal and find out about your mistakes. I would also like to express my gratitude to prof. Ramil Nasibulin, the instructor of the "Introduction to Optimization" course, who always found time for consultations on his course.

I am glad that I have spent the whole semester here. Thank you, Innopolis, for this great opportunity!

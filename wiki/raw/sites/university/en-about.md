## RESEARCH COMMERCIALIZATION

Innopolis University contains 21 research laboratories and 10 scientific centres, as well as holds partnership with 400 industrial IT companies.

Students of Innopolis University obtain cutting-edge education and take on independent research projects in IT and robotics. At the end of studies students implement an industrial project, which is counted as their graduation work.

## Industrial Robotics Development Center

The Center was opened in 2024 on the basis of Innopolis University with the support of the Russian Ministry of Industry and Trade as part of the federal project "Development of Industrial Robotics and Production Automation".

The center's objectives are the development of robotic solutions for industry, support for technological projects, technical and technological audits, educational and outreach activities, and coordination of a nationwide network of future industrial robotics development centers.

[Learn more](https://robotics-center.ru/)

## Olympiad activities and pre-University education

The university team built and tested a system of educational activities for students in grades 7—11 from learning basic skills to preparing for studying at an IT university.

The university holds annual international cybersecurity tournaments InnoCTF and InnoCTF Junior. Innopolis university is an academic partner or venue for competitions in Robotics and Programming.

Innopolis Open Olympiads bring together thousands of participants from Russia and other countries. The Olympiad is included in the list of the Russian Council of Olympiads for Schoolchildren approved by the Ministry of Science and Higher Education of the Russian Federation. Innopolis Open winners and prize-winners receive benefits when entering Russian universities.

## Faculty

## of Humanities and Social Sciences

Faculty of Humanities and Social Sciences is a multidisciplinary community of faculty and researchers whose teaching and research interests comprise philosophy, technological entrepreneurship, education and pedagogy, cross-cultural communication, communication for academic purposes, communication for business purposes, psychology and history.

We see the mission of the Faculty as contribution to the development of communication and critical thinking skills of the students, the mindset of curiosity, creativity, socially responsible innovation, and entrepreneurship - the skills we believe to be essential for the graduates’ employability and well-being.

Oksana Zhirosh, Institute of Humanities and Social Sciences Director

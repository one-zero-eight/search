## HOW TO APPLY

## APPLICATION FOR INCOMING STUDENTS

1. You are a student from our partner university:

- Contact the International Relations Office of your home university to nominate you.

- After the officical nomination you will receive an e-mail from us with further instructions for application.

- Make sure to meet the deadlines of the application that will be announced in the e-mail.

2. You are a student from a non-partner university:

- Please contact the International Relations Office of Innopolis University via email to: international@innopolis.university.

## USEFUL INFORMATION

![](https://innopolis.university/upload/landing/ef6/Монтажная_область_10@2x.png)

VISA

International Relations Office will provide you with the administrative support to successfully apply for your visa including issuing the necessary documents. Detailed information on the actual procedure is sent to the students via email after their application is successfully reviewed.

![](https://innopolis.university/upload/landing/105/Монтажная_область_12@2x.png)

TRAVELLING TO IU

Innopolis is located near Kazan, the capital of the Republic of Tatarstan. However, to make your arrival as smooth as possible, the International Relations Office of Innopolis University will gladly assist you in organizing transfer directly fr om the International Airport of Kazan to the building of the accommodation wh ere you are going to stay.

![](https://innopolis.university/upload/landing/e32/Монтажная_область_14@2x.png)

WELCOME WEEK

Upon your arrival, the International Relations Office of Innopolis University will conduct an orientation presentation that will equip you with the necessary information for a productive, joyful and fun time at Innopolis University.

![](https://innopolis.university/upload/landing/33c/Монтажная_область_11@2x.png)

HEALTH

Innopolis University has a medical center that is conveniently located within a walking distance from the University. However, please make sure that during your travel you have a valid in Russia medical insurance covering the period of your stay to be able to get the qualified medical help if needed.

![](https://innopolis.university/upload/landing/ff6/Монтажная_область_13@2x.png)

LIVING COSTS

Innopolis University offers its exchange students and interns a discount for the accommodation, meal plans, and also waives the fees for the usage of sport facilities. As of 2022, the approximate cost of accommodation is 35 Euros and average meal at the on-campus canteens (breakfast, lunch, and dinner) of 2 Euros.

![](https://innopolis.university/upload/landing/35b/Монтажная_область_15@2x.png)

AFTER EXCHANGE

Innopolis University will issue for you a transcript of records with the grades and courses taken at Innopolis University. Based on the transcript of records your home university will transfer the credits you earned at Innopolis University. We use European Credit Transfer System.

All in all, the process consists mostly of balancing the lectures, labs and assignments, which can be achieved through efficient time management and dedication.

## Nils Becker

University of Mannheim, Germany

Fall Semester 2021/22

## Знакомство с Иннополисом

Before arrival I hoped to gain contemporary insights especially into AI topics as they are of extreme importance in our today life. Apart from studies, I hoped to connect with Russian and international students to learn about different approaches towards life and new cultures.

In Innopolis I chose “Introduction to Practical Artificial Intelligence” and “Fundamentals of Robotics”. They are not related to any specific major. I was given the opportunity to select courses on my own based on my personal preferences.

In IPAI the lecture and the labs were mixed, so there were no designated lectures or labs in the schedule. Labs were integrated into the lectures to deepen the understanding of the currently taught topic. This resulted in a diversified learning procedure. Every week we had to do a home assignment on the topic we discussed during the week. The home assignments were coding projects. The focus didn’t lay on the way of implementation, but on the output. We were given a lot of freedom on our approaches to tasks, so we could develop our own strategies for solving. Every week the assignments of the previous week were shortly discussed in class, so everybody could learn from the others.

In FoR labs and lecture were divided. Depending on the topic, one or two lecture blocks were spent on a topic. While the lecture focused more on the theoretical basis of the topics, the labs aimed to teach the students the practical application of the theories. Accompanying the labs, we went to the garage in the basement to work on real industry robots, which I liked especially, because it provides a direct link between the taught material and the real-world application. Every week, a short quiz about the lecture material was conducted and a home assignment was due. In the home assignments we were asked to implement a concept of the weeks topic into a code and write a report for our implementation.

This was indeed my first time in Russia or any Russian speaking country. I was naïve to believe, that I could learn Russian within one semester before arrival, but luckily my lack of skill in the Russian language did not turn out to be a huge disadvantage as I was spending a lot of time with the other exchange students (all from Kazakhstan) or new Russian friends. My overall impression of the city of Innopolis is marvelous. I really enjoyed the sports complex but also the activities possible in this still small city. With my friends we visited the cinema and the numerous restaurants multiple times. Also, I often visited Kazan, which offered limitless opportunities to spend free time. In general, I will keep the Russian people as a warm-hearted and friendly towards strangers. For everybody planning to visit Innopolis in months later than October, I can only advise to bring a lot of warm clothes, because the low temperatures and strong winds will demand good cold resistance.

## Yaroslav Mustafin

International Information Technology University, Kazakhstan

Fall Semester 2021/22

## Знакомство с Иннополисом

I chose three courses. The program turned out to be quite intense. The Machine Learning course had very interesting tasks for building generative adversarial and convolutional neural networks. I have spent many hours figuring out how it works and have enjoyed it. At one of the exams, we were to pitch our startup to the team among the shareholders, played by the rest of the students of the group. My team and I won a second prize.

Dormitories at the University are very convenient. Students do not need to spend time on the road and the life is convenient. I liked the fact that every room has a bathroom and a kitchen. In the summer, the air conditioner saved us. Cleaning every 10 days is generally magic. Upon arrival home, I will also change bedding every 10 days, Innopolis taught me that. Fast Internet is very important for IT people. There are vending machines in case you want to buy something at night when the shops are closed. The access to the campus is 24/7. No need to worry about not being able to get in campus on time or having to explain why you are so late. However, the sound insulation could be improved. Innopolis University looks like something alien and futuristic. There is a lot of free space where you can work around the clock.

This is not my first time in Russia. I used to come here as a child, but to the southern part. Tatarstan for me turned out to be something different from the rest of Russia. Due to the similarities between the Kazakh and Tatar culture Tatarstan turned out to be closer to me.

Innopolis is a very modern city. The average age of residents is 31 years. Most of them have an IT education. There is no crime here, communication between the authorities and residents is via messengers, and, most importantly, people’s opinion matters. For example, when the dog walking area was opened, the residents were the ones to come up with the idea of its name.

Summing up: Innopolis has become my home for so many months, there is everything for a comfortable life, and in the future, passing by, I will definitely come here again :)

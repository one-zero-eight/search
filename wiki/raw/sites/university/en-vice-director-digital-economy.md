# Deputy Director for Digital Projects Development

![Бариев](https://innopolis.university/upload/landing/780/6b1yit4l00zhivsmnjluk38vdzgrc4kg/IMG_2299@2x.jpg)

[DIRECTOR](/raw/sites/university/en-team-director.md)

Pavlov Alexey Vladimirovich

Before joining Innopolis University in 2015, he worked on SAP implementations for Russian oil and gas companies. Currently he is Deputy Director for Digital Projects Development, responsible for establishing partnership relations and developing cooperation with Russian companies, technology transfer and implementation of research and development results created by the University's employees and technology centers.

During his work at Innopolis University, over 12.3 billion rubles were attracted for research, project and educational activities.

# Andrey Frolov

![](https://innopolis.university/upload/resize_cache/iblock/8ed/ogs1jtw3r0jay4efe1c9nmq7aj0kdg0v/330_420_2/Фролов%20для%20сайта.jpg)

Full Professor, Institute of Data Science and Artificial Intelligence, Head, Lab of Algorithmic and Numerical Methods in Mathematics and Artificial Intelligence

Doctor of Sciences, Russia, Kazan Federal University, Full Professor, Institute of Data Science and Artificial Intelligence, Head, Lab of Algorithmic and Numerical Methods in Mathematics and Artificial Intelligence In 2004 Andrey Frolov defended his PhD thesis at Novosibirsk State University (Russian Federation). In 2014 he defended thesis of Doctor of Science at Kazan Federal University (Russian Federation). From 2004 to 2017 he held various positions at Kazan Federal University. From 2018 to the present he has held the position of full professor at Innopolis University, and since 2025, the head of the laboratory. Research interests: Theoretical Computer Science, Algorithm therory, Mathematical and computer modeling.

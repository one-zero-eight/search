## Project-based activities

The university’s project-based activities are aimed at implementing grant-based and commercial projects, as well as at enhancing the availability of education in IT areas. Our university also implements the public-private partnership projects and collaborates with private customers in developing tailor-made engineering solutions.

## Grant-related activities

Over thirty fundamental and applied projects have been / are being implemented by the university in the areas of IT and robotics, together with the NTI Fund, Russian Science Foundation, Russian Foundation of Fundamental Research, Ministry of Science and Higher Education of Russia, Ministry of Digital Development, Communications and Mass Media, and other partners.

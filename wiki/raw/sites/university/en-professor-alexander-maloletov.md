# Alexander Maloletov

![](https://innopolis.university/upload/resize_cache/iblock/faf/480zokue15tachiy5cq4555rv9qo6slf/330_420_2/Александр%20джипег%20%281%29.jpg)

Doctor of Sciences degree in Physics and Mathematics, Volgograd State Technical University, Russia, Full Professor, Director of the Institute, Lab of Robotics and Computer Vision, Scientific Head of the Center for Technologies in Robotics and Mechatronics Components

Volgograd State Technical University, Russia

Alexander Maloletov is an expert in theoretical mechanics and robotics. He is the author of over 150 scientific publications, including 3 monographs, over 20 patents and over 40 software copyrights. He serves on a Dissertation Council and on the editorial boards of five scientific journals. In 2015, he was awarded the degree of Doctor of Physical and Mathematical Sciences with a specialization in "Theoretical Mechanics". As a research supervisor, he has mentored several students to the completion of their Ph.D. degrees. Alexander Maloletov has an extensive network within the Russian scientific community, keeps abreast of the latest advancements in modern global science, and is an active participant in leading international conferences and forums.

# Yegor Bugayenko

![](https://innopolis.university/upload/resize_cache/iblock/593/1ho62fi4khutspx9uzyae73nztioxlgk/330_420_2/Егор%20Б.jpg)

Professor of Practice, Institute of Software Development and Engineering, Lab of Software and Service Engineering

Dnipropetrovsk National University (DNU)

Yegor Bugayenko graduated from Dnipropetrovsk National University (DNU) in 1998 with a degree in Computer Systems and Networks. He has built a distinguished career in software development and AI. His early research focused on automating database system configuration. After founding Zerocracy (USA), he served as CTO until 2019, developing AI-driven systems for managing developer teams. Currently, he is the Director of the Development Tools Lab at Huawei and teaches software engineering and object-oriented programming.

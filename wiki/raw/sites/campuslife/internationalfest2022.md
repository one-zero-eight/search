# International Fest

> This page is from 2022: what it says may be out of date.

![](https://static.tildacdn.com/tild3366-6533-4839-b732-643563323137/i-20.jpg)

International Fest 2022

Explore the variety of cultures at Innopolis University!

Call to action

Fest Schedule, 24-27 March

![](https://static.tildacdn.com/tild6334-3639-4931-a138-386230393766/TASS_36996782_d_850.jpg)

International Round Table Discussion

This event is largely aimed at international students. Address your questions and concerns related to your life at Innopolis University and Russia. We will do our best to provide answers to all your questions. This meeting will have formal format, so it is advisable to wear formal clothes. Register [here](https://forms.gle/qDzUFfgVv2JWtGHy6) to ask your question and attend.

Thursday, 24

16:00-18:00

Room 425

![](https://static.tildacdn.com/tild3138-3638-4631-b532-333966366130/photo_2022-03-05_21-.jpg)

Presentation Karaoke

Join our event where participants perform impromptu presentations with no clue what the next slide will be! You can join as a presenter or just spectator! Text [Kamil](http://t.me/KamilSabbagh) if you want to try yourself on a stage with a random presentation!

Thursday, 24

18:30-20:30

Room 313

![](https://static.tildacdn.com/tild3134-3933-4236-a139-323663353538/International-Cuisin.jpg)

Cooking Show

Become part of the cooking show where you'll be randomly assigned to one of our eight head chefs from different countries and cook their national cuisine together! Each chef may have 3 people, so places are limited! Register [here](https://forms.gle/EwTBGfSqBthCSuGG7) by Wednesday.

Friday, 25

18:00-20:00

IU Kitchen

![](https://static.tildacdn.com/tild3261-3530-4431-a332-396539663135/RS277_113591519-hig.jpg)

International Fair: Airport Theme

International Fair will entail country presentations in free format: games, language lessons, quizzes, etc. We'll have country stations that will welcome tourists (you) to spend holidays together. You'll be getting tickets to "fly" to a certain country for 15 minutes and feel the spirit of respective country!

Saturday, 26

18:00-20:00

Reading Hall

![](https://static.tildacdn.com/tild3564-3732-4439-a566-303064326631/i-339.jpg)

Ethno Disco

International Fair will be followed by Ethno Disco! Send your music tracks to [Nastya](http://t.me/nastyaryabkova) to be played at the event! Please make sure they can relate to International Fest theme.

Saturday, 26

20:30-22:00

Reading Hall

![](https://static.tildacdn.com/tild3635-3035-4233-a435-613233666632/i-77.jpg)

Quiz

Register for the Quiz, which will have slightly new format: buzzer system will enable faster teams to provide answers first! Full rules will be given in participants' group. Max number of people per team: 5. Max number of teams: 8. Please [register](https://forms.gle/BCMszg6pSrHVG8q38) by Thursday. If you don't have a team, you can be drawn to one.

Sunday, 27

16:00-18:30

Room 313

![](https://static.tildacdn.com/tild3137-3636-4736-b832-353663656461/b808d2543f62acac96ce.png)

Holi Fest

Holi Fest is a celebration of colors where participants exchange with painted powder throws. This event will cover you in dry paint, so please wear clothes that are unlikely to re-use. Check out what [Holi Fest](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Hh-o5g4tLVE) is! Register [here](https://forms.gle/Sd7f4aNc7cWo7HZw5) by Thursday!

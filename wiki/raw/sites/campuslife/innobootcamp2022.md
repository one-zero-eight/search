# Иннобуткамп2022

> This page is from 2022: what it says may be out of date.

![](https://static.tildacdn.com/tild6663-6663-4661-b364-353763303630/noroot.png)

InnoBootCamp

IBC is a summer adaptation program for freshers,

which aims to provide exceptional hospitality for new students

and give them a taste of life at Innopolis University!

Call to action

![](https://static.tildacdn.com/tild6263-6261-4633-a133-363566376561/noroot.png)

What does the program

look like?

The program consists of numerous events,

which target academic, administrative, social,

extracurricular aspects of student life.

Freshers will attend classes in math, programming

and English language; take part in various out-of-class

activities and spend time with buddies - assigned

student mentors for a week period of the program.

![](https://static.tildacdn.com/tild6232-3537-4238-a264-656333343638/Bootcamp-22.jpg)

Who is buddy?

⭑⭑⭑⭑⭑

Buddy is your guide to the world of the Bootcamp and Innopolis.

This is your friend and mentor, to whom you can ask questions and who'll provide you with lifehacks during the Bootcamp.

You will be randomly assigned to groups, each group will have its own buddy.

![](https://static.tildacdn.com/tild3133-3865-4231-b764-383662633366/Bootcamp-2.jpg)

When should I come

to the BootCamp?

⭑⭑⭑⭑⭑

InnoBootCamp starts at the beginning of August. We will announce the dates of the BootCamp in advance. Intro meeting will be before the official start of the program, during this meeting you will get all information about the schedule of the program.

In case, if you're not able to attend the Intro meeting, we'll share its highlights in the channel.

![](https://static.tildacdn.com/tild6233-3630-4265-b835-633263653633/Bootcamp-132.jpg)

What if I arrive before/later start

of the program?

⭑⭑⭑⭑⭑

You can arrive much earlier. Accommodation costs before Innobootcamp are not covered, so you'll have to pay yourself.

If you arrive later, you can still join us, but we advise you to arrive on time not to miss anything valuable and useful for you.

![](https://static.tildacdn.com/tild6537-6535-4831-b865-623565313364/Bootcamp-288.jpg)

Where should I go when I arrive in Innopolis?

⭑⭑⭑⭑⭑

You need to check in at the University administration in dorm 3, 1st floor (Universitetskaya 1). Check-in time is 24/7.

You will have one week break between the Bootcamp and the start of your studies, during which you can stay or leave.

![](https://static.tildacdn.com/tild3463-6233-4932-b466-303430623534/Bootcamp-17.jpg)

What do I need to bring with me?

⭑⭑⭑⭑⭑

- personal laptop

- necessary stationery, pens, notebooks

- hygiene products

- dishes and utensils

In Innopolis we have stores and Ozon delivery to purchase what you need.

How to get to Innopolis?

University address: г. Иннополис, ул. Университетская, д. 1

You will have to make your own way to Innopolis

![](https://static.tildacdn.com/tild3932-3833-4333-b466-333936613866/img_5962.jpg)

From the train station

Taxi to Kazan city centre "Комбинат Здоровье" bus station. Then shuttle bus 108 service to Innopolis. Use a [help bot](https://t.me/InnoHelpBot) for schedules.

![](https://static.tildacdn.com/tild3035-6532-4665-a137-336565326233/noroot.png)

From the airport

[Train](https://aeroekspress.com/gorod/aeroport-kazan/#/) to Kazan. Then get to "Комбинат Здоровье" bus station and take [shuttle bus](https://t.me/InnoHelpBot) 108 service to Innopolis.

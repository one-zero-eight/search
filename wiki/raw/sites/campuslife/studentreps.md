# Student Reps

Student Representative Scheme

The Student Rep scheme is designed to promote student voice at Innopolis University. A student Rep is a student from any course who represents the views of their fellow groupmates.

Why should I become a Student Rep?

Becoming a student representative enables students to develop and strengthen leadership skills, connect with various internal agencies, assist fellow students with having their voice heard and gain appropriate rewards.

![](https://static.tildacdn.com/tild3631-3639-4230-b437-633565316563/ChatGPT_Image_Jul_20.png)

How to become a Student Rep?

- Participate in the Reps elections at the beginning of the 1st and the middle of the 2nd academic year*

- Get elected and attend student rep meetings

- Start getting to know your study group better!

* Russian groups' reps only elect once

![](https://static.tildacdn.com/tild3765-3332-4339-b163-306130663233/rep2.png)

Student Rep responsibilities

- Unite your study group and represent its interests communicating with teachers and IU administration

- Be attentive to the opinion of your study group, be able to listen and ask questions

- Share with your study group by important news and information that can be useful for study and life at Innopolis

![](https://static.tildacdn.com/tild3534-6239-4663-a337-653963653063/rep3.png)

About Our Methods

![](https://static.tildacdn.com/tild3637-3436-4639-b662-613831323664/rep5.png)

- Become part of active students that network with professionals at the University

- Contribute to decision making at IU

- Have a great understanding of university processes and systems

- Serve as liaison in bringing any issues/suggestions/feedback to university

- Deliver key messages from IU administration to the student body

- Support your student group and have your voice heard

- Suggest, develop and implement solutions related to campus life development

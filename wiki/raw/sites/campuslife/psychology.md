# Psychology Services

![](https://static.tildacdn.com/tild6637-3261-4830-b539-343066636461/IMG_6210.jpg)

Psychological Service

Feeling low sometimes is normal. Get free counselling help and psychological trainings at Innopolis University to be equipped for unexpected storms .

Book an appointment

If you feel you need help - don't hesitate to contact qualified professional at IU. We are here to help you stay on track.

Art Therapy

Want to discover more about yourself through arts? Relax and draw together in a friendly setting to find answers to deep questions.

Psychological Survey

Psychological comfort is an absolute necessity for efficient studying and productive work. Every year we conduct a survey that helps to see the level of student psychological comfort at Innopolis University.

Psychological Trainings

Life experience can be painful sometimes. Now you have free opportunity to get essential knowledge to avoid harmful decisions in your life. Contact our psychologist to find out about upcoming events.

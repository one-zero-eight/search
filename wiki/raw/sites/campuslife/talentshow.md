TALENT SHOW

Everyone can express themselves and show a creative performance. Below you can find available nominations. For each nomination we'll have a winner!

Register your performance by 12 November.

Event date: 26 of November, Popov Technopark

[Apply](https://forms.yandex.com/u/6537d3b9068ff0da0d2c0377/)

![](https://static.tildacdn.com/tild6362-6263-4338-b337-626238323433/i-395.jpg)

TALENT SHOW NOMINATIONS

This nomination includes performing a song. It may be a cover song or your own composition: folk singing, academic, pop, jazz singing, author's song, rap, etc. The performance should last no longer than 3.40 minutes

This nomination includes three sub-nominations:

1) Playing instruments. Solo and group performances should be no longer than 3 minutes. The choice of material and the quality of performance are evaluated

2) Playing instruments + vocal. The performance should last no longer than 3.40 minutes. The vocal, choice of material and the quality of performance are evaluated.

3) DJing. Show us the DJ set on the organiser's equipment. Play for 5 - 6 minutes. The choice of material, quality of DJing theory, good construction of DJ set, musical style, artistry are evaluated

This nomination is for all lovers of choreography. Show how cool you can move on the stage. Perform solo or in a collective in Street dance, Folk, Pop (Estrada), Contemporary, Ballroom and sports dance or other genre. The performance should last no more than 3.45 minutes

Show your creative skills on stage in the "Тheatre" nomonation. It includes theatrical performance, stand up, poem, miniature. The performance should be between 1.5 minutes and 10 minutes.

All the most unusual things you can show here. Juggling, tricks, clownery, pantomime and much more. Or do you have an idea to create a cartoon or original video - welcome. The performance should last no more than 3.45 minutes

Apply and surprise everyone!

ART EXHIBITION

Students and employees, everyone who is fond of arts and crafts, we invite you to take part in Innopolis Art Exhibition.

It can be paintings, photos, paper crafts, beadwork, knitting, embroidery, and much more.

The exhibition will be held from 20 - 26 November.

[Apply](https://forms.yandex.com/u/6537daef505690dbedbf9ca6/)

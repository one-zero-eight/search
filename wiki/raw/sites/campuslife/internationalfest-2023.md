# International Fest 2023

> This page is from 2023: what it says may be out of date.

![](https://static.tildacdn.com/tild3334-3762-4661-b261-373533653834/i-6__.jpg)

Explore the variety of cultures at Innopolis University!

Fest Schedule, 3-5 March

![](https://static.tildacdn.com/tild3361-6631-4930-b365-616433376661/photo_2022-03-21_18-.jpg)

Round Table Discussion

This event is largely aimed at international students. Address your questions and concerns related to your life at Innopolis University and Russia. We will do our best to provide answers to all your questions. Register [here](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/1WdXZuGn95knXxs9zfG9wHL1fa2vNMGwTqVfNqUwIXMM/edit) to ask your question and attend.

3 March

18:00 – 19:30

Room 425

![](https://static.tildacdn.com/tild3861-3632-4131-b430-383839613534/noroot.png)

Quiz

Register your team to take part in the International Festival Quiz prepared by the [IU Quiz Club](https://t.me/quiz_iu) and their friends from outside the university!

Topics:Culture, Culinary, Places, Famous Persons, Music..

Max of 6 people per team, 8 teams max.

Register [here](https://forms.gle/h4xiYEt3Q2XHRQqP9) by 28 February 23:59.

If you don't have a team, you can be drawn to one.

3 March

19:30 – 20:30

Main Hall

1st floor

![](https://static.tildacdn.com/tild3535-3863-4164-a436-303961656365/i-17.jpg)

Language exchange

Language Exchange is a place where you can visit different tables dedicated to a language, learn new words or just communicate!

If you are willing to share your knowledge and give a foreign language workshop, then fill in the form [here](https://forms.yandex.ru/u/63f5ce325d2a0606f575e231/)

4 March

13:00 – 14:30

Reading hall

3d floor

![](https://static.tildacdn.com/tild3964-3734-4835-b064-663230366330/i-247.jpg)

Grand Concert

Show a creative performance (song, dance, poem or presentation) showcasing the culture and traditions of your home country or a country you love.

Fill in the form [here](https://forms.yandex.ru/u/63f36cb5f47e73bef38c802a/) to take part.

And of course, you are very welcome to come as a spectator to the event!

4 March

18:00 – 19:30

Artspace

![](https://static.tildacdn.com/tild3662-3330-4830-b263-633134636333/E57A0486.JPG)

Disco

Grand Concert will be followed by Ethno Disco! Send your music tracks [here](https://forms.gle/cqTBrwW92TViV32B7) to be played at the event! Please make sure they can relate to International Fest theme.

4 March

19:30 – 22:00

Artspace

![](https://static.tildacdn.com/tild3266-3431-4438-b862-396534653730/photo_2023-02-23_15-.jpg)

Pakistani Breakfast

Become part of the our tradition of having an International breakfast.

This time the students from Pakistan will cook for your their traditional meals that they eat for breakfast.

If you wish to help with organization, please register [here](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/1LTi0KM2nu8b6LHXPGxjXW0OjGbwsfq-lqVkk5php-5I/edit)

If you're up only for food, pleased register [here](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/1yMBJQ1CEO3mOroXtOUbR3YcVa-Az2N9MsVa9UNON-Rk/edit)

5 March

11:00 - 13:00

Canteen 2nd floor

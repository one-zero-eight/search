# International Project school on self-driving Vehicles

[Вернуться](/raw/sites/robotics/activities.md)

06.04.2019

![](https://university.innopolis.ru/en/research/self-driven-school/static/img/bg.png)

The International Project School on Unmanned Vehicles offers you an ideal way to get a great practice in the unique direction of robotics – autonomous transport vehicles.

## Who?

The Project school is aimed to get together researchers and engineers from industry and academia as well as enthusiasts in sphere of mobile robots.

## What?

The Project School offers an excellent platform for researchers and engineers from all over the world to present and exchange their research ideas, to receive feedback from senior members of the self-driven cars community, in academia and industry, on current research and future developments in the research area. It provides a valuable opportunity to establish contacts and R&D cooperation between young researchers, professors and industry experts.

![](https://university.innopolis.ru/en/research/self-driven-school/static/img/64.jpg)

## How?

Project school will include: educational program and a competition between 5 groups of students under the guidance of researchers at the Autonomous Transportation Systems Lab, Innopolis University and external experts.

The main goal is to develop modern and effective approaches for self-driven cars, their navigation and services.

7 Days of a great practice and work in team. The number of participants is limited (about 30).

The working language of the Project School is English.

## Technical requirements

We are expecting that participants will come with their own laptops, please contact organizers before if you will not be able to bring a laptop with you. The requirements for pre-installed software: Ubuntu 16.04, ROS (Kinetic), Unity 3D, Apollo auto (version 3.0).

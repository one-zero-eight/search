# International Conference “Nonlinearity, Information and Robotics”

[Вернуться](/raw/sites/robotics/activities.md)

22.05.2020

The International Conference “Nonlinearity, Information and Robotics” is orientated towards academic and engineering experts in the multidisciplinary areas of Nonlinear sytems, Information technologies and Robotics with emphasis on both theory and applications.

Prospective authors are invited to submit their papers on the topics.

Submissions

All papers submitted to NIR 2020 must be in English. All contributions must not have been previously published, must not essentially duplicate already published material, and may not be simultaneously considered for publication elsewhere. To ensure high scientific quality, all papers will be reviewed by the Scientific Committee Members.

More information: [http://nir.innopolis.university/](http://nir.innopolis.university/)

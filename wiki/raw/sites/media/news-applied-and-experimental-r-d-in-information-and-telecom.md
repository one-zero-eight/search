# Applied and Experimental R&D in Information and Telecommunication Systems Conference in Zelenograd

> Страница относится к 2014 году: сведения на ней могли устареть.

4 декабря 2014

Conference took place at the National Research University of Electronic Technology (MIET) on December 2-3

[The Ministry of Education and Science of the Russian Federation](http://xn--80abucjiibhv9a.xn--p1ai/) organized a conference “Applied and Experimental R&D in Information and Telecommunication Systems” within the Federal Program framework “Research and developments in priority areas of science and technology of Russian Federation for 2014 – 2020”. The conference took place at the [National Research University of Electronic Technology (MIET)](http://eng.miet.ru/) in Zelenograd on December 2-3. Professor Evgeni Magid presented a report “Locomotion control systems of anthropomorphic robots” in “Technology of information, control, navigation systems” section of the conference.

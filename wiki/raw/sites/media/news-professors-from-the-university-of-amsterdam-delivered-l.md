# Professors from the University of Amsterdam delivered lectures at Innopolis University

> Страница относится к 2015 году: сведения на ней могли устареть.

11 сентября 2015

Faculty members of the Dutch partner university will deliver the full course of lectures in Computer Architecture during the current academic year in the framework of the cooperation agreement between the universities.

![](https://media.innopolis.university/upload/iblock/972/16767909365ba6df59c4.jpg)

[Taco Walstra](https://staff.fnwi.uva.nl/t.r.walstra/) and [Edwin Steffens](http://www.uva.nl/en/about-the-uva/organisation/staff-members/content/s/t/e.h.steffens/e.h.steffens.html), professors from one of the top European universities delivered lectures in Computer Architecture to Master’s students of Innopolis University as part of cooperation with the University of Amsterdam.

Apart from inclusion of selected courses of the Dutch university in the curriculum of the Russian IT-University, academic partners collaborate in realization of the Master’s degree program in Secure systems and network engineering.

Reference:

[University of Amsterdam](http://www.uva.nl/en/home) is a [partner](http://university.innopolis.ru/en/global/) of Innopolis University. The Dutch institution is among the world’s top-50 universities (QS World university rankings, 2014/2015). The [SNE program](http://gss.uva.nl/future-msc-students/information-sciences/content35/system-and-network-engineering.html) is recognized as the top rated program and the best Dutch Master’s in the category “Informatics” (Keuzegids Masters 2015).

# Innothon

> Страница относится к 2022 году: сведения на ней могли устареть.

[Мероприятия](/raw/sites/media/events.md)

23 апреля 2022

![](https://media.innopolis.university/upload/iblock/2a4/zyz23yn8af02lxfvk33o4uruxoxwirn3/photo_2022-04-12_10-21-35.jpg)

Innothon is like a usual hackathon, but the main task is to generate ideas, not code. Experienced mentors will help student teams to be able to bring their ideas to the stage of presentation to the expert jury. With the support of Innopolis Startup Studio, the best projects will be able to apply for grant programs.

Foreign students can participate, but the leader of the team must be a citizen of the Russian Federation (to get grants)

[Register here](http://docs.google.com/forms/d/1fJWpPT8vkjfXU2f-Wd15ovu48QXzpU_azEefnNyp4TI) before the 20th of April, 23:59! You can find your team [here](http://t.me/+5-pGtdQb4CRjY2Y6).

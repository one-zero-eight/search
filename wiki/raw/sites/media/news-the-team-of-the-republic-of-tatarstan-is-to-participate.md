# The team of the Republic of Tatarstan is to participate in the International Olympiad in Informatics (IOI) 2016

> Страница относится к 2016 году: сведения на ней могли устареть.

7 марта 2015

Members of the team are trained at Innopolis University.

![](https://media.innopolis.university/upload/iblock/a19/photo_2015-03-07_12-15-17.jpg)

The 9th IOI will take place in Moscow on March 6-8. Participation in this competition is included in the action plan for preparation for the IOI 2016 which will be held in Tatarstan.

“The IOI 2016 roadmap foresees weekly lessons, study camps etc. It is important that schoolchildren participate in different competitions. Firstly, so they will get used to the spirit of competition that they will certainly experience during the IOI 2016. Secondly, they will be able to estimate their own strengths and weaknesses; therefore, they will know what they should emphasize in the future training for the international contest,” – as Sergey Masyagin, Vice-Provost for Pre-University Education, states.

Yesterday, the team registered for the competition and had time to have a tour around Moscow. We wish them good luck. Fingers crossed!

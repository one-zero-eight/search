# Collaboration of Innopolis University and National University of Singapore

> Страница относится к 2014 году: сведения на ней могли устареть.

16 сентября 2014

On September 10, 2014, Innopolis University and the National University of Singapore signed a collaboration agreement in the area of education and science. The event took place during the 5th meeting of high-level Russia-Singapore intergovernmental commission.

![](https://media.innopolis.university/upload/iblock/e7a/e7aea2a9a2dcefc60b42ba70551e1063.jpg)

The agreement was signed in the presence of Russia’s First Deputy Prime Minister Igor Shuvalov, Minister of Communications and Mass Media Nikolai Nikiforov, President of the Republic of Tatarstan Rustam Minnikhanov, Finance Minister of Singapore Tarman Shanmugaratnam, Minister of Culture and Youth Lawrence Wong and others.

# Innopolis University and Huawei established a joint educational program

> Страница относится к 2015 году: сведения на ней могли устареть.

9 июня 2015

The best students of Innopolis University received the vouchers to study at the Huawei headquarters in Shanghai.

![](https://media.innopolis.university/upload/iblock/b27/IMAG0557.jpg)

Education will be conducted on the base of the «Huawei Seeds for the Future» program. In the framework of this program, Huawei cooperates with more than 100 Universities from 35 countries. The program has been being offered in Russia since 2012.

«We are delighted that our students will have an opportunity to be trained at one of the biggest telecommunication companies in the world. I think that such experience will become an excellent platform for the further development of our students and shall shape them as professionals in IT», - as mentioned by Alexander Tormasov, rector of Innopolis University.

Huawei traditionally pays much attention to educational programs because the company believes that this has positive influence on the development of the IT sphere.

# Innopolis University Professor Evgeni Magid gave a talk at the Yamabico Symposium

> Страница относится к 2015 году: сведения на ней могли устареть.

3 марта 2015

The event took place at the National Olympics Memorial Youth Center in Tokyo.

![](https://media.innopolis.university/upload/iblock/94d/yamabico.jpg)

The 3-days [symposium](http://www.roboken.iit.tsukuba.ac.jp/event/Sympo/14-3/EnglishPage/) gathered about 100 researchers in the Japanese capital. Yamabico Symposium serves as a discussion platform for students and young researchers from Japan and all over the world where they present research results and share their views on the future of robotics. It was a great opportunity for information exchange in such areas as mobile robotics, vision, sensors and manipulators. Professor Evgeni Magid, Head of the [Laboratory of Intelligent Robotic Systems (LIRS)](http://university.innopolis.ru/en/research/robolab/) from Innopolis University, made a presentation titled "Development of static and dynamic balance based control algorithms for anthropomorphic bipedal robot locomotion: state-of-art and trends". Professor presented the current state of LIRS’s research on development of energy efficient locomotion control. For this research LIRS team uses the Russian robot AR-601M.

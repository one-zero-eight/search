# 25 teams will represent the Russia at the World Robot Olympiad 2015

> Страница относится к 2015 году: сведения на ней могли устареть.

3 ноября 2015

Winners of the Russian Robot Olympiad and the Federal Training Boot Camp hosted by Innopolis University will go to Qatar.

![](https://media.innopolis.university/upload/iblock/13d/photo329289400669284819.jpg)

The finals of the World Robot Olympiad 2015 (WRO) will take place on November 6 – 8, 2015 in Doha (Qatar). The theme for this year’s competition is Robots-Explorers. The Russian Federation will be represented by 25 teams comprising school and university students from 10 regions: the Chelyabinsk Region, Moscow, Saint Petersburg, the Sverdlovsk Region, the Nizhny Novgorod Region, the Republic of Tatarstan, the Lipetsk Region, the Murmansk Region, the Novosibirsk Region and the Krasnoyarsk Region. The Russian Federation is a WRO member with the largest quota of the number of teams and uses this privilege in full. It is noteworthy that the team from the Lipetsk Region takes part in the World Robot Olympiad for the first time, while the teams from Moscow and Saint Petersburg are permanent WRO participants. This year the majority of teams attending the international finals are from the Chelyabinsk Region.

At the WRO 2015, the teams will compete in the following categories:

- Regular Category

a. Pearl Diving – 4 teams

b. Treasure Hunt – 4 teams

c. Mountaineering – 4 teams

- University Regular Category

a. WRO Bowling – 2 teams

- WRO Gen II Football

a. WRO Gen II Football – 5 teams

- Open Category – 6 teams

The Russian national team consists of the teams that passed the regional and national tournaments of the Olympiad and had good scores at the Federal Training Boot Camp, which in fact is the selection finals. The youngest member of the national team is 10 years old. The oldest one is 20. The international finals will welcome 6 Russian experts to join the judge committee, including 3 members representing Innopolis city.

Sergey Masyagin, Vice-Rector, Head of Department for Student Affairs and Admission of Innopolis University said: “Every year the Russian Federation acts as a strong competitor to other countries. 2012 was the breaking point for the national team. Starting from then, the Russian national team has been the top-leader, prizewinner and leader of the team rating.

Traditionally, one of the strongest points of the national team is the WRO Gen II Football where our team has been among the top three leaders for 3 years in a row. Besides, in the Open Category our projects are honored with prizes and special nominations marking a creative approach and a complex technical design. This year we are ready to struggle for the top places in the Open Category

Facts

- Russian Federation joined the WRO competition from the very beginning.

- Russian Federation takes part in the WRO for the 12th time.

- Russian Federation is one of major WRO member countries, considering the number of teams taking part in the local stage of the competition.

- Republic of Tatarstan participates in the WRO finals for the second time.

- Russian national team includes the members who have been taking part in the WRO more than 7 years. Having started at school, now they are university students. The national team includes the previous WRO champions as well as the first-time participants.

- Russian national team includes 6 girls, who take part in all the categories, including the WRO Gen II Football.

Innopolis University is the operator of the Russian stage of the World Robot Olympiad (WRO) since 2014. It is a new Russian IT University focused on the education and scientific research in the field of IT and Robotics which is aimed at training of high-quality IT experts and development and commercialization of break-through technologies in order to facilitate the advancement of the domestic IT industry. Innopolis University collaborates with the world’s leading universities and research institutions in order to integrate the best Russian traditions and global practices, establish and develop a high-class education and research system and comply with the international academic standards. The faculty of Innopolis University comprises representatives of reputed research schools and universities of Russia, Switzerland, China, Canada, Italy, Sweden, Slovenia and the Republic of Korea.

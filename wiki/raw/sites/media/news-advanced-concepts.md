# LIRS members manuscript was accepted for International Conference on Advanced Concepts for Intelligent Vision Systems (ACIVS 2015)

> Страница относится к 2015 году: сведения на ней могли устареть.

1 октября 2015

The event is to be held in Catania, Italy on October 26-29, 2015

![](https://media.innopolis.university/upload/iblock/f1f/catania-kathedrale-hl-agatha-11032009-26889.jpg)

[International Conference on Advanced Concepts for Intelligent Vision Systems (ACIVS 2015)](http://acivs.org/acivs2015/) is focusing on techniques for building adaptive, intelligent, safe and secure imaging systems.

[ACIVS 2015](http://acivs.org/acivs2015/) consists of lecture sessions, poster sessions and special session on large scale video processing and analysis.

Members of Intelligent Robotic Systems Laboratory Ilya Afanasyev, Artur Sagitov and Evgeni Magid prepared a research paper «ROS-based SLAM for a Gazebo-simulated mobile robot in image-based 3D model of indoor environment», which will be presented at the Conference in October 2015. The manuscript will be indexed in [Scopus](http://www.scopus.com/) citation database of peer-reviewed literature. Abstract of the manuscript is available [here](http://acivs.org/acivs2015/AbstractList.php#186).

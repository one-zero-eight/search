# Students of the Innopolis University school placed second in the “Chisty Vzglyad” open republican competition

> Страница относится к 2015 году: сведения на ней могли устареть.

29 сентября 2015

Social and environmental advertising was the main topic of the event.

![](https://media.innopolis.university/upload/iblock/344/_MG_60581.jpg)

The awarding ceremony for the winners of the “Chisty Vzglyad” (Clear Vision) competition took place in Innopolis, a new Russian city. It is the fourth time when the event supported by the Ministry of Ecology and Natural Recourses of the Republic of Tatarstan gathered people concerned about environmental problems of our planet. The purpose of the competition is to introduce the community to environmental protection issues, provide the young generation with the opportunity to show their attitude to environmental problems, suggest solutions and make its own contribution to the development of social advertising in Russia. More than a thousand of video materials, photos and pieces of art from 36 subjects of the Russian Federation were submitted to the competition this year. Authors of the best works received awards at the official ceremony that took place at A.S. Popov Technological Park.

Students of the Innopolis University school, Alfia Karimova, Dzhamil Mukhutdinov and Bogdan Yuranets placed second in the “Social Video Clip” nomination. Their project named “Garbage has Home” was devoted to the lifecycle of aluminum tins and emphasized the need for further wastes treatment.

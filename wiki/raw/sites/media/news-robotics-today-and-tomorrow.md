# Professor Evgeni Magid gave a lecture «Robotics today and tomorrow»

> Страница относится к 2015 году: сведения на ней могли устареть.

17 августа 2015

The lecture was organized for the participants of the boot camp for Robot Olympiad 2015 in Qatar

![](https://media.innopolis.university/upload/iblock/6e8/1z.jpg)

During August 10-20, the university hosted [Federal boot camp](http://robolymp.ru/events/uchebno-trenirovochnye-sbory-sbornoy-rf/) for [the World Robot Olympiad 2015](http://www.wro2015.org/). [The boot camp](http://robolymp.ru/events/uchebno-trenirovochnye-sbory-sbornoy-rf/) is a final preliminary and selective event, where the strongest teams will be selected for [the World Robot Olympiad 2015](http://www.wro2015.org/) in Doha, Qatar. More than 150 children from 18 regions of the Russian Federation took part in the event.

Professor of Intelligent Robotic Systems Laboratory (LIRS) Evgeni Magid delivered a lecture “Robotics today and tomorrow” for the boot camp participants on August 17, 2015. Evgeni talked about the increasingly important role of the robots nowadays and the prospects of this sphere. He presented the most famous of the currently existing robots that are able to replace human in DDD-jobs (dirty, dangerous, and demanding). Before the lecture the children visited LIRS and met our anthropomorphic robot AR-601M designed by “[Android Technics](http://en.npo-at.com/)” in Magnitogorsk.

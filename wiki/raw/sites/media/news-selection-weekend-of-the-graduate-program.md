# Selection Weekend of the Graduate Program

> Страница относится к 2014 году: сведения на ней могли устареть.

25 августа 2014

From 9 to 11 of August Innopolis University held a selection weekend for its graduate program “IT – software engineering management”. Some top IT companies – Fix group, Etton, KamAZ, IQ-300, ICL, Digital Loyalty System, RT-Inform participated in the selection.

This year the master program has applicants not only from Russia, but also from Kazakhstan. During three selection days they had tests for basic knowledge in computer science, had an interview with a psychologist and interviews with IU's partners from the industry.

The final stage of the weekend included an interview with Alexander Tormasov and online meeting with the partner university. Applicants were rated at each stage of the selection process.

# Innopolis University supported the Education for All charity program

> Страница относится к 2015 году: сведения на ней могли устареть.

5 марта 2015

A master class on robotics for children from the DetskieDomiki fund took place at Innopolis STEM center.

![](https://media.innopolis.university/upload/iblock/817/IMG_2164.JPG)

Konstantin Burdinov, STEM senior lecturer, conducted the master class.

During this charity lesson, children built up their first robot and learned basics of software engineering. Moreover, children evaluated abilities of the robot equipped with different sensors. The most important thing is that children learned how fascinating the study of robotics actually is. It involves maths, physics and software engineering. These subjects are the basis for the training of high-tech specialists.

# Team led by Innopolis University student is among the TOP-10 rating of the international JacobsHack

> Страница относится к 2015 году: сведения на ней могли устареть.

27 октября 2015

The event took place in Bremen, Germany, at Jacobs University Bremen.

![](https://media.innopolis.university/upload/iblock/fd0/photo344707362599315756.jpg)

“I hack, therefore I am!”, - under this slogan the [JacobsHack](https://2015f.jacobshack.com/), probably the largest international hackathon in Europe, was opened for the third time. In 2015, Jacobs University Bremen welcomed students from over 30 countries of the world who gathered to work on the project for 19 hours.

This year the event organizers gave fifty teams 24 hours for development of concepts of their IT projects. The team led by Innopolis University student Dumitry Savva comprised members from India, Poland and Germany. The group developed a prototype of the PlaceVentura social game.

The task of a player searching through the online urban space is to find sites encrypted in the task. “By the end of the competition, our team developed the working MPV with 99% code coverage. We used the following technologies: Vagrant, Git, Django, Postgres, HTML5, GeoLocation and Flex Box, including Droplet and DigitalOcean as the server”, - shared Dumitry Savva, student of Innopolis University.

The application is deeply integrated with a social network account in where a user can see the full range of quests. By pressing the quest button, the player will open the page showing detailed description of the quest. If the user has solved the quest and came to the right place, the server would check his or her geolocation after the corresponding button is pressed. If there is matching, the player will see the encouraging message “Good Job!”, - said Dumitry Savva. ​

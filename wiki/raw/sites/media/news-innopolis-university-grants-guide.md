Information about competitions and grants in IT and Robotics

![](https://media.innopolis.university/upload/iblock/829/kDBtWW_4_aY.jpg)

## Samsung Advanced Institute of Technology

### [Samsung Global Research Outreach (GRO) Program](http://www.sait.samsung.co.kr/saithome/Page.do?method=main&pagePath=01_about/&pageName=gro_overview)

- Proposal submission deadline June 1, 2015.

- Project support amount up to 100,000 USD.

- Project term 1 - 3 years.

## Perspective Research Foundation (Russian analogue of DARPA)

### [Competition on Information Search Technologies on the Internet](http://fpi.gov.ru/activities/ideas/search)

- Proposal submission deadline will be determined by the Committee.

- Project support amount up to 20,000,000 RUB per year.

- Project term up to 2 years.

## Federal Targeted Program

### [Scientific Research in Quantum Technologies and Photonics, Robotics, Composite Materials in Partnership with Swiss Scientific Research Institutions](http://2014.fcpir.ru/participation_in_program/contests/list_of_contests/1_published/2016-14-585-0001/)

- Proposal submission deadline June 5, 2015.

- Project support amount for Russian participant 3,000,000 RUB per year.

- Project term 2016-2018.

### [Selection of Research Projects in Priority Areas in Partnership with French Scientific Research Institutions and Universities](http://2014.fcpir.ru/participation_in_program/contests/list_of_contests/1_published/2015-14-588-0001/)

- Proposal submission deadline June 11, 2015.

- Project support amount for Russian participant 5,000,000 RUB per year.

- Project term 2016-2018.

## Russian Foundation for Basic Research (RFFR)

### [Project Competition on Organization of Russian-British Seminars for Young Scientists on the Territory of Russia conducted by RFBR and the Royal Society of London in Partnership with the British Council](http://www.rfbr.ru/rffi/ru/international_announcement/o_1931267)

- Proposal submission deadline July 20, 2015.

- Project support amount up to 1,500,000 RUB.

- Project term November 2015 – March 2016.

### [Initiative Scientific Project Competition 2016 directed by the Russian Foundation for Basic Research and Vietnam Academy of Science and Technology](http://www.rfbr.ru/rffi/ru/international_announcement/o_1930844)

- Proposal submission deadline May 22, 2015.

- Project support amount up to 1,000,000 RUB per year.

- Project term 2016-2017.

### [Project Competition on Oriented Fundamental Interdisciplinary Research 2015 (New additional topics 506, 507)](http://www.rfbr.ru/rffi/ru/contests_announcement/o_1930132)

Topic 506. Fundamental issues of information technologies in arctic space monitoring.

Topic 507. Fundamental issues of the distributed cloud computing organization in solving large-scale scientific problems.

- Proposal submission deadline May 25, 2015.

- Project support amount 5,000,000 RUB per year.

- Project term 2015-2017.

### [Initiative Scientific Project Competition 2016 directed by the Russian Foundation for Basic Research and Austrian Science Fund (FWF)](http://www.rfbr.ru/rffi/ru/international_announcement/o_1930312)

- Proposal submission deadline February 1, 2016. Proposal will be reviewed upon its receipt.

- Project support amount up to 1,000,000 RUB per year.

- Project term 2016-2018.

### [Interdisciplinary Fundamental Research Project Competition 2016 directed by the Russian Foundation for Basic Research and National Natural Science Research Foundation of China (NSFC)](http://www.rfbr.ru/rffi/ru/international_announcement/o_1923911)

- Proposal submission deadline June 1, 2015.

- Project support amount up to 1,500,000 RUB per year.

- Project term 2016-2018.

### [Initiative Scientific Project Competition 2016 directed by the Russian Foundation for Basic Research and Ministry of Science and Technology of Taiwan](http://www.rfbr.ru/rffi/ru/international_announcement/o_1923741)

- Proposal submission deadline July 15, 2015.

- Project support amount up to 1,000,000 RUB per year.

- Project term 2016-2018.

### [Initiative Scientific Project Competition directed by the Russian Foundation for Basic Research and the German Research Foundation (DFG)](http://www.rfbr.ru/rffi/ru/international_announcement/o_1895583)

- Proposal submission deadline December 31, 2015. Proposal will be reviewed upon its receipt.

- Project support amount up to 1,500,000 RUB per year.

- Project term 2016-2018.

### «Innopraktika», Joint institute for nuclear research (JINR, Dubna), European Organization for Nuclear Research (CERN, Geneva)

[International School on IT and GRID Technologies for young scientists](http://innopraktika.ru/news/481/?sphrase_id=2747)

- Selection for participation in the international school is conducted in two stages:

- You need to send a [completed application](https://yadi.sk/i/v_9eB7XSgMQA7) form in English to grants manager of Innopolis University by e-mail: a.pavlov@innopolis.ru until 10 June, 2015.

- According to the results of internal selection the composition of potential participants in international school will be determined, which until 15 June, 2015 will take part in the selection organized by "Innopraktika"

- Target group: students, graduate students and young scientists

- Venue: Tivat, Montenegro

- Date of the event: September 28 - October 2, 2015

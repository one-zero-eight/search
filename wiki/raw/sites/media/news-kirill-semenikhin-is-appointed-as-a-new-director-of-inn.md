# Kirill Semenikhin is appointed as a new Director of Innopolis University

> Страница относится к 2015 году: сведения на ней могли устареть.

1 октября 2015

Kirill Semenikhin, Regional Director, member of the Board of Directors of Microsoft Russia who devoted 11 years to the company, has become the head of the IT University.

![](https://media.innopolis.university/upload/iblock/5ef/SHI_3054_3.jpg)

Innopolis University, a Russian university that was established in December 2012 and has already made a name recognized by leading domestic universities, has welcomed a new CEO. Kirill Semenikhin, the former Regional Director of Microsoft Russia has taken over Dmitry Kondratyev, who used to be the head of the educational institution since its establishment and now has become a member of the Board of Trustees. Kirill has extensive expertise in project implementation in the field of information and communication technologies as well as comprehensive international experience in cooperation with the key business sectors. As Director of Innopolis University, Kirill is to deal with sponsors and university partners along with coordination of business and administrative issues of the university.

Open competition for the vacant position of a Director was announced on September 9th. More than 20 applications were submitted during 12 review days. The Attestation Board consisting of top-managers and representatives of the university faculty members had selected two candidates. Each of them prepared a strategy and a vision of the future growth of the university and presented a three-year work plan. The new director was approved on September 25th at the session of the [Board](http://university.innopolis.ru/about/board) of Trustees in accordance with the voting results.

Dmitry Kondratyev: “Both candidates were strong. I have learned some new interesting ideas on the options of the university development from their reports. It is important to notice, that the candidates were oriented towards the current model of a modern world-class university, and the Board of Trustees encouraged this. Kirill’s arguments for the future development of the existing model properly correlated with the prospects for fundraising. I voted for Kirill because I saw he was ready to assume responsibility for this project, which has become so valuable and important for me. I realized that Kirill is ready to use the state-of-the-art educational technologies and care about students and employees. I believe that together with the best team Kirill will accomplish most challenging tasks using creative approaches».

Kirill Semenikhin: “It is a great privilege for me to become a part of this strong and break-through team, lead the group of young professionals, and pursue the work successfully started by Dmitry. We all are to build an efficient system of interaction between business and science in order to fulfil the related to the establishment of the best Russian IT University, integrated in the international academic environment, and train high-quality in-demand IT experts so as to make a significant contribution to the quality breakthrough of the domestic IT industry on the global scale. Understanding the needs, tasks and strategies of the market development from the viewpoint of industry, I am going to enhance the engagement of employers and consumers of high technologies in education and research”

Reference

Innopolis University is a new Russian university focused on education and research in the field of IT and robotics. University has proved to be a platform for effective interaction between science, education and business due to its original commitment to the needs of IT industry. University was established by means of donations and sponsorship support without any budgetary financing.

Under guidance of Dmitry Kondratyev, Innopolis University developed from an idea and a leased office to an ultra-modern campus in the city of high technologies. Today the University hosts about 500 employees and students majoring in IT. It has made three admission sessions for undergraduate and graduate programs, created state-of-the-art scientific laboratories and attracted faculty members from prestigious educational and research institutions in order to implement academic programs, and made a name recognized by the leading Russian universities.

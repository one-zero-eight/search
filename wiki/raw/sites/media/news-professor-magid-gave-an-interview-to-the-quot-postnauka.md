# Professor Magid gave an interview to the "PostNauka" online magazine about Anthropomorphic Robots

> Страница относится к 2015 году: сведения на ней могли устареть.

10 апреля 2015

Anthropomorphic robotics and its prospects

Professor Evgeni Magid discussed anthropomorphic robots and prospects of their integration into our daily life. He is convinced that anthropomorphic robotics belongs to the most promising robotics research areas primarily because humanity is interested to create robots that could replace humans in flexible scenarios and perform generic multifunctional tasks within the existing human-oriented infrastructure.

# Assistant Professor of LIRS Ilya Afanasyev participated in Summer School on Autonomous Micro Aerial Vehicles

> Страница относится к 2015 году: сведения на ней могли устареть.

28 августа 2015

The event took place at Birlinghoven Castle, Sankt Augustin, Germany on August 24-28, 2015.

![](https://media.innopolis.university/upload/iblock/2e5/51d3b73d6f.jpg)

Aerial vehicles have become a popular research tool in recent years – such vehicles are actively used in an increasing number of application domains such as aerial photography and inspection tasks. Most aerial vehicles are remotely controlled or follow GNSS-based waypoints (such as GLONASS or GPS) in obstacle-free heights. Many tasks require navigation in complex 3D environments in a close proximity to obstacles and thus the degree of autonomy of the UAVs/MAVs should be increased.

The objective of the school was to give the participants deep insights into the currently leading approaches to 3D environment perception, mapping, navigation planning, and control of autonomous aerial vehicles. The member of Intelligent Robotic Systems Laboratory Ilya Afanasyev took part in the event.

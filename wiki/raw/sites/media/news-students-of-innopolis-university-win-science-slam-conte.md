# Students of Innopolis University Win Science Slam Contest

> Страница относится к 2014 году: сведения на ней могли устареть.

28 ноября 2014

Bachelor’s students of Innopolis University became winners of Science Slam, which was held as part of the Kazan Startup Week. Young scientists shared first place.

Science Slam is an international science promotion project, aimed at creating a favorable image of young scientists and researchers. Every science slam includes a series of ten-minute speeches, given by participants, during which they present their scientific research work. Any attendee had the chance to ask a speaker any question about their research topic. Bachelor’s student Bulat Nasrullin talked about behavioral models of wireless network participants, and Mikhail Usvyatsov gave a speech about available anthropomorphic robots. The best slammers were chosen by the applause of the audience.

# Study of Innopolis University Receives Award at International Conference

> Страница относится к 2014 году: сведения на ней могли устареть.

5 сентября 2014

Research work written by IU’s Professor Manuel Mazzara together with professors M.Bersani and L. Ferrucci, received a Best Paper Award at the 9th international conference ICSOFT 2014. The conference was held in July 2014 in the capital of Austria, Vienna. The topic of the work is “An LTL Semantics of Business Workflows with Recovery”.

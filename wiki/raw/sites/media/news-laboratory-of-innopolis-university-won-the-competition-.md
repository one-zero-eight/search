# Laboratory of Innopolis University won the competition of initiative research projects 2015

> Страница относится к 2015 году: сведения на ней могли устареть.

6 октября 2015

Intelligent Robotic Systems Lab will receive a grant in the amount of 110,586 USD (7.2 million RUB) from Russian Foundation for Basic Research (RFBR) and the Ministry of Science and Technology of Israel.

![](https://media.innopolis.university/upload/iblock/f15/photo239208539014408147.jpg)

Nowadays collaboration between unmanned ground (UGVs) and unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) is one of the most relevant issues in robotics. Considering different specific character of installed sensor systems and multi-robot operating space, heterogeneous robot groups functioning as a single team are able to solve the problem of data collection and safety assurance including the search-and-rescue and transport-logistic tasks in a more efficient way.

“The aim of this project is theoretical and experimental study of collaboration within heterogeneous group of robots comprising a UGV and a number of UAV integrated in the search and rescue tasks under uncertain operating conditions”, - as Evgeni Magid, Head of Intelligent Robotic Systems Lab. He explained that robots will jointly perceive the surrounding environment using active collaborative vision and infer their own technical condition in order to provide reliable autonomous group operation.

“Robots will always do the mapping using the real-time navigation method and map-making”, - noticed the Head of the Laboratory. He emphasized the most important research aspect: the group of robots will operate as a single mechanism i.e. plan its actions and calculate the most optimal paths in order to complete the group task considering different external and internal sources of uncertainty.

The project will result in elaboration of a concept and development of an experimental system prototype of collaborative autonomous real-time path planning by a heterogeneous group of robots in the unknown dynamic environment under uncertain conditions: «We expect that our research results will significantly improve capabilities of the multi-robot groups collaborative operation due to combination of approaches used by Russian and Israeli research teams, and thus enable the most efficient application of autonomous groups of inexpensive momentarily deployed robots operating in the search and rescue scenarios in particular”, - said Professor Evgeni Magid.

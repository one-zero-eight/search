# Innopolis University and Skolkovo Institute of Science and Technology signed the Agreement on Cooperation

> Страница относится к 2015 году: сведения на ней могли устареть.

17 декабря 2015

Fields of cooperation will comprise energy, nuclear, space, information and biomedical sciences and technologies.

![](https://media.innopolis.university/upload/iblock/e45/POL_8335.JPG)

This Wednesday Kirill Semenikhin, Director of Innopolis University and Edward Crawley, Rector of the Skolkovo Institute of Science and Technology signed the Agreement on Cooperation in several key areas. The event was featured by Rustam Minnikhanov, President of the Republic of Tatarstan, and Viktor Vekselberg, President of the Skolkovo Foundation.

The Parties are going to collaborate in the following priority fields: Energy Science and Technology, Nuclear Science and Technology, Space Science and Technology, Information Science and Technology, and Biomedical Science and Technology. Cooperation will be carried out with the regard to Master’s and PhD degree programs of Innopolis University.

In the framework of the Agreement on Cooperation, the Parties will perform information exchange, participate in the development and implementation of joint educational programs for training of specialists in the priority cooperation areas and other key fields of the national technological development. The Agreement also foresees the creation of working groups in the focus areas and organization of joint events that contribute to the subject matter of the Agreement.

Kirill Semenikhin, Director of Innopolis University claimed: “This cooperation is aimed at strengthening close relations between Innopolis University and Skolkovo Institute of Science and Technology. It will be an integral part of education of specialists in the field of high technology.”

He summed up: “Bilateral measures for the development of power industry, space and biomedicine are already scheduled. We have set ambitious goals that require mutual efforts for the benefit of the IT-sphere.”

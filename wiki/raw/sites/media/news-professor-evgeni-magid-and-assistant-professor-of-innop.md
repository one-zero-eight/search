# Professor Evgeni Magid and Assistant Professor of Innopolis University Joseph Brown shared their opinion on the future of robotics in Russia

> Страница относится к 2015 году: сведения на ней могли устареть.

26 июня 2015

Robotics is one of the fastest growing areas in the IT-industry worldwide

![](https://media.innopolis.university/upload/iblock/28d/%D0%91%D0%B5%D0%B7%D1%8B%D0%BC%D1%8F%D0%BD%D0%BD%D1%8B%D0%B9.png)

Professor Evgeni Magid gave an interview to the National Television of Chuvashia and described the current state of robotics in Russia: “I believe that robotics is one of the most promising areas in the IT-industry. Robotics is a field that gives us good chances to catch up with the USA, Japan, and the EU countries. Nowadays, Russia is following a so-called ‘catch-up modernization’: we see what has been created in other countries and try to repeat and improve their achievements. The fact that our children are keen on robotics helps them to get used to the fact that robots are already part of our society. Even if we try to stop the wheel of history, we will not be able to ignore robots, as they are already integrated into our life ”.

Assistant Professor Joseph Brown, a PhD in computer science from Canada, also shared his opinion on the future of robotics in Russia. When asked, “When will Russia be able to catch up with the leaders in the robotics development?” Dr. Brown responded, “Given the abilities of the current generation, I believe this is going to happen very soon. I would love to see these students in my classes”.

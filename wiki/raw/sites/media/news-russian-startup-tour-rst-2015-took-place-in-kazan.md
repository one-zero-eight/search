# Russian Startup Tour (RST) 2015 took place in Kazan

> Страница относится к 2015 году: сведения на ней могли устареть.

4 марта 2015

Innopolis University researcher assessed new IT projects.

![](https://media.innopolis.university/upload/iblock/758/DSC01103.JPG)

The event took place at IT-park Kazan on March 3-4. It became an important stage of the biggest project on searching new ideas and development of core competencies of young specialists working in the field of high technology.

The first day of the RST was devoted to presentations and master classes for young entrepreneurs. The second day was entirely reserved for pitch presentations. Teams competed in five areas: information technology, innovations for children, energy and energy efficient technology, industrial technology and materials, biological and medical technologies.

Konstantin Urysov, researcher and alumnus of Innopolis University, became a jury member. After dozens of presentations, the experts chose the winner, who received a grant in the amount of 1 million roubles. Ayrat Mukhametzyanov and his RTsim project won the competition. Pavel Kochkarev and his Domo Sapiens, platform for a smart home system, took the second place.

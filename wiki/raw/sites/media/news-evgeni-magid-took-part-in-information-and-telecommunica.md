# Evgeni Magid took part in Information and Telecommunication Systems Applied and Experimental R&D Conference

> Страница относится к 2015 году: сведения на ней могли устареть.

4 декабря 2015

<p>

The conference was held within the 3<sup>rd</sup> National Annual Exhibition and VUZPROMEKSPO 2015 Forum framework

</p>

![](https://media.innopolis.university/upload/iblock/6a0/%D0%B4%D0%BE%D0%BA%D0%BB%D0%B0%D0%B4.JPG)

[The Ministry of Education and Science of the Russian Federation](http://xn--80abucjiibhv9a.xn--p1ai/) organized a conference “Information and Telecommunication Systems Applied and Experimental R&D” within the Federal Program framework “Research and developments in the priority areas of science and technology of the Russian Federation for 2014 – 2020”. The main purpose of the conference was to discuss applied research in top priority areas of Russian science and technology and to share the obtained in 2015 research results in these areas.

Professor Evgeni Magid presented the research results of “[R&D of the balance based energy efficient locomotion control system of a humanoid robot](https://xpir.ru/conference2015/theses/14.609.21.0004)” project in “Robotics, Information, Control, and Navigation Systems Technology” session.

“Three days of the conference were full of various exciting activities: seventeen parallel sessions of scientific presentations were held simultaneously in different chambers; teams from all over the country presented their R&D projects at [VuzPromFest exhibition](http://vuzpromexpo.ru/); [Russian Venture Company](http://www.rusventure.ru/en/) organized robotics competitions. At the exhibition, we successfully identified future potential cooperations between Intelligent Robotic Systems Laboratory and robotics laboratories of [Saint Petersburg Electrotechnical University "LETI"](http://www.eltech.ru/en/university), [ITMO University](http://en.ifmo.ru/), [Petrozavodsk State University](http://study-in-russia.org/) and others. Presentation of our research results attracted great interest among the audience. Especially, I was excited to meet with the [Far Eastern Federal University](https://www.dvfu.ru/en/) professor [Vladimir Filaretov](https://www.dvfu.ru/schools/engineering/news/vladimir-filaretov-molodyh-issledovatelej-a-ucu-predelnoj-cestnosti-i-poradocno): for several hours we discussed anthropomorphic robotics past and future prospects, Russian research grants system, the critical shortage of young specialists in the field of robotics and many other R&D related topics”, - shared his impressions Evgeni Magid.

The conference was also attended by Vice-Rector for Administrative Policy and IT Support Anatoly Lysov and research engineer of [Lungs pathology diagnostics lab](http://university.innopolis.ru/en/research/iis/diagnostics_lab) Ramil Kuleev, who also presented their research results within the Federal Program framework projects.

# Intelligent Robotic Systems Laboratory members participated in the sport activities of Innopolis

> Страница относится к 2015 году: сведения на ней могли устареть.

4 декабря 2015

<p>

Four LIRS members became medalists of Innopolis sport tournaments

</p>

![](https://media.innopolis.university/upload/iblock/131/%D1%80%D0%B0%D0%BC%D0%B8%D0%BB%D1%8C.jpg)

About 40 participants took part in the swimming competition. In total 23 gold, 9 silver and 5 bronze medals were awarded in various heats from 50 to 200 m. Ilya Shimchik received a gold medal in the 200 m freestyle race and Leysan Sabirova won a silver medal in the 50 m freestyle race.

In functional training multidiscipline competition for men Ramil Khusainov performed the exercises set within 10,48 min and won a gold medal. Veronika Dudkina took a bronze medal in women competition.

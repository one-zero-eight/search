# Professor Magid presented LIRS in two Japanese robotics research laboratories

> Страница относится к 2016 году: сведения на ней могли устареть.

8 февраля 2016

Evgeni Magid visited Mechatronics Laboratory of Kyoto University and Advanced Intelligent System Laboratory of Ritsumeikan University

![](https://media.innopolis.university/upload/iblock/728/photo_2016-02-24_09-29-01.jpg)

Professor Evgeni Magid visited [Mechatronics Laboratory](http://www.mechatronics.me.kyoto-u.ac.jp/index.php?ml_lang=en) (Matsuno Lab) of Kyoto University with a lecture on "The research in LIRS within the year 2015 and plans for 2016-2017", which introduced the innovative Innopolis city project, Innopolis University and the ongoing in LIRS research projects. After the lecture, in a friendly atmosphere the head of Japanese laboratory Professor Matsuno Fumitosi and Professor Magid discussed prospects for joint submission of grant applications, students exchange, joint supervision of PhD students, and potential involvement of Mechatronics Laboratory graduates in LIRS projects.

Professor Magid also gave a talk at [Advanced Intelligent System Laboratory](http://www.aislab.org/) of Ritsumeikan University. After the talk, he familiarized with current research projects of the laboratory and discussed with Professor Joo-Ho Lee the prospects of undergraduate and graduate student internships, joint scientific supervision of graduate and PhD students, joint Russian-Japanese summer schools for robotics students as well as a wider format of computer science summer schools.

# Professor Andy Pimentel paid a working visit to Innopolis University

> Страница относится к 2015 году: сведения на ней могли устареть.

26 октября 2015

Representative of the University of Amsterdam, a partner of the Russian IT-University, gave an intensive course of lectures in Computer Architecture.

![](https://media.innopolis.university/upload/iblock/3d9/_MG_6822.JPG)

Andy Pimentel, Director of Education at the Graduate School of Informatics, University of Amsterdam, visited the Russian IT-University to give a course of lectures in Computer Architecture. Besides, Dr. Pimentel had a meeting with the faculty of Innopolis University in order to identify potential directions for future scientific research. “In the discussions I have had with IU staff members, I noticed the eagerness to cooperate in terms of research. I highly appreciate and encourage this”, - said the professor.

Andy Pimentel has already visited the new Russian city earlier in March. Professor shared his thoughts about the new trip to Innopolis: “I am deeply impressed by the achievements of IU. What has been established in only a timeframe of a couple of years is truly amazing”. In addition, Dr. Pimentel mentioned a high level of competence shown by graduate students of Innopolis University and their active engagement in classes: “During my lectures at IU, I encountered a group of very bright and highly motivated students who are eager to learn and actively ask excellent questions. This simply is a joy for every teacher”.

Earlier this year Taco Walstra and Edwin Steffens, professors of the University of Amsterdam, came to Innopolis University with a series of lectures in Computer Architecture.

# Webinar for international students

> Страница относится к 2017 году: сведения на ней могли устареть.

8 ноября 2017

How to get enrolled at Innopolis University to study IT with a 100% scholarship? Attend a webinar for international students organized by Innopolis University on November 30 to learn about it.

![](https://media.innopolis.university/upload/iblock/604/photo_2017-03-31_17-50-19.jpg)

During the webinar you will also learn about:

- Key advantages for the international students to study at Innopolis University.

- Educational programs and their requirements (all programs at Innopolis University are taught in English).

- How to get 100% scholarship to study at Innopolis University.

- International faculty and world-class experts of the University.

- Campus, environment and sport facilities.

After a brief presentation of Innopolis University, participants will have a chance to ask questions.

Dates: 30 November 2017

Time: 15:00 (UTC+03:00, Moscow time)

Links: [https://pruffme.com/landing/u139557/IU2017](https://pruffme.com/landing/u139557/IU2017)

Language of the webinar: English

For more information contact Admission Office!

Phone: +7 (843) 203-92-53 (ext.191)

Email: admissions@innopolis.ru

# Innopolis University participates in European summit on Computer Science

> Страница относится к 2014 году: сведения на ней могли устареть.

17 октября 2014

Vice-Provost for Education of Innopolis University Tanya Stanko took part in the European summit for computer science which took place in Wroclaw (Poland) on 13 to 15 of October. This forum marked its 10th anniversary and gathered IT education professionals from across Europe. T. Stanko spoke about Innopolis University during the seminar for deans of IT departments.

# The first practical homework for students of Introduction to Robotics course

> Страница относится к 2015 году: сведения на ней могли устареть.

21 сентября 2015

Students created their own manipulators.

![](https://media.innopolis.university/upload/iblock/87e/20150921_120722.jpg) On September 21, M.Sc. Robotics students of "Introduction to Robotics" course presented their first practical homework on manipulator design and control. Four groups used LEGO kits for creating their own manipulator and demonstrating their newly obtained knowledge in forward kinematics field.

# Intelligent Robotic System Laboratory and IntroScan Technology Company signed the Cooperation Agreement

> Страница относится к 2015 году: сведения на ней могли устареть.

16 декабря 2015

<p>

Cooperative projects on robotics for in-pipe diagnosis and control were discussed at the meeting.

</p>

![](https://media.innopolis.university/upload/iblock/5a3/%D0%BF%D0%BE%D0%B4%D0%BF%D0%B8%D1%81%D0%B0%D0%BD%D0%B8%D0%B5.jpg)

On December 16, 2015 Intelligent Robotic Systems Laboratory hosted a research meeting of [IntroScan Technology](https://introscan.ru/) and [ENTE](http://www.ente-ltd.ru/) companies representatives and Innopolis University professors. The event was featured by Professor Evgeni Magid, Assistant Professors Ilya Afanasyev, Alexandr Klimchik and Adil Khan, and Research Engineers Ramil Kuleev and Sadegh Nobari. Participants of the meeting discussed collaboration possibilities within a joint project on robotics systems for in-pipe diagnosis and control for PJSC Gazprom.

After the meeting, Director of Innopolis University Kirill Semenikhin and CEO of IntroScan Technology company Stanislav Voronchikhin signed the Cooperation Agreement in several areas including research and development projects in the field of robotics, big data analysis and databases.

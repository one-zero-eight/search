# Colleagues from the University of Amsterdam participated in the opening ceremony of Innopolis city and Innopolis University

> Страница относится к 2015 году: сведения на ней могли устареть.

9 июня 2015

On June, 9th, Dr. Karst Koymans, System and Network Engineering (SNE) program director, Dr. Hans Dekkers, head of the lecturers group of the Faculty of science, and Dr. Jaap van Ginkel, security researcher and lecturer, visited Innopolis. Dutch guests met their Russian colleagues and negotiated the details of joint realization of the SNE program, attended the Russian Internet Forum and the Russian stage of the World Robot Olympiad.

![](https://media.innopolis.university/upload/iblock/12e/photo_2015-06-10_11-36-55.jpg) Reference: University of Amsterdam is a partner of Innopolis University. The Dutch institution is among the world’s top-50 universities (QS World university rankings, 2014/2015). The SNE program is recognized as the top rated program and the best Dutch Master’s in the category “Informatics” (Keuzegids Masters 2015).

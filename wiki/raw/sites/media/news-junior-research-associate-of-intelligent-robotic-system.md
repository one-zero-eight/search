# Junior Research Associate of Intelligent Robotic Systems Laboratory Igor Danilov has become a winner of “The Best 50 Innovative Ideas of the Republic of Tatarstan” contest

> Страница относится к 2015 году: сведения на ней могли устареть.

10 декабря 2015

<p>

The event was organized by Investment and Venture Fund and Academy of Science of the Republic of Tatarstan.

</p>

![](https://media.innopolis.university/upload/iblock/98f/%D0%94%D0%B0%D0%BD%D0%B8%D0%BB%D0%BE%D0%B2.jpg)

On December 10, 2015 at the Korston Hotel & Mall the winners of “The Best 50 Innovative Ideas of the Republic of Tatarstan” contest received their awards. The competition was targeted to increase the innovation potential of Tatarstan Republic and to draw young generation’s attention towards research and technical creativity. The event was organized by Investment and Venture Fund and Academy of Science of the Republic of Tatarstan.

Junior Research Associate of LIRS Igor Danilov presented his project “Automatic copter landing system development and the wireless charging platform” which is supported by innovative projects grant [UMNIK-2015](http://umnik.fasie.ru/). His project received significant attention of the jury and became one of the contest winners.

“It is a great pleasure and honor to become a winner of the competition. I am grateful to the Government of the Republic of Tatarstan for supporting young people in their desire for innovation and high technologies development”, - shared his impressions Igor Danilov.

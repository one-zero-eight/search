# The team of the Big Data and Information Systems Lab of Innopolis University took part in the International IEEE Conference

> Страница относится к 2015 году: сведения на ней могли устареть.

19 ноября 2015

The event was devoted to Data Mining.

![](https://media.innopolis.university/upload/iblock/560/Photo%20Nov%2015%2C%2010%2050%2055.jpg)

The IEEE International Conference on Data Mining series (ICDM) has established itself as the world's premier research conference in data mining.

It provides an international forum for presentation of original research results, as well as exchange and dissemination of innovative, practical development experiences.

In this year, a Nobel Prize winner in economics, Dr. Robert F. Engle, the Pehong Chen Distinguished Professor (a Fellow of the AAAI, ACM, ASA, CSS, IMS, IEEE and SIAM), Michael I. Jordan, and a leading data scientist of Facebook, Dr. Lada Adamic gave keynote talks in the conference

Dr. Qiang Qu, Professor of Innopolis, attended International Conference on Data Mining, organized and chaired a session on mobility social data analysis. Dr. Jooyoung Lee and Dr. Sadegh Nobari gave talks in the conference.

The talk given by Dr. Jooyoung Lee was focusing on VK data analysis. The study firstly examined Independent Cascade (IC) information diffusion model which assumes that each node independently influences its neighboring nodes on VK.

The results show that the behavior of information diffusion in social media is different based on the topics shared.

The talk given by Dr. Sadegh Nobari was on how to model social topics, user behaviors, and geolocations in one generated learning model, which can be used for social content analysis and location prediction.

In the study, experimental results on a very large data set collected from Twitter show that the model is very effective to detect interesting topics with location intent as well as prediction tasks.

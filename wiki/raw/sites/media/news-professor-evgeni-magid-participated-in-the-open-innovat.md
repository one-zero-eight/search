# Professor Evgeni Magid participated in the “Open Innovations” forum

> Страница относится к 2015 году: сведения на ней могли устареть.

1 ноября 2015

<p>The event focused on new technologies and perspectives of the international cooperation on innovations.</p>

![](https://media.innopolis.university/upload/iblock/10f/rwUnecOEnUo.jpg)

[“Open Innovations”](http://www.forinnovations.ru/en/) (Annual International Forum and Technology Show) has been held since 2012 with the support of [the Ministry of Economic Development of the Russian Federation.](http://economy.gov.ru/en/home/about/) Among Forum participants were CEOs and top-managers of leading Russian and foreign companies, investors and experts in the field of innovations and IT-industry.

In 2015, Forum and Exhibition took place in Moscow on October 28 - November 1 at Pavilion 75, [VDNH](http://vdnh.ru/) (the Exhibition of Achievements of National Economy). The participants of the forum discussed how technologies influence productivity, habitat, education, health and entertainment.

Professor of the Intelligent Robotic Systems Laboratory Evgeni Magid participated in “Open Innovations”. During the event, Evgeni met with colleagues and experts in the field of robotics and visited Open Innovations Technology Show exhibition of high-tech achievements. As the result of the meetings there were achieved several informal agreements to apply for joint research and industrial grant proposals in the field of robotics together with a number of Russian Universities and start-up companies.

# University professors visited Innopolis

> Страница относится к 2015 году: сведения на ней могли устареть.

6 февраля 2015

Faculty members evaluated readiness of Innopolis University campus.

![](https://media.innopolis.university/upload/iblock/9c2/0_10ef86_5be9ab39_orig.jpg)

The group of visitors consisted of: the Head of the Software Engineering laboratory Bertrand Meyer; his deputy, Associate Professor Manuel Mazzara; Visiting Professor Salvatore Distefano; and Assistant Professor Mohamad Kassab.

They examined University’s residential facilities and its canteen, and spoke well of spacious lecture rooms and labs. The delegation especially emphasized warm passages linking residential blocks with the University building. Very soon they will be crowded with students hurrying to their classes.

# ANCOR Holding Company and Innopolis University signed a Cooperation Agreement

> Страница относится к 2016 году: сведения на ней могли устареть.

22 апреля 2016

- [Industry](/raw/sites/media/home.md)

The Russian IT University and the leader of the recruitment market in the CIS countries are working on a joint program in order to enhance the overall performance of the University.

![](https://media.innopolis.university/upload/iblock/d91/_MG_2162.JPG)

Innopolis University has announced the signing of the agreement with ANCOR Holding Company, leader of the recruitment market in Russia and other CIS countries. The network of the holding company includes over 40 branches in Russia, Ukraine, Kazakhstan, and Belarus. Companies conducting business in the holding company deliver services to corporate clients from various market segments, including the product, industry and local expert examination.

A wide practical experience on the labour market and highly professional team of the new partner of the University is a guarantee of successful achievement of the targets set. Priority areas of cooperation will include the following: securing the demand for Innopolis University and its graduates on the job market, management and supply of human resources of the first Russian private IT University and other areas of recruitment policy.

Both parties have agreed on issues of expert support of the University in the field of human capital management, organization of workshops and seminars in order to improve skills and competences of students and facilitate their adaptation to the business environment, along with organization of internships and practices for graduates and students of Innopolis University.

"I am sure that cooperation with ANCOR holding company, one of the leaders of the labour market of the CIS, will exert a positive influence on the whole range of business processes and functional mechanisms of our University, - said Sofia Chernogortseva, Vice-Provost for Business Development. She continued that developing the human capital is a top target of Innopolis University. It is pleasant to know that the new partner of the University shares our values and is focused on fruitful work in the areas important for us".

# Innopolis University teams won all prizes at the federal competition on computer security in the Republic of Tatarstan

> Страница относится к 2015 году: сведения на ней могли устареть.

30 ноября 2015

More than 700 members of 208 teams from Russia and Belarus took part in the QCTF Starter 2015 competition.

![](https://media.innopolis.university/upload/iblock/023/photo26328257354904706.jpg)

This weekend, the Innopolis University campus became one of the federal platforms for the QCTF Starter 2015 competition on computer security. The main goal of the competition that was first held in the Urals, Russia in 2006, is to evaluate skills of participants (programmers) to attack and protect computer systems. Teams of 4 people had 8 hours to complete cryptography, encryption and steganography tasks. The solution to tasks was the so-called “key string” which the programmers were to send to judges for checking.

Results at the Innopolis University platform (Republic of Tatarstan):

· 1st place Espacio (Innopolis University)

· 2nd place 4.42 (Innopolis University)

· 3rd place REHack (Innopolis University)

Anna Makarova, representative of the Innopolis University team “Espacio” noted: “We are glad that we have taken all the prize-winning places in the Republic of Tatarstan and became the 8th among 208 teams in the national QCTF Starter standing. Results are especially pleasing in the light of the fact that this event is our first competition in the sphere of computer security. This is an excellent start and we’ve gained invaluable experience, which will certainly help us to reach great hights at similar competitions in future”.

The competition on computer security that took place in more than 20 cities of Russia and Belarus, was sponsored by the SKB Kontur IT company based in Yekaterinburg and supported by the universities acting as competition platforms.

# Innopolis University opens Summer School for Olympiad Training in Computer Science

> Страница относится к 2015 году: сведения на ней могли устареть.

5 августа 2015

More than 40 talented students from schools of the Republic of Tatarstan, Republic of Bashkortostan and Chelyabinsk Oblast have gathered for the training.

![](https://media.innopolis.university/upload/iblock/08c/photo329289400669284639.jpg)

During the week of Summer School for Olympiad Training in Computer Science participants will have an intensive program ahead, in particular: problem-solving and analysis of the most complex Olympiad contests, team-making games and trainings, meetings with Pavel Mavrin and Niyaz Nigmatullin, Programming World Champions of ACM ICPC and talks with Ilshat Safiullin, the leading instructor in Olympiad Computer Science of the Republic of Tatarstan.

"The main goal of the Summer School for Olympiad Training is to give talented students the opportunity to study under supervision of experienced instructors prior to the new Olympiad session. Besides, it’s a great chance to see the new and ultramodern Innopolis University from the inside", - Sergey Masyagin, the Vice-Rector for Students Affairs shared about the Summer School.

# New competition for educational grants of Innopolis University

> Страница относится к 2016 году: сведения на ней могли устареть.

1 декабря 2015

Innopolis University will award 300 educational grants in 2016. Each grant will cover tuition fees for the entire training period.

![](https://media.innopolis.university/upload/iblock/f29/vjpohHEseJc.jpg)

The grant competition will consist of two stages: remote phase (submission of documents and online application) and on-site selection that includes tests and interviews with the international faculty of the University. Based on the screening results, a list of students who received educational grants and the right to be enrolled at Innopolis University will be formed.

Admission session 2016 will be the forth one held by the IT University. Its graduates are currently employed at scientific and research laboratories of the University and resident companies of Innopolis Special Economic Zone, and leading IT companies.

“Innopolis University provides all necessary conditions to train high-quality IT experts: world-class faculty members with an experience in teaching and studying at the world’s top-100 universities, education that is demanded by the industry, international academic exchange programs and individual approach to each student. In other words, our students are offered the best opportunities to acquire professional competences and build their own IT career. The University welcomes motivated young students who can manage with high academic load and want to invest their time and efforts into knowledge in order to become world-class professionals.” - said Sergey Masyagin, Vice-rector, Head of the Department of Student Affairs, Enrollment and Admission.

First on-site selection will take place in January 2016. Innopolis University offers programs for schoolchildren, students and young experts in the following fields: Computer Science, Big Data, Software Engineering, Secure Systems and Network Engineering, and Robotics.

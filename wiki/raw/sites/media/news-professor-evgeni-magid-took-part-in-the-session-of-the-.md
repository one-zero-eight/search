# Professor Evgeni Magid took part in the session of the Interim Commission of the Council of the Federation on the information society development

> Страница относится к 2015 году: сведения на ней могли устареть.

17 ноября 2015

The meeting covered the issues related to the development of the educational robotics in the Russian Federation.

![](https://media.innopolis.university/upload/iblock/ed3/11.jpg)

This week Evgeni Magid, Head of the Laboratory of Intelligent Robotic Systems (LIRS) of Innopolis University took part in the session of [Interim Committee of the Russian Federation Federal Assembly’s Federation Council](http://council.gov.ru/press-center/announcements/60723/) on the information society development concerning promotion of the educational robotics in the Russian Federation.

The session gathered the members of the Ministry of Education and Science, Ministry of Economic Development and Ministry of Industry and Trade of Russia along with the representatives of Russian regions and industry experts. Professor of Innopolis University was invited as the expert in robotics.

The agenda included a number of crucial issues: contribution of Russian regions to the development of the Federal Target Program roadmap for educational advancement, methods consolidation and pilot projects by non-public entities in robotics and promotion of science, as well as the establishment of the National Competition Robotics League in technical creativity.

“Today almost all Russian experts in robotics know each other in person. That is why the first session was held in a cordial and friendly atmosphere”, - shared his impressions Evgeni Magid. Later on, the expert told about specific suggestions, which were made in the course of the meeting: the widening of [Robotics Coordination Council](http://xn--80abucjiibhv9a.xn--p1ai/%D0%BD%D0%BE%D0%B2%D0%BE%D1%81%D1%82%D0%B8/6559) (under the jurisdiction of Ministry of Education and Science of the Russian Federation), the governmental support and formation of Russian-made robotic platform for the National Competition Robotics League etc. In conclusion, Prof. Magid also emphasized that the Ministry of Education and Science policy should be modified with regard to international experience and Russian robotics experts’ opinion”.

Evgeni Magid pointed out that special attention was paid to the modernization of higher education programs in robotics. «So far, there were no clear announcements about new higher education in robotics programs availability, except for the offered by [Bauman Moscow State Technical University](http://www.bmstu.ru/) (represented by Prof. A.S. Yuschenko, one of the pioneers of robotics in the Soviet Union) and Innopolis University programs. The lack of such programs make impossible further development in robotics for high school programs’ graduates», - said the Head of LIRS. The researcher clarified that certain higher education institutions are currently offering training in mechatronics and robotics. “As a part of Mechanical Engineering Departments, such training is focused on industrial robotics and production automation. In other words, such academic departments train experts in CNC-machine building and maintenance of automated lines rather than experts in the algorithm and software design for complex robotic systems”, - noticed Evgeni Magid.

«It was a pleasure to talk with the Ministry of Education and Science officials who managed to answer our quite tricky questions and demonstrated their interest in positive criticism and rational suggestions which aim further improvement of the Ministry’s operation”, - concluded Innopolis University Professor. .

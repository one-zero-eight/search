# Intelligent Robotic Systems Laboratory celebrated the first anniversary

> Страница относится к 2015 году: сведения на ней могли устареть.

2 октября 2015

<p>Professor Evgeni Magid established Intelligent Robotic Systems Laboratory (LIRS) at Innopolis University on October 2, 2014.</p>

![](https://media.innopolis.university/upload/iblock/576/5.jpg)

Main LIRS achievements during the first year of its existence are:

· The team has grown to 10 members

· Laboratory has received 2 research grants for a total amount RUB 68,4 million

· Three research papers were published at the local conferences (one of them is indexed in Russian Science Citation Index database) and one research paper was published at the international conference (indexed in Scopus database)

· One research paper was accepted for international journal, two research papers were accepted for two different international conferences (indexed in Scopus database)

· LIRS members took part in seven domestic and four International robotic events.

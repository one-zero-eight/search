# Information on New Competitions and Grants

> Страница относится к 2016 году: сведения на ней могли устареть.

3 июня 2015

Innopolis University released the second review of grants

![](https://media.innopolis.university/upload/iblock/1c4/bigstock-Happy-group-of-students-in-the-29868500.jpg)

Russian Foundation for Basic Research

[Initiative Scientific Projects Competition 2016 arranged by the Russian Foundation for Basic Research and the Japan Society for Promotion of Science (JSPS)](http://www.rfbr.ru/rffi/ru/international_announcement/o_1934048)

- Proposal submission deadline September 8, 2015.

- Project support amount up to 1,200,000 RUB per year.

- Project term 2016-2017.

[Initiative Scientific Projects Competition 2016 arranged by the Russian Foundation for Basic Research and the National Natural Science Foundation of China (NNSF)](http://www.rfbr.ru/rffi/ru/international_announcement/o_1932742)

- Proposal submission deadline July 3, 2015.

- Project support amount up to 600,000 RUB per year.

- Project term 2016-2017.

[Competition on Scientific Projects by Young Researchers under Guidance of PhDs and ScDs at Scientific Institutions of the Russian Federation in 2016](http://www.rfbr.ru/rffi/ru/contests_announcement/o_1930664)

- Proposal submission deadline June 14, 2016.

- Project support amount 100,000 RUB per month for PhD researchers and 80,000 RUB per month for non-PhD researchers.

- Project term 1-5 calendar months.

If you are interested, do not hesitate to contact us. We will be glad to render you any possible assistance related to proposal preparation.

Grants Department Grants.department@innopolis.ru

# Delegation of Innopolis University visited educational institutions in Armenia

> Страница относится к 2015 году: сведения на ней могли устареть.

16 октября 2015

Sofia Chernogortseva, Vice-Provost for Business Development made a presentation on Innopolis University at Dilijan College, Ayb School and the TUMO Center.

![](https://media.innopolis.university/upload/iblock/266/ce55P5dgm4E.jpg)

Dilijan College, a member of the United World Colleges (UWC) network, was the first location where the University delegation stopped by. During the meeting, students discovered the study opportunities at the new Russian IT University, learned about the educational programs and IT specialties that are currently in-demand. After the presentation, the delegation from Russia took part in planting of the Innopolis University tree on occasion of the [Dilijan College](http://www.uwcdilijan.org/) anniversary.

Today 15 schools and colleges making up the United World College movement in 14 countries of the world follow one of the two key models: colleges with the International Baccalaureate (IB,) a two-year pre-university program, and schools awarding both the IB diploma and the International Certificate of Secondary Education (IGCSE). At Dilijan College, along with other UWC colleges, the IB Diploma program, recognized by the world’s leading universities, is taught in English. Apart from the core subjects of this program, college students shall develop and implement an individual social project.

Following the visit to Dilijan, the delegation of Innopolis University left to Yerevan, the capital of Armenia, to meet with students and teachers in modern and spacious lecture rooms of the [Ayb School](http://www.aybschool.am/). It is noteworthy that Ayb School is a perfect example of the institution applying modern educational technologies. It combines the up-to-date international practices in teaching Information and Communication Technologies, Mathematics and Foreign Languages.

The final meeting was arranged in a truly unique place that can be hardly referred to as an ordinary educational institution. The [TUMO](https://www.tumo.org/) Center is regarded as “the school of future” or “the center for creative technologies”. In fact, the center provides additional school education and is considered to be a pilot model showing how schools can look like in the near future. Every week over 6,000 children aged 12 - 18 years attend the TUMO programs in Programming, Web-Design, Robotics, 3D-Modeling, Game Development, etc.

“Our institution can serve as an illustration of what happens when students select interesting classes on their own. Today, our experiment allows to see the evolution of the educational system if we refuse from the former framework in favour of the new one which is free from the form-based division of children and any constraints”, – told Maria Lou Papazian, Managing Director of the TUMO Center in her interview to an online-media.

Sofia Chernogortseva, Vice-Provost for Business Development of Innopolis University, shared her impressions on the trip to Armenia: “We were glad to exchange the teaching experience with the best educational institutions of Armenia and invite the most talented children who are interested in IT to study at our University. Innopolis is looking forward to a return visit of the leadership of Ayb School, TUMO, and Dilijan College”.

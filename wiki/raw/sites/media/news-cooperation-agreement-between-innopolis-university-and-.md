# Cooperation agreement between Innopolis University and Seoul National University

> Страница относится к 2015 году: сведения на ней могли устареть.

13 августа 2015

Universities will implement the academic exchange program for students.

![](https://media.innopolis.university/upload/iblock/c1e/%D0%B9%D0%B9.jpg)

Seoul National University has become a partner of Innopolis University. In the framework of the signed agreement, students of Russian IT-university will have an opportunity to study at South Korean university during one semester.

“Agreement on the academic exchange program with Seoul National University is another step to enhance our network of partnership relations with the world’s leading universities. Students of Innopolis University have a chance to complete a part of the academic program abroad, this will help them to enhance their knowledge, learn the best practices of our foreign colleagues, exchange ideas and share experience. We are sure that SNU students will also find their academic semester in Innopolis useful and interesting”, said Elmira Khalitova, the Head of International Relations Department.

About Seoul National University ( SNU)

SNU was founded on August 22, 1946. It has a status of a national research university of the Republic of Korea and is among the world’s top-50 science&technology universities. Every year more than 28 thousands of students study at the university. In 2009 l’Ecole des Mines de Paris - MINES Paris Tech reported that Seoul National University was ranked 5th in the world in terms of the number of alumni currently holding CEO positions in companies.

Among the notable alumni of Seoul National University: Ban Ki-Moon, the eighth and current Secretary-General of the United Nations, Song Sang-Hyun, the former President of the International Criminal Court, Lee Jong-Wook, former Director-General of the World Health Organization, Kang Jin-Ku and Yun Jong-Yong, former CEO of Samsung Electronics, Hwang Chang Gyu, CEO of Korea Telecom, Koo Pyong Hwoi, founding member of LG Group and many other famous scientists, businessmen and men of art.

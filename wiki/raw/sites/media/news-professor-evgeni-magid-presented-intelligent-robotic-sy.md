# Professor Evgeni Magid presented Intelligent Robotic Systems Laboratory on the ceremony of signing a cooperation agreement between Innopolis University and Skolkovo Foundation

> Страница относится к 2015 году: сведения на ней могли устареть.

16 декабря 2015

<p>

The event took place at Innopolis University on December 16, 2015

</p>

![](https://media.innopolis.university/upload/iblock/136/%D1%81%D0%BE%D0%B3%D0%BB%D0%B0%D1%88%D0%B5%D0%BD%D0%B8%D0%B5%20%D1%81%20%D1%84%D0%BE%D0%BD%D0%B4%D0%BE%D0%BC%20%D1%81%D0%BA%D0%BE%D0%BB%D0%BA%D0%BE%D0%B2%D0%BE.JPG)

A number of strategic agreements has been signed at Innopolis University on December 16, including [Republic of Tatarstan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tatarstan) innovative development agreements. The event was featured by [Rustam Minnikhanov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rustam_Minnikhanov), President of the Republic of Tatarstan, and [Viktor Vekselberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viktor_Vekselberg), President of the [Skolkovo Foundation](https://sk.ru/foundation/).

Professor Evgeni Magid presented the Intelligent Robotic Systems Laboratory team and the achievements of LIRS within the period of 2014-2015. He also mentioned previous successful cooperation of LIRS and [Skolkovo Robotics Center](https://sk.ru/foundation/itc/robotics/) within [Skolkovo Robotics](https://sk.ru/foundation/itc/robotics/skrobotics2015/), [OS DAY](http://osday.ru/), and [RIF. Innopolis forum](https://regions.rif.ru/events/innopolis/) conferences framework, and emphasized the importance of signing the agreements that will help expanding the cooperation between the two leading IT universities of Russia.

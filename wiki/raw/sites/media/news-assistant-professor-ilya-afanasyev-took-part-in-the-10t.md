# Assistant Professor Ilya Afanasyev took part in the 10th International Symposium on Mechatronics and its Applications

> Страница относится к 2015 году: сведения на ней могли устареть.

10 декабря 2015

<p>

The main topics of the event were theoretical and practical aspects of mechatronics.

</p>

![](https://media.innopolis.university/upload/iblock/6f6/image01.jpg)

The [10th International Symposium on Mechatronics and its Application](http://www.ismaconf.org/index.php)s (ISMA 2015) was held by the Mechatronics Center at the [American University of Sharjah](http://www.aus.edu/), UAE, on December 8-10, 2015. The event brought together scientists, scholars, engineers, and students in order to exchange and promote research in the mechatronics field. The symposium covered the synergetic integration of mechanical engineering with electronics, intelligent control, and design and manufacturing of industrial products and processes.

Researchers of Innopolis University Bulat Gabbasov, Igor Danilov, Assistant Professor Ilya Afanasyev, and Professor Evgeni Magid prepared a research paper “Toward a human-like biped robot gait: Biomechanical analysis of human locomotion recorded by Kinect-based Motion Capture System”, which was presented as a full paper talk by Ilya Afanasyev. Proceedings of the symposium are indexed in [Scopus](http://www.scopus.com/) citation database.

“Conference brought together scientists mainly from the Middle East and North Africa and their project partners from Portugal, India, Singapore, Canada and the United States. Most reports covered robotics and mechatronic systems (e.g., manipulators, crawler excavators, solar power panels cleaning robots, exoskeletons, etc.), control systems for robots and industrial machines. Russia was presented with two talks: our Motion Capture System and the report on exoskeleton by Professor Sergey Yatsun from [Southwest State University](http://www.swsu.ru/eng/index.php). The conference left a positive impression. After visiting Mechatronics and Aerodynamic Systems Laboratories, I would note that the UAE universities are successfully carrying out their own independent developments in robotics and mechatronic systems areas, including 3D CAD models creation, simulation use, assembly, programming and prototype designing”, – shared his impressions Ilya Afanasyev.

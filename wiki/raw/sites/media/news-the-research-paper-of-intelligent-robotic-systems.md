# LIRS members manuscript was accepted for “Unmanned Vehicle with Artificial Intelligence Elements” Workshop

> Страница относится к 2015 году: сведения на ней могли устареть.

3 августа 2015

International Conference «Extreme Robotics» is to be held in Saint Petersburg on October 8-9, 2015

![](https://media.innopolis.university/upload/iblock/3ca/%D0%B1%D1%82%D1%81.jpg)

[Extreme Robotics conference](http://er.rtc.ru/index.php/en/) is acknowledged to be one of the most important scientific events in the field of robotics in Russia. [Russian Association of Artificial Intelligence](http://raai.org/) organizes the second [Unmanned Vehicles with Artificial Intelligence Elements](http://www.ai-uv.ru/)workshop that is to take place within the framework of ER-2015. The workshop will bring together researchers, engineers and practitioners interested in development and intellectualization of unmanned vehicles.

Members of Intelligent Robotic Systems Laboratory Ilya Afanasyev, Artur Sagitov, Igor Danilov and Evgeni Magid prepared a research paper titled “Maze navigation of a heterogeneous group of robots (UAVs and UGVs) with probabilistic roadmap in Gazebo 3D simulator”. The manuscript will be indexed in Russian Science Citation Index database (bibliographic database of scientific publications in Russian).

The picture from the LIRS manuscript: UAV and UGV in Gazebo maze sample (on the left), calculated with PRM optimal path (in the center) and the navigation through the maze (on the right).

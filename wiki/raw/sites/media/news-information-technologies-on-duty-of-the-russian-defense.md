# «Information technologies on duty of the Russian defense industry» conference

> Страница относится к 2015 году: сведения на ней могли устареть.

25 мая 2015

The event takes place on May 26-29 in Innopolis.

![](https://media.innopolis.university/upload/iblock/970/2%5B729%5D.jpg)

The new Russian IT city hosts the 4th Conference on «Information technologies in the Russian Defense industry» on May 26-29. The conference is held in the brand new campus of Innopolis University.

During 4 days, more than 1000 best Russian specialists in IT and defense industry will discuss issues of strategic development and improvement of information technologies in the defense industry. A particular emphasis will be put on the import substitution in the IT sphere.

Rostec Corporation, Government of the Republic of Tatarstan and Military-Industrial Commission of the Russian Federation are the main organizers of the event.

High-ranking officials as Rustam Minnikhanov, acting President of Tatarstan, Nikolai Nikiforov, Minister of Telecom and Mass Communication of the Russian Federation, Sergey Chemezov, CEO of the Rostec Corporation, etc. are among the participants.

Roman Shaykhutdinov, Deputy Prime Minister of the Republic of Tatarstan:

«I will not exaggerate, if I say that the Republic of Tatarstan is one of the leading regions in the development of the IT industry. We truly have all that is needed for its further development. This gives us a significant impulse to boost innovation in the defense industry of the Republic.

It is my sincere pleasure to mention that the conference is being conducted in Innopolis – the first Russian IT city. In the long-term perspective, Innopolis is to become one more growing point of the Russian economy».

Reference:

Innopolis – a new Russian IT city. The new city is designed to bring together young highly qualified specialists from all over the country, thus strengthening the innovative capacity of the Russian Federation.

Innopolis University is an integral element of the ambitious Innopolis project. Being the first Russian university with a focus on advanced Computer Science and its applications, the University is an intellectual driving force for the development of the national IT industry. The University offers unique programs in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Cybersecurity, Data Science, and Robotics.

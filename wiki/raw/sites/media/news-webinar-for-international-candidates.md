Don’t miss your chance to apply for up to 100 % scholarship to study at Innopolis University.

Date: Feb 27, 2019

Time: 8:00 p.m. (UTC+03:00, Moscow time)

Registration link: [https://pruffme.com/landing/u139557/tmpui27022019](https://pruffme.com/landing/u139557/tmpui27022019)

Guidelines to applying for up to 100 % scholarship

International community of the University

Q&A session

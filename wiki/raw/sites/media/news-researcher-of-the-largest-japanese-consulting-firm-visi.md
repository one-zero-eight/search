# Researcher of the largest Japanese consulting firm visited Innopolis University

> Страница относится к 2015 году: сведения на ней могли устареть.

21 сентября 2015

Mr. Yuzo Ishida discussed potential joint projects with the University leadership.

![](https://media.innopolis.university/upload/iblock/3b5/_MG_5658.JPG)

Yuzo Ishida is a member of the Distribution Systems Development Department of Nomura Research Institute, Japan. IT consulting is one of the major activities of the institute. Mr. Ishida is a software architect, he is engaged in designing and developing an application framework by integrating open source products.

It is noteworthy that Mr. Ishida completed the Master’s degree program in Software Engineering at Carnegie Mellon University, USA, which serves as a basis for a Master’s degree program of Innopolis University.

During his week-long visit, Yuzo Ishida had a series of meetings with faculty members of Innopolis University and discussed joint educational and research projects in the field of IT.

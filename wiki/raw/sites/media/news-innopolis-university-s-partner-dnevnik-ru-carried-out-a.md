# Innopolis University’s partner Dnevnik.ru carried out a global research on the introduction of tablets in the educational process

> Страница относится к 2015 году: сведения на ней могли устареть.

15 декабря 2015

Over 50,000 respondents from 85 regions of the Russian Federation took part in the national survey lasting 2.5 weeks.

![](https://media.innopolis.university/upload/iblock/a0e/school-children-using-digital-tablet-outside.jpg)

In September 2015, a partner of Innopolis University, Dnevnik.ru carried out the largest online research among school teachers, students and parents devoted to the introduction of tablets in the educational process. Despite the fact that using of devices for educational purposes is a global trend, some schools in Russia still have concerns about this issue. Dnevnik.ru decided to investigate how all participants of the educational process feel about innovations and what is the future of a mobile education in our country.

The survey showed that Russian schools use tablets quite rarely: 81% of teachers, 90% of parents and 88% of students admitted that no such lessons have been given at their schools so far. However, many schools have already started using electronic textbooks – this means that use of tablets at lessons will increase. For instance, few years ago, many people did not trust electronic school diaries and class registers. Today most of Dnevnik.ru users speak of the informatization benefits. It means that modern teachers and students are ready for a new format of education.

The majority of students participated in the poll approved the introduction of tablets in the educational process (76%). They were supported by 48% of teachers and a slightly smaller number of parents (32%). Doubts of adults are understandable. Teachers think that children will have an extra distracting factor, thiswas marked by 48% of respondents. Nevertheless, 76% of teachers would like to attend courses for advanced tablet users.

In general, parents are interested in the introduction of tablets in the educational process. However, they do not rush to make conclusions on benefits and risks of this tendency. Interestingly enough, all groups of respondents marked the advantages of tablet use: easy data storage, ability to give interactive lessons, access to libraries and knowledge bases, multiple options for artistic development and, of course, a less heavy schoolbag. 59% of students chose an electronic textbook instead of a paper book.

Dnevnik.ru and “Beeline” started a special project “Look and Learn!” to prepare users for this innovation. The project goals are as follows: to show users the most effective way of using tablets in education, give advice and share useful sources, discuss issues of a safe Internet behavior. To ensure the maximum audience involvement, the project developers organized a test-drive of educational tablets engaging students aged 8-17. Organizers of the special project and over 21,000 users are waiting for impressions of young test-drivers. In December, Dnevnik.ru and “Beeline” will present project outcomes and prepare recommendations for Russian schools, that will decide to use tablets.

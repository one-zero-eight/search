# Representatives of the Republic of Tatarstan visited Innopolis University.

> Страница относится к 2015 году: сведения на ней могли устареть.

19 февраля 2015

Academic programs of the University and the concept of the Innopolis Special Economic Zone were presented to the guests.

![](https://media.innopolis.university/upload/iblock/9cb/6p23jgNXNOI.jpg)

Igor Nosov, General Director of the Innopolis Special Economic Zone, delivered a presentation on the economic site of the new Russian city. He expressed his confidence that Innopolis is to become a basic element for the development of the Russian IT industry. He also told the visitors about conditions for doing business and preferences offered by the Special Economic Zone. Then the floor was given to Alexander Tormasov, Rector of the University.

Professor Tormasov pointed out that Innopolis University is a truly unique project. Rector mentioned peculiarities of academic programs created in collaboration with the world’s leading universities, told the guests about faculty members and introduced the pre-university STEM program. He also informed the audience about the on-going admissions process.

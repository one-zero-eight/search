# Innopolis University announced results of the admission campaign 2015

> Страница относится к 2015 году: сведения на ней могли устареть.

27 октября 2015

More than 20,000 candidates applied for degree studies at Innopolis University.

![](https://media.innopolis.university/upload/iblock/9f3/result.jpg)

This year Innopolis University completed the first mass student selection for the Bachelor and Master’s degree programs. From March until June more than 700 applicants took part in the on-site selection. Candidates came to the IT University in Innopolis city to prove their academic knowledge and the English language skills and had interviews with the international faculty and representatives of the major companies of the Russian IT industry, in particular, Mail.Ru Group, Parallels, ABBYY, Etton, 2Gis, Flatstack, Nvision Group, ICL-КME CS, Infomatica, Polymedia, Acronis, and GK Lanit. According to the selection results, 352 people were enrolled at Innopolis University and started their studies in the field of Information Technology.

In total, 72 school graduates and 185 students passed the admission tests and received funding to pay for the 1st and the 3rd year of the Bachelor degree studies respectively. Meanwhile, 95 young IT experts were enrolled in the Master’s degree program of Innopolis University on the same funding conditions. The city of high technologies welcomed students from 45 Russian regions and 10 foreign countries.

“This year Innopolis University has completed the first mass selection of students. Over 20 thousands of school graduates, students and young IT experts applied to our university. I would like to note that the applicants proved their skills and high motivation for getting higher education in the field of IT. In the new academic year over 350 students have started their studies. On November 15th, we will announce the launch of the admission campaign 2016. Applicants and their parents are welcome to visit our University and learn about the educational environment, student accommodations and leisure activities”, – told Sergey Masyagin, Vice-Provost for Student Affairs, Enrollment and Admission of Innopolis University about the selection campaign.

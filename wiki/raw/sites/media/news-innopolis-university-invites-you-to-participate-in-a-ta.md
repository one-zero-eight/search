# Innopolis University invites you to participate in a talk about Maven and recommendation engines

> Страница относится к 2015 году: сведения на ней могли устареть.

8 августа 2015

The talk is organized by the Data Science and Information Systems Lab.

![](https://media.innopolis.university/upload/iblock/cc4/122.jpg)

Oleg Gusakov, System Architect from Cinarra Systems, Silicon Valley & Russian startup company and Emeritus Maven contributor, will talk about Maven – a project knowledge & build tool, which has a lot of buzz, and, sometimes, misconceptions about its utility and application, share his experience working on the software development process in global organizations. What is the difference between writing code and delivering software products. For those know what SW development process and build systems are and want to deepen their understanding of the subject. While presenting Oleg Gusakov will also deviate into recommendation engines. The working language is English.The talk will be held at the Innopolis University by the address: Innopolis city, 1 Universitetskaya St, room 108. The talk starts on August 12th at 15:00.

# The fourth grants guide in IT and Robotics of Innopolis University

> Страница относится к 2015 году: сведения на ней могли устареть.

25 декабря 2015

The guide includes information on competitions from the Ministry of Science and Education of the Russian Federation, Skolkovo Foundation, Russian Foundation for Basic Research and the Russian Science Foundation.

## Russian Foundation for Basic Research

[Competition on Scientific Projects by Young Researchers under Guidance of PhDs and ScDs at Scientific Institutions of the Russian Federation in 2016](http://www.rfbr.ru/rffi/ru/contests_announcement/o_1930664)

- Proposal submission deadline June 14, 2016

- Project support amount 100,000 RUB per month for PhD researchers and 80,000 RUB per month for non-PhD researchers

- Project term 1-5 calendar months

[Initiative Scientific Project Competition 2016 directed by Russian Foundation for Basic Research and Scientific Foundation of Austria](http://www.rfbr.ru/rffi/ru/international_announcement/o_1930312)

- Proposal deadline February 1, 2016. Proposal will be reviewed upon its receipt

- Project amount up to 1,000,000 RUB a year

- Project term 2016-2018

[Initiative Scientific Research Competition directed by Russian Foundation for Basic Research and German Scientific Research Society (Germany)](http://www.rfbr.ru/rffi/ru/international_announcement/o_1895583)

- Proposal deadline December 31, 2015

- Proposal will be reviewed upon its receipt.

- Project amount up to 1,500,000 RUB a year

- Project term 2016-2018

[Competition 2016 for Oriented Fundamental Scientific Research on Current Interdisciplinary Topics](http://www.rfbr.ru/rffi/ru/contests_announcement/o_1944094)

- Proposal submission deadline January 12, 2016

- Project support amount is determined by Foundation

Last year, the amount of funding was 3,000,000 Rub (per year):

- Project term 2016-2018

Research Topics

- Topic 604 Fundamental problems of cooperation between robot groups.

- Topic 607 Use of informational and analytical methods in the development of biomedical cellular and regenerative medicine technologies.

- Topic 608 Design of intelligent sensing and biomechatronic technologies to provide rehabilitation for the patients with serious injuries of sensomotor system based on the integration of the latest achievements in brain science, modern methods of adaptive processing and decoding of brain signals and success in mechatronics science advancement.

[Competition 2016 for Oriented Fundamental Scientific Research on Current Interdisciplinary Topics (Topic 609 Security and counter-terrorism)](http://www.rfbr.ru/rffi/ru/contests_announcement/o_1944667)

- Proposal submission deadline January 25, 2016

- Project support amount is determined by Foundation

Last year, the amount of funding was 3,000,000 Rub (per year):

- Project term 2016-2018

List of subject headings

609.1. Fundamental challenges in protection of large economic complexes against cyberattacks.

609.2. Problems of vulnerability detection in the automated control systems of the information infrastructure.

609.3. Development of the topological models of the virtual social systems and local social constructions.

609.4. Development of some new methods of linguistic and thematic text analysis to counter terrorism.

609.5. A case study of information-psychological impact of the Internet on the user conscience.

609.6. Fundamental research principles of the plants range based on the multispectral data of the remote sensing of the Earth for specialized criminalistic examinations.

609.7. New physicochemical solutions for creation and diagnostics of small-sized power-consuming supply sources based on advanced nanomaterials.

609.8. A case study of fundamental aspects of generation, registration and management of far infrared band electromagnetic interference to create remote diagnostic systems.

609.9. Development of mathematical models, methods and counter-terrorism security means of critical infrastructure and mass populated places.

609.10. A case study of cognitive analysis methods and object identification in oversized heterogeneous data flows.

- Project support amount:

- Project term 2016-2017

Russian Science Foundation

[Competition for grants in the priority area of RSF activities “Basic scientific research and exploratory scientific research, conducted by international research teams in cooperation with Taiwanese research teams.”](http://www.rscf.ru/sites/default/files/docfiles/CD-015_0.pdf)

- Proposal submission deadline March 15, 2016

- Project support amount from 4,000,000 RUB up to 6,000,000 RUB per year

- Project term 2016-2018

- Competition results: July 2016

Skolkovo Foundation

[Startup Tour 2016](https://startup-tour.ru/en/#roadshow)

Start - February, 2016

Prize fund – 100 000 000 rubles

For registration, use the link [https://startup-tour.ru/mnmx/en/register/](https://startup-tour.ru/mnmx/en/register/)

DIRECTIONS OF THE COMPETITION

- Information Security: data protection, network security, prevention of cyber threats, the protection of engineering and business systems, biometric and identification systems.

- Mobile Technologies and Platforms: devices and applications for health, wearable electronics, the new man-machine interfaces

- New Communication Systems and the Internet of Things: sensors and sensor networks, embedded control systems, smart city / smart home. The new radioelectronic devices.

- Information and Entertainment, Gaming and Educational Technologies. Devices and applications for augmented and virtual reality.

- Digital Manufacturing: systems for the development, testing and operation of new products and devices. Automation and the "digitization" of planning and management.

- Future of Retail: innovative technologies that provides a new level of interaction between buyer and seller.

- Intelligent robotics and autonomous vehicles.

# Research manuscript “Toward a human-like locomotion: Modelling dynamically stable locomotion of an anthropomorphic robot in Simulink environment” was accepted for ICINCO 2015

> Страница относится к 2015 году: сведения на ней могли устареть.

19 мая 2015

12th International Conference on Informatics in Control, Automation and Robotics (ICINCO 2015) will be held in Colmar, France on July 21-23, 2015.

The purpose of [ICINCO 2015](http://www.icinco.org/) is to bring together researchers, engineers and practitioners interested in the application of informatics to Control, Automation and Robotics. Four simultaneous tracks will be held, covering Intelligent Control Systems, Optimization, Robotics, Automation, Signal Processing, Sensors, Systems Modelling and Control, and Industrial Engineering, Production and Management.

Members of Intelligent Robotic Systems Laboratory prepared a research paper titled “Toward a human-like locomotion: Modelling dynamically stable locomotion of an anthropomorphic robot in Simulink environment”. The manuscript, which is indexed in [Scopus](http://www.scopus.com/) citation database of peer-reviewed literature, was accepted for ICINCO 2015.

# Students of Innopolis University placed first in the “Hack” nomination at the HackDay in Penza

> Страница относится к 2015 году: сведения на ней могли устареть.

29 сентября 2015

The team decided to present their first-place prize – a 3D-printer - to the University.

![](https://media.innopolis.university/upload/iblock/4ca/wPltmNgy30A.jpg)

Results of the HackDay, the two-day competition with 4 nominations, were summed up in Penza. In the end of the marathon, the participants introduced to the jury the prototype products created by the teams in the framework of the competition in three areas, i.e. “Startup”, “Tech”, “Hack” and “Original 3D application for the WEB”.

The WodaBata project became the winner in the first nomination - the “Startup”. It is a mobile and a web-application for functional training and adaptation of exercises to the individual user’s goals, needs and demands.

The Firtfly Live team consisting of two programmers, and graphic and sound designers placed first in the “Tech” nomination. It presented a program helping participants of music festivals to make shows using coloured lights and computer vision technologies.

The team of Innopolis University: Dumitru Savva, Ilgiz Mustafin, Andrey Tropin and Gheorghe Pinzaru won in the “Hack” nomination. It took two days for the teammates to work on ideas that will help journalists to prevent the loss of important video records. As a result, the team developed the Optimus Video program and a cloud platform. Using this application, all the materials recorded on any portable media with Internet access will be automatically downloaded on a server during the video recording. Materials will be available even in case of a technical failure, mechanical damage or loss of the recording device.

“I am grateful to Innopolis University for giving me the opportunity to take part in this event and check my knowledge and skills. Thanks to the “HackDay” I got an invaluable experience that will help me to improve my programming skills. I'd like to give special thanks to my teammates for the hard work during these days which has let us win”.

In total, the HackDay event welcomed more than 100 participants from Russia, who presented 21 project developed exclusively in the framework of the hackathon in 48 hours.

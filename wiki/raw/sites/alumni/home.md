## IU Alumni Awards

IU Award for outstanding graduates and community leaders is given from 2021. This honorable recognition is awarded for special achievements in professional and other socially important areas, as well as for contribution to fostering relationships between Innopolis University and its graduates.

Candidates for IU Awards are proposed by alumni themselves.Final voting is conducted among all graduates, professorship team, IU administration and current students. Moreover, previous winners' candidacy is not considered.

- common questions from IU alumni

Can't remember my password from IU email. How can I solve this?

If you have forgotten or lost your password for @innopolis domain, please request one from it@innopolis.ru, and they will send password recovery instructions to you. The alumni mailbox is not blocked, you can use it without expiration date.

I am IU graduate, how can I order an academic reference?

You can order an academic reference on www.my.university.innopolis.ru. Just leave your request in References section and in it must be ready for collection within 7 days in office 319. You can also ask @StudentAffairs_bot for its scan in case you cannot pick it up in office 319.

My diploma is still in IU, how can I obtain it?

You can get your diploma in office 319 on weekdays from 9am to 6pm. Before receiving it, you'll have to complete the checkout sheet to ensure you have returned all properties that belong to IU. If you are in another city or country, please write to @StudentAffairs_bot, and we'll try to find a way to send documents by mail.

How can I order European Diploma Supplement?

The European Diploma Supplement is an additional educational document in English, that you can order by messaging @StudentAffairs_bot. Once your request is received we'll send you an application template and payment details for document processing services. The cost of the EDP is 2000 rubles, processing time: 1 month.

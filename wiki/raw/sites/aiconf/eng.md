Digital twins, art generators, chatbots, and code completion have reignited discussions about the future of artificial intelligence. Currently, AI is revolutionizing our world and is considered the most prominent technological trend in both business and government.

A total of 19 expert talks with industry professionals will focus on effectively incorporating AI into businesses and governments at four different levels, ranging from global to urban.

Who will benefit from the conference?

Join us to gain fresh perspectives on growth and expanding into new markets. Network with potential investors, partners, and emerging start-ups.

Transnational companies

Find potential investors, donors and partners to grow your company.

SMEs and start-ups

Exchange insights on how to effectively integrate artificial intelligence into your business and production workflows.

Big business

Learn about the development and control of national AI strategies, and exchange knowledge on how governments can effectively collaborate with businesses.

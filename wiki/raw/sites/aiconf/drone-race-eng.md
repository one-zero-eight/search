Drone Race Rules

NON PRO (amateurs)

PRO (team classification)

Pilots go through qualifying sorties according to the rules of MultiGP. Based on the qualification results, a list of top 16 pilots is formed. The following sorties are held according to the rules of Double Elimination (elimination after two defeats).

Two-person teams: one controls the drone, the second one performs the role of a mechanic in the pit stop zone. After 50 laps, they change places. Compete in passing 100 laps of the track for speed.

See you at the international drone racing competition!

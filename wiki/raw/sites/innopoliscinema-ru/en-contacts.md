ATTENTION

Tickets refund in case of cancellation / change (of a session or terms of the show):

- You will be refunded automatically. In certain circumstances you might need to fill in a refund form. You can find the refund policy in your e-ticket.

Tickets refund in case of other circumstances (excluding cancellation):

Tickets bought online via [Rambler/Kassa](https://kassa.rambler.ru/):

- Refund should be claimed at least 30 minutes before the advertised show start time. You can get a full refund via [the online form](https://kassa.rambler.ru/refund)

- If the show has ended or there is less than 30 minutes till the start, please contact us via e-mail

Tickets bought online via other online services:

- Please, find the refund policy in your e-ticket.

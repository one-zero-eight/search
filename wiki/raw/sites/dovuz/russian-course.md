Practical focus

During the intensive course, participants analyze their findings and immediately explain them with examples. Throughout the course, you'll practice speaking Russian, helping you overcome the language barrier.

Individualized approach

Topics are selected based on the needs and abilities of students at a given level. The program includes authentic materials covering such aspects of language development as listening, speaking, reading, and writing

This course is designed for international applicants planning to study at IU. It covers three levels — A1, A2, and B1 — and develops essential skills in Russian phonetics, grammar, and everyday communication.

Online group classes focus on spoken and written practice and provide targeted preparation for the corresponding TORFL (TEU / TBU / TORFL-I) examinations required for university admission. At the end of the course students are granted a certificate that is required for IU admission.

II level of the Olympiad in the list of RCSO (Russian Council of School Olympiads)

Innopolis Open Olympiad in Math is held for students who are passionate about mathematics and related disciplines in order to involve them in the Olympiad movement, develop a competitive spirit and the ability to make decisions quickly.

At the stages of the Olympiad, participants solve complex problems using their knowledge and skills from different areas of mathematics: logic, combinatorics, number theory, geometry, theory of algorithms and properties of polynomials, probability theory and others.

II level of the Olympiad in the list of RCSO( Russian Council of School Olympiads)

Innopolis Open in Сyber Security is designed for students who are ready to demonstrate their analytical skills to solve problems in the field of the main categories of cyber security: OS administration, net, forensics, cryptography, reverse, web, etc.

Olympiad Stages (online)

Registration is open till 6th of December, 2024

1st qualification round (individual, online): December 8, 2024

Start at 11:00

The stage will be held in CTF task-based format. Participants will individually solve tasks from the categories listed below. For the number of solved tasks and the time spent, points will be awarded and a rating will be formed. Participants with the required number of points advance to the team stage.

2nd round (in teams of 5 people, online): January 18-19, 2025

The stage will be held in the Pentest format. Participants will need to find vulnerabilities in a dedicated machine and compile a report on the work done. The participants from the teams with the required number of points pass to the final.

Final round (offline, in Innopolis University): February 21-24, 2025

Participation format: individual tour (CTF task-based) and team tour (Pentest)

III level of the Olympiad in the list of RCSO( Russian Council of School Olympiads)

Innopolis Open in Robotics is dedicated to those who love not only to see, but also to "touch" the result of their work. Participants can gain skills and knowledge that will help in solving real human problems. For example, to simplify people's lives by automating production.The tasks are developed by methodologists of Innopolis University and employees of IT companies. The Olympiad is designed to identify the most talented students programming robots.

Olympiad stages (online)

Registration is open till 22th of November, 2024.

1st qualification round (individual) November 23, 2024 - December 8 2024

The first qualifying stage is dedicated to pumping and testing knowledge in computer science and programming. Participants will benefit from knowledge of recursion, combinatorics and other sports programming techniques. The tour is common for the participants of the profiles "Robotics" and "Artificial Intelligence", the participants can continue to participate in the second rounds of both profiles.

2nd round (Individual): January 13 - February 10, 2025

The second qualifying stage will prepare participants to solve robotics problems from areas relevant to the current Olympiad season: sensors, navigation and localization, kinematics and odometry. The quality of the material assimilation by the participants will be assessed with the help of interesting tasks.

During the second round, several webinars will be held, at the first of them we will tell you in detail about the conditions of the tasks of the tour. At the following webinars, we will answer questions about approaches to solving problems, explain theoretical material from courses and video lectures for its better assimilation by participants.

Final round: March 3-8, 2025

The final stage consists of several rounds, the first of which is solving computer science problems, similar to the first remote tour. This tour will be held in the format of individual participation. To participate in the next round, participants will be grouped into teams of 2 people, after which they will receive robotic platforms and tasks for the main final stage of the Olympiad.

Spend a week in the walls of Innopolis University with like-minded people with benefits:

- meet with like-minded roboticists

- lectures from partners

and of course, the opportunity to become the winner of the Olympiad with the level of RSOSH 3

The Innopolis International Olympiad is available for students in grades 7-11(12). Schoolchildren from Russia and other countries are invited to participate.

Winners and prize-winners will receive:

– bonuses for admission to Innopolis University;

– merch and gifts from Innopolis University;

– points in the service "Another Matter";

Top 5 winners of each direction will receive personal consultations with experts.

To participate in the Olympiad, go to the "Profiles" section, select the area you are interested in, click on the registration button and fill in the data in the form

The Olympiad consists of several stages, including online qualifying stages and the final stage of the competition (face-to-face). Detailed dates, format and venues are indicated on the pages of each Olympiad profile.

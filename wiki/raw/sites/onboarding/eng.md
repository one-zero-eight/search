FAQ

Smart intercoms and biometric locks work in the houses.

To set up, call a specialist:

Intercom: 8(843) 222−22−22 ext. four

Biometrics: 8(800) 555−46−62.

In case of errors or problems in the apartment upon check-in, contact the [commandant](https://t.me/inno_comendant).

Also in all houses there is a parking where you can reserve a place. To do this, you need to contact [@Parking_Innopolis](https://t.me/Parking_Innopolis)

The [concierge service](http://t.me/InnopolisHelp) will prompt you with all the necessary information and give you the contacts of the essential specialists

We made sure that the city has all the necessary services: registry office, MFC (multifunctional center), cafes and restaurants, post office, notary services, hostel, atelier, consumer services center, laundry, beauty salons, flower shop, sports equipment rental, bank branches, ATMs, OZON, Kazan Express, Wildberries.

All of them are located within walking distance for your convenience.

Addresses, telephone and work schedule can be inquired from [Inna](https://t.me/InnaHelpBot) or the [concierge service](http://t.me/InnopolisHelp)

There is a Medical Center in the city, where doctors from the state autonomous healthcare institution "City Hospital No. 7" conduct appointments. The Children’s Polyclinic operates in the building of the Medical Center, patients are received by specialists from the Children’s Republican Clinical Hospital. Also in Innopolis there is a dental clinic "City Dentistry" and a private laboratory "DIALAB" and three pharmacies.

[Inna](https://t.me/InnaHelpBot) or [concierge service](http://t.me/InnopolisHelp) will tell you all the details

Shuttles run between Innopolis and Kazan. There is also a route to Upper Uslon. You can find out about the schedule, stops and location through [@InnoHelpBot](https://t.me/InnoHelpBot) on Telegram or by phone +7 800 222-22-87.

Intercity buses:

Innopolis ⟷ Kazan (106, 108) — 160 rubles by card and 200 rubles in cash.

Innopolis ⟷ Verkhny Uslon — 50 rubles, route through the village. Kuralovo — 100 rubles

Also in Innopolis there is a [chat of fellow road tripper](https://t.me/joinchat/BZaU2UDW8zpUizpiMvDRQA)

The service is free, the taxi can be used by everyone who is already 7 years old. Children from 7 to 18 years old can ride on an unmanned taxi accompanied by their parents. You can call a taxi through the Yandex GO app

The entire necessary educational system has been created in the city — day-cares, a school, a physics and mathematics lyceum and a university.

You can learn more about education and admission through the [concierge service](http://t.me/InnopolisHelp)

In your spare time in Innopolis, you can go to the [Artspace](https://t.me/ArtSpaceEvents) urban space to watch a movie or play a quiz, find a new hobby in , come up with an idea for a startup on the treadmill of the [Innopolis sports complex](/raw/sites/sport/home.md) and relax in the massage and yoga center [I_O_GA center](https://t.me/I_o_ga)

In total, Innopolis has more than 200 chats and channels: volunteer movement, ecology and separate collection of waste, skiers' chat, urbanism and others.

To understand, go to [chat with chats](https://t.me/innolinks) and find what is interesting for you

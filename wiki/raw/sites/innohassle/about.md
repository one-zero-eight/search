# About one-zero-eight

About us

## Welcome to

one-zero-eight community!

## Who we are

one-zero-eight is a student community enthusiastic about technology, striving to improve the Innopolis University environment. We develop projects, participate in hackathons, organize events, combining learning, collaboration, and fun.

## Projects

We develop projects for Innopolis University students and faculty. Our flagship project is [InNoHassle](https://innohassle.ru/).

The website is an ecosystem that brings together all team services and useful Innopolis resources for students in one place.

We are not limited to a single website: we also build internal university tools and maintain the sports website. See the full project list [here](https://github.com/one-zero-eight/.github/blob/main/profile/README.md).

Let us know if you have ideas for our projects or run into any issues.

[Report an issue](https://forms.gle/2vMmu4vSoVShvbMw6)

## Development

We love open source, we are open source, and we support open source. Projects for Innopolis University are built by students and everyone who cares, on a voluntary basis. You can support us by [contributing](https://github.com/orgs/one-zero-eight/projects/4).

Our areas of interest include product engineering, NLP, robotics, and whatever else comes our way. We are constantly expanding our skills through hands-on experience, including hackathons.

[Our GitHub](https://github.com/one-zero-eight)[Our task board](https://github.com/orgs/one-zero-eight/projects/4)[CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/one-zero-eight/.github/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md)

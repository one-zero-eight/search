## Fitness test

I'm from Special 2 group, how can I pass the fitness test?

You will defend a project. It will be an presentation for 10 minutes and 5 minutes for questions. Check out your email box. There will be instructions.

I'm from Special 1 health group? Do I need to pass fitness test?

Yes, you are. But without any standards. In other words, you need to participate in this event. And to make your best according to your health and abilities, as much as you think it won't hurt you.

When will be the retake?

First retake will be 24.09.2022.

Second retake will be 26.11.2022.

## Medical check-up

Why do I need to undergo a medical check-up?

To be able to get hours, and not just visit a sports complex.

What kind of certificate do I need to get? Will the help on the form "086u" be suitable?

You need to get a certificate about the health group for physical education classes. If the therapist indicates a health group in the reference "086u", then this will also work.

What should I do to get a medical check up in Innopolis?

1) If you are a student from another region of Russia, you need to change your OMS medical insurance to a local one. 2) Go to the reception at the medical center and say that you are a student and want to pass a medical check-up to get a health group. 3) You will need to pass tests and bring a fluorography (optional) in printed form.

How can I change my OMS medical insurance to a local one?

1) At the beginning of the semester, we invite insurance agents to do this. If you couldn't change it with our help, then: 2) Find any insurance point on the map of Kazan and change it yourself there. For example: 65 Vosstaniya str., Kazan, Rep. Tatarstan, 420095 or call the insurance company's hotline.

Do I need to pass medical exmination again?

If you're 2,3 and 4 year student and your medical group is Special 2 then yes. If general and preparation group then no.

## Sport hours

How many hours should I get per semester?

You should get 30 hours.

How can I get sport hours throught a semester?

Firstly, choose a sport or sportclub for whole semester. Secondly, try to train at least once per week on schedule. Additional opportunities to get extra hours: 1) Use self sports variants and upload results on site. 2) Participate on sports events.

What are online students supposed to do to pass the sport course?

The same as for students with Special 2 health group.

I have medical reference about illness what do I need to do?

Upload this reference on site. After approving you'll get 2 sport hours per week depending on the time frame.

I have an injury or illness? What have I do to do?

Take a medical reference and upload it to the site.

How can I change choosen sport?

The selected sport will be reset automatically at the beginning of the new semester.

I`m an online student, why do I need to pass online if I got 30 hours?

The exam consists of 2 parts. 1) Get 30 hours. 2) Pass the fitness test. You can't pass the fitness test, so you're defending the project online. Online students can pass by doing only the project.

## Admission for fall 2026

### APPLY FOR GRANT

Register on admissions portal account, fill in an application form with all the mandatory fields, take the tests (English and profile testing) and click “SUBMIT” to complete the application.

GO THROUGH THE CONTEST

Successful applications are selected for further consideration. Learn more about selection criteria on a particular program’s page.

[Learn more](https://apply.innopolis.university/en/faq/)

### GET THE OFFER

Get the offer by email and confirm it. Provide all enrollment and visa documents, pay tuition fee (for Partial Grant candidates) and come to Innopolis in mid-August.

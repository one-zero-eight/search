Remote connection to a workstation (PC)

Connecting from another network to the University workstation

This instruction will describe the steps to remotely connect to a corporate PC from another computer.

To connect to a remote computer you need to make a request for a file.

Log in to the portal [https://ssp.innopolis.university](https://ssp.innopolis.university) (login and password are the same as for email or computer login)

Go to “IT services for employees”

![](https://help.university.innopolis.ru/images/image_1699006412427.png)

Select "Request remote connection to workstation"

![](https://help.university.innopolis.ru/images/image_1699006429452.png)

Click the "Submit" button. After processing the request, a file will be sent to your work email address for remote connection to your work computer

![](https://help.university.innopolis.ru/images/image_1699006573722.png)

Download this RDP file and open it from your home computer. The computer you want to connect to must be turned on.

The connection file may not arrive immediately, you need to wait a little

![](https://help.university.innopolis.ru/images/image_1699006653004.png)

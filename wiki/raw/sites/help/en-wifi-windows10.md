## ¶ Connection for guests

Guests of the Innopolis University must connect to wireless network (Wi-Fi) «UniversityGuest» according to the manual below

- Click the network icon and select "UniversityGuest" from the network list

![](https://help.university.innopolis.ru/images_en/image_1699437464597.png)

- Wait for lobby page in your browser (Edge, Chrome, Mozilla Firefox). Enter your mobile phone number and click "Получить код" (Get the code) button. Next, enter a code from the SMS and click "Ввести код" (Confirm). Connection is complete

![](https://help.university.innopolis.ru/images_en/image_1699437472730.png)

## ¶ Сonnection for employees

- Сlick the network icon and select "UniversityStaff" from the network list:

![](https://help.university.innopolis.ru/images_en/image_1699437477784.png)

- Click "Connect" button. In "Username" and "Password" fields enter your domain credentials. For example, i.ivanov@innopolis.ru and your e-mail password. Confirm the connection in the appeared window by clicking "Connect". Connection is complete.

![](https://help.university.innopolis.ru/images_en/image_1699437489354.png)

## ¶ Сonnection for students

- Click the network icon and select "UniversityStudent" from the network list

![](https://help.university.innopolis.ru/images_en/image_1699437489397.png)

- Click "Connect" button. In "Username" and "Password" fields enter your domain credentials. For example, i.ivanov@innopolis.ru and your e-mail password. Confirm the connection in the appeared window by clicking "Connect". Connection is complete.

![](https://help.university.innopolis.ru/images_en/image_1699437493496.png)

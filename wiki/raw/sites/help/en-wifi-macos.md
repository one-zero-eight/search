## ¶ Connection for guests

Select "UniversityGuest" from the networks list. Wait for the opening of your web browser window (for example, Google Chrome, Safari).

![](https://help.university.innopolis.ru/images_en/image_1699447129524.png)

In this window, enter your mo-bile phone number and click "Получить код" (Get the code) button. Next, enter a code from the SMS and click " Ввести код " (Confirm).

Connection is complete

![](https://help.university.innopolis.ru/images_en/image_1699447147306.png)

## ¶ Connection for employees

Click the network icon and se-lect "UniversityStaff" from the networks list.

![](https://help.university.innopolis.ru/images_en/image_1699447180418.png)

In "Username" and "Password" fields, enter the name of your University lnnopolis domain ac-count and password, click "Join".

Connection is complete.

![](https://help.university.innopolis.ru/images_en/image_1699447200899.png)

## ¶ Connection for students

Click the network icon and se-lect "UniversityStudent" from the networks list.

![](https://help.university.innopolis.ru/images_en/image_1699447222165.png)

In "Username" and "Password" fields, enter the name of your University lnnopolis domain ac-count and password, click "Join".

Connection is complete.

![](https://help.university.innopolis.ru/images_en/image_1699447244722.png)

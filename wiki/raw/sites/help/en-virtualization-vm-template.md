## ¶ Fields required for completing the request

Please provide the following information to create a request for resource allocation.

- Field 1: (Characteristics)

- Field 2: (Departament)

- Field 3: (Project/Product)

- Field 4: (Environment)

Please specify the characteristics of the virtual machine (VM):

- Hostname

- CPU

- RAM

- HDD

- OS

- Subnet

Write your department:

- CNT – Center for Oil and Gas Technologies

- IGT – InnoGeoTech

- CRPO – Software Development Center

- DIT – Department of Information Technologies

- PISH – Advanced Engineering School

- IFE – Institute of Further Education

- AII – Artificial Intelligence Institute

- MO – Marketing Office

- CGIS – Center for Geographic Information Systems

- DUD – Department of University Development

- ISC – Information Security Center

- CE – Center of Energetics

- CDEPD – Center for Digital Economy Projects Development

- DSPC – Digital Solutions Promoting Center

- ICDS – Intersectoral Center for Design Solutions

- LCBR – Leading Center for Blockchain Research

- IU – Innopolis University

- RC - Robotics Center

- CUT - Center for Unmanned Technologies

- Project/Product - Project/Product Name

- URL: Project/Product URL, for example, a link in AzureDevOPS or a resource link.

- Owner Department – Specify the email of the user for whom the VM is being created.

- Expired – Specify the VM expiration date in the format DD.MM.YYYY

- Provide justification/necessity for creating the VM. Brief information on what is planned to be created/launched on it.

Environment - Specify the project environment

- TEST – testing environment

- DEV – development environment

- STAGE – pre-production environment

- PROD – production environment

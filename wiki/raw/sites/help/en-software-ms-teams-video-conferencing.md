How to Set Up Video Conferencing

Microsoft Teams allows to make videoconferences with teammates. It is very convenient to hold online meetings.

Making videocalls in MS Teams:

To make videocall you must open chat with person and at the top of the chat window you can choose option to communicate.

![](https://help.university.innopolis.ru/images/image_1699350079725.png)

Audio call

Making meeting in MS Teams

- Open required channel and at the top of the channel window click “Meet”

![](https://help.university.innopolis.ru/images/image_1699350106829.png)

- Enter meeting topic and click button “Meet now”

- In the search bar you can find and invite meeting participants

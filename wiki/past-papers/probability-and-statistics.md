3. (8 pts) Approximately 86 450 malware attacks occur in our country every day. Calculate the approximate
probability that at least 1 attack occurs in one second.

Замечание: точность приближения до второго десятичного разряда (включительно) будет достаточно.

Solution. It’s best to use the Poisson approximation in this question. The day has 24 × 3600 = 86400
seconds, so approximately 1.0006 ≈ 1 attack occurs during one second. We use ξ ∼ Poi(1) as the model.
Thus,
P(ξ ≥ 1) = 1 − P(ξ = 0) ≈ 1 − e−1 ≈ 1 − 0.37 ≈ 0.63.

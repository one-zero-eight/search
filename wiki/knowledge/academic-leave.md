# Academic leave

## Grounds

An academic leave is granted to students of the university's main professional educational programmes (students and postgraduates) when they are temporarily unable to master the programme [1]. The grounds are [1]:

- conscription for military service or concluding a contract for military service (in short, military service) [1];
- medical indications [1];
- other exceptional cases, including natural disasters and family circumstances [1].

## Duration

A leave may not exceed 12 calendar months at a time [2]. The exception is a leave in connection with military service or on medical indications, whose term may not exceed 2 years [2]. The number of academic leaves granted to a student during study on the given programme is not limited [2].

## Application and documents

The basis for the decision is the student's personal application addressed to the director of the university (form in annex 1), together with [3]:

- a medical conclusion issued by a medical organisation belonging to the state or municipal healthcare system, for a leave on medical indications [3];
- a mobilization order, instruction or summons from the military commissariat containing the time and place of departure to military service, or a copy of the military service contract certified in the manner established by Russian legislation, for a leave in connection with military service [3];
- other documents confirming the grounds, if available [3].

If for objective reasons (in particular military service, medical indications, force majeure) the student cannot submit the application and documents in person, the application (form in annex 2) may be submitted by the student's legal representative, by a person acting under a power of attorney issued by the student, by registered mail with acknowledgement of receipt, or in another available way [4].

## Who decides

For a leave in connection with military service or on medical indications, the decision is taken by the director of the university, a person acting as director, or another official authorised by them (together, the Head) [5]. In other exceptional cases, and where the student cannot produce documents confirming the grounds, the decision is taken by a commission specially created by the university, the commission on academic leaves [6].

The commission considers applications for leave in other exceptional cases (including natural disasters and family circumstances) and cases where supporting documents cannot be produced, decides to grant or refuse a leave, and decides on its extension [7]. A refusal must be reasoned [8].

The commission consists of a chairperson and at least 4 members; a secretary is appointed by an order of the Head to support its work and is not a member of the commission, and the chairperson, members and secretary are university employees responsible for organising and carrying out educational activity [9]. The composition of the commission is approved by an order of the Head for an indefinite term [10]; it may be approved for separate levels of professional education, for a combination of such levels, or for the whole university [11].

The university sends the application to the commission within 2 working days of its receipt by the structural units responsible for work with students [12]. The commission considers the application and attached documents within no more than 5 working days of receiving them from the university [13]. A session is valid if at least half of the members are present, and sessions may be held in person or remotely by video conference [14]. Decisions are taken by a simple majority of the members, and in a tie the chairperson's vote is decisive [15].

On the day the application reaches the commission, the secretary of the commission brings the draft agenda of the session to the members and agrees with them the date, time, format and, if necessary, place of the session [16]. Several applications may be considered at one session, but each application and its documents are considered personally [17]. Persons who are neither members of the commission nor its secretary may not be present at a session [18].

## Formalisation and publication

The grant of a leave is formalised by a university order issued by the Head no later than 5 working days from the date the decision was taken [19]. Information about the leave is brought to students' notice without the student's surname, name and patronymic, with the insurance number of the individual personal account (if any) or the student's unique code and the details of the order, in telecommunications networks including the student's personal account or on the university's official website [20]. The commission's decision is communicated in the same way [21].

The secretary keeps the minutes of the commission's sessions [22]. A decision of the commission is formalised by the minutes of the session, which are signed by the chairperson and the secretary; after signature the secretary sends a copy of the minutes to the responsible structural units [23].

The regulation itself is brought to students' notice by placing it on the university's official website in the section "Сведения об образовательной организации" [24].

## During the leave

The student is released from the duties connected with mastering the educational programme and is not admitted to the educational process until the leave is over [25]. If the student studies under an education contract at the expense of a natural person and/or a legal entity, no tuition fee is charged during the leave [26]. Disciplinary measures may not be applied to students during the leave [27]. Time spent on leave is not included in the period set by the university for clearing academic debts [28]. The use of the residential complex, the payment of scholarships and other forms of material support during the leave are determined by the university's local normative acts [29]. If a leave is granted to a foreign citizen or a stateless person, the university notifies other organisations of this in accordance with the list, procedure and time limits established by the relevant normative legal acts and/or local normative acts [30].

## End and extension

A leave ends at the end of the period for which it was granted, or before the end of that period on the student's application (form in annex 4); in the latter case the student is admitted to study by an order of the Head [31]. If, due to circumstances beyond the student's control, the student has not resumed the duties connected with mastering the programme at the end of the leave, the commission may decide to extend the leave [32].

In that case the staff of the responsible structural units report the fact to the commission on the day it is discovered, with information on the specific circumstances beyond the student's control and the relevant documents, if any [33]. The commission considers this information within no more than 5 working days [34]. It decides either to extend the leave by 12 calendar months or to refuse the extension, and a refusal must be reasoned [35]. The model form of the minutes on the extension decision is given in annex 6 to the regulation [36], and matters not regulated by clause 3.7 are governed by section 2 and clause 3.6 of the regulation [37].

## What the procedure on educational relations says

Under the procedure on the emergence, suspension and termination of educational relations, educational relations are suspended when a student is granted an academic leave under the procedure established by order of the Ministry of Education and Science of Russia of 13.06.2013 No. 455 [38]. That procedure names as grounds the impossibility of mastering a secondary vocational or higher education programme for medical indications, family and other circumstances, and sets a period not exceeding two years [39]. A leave may be granted an unlimited number of times [40].

Under that procedure the basis for the decision is the student's personal application together with a conclusion of a medical commission of a medical organisation (for medical indications), a summons of the military commissariat containing the time and place of departure to military service (for conscription), and documents confirming the ground if available [40]. The decision is taken by the director of the university or an official authorised by them within the period that procedure calls "десятидневный срок" (a period counted in days), from receipt of the application and the attached documents, and is formalised by an order [41].

During the leave the student is released from the duties connected with mastering the programme and is not admitted to the educational process until the leave ends; where the student studies under a contract, no tuition fee is charged during the leave [42]. The leave ends at the end of the period for which it was granted, or before its end on the student's application, and the student is admitted to study by an order of the director [42].

The same procedure also provides that students on leave for medical indications are assigned and paid monthly compensation payments under a decree of the Government of the Russian Federation of 3 November 1994 No. 1206 [43].

The two documents differ: the procedure sets a single maximum of two years for any leave [39], while the academic leave regulation sets 12 calendar months at a time, with up to 2 years only for military service or medical indications [2]; and the procedure gives the decision to the director or an authorised official in every case [41], while under the academic leave regulation the commission on academic leaves decides in other exceptional cases [6], and the medical conclusion must come from a medical organisation of the state or municipal healthcare system [3].

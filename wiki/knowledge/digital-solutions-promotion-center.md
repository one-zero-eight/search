# Digital Solutions Promotion Center (Центр продвижения цифровых решений) — place, tasks, functions and rights

## The document

The page is the Regulation on the Digital Solutions Promotion Center (Центр продвижения цифровых решений) of ANO VO «Innopolis University» [1]. It regulates the Center's activity and determines its tasks, functions, rights and duties [2]. The page presents the Regulation as approved by order of the University № ОД/АД-03/2021/07/07, signed by R.F. Valiev, the director for development and HR policy [1]. The wiki copy was read by OCR, so its numbers and dates should be checked against the PDF [1].

## Place in the structure

- The Center is a structural subdivision of the University's Department for Project and Research Activity (Управление по проектной и научно-исследовательской деятельности) and belongs to the category of other structural subdivisions (иные структурные подразделения) [3].
- The Center reports directly to the vice-rector — head of the Department for Project and Research Activity [4].
- In its activity the Center is guided by the Constitution of the Russian Federation, the Labour Code of the Russian Federation, the Federal Law of 29.12.2012 № 273-ФЗ «Об образовании в Российской Федерации», other legislative and normative legal acts regulating the activity of higher education institutions, the University's Charter, decisions of the University's Academic, Supervisory and Operational councils, orders and instructions of the University director, other local acts and this Regulation [5].
- The structure, headcount and staffing table of the Center, and changes to them, are approved by the University director [1].
- The Center is headed by a head of the Center, hired and dismissed by order of the University director [6].
- The working conditions of the Center's employees are determined by the employment contracts concluded with each employee and by the University's internal labour rules [7]; their rights, duties and degree of responsibility are set by job descriptions [8].
- Within their competence the Center's employees may be involved in work under grants, projects, subsidies and other programmes carried out by the University [9].
- The Regulation and additions and amendments to it are approved by order of the University director or another person authorised by the director [10].

## Tasks

- Creating competitive digital solutions and software on the base of the University [11].
- Analysing the results of scientific and research work in order to determine the possibility and expediency of their commercialisation [12].
- Providing consulting services in the commercialisation of the University's intellectual property and scientific-technical products: developing a commercialisation strategy, preparing a commercialisation plan and so on [13].
- Conducting marketing research to determine the market potential and to analyse the sales markets of the University's intellectual property and scientific-technical products [14].
- Preparing advertising and information products to promote the results of the University's scientific-technical activity at exhibitions, round tables, meetings and so on in the interests of industrial partners [15].
- Preparing and holding negotiations with potential investors, licensees and buyers of the University's intellectual property and scientific-technical products, in order to help the University's subdivisions and the authors of intellectual activity results and scientific-technical products to realise intellectual property objects commercially [16].
- Consulting the University's employees and students on managing its intellectual property and scientific-technical products [17].
- Developing cooperation with various legal entities to promote the University's intellectual property and scientific-technical products [18].
- Taking part in preparing innovative projects connected with introducing the University's intellectual property, for presenting them to investment committees, industrial enterprises and corporations in order to obtain financing [19].
- Developing and introducing robotic-technological complexes for various purposes for industrial customers [20].
- Forming the technical reporting documentation on the digital solutions being developed [21].

## Functions

- Conducting explanatory work on the commercialisation and introduction into production of the results of scientific and innovation activity [22].
- Initiating and organising marketing events connected with the University's activity [23].
- Meeting the key performance indicators set by the management [24].
- Analysing the internal business processes of customer companies [25].
- Carrying out the full cycle of development, packaging and support of digital solutions and software created on the base of the University [26].

## Rights

- Requesting from the University's structural subdivisions the information and materials needed for the work within the Center's competence [27].
- Involving, with the consent of the heads of the University's structural subdivisions, their employees for preparing drafts of local acts and other documents and for developing and carrying out the Center's activities [28].
- Taking part in meetings held by the University's management where matters within the Center's competence are discussed [29].
- Proposing to the University's management the repeal of, amendments to and additions to the University's local acts [30].
- Interacting with state authorities and bodies of administration, legal entities and subdivisions of the University on matters within the Center's competence [31].

## Interaction with other subdivisions

- Interaction with the other structural subdivisions is determined by the tasks and functions assigned to the Center by this Regulation [32].
- With the Personnel Selection Sector it concerns hiring into project and product teams [33].
- With the Finance and Economics Department it concerns attracting third persons to support the Center's activity within that department's competence [34].
- The Center may be a base for the practical preparation of the University's students [35].
- Interaction with the other structural subdivisions concerns preparing drafts of local acts, orders, instructions, contracts and other documents and preparing opinions and written and oral reports on matters within the Center's competence that arise in practical activity [36].

## Reporting

On the basis of the relevant local acts, orders and instructions the Center may be responsible, within its activity, for preparing and keeping up to date information, reports and accounts [37].

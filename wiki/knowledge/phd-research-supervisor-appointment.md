# PhD research supervisor — appointment, release, duties and rights (Порядок of 27 November 2024)

## The document

The Порядок назначения научного руководителя аспиранту в АНО ВО «Университет Иннополис» was approved by order of the University of 27 November 2024 № ОД/АД-02/2024/11/27 and is dated Innopolis, 2024 [1]. It establishes the procedure and conditions of appointing a research supervisor for an aspirant and of releasing him from supervision, and the supervisor's rights and duties [2]. Its requirements are obligatory for all structural units of the University that take part in implementing aspirantura programmes [3].

The Порядок was developed in accordance with Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29 December 2012, Government Decree No. 2122 of 30 November 2021 on the preparation of scientific and scientific-pedagogical staff, Ministry of Science and Higher Education order No. 951 of 20 October 2021 on the federal state requirements to aspirantura programmes, the University's Устав and other acts [4]. It is approved by order of the Director or an authorised person and enters into force on approval; amendments are made by the same order [5], [6].

## What the supervisor does for the aspirant

Within the programme the aspirant carries out research under the guidance of the supervisor in order to prepare the dissertation of Candidate of Sciences for defence; this preparation includes fulfilling the individual plan of research activity and writing, formatting and presenting the dissertation for the final attestation [7].

## Who may be a supervisor

The supervisor is appointed from among the University's employees and from among persons engaged in implementing the aspirantura programme under a civil-law contract [8]. The supervisor must:

- hold a doctor's degree, or a candidate's degree, or a degree obtained in a foreign state and recognised in the Russian Federation [9];
- carry out research (or take part in it) in the corresponding direction within the scientific specialty over the last 3 years [9];
- have at least 2 publications on the results of that activity in peer-reviewed domestic and (or) foreign scientific journals and editions over the last 3 years [9];
- have at least 2 approbations of the results of that activity, including reports on the topic at Russian and (or) international conferences, over the last 3 years [9].

Where the programme provides for interdisciplinary research or is implemented in a network form, the aspirant may have 2 supervisors, or a supervisor and a scientific consultant, including one from among the employees of another organisation; the consultant must hold a candidate's or doctor's degree, or a foreign degree recognised in Russia [10].

## How a supervisor is permitted and appointed

- A candidate for supervision forms information on his research activity in the established form (appendices 1, 2) and gives it to the head of the responsible structural unit by 20 January of the year of admission to aspirantura programmes [11].
- An expert commission created under a local act of the University examines that information by 1 February of the year of admission and gives the results to the head of the responsible structural unit (appendix 3) [12].
- The Учёный совет of the University decides to permit supervision, on the representation of the head of the responsible structural unit [13].
- Not later than 30 calendar days from the date the aspirantura programme starts, the Director or another person authorised by him appoints by order the supervisor and the scientific consultant (if any) and approves the dissertation topic within the programme and the University's main directions of research [14].
- The appointment is made on the written consent of the candidate for supervision (appendix 5) [15].
- For the draft order the head of the responsible structural unit gives the unit responsible for organising aspirantura studies an extract from the minutes of the Учёный совет meeting permitting the supervision, the results of the expert review (appendix 3), a copy of the approved thematic plan of the University's research activity (appendix 6) and the consents of supervisors and consultants (appendix 5) [16].

To change or re-edit the dissertation topic the aspirant submits an application to the head of the responsible structural unit, on the basis of which an order of the Director or an authorised person on the change of topic is formed (appendix 4) [14].

## Release from supervision and a new supervisor

The supervisor may be released from supervising the aspirant by order of the Director or an authorised person on one of the following grounds [17]:

- the supervisor's personal application with the reasons for refusing the supervision (appendix 7) [17];
- the aspirant's personal application with the reasons for asking for a change of supervisor (appendix 8) [17];
- the supervisor's non-compliance with the requirements of п. 2.3 [17];
- a decision of the University's expert commission that the supervisor has not performed the duties laid on him, on the representation of the head of the responsible structural unit [17];
- termination of the supervisor's employment relations with the University or of the civil-law contract concluded with him [17].

A new supervisor must be appointed by order of the Director or an authorised person within 30 calendar days from the release of the previous one [18].

## The annual review and the number of aspirants

Every year an expert review checks supervisors against the requirements of п. 2.3 [19]; supervisors form information on their research activity (appendices 1, 2) and give it to the head of the responsible structural unit by 20 January [20], the expert commission created under a local act examines the documents [21] and gives the results of the annual review to the head of the responsible structural unit by 1 February (appendix 3) [22].

The number of aspirants supervised by one supervisor at the same time is regulated by an order of the Director or an authorised person [23].

## Duties of the supervisor

The supervisor:

- helps the aspirant choose the dissertation topic and draw up the individual plan of research activity [24];
- guides the aspirant's research activity, including where necessary during experiments, technical developments, observations and measurements and the study of scientific-technical information and of domestic and foreign experience on the topic, aimed at preparing the dissertation [24];
- consults the aspirant on preparing the dissertation for defence [24];
- carries out the primary review of the dissertation text prepared by the aspirant and of the texts of his scientific articles and (or) reports prepared within the individual plan, for presentation at conferences, symposia and other collective discussions [24];
- controls the fulfilment of the individual plan of research activity [24];
- gives, during interim assessment, a review of the quality, timeliness and success of the aspirant's stages of research activity [24];
- forms a draft conclusion on whether the dissertation corresponds to the criteria established under Federal Law No. 127-ФЗ of 23 August 1996 «О науке и государственной научно-технической политике» [24].

The consent form of a supervisor repeats these duties [1].

## Rights of the supervisor

The supervisor may [25]:

- take part in admitting applicants to aspirantura programmes and in candidate examinations [25];
- determine the forms and methods of work with the aspirant [25];
- recommend that the aspirant change the dissertation topic or amend the individual plan of research activity [25];
- recommend for expulsion an aspirant who does not fulfil the individual plan on time [25];
- be present at the final attestation of the programme, held as an evaluation of the dissertation against the criteria of Federal Law No. 127-ФЗ of 23 August 1996 [25];
- take part in developing and updating aspirantura programmes [25];
- interact with the University's structural units on the supervision of aspirants [25].

## How publications and approbations are counted

The forms of information on research activity (appendices 1 and 2, in Russian and in English) count publications in domestic journals and editions included in the RINC list, with an assessment of whether the title and content of the publication correspond to the scientific specialty; publications in foreign journals and editions — publications in a foreign language in journals founded by Russian organisations are not considered foreign regardless of their indexing in Web of Science or Scopus; and monographs included in the RINC list for the scientific specialty [1]. At least two publications are required [1].

For approbations at conferences no more than 1 report or publication from a given conference is counted (more than 1 only if they correspond to different scientific specialties of aspirants), presentations at national conferences count as presentations at Russian conferences, and presentations at Russian (national) conferences with international participation also count as Russian ones; at least two approbations are required [1].

The topic of the research activity must be included in the University's thematic plan of research work for each scientific specialty of the aspirants supervised over the last 3 years, or a request to include it in the thematic plan must be submitted (the current year included, no monthly calculation) [1]. The thematic plan of research work (appendix 6) is drawn up for a period of not less than three years [1].

# Competitive selection for transfer to Innopolis University from another higher education institution

## What the regulation covers

The Regulation on the procedure and timing of the competitive selection for the transfer of students to ANO VO «Innopolis University» from organizations carrying out educational activity under higher education programmes was approved by order No. ОД/УД-18/2017/08/01 of 01 August 2017 and signed by Director K.V. Semenikhin [1]. It determines the procedure and timing of the University's competitive selection among persons applying for vacant places in bachelor programmes, master programmes and programmes of training of scientific-pedagogical staff in aspirantura when transferring from another educational organization [2].

It is based on Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29.12.2012 «Об образовании в Российской Федерации», on the order of the Ministry of Education and Science of Russia No. 124 of 10.02.2017 approving the procedure for transferring students to another organization carrying out educational activity, and on the University's Charter and other local acts [3]. The competitive selection is held when more applications for transfer have been submitted than there are vacant places [4]. The regulation is obligatory when the competitive selection is held among persons seeking transfer from other organizations of higher education [5].

The University's regulation on transfer, expulsion and reinstatement likewise provides that where applications exceed vacant places the University additionally holds a competitive selection, and leaves the procedure and timing of that selection to the corresponding local normative act of the University [6], [7]. That regulation adds that the University decides on admission of the applicants most prepared for the programme, guided by the average grade of academic performance and by the set and significance of individual achievements, and refuses those who did not pass the selection [6].

## Application and evaluation of documents

- Transfer to the University is possible during the year, but not earlier than after passing the first interim assessment [8].
- A student transferring from another educational organization submits an application for transfer to the University with the certificate of the period of study issued by the previous organization and other documents confirming the student's educational achievements [9].
- On the basis of the application, the responsible structural unit of the University (designated by an order of the director) evaluates the documents no later than 14 calendar days from the day the application is submitted — whether the student meets the requirements of the legislation on education and the University's Charter — and determines the list of studied disciplines, completed internships and performed research that will be credited or re-assessed upon transfer, and the period from which the applicant will be admitted to study [10], [11].
- If the University decides on admission, a certificate of transfer is issued within 5 calendar days from the day of that decision, stating the level of secondary vocational or higher education and the code and name of the profession, specialty or field of training to which the student will be transferred; the list of disciplines, internships and research to be credited or re-assessed is attached to the certificate [12].
- If more applications are submitted than vacant places, the University, besides evaluating the documents, holds the competitive selection no later than 14 calendar days from the day the application is submitted, and then takes either a decision on admission to the vacant places of the applicants most prepared for the programme, or a decision refusing admission to those who did not pass the selection [11].

## Competitive commission

- The commission's function is to hold the competitive selection of persons who submitted transfer applications and to form rating lists of participants recommended for admission [13].
- The commission may include no more than 5 members including the chair of the commission; the composition is approved by the University rector for one academic year; the commission is competent to decide when not less than 2/3 of its composition is present [14].
- The criteria of the competitive selection are the assessment of the list and volume of studied disciplines, completed internships and performed research; the assessment of the level and degree of formation of competencies against the requirements of the federal state educational standards of higher education; the assessment of documents confirming educational achievements; and the determination of the course to which the student may be recommended and of the period from which the student will be admitted to study [15].
- Following the selection the commission forms a rating list of participants recommended for admission and lists of persons refused admission; the results are drawn up in a protocol of the commission [16].
- No appeal against the results of the competitive selection is submitted [17].

## Admission after a positive decision

- The person decided to be admitted submits an extract from the order on expulsion in connection with transfer and the document of previous education — the original, a copy certified in the established order, or a copy with the original presented for certification of the copy by the University [18].
- On a positive decision an agreement on education under higher education programmes is concluded with that person [19].
- The order on admission is issued by the University director or another authorized person within three days from the day the student submits the documents listed above [20].
- After registration of the admission order the responsible structural unit forms the student's personal file, which includes the transfer application, the certificate of the period of study, consent to personal data processing, the document of previous education (original or copy), other documents confirming educational achievements (if any), the extract from the order on expulsion in connection with transfer, the extract from the order on admission by way of transfer, the agreement on education and other documents provided by the University's local acts [21].

## Where the two regulations differ on the certificate of transfer

The selection regulation says the certificate of transfer is issued within 5 calendar days from the day the admission decision is taken, with the list of credited disciplines attached to it [12]. The University's regulation on transfer, expulsion and reinstatement says the certificate is issued within 5 working days from the day the decision on admission is taken, on the University's official blank, signed by the director or an authorized person and certified by the University's seal, and that the list of studied disciplines, completed internships and performed research to be credited or re-assessed is attached to the certificate upon the student's application [22].

## Who may transfer in, and on what conditions (Regulation of 12 May 2026)

The Regulation on the procedure and grounds for the transfer, expulsion and restoration of students, approved by order of 12 May 2026 № ОД/АД-01/2026/05/12, sets the general requirements for transfer into the University [23]:

- Transfer is made where the person has the education required for the corresponding programme, including education obtained abroad [24].
- Transfer is made where there are vacant places for transfer; their number is determined and published on the official website with detail by programme, form of study and course, stating how many are financed from budget allocations and how many under contracts at the expense of natural and (or) legal persons [25].
- Aspirants admitted under federal state educational standards or educational standards may transfer to programmes under federal state requirements or under requirements set by the higher education organisation itself [26].
- Transfer to places financed from budget allocations requires that there are no restrictions for studying that programme at budget expense and that the programme is not a second or subsequent higher education [27], and that the total duration of study does not exceed the term set by the standard or requirements by more than one academic year [28].
- Transfer is allowed not earlier than after passing the first interim assessment [29].
- The requirements set by clauses 2.2–2.3 of the Regulation do not extend to the cases listed in clause 2.1.6 [30]:
  - transfer out of a programme where the organisation's activity ceases, its licence is suspended or annulled or state accreditation is lost or expires [31];
  - transfer within the network form of a programme [32];
  - transfer from course to course, including conditionally [33];
  - transfer to study under an individual curriculum, including accelerated study [34].
- Transfer into the University from a programme that has no state accreditation is allowed [35]; it is made from a bachelor's programme to a bachelor's programme, from a master's to a master's, from a specialist's to a bachelor's, from an aspirantura programme to an aspirantura programme, from an old-type aspirantura programme to an old-type or new aspirantura programme, from an adjuncture programme to an aspirantura programme, and from an old-type adjuncture programme to an old-type aspirantura programme [36].

## Transfer in: documents and steps (Regulation of 12 May 2026)

- The student applies to the original organisation for a certificate of the period of study because of the intention to transfer; the original organisation issues it within 5 working days, stating the level of education, the list and volume of studied subjects, courses and disciplines (modules), completed internships, performed research and the marks given at interim and final attestations [37], [38].
- The student submits to the University's unit responsible for admitting students an application addressed to the director for transfer from another organisation (annex 3 to the Regulation) with the certificate of the period of study and, at the student's discretion, other documents confirming individual achievements; on transfer to budget places compliance with the requirements of clause 2.1.4.1 is certified by the student's signature, and on transfer to paid places the application records, certified by signature, that the student is acquainted with the conditions of study with full reimbursement of costs [39].
- Not later than 10 working days from receipt of the documents the University evaluates them for compliance with the Regulation, determines the studied disciplines, internships and research that will be credited or re-assessed, and determines the period from which the student will be admitted to study [40].
- If the applications exceed the vacant places, the University holds a competitive selection under a separate local act [6].
- Within 5 working days from the decision on admission the University issues the certificate of transfer (annex 7), stating the level of higher education, the code and name of the field of training or the cipher and name of the scientific specialty to which the student will be transferred; it is drawn up on the official blank, signed by the director or an authorised person and certified by the seal, and the list of disciplines, internships and research to be credited may be attached on the student's application [22].
- The student presents to the original organisation a written application for expulsion by way of transfer to the University with the certificate of transfer [41]; the original organisation issues the expulsion act within 5 working days [42] and within 3 working days gives the person a certified copy or extract of the act, the original document of previous education and a certificate of study [43].
- The documents are handed to the student or a person with a power of attorney, or sent by post with notice of delivery and an inventory, or signed with an electronic signature and sent by e-mail on the student's application [44].
- Where the student studies abroad, clauses 2.3.3–2.3.5 and 2.3.9–2.3.12 do not apply [45].
- Within 10 working days after receiving the copy or extract of the expulsion act or the notice of its being sent to the University, the person presents to the University that act and the document of previous education (original or certified copy) if they were not sent under clause 2.3.12; for education obtained in a foreign organisation a certificate of recognition of the foreign education is presented unless recognition is not required [46].
- The University issues the order on admission by way of transfer within 5 working days from receipt of those documents; where study is under a contract at the expense of natural and (or) legal persons, the education contract is concluded before the order [47].
- Within 5 working days from the order the student is given the student card and record book or another document confirming study [48]; the personal file is formed after the order [49], and the specifics of obtaining a grant by persons admitted by way of transfer are governed by a local act [50].

## Amending the regulation

The regulation, amendments and additions to it are approved by the University director [51]; amendments and additions may be introduced by order of the University director [52].

# Entrance examinations for PhD aspirantura admission (2026 admission rules)

## Where these rules come from

The page is section IV of the university's admission rules — "Проведение вступительных испытаний и учет индивидуальных достижений поступающих" — and states that admission is conducted on the results of entrance examinations, whose list and form are given in an annex to the admission rules [1]. The university sets the list of entrance examinations and their priority for ranking the lists of applicants [2].

## How an examination is scored

Each entrance examination is assessed by the examination commission on a 100-point scale; the minimum number of points confirming successful passing is 60, and the maximum is 100 [3]. Each applicant takes each entrance examination once [4], and the results are valid for admission to the academic year for which admission is carried out [5].

## How and in what language

Entrance examinations are held in person or with the use of distance technologies, provided the applicants are identified when taking them [6]. They are held in Russian or in English; an applicant who wants an examination in English notifies the admissions office of this intention 10 days before the start of the examinations, in person or by e-mail to admissions@innopolis.ru [7].

Examinations are held on different dates for different groups of applicants as those groups are formed from persons who have filed applications, not less than once a week; one examination may be held simultaneously for all applicants or on different dates for different groups; one examination is held per applicant per day, and at the applicant's wish several examinations may be taken in one day [8]. The university sets the schedule of entrance examinations, including one or several reserve days for those who did not pass an examination for a valid reason [9].

## Candidate examination results counted as entrance examination results

The university may take into account, as the results of entrance examinations, the results of candidate examinations passed by the applicant at Innopolis University and/or in another organisation [10]. It does so for candidate examinations in the special disciplines «Математическое моделирование, численные методы и комплексы программ» and «Основы научных исследований», where confirming documents are available (a certificate of completion of aspirantura, an aspirantura diploma, a certificate of study and others) [10]. Grades are converted from the 4-point scale to the 100-point scale as follows: excellent (5) — 100, good (4) — 85, satisfactory (3) — 70, unsatisfactory (2) — 55 [10].

## Missing an examination and violations

Persons who did not pass entrance examinations for a valid reason (illness or other circumstances confirmed by documents) are admitted to take them in another group or on a reserve day [11]. If an applicant violates the admission rules during an entrance examination, authorised officials of the university draw up an act on the violation and on the applicant's not passing the examination without a valid reason, and where the examination is held in person the applicant is also removed from the place of the examination [12].

## Results and appeal

The results of an entrance examination are announced on the official site within 3 working days after the day of the examination, but not later than 1 day before the publication of the competitive lists [13]. On the day the results are announced or during the following working day the applicant has the right to look at the results of the checking and assessment of the work done at the examination [14].

On the results of an entrance examination the applicant has the right to file an appeal about an alleged violation of the established procedure of the examination and/or about disagreement with the grade received [15]; the rules for filing and considering appeals are set in clauses 63–69 of the admission rules [16].

## Dates of the 2026 examinations

For admission to places within the control figures of admission (budget places) in aspirantura programmes, full-time form, the entrance examinations run from 17 August 2026 to 21 August 2026; for admission to paid places in aspirantura programmes, full-time form, they run from 20 August 2026 to 24 August 2026 [17].

## The published schedule for 2026/2027: consultations, examinations and reserve days

A separate document — «Расписание вступительных испытаний для приема на обучение по образовательным программам высшего образования – программам научных и научно-педагогических кадров в аспирантуре в АНО ВО «Университет Иннополис» на 2026/2027 учебный год» — gives, per educational programme and entrance examination, the date of the consultation, the date of the entrance examination and the reserve day [18].

- The schedule is divided into admission within the control figures (budget places) and admission to places under contracts for the provision of paid educational services [18].
- The only programme it covers is scientific specialty 1.2.2 «Математическое моделирование, численные методы и комплексы программ», with two entrance examinations — «Математическое моделирование, численные методы и комплексы программ» and «Основы научных исследований» [18].
- For the budget places it contains the dates 17.08.2026, 18.08.2026 and 21.08.2026, and for the paid places the dates 20.08.2026, 21.08.2026 and 24.08.2026 [18]. These are the same spans as those fixed by the admission rules for 2026 — 17 to 21 August 2026 for budget places and 20 to 24 August 2026 for paid places [17].
- The times appearing in the schedule are 09:00, 10:00 and 14:00 [18]. In the wiki copy the cells of the table are scrambled by extraction, so which time and which role (consultation, examination or reserve day) belongs to which date and examination cannot be read reliably from it, and the PDF itself should be consulted [18].

## The list of examinations for 2026/2027 (annex to the admission rules)

A separate document is an annex to the admission rules for studies under higher education programmes — programmes for the training of scientific and scientific-pedagogical staff in aspirantura — at ANO VO «Innopolis University» for the 2026/2027 academic year; it lists the entrance examinations with their maximum and minimum points, priority, form, languages of taking and programmes [19].

For scientific specialty 1.2.2 «Математическое моделирование, численные методы и комплексы программ» the annex shows two entrance examinations in order of priority, one of them in the subject «Основы научных исследований» [19]. The figures and conditions given for the examinations are a minimum of 60 and a maximum of 100 points, the form of holding "in person or with the use of distance technologies", and Russian or English as the languages in which the examination is taken [19]. The forms of entrance examination named in the annex are computer testing (компьютерное тестирование) and an interview (собеседование) [19]. In the wiki copy the cells of the table are scrambled by extraction, so which form belongs to which examination cannot be read reliably from it and the annex itself should be consulted [19].

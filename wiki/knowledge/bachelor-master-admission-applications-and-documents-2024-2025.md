# Applications and documents in bachelor's and master's admission 2024/2025 — informing, priorities, documents and withdrawal

## Information about the admission

- The University acquaints the applicant and/or his parents (legal representatives) with the documents and information named in part 2 of article 55 of Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ [1].
- The official site publishes, not later than 20 January 2024: the admission rules (including the maximum number of areas of study for simultaneous participation, the terms of the admission, information on special rights and the special advantage, the list and procedure of account of individual achievements, information on entrance examinations held in person and/or with distance technologies, the special features of examinations for disabled persons and persons with health limitations, and the procedure of filing and considering appeals); the number of places within the control figures under the various conditions of admission without the quotas; and the list of entrance examinations with the name, maximum and minimum points, priority, and for internal examinations the form, languages and programme [1].
- The same publication of 20 January 2024 includes information that no compulsory preliminary medical examination is required, the places of acceptance of documents with postal and e-mail addresses, the possibility of filing documents through the super-service «Поступление в вуз онлайн» on ЕПГУ, a sample contract on paid educational services and information on the availability of dormitories [1].
- Not later than 10 April 2024 the number of places within the control figures with the special, target and separate quotas is published; not later than 1 June 2024 — the number of places in dormitories for non-resident students and the schedule of entrance examinations; not later than 5 months before the start of enrolment to paid places — the number of such places [1].
- The information is accessible from the date of publication until the day of completion of the acceptance of documents inclusive, and is also placed in free access in the electronic information system approved by the University's local act [1].
- The University maintains telephone lines and a section of the official site for answers to enquiries about admission [1].
- From the day of the start of the acceptance of documents until the start of enrolment the site publishes and daily updates the number of applications submitted and the lists of persons who submitted documents, for each competition [1].

## The application

- The applicant files an application with the necessary documents attached; the University accepts them on presentation of a consent to the processing of personal data, including a consent to the processing of data permitted by the applicant for dissemination under article 10.1 of Federal Law No. 152-ФЗ [1].
- On bachelor's programmes an applicant for places within the control figures files one application for those places, and an applicant for paid places files one application for those places; on master's programmes the applicant files one or several applications — one for places within the control figures and one for paid places [1].
- In the application the applicant states the conditions of admission under which he wants to be enrolled and the priorities of enrolment, separately for places within the control figures and for paid places; within the control figures he states the priority of the target quota and the priority of the other places — the main places and/or the separate quota and/or the special quota [1].
- Priorities are marked with ordinal numbers, and the priority falls as the number grows [1].
- By his personal signature the applicant certifies: that he is acquainted with the requirement to give reliable information and present genuine documents; that he is acquainted with the admission rules and with the documents named in part 2 of article 55 of Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ; that, when applying for places within the control figures, he receives the corresponding higher education for the first time (on bachelor's programmes — that he has no diploma of bachelor, specialist or master; on master's programmes — no diploma of specialist or master), except the cases established by law; that on bachelor's programmes he simultaneously files applications to not more than 5 organisations including the University, and to areas of study within the maximum number set by the University; and that, where he applies on the basis of the right to admission without entrance examinations under part 4 and/or part 12 of article 71, that application is filed only to the University and only for that educational programme [1].
- The application states the insurance number of the individual personal account (СНИЛС) in the individual (personalised) accounting system [1].
- The applicant may change the application, including the priorities of enrolment, in the procedure and within the terms set by the University; on bachelor's places within the control figures such changes may be made until the day of completion of the acceptance of documents inclusive [1].
- Where the application is filed electronically through the University's electronic information system or through ЕПГУ, the confirmation of the facts listed above is made by a mark in the application [1].

## Documents necessary for admission

The applicant presents [1]:

- a document or documents proving identity and citizenship (including a passport of a citizen of the Russian Federation valid outside Russia) [1];
- the document of education of the established form, one or several, with a certificate of recognition of foreign education where recognition is required; for enrolment to places within the control figures the original of only one of them is used [1];
- a document confirming registration in the individual (personalised) accounting system, if available [1];
- for disabled applicants who intend to take internal general education examinations on bachelor's programmes — a document confirming disability [1];
- where special conditions at the examinations are needed — a document confirming disability or health limitations requiring those conditions [1];
- for the use of the results of centralised testing (examination) — a document confirming that it was passed [1];
- a document confirming the right to admission without entrance examinations under part 4 of article 71, the special rights from pupil olympiads or the special advantage (bachelor's programmes) [1];
- documents confirming the special rights under parts 5, 9 and 10 of article 71 (bachelor's programmes) [1];
- documents confirming individual achievements, at the applicant's discretion [1];
- other documents at the applicant's discretion, and a photograph for persons admitted on the results of internal entrance examinations [1].

- The document of education is presented with the documents or later, but not later than the day of completion of the acceptance of documents (on bachelor's places within the control figures — not later than the day set in clause 9 of the rules); the certificate of recognition of foreign education — not later than the day of completion of the marks about the original, of the acceptance of the original and of the conclusion of paid contracts [1]; the document on centralised testing is presented within the same terms as the document of education [1].
- Documents confirming disability (and health limitations) are accepted if they are valid on the day of the application; documents confirming the special rights under parts 5, 9 and 10 of article 71 are accepted if they confirm the right on the day of completion of the acceptance of documents, and an applicant may present such a document that confirms the right on the day of the application if by the day of completion of acceptance he presents a document confirming the right on that day [1].
- Documents confirming the right to admission without entrance examinations and the olympiad rights are accepted taking account of the periods of those rights set by parts 4 and 12 of article 71 [1].
- Documents may be presented as originals or as copies (electronic images) without presentation of the originals, and certification of the copies is not required; where the application is filed through ЕПГУ, the document of education is considered presented in copy if the information is confirmed in the federal register of documents of education, and other documents if confirmed by the data of ЕПГУ or other state information systems, and otherwise the document is presented in the general procedure [1].
- The application is submitted in Russian or English; documents in a foreign language are translated into Russian unless an international treaty provides otherwise, and documents obtained abroad are legalised unless a treaty or Russian legislation provides otherwise [1].
- Documents are presented in person, sent through public postal operators, or sent electronically through the University's electronic information system and through ЕПГУ; the University must ensure all these ways [1].
- Documents are accepted at the address: Republic of Tatarstan, Verkhneuslonsky municipal district, town of Innopolis, Universitetskaya street, house 1, room 100 (the admission office); where documents are presented in person, the applicant is given a receipt [1].
- The University checks the reliability of the information stated in the application and the authenticity of the documents, including by requests to state information systems, state and municipal bodies and organisations [1].

## Withdrawal and return of documents

- At any stage of the admission the applicant may file an application to withdraw from the University the original of the document of education (or the mark about the original placed on ЕПГУ) or an application to withdraw the submitted documents; a person already enrolled may file a refusal of enrolment [1].
- Withdrawal of the original does not remove the applicant from the lists of persons who submitted documents or from the lists of applicants; withdrawal of the documents removes him from those lists and he is not subject to enrolment (is excluded from among the enrolled); refusal of enrolment removes him from among the enrolled [1].
- A person enrolled to places within the control figures who wants to withdraw the original files the application to withdraw the original together with a refusal of enrolment [1].
- Before the end of the period of acceptance for places within the control figures, the documents or the original are given back on a personal application within two hours after it is filed if it was filed not less than two hours before the end of the working day, or within the first two hours of the next working day if it was filed later [1].
- After that period the originals are returned within one working day after the application is received, and if return is impossible they are kept at the University [1].
- Where the application is filed through ЕПГУ, the applicant's personal file is formed in electronic and/or paper form on the basis of the information and documents received from ЕПГУ and/or presented by the applicant [1].

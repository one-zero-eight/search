# Elective disciplines in physical culture and sport (Порядок освоения, 2021)

## The document

The Порядок освоения дисциплин (модулей) по физической культуре и спорту в АНО ВО «Университет Иннополис» was approved by order of 2 July 2021 № ОД/УДд-01/2021/07/02 signed by Rector A.G. Tormasov [1]. It determines the organisation of the study process in the disciplines «Элективные дисциплины (модули) по физической культуре и спорту» for bachelor students, including disabled persons and persons with health limitations studying bachelor programmes [2].

It was developed in accordance with Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29 December 2012 «Об образовании в Российской Федерации», Federal Law No. 329-ФЗ of 4 December 2007 «О физической культуре и спорте в Российской Федерации», Federal Law No. 181-ФЗ of 24 November 1995 «О социальной защите инвалидов в Российской Федерации», the Порядок организации и осуществления образовательной деятельности по программам бакалавриата, специалитета и магистратуры approved by order of the Ministry of Education and Science of 05.04.2017 № 301, the federal state educational standards of higher education (ФГОС ВО), the University's Charter and its local acts [3].

## Who takes them and who runs them

The School of Physical Culture and Healthy Lifestyle (Школа физической культуры и здорового образа жизни, the School) is the structural subdivision responsible for organising the study process in these disciplines [4]. The study process is based on the ФГОС ВО and on the work programmes of the disciplines [5]. Mastering the disciplines in the volume set by the ФГОС ВО is obligatory for all bachelor students, regardless of the form of education, in the volume provided by the educational programme [6]. The list of sports within the disciplines is formed by the School, taking into account its staff resources and the University's material-technical resources [7]. Their aim is to form a personality with an active life position, independence in decision-making, a sense of responsibility, moral qualities, the ability for successful socialisation and the ability to use various forms of physical culture and sport to preserve and strengthen health, improve the quality of life and the effectiveness of professional activity [8].

## Medical examination and the four medical groups

Under the order of the Ministry of Education of 01.12.1999 № 1025 «Об организации процесса физического воспитания в образовательных учреждениях начального, среднего и высшего профессионального образования», the University systematically controls students' health through regular medical examination; students who have not passed a medical examination are not admitted to classes [9]. Preventive medical examinations are carried out by medical organisations licensed for the relevant work, and the University reserves the right to determine the medical institution for the annual medical examination [9].

The medical examination is held at the beginning of the autumn semester for first-course bachelor students and determines the student's assignment to one of four medical groups [9]:

- основная группа — physical culture classes without restrictions and participation in competitions are allowed [9];
- подготовительная группа — classes with insignificant limitations of physical loads; participation in competitions only with the permission of a medical organisation [9];
- специальная группа один — classes with significant limitations of physical loads [9];
- специальная группа два — physical culture classes are forbidden; the student passes the discipline by developing a project or preparing a report according to the work programme [9].

For students of the основная and подготовительная groups, participation in a medical examination in later years to determine the health group is optional [10]. A student who wants to confirm a special health group, or to change the основная or подготовительная group to one of the above, passes a medical examination at the beginning of each academic year [11]. Students of the основная, подготовительная and специальная один groups may study together; students of the специальная два group are combined into groups separate from the other students, regardless of their course of study [12]. Education of students with health limitations may be organised both together with other students and in separate groups [13]. In case of illness students must obtain medical certificates at a medical institution and present them to be admitted to classes; the term of release from physical culture classes for health reasons is determined by the medical institution [14].

## Choice of sport and study groups

Study activity in the programme is conducted in the form of contact work with teaching staff and (or) persons attracted by the University, in the form of students' independent work and in other forms determined by the University [15]. For disabled persons and persons with health limitations the University establishes a special procedure for mastering these disciplines, taking account of their state of health [16]. The disciplines ensure the formation of all the competences declared for mastery in the University's educational programmes [17].

- Study groups for practical classes within the sports are formed with no more than 20 people, taking account of students' state of health, physical development and physical fitness in accordance with clause 3.1; quotas on the number of groups formed may be set for each sport depending on material-technical and staff resources [18].
- Before the start of the semester students are offered a list of modules (sports) they may attend within the discipline in that semester [19].
- Students choose the module (sport) they will attend in the semester through the student's personal cabinet on the University's website [20].
- Groups are formed from first- to fourth-course bachelor students; one group may contain students of different courses [21].
- Several study groups are formed in each sport; a student chooses a group taking account of the health group, physical development and physical fitness, and the list of groups available to the student is formed automatically from those criteria [22].
- A student may transfer to another group within one sport during the semester not less than a week before the upcoming class and if there are vacant places in that group; transfer to another group with a change of sport during the semester is not allowed [23].
- A student who has not made the choice within the set terms is distributed automatically to a sport that has vacant places; the number of vacant places per sport is determined by the number of groups in that sport [24].
- The composition of students for classes is formed by sport and approved by order of the Director of the University or a person authorised by him before the start of each semester [25].
- The responsible structural subdivision forms the class timetable and brings it to students' knowledge; the timetable is approved by order of the Director or an authorised person before the start of each semester [26].

Students attend the chosen module for one semester, and the choice is made anew before each new semester [27]. The discipline is conducted as practical (seminar) classes in the chosen sport and as independent work; its content, forms of control and the competences it forms are reflected in the discipline's work programme [28]. The School determines the form (forms) of independent work, taking account of the University's material-technical resources, and brings it to students' knowledge [29].

## Assessment

- Successful mastery of the study programme ends with interim assessment in the form of a pass (зачёт), held as a fitness test whose requirements are set by the discipline's work programme; in case of a missed class for a valid reason the missed material is mastered under the guidance of the School's teachers [30].
- Students' performance is controlled within ongoing assessment; the material missed according to the timetable is mastered under the guidance of the School's teachers, and in case of missed classes there is the possibility to prepare for the interim assessment (the fitness test) on an individual schedule under a teacher's guidance; the teacher sets the minimum volumes of preparation depending on the sport and the student's health group or individual physical capabilities [31].
- To care for health and prevent overwork and reduce the risk of injury, only prepared students are admitted to the fitness test; preparation includes a planned increase of the load [32].
- A student studying on an exchange at a partner organisation passes the assessment in the discipline by defending a report on the list of topics approved by the School within the terms set by the University [33].

## Students with disabilities and health limitations

For these students the study process follows the principles of health-saving technologies and adaptive physical culture [34]. The University ensures measures aimed at preserving health and developing the adaptive potential and adaptability to study [35]. Teachers with the corresponding preparation in adaptive physical culture are allowed to conduct lectures and practical classes with them [36]. The lecture materials reflect methods of building models of physical-culture and sports activity to address the problems of reduced or limited motor activity [37]. On the basis of independent research and the lecture material students develop their own research on physical-culture and sports activity in a particular disease (diseases) in the form of a report or a project [38]. Depending on the student's nosology and the degree of limitation, and in accordance with the recommendations of the medical-social expertise service, classes for students with health limitations may be organised as [39]:

- mobile adaptive physical culture classes in specially equipped gyms or outdoors [39];
- lectures on health-saving technologies [39];
- seminars (practical classes) in the form of group work, trainings, and the preparation and defence of presentations and reports [39].

The University provides these students with access routes to the sports complex, the large gym, the changing rooms and the shower that meet accessibility requirements [40]. For ongoing assessment and interim assessment the University creates assessment funds adapted for students with health limitations, allowing evaluation of the achievement of the results planned in the main professional educational programme and the level of formation of all declared competences [41].

## Distance technologies

Study activity with e-learning and distance educational technologies is conducted as lectures and practical classes organised with those technologies, as students' independent work and in other forms determined by the University [42]. The disciplines taught this way ensure the formation of all competences declared in the University's educational programmes [43]. Groups for the disciplines taught with e-learning and distance technologies are formed in the same way as in clause 4 [44]. The discipline is conducted as practical (seminar) classes in the chosen sport with e-learning and distance technologies, and as independent work; content, forms of control and competences are reflected in the work programme [45].

# Approval, actualisation and publication of secondary vocational education (СПО) programmes

## What this card covers

This card follows the same Regulation — the Regulation on the procedure of development and approval of secondary vocational education programmes of ANO VO «Innopolis University», approved by order of 27 January 2025 No. ОД/АД‐05/2025/01/27 [1] — through the stages that come after the programme has been built: the calendar study schedule and the academic year, agreement and approval, annual actualisation, storage, publication and entry into force. The parts of the programme themselves are described in the companion card.

## Calendar study schedule and the academic year

- The calendar study schedule for an academic year is developed every year in accordance with the requirements of ФГОС СПО and reflects the delivery of the programme in the current academic year by years of study, including the distribution of the time budget in weeks for theoretical study, practices, interim assessment, ГИА (ИА) and vacations [2].
- The academic year under a programme begins on 1 September and ends in accordance with the curriculum; one academic year lasts 52 weeks and the last year of study lasts 43 weeks [3].
- The total duration of vacations in an academic year is set from 8 to 11 weeks, including not less than 2 weeks in the winter period [4].
- The schedule for the current academic year is drawn up as a separate document and approved by order of the Director of the University or of another responsible person authorised by him before the start of the academic year; its immediate developer is the head (deputy head) of the structural unit delivering the educational programmes [5].
- The schedule for the current academic year is placed in the University's electronic information and educational environment [6]. Under an individual curriculum, including accelerated study, the calendar study schedule is part of the individual curriculum [7]; a model form of the schedule for full-time study is given in appendix 2 to the Regulation [8].

## Who agrees and who approves

- The curriculum is agreed by the head of the structural unit delivering the СПО programmes, the head of the educational-methodological centre and the deputy director — head of the department for the organisation and support of project and educational activity [9], and is approved by the University's Academic council as part of the educational programme [10].
- The work programme of practice is approved by the Academic council as part of the educational programme [11].
- The programme of the state final certification is approved by the Academic council with the participation of the chairmen of the state examination commissions [12].
- The work programme of upbringing is approved by order of the Director of the University or of another responsible person authorised by him before the start of the academic year [13].
- The developed educational programme is considered and approved by the University's Academic council before the start of its delivery and, as a rule, before the announcement of admission to the corresponding programme [14].

## Annual actualisation

Actualisation means the updating of the programme by introducing additions or changes into its components [15]. It is carried out every year with account of the requests of employers, the features of the educational process, and the development of the region, science, technology, culture, economy, technologies and the social sphere, within the limits set by ФГОС СПО [16]. The development and the annual updating of the programme are carried out with account of the recommendations received from the participants concerned [17].

- Actualisation may concern the composition and (or) the volume of hours of the disciplines and professional modules set in the curriculum, the content of the programme's elements (РПД, РППМ, РПП, РПВ, the programme of ГИА (ИА), ФОС, methodological materials and others), including changes in the material-technical and information support of a discipline (module, practice, ГИА), in the provision of upbringing activity and in other conditions of delivery; the necessary changes and additions are entered into the respective documents keeping the rubrication adopted in them [18].
- The actualised version is considered and approved at a meeting of the University's Academic council, and a record with the number and date of its protocol is entered in the sheet of updates of the programme [19].
- When the actualised programme is approved, the previous version of the updated components is considered to have lost force; the title page of the document withdrawn from use is marked «Аннулирован», with the number and date of the protocol and the signature of the head of the structural unit delivering the programme [20].

## Storage of the documents

The original set of documents on paper and in electronic form for a programme being delivered is kept in the structural unit responsible for delivering the programme, in accordance with the University's consolidated nomenclature of files; the sets are kept until they are replaced by new ones on actualisation [21]. For a programme whose delivery has been completed, the set is kept in that structural unit in accordance with the consolidated nomenclature of files for one year and, when that period ends, is transferred to the University's archive; the versions of the components approved at their last actualisation are subject to storage after the completion of delivery [22].

## Publication on the website

Information about a programme being delivered, and its components, are placed on the University's official website in the information and telecommunications network «Internet», in the section «Сведения об образовательной организации», subsection «Образование» [23]. The components are placed there within 10 working days after the approval or actualisation of the programme, and only the current version of the programme is placed on the site [24]. Information about the programme is placed on the site in accordance with the Rosobrnadzor order mentioned in the Regulation [25].

## Entry into force and changes

The Regulation is approved by order of the Director of the University or of another person authorised by him and comes into force from the moment of signing [26]. It is valid until the date on which a new local normative act regulating the matters it covers is adopted [27]. Changes and additions to the Regulation are made by order of the Director of the University or of another person authorised by him [28].

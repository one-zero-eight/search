# Sector for Support of the Administration's Activity (Сектор по обеспечению деятельности администрации) — place, tasks, functions and rights

## The document and the Sector's place in the structure

The Regulation on the Sector for Support of the Administration's Activity (Положение о Секторе по обеспечению деятельности администрации) of the Department for Documentary Support of Managerial Activity of ANO VO «Innopolis University» was approved by order of 27 March 2026 No. ОД/АД‐03/2026/03/27, signed by R.F. Valiev as Director for Development and HR Policy, in Innopolis; the number is printed with an unclear trailing token «МНРЕГ» in the text layer [1]. It regulates the Sector's activity and determines its tasks, functions, rights and duties [2].

- The Sector for Support of the Administration's Activity (the Sector) is a structural subdivision of the Department for Documentary Support of Managerial Activity and belongs to the category «иные структурные подразделения» (other structural subdivisions) [3]. It reports directly to the head of the Department for Documentary Support of Managerial Activity [4].
- In its activity the Sector is guided by the Constitution of the Russian Federation, the Labour Code of the Russian Federation, the Federal Law of 29 December 2012 No. 273-ФЗ «Об образовании в Российской Федерации», other legislative and normative legal acts regulating the activity of higher education institutions, the University's Charter, decisions of the University's Academic, Supervisory and Operational councils, orders and instructions of the University director, other local normative acts and this Regulation [5].
- The structure, headcount and staffing table of the Sector, and changes to them, are approved by the University director [6]. The Sector is headed by a head of the sector for support of the administration's activity, hired and dismissed by order of the University director [7].
- The working conditions of the Sector's employees are determined by the employment contracts concluded with each of them, by the University's internal labour regulations and by other local normative acts [8]; their rights, duties and degree of responsibility are established by job descriptions [9].
- Within their competence the Sector's employees may be involved in work under grants, projects, subsidies and other programmes implemented by the University [10]. The Regulation and the additions and changes to it are approved by the University director or by another person authorised by him [11].

## Tasks

- organisational and documentary support of the head's activity, including planning the working schedule and coordinating meetings and events [12];
- ensuring timely and high-quality document flow in the head's office, including receipt, registration, distribution and control of the execution of incoming and outgoing documents [13];
- organising and coordinating the head's interaction with vice-rectors, heads of structural subdivisions and external organisations (state bodies, partners, mass media, public organisations) [14];
- preparing drafts of documents (orders, instructions, letters, official memoranda, presentations, reports) on the head's instructions and ensuring that they are properly formatted [15];
- organising and supporting the activity of advisory bodies under the head (preparing agendas, keeping minutes, controlling the execution of decisions) [16];
- interacting with public organisations, analysing public opinion and preparing information and analytical materials on questions within the Sector's competence [17];
- keeping the records and the archive of the head's office documents, ensuring their safety and confidentiality [18].

## Functions

- Planning and coordinating the head's working schedule (meetings, conferences, visits, business trips) and informing him in good time of forthcoming events [19].
- Managing access to the head, carrying out the initial reception of visitors and processing incoming requests [20].
- Organising the head's workplace and providing him with the resources needed for effective work [21].
- Preparing materials for meetings, conferences and events in which the head takes part [22].
- Receiving, registering, initially processing, distributing and controlling the execution of incoming and outgoing correspondence (including e-mail) addressed to the head [23].
- Preparing drafts of orders, instructions, letters, official memoranda, presentations, reports and other documents on the head's instructions [24].
- Systematising information and preparing summaries, reports, references, analytical notes, comparative tables and reviews at the head's request [25].
- Coordinating the head's interaction with vice-rectors, deans and heads of structural subdivisions [26].
- Organising interaction with external organisations (state authorities, partners, mass media, public organisations) on questions within the Sector's competence [27].
- Preparing and organising visits of delegations and high-level meetings, with their documentary and organisational support [28].
- Organising and supporting the activity of advisory bodies under the head: preparing the agenda and taking the minutes of sittings [29].
- Controlling the execution of decisions taken at meetings and sittings of advisory bodies and informing the head of the status of execution [30].
- Collecting, analysing and initially processing data the head needs for decisions (on academic, financial and administrative questions) [31].
- Assisting in preparing strategic documents, development plans and reports on the University's activity [32].
- Monitoring the fulfilment of the head's instructions, controlling deadlines and reporting on the status of execution [33].
- Studying and analysing practices of interaction with federal, regional and local public organisations and submitting proposals for further interaction [34].
- Preparing information on the activity of regional and local public organisations and their positions in relation to the University and to organisations of the IT sphere [35].
- Taking part in preparing reports and speeches on public themes and information and congratulatory materials of a public character [36].
- Preparing proposals on the University's participation in programmes of support for socially oriented non-commercial organisations [37], and taking part in preparing and holding measures on providing support to such organisations [38].
- Taking part in organising and holding the University's key events [39].
- Observing the requirements of the legislation in force and of the University's local normative acts on the processing and confidentiality of personal data [40].

## Rights

In carrying out their activity the Sector's employees have the right to [41]:

- request from the University's structural subdivisions the information and materials needed for the work within the Sector's competence [41];
- with the consent of the heads of the University's structural subdivisions, engage the employees of those subdivisions to prepare draft local acts and other documents and to develop and carry out the measures conducted by the Sector [42];
- take part in the meetings held by the University's management when questions whose solution falls within the Sector's competence are discussed [43];
- submit to the University's management proposals to cancel, amend or supplement the University's local acts [44];
- interact with state authorities and administration, legal entities and the University's subdivisions on questions within the Sector's competence [45].

## Interaction with other structural subdivisions and reporting

Interaction with all the University's structural subdivisions is carried on questions of preparing draft local acts, orders, instructions, contracts and other documents, and of preparing conclusions and written and oral references on questions within the Sector's competence that arise in practical activity [46]. The Sector may be a base for the practical training of the University's students [47].

On the basis of the relevant local acts, orders and instructions the Sector may be responsible, within its activity, for preparing and keeping up to date information, references and reports [48].

# Final attestation on PhD aspirantura programmes without state accreditation — the Regulation of 4 September 2017

## The document

The Regulation on conducting the final attestation (итоговая аттестация) on higher education programmes — programmes of training scientific and scientific-pedagogical staff in aspirantura — that have no state accreditation was approved by order No. ОД/УД-06/2017/09/04 of 4 September 2017, signed by Director V. Semenikhin; control of its execution was entrusted to the Vice-Rector — head of the department for work with students and applicants S.V. Masyagin [1]. The page is an OCR scan of the PDF and parts of its text are corrupted, so numbers and wording should be checked against the PDF [1].

The Regulation governs the organisation and conduct of the final attestation of aspirants completing programmes without state accreditation: its forms, the requirements on the use of means of education and of communication, the requirements on the persons involved, the procedure for appeals, changes and (or) annulment of results, and the specific features for students with health limitations [2]. It is based on Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29 December 2012, Government Decree No. 842 of 24 September 2013, order No. 1259 of 19 November 2013, order No. 875 of 30 July 2014 approving the federal state educational standard for field 09.06.01 Informatics and Computer Engineering, the Charter and other local acts [3].

## Purpose, admission and general rules

- The attestation is conducted by final examination commissions to determine the compliance of the results of mastering the programmes with the requirements of the federal state educational standards of higher education (ФГОС ВО) [4]; the University ensures its conduct [5] and uses the means necessary for the organisation of educational activity [6].
- A student who has no academic debt and has fully completed the curriculum or the individual curriculum of the programme is admitted [7].
- Persons who master a programme in the form of self-education, or who studied on a programme without state accreditation, may pass the state final attestation externally (экстерном) in another organisation having state accreditation for the chosen programme, under Russian legislation on education [8].
- No fee is charged from students for passing the attestation [9]; during the tests students and the persons involved are forbidden to have on them and to use means of communication [10].
- The volume in credit units, the structure and the content are set by the University in accordance with ФГОС ВО [11]; the dates are set by the University independently [12].
- Results are graded «отлично», «хорошо», «удовлетворительно», «неудовлетворительно»; the grades «отлично», «хорошо» and «удовлетворительно» mean a successful pass [13]. Successful passing is the basis for a document of higher education and qualification of the form established by the University, and graduates are also issued the conclusion (заключение) under paragraph 16 of Decree No. 842 [14].
- Tests with e-learning and distance technologies are determined by the educational programme [15]; the language of the attestation is determined by its programme [16].

## Forms and programme of the attestation

- The attestation is held as a final examination (итоговый экзамен) and a research report on the main results of the prepared scientific qualification work (thesis) — the научный доклад; together they are the attestation tests [17]. The examination is written, the report is presented orally [17].
- The examination is held on one or several disciplines and (or) modules whose results are decisive for the professional activity of graduates [18]; the requirements on the report, the procedure for its preparation and presentation and the criteria for its assessment are set by the attestation programme [19].
- The attestation programme is part of the educational programme, is developed by the responsible structural subdivision on the model of Annex 1 and approved by the Academic Council [20]; it is communicated to students not later than six months before the attestation starts through the electronic information and educational environment [21].
- The final examination follows the programme approved by the Academic Council, containing the questions put and recommendations for preparation including literature; before it a pre-examination consultation is held [22].

## The research report and its review

- The report is drawn up according to the author's abstract requirements of paragraph 25 of Decree No. 842 and is submitted with the thesis to the research supervisor on paper as a manuscript and electronically not later than one month before the attestation; the title page forms are in Annexes 2–3 [23].
- The texts of reports, except those containing state secrets, are placed in the University's electronic library system and checked for borrowing, with access under legislation and exclusions, by decision of the rights holder, of production, technical, economic, organisational and other information of commercial value; the procedure, including substantive checking and identification of improper borrowings, is set annually by a local act [23].
- The supervisor submits to the final examination commission a review (Annex 4) and a statement on originality after the check for improper borrowings, not later than 7 working days before the attestation [23].
- Reports are subject to review by two reviewers — one from among the University's workers and one who is not a University worker, not a co-author of works published on the thesis topic and has no conflict of interest; a reviewer holds an academic degree (including a recognised foreign one) and publications on the topic; the reviews (Annex 5) go to the commission not later than 7 working days before the start [24].
- The student is acquainted with the review and the review(s) not later than 5 calendar days before the day of presenting the report [25].

## Schedule and announcement of results

- Not later than 30 calendar days before the first test the University approves a schedule stating the dates, times and places of tests and pre-examination consultations and communicates it to the student, the chairs and members of the commissions, the secretaries and the supervisors and consultants of reports; the schedule provides a break of not less than 7 calendar days between tests and is placed in the electronic information and educational environment [26].
- The results of a written examination are announced on the next working day after it, those of the report on the day of its presentation [27].

## Absence, unsatisfactory results and retaking

- Students who did not appear for a test for a valid reason (temporary incapacity, public or state duties, court summons, transport problems such as a cancelled flight or no tickets, weather conditions or other cases recognised by the University as valid) may pass it within 6 months after the attestation ends, on submitting a document confirming the reason; a student who missed one test for a valid reason is admitted to the next test if there is one [28].
- Students who did not appear without a valid reason or received «неудовлетворительно», and those mentioned in clause 3.12 who did not pass a test within the term set for them, are expelled with a certificate of study (справка об обучении), as not having fulfilled the duties of conscientiously mastering the programme and completing the curriculum [29].
- A person who did not pass the attestation may pass it again not earlier than one year and not later than five years after the date of the failed attestation; on the person's application the person is reinstated for a period set by the University but not less than the period of the calendar study schedule for the attestation on the programme [30].

## Commissions

- Final examination commissions are created to conduct the attestation (chair, secretary and members) and appeal commissions to consider appeals (chair and members); both act for one calendar year [31] and are created for each field of study, each programme, several fields or several programmes [32].
- The chair of the final examination commission is approved not later than 31 December of the preceding year by decision of the Academic Council [33], and the compositions not later than one month before the start [34].
- That chair is approved from among persons not working at the University who hold a Doctor of Sciences degree (including a recognised foreign one) in the scientific specialty corresponding to the field of training; the chair of the appeal commission is the Director or another person authorised by him by an administrative act [35]. Chairs organise and control the commissions' activity and ensure unity of the requirements set for students [36].
- The final examination commission has not less than 5 persons, of whom not less than 50 per cent are leading specialists — representatives of employers and (or) their associations in the field and (or) representatives of state and municipal authorities exercising powers in it, the rest being teaching staff and (or) research workers of the University and (or) other organisations holding a rank and (or) degree and (or) a state honorary title and (or) laureates of state prizes; the appeal commission includes not less than 4 persons from the teaching staff and (or) research workers who are not members of the final examination commissions [37].
- The Director appoints a secretary of the final examination commission for the period of the attestation from among teaching staff, research workers or administrative workers; the secretary is not a member, keeps the minutes and submits materials to the appeal commission [38].
- A meeting is competent if not less than two thirds of the commission take part; meetings are conducted by the chairs, decisions are taken by a simple majority of the members taking part, and in case of equal votes the chair has a casting vote [39].
- Decisions are recorded in minutes in Russian and English; the minute on a test records the questions put, the characteristics of the answers and the members' opinions on the student's preparedness and shortcomings; minutes are signed by the chair (the examination commission's also by its secretary), bound into books and kept in the archive, in the forms of Annexes 6–7 [40].

## Appeals

- A student has the right to appeal [41] in writing about a violation, in his opinion, of the procedure of the test and (or) disagreement with the results of the final examination [42], personally, not later than the next working day after the results are announced [43].
- For its consideration the secretary of the final examination commission sends the minute, the conclusion of its chair on procedural compliance and the student's written answers where they exist [44]. The appeal is considered not later than 2 working days after submission at a meeting to which the chair and the student are invited, and the decision is communicated within 3 working days, the student's acquaintance being certified by his signature [45].
- On a procedural appeal the commission rejects it if the facts are not confirmed and (or) did not influence the result, or grants it if they are confirmed and influenced the result; then the result is annulled, the minute is passed to the examination commission not later than the next working day, and the student may pass the test within the dates set by the University [46].
- On disagreement with the results the commission rejects the appeal and keeps the result or grants it and sets another result; the decision is passed to the examination commission not later than the next working day and is the basis for annulling the earlier result and setting a new one [47].
- The decision is final and not subject to review [48]; a repeat test is held in the presence of one member of the appeal commission not later than the end of the student's studies, and an appeal against it is not accepted [49], [50].

## Students with disabilities and health limitations

- The attestation is conducted with account of the features of psycho-physical development, individual possibilities and state of health [51]: it may be held in one room with students without such limitations if this creates no difficulties, an assistant gives technical help (workplace, movement, reading and formatting the task, communication with the commission), technical means may be used, and access is unhindered (ramps, handrails, widened doorways, lifts, the room on the first floor where there are no lifts, special chairs) [52].
- Local acts on the attestation are communicated to such students in an accessible form [53]. On a written application the duration may be increased by not more than 90 minutes for a written examination, 20 minutes for preparation at an oral examination and 15 minutes for presenting the report [54].
- Depending on the features: for the blind — tasks in Braille or in an electronic form readable with specialised software, or read by an assistant, written tasks in Braille, on such a computer or dictated, and where needed writing accessories, Braille paper and the computer; for the visually impaired — enlarged font, even lighting of not less than 300 lux and, where needed, a magnifying device (own allowed); for the deaf and hard-of-hearing and those with severe speech disorders — collective sound-amplifying apparatus and, where needed, individual apparatus, and at their wish a written form; for disorders of the musculoskeletal system — work on a computer with specialised software or dictation, and at their wish an oral form [55].
- Not later than 3 months before the start the student submits a written application on the special conditions needed, stating the features and whether an assistant and extra time are needed for each test, with documents confirming them where the University does not hold them [56].

## Final provisions

The Regulation is approved by order of the Director or another authorised person and enters into force on approval [57]; changes are made in accordance with Russian legislation on the submission of the department of educational activity by order of the Director or another authorised person [58]. Annexes contain the model attestation programme, the title pages of the thesis and report, the forms of the supervisor's review and of the reviewer's review, and the forms of the commissions' minutes [1].

# Department of Pre-University Preparation (Отдел довузовской подготовки) — the order approving its Regulation in a new edition (1 December 2025)

## The order

The order of 1 December 2025 № ОД/АД-02/2025/12/01, issued in Innopolis and signed by the Director for Development and HR Policy R.F. Valiev, approved a new edition of the Regulation on the Department of Pre-University Preparation (Отдел довузовской подготовки) of the Centre for the Organisation of the Admission Campaign and Pre-University Preparation of ANO VO «Innopolis University» [1]. The order states that it is issued in order to raise the efficiency of the activity of the University's structural subdivision [1].

- Clause 1 approves the Regulation attached to the order, in a new edition [2].
- Clause 2 obliges the head of the Department of Pre-University Preparation S.M. Slobodchikova to ensure timely familiarisation (against signature) with the approved Regulation of the unit's workers, and also of workers newly hired and transferred (moved) into the unit from other units of the University [3].
- Clause 3 recognises as no longer in force the Regulation on the Department of Pre-University Preparation of the Centre of Pre-University Preparation and Admission of Applicants of ANO VO «Innopolis University», approved by order of the University of 03.06.2024 № ОД/АД-05/2024/06/03 [4].
- Clause 4 leaves control over the execution of the order, and of the Regulation approved by it, with the signatory [5].

## What the page does not contain

The page reproduces the order only; it does not give the text of the approved Regulation, so it says nothing about the Department's tasks, functions, rights, its place in the structure or its head as a permanent position [1].

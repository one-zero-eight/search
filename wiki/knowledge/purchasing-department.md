# Purchasing Department (Отдел закупок) — place, tasks, functions and rights

## The document and the Department's place in the structure

The Regulation on the Purchasing Department (Положение об Отделе закупок) of ANO VO «Innopolis University» was approved by order of 18 March 2026 No. ОД/АД‐01/2026/03/18, signed in Innopolis by the Director for Development and HR Policy R.F. Valiev [1]. It regulates the activity of the Purchasing Department and determines its tasks, functions, rights and duties [2].

- The Purchasing Department is a structural subdivision directly subordinate to the deputy director for the optimisation of business processes, internal control and audit, and belongs to the category of other structural subdivisions [3]. It reports directly to that deputy director in accordance with the University's organisational structure [4].
- In its activity the Department is guided by the Constitution of the Russian Federation, the Labour Code of the Russian Federation, Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ «Об образовании в Российской Федерации», other legislative and normative legal acts regulating the activity of higher education institutions, the University's Charter, decisions of the University's Academic, Supervisory and Operational councils, orders and instructions of the University director, other local normative acts and this Regulation [5].
- The structure, headcount and staffing table of the Department, and changes to them, are approved by the University director [6]. The Department is headed by a head of the Department, hired and dismissed by order of the University director [7].
- The working conditions of the Department's employees are determined by the employment contracts concluded with each of them and by the University's internal labour regulations [8]; their rights, duties and degree of responsibility are established by job descriptions [9].
- Within their competence the Department's employees may be involved in work under grants, projects, subsidies and other programmes implemented by the University [10]. The Regulation and the additions and changes to it are approved by the University director or by another person authorised by him [11].

## Tasks

The tasks of the Department are [12]–[13]:

- organising and improving the University's procurement activity in strict accordance with the requirements of the Unified Procurement Standard of ANO VO «Innopolis University» (Стандарт закупок) [12];
- methodological and organisational support of the University's structural subdivisions on questions of conducting procurements [14];
- raising the efficiency of the procurement system [15];
- ensuring the efficiency, transparency and competitiveness of procurement procedures [16];
- monitoring and analysing the market of goods, works and services to meet the University's needs [13].

## Functions

Organisation of procurement procedures [17]:

- receiving, processing and checking the procurement requests coming from initiators through the corporate portal «Битрикс» [17];
- determining, together with the initiator, the optimal procurement method in accordance with the Procurement Standard [17];
- preparing and posting notices, procurement documentation and requests for commercial proposals, including on the electronic trading platform [17];
- collecting commercial proposals within the request for proposals [17];
- preparing competitive sheets and draft protocols on the results of the procedures [17].

Support of procurements: consulting the initiators of procurements on the application of the Procurement Standard and of the Regulation on the interaction of the University's structural subdivisions in carrying out procurements of goods, works and services (Регламент взаимодействия) [18].

Support of the procurement commission (ЗК) [19]:

- organisational and technical support of the work of the ЗК [19];
- preparing and timely sending to the members of the ЗК the materials for its meetings [19];
- keeping the document flow of the ЗК, including the drawing up, agreement and storage of its protocols and decisions [19];
- informing the members of the ЗК, by sending information and documents, for taking decisions with the use of the «Битрикс» corporate portal [19].

Contract work, in the procurement part [20]:

- preparing and initiating the procedure of agreeing draft contracts with the winners of procurement procedures with the use of the «Битрикс» corporate portal, in the cases established by the Regulation on interaction [20];
- controlling that the requisites are filled in correctly and that the terms of the contract correspond to the results of the procedure carried out [20].

Monitoring and analytics [21]:

- monitoring prices for the goods, works and services being procured [21];
- preparing and presenting to the University's management information (reports) on the results of procurement activity as necessary or on request [21];
- collecting, systematising and summarising data on the procurements carried out, to prepare information on compliance with the established deadlines and procedures [21].

Methodological work and document flow [22]:

- developing and updating templates and standard forms of the documents needed for procurement [22];
- keeping information on procurements up to date in the internal corporate systems — the «Битрикс» corporate portal and the network folder at P:\DepartmentFiles\Purchasing department\Public [22];
- considering the requests and proposals of structural subdivisions on improving procurement activity, systematising the information received and using it as initial data for the analysis of п. 3.5 [22].

## Rights

In carrying out their activity the Department's employees have the right to [23]:

- request and receive from the University's structural subdivisions the information, documents and materials needed for carrying out procurement procedures [23];
- require from the initiators of procurements that technical specifications be drawn up properly and the initial (maximum) price justified in accordance with the requirements of the Procurement Standard [24];
- take part in meetings and working groups discussing questions connected with procurement activity [25];
- submit to the management proposals on optimising and improving procurement processes and on changing the local normative acts in the procurement part — the Regulation numbers this right as п. 4.3 as well, repeating the number [25];
- control the observance by the University's structural subdivisions of the requirements of the Procurement Standard and of the Regulation on interaction [26].

## Interaction with other structural subdivisions

Interaction with other structural subdivisions is built on this Regulation, the Procurement Standard and the Regulation on interaction, with the use of the «Битрикс» corporate portal [27].

- With the initiators of procurements — on planning procurements, drawing up technical specifications, choosing the procurement method, agreeing the results of procedures and concluding contracts [28].
- With the Corporate Security Department — on checking counterparties, identifying affiliation and preparing due diligence reports [29].
- With the Financial and Economic Department — on planning the procurement budget, the availability of limits, the application of expenditure items and the formation of reporting [30].
- With the Legal Department — on the legal examination of draft contracts drawn up on the results of procurements and on the compliance of procurement documentation with Russian legislation [31].
- With the University's management and the ЗК — on presenting analytical information, draft decisions and protocols for approval [32].
- The Department may be a base for the practical training of the University's students [33].

## Reporting

On the basis of the relevant local acts, orders and instructions, the Department may be responsible, within its activity, for preparing and keeping up to date information, references and reports [34].

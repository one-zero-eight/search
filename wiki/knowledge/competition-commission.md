# Competition Commission (конкурсная комиссия) — status, composition, meetings and voting

## What the Commission is

The Competition Commission (конкурсная комиссия) is an authorised collegial body attached to the Academic Council of Innopolis University, created to hold the competition to fill positions of pedagogical workers belonging to the teaching staff [1]. Its rules are part of the Regulation on the Competition Commission and the procedure of the competition, approved by order of the director of 1 April 2024 № ОД/АД-04/2024/04/01 [2]. The regulation on the Commission, its composition and working order are placed on https://innopolis.university [3].

## Composition

The Commission is created by order of the director from members of the Academic Council and, where necessary, other administrative and managerial staff; the chair, the deputy chair and the secretary are appointed from among them, and a change of composition is approved by order of the director [4]. The same order may appoint an assistant to the secretary, who is not a member of the Commission but helps the secretary with correspondence with candidates and with the collection of documents for the meeting [4].

The composition is formed so as to exclude the possibility of a conflict of interest that could affect its decisions [4]. Persons who have left the Academic Council, or whose employment with the University has ended, are considered to have automatically left the Commission [4].

## Tasks and powers

The Commission's tasks are to hold the competition under the Regulation, to ensure equal access of candidates (Претенденты) to it and their right to career growth on a competitive basis, to select a highly professional teaching staff on a competitive basis and to improve the selection and placement of teaching personnel [2].

It considers the applications, the documents attached to them and other materials characterising the candidate; it evaluates the main results the candidate obtained earlier, which he sends to the University, against the expected performance indicators published by the University; and it evaluates the candidates' qualifications and experience, holding an interview with a candidate where such a decision is taken [5].

It determines the winner by secret ballot [6], and its protocol with the information that a candidate is recognised as the winner of the competition is the basis for appointing him to the teaching position [7]. The Commission may remove a candidate from the competition if he does not meet the qualification requirements or if information in the documents submitted is found to be unreliable [2].

## Chair and secretary

The chair organises the work of the Commission and conducts its meetings, which are held as needed [2]. The deputy chair performs the chair's duties during his temporary absence [2].

The secretary: notifies the members of the time and place of the meeting in advance; prepares the meetings; makes the ballots, ensures their processing and storage and takes part in the voting; keeps the protocol of the meetings on the form of Annex 1 and, where necessary, an extract from the protocol on the form of Annex 2, both signed by the chair and the secretary; keeps the protocol until it is transferred to archival storage; ensures the destruction of working materials, including the documents submitted by candidates, that contain personal data and are not subject to storage; after the period of operational storage transfers the protocols and other documents of meetings to the archive (the Sector for Work with Archival Documents); performs other functions under the Regulation and the chair's instructions; and is responsible for checking the completeness of the candidates' document sets [8]–[9].

## Meetings, quorum and voting

A candidate is considered elected if by secret ballot he receives more than half of the votes of the members who took part in the voting, with not less than 2/3 of the Commission's list taking part [2].

Meetings are held in the form of joint presence of the members; members may take part remotely by electronic or other technical means that allow their identity to be established reliably, discussion of the agenda and voting (video conference), and a meeting held by video conference counts as a meeting by joint presence, its venue being where the chair is [2]. By decision of the University meetings may be held fully remotely or in a hybrid format (part of the members physically present, part participating remotely), and a meeting in the form of absentee voting is allowed; in the absentee, distant or hybrid format the members send the ballot by e-mail and the secretary, on conditions of anonymity and confidentiality, prints the ballots, places them in the ballot box at the moment of voting and organises the count [2].

The members of the Commission also perform the functions of members of the counting commission and take part in the count of votes, while the secretary performs the functions of secretary of the counting commission and directly organises the voting and the count [2].

The order of actions at a meeting is: the secretary, not less than 1 working day before the meeting, sends the members the summary data on the candidates on the form of Annex 3; the Commission considers the materials on each candidate, and the candidate may be invited to the meeting where necessary; when the materials have been considered a secret ballot is held on the ballots of Annex 4; and the secretary, in the presence of the members, counts the votes and records the data in the protocol [10]–[11]. If the votes are equal, the chair's vote is decisive [2]. If no application for participation in the competition has been submitted by the end of the application period, the protocol records that the competition is recognised as not having taken place [12].

# Certificate of completion of aspirantura (свидетельство об окончании аспирантуры) — filling, signing, issue, accounting and duplicates

## The document

The Порядок заполнения, учета и выдачи свидетельства об окончании аспирантуры, приложения к нему и их дубликатов establishes the requirements for filling the certificate of completion of aspirantura, its annex and their duplicates, and the rules for issuing and accounting for them in the University [1]. It was approved by an order of the University of 23 June 2025 [1].

It was developed in accordance with Federal Law No. 127-ФЗ of 23 August 1996 «О науке и государственной научно-технической политике», Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29 December 2012 «Об образовании в Российской Федерации», Постановление of the Government of the Russian Federation of 30.11.2021 No. 2122, приказ Минобрнауки России of 24.02.2021 No. 118, приказ Минобрнауки России of 20.10.2021 No. 951, the University's Положение об итоговой аттестации по программам подготовки научных и научно-педагогических кадров в аспирантуре and its Положение о порядке приобретения, учета, хранения и списания бланков строгой отчетности [1].

The Порядок is approved by an order of the Director or of a person authorised by him and enters into force from the moment of approval; changes are made by an order of the Director or of an authorised person [2], [3].

## Who receives the certificate

- The certificate of completion of aspirantura is issued to persons who have successfully passed the final attestation on the programmes of training of scientific and scientific-pedagogical staff in aspirantura [1].
- The certificate and its annex are drawn up in the state language of the Russian Federation and certified with the University's seal [1].
- The blanks of the title and of the annex are filled by printer in black Times New Roman, 11 pt or the size indicated in the relevant clauses, with single line spacing; the font size may be changed where necessary [1].
- The filled blanks must be checked for accuracy; blanks with errors or other defects introduced in filling are considered spoiled and are to be replaced, and spoiled blanks are destroyed in the procedure established by the University [4].

## Filling the title of the certificate

- In the left part of the reverse side of the title: after the line «РОССИЙСКАЯ ФЕДЕРАЦИЯ» — the University's logo on a separate line, the full official name of the University in the nominative case according to the Charter, and the name of the settlement where the University is located with the type of settlement per the abbreviations of ОКАТО [5].
- Also in the left part: after «Регистрационный номер» — the registration number of the certificate from the book of registration of issued certificates, and after «Дата выдачи» — the date of issue with the day in figures, the month in words and the year [5].
- The date of issue of the certificate and of its annex corresponds to the date of approval of the conclusion that the thesis complies with the criteria established in accordance with Federal Law No. 127-ФЗ of 23 August 1996 [5].
- In the right part of the reverse side: after «Настоящее свидетельство подтверждает, что» — the graduate's surname and then the name and patronymic (if any), centred, 18 pt, in full according to the passport or another identity document; for a foreign citizen — according to the internal (general civil) or foreign passport, at the graduate's wish, in Russian transcription, the transcription and the kind of passport being agreed with the graduate in writing on the form of annex 3 [6].
- After the lines «освоил(а) программу подготовки научных и научно-педагогических кадров в аспирантуре по научной специальности» — the code and name of the scientific specialty per the nomenclature of scientific specialties for which academic degrees are awarded [6].
- After «и успешно прошел(а) итоговую аттестацию» — the number and date of the protocol with the decision of the expert-attestation commission [6].
- After the lines «Руководитель организации,» and «осуществляющей образовательную» — in the line «деятельность», right-aligned, the initials and surname of the Director (of the acting Director or of a person authorised by the Director) [6].

## Filling the annex

- Left column of the first page: the logo, the full official name of the University and the settlement, which must coincide with the title; after «ПРИЛОЖЕНИЕ К СВИДЕТЕЛЬСТВУ» — the words «об окончании аспирантуры»; the registration number and the date of issue, which must also coincide with the title [7].
- Section 1 «СВЕДЕНИЯ О ЛИЧНОСТИ ОБЛАДАТЕЛЯ СВИДЕТЕЛЬСТВА»: the graduate's surname, name, patronymic (if any) in the nominative case and the date of birth, which must coincide with the title; and after «Предыдущий документ об образовании и о квалификации» — the name of the document on education and qualification on the basis of which the graduate was admitted, and the year of its issue; for a document obtained abroad — its name translated into Russian and the country that issued it [8].
- Section 2 «НАУЧНАЯ СПЕЦИАЛЬНОСТЬ»: the code and name of the scientific specialty, which must coincide with the title, and the term of mastering the programme in full-time form established by the federal state requirements — in years — including where the term is extended because the education is obtained by disabled persons and persons with health limitations [9].
- Section 3 «СВЕДЕНИЯ О СОДЕРЖАНИИ И РЕЗУЛЬТАТАХ ОСВОЕНИЯ ПРОГРАММЫ ПОДГОТОВКИ НАУЧНО-ПЕДАГОГИЧЕСКИХ КАДРОВ В АСПИРАНТУРЕ»: each studied discipline (module), except facultative disciplines (modules), in the sequence of the curriculum — its name, its volume in credits (figures and «з.е.») and the mark obtained at the interim assessment [10].
- Also in section 3: the practices — «Практики» with the total volume in credits and «х» in the third column, then «в том числе:» and each type of practice with its volume and the mark obtained at the interim assessment [10].
- Also in section 3: the final attestation — «Итоговая аттестация» with its volume in credits and «х», then «в том числе:» and the form in which it was held, «оценка диссертации на предмет ее соответствия критериям, установленным в соответствии с Федеральным законом от 23 августа 1996 г. N 127-ФЗ «О науке и государственной научно-технической политике», with its volume and «х» [10].
- Also in section 3: «Объем образовательной программы» with the volume of the programme in credits and «х» [10], and, by agreement with the graduate, «Факультативные дисциплины (модули)» with «в том числе:» and each facultative discipline (module) with its volume and mark [10].
- In section 3 the word «дисциплина» is not used; for a module the word «модуль» is added in brackets after its name, and for disciplines on foreign languages and (or) languages of the peoples of the Russian Federation the particular languages may be given in brackets where the curriculum names them [11].
- Section 4 «НАУЧНЫЕ ИССЛЕДОВАНИЯ»: «Научный компонент» with the final mark for the scientific component obtained at the interim assessments, then «в том числе:» and the components «Научная деятельность, направленная на подготовку диссертации к защите» and the preparation of publications and (or) applications for patents, certificates of state registration of computer programmes, databases and topologies of integrated circuits envisaged by the federal state requirements, each marked «х»; the information is given without detail [12].
- In sections 3 and 4 the first column is left-aligned and the second and third are centred, and the marks are written out in words (отлично, хорошо, удовлетворительно, зачтено) [13].
- Section 5 «ДОПОЛНИТЕЛЬНЫЕ СВЕДЕНИЯ», on the fourth page: where the University's full official name changed during the studies, the year of the change and the previous full official name, repeated in chronological order for each change [14]; at the graduate's wish — the form of study, and, where part of the programme was mastered in another organisation (in the network form or under another aspirantura programme), the words «Часть образовательной программы в объеме ______ освоена в ______________________.» with the volume in credits and the full official name of the organisation [14].
- Whether the facultative disciplines (п. 3.4 д) and the additional data (п. 3.8 б) are filled must be agreed with the graduate in writing on the form of annex 5 [15].
- On the fourth page of the annex the initials and surname of the Director (or of the acting Director or of a person authorised by the Director) are placed, right-aligned [16].

## Signing and certification

- The certificate and its annex are signed by the Director of the University; they may be signed by the acting Director or by an official authorised by the Director on the basis of an order, in which case «/» is placed before «Руководитель организации,» and the initials and surname of that person are added [17].
- The signatures are made in black, blue or violet ink, paste or ink; signing by facsimile is not allowed, and the signatures on the certificate and on the annex must be identical [18].
- The University's seal is placed on the certificate and on the annex in the place reserved for it [19].

## Issue

- The certificate and its annex are issued not later than 30 calendar days from the date of completion of the final attestation; the graduate may receive them after that period has expired [20].
- They are issued by the structural unit responsible for the training of scientific and scientific-pedagogical staff in aspirantura [21].
- On paper: handed to the graduate in person or to another person under a power of attorney certified in the procedure established by Russian legislation, or, on the graduate's application, sent to the address he indicated by registered post with acknowledgement of receipt [21].
- In electronic form: on the graduate's written personal application (form of annex 6) a copy of the certificate and annex, certified with the electronic signature of the Director (or of the acting Director or of a person authorised by the Director), may be sent as an electronic document by the Internet to the e-mail address he indicated [21].
- The certificate is valid without its annex, while the annex is invalid without the certificate [22].
- A copy of the issued certificate and annex, a power of attorney and the applications (to send the documents by post, to agree the surname, name and patronymic) are kept in the graduate's personal file [23].
- The unit responsible for aspirantura training must give information about the issued certificates and annexes to the unit responsible for entering data into ФИС ФРДО («Федеральный реестр сведений о документах об образовании и (или) о квалификации, документах об обучении») within 1 working day after their issue, and that information is entered into ФИС ФРДО within 3 working days from the issue [24].

## Registration book

The book of registration of issued certificates (form of annex 2) records the registration number of the certificate or duplicate, the graduate's surname, name and patronymic (or those of the person receiving the document under a power of attorney), the series and number of the blanks of the certificate and of the annex, the date of issue, the code and name of the scientific specialty, the date and number of the protocol of the expert-examination commission, the date and number of the order on the graduate's expulsion, the signature of the head of the responsible unit and the signature of the recipient, with the power of attorney or postal item data where applicable [1].

## Duplicates

- Duplicates are filled, signed and certified according to the requirements for filling blanks set out in the Порядок for the certificate itself, taking account of the special rules on duplicates [25].
- A duplicate is issued on the holder's personal application (form of annex 7) within a month after the University receives it: on loss or damage of the certificate and (or) its annex or of a duplicate; where errors are found in them after receipt; and on a change of the holder's surname, name and (or) patronymic [26].
- If only the certificate (or its duplicate) is lost or damaged, or errors are found only in it, a duplicate of the certificate and a duplicate of the annex are issued; if only the annex (or its duplicate) is lost or damaged, or errors are found only in it, a duplicate of the annex is issued [27].
- On damage, on errors and on a change of name the surviving certificate and (or) annex (or their duplicates) are withdrawn from the holder when the duplicate is issued and are destroyed in the procedure established by the University [28]; on a change of name copies of the confirming documents are attached to the application [29].
- A duplicate is issued in the ways of п. 5.2 [30].
- The word «ДУБЛИКАТ» is printed on a separate line, centred — on the title before the line «СВИДЕТЕЛЬСТВО ОБ ОКОНЧАНИИ АСПИРАНТУРЫ», and on the annex before the lines «ПРИЛОЖЕНИЕ К СВИДЕТЕЛЬСТВУ об окончании аспирантуры» [31].
- Where a duplicate of the certificate and a duplicate of the annex are issued, they bear the registration number and the date of issue of the duplicate of the certificate; where only a duplicate of the annex is issued, it bears its own registration number and date of issue [32].
- The entries in the duplicate are made according to the documents in the graduate's personal file; where the duplicate of the annex cannot be filled, the duplicate of the certificate is issued without an annex [33]. Where information is absent, «х» or «-» is placed in its stead, and the duplicate bears the registration number and the date of issue of the duplicate [34].
- A duplicate is issued by the University or its legal successor; where the University (or its successor) has been liquidated, by the founder, and in the absence of the founder by the state body or local self-government body in charge of the archive holding the graduates' documents or by another organisation to which the University's archive was transferred [35].
- A duplicate of the certificate without an annex is valid, while a duplicate of the annex is invalid without the certificate or a duplicate of the certificate [36].
- A copy of the issued duplicate, a power of attorney and the applications are kept in the graduate's personal file [37].

## Forms attached to the Порядок

Annex 1 is the form of the certificate, annex 2 the book of registration, annex 3 the application agreeing the name of a foreign citizen, annex 4 the form of the annex to the certificate, annex 5 the application on the information to be entered in the annex (form of study, part of the programme mastered in another organisation, facultative disciplines), annex 6 the application to send the documents by e-mail or post, and annex 7 the application for duplicates [1].

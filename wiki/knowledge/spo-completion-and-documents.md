# Completion of secondary vocational education (СПО) studies and the documents issued — the Regulation of 27 January 2025

## Where these rules come from

The Regulation on the procedure of organising and carrying out educational activity under secondary vocational education programmes of ANO VO «Innopolis University» was approved by order of the University of 27 January 2025 No. ОД/УД-04/2025/01/27 and put into force from 1 September 2025 [1]. Its clauses on the end of study under an СПО programme, the documents issued and the vacations after certification are set out below [1].

## Final state certification or final certification

- Mastering an СПО programme that has state accreditation ends with the obligatory state final certification (ГИА); mastering an СПО programme that has no state accreditation ends with a final certification (ИА) [2].
- Students who have no academic debt and have completed in full the curriculum or the individual curriculum are admitted to the ГИА or ИА [2].

## Documents on education

- Persons who have successfully passed the ГИА or ИА receive a diploma of secondary vocational education confirming the obtaining of СПО and of the qualification in the corresponding СПО speciality [3].
- Persons who did not pass the ГИА or ИА or received unsatisfactory results there, as well as persons who mastered part of the educational programme and (or) were expelled from the University, receive a certificate of study or of the period of study (справка об обучении или о периоде обучения) on the form established by the University [4].
- Where ФГОС СПО provides within one of the types of professional activity for mastering the profession of qualified worker or the position of employee, the student receives, on the results of mastering the professional module of the СПО programme that includes practice, a certificate of the profession of worker or of the position of employee, and the obtaining of professional training in such a profession or position within the programme ends with the passing of a qualification examination [5].
- The document of education presented on admission is issued from the personal file to a person who has completed the University or was expelled before completing it, and to a student wishing to enter another educational organisation, on his application; a certified copy of the document of education is kept in the personal file [6].

## Secondary general education and external certification

- Students who do not have secondary general education have the right to pass the state final certification that completes the mastering of the educational programme of secondary general education; on its successful passing they receive an аттестат of secondary general education, and this state final certification is held free of charge [7].
- Persons who studied an educational programme without state accreditation may pass the interim and state final certification externally (экстерном) at the University under the corresponding state-accredited programme; such persons who do not have secondary general education may pass the interim and state final certification externally under the corresponding state-accredited programme free of charge, and during the certification externs enjoy the academic rights of students of the corresponding СПО programme [8].

## Vacations and expulsion at the end of study

After passing the ГИА or ИА vacations are given on the students' application within the term of mastering the corresponding educational programme, and at their end the students are expelled in connection with the completion of education [9].

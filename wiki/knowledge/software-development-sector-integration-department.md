# Software Development Sector of the Integration Department (Сектор разработки программного обеспечения) — place, tasks, functions and rights

## The document

The Regulation governs the activity of the Software Development Sector (Сектор разработки программного обеспечения) of the Integration Department of the Centre for the Development of Industrial Robotics of the Centre for Automation and Robotisation of ANO VO «Innopolis University» and determines its tasks, functions, rights and duties [1]. The wiki page is titled «Сектор разработки программного обеспечения 1 — Положение» [2].

The copy on the wiki carries an approval block «УТВЕРЖДЕНО приказом АНО ВО «Университет Иннополис»» with the date and the order number left blank, signed by the Director for Development and HR Policy R.F. Valiev, Innopolis [2]. So no order number or approval date can be taken from this page [2].

## Place in the structure

- The Sector is a structural subdivision of the Integration Department and belongs to the category of research subdivisions (научно-исследовательские подразделения) [3].
- It reports directly to the head of the Integration Department [4].
- In its activity the Sector is guided by the Constitution of the Russian Federation, the Labour Code of the Russian Federation, Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29 December 2012 «Об образовании в Российской Федерации» and other legislative and normative legal acts regulating the activity of higher education institutions, the University's Charter, decisions of the University's Academic, Supervisory and Operational councils, orders and instructions of the University director, other local acts and this Regulation [5].
- The Sector's structure, headcount and staffing table, and changes to them, are approved by the University director [6].
- The Sector is headed by a head of the software development sector (руководитель сектора разработки программного обеспечения), hired and dismissed by order of the University director [7].
- The working conditions of the Sector's employees are determined by the employment contracts concluded with each employee and by the University's internal labour regulations [8]; their rights, duties and degree of responsibility are established by job descriptions [9].
- Within their competence, the Sector's employees may be involved in work under grants, projects, subsidies and other programmes implemented by the University [10].
- The Regulation and additions and changes to it are approved by the University director or another person authorised by him [11].

## Tasks

The Sector's tasks are:

- participation in the commercialisation of the results of the Sector's activity — commercialisation being activity bringing the results of research, developments and technologies of the Centre for the Development of Industrial Robotics into economic circulation by transferring rights to them on a paid basis, carrying out development on third-party orders or in other ways providing income [12];
- development and issue of working software documentation (рабочая программная документация), both for whole solutions and for their individual parts, for the implementation of commercial projects in industrial robotics [13];
- carrying out the software part of applied scientific research in industrial robotics [14];
- development of the software parts of complexes of scientific and technical solutions in industrial robotics [15];
- carrying out research, development and technological work (НИР, ОКР, НИОКТР), industrial projects and grants in the profile of the Centre for the Development of Industrial Robotics, on its own initiative and under grant support, from budget and non-budget funds [16];
- participation, as part of the Integration Department, in the implementation of state programmes, federal and national projects in industrial robotics and other related fields [17];
- popularisation of robotisation and automation of industrial enterprises [18];
- identification of the potential and directions of robotisation and automation of industrial enterprises [19].

## Functions

The Sector performs the following functions:

- step-by-step planning and conduct of software development within НИР, ОКР, НИОКТР, projects and grants [20];
- development of scientific research programmes in the field of activity of the Centre for the Development of Industrial Robotics, in the part on software and project documentation [21];
- technical and technological audits of industrial enterprises in order to identify their robotisation potential and propose technical solutions [22].

## Rights

In carrying out their activity the Sector's employees have the right to:

- request from the University's structural subdivisions the information and materials needed for the work within the Sector's competence [23];
- involve, with the consent of the heads of the University's structural subdivisions, employees of those subdivisions for preparing drafts of local acts and other documents and for developing and carrying out projects conducted by the Sector [24];
- take part in meetings held by the University's management where matters within the Sector's competence are discussed [25];
- put before the University's management proposals to repeal, amend or supplement the University's local acts [26];
- interact with state authorities and bodies of administration, legal entities and subdivisions of the University on matters within the Sector's competence [27].

## Interaction with other subdivisions

- Interaction with the University's other structural subdivisions is determined by the tasks and functions assigned to the Sector by the Regulation [28].
- The Sector has to agree its decisions with the other structural subdivisions of the Integration Department where those decisions touch their competence and activity [29].
- The Sector may serve as a base for the practical training of the University's students [30].
- Interaction with the Cross-industry Centre for Technology Transfer (Межотраслевой центр трансфера технологий) concerns the legal protection of results of intellectual activity created at the Centre for the Development of Industrial Robotics, the exercise, disposal and protection of the University's rights to such results, their effective use and commercialisation [31].
- Interaction with other subdivisions also takes place in the course of preparing drafts of local acts, orders, instructions, contracts and other documents and of preparing opinions and written and oral references on matters within the Sector's competence [32].

## Reporting

- On the basis of the relevant local acts, orders and instructions the Sector may, within its activity, be responsible for preparing and keeping up to date information, references and reports [33].
- The Sector is responsible for preparing reports on НИР, ОКР, НИОКТР, industrial projects and grants and for passing them in time to the University's internal structural subdivisions [34].
- The Sector reports as required by federal executive authorities, executive authorities of the subjects of the Russian Federation and other bodies, agencies and organisations — in line with obligations under grants (subsidies) provided from the budgets of the budget system of the Russian Federation, and in other cases provided by law; in other cases reporting is determined by the contracts concluded with third parties [35].

# Centre for Digital Economy Project Development (Центр развития проектов цифровой экономики) — place, tasks, functions and rights

## The document

The page is the Regulation on the Centre for Digital Economy Project Development (Центр развития проектов цифровой экономики) of ANO VO «Innopolis University» [1]. It regulates the Centre's activity and determines its tasks, functions, rights and duties [2]. The page presents the Regulation as approved by order of the University of 31 May 2021 № ОД/АД-07/2021/05/31, signed by the Director for Development and HR Policy (R.F. Valiev) [1]. The wiki copy was read by OCR, so its numbers and dates should be checked against the PDF [1].

## Place in the structure

- The Centre is a structural subdivision of the University's Department for Project and Research Activity and belongs to the category of other structural subdivisions (иные структурные подразделения) [3].
- The Centre reports directly to the vice-rector — head of the Department for Project and Research Activity [4].
- In its activity the Centre is guided by the Constitution of the Russian Federation, the Labour Code of the Russian Federation, the Federal Law «Об образовании в Российской Федерации», other legislative and normative legal acts on higher education institutions, the University's Charter, decisions of the University's Academic, Supervisory and Operational councils, orders and instructions of the University director, other local acts and this Regulation [5].
- The structure, headcount and staffing table of the Centre, and changes to them, are approved by the University director [6].
- The Centre is headed by a head of the Centre, hired and dismissed by order of the University director [7].
- The working conditions of the Centre's employees are determined by the employment contracts concluded with each employee and by the University's internal labour rules [8]; their rights, duties and responsibility are set by job descriptions [9].
- Within their competence the Centre's employees may be involved in work under grants, projects, subsidies and other programmes carried out by the University [10].
- The Regulation and amendments to it are approved by the University director or another person authorised by the director [11].

## Tasks

- Searching for and analysing tenders, grants and competitions, submitting and preparing applications and interacting with other universities and scientists in this field, in Russia [12] and internationally [13]; finding and maintaining partner links with international and foreign industrial companies that raise the level of educational and scientific work, improve the material and technical base and raise the University's international reputation and recognition [14]; attracting organisations [15] and international and foreign universities and organisations [16] for joint НИР, НИОКР, joint laboratories and departments and consulting in information technology and robotics.
- Promoting the development of the University's innovative activity, creating conditions for the effective management of intellectual activity results and for the transfer (commercialisation) of technologies and developments, and providing consulting and scientific-technical services in the innovation sphere [17]; creating an organisational structure for that transfer and commercialisation [18]; helping the authors of developments realise their project initiatives and commercialise innovative developments [19]; promoting innovative projects based on created intellectual property objects [20].
- Promoting the use of the exclusive rights to the University's intellectual property [21]; keeping a register and evaluating commercially promising scientific, technical and technological work [22]; protecting the University's interests in the distribution of rights to intellectual property objects created in scientific, technical and technological work [23]; increasing the value of the University's intangible assets through a qualitative growth of its intellectual property portfolio [24]; developing and carrying out a single patent and licence policy [25]; securing for the University the exclusive rights to objects created in its educational, scientific, production, international and other activity [26]; protecting the University's exclusive rights and the authors' exclusive and personal non-property rights [27]; increasing the portfolio of objects whose rights belong to the University [28]; seeking out ideas, projects and developments with high commercial potential [29]; examining the commercial potential of created objects [30].
- Information and analytical support of developments in the performance of research and development work [31].
- Developing the University's international activity through international НИР, НИОКР, educational projects and programmes [32]; coordinating the University's subdivisions in developing and performing the international agreements, programmes, projects and contracts in which the University takes part [33].
- Systematically controlling the progress of НИР and the preparation of reporting [34]; observing the normative requirements and the completeness and quality of documentation and the procedure for its approval [35]; organising the provision of timely and good-quality reporting documentation on grants and projects [36].

## Functions

- Organising the information and analytical search for competitions for grants and tenders, in Russia and internationally, in research and innovation [37]; making a first examination and selection of open programmes and competitions in order to decide whether participation is possible [38]; collecting and processing materials on the subjects of competitive projects [39].
- Organising and controlling the application process by coordinating the actions of all participants in writing and submitting an application [40]; negotiating with them [41]; preliminary planning of work on projects [42]; consulting on the substantive part of applications [43]; preparing presentations of projects [44]; completing applications for tenders and grants and preparing their printed copies [45] [46]; consulting and methodological help to the University's employees in completing applications [47]; long-term and current planning of employees' participation in tenders and grants [48].
- Monitoring the results of competitions and informing the participants [49]; compiling sample and final reports on the participation of the University's employees and subdivisions in scientific competitions and programmes of domestic and foreign funds, organisations and agencies [50] and in international competitions and programmes [51].
- Organising and controlling the signing of agreements with the grant provider, the industrial partner and co-executors [52]; concluding cooperation contracts, cooperation agreements and memoranda of understanding with foreign legal entities, industrial partners and international companies, and controlling the performance of agreements with foreign industrial partners [53]; negotiating with the parties to grant and other agreements, contracts and memoranda [54].
- Forming and maintaining databases on international cooperation, existing programmes, competitions and foreign scientific and practical forums and providing that information to subdivisions and employees, including by telecommunication technologies [55]; organising information work to draw subdivisions, employees, teachers and students into programmes and competitions for tenders and grants [56]; generalising the experience of subdivisions and employees in preparing and promoting research projects to grant-making organisations [57]; organising information support for specialists carrying out research, development and technological work [58]; conducting the University's international correspondence with interested persons and organisations and processing foreign correspondence on the Centre's activity [59].
- Developing proposals on the University's participation in tenders and grants, Russian and international, and on improving the organisation of research and of international research activity [60]; developing methodological recommendations ordering work in tenders, grants and international scientific and technical project activity [61]; preparing recommendations on priority lines and forms of interaction with foreign partners and on its effectiveness [62].
- Organising the University's interaction with scientific funds, national competition operators, international organisations, operators of international programmes and other grant-making organisations [63]; strategic planning, initiating, organising, coordinating and analysing all types of international scientific and technical activity [64].
- Conducting patent research on the research and development work performed and on the University's priority lines of development [65]; organising patent research, searching patent materials and evaluating the technical level of developments [66]; organising patent examination of work and determining the stages at which the patent clearance of created objects must be checked [67]; preparing application materials for the legal protection of intellectual property objects, including abroad [68].
- Consulting management on the University's intellectual property policy [69]; analytical and consulting support for putting rights to intellectual property and its media into circulation [70]; analytical and information support of international cooperation in intellectual property [71]; help to the planning and economic subdivisions in determining authors' remuneration [72]; forming the register of the University's intellectual property objects [73]; consulting employees on intellectual property management [74]; forming the register of scientific and technical developments for commercialisation [75]; technological audit of subdivisions and projects to identify protectable results [76]; preparation, control and support of licence agreements and other forms of commercialisation [77]; consulting on technology transfer and commercialisation as regards the University's exclusive rights [78].
- Supporting the state registration of НИР [79]; controlling the work and reporting on the projects implemented as regards deadlines and the grant provider's or customer's requirements [80]; making the responsible executives acquainted with those requirements [81]; training them to work with the grant provider's information and analytical systems for reporting [82]; controlling the fulfilment of formal indicators [83]; keeping the project records required by the grant provider or customer [84]; submitting reporting on the projects in time [85]; analysing the execution of the expenditure estimate and the accounting statements [86]; recording and controlling expenses, correcting estimates and reconciling planned and actual expenses with the finance and economics department [87].
- Keeping the necessary records [88]; presenting analytical, statistical and other information on the Centre's activity [89]; carrying out the instructions of the head of the Centre, of the vice-rector — head of the Department for Project and Research Activity and of the University director [90].

## Rights

- Requesting from the University's subdivisions the information and materials needed for work within the Centre's competence [91].
- Involving, with the consent of the heads of the University's subdivisions, their employees for preparing drafts of local acts and other documents and for developing and carrying out the Centre's activities [92].
- Taking part in meetings held by the University's management on matters within the Centre's competence [93].
- Proposing to the University's management participation in tenders and grants, improvement of the organisation of НИОКР, and the repeal of, amendments to and additions to local acts [94].
- Interacting with state authorities, legal entities, subdivisions of the University, invited scientists and other participants in submitting applications and implementing projects [95].
- Setting tasks for the heads and members of the University's project teams on preparing and processing information and requiring documents and materials in time [96]; setting tasks on project support for the heads of the subdivisions involved in project activity [97].

## Interaction with other subdivisions

- Interaction with the other structural subdivisions is determined by the tasks and functions assigned to the Centre by this Regulation [98], and with their heads it takes place on matters within the Centre's competence [99].
- Interaction with state institutions and Russian and international organisations and with structures of the real sector of the economy takes place according to the Centre's functions and in the procedure determined by the University's Charter and local acts [100]; with the head of the personnel department it concerns hiring employees for the Centre [101].
- With the Finance and Economics Department it concerns preparing agreements with project participants and reports on the projects implemented [102]; with the Finance and Economics and Analytical departments and the Legal Directorate it concerns agreements on won tenders and grants and reports on projects [103].
- With the other subdivisions it concerns applications for tenders and grants, the distribution of rights to the results of НИР, НИОКР, industrial projects and grants, accounting for the intellectual activity results created, drafts of local acts, orders, instructions and contracts and opinions and written and oral reports [104].
- The Centre may be a base for the practical preparation of the University's students [105].

## Reporting

On the basis of the relevant local acts, orders and instructions the Centre may be responsible, within its activity, for preparing and keeping up to date information, reports and accounts [106].

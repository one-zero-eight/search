# Rules of conduct and discipline in the University

## The basis of discipline

Academic discipline at the University rests on respect for the human dignity of students, teaching, research and administrative staff, and on students' conscious and conscientious performance of their duties; the University creates the conditions needed for students to keep discipline [1]. Application of physical and (or) mental violence towards students and employees is not allowed [2].

Compliance with the Rules, including discipline in classes and conduct in the University, is controlled by teaching and managerial staff and by other persons charged with those duties [3].

These rules are section 4 and section 8 of the Rules of internal order for students (Правила внутреннего распорядка обучающихся), approved by the director of the University [4]; the wiki page is an OCR reading of a scanned document marked draft, with a warning to check numbers and dates against the PDF [4].

## What students must observe

Students must strictly observe the rules of conduct in the University and discipline in classes, including discipline connected with ongoing assessment, with passing interim assessment and final (state final) attestation, and with the requirements for writing papers (including final qualification works and final attestation (qualification) works) [5].

Students are forbidden [6]:

- to perform actions contrary to the charter, these Rules, the University's local acts and Russian legislation [7];
- to do work or perform other actions in the University's premises and classrooms that create heightened noise, disrupt the study process or hinder the work of employees and students [8];
- to provide forged (false) documents, to provide and spread knowingly false information [9];
- to paste announcements, schedules, leaflets, advertising posters, flags and the like on the walls of study buildings, administrative buildings, campuses, stands and common areas [10];
- to smoke in the University's buildings and on its territory (including hookahs and steam cocktails), except in places specially equipped for smoking, and to use, keep or carry electronic vaping devices or devices imitating cigarette smoke in the University's buildings [11];
- to bring outsiders into the study-laboratory building and the residential campus without observing the established access and internal security rules, or to hand over to outsiders electronic passes or other documents giving the right to enter the University's buildings [12];
- to appear in the University's buildings and on the adjoining territory in a state of alcohol or drug intoxication, or to consume, keep or distribute alcoholic drinks, narcotic and psychotropic substances [13];
- to engage in unauthorised trading [14];
- to organise gambling or take part in it [15];
- to take objects and equipment out of laboratories, offices, classrooms, study buildings, residential rooms and other premises without the corresponding permission [16];
- to keep or carry weapons and their mock-ups (regardless of a licence) in the University's buildings and premises and the residential campus, or to bring in, keep and use flammable liquids and explosive or chemically hazardous substances [17];
- to hold meetings not connected with the educational and extracurricular activity of student clubs and communities, to hold rallies and demonstrations, dances, discos and other events without the permission of the University's leadership [18];
- to use sources of open fire in the buildings, including the residential complex, on the territory and in common areas [19];
- to take part in committing unlawful actions provided by Russian legislation [20];
- to use obscene language and allow other antisocial behaviour, to be rude, to insult other students, employees, visitors and other third persons [21];
- to litter, to spit, to throw household waste out of windows, to lean out of windows, to enter and leave the University's buildings through windows, or otherwise to behave improperly in breach of the rules of conduct in the University [22];
- to enter service and technical premises, emergency exits, attics and basements without permission, or to go out onto the roofs of buildings and onto external canopies [23];
- to sit or lie and sleep on tables and desks, window sills, cornices and handrails [24];
- to violate the rights and lawful interests of other students and employees of the University [25];
- to bring or lead animals into the University's buildings and territory, and to keep animals, birds, reptiles and other living creatures in the University's residential complex [26];
- to conduct religious activity publicly on the territory of the University and the residential complex, or to perform any religious rites [27];
- to perform actions creating noise and disturbing other students and the teacher, or to interrupt the teacher [28];
- to take photographs or video of classroom classes without the written permission of the teacher and the University's administration [29];
- to be in classrooms in outer clothing or headwear [30].

## Mobile phones during classes and assessment

It is forbidden to use mobile phones for personal purposes during classes, interim assessment and final (state final) attestation, including portable devices connected to mobile phones (headphones, electronic watches, external sound reproduction devices and the like) [31]. The exceptions are [31]:

- a threat to the life or health of students or employees of the University [31];
- cases where the use of a mobile phone, including its separate functions (camera, calculator, dictaphone and others), is directly provided by the working programme of the discipline (module), practice, the programme of the final (state final) attestation, the assessment funds, the means or method of teaching or the educational technology [31];
- contact work of students with teachers using e-learning and distance educational technologies [31].

## Other prohibitions in class and in communication

- Not to interrupt classes and not to enter or leave classrooms during them without the permission of the person conducting the class [32].
- Not to miss without a valid reason meetings appointed by the University's employees and (or) leadership concerning the student's educational activity and duties [33].
- Not to ignore messages received by corporate e-mail and in Telegram from the University's employees concerning the student's duties and administrative and academic activity [34].
- Not to perform actions aimed at breaching the rules of military registration and Russian migration legislation [35].

## Order in the University's buildings

The University must ensure the protection of its buildings, keep them in a technically sound condition permitting the normal functioning of all its units, and ensure the safety of equipment and other property used in educational, research and economic activity [36]. While in the University's buildings, students must observe the generally accepted norms of behaviour in public places [37]. Students are recommended to come to the University neatly dressed, and it is strongly recommended to avoid details emphasising belonging to a subculture (abundance of metal chains, pins, rivets and the like) in clothing [38]. All the University's premises are used exclusively for the purpose determined by the University to ensure the educational process [39].

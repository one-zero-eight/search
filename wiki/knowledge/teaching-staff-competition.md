# Competition for teaching staff positions (конкурс на замещение должностей ППС)

## The document

The Regulation governs the competition (конкурс) to fill positions of pedagogical workers belonging to the teaching staff (профессорско-преподавательский состав) in Innopolis University; it was approved by order of the director of 1 April 2024 № ОД/АД-04/2024/04/01 [1].

It determines the aims and tasks, the creation, composition and activity of the Competition Commission and the procedure of the competition, the criteria of evaluation and the deadlines for advertising positions, and the functions of structural subdivisions in the competitive selection, including the rights, duties and responsibility of the University's officials and of the Commission's members [1].

It rests on the Labour Code of the Russian Federation, Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29 December 2012 «Об образовании в Российской Федерации», orders of the Ministry of Education and Science on the procedure for filling teaching staff positions and on attestation, a Government decree on the nomenclature of positions, an order of the Ministry of Health and Social Development approving the qualification handbook, the Charter and other applicable local acts [2].

The Regulation is approved by the director and is placed on the official site under «Сведения об образовательной организации»/«Документы»/«Основные документы», and changes to it are approved by the director [3] [4]. The rule on the Commission, its composition and working order, and the procedure of the competition are published on https://innopolis.university [5].

## Positions covered, election and its term

The positions of the teaching staff are the assistant (ассистент), dean of a faculty, director of an institute, доцент, head of a department (заведующий кафедрой), professor, преподаватель and senior lecturer (старший преподаватель) [6].

Conclusion of an employment contract for a teaching position, and transfer to such a position, is preceded by election by competition, with the term of election stated [7]. Passing the competition is an obligatory condition for concluding such a contract for anyone applying for a teaching position — both persons newly joining the University and those already working in it [8]. A contract is concluded with the person who passed the competition in the manner set by labour legislation [9].

The term of election is set by the Commission; it may be indefinite or fixed within not less than 3 and not more than 5 years [7]. To preserve the continuity of the educational process a contract may be concluded without election for part-time work for not more than 1 year, and to replace a temporarily absent worker for whom the workplace is preserved — until that worker returns to work [7]. Where a contract is concluded for a certain work of a knowingly fixed-term (temporary) nature, election may be for less than 3 years, but not less than 1 year [7].

Teachers with whom contracts for an indefinite term were concluded do not pass a competition for that position during the whole period of filling it; to confirm that the worker corresponds to the position, attestation is held once every 5 years under order of the Ministry of Education and Science on attestation and the Regulation on the attestation commission [10].

A competition for vacant positions is not held when a teacher is transferred with his consent — including in connection with the reorganisation of the University or of its structural subdivision and/or a reduction of staff — to a position analogous to or lower than the one he holds, or on part-time terms in the same structural subdivision, or when he is transferred to another structural subdivision before the end of his contract [11].

## Qualification requirements

Every candidate for any teaching position must meet the qualification requirements for positions in higher education institutions set out below; they were drawn up in accordance with the qualification handbook approved by an order of the Ministry of Health and Social Development [1].

- Professor: a doctor's degree and not less than 5 years of research and teaching work, or the title of professor [12].
- Доцент: higher professional education, a candidate's (doctor's) degree and not less than 3 years of research and teaching work, or the title of доцент (senior research fellow) [13].
- Senior lecturer: higher professional education and not less than 3 years of research and teaching work, and with a candidate's degree not less than 1 year; priority goes to candidates with a candidate's degree [14].
- Преподаватель: higher professional education and not less than 1 year of work in an educational institution, and with postgraduate education (aspirantura, ordinatura, adjunctura) or a candidate's degree — without requirements to length of service; priority goes to candidates with a candidate's degree [14].
- Assistant: higher professional education and not less than 1 year of work in an educational institution, and with postgraduate education or a candidate's degree — without requirements to length of service; priority goes to candidates with a candidate's degree [15].

By way of exception, on the recommendation of the attestation commission, persons without the special training or length of service of section 6 but with sufficient practical experience who perform their duties well and in full may take part in the competition for these positions [16]. Persons without a candidate's or doctor's degree and without an academic title but with research and teaching work, or work in organisations in a field of activity corresponding to the University's, may be appointed to доцент or professor positions, elected by competition or without election when employed part-time for not more than 1 year, or to replace a temporarily absent worker until that worker returns [16]. Education and/or qualifications obtained in a foreign state and recognised in the Russian Federation under international treaties and Russian legislation are taken into account [17].

## The four stages

The secretary of the Commission is responsible for organising and holding the competitive selection [18]. It runs in four stages: publication of the announcement and collection of applications; assessment of the candidates (initial review of the documents, review of the documents by experts, interviews at the University or remotely, trial class or classes at the University or remotely); the competition and election of the final candidate; and the formalisation of the employment relationship [19].

### Stage 1: announcement and applications

- Not later than 1 July an order of the director approves the list of teaching workers (name and position) whose employment contract expires in the next academic year, and it is placed on the official site [20].
- Where a teaching position is vacant, the competitive selection is announced by the director during the academic year; heads of structural subdivisions or a vice-rector submit to the director applications for holding a competition on the form of Annex 5, and a competition may be announced during the academic year in the same order for a vacancy in the subdivision [21] [22].
- On the basis of the director's order and the applications the secretary forms and places the announcement on the official site not less than 2 months before the date of the competition (and, where necessary, on the vacancy portal); it is approved by the director [23].
- The announcement states the list of positions, the qualification requirements under the qualification handbooks and/or professional standards, the duties, the term of the contract corresponding to the term of election, the place where applications are received, the e-mail and postal address for applications, the period for applications (not less than 1 month from publication), the place, date and form of the competition and its procedure in that form, and the procedure and periods for changing the conditions or cancelling the competition (only in the first half of the application period and in the same way as it was announced) [24].
- To attract persons with experience of educational work in leading foreign educational and other organisations, the secretary sends invitations to leading foreign specialists [25].
- The application must reach the University before the end of the application period stated in the announcement [26].
- In the remote form of the competition, documents may be submitted electronically and the competitive procedures held with telecommunication means [27].
- By decision of the Commission the period for considering applications may be extended to 30 working days from the end of the application period where an interview is needed, including by video conference; the extension is published on the official site (and, where necessary, on the vacancy portal) [28].
- The application must be accompanied by copies of documents confirming that the candidate meets the qualification requirements and has no restrictions on work in education; an approximate list is in section 7, and the announcement may require additional documents [28].
- A person without the special training or the length of service but with sufficient practical experience, who performs his duties well and in full, may by way of exception be appointed to the position on the recommendation of the attestation commission, in the same way as a person with the training and service [29].
- A candidate is not admitted if his documents do not meet the requirements for the position, if the documents named in the announcement are not submitted, or if the application period is missed [30].

### Stage 2: assessment

The secretary checks the candidate's documents against the requirements of section 5, forms the list of candidates and the document package and sends it to the dean of the faculty, to the vice-rector – head of the department of academic policy and organisation of educational activity and to the head of the department for the organisation of bachelor's and master's programmes [31]. Candidates for teaching positions may be asked to give a trial class or classes, both at the University and remotely by video conference, and the University may compensate the candidate's transport, accommodation and visa expenses connected with taking part in the competition [32]. When the assessment is complete the secretary forms the list of candidates taking part and sends the package of documents on each of them to the Commission [33].

### Stage 3: the competition and election

The candidate has the right to be present when his candidature is considered, and his absence does not prevent the competition from being held [34] [35]. The decision on each candidate is taken by secret ballot and recorded in a protocol [36]. If the ballot was on a single candidate and he did not obtain the required number of votes, the competition did not take place; if two or more candidates took part and none obtained it, a second round of election is held on the day of the competition, with a repeat secret ballot on the two candidates who obtained the most votes in the first round, and if again no one obtains more than half of the votes the competition did not take place; the competition also did not take place if no application was submitted or none of the applicants was admitted to it [36].

### Stage 4: formalisation of the employment relationship

A contract for the position is concluded with the person who passed the competition, corresponding to the term of election, or an additional agreement to the existing contract is drawn up stating the position, the term and the other new conditions, and the parties sign it [37]. Where a foreign citizen passes a competition for a vacant position, the Department for the Attraction and Support of Teaching Staff prepares and sends him an offer of work and, if he accepts it, provides administrative support of the invitation and hiring, including the visa invitation and the work permit, including as a highly qualified specialist [38]. Contracts may be concluded both with election by competition and without it, for the terms of пп. 1.5–1.7 [39].

A teaching position is declared vacant if within 30 calendar days from the Commission's decision the person who passed the competition has not concluded or extended the contract on his own initiative [40]. The expiry of the contract is a ground for ending the employment relationship if the teacher refuses to take part in the competition for a new term, does not submit the documents for the competition, does not pass attestation, or if the competition is not announced because the position is absent from the staffing table or the staffing table changes [41]. Disputes under the contract are settled by agreement of the parties or, failing that, in the manner established by law [42].

## Documents

After the competition is announced, anyone applying sends to the e-mail address in the announcement copies of documents confirming that he meets the qualification requirements and has no restrictions on work in education, in particular: an application (Annex 6); a CV in English (optionally duplicated in Russian); a list of scientific publications; a list of the courses taught with the periods and organisations; a cover letter on teaching activity in English (optionally duplicated in Russian); a cover letter on research (scientific) activity where the candidate does scientific work (optionally duplicated in Russian); recommendation letters, if any, in English (optionally duplicated in Russian); links to profiles in social networks and electronic research communities; documents on education and qualifications; documents confirming teaching, scientific or research and teaching service; a document confirming the absence of restrictions on work in education; and consent to the processing of personal data (Annex 7) [43].

On employment the candidate presents to the staff of the Department for the Attraction and Support of Teaching Staff or of the Department of Personnel Records and Movement: passport; ИНН; insurance certificate; the diploma, including one obtained abroad, legalised and recognised in the Russian Federation; the supplement to the higher education diploma, if any; documents on the academic degree and/or title; a notarised translation into Russian of documents in a foreign language; documents confirming teaching, scientific or research and teaching service; qualification certificates for the last 3 years; the military record book (all pages with entries); the work record book or data from the СФР on all employment; the marriage certificate where the education documents are in another surname; children's birth certificates where there are children under 18; a 2-НДФЛ certificate from the previous place of work for the current year; a 3×4 photograph; a certificate on the presence or absence of a criminal record or prosecution or on its termination on exculpatory grounds; a medical book and the conclusions of a medical examination and psychiatric examination passed on referrals issued by the department; and bank details for the payment of wages [43]. Scanned copies must have clear images with legible text [43].

Foreign citizens present the same staff with: passport; diploma, including one obtained abroad, legalised and recognised in the Russian Federation; the supplement to the diploma; documents on the academic degree and/or title; documents confirming service; a certificate on criminal record or prosecution legalised for the Russian Federation; a marriage certificate legalised for the Russian Federation; children's birth certificates (if there are children under 18) legalised for the Russian Federation; a notarised translation into Russian of documents in a foreign language; a medical book and the conclusions of a medical and psychiatric examination passed on referrals of the Department for the Attraction and Support of Teaching Staff; the work permit; 6 photographs 3×4; and bank details [44].

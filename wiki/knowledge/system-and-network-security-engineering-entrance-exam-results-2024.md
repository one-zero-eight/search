# Entrance examination for the master's programme «Инженерия безопасности систем и сетей» (5–6 August 2024) — published results

## What the document is

The page is the published protocol of the entrance examination for the master's programme «Инженерия безопасности систем и сетей» (Security Engineering of Systems and Networks) held by Innopolis University on its own, under the heading «Результаты вступительного испытания проводимого АНО ВО "Университет Иннополис" самостоятельно» [1].

The title of the document gives the period 05.08.2024–06.08.2024, and the table names 05.08.2024 as the date on which the examination was held [1]. For each participant the table gives the number, the СНИЛС/уникальный код, the score (оценка) and the same score written out in words (оценка прописью) [1].

Scores are published against an identifier rather than a name: most identifiers are 11-digit numbers, some are short codes such as [скрыто], [скрыто], [скрыто] and 78960, and one is written as [скрыто] [1].

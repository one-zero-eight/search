# Entrance examination «Основы информатики» for bachelor programmes (9 July 2024) — published results

## What the document is

The page is the published protocol of the entrance examination «Основы информатики» (basics of computer science) for bachelor programmes, held by Innopolis University on its own on 09.07.2024 [1].

Its heading names the examination as «Вступительное испытание на программам бакалавриата "Основы информатики" 09.07.2024», so the page concerns an entrance examination for bachelor programmes, and the results table carries the heading «Результаты вступительного испытания проводимого АНО ВО "Университет Иннополис" самостоятельно» — the results of an entrance examination conducted by the University itself [1].

## What the protocol lists

The results table has the columns №, СНИЛС/Уникальный код, оценка and оценка (прописью): a serial number, the participant's СНИЛС or unique code, the score as a figure and the same score written out in words [1].

Scores are published against a СНИЛС or an identifier rather than a name, here in the form [скрыто] [1].

## The results of 9 July 2024

The protocol contains nine rows, in the order of the table [1]:

- row 1: identifier [скрыто], score 65 (Шестьдесят пять) [1];
- row 2: identifier [скрыто], score 11 (Одиннадцать) [1];
- row 3: identifier [скрыто], score 27 (Двадцать семь) [1];
- row 4: identifier [скрыто], score 60 (Шестьдесят) [1];
- row 5: identifier [скрыто], score 11 (Одиннадцать) [1];
- row 6: identifier [скрыто], score 75 (Семьдесят пять) [1];
- row 7: identifier [скрыто], score 67 (Шестьдесят семь) [1];
- row 8: identifier [скрыто], score 64 (Шестьдесят четыре) [1];
- row 9: identifier [скрыто], score 60 (Шестьдесят) [1].

Two entries record the same lowest score of 11, the score 60 appears twice, and the highest score on the page is 75 [1].

## What the page does not say

The page states no pass mark and no minimum score, and it records no decision on admission [1].

The name of the examination and the date appear a second time below the results, split into two further blocks («Наименование вступительного испытания | Осн | овы / информатики» and «Дата проведения вступительного испытания … | 09.07 | .2024»), so the scores have to be taken from the rows of the results table itself [1].

# Entrance examinations for bachelor's and master's admission 2026/2027 — lists, points, procedure and appeals

## How entrance examinations work in admission

The rules provide the following [1]:

- on bachelor's programmes admission is conducted on the results of the ЕГЭ, assessed on a hundred-point scale and recognised as results of entrance examinations, and/or on the results of internal entrance examinations; without entrance examinations under parts 4 and 12 of article 71 of Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ (to places within the target quota, the special quota, the main budget places and paid places); and without entrance examinations, except additional entrance examinations of professional orientation, to places within the separate quota
- on master's programmes admission is conducted on the results of internal entrance examinations
- the University sets the list of entrance examinations and their priority for ranking the lists of applicants according to Appendix 1 to the rules
- the maximum number of points for each entrance examination on bachelor's programmes is 100 (one hundred)

## Lists of examinations by the education the applicant has

The University sets the examinations according to the education the applicant has [1]:

- for those applying on the basis of secondary general education the University sets entrance examinations in general education subjects in which the ЕГЭ is held: the examination in the Russian language under section 1 of the federal list of entrance examinations, and examination-2 and examination-3 under section 2 of that list (the profile general education examinations)
- examination-2 is set in one subject from the subsection «вступительное испытание-2» and examination-3 in one or several subjects from the subsection «вступительное испытание-3»; the subject of examination-2 is not used for examination-3
- if examination-3 is set in several subjects, they are subjects of the applicant's choice, and the applicant may use ЕГЭ results in one or several of them or choose one or several of them for an internal entrance examination where he has the right to take one
- for those applying on the basis of secondary professional education of the corresponding profile and on the basis of higher education the University determines the form and the list of examinations itself: the number of examinations on the basis of professional education equals the number of general education examinations; the Russian language examination is included, held as the general education examination in the Russian language; the University may establish that the form is the ЕГЭ and then does not hold an internal examination
- for those applying on the basis of higher education the University determines the content of the internal examinations itself and holds them
- the internal entrance examinations held by the University are: internal general education examinations (bachelor's programmes), examinations on the basis of professional education (bachelor's programmes) and examinations for master's programmes

## Who may take internal general education examinations

On bachelor's programmes the following applicants, including those applying on the basis of professional education, have the right to take internal entrance examinations in general education subjects [1]:

- to places within the separate quota — persons entitled to admission to those places on the results of the ЕГЭ or entrance examinations under part 5.2 of article 71 of Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ, regardless of whether they took the ЕГЭ and of its result
- to places within the special quota, the target quota, the main budget places and paid places — disabled persons (including disabled children), regardless of whether they took the ЕГЭ and of its result
- persons named in part 5.1 of article 71 of Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ, regardless of whether they apply for the separate quota, whether they took the ЕГЭ and of its result
- foreign citizens — in the subjects in which they have no ЕГЭ results obtained in the year of admission and/or within 4 (four) years before the year of admission, except results of the ЕГЭ in basic-level mathematics
- citizens of the Russian Federation who have a document of secondary general education obtained in a foreign organisation — in the subjects in which the applicant did not take the ЕГЭ in the current calendar year

## The examinations and the points in Appendix 1

Appendix 1 and Appendix 2 to the rules are also published in the wiki as a separate page, [2]; the descriptions below rest on that page and on the rules [2] [1]:

- section 1 of Appendix 1 (applicants on the basis of secondary general education) gives, for all profiles of areas 09.03.01 and 15.03.06, three examinations in order of priority: 1. mathematics, 2. informatics/physics, 3. Russian language, each with a minimum of 60 and a maximum of 100 points and the form «ЕГЭ/ВВИ» for the persons named in п. 29 of the rules
- sections 2 and 3 (applicants on the basis of secondary professional education and on the basis of higher education) give the same three lines with the internal examinations «Основы математики» and «Основы информатики / Основы физики» next to the ЕГЭ subjects, each with a minimum of 60 and a maximum of 100 points
- section 4 gives one master's entrance examination per programme, named after the profile (for example «Управление цифровым продуктом», «Программная инженерия», «Искусственный интеллект и инженерия данных», «Робототехника» for the profile «Робототехника и компьютерное зрение»), in the form of computer testing, with a minimum of 40 and a maximum of 100 points
- sections 5 and 6 are the lists for foreign citizens and stateless persons applying on paid places: section 5 (bachelor's programmes) gives, for the profiles of areas 09.03.01 and 15.03.06, mathematics or «Основы математики», informatics/physics or «Основы информатики / Основы физики» and the Russian language, each with a minimum of 60 and a maximum of 100 points; section 6 (master's programmes) gives one examination per programme, named after the profile, in the form of computer testing, with a minimum of 40 and a maximum of 100 points
- Appendix 2 on the same page repeats the minimum and maximum number of points confirming successful passing: 60 and 100 for the bachelor's programmes and 40 and 100 for the master's programmes
- internal entrance examinations on bachelor's and master's programmes are held in Russian or in English; for master's programmes with a foreign language or languages of instruction the University establishes that the examination is held in English; where the examination is held in several languages, the applicant chooses one
- Appendix 5 lists the correspondence between secondary professional education specialities and bachelor's areas of study (for area 09.03.01 — the specialities of group 09.00.00, of group 10.00.00 and a number of older codes such as 230115 «Программирование в компьютерных системах»; for area 15.03.06 — group 11.00.00, 11.02.07, 11.02.17, 15.02.10 and older codes), and allows a deviation in the codes and names where the content of education is similar

## How the internal examination is held

The rules set the conduct of an internal entrance examination as follows [1]:

- internal entrance examinations are held in person or with the use of distance technologies, with identification of the applicants, through the University's electronic information system; identity is confirmed by entering the personal identifier and password obtained at registration; the examinations are held in the form of computer testing
- during an examination its participants and the persons involved in holding it may not carry or use means of communication; participants may not carry or use reference materials and electronic computing equipment other than that needed for the examination
- for a distance examination the applicant needs a computer, notebook or tablet and a connection: the browser Google Chrome of version not lower than 72, an operating system not lower than Windows 7, macOS 10.12 «Sierra» or Linux, and an internet connection speed of not less than 512 Kbps
- the room must have walls and a closed door, no outsiders may be present, additional computers and monitors must be switched off, the desk must be free of all objects (including pocket computers, watches, notebooks, books, notes and printed papers), the web camera must not face a light source and must show the room within 2–3 metres of the applicant; only identity documents, blank sheets of paper and a pen are allowed on the desk
- authorised officials may ask the applicant to show the room and workplace; on refusal or non-compliance they may remove him from the examination with an act of removal
- the applicant must enter his personal account in the University's electronic information system not later than 5 (five) minutes before the start of the examination and follow the link to the computer testing page; auxiliary means may be allowed where the examination programmes provide for them
- on violation of the rules the members of the admission or examination commission draw up an act, an electronic copy of which is sent to the applicant; the applicant is considered not to have passed the examination without a valid reason; the applicant may not record the materials of the examination or pass them to third persons

## Attempts, languages, schedule and results

The rules provide the following on attempts, languages, the schedule and the results [1]:

- the results of internal entrance examinations are valid for admission to the academic year for which the admission is conducted
- an applicant takes each internal entrance examination once; where subjects of choice are set, he takes the internal examination once for each chosen subject
- one internal examination is held at the same time for all applicants or at different times for different groups; one examination is held per day for each applicant, and at the applicant's wish he may be given the opportunity to take several in one day
- a person who did not pass an internal examination for a valid reason (illness or other documented circumstances) is admitted to take it in another group or on a reserve day; the University sets the schedule, including one or several reserve days
- the results of an internal examination are announced on the official site within 3 (three) working days after the day of the examination, but not later than 1 (one) day before the publication of the competitive lists; the applicant may on the day of announcement or on the next working day see the results of the checking and assessment of his work
- if several results are available, the highest of the results of the ЕГЭ and/or internal entrance examinations (including centralised testing results) that is not below the minimum number of points is counted as the result of the entrance examination
- citizens of the Republic of Belarus and citizens of the Russian Federation may use the results of the centralised testing held in the Republic of Belarus in the current or the preceding calendar year if the applicant did not take the ЕГЭ in the corresponding general education subject in the year in which the centralised testing was taken; those results are recognised as results of the internal general education entrance examinations, the table of correspondence lists the Russian language, mathematics and physics, results in subjects not named in the decree of the Ministry of Education of the Republic of Belarus No. 166 of 17 September 2025 are not counted, the score obtained is taken as the score of the University's examination with the minimum points of the internal examinations, and the results are submitted not later than the day of completion of the acceptance of documents

## Appeals against an entrance examination

On the results of an internal entrance examination the applicant (or his authorised representative) may file an appeal with the appeal commission about a violation of the established procedure of the examination and/or disagreement with the assessment [1]:

- the appeal is filed with the use of distance technologies through the University's electronic information system
- the appeal is filed on the day the results are announced or within the next working day; an appeal about a violation of the procedure may also be filed on the day of the examination; it is considered not later than the next working day after filing
- the applicant (or his authorised representative) may attend the announcement of the results of the appeal remotely; a parent or legal representative may attend with a minor applicant who is under 18, except minors recognised as fully capable before the age of majority
- after the review the commission decides to change the assessment or to leave it unchanged; the decision, recorded in a protocol, is brought to the applicant by sending him the protocol in electronic form
- during the review the commission checks compliance with the established procedure and/or the correctness of the assessment; a letter of appeal may be filed by an authorised representative as well as by the applicant

## Special conditions for applicants with disabilities

For applicants who are disabled persons or persons with health limitations the University creates conditions taking into account their individual characteristics and gives them information about the procedure of the examinations in an accessible form [1]:

- the duration of an internal examination for such applicants is increased by 1 (one) hour
- in-person examinations are held in a separate auditorium with unhindered access (ramps, lifts, widened doorways, handrails; where there is no lift the auditorium must be on the ground floor); the number of such applicants in one auditorium must not exceed 12 (twelve) for a written examination and 6 (six) for an oral one; an assistant may be present, and the applicants may use technical means they need
- additional requirements are set for the blind (Braille or a computer with special software, tasks read out by an assistant), the visually impaired (illumination of not less than 300 lux, magnifying devices, enlarged print), the deaf and hard of hearing (sound-amplifying equipment, a sign language interpreter), the deaf-blind (a tactile sign language interpreter), persons with severe speech disorders, the deaf and hard of hearing (oral examinations in written form; on master's programmes by decision of the University) and persons with disorders of the musculoskeletal system (written tasks on a computer or dictated to an assistant; written examinations in oral form)
- the applicant states the need for special conditions in the application, and the disability or health limitation document is submitted not later than the day of completion of the acceptance of documents

## Features for applicants from the new regions and the border territories

For the persons named below separate rules apply [1]:

- persons who completed secondary general education and passed the state final certification in the Donetsk and Lugansk People's Republics and the Zaporozhye and Kherson regions before their admission to the Russian Federation, persons who passed the final certification with the features of part 1 of article 5 of Federal Law No. 19-ФЗ of 17.02.2023, and persons who studied in organisations in the Belgorod, Bryansk and Kursk regions and in Sevastopol listed by order No. 146
- the University holds the general education entrance examinations itself for those applying on the basis of secondary general education; the examinations in the Russian language (general education and on the basis of professional education) are held in the form of an interview if the applicant wishes; other examinations are held in the forms set by the rules; the results are assessed on a hundred-point scale regardless of the form; the minimum points are those of Appendix 2
- such applicants may take the University's examinations and/or use ЕГЭ results; for the border territories the University holds the examinations in the form of an interview (in person or with distance technologies) or in the form set by the rules, at the applicant's choice
- a document of education with honours obtained in the Donetsk or Lugansk People's Republic or in Ukraine earns the same points as a document with honours obtained in Russian organisations under Appendix 3; for winners and prizewinners of olympiads the number of points confirming the special right is not less than 75
- winners and prizewinners of the IV stage of the all-Ukrainian pupil olympiads, of the republican olympiad of pupils and members of Ukrainian national teams get the right to admission without entrance examinations as winners and prizewinners of the final stage of the All-Russian olympiad and members of Russian national teams, and a 100-point advantage where the subject corresponds to the profile of the olympiad

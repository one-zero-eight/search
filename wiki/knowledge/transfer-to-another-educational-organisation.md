# Transfer from Innopolis University to another educational organisation — procedure and documents (Regulation of 12 May 2026)

## The document

The Regulation on the procedure and grounds for the transfer, expulsion and restoration of students at ANO VO «Innopolis University» was approved by order of 12 May 2026 № ОД/АД-01/2026/05/12 and signed by the Director D.V. Vandyukov [1]. It establishes the general requirements for the procedure and grounds of transfer, expulsion and restoration [2], its requirements are obligatory for the structural subdivisions and employees dealing with these matters in bachelor's, master's and aspirantura programmes [3], and it is in force until repealed or until normative acts, the Charter or other local acts provide a different procedure [4]. The wiki copy is a scan read by OCR, so its numbers and dates should be checked against the PDF [1].

## General requirements for transfer

- Transfer requires the education needed to master the corresponding programme, including education obtained abroad [5].
- Transfer requires vacant places for transfer; their number is determined and published on the official website in the Internet with detail by educational programme, form of study and course, stating how many places are financed from budget allocations and how many are under contracts at the expense of natural and (or) legal persons [6].
- Students on programmes of training of scientific and scientific-pedagogical personnel in aspirantura (adjuncture) admitted under federal state educational standards or educational standards may transfer to programmes under federal state requirements or under the requirements set by higher education organisations themselves [7].
- Transfer to places financed from budget allocations is made where there are no restrictions for mastering the programme at budget expense and the programme is not a second or subsequent higher education [8], and where the total duration of the student's study will not exceed the term set by the standard or requirements for the programme to which the student transfers by more than one academic year [9].
- Transfer is allowed not earlier than after passing the first interim assessment [10].
- A student who is a foreign citizen or a stateless person must, not later than 30 (thirty) days from the day the expulsion act is issued, present to the territorial body of the federal executive authority in the sphere of internal affairs that issued the residence permit for the purpose of study an extract from the act on admission by way of transfer to the receiving organisation for full-time study; the receiving organisation issues that extract within 5 (five) working days from the day the act on admission by way of transfer is issued [11].

The requirements set by clauses 2.2–2.3 (including their subclauses) do not extend to the cases listed in clause 2.1.6 [12]:

- transfer out of a programme where the organisation's activity ceases, its licence is suspended or annulled, state accreditation is lost or the certificate expires [13];
- transfer within the network form of a programme [14];
- transfer from course to course, including conditionally [15];
- transfer to an individual curriculum, including accelerated study [16].

## From which programmes transfer out is possible

Transfer out of the University is made from a bachelor's programme to a bachelor's programme, from a master's programme to a master's programme, from a bachelor's programme to a specialist's programme, from an aspirantura programme to an aspirantura programme, from an old-type programme of training of scientific-pedagogical personnel in aspirantura to such a programme or to an aspirantura programme, from an aspirantura programme to an adjuncture programme, and from an old-type aspirantura programme to an old-type adjuncture programme [17]. Transfer from a bachelor's programme to a programme of training of middle-level specialists or of qualified workers and employees is made under the order of the Ministry of Education of Russia No. 533 of 06.08.2021 and the local acts of the receiving organisation [18].

## Certificate of the period of study

- The student applies to the structural unit responsible for work with students (the responsible structural unit) with an application addressed to the director for a certificate of the period of study (справка о периоде обучения) because of the intention to transfer to another organisation; the application is submitted on paper (annex 1 to the Regulation) or by electronic appeal through the University's information portal in the Internet or in the electronic information and educational environment [19].
- The form of the certificate is approved by order of the director, and the procedure for issuing, drawing up and keeping certificates is set by the University's local acts [19].
- Within 5 (five) working days from receipt of the application the responsible structural unit issues the student the certificate, which states the level of education on the basis of which the student was admitted, the list and volume of studied subjects, courses and disciplines (modules), completed internships and performed research, and the marks given by the University at the interim and final attestations [20].

## Steps of the transfer out

- To have the transfer considered, the student submits to the receiving organisation an application for transfer with the certificate of the period of study and, at the student's discretion, other documents confirming individual achievements [21].
- On a positive decision of the receiving organisation the student submits to the responsible structural unit a written application addressed to the director for expulsion by way of transfer to the receiving organisation (annex 2), attaching the certificate of transfer issued by the receiving organisation [22].
- The University issues the order on expulsion in connection with the transfer within 5 (five) working days from receipt of the application for expulsion [23].
- Within 3 (three) working days from the day the expulsion act is issued the person expelled in connection with the transfer is given a copy of the act certified by the University and (or) an extract from it, the original document of education (or of education and qualification) on the basis of which the person was admitted (if it was kept), and a certificate of study in the form set by the University [24].
- The documents are handed to the student or to a person acting under a power of attorney, or sent on the student's application to the indicated person or to the receiving organisation by post with notice of delivery and an inventory; they may be signed with an electronic signature and sent as an electronic document by e-mail to the student's address and to the receiving organisation's address [25].
- The personal file of the person expelled in connection with the transfer keeps a copy of the document of previous education certified by the University and a copy of the expulsion act or an extract from it [26].
- The person expelled in connection with the transfer hands in the student card and record book (where a paper record book was issued) or other documents confirming study at the University to the responsible structural unit [27].
- Where the student used a grant to pay for study, transfer to another organisation may entail the obligation to return the cost of the used part of the educational grant under the grant agreement [28].
- On transfer of a foreign citizen or stateless person the University notifies other organisations in the list, procedure and terms set by the corresponding local act [29].

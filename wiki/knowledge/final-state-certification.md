# Final state certification (ГИА)

## What is the Final State Certification

The Final State Certification (ГИА) is the final stage of completing state-accredited Bachelor's and Master's degree programs at Innopolis University [1]. It is conducted by State Qualifying Committees to determine whether students' learning outcomes comply with Federal State Educational Standards [2]. The schedule, scope, structure, and content are set independently by the University for each program [3], [4].

The regulation was approved by order of the Director of 26 January 2026 [5]. It enters into force on the date of its approval and is amended or supplemented by order of the Director [6], [7].

## Who is admitted

Students are admitted to the FSC if they have no academic debts and have fully completed the curriculum or individual study plan for their program [8]. Students studying via self-education or in programs without state accreditation may take the FSC externally [9].

## Form of the FSC

At Innopolis University, the FSC is conducted as a defense of a Final Qualification Work (FQW) [10]. For bachelor's students this is a Bachelor's Thesis; for master's students it is a Master's Thesis [11]. The type of FQW, the requirements for it, the procedure for its completion and the criteria for its assessment are set by the University in accordance with the educational standards [12]. The FSC may be conducted in Russian or English according to the University's local regulations [13]. The University may use e-learning and distance learning technologies during the FSC [14].

## FSC Program

The FSC Program, including requirements for FQWs and defense assessment criteria, is communicated to students no later than six months before the FSC begins [11]. It is approved by the Academic Council and contains: intended learning outcomes; procedure and form of the FSC; structure and content; general requirements for the FQW; requirements for the FQW topic and approval procedure; a list of sample topics; supervision and consultation procedures; review procedures for master's programs; defense regulations; the list of competencies; assessment criteria for FQWs and for the quality of mastering the educational program [15].

## Schedule

The FSC schedule, including dates, times, and venues for tests and consultations, is approved by the University Director (or authorized representative) no later than 30 calendar days before the first FSC test [16]. It is distributed to students, committee members, secretaries, supervisors, and consultants via corporate email [16].

## Grading

FSC results are graded as A (Excellent), B (Good), C (Satisfactory), or D (Unsatisfactory) in the English text [17]; the Russian text of the same clause names them «отлично», «хорошо», «удовлетворительно», «неудовлетворительно» [17]. Grades A, B, and C (respectively «отлично», «хорошо», «удовлетворительно») signify successful completion [17]. Results are announced on the day of the defense [18]. Successful completion is grounds for issuing a state-recognized higher education diploma [19].

## Honors diploma

A Bachelor's or Master's Diploma with Honors is issued when all conditions are met [20]:

- All grades for subjects, term papers, and internships (except "Pass") in the Diploma Supplement are "Excellent" or "Good"; for multi-semester subjects, the final semester grade is used [20].
- All FSC results are "Excellent" [20].
- At least 75% of all grades in the Diploma Supplement (excluding "Pass") are "Excellent," including FSC grades [20].

## Absence and retakes

Students who miss the FSC for a valid reason (temporary disability, public duties, court summons, transport issues, weather, or other reasons recognized by the University) may retake it within six months after the FSC period ends, provided they submit a document confirming the reason [21].

Students who miss the FSC without a valid reason or receive an "Unsatisfactory" grade are expelled from the University with a certificate of study [22]. Students with disabilities who miss the FSC within their established period face the same consequence [22].

A person who did not pass the FSC may retake it no earlier than 10 months and no later than 5 years after the original FSC session, up to two times [23]. For retaking, the person is reinstated to the University for a period set by the University (no less than the FSC period in the academic calendar) [24]. At the student's request, a new FQW topic may be assigned under the procedure of clause 6.7 [24].

## Appeals

A student may appeal FSC results on grounds of procedural violations [25]. The appeal procedure is communicated to students no later than six months before the FSC [26]. A written appeal must be submitted in person to the Appeal Committee no later than the next working day after the FSC results are announced [27], [28]. For the appeal, the Secretary of the State Examination Committee sends the committee's minutes, the Chair's conclusion on procedural compliance, the FQW, the review(s) if any, and the abstract to the Appeal Committee [29].

The Appeal Committee reviews the appeal within two working days, inviting the State Examination Committee Chair and the student [30]. The meeting may be held in the student's absence if the student does not attend; the decision is communicated to the student within three working days and the student's acquaintance with it is confirmed by signature [30]. If violations are confirmed and affected the result, the result is annulled and the student is given a new FSC date within the University's deadlines [31]. The Appeal Committee's decision is final [32]. A re-test after a successful appeal is conducted in the presence of an Appeal Committee member no later than the end of the student's studies [33]. No further appeals are accepted for a re-test [34].

## Committees

State Examination Committees and Appeal Committees are established for each program or group of programs and operate for one calendar year [35]. The State Examination Committee Chair is approved by the Ministry of Science and Higher Education from among non-University persons with a Doctor of Sciences degree or Professor title, or leading employer representatives, no later than December 31 of the preceding year [36]. The Appeal Committee Chair is the University Director or authorized representative [36]. Committee compositions are approved no later than one month before the FSC [37]. The chairs organize and control the committees' activity and ensure uniform requirements towards students [38].

The State Examination Committee includes the Chair and at least four members; at least 50% must be employer representatives [39]. The Appeal Committee includes the Chair and at least three members from University teaching staff who are not on State Examination Committees [39]. A Secretary is appointed but is not a committee member [40]. Meetings require at least two-thirds of members; decisions are by simple majority, with the Chair having a casting vote [41]. Minutes are kept in Russian and English (or Russian only, per the program's language) and stored in the University archive [42].

## Final Qualification Work requirements

The FQW demonstrates a graduate's preparedness for independent professional activity and may be completed jointly by several students, with each student's individual contribution identified [43], [44]. A list of FQW topics is published in the University's electronic system no later than six months before the FSC [45]. Students may propose their own topic with justification [46]. A supervisor is assigned by University order [47]. Topic changes require a written application approved by the supervisor, Dean, and FSC-organizing unit, no later than one month before the FSC [48]. Supervisor replacement also requires an order [48].

The FQW must be on A4 paper, Times New Roman font, 14 pt size, with one and a half line spacing, margins of 25 mm on the left and 20 mm on the other sides, formatted by width, with each chapter starting on a new page, continuous page numbering (title page unnumbered), and in-text citations linked to a reference list [49]. It must include a title page, table of contents, introduction, literature review, main body, conclusion, references, and appendices; the recommended length is at least 40 pages (appendices excluded) [50]. The FQW is prepared in English or Russian in accordance with the University's local act and the FSC Program of the program [50]. An abstract of 4–5 pages in Russian is also required; it has a title page (not counted in its volume), introduction with aims and objectives, literature review, a brief description of the main content, conclusion and references [51].

The supervisor provides a written review of the student's work [52]. Master's FQWs must also be reviewed by one or more external reviewers (not from the student's department or University) [53]. Students submit the FQW and abstract electronically via corporate email no later than 10 calendar days before the defense date [54]. The supervisor familiarizes the student with the review(s) no later than 5 days before the defense; signed reviews are submitted to the organizing unit by the same deadline [55]. The FQW, reviews, and abstract are delivered to the State Examination Committee no later than 2 calendar days before the defense [56]. FQW texts (except those with state secrets) are published in the University's electronic library system and checked for plagiarism [57]. On the rights holder's decision, production, technical, economic, organizational and other information of commercial value may be excluded from the published text [57].

## Forms in the appendices

The regulation's appendices contain the templates used in the procedure: committee minutes (Appendices 1 and 2) [42]; the application for a topic proposed by the student (Appendix 3) [46]; the application to change the FQW topic (Appendix 4) and to replace the supervisor (Appendix 5) [48]; FQW title pages (Appendices 6–9) [50] and abstract title pages (Appendices 10 and 11) [51]; the supervisor's review forms (Appendices 12–15) [52]; the reviewer's review forms (Appendices 16 and 17) [53]; the application to exclude part of the FQW when it is placed in the electronic library system (Appendix 18) [57]; the appeal application (Appendix 19) [27] and the Appeal Committee minutes (Appendix 20) [30].

## Prohibitions and fees

Students are prohibited from having or using communication devices during the FSC [58]. No fees are charged for the FSC [59].

## Accommodations for students with disabilities

The FSC is conducted taking into account students' individual characteristics [60]. General requirements include: conducting the FSC in the same room as other students if it does not create difficulties; providing an assistant for technical help; allowing assistive devices; ensuring accessible premises [61]. Local regulations must be communicated in accessible formats [62]. Presentation time may be extended by up to 15 minutes upon written request [63].

Specific accommodations include [64]:

- Blind students: materials in Braille or electronic format with specialized software, or read by an assistant; written assignments in Braille, on a computer, or dictated; provided with Braille writing supplies [64].
- Visually impaired students: enlarged print materials; lighting of at least 300 lux; magnification devices [64].
- Deaf and hard-of-hearing students: sound-amplifying equipment; tests may be conducted in written form upon request [64].
- Students with motor impairments: written assignments on a computer or dictated; tests may be conducted orally upon request [64].

Students with disabilities must submit a written request for special arrangements no later than three months before the FSC, indicating their needs and attaching confirming documents [65]. In the request the student states whether an assistant is needed and whether the duration of each test must be extended [65].

## The text approved on 2 August 2022

An earlier version of the same Regulation was approved by order of the Director of 2 August 2022 № ОД/УД-03/2022/08/02 and signed by Director K.V. Semenikhin; the page sets out the procedure in Russian and then the same text in English [66]. Its articles and clause numbers are the same as in the current text, but several requirements differ:

- the abstract to the FQW is 10–15 pages in the earlier text [67], and 4–5 pages in the current one [51];
- the FQW is drawn up in English in the earlier text [68], while the current text allows English or Russian in accordance with the local act and the FSC Program [50];
- the earlier text recommends at least 40 pages and does not say that appendices are excluded [68], whereas the current text excludes appendices from the volume [50];
- in the earlier text the list of FQW topics is placed in the electronic information-educational environment by staff of the Academic Policy Department [69], a change of topic is agreed with the supervisor and with the vice-rector — head of that department no later than one month before the deadline for submitting the work [70], and a new topic on retaking is assigned under clause 6.6 [71];
- in the earlier text the work and its abstract are submitted on paper and in electronic form to the Methodological Department of the Centre for the Organisation of Educational Programmes 10 calendar days before the appointed defense date [72].

# Accessibility of the campus and of services for people with disabilities (accessibility passport and roadmap)

## The passport and the object it describes

Innopolis University has an approved "passport of accessibility for people with disabilities of the object and of the services provided on it in the field of education, as well as of the necessary assistance provided to them"; it is approved by the director of the University [1].

The object where services are provided is at 420500, Republic of Tatarstan, Verkhneuslonsky district, Innopolis, Universitetskaya st., 1, and Sportivnaya st., 107; the service provided is education [2]. The object is a part of a building of 16581 sq m, including the area of covered sports facilities; there is no adjacent land plot [2]. The provider is ANO VO «Innopolis University», located at Universitetskaya st., 1 [2]. The object is used on the basis of lease (аренда), the form of ownership is private [2]. The founder is AO «Innopolis City», Sportivnaya st., 1 [2].

## Operating parameters of the object

- Sphere of activity: educational and other activity [2]
- Planned capacity (attendance, number served per day): about 800 people [2]
- Form of service: on the object [2]
- Age categories of the population served: all age categories [2]
- Categories of disabled people served (musculoskeletal disorders, vision disorders, hearing disorders): none [2]
- Number of disabled people in the educational organization: none [2]

## Assessed accessibility of the object

The passport records each indicator as "да" (available), "частично" (partial) or "нет" (absent) [1]:

- unobstructed entry into the object and exit from it: да [1]
- independent movement on the territory of the object to the place where the service is provided, including with the help of the object's employees, assistive and auxiliary technologies and a replacement wheelchair: частично [3]
- conditions for individual mobility of disabled people: designated parking places — да; replacement wheelchairs — нет; adapted lifts — частично; handrails — да; ramps — частично; lifting platforms (aprelli) — нет; sliding doors — нет; accessible entrance groups — да; accessible sanitary rooms — да; sufficient width of doorways in walls, stair flights and landings — да [4]
- possibility of boarding and alighting from a vehicle in front of the entrance to the object, including with a wheelchair and, where necessary, with the help of the object's employees: да [5]
- accompanying disabled people with persistent visual function disorders and their independent movement on the territory of the object: частично [6]
- assistance to a disabled person at entry into and exit from the object, informing about accessible public transport routes: да [7]
- proper placement of equipment and information carriers, including duplication of the sound and visual information needed to obtain the service: нет; inscriptions, signs and other text and graphic information in relief-dot (Braille) script and on a contrasting background: нет [7]
- access to the object with a guide dog having a document confirming its special training, issued in the form and manner approved by an order of the Ministry of Labour and Social Protection of the Russian Federation of 22 June 2015, including a specially designated place for the guide dog: частично [7]
- provision of necessary services remotely or at the disabled person's place of residence when full accessibility cannot be ensured: нет [7]

## Assessed accessibility of the services

- a sign at the entrance with the organization's name, its working hours and the building plan, made in relief-dot (Braille) script and on a contrasting background: нет [7]
- assistance to disabled people in obtaining, in a form accessible to them, information about the rules of providing the service, including on completing the documents needed for it: частично [7]
- provision to disabled people with hearing impairments of a service using Russian sign language, including admission of a sign-language interpreter or a typhlo-interpreter to the object: нет [7]
- induction loops and sound-amplifying equipment in one of the premises intended for mass events: частично [7]
- adaptation of the official website for people with visual impairments (low vision): да [7]
- provision of tutor services on the basis of a corresponding recommendation in a conclusion of the PMPK or IPRA: нет [7]
- free provision of textbooks, study guides and other educational literature, as well as special technical means of collective and individual learning: частично [7]
- other necessary assistance by employees of the educational organization in overcoming barriers: частично [7]
- instruction of employees providing services to the population on working with disabled people and on ensuring accessibility of the object and services: частично [7]
- employees on whom an administrative act places the provision of assistance to disabled people: да [7]
- teaching staff with education and (or) qualification allowing them to teach under adapted educational programs: частично [1]
- other conditions of accessibility of services in the field of education for disabled people: частично [1]

## Planned measures and costs

The passport lists measures for bringing the object (section V) and the order of providing services (section VI) into compliance with the requirements of Russian legislation, with deadlines and volumes of expenses in thousands of rubles [1]. The list is filled in on the basis of the assessment of the state and shortcomings in sections III and IV of the passport and must include, among other things, proposals for creating accessibility conditions where the object cannot be fully adapted before its reconstruction or capital repair [8].

For the object [1]:

- a sign with the organization's name, working hours and building plan in relief-dot script and on a contrasting background: until 2020, 100 thousand rubles [1]
- purchase and placement of equipment and information carriers (tactile-visual accessibility signs, sound beacons and informators; Braille and contrasting inscriptions): until 2030, as funding is allocated, 300 thousand rubles [1]
- conditions for individual mobility (adapted lifts, ramps, sliding doors): until 2030, 500 thousand rubles [1]
- access with a guide dog, including a designated place for it: until 2030 [1]

For the order of providing services [1]:

- assistance in obtaining information in an accessible form, including a contract with an organization or fund providing support services (tutor, surdopedagogue, sign-language interpreter, typhlopedagogue, educational psychologist, social pedagogue, specialist in special technical and software means) by 01 June 2018, and a tactile-sound informator: until 2020 [1]
- additional training of teachers and other employees working with disabled people: annually [1]
- an instruction for instructing employees on working with disabled people: 2018 [1]
- documents on teaching disabled people and persons with health limitations for the University's website (regulation on organizing the learning process, regulation on developing and implementing adapted programs, procedure for mastering physical education disciplines): by 01 September 2018 [1]
- model adapted educational programs for persons with health limitations and disabled people (hearing, musculoskeletal, vision disorders) for all areas of study: 2019 [1]
- post-diploma support programs for graduates with health limitations and disabled graduates: 2020 [1]
- purchase of textbooks, study guides and other literature: until 2030, as funding is allocated, 200 thousand rubles [1]
- remote provision of services and provision of services at the disabled person's place of residence: until 2020, 100 thousand rubles [1]
- equipping the learning process with special technical means (Braille display, relief-dot printer with paper, video magnifier, portable information induction system, FM system, special software including the MonVisual Desktop Access program, digital audio recording of learning materials in DAISY format): until 2025, 1350 thousand rubles in total [1]
- purchase of a wheelchair: until 2025, 50 thousand rubles [8]

## Roadmap for raising accessibility

The University also has a "roadmap" — a plan of measures to raise the values of accessibility indicators for disabled people of the object and of the services provided on it in the field of education [9]. It is approved by the director of the University and is directed at ensuring conditions of accessibility for disabled people of the University's objects [9]. Its purpose is a staged, financially feasible rise in the level of accessibility of the object and services [9]. In accordance with the order of the Ministry of Education and Science of Russia of 09.11.2015 No. 1309, the roadmap determines the goals of ensuring accessibility, the values of the indicators (for the period 2018–2030) and the list of measures to reach the planned values, which is given in the University's accessibility passport [9].

Goals of the roadmap include creating conditions of accessibility and providing the necessary assistance, including by alternative methods where the object cannot be fully adapted before reconstruction or capital repair; establishing indicators for assessing the degree of accessibility; equipping the object with devices and information sources in an accessible form; excluding from 1 July 2016 acceptance of objects put into operation or reconstructed after that date that are not fully adapted to the needs of disabled people; and carrying out certification (паспортизация) of the object and services [10].

The roadmap names these main problems with ensuring unhindered access of disabled people to the object and services [9]:

- objects with a low degree of accessibility, and objects fully inaccessible to disabled people, where services are provided to them [9]
- objects that cannot, before their reconstruction or capital repair, be adapted to the needs of disabled people [9]
- the absence or incomplete equipping of part of the objects with devices, means and sources of information in an accessible form [9]
- employees providing services to disabled people who have not been instructed or trained on ensuring accessibility and assisting disabled people [9]
- the absence of an accessibility passport of the object with decisions on the volume and timing of measures for staged access [9]
- the absence, in the University's local normative documents and in the employees' job instructions, of provisions defining their duties and the procedure for helping disabled people overcome barriers [9]

The requirements of Russian legislation on accessibility are taken into account in design decisions for new construction and reconstruction of the object, or, where the existing object cannot be fully adapted, the access of disabled people to the place where the service is provided is ensured [9]. The roadmap envisages measures for raising the indicator values in stages, taking into account the impairments of the body functions of disabled people, such as [9]:

- inserting into the normative legal acts of ANO VO «Innopolis University» provisions ensuring compliance with the accessibility conditions for objects and services established by Russian legislation [9]
- organising work on providing services to disabled people, including by alternative methods [9]
- expanding the list of services accessible to persons with limited health possibilities through the Internet [9]

The planned rise of the indicator values and the deadlines for reaching them are determined on the basis of Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29.12.2012, Federal Law No. 419-ФЗ of 01.12.2014, order of the Ministry of Education and Science of Russia No. 1309 of 09.11.2015, order No. 1399 of 02.12.2015, the federal state educational standards of higher education, orders of the Ministry of Education and Science of Russia No. 301 of 05.04.2017, No. 1259 of 19.11.2013, No. 499 of 01.07.2013, No. 1008 of 29.08.2013 and No. 1015 of 30.08.2013, and the methodological recommendations approved by order of the Ministry of Education and Science of Russia of 08.04.2014 No. АК-44/05вн [9].

The expected result is an increase in the share of accessibility of the object and of the educational services provided on it for disabled people in their total number by 2030 [9].

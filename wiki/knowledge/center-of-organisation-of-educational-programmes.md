# Center of Organisation of Educational Programmes (Центр организации образовательных программ) — place, tasks, functions and rights

## The order and what it approved

The page carries the order of 31 October 2024 № ОД/АД-04/2024/10/31, issued in Innopolis and signed by the Director for Development and HR Policy R.F. Valiev, together with the Regulation on the centre of organisation of educational programmes (Положение о центре организации образовательных программ) it approves [1]. The wiki copy is a scan read by OCR, so its numbers and dates should be checked against the PDF [1].

- The order is issued in order to raise the effectiveness of the activity of the structural subdivision and approves the Regulation attached to it [1].
- The head of the centre of organisation of educational programmes D. Ushakov is to ensure timely acquaintance, against signature, with the approved Regulation of the subdivision's workers and also of workers newly hired and transferred (moved) into the subdivision from other subdivisions of the University [1].
- The order repeals the Regulation on the centre of organisation of educational programmes approved by order of the University of 2 March 2021 № ОД/АД-08/2021/03/02 [1].
- Control over the execution of the order and of the Regulation it approves is retained by the signatory [1].

## Place in the structure

The Regulation regulates the activity of the centre of organisation of educational programmes of ANO VO «Innopolis University» and determines its tasks, functions, rights and the duties of the Centre's workers [2].

- The Centre is a structural subdivision of the department (управление) of academic policy and higher education and belongs to the category of methodical and educational-methodical subdivisions (методические и учебно-методические подразделения) [3].
- It reports directly to the vice-rector — head of the department of academic policy and higher education [4].
- In its activity the Centre follows the Constitution of the Russian Federation, the Labour Code of the Russian Federation, Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29 December 2012 «Об образовании в Российской Федерации» and other legislative and normative legal acts on higher education institutions, the University's Charter, decisions of the Academic, Supervisory and Operational councils, orders and instructions of the University's director, other local normative acts and this Regulation [5].
- The structure, the number of staff and the staffing table of the Centre, and changes to them, are approved by the director of the University or another person authorised by him [6]; the Centre is headed by its head, hired and dismissed by order of the director [7].
- The working conditions of the Centre's employees are determined by their employment contracts and by the University's internal labour regulations [8]; their rights, duties and degree of responsibility are established by job descriptions [9].
- Within their competences the Centre's employees may be involved in work under grants, projects, subsidies and other programmes implemented by the University [10]; the Regulation and additions and changes to it are approved by order of the director or another person authorised by him [11].

## Tasks

- Organising and coordinating the work on the development, actualisation and implementation of the main professional educational programmes of higher education [12].
- Methodical support of the educational process in the main professional educational programmes of higher education [13].
- Planning, organising, coordinating and controlling the educational process in those programmes [14].

## Functions

The Regulation opens section 3 by stating that functions are distributed between the departments of the Centre and fixed by separate regulations on those departments [1]. The Centre itself:

- draws up the calendar study schedule (календарный учебный график) and the class schedule for the higher education programmes [15];
- develops, together with the employees of the faculties, institutes and laboratories and the teaching staff, the set of competences formed by the programmes [16] and forms and updates their content, including the content of the disciplines (modules) taught [17];
- organises the process of developing and actualising the components of the programmes together with the teaching staff and the employees of the faculties, institutes and laboratories [18];
- gives methodical support to the development and actualisation of those components: the curriculum, the competency matrix, the working programmes of disciplines (modules), the internship programmes, the state final attestation programme, the assessment means and methodological materials and the annotations to the working programmes [19];
- develops students' individual curricula (индивидуальные учебные планы) in the higher education programmes [20];
- forms the set of documents on the programmes [21] and prepares drafts of local normative acts regulating educational activity in them [22];
- organises, supports and controls the educational process in the programmes [23];
- forms the schedule of classes held in the form of contact work [24] and the schedule of interim assessments and repeat interim assessments [25];
- forms and controls the completion of the record sheets (ведомости) of interim and repeat interim assessments in disciplines (modules) and internships [26];
- organises the state final attestation of students [27], namely:
  - forming the list of topics of the final qualification works and the schedule of state attestation tests, the composition of the state examination and appeal commissions and their secretaries [27];
  - supporting the work of the state examination commission and organising the storage of the final qualification works and the documents relating to them (annotations, reviews, supervisors' opinions, acknowledgement sheets, transfer acts, certificates of the check of the works for the volume of borrowing) [27];
  - controlling the completion of the protocols of the state examination commission and the record sheets of the state final attestation, and supporting the preparation of reports on its results [27];
- organises and controls the holding of candidate examinations [28];
- takes part in calculating the teaching load of teaching staff and in forming their individual work plans together with the faculties, institutes and laboratories subordinate to the vice-rector — head of the department of academic policy and higher education [29];
- agrees students' participation in an academic mobility (exchange) programme and their individual curricula for the period of participation [30], and controls the fulfilment of the curriculum or individual curriculum [31];
- forms study groups in the programmes [32] and forms and issues documents on education and on qualification and their annexes [33];
- controls the use of the University's classroom fund according to the schedules of classes, interim and repeat interim assessments [34];
- communicates on questions of educational activity between the teaching staff, students and other participants of the educational process [35], and provides information and prepares documents on educational activity at the request of the University's subdivisions and of bodies of state authority and administration [36];
- supports the internal and external assessment of the quality of educational activity and of the preparation of students in higher education programmes [37], gives expert and consultative support to the University's structural subdivisions [38] and takes part in round tables, conferences, seminars, webinars and other events on matters within its tasks and functions [39];
- interacts with external organisations, employers and the University's structural subdivisions on the organisation of the educational process [40].

## Rights

In carrying out their activity the Centre's employees have the right to:

- request from the University's structural subdivisions the information and materials needed for work within the Centre's competence [41];
- with the consent of the heads of those subdivisions, involve their employees in preparing drafts of local normative acts and other documents and in developing and carrying out measures conducted by the Centre [42];
- take part in meetings held by the University's management where matters within the Centre's competence are discussed [43];
- put before the University's management proposals to cancel, amend or supplement the University's local normative acts [44];
- control the fulfilment of local normative acts, orders, instructions and decisions of the Academic council on the organisation of the educational process in higher education programmes [45];
- interact with bodies of state authority and administration, legal entities and the University's subdivisions on matters within the Centre's competence [46].

## Interaction with other subdivisions

The Centre's interaction with other structural subdivisions is determined by the tasks and functions assigned to it by the Regulation [47]. The Regulation names these counterparts and matters:

- the faculties, institutes and laboratories — on the development, actualisation and implementation of the programmes, the teaching load, the individual work plans of teaching staff, the completion of record sheets of interim and repeat interim assessments and entering information into the electronic information-educational environment [48];
- the department of control of educational activity — on normative-legal support of educational activity and on assessing its quality and the preparation of students [49];
- the centre of pre-university preparation and admission of applicants — on the enrolment of students, including by transfer from other educational organisations, and on reinstatement after expulsion [50];
- the centre of interaction with students and graduates — on the movement of the student body, admission to study after an academic leave, students' personal files, educational work, the organising and holding of internships and practical preparation, and interim assessment on internships [51];
- the centre of international and academic cooperation — on the development and actualisation of network programmes and their components, support of their implementation and students' participation in academic mobility (exchange) programmes [52];
- the department of records and movement of personnel and the department of attraction and support of teaching staff — on the staffing of the programmes [53];
- the department of documentation support of managerial activity — on document flow, the drafting of local normative acts, the Centre's files under the consolidated nomenclature and transfer of documents to the archive [54];
- the organisational and technical department — on planning, distributing and using the classroom fund, booking rooms and material and technical provision of the programmes [55];
- the IT department — on the information systems, technical support of the educational process, the electronic information environment, access to professional databases and provision of licensed and freely distributed software [56];
- the library — on access to electronic library systems and the educational-methodical provision of the programmes [57];
- other structural subdivisions — in the development and implementation of the programmes and in preparing drafts of documents, conclusions and references [58].

The Centre may be a base for the practical preparation of the University's students [59].

## Reporting

On the basis of the relevant local normative acts, orders and instructions the Centre may, within its activity, be responsible for preparing information, references and reports and keeping them up to date [60]; it is responsible for preparing and keeping up to date the components of the higher education programmes and the documents recording the results of students' mastering of those programmes [61].

# Center for International Fundamental Research (Центр международных фундаментальных исследований) — place, tasks, functions and rights

## The document and the Centre's place in the structure

The Regulation on the Center for International Fundamental Research (Положение о Центре международных фундаментальных исследований) of ANO VO «Университет Иннополис» was approved by an order of 5 December 2025, whose number is printed as «№ ОД/АД‐02/2025/12/05 МНРЕГ», signed by the Director for Development and HR Policy R.F. Valiev and issued in «г. Иннополис» [1]. The Regulation regulates the activity of the Centre and determines its tasks, functions, rights and duties [2].

The Centre is a structural subdivision subordinate to the Deputy Director for project activity and infrastructure (заместитель директора по проектной деятельности и инфраструктуре) and belongs to the category of research and development (научно-исследовательских и опытно-конструкторских) subdivisions [3]. It reports directly to that Deputy Director [4].

In its activity the Centre follows the Constitution of the Russian Federation, the Labour Code of the Russian Federation, Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29 December 2012 «Об образовании в Российской Федерации» and other legislative and normative legal acts regulating the activity of higher education institutions, the University's Charter, decisions of the Academic, Supervisory and Operational councils of the University, orders and instructions of the director of the University, other local normative acts of the University and this Regulation [5].

- The structure, the number of staff and the staffing table of the Centre, and changes to them, are approved by the director of the University [6].
- The Centre is headed by a director of the Center for International Fundamental Research, hired and dismissed by order of the director of the University [7].
- The working conditions of the Centre's employees are determined by employment contracts concluded with each employee and by the University's internal labour regulations [8].
- The rights, duties and degree of responsibility of the Centre's employees are established by job descriptions [9].
- Employees of the Centre may, within their competences, be involved in work under grants, projects, subsidies and other programmes implemented by the University [10].
- The Regulation and additions and changes to it are approved by order of the director of the University or of another person authorised by him [11].

## Tasks

- carrying out research in the field of information technologies [12]
- performing research and development work (научно-исследовательские и опытно-конструкторские работы) in the field of information technologies [13]
- conducting experimental and applied development [14]
- holding thematic seminars, trainings and hackathons for a wide circle of listeners, including the University's students [15]
- attracting new projects to the Centre [16]
- creating a partner and expert network for solving the Centre's tasks [17]
- attracting outside specialists to cover the Centre's tasks [18]
- implementing climate projects and rendering services for the validation and verification of greenhouse gases [19]

## Functions

- organising the process of carrying out project work [20]
- planning and forming the portfolio of projects and their financing [21]
- analysing open programmes, competitions and requests of potential customers as to technological feasibility and the availability of ready solutions [22]
- promoting the Centre's solutions [23]

## Rights

In carrying out their activity the Centre's employees have the right to:

- request from the University's structural subdivisions the information and materials needed for the work within the Centre's competence [24]
- with the consent of the heads of the University's structural subdivisions, involve the employees of those subdivisions in preparing draft local acts and other documents and in developing and carrying out measures conducted by the Centre [25]
- take part in meetings held by the University's management when discussing questions whose solution falls within the Centre's competence [26]
- put before the University's management proposals to cancel, amend or supplement the University's local acts [27]
- interact with bodies of state authority and administration, legal entities and the University's subdivisions on questions within the Centre's competence [28]
- set tasks for accompanying projects (задачи по сопровождению проектов) to the heads of the University's structural subdivisions involved in project activity [29]

## Interaction with other subdivisions

The Centre's interaction with other structural subdivisions is determined by the tasks and functions assigned to it by the Regulation [30]. Interaction with state institutions, Russian and international organisations and structures of the real sector of the economy takes place according to the functions assigned to the Centre, in the order determined by the University's Charter and its local acts [31]. The Regulation names these counterparts and matters:

- the head of the Department for Records and Movement of Personnel (Отдел учета и движения персонала) — on hiring employees into the Centre [32]
- the Financial and Economic Department — on preparing agreements with the participants of projects and reports on the projects being implemented, within that Department's competence [33]
- the Financial and Economic Department, the Analytical Department and the Legal Department — on preparing agreements on won tenders and grants and reports on the projects being implemented [34]
- the Sector of Documentary Support (Сектор документального сопровождения) — on preparing the project's reporting documentation and working off remarks [35]
- the Purchasing Department — on carrying out the necessary procurement procedures [36]
- other structural subdivisions — on preparing draft local acts, orders, instructions, contracts and other documents, on applications for tenders and grants, on distributing rights to the results of research work, research and development and industrial projects and grants, on accounting for intellectual results created within that work, and on preparing conclusions and written and oral certificates on questions within the Centre's competence [37]

The Centre may be a base for the practical training of the University's students [38].

## Reporting

On the basis of the relevant local acts, orders and instructions, the Centre may, within its activity, be responsible for preparing information, certificates and reports and for keeping them up to date [39].

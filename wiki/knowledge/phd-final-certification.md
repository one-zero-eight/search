# PhD aspirantura final certification procedure

## What is the final certification

Final certification (ИА) for PhD aspirantura programs at Innopolis University is mandatory [1]. It is conducted as an evaluation of a thesis for the degree of Candidate of Sciences to assess its compliance with criteria established by Federal Law No. 127-FZ of 23.08.1996 "On Science and State Scientific and Technological Policy" [2]. The certification is conducted by a structural unit responsible for PhD training in the format of an Expert-Attestation Commission (ЭАК) session [3]. No fees are charged for passing the final certification [4].

## Admission

Students are admitted to the final certification if they have no academic debts and have fully completed their individual research plan, including preparing the thesis for defense [5]. For identity verification, the student presents a passport [6]. Theses containing state secrets are certified in compliance with Russian legislation on state secrets [7].

## Notification and schedule

The final certification program and this regulation are communicated to students no later than 3 months before the start, by signing an acknowledgment form or by automated electronic notification to corporate email [8]. The ЭАK meeting schedule is approved by the University Director no later than 30 calendar days before the start and communicated to students, appeal commissions, supervisors, and participants no later than 20 calendar days before the start [9], [10]. Meeting date changes are arranged by order on the student's written application with supporting documents [11]. Additional ЭАK sessions (if needed) are organized within 6 months after the student's application for a missed certification [12].

A decree admitting students to the final certification is issued no later than 1 calendar day before the start [13].

## Documents the student submits and the reviewers

A student admitted to the final certification submits, within 7 calendar days after the admission decree, a package of documents in paper and electronic form to the structural unit responsible for PhD training: the thesis as a manuscript in Russian or English (for a foreign citizen), formatted according to legislation, GOST R 7.0.11-2011 and the University's local acts; a list of scientific works; the supervisor's review or reviews; and a copy of the order approving the topic and the supervisor or supervisors [14]. Within the same 7 calendar days the student submits the thesis in electronic form to the reviewer [15].

Within 3 calendar days after the package is submitted, the responsible unit checks the thesis for the volume of borrowing and gives a statement on the results to the ЭАК Secretary and a copy to the student [16]. The thesis undergoes review — 2 reviews per thesis; reviewers are appointed from the University's full-time scientific and scientific-pedagogical workers or employees holding a Doctor of Sciences, Candidate of Sciences or recognised foreign degree and being specialists in the profile of the thesis or having scientific works in its scientific specialty, and specialists of enterprises, organisations and institutions with such degrees may also be reviewers [17]. The order appointing the reviewers is approved no later than 30 calendar days before the start [18].

The reviewer analyses the thesis and evaluates the relevance of its topic, the author's personal participation in obtaining the results, the substantiation and reliability of the scientific positions, conclusions and recommendations, the scientific novelty, the theoretical and practical significance of the results and the possibility of using them, the value of the student's scientific works and the completeness of the thesis materials in published works, the thesis structure, language and style, its compliance with the scientific specialty by which it was written (with the points of the specialty passport), and its compliance with the criteria of Federal Law No. 127-FZ [19]. The reviewer may make critical remarks, propose to revise the thesis and request its re-examination, and a negative review does not prevent the student from passing the final certification [19]. No later than 5 calendar days before the ЭАК meeting the reviewer writes the review and passes it to the responsible unit, and no later than 3 calendar days before the meeting the unit ensures that the student is acquainted with the reviews [19].

## Expert-Attestation Commission (ЭАК)

The ЭАК is created for each calendar year, possibly by scientific specialty or group of specialties [20], [21]. The ЭАК composition is approved by the University Director (or authorized representative) no later than 30 calendar days before the start of the final certification, on the proposal of the structural unit responsible for PhD training [22]. The ЭАК includes a Chair and at least four members, all of whom are full-time scientific and teaching staff of the University with a Doctor of Sciences, Candidate of Sciences, or foreign equivalent degree, and are specialists in the profile of the thesis being considered; at least two must be Doctors of Sciences [22]. The student's research supervisor cannot be the ЭАК Chair [22]. For interdisciplinary theses, specialists from the second specialty or representatives from another organization are included [22].

A Secretary is appointed for the ЭАК no later than 30 calendar days before the start; the Secretary is not a member of the ЭАК and does not vote [23]. ЭАK meetings are chaired by the ЭАK Chair; decisions are recorded in protocols stored in the responsible structural unit and transferred to the University archive [24], [25], [26].

## Session procedure

Meetings may use distance learning technologies [27]. A meeting is quorate with at least two-thirds of ЭАK members present [28]. The procedure includes: the Chair announces student and thesis information and plagiarism check results; the student presents a report of no more than 30 minutes (approximately 80% of time devoted to own research stages with emphasis on novelty and practical significance); the student answers questions; supervisor reviews and reviewer opinions are read; the thesis is discussed [29].

After discussion, the Chair conducts open voting on separate decisions: to issue a conclusion on compliance or non-compliance of the thesis with the criteria of Federal Law No. 127-FZ; to recommend or not recommend the thesis for defense in a dissertation council; to issue a certificate of completion of postgraduate studies or a certificate of study [30]. Each decision is by separate vote, simple majority; the Chair has a casting vote in case of a tie [30]. The result is announced to the student on the same day [31]. A protocol and a draft University conclusion are prepared for each student [32]. The ЭАK Chair submits a report to the responsible structural unit no later than 2 working days after the end of the ЭАK session in the academic year [33].

## Students with disabilities and health limitations

For students with disabilities the final certification is conducted taking account of their psycho-physical development, individual possibilities and state of health [34]. It is ensured that the certification may be held in one room together with students who are not disabled if this creates no difficulties; that an assistant is present who gives the necessary technical help (taking a workplace, moving, communicating with the members of the session); that the students may use the necessary technical means; and that they have unhindered access to the rooms, including toilets (ramps, handrails, widened doorways, lifts, with the room on the first floor where there are no lifts, special chairs and other devices) [35]. All local acts on the final certification are communicated to such students in a form accessible to them [36].

Not later than 3 months before the start of the final certification the student submits a written application on the special conditions needed, stating individual features and whether an assistant is needed, and attaches documents confirming those features where the University does not hold them [37]. Depending on the features, the University provides: for the blind, a set of writing accessories and Braille paper and a computer with specialised software where needed; for the visually impaired, individual even lighting of at least 300 lux and, where needed, a magnifying device, the students' own magnifying devices being allowed; for the deaf and hard-of-hearing and those with severe speech disorders, collective sound-amplifying apparatus and, where needed, individual sound-amplifying apparatus [38].

## Results and documents

A student who passes the final certification receives within 30 calendar days: a conclusion on the thesis compliance with Federal Law No. 127-FZ criteria and a certificate of completion of postgraduate studies [39]. A student who receives an unsatisfactory result receives within 30 calendar days: a conclusion on non-compliance with the criteria and a certificate of study confirming completion of the aspirantura program [40].

## University conclusion on the thesis

The ЭАK Secretary provides a draft University conclusion to the responsible structural unit no later than 10 working days after the ЭАK session [41]. The conclusion reflects the student's personal contribution, reliability, novelty, relevance, practical significance, value of publications, scientific specialty, field of science, and completeness of publication [41]. The conclusion is valid for 3 years from approval [41]. The responsible structural unit checks the draft for compliance with University regulations within 14 calendar days [42]. The signed conclusion (in 3 copies) is provided within 4 calendar days after the check [43]. The student receives 2 copies [44].

## Absence and retakes

A student who misses the final certification for a valid reason (temporary disability, public or state duties, court summons, transport problems, weather conditions, or other cases) may retake it within 6 months after the certification period ends, provided a document confirming the reason is submitted [45]. A student who misses without a valid reason receives a certificate of study [46].

## Early final certification

A student may take the final certification early if they have completed all aspirantura obligations and the individual plan, finished the thesis, and have no academic debts [47]. This requires a written application approved by the research supervisor [47]. The University Director sets the session date no earlier than 30 days after the admission order; the standard procedure then applies [48].

The Regulation on the preparation of scientific and scientific-pedagogical staff in aspirantura sets the same conditions for an early final attestation — early fulfilment of the duties of mastering the programme and of the individual work plan, a finished thesis and no academic debt, on a personal application agreed with the supervisor [49].

## What the Regulation on PhD training adds

The final attestation is conducted as an evaluation of the thesis against the criteria of the Federal Law "On Science and State Scientific-Technical Policy" [50]; an aspirant who has fully fulfilled the individual work plan, including preparing the thesis for defense, is admitted to it [51], and it is obligatory [52]. The University gives the conclusion on the thesis compliance, signed by the Director or, on his instruction, by a deputy head [53], and for preparing it may attract members of dissertation councils who are specialists in the problems of the thesis's scientific specialty [54]. The conclusion states the aspirant's personal participation in obtaining the results, the degree of reliability of the results, their novelty and practical significance, the value of the aspirant's scientific works, the thesis compliance with the requirements of that Federal Law, the scientific specialty and field of science, and the completeness of the thesis materials in the publications [55].

A graduate who has passed the final attestation receives the conclusion and a certificate of completion of aspirantura not later than 30 calendar days from its date [56]. Aspirants who did not pass the final attestation, or who mastered part of the programme or were expelled, receive a certificate of mastering the aspirantura programme or of the period of its mastering [57]; those with unsatisfactory results receive such a certificate together with a conclusion stating that the thesis does not comply with the criteria [58]. No payment is charged from aspirants for the final attestation [59].

## The document

The Regulation on the final certification on aspirantura programmes was approved by order of the University of 25 February 2025 No. ОД/УД-01/2025/02/25 [19]; it is approved by order of the Director or a person authorised by him and enters into force from the moment of approval, and changes are made by order of the Director or an authorised person [60], [61].

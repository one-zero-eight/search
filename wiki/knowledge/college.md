# College — place, tasks, functions and rights

## Document and place in the structure

The Regulation on the College of ANO VO «Innopolis University» was approved by the University's order of 14 October 2024 No. ОД/АД‐05/2024/10/14, signed by the director for development and HR policy R.F. Valiev, and carries the place «г. Иннополис» [1]. The Regulation regulates the College's activity and determines its tasks, functions, rights and duties [2].

The College is a structural subdivision of the University that prepares students under educational programmes in accordance with the federal state educational standards of secondary vocational education (СПО) [3]. It does not have the status of a legal entity and is a structural subdivision subordinate to the head of the department for the organisation and support of project and educational activity [4]. The College is created, renamed and liquidated by an order of the University's director in accordance with Russian legislation [5].

In its activity the College follows the legislation and other normative legal acts of the Russian Federation, the University's Charter, the University's internal labour regulations, the procedure for admission to secondary vocational education programmes, the Regulation on the organisation and implementation of educational activity under secondary vocational education programmes in the College, other local normative acts of the University and this Regulation [6].

The working conditions of the College's employees are determined by the employment contract concluded with each employee and by the University's internal labour regulations [7]. Their rights, duties and degree of responsibility are established by job descriptions and by the norms of labour and civil legislation of the Russian Federation [8]. Employees of the College may, within their competences, be involved in work under grants, projects, subsidies and other programmes implemented by the University [9]. The Regulation and additions and changes to it are approved by the University's director or by another person authorised by him [10].

## Purpose and tasks

The purpose of creating the College is to implement secondary vocational education programmes aimed at training qualified specialists who meet labour market requirements and СПО standards [11].

The main tasks of the College are [12]:

- organising and controlling the conduct of the educational process in accordance with federal legislation on educational activity, the federal state educational standards (ФГОС) and other federal, regional and local normative acts of the University [12]
- introducing practice-oriented learning and developing students' professional competences [12]
- the intellectual, cultural, physical and moral development of students [12]
- satisfying society's need for qualified specialists, including in information technology, robotics and mechatronics [12]
- developing and delivering secondary vocational education programmes and satisfying the individual's need for СПО and a qualification in the chosen field of professional activity [12]
- forming students' civic position and diligence, developing responsibility, independence and creative activity [12]
- preserving and augmenting the moral and cultural values of society [12]
- developing students' digital competences needed for successful professional activity in the digital economy [12]

## Functions

The main functions of the College are [13]:

- planning, organising, coordinating and controlling academic, academic-methodological, practical and educational work in delivering secondary vocational education programmes and conducting the educational process in accordance with Russian legislation, the University's Charter and the University's local normative acts [13]
- meeting ФГОС requirements when organising the educational process and preparing the corresponding local normative acts [13]
- maintaining links with general education institutions and conducting career guidance and advertising and information work in accordance with plans approved by the admission commission [13]
- interacting with scientific and public organisations to create favourable conditions for the growth of the volume and quality of students' knowledge [13]
- ensuring interaction with state and municipal authorities and other institutions and enterprises to the extent needed for solving the College's tasks [13]
- carrying out civil defence, labour protection and fire safety measures in the College [13]
- developing the College's development strategy and annual work plan [13]
- ensuring the introduction of new technologies of teaching and of controlling students' knowledge [13]
- coordinating the activity of the teaching staff, the educational support personnel and the College's students [13]
- organising and conducting academic, methodological and educational work in the College [13]
- drawing up contracts for the provision of paid educational services under secondary vocational education programmes and controlling the timeliness of tuition payment [13]
- compiling the class schedule and the schedule of tests and examinations and controlling their fulfilment [13]
- organising the recording of students' progress and attendance and analysing the results [13]
- organising and controlling the implementation of students' interim and final assessment [13]
- organising and conducting educational and outreach work among students and coordinating the socially useful activity of the College's students and employees [13]
- creating and ensuring favourable conditions for the College's employees and students [13]
- organising and controlling the raising of qualifications and retraining of the College's employees [13]
- keeping the College's documentation on academic, methodological and educational work [13]
- controlling the state of the academic, production and office premises assigned to the College [13]
- interacting with enterprises that serve as bases for practices and organising various types of practices and internships [13]
- organising interaction with employers to ensure graduates' employment and adapting educational programmes to labour market requirements [13]
- maintaining links with the College's graduates and helping them find employment [13]
- summing up the College's results and presenting them to the University's management in good time [13]

## Management and the College director

Management of the College is carried out in accordance with Russian legislation, the University's Charter, other local normative acts and this Regulation [14]. The activity of the College is headed by a director appointed by an order of the University's director on the basis of a concluded employment contract, a job description and the University's local normative acts [15].

Within the competences determined by the University's director, the College director leads the College in accordance with laws and local normative acts and is answerable to the University's director for the results of the College's activity; develops and implements the College's development strategy and organises interaction with other educational institutions and organisations; directly organises and leads academic, educational and innovation work in the College; ensures the delivery of mid-level specialist training programmes in accordance with ФГОС СПО requirements; controls the provision of students with textbooks in all disciplines according to existing norms and their timely updating; organises and controls the information support of the educational process through modern information sources; organises and controls the implementation of curricula in all their components; organises a system of quality control of classes, practices and other forms of organising the educational process; on the basis of the absence of academic debt and of fulfilment of the curriculum or individual curriculum for the secondary vocational education programme, organises in the established procedure the admission of students to the state final certification; issues, within his competence, normative acts binding on all the College's employees and students; submits draft staffing tables and plans of financial and economic activity for approval; develops proposals and carries out measures to strengthen and develop the academic and material-technical base and to improve financial and economic activity, discipline and legality, the safekeeping and economical use of material-technical resources and funds, and the equipping with modern equipment, textbooks, manuals and technical means of teaching; organises and ensures the participation of teachers and employees in improving the educational process; takes measures to staff the College with qualified personnel, to organise regular raising of qualifications of the teaching staff and other categories of employees, exchange of experience and favourable and safe working conditions, and to comply with labour protection and fire safety rules; promotes the development of work motivation, initiative and activity of the College's employees; submits proposals on creating the necessary social and living conditions for the College's students and employees; coordinates the preparation and review of academic-methodological documents and the performance of scientific-methodological work; ensures the timely compilation of the established reporting documentation; organises the work and interaction of the College with other structural subdivisions of the University and interested organisations within the powers granted; ensures that the College's employees observe Russian legislation, service (labour) discipline, the internal labour regulations, this Regulation and the job descriptions of employees taking part in the delivery of secondary vocational education programmes; organises feedback from the College's students and employees aimed at identifying problems of the educational process and optimising it; within his powers heads the activity of the College's collegial bodies; reports on work on educational, academic-methodological and other activity of the College; is responsible for the safekeeping and confidentiality of service information and of the personal data of employees and students; and ensures compliance with the University's anti-corruption policy at the College's level [16].

The College director is personally responsible for organising the educational process, spending funds, the results of financial and economic activity, the complete and timely submission of statistical reporting, labour protection, compliance with general regulations and fire safety requirements, and the safekeeping of property, academic, financial, staffing and other documents [17]. He may delegate part of his powers to his deputies and other employees of the College; the distribution of duties is established by an order of the College director and by the job description, the deputies directly manage and are responsible for the type of activity and area of work assigned to them in accordance with job descriptions, orders of the University's director and instructions of the College director, and the deputies are appointed and dismissed by an order of the University's director on the proposal of the College director [18].

## Rights

To perform the tasks assigned to the College, its employees have the right to [19]:

- request from the heads and employees of the University's structural subdivisions the information materials needed on questions of academic and methodological work [19]
- take part in competitions, grants and projects aimed at developing educational activity and improving the quality of educational services [19]
- resolve questions of improving and organising the educational process in the College within their competence and make corresponding proposals to the management [19]
- require from the relevant structural subdivisions of the University the creation of the technical conditions needed for organising the educational process and the work of the College's employees [19]
- take part in discussing and resolving questions of the College's academic and methodological activity [19]
- put before the University's management questions connected with the organisational and material-technical support of their professional activity [19]
- use the services of the library and of the information fund of the academic and research subdivisions [19]
- control the observance by the College's students of the internal regulations [19]

## Responsibility

The College's employees are responsible for the performance of the tasks, functions and duties assigned to them by job descriptions and by the labour legislation of the Russian Federation; for preparing students with a level of knowledge corresponding to ФГОС СПО requirements; for observing the rights and academic freedoms of the College's students and employees; for the safekeeping of the available material-technical equipment; and for observing the norms of professional ethics, the University's internal labour regulations and the provisions established by the University's local normative acts within the limits provided by Russian legislation [20]. Responsibility for the College's activity is borne by the College director in accordance with the University's Charter, the job description and the employment contract [21].

The College director bears personal responsibility for the timeliness and quality of the performance of the functions and tasks provided by this Regulation; for the timeliness and quality of the information presented to the University's director; for the proper quality of organising the educational process; for the proper quality of the assessment of students; for compliance with ФГОС СПО in the educational process; for observing the ethics of relations in the team and with the University's students and employees; for compliance with the rules of labour protection, safety engineering, fire safety, civil defence, and of commercial, service and other secrets protected by law; for the rational use of working time and of labour, financial, material, information and technical resources; and for observing financial and staffing discipline, the University's Charter and other local normative acts [22].

## Interaction, financing and changes

In its activity the College interacts with other subdivisions of the University on ensuring the educational process, methodological and economic work [23]. It uses the University's academic and material-technical base, its library funds and laboratory premises for delivering secondary vocational education programmes [24]. The financial and staffing support of the College is carried out within the University's overall resource management system [25]. The College may be a base for the practical training of the University's students [26].

The College's activity is financed from the University's funds formed from budget and non-budget sources, on the basis of the approved annual financial plan (budget) [27]. The College has the right to attract additional financial and material resources to improve the quality of the educational process [28].

Changes to the Regulation are approved by the University's director or by another person authorised by him [29]. The College's activity is terminated in the form of its reorganisation or liquidation [30]; termination of the College's activity as a structural subdivision is possible on the basis of an order of the University's director in the procedure provided by legislation in force [31].

The Regulation comes into force from the moment of its approval by the University's director or another person authorised by him and is valid until it is changed or repealed [32]. Changes and additions are made as necessary, by preparing a draft by the College director, and are approved by an order of the University's director or another person authorised by him [33]. Questions not regulated by the Regulation are governed by Russian legislation and the University's local normative acts [34]. The Regulation is to be posted on the University's official website at https://innopolis.university/sveden/ [35].

# Transfer within Innopolis University — change of field, profile, programme or form of study (Regulation of 12 May 2026)

## The document

Internal transfer is regulated by the Regulation on the procedure and grounds for the transfer, expulsion and restoration of students at ANO VO «Innopolis University», approved by order of 12 May 2026 № ОД/АД-01/2026/05/12 and signed by the Director D.V. Vandyukov [1]. Its section 2.4 governs the transfer of students inside the University [2]. The wiki copy is a scan read by OCR, so its numbers and dates should be checked against the PDF [1].

## Who applies and when

A student who wishes to transfer is transferred from one field of training to another field of the same level of education, from one scientific specialty to another scientific specialty, from one profile (направленность) to another profile within a field of training, from one educational programme to another educational programme, or from one form of study to another form of study [3]. Such a student submits an application for internal transfer addressed to the director to the responsible structural unit (annex 4 to the Regulation) [3].

The application for transfer from one field of training to another, from one scientific specialty to another, from one profile to another, or from the part-time to the full-time form of study is submitted not earlier than after passing the first interim assessment, with no academic debts, and not later than 7 working days before the start of the semester or trimester; an application for transfer from the full-time to the part-time form is submitted not later than one month before the start of the next interim assessment [3].

## The attestation commission

- On the basis of the application the University, within 14 working days from receipt of the application, organises the work of the attestation commission on the application [4].
- Within that time the commission evaluates the student's results of mastering the programme in order to determine the studied disciplines, completed internships and performed research that will be credited on transfer under the University's local act on credit, determines the period from which the student will be admitted to study, and decides on the possibility of the transfer [4].
- If more applications have been submitted than there are vacant places, the University, besides evaluating the documents, holds a competitive selection and, on its results, takes either a decision on transfer or a decision refusing transfer to those who did not pass; the procedure and timing of that selection are set by a local act [5].
- The decision on transfer is taken by the attestation commission on the day of its meeting and recorded in the protocol of the meeting, to which the list of studied disciplines, completed internships and performed research to be credited or re-assessed is attached; the credit itself is made on the basis of the commission's decision under the local act on credit [6].

## Consequences of the transfer

- If the attestation shows that disciplines or sections of disciplines were not studied because of differences in the curricula, they are included in the individual curriculum, which is drawn up under a local act of the University [7].
- The order on internal transfer is issued within 3 working days after the attestation commission takes its decision [8].
- The corresponding changes are entered in the record books and student cards (where they exist, depending on the category of the student) [9].
- Records of credit of results of mastering disciplines, practice and additional programmes in other organisations are entered in the record books (electronic record books) on the basis of the attestation commission's decision under the University's local act on credit [10].

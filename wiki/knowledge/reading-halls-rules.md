# Use of the reading halls — rights, duties and prohibitions (Rules of 1 June 2023)

## The document

The Rules of use of the reading halls of ANO VO «Innopolis University» (Правила пользования читальными залами) were approved by order of the University of 1 June 2023 (Innopolis, 2023) [1]. They are a local normative act regulating the rights and duties of the users of the reading halls, the rules of behaviour in the reading halls of the University, responsibility for violating the Rules and other questions of the organisation of the use of the reading halls [1].

The Rules extend to all users of the University's reading halls, including employees of the University, students of the University and visitors of the University (Users) [1]. The University places the Rules on its official website in the information and telecommunications network «Internet» in order to inform Users, and also informs employees by placing them on the corporate portal «Битрикс» [2].

The wiki copy is a draft read by OCR, so numbers and dates should be checked against the PDF, and the numbering of some clauses is garbled in that copy [1].

## What the terms mean

- A reading hall (читальный зал) is a public space located in the University and intended for work and for holding events; reading halls include zones [1].
- A reading zone (читальная зона) is a specific workplace for a single User, located in a reading hall [1].
- The zone for accommodating aspirants (зона для размещения аспирантов) is space in the reading hall located on the 2nd floor of the University, fenced off by partitions with mechanical locks with keys, equipped with a sign «Зона для размещения аспирантов» and intended primarily for aspirants [1].

## Events in the reading halls and control

Reading halls may be used for holding various events; when events are prepared and held, these Rules do not extend to the participants and organisers of the events [1].

Control over ensuring compliance with the Rules is assigned to the head of the department of administrative policy and informatisation M.E. Ivashkevich [3].

## Rights of Users

When using the reading halls, reading zones and the zone for accommodating aspirants (in the text further — the Reading halls), Users have the right [4]:

- to occupy the Reading halls for study or work [4];
- to use, for their intended purpose, the tables, chairs, armchairs, electric sockets, extension leads and other equipment located in the Reading halls [4];
- to bring personal laptops, headphones, stationery and other means necessary for work, and bottled water [4];
- to ask for clarification of the Rules: students — of employees of the Department of support and development of students, and employees and visitors of the University — of the commandant of the Organisational-technical department [4].

## Duties of Users

When using the University's Reading halls, Users are obliged [5]:

- to treat the premises of the Reading halls, the University's property (furniture, interior items, equipment) and the property of other Users with care [5];
- to observe public order and silence in the University's Reading halls and to switch mobile telephones to silent mode [5];
- to maintain cleanliness and order in the University's Reading halls, and when leaving a Reading hall (zone) to prepare the zone for subsequent use by other Users and to remove litter — if a group of Users occupied the zone, the last member of the group to leave must check the state of the zone and, where necessary, put it in order [5];
- where damage, breakage or soiling of the University's property located in the reading hall is caused or discovered, to report it by e-mail to the address given in the Rules (that address is garbled by OCR in the wiki copy) [5];
- to leave the reading hall (zone) of the University at the demand of an employee of the University when events are being prepared and held, and also where the prohibitions established in clause 4 of the Rules are violated [5];
- to take another free zone outside the zone for accommodating aspirants at the request of an aspirant and of other persons named in clause 1.2 of the Rules [5].

## What Users are prohibited from doing

When using the University's Reading halls, Users are prohibited from [6]:

- dismantling and moving furniture and interior elements, changing their arrangement, and moving furniture between premises and zones [6];
- eating, and bringing kitchen utensils (glasses, cups, thermoses, tableware, food containers and so on), except drinking water in closed containers [6];
- using Reading halls that have not been put into operation, including, but not exclusively, Reading halls that are incompletely equipped, subject to repair or in the stage of repair, deformed and so on [6];
- using zones occupied by other Users for work, and occupying several reading zones at the same time [6];
- damaging the University's property or the property of other Users of the University's Reading halls [6];
- violating public order, including behaving disrespectfully towards other Users of the University and creating obstacles to other Users' work (making noise, uttering loud sounds, talking on the telephone, holding negotiations by video conference or otherwise hindering the work and presence of other Users in the Reading halls and so on) [6];
- entering the University's Reading halls in outer clothing or with bulky hand luggage [6];
- using personal household items (pillows, blankets and so on) in the University's Reading halls [6];
- occupying a reading zone without using it for more than 15 minutes in a row [6];
- leaving personal belongings unattended, including creating the impossibility of using a reading zone by other Users by leaving personal belongings for more than 15 minutes [6];
- committing other actions violating the legislation of the Russian Federation, the Charter, the Rules of internal order of students of ANO VO «Innopolis University» or other local normative acts of the University [6].

## Responsibility

Where the Rules are violated, measures of disciplinary liability (a remark, a reprimand, expulsion for students; a remark, a reprimand, dismissal for employees) and of material liability (in the form of compensation of material damage in kind or in money) provided for by the current legislation of the Russian Federation, the Rules of internal order of students of ANO VO «Innopolis University», the Rules of internal labour regulations of ANO VO «Innopolis University» and other local normative acts of the University may also be applied to Users of the University's reading halls [7].

# Internal entrance examinations in person and with distance technologies — equipment, room and conduct rules

## What the document is

The page is the University's information document «Информация о проведении вступительных испытаний очно и (или) с использованием дистанционных технологий» on how Innopolis University holds its internal entrance examinations in person or with distance technologies [1]. It gives no date and no mention of an admission year [1]. The same provisions, in the same wording, stand in the 2026/2027 admission rules for bachelor's and master's programmes [2].

## Form of the examination and identity check

- The University holds internal entrance examinations in person or with distance technologies, on condition that the applicants are identified when taking them, through the University's electronic information system [1].
- Identity is confirmed by entering the personal identifier and the password obtained at registration in the University's electronic information system [1].
- Internal entrance examinations are held in the form of computer testing [1].
- During an examination its participants and the persons involved in holding it may not carry or use means of communication; participants may not carry or use reference materials and electronic computing equipment other than that needed for taking the examination [1].

## Equipment and connection for a distance examination

- The applicant needs means of computing technology — a desktop computer, a notebook or a tablet — and communication equipment giving access to the software and information systems of the University's electronic information system through local networks and the Internet [1].
- The software requirements are: the internet browser Google Chrome of a version not lower than 72, an operating system not lower than Windows 7, macOS 10.12 «Sierra» or Linux, and an internet connection speed of not less than 512 Kbps [1].

## The room and the workplace

- The room must have walls and a closed door [1].
- No outside persons may be in the room during the examination [1].
- Additional computers and other monitors must be switched off [1].
- The working surface of the desk on which the applicant's computer stands must be free of all objects, including pocket computers or other computer devices, watches, notebooks, books, notepads, sticky notes, notes or papers with printed text [1].
- The web camera must not be placed opposite a light source and must give a view of the room within a radius of 2–3 metres from the applicant [1].
- On the desk only the documents identifying the applicant, clean sheets of paper and a pen are allowed [1].
- Authorised officials of the University involved in holding the examination have the right to ask the applicant to demonstrate the room and the workplace for compliance with these requirements [1].

## What the applicant does during a distance examination

- Not later than 5 (five) minutes before the start of the examination the applicant must enter the personal account of the applicant in the University's electronic information system, passing the identification procedure that way by entering the personal identifier and password [1].
- He then follows the link to the computer testing page [1].
- During the computer testing he may be given the right to use auxiliary means, the list of which is established by the programmes of the entrance examinations [1].

## Violations and removal

- On refusal to show the room and the workplace or on their non-compliance with the requirements, the authorised officials may remove the applicant from the place of the entrance examination, with an act of removal being drawn up [1].
- On a violation of the admission rules approved by the University during an examination with distance technologies, the members of the admission commission or of the examination commission draw up an act of violation of the admission rules, an electronic copy of which is sent to the applicant [1].
- An applicant in respect of whom such an act is drawn up is considered not to have passed the entrance examination without a valid reason [1].
- The applicant may not record the materials and the content of the internal entrance examination in any way, nor pass them to third persons [1].

# Laboratory of Machine Learning and Data Representation (Лаборатория машинного обучения и представления данных) — place, tasks, functions and rights

## The document

The page is the Regulation on the Laboratory of Machine Learning and Data Representation (Лаборатория машинного обучения и представления данных) of ANO VO «Innopolis University» [1]. It regulates the Laboratory's activity and determines its tasks, functions, rights and duties [2]. The Regulation is approved by the University director, and the additions and amendments made to it are approved by order of the University director [3]. The page carries the status draft, and the text is a text-layer extraction, so it should be checked against the original document [1].

## Place in the structure

- The Laboratory is a structural subdivision of the University [4].
- The Laboratory reports directly to the head of the Laboratory, and in the absence of the head — to the director of the Institute of Robotics [4].
- In its activity the Laboratory is guided by the Constitution of the Russian Federation, the Labour Code of the Russian Federation, the Federal Law «Об образовании в Российской Федерации», other legislative and normative legal acts regulating the activity of higher education institutions, the University's Charter, decisions of the University's Academic and Supervisory councils, orders and instructions of the University director, other local acts of the University and this Regulation [5].
- The structure, headcount and staffing table of the Laboratory, and changes to the structure and staffing table, are approved by the University director [6].
- The Laboratory is headed by the head of the Laboratory, appointed and dismissed by order of the University director in accordance with the Regulation on the procedure for filling positions of scientific and pedagogical workers in a higher education institution of the Russian Federation [7].
- The working conditions of the Laboratory's employees are determined by the employment contracts concluded with each employee and by the University's internal labour rules [7].
- The rights, duties and degree of responsibility of the Laboratory's employees are established by job descriptions [8].

## Tasks

- Creating a scientific and methodological database for carrying out fundamental and applied research in accordance with the University's plans for research work [9].
- Opening experimental sites for scientific-theoretical and practical research and for testing innovative technologies [10].
- Helping to build the research skills of students, postgraduate students and teachers [11].
- Expanding and strengthening scientific ties with institutions and organisations of a similar profile [12].
- Promoting scientific knowledge among the public [13].
- Carrying out research and development work (НИР, НИОКР), industrial projects and grants in the Laboratory's profile, by its employees on their own initiative and also under grant support and budget and non-budget contracts [14].
- Preparing for publication and publishing annotated and abstracted materials on the latest experience and the University's own materials and the developments of its scientific and pedagogical workers and students [15].

## Functions

- Developing scientific research programmes in the Laboratory's field of activity [16].
- Step-by-step planning and conduct of НИР, НИОКР, industrial projects and grants [17].
- Improving the methodologies, technologies, methods and tools of research work [18].
- Creating a database for research work of students while they do course papers and final qualification works [19].
- Developing and publishing scientific and methodological manuals based on the Laboratory's activity [20].
- Publishing scientific articles in journals, including those in the international citation databases Web of Science (WOS), Scopus and others [21].
- Holding thematic seminar-workshops and consultations for the University's teachers, postgraduate students and students [22].
- Preparing practical (experimental) material for inclusion in candidate and doctoral dissertations [23].
- Drawing the University's students into research activity [24].
- Unlocking the scientific potential of young specialists [25].

## Rights

- Requesting from the University's structural subdivisions the information and materials needed for the work within the Laboratory's competence [26].
- Involving, with the consent of the heads of the University's structural subdivisions, employees of those subdivisions for preparing drafts of local acts and other documents and for developing and carrying out the Laboratory's activities [27].
- Taking part in meetings held by the University's management where matters within the Laboratory's competence are discussed [28].
- Proposing to the University's management the repeal of, amendments to and additions to the University's local acts [29].
- Using the classroom fund, objects and equipment of the University's material and technical base [30].
- Using the University's logo in all presentation materials [31].
- Carrying out publishing activity in the established procedure, in compliance with the rules in force in the University [32].

## Interaction with other subdivisions

- With the Department for Attracting Grants on participation in НИР, НИОКР, industrial projects and grants [33].
- With the Department for Support and Quality Control of Projects on reporting under grants, budget and non-budget contracts [34].
- Interaction with the other structural subdivisions is determined by the tasks and functions assigned to the Laboratory by this Regulation in accordance with the University's structure, by the organisational, administrative and normative documents of the University's management and by the University's Charter [35].

## Reporting

- Within its activity the Laboratory is responsible for preparing reports on НИР, НИОКР, industrial projects and grants and for passing them in time to the University's internal structural subdivisions [36].
- The Laboratory submits reporting in accordance with the requirements of the Ministry of Education of the Russian Federation and of other state statistics bodies, as needed for managerial and financial decisions [37].

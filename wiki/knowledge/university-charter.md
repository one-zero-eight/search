# Charter (Устав) of Innopolis University — legal status, founders, activities and local acts

## The document

The Устав (charter) of the autonomous non-commercial organisation of higher education «Innopolis University» is the founding document of the legal entity: the copy on the wiki records that it was presented when the ЕГРЮЛ entry of 23.04.2025 was made under ГРН 2251602216820 for a legal entity with ОГРН 1121600006142 [1]. The charter in a new edition was approved by a protocol of a meeting of the Founders of 10 April 2025 No. 23 [1]. The copy is signed with an enhanced qualified electronic signature; the block at the end of the document records the signing time 2025-04-21 18:08:20 and the certificate owner as the Directorate of the Ministry of Justice of the Russian Federation for the Republic of Tatarstan, the certificate valid from 2024-03-05 to 2025-05-29 [2]. The page is a scan read by OCR and warns that numbers and dates should be checked against the PDF before relying on them [1].

Amendments and additions to the charter are approved by the Founders and are subject to state registration in the same order as the state registration of the charter [2].

## Legal status and names

The University is a unitary non-commercial organisation without membership, established under the legislation of the Russian Federation on the basis of voluntary property contributions, created to achieve educational, scientific, social, cultural and management goals that serve the spiritual and other non-material needs of citizens in education and goals directed at achieving public goods [3]. In its activity it is guided by the Constitution of the Russian Federation, the Civil Code, Federal Law No. 7-ФЗ of 12.01.1996 «О некоммерческих организациях», Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29.12.2012 «Об образовании в Российской Федерации» and other federal laws and normative legal acts [4].

The full name in Russian is Автономная некоммерческая организация высшего образования «Университет Иннополис» [5]; the short name is АНО ВО «Университет Иннополис» [6]. The charter also gives full and short names in English, but the OCR text of those lines is corrupted [7], [8]. The place of location (legal address) is 420500, Russian Federation, Republic of Tatarstan, city of Innopolis [9].

The University is a legal entity from the moment of its state registration, has an independent balance, settlement and other bank accounts, may acquire and exercise property and personal non-property rights and bear duties in its own name, and be a plaintiff and defendant in court; the Founders are not liable for the University's obligations and the University is not liable for the Founders' obligations [10]. Its activity is subject to licensing in the order established by the legislation of the Russian Federation [11]. It has a seal and may have stamps and letterheads with its name, an emblem and other means of individualisation [12].

## Founders, branches and representative offices

The Founders are the Russian Federation, represented by the Ministry of Digital Development, Communications and Mass Media of the Russian Federation (ОГРН 1047702026701), and the Republic of Tatarstan, represented by the Ministry of Land and Property Relations of the Republic of Tatarstan (ОГРН 1021602851753) [13]. The University may create branches and open representative offices; they are not legal entities, are endowed with property and act on the basis of regulations approved by the University, and their heads are appointed by the sole executive body of the University and act under its power of attorney [13]. On reorganisation of a Founder its rights and duties pass to its successor [14].

## Purpose, tasks and activities

The main purpose of creating the University is the training of highly qualified personnel, the creation and commercialisation of breakthrough technologies to bring the Russian information technology industry to a qualitatively new level, raising the density of robotisation and the level of production automation, promoting the technological development of society and the state, and performing aviation work [15].

The main tasks include the acquisition by students of knowledge, skills and competences through programmes of secondary vocational and/or higher education, the satisfaction of educational needs through additional education without raising the level of education, the expansion and deepening of research in information technology, robotics and unmanned technologies, the creation of conditions for the preparation of doctoral and candidate dissertations, the popularisation of these fields among children, young people and the wider public, the use of the newest scientific achievements and technologies in teaching, and the provision of social, cultural, sports and recreation infrastructure for study, research and the health of students and staff [16].

The subject of the University's activity includes the delivery of basic professional educational programmes — secondary vocational programmes, bachelor, specialist and master programmes, and programmes for scientific and scientific-pedagogical personnel in aspirantura — as well as basic general education programmes, basic professional training programmes, additional general education programmes and additional professional programmes (повышение квалификации and professional retraining) [17]. The clause on the types of educational programmes implemented repeats this list, including programmes of initial, basic and secondary general education [18].

To achieve its purposes the University may carry on income-generating activity, including paid educational services within the limits of its licence, development and adaptation of programmes for particular customers, research and development work, realisation of rights to intellectual property, development and sale of software and high-technology products, production and commercialisation of aircraft and unmanned aviation systems, consulting, expert, patent and information services, leasing of property, participation in grant programmes, provision of places for temporary residence, publishing and employment services, and public catering [19]. The income received is not subject to distribution or transfer to the Founders [20]. The University may establish and pay special prizes, remuneration and targeted grants to support students, creative initiative and research work [21].

## Structure

The University is independent in forming its organisational structure [22]. It may have structural subdivisions ensuring educational activity — branches, representative offices, departments, faculties, institutes, centres, chairs, preparatory departments and courses, research, methodological and educational-methodological subdivisions, laboratories, practice bases, demonstration centres, exhibition halls, libraries, museums, sports clubs, student sports clubs, dormitories and other subdivisions provided for by local normative acts — as well as production and social infrastructure objects; structural subdivisions are not independent legal entities and their legal status, functions and powers are determined by regulations on them [23]. The University independently sets its staffing table according to the volume and forms of the programmes, services and works it carries out, determines the number of employees in its subdivisions, hires employees, concludes and terminates employment contracts with them and distributes job duties [24].

## Educational activity, admission and tuition

Educational programmes are developed and approved by the University independently, taking into account Russian and world experience, in accordance with federal state educational standards, federal state requirements, professional standards or qualification requirements [25]. A combination of various methods and technologies of obtaining education is allowed, including with the use of e-learning and distance educational technologies [26].

An admissions commission is created, chaired by the Director; its work and record-keeping are organised by an executive secretary appointed by an order of the Director [27]. On admission the University guarantees observance of citizens' rights to education established by legislation, and the publicity and openness of the admissions commission's work [28]. A personal file is formed for each student in the established order, and the procedure for forming and keeping it is set by a local normative act of the University [29].

The cost of study on general, secondary vocational and higher education programmes is approved by the Director on the basis of a decision of the Operations Council; the cost of study on additional general education programmes, additional professional programmes and basic professional training programmes, and the amount of payment for additional educational services, are approved by the Director [30].

## Local normative acts

The University adopts local normative acts containing norms regulating the activity it carries out, within its competence and in accordance with the legislation of the Russian Federation; they may not contradict legislation, and the activity of the University is regulated by orders, instructions, protocol decisions, regulations, rules, codes, instructions and other documents [31].

Local normative acts affecting the rights of students and employees are adopted taking into account the opinion of the student council and of councils of parents (legal representatives) of minor students, where they exist, and, in the cases and order provided by labour legislation, of representative bodies of employees [32]. The student council and the council of parents are created on the initiative of students or parents respectively, are forms of their public self-activity, and may represent the interests of all or part of the students or parents [32]. Before adopting a local normative act affecting students' rights, the management bodies send the draft to the student council and the council of parents of minor students [33]. Within five working days from receipt of the draft they send a reasoned written opinion to the relevant collegial body or to the Director [34]. If they agree with the draft, or if no reasoned opinion arrives within the stated period, the management body adopts the act [35]. If the opinion does not contain agreement or contains proposals for improvement, the management body may agree fully or in part and amend the draft, or disagree and adopt the act in its original wording [36].

Norms of local normative acts that worsen the position of students or employees compared with the position established by legislation on education or labour legislation, or that are adopted in breach of the established order, do not apply and are subject to cancellation by the Director [37].

## Staff, property, financing and reporting

The conclusion of an employment contract for the post of a pedagogical worker belonging to the professorial and teaching staff, and transfer to such a post, is preceded by election by competition for the corresponding post [38].

The creation and activity of organisational structures of political parties, socio-political movements and religious associations is not allowed in the University [39].

The University may own buildings, structures, housing stock, equipment, inventory, money in roubles and foreign currency, securities and other property; its property was formed at its establishment and is formed in accordance with legislation and the charter [40]. Its financial support comes from funds received from its activity, funds of budgets of the budget system of the Russian Federation, voluntary property contributions and donations of the Founders and other persons, grants and subsidies, dividends and interest on securities and deposits, proceeds from the sale of goods, works, services and rights to intellectual property, income from the use of property and other sources not prohibited by legislation [41].

The University posts an annual report on its activity on its official website, including information on the distribution of financial funds by directions of activity [42].

## Reorganisation and liquidation

Reorganisation of the University, including transformation into a fund, or its liquidation, the appointment of a liquidation commission (liquidator) and the approval of the liquidation balance sheet are carried out by decision of the Founders; the University may also be liquidated by a court decision on the grounds and in the order provided by legislation [43]. The University may be transformed into a fund in accordance with the legislation of the Russian Federation [44].

# Termination of educational relations

## The document

These rules are in the Порядок оформления возникновения, приостановления и прекращения отношений between the University and students and (or) the parents (legal representatives) of minor students, approved in a new edition by an order of the University signed by the director K.V. Semenikhin and issued in accordance with the requirements of Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29.12.2012 in order to regulate the formalisation of educational relations in the University [1]. The same text is reproduced on the wiki page of the document [2].

The procedure was developed on the basis of the Federal Law of 29.12.2012 No. 273-ФЗ, the University's Charter, Постановление of the Government of the Russian Federation of 15.08.2013 No. 706 «Об утверждении правил оказания платных образовательных услуг», the rules on paid educational services approved at the University and other local normative acts [3]. It regulates the formalisation of the emergence, suspension and termination of relations between the University and students and (or) the parents (legal representatives) of minor students [4].

## Completion of studies

Educational relations are terminated upon completion of education (finishing studies) [5].

## Early termination by the student

Educational relations may be terminated early by the student's own wish or by the wish of the parents (legal representatives) of a minor student, including when transferring to another educational organization [6].

The student (or parents/legal representatives of a minor) must apply to the University administration with the following documents [7]:

- A personal application to the Director requesting dismissal on their own wish [7].
- A clearance sheet in the form established by the University [7].
- A receipt for tuition payment for the relevant period of study, if applicable [7].
- The student ID card and grade book, if they were issued [7].

Dismissal on the student's own wish is processed within no more than one month from the date of submitting the application [7].

## Early termination by the University

Grounds for dismissal by the University's initiative include [8]:

- Dismissal as a disciplinary sanction applied to a student who has reached the age of fifteen [8].
- Failure by the student to perform obligations to diligently master the educational program and complete the curriculum [8].
- Establishment of a violation of the admission procedure that resulted in the student's illegal enrollment [8].
- Delay in payment for paid educational services [8].
- Impossibility of properly fulfilling obligations to provide paid educational services due to the student's actions (or inaction) [8].

## Early termination due to circumstances beyond control

Educational relations may also be terminated early due to circumstances beyond the control of the student (or parents/legal representatives) and the University, including the liquidation of the University [6].

## Formalities

Termination is formalized by an order of dismissal from the University [9]. If a contract was concluded, it is terminated based on the dismissal order [9]. The student's rights and obligations related to educational activities cease from the date of dismissal [9]. Other civil-law agreements continue to operate until the parties fulfill their obligations under them [9].

After the dismissal order is issued, the student is given a certificate of education and the original education document that was in their personal file [10]. An extract from the dismissal order is placed in the student's personal file [10].

## Expulsion under the Regulation of 12 May 2026

The Regulation on the procedure and grounds for the transfer, expulsion and restoration of students (Положение о порядке и основаниях перевода, отчисления и восстановления обучающихся) was approved by order of 12 May 2026 № ОД/АД-01/2026/05/12 and signed by the Director D.V. Vandyukov [11]. Its requirements are obligatory and extend to the structural subdivisions and employees dealing with transfer, expulsion and restoration in bachelor's, master's and aspirantura programmes [12], and it remains in force until repealed or until normative acts, the Charter or other local acts provide a different procedure [13]. The wiki copy is a scan read by OCR, so its numbers and dates should be checked against the PDF [11].

### Grounds for expulsion

- A student may be expelled in connection with obtaining education (completion of studies) or early, on the grounds established in clause 3.3 [14].
- Expulsion in connection with obtaining education is ordered by the director or another official authorised by him after the full course of study and passing the final (state final) attestation; after that attestation students are given holidays on their application within the term of the programme, and are expelled when the holidays end [15].
- Early expulsion at the student's initiative is made on a personal application, including where the student transfers to another organisation to continue the programme [16].
- Early expulsion at the University's initiative is possible [17]:
  - as a disciplinary sanction applied to a student who has reached the age of fifteen, for non-performance or breach of the Charter, the internal regulations, the rules of residence in the residential complex and other local acts on the organisation of educational activity [18];
  - for failure to perform the duty to diligently master the programme and fulfil the curriculum (for aspirants the individual curriculum) — for an academic debt not cleared in time, for not passing a state attestation test through absence without valid reason or through a grade «неудовлетворительно», and also as not having started classes after a transfer, restoration or the end of an academic leave [19];
  - where a violation of the admission procedure is established that led, through the student's fault, to unlawful enrolment [20];
  - for breach of the education contract (late payment for paid educational services) [21];
  - where the University cannot properly perform its obligations to provide paid educational services owing to the student's actions or inaction [22];
  - for an aspirant's dishonest performance of the duties to master the aspirantura programme (failure of the individual plan of scientific activity established during interim attestation) [23].
- Expulsion is also possible for circumstances beyond the will of the student or the parents (legal representatives) of a minor student and of the University, including the liquidation of the University [24].

### Expulsion at the student's initiative

- The expulsion order is issued by the director or another official authorised by him on the basis of the student's personal application within 7 working days from registration of the application; expulsion in connection with a transfer to another organisation follows the procedure of section 2 of the Regulation [25].
- The student must hand in the student card and a clearance sheet (обходной лист) signed by the University's employees to the responsible structural unit within 3 working days from the date of the application; if the clearance sheet is not handed in, the University may claim outstanding debts [26].
- Students studying under education contracts where the customer is another natural or legal person must present a document confirming the customer's consent to termination of the contract at the student's initiative [26].

### Expulsion at the University's initiative

- The responsible structural unit notifies the customer (where the student studied under a tripartite education contract) and the student through one of the available channels: post (written notice, telegram or telephone message), mobile phone (text and other messages, including through electronic services and messengers such as MAX), fax, the e-mail addresses in the education contract, University corporate e-mail, or other channels where the University has reliable information that the channel works [27].
- An automatic software notification of delivery of an electronic message is treated as an equivalent confirmation of receipt [28].
- The expulsion order is issued by the director or another official authorised by him within 14 calendar days from the day the student and (or) the customer confirm receipt of the notice [29].

### Expulsion as a disciplinary sanction

- Expulsion as a disciplinary sanction is not applied during the student's illness, holidays, academic leave, maternity leave or childcare leave [30].
- It is applied not later than one month from the day the offence is discovered, not counting the time of the student's absence mentioned above and the time needed to take into account the opinion of the student councils, representative bodies of students and councils of parents, but not more than 7 working days from the day the reasoned opinion in writing is presented to the director [31].
- Before applying it the University must request a written explanation from the student; if the explanation is not presented after 3 study days, a corresponding act is drawn up, and refusal or evasion is no obstacle to the sanction [32].
- The decision on the form of the disciplinary sanction, including expulsion, is taken by the director or another official authorised by him [33].
- Expulsion of a minor student who has reached fifteen as a disciplinary sanction is allowed for repeated commission of disciplinary offences, where other disciplinary and pedagogical measures have not produced a result and further presence of the student has a negative influence on other students, violates their rights and those of the University's employees, and the normal functioning of the University; it is not applied if the terms of earlier sanctions have expired or the sanctions have been lifted, and a sanction for an offence may not be applied later than 6 months from the commission of the offence [34].
- The University immediately informs the local self-government body that manages education of the expulsion of a minor student as a disciplinary sanction [35], and the expulsion may be appealed by the student in the procedure established by the legislation of the Russian Federation [36].

### Expulsion for failure to master the programme

- Such expulsion is made on the representation of the vice-rector — head of the department of academic policy and higher education or a person authorised by him, after the fact of non-performance of the duties is established [37].
- The fact of an academic debt that is a ground for expulsion is established by the results of the second repeat interim assessment; the procedure for repeat interim assessments is set by the local act on the forms, periodicity and procedure of ongoing control and interim assessment [38].
- The fact of a breach of academic discipline, including absence from classes after a transfer, restoration or the end of an academic leave, is established by recording the student's absence from classes on the basis of the teachers' internal memos [39].

### Consequences and documents

- Where an education contract has been concluded with the student or the parents of a minor student, the contract is terminated on the basis of the expulsion order; the student's rights and duties in the educational part cease from the date of expulsion, while other civil-law agreements continue until the parties perform their obligations [40].
- After the expulsion order the student is given, within the period set by the Regulation (в трехдневный срок), a certificate of study or of the period of study and the original document of education kept in the personal file; a copy certified by the University of the education document and a copy of the certificate remain in the file, and documents not collected are kept in the personal file [41].
- On the expulsion of a foreign citizen or stateless person the University notifies other organisations in the list, procedure and terms set by the corresponding local act, and the person leaves the Russian Federation in the procedure established by legislation [42].

# Entrance examination for the master's programme «Искусственный интеллект и инженерия данных» (5–6 August 2024) — published results

## What the document is

The page is the published protocol of the entrance examination for the master's programme «Искусственный интеллект и инженерия данных» (artificial intelligence and data engineering) conducted by Innopolis University on its own, under the heading «Результаты вступительного испытания проводимого АНО ВО "Университет Иннополис" самостоятельно» [1].

The document title gives the period 05.08.2024–06.08.2024, and the heading table of the examination names 05.08.2024 as the date on which it was held [1]. The table then gives, for each participant, the number, the СНИЛС/уникальный код, the score (оценка) and the same score written out in words (оценка прописью) [1].

Scores are published against an identifier rather than a name: most identifiers are 11-digit numbers, and some are short codes (for example [скрыто], [скрыто], [скрыто], [скрыто] and [скрыто]) [1].

## Results recorded

The protocol numbers the results from 1 to 31; in the order of the protocol the scores are 60, 44, 48, 40, 48, 52, 40, 44, 40, 48, 40, 52, 44, 48, 60, 48, 40, 44, 52, 60, 40, 48, 40, 60, 72, 44, 44, 40, 60, 44 and 60 [1].

The highest score in the protocol is 72 and the lowest is 40 [1]. The scores written out in words are «Шестьдесят», «Сорок четыре», «Сорок восемь», «Сорок», «Пятьдесят два» and «Семьдесят два», matching the figures [1].

## What the page does not say

The page states no pass mark, no minimum score and no decision on admission [1].

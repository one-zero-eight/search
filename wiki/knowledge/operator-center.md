# Operator Center (Операторский центр) — place, tasks, functions and rights

## The document

The Regulation on the Operator Center of ANO VO «Университет Иннополис» was approved by order of 22 September 2025 № ОД/АД‐02/2025/09/22 МНРЕГ, signed by the Director for Development and HR Policy R.F. Valiev, and is dated Innopolis 2025 [1].

The Regulation regulates the activity of the University's Operator Center and determines its tasks, functions, rights and duties [2].

## Place in the University

- The Operator Center (also «Центр») is a structural subdivision of the University and belongs to the category «иные структурные подразделения» [3].
- It reports directly to the Deputy Director for Project Activity [4].
- In its work the Center is guided by the Constitution of the Russian Federation, the Labour Code of the Russian Federation, Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29 December 2012 «Об образовании в Российской Федерации», other legislative and normative legal acts of the Russian Federation, the University's Charter, decisions of the Supervisory and Operations Councils, orders and instructions of the Director, other local normative acts of the University and this Regulation [5].
- The structure, the number of staff and the staffing table of the Center, and changes to them, are approved by the Director of the University [6].
- The Center is headed by the head of the Operator Center, who is hired and released from the position by order of the Director [7].
- The working conditions of the Center's employees are determined by the employment contracts concluded with each employee and by the University's internal labour regulations [8].
- The rights, duties and degree of responsibility of the Center's employees are established by job descriptions [9].
- Employees of the Center may be involved, within their competences, in work under grants, projects, subsidies and other programmes run by the University [10].
- The Regulation and the additions and changes to it are approved by the Director of the University or by another person authorised by the Director [11].

## Tasks

The tasks of the Center are:

- organising interaction with the Ministry of Industry and Trade of the Russian Federation (the Ministry) so as to secure the achievement of the result values of the agreement of 10.04.2024 № 020-10-2024-007 on the provision of subsidies from the federal budget, including grants in the form of subsidies, to legal entities, individual entrepreneurs and individuals (the Agreement) [12];
- monitoring the proper achievement of the values of the results of the subsidy (grant) provision and of the control points set by the Agreement and by the programmes of the centres for the development of industrial robotics [13];
- organisational, technical and information support for the competitive selection of recipients of grants for state support for creating centres for the development of industrial robotics (the competitive selection), and support and monitoring of the activity of the centres already created [14];
- ensuring the conclusion of agreements on the provision of grants for state support with the winners of the competitive selections, and of supplementary agreements to such agreements [15];
- organising interaction with the expert council formed by the Ministry to ensure the effective implementation of the programmes of the centres for the development of industrial robotics [16].

## Functions

The Center carries out the following functions:

- coordination of measures for achieving the values of the results of the subsidy (grant) provision, control over compliance with their stages and deadlines, and assessment of risks in the implementation of the programmes of the centres for the development of industrial robotics [17];
- interaction with the Ministry in the established procedure, including sending proposals to amend the Agreement, obtaining explanations in connection with its execution, preparing analytical and reference materials and the reporting forms submitted in the framework of creating and developing the centres for the development of industrial robotics, and preparing drafts of official appeals to the Ministry in the framework of executing control assignments under the Agreement [18];
- supporting the competitive selection of recipients of grants for state support for creating centres for the development of industrial robotics, including control over the stages of the competitive procedures [19];
- organising the expert review of the applications of the participants of the competitive selection [20];
- organising the conclusion of agreements on the provision of grants for state support with the winners of the competitive selections [21];
- organising support and monitoring of the activity of the centres for the development of industrial robotics [22];
- preparing reports, papers, theses, presentations and other reference and analytical materials on the progress of the Agreement and at the Ministry's request [23];
- control over the elimination of the fact or facts of violation of the conditions set for the provision of the subsidy (grant) within the deadlines determined in the Ministry's demand [24].

## Rights

In carrying out its activity the employees of the Center have the right:

- to request from the University's structural subdivisions the information and materials needed for the work within the Center's competences [25];
- to involve, with the consent of the heads of the University's structural subdivisions, the employees of those subdivisions in preparing drafts of local acts and other documents and in developing and carrying out measures conducted by the Center [26];
- to take part in meetings held by the University's management when issues whose resolution falls within the Center's competences are discussed [27];
- to submit to the University's management proposals on the repeal of, and on amendments and additions to, the University's local acts [28];
- to interact with state authorities and administration and with legal entities, and with the University's subdivisions, on matters within the Center's competences [29].

## Interaction with other subdivisions and reporting

- The Center's interaction with the University's other structural subdivisions is determined by the tasks and functions assigned to it by this Regulation [30].
- Interaction with the financial-economic department takes place on matters of accounting for material values, writing off and transferring funds under contracts [31].
- Interaction with the other structural subdivisions takes place in the course of preparing drafts of local acts, orders, instructions and contracts and of preparing conclusions and written and oral references on matters within the Center's competence [32].
- On the basis of the relevant local acts, orders and instructions the Center is responsible, within its activity, for preparing and keeping up to date the information, references and reports in the framework of conducting the competitive selections [33].
- At the Ministry's relevant request the Center provides information on the progress of the Agreement as regards the results, indicators and control points for which the University is the executor [34].

# Admission of foreign citizens and stateless persons to PhD aspirantura (2026/2027 rules)

## Where this comes from

Section X of the Rules of admission to aspirantura programmes of ANO VO «Innopolis University» for the 2026/2027 academic year, approved by order No. ОД/УД-01/2026/01/19 of 19 January 2026 [1]. The page is a scanned document read by OCR, and the wiki warns that its numbers and dates should be checked against the PDF before relying on them [1].

## The right to study and the ways of financing it

Foreign citizens and stateless persons have the right to receive higher education at the expense of the budget allocations of the Russian Federation in accordance with international treaties of the Russian Federation, federal laws or the quota for the education of foreign citizens and stateless persons established by the Government of the Russian Federation, and also at the expense of the funds of natural persons and legal entities under contracts on education [1]. Admission within the quota for the education of foreign citizens is carried out in accordance with the directions of the federal executive body that elaborates state policy and normative-legal regulation in the sphere of higher education, and enrollment within that quota is formalised by a separate order (orders) of the University [1].

## Documents

- Foreign citizens who enter on the basis of international treaties submit, in addition to the documents listed in clause 25 of the rules, originals or copies of documents confirming that they belong to the persons named in the corresponding international treaties [1].
- Foreign citizens and stateless persons who are compatriots living abroad submit, in addition to the documents listed in clause 25, originals or copies of the documents provided by article 17 of Federal Law No. 99-ФЗ of 24 May 1999 «On the state policy of the Russian Federation in relation to compatriots abroad» [1].
- When submitting documents, a foreign citizen or stateless person submits, in accordance with sub-clause 1 of clause 25 of the rules, the original or a copy of the document proving identity and citizenship or, respectively, the document proving the identity of a foreign citizen in the Russian Federation or the identity of a stateless person in the Russian Federation in accordance with article 10 of Federal Law No. 115-ФЗ of 25 July 2002 «On the legal position of foreign citizens in the Russian Federation» [1].

A document of education obtained abroad is submitted together with a certificate of recognition of foreign education, and documents in a foreign language need a notarised translation into Russian; documents obtained abroad must be legalised unless an international treaty or Russian law provides otherwise [2], [3], [4]. Among the grounds of education confirming access to aspirantura a foreign document of education recognised in Russia is named [5].

## Programmes containing state secrets

Admission of foreign citizens and stateless persons to study under aspirantura programmes containing information constituting a state secret is carried out within the quota for the education of foreign citizens in compliance with the requirements provided by the legislation of the Russian Federation on state secrets [1].

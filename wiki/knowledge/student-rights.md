# Rights of students under the Rules of internal order for students

## The document and whom it covers

The Rules of internal order for students (Правила внутреннего распорядка обучающихся) are a local normative act regulating the rights and duties of students, academic discipline and conduct in the University, the conditions needed for students to keep discipline, the organisation of classes on the programmes delivered, rewards for success in study, liability for breaches of academic discipline and other questions of the educational process [1]. They were drawn up on the basis of Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29 December 2012 «Об образовании в Российской Федерации», other legal acts of Russia, the University's charter and its other local acts [1]. They were approved by the director of the University, K.V. Semenikhin [2].

The Rules apply to all persons studying at the University who are direct participants in educational relations and have the rights and duties established by Russian legislation; in the part not concerning rights and duties connected with the study process they also apply to participants of all events organised or co-organised by the University and held on its territory [3].

For the purposes of the Rules, learners (обучающиеся) are [4]:

- students — persons mastering bachelor's programmes and master's programmes [4];
- aspirants — persons studying in aspirantura on programmes of training of scientific and scientific-pedagogical staff [4];
- listeners — persons mastering additional professional programmes [4];
- pupils — persons mastering programmes of primary general, basic general and secondary general education and additional general education programmes [4];
- externs — persons enrolled at the University for accredited programmes in order to pass interim and state final attestation [4].

Learners enjoy equal rights and bear equal duties as regards receiving educational services, unless Russian legislation provides otherwise [4].

Students are made acquainted with the Rules against signature when they submit an application for admission to study [5]. The Rules enter into force on the moment of their approval by the director and are valid without a time limit until amendments are made or new rules are approved [6]. Compliance with them, including discipline in classes and conduct in the University, is controlled by the teaching and managerial staff and by other persons charged with those duties [7].

To inform students about the charter documents, study documentation and other documents governing educational activity, the University posts them on its official website (https://innopolis.university/) and lets students read them, and the changes and amendments to them, on their own through student information channels — for example Telegram mailings or the Outlook corporate mail [8].

## Rights in study and assessment

A student of the University has the right [9]:

- to conditions for learning that take account of the peculiarities of their psycho-physical development and state of health, to social-pedagogical and psychological help and to free psycho-medico-pedagogical correction [10];
- to study under an individual curriculum, including accelerated study, within the programme being mastered, in the order set by the University's local acts [11];
- to choose facultative (optional for the given level of education, field of study or scientific specialty) and elective (compulsorily chosen) subjects, courses, disciplines (modules) from the list offered by the University, after receiving basic general education [12];
- to master, alongside the subjects of their own programme, any other subjects, courses, disciplines (modules) taught at the University, and subjects taught in other organisations carrying out educational activity, to master several main professional programmes at the same time and to obtain one or several qualifications [13];
- to combine education with work without damage to mastering the programme and carrying out the individual curriculum [14];
- to have the University credit the results of mastering subjects, courses, disciplines (modules), practice and additional educational programmes in other organisations, in the order set by the federal executive body for higher education together with the one for general education [15];
- to take part in forming the content of their professional education, subject to the federal state educational standards of higher education and the federal requirements for aspirantura programmes; this right may be limited by the conditions of a contract on targeted training [16];
- to vacations — planned breaks in education for rest and other social purposes in accordance with education legislation and the calendar study schedule [17];
- to academic leave on the grounds and in the order set by the federal executive body for higher education, and to maternity leave and childcare leave until the child reaches the age of three in the order set by federal laws [18];
- to transfer for education to another field of study or scientific specialty, to another form of study at the University, in the order set by education legislation and the University's local acts [19];
- to transfer to another educational organisation implementing a programme of the corresponding level, in the order set for the level of the programmes concerned [20];
- to reinstatement for education at the University in the order set by education legislation and the University's local acts [21];
- to read the certificate of state registration, the charter, the date of issue and registration number of the licence for educational activity, the date of issue and registration number of state accreditation for the programmes delivered, the study documentation and other documents governing the organisation and carrying out of educational activity [22];
- to appeal the University's acts in the order established by Russian legislation [23].

## Rights to the University's facilities, research and support

- To use the library and information resources and the University's study, production and research base free of charge [24].
- To use the University's health-improvement infrastructure, cultural facilities and sports facilities in the order set by its local acts and Russian legislation [25].
- To develop creative abilities and interests, including participation in contests, olympiads, exhibitions, reviews, physical-culture and sports events, including official sports competitions, and other mass events [26].
- To take part, in accordance with Russian legislation, in the research, scientific-technical, experimental and innovation activity carried out by the University, under the guidance of teaching staff of higher education organisations and (or) researchers of research organisations [27].
- To obtain referrals for study and research on chosen topics, for internships, including academic exchange to other educational and research organisations, including higher education organisations and research organisations of foreign states, in the order established at the University [28].
- To publish the results of the research done in the University's editions, and to publish one's own works in the University's editions free of charge after receiving the corresponding approval [29].
- To receive rewards for success in study, physical culture, sport, social, research, scientific-technical, creative, experimental and innovation activity [30].
- To receive from the University information on the employment situation in Russia and in Innopolis in the fields of study and scientific specialties being mastered [31].
- To take part in public associations, including trade unions created under Russian legislation, and to create students' public associations in the order of Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ [32].
- To use the residential complex and medical service in the order set by Russian legislation and the University's local acts [33].
- To receive scholarships and other forms of material support in the established order, in accordance with Russian legislation and the University's local acts [34].
- To be provided with food in the cases and in the order set by federal laws and the laws of the regions of Russia [35].
- To other academic rights provided by Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ, other legal acts of Russia and the University's local acts [36].
- To transfer from paid education to free education in the cases and in the order set by the federal executive body for higher education [37].

## Rights of dignity, opinion and participation

- To respect of human dignity, protection from all forms of physical and mental violence and insults of the person, and protection of life and health [38].
- To freedom of conscience, information and free expression of one's own views and convictions [39].
- To take part in managing the University in the order set by its charter [40].
- To attend, by one's own choice, events held at the University that are not provided by the curriculum, in the order set by the University's local acts; involving students in work not provided by the educational programme is done according to labour legislation [41].
- To attend, with the written consent of the University's leadership, any additional types of classes, subjects, courses, disciplines (modules) taught at the University [42].
- To use, in agreement with the University's leadership, rooms free from classroom classes for independent work [43].

## Military service deferral, externs and student clubs

Students studying full-time have the right to a deferral from conscription for military service under Federal Law No. 53-ФЗ of 28 March 1998 «О воинской обязанности и военной службе» [44].

Persons mastering a main educational programme through self-education or family education, or having studied under a programme without state accreditation, may pass interim and state final attestation as externs at the University on the corresponding programme that has state accreditation; during the attestation externs enjoy the academic rights of students of that programme [45].

Students mastering main educational programmes of secondary general and higher education have the right to create student clubs — public associations of students whose aim is to organise the temporary employment of students who wish to work in their free time in various sectors of the economy [46].

## If the University stops operating or loses accreditation

If the University's activity ceases, its licence for aspirantura programmes is suspended or annulled, it is deprived of state accreditation for a programme concerned, or the state accreditation stops, the founder and (or) the management body authorised by it ensure the transfer of adult students with their consent, and of minor students with the consent of their parents (legal representatives), to other organisations carrying out educational activity on programmes of the corresponding level and orientation [47]. If the licence is suspended for main general education, bachelor's or master's programmes, the founder and (or) the authorised management body ensure the transfer of adult students on their applications, and of minor students on their parents' (legal representatives') applications, to other organisations carrying out accredited main educational programmes of the corresponding level and orientation [47]. The order and conditions of such transfer, depending on the level of the programmes, are set by the federal executive body for general education or for higher education [47].

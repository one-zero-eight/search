# Interim attestation with distance technologies in PhD aspirantura — the Порядок of 30 April 2020

## The order and the document

The Порядок проведения промежуточной аттестации с применением дистанционных образовательных технологий по образовательным программам высшего образования — программам подготовки научно-педагогических кадров в аспирантуре was approved by order of the University of 30 April 2020 № ОД/УД-03/2020/04/30, signed by the vice-rector — head of the management of academic policy and organisation of educational activity S.V. Masyagin, and issued under the requirements of Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29 December 2012 «Об образовании в Российской Федерации» [1].

The same order approves a second Порядок — on conducting the state final attestation with distance educational technologies on aspirantura programmes — and puts control over the execution of the order on the head of the department for the organisation of programmes of training highly qualified staff T.S. Kamaletdinova [1]. The page carries the text of the Порядок on interim attestation; the text of the Порядок on state final attestation is not reproduced on it [1].

The page is OCR of a scanned document, so its figures and dates should be checked against the PDF before relying on them [1].

## What the Порядок rests on

The Порядок was developed in accordance with [1]:

- order of the Ministry of Education and Science of the Russian Federation of 19.11.2013 № 1259 on the organisation and carrying out of educational activity on aspirantura (adjunktura) programmes [1];
- order of the Ministry of Education and Science of the Russian Federation of 23.08.2017 № 816 on the use of e-learning and distance educational technologies by organisations carrying out educational activity [1];
- the University's Положение on ongoing assessment and interim attestation of students on programmes of training highly qualified staff in aspirantura, approved by order of 04.06.2018 № ОД/УДд-04/2018/06/04 [1];
- the University's Положение on the use of e-learning and distance educational technologies on bachelor's, master's and aspirantura programmes, approved by order of 14.01.2020 № ОД/АД-01/2020/01/14 [1].

## Forms of the assessment and who decides

- Interim attestation is held in the form determined by the curriculum of the programme, the discipline (module) work programme, the practice programme and the research programme; its forms are the зачёт and the экзамен (including the кандидатский экзамен) [2].
- The kind of the assessment is determined by the teacher responsible for the discipline (module) and for the supervision of practice and research, and is fixed in the discipline work programme (including the candidate examination programme), the practice programmes and the research programmes [3].
- With distance educational technologies the assessment may be held as testing (online/offline), an oral survey (online/offline), written work (online/offline), defence of a referat (online/offline), defence of a project (online/offline), or other forms provided by the work programme (including the candidate examination programme), the practice programme and the research programme (online/offline) [3].
- The teacher responsible for the discipline (module) and for the supervision of practice and research is responsible for preparing and conducting the assessment; staff of the department for the organisation of programmes of training highly qualified staff and of the IT department may give organisational, methodological and technical support [4].
- The candidate examination is held under points 2.1–2.6 with all the particularities set by the Regulation on candidate examinations and on attachment for taking candidate examinations, including its conduct by an examination commission; its results are recorded as in point 2.7 and are also reflected in the protocol of the examination commission [5].

## Equipment and connection

For an assessment with distance technologies a student needs computing equipment (a desktop computer, laptop or tablet), communication equipment providing access to the software and information of the University's electronic information-educational environment (ЭИОС) through local networks and the Internet, and the corresponding software [6].

For a video conference the following is recommended [7]:

- a web camera with resolution not lower than standard, 640×480 (0.3 MP) [7];
- a monitor/screen with resolution not lower than 1280×1024 [7];
- a microphone with speakers or a computer headset [7];
- configured video conferencing software (Microsoft Teams, Zoom or a similar one allowing remote audio-visual contact) [7];
- Internet connection speed not lower than 2 MBps (or 1024 Kbps per workplace) [7];
- the Google Chrome browser, version not lower than 81.0 [7].

For a proctoring system the following is recommended [8]:

- the Google Chrome browser, version not lower than 72 [8];
- an operating system not lower than Windows 7, macOS 10.12 «Sierra», Linux [8];
- a web camera with resolution not lower than standard, 640×480 (0.3 MP) [8];
- a microphone with speakers or a computer headset for communication with the proctor [8];
- Internet connection speed not less than 512 KBps [8];
- a browser plugin where necessary [8].

For online testing the following is recommended: the Google Chrome browser version not lower than 72, an operating system not lower than Windows 7, macOS 10.12 «Sierra» or Linux, and Internet connection speed not less than 512 KBps [9].

For any other form chosen by the teacher the requirements and recommendations on hardware and software must be brought to the students' notice not later than 3 working days before the day of the assessment, by sending them to the students' corporate e-mail addresses and/or through other information-communication technologies [10].

## The room and the workplace

During the assessment the room in which the student is and the workplace must meet these requirements [11]:

- the room must have walls and a closed door [11];
- no outside persons may be in the room during the assessment test [11];
- additional computers and other monitors must be switched off [11];
- the working surface of the desk with the student's computer must be free of all objects, including palmtop computers or other computer devices, watches, notebooks, books, notepads, sticky notes, notes or papers with printed text [11];
- the web camera must not be placed opposite a light source and must provide a view of the room within a radius of 2–3 metres from the student [11];
- documents identifying the student's personality, clean sheets of paper and a pen are allowed on the desk [11].

The teacher has the right to ask the student to show the room and the workplace for compliance with these requirements; in case of refusal or non-compliance the teacher may enter in the sheet the mark «О (неудовлетворительно)» or «Е (не зачтено)» [11].

## How the assessment is taken

### By video conference

The student must [12]:

- log in to the personal account in the University's ЭИОС not later than 5 minutes before the start of the assessment test, passing the ordinary identification (personal identifier and password), unless the teacher has agreed otherwise in advance [12];
- test the web camera and microphone [12];
- connect to the video conference by the link placed in the personal account [12];
- act according to the instructions of the teacher organising the video conference [12];
- keep the microphone switched off when not speaking [12];
- observe the ethics of business communication [12];
- switch off other programs using the web camera on the computer [12];
- not record the materials and content of the assessment in any way and not pass them to third parties [12];
- not use audio, visual or any other hints, unless the teacher has agreed otherwise [12].

The teacher may record the video conference and may check compliance with the requirements of the assessment procedure; a violation of the procedure during the video conference session is a ground for entering in the sheet the mark «О (неудовлетворительно)» or «Е (не зачтено)» and for disciplinary measures against the student [12].

### With a proctoring system

The student must [13]:

- read the instructions for using the system not later than 1 day before the assessment [13];
- test the web camera and microphone [13];
- read the rules and give consent to the conditions of the assessment [13];
- place the web camera strictly in front of the face (a camera placed to the side is not allowed); the head must fit fully in the frame, the face must be in the centre of the camera's field of view and occupy not less than 10% of the space captured by the camera throughout the session, be evenly lit, with no light source directed into the camera or lighting only one half of the face, and not covered by hair, clothing, hands or anything else [13];
- not wear sunglasses; only glasses with clear lenses may be used [13];
- ensure no outside persons are in the room [13];
- perform the actions necessary for identification in the system under the requirements set for the particular proctoring system [13];
- connect a mobile camera where necessary and show the space around and the workplace with it [13];
- not leave the workplace during the assessment [13];
- keep voices and noise out of the background — silence is ideal [13];
- take the assessment in a browser window expanded to full screen, without switching to other applications (including other browsers), minimising the browser or opening third-party tabs [13];
- switch off all programs using the web camera except the browser [13];
- not record the materials and content of the assessment in any way in order to pass them to third parties [13];
- not use audio, visual or any other hints [13];
- not try to deceive the system or the proctor [13].

At the teacher's discretion a video recording of the assessment test may be made [13]. Detection of an attempt at deception is a ground for entering in the sheet the mark «О (неудовлетворительно)» or «Е (не зачтено)» and for disciplinary measures against the student [13].

### By online testing

The student must log in to the personal account in the University's ЭИОС not later than 5 minutes before the start of the assessment test, unless the teacher has agreed otherwise in advance, and go by the link to the online testing resource [14]. At the teacher's discretion the student may be allowed during the online testing to use study programmes, reference books and other sources of information, the list of which is set by the teacher in advance [14]. Use of materials not on the teacher's list and violation of the requirements of academic discipline (cheating, an attempt to communicate with other persons etc.) are a ground for entering in the sheet the mark «О (неудовлетворительно)» or «Е (не зачтено)» and for disciplinary measures [14]. The student is forbidden to record the materials and content of the assessment in any way and to pass them to third parties [14].

### In another form

For an assessment in another form chosen by the teacher, the order of the student's actions, including the order of identification, must be brought to the students' notice not later than 3 working days before the day of the assessment, by sending it to the students' corporate e-mail addresses and/or through other information-communication technologies [15].

## Recording the results

The results of interim attestation in a discipline (module), practice and research are recorded in the University's ЭИОС (the Moodle educational content management system, the «1С: Университет» educational process management system, the student's portfolio, the student's personal account my.university.innopolis.ru) not later than 1 working day after the assessment [16]. The results may be recorded in one of the following ways [16]:

- the teacher enters the points and marks in Moodle, after which the results are transferred into the interim attestation sheet [16];
- the teacher enters the results in the interim attestation sheet and submits it to the subdivision implementing the programme, whose staff transfer the results from the sheet into Moodle [16].

## Academic debt and repeat attestation

Unsatisfactory results of interim attestation in one or several disciplines (modules), practice and research, or failure to pass interim attestation without valid reasons, are recognised as academic debt, and students must eliminate it [17].

The University sets for students with academic debt the deadlines of the repeat interim attestation for each discipline (module), practice and research [18]. A student who has not eliminated the debt at the first repeat interim attestation is given the opportunity of a second repeat interim attestation held by a commission created by the University [18].

- The first repeat is held by the same teacher to whom the student took the examination (pass) the first time; if that teacher cannot take it at the appointed time, the head of the department for the organisation of programmes of training highly qualified staff appoints another teacher [19].
- The first repeat is held under points 2.4–2.6 [20].
- For the second repeat a commission is created, its composition approved by a распоряжение of the vice-rector — head of the management of academic policy and organisation of educational activity [21].
- The second repeat is held by video conference with the necessary number of broadcast users, under points 2.4.1, 2.5 and 2.6.1 [22].
- If a connection problem arises and is not eliminated within 20 minutes, the student is given the opportunity to take the second repeat on another day, which is communicated by telephone and e-mail [22].
- The result of the second repeat is given by agreement of all the commission members; during the commission's deliberations the student may be disconnected from the video conference [22].
- The results of the first and of the second repeat are recorded as in point 2.8 [23].
- Students who have not eliminated their academic debt within the established deadlines are expelled from the University as not having fulfilled their duty to master the programme in good faith and to carry out the curriculum [24].

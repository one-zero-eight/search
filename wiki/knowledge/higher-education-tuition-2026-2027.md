# Higher education tuition cost for 2026/2027 (first and later courses)

## What the order sets

The order of 14 May 2026 № ОД/УД-01/2026/05/14 (г. Иннополис) «Об утверждении стоимости обучения по образовательным программам АНО ВО "Университет Иннополис" на 2026/2027 учебный год» approves the tuition cost for the university's higher education programmes for 2026/2027 [1]. It was issued in accordance with Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29 December 2012 «Об образовании в Российской Федерации», the Rules for the provision of paid educational services approved by Government Resolution No. 1441 of 15 September 2020, on the basis of a decision of the Operational Council of the university of 12.02.2026 № П/1/2026, and taking into account the features of implementing educational programmes by types of training and forms of study [1].

Point 1 sets the cost of study on the first course of bachelor programmes, master programmes and programmes for the training of scientific and scientific-pedagogical personnel in aspirantura implemented in 2026/2027, for citizens of the Russian Federation, foreign citizens and stateless persons studying at places under contracts for the provision of paid educational services, according to Annex 1 [2]. Point 2 sets the cost on the second and subsequent courses of the same kinds of programmes for the same categories of students, according to Annex 2 [3]. Control over execution of the order is assigned to the vice-rector — head of the management of academic policy and higher education E.S. Bobrov, and the order is signed by director D.V. Vandyukov [4].

Annex 1 is headed «Стоимость образовательных услуг при приеме на первый курс на 2026/2027 учебный год»; its columns are the direction of training / group of scientific specialties, the educational programme / scientific specialty, the form of study, the course and the cost of study per academic year in rubles, and every row is full-time (очная) with course 1 [1]. ## Where the two annexes differ

Some amounts and groupings are not the same in the two annexes of the same order:

- «Робототехника и компьютерное зрение» is listed under 15.04.06 Мехатроника и робототехника in Annex 1 but under 09.04.01 Информатика и вычислительная техника in Annex 2, at 800 000 in both [1].
- «Кибербезопасность» appears once in Annex 1 (09.04.01, 390 000) but twice in Annex 2: under 09.04.01 at 390 000 and under 10.04.01 Информационная безопасность at 800 000, both for course 2 [1].
- For «Управление на основе данных» and «Управление цифровым продуктом» the amount falls from 450 000 on the first course to 390 000 on the second course, while for «Информационное технологическое предпринимательство» it rises from 450 000 on the first course to 800 000 on the second course [1].
- Course numbers are given only in Annex 2; Annex 1 shows course 1 for every row [1].

# Master's programme «Управление разработкой ПО» (software development management) — place, tasks, functions and rights

## The document and its subject

The page is the Regulation (Положение) on the master's programme «Управление разработкой ПО» of ANO VO «Innopolis University»; it regulates the activity of the programme and defines its tasks, functions, rights and duties [1].

The text is written on the template of a subdivision regulation: it calls the master's programme «Отдел» (a department) and speaks of «Факультет», of «institutes of the faculty» and of the faculty's structural subdivisions [1]. The section on interaction with other subdivisions ends with a further, different paragraph under the same clause number [2].

In its activity the programme is guided by the Constitution of the Russian Federation, the Labour Code of the Russian Federation, Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29.12.2012 «Об образовании в Российской Федерации», other legislative and normative legal acts of the Russian Federation regulating the activity of higher education institutions, the University's Charter, decisions of the Academic, Supervisory and Operational councils of the University, orders and instructions of the director of the University, other local normative acts of the University and this Regulation [3].

The Institute of Software Development and Software Engineering's own Regulation lists the master's programme «Управление разработкой ПО» among the Institute's structural subdivisions, alongside its laboratories [4].

## Management, staff and approval

- The structure, number of staff and staffing table of the programme, and changes to them, are approved by the director of the University [5].
- The programme is headed by its head, who is hired and dismissed by order of the director of the University [6].
- The working conditions of the employees are determined by the employment contracts concluded with each employee, by the University's internal labour regulations and by other local normative acts [7].
- The rights, duties and degree of responsibility of the employees are established by job descriptions [8].
- Employees may be involved, within their competence, in work on grants, projects, subsidies and other programmes carried out by the University [9].
- The Regulation and additions and changes to it are approved by the director of the University or by another person authorised by him; the page gives no date of approval and no number of an approving order [10].

## Tasks

- recruitment of students [11]
- multi-level training of students (training of bachelors, masters and scientific-pedagogical cadres of higher qualification) [12]
- integration of education, science and production by using the results of scientific research in the educational process and by establishing mutually beneficial links between educational, scientific, experimental-production and research-production institutions, enterprises and innovation organisations as a single collective system for obtaining and using new scientific knowledge and technologies in education, the economy and the social sphere [13]
- raising the professional level of academic and research-pedagogical workers through scientific research, the delivery of qualification improvement programmes and creative activity, and using the scientific results obtained in the educational process [14]
- organising, coordinating and controlling the academic, methodological and scientific work of the units that form part of the institutes of the faculty [15]

## Functions

- planning and organising the educational process at the faculty in accordance with the approved curricula [16]
- organising and controlling the development of curricula and teaching-methodical complexes in accordance with the requirements of the federal state educational standards [17]
- forming the staffs of academic, research and educational-support personnel and selecting the heads of the faculty's structural subdivisions [18]
- general management and control of students' academic and industrial practice [19]
- control over the qualification improvement of the academic staff [20]
- other functions provided by the legislation of the Russian Federation, the University's Charter and other local normative acts of the University [21]

## Rights

- to request from the University's structural subdivisions the information and materials needed for the work within the programme's competence [22]
- with the consent of the heads of the University's structural subdivisions, to involve the employees of those subdivisions in preparing drafts of local acts and other documents and in developing and carrying out measures conducted by the programme [23]
- to take part in the meetings held by the University's management when questions whose solution falls within the programme's competence are discussed [24]
- to put before the University's management proposals to cancel, amend or supplement the University's local acts [25]
- to interact with bodies of state authority and administration, legal entities and the University's subdivisions on questions of its competence [26]

## Interaction with other subdivisions

- Interaction with the University's other structural subdivisions is determined by the tasks and functions assigned to the programme by the Regulation [27].
- The programme interacts with public organisations in order to organise its work as effectively as possible, to raise the level of the faculty's and the University's public events, to help those in need and to defend the interests of employees and students [28].
- It interacts with the University's Academic council, the University's scientific-methodological council and the department for organisation of educational programmes on all questions of academic, research and educational work and of the competitive filling of academic staff positions [2].
- Interaction with other subdivisions takes place on preparing drafts of local acts, orders, instructions, contracts and other documents and on preparing conclusions and written or oral information notes on questions within the programme's competence [2].

## Reporting

- Within its activity the programme is responsible for preparing and keeping up to date information on the number of students, graduates and academic staff, on the curricula and on the reports on students' academic and industrial practice [29].
- It is responsible for providing statistical and other information established by legislation to the authorised bodies [30].
- On the basis of the relevant local acts, orders and instructions it may be responsible, within its activity, for preparing and keeping up to date information, information notes and reports [31].

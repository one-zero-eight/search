# Center of Educational Content — place, tasks, functions and rights

## The document and the Centre's place in the structure

The Regulation on the Center of Educational Content (Положение о Центре образовательного контента) was approved by order of 4 August 2025 № ОД/АД-02/2025/08/04, signed by the Director for Development and HR Policy R.F. Valiev [1]. The Regulation regulates the activity of the Center of Educational Content of ANO VO «Innopolis University» and determines its tasks, functions, rights and duties [2].

The Centre is a structural subdivision of direct subordination to the Deputy Director of the University, the head of the Department for the Organisation and Support of Project and Educational Activity (управление по организации и сопровождению проектно-образовательной деятельности) [3]; it reports directly to that Deputy Director [4].

In its activity the Centre follows the Constitution of the Russian Federation, the Labour Code of the Russian Federation, Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29 December 2012 «Об образовании в Российской Федерации» and other legislative and normative legal acts regulating the activity of higher education institutions, the University's Charter, decisions of the Academic, Supervisory and Operational councils, orders and instructions of the University's director, other local normative acts and this Regulation [5].

- The structure, the number of staff and the staffing table of the Centre, and changes to them, are approved by the director of the University [6].
- The Centre is headed by the head of the Center of Educational Content, hired and dismissed by order of the director of the University [7].
- The working conditions of the Centre's employees are determined by employment contracts concluded with each employee and by the University's internal labour regulations [8].
- The rights, duties and degree of responsibility of the Centre's employees are established by job descriptions [9].
- Employees of the Centre may, within their competences, be involved in work under grants, projects, subsidies and other programmes implemented by the University [10].
- The Regulation and additions and changes to it are approved by order of the director of the University or of another person authorised by him [11].

## Tasks

- Supporting the measures implementing the project «Моя школа», whose purpose is to provide access to electronic educational content and digital educational services from external providers for students and teaching staff of educational organisations (the Project) [12]:
  - achieving the result (performance of the measure) of the federal project «Цифровые платформы в отраслях социальной сферы» of the national project «Экономика данных и цифровая трансформация государства» of the state programme «Информационное общество» [13];
  - financial provision of the costs of the subsidy recipient (ANO VO «Innopolis University») connected with rendering services of providing access to electronic educational content and digital educational services from external providers for students and teaching staff of educational organisations [14].
- Observing the terms of the agreement between the Ministry of Digital Development, Communications and Mass Media of the Russian Federation and ANO VO «Innopolis University» on the provision from the federal budget of subsidies, including grants in the form of subsidies, to legal entities, individual entrepreneurs and natural persons for the implementation of the Project [15].

## Functions

- organising interaction with the Ministry of Digital Development, Communications and Mass Media of the Russian Federation, the Ministry of Education of the Russian Federation and their subordinate organisations within the implementation of the Project [16]
- organising interaction with local and regional education authorities and with educational organisations in the regions of the Russian Federation [17]
- organising travelling events to attract educational organisations, teachers and pupils in the regions to participation in the Project [18]
- carrying out comprehensive work to involve educational institutions of the Russian Federation in the Project [19]
- organising interaction with providers of electronic educational resources (ЭОР) [20]
- monitoring the information resources of ЭОР providers as regards tariff plans, availability of content and other information connected with providing access to ЭОР [21]
- analysing the consumption of ЭОР by users on the basis of statistical data from organisations subordinate to the Ministry of Digital Development, Communications and Mass Media [22]
- keeping the records (документооборот) of the Centre's and the Project's activity [23]
- forming and checking reporting and financial documents as regards the volumes of ЭОР consumed by users for the reporting period [24]
- collecting and analysing feedback from the Project's participants [25]
- developing the structure of information and methodological events and materials together with ЭОР providers [26]
- holding information and methodological events for educational organisations and teachers of the Russian Federation on the use of ЭОР in the educational process, together with ЭОР providers [27]
- preparing and forming reporting documents and analytical references on the activity of the Centre and of the Project [28]

## Rights

In carrying out their activity the Centre's employees have the right to:

- request from the University's structural subdivisions the information and materials needed for the work within the Centre's competence [29]
- with the consent of the heads of the University's structural subdivisions, involve the employees of those subdivisions in preparing draft local acts and other documents and in developing and carrying out measures conducted by the Centre [30]
- take part in meetings held by the University's management when discussing questions whose solution falls within the Centre's competence [31]
- put before the University's management proposals to cancel, amend or supplement the University's local acts [32]
- interact with bodies of state authority and administration, legal entities and the University's subdivisions on questions within the Centre's competence [33]

## Interaction with other subdivisions

The Centre's interaction with other structural subdivisions is determined by the tasks and functions assigned to it by the Regulation [34], and the Centre may be a base for the practical training of the University's students [35]. The Regulation names these counterparts and matters:

- the Department for Personnel Records and Movement — on the Centre's personnel questions [36]
- the Occupational Health and Fire Safety Service — on the safe organisation of the workplace of the Centre's employees [37]
- the Centre for Staff Development and Corporate Culture — on selecting new employees for the Centre, their adaptation and the development of their professional competences [38]
- the Marketing Department — on forming the marketing and PR policy and promoting the Project in the regions, and on working out a single media plan for promoting the Project [39]
- the Department of Documentation Support of Management Activity — on organising the Centre's document flow [40]
- the Department for Organising and Holding Events — on organising events connected with the Centre's activity [41]
- the Centre for Software Development — on the use of LMS systems in organising information and methodological events for educational organisations and teachers on the use of ЭОР in the educational process [42]
- the IT Department — on organising access to and work with the portal https://portal.university.innopolis.ru/, on technical support for work with the University's internal infrastructure and services, and on consultations on computer and server equipment [43]
- the Organisational and Technical Department — on organising workplaces for the Centre's employees [44]
- the Department for Organising Accommodation — on accommodating guests and the Centre's employees on the University's campus [45]
- the Department for Records and Movement of Material Valuables — on additionally equipping the workplaces of the Centre's employees [46]
- the Accounting Department — on payment of wages to the Centre's employees (including holiday pay, travel allowances and other payments), on paying ЭОР providers for the use of educational content within the Project, and on other interaction connected with paying invoices within the Centre's activity [47]
- the Procurement Department — on organising the selection of suppliers of digital educational content and on procurement of material valuables, equipment, services and other purchases within the Centre's activity on the Project [48]
- the Legal Department — on legal support of the Centre's activity on the Project [49]
- the Financial and Economic Department — on spending the subsidies from the Ministry of Digital Development, Communications and Mass Media [50]
- other structural subdivisions — on other questions of organising work on the Project and the Centre [51]

## Reporting

On the basis of the relevant local acts, orders and instructions, the Centre may, within its activity, be responsible for preparing information, certificates and reports and for keeping them up to date [52].

## The Methodological Department of the Center

An order of 1 September 2025 № ОД/АД-02/2025/09/01, signed by the Director for Development and HR Policy R.F. Valiev and issued in connection with the creation of a new structural subdivision in order to optimise the University's activity, approved the Regulation on the Methodological Department of the Center of Educational Content of ANO VO «Innopolis University» [53]. The head of that Department R.R. Gabidullova is to ensure timely acquaintance, against signature, with the approved Regulation of the Department's employees and of employees newly hired and transferred (moved) into it, and control over the execution of the order and of the Regulation is retained by the signatory [53]. The page contains only the order, not the text of the Regulation it approves [53].

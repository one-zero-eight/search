# Rewards and disciplinary measures for students

## The document

Sections 6 and 7 of the Rules of internal order for students (Правила внутреннего распорядка обучающихся) of the University, approved by its director, set out the rewards for learners and their liability for breaches of discipline [1]. The rules apply to students of bachelor's and master's programmes, aspirants, listeners of additional professional programmes, pupils and externs [2]. ## Rewards

For success in study, physical culture, sport, social, research, scientific-technical, creative, experimental and innovation activity, and for active participation in social activity, the following forms of moral and material reward are established for the University's learners [1]:

- announcement of gratitude (объявление благодарности) [1];
- awarding a valuable gift [1];
- appointment of a named scholarship (именная стипендия) [1].

The choice of the form of reward is made by the director of the University and the University's scholarship commission [1]. A reward is announced by order of the director of the University or of another official authorised by him and is brought to the students' notice; an extract from the order on the reward is kept in the student's personal file [4].

During the period a disciplinary penalty is in force, the rewards provided by the Rules are not applied to the student, and the director of the University or a person authorised by him may, on the proposal of the University's scholarship commission, decide to reduce the size of the University's scholarships until a scholarship is appointed on the results of the next interim assessment, or to deprive the student of a previously appointed University scholarship for a period recommended by the scholarship commission [5].

## Disciplinary measures

For non-performance or breach of the University's charter, these Rules — including the requirements on discipline in classes and the rules of conduct in the University — the rules of residence in the residential complex and other local acts on the organisation and carrying out of educational activity, the following disciplinary measures may be applied to students: a remark (замечание), a reprimand (выговор), expulsion (отчисление) [6]. The University chooses the concrete measure taking account of the requirements of legislation, and the measures may be applied to a student in any order [7].

When choosing the measure, the University takes into account the gravity of the disciplinary offence, the reasons and circumstances in which it was committed, the student's previous behaviour, their psycho-physical and emotional state, and the opinion of the students' council [8].

Measures are applied to students [9]:

- who committed the disciplinary offence directly [9];
- who organised and/or led the commission of the offence, including by creating and/or leading an informal association of students that breaches the requirements of the University's local acts [9];
- who induced other students to commit the offence [9];
- who assisted the commission of the offence [9];
- who used other persons not subject to disciplinary liability to commit the offence [9].

Offences such as drinking alcoholic drinks, using narcotic or toxic substances, inflicting grave bodily injuries, insulting the honour and dignity of the University's employees or other students, and committing or taking part in committing immoral acts, including attempts to cause physical or psychological harm to oneself or those around, are gross (грубые) and may lead to expulsion without a remark and/or reprimand being issued first [10].

## Procedure and time limits

Before applying a disciplinary measure the University must request a written explanation from the student, except in cases of expulsion for academic failure; if the explanation is not provided within 3 (three) study days, a corresponding act is drawn up in the established form [11]. Refusal or evasion by the student does not prevent the measure from being applied [12].

Measures are applied not later than 1 (one) month from the day the offence was discovered and 6 (six) months from the day it was committed, not counting the time of the student's illness and (or) being on vacation, on academic leave, on maternity leave or on childcare leave, and the time needed to take into account the opinion of the University's students' council, but not more than 7 (seven) study days from the day the reasoned written opinion of that council is presented to the director [13]. It is not allowed to apply measures to students during their illness, vacations, academic leave, maternity leave or childcare leave, or to students with health limitations (with mental retardation and various forms of mental deficiency) [13].

Expulsion of a minor student is applied if other disciplinary measures and measures of pedagogical influence have not produced a result and the student's further presence at the University has a negative influence on other students, breaches their rights and those of the University's employees, and the normal functioning of the University [14].

The application of a measure is formalised by an order (instruction) which is brought to the student's notice, and to the notice of the parents (legal representatives) of a minor student, against signature within 3 (three) study days from its issue, not counting the time the student is absent from the University; refusal to become acquainted with the order against signature is formalised by a corresponding act [15].

If within a year from the day the measure was applied no new measure is applied to the student, the student is considered as having no disciplinary measure [16]. Before the year expires the director has the right to lift the measure on his own initiative, on the student's request, on the request of the parents (legal representatives) of a minor student, or on the petition of the students' council [17].

A student, and the parents (legal representatives) of a minor student, may appeal the disciplinary measures and their application to the commission for the settlement of disputes between participants of educational relations [18].

The procedure for applying disciplinary measures to students of higher education programmes and the corresponding additional professional programmes, and for lifting them, is established by the federal executive body for higher education [19].

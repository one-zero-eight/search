# Applications and documents in bachelor's and master's admission 2026/2027 — priorities, document list and deadlines

## Where these statements come from

- The statements below rest on the admission rules for bachelor's and master's programmes of Innopolis University for 2026/2027, approved by the University's order of 10 April 2026 No. ОД/УД‐01/2026/04/10; the application and the documents are the subject of section VII of those rules, «Прием заявлений и документов» (clauses 64–82) [1].
- The wiki holds those rules in two copies, [1] and [2]; the clause numbers named below are the numbers these rules carry [1].
- The text of clause 66 of the rules is also reproduced on a separate extract page of the sveden section, with the same address and the same receipt [3].

## How many applications and how they are filed

- Under clause 64, a person applying for bachelor's programmes files one application for admission to the University for places within the control figures (if he wants those places), one application for admission to paid places (if he wants those places) and the documents necessary for admission [1].
- Under clause 65, a person applying for master's programmes files in the same way one application for places within the control figures, one application for paid places and the documents necessary for admission [1].
- Under clause 66, the application and the documents necessary for admission are filed in one of these ways: presented to the University in person, sent to the University through a general-purpose postal operator, or submitted through the federal state information system «Единый портал государственных и муниципальных услуг (функций)» (ЕПГУ); the University ensures the possibility of all these ways [3] [1].
- Under clause 66, the University accepts applications and documents at the address: Republic of Tatarstan, Verkhneuslonsky municipal district, town of Innopolis, ul. Universitetskaya, house 1, cabinet 100 (the admission committee, Приемная комиссия), within the terms set in section VII of the rules [3] [1].
- Under clause 66, where the documents necessary for admission are presented to the University in person by the applicant, the applicant is given a receipt for the acceptance of the application and documents [3] [1].

## The days of completion of the acceptance

- Under clause 67, for admission to bachelor's programmes the University sets the day of completion of the acceptance of applications and documents from those who have to take internal entrance examinations, and the day of completion of the acceptance from those who do not have to take them, including admission without entrance examinations under part 4 and/or part 12 of article 71 of Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ and admission to places within the separate quota without entrance examinations under part 5.2 of article 71 of that law where no additional entrance examinations of a creative and/or professional kind are held [1].

## Competition groups and priorities of enrolment

- Under clause 68, in the application the applicant names the competition groups in which he wants to be enrolled at the University and the priorities of enrolment for each of them [1].
- Under clause 68, for admission to places within the control figures the applicant states the priority of enrolment to places within the target quota (the priority of the target quota) and a single priority of enrolment to the main budget places and/or places within the separate quota and/or places within the special quota (the priority of other places); for admission to paid places he states the priority of enrolment to paid places [1].
- Under clause 69, priorities are marked with ordinal numbers (whole numbers starting from one); the height of the priorities decreases as those numbers grow, so the higher the number, the lower the priority; the applicant states a separate sequence of priorities for places within the control figures and a separate sequence for paid places [1].

## What the applicant certifies in the application

By his personal signature — and, where the application is filed through ЕПГУ, by a mark made in the application — the applicant certifies the following facts under clause 70 [1]:

- his acquaintance with the information that the application must state reliable information and that genuine documents must be presented [1];
- his acquaintance with the admission rules approved by the University and with the documents and information named in part 2 of article 55 of Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ [1];
- that, when applying for places within the control figures, he receives the corresponding higher education for the first time — for bachelor's programmes that he has no diploma of bachelor, diploma of specialist or diploma of master and no document of education and qualification under the programmes of basic higher education or of specialised higher education provided for by Government Decree No. 1302 of 9 August 2023, for master's programmes that he has no diploma of specialist or diploma of master and no such document under the programmes of specialised higher education — except the cases established by the legislation of Russia in which higher education is received at the expense of budget allocations while the person already has the corresponding higher education [1];
- that, when applying for bachelor's programmes, he simultaneously files applications to not more than 5 (five) organisations, including the University to which this application is filed, and to not more than 5 (five) areas of study [1];
- that, when applying for bachelor's programmes to places within the control figures on the basis of the right to admission without entrance examinations under part 4 and/or part 12 of article 71 of Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ, he files the application on that ground only to the University [1].

- Under clause 71, the application states whether the applicant needs special conditions at the internal entrance examinations because of his disability or health limitations [1].
- Under clause 72, the application is submitted in Russian [1].

## Changes to the application

- Under clause 73, the applicant may change the application, including changing the competition groups (adding or excluding them), changing the priorities of enrolment and other information, and may present and/or withdraw the documents attached to the application (except the withdrawal of documents presented in electronic form) [1].
- Under clause 73, changing the priorities of enrolment on ЕПГУ is done not more often than 1 (one) time in 2 (two) hours [1].
- Under clause 73, these actions are taken not later than the day of completion of the acceptance of applications and documents (on bachelor's programmes — not later than the day of completion of the acceptance of documents without entrance examinations); after the day of completion of the acceptance of documents with entrance examinations the applicant may not add to the application competition groups in which internal entrance examinations are required, other than the examinations for the competition groups already named in the application [1].

## Documents necessary for admission

Under clause 74 the applicant presents [1]:

- the document or documents proving identity and citizenship — in particular he may present a passport of a citizen of the Russian Federation proving the identity of a citizen of the Russian Federation outside Russia; presented together with the application [1]
- the document of education — not later than the day of completion of the acceptance of documents (on bachelor's programmes — not later than the day of completion of the acceptance of documents without entrance examinations); one or several documents of education may be presented; a document of a foreign state is presented with a certificate of recognition of the foreign education unless recognition is not required by the legislation of Russia and/or an international treaty, and that certificate is presented not later than the end of the term for presenting the consent to enrolment (for places within the control figures) or not later than the day of completion of the conclusion of contracts (for paid places) [1]
- the document confirming registration in the system of individual (personalised) accounting — together with the application, if available [1]
- an application of consent to the processing of personal data — together with the application [1]
- for taking internal general education entrance examinations because of disability (bachelor's programmes) — a document confirming the disability on the day it is presented; presented together with the application or later, but not later than the day of completion of the acceptance of documents without entrance examinations, and where it is presented later the applicant is admitted to the entrance examinations held after its presentation [1]
- where special conditions for taking internal entrance examinations are needed — a document confirming disability or health limitations on the day it is presented; presented together with the application or later, but not later than the day of completion of the acceptance of documents, and where it is presented later the University creates the special conditions for the entrance examinations held after its presentation [1]
- for using the results of centralised testing (bachelor's programmes) — a document confirming that the centralised testing was passed; not later than the day of completion of the acceptance of documents without entrance examinations [1]
- for using the special rights under parts 4 and 12 of article 71 of Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ (bachelor's programmes) — a document confirming that the applicant belongs to the persons to whom the corresponding special right is granted; not later than the day of completion of the acceptance of documents without entrance examinations, and used with account of the terms of the special rights set by those parts [1]
- for using the special rights under parts 5, 5.1, 5.2, 9 and 10 of article 71 of Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ (bachelor's programmes) — a document confirming that the applicant belongs to the persons to whom the corresponding special right is granted; not later than the day of completion of the acceptance of documents without entrance examinations, and where the document's effect is limited to a period it must confirm the special right on that day [1]
- documents confirming individual achievements taken into account at admission — at the applicant's discretion, not later than the day of completion of the acceptance of documents (on bachelor's programmes — not later than the day of completion of the acceptance of documents without entrance examinations) [1]
- the documents named in clauses 155–157 of the rules — not later than the day of completion of the acceptance of documents (on bachelor's programmes — not later than the day of completion of the acceptance of documents without entrance examinations) [1]
- other documents — at the applicant's discretion, not later than the day of completion of the acceptance of documents (on bachelor's programmes — not later than the day of completion of the acceptance of documents without entrance examinations) [1]
- a photograph of the applicant — not later than the day of completion of the acceptance of documents for applicants who take internal entrance examinations [1]

The documents referred to in clauses 155–157 are those presented in addition by foreign citizens applying on the basis of international treaties and by compatriots living abroad, and the identity document of a foreign citizen or stateless person [1].

- Under clause 75, for each document confirming a special right under part 4 or part 12 of article 71 of Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ the University determines, at admission to bachelor's programmes, whether the document is used to obtain the special right or to obtain points for an individual achievement, with account of the greater number of points awarded to the applicant [1].

## In what form documents are presented

- Under clause 76, the documents necessary for admission are presented as originals or as copies (electronic images) without presentation of the originals; certification of those copies (electronic images) is not required [1].
- Under clause 76, a document is considered presented in copy if the information about it (about the right it confirms) is confirmed by the data available on ЕПГУ or in other state information systems, including the federal information system «Федеральный реестр сведений о документах об образовании и (или) о квалификации, документах об обучении»; no original or copy then has to be presented, though the applicant may present one at his discretion [1].
- Under clause 76, if that information is not confirmed, the applicant presents an electronic image of the document through ЕПГУ or the University's electronic information system, or presents the original or a copy to the University — except a document confirming the special right under parts 5.1 and 5.2 of article 71 of Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ (bachelor's programmes) [1].
- Under clause 76, where the information about the special right under parts 5.1 and 5.2 of article 71 is not confirmed by ЕПГУ or other state information systems, the applicant presents the original or a copy of the document confirming that right in person or through a postal operator [1].
- Under clause 76, the status of participants of the special military operation under part 5.1 of article 71 and of their children may be confirmed by a certificate issued (including through ЕПГУ) under Government Decree No. 1354 of 9 October 2024, or by the data provided under that decree, and/or by another certificate issued by the federal state body or other organisation that sent the person to participate in the special military operation or in combat actions, and/or by another document; where a person named in parts 5–7 of part 5.1 of article 71 is not named in that certificate or data, his status is confirmed on presentation of a document certifying the fact of birth or adoption (удочерения) naming that person as parent [1].
- Under clause 77, documents made in a foreign language are presented with a translation into Russian certified by a notary (including a consular official) [1].
- Under clause 78, documents obtained in a foreign state must be legalised unless otherwise provided by an international treaty of Russia or by the legislation of Russia [1].

## After the documents are accepted

- Under clause 79, the University checks the reliability of the information stated in the application and the authenticity of the documents necessary for admission, including by requests to state information systems, state (municipal) bodies and organisations [1].
- Under clause 80, the applicant receives a unique code on ЕПГУ (where the application is filed through ЕПГУ) or from the University (where it is filed in another way) [1].
- Under clause 81, the University forms the applicant's personal file in electronic and/or paper form on the basis of the information and/or documents received from ЕПГУ and/or presented by the applicant in other ways [1].
- Under clause 82, on the results of the acceptance of applications and documents and of the internal entrance examinations the University decides on the admission of the applicants to participation in the competition [1].

# Ongoing and interim assessment in the College (secondary vocational education)

## The document and what it covers

The Положение о формах, периодичности и порядке текущего контроля успеваемости и промежуточной аттестации обучающихся, осваивающих образовательные программы среднего профессионального образования was approved by order of the University of 17 April 2025 № ОД/АД-01/2025/04/17 and signed by the director I.I. Bariev [1]. It regulates the forms, periodicity and procedure of current control and interim assessment in every element of a secondary vocational education programme (ОП СПО) — disciplines, interdisciplinary courses (МДК), professional modules (ПМ) and practices — under the federal state educational standards of secondary vocational education (ФГОС СПО) [2].

It was developed with the requirements of the education law of 29 December 2012 № 273-ФЗ, the order of the Ministry of Education of 24 August 2022 № 762, the ФГОС СПО, the University Charter and other normative documents and local acts of the University [3]. Its requirements are binding on all structural units that deliver and control the study process in ОП СПО [4].

The evaluation of the quality of mastering the elements of an ОП СПО consists of current control and interim assessment [5]; their aim is to assess how far the quality of the students' education corresponds to the requirements of ФГОС СПО [6]. The course of the educational process and the results of interim assessment are recorded with the use of the University's electronic information-educational environment [7]. Funds of assessment means (ФОС) are created, containing model tasks, control works, tests and methods of control, and the assessment materials are developed within the work programmes of disciplines, МДК, ПМ and practices [8].

The level and quality of mastery is graded on the scale «отлично», «хорошо», «удовлетворительно», «неудовлетворительно» or on the «зачтено/не зачтено» basis, the first of them being preferable in current control [9]. No fee is charged from students for current control or interim assessment [10]. For students who are invalids or have health limitations both are conducted with account of their psychophysical development, individual capabilities and state of health: special conditions are created without which the requirements of current control and interim assessment cannot be met or are hard to meet [11].

## Current control (текущий контроль успеваемости)

Current control evaluates the level of knowledge, skills and mastery of the study material in a discipline, МДК, module or practice as it is studied, and stimulates systematic work, control of study achievements and their regular objective evaluation [12]. It covers all students on ОП СПО [13] and is held within the study time allotted to the element concerned [14].

Its forms may be an oral quiz, testing including computer testing, a control work or a colloquium, a referat, a business game, a report on laboratory work, graphic work, checking written homework, practical and computation-graphic works, or other forms [15]. The concrete types, forms, criteria of evaluation, methods and means for each discipline, МДК or practice are developed and determined by the teacher independently in the work programme and the ФОС and are brought to the students' attention [16]. Students must go through all the established forms diligently [17]. At the start of a discipline or МДК the teacher may hold an entry control; its results are the basis for corrective measures and additional consultations [18].

Every control event held within current control is graded [19]; account is taken of all types of class and out-of-class work, of the results of control works and tests, of homework, activity and results in class, and of laboratory, practical and control works [20]. Attendance is recorded by teachers and by the curators of the study groups [21].

The teacher who holds the classes records the results in the class journal of the study group; the summary sheet of current control (Appendix 1) is formed in the College — the University's unit that delivers ОП СПО — on the basis of the journal [22]. The results are used by teachers, curators and the methodological commission of the College to organise effective study work, to identify lagging students in time and to improve teaching methods [23]; on their analysis the College holds corrective measures such as educational conversations with failing students and analysis of the quality of the work programme used [24].

The results of current control, including the full performance of laboratory and practical works and course projects, are taken into account when the results of mastering the discipline, МДК, practice or ПМ are graded in interim assessment; whether current control results may be retaken is decided by the teachers for each element independently [25]. A student (or their parent or legal representative) has the right to obtain information about the grade of their current control from the curator of the study group or from the teacher [26].

## Interim assessment: purpose, forms and schedule

Interim assessment is the part of the educational programme that completes the mastery of a separate part or the whole volume of a discipline, МДК, practice or ПМ [27]. It is obligatory and is held according to the curriculum of the specialty and the calendar study schedule for the academic year [28]. It determines the correspondence of the level and quality of preparation to the requirements of ФГОС СПО, the completeness and strength of knowledge and practical skills, and the full or partial formation of general and professional competences [29].

The main forms of interim assessment are [30]:

- a pass, including a graded pass [30];
- an exam [30];
- an exam on a professional module [30];
- a qualification exam on a professional module with the award of a qualification [30];
- another form of control — a control work, defence of a course work (project), defence of an individual project, an accumulative system of grades, other forms provided by the work programme [30].

A complex exam or complex pass over two disciplines, МДК or practices is allowed [30]. An exam or pass may be held orally or in writing, as testing including with computers and hardware, as defence of a referat or project, in combined form or in other forms; as a rule the teacher determines the form and sets it in the work programme, and the assessment materials in the ФОС must correspond to that form [31]. Exams are held at the expense of the time allotted to the disciplines, МДК and ПМ within the time for interim assessment in the curriculum [32], and passes at the expense of the time allotted to study the disciplines, МДК and practices [33].

Interim assessment is held by the teacher who leads the discipline, МДК or practice or with the participation of a commission; if that teacher cannot hold it, another teacher is appointed by decision of the College director [34]. The schedule of interim assessment is drawn up on the basis of the calendar study schedule and brought to the attention of students and teachers not later than 10 (ten) days before it begins [35]; before each exam time is set aside for a consultation, as a rule one day before the exam [36]. When forming the result of interim assessment the teacher takes into account the results of current control, knowledge of the discipline, practical skills, attendance, activity in class and the result obtained at the exam or pass [37].

## Misconduct, absence and extensions

The use of materials not allowed by the teacher or the commission, a breach of study discipline — cheating, use of mobile communication, a computer, audio players or other technical means, an attempt to communicate with other students, unauthorised movement — and a breach of the internal rules of conduct of students are grounds for removing the student from the room and entering «неудовлетворительно» or «не зачтено» in the sheet; the teacher (chair of the commission) informs the College director in writing or by corporate mail, and the director sends a memo to the University director for further disciplinary measures [38].

Failure of a student to appear for interim assessment without a valid reason is equated to an academic debt [39]; at an exam the non-appearance is marked «не явился», and non-appearance without a valid reason is equated to an unsatisfactory grade [40].

Students who could not pass interim assessment in the established time for documented valid reasons — temporary disability, performance of public or state duties, a court summons, transport problems (a cancelled flight, no tickets), weather conditions or other cases recognised by the University as valid — are given individual dates approved by order of the University director or another authorised person [41]. A student who presents a document confirming absence for a valid reason not later than 3 (three) working days after receiving it may have the period of interim assessment extended by the number of calendar days stated in the document (sick leave, certificate, medical conclusion); the student submits to the College an application to the University director (Appendix 2) with the documents, the College director considers and visas it and a submission for the order is prepared; if the document is presented late, the dates are not extended [42].

## Results, recording and transfer

The results of an oral exam or pass are entered on the day it is held; those of a written exam or pass within 3 (three) working days after it but not later than the end of the interim assessment period [43]. They are fixed in the interim assessment sheet, the journal and the students' record books, including with the use of the electronic information-educational environment [44]: the teacher enters them in the record books (where paper versions exist) and in the interim assessment sheet (Appendix 3) which he hands to the College, and the College staff transfer them into the journal and the electronic environment [45]. The results of interim assessment are not subject to appeal [46].

Students who have fully completed the curriculum of the course (year of study) and have successfully passed interim assessment are transferred to the next course by order of the University director or another authorised person [47]; students who did not pass interim assessment for valid reasons or have an academic debt are transferred conditionally [48].

## Limits on examinations and passes (the Regulation of 27 January 2025)

The Regulation on the procedure of organising and carrying out educational activity under СПО programmes was approved by order of the University of 27 January 2025 No. ОД/УД-04/2025/01/27 and put into force from 1 September 2025 [49]. Under it the mastering of a programme, including a separate part or the whole volume of a study subject, discipline (module), is accompanied by current monitoring of academic performance and by interim assessment, and their forms, periodicity and procedure and the system of grades are determined by the University's local normative acts [50]. The number of examinations in interim assessment must not exceed 8 examinations in an academic year and the number of passes must not exceed 10, and examinations and passes in physical culture and in facultative study courses and disciplines (modules) are not included in that number [51].

## Criteria for grades

The general criteria are knowledge (terms, principles, volume of material, completeness, correctness, clarity), skills (solving typical practical tasks, use of theory, checking and presenting results) and practical experience (solving standard and non-standard tasks, speed, volume and quality of work actions, independence) [52]; criteria for particular disciplines, МДК and practices are set in their work programmes and assessment materials [53].

- «Отлично»: the whole programme material is mastered deeply and firmly, the answer is exhaustive, consistent, correct and logically coherent, the student is not hindered by a changed task, copes freely with practical tasks, justifies decisions and generalises independently without errors [54].
- «Хорошо»: the material is known firmly and presented correctly in substance without substantial inaccuracies, theory is applied correctly and the necessary skills are held [55].
- «Удовлетворительно»: only the main material is mastered, details are unknown, inaccuracies and imprecise formulations occur, the sequence is broken and practical tasks cause difficulty [56].
- «Неудовлетворительно»: a significant part of the material is not known, substantial errors are made and practical tasks are performed with great difficulty [57].

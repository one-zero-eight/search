# Entrance examination for the master's programme «Управление на основе данных» (22 August 2024) — published results

## What the document is

The page is the published protocol of the entrance examination for the master's programme «Управление на основе данных» (data-driven management), held by Innopolis University on its own on 22.08.2024 [1].

Its heading names the examination as «Вступительное испытание по программе магистратуры "Управление на основе данных" 22.08.2024», so the page concerns admission to a master's programme, and the results table carries the heading «Результаты вступительного испытания проводимого АНО ВО "Университет Иннополис" самостоятельно» — the results of an entrance examination conducted by the University itself [1].

## What the protocol lists

The results table has the columns №, СНИЛС/Уникальный код, оценка and оценка (прописью): a serial number, the participant's СНИЛС or unique code, the score as a figure and the same score written out in words [1].

The identifier in this protocol is a bare number, [скрыто], not a СНИЛС written in groups, so the page gives no name and no document number of the participant [1].

## The result of 22 August 2024

The protocol contains a single row: № 1, identifier [скрыто], score 65 (Шестьдесят пять) [1]. The page therefore records one result only [1].

## What the page does not say

The page states no pass mark and no minimum score, and it records no decision on admission [1].

The name of the examination and the date appear again below the results, split into further blocks («Наименование вступительного испытания | Вступит | ельное … магист … Управл … на данных» and «Дата проведения вступительного испытания … | 22.08 | .2024»), so the score has to be taken from the row of the results table itself [1].

## Another examination of the same date

A separate protocol on the wiki covers the entrance examination for the master's programme «Управление Цифровым продуктом», likewise held on 22.08.2024; that page lists three results (41, 50 and 60) and is a different examination from this one [2].

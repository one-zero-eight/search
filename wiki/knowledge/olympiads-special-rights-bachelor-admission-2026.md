# Olympiads counted for the special rights and the special advantage in bachelor's admission 2026 (order of 19 January 2026)

## The order

The order of 19 January 2026 № ОД/УД-03/2026/01/19 establishes the correspondence of the profiles of international olympiads of pupils in general education subjects, of the All-Russian olympiad of pupils and of olympiads of pupils for grades 9–11 to the areas of study, and their correspondence to general education subjects for admission on the basis of the special right under parts 4 and 12 of article 71 of Federal Law of 29 December 2012 № 273-ФЗ, on admission to bachelor's programmes of the University in 2026 [1]. It rests on paragraphs 63, 64 and 65 of the order of the Ministry of Science and Higher Education of the Russian Federation of 27.11.2024 № 821 approving the Procedure of admission to higher education programmes, and is signed by the vice-rector — head of the department of academic policy and higher education E.S. Bobrov, who retains control over its execution [1].

## What each annex sets

- Annex 1 — the correspondence of the general education subjects of international olympiads of pupils (grades 9–11) to areas of study and to the general education subjects of the entrance examinations for admission on the special right under part 4 of article 71 of Federal Law № 273-ФЗ [1].
- Annex 2 — the same correspondence for the subjects and profiles of the All-Russian olympiad of pupils (grades 9–11) under part 4 of article 71 [1].
- Annex 3 — the correspondence of the profiles of pupil olympiads (grades 9–11) under part 12 of article 71 on the basis of the list of pupil olympiads and their levels for the 2025/26 school year, approved by order of the Ministry of Science and Higher Education of 30.08.2025 № 669 [1].
- Annex 4 — the same for the list for the 2024/25 school year, approved by order of 30.08.2024 № 571 [1].
- Annex 5 — the same for the list for the 2023/24 school year, approved by order of 28.08.2023 № 823 [1].
- Annex 6 — the same for the list for the 2022/23 school year, approved by order of 30.08.2022 № 828 [1].

Each of Annexes 3–6 therefore covers the olympiads of one school year; the results of a pupil olympiad are matched to the area of study, the level of the olympiad (I, II or III) and the general education subjects of the entrance examinations of the University [1].

## The areas of study and subjects named in Annexes 1 and 2

- 09.03.01 «Информатика и вычислительная техника»: an international olympiad in mathematics corresponds to the entrance examination in mathematics, and an international olympiad in informatics to the entrance examination in informatics [1].
- 15.03.06 «Робототехника и мехатроника»: an international olympiad in physics corresponds to the entrance examination in physics [1].
- For the All-Russian olympiad of pupils (Annex 2) the same two areas are covered: mathematics and informatics for 09.03.01 and physics for 15.03.06, together with the school subject «Труд (технология)» with the profiles «Информационная безопасность» and «Робототехника» for 09.03.01 and the profile «Робототехника» for 15.03.06; the layout of the table is scrambled in the wiki copy, so the pairing of each «Труд (технология)» profile with an entrance examination subject must be checked in the PDF [1].

## How the lists of olympiads are built

For each olympiad the tables of Annexes 3–6 give the full name of the olympiad or its profile, the area or group of areas of study it corresponds to, the level of the olympiad, the general education subject or subjects, and the subject or subjects of the entrance examinations at the University (mathematics, informatics and physics occur throughout) [1]. The same olympiad may have several profiles with different levels and different entrance subjects, for example «Формула Единства»/«Третье тысячелетие» — mathematics at level II and physics at level III in the 2022/23 list [1], and the Открытая физико-математическая олимпиада школьников «Росатом» — mathematics at level II, physics at level I and informatics at level II in the 2025/26 list [1].

Examples of the entries readable in the wiki copy [1]:

- Всероссийская олимпиада школьников «Высшая проба» — informatics (level I), mathematics (level I), physics (level II), industrial programming (level II), engineering sciences (level II) [1]
- Международная олимпиада Innopolis Open — informatics (level III), artificial intelligence (level III), mathematics (level II), robotics (level III) [1]
- Московская олимпиада школьников — physics (level I), robotics (level II), pre-professional profile (level III) [1]
- Вузовско-академическая олимпиада по информатике — informatics, level I; Инженерная олимпиада школьников — physics, level I; Санкт-Петербургская олимпиада школьников — mathematics, level I; Турнир городов — mathematics, level I [1]
- Университетская олимпиада школьников «Бельчонок» — mathematics (level III) and informatics (level III); Турнир имени М.В. Ломоносова — mathematics (level II) and physics (level III) [1]
- Всероссийская междисциплинарная олимпиада школьников 8–11 классов «Национальная технологическая олимпиада» — profiles such as autonomous transport systems, unmanned aircraft systems, flying robotics, big data and machine learning, virtual and augmented reality technologies, intelligent robotic systems, intelligent energy systems, information security, artificial intelligence, space systems, neurotechnologies and cognitive sciences, software engineering in financial technologies, mobile application development, wireless communication technologies, smart systems and technologies — at levels II and III, with informatics, physics and mathematics as entrance subjects [1]

The text of the annexes is extracted from tables in a way that scrambles many rows of the wiki copy, so a particular olympiad's level and entrance subjects cannot be relied on from the wiki and must be read from the PDF [1].

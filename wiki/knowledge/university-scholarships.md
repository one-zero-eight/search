# University scholarships: the academic scholarship of the University, its calculation and conditions, the increased scholarships and named scholarships

## Who may receive a University scholarship

- Students enrolled for study at the University from 2024 inclusive must meet these requirements for the payment of the University scholarships and the increased University scholarships: no grade «удовлетворительно» following the results of the period of study, and no academic debt [1].
- The academic scholarship of the University is appointed to students who have concluded with the University a grant agreement providing for the payment of scholarships established by the University [1].
- In the period from the date of enrolment to the month in which the first interim assessment ends, the scholarship of the University is appointed according to the calendar study schedule [1].

## When a University scholarship is not appointed

University scholarships and increased University scholarships are not appointed in the last year of study, and also from the first day of the month after the end of the last interim assessment in the penultimate year of study; if a student is on academic exchange in that period, they are not appointed either [1].

## Suspension, reduction and deprivation

The University may suspend the payment, take a decision to reduce the size of the payments or deprive a student of a previously appointed University scholarship for a period recommended by the Commission in the following cases [1]:

- systematic absence from classes (80% of the classes and more) during a month and unsatisfactory results of the ongoing assessment (more than 80% of grades «неудовлетворительно» following the current monitoring of academic performance) [1]
- application of a disciplinary penalty to the student [1]
- formation of an academic debt following interim assessment; in this case the payment of University scholarships resumes after the debt is cleared, from the month following the month of the last repeat interim assessment, and the scholarship not paid for that period is not compensated (for students of the programmes of training of scientific and scientific-pedagogical personnel in aspirantura) [1]

## Academic leave, recalculation, damage and holidays

- The payment of University scholarships is suspended from the first day of the month following the month in which academic leave, maternity leave or childcare leave until the child reaches the age of three is granted, and resumes from the first day of the month in which the academic leave ends, on the results of interim assessment that the student had on the date the leave was granted; if the interim assessment takes place after the end of the academic leave and before the appointment of the scholarship, its size is calculated on the results of the last interim assessment [1].
- The University scholarship is not recalculated after academic debts are cleared [1].
- If a student causes damage to the University's property and the University's claims are not satisfied, or the student does not answer the University's claim, the amount of the damage may be written off from the University scholarships [1].
- If a student is granted holidays on his application after successfully passing the state final certification, the payments of University scholarships stop for the period of those holidays and until the student is expelled from the University [1].

## The sizes of the scholarships for 2026

The sizes are set by the order of the University No. ОД/УД‐03/2025/12/02 of 2 December 2025, issued in accordance with the Regulation on scholarships and on the basis of a decision of the scholarship commission (protocol No. 203 of 1 December 2025); the order covers the period from 1 January to 31 December 2026 [2].

- The academic scholarship of the University for full-time students of the bachelor's and master's level: bachelor's course 1 — minimum 3 000 rubles, maximum 10 000 rubles; bachelor's courses 2 and 3 — the same minimum and maximum; master's course 1 — minimum 3 000 rubles, maximum 20 000 rubles; the payment period for all these lines is 01.01.2026–31.12.2026 [2].
- The order's table for the bachelor's and master's level names only courses 1, 2 and 3 of the bachelor's programmes and course 1 of the master's programmes [2].
- The academic scholarship of the University for full-time students of the level of training of higher qualification personnel: courses 1 and 2 — minimum 15 000 rubles, maximum 20 000 rubles for 01.01.2026–31.12.2026; course 3 — minimum 15 000 rubles and maximum 36 000 rubles for 01.01.2026–31.08.2026, and minimum 15 000 rubles and maximum 20 000 rubles for 01.09.2026–31.12.2026 [2].
- The increased scholarship of the University is set at 6 000 rubles, and the increased academic scholarship of the University at 10 000 rubles, for full-time students for 1 January–31 December 2026 [2].

## How the academic scholarship of the University is calculated

- After interim assessment the University scholarship is calculated by a formula that the page reproduces as an image; the text names its parameters: S — the size of the University scholarship; Smin and Smax — the minimum and maximum sizes set by an order of the director of the University or of a person authorised by him; GPA — the student's average grade following the last interim assessment; Sгас — the sum equal to the state academic scholarship paid to the student (if any) [1].
- For the GPA the grades «А» and «P» are equated to the grade «5», the grade «В» to «4», the grade «С» to «3», the grades «D» and «F» to «2» and «Неявка» to «0»; grades received during the state final certification are not taken into account [1].
- For students of the programmes of training of scientific and scientific-pedagogical personnel in aspirantura enrolled up to 2024 inclusive the page gives a separate calculation in which the state scholarship for aspirants paid to the student is subtracted [1].
- If the sum obtained by the formula is not a multiple of 100 rubles, it is rounded down to a multiple of 100 rubles [1].
- On admission by transfer from other educational organisations, the Commission may appoint a University scholarship of not less than the minimum size set by an order of the director of the University, until the scholarship is appointed on the results of the next interim assessment [1].

## Increased scholarship and increased academic scholarship of the University

- The increased scholarship of the University is appointed for special achievements in one or several areas of activity (academic, research, social, cultural-creative and sports) [3].
- The procedure for appointing it is set out in Appendix 3 to the Regulation [4], and it may be appointed to all students of the University on higher education programmes [5].
- The increased academic scholarship of the University is appointed to students of bachelor's programmes who received grades «отлично» for the periods of study preceding the appointment named in the clause and have no academic debts [6].
- The increased academic scholarship of the University may be appointed to students who have concluded with the University a grant agreement providing for the payment of scholarships established by the University [7].
- Its payment is suspended from the first day of the month following the month in which academic leave, maternity leave or childcare leave until the child reaches the age of three is granted, and resumes from the first day of the month in which the academic leave ends, on the results of interim assessment that the student had on the date the leave was granted [8].

## Named scholarships and scholarships of other bodies

- Named scholarships of the University are appointed to students on the submission of a structural subdivision of the University; the procedure of appointment, the sizes and the term of payment of named scholarships are approved by the relevant local normative acts of the University [9].
- The procedure of appointment and the terms of payment of scholarships appointed by legal and physical persons, including those who sent the students for study, are approved by those persons who established the scholarships [10].
- The procedure of appointment and the terms of payment of the scholarships of the President of the Russian Federation, of the Government of the Russian Federation and of other state bodies are determined by the normative legal acts of the President, of the Government and of those bodies [11].

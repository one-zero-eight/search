# Credit unit (зачётная единица) — its value and what it measures

## The order and the value it sets

The order of 30 May 2025 No. ОД/УД‐05/2025/05/30 «Об установлении величины зачетной единицы в АНО ВО «Университет Иннополис»» was issued in Innopolis in accordance with the Federal Law «Об образовании в Российской Федерации», in order to regulate the procedure for carrying out educational activity in the main professional educational programmes and additional professional programmes [1].

- The value of the credit unit (зачётная единица) is set at 27 astronomical hours, which is equivalent to 36 academic hours, with an academic hour lasting 45 minutes [2].
- That value applies to bachelor's programmes, master's programmes, programmes for the training of scientific and scientific-pedagogical cadres in aspirantura, programmes for the training of middle-level specialists, professional development (повышение квалификации) programmes and professional retraining programmes [2].
- The order of 23 November 2022 No. ОД/УД-02/2022/11/23 «Об установлении величины зачетной единицы в АНО ВО «Университет Иннополис»» is recognised as having lost force [3].

## Who controls execution

Control over the execution of the order is assigned to the following officials [4]:

- to M.N. Obraztsova, deputy director, head of the directorate for the organisation and support of project and educational activity — for the implementation of programmes for the training of middle-level specialists, professional development programmes and professional retraining programmes by the University's structural subdivisions directly subordinate to her [4];
- to E.S. Bobrov, vice-rector – head of the directorate of academic policy and higher education — for the implementation of bachelor's programmes, master's programmes, programmes for the training of scientific and scientific-pedagogical cadres in aspirantura, professional development programmes and professional retraining programmes by the University's structural subdivisions directly subordinate to him [4].

## What a credit unit measures

The Regulation on the procedure for developing and approving the University's higher education programmes — bachelor's and master's programmes — describes the credit unit as follows [5]:

- a system of credit units may be used to determine the structure of an educational programme and the labour intensity of mastering it [5];
- a credit unit is a unified unit of measurement of the labour intensity of a student's academic load, including all types of learning activity provided by the curriculum (including classroom and independent work) and practice [5];
- the labour intensity of an educational programme (or of its part) in credit units characterises the volume of the programme (or of its part), and the volume of a part of the programme must be a whole number of credit units [6];
- the University itself sets the value of the credit unit in astronomical or academic hours (with an academic hour of 40 or 45 minutes) within 24 to 30 astronomical hours, unless otherwise established by a federal state educational standard of higher education, and the value set is the same within the curriculum [7].

# Support Department (Отдел сопровождения) of the Department for the Popularisation of Science — place, tasks, functions and rights

## The document and the Department's place

The page is the Regulation on the Support Department (Положение об отделе сопровождения) of the Department for the Popularisation of Science of ANO VO «Innopolis University», titled «Отдел сопровождения 1 — Положение»; it was approved by order of 1 July 2025 № ОД/АД-01/2025/07/01, the signature block naming the Director for Development and HR Policy Valiev R.F. [1]. The Regulation governs the Department's activity and determines its tasks, functions, rights and duties [2].

- The Support Department is a structural subdivision of the Department for the Popularisation of Science and belongs to the category «иные структурные подразделения» [3].
- The Department reports directly to the rector of the University [4].
- In its activity it follows the Constitution of the Russian Federation, the Labour Code of the Russian Federation, Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29 December 2012 «Об образовании в Российской Федерации» and other legislative and normative legal acts regulating the activity of higher education institutions, the University's Charter, decisions of the Academic, Supervisory and Operational councils, orders and instructions of the University's director, other local normative acts and this Regulation [5].
- The structure, the number of staff and the staffing table of the Department, and changes to them, are approved by the director of the University [6]; the Department is headed by its head, hired and dismissed by order of the director [7].
- The working conditions of the Department's employees are determined by the employment contracts concluded with each of them and by the University's internal labour regulations [8]; their rights, duties and degree of responsibility are established by job descriptions [9].
- Within their competences the Department's employees may be involved in work under grants, projects, subsidies and other programmes implemented by the University [10]; the Regulation and additions and changes to it are approved by order of the director or another person authorised by him [11].

## Tasks

- Supporting measures under projects for the popularisation of science in the Republic of Tatarstan [12].
- Interaction with bodies of state authority in the field of education [13].

## Functions

- Organising interaction with bodies of state authority in the field of education and with educational organisations in the Republic of Tatarstan [14].
- Organising outreach measures for the popularisation of science and PR campaigns in educational organisations of the Republic of Tatarstan [15].
- Organising interaction with, and attracting within project activity, science popularisers [16].
- Organising interaction with organisations that provide methodological support to teachers and pupils [17].
- Organising work on additional education of schoolchildren in the physics and mathematics direction in the Republic of Tatarstan [18].
- Organising and holding information and awareness measures for the popularisation of science [19].
- Organising work on the creation, development, modernisation and support of an information system for distance and online learning [20].
- Organising the document flow on the Department's activity [21] and procurement procedures within it [22].
- Organising methodological support for the Department's measures [23] and technical support for them [24].
- Organising work on forming analytical and reporting information on the Department's activity [25].
- Controlling financial activity within the spending of the University's budget and own funds on the Department's activity [26].

## Rights

In carrying out their activity the Department's employees have the right to:

- request from the University's structural subdivisions the information and materials needed for work within the Department's competence [27];
- with the consent of the heads of those subdivisions, involve their employees in preparing drafts of local acts and other documents and in developing and carrying out measures conducted by the Department [28];
- take part in meetings held by the University's management where matters within the Department's competence are discussed [29];
- put before the University's management proposals to cancel, amend or supplement the University's local acts [30].

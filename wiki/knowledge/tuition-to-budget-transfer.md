# Transfer from paid education to study at the expense of budget appropriations (or the University's own funds) — the Regulation of 3 July 2025

## The document

The Положение о порядке и случаях перехода лиц, обучающихся по образовательным программам среднего профессионального и высшего образования, с платного обучения на обучение за счет бюджетных ассигнований либо за счет собственных средств организации, осуществляющей образовательную деятельность, в АНО ВО «Университет Иннополис» was approved by an order of the director dated 3 July 2025 and signed by director I.I. Bariyev [1].

It determines the rules and cases in which citizens of the Russian Federation studying under secondary vocational and higher education programmes move from paid education to study at the expense of budget appropriations of the federal budget, budgets of the subjects of the Russian Federation and local budgets, or at the expense of the University's own funds — including funds received from income-generating activity, voluntary donations and targeted contributions of physical and/or legal persons — within the University [2]. It also extends to foreign citizens who, under the legislation of the Russian Federation, are entitled to study at the expense of budget appropriations [2].

Its requirements are obligatory and extend to the officials and staff of the University who deal with such transitions [3]. It is in force without a fixed term until a new regulation is adopted and/or it is cancelled [4], and it is brought to the students' knowledge by placing it on the University's official site in the section «Сведения об образовательной организации» [5].

## Vacant places

- A transition to study at the expense of budget appropriations is possible when there are free places financed from budget appropriations under the corresponding educational programme, specialty, field of training, scientific specialty and form of study on the corresponding course (Вакантные бюджетные места), or at the expense of the University's own funds [6].
- The number of vacant budget places is determined by the University as the difference between the control figures of the corresponding year of admission (the number of places of admission to the first year of study) and the actual number of students admitted on budget appropriations in the corresponding programme, specialty, field, scientific specialty and form of study on the corresponding course; it is determined not less than twice a year, at the end of the study periods allocated within the courses [7].
- The number of vacant places financed from the University's own funds is determined by an order of the director or of a person authorised by the director, taking into account the volume of the University's budget allocated to educational activity under the corresponding programmes; if the University has no financial possibility, such places are not allocated [7].
- The deadlines for students to submit applications for a transition, where vacant places exist, are set by the University after a vacant place appears [8].
- The University ensures openness of information about the number of vacant budget places, the number of places at the University's own expense and the deadlines for applications by placing that information in information and telecommunication networks, including its official site [9].

## Who is entitled to a transition (п. 9)

The right to move from paid education to study at the expense of budget appropriations or of the University's own funds belongs to a person studying at the University under a contract for the provision of paid educational services who, at the moment of submitting the application, has no academic debt, no disciplinary penalties and no tuition arrears, and who meets one of the following conditions [10]:

- passing the interim attestation in the last study period allocated within the courses that precedes the application, with marks «отлично», or «отлично» and «хорошо», or «хорошо» [10];
- belonging to the following categories of citizens (except foreign citizens, unless an international treaty of the Russian Federation provides otherwise): orphans and children left without parental care, and persons from among them; citizens under twenty who have only one parent — a group I disabled person, if the average per capita family income is below the subsistence minimum established in the corresponding subject of the Russian Federation; women who gave birth to a child during their studies; children of persons taking or having taken part in the special military operation in the territories of Ukraine, the Donetsk People's Republic, the Lugansk People's Republic, the Zaporozhye region and the Kherson region [10];
- loss by the student during the period of study of one or both parents (legal representatives) or of the only parent (legal representative) [10].

## Participants of the special military operation (п. 10)

Participants of the special military operation who have no academic debt, no disciplinary penalties and no tuition arrears at the moment of applying are transferred to vacant budget places redistributed in accordance with the Rules on the redistribution of vacant places financed from budget appropriations approved by Resolution of the Government of the Russian Federation of 8 August 2023 № 1292 [11]. A participant of the special military operation who wishes to move to a vacant budget place submits to the University an application for the transition addressed to the director and a document confirming participation in the special military operation (if that document is not in the student's personal file) [11].

## The Commission

- The decision on the transition is taken by a Commission specially created by the University, with account of the opinion of the University's student council, of the trade union of students (if there is one) and of the council of parents (legal representatives) of minor students (if there is one, in respect of minor students), or with account of the designated purpose of voluntary donations or targeted contributions of physical and/or legal persons [12].
- The materials for the Commission's work are presented by the structural units of the University responsible for work with students, which receive the applications and supporting documents from students; the composition of the Commission is approved by a separate order of the director or of a person authorised by the director [12].
- If the chair or the secretary of the Commission is absent, they are elected from among the members by a majority of votes [13].
- The Commission may take decisions when not less than 2/3 of its composition is present; decisions are taken by open vote and are considered adopted if a majority (50 % + 1 vote) of those present voted for them; the decision is drawn up as a protocol [14].

## Order of priority (пп. 13–14)

- In the first place come students who meet the condition of the interim attestation with the marks named above; in the second place come students who meet the conditions of belonging to the listed categories of citizens and of the loss of parents, respectively [15].
- When a transition is made to a vacant budget place that arose as a result of the expulsion of a person studying under a targeted training contract, priority is given to students who have concluded a targeted training contract in the corresponding specialty of secondary vocational education, field of training of higher education or scientific specialty [15].
- Where two or more candidates of one queue compete for one vacant place, priority is given to students who have special achievements in academic, research, social, cultural-creative and sports activity of the University; the special status of an achievement is determined by the Commission [16].

## The application and its documents (пп. 15–17)

- A student who wishes to move to a vacant budget place or to study at the University's own expense submits to the responsible structural unit an application for the transition addressed to the director, in the form given in the annex to the Regulation [17].
- Attached to the application are documents confirming that the student belongs to the categories of citizens named in п. 9 (if they are not in the student's personal file) and documents confirming special achievements in academic, research, social, cultural-creative and sports activity of the University (if any) [17].
- The application must be submitted not later than the deadline set by the University and published in information and telecommunication networks, including the official site [18].
- A staff member of the responsible structural unit endorses the application within two days after it is received — with the head of the responsible structural unit and the vice-rector (deputy director) [19].
- Within 2 calendar days from the date on which the period for receiving applications ends, the staff member sends the applications with the attached documents to the Commission, together with information on the results of the student's interim attestation for the study period preceding the application, on the absence of disciplinary penalties and on the absence of tuition arrears [19].

## The Commission's decisions (пп. 19–23)

- Having considered the application, its documents and that information, within not more than 10 calendar days from the date the application reaches the Commission, the Commission takes one of the following decisions: on the student's transition from paid education to a vacant budget place; on the student's transition to study at the expense of the University's own funds; or on refusal of the transition to study at the expense of budget appropriations or of the University's own funds [20].
- The decision on a transition at the expense of budget appropriations is taken with account of the number of vacant budget places and of the priorities established under пп. 13 and 14 [21].
- Once the available vacant budget places are filled, in respect of the remaining applications the Commission takes a decision refusing the transition at the expense of budget appropriations or a decision on the student's transition to study at the expense of the University's own funds [22].
- The Commission's decision is brought to the students' knowledge by publishing it without the surname, first name and patronymic (if any) of the students, with the insurance number of the individual personal account (if any) or a unique code assigned to the student, in information and telecommunication networks, including the official site [23].
- Within 5 calendar days after the meeting the secretary of the Commission hands the protocol, all applications and the attached documents received from the students to the responsible structural units for storage [24].

## Formalisation of the transition (п. 24)

The transition is formalised by an administrative act of the University issued by the director or a person authorised by the director, not later than 10 calendar days from the date on which the Commission took the decision on the transition [25]. In the case of a transition to study at the expense of the University's own funds, an education contract or a targeted training contract is concluded with the student [25].

## The application form

The annex to the Regulation contains the form of the application for the transition: it is addressed to the director of АНО ВО «Университет Иннополис», gives the student's full name, course, form of study (full-time, part-time or correspondence), the field of training, specialty or scientific specialty with its code and name and the focus (profile) of the programme; the student asks for consideration of a transition from paid education under a specified education contract to study at the expense of budget appropriations or of the University's own funds, states the compliance with the conditions of the transition, confirms having read the Regulation and lists the supporting documents attached; the form provides for the date and signature and for the endorsements of officials [25].

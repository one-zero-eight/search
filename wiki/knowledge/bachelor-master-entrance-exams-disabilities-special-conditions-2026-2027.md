# Special conditions at entrance examinations for applicants with disabilities and health limitations (section V of the admission rules, clauses 23–29)

## What the document is

The page is the extract «Особенности проведения вступительных испытаний для инвалидов и лиц с ограниченными возможностями» — section V, clauses 23–29, of the University's admission rules, published separately for applicants [1]. It concerns entrance examinations for applicants from among disabled persons and persons with health limitations (поступающие с ограниченными возможностями здоровья) [2]. The extract itself does not name the admission year; its last clause refers to «пункт 22 настоящих Правил приема» [3].

## General conditions and extra time

- When holding entrance examinations the University creates conditions taking into account the features of the applicants' psychophysical development, their individual possibilities and their state of health, and gives such applicants information about the procedure of the entrance examinations in a form accessible to them [2].
- The duration of an entrance examination for such applicants is increased by 1 (one) hour [4].

## Premises, auditorium, number of applicants and assistants

- The University has created material and technical conditions ensuring the possibility of unhindered access of such applicants to the auditoria, toilets and other premises, and of their stay in those premises, including ramps, hoists, handrails, widened doorways and lifts; where there are no lifts the entrance examinations are held in auditoria located on the first floor of the building [5].
- Entrance examinations for such applicants are held in a separate auditorium [6].
- The number of such applicants in one auditorium must not exceed 12 (twelve) persons when the entrance examination is taken in written form and 6 (six) persons when it is taken in oral form [6].
- A larger number of such applicants may be present in the auditorium, and their entrance examinations may be held in one auditorium together with other applicants, if this does not create difficulties for the applicants in taking the examination [6].
- An assistant from among the employees of Innopolis University or of engaged persons may be present in the auditorium during the examination and gives such applicants the necessary technical help with account of their individual features — to take a workplace, to move, to read and format the task, to communicate with the persons conducting the entrance examination [6].
- Such applicants may use technical means they need in connection with their individual features while taking the entrance examination [7].

## Requirements by individual features

Additional requirements are ensured depending on the individual features of the applicants with health limitations [8]:

- for the blind: the tasks for the entrance examination are formatted in relief-dot Braille or as an electronic document accessible with a computer with special software for the blind, or are read out by an assistant; written tasks are performed on paper in relief-dot Braille or on a computer with special software for the blind, or are dictated to an assistant; where needed the applicant is given a set of writing accessories and paper for writing in relief-dot Braille and, at in-person examinations, a computer with special software for the blind [8];
- for the visually impaired: individual even illumination of not less than 300 lux at in-person examinations; where needed a magnifying device, and the applicants' own magnifying devices may also be used at in-person examinations; the tasks and the instruction on the procedure of the entrance examinations are formatted in enlarged print [8];
- for the deaf and the hard of hearing: sound-amplifying apparatus for collective use, and where needed sound-amplifying apparatus for individual use at in-person examinations; the services of a sign language interpreter are provided [8];
- for the deaf-blind: the services of a tactile sign language interpreter, in addition to the requirements fulfilled for the blind and for the deaf respectively [8];
- for persons with severe speech disorders, the deaf and the hard of hearing: entrance examinations held in oral form are, by decision of Innopolis University, held in written form [8];
- for persons with disorders of the musculoskeletal system, with disorders of the motor functions of the upper limbs or without upper limbs: written tasks are performed on a computer with special software or are dictated to an assistant; entrance examinations held in written form are, by decision of Innopolis University, held in oral form [8].

## How the conditions are obtained

The conditions provided for by clause 22 of the admission rules are given to such applicants on the basis of the application for admission containing information about the need to create the corresponding special conditions [3]. The page does not itself reproduce clause 22 [3].

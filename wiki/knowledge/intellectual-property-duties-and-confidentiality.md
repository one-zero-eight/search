# Duties, confidentiality and liability in intellectual property

## Notifying the University about a result

The IP-Policy of Innopolis University was approved by an order of the University of 9 August 2022 [1]. Under it, an employee or student who created a result whose exclusive right and/or right to obtain a patent may be acquired by or belongs to the University must [2]:

- send the МЦТТ a notification of the creation of the result and provide information about it and its copies — on paper signed by the author, as a scanned copy of the signed notification sent from the author's corporate e-mail, or through the Bitrix corporate portal [2];
- take part in preparing the documents needed to secure the University's rights to the result and to fulfil the University's obligations to third parties, including patent applications, documents needed to accept the result for accounting and tax records and similar documents [2];
- where necessary carry out additional research, tests and experiments and take part in assessing the prospects of commercial use of the result [2];
- follow the legislation, the Regulation on the Commission on intellectual property, the Policy and the University's administrative documents in the field of intellectual property [2];
- not disclose the essence of the result or information about the subject of contracts for research, development and technological work, their performance and results, and observe the commercial secrecy regime for information with restricted access [2];
- not commit actions entailing the loss of confidentiality of information constituting a production secret (know-how) [2];
- not disclose, provide copies of, publish or otherwise reveal to third parties, without the prior written consent of the Chairman of the Commission, any information learned in the course of being involved in the Commission's work [2].

Employees and students must also respect the intellectual rights of the University and of third parties, including refraining from unlawful borrowing of results of intellectual activity, appropriation of authorship and similar actions; ensure the confidentiality of information about results in accordance with section 7 of the Policy; indicate the University's support when mentioning a created result where local acts or contracts so require; and bear other duties provided by Russian legislation, the Policy, other local acts and the conditions of contracts [3].

Employees must additionally notify the University through the МЦТТ, in the same ways (paper signed by the author, scanned copy from corporate e-mail, or the Bitrix portal), about results they obtained before concluding an employment contract with the University and relating to the field of scientific, creative or teaching activity they carry out in connection with their duties, and about their participation in projects organised and/or financed by third parties in the course of which results are or may be created in the field of research they carry out at the University [4].

## Confidentiality of information about results

Employees in labour relations with the University, and citizens with whom the University has concluded a civil-law contract, including students who by the nature of their activity have access to information about the results of work in which results are or may be created, must keep that information confidential from the moment they obtain access until it is made public at the University's initiative or until the University takes a decision refusing to keep it confidential [5].

Disclosure of information about a result — including any publication (a scientific article, a monograph, a press release and the like) or a public oral statement (a presentation, a speech at an exhibition and the like) — may prevent the legal protection of the result in Russia and in foreign countries; a person planning disclosure must therefore apply to the МЦТТ before the public disclosure, and the МЦТТ must explain the consequences of public disclosure for legal protection and give a written assessment of the possibility of disclosure before the publication or speech, while the decision on ensuring legal protection must be taken within 5 (five) working days of the application, so as not to delay the publication or public statement [6].

If the University decides to keep information about a result as a commercial secret, its confidentiality is protected on the basis of the relevant local acts establishing measures to protect the confidentiality of information about results, including the results of the University's scientific and technical activity [7].

## Disputes and reports of infringement

Disputes on the legal protection of intellectual property objects and on the exercise of intellectual rights must first be settled by negotiations between the parties and, if that is impossible, in court under the applicable legislation [8]. Employees and students whose duties or contract presuppose the creation of results must ensure that the result is free of third-party rights and, where third-party intellectual property has to be used, notify the head of the structural subdivision or another person responsible for the work [9]. Those who take decisions on the use of a result — including in the educational process, in research, in preparing publications and when installing software — must make sure the intended forms of use do not infringe the intellectual rights of the University or of third parties and otherwise refuse such use [10].

The University may on its own initiative carry out measures to establish facts of infringement of third parties' intellectual rights in the activity of its employees and students, such as checking created results with the «Антиплагиат» system, patent research, information searches and expert examinations [11].

Any person who believes that the actions of the University's employees or students infringe his rights to results of intellectual activity may send the University a corresponding report with documentary evidence supporting the stated facts [12]. On receiving information about an infringement by an employee or student of the rights of the University or of third parties, the МЦТТ informs the Commission on intellectual property — which is authorised to take decisions in the field of intellectual property — the persons about whom information of infringement has been received, and the heads coordinating the structural subdivisions whose activity gave rise to the report [13]. Those persons take measures to stop immediately the actions reported as infringing rights to results [14], and the Commission examines the factual circumstances with the participation of the persons concerned [15].

On the results of its work the Commission prepares a protocol containing conclusions on the established facts, recommendations to remedy the infringements, to minimise the University's liability for the actions of employees and students, to apply measures preventing such infringements, and to apply liability measures to those who infringed rights to results [16]. On the basis of the protocol, the University official authorised to take decisions in the field of intellectual property takes a decision, of which the person who sent the report, the person named in it as having infringed intellectual rights, and the head of the structural subdivision responsible for the activity that gave rise to the report are notified [17].

## Types of liability

Employees and students who infringe the legislation on intellectual property and the intellectual rights of third parties may face the liability measures provided by legislation and applied in the manner it establishes, in particular [18]:

- under civil legislation, including the provisions of arts. 1250, 1251, 1252, 1301, 1311, 1406.1, 1472 and 1515 of the Civil Code [19];
- under labour legislation, including подп. «в» п. 6 ч. 1 ст. 81 of the Labour Code of the Russian Federation [20];
- under administrative legislation, including arts. 7.12, 7.28, 13.14 and 14.10 of the Code of the Russian Federation on Administrative Offences [21];
- under criminal legislation, including arts. 146, 147, 180 and 183 of the Criminal Code of the Russian Federation [22].

Non-performance or improper performance by employees of the duties under the Policy is a disciplinary offence and may entail a disciplinary penalty — a remark, a reprimand, or dismissal on the corresponding grounds; where a violation is revealed, the head of the МЦТТ sends information to the person under whose direct subordination the employee works and to the director for development and HR policy so that a decision is taken to initiate the procedure for applying a disciplinary penalty [23].

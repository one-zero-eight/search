# Facultative and elective disciplines (modules) — choice, volume and assessment

## What the procedure governs

The Procedure for mastering facultative (факультативные) and elective (элективные) disciplines (modules) in the implementation of higher education programmes — bachelor, master and research and teaching staff training (aspirantura) programmes — at ANO VO «Innopolis University» regulates the conditions, grounds and organisation of mastering those disciplines [1]. It was approved by order of the University of 6 October 2025 No. ОД/УД-02/2025/10/06, signed by Rector A.V. Gasnikov [2].

Facultative disciplines (modules) are disciplines that are not obligatory for study in mastering an educational programme; elective disciplines (modules) are disciplines chosen on a mandatory basis. Both are included in the basic professional higher education programmes — bachelor, master and aspirantura programmes [1].

The procedure rests on Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29 December 2012 «Об образовании в Российской Федерации», the Government Decree of 30 November 2021 No. 2122 on the training of research and teaching staff in aspirantura, the order of the Ministry of Science and Higher Education of 6 April 2021 No. 245, the order of the Ministry of Education and Science of 19 November 2013 No. 1259, the order of the Ministry of Science and Higher Education of 20 October 2021 No. 951 approving the federal state requirements (ФГТ), the federal state educational standards (ФГОС ВО), the University's Charter and other federal and local acts [3].

## Who must be offered these disciplines

- In bachelor, master and research and teaching staff training programmes developed under ФГОС ВО, the University ensures that students have the possibility of mastering facultative and elective disciplines (modules) [4].
- In aspirantura programmes for research and research-teaching staff developed under ФГТ, the University may provide aspirants with the possibility of mastering facultative and elective disciplines (modules) from the list it offers [4].
- The work programmes of elective and facultative disciplines (modules) are developed and updated in accordance with the University's local acts on the development and approval of educational programmes [5].
- The list of elective and facultative disciplines (modules) for bachelor and master programmes is formed according to the field of study and the focus (profile) of the programme and is updated at least once a year depending on the needs of the IT industry market [6].

## Volume of the disciplines

- For bachelor, master and research-teaching staff training programmes under ФГОС ВО, the volume of facultative disciplines (modules) follows the requirements of the relevant ФГОС ВО; where the ФГОС ВО sets no such requirements, the University determines the volume itself when developing the programme. For aspirantura programmes under ФГТ, the volume, if such disciplines are included, is determined by the University itself when developing the programme [7].
- Facultative disciplines (modules) are not obligatory for study in mastering the programme and are not included in the volume (annual volume) of the educational programme; on the page this rule is numbered 2.2 and printed inside a table of clause 2.1 [2].
- For bachelor, master and research-teaching staff training programmes under ФГОС ВО, the volume of elective disciplines (modules) follows the requirements of the relevant ФГОС ВО; where the ФГОС ВО sets no such requirements, the University determines the volume itself when developing the programme. For aspirantura programmes under ФГТ, the volume, if such disciplines are included, is determined by the University itself when developing the programme [8].

## Facultative disciplines: how students take them

- The University informs students of the list of facultative disciplines (modules) provided by the programme by corporate e-mail and, where necessary, organises consultations on their content [9].
- Students sign up for facultative disciplines (modules) through corporate e-mail using survey administration services before the start of the discipline (module) according to the class timetable; the exact deadlines are set by the structural subdivision responsible for organising studies under the educational programmes and communicated to students by corporate e-mail [10].
- Students may change the list of facultative disciplines (modules) they had planned to take and (or) refuse to take them; all changes are agreed with the structural subdivision responsible for organising studies, taking into account the objective possibility of organising the educational process. Changes to the planned list are not allowed after the study of the relevant facultative discipline (module) has begun according to the timetable [11].
- Data on the student's choice of facultative disciplines (modules) are recorded in the electronic information and educational environment and used to organise the educational process [12].
- If a student has not made the choice within the established deadline, the student is released from studying facultative disciplines (modules) [13].
- The class timetable for facultative disciplines (modules) is formed according to the curriculum of the programme and the academic calendar [2].
- On the results of mastering facultative disciplines (modules) an interim assessment is conducted according to the curriculum and academic calendar of the programme, in the manner established by the University's local acts; the results of successfully passing it are entered, on the student's personal application (form in the annex to the procedure), in the supplement to the document on higher education and qualification [2].

## Elective disciplines: blocks, choice and groups

- Elective disciplines (modules) in programmes under ФГОС ВО belong to the part of the programme formed by the participants of educational relations; in aspirantura programmes under ФГТ, if they are included in the programme, they belong to the educational component [14].
- Elective disciplines (modules) are obligatory for students of bachelor, master and research-teaching staff training programmes under ФГОС ВО, and for aspirants under ФГТ programmes if the University has included them in the aspirantura programme [15].
- In curricula elective disciplines (modules) are given alternatively: at least two disciplines (modules) in a block, and the student may choose no more than one discipline. If a discipline (module) appears in two or more blocks of the curriculum, the student may choose it for study no more than once [16].
- The maximum number of students in a study group for elective disciplines (modules) at the University, as a rule, does not exceed 30 people [17].
- The University informs students of the list of elective disciplines (modules) provided by the programme by corporate e-mail and, where necessary, organises consultations on their content [18].
- Students choose elective disciplines (modules) through corporate e-mail using survey administration services before the start of the semester in which they will study the chosen elective disciplines (modules) according to the curriculum and academic calendar; the exact deadlines are set by the structural subdivision responsible for organising studies and communicated to students by corporate e-mail [19].
- Elective disciplines (modules) included in different blocks of the curriculum may be combined into specialisations (tracks); in that case the student's choice of the relevant elective disciplines (modules) is made when choosing the first elective discipline (module) according to the curriculum [20].
- Changes to the planned list of elective disciplines (modules) are not allowed after the study of the relevant elective discipline (module) has begun according to the timetable [21].
- If a student has not made the choice within the established deadline, the student is assigned to a study group studying a particular elective discipline (module) by decision of the structural subdivision responsible for organising studies under the educational programmes [22].
- Data on the student's choice of elective disciplines (modules) are recorded in the electronic information and educational environment and used to organise the educational process [23].

## Assessment and timetable of elective disciplines

- The class timetable for elective disciplines (modules) is formed according to the curriculum of the programme and the academic calendar [2].
- On the results of mastering elective disciplines (modules) an interim assessment is conducted according to the curriculum and academic calendar of the programme, in the manner established by a local act of the University [2].

## Approval and amendment of the procedure

The procedure is approved by order of the Director of the University or of another person authorised by the Director, and changes or additions to it are introduced by the same order [24], [25].

## Facultative and elective disciplines in the College (secondary vocational education)

A separate and later document governs secondary vocational education (СПО) programmes: the Порядок освоения факультативных и элективных дисциплин (модулей) при реализации образовательных программ среднего профессионального образования, approved by order of the University of 28 July 2025 No. ОД/АД‐03/2025/07/28 and signed by Director I.I. Bariev [26]. It regulates the conditions, grounds and organisation of mastering facultative and elective disciplines (modules) by students mastering СПО programmes [27].

It rests on Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29 December 2012, the order of the Ministry of Education of Russia of 24.08.2022 No. 762 on the organisation of educational activity in СПО programmes, the federal state educational standards of secondary vocational education (ФГОС СПО), the University's Charter, the Regulation on the College and other local acts [28].

Rules common to both kinds of discipline in the College:

- The University ensures students the possibility of mastering facultative and elective disciplines (modules) [29].
- The work programmes of these disciplines are developed and updated according to the University's local act on the development and approval of СПО programmes [30].
- The list is formed according to the focus of the СПО programme on the basis of an analysis of needs for widening and deepening knowledge, developing interests and abilities and preparing for future professional activity; it is updated at least once a year, taking into account current requests of the regional labour market [31].
- The list of disciplines, their distribution over semesters, the volume of hours (трудоемкость) and the form of assessment are established by the University itself and determined by the curriculum [32].
- Information on the delivery of these disciplines is recorded in the journals of study sessions on specially allocated pages [33].
- At interim assessment, passes in facultative disciplines do not count in the total number of passes for the academic year [34].

Facultative disciplines in the College:

- They are established by the University in addition to the programmes being implemented, are not obligatory for study and are not included in the volume of the educational programme [35].
- The University announces the list through the electronic information and educational environment and by corporate mail and, if necessary, organises consultations on their content [36].
- Students choose them voluntarily according to their individual educational needs [37], in the semester preceding the semester in which the chosen disciplines are delivered [38].
- Students sign up through their personal cabinet before the start of the semester in which the discipline will be studied, according to the academic calendar; the exact deadlines are set by the University and announced through the electronic environment and corporate mail. Before study begins the student may change the list and (or) refuse to study them, with all changes agreed with the College and subject to the objective possibility of organising the educational process; changes are not allowed after the study of the discipline has begun according to the timetable [39].
- If the student did not make the choice in the established term, the student is released from studying facultative disciplines [40].
- The timetable is formed according to the curriculum and the academic calendar [41], and interim assessment is conducted according to the curriculum in the manner of the University's local act [42].
- The results of successfully passing interim assessment are entered, on the student's personal application (form in the annex), in the supplement to the document on education and qualification [43].

Elective disciplines in the College:

- Their presence in a СПО programme is not a compulsory condition and is established at the University's discretion [44]; they are delivered from the variable part of the programme and are obligatory for mastering [45].
- In curricula they are given alternatively: at least 2 disciplines (modules) in a block, and the student may choose no more than 1 [46].
- The maximum number of students in a study group for elective disciplines is 25 people [47]; in the higher education procedure the figure is, as a rule, 30 [17].
- The College announces the list through the electronic environment (the student's personal cabinet) and corporate mail and, if necessary, organises consultations [48].
- Students sign up through the personal cabinet before the start of the semester; the exact deadlines are set by the College and announced through the electronic environment and corporate mail; changes are not allowed after study of the discipline has begun according to the timetable [49].
- If the student did not choose in time, the student is distributed by decision of the College into the study group studying a particular elective discipline [50].
- The timetable follows the curriculum and academic calendar [51], and interim assessment is conducted according to the curriculum in the manner of the University's local act [52].

The College procedure is approved by order of the Director or of a person authorised by the Director and enters into force from the moment of approval; changes and additions are introduced by the same order [53], [54]. Its annex is the form of a student's application to the Director of the College asking to enter information on facultative disciplines mastered into the diploma supplement, with the discipline name, number of credits and form of control [26].

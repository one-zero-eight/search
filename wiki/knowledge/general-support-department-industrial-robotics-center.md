# General Support Department (Отдел общего обеспечения) of the Center for Industrial Robotics Development — place, tasks, functions and rights

## The document and the Department's place in the structure

The Regulation on the General Support Department (Положение об отделе общего обеспечения) regulates the activity of the General Support Department of the centre for the development of industrial robotics of the centre for automation and robotics of ANO VO «Innopolis University» and determines its tasks, functions, rights and duties [1]. The page presents it as approved by order of the University of 2 January 2024 № ОД/АД-07/2024/01/22, signed by the Director for Development and HR Policy R.F. Valiev [1]. The page is a scanned document read by OCR, and the wiki warns that its numbers and dates should be checked against the PDF before relying on them [1].

- The Department is a structural subdivision of the centre for the development of industrial robotics of the University (the Centre) and belongs to the category «иные структурные подразделения» [2].
- The Department reports directly to the director of the centre for the development of industrial robotics [3].
- In its activity the Department follows the Constitution of the Russian Federation, the Labour Code of the Russian Federation, Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29 December 2012 «Об образовании в Российской Федерации» and other legislative and normative legal acts regulating the activity of higher education institutions, the University's Charter, decisions of the Academic, Supervisory and Operational councils, orders and instructions of the University's director, other local normative acts and this Regulation [1].
- The structure, the number of staff and the staffing table of the Department, and changes to them, are approved by the director of the University [4].
- The Department is headed by the head of the Department, hired and dismissed by order of the director of the University [5].
- The working conditions of the Department's employees are determined by employment contracts concluded with each employee and by the University's internal labour regulations [6].
- The rights, duties and degree of responsibility of the Department's employees are established by job descriptions [7].
- Employees of the Department may, within their competences, be involved in work under grants, projects, subsidies and other programmes implemented by the University [8].
- The Regulation and additions and changes to it are approved by the director of the University or another person authorised by him [9].

## Tasks

The Department's tasks are [10], [11], [12]:

- providing legal support of the Centre's activity: protection of the Centre's lawful interests in relations with counterparties and state bodies [13]; legal support of the Centre's contractual and claims work [14]; monitoring and analysis of legislation and of drafts of normative legal acts concerning the Centre's activity and prompt informing of the Centre's employees about the changes made [15];
- ensuring the recording and movement of the Centre's employees: personnel records [16]; records of persons liable for military service and of employees subject to call-up [17]; registration for migration of the Centre's foreign employees [18]; statistical reporting on personnel questions, including to statistics bodies and state bodies [19]; ensuring the rights, benefits and guarantees of the Centre's employees in accordance with labour legislation [20];
- organising accounting and tax records of the Centre's economic and financial activity [12].

## Functions

As part of the legal support of the Centre's activity the Department [21]:

- advises the Centre's employees on the application of legal norms [22];
- gives opinions on legal questions arising in the Centre's activity, including on the rules and procedure of granting subsidies and on the legal regulation of intellectual property [23];
- takes part together with other University subdivisions in negotiations with potential and existing counterparties under concluded and being concluded contracts, agreements of intent and other documents legalising the relations between the counterparty and the University [24];
- develops forms of contracts and carries out the legal analysis and approval of contracts concluded by the Centre [25];
- represents the Centre's interests in controlling and supervisory bodies and in judicial and other state bodies [1];
- prepares answers to claims and statements of claim, drafts reviews and other procedural documents [26].

As part of the recording and movement of the Centre's employees the Department [27]:

- forms the Centre's staffing table [28];
- organises personnel records with the use of the electronic automated systems applied at the University [29];
- documents the hiring, transfer and dismissal of the Centre's employees in accordance with labour legislation: draws up employment contracts with the Centre's employees and changes to them and prepares drafts of orders on personnel and on the University's main activity [30];
- keeps the electronic database of the working activity of the Centre's employees in accordance with Russian legislation and the normative documents on electronic labour books [31];
- works with paper labour books, including accepting them from newly hired employees of the Centre, recording, storing and filling them in, and issuing them when the employment relation ends [32];
- ensures the protection of personal data when the Department's employees work with documents containing the Centre's employees' personal data [33];
- prepares information about the Centre's employees from personnel records and personnel office work at the requests of authorised officials and structural subdivisions for their reporting [34];
- draws up vacation schedules, issues orders on granting the Centre's employees the various types of leave and records the number of days of leave used [1];
- processes and records official business trips, preparing drafts of orders [35];
- works with certificates of incapacity for work [36];
- checks the correctness and completeness of the time sheets of working time filled in by the Centre's employees [37];
- prepares and issues to the Centre's employees certificates of the position held and of the period of work at the University, and certified copies of documents connected with their working activity [38];
- prepares personnel documentation for transfer to the University's archive for further storage [39];
- keeps timely and correct records of the Centre's employees liable for military service and subject to call-up for military service [40];
- advises the Centre's employees on labour legislation questions, including answers to requests, complaints and appeals [41].

As part of the Centre's accounting the Department [1]:

- organises accounting and reporting in the Centre on the basis of maximum centralisation of accounting and computing work and the use of modern technical means and information technologies, progressive forms and methods of accounting and control [42];
- controls the conduct of economic operations [43];
- ensures the procedure of inventories [44];
- ensures observance of the technology of processing accounting information and of the order of document flow [45];
- forms and timely submits complete and reliable accounting information about the Centre's activity, its income and expenses [46];
- records property, liabilities and economic operations, incoming fixed assets, goods and materials and money [47];
- timely reflects on the accounting accounts the operations connected with the movement of fixed assets, goods and materials and money [48];
- timely and correctly draws up documents [49];
- ensures settlements for wages [50];
- correctly calculates and transfers taxes and fees to the budgets, insurance contributions to state non-budgetary social funds and payments to banking institutions [51];
- takes measures to prevent shortages, unlawful spending of money and goods and materials, and violations of financial and economic legislation [52];
- ensures strict observance of staff, financial and cash discipline and the safekeeping of accounting documents, their drawing up and handing over to the archive in the established order [1];
- draws up the balance sheet and operational summary reports on income and expenditure of funds, on the use of targeted funds and other accounting and statistical reporting, and submits them in the established order to the relevant bodies [53];
- observes the established rules for drawing up the acceptance and issue of goods and materials [54];
- controls the correctness of spending the wage fund and strict observance of staff, financial and cash discipline [55];
- observes the established rules for conducting inventories of money, goods and materials, fixed assets, settlements and payment obligations [56].

## Rights

In carrying out their activity the Department's employees have the right to:

- request from the University's structural subdivisions the information and materials needed for the work within the Department's competence [57];
- with the consent of the heads of the University's structural subdivisions, involve the employees of those subdivisions in preparing drafts of local acts and other documents and in developing and carrying out measures conducted by the Department [58];
- take part in meetings held by the University's management when discussing questions whose solution falls within the Department's competence [59];
- put before the University's management proposals to cancel, amend or supplement the University's local acts [60];
- interact with bodies of state authority and administration, legal entities and the University's subdivisions on questions within the Department's competence [61].

## Interaction with other subdivisions and reporting

- The Department's interaction with the University's other structural subdivisions is determined by the tasks and functions assigned to it by this Regulation [62].
- The interaction is carried out in the course of preparing drafts of local acts, orders, instructions, contracts and other documents and of preparing conclusions, written and oral references on questions within the Department's competence [63].
- On the basis of the relevant local acts, orders and instructions the Department may, within its activity, be responsible for preparing and keeping up to date information, references and reports [64].

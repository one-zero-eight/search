# Externs (экстерны) — enrollment and passing interim and state final attestation

## What the Regulation is

The Regulation on the conditions and procedure for enrolling externs at ANO VO «Innopolis University» and on the terms of their interim and state final attestation was approved by order of the University of 27 April 2024 No. ОД/АД-03/2024/04/27 and signed by Director K.V. Semenikhin [1]. It determines the procedure of enrolling students at the University as externs (экстерны) in order to pass interim and state final attestation in a corresponding state-accredited higher education programme [2].

It rests on Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29 December 2012 «Об образовании в Российской Федерации», the orders of the Ministry of Education and Science of 19 November 2013 No. 1259 (organisation of studies in aspirantura programmes), of 29 June 2015 No. 636 (state final attestation in bachelor, specialist and master programmes), of 18 March 2016 No. 227 (state final attestation in aspirantura, ordinatura and assistantship-internship programmes) and No. 245 of 2021 on the organisation of studies in bachelor, specialist and master programmes, the federal state educational standards of higher education, the University's Charter and other normative acts and local acts of the University [3].

## Who counts as an extern and who may be enrolled

Under Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ, externs are persons enrolled in an organisation carrying out educational activity in state-accredited educational programmes in order to pass interim and state final attestation [4].

- Persons mastering an educational programme in the form of self-education (where the educational standard allows obtaining higher education in that programme in that form) may be enrolled as externs to pass interim and state final attestation at an organisation carrying out educational activity in the corresponding state-accredited programme [5].
- The same applies to persons who studied in an educational programme that has no state accreditation [5].
- In passing interim and state final attestation, externs enjoy the academic and other rights granted to students by the normative legal acts of the Russian Federation and the local acts of the University [6].

## Conditions of enrollment

- Whether externs can be enrolled for interim and state final attestation is determined by an order of the Director of the University or of a person authorised by the Director, on the basis of the availability of the corresponding material-technical, teaching-methodical, staff and financial conditions at the University [7].
- Enrollment of externs takes place under contracts on education at the expense of individuals and (or) legal entities [8].
- Enrollment is allowed only if in the current academic year the University implements a state-accredited educational programme of the corresponding level and in the corresponding field of study [9].

## Application and documents

- A person wishing to be enrolled as an extern submits to the structural subdivision responsible for admission of applicants a personal application for enrollment addressed to the Director of the University (form in the annex to the Regulation) [10].
- Attached to the application: a copy of the document on education or of the document on education and qualification; a copy of the certificate (or copies of certificates) on the study period confirming successful passing of previous interim attestations in other educational organisations (for persons mastering the programme in the form of self-education); a photograph 3x4 cm; and other documents whose submission serves the interests of the person wishing to be enrolled as an extern [10].
- When submitting the application the person presents an identity document [11].
- The person also gives consent to the processing of personal data as required by Federal Law No. 152-ФЗ of 27 July 2006 «О персональных данных»; on the basis of that consent the University has the right to enter information about the extern into federal, regional and municipal information systems [12].
- Applications and attached documents are accepted no later than one month before the start of the academic year as established by the University [13].

## Decision, contract and enrollment

- The decision on whether an extern can be enrolled is taken by the University's attestation commission on the basis of the submitted documents; the decision is recorded in a protocol [14].
- On the basis of the commission's protocol the University concludes with the extern a contract on the provision of paid educational services and issues an order enrolling the extern for interim and state final attestation [15].
- Within 5 working days from the day of that order the extern is issued a record book (зачетная книжка) of the established form [16].
- For the time of the attestation a personal file of the student is formed, holding the application with annexes, the protocol of the attestation commission, the contract on education, an extract from the enrollment order and other documents formed in the course of the extern's attestation, kept according to the University's instruction on personal files [17].

## Terms of the attestation and the individual curriculum

- The terms of the extern's interim and state final attestation are determined by the attestation commission depending on the number of disciplines (modules), practices, other components of the curriculum and the state final attestation declared for it; the number of attestation tests per calendar month must not be more than ten [18].
- After enrollment, within a term set by the University but not later than one month from enrollment, the extern's individual curriculum is approved in the established order; it is developed in agreement with the extern and contains the extern's surname, name and patronymic (if any), the field of study, the focus (profile) of the educational programme, the list of attested disciplines (modules), practices and other components of the curriculum and of the state final attestation, and the planned dates of attestation for each of them [19].
- The individual curriculum for interim attestation includes the days set aside for passes, examinations, defence of course works (projects) and reports on all types of practices provided by the curriculum; the individual curriculum for state final attestation includes the days set aside for consultations, the state examination and the defence of the final qualification work [20].
- The University gives the extern access to the teaching-methodical materials for all disciplines (modules), practices and other components to be attested and for the state final attestation, including work programmes, practice programmes, assessment materials, access to electronic library systems and other materials needed for successful passing [21].

## Interim attestation

- Interim attestation comprises the forms of interim attestation established by the curriculum for disciplines (modules), practices and other components of the curriculum [22].
- It is conducted according to the local acts of the University on the forms, frequency and procedure of ongoing assessment and interim attestation [23].
- Interim attestation is received by the teacher of the University (or a person engaged to deliver the programmes on other terms) to whom the teaching of the discipline (module) or practice is assigned; learning results are assessed according to the University's grading system and recorded by the teacher in the interim attestation sheet [24].

## State final attestation and documents issued

- State final attestation comprises preparation for and passing the state examination, carrying out and defending the final qualification work, or other forms provided by the federal state educational standards and the educational programmes [22]. It is held within the terms of the academic calendar, according to the University's local acts on state final attestation [23].
- An extern who has fully completed the individual curriculum for interim attestation and has no academic debt is admitted to the state final attestation; admission is formalised by an order of the Director or of a person authorised by the Director [25].
- On the extern's personal application the topic of the final qualification work is approved and a supervisor of the work is assigned from among the University's full-time teachers [26].
- On successful passing of the state final attestation the extern is issued a document on education and qualification of the form established by the federal executive body responsible for higher education policy [27]; a certified copy of that document is placed in the extern's personal file [28].
- Externs who did not pass the state final attestation or received unsatisfactory results there, and externs who mastered only part of the programme and were expelled, are issued a certificate of study (справка об обучении) of the form established by the University [29].

## Expulsion and entry into force

- An extern may be expelled on the grounds established by the University's local act on the procedure and grounds of expulsion of students [30].
- After expulsion the extern's personal file is transferred to the University's archive according to the established rules and terms, by an act of acceptance and transfer [31].
- The Regulation enters into force from the day of its approval by the Director or a person authorised by the Director and is valid until cancelled or replaced by a new one [32], [33]; matters it does not regulate are settled by the legislation of the Russian Federation and the Charter [34], and staff and students bear responsibility for non-performance of its requirements as provided by legislation, the Labour Code, the Charter and other local acts [35].

## The application form (annex to the Regulation)

The annex is a form of application addressed to the Director (the form names K.V. Semenikhin), asking to be enrolled as an extern for interim and state final attestation from a stated date in a bachelor, master or aspirantura programme, with the code and name of the field of study and the profile [1]. It carries the applicant's surname, citizenship, name, patronymic, date and place of birth, identity document data (type, series, number, when and by whom issued, subdivision code), postal address, e-mail, СНИЛС and contact telephone [1].

The form offers two alternative grounds to choose from — studying in the form of self-education (with the details of the certificate(s) on the study period confirming earlier interim attestations) or having studied under a programme without state accreditation (with the details of the education document) — and a field for the need for special conditions at the attestation because of health limitations or disability, with the supporting document [1]. The applicant confirms familiarisation with the Charter, with the licence and state accreditation information and with the educational programme, with this Regulation, the procedure for providing paid educational services, the internal rules for students and other local acts, and confirms the accuracy of the data; the form records the attached documents, the date of receipt of documents and the official who received them [1].

## Externs in the attestation regulations

- The Regulation on the procedure of state final attestation in bachelor and master programmes, approved on 26 January 2026 [36], provides that persons mastering the educational programme in the form of self-education, or who studied under a higher education programme without state accreditation, have the right to pass the state final attestation as an extern at the University in the corresponding state-accredited programme, in accordance with that Regulation [37].
- The Regulation on the procedure of state final attestation in secondary vocational education programmes, approved on 28 May 2025 [38], contains the same rule for secondary vocational education programmes [39].

# Attachment to the university for taking candidate exams

## What the procedure governs

The Порядок прикрепления лиц для сдачи кандидатских экзаменов was approved by order of the University of 1 October 2025 № ОД/УД-03/2025/10/01, signed by the rector A.V. Gasnikov [1]. It establishes the rules for attaching persons to the University to take candidate exams without completing programmes of training of research and research-pedagogical staff in aspirantura, the term of attachment and the list of candidate exams [1]. It was developed in accordance with the federal law of 27 July 2006 № 152-ФЗ on personal data, the federal law of 29 December 2012 № 273-ФЗ on education, the government decree of 24 September 2013 № 842, the order of the Ministry of Education and Science of 28 March 2014 № 247 and its order of 24 February 2021 № 118, and other acts [1].

## The exams

The list of candidate exams comprises history and philosophy of science; a foreign language; and the special discipline corresponding to the topic of the thesis for the degree of candidate of sciences [2]. Candidate exams are a form of assessing how far the applicant for the degree of candidate of sciences is prepared to conduct research in the specific scientific specialty and branch of science in which the thesis is being or has been prepared [3].

## Who may be attached

Persons with higher education confirmed by a specialist's or master's diploma are attached to the University to take candidate exams [4]. Attachment is made in the scientific specialty and branch of science provided by the nomenclature of scientific specialties approved by the Ministry of Science and Higher Education, in which the thesis is prepared [5].

## Deadlines and term of attachment

Documents are accepted from 1 to 25 September and from 1 to 20 February every year [6]. Attachment lasts no more than 6 (six) months: from 1 October to 31 March, or from 1 March to 31 August [7]. Candidate exams in the foreign language, history and philosophy of science and the special discipline are held within the period of interim assessment of aspirants according to the calendar academic schedule, within the term of attachment [8].

## Documents

The package is submitted to the structural subdivision responsible for the training of research and research-pedagogical staff in aspirantura and includes [9]:

- an application for attachment, on the form of Appendix 1 [9];
- consent to the processing of personal data, on the form of Appendix 2 [9];
- a copy of the identity document of the person attached [9];
- a copy of the document of higher education held by that person and of its annex [9];
- where necessary, a copy of the document on recognition of education obtained abroad [9].

The package is submitted in person or electronically by email; when it is submitted electronically, the original application is sent through postal operators; on personal submission the person may present the originals, and the subdivision itself makes the copies [9]. No fee is charged from attaching persons for submitting these documents [10]. If the application lacks some information and/or the documents required by clause 2.6 are not submitted in full, the University returns the documents to the person [11].

## Orders on attachment and отчисление

Within 5 (five) working days from the end of document acceptance an order of the pro-rector – head of the department of academic policy and higher education, or of a person authorised by him, on attachment is issued, on the form of Appendix 3 [12]. Within 5 (five) working days from the end of the term of attachment, of the passing of the candidate exams or of the passing of a candidate exam, an order on the отчисление of the attached persons is issued, on the form of Appendix 4 [13]. The procedure for conducting candidate exams and for forming the commissions that conduct them is regulated by a separate local normative act of the University [14].

## The application and the personal data consent

The application of Appendix 1 is addressed to the Director of the University and asks to attach the person for a stated period to take candidate exams without completing an aspirantura programme, indicating history and philosophy of science, the foreign language and the special discipline, and the code and name of the scientific specialty and the branch of science in which the thesis is prepared or has been prepared; it lists the attached copies, records that the person is familiar with this Порядок and with the Порядок проведения кандидатских экзаменов, and contains consent to personal data processing [1].

The consent of Appendix 2 is given for the purposes of attachment to the University for a candidate exam and of taking the candidate exam, covers the listed personal data (name, date and place of birth, identity document data, data from the education document, registration address, telephone, email), is valid indefinitely in accordance with the letter of the Ministry of Science and Higher Education of 15 August 2023 № МН-3/7485, may be withdrawn by a statement to the University in any convenient form, and leaves the University the right to continue processing without the consent on the grounds of the law on personal data [1].

## Final provisions

The Порядок is approved by order of the University director or of a person authorised by him and comes into force from the moment of its approval [15]; changes and additions to it are made by the same order [16].

# State academic, social and increased social scholarships — who receives them, how they are appointed and when they stop

## State academic scholarship and state scholarship for aspirants

The state academic scholarship and the state scholarship for aspirants (together called the state scholarship) are appointed to students studying full-time on places financed from federal budget allocations [1].

A student to whom a state scholarship is appointed must meet these requirements: there must be no grade «удовлетворительно» following the results of interim assessment, and there must be no academic debt [2].

- All first-year students studying full-time at the expense of federal budget allocations receive the state scholarship from the date they are enrolled among the students or aspirants of the University until the month in which the first interim assessment ends, according to the calendar study schedule [3].
- Foreign citizens and stateless persons studying within the quota set by the Government of the Russian Federation receive the state scholarship for the whole period of study, regardless of their success in studies [4].
- The payment of the state scholarship is suspended from the first day of the month following the month in which academic leave, maternity leave or childcare leave until the child reaches the age of three is granted, and resumes from the first day of the month in which the academic leave ends, on the results of interim assessment that the student had on the date the leave was granted [5].
- The payment stops from the first day of the month following the month in which the student receives a grade «удовлетворительно» during interim assessment or an academic debt arises [6].
- If a student is granted holidays on his application after successfully passing the state final certification, the appointment of the state scholarship continues during those holidays until the student is expelled from the University [7].
- The payment stops at the moment of the student's expulsion; the amount for the month in which the expulsion happens is determined in proportion to the number of days from the first day of the month to the date of expulsion [8].

## Who receives the state social scholarship

The state social scholarship is appointed to students studying full-time on places financed from federal budget allocations who are [9]:

- orphans and children left without parental care, persons from among orphans and children left without parental care, and persons who lost both parents or the only parent during their studies [9]
- disabled children, persons disabled of groups I and II, and persons disabled since childhood [9]
- persons exposed to radiation as a result of the catastrophe at the Chernobyl nuclear power station and other radiation catastrophes and as a result of nuclear tests at the Semipalatinsk site [9]
- persons disabled as a result of a military injury or illness received during military service, and veterans of combat actions [9]
- persons who have received state social assistance [9]
- citizens who served under contract for the term stated in the clause in the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation and in the other troops and formations listed there, in military positions to be filled by soldiers, sailors, sergeants and starshinas, and were discharged on the grounds listed there [9]

## Appointment and ending of the state social scholarship

- The scholarship is appointed by an order of the director of the University or of another person authorised by him from the day the document confirming that the student belongs to one of the categories is submitted to the University, until the month in which the ground for its appointment ceases (except for persons who received state social assistance) [10].
- If the confirming document is indefinite, the state social scholarship is appointed until the end of the studies [10].
- To students who received state social assistance the scholarship is appointed from the day the document confirming the appointment of the state social assistance is submitted, for one year from the day that assistance was appointed, and the document is accepted on the basis of the student's application [10].
- Being on academic leave, on maternity leave or on childcare leave until the child reaches the age of three is not a ground for stopping or not appointing the state social scholarship [10].

## Increased state social scholarship (ГССПР)

- The state social scholarship in an increased amount is appointed to students of the first and second courses who have grades «отлично» or «хорошо», or «отлично» and «хорошо», and belong to a category of persons entitled to the state social scholarship, or who are students under 20 years of age having only one parent — a person disabled of group I [10].
- It is appointed by an order of the director of the University or of another person authorised by him on the results of interim assessment from the day the confirming document is submitted, until the month in which the ground for its appointment ceases (except for persons who received state social assistance) [10].
- The payment of the ГССПР stops from the day following the day on which the documents confirming compliance with the conditions of the categories end, and, for students under 20 years of age having only one parent — a person disabled of group I, from the day they reach the age of 20; a student is considered to have reached that age on the day corresponding to the date of birth [10].
- Being on academic leave, on maternity leave or on childcare leave until the child reaches the age of three is not a ground for stopping or not appointing the increased state social scholarship [10].

## Amounts from 1 January to 31 August 2026

The amounts of the state scholarships for that period are set by the order of the director of 2 December 2025 № ОД/УД-04/2025/12/02 «Об утверждении размеров государственных стипендий» [11]. The order is issued under the Federal Law of 28 November 2025 № 426-ФЗ «О федеральном бюджете на 2026 год и на плановый период 2027 и 2028 годов» and under the Regulation on scholarships and other forms of material support of students and aspirants approved by the order of 28 May 2025 № ОД/АД-01/2025/05/28, and on the basis of a decision of the scholarship commission (minutes of 28 November 2025 № 202) [11].

All the amounts below are set for the period from 1 January to 31 August 2026 and apply to students studying full-time on places financed from federal budget allocations [12]:

- state academic scholarship, bachelor's and master's level — 3 500 roubles [12]
- state scholarship for aspirants, level of training of highly qualified personnel — 11 000 roubles [13]
- increased state academic scholarship, bachelor's and master's level — 12 000 roubles [14]
- state social scholarship, bachelor's and master's level — 4 000 roubles [15]
- state social scholarship in an increased amount, bachelor's and master's level — 15 500 roubles [16]

Control over the execution of the order is placed on the vice-rector — head of the department of academic policy and higher education Ye.S. Bobrov; the order is signed by the director D.V. Vandyukov [17].

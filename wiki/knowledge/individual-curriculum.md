# Individual curriculum (индивидуальный учебный план) for bachelor, master and aspirantura students

## The document

The Regulation on study under an individual curriculum (индивидуальный учебный план) at ANO VO «Innopolis University» for bachelor, master and aspirantura programmes was approved by the University's order No. ОД/УД-11/2022/08/02 of 2 August 2022 [1] and comes into force on 1 September 2022 [2]. It regulates the conditions, grounds and procedure for transferring students of bachelor, master and programmes for the training of scientific and scientific-pedagogical staff in aspirantura to study under an individual curriculum [3]. It was developed on the basis of Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ, the orders of the Ministry of Science and Higher Education No. 245 of 6 April 2021 and of the Ministry of Education and Science No. 1259 of 19 November 2013, the federal state educational standards, the Charter and other local acts [4]. Changes and additions are made by order of the director of the University or of a person authorised by him [5].

## What an individual curriculum is

- An individual curriculum is a curriculum that ensures the mastery of the educational programme on the basis of individualisation of its content, taking into account the characteristics and educational needs of the particular student [6].
- It determines the list, the labour intensity, the sequence and the distribution over study periods of subjects, courses, disciplines (modules), practices and other types of study activity and, unless otherwise established by law, the forms of interim assessment of the particular student [7].

## When study follows an individual curriculum

Study under an individual curriculum is carried out in the following cases [8]:

- transfer of a student to the University from another educational organisation [8];
- transfer from one direction of training of the educational programme to another [8];
- transfer from one focus (profile) of the educational programme to another [8];
- transfer from one form of study to another [8];
- the student's return from academic leave [8];
- reinstatement of a previously expelled person among the University's students [8];
- participation of a student in academic mobility programmes [8];
- transfer of a student to accelerated study [8];
- credit for study results in disciplines (modules) and other components mastered by the student in other educational organisations or within the University [8];
- study under aspirantura programmes developed in accordance with the federal state educational standards of higher education [8];
- other cases by decision of the attestation commission (аттестационная комиссия) [8].

The decision on study under an individual curriculum is taken by the attestation commission and approved by order of the director of the University or of another person authorised by the director; the rules of work of the attestation commission are determined by a local normative act of the University [9]. Transfer to accelerated study follows the local acts regulating accelerated study [10].

## Transfer to the University from another organisation

The person wishing to transfer submits to the structural unit responsible for admitting students an application addressed to the director in the form set by the local act on transfer from other organisations, attaching a certificate of the period of study (справка о периоде обучения) [11]. Not later than ten working days from receipt of the documents the University organises the work of the attestation commission, which checks the documents against the requirements, determines the disciplines (modules), practices and research that would be credited on transfer and sets the date from which the applicant would be admitted to study [12]. Within 5 working days from the decision the admissions unit issues the applicant a certificate of transfer [13]. After expulsion from the other organisation the applicant provides an extract from the expulsion order made in connection with the transfer and the document of previous education not later than three working days from receiving them [14]. Within five working days after receiving the documents an order is issued enrolling the applicant by transfer, and the individual curriculum is approved for him [15].

## Transfer between programmes, profiles or forms of study

Transfer from one direction of training to another and from one profile of the educational programme to another is allowed only after the student has passed the first interim assessment and where there are no academic debts [1]. The student submits an application addressed to the director in the form set by the local act on transfer to the unit responsible for organising educational activity not later than seven working days before the start of the semester [16]. If the list, labour intensity and periods of mastering the components and the calendar study schedule of the two programmes correspond, the student may be transferred to study under the base curriculum [17]. The attestation commission determines the components to be credited, decides on the possibility of transfer and sets the date of transfer; the decision is communicated to the student in writing under his personal signature and/or as an electronic document through the University's electronic information-educational environment within ten working days after it is taken [18]. An order on the transfer is then issued and the individual curriculum is approved [19].

## Reinstatement after expulsion

Reinstatement is made on the personal application of the previously expelled person in the form set by the local act, submitted not later than 30 days before the start of the semester in which reinstatement is planned [20]. The attestation commission establishes the difference between the components mastered earlier and the curriculum in force for the programme to which the person wants to return and decides on the possibility of reinstatement and on credit of the results [21]. If that direction or profile is not implemented, reinstatement is made to another direction or profile by agreement with the person [22]. An order on reinstatement is then issued and the individual curriculum is approved [23].

## Return from academic leave

The student submits an application for exit from academic leave to the responsible structural unit, addressed to the director, in the form set by the local act on academic leave [24]. The attestation commission establishes the difference between the components mastered earlier and the curriculum in force and decides on transfer to the base or the individual curriculum after the leave and on credit of the results [25]. If the direction or profile is not implemented, the exit is made to another direction or profile by agreement with the student [26]. An order admits the student to study after the leave and approves the individual curriculum [27].

## Academic mobility and credit for previous study

After an academic mobility programme the student submits, within three working days of completing it, an application for credit of the results obtained in the other organisation, addressed to the director; the attestation commission carries out the credit on the basis of the document of study issued after the mobility [28], [29], and an order on credit is issued and the individual curriculum is approved [30].

For credit of results obtained in other organisations or within the University the student submits an application addressed to the director not later than fourteen calendar days before the start of the semester, attaching a document of education and (or) qualification or a document of study; students admitted to the first course submit it in the first semester or trimester not later than fourteen calendar days after the start of study [31]. The decision on credit is taken by the attestation commission [32]; after it an order on credit is issued and the individual curriculum is approved [33].

## Aspirantura

The procedure for study under an individual curriculum in aspirantura is determined by the local act regulating study in aspirantura programmes [34]. For each aspirantura student an individual curriculum is formed on the basis of the curriculum; it ensures the mastery of the programme by individualising its content and (or) the schedule of study, taking into account the student's level of readiness and the topic of his research work [35]. It is developed by the University and approved by order of the director or an authorised person after the student is assigned a research supervisor and the topic of the research work is approved, not later than 3 months after enrolment in the aspirantura programme [36].

## At the student's own request

A student may be transferred to an individual curriculum in order to individualise the content of the programme with account of his characteristics and educational needs on the basis of his personal application [37]. The application, in the form of the annex to the Regulation, is submitted to the director not later than fourteen calendar days before the start of the semester; first-course students submit it in the first semester or trimester not later than fourteen calendar days after the start of study [38]. The attestation commission considers the possibility of the transfer and either recommends it or refuses it [39], [40]. It may refuse where the student breached the application deadlines, where the annual volume of the programme would be exceeded, where the logical sequence and content-methodological interconnection of the disciplines (modules) and other components would be broken, where the student has an academic debt, and in other cases by decision of the commission [41]. The decision is communicated to the student in writing under his personal signature and/or as an electronic document through the electronic information-educational environment within ten working days after it is taken [42]. The transfer and approval of the individual curriculum are made by order of the director or an authorised person on the basis of the commission's decision [43].

## How the plan is formed and approved

- The individual curriculum is formed on the basis of the base curriculum of the corresponding direction of training, profile, level of training, form of study and year of admission [44].
- For storage in the student's personal file it contains a title page, the calendar study schedule, the consolidated curriculum, the curriculum and summary values [45].
- It is approved by order of the director of the University or an authorised person [46].
- The calendar study schedule is approved as part of the individual curriculum or separately by order of the director or an authorised person for bachelor and master programmes [47], and by order of the director or an authorised person for aspirantura programmes [48].
- The terms of study for the forms of study, the network form, accelerated study and for disabled persons and persons with health limitations are set by the federal state educational standard [49]. The term of study under an individual curriculum cannot exceed the term set by the standard for the corresponding direction, profile, level and form of study [50].
- The volume of the programme under an individual curriculum, including accelerated study and taking credited results into account, must correspond to the volume set by the federal state educational standard [51]; the annual volume, without the credited components, cannot exceed the volume set by the standard [52].
- The individual curriculum is updated when the base curriculum is updated, and the updated plan is approved by order [53]. Changes at the student's initiative are made on his personal application, submitted not later than fourteen calendar days before the start of the semester, and a decision of the attestation commission, and approved by order [54].
- After approval the plans go to the unit responsible for the students' personal files, while electronic versions are kept by the unit organising the educational process [55].

## Studying under the plan, transfer to the next course and expulsion

The student attends classes and passes interim assessment within the terms of the calendar study schedule included in the individual curriculum or approved by order [56]. The working programmes of the disciplines (modules), practices, state final certification and other components developed for the base curriculum are used [57]. The forms, periodicity and procedure of ongoing control and interim assessment, the grading system, the procedure and deadlines for removing academic debt and the forms of state final certification are regulated by the University's local acts [58].

A student who has no academic debt in the disciplines (modules), other components, practices and research and who has fully completed the individual curriculum of the current course is transferred to the next course [59]. A student who has not removed academic debt within the established deadlines is expelled from the University as not having fulfilled the duties of conscientiously mastering the programme and completing the individual curriculum [60]. A student who has completed the programme under the individual curriculum and passed the state final certification receives the document of education and qualification [61].

A student may be transferred back to the base curriculum by order of the director or an authorised person when the correspondence of the list, labour intensity and periods of mastering the components and of the calendar schedules of the individual and the base curriculum is established, on the basis of a decision of the attestation commission; the grounds for the commission's meeting may be the student's written application or a representation of the responsible structural unit [62].

## Application form

The annex to the Regulation is the approximate application form for transfer to study under an individual curriculum, addressed to the director K.V. Semenikhin, with the applicant's personal data, course, form and level of study, direction and profile, the reason and the document attached [1]. It is agreed by the head of the Methodological Department of the Center for the Organisation of Educational Programmes for bachelor and master students and by the head of the Department for the Organisation of Programmes for the Training of Highly Qualified Personnel for aspirantura students [1].

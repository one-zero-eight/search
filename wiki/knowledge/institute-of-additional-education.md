# Institute of Additional Education (ИДО) — its Regulation and the new edition approved on 16 January 2026

## The order of 16 January 2026

The page is the University's order approving the Regulation (Положение) on the Institute of Additional Education of ANO VO «Innopolis University» in a new edition; the order is dated 16 January 2026 and numbered ОД/АД-01/2026/01/16 and was issued in order to raise the effectiveness of the activity of the University's structural subdivision [1]. By its item 1 the order approves the Regulation on the Institute of Additional Education attached to the order, in a new edition [2].

The page carries only the text of the order; the text of the Regulation itself is not reproduced on it, and the Regulation is referred to as attached to the order [1].

## Familiarisation of employees

The director of the Institute of Additional Education Yu.M. Guseva must ensure timely familiarisation, against signature, with the Regulation approved by the order — both of the subdivision's employees and of employees newly hired and transferred (moved) into the subdivision from other subdivisions of the University [3].

## The repealed edition and control

The Regulation on the Institute of Additional Education approved by the University's order of 16 October 2024 No. ОД/АД-03/2024/10/16 is declared void [4]. Control over the execution of the order, and of the Regulation approved by it, is retained by the signatory [5]. The order was signed by the Director for Development and HR Policy R.F. Valiev [1].

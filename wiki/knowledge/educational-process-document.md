# The overarching educational process document and its version for an academic year

## What the document is

The document is the overarching document summarizing the educational process in place at Innopolis University [1].

## Who updates it

The document is permanently updated by a permanent committee working under the direction of the head of the Department of Education and of the Associate Dean for Teaching of the Faculty of Computer Science and Engineering [1].

## The version that applies in an academic year

One week before the beginning of the academic year the head of the department of education "freezes" the current version of the document, signs it and stores it in the official document repository system [1]. This version of the document is considered the current version for that academic year [1].

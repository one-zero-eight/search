# Laboratory of Multi-Agent Systems (regulation)

## What the order does

By order of 20 January 2026 № ОД/НИД-01/2026/01/20 the University approved the Regulation on the Laboratory of Multi-Agent Systems of the Centre for Scientific Research of the Research Centre of the Institute of Artificial Intelligence [1]. The order was issued because a new structural unit is being created, with the aim of optimising the University's activity [2].

## Familiarisation with the Regulation

The Director of the Research Centre (R.F. Kuleev) must ensure timely familiarisation, against signature, with the approved Regulation: of the employees of the unit, and of employees newly hired into the unit and employees transferred (moved) into the unit from other units of the University [3].

## Control

Control over the execution of the order and of the Regulation approved by it is retained by the Director for Development and HR Policy (R.F. Valiev) [4].

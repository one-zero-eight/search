# Occupational health and safety policy (Политика в области охраны труда) — aims, principles and obligations

## The document

The Policy of ANO VO «Innopolis University» in the field of occupational health and safety (Политика АНО ВО «Университет Иннополис» в области охраны труда) was approved by order of the University of 19 July 2022 [1]. The wiki copy is a draft produced by OCR of a scanned document, so numbers and dates should be checked against the PDF [1].

The University is described as a university of international level in which both educational and research activity is carried out [1].

## What the policy rests on and its aim

The policy is based on organising and improving the occupational health and safety management system, whose aim is the protection of the life and health of workers in the course of their employment, the provision of safe working conditions, the management of professional risks and the exclusion of accidents [1].

## Goals

The University's goals are:

- creating safe working conditions at workplaces [1];
- excluding accidents [1];
- preserving the life and health of workers in the course of their employment [1].

## Principles

The University's principles are:

- conformity with the main principles and directions of the state policy in the field of occupational health and safety [1];
- ensuring the priority of the life and health of workers [1];
- forming in workers the understanding that compliance with occupational health and safety requirements is an integral part of their employment [1];
- protecting the life and health of workers by preventing accidents and occupational diseases [1];
- ensuring the functioning and improvement of occupational health and safety management [1].

## Obligations

The University's obligations are:

- compliance with the requirements of the legislation of the Russian Federation [1];
- holding consultations with workers on questions of occupational health and safety [1];
- planning and carrying out measures to ensure occupational health and safety [1];
- providing appropriate and good-quality training of workers in occupational health and safety [1].

## Revision

The policy may be reviewed and adjusted when the priorities of the University's development and the conditions of its activity change [1].

# Accelerated study under an individual curriculum (bachelor, master, aspirantura) — the Regulation of 2 August 2022

## The document

The Regulation on accelerated study under an individual curriculum at ANO VO «Innopolis University» for bachelor, master and programmes for the training of scientific and pedagogical staff in aspirantura was approved by the University's order No. ОД/УД-10/2022/08/02 of 2 August 2022 [1]. It is approved by order of the director of the University or of a person authorised by the director and came into force on 1 September 2022 [2]. Changes and additions are made by order of the director of the University or of a person authorised by the director [3].

The Regulation governs the conditions, grounds and procedure for transfer to accelerated study under an individual curriculum within the main professional educational programme being mastered — a bachelor programme, a master programme or a programme for the training of scientific and pedagogical staff in aspirantura [4]. It was drawn up on the basis of the Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29 December 2012 «Об образовании в Российской Федерации», the order of the Ministry of Science and Higher Education No. 245 of 6 April 2021, the order of the Ministry of Education and Science No. 1259 of 19 November 2013, the federal state educational standards of higher education, the University's Charter and other normative and local acts [5].

## Who may be transferred to accelerated study

- In a bachelor or master programme: a student who has secondary vocational, higher or additional education and (or) studies or studied under a programme of secondary vocational, higher or additional education, and (or) has abilities and (or) a level of development allowing him to master the programme in a shorter term than the term set by the University in accordance with the federal state educational standards; by decision of the University such a student studies in an accelerated way under an individual curriculum [6].
- In an aspirantura programme: a student who has a diploma of completion of aspirantura (adjunctura) and (or) a diploma of candidate of sciences and (or) a diploma of doctor of sciences and (or) studies under another aspirantura programme, and (or) has abilities and (or) a level of development allowing him to master the aspirantura programme in a shorter term than the term set by the University in accordance with the educational standard [7].

## How the transfer is made

The transfer to accelerated study under an individual curriculum is made by order of the director of the University or of a person authorised by the director on the basis of a decision of the attestation commission (аттестационная комиссия), on the student's personal application in the form of the annex to the Regulation, provided the requirements of clauses 1.3 and 1.4 are met and after the student has been enrolled in the educational programme with the full term of study [8].

Raising the pace of mastering the programme may be applied to students who have the corresponding abilities and (or) level of development, by order of the director of the University on the basis of a decision of the University's attestation commission and on the student's personal application [9].

## How the term of study is shortened

- In bachelor and master programmes the reduction of the term is achieved by crediting the results of study in individual disciplines (modules) and (or) individual practices mastered by the student in obtaining secondary vocational and (or) higher education, and also additional education where present, and (or) by raising the pace of mastering the programme [10].
- In aspirantura programmes the reduction of the term is achieved by crediting (in the form of переаттестация), fully or partially, the results of study in individual disciplines (modules), individual practices and (or) individual types of research work, and (or) by raising the pace of mastering the aspirantura programme [11].
- Crediting of study results is carried out in accordance with the local normative acts regulating the procedure for crediting results mastered by students in other organisations carrying out educational activity and within the University [12].

## The individual curriculum under accelerated study

- It ensures the mastery of the educational programme on the basis of individualisation of its content with account of the characteristics and educational needs of the particular student, on the basis of the base curriculum of the corresponding direction of training, focus (profile), level of training, form of study and year of admission, in accordance with the federal state educational standard in force [13].
- It determines the list, labour intensity, sequence and distribution over study periods of subjects, courses, disciplines (modules), practices and other types of study activity and, unless otherwise established by law, the forms of interim assessment of the particular student [14].
- Under accelerated study the annual volume of the educational programme is calculated without the volume of the disciplines (modules) and (or) other components, including practices, whose results were credited to the student, and it cannot exceed the volume set by the federal state educational standard of higher education [15].
- As the working programmes of disciplines (modules), practices, state final certification and other components, the programmes developed for the educational programme with the full term of study at the University are used [16].
- The individual curriculum is formed by the structural subdivision responsible for implementing the educational programmes on the basis of the curriculum of the full term of study of the programme the student is enrolled in, and is approved by order of the director of the University or of a person authorised by the director [17].

## Studying, return to the full term and expulsion

- A student under an individual curriculum attends classes and passes interim assessment within the terms set by the calendar study schedule included in the individual curriculum [18].
- On a written application such a student may be transferred back to study under the educational programme with the full term of study, by order of the director of the University or of a person authorised by the director [19].
- A student under an individual curriculum who has not cleared his academic debt within the established terms is expelled from the University as not having fulfilled the duties of conscientiously mastering the educational programme and completing the individual curriculum [20].
- A student who has mastered the educational programme under the individual curriculum and has successfully passed the state final certification receives the document of education and of qualification [21].

## The application

The annex to the Regulation is the approximate form of the application for transfer to accelerated study under an individual curriculum; it is addressed to the director of ANO VO «Innopolis University» K.V. Semenikhin and states the applicant's full name, course, form of study (full-time or part-time), the code and name of the direction of training, the level (bachelor, master, aspirantura) and the focus (profile) of the programme, and the document attached (a diploma of secondary vocational, higher education, a bachelor's or master's diploma and so on), with the date and signature [1]. The form is agreed by the head of the Methodological Department of the Center for the Organisation of Educational Programmes for students of bachelor and master programmes and by the head of the Department for the Organisation of Programmes for the Training of Highly Qualified Personnel for aspirantura students [1].

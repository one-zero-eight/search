# Secondary vocational education (СПО) programmes — composition and development (University Regulation of 27 January 2025)

## The document and what it governs

The Regulation on the procedure of development and approval of secondary vocational education programmes of ANO VO «Innopolis University» was approved by order of the University of 27 January 2025 No. ОД/АД‐05/2025/01/27, signed by the Director I.I. Bariev and issued in Innopolis in 2025 [1]. It determines the requirements to the structure and content, to the order of development and to the procedure of approval and actualisation of secondary vocational education programmes — programmes of training of mid-level specialists (программы подготовки специалистов среднего звена) — implemented at the University [2].

Its basis is the federal legislation on education, including the Federal Law «Об образовании в Российской Федерации», the acts of the Ministry of Education and of the Ministry of Science and Higher Education on the organisation of educational activity under СПО programmes, on the practical training of students and on the state final certification for СПО programmes, acts of the Government and of Rosobrnadzor, the methodological recommendations on the development of basic professional and additional professional programmes taking professional standards into account, ФГОС СПО, the model programmes included in the register (ПОП СПО), the University's Charter and its local normative acts [3].

- A programme delivered on the basis of secondary general education is developed in accordance with ФГОС СПО, taking ПОП СПО into account where they exist [4].
- Where secondary vocational education is obtained on the basis of basic general education, secondary general education is obtained simultaneously within the programme; such a programme is developed in accordance with ФГОС СПО (with ПОП СПО where available) and with the federal state educational standard of secondary general education, taking the speciality into account [5].
- Within the programme students master one or several working professions or employee positions from the list of professions for which vocational training is carried out, in accordance with ФГОС СПО [6].
- The University sets the orientation (направленность) of the programme in accordance with ФГОС СПО and taking the corresponding ПОП СПО into account [7], and may itself establish additional types of activity within the variable part [8].
- Distance educational technologies and e-learning are used, and the procedure for their application is determined by the University's local normative acts [9]. Adapted programmes are developed in accordance with those local acts [10].

## What the programme consists of

An СПО programme is a complex of the main characteristics of education (volume, content, planned results) and of organisational and pedagogical conditions, presented as the curriculum, the calendar study schedule, the work programmes of disciplines (modules), other components, assessment and methodological materials, and also the work programme of upbringing, the calendar plan of upbringing work and the forms of attestation [11]. The description of the main characteristics and of the conditions of delivery is contained in the explanatory note ОХОП [12].

The programme is formed as a set of documents [13]:

- explanatory note;
- curriculum;
- calendar study schedule;
- work programmes of disciplines;
- work programmes of professional modules;
- work programmes of educational and industrial practices;
- programme of the state final certification (final certification);
- methodological materials;
- work programme of upbringing and the calendar plan of upbringing work.

The programme is developed by a team including the head of the programme (if there is one), the leading teachers taking part in delivering it and the staff of the structural unit that delivers it; academic staff, staff of the University's structural units and representatives of profile enterprises (organisations) may be involved, and the work is coordinated by the head (deputy head) of the structural unit delivering the СПО programmes [14]. Its structure and content may change because of new requirements to the organisation of the educational process or of the introduction of innovative educational technologies [15].

## Language of instruction

The language or languages of instruction are determined by the University's local normative acts in accordance with Russian legislation: the activity is carried out in the state language of the Russian Federation unless a local act provides otherwise, and secondary vocational education may be obtained in a foreign language in accordance with the programme and the procedure established by legislation on education and the University's local acts [16]. The procedure of receiving education in the language of instruction depending on the structural components of the programme is established by an order of the Director of the University or of a person authorised by him [17].

## Volume, academic hour and credit units

The volume of the programme includes all types of learning activity and is established by ФГОС СПО; the volume of the programme and of its elements is measured in academic hours, an academic hour lasting 45 minutes for all types of classes, and the volume of classes and practice must not exceed 36 academic hours a week [18]. A system of credit units may be applied: a credit unit is a unified unit of measurement of the student's workload including all the types of his activity provided by the curriculum, among them classroom and independent work and practice [19].

The University sets the size of a credit unit itself, in academic hours (with a 45-minute academic hour), within 32 to 36 academic hours unless ФГОС СПО provides otherwise; the size is uniform within the curriculum and is established by a local normative act [20]. The number of credit units of a programme does not depend on the form of study, on the use of e-learning or distance technologies, or on study under an individual curriculum including accelerated study [20]. Facultative disciplines (modules) are not included in the volume of the programme [21].

## The explanatory note (ОХОП)

The explanatory note is part of the programme [22] and includes [23]:

- a title page with the University's logo and name, the stamp of approval of the programme, the code and name of the speciality, the qualification awarded, the duration of study, the form of study, the language of instruction and the year of admission [24];
- a sheet of updates of the programme [25], the contents [26], and general provisions including the normative documents on which the programme is formed [27];
- the general characteristics of the programme — level of education, form(s) and language of study, qualification of graduates, duration of study including under an individual curriculum for disabled persons and persons with health limitations, volume, and ГИА or ИА — with an annotation of its purpose and its upbringing and learning tasks, the requirements to the level of preparation needed, and the features of delivery: network form, e-learning, distance technologies, duration of the academic hour, maximum weekly volume of classes and practice, and mastering a working profession under ФГОС СПО [28];
- the characteristics of the graduate's professional activity [29] and the results of mastering the programme, including general and professional competences [30];
- the structure and volume of the programme and the documents regulating the educational process [31], system-wide requirements to its delivery [32], its material-technical and methodological support [33], staffing conditions [34] and financial conditions [35];
- mechanisms of quality assessment, including the internal system of quality assessment of the programme [36], and the conditions of delivery for disabled persons and persons with health limitations [37].

## Curriculum

The curriculum is part of the programme [38]. It is the main document regulating the educational process: it determines the list, the volume in academic hours, the sequence and the distribution over study periods of disciplines, professional modules (ПМ) and their elements (interdisciplinary courses — МДК, educational and industrial practices), of independent work and other types of learning activity, and the forms of interim assessment and of ГИА (ИА) [39]. It is developed in accordance with ФГОС СПО, taking ПОП СПО into account, for the whole period of study, and is valid, with account of its actualisation, for the whole period of study of the students of the corresponding year of admission [40].

- The curriculum has a mandatory part and a part formed by the participants of educational relations (variable part). The mandatory part, without the volume of ГИА, must be not more than 70 per cent of the total time allotted to the programme; the variable part, of not less than 30 per cent of that time, serves the further development of general and professional competences, including by extending or introducing types of activity and the competences needed for competitiveness. The concrete ratio and the volume parameters of the study cycles and practice are determined by the University itself in accordance with ФГОС СПО and taking ПОП СПО into account [41].
- The academic hours of the variable part may be used to increase the hours of disciplines and (or) МДК of the mandatory part, to introduce new disciplines, to introduce new МДК into a ПМ, to increase the volume of educational and (or) industrial practice within a ПМ and of the pre-diploma practice, and to introduce new ПМ. New МДК must not repeat the knowledge and skills of the mandatory part, and a new ПМ must not repeat the existing ПМ either in name or in the requirements to the competences and learning results formulated [42].
- Practice has the following kinds — educational practice and industrial practice — and is delivered in the form of practical training; both may be delivered in several periods, concentrated (mainly after the theoretical study of a semester) or distributed, alternating with classes in a semester or trimester; the types of practice are set by the University itself taking ПОП СПО into account [43].
- Individual curricula are formed on the basis of the curriculum, which is then considered basic in relation to them [44]. Curricula of the part-time (заочная) and part-time-evening (очно-заочная) forms are developed on the basis of full-time curricula; the names of disciplines, modules and practices, their workload and the forms of assessment must be identical to those of the full-time curricula, while the dates of the academic year, of interim assessment, of all practices and of ГИА (ИА) are set by the schedule [45].

## Work programmes of disciplines and professional modules

The work programme of a discipline (РПД) and the work programme of a professional module (РППМ) determine the content, structure, sequence, forms, methods and results of mastering a discipline or professional module, as well as the assessment materials used to evaluate the level and quality of the formed learning results and competences [46]. They are developed for all disciplines and ПМ of the curriculum, including elective and facultative disciplines where they exist [47]. The immediate developer is the teacher to whom the discipline or ПМ is assigned, or a team of authors formed on the instruction of the head of the delivering structural unit; academic staff, staff of the University's units, methodologists and programme managers may be involved [48]. Model forms of the РПД are in appendices 3 and 4, of the РППМ in appendices 5 and 6 [49]; [50].

## Assessment funds (ФОС)

The fund of assessment means for interim assessment and for monitoring of current academic performance is part of the programme [51] and is developed for each discipline and ПМ of the programme and also for ГИА (ИА) [52]. The ФОС of each discipline is drawn up as an appendix to the corresponding РПД [53]. The ФОС of the pre-diploma practice is included in the corresponding practice programme and contains only the section «Оценочные средства и критерии оценивания результатов обучения для проведения промежуточной аттестации по практике» [54].

## Work programmes of practice

The work programme of practice (РПП) is part of the programme [55] and determines the content of the practice, its sequence and the methods of mastering the practical skills needed by the students [56]. It is developed in accordance with the order of 05.08.2020 No. 885/390, ФГОС СПО and the University's local normative acts [57], for all practices provided by the curriculum [58]. The programme of educational practice is a single document containing the programmes of the educational practices for all ПМ, and the list of educational practices must correspond to the list in the РППМ [59]; the programme of industrial practice is likewise a single document [60]. A practice programme that is not a structural element of a professional module is a separate document [61]. The immediate developer is a teacher or team of teachers to whom the practice is assigned [62].

## Programme of the state final certification

The ГИА programme is part of the programme [63] and regulates the order and rules of organising and conducting the ГИА under the programme [64]. Its purpose is to establish the conformity of the students' results of mastering the programme with the requirements of ФГОС СПО; the ГИА reveals the graduate's level of preparation for independent professional activity [65]. Its theoretical content rests on the disciplines of the study cycles and on the professional modules, and the practical skills needed for it are acquired in the professional modules and practices [66]; [67].

The programme contains a title page, general provisions, the forms, volume and dates of the ГИА, requirements to the checking of results, the structure and order of the procedures, the completion of and preparation for the defence of the diploma project (work), the features of the ГИА for disabled persons and persons with health limitations, and an appendix with the topics of diploma projects (works) and assessment materials [68]; [69]–[70].

It is developed by a team from among the leading teachers taking part in delivering the programme [71] and is approved by the University's Academic council with the participation of the chairmen of the state examination commissions, after which it is brought to the notice of the graduates not later than 6 months before the start of the ГИА [72]. For СПО programmes without state accreditation the University develops and approves a final certification (ИА) programme, drawn up in free form, with the same procedure of approval [73].

## The upbringing programme

The work programme of upbringing (РПВ) and the calendar plan of upbringing work (КПВР) are part of the programme [74] and are developed with account of the model work programme of upbringing and the model calendar plan of upbringing work [75]. The РПВ is developed with the participation of the pedagogical council, the students' council and the parents' council where they exist [76], and is approved by order of the Director or of another responsible person authorised by him before the start of the academic year [77]; the КПВР for the current academic year is a separate document approved by the same kind of order [78].

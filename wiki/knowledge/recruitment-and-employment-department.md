# Recruitment and Employment Department — place, tasks, functions and rights

## Document and place in the structure

The regulation on the Recruitment and Employment Department of the Centre for recruitment and career solutions of the University Development Directorate of ANO VO «Innopolis University» was approved by an order of the University of 1 April 2025 No. ОД/АД-01/2025/04/01, signed by the Director for Development and HR Policy R.F. Valiev [1]. It regulates the department's activity and defines its tasks, functions, rights and duties [2].

The department is a structural subdivision of the Centre for recruitment and career solutions of the University Development Directorate and belongs to the category «Иные структурные подразделения» (other structural subdivisions) [3]. It reports directly to the Director for Development and HR Policy [4].

In its work the department follows the Constitution of the Russian Federation, the Labour Code, Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29 December 2012 «Об образовании в Российской Федерации» and other legal acts on higher education institutions, the University's Charter, decisions of the Academic, Supervisory and Operational councils, orders and instructions of the University's director, other local acts and this regulation [5].

The department's structure, number and staffing table, and changes to them, are approved by the director of the University [6]. The department is headed by its head, who is hired and dismissed by order of the director [7]. Working conditions of its employees are set by their employment contracts and the University's internal labour regulations [8]; their rights, duties and degree of responsibility are set by job descriptions [9]. Employees may be involved, within their competences, in work under grants, projects, subsidies and other programmes of the University [10]. The regulation and its amendments are approved by order of the director or another authorized person [11].

## Tasks

- interaction with the University's graduates on employment matters [12]
- searching for and attracting candidates for the University's internal vacancies [13]
- searching for and attracting candidates when fulfilling requests to close vacancies for external customers on a commercial basis, within the deadlines and in the manner agreed in the contract between the University and the external customer [14]

## Functions

- ensuring graduate employment within the fulfilment of the study grant (отработка гранта на обучение) and monitoring compliance with its conditions; if a graduate breaches the grant conditions, the department hands the documents over for claims and litigation work after agreeing its actions with the management [15]
- organizing events that help graduates find employment [16]
- helping to find organizations where students can do internships [17]
- keeping statistics, analysing graduate employment indicators, monitoring, and preparing answers to requests for information about graduate employment [18]
- promoting the University's brand as a priority employer for graduate employment and internships [19]
- preparing internal local acts relating to the department's activity [20]
- collecting graduates' documents confirming employment, including employer certificates and income certificates (2-НДФЛ), in compliance with personal data legislation and with the graduates' consent to processing their personal information [21]
- preparing and providing reports within the department's functional tasks [22]
- selecting candidates for external customers when fulfilling requests, meeting the deadlines and procedure set by the contract between the University and the external customer [23]
- finding and attracting external customers to conclude contracts for staff selection services [24]
- supervising the conclusion of contracts with external customers and controlling their execution, including compliance with contract terms and fulfilment of obligations [25]

## Rights

- request from the University's structural subdivisions the information and materials needed for the department's work [26]
- with the consent of the heads of structural subdivisions, involve their employees in preparing draft local acts and other documents and in organizing and holding events held by the department [27]
- take part in meetings held by the University's management when questions within the department's competence are discussed [28]
- propose to the University's management changes and additions to local acts, as well as their cancellation [29]
- interact with state authorities and administration, legal entities and the University's subdivisions on matters within the department's competence [30]
- represent the University's interests on matters relating to the department's competences [31]

## Interaction and reporting

Interaction with other structural subdivisions is determined by the tasks and functions assigned to the department, including when preparing draft local acts, orders, instructions, contracts and other documents, and when preparing conclusions and written or oral information on matters within the competence of the Directorate [32]. The department may be a base for the practical training of the University's students [33].

On the basis of the relevant local acts, orders and instructions, the department may be responsible for preparing and keeping information, certificates and reports up to date [34]. It is responsible for providing statistical and other information established by law to the authorized bodies on request, within its competences [35].

# Admission to PhD aspirantura 2024/2025 — the rules of 15 January 2024: places, documents and examinations

## The document

The Rules of admission to higher education programmes — programmes for the training of scientific and scientific-pedagogical staff in aspirantura — at ANO VO «Innopolis University» for the 2024/2025 academic year were approved by order of the University No. ОД/АД-03/2024/01/15 of 15 January 2024 and signed by the rector A.V. Gasnikov [1]. They regulate the admission of citizens of the Russian Federation, foreign citizens and stateless persons to aspirantura programmes, including the features of entrance examinations for disabled persons [1].

They were developed on the basis of Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29 December 2012 and of the order of the Ministry of Science and Higher Education No. 721 of 06.08.2021 on the procedure of admission to aspirantura programmes; admission is announced on the basis of the licence for educational activity No. 1845 issued by the Federal Service for Supervision in Education and Science on 25 December 2015 for an indefinite term, series 90Л01 No. 0008868 [1].

## Who may apply, and to which places

- Persons with education not lower than higher (specialist or master) are admitted; the applicant presents a document of education and qualification of the established format — a document of the format set by the federal executive body in charge of education, health care or culture, a state-format document obtained before 1 January 2014, a document of the format of Moscow State University or Saint Petersburg State University or of the collegiate body of an educational organisation issued to a person who passed state final attestation, a document issued by a private organisation operating in the Skolkovo innovation centre or by an organisation of an innovative scientific-technological centre, or a foreign document of education recognised in Russia at a level not lower than specialist or master [1].
- Admission is to the first course [1].
- In 2024 the University admits within the control figures of admission at the expense of the federal budget and under contracts on paid educational services, and within the control figures a target quota for targeted training is allocated [1].
- A separate competition is held for each set of conditions of admission: separately by the profile of the aspirantura programme, i.e. by scientific specialty; separately within the control figures and under paid contracts; separately for the target quota and for the main places within the control figures left after the target quota [1].
- For all competitions within one specialty condition the University sets the same list of entrance examinations and the same minimum and maximum points, while different minimum points may be set for the control figures and for paid places [2].
- The University may hold an additional admission to vacant places; its dates are set by an act of the director or an authorised person not later than 2 working days after the completion of enrolment under clause 56 and are published on the official site [3].

## Commissions and information

The organisational support of admission is provided by the admission committee created by the University; its chair is the director of the University, who appoints a responsible secretary organising the work of the committee and the personal reception of applicants and their legal representatives; for the entrance examinations examination and appeal commissions are created, and the powers and procedure of all these commissions are determined by regulations approved by the director [4].

- Not later than 20 January of the year of admission the site publishes the admission rules with the dates of admission, the features of entrance examinations for disabled persons, the procedure of appeals, the list of individual achievements taken into account, the number of places within the control figures without the target quota, the list of entrance examinations with their maximum and minimum points, priority, form, languages and programmes, information on holding them in person and (or) with distance technologies, the places and addresses for submitting documents, the possibility of using the EPGU portal, the sample contract for paid services and information about dormitories [5].
- Not later than 10 April the site publishes the number of places within the control figures by conditions of admission with the target quota, the dates of enrolment and the number of places in dormitories for non-resident students [5].
- Not later than 2 months before the start of enrolment to paid places the site publishes the number of those places, and not later than 14 calendar days before the start of the entrance examinations the schedule of the examinations, which stays available until the end of admission [5].

The University maintains telephone lines and a section of the official site for questions about admission [6], and from the start of the acceptance of documents until the start of enrolment it publishes and updates daily the number of applications and the lists of persons who submitted documents for each competition [7].

## Application and documents

Admission is carried out on the application of the applicant, who may change it or file a second application under other conditions of admission until the end of the acceptance of documents, and the later application cancels the earlier one; the University accepts documents together with a consent to the processing of personal data [8]. By signature the applicant confirms acquaintance with the requirement to give reliable information and with the admission rules, and, when applying for places within the control figures, the absence of an aspirantura diploma, an adjunctura diploma, a certificate of completion of aspirantura or adjunctura or a candidate of sciences diploma [9]. The application states the conditions of admission and the priority of enrolment and the insurance number of the individual personal account, if there is one [10].

Together with the application the applicant presents [11]:

- an identity and citizenship document [11];
- the document of education and qualification, possibly several, including a foreign document with a certificate of recognition of foreign education where recognition is required [11];
- a document confirming registration in the individual (personalised) accounting system, if there is one [11];
- a document confirming disability, if special conditions are needed, provided it is valid on the day of the application [11];
- documents confirming individual achievements, and other documents, both at the applicant's discretion [11];
- two photographs [11].

The document of education is presented on the day of the application or later, but not later than the day of completion of the acceptance of the document of education [12]. Documents may be presented as originals or as copies (electronic images) without certification; when applying through the EPGU portal, the identity and registration documents are considered presented if the information is confirmed there, and the document of education is considered presented in copy if confirmed in the federal register of documents on education [13]. The application is presented in Russian, may be presented in English, and documents in a foreign language must be translated into Russian and legalised unless an international treaty or Russian law provides otherwise [14].

Documents may be presented in person, sent by post or sent electronically through the University's information system and the EPGU portal; the address is 420500, Republic of Tatarstan, Verkhneuslonsky municipal district, Innopolis, Universitetskaya st., 1, room 100 (admission committee); a receipt is issued for documents presented in person, and documents are accepted from 20 June 2024 to 22 August 2024 [1].

The University checks the reliability of the information and the authenticity of the documents [15]. An applicant may withdraw the documents at any stage, whereupon he is excluded from the lists and is not enrolled, or withdraw the original of the document of education, whereupon he stays in the lists; the documents or the original are returned within 5 days of the application [16]. Documents of an applicant who was not admitted are returned within 5 days of the enrolment order in the way they were presented [17].

## Entrance examinations

Admission is based on the results of entrance examinations whose list and form are given in annex 1 to the rules; each examination is assessed by the examination commission on a 100-point scale, with the minimum confirming success set at 60 points and the maximum at 100 points [18]. Each applicant takes each examination once [19], and the examinations are held in English [20]. They are held in person or with distance technologies provided the applicants are identified [21].

Examinations are held on different dates for different groups formed from the applicants who submitted documents, not less often than once a week, with one examination for a group per day [22]. Persons who did not pass an examination for a valid reason (illness or other documented circumstances) are admitted to take it in another group or on a reserve day [23]. A violation of the rules during an examination is recorded in an act on not passing the examination without a valid reason, and at an in-person examination the applicant is also removed from the place of the examination [24].

The results are announced on the official site not later than the third working day after the examination, and after a computer test the applicant may look at the checked work on the day of the announcement or during the next working day [25]. An appeal may be filed under section VI of the rules [26].

## Individual achievements

Applicants may present information about individual achievements, which is taken into account by awarding points added to the sum of competitive points and (or) as an advantage when the ranking criteria are equal; copies of the confirming documents are presented [27]. Points are awarded for [28]:

- scientific publications on the profile of the programme in Scopus over the last 3 years — 5 points for each publication, but not more than 20 points in total [28];
- scientific publications on the profile of the programme in Web of Science over the last 3 years — 5 points for each publication, but not more than 20 points in total; a paper published in both systems counts as one achievement worth 5 points [28].

The confirming document is the output data of the published article, and not more than 40 points may be awarded for individual achievements [28], [29].

## Annexes: examinations and places for 2024/2025

Annex 1 to the rules lists, for scientific specialty 1.2.2 «Математическое моделирование, численные методы и комплексы программ», two entrance examinations in order of priority — computer testing in the scientific specialty, held in writing, and an interview in the scientific specialty, held orally — both in English, with a minimum of 60 and a maximum of 100 points [1].

Annex 2 gives the number of places for the same specialty, full-time form: the figures 28, 28, 10, 0 and 18 appear for the total of places, the places within the control figures, the places of the main competition, the target quota and the places under paid contracts; in the wiki copy the columns are scrambled by the extraction, so which figure belongs to which column should be checked against the PDF [1].

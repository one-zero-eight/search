# Student Council (Совет обучающихся) — formation, structure, goals, rights and duties

## Status, basis and who takes part

The Regulation on the Student Council (Совет обучающихся) of ANO VO «Innopolis University» was approved by order of the University of 1 June 2017 № АД-01/2017/06/01 and signed by A.G. Tormasov, Innopolis, 2017 [1].

The Student Council is a body of student self-government — a special form of initiative, independent and responsible public activity of students aimed at solving important questions of the students' life and at developing their social activity [2]. The Regulation determines the procedure of forming the Council, its structure, goals and tasks, rights and duties, on the basis of п. 2 ст. 12 of Federal Law № 125-ФЗ, п. 2 ст. 35 of Federal Law № 12-ФЗ and other local acts of the University [3].

All students of the University may take part in the Council's activity, and only a current student of the University may be a member of the Council [4]. The Council is guided by the principles of association, voluntariness, autonomy, partnership, education and development, electivity and rotation of the leadership, and it follows the Constitution of the Russian Federation, Russian legislation, the Charter and other local acts of the University [5]. Its activity is aimed at all students of the University [6].

## Goals and tasks

The main goals of the Council are [7]:

- protection and representation of the rights and interests of the University's students [7];
- ensuring the exercise of the right for the students' opinion to be taken into account on questions of managing the University and in adopting local acts that affect students' rights [7];
- representing the students' interests and ensuring their rights in interaction with the administration and the teaching staff of the University [7];
- forming students' skills of self-government and preparing them for competent and responsible participation in the life of society [7];
- improving the quality of the organisation of the study process [7];
- forming a civic culture and an active civic position of students, promoting their social maturity, independence and ability to self-organise and self-develop [7].

Its tasks are [8]:

- to establish a dialogue between the students and the University administration for cooperation on educational, cultural, social and everyday questions affecting students [8];
- to draft proposals for improving the quality of the educational process taking the students' interests into account [8];
- to interact with the University in solving educational and scientific tasks, in organising leisure and everyday life and in other questions affecting students' interests [8];
- to preserve and develop the traditions of the University's student community [8];
- to strengthen links within the student community and to promote inter-university, inter-regional and international links [8];
- to develop the students' skills of managerial activity [8];
- to identify the creative and managerial potential of students and to realise it through participation in student self-government [8].

## Formation and the Conference of students

The Council is created on the initiative of students [9]. Every student of the University has the right to elect and to be elected to the Council in accordance with the Regulation [10]. The Council is formed by elections at the Conference of students of the University at least once every two years, and the decision on the number of members of the Council is taken at the Conference [11].

The Conference may consider [12]:

- the election of the members of the Council [13];
- the approval of the Regulation on the Council and the introduction of amendments and additions to it [14];
- planning, setting the strategic course of the Council's development and its lines of work, and changing the Council's structure [15];
- other questions connected with the Council's activity [16].

The Conference is competent if at least 2/3 of the number of delegates are present [17]. Decisions on the questions put to the Conference are taken by a simple majority of the delegates present, unless the Regulation provides otherwise [18]. The delegates of the Conference are representatives from among the students — one student from each academic group of the University at all levels of study [19]. The delegates are elected at local meetings by simple majority of votes [20].

## Structure of the Council

- The Council consists of the chairman, the administrative block (SO Core), the organisational block (SO Active) and the development and support block (SO Mentors) [21].
- The chairman, the members of the administrative and organisational blocks and the members of the development and support block are elected at the first meeting of the newly elected Council by simple majority, provided at least 2/3 of the Council take part in the meeting; the elections are open [22].
- The chairman is elected for a term of two years [23].
- The administrative block forms the administrative structure, creates meetings for the participants, develops and supports media content and the social networks of the organisation, represents students' interests at meetings with the University administration and regulates and directs the activity of the organisational block; it determines the priority lines of the Council's activity [22].
- The organisational block is engaged in organising events, project activity and volunteering [23].
- The development and support block acts as consultant, mentor and preceptor for the SO Core and SO Active lines [24].
- Each meeting of an association with a vote on a question raised must have the corresponding Protocol attached (Annex 1) [25].
- The grounds for early termination of the duties of the chairman or of a member of the Council and for electing a new chairman or member are expulsion from the University and a decision of more than 50% of the members of the Council [26].

## Rights and duties

The Council has the right to [27]:

- take part in developing and improving local acts affecting the interests of the University's students [27];
- take part in assessing the quality of the educational process and prepare and submit proposals to the University's management bodies on optimising it, taking the students' scientific and professional interests into account [27];
- take part in solving social, everyday and financial questions affecting students' interests, including the distribution of the funds of the scholarship fund [27];
- take part in considering questions connected with students' breaches of academic discipline and of the internal regulations of the University and of the student halls of residence [27];
- take part in developing and implementing the system of rewards for students for achievements in various spheres of academic and extracurricular activity [27];
- use, in the established procedure, the information at the disposal of the University's management bodies [27];
- use the material and technical base of the University in agreement with the University's department for support and development of students [27];
- where the rights and freedoms of students or the rights of the Student Council are breached or restricted, submit proposals to the University's management bodies on taking measures to restore the breached rights and on applying disciplinary measures to those responsible [27];
- have its own logo, emblem, letterheads, stamp and other symbols approved in the established procedure [27];
- take direct part in planning, preparing, holding and analysing the University's extracurricular events [27].

The Council is obliged to [28]:

- be guided in its activity by Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29.12.2012 «Об образовании в Российской Федерации» and other legal acts in the field of education, the University's Charter, the Rules of internal order for students, the Regulation and other local acts of the University [28];
- assist the University's management bodies in questions of organising educational activity and extracurricular activity [28];
- represent and protect the interests of students before the University's management bodies, state bodies, public associations and other organisations and institutions [28];
- carry out work aimed at raising the students' awareness and their exactingness as to the level of their knowledge, and at fostering a careful attitude to the University's property [28];
- support socially significant initiatives of students [28];
- inform the University's management bodies of the corresponding level about its activity [28].

## Annex 1: the protocol of a meeting

Annex 1 to the Regulation gives the form of the protocol of a meeting of the Student Council: the number and date of the meeting, the number of students and of staff present, the agenda of the meeting and the results of voting («за», «против», «воздержался») [1].

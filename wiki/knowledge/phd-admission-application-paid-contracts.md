# Application for PhD aspirantura admission under paid education contracts — sample form

## What the form is

The page is the sample application (образец заявления) for admission to study on aspirantura programmes — programmes of training of scientific and scientific-pedagogical staff — under contracts for the provision of paid educational services; it is addressed to the director of ANO VO «Innopolis University», K.V. Semenikhin [1]. The University's address in the form is 420500, Innopolis, Universitetskaya st., 1 [1]. The form ends with the date line «___» __________________ 2024 г. and the applicant's signature and full name [1].

## Personal data the applicant gives

The application contains the applicant's surname, name and patronymic (if any), sex, date and place of birth, registered address, citizenship, identity document (type, series, number, by whom issued, department code), the insurance number of the individual personal account (SNILS, if any) and the applicant identifier [1].

## Education and programme chosen

- On education: the name of the completed educational institution, the year of graduation, and the document of education and qualification with its series and number [1].
- The applicant asks to be admitted to the competition for study on the first course of the following programmes of training of scientific and scientific-pedagogical staff in aspirantura, with the number, scientific specialty, form of study, basis of study and grounds of admission [1].
- In the sample the row is filled in as scientific specialty 1.2.2 Mathematical modelling, numerical methods and software complexes, full-time, on places under contracts for the provision of paid educational services on the general competition, at the expense of extra-budgetary funds [1].

## Entrance examinations

The applicant asks to be admitted to the entrance examinations conducted by Innopolis University itself, giving for each examination its name, form, language (Russian or English) and place [1]. In the sample these are computer testing on scientific specialty 1.2.2 Mathematical modelling, numerical methods and software complexes and an interview on the same scientific specialty, both with the use of distance technologies at the address of residence [1].

## Special conditions at entrance examinations

An applicant who needs special conditions at the entrance examinations because of health limitations or disability marks this in the application, lists the entrance examinations and gives the details of the document confirming the disability or health limitation that requires the special conditions [1]. The form offers to mark the needed conditions [1]:

- written assignments in relief-dot (Braille) script, or as an electronic document readable with a computer with specialised software for the blind, or read aloud by an assistant [1];
- a set of writing tools and paper for writing in Braille, or a computer with specialised software for the blind, or an assistant for dictation [1];
- individual even lighting of not less than 300 lux [1];
- a magnifying device for taking the internal entrance examinations [1];
- assignments and instructions on the procedure of the entrance examinations in enlarged font [1];
- collective-use sound-amplifying equipment [1];
- individually used sound-amplifying equipment [1];
- the services of a sign language interpreter [1];
- the services of a tactile sign language interpreter [1];
- a computer with specialised software for persons with musculoskeletal disorders, impaired motor functions of the upper limbs or absence of the upper limbs, or dictation to an assistant [1].

## Individual achievements, dormitory and return of originals

- The applicant may mark individual achievements; in the sample the options are scientific publications in the profile (focus) of the master's programme — as the form words it — in the international database Web of Science over the last 3 years and in the international database Scopus over the last 3 years [1].
- The applicant may state that they need a place to live in a dormitory [1].
- The applicant chooses the way the submitted originals are returned if they are not enrolled — in person, by Russian Post or by courier service — and gives a postal address; this is not indicated when the documents are submitted in electronic form [1].

## What the applicant confirms and undertakes

The form contains acknowledgements, each confirmed by the applicant's signature [1]:

- acquaintance with the admission rules approved by Innopolis University itself, including the rules for filing an appeal on the results of the entrance examinations conducted by the University itself, with the charter, with the date of issue and registration number of the licence for educational activity, with the date of issue and registration number of state accreditation for the educational programmes delivered, with the educational programmes and other documents governing the organisation and carrying out of educational activity, and with the rights and duties of students [1];
- acquaintance with the dates of completion of admission of the document of the established form [1];
- acquaintance with the requirement to indicate reliable information in the application for admission and to submit genuine documents [1];
- an undertaking to provide the certificate of recognition of foreign education and confirmation of the legalisation procedure not later than the day of submitting the document of the established form; this is filled in by applicants who submitted a document (documents) of a foreign state on education or on education and qualification when those procedures are needed [1].

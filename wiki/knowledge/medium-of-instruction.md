# Medium of instruction

## English as the medium of instruction

Innopolis University offers the opportunity to study Computer Science and Engineering in English, developing fluency and command of technical and non-technical vocabulary; this is made possible by the University's international community of staff and students who speak several foreign languages, in particular English, on a daily basis [1]. The University uses English as the language of instruction for all academic activities [1]. All classes, teaching, labs, assignments, exercises, and practical material are provided in English [1]. English is expected to be used in the following situations [1]:

- Frontal classes, including questions [1].
- Frontal labs, including questions [1].
- Class and labs material [1].
- Technical and non-technical assignments [1].
- Theses and projects [1].
- Written and oral exams [1].
- Group office hours [1].
- Public chats related to university life [1].
- Emails to students or colleagues [1].
- Student groups [1].
- Any other document related to education [1].

## Rationale

English is important for every graduate for three reasons [1]:

- English as a communication means: it is the language of international communication, enabling graduates to travel, encounter new cultures and ideas, and exchange technical knowledge [1].
- English as a language of employment: language skills are important for the international job market and for high-profile employment in the Russian Federation; English skills are desirable to employers in any country [1].
- English as a language of science: fluency allows graduates to access scientific literature and attend international conferences, which is essential for a scientific career [1].

## Language levels and support for students

To gain admission to Innopolis University, an applicant must provide evidence of specific language levels based on the specific programme of enrollment [1]. Over the period of study the English level is expected to grow [1]. The University supports students in this path through, among other things, English courses, communication courses, technical essays in English, lab internships and international projects [1].

## Exceptions

Specific exceptions can be made by the Department of Education for courses that need to comply with laws and regulations of the Russian Federation (for example "Safety") [1]. The University commits to providing the opportunity to learn the material of these courses to all international students [1].

Instructors who are fluent in Russian and possess the appropriate established terminology used in Russian industry and research may familiarize students with bilingual terminology [1].

## Other languages

Being English the official medium of instruction does not prevent the use of other languages, and in particular Russian, in situations where two or more people share a common language [1]. English is not required in situations such as informal conversation (questions after classes), individual office hours, private emails, and private chats [1]. Other shared languages can be effectively used in point-to-point communication whenever there is certainty that no involved participants could be excluded for lack of fluency in the used language [1].

## Status of the document

Staff, teachers and students commit to use English for classes and other major educational situations [1]. The manifesto presents itself as a chapter of the teaching regulations that collects suggested practices for staff, teachers and students [1]. It is listed among the key university documents collected on the Overall Regulations page [2].

# Attestation of teaching staff (ППС) — the attestation commission, its procedure and decisions

## The document

The Regulation on the attestation commission for attesting workers who hold positions of pedagogical workers belonging to the teaching staff (профессорско-преподавательский состав) of ANO VO «Innopolis University» was approved by order of 30 December 2020, signed by K.V. Semenikhin [1]. It determines how the attestation commission (Аттестационная комиссия) is formed and the rules of its activity [2].

The procedure of attesting teaching staff is set by this Regulation in accordance with the order of the Ministry of Education and Science of the Russian Federation of 30 March 2015 No. 293 «Об утверждении Положения об аттестационной комиссии по проведению аттестации работников, занимающих должности педагогических работников, относящихся к профессорско-преподавательскому составу» [3].

The page is a scan read by OCR, and its front matter warns that numbers and dates should be checked against the PDF before relying on them [1]; the final section of the page also runs «6.2» twice, so the numbering there is unreliable [4].

The attestation confirms that a teacher corresponds to the position held: under the Regulation on the competition for teaching staff positions, teachers with contracts for an indefinite term do not pass a competition for that position while they fill it, and attestation is held once every 5 years to confirm that they correspond to it [5].

## Who is not attested

The following workers are not subject to attestation [6]:

- those who have worked in the position held for less than 2 years in the University [6];
- pregnant women [6];
- women on pregnancy and childbirth leave [6];
- those on childcare leave until the child reaches the age of 3 years [6];
- those absent from the workplace for more than 4 months in a row because of illness [6].

Workers under the pregnancy and childcare grounds may be attested not earlier than 2 years after their return from those leaves; workers absent through illness — not earlier than a year after their return to work [6].

## The commission and its members

The commission consists of a chairman (as a rule the rector or a vice-rector), a deputy chairman (as a rule a vice-rector or a dean), a secretary (as a rule an employee of the personnel department or another person competent in personnel policy) and members in a number of not less than 5 (five) people, as a rule deans of faculties, professors, heads of institutes and so on [7].

- The composition is formed so that a conflict of interest that could influence the commission's decisions is excluded [8].
- All members have equal rights in taking decisions [9]. The chairman directs the commission's work, and in his absence the deputy chairman [10].
- The secretary keeps the minutes of the sitting, or, if the secretary is absent, a member appointed by the chairman; the minutes follow the form of Annex 1 [11].
- The commission may engage qualified experts in various fields, including employees of third-party organisations [12].

## Preparing an attestation

The procedure of attestation is approved by order of the University director or of another person authorised by him; the order appoints the place, date and time of the attestation, establishes the personal composition of the commission, approves the attestation schedule, determines the list of workers subject to attestation, and states the list of documents needed by the commission and the periods for their submission [13].

The schedule, drawn up in the form of Annex 2, is brought to the notice of each attested worker under signature not less than 30 calendar days before the date of attestation planned in the schedule [14].

Attestation is held on the basis of a representation of the structural subdivision (form of Annex 3) and of an objective and comprehensive assessment of the worker's activity; the representation is passed to the commission and must contain a reasoned assessment of the worker's professional and business qualities and of the results of his work [15]. The head of the subdivision in which the worker works must acquaint him with the representation under signature not later than 30 calendar days before the attestation; if the worker refuses, an act in the form of Annex 4 is drawn up in the presence of not less than 2 other employees of the University [15].

At each subsequent attestation an extract from the minutes with the data of the previous attestation is also submitted to the commission by an authorised employee [16].

Not later than 14 calendar days before the date of attestation the worker may submit to the commission additional information on his work, in particular [17]:

- a list of scientific works by sections (monographs and chapters in them, articles in collections and periodicals, patents and certificates for intellectual property, publications in materials of scientific events, in registered electronic scientific editions, preprints, popular science books and articles) [17];
- the titles of educational editions he prepared or in whose preparation he took part [17];
- a list of teaching aids, curricula, work programmes of subjects, courses and disciplines (modules) and electronic educational resources in whose development he took part [17];
- information on the volume of his teaching load [17];
- a list of grants, contracts and (or) agreements for research and development work in which he took part, with his specific role [17];
- information on his personal participation in scientific events (congresses, conferences, symposia and other scientific events), with the status of the report and the level of the event [17];
- information on his participation in editorial boards of scientific and pedagogical periodicals [17];
- information on the organisation of educational work with students [17];
- information on prizes and awards in the spheres of education and science [17];
- information on qualification improvement and professional retraining [17];
- a statement setting out the reasons for his disagreement with the representation of his immediate head [17].

## The sitting

The sitting is held in the presence of the worker being attested; his non-appearance does not prevent the attestation from being held [18]. The sitting is competent if «не менее двух третей ее членов» are present [19].

At the sitting the commission holds an objective and benevolent discussion of the worker's professional activity, examines the documents submitted and draws preliminary conclusions on his business qualities and the results of his work, hears the worker's report and, where necessary, that of his immediate head, asks them questions on the worker's professional activity during the attested period, assesses his observance of labour discipline, gives an assessment of his business qualities and results, works out recommendations for a fuller and better performance of his duties, and formulates and discusses proposals on his further business purpose [20].

The attestation must objectively assess the results of the worker's scientific and pedagogical activity in their dynamics, his personal contribution to raising the quality of education in the disciplines he teaches, to the development of science and to the solution of scientific problems in the corresponding field of knowledge, his participation in developing methods of teaching and upbringing and in mastering new educational technologies, the raising of his professional level, and other circumstances allowing an objective assessment [21].

## The decision and its consequences

The commission takes one of the decisions «соответствует занимаемой должности» or «не соответствует занимаемой должности» [22]. The decision is taken in the absence of the attested worker by open vote by a simple majority of the members present and is recorded in the minutes; if the votes are equal, the worker is recognised as corresponding to the position held [22]. When a member of the commission is attested, the decision is taken in his absence in the general order [22].

The results are entered in the minutes, which are kept by the secretary together with the representations of the structural subdivision and the information under п. 3.5 (if any) and are signed by the chairman and the secretary [23]. An extract from the minutes in the form of Annex 5 is drawn up for the attested worker by the secretary not later than 2 working days after the attestation and contains his surname, name and patronymic, the title of his position, the date of the sitting, the results of the vote and the decision taken; the secretary acquaints the worker with it under signature within 10 working days after the sitting, and a copy is kept in his personal file in the personnel department [24]. If the worker refuses to be acquainted with the extract, an act is drawn up (Annex 4) [24].

- The commission may enter in the minutes recommendations on improving the worker's professional activity and on the need to raise his qualification, with the specialisation, and other recommendations [25]; the immediate head then informs the commission of their fulfilment within the period set by the commission [26].
- The results are communicated to the worker present at the sitting after the vote is counted; in his absence they are communicated on the next working day, and if a personal acquaintance is impossible a notification is sent to him by e-mail [27].
- The worker may appeal the results of the attestation in accordance with the legislation of the Russian Federation [28].
- The commission passes the attestation materials to the personnel department not later than 5 working days after the sitting for storage and for decisions under the Labour Code of the Russian Federation [29].
- If the worker is recognised as not corresponding to the position because of insufficient qualification, the employment contract with him may be terminated under п. 3 ч. 1 ст. 81 of the Labour Code of the Russian Federation; dismissal on that ground is allowed only if it is impossible to transfer the worker, with his written consent, to another job available to the employer (a vacant position or work corresponding to his qualification, or a vacant lower position or lower-paid work) that he can perform taking his state of health into account (ч. 3 ст. 81 of the Labour Code) [30].
- On the results of the attestation the University director, taking the commission's recommendations into account, takes a decision to issue the corresponding order within a period not exceeding 30 calendar days from the attestation [31].
- The commission's documents are kept by its secretary until they are no longer needed, with measures against unauthorised access to them [32].

## Final provisions and forms

Labour disputes and disagreements between the attested worker and the members of the commission on the observance of this Regulation are settled by mutual agreement or, failing that, in the manner established by the legislation of the Russian Federation [33]. In what the Regulation does not provide for, the worker and the University follow the legislation of the Russian Federation [34]. The Regulation is approved by the University director and enters into force on approval, and changes and additions to it are approved by orders of the director or of a person authorised by him [4] [34].

The page carries the forms: Annex 1 — minutes of a sitting of the attestation commission (those present and absent, the agenda, the report, the questions, the decision, the recommendations and the results of the vote) [1]; Annex 2 — the schedule of attestation with the worker's signature confirming acquaintance with it [1]; Annex 3 — the representation on the worker (position, service record, education documents, the decision of the previous attestation, an assessment of his business qualities and results, a reasoned assessment, proposals on his further business purpose) [1]; Annex 4 — the act on refusal to sign the representation or the extract [1]; Annex 5 — the extract from the minutes [1].

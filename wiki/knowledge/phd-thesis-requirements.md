# PhD thesis requirements and evaluation

## Thesis criteria

The thesis for the degree of Candidate of Sciences must be an independent scientific qualification work that solves a scientific problem significant for the development of the relevant field of knowledge, or presents new scientifically grounded technical, technological, or other solutions of substantial importance for the country [1]. It must have internal unity, contain new scientific results and positions presented for public defense, and demonstrate the author's personal contribution to science [1]. Applied theses must include information on practical use of the results; theoretical theses must include recommendations for using the scientific conclusions [1]. Proposed solutions must be argued and evaluated compared to other known solutions [1]. The author must cite all sources of borrowed materials; when using results from the author's own or co-authored works, this must be noted [1].

## Publication requirements

The thesis must be formatted according to GOST R 7.0.11-2011 [1]. At least two publications in refereed editions (journals from the Higher Attestation Commission list of the Ministry of Science and Higher Education of Russia) must contain the main scientific results of the thesis [1]. Publications indexed in international databases (as determined by the Higher Attestation Commission) are treated as refereed publications for this purpose [1]. Patents, utility models, industrial designs, software registration certificates, database registration certificates, and topologies of integrated circuits are also treated as refereed publications on the recommendation of the Higher Attestation Commission [1].

## Reviewer evaluation

Reviewers assess the thesis on the following criteria [2]:

- relevance of the thesis topic [2];
- personal involvement of the author in obtaining the scientific results presented in the thesis [2];
- degree of substantiation and reliability of scientific statements, conclusions, and recommendations [2];
- scientific novelty of the research results [2];
- theoretical and practical significance of the results and their applicability [2];
- value of the student's scientific works and completeness of the thesis materials in published works [2];
- thesis structure, language, and style [2];
- compliance of the thesis with the scientific specialty for which it was completed (with reference to the specialty passport points) [2];
- compliance of the thesis with the criteria established by Federal Law No. 127-FZ "On Science and State Scientific and Technological Policy" [2].

A negative reviewer opinion does not prevent the student from passing the final certification [2]. Reviewers may raise critical remarks, suggest revisions, and request a re-examination [2].

## University conclusion

The University conclusion on the thesis reflects the student's personal contribution to obtaining the results, the degree of reliability, novelty, relevance, and practical significance, the value of the student's scientific works, results from personal and co-authored research, the scientific specialty and field of science, and the completeness of thesis materials in published works [3]. The conclusion is valid for 3 years from approval [3].

# Institute of Robotics and Computer Vision (Институт робототехники и компьютерного зрения) — place, tasks, functions and rights

## The document

The Regulation on the Institute of Robotics and Computer Vision (Положение об Институте робототехники и компьютерного зрения) regulates the activity of the Institute of Robotics and Computer Vision of the autonomous non-commercial organisation of higher education «Innopolis University» and determines its tasks, functions, rights and duties [1].

The page is marked as approved by an order of ANO VO «Университет Иннополис» of «9» January 2019 and carries the place «г. Иннополис» [2]. The approving block is signed by a director for development and HR policy whose surname the OCR gives in a garbled form as «А.М.Саматова», and the page states no number for the order [2]. ## Place in the structure

The Institute is a structural subdivision of the Faculty of Computer and Engineering Sciences (Факультет компьютерных и инженерных наук) of the University and includes the following structural subdivisions [3]:

- the Laboratory of Intelligent Robotic Systems (Лаборатория интеллектуальных робототехнических систем) [3]
- the Laboratory of Cognitive Robotic Systems (Лаборатория когнитивных робототехнических систем) [3]
- the Laboratory of Design of Electronic Systems and Automation (Лаборатория дизайна электронных систем и автоматизации) [3]
- the master's programme «Робототехника и компьютерное зрение» [3]

The Institute reports directly to the director of the institute; in the absence of the director of the institute it reports to the dean of the faculty [4].

In its activity the Institute follows the Constitution of the Russian Federation, the Labour Code of the Russian Federation, Federal Law «Об образовании в Российской Федерации» and other legislative and normative legal acts of the Russian Federation regulating the activity of higher education institutions, the University's Charter, decisions of the Academic, Supervisory and Operational councils, orders and instructions of the Director of the University, other local normative acts of the University and this Regulation [5].

The structure, the number of staff and the staffing table of the Institute, and changes to them, are approved by the Director of the University [6]. The Institute is headed by its director, who is hired and dismissed by an order of the Director of the University [7]. The working conditions of the Institute's employees are determined by the employment contracts concluded with each employee and by the University's internal labour regulations [2]. The rights, duties and degree of responsibility of the Institute's employees are established by job descriptions [8]. The Regulation and additions and changes to it are approved by the Director of the University or by another person authorised by him [9].

## Tasks

The tasks of the Institute are [2]:

- creating a scientific-methodological database for fundamental and applied research in robotics and computer vision in accordance with the University's research plans [10]
- opening experimental sites for scientific-theoretical and practical research and for testing innovative technologies in robotics and computer vision [11]
- helping to form the research skills of the University's students, aspirants and teachers [12]
- expanding and strengthening scientific ties with institutions and organisations in robotics and computer vision [13]
- carrying out research and development work (НИР, НИОКР), commercial projects, grants and subsidies [2]
- preparing for publication and publishing annotated and abstracted materials on the newest experience and the own materials and developments of the University's scientific-pedagogical workers and students in robotics and computer vision [14]

## Functions

The Institute performs the following functions [2]:

- developing educational and research programmes in robotics and computer vision [15]
- step-by-step planning and conduct of НИР, НИОКР, industrial projects, grants and subsidies [16]
- improving the methods, technologies, techniques and instruments of research work [17]
- creating a database for the research work of students in the course of their coursework and final qualification works [18]
- developing and publishing scientific-methodological manuals based on the Institute's results [19]
- publishing scientific articles in journals, including those in international citation bases such as Web of Science and Scopus [20]
- holding thematic seminars, practical sessions and consultations for the University's teachers, aspirants and students [21]
- preparing practical (experimental) material for inclusion in candidate and doctoral dissertations [22]
- drawing the University's students into research activity [23]
- revealing the scientific potential of the University's young specialists [24]
- popularising research and knowledge in robotics and computer vision [25]

## Rights

In carrying on its activity the employees of the Institute have the right to [2]:

- request from the University's structural subdivisions the information and materials needed for the work within the Institute's competence [26]
- with the consent of the heads of the University's structural subdivisions, involve the employees of those subdivisions in preparing draft local acts and other documents and in developing and carrying out measures conducted by the Institute [27]
- take part in meetings held by the University's management when discussing questions whose solution falls within the Institute's competence [28]
- submit to the University's management proposals to cancel, amend or supplement the Institute's local acts [29]
- interact with bodies of state authority and administration, legal entities and the University's subdivisions on questions within the Institute's competence [30]

## Interaction and reporting

Interaction of the Institute with the University's other structural subdivisions is determined by the tasks and functions assigned to it by this Regulation [31]. The page's next passage is garbled by the OCR and speaks of a «Лаборатория» rather than of the Institute [2]. Under п. 5.3, interaction with other structural subdivisions takes place on questions of preparing draft local acts, orders, instructions, contracts and other documents and of preparing conclusions and written and oral certificates on questions within the Institute's competence arising in practical activity [32].

Within its activity the Institute is responsible for preparing scientific-educational reports and reports on НИР, НИОКР, commercial projects, grants and subsidies, and for passing them in time to the University's internal structural subdivisions [33].

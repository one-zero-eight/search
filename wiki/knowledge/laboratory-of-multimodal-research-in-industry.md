# Laboratory of Multimodal Research in Industry (Лаборатория мультимодальных исследований в промышленности) — place, tasks, functions and rights

## The document and the Laboratory's place

The Regulation on the Laboratory of Multimodal Research in Industry of the Centre for Scientific Research of the Research Centre of the Institute of Artificial Intelligence of the Directorate for Project and Research Activity of ANO VO «Innopolis University» regulates the Laboratory's activity and determines its tasks, functions, rights and duties [1]. It was approved by order of the University of 28 March 2025 № ОД/НИД‐02/2025/03/28, signed by the Director for Development and HR Policy R.F. Valiev [2].

The Laboratory is a structural subdivision of the Centre for Scientific Research of the Research Centre of the Institute of Artificial Intelligence of the Directorate for Project and Research Activity and belongs to the category «научно-исследовательские подразделения» [3]. It reports directly to the head of the Centre for Scientific Research [4].

In its activity the Laboratory follows the Constitution of the Russian Federation, the Labour Code of the Russian Federation, Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29 December 2012 «Об образовании в Российской Федерации», other legislative and normative legal acts regulating the activity of higher education institutions, the University's Charter, decisions of the Academic, Supervisory and Operations Councils, orders and instructions of the Director, other local normative acts and this Regulation [5].

The structure, the number of staff and the staffing table, and changes to them, are approved by the Director of the University [6]. The Laboratory is headed by a head of the Laboratory, hired and dismissed by order of the Director [7]. The working conditions of its employees are set by the employment contracts concluded with each employee and by the internal labour regulations [8]; their rights, duties and degree of responsibility are established by job descriptions [9]. Employees may be involved, within their competences, in work on grants, projects, subsidies and other programmes carried out by the University [10]. The Regulation and the additions and changes to it are approved by order of the Director or an authorised person [11].

## Tasks

- obtaining significant results in multimodal research of artificial intelligence (AI) in industry — research in AI with multimodal approaches in industry [12], standardisation of the accumulated data in multimodal research in industry [13], development and introduction of machine-learning models [14], creation of predictive models on the basis of AI algorithms [15], formation of mathematical models [16] and validation of the developed models [17] [18]
- conducting research oriented to world standards of the quality of scientific activity in multimodal research in industry [19]
- preparing for publication scientific articles, monographs and other publications containing the Laboratory's results; publishing in editions included in international scientific citation bases, placing materials in first-quartile journals and taking part in A* level conferences [20]
- attracting Russian and foreign scholars with significant indicators in scientific activity in the Laboratory's field to research cooperation [21]
- disseminating the results and popularising the AI research carried out by the Laboratory's employees; participation of its employees in world-scale scientific events [22]

## Functions

- organising and holding scientific events according to the Laboratory's approved plan, and within direct contracts with scientific organisations and real-sector enterprises and grants received from various Russian funds and organisations [23]
- reaching the world level of the quality of research and promoting Russian science abroad [24]
- organising and holding scientific forums, seminars, conferences and symposia to world standards of organising scientific events [25]
- interacting with other subdivisions to promote and sell products and projects connected with the Laboratory's direction of AI application [26]
- taking part in projects of other subdivisions on tasks connected with the Laboratory's direction of AI application [27]

## Rights

In carrying out its activity the Laboratory's employees have the right to:

- request from the University's structural subdivisions the information and materials needed for the work within the Laboratory's competence [28]
- with the consent of the heads of the structural subdivisions, involve employees of those subdivisions in preparing draft local acts and other documents and in developing and carrying out the Laboratory's measures [29]
- take part in meetings held by the University's management when questions within the Laboratory's competence are discussed [30]
- submit to the University's management proposals to cancel, amend or supplement local acts [31]
- interact with state authorities and administration, legal entities and the University's subdivisions on questions within the Laboratory's competence [32]
- become acquainted with draft decisions of the University's management concerning the Laboratory's activity [33]
- propose improvements to the work connected with implementing in the University product developments in artificial intelligence using multimodal approaches in industry [34]
- use the University's information resources and place news and other information materials about the Laboratory's activity and announcements, forming its own image within the image policy of the University [35]

## Interaction and reporting

- Interaction with the University's other structural subdivisions is determined by the tasks and functions assigned to the Laboratory by the Regulation [36].
- The Laboratory may be a base for the practical training of the University's students [37].
- Interaction with the financial-economic department covers the application of legislation and local acts regulating pay in the University, the drafting of local acts on its financial activity, legal expertise of situations in which employees are made materially liable, and the preparation and transfer of materials to courts on recovering receivables from organisations and on tax disputes [38].
- On the basis of the relevant local acts, orders and instructions the Laboratory may be responsible for preparing and keeping up to date information, certificates and reports [39].

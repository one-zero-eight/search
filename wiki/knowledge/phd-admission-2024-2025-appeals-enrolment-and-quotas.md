# Admission to PhD aspirantura 2024/2025 — appeals, enrolment, target quota and foreign applicants

## Where these rules come from

These are sections V–IX of the Rules of admission to higher education programmes — programmes for the training of scientific and scientific-pedagogical staff in aspirantura — at ANO VO «Innopolis University» for the 2024/2025 academic year, approved by order of the University No. ОД/АД-03/2024/01/15 of 15 January 2024 [1]. The earlier stages of the same campaign — who may apply, documents and entrance examinations — are in the card on those subjects (see See also).

## Entrance examinations for disabled applicants

Examinations are held for disabled applicants with account of the features of their psychophysical development, individual possibilities and state of health [2]. The University has material and technical conditions giving unimpeded access to classrooms, toilets and other premises (ramps, hoists, handrails, widened doorways, lifts; where there are no lifts the examinations are held in classrooms on the ground floor) [3].

The examinations are held in a separate classroom, with not more than 12 applicants at a written examination and not more than 6 at an oral one; a greater number and joint holding with other applicants are allowed if this creates no difficulties, and an assistant from the University's employees or engaged persons may be present to give technical help [4]. The duration of an examination is increased by decision of the University by not more than 1,5 hours [5]. Information about the procedure is given in an accessible form [6], and technical means needed because of individual features may be used [7].

Additional requirements depend on the individual features [8]:

- for blind applicants the tasks are given in Braille relief-dot font or as an electronic document readable with a special program or read out by an assistant, written tasks are done in Braille or on a computer with a special program or dictated to an assistant, and writing materials and paper for Braille are provided [8];
- for visually impaired applicants individual even lighting of not less than 300 lux is provided, a magnifying device may be given or their own used, and tasks and instructions are printed in enlarged font [8];
- for deaf and hard of hearing applicants collective sound-amplifying equipment is provided, individual equipment on request, and sign-language interpreter services [8];
- for deaf-blind applicants the services of a typhlo-sign-language interpreter are provided [8];
- for applicants with severe speech disorders, deaf and hard of hearing applicants, oral examinations are held in written form by decision of the University, and for applicants with disorders of the musculoskeletal system or of the functions of the upper limbs written tasks are done on a computer or dictated to an assistant and written examinations are held orally by decision of the University [8].

These conditions are provided on the basis of the application for admission containing the need for them [9].

## Appeals

An applicant may appeal to the appeal commission about an alleged violation of the established procedure of an examination and (or) about disagreement with the grade [10]. The appeal is filed in one of the ways listed in clause 21 [11]; during its consideration the observance of the procedure and the results of the checking and assessment of the work are verified [12].

The appeal is filed on the day the results are announced or during the next working day, and an appeal about a violation of the procedure may also be filed on the day of the examination [13]. It is considered not later than the next working day after filing [14], and the applicant may be present [15]. The commission either changes the grade or leaves it unchanged, and its decision, recorded in a protocol, is communicated to the applicant against his signature [16].

## Ranked lists and enrolment

For each competition a separate ranked list is formed of applicants who scored at least the minimum points; the lists are published on the site and on the EPGU portal and updated daily until the day following the completion of the acceptance of originals of the document of education [17].

- The list is ranked by the descending sum of competitive points (points for the examinations and for individual achievements), then, where those are equal, by the descending sum of points for the examinations and (or) for separate examinations in accordance with the priority of the examinations, and then by individual achievements [18].
- The list shows, without surnames, the insurance number of the individual personal account or a unique code, the sum of competitive points, the sum for the examinations, the points for each examination, the points for individual achievements and whether an original document or a consent to enrolment has been submitted [19].

The deadlines for the document of education are: not later than 18:00 on 23 August 2024 — the original for places within the control figures; not later than 18:00 on 26 August 2024 — the original, or a consent to enrolment with a certified copy or a copy with the original shown, or a consent with the information confirmed in the federal register of documents on education — for paid places [20].

Those who submitted the original (or the consent) are enrolled down the ranked list until the places are filled; for places within the control figures the original must be held by the University on the day of the order; the order on admission to places within the control figures is issued on 24 August 2024, and the order on admission to paid places on 27 August 2024 [21]. Unfilled places of the target quota are used for applicants to the main places within the control figures [22], and after enrolment the University may hold an additional enrolment to remaining places [23].

For paid places the number of places may be exceeded by decision of the University, which then enrols all applicants who scored at least the minimum points and at least 80 competitive points [24]. Enrolment is formalised by orders [25], announced by scanned copies on the site [26]; the enrolment data without names, with the account number or unique code and the points, are available on the site for 6 months from the order [27].

## Target admission

The target quota is set in accordance with the quota for targeted training established by the Government, state authorities of the regions or local self-government, or with the number of places set by the founder [28]; the competition is held by each scientific specialty [29]. Admission to targeted training requires a contract on targeted training concluded with the customer [30], presented with the application as the original, a copy certified by the customer or an uncertified copy with the original shown; where training is in the interests of state security, the University acts on information about the contract received from the state body and the applicant does not present it [31].

Where the state body has detailed the target quota by places with named customers, a separate competition is held for each detailed quota, an applicant takes part in one detailed quota per specialty, places without named customers form a quota for applicants whose customers are not named elsewhere, and unfilled places are used under clause 57 [32]. Information relating to admission in the interests of state security is not shown in the lists of applicants [33], and admission of such persons is formalised by a separate order which is not published on the site [34].

## Foreign citizens and stateless persons

Foreign citizens and stateless persons may receive higher education at the expense of the budget under international treaties, federal laws or the quota of the Government for the education of foreign citizens, and at the expense of persons and legal entities under paid contracts [35]. Admission within that quota follows the directions of the federal executive body in charge of higher education and is formalised by a separate order [36]. Applicants under international treaties additionally present documents confirming that they belong to the persons named in the treaty [37], compatriots living abroad present the documents provided by part 6 of article 17 of Federal Law No. 99-ФЗ of 24 May 1999 [38], and a foreign citizen or stateless person presents an identity document under article 10 of Federal Law No. 115-ФЗ of 25 July 2002 [39].

# Commission for the settlement of disputes between participants of educational relations

## What the Commission is

The Regulation on the commission for the settlement of disputes between the participants of educational relations was approved by order of ANO VO «Университет Иннополис» of 5 February 2025 № ОД/АД-02/2025/02/05, signed by director I.I. Bariev (г. Иннополис 2025); it was developed in accordance with part 6 of article 45 of Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29 December 2012 «Об образовании в Российской Федерации» and establishes the procedure for creating the Commission, organising its work, and adopting and executing its decisions [1] [2].

The Commission is created to settle disagreements between participants of educational relations on questions of the realisation of the right to education, including in cases of a conflict of interests of a pedagogical worker, the application of local normative acts, and the appeal of decisions on applying a disciplinary penalty to students [1].

Its aim is the protection of the rights and lawful interests of the participants of educational relations [3]. Its tasks are settling disagreements arising between participants of educational relations on questions of the realisation of the right to education and promoting the development of conflict-free interaction at the university [4].

## What the Commission does not do

Appeals of students connected with appealing the results of ongoing assessment, interim assessment and final assessment are considered by the Commission exclusively as to the observance by the participants of educational relations of the established procedures [5]. Appeals concerning interim or final assessment are considered only as to the observance of procedures by the appeal commissions that considered the corresponding applications for appeal from the students [5]. The Commission does not carry out a re-assessment of the results of ongoing, interim and final assessment [5]. If an appeal does not indicate the alleged procedural violations in the conduct of ongoing assessment or of the appeal on the results of interim or final assessment, such appeals are not accepted for consideration by the Commission [5].

The Commission does not consider reports of crimes or administrative offences, nor anonymous appeals [6].

## Composition and term

The Commission is a permanently acting body whose quantitative composition and formation procedure are determined by the Regulation [7]. It is created in a composition of 6 (six) persons from an equal number of representatives of students, of parents (lawful representatives) of minor students and of employees of the university; the formed composition is approved by an order of the director for 1 (one) academic year [8].

- Two representatives of students are determined by the Student Council of the university [9].
- Two representatives of employees of the university are appointed by the director of the university or another person authorised by the director [10].
- Two representatives of parents (lawful representatives) of minor students are determined by the director of the university and take part in the work of the Commission exclusively when appeals received from minor students or in their interests are considered [11].

The director of the university appoints, from among the members of the Commission, its chair and a deputy chair who performs the functions of the chair in the chair's absence [12]. Organisational and technical support of the Commission's activity is carried out by a secretary appointed by the chair of the Commission; the secretary is not a member of the Commission and takes part in its work without the right to vote [13]. A member of the Commission is replaced by an order of the director [14].

The powers of a member of the Commission end on the following grounds:

- the member's personal application for exclusion from the composition of the Commission [15];
- the written demand of not less than 2/3 of the members of the Commission [16];
- for representatives of employees of the university — dismissal [17];
- for representatives of parents (lawful representatives) of minor students — their child reaching majority [18];
- for representatives of students — expulsion [19].

## How to apply

The Commission accepts appeals from students, from parents (lawful representatives) of minor students, and from employees of the university participating in the educational process [20]. Participants of educational relations may apply to the Commission within 1 (one) month from the day the corresponding dispute arose; if the term is missed for valid reasons, the Commission may restore the term and resolve the dispute on the merits [21].

An application for consideration of a disputed situation is submitted to the chair of the Commission in free written form through the secretary of the Commission by corporate electronic mail or on paper, together with a description of the disputed situation, a statement of the applicant's position and the materials relating to the subject of the dispute [22].

## Consideration and decision

The Commission meets when it receives an application from a participant of educational relations about a disputed situation [23]. It must consider the application and adopt a decision within 1 (one) month from the day the application is received; if additional information is required, this term may be extended by a decision of the Commission, but by no more than 2 (two) months [24].

By decision of the chair, an application may be considered in absentia (заочно), in which case the secretary sends the scanned materials of the session to the members by electronic mail and the discussion, exchange of documents and voting are conducted by electronic mail and coordinated by the secretary [25]. In the in-person form, the applicants and the persons in respect of whom the application was submitted, and where necessary interested persons, are invited to the session; telecommunication channels (the Internet) may be used, and if personal presence is impossible the parties may set out their position in writing [26]. The Commission may invite officials of the university and other participants of educational relations, besides the parties to the dispute, to its sessions [27].

A decision of the Commission is competent if at least 4 (four) of its members were present at the session when disputes are decided in the interests of minor students, and at least 3 (three) members in other cases; when disputes concerning minor students are considered, at least 1 (one) member who is a parent (lawful representative) of a minor student, 1 (one) member who is a student and 1 (one) member who is an employee of the university must take part [28]. The decision is taken by open voting by a simple majority of the members present, if there is a quorum; in the case of an equal number of votes the vote of the chair of the Commission is decisive [29].

When adopting a decision the Commission simultaneously decides the procedure and terms of its execution [30]. The decision is recorded in a protocol signed by the chair and the secretary, and the secretary provides extracts from the protocol to interested persons for familiarisation on written request [31]. The decision is sent by corporate electronic mail to the heads of the university's structural subdivisions responsible for implementing it, and the originals of the protocols are kept by the secretary [32].

## Force of the decision

A decision of the Commission is obligatory for execution by all participants of educational relations at the university within the terms provided in it [33]. It may be appealed in the manner established by the legislation of the Russian Federation [34].

Members of the Commission carry out their activity free of charge [35]. The documents and materials of the Commission are kept in the organisation for 3 (three) years [36].

## Rights and duties

The Commission has the right to request additional documentation and materials from the participants of the disputed situation and from representatives of the university's structural subdivisions and to seek consultations with employees of the university and of other institutions within the study of the question; to recommend suspending the adoption of a decision or cancelling an earlier decision on the basis of the study carried out, with the consent of the conflicting parties; and to recommend to the director of the university that amendments be made to the university's local normative acts in order to promote conflict-free interaction [37].

Members of the Commission are obliged to be present at its sessions, to take an active part in the consideration of applications and to adopt decisions on the questions raised in good time [38].

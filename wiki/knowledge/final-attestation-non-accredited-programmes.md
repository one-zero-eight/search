# Final attestation for bachelor's and master's programmes without state accreditation — procedure and organisation (regulation of 27 April 2024)

## The document

The Regulation on the procedure for conducting the final attestation (итоговая аттестация) on higher education programmes — bachelor's and master's programmes — that have no state accreditation was approved by order of the Director of 27 April 2024 No. ОД/АД-02/2024/04/27 and signed by Director K.V. Semenikhin [1].

It establishes the procedure for organising and conducting the University's final attestation of students completing educational programmes that have no state accreditation, including the forms of the attestation, requirements on the use of means of education and upbringing and of means of communication during it, requirements on the persons involved in conducting it, the procedure for submitting and considering appeals, the change and (or) annulment of attestation results, and the specific features of conducting the attestation for students with health limitations [2].

The Regulation is based on Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29 December 2012 «Об образовании в Российской Федерации», order of the Ministry of Science and Higher Education of 29.06.2015 No. 636 on the procedure for the state final attestation, order of 06.04.2021 No. 245 on the organisation of educational activity, the federal state educational standards of higher education (the Standards), the University's Charter and other local acts [3].

The Regulation is in the wiki in two copies. The first carries an unrelated prefix in its title («Сведения о наличии оборудованных объектов для проведения практических занятий») [1]; the second, taken from the text layer of the same PDF, has the clean title [4]. Their clauses read the same, so either copy may be used [1], [4].

The attestation is conducted by final examination commissions in order to determine whether the results of mastering the programmes comply with the requirements of the Standard [5], on the principles of objectivity and independence of the assessment of the quality of students' preparation [6]. It is obligatory for completing a programme and is conducted in the procedure and forms established by the University independently [7], in Russian and (or) in English in accordance with the University's administrative acts and the attestation programme [8].

## Who is admitted, fees, prohibitions

- A student who has no academic debt and has fully completed the curriculum or the individual curriculum of the corresponding higher education programme is admitted to the attestation [9].
- During the attestation, students and the persons involved in it are forbidden to have on them and to use means of communication [10].
- No fee may be charged to students for passing the attestation [11].
- Persons who studied at the University on a programme without state accreditation may be enrolled as external students (экстерны) to pass the interim and the state final attestation at another educational organisation running the corresponding programme that has state accreditation, in accordance with that organisation's local acts [12].

## Forms of attestation

- The attestation takes the form of a final examination and (or) a defence of a final qualification work (together — attestation tests) [13].
- The final examination is held on one or several disciplines and (or) modules of the programme whose results are decisive for the professional activity of graduates; it is held orally or in writing [14].
- The final qualification work is work performed by a student (by several students jointly) demonstrating the level of the graduate's preparedness for independent professional activity [15]. Its type, requirements, procedure of performance and assessment criteria are set by the University in accordance with the requirements of the Standard (where such requirements exist) and the attestation programme [16].
- The volume, structure and content of the attestation are set by the educational programme [17]; its dates are set by the University in accordance with the approved calendar study schedule of the programme [18].
- The University may apply e-learning and distance educational technologies when conducting attestation tests [19].

## Grades and the document issued

The results of each attestation test are graded «отлично», «хорошо», «удовлетворительно», «неудовлетворительно»; the grades «отлично», «хорошо» and «удовлетворительно» mean that the test has been passed successfully [20]. Successful completion of the attestation is the basis for issuing to the student a document of higher education and qualification of the form established by the University [21].

## Commissions

- Final examination commissions are created to conduct the attestation [22], and appeal commissions to consider appeals against its results [23]. Both act for one calendar year [24] and may be created for each field of study, for each programme, or for several fields or programmes [25]. Their compositions are approved no later than 1 month before the attestation begins [26].
- The chair of the final examination commission is approved from among persons not working at the University who hold a Doctor of Sciences degree and (or) the rank of professor, or who are leading specialists — representatives of employers or their associations in the relevant field [27]. The chair of the appeal commission is the Director of the University (the person acting as Director, or a person authorised by the Director by an administrative act) [28].
- The chairs organise and control the commissions' activity and ensure uniform requirements towards students [29].
- The final examination commission consists of its chair and not fewer than 4 members; members are leading specialists — representatives of employers or their associations and (or) teaching staff or research workers of the University (or other organisations) with a rank and (or) a degree; the share of employer representatives, including the chair, must be not less than 50 per cent [30].
- The appeal commission consists of its chair and not fewer than 3 members, formed from the University's teaching staff who are not members of the final examination commissions [31].
- For the period of the attestation the University appoints a secretary of the final examination commission from among teaching staff, research workers or administrative workers; the secretary is not a member of the commission, keeps the minutes of its meetings and submits the necessary materials to the appeal commission [32].
- Meetings are the main form of the commissions' activity; a meeting is competent if not less than two thirds of the persons included in the commission take part, and meetings are conducted by the chairs [33]. Decisions are taken by a simple majority of the members taking part; in case of an equal number of votes the chair has a casting vote [34].
- Decisions are recorded in minutes; the minute of the final examination commission on a test records the list of questions put to the student and the characteristics of the answers, the opinions of the chair and members on the level of the student's preparedness and on shortcomings in the student's theoretical and practical preparation [35]. Minutes are signed by the chair, and the final examination commission's minute also by its secretary [36]; they are bound into books and kept in the University's archive [37].

## Preparation and conduct

- The attestation programme, including the programmes of the final examinations or the requirements to the final qualification works and the procedure for their performance and formatting, the assessment criteria and the procedure for submitting and considering appeals are communicated to students no later than six months before the attestation begins [38].
- The final examination is held according to a programme approved by the University that contains the list of questions, recommendations for preparation including recommended literature; before the examination students are consulted on the questions of the programme (pre-examination consultation) [39].
- The University approves the list of final qualification work topics and communicates it to students no later than 6 months before the attestation begins [40]. On a written application of a student (of several students working jointly), the University may allow a topic proposed by the student(s), where the expediency of its development for practical application in the relevant field or at a specific object is justified [41].
- A supervisor of the final qualification work, and where necessary a consultant or consultants, are assigned to the student(s) by an administrative act of the University from among the University's employees [42].
- The schedule of attestation tests, stating the dates, times and places of the tests and of the pre-examination consultations, is approved by an administrative act no later than 30 calendar days before the first test and communicated to the student, the chairs and members of the commissions, the secretaries, the supervisors and the consultants; the schedule provides a break of not less than 7 calendar days between attestation tests [43].

## Reviews of the final qualification work

- After the work is prepared, its supervisor submits a written review (отзыв) on the student's work during its preparation; where several students prepare the work jointly, the review concerns their joint work [44].
- Final qualification works of master's programmes are subject to review (рецензирование); the work is sent to one or several reviewers who are not in an employment relationship with the University; an interdisciplinary work is sent to several reviewers, otherwise to one reviewer, and the reviewer submits a written review [45].
- The student is made acquainted with the supervisor's review and with the review(s) no later than 5 calendar days before the day of the defence [46].
- The work, the supervisor's review, the review(s) and the annotation to the work are passed to the final examination commission no later than 2 calendar days before the day of the defence [47].
- The texts of the works are placed by the University in its electronic library system and checked for the volume of borrowing; the procedure for placing, checking, including substantive checking, and identifying improper borrowings is established by the University [48].
- Access to the texts must be ensured in accordance with Russian legislation, with the exclusion, by decision of the rights holder, of production, technical, economic, organisational and other information, including on results of intellectual activity and on methods of professional activity that have actual or potential commercial value because they are unknown to third parties [49].

## Announcement of results, absence and retaking

- The results of an attestation test held orally are announced on the day of the test, those of a test held in writing on the next working day after the test [50].
- Students who did not pass the attestation because they did not appear for a test for a valid reason (temporary incapacity, performance of public or state duties, court summons, transport problems such as a cancelled flight or no tickets, weather conditions or other cases recognised by the University as a valid reason) may pass it within 6 months after the attestation ends; the student must submit a document confirming the valid reason [51].
- A student who did not pass one attestation test for a valid reason is admitted to the next test (if there is one) [52].
- Students who did not pass a test because they did not appear without a valid reason, or who received the grade «неудовлетворительно», are expelled from the University with a certificate of study, as not having fulfilled the duties of conscientiously mastering the programme and completing the curriculum [53].
- A person who did not pass the attestation may pass it again not earlier than 10 months and not later than five years after the date of the attestation not passed, and may do so not more than two times [54]. For this, on the person's application, the person is reinstated at the University for a period set by the University but not less than the period provided by the calendar study schedule for the attestation on the programme [55]. At the student's wish the University may set a different topic of the final qualification work [56].
- After passing the attestation, students are granted, on their application, vacations within the period of mastering the programme, at the end of which they are expelled in connection with obtaining the education [57].

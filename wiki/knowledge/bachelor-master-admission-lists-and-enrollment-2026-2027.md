# Lists, consent and enrolment in bachelor's and master's admission 2026/2027 — stages and deadlines

## Lists of those who applied and competitive lists

The rules of admission to bachelor's and master's programmes for the 2026/2027 academic year were approved by order of 10 April 2026 № ОД/УД‐01/2026/04/10 [1].

- The lists of those who applied are formed during the acceptance of applications and documents and of the internal examinations and are published on the official site and on ЕПГУ from the day the acceptance starts; they are updated daily while there are changes, until the competitive lists are published [1].
- The competitive lists are formed on the results of the acceptance of applications and documents and of the internal examinations and are published on the official site and on ЕПГУ; they are updated daily while there are changes, until the day of the enrolment order for the given competition inclusive, not less than 5 (five) times a day [1].
- The competitive list includes, on bachelor's programmes except the list for places within the separate quota, those admitted without entrance examinations under parts 4 and/or 12 of article 71 of Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ and those admitted on the results of the ЕГЭ and/or internal examinations with not less than the minimum number of points; for places within the separate quota — those admitted without entrance examinations and those admitted on the results of the examinations; on master's programmes — those with not less than the minimum number of points [1].
- In a bachelor's competitive list those admitted on the results of the ЕГЭ and/or internal examinations are placed after those admitted without entrance examinations under parts 4 and/or 12 of article 71 and after those admitted without entrance examinations at all [1].
- The competitive list shows the unique code, the grounds for admission without entrance examinations, the points for general and for target individual achievements, the preferential rights under parts 9 and 10 of article 71, the achievements taken into account where applicants are equal, the sum of competitive points, the points for each entrance examination, the consent to enrolment (for places within the control figures) or the concluded contract on education (for paid places), the priority stated by the applicant in this competition group and the highest priorities [1].
- The list of those who applied shows the same information except the achievements taken into account where applicants are equal and the highest priorities, and also information on the consideration of the application, including on admission to participation in the competition; the applicants are ordered by decreasing sum of competitive points, and where the sums are equal — by the unique code [1].

## How the competitive lists are ranked

- Applicants admitted without entrance examinations on bachelor's programmes are ranked by the status of the persons having the right: members of teams that took part in international pupil olympiads; winners of the final stage of the All-Russian pupil olympiad; prizewinners of that stage; persons named in п. 2 of part 4 of article 71; winners of pupil olympiads; prizewinners of pupil olympiads; by decision of the University these persons may be ranked by decreasing classes in which they took part; then by decreasing points for individual achievements (on the target quota — general plus target achievements), by the preferential right under part 9 and then under part 10 of article 71 and by the achievements taken into account where applicants are equal [1].
- On bachelor's programmes the applicants admitted on the results of the ЕГЭ and/or internal examinations are ranked by decreasing sum of competitive points, then by decreasing sum of the points for the ЕГЭ and/or the entrance examinations, then by decreasing points for the individual examinations according to their priority for ranking, then by individual achievements (on the target quota — general plus target achievements), then by the preferential rights under part 9 and part 10 of article 71 and by the achievements taken into account where applicants are equal [1].
- On master's programmes the applicants are ranked by decreasing sum of competitive points, then by decreasing sum of points for the entrance examinations, then by decreasing points for the individual examinations according to their priority, then by individual achievements and by the achievements taken into account where applicants are equal [1].

## Consent to enrolment and the contract for paid places

- Enrolment is conducted according to the competitive lists and the priorities of enrolment stated in the application, until the established number of places is filled [1].
- For enrolment to places within the control figures the applicant presents a consent to enrolment: in electronic form through an electronic mark on ЕПГУ or on paper through an application brought in person or through a postal operator; an electronic consent is presented not more often than 1 (one) time in 2 (two) hours [1].
- The consent applies to all competition groups for places within the control figures at the University, for bachelor's programmes or for master's programmes; an applicant who wants to give consent to another organisation must withdraw the consent already given [1].
- The University sets the day of completion of the presentation of consents; the consent may be given from the day the acceptance of applications starts until the time set on that day [1].
- For enrolment to paid places the applicant concludes a contract on education and informs the University of the need for his enrolment under it by a message to admissions@innopolis.ru from the e-mail address stated in the application, in person or through a postal operator, attaching a receipt for the payment of at least one semester of study, not later than the date preceding the date of the enrolment order [1].
- The University sets the day of completion of the conclusion of contracts; contracts are concluded from the day the acceptance of applications starts until that day inclusive [1].
- On the day of completion of the presentation of consents the University ensures, not later than 14:00 Moscow time, that the consents presented to it are taken into account in the competitive list [1].

## Highest priorities and who is enrolled

- For enrolment the University determines two highest priorities: the main highest priority — the highest priority at which the applicant passes the competition, determined for all applicants included in the competitive list regardless of consent; and the highest pass priority — the highest priority at which the applicant passes the competition, determined for those who have presented consent [1].
- An applicant is enrolled to places within the control figures by the highest pass priority if he passes the competition within the established number of places and a consent is present at the term of completion of the presentation of consents, provided he has not withdrawn it until the day of the enrolment order inclusive [1].
- An applicant is enrolled to paid places if he passes the competition within the established number of places and a contract is concluded on the day of completion of the conclusion of contracts, provided he informed the University as described above not later than the date preceding the order; enrolment to paid places is conducted regardless of enrolment to places within the control figures [1].

## Withdrawal, changes and the orders

- At any stage the applicant may withdraw the consent (on ЕПГУ or by an application in person or by post) and withdraw the application for admission (then he is excluded from the lists of those who applied, from the competitive lists and from among those enrolled) [1].
- A person already enrolled may refuse enrolment without withdrawing his consent; a person enrolled to places within the control figures who wants to withdraw the consent must refuse enrolment simultaneously with the withdrawal [1].
- Changes to the competitive lists and to the lists of those who applied are made within 2 (two) hours if the data or the application arrive not less than 2 hours before the end of the working day, within the first 2 hours of the next working day if they arrive later, and not later than the next working day for applications received by post; these actions are not made on the day of the enrolment orders, except on master's programmes and on paid places [1].
- After the completion of admission to places within the control figures the originals of the documents are returned within 1 (one) working day after the application for withdrawal of the application is received, and if return is impossible they are kept at the University [1].
- Enrolment is formalised by an order (orders) at each stage; the orders may be published with the unique code; information on the results of enrolment (unique code, sum of competitive points, points for the entrance examinations and for individual achievements, grounds for admission without entrance examinations) is published on the day of the order and must be available for 6 (six) months [1].

## Stages of enrolment

- Enrolment to places within the control figures is conducted on bachelor's programmes in 3 (three) stages — the priority stage, the main stage and the additional stage — and on master's programmes in 2 (two) stages — the main stage and the additional stage [1].
- At the priority stage of bachelor's enrolment the places filled are the main budget places without entrance examinations, the special quota places (without entrance examinations and on results), the target quota places (without entrance examinations and on results) and the separate quota places (without entrance examinations and on results); where the highest pass priority is the priority of the target quota, the applicant is enrolled to the target quota, and where it is the priority of other places the applicant is enrolled to the main budget places without entrance examinations, otherwise to the separate quota, otherwise to the special quota [1].
- At the main stage of bachelor's enrolment the main budget places are filled on the results of the ЕГЭ and/or internal examinations according to the priority of other places; at the additional stage the unfilled main budget places are filled on the same basis [1].
- Places within the special, separate and target quotas that remain unfilled or become free after the priority stage are added to the main budget places; persons enrolled to the special and separate quotas are excluded from the competitive lists for the main budget places for the conditions under which they are enrolled [1].
- A person enrolled at an earlier stage who wants to take part in a later stage must refuse the enrolment already made (and, to take part in a later stage in another organisation, also withdraw the consent); those who did not refuse by the term of presentation of consents of the later stage are not enrolled at that stage [1].
- At the main stage of master's enrolment the target quota places and the main budget places are filled, and an applicant is enrolled to the target quota where the highest pass priority is the priority of the target quota and to the main budget places where it is the priority of other places; at the additional stage the unfilled main budget places are filled; unfilled target quota places are added to the main budget places [1].

## Deadlines of the 2026/2027 admission

- Bachelor's, control figures, full-time: acceptance of applications and documents starts on 20 June, ends on 20 July for those taking entrance examinations and on 25 July for those not taking them (until 17:00 Moscow time); entrance examinations from 1 July to 25 July; competitive lists on 27 July; at the priority stage consent until 1 August 12:00 and the order on 3 August; at the main stage consent until 5 August 12:00 and the order on 7 August; at the additional stage consent until 9 August 12:00 and the orders on 11 August; the number of places for additional admission is set not later than 12 August [1].
- Additional admission, bachelor's, control figures, full-time: acceptance of documents from 12 August, ending on 20 August for those taking entrance examinations and on 21 August for those not taking them (until 17:00 Moscow time); entrance examinations from 13 August to 21 August; competitive lists on 22 August; at the priority stage consent until 25 August 12:00 and orders on 26 August; at the main stage consent until 28 August 12:00 and orders on 29 August [1].
- Bachelor's, paid places, full-time: acceptance of documents from 20 June to 11 August; entrance examinations from 1 July to 18 August; competitive lists on 20 August; conclusion of contracts until 24 August; enrolment order on 25 August [1].
- Master's, control figures, full-time: acceptance of documents from 20 June to 14 August; entrance examinations from 1 July to 20 August; at the main stage competitive lists on 21 August, consent until 24 August 12:00 and the order on 25 August; at the additional stage consent until 26 August 12:00 and the orders on 27 August; the number of places for additional admission is set not later than 30 August [1].
- Additional admission, master's, control figures, full-time: acceptance of documents starts on 31 August and ends not later than 10 September; consent until 15 September 12:00; enrolment completed not later than 20 September [1].
- Master's, paid places, full-time: acceptance of documents from 20 June to 18 August; entrance examinations from 1 July to 21 August; competitive lists on 22 August; contracts until 27 August; enrolment order on 28 August [1].
- Additional admission to paid places on bachelor's and master's programmes is held only for persons applying on the basis of a document of a foreign state on education; it is held once and ends for the full-time form not later than 31 October of the year of admission [1].
- Enrolment to paid places is conducted on the same dates for all conditions of admission — for bachelor's programmes and, separately, for master's programmes [1].

## Additional admission and transfer of places

- Additional admission is held once to places within the control figures (where the number of unfilled places for a competitive profile and a form of study is less than 10 (ten) — by decision of the University) and once to paid places by decision of the University for persons applying on the basis of a document of a foreign state on education; it is held on bachelor's programmes independently of master's programmes and no additional enrolment is held during it [1].
- In additional admission the University accepts applications and documents, holds internal examinations and enrols; by decision of the University the results of internal examinations passed during the main admission may be taken into account [1].
- Additional admission to places within the control figures is held to the special quota places, the separate quota places (bachelor's programmes) and the main budget places [1].
- In additional admission a person having the right to admission without entrance examinations may use it regardless of whether he used it in the main admission; the number of organisations and of areas of study for simultaneous application includes those of the additional admission and of the main admission to paid places [1].
- If the offers of the customers cover fewer places than the target quota, the University may, not earlier than 15 June and not later than 19 June, transfer target quota places — to the separate quota on bachelor's programmes and to the main budget places on master's programmes [1].
- At the priority stage of bachelor's enrolment, between the completion of the presentation of consents and the enrolment orders, the University transfers surplus special quota and/or target quota places to the separate quota, and surplus separate quota places — to the special quota where it lacks places, otherwise to the main budget places; at the main stage of master's enrolment surplus target quota places are transferred to the main budget places [1].

## Information about the admission

The official site publishes, not later than 20 January of the year of admission, the list of areas of study with places within the control figures at the expense of the federal budget (without quotas), the admission rules, the terms of admission, the list of entrance examinations with their maximum and minimum points, priority, form, languages, programme and conditions for the disabled, the procedure and list of individual achievements and information on special rights, medical examinations, the places of acceptance of documents and dormitories; not later than 1 April — the areas with places at the expense of a regional or local budget; not later than 15 April — the number of places under various conditions including the quotas, the number of paid places (which cannot be changed after 15 April) and a sample contract on education; not later than 1 June — the number of places in dormitories and the schedule of entrance examinations; the information is accessible from the date of publication until the day of completion of the admission inclusive [1]. Information about applicants is published without surnames, first names and patronymics, with the applicant's unique code [1].

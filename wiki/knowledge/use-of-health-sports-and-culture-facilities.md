# Use of the University's health-improving, cultural and sports facilities — the Порядок of 2 March 2021

## The document

The «Порядок пользования лечебно-оздоровительной инфраструктурой, объектами культуры и спорта» of ANO VO «Университет Иннополис» was approved by order of 2 March 2021 № АХД-01/2021/03/02, signed by the director A.G. Tormasov, and is dated Innopolis 2021 [1]. The page is a scan read by OCR, so numbers and dates should be checked against the PDF before relying on them [1].

The Порядок regulates the use by the University's students (обучающиеся) of the University's health-improving infrastructure, cultural objects and sports objects, in order to ensure the students' development and the protection of their health in the course of the educational process [2]. Students have the right to free use of the health-improving infrastructure, cultural objects and sports objects [3].

It was developed in accordance with the Constitution of the Russian Federation, the legislation of the Russian Federation, decrees of the President and decisions and instructions of the Government of the Russian Federation, normative legal acts of federal executive bodies, legislative and normative acts of the state bodies of the Republic of Tatarstan, the University's Charter, the Internal Rules for students, and the University's internal normative and administrative documents [4].

## Health-improving infrastructure

Within the implementation of the current legislation on the protection of citizens' health, the University uses its health-improving infrastructure for providing qualified help to students: primary medical-sanitary help, and sanitary-antiepidemic and preventive measures ensuring the students' safety while they are at the University [5].

- Primary medical-sanitary help to students is provided on the basis of the activity of a round-the-clock medical point on the University's territory [6].
- Medical personnel, together with the University's administration and staff, are responsible for carrying out health-improving, antiepidemic and preventive measures and for observing sanitary-hygienic norms [7].
- The University provides premises with the corresponding conditions for the work of medical workers for free use [8].

The tasks of the medical personnel are [9]:

- providing first emergency pre-doctor medical help to patients with acute and sudden illnesses, injuries, poisoning and other accidents;
- providing emergency help in accordance with the available list of medicines;
- carrying out primary diagnostics in accordance with the available equipment;
- taking part in the University's measures to cultivate in students the qualities needed to preserve and strengthen their moral and physical health, spiritual development and self-improvement.

## Visiting the medical point

Students have the right to visit the University's medical point in the following cases [10]:

- when their state of health worsens;
- when chronic illnesses become acute;
- when they receive injuries and poisoning, regardless of where these were received (on the way to the University, on the adjacent territory, in class, during a break, while taking part in an event).

When visiting the medical point students have the right to receive the following medical services free of charge [11]:

- measuring their body temperature, pressure, pulse, weight and height;
- receiving first pre-doctor help;
- receiving preventive vaccinations;
- consultation on ways to improve their health and on a healthy way of life.

When receiving medical help students are obliged to inform the medical worker about [12]:

- changes in their state of health;
- the particularities of their health;
- the presence of chronic illnesses;
- illnesses they have had;
- allergy to food products and other substances and to medical preparations;
- the inadmissibility (intolerance) of particular medical preparations;
- their health group for physical culture classes;
- their telephone number, including the contact details of their parents (legal representatives).

When visiting the medical point students are obliged to be careful in using the medical instruments and to follow the medical worker's instructions promptly and in full [13].

The working hours of the University's medical point are set by an approved schedule; use of the health-improving infrastructure in the absence of medical personnel is strictly prohibited [14].

## Sports objects

The University uses sports objects taken by it on lease under lease contracts with АО «Иннополис» [15].

The sports objects available to students are [16]:

- a sports hall with an area of 923,7 sq. m.;
- a 25 m lane in the swimming pool;
- a hall for group classes for 20 people;
- a hall for group classes for 25 people;
- a hall for group classes for 35 people;
- a hall for group classes for 40 people;
- a basketball court;
- a volleyball court (classic);
- a volleyball court (beach);
- a tennis court;
- running tracks;
- a football field;
- sector № 21 of the sports core.

All the objects are located at the address: Republic of Tatarstan, Verkhneuslonsky district, the city of Innopolis, Sportivnaya street, building 107 [16].

- The sports objects may be used for classes in the elective courses in physical culture and sport, in adaptive physical culture, for training in student sports clubs, for sports competitions and for other University events provided by the plan [17].
- The organisation of the activity of the sports objects is regulated by the class schedule and by the working schedule of the sports sections [18].
- Use of the objects listed above within the approved schedule is free of charge for students [19].
- Contraindications to attending classes and sports sections are issued on the results of the students' medical examination [20].

Classes in the elective courses in physical culture and sport and sports competitions may be held only when the weather conditions present no danger to the students' health and when the places of the classes, the equipment and the clothing of the participants correspond to the meteorological conditions and to sanitary-hygienic norms [21].

Those responsible for holding classes in the elective courses in physical culture and sport and competitions are obliged to [22]:

- personally inspect the place of the classes or competitions and check that the equipment and inventory are in working order and correspond to the safety norms adopted in the respective sports;
- check the quality of the personal inventory and equipment used by the students;
- be personally present when students visit the sports object and during trainings and sports events, and never leave students without supervision;
- in case of emergency ensure the evacuation of students from the sports objects.

At classes in the elective courses in physical culture and sport and when visiting the sports hall students must have sports clothing and footwear; it is prohibited to visit the sports hall without sports footwear and sports clothing [23].

## Cultural objects

The organisation of the activity of the cultural objects is directed at developing the students' personality [24]. The University's cultural objects are the reading hall of the study-laboratory building, the hall on the first floor of the study-laboratory building, and the recreation area «Зеленые ступеньки» of the study-laboratory building [24].

These objects are used for cultural-mass measures, concerts, exhibitions and leisure events [25]. Attendance of studios and events at the University's cultural objects is free of charge [26]. The organisation of their activity is regulated by the plan of extracurricular events [27].

When using the University's cultural objects students are obliged to [28]:

- maintain cleanliness and order;
- comply with the requirements of the persons responsible for the object;
- immediately inform the responsible persons about cases of discovering suspicious objects or things and about cases of smoke or fire;
- when receiving information about evacuation, act according to the instructions of the responsible persons, keeping calm and not creating panic.

While using the cultural objects students are prohibited from [29]:

- bringing in and (or) consuming alcoholic drinks, narcotic and toxic substances;
- bringing in weapons, flammable, explosive, pyrotechnic, poisonous and strong-smelling substances, stabbing and cutting objects, glassware, gas canisters;
- smoking;
- bringing or leading in animals;
- committing acts that humiliate or insult the human dignity of other students or of the University's employees;
- displaying signs or other symbols aimed at inciting racial, religious or national hatred or insulting students or the University's employees.

Students who have caused damage to the University's cultural objects bear liability in accordance with the current legislation of the Russian Federation [30].

On the basis of the current legislation students and employees of the University are granted the right to use library-information services [31]. The rules of use of the scientific-technical library are set out in order of ANO VO «Университет Иннополис» № АД-01/2018/05/16 of 16 May 2018 [32].

# E-learning and distance educational technologies in bachelor's, master's and aspirantura programmes — the Regulation of 2 August 2022: terms, process, identification, students and teachers

## The document

The Regulation on the procedure for applying e-learning and distance educational technologies in the implementation of higher education programmes — bachelor's, master's and the training of scientific and scientific-pedagogical staff in aspirantura — was approved by order of ANO VO «Innopolis University» of 2 August 2022 (Innopolis, 2022) and regulates how those programmes are implemented with e-learning and distance educational technologies (ЭО and ДОТ) [1]. The wiki page is an OCR of a scanned document; its review warns that numbers and dates should be checked against the PDF, including the garbled order number in the approval block [2].

The Regulation was developed in accordance with Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29 December 2012 «Об образовании в Российской Федерации», the federal laws of 27 July 2006 on information, information technologies and information protection and on personal data, the order of the Ministry of Education and Science of 23 August 2017 No. 816 on the procedure for applying e-learning and distance educational technologies, the joint order of 5 August 2020 No. 885/390 on practical training, the orders on the organisation of educational activity under aspirantura programmes and on the procedure for state final attestation under bachelor's, specialist's, master's and aspirantura programmes, the federal state educational standards of higher education, and the University's Charter and local acts [3].

It enters into force from 1 September 2022, and amendments are made by order of the University director or of a person authorised by the director [4] [5].

## Terms

- E-learning (ЭО) is the organisation of educational activity using information contained in databases and used in the implementation of the programmes, the information technologies and technical means that process it, and information and telecommunication networks that transmit that information and provide interaction between students, pedagogical workers and persons engaged by the University on other conditions [6].
- Distance educational technologies (ДОТ) are educational technologies implemented mainly with information and telecommunication networks, with mediated (at a distance) interaction of students and pedagogical workers [6].
- The electronic information and educational environment (ЭИОС) is the combination of electronic information and educational resources, information and telecommunication technologies and corresponding technical means that ensures students master the programmes regardless of their location [6].
- An information and telecommunication network is a technological system for transmitting information over communication lines, access to which is obtained with computer equipment, including the Internet [6].

## Purposes and general rules

The purposes of ЭО and ДОТ are: raising the quality of higher education by involving leading Russian and foreign specialists, pedagogical workers and scientists who are not on the University's staff; individualisation and personalisation of learning taking into account each student's initial level, speed of perception, preferred forms of presentation and the volume and depth of material, including through the individual curriculum; accessibility of higher education by allowing students to master the programmes at their place of residence or temporary stay; and intensification of the educational process through new principles, methods and technical means [7].

The University determines independently the ratio of the volume of classes held through direct interaction of a pedagogical worker with a student, including with ЭО and ДОТ; the place of educational activity is the one determined by the licence, regardless of where the student or the pedagogical workers are [8] [9].

## Organisation of the educational process

- The process with ЭО and ДОТ is conducted in accordance with Russian legislation, the University's educational programmes and the local acts regulating the educational process [10].
- Educational activity is conducted in the form of contact work of students with pedagogical workers, in the form of students' independent work, and in other forms established by the University, including during practice; contact work may be conducted with ЭО and ДОТ in the ЭИОС [11] [12].
- Students' independent work with ЭО and ДОТ means purposeful, controlled and intensive work at a place convenient for the student, with special learning tools and an agreed possibility of contact with pedagogical workers through information and telecommunication technologies [13].
- For other forms of educational activity the use of ЭО and ДОТ is regulated by the University's local acts [14].
- The University informs participants of educational relations by placing the relevant local acts and information materials in the ЭИОС, sending acts and materials to corporate e-mail addresses, publishing materials and links to acts in its official accounts in messengers and social networks, and, where necessary, familiarising participants with the local acts against signature [15].
- Matters of organising the process with ЭО and ДОТ are decided by the subdivision implementing the educational programme, which prepares the necessary documentation and may involve specialists of organisations and enterprises in drafting it [16] [17].
- The main interaction, including contact work, takes place in the University's ЭИОС, and with ЭО and ДОТ the absence of classroom classes held through direct interaction of teachers with students is allowed [18] [19].
- A fully remote process, as a whole or for individual disciplines (modules) of the curriculum, is organised only where there is teaching and methodological help for students, including individual consultations given remotely, and appropriate qualification of the pedagogical and other staff who use and support the ЭИОС [20].
- Students and pedagogical workers must have access to the educational programme with its curriculum, academic calendar, work programmes of disciplines (modules), other components, assessment and methodological materials, the work programme of upbringing and the calendar plan of upbringing work, and to educational resources — notes, textbooks, manuals, problem books, simulators, tests, glossaries, reference and further literature [21].
- Records of the results of the educational process and internal document flow are kept on paper and/or in electronic digital form in accordance with the Russian legislation on state secrets, personal data and archiving [22].
- Ongoing assessment, interim attestation and elimination of academic debt may be organised with ЭО and ДОТ subject to the University's local acts on those procedures, and a student who has fully mastered a programme implemented entirely with ЭО and ДОТ is admitted to the state final attestation [23] [24].

## Identification in the ЭИОС

- Identification is the process of establishing the identity of a student or pedagogical worker by a set of features, through their comparative study [25].
- Every student and pedagogical worker has an ЭИОС subject identifier — a personal e-mail address tied to the name and surname [26].
- A student receives the identifier after enrolment together with a primary password; pedagogical workers receive identifiers and primary passwords on hiring, and responsibility for losing passwords or passing them to third parties lies with the subject [27] [28] [29].
- The ordinary level of identification is entering the identifier (login) and password, after which the subject obtains access to all data and to the ЭИОС modules defined for that subject [30].
- The control level of identification is the reconciliation of online data with personal data, including data from the personal file; online data may be streamed video from a camera showing the person's face or biometric data, obtained with Apple Touch/Face ID or Android finger or face scanning [31].
- The control level may be used for ongoing knowledge assessment and interim attestation and is obligatory when students pass the final (state final) attestation [31].
- The University protects the identifier and password base by technical restrictions on password length and characters, limited password validity periods requiring periodic replacement, restricted and protected access to the password file and the biometric image bank, a limit on failed identification attempts, and preliminary training and consultations for users [32].

## Students

- After enrolment each student is assigned an identifier (personal login) and a primary password for entering the ЭИОС [33].
- The rights and duties of students studying with ЭО and ДОТ are determined by the Charter, the University's internal study rules and applicable legislation [34].
- The University gives students (remote) access to modern professional databases and information reference systems, the list of which is set in the work programmes of disciplines (modules) and updated as necessary [35].
- For people with disabilities and health limitations ЭО and ДОТ provide for the receipt and transmission of information in forms accessible to them, while learning and methodological materials are passed to the student without the right to reproduce them or pass them to third parties and organisations [36] [37].
- The University determines the procedure of teaching and methodological help, including individual consultations given remotely [38].
- Interaction of students and pedagogical workers during learning activities covers lectures; seminars, practical classes (including in physical culture and sport), practicals, laboratory work, colloquia and similar classes, as well as chat interviews; group consultations (including supervision of practice) by e-mail, chat conferences and forums; individual consultations (including supervision of practice and of the preparation of the final qualifying work); practice; research work under aspirantura programmes; ongoing assessment and interim attestation in the form of testing and other assessment measures; state final attestation; and independent work with textbooks, manuals and materials — each online/offline as indicated [39].
- On the Moodle portal, before the start of the semester the pedagogical worker assigned to a discipline (module) places the work plan (syllabus), information on the grading system and criteria (grading policy), the academic honesty policy and other materials at the teacher's discretion; during the study of the discipline the teacher places learning materials for each topic and fills in the attendance section and the electronic journal of classes [2].

## Pedagogical workers

- Coordination and control of the use of ЭО and ДОТ is assigned to the subdivision implementing the educational programme [40].
- A pedagogical worker has the right to control the learning process through tests and test tasks provided by the programme, to organise forums and online consultations, and to receive consultative and technical help from the subdivision implementing the programme and/or other University staff performing those functions [41].
- The pedagogical worker's duties are to design and deliver good-quality teaching with ЭО and ДОТ; to place, add to and edit learning materials in the ЭИОС in time; to conduct all types of classes and assessment procedures provided with ЭО and ДОТ in accordance with the calendar schedule and timetable; to monitor attendance at online classes where necessary, including with ordinary or control identification, single sign-on (SSO) data and other technologies; to organise individual work with students; to monitor all students' actions in the ЭИОС; to help with processing information, advise students on presenting the results of independent work, give feedback on tasks, answer questions in time and diagnose difficulties; and to systematically improve professional qualification, including in the specific tools of ЭО and ДОТ [42].

## Software, hardware and networks

- The procedure for creating and operating the ЭИОС is determined by the University in accordance with Federal Law No. 149-ФЗ of 27 July 2006 on information, information technologies and information protection and other federal laws [43].
- Technical support of ЭО and ДОТ includes computer equipment and other equipment needed to operate, develop and store the ЭИОС software and information provision, to give teachers and students access to electronic information resources and to connect the teacher with students over the Internet, and communication equipment providing access to the ЭИОС through local networks and the Internet [44].
- The ЭИОС software provides access to curricula, work programmes of disciplines (modules) and practices, and the electronic publications and resources listed in them; forms the student's electronic portfolio with their works and marks; fixes the course of the educational process, interim attestation results and the results of mastering the programmes; allows classes and assessment procedures with ЭО and ДОТ; and supports synchronous and/or asynchronous interaction of participants over the Internet [45].
- Information and telecommunication networks, including the Internet, are used in compliance with Russian legislation on communications, Federal Law No. 149-ФЗ and other legal acts; the use of networks open to an unrestricted circle of persons follows the generally accepted international practice of self-regulating organisations, and the use of other networks is determined by their owners under Russian legislation [46] [47].
- Information is transmitted through the networks without restrictions provided the requirements of federal laws on the dissemination of information and the protection of intellectual property are met, and may be restricted only in the procedure and on the conditions established by federal laws; restriction of access to information disseminated in violation of copyright and/or related rights is regulated by Article 15.2 of Federal Law No. 149-ФЗ [48] [49].

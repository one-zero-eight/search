# Interim assessment in PhD aspirantura (2018 and 2022 regulations)

## Two documents

Interim assessment of aspirants at Innopolis University is governed by aspirantura regulations separate from the bachelor's and master's regulation [1]. The wiki holds two of them: the Regulation on ongoing assessment and interim assessment of students on higher education programmes training highly qualified personnel in aspirantura, approved by order No. ОД/УД-04/2018/06/04 of 4 June 2018 [2], and the Regulation on ongoing assessment and interim assessment of aspirants approved by order No. ОД/АД-12/2022/03/01 of 1 March 2022 and put into force on 1 March 2022 [3]. Neither page says that it repeals the other, so what each says is given below separately [2], [3]. Both pages are OCR of scanned documents, so their figures and dates should be checked against the PDFs [2], [3].

## The earlier regulation: scope and schedule

The scope of interim assessment and its distribution over periods of study are determined by the curriculum and the academic calendar [4]. The schedule of interim assessment for all forms of study and all fields, with rooms, is drawn up by the structural unit responsible for organizing education in aspirantura, approved by the director of the University and communicated to teachers and aspirants by corporate e-mail not later than one week before the start of interim assessment [5].

The forms of interim assessment in aspirantura are the pass (зачёт) and the exam (экзамен) [6]. It may be held orally and/or in writing, as testing including with computers and hardware, as defence of a referat or project, or in combined form; the type is determined by the teacher responsible for the discipline (module) and for the supervision of internships and research, fixed in the discipline work programme and communicated to students [7]. The results of interim assessment in a discipline (module), internship or research work may include all the aspirant's ongoing assessment results in it [8]. At the teacher's discretion the aspirant may be allowed during an exam or pass to use curricula, reference books and other sources of information, the list being set by the teacher [9]. An aspirant who does not appear for an exam or pass without a valid reason is considered to have failed it [10].

An aspirant who presents a document confirming absence from exams and passes for a valid reason (illness, family circumstances — death or illness of a close relative — cancellation of a flight), not later than three working days from receiving that document and on the basis of a personal application (Appendix No. 3), may have the interim assessment extended by the number of calendar days stated in the document (sick leave, certificate, medical conclusion etc.); the extension is formalized by an order of the director of the University (Appendix No. 4) [11].

Recording of results in aspirantura is done by staff of the responsible structural unit, who transfer them from the interim assessment sheet filled in by teachers into Moodle [12]; the results are fixed in the University's electronic information-educational environment — Moodle, the «1С: Университет» educational process management system, the student's portfolio and personal cabinet [13].

### Grades for aspirants (earlier regulation)

The regulation sets rules for translating the grades of the assessment system into the five-point system and criteria for each grade [2]:

- «отлично» (A): the aspirant masters the subject in the full volume of the programme, answers all questions independently, in logical sequence and exhaustively, can analyse, compare, generalize, specify and systematize the material, establish causal links, solve situation tasks of increased complexity, knows the main literature and research methods and links theory with practical tasks; all universal, general professional and professional competences provided by the aspirantura programme are formed at a high level [2];
- «хорошо» (B): the subject is mastered practically in full, with the competences formed at a level above average [2];
- «удовлетворительно» (C): the aspirant knows the main volume, has difficulties in independent answers, uses imprecise formulations and makes errors on the substance, can solve only the easiest tasks and knows only the mandatory minimum of research methods; the competences are formed at an average level [2];
- «неудовлетворительно» (D): the mandatory minimum of knowledge is not mastered and the aspirant cannot answer even with leading questions; the competences are formed at an insufficient level [2];
- «зачтено» (P): the aspirant knows the main programme material in the volume needed for further study and work in the specialty and has coped with the tasks provided by the discipline programme; the competences are formed at a sufficient level [2];
- «не зачтено» (F): the mandatory minimum is not mastered, the aspirant cannot answer even with leading questions, the competences are formed at an insufficient level [2].

Results A, B, C and P are positive and are not subject to a repeat sitting aimed at improving the result [14]. The second sentence of that clause, which names the unsatisfactory results, is corrupted in the published text (it reads «Р и Е»); unsatisfactory results of interim assessment in one or more disciplines (modules), internships or research work are recognized as academic debt [14], [15].

### Transfer to the next year (earlier regulation)

Aspirants who have fully fulfilled the requirements of the curriculum of the current year of study and have successfully passed all passes and exams are transferred to the next year by order of the director on the presentation of the vice-rector — head of the educational activity management, one week before the start of the next academic year [16]. Aspirants who have academic debt on the results of interim assessment are transferred to the next year conditionally [17].

### Aspirants with health limitations (earlier regulation)

For ongoing assessment and interim assessment of aspirants with health limitations (ОВЗ), adapted assessment materials are created that allow evaluating the achievement of the learning results planned in the aspirantura programme and the level of all the competences it declares [18]. The form of the assessment is set taking into account individual psycho-physical features — orally, in writing on paper, in writing on a computer, as testing etc. [19]. For visual impairment the University provides the presence of an assistant who helps the aspirant at passes and exams, dictates written tasks to the assistant, and provides a magnifying device where needed; the aspirant's own magnifying devices may also be used [20]. For persons with severe speech disorders, deaf and hard-of-hearing persons, assessment tests held orally must be held in writing [21]. Where necessary, an aspirant with ОВЗ is given additional time to prepare an answer at a pass or exam upon a written application, the total duration of the assessment test being increased by not more than 1.5 hours [22]. Such aspirants may use technical means they need in connection with their individual features during the assessment test [23].

## The later regulation: scope, forms and schedule

The second document is the Regulation approved by order No. ОД/АД-12/2022/03/01 of 1 March 2022, which put it into force on 1 March 2022 [3]. It determines the forms, periodicity and procedure of ongoing and interim assessment of aspirants, the grading system and criteria for grades, and the procedure and deadlines for clearing academic debt [24]. It rests on Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29 December 2012, Government Decree No. 2122 of 30 November 2021, Ministry of Science and Higher Education orders No. 247 of 28 March 2014 and No. 951 of 20 October 2021, and the University's Устав [25]. Control of the quality of mastering the programme consists of ongoing assessment, interim assessment and итоговая аттестация [26].

Interim assessment evaluates the results of the stages of the aspirant's research (научная деятельность), of mastering disciplines (modules) and of internships, in accordance with the individual research plan and the individual curriculum [27]. Its volume and distribution over periods of study are set by the curriculum and the academic calendar [3]. The schedule for all forms of study and all scientific specialties, with rooms, is drawn up by the structural unit responsible for organising education in aspirantura, approved by the director or an authorised person and brought to teachers and aspirants by corporate e-mail not later than one week before the start of interim assessment [28].

The forms of interim assessment are the зачёт, the экзамен and the кандидатский экзамен [29]; the form is determined by the curriculum [30]. The earlier page names only the pass and the exam [6]. The procedure of candidate examinations is set by the University's local acts in accordance with the federal procedure for taking candidate examinations and their list [31].

Interim assessment may be held orally and/or in writing, as testing including with computers and hardware, as defence of a referat or project, or in combined form; the kind is chosen by the teacher responsible for the discipline (module) and for the supervision of the internship and research, fixed in the discipline work programme, the internship programme and the individual research plan, and communicated to aspirants [32]. At the teacher's discretion the aspirant may use curricula, reference books and other sources at an exam or pass, the list being set by the teacher [33].

### Supervisor's review and recording (later regulation)

During interim assessment the scientific supervisor presents a review (отзыв) on the quality, timeliness and success of the stages of research carried out by the aspirant [34]. The results of interim assessment in a discipline (module), internship and research may include all the aspirant's ongoing assessment results in it [3]. Staff of the responsible structural unit transfer results from the interim assessment sheet (Appendix 1) filled in by teachers into Moodle [3]; results are fixed in Moodle, in the «1С: Университет» educational process management system, in the student's portfolio and personal cabinet (my.university.innopolis.ru) [35]. The forms of the interim assessment sheet, of the repeat and of the second repeat interim assessment sheet are its Appendices 1, 2 and 3 [3].

### Absence, extension and grades (later regulation)

An aspirant who fails to appear for an exam or pass without a valid reason is considered to have failed it [36]. An aspirant who presents a document confirming absence for a valid reason (illness, family circumstances — death or illness of a close relative — cancellation of a flight), not later than three working days from receiving that document and on the basis of a personal application, may have the interim assessment period extended by the number of calendar days stated in the document (sick leave, certificate, medical conclusion etc.); the extension is formalised by an order of the director or an authorised person [37].

The regulation gives the criteria for the grades «отлично», «хорошо», «удовлетворительно», «неудовлетворительно», «зачтено» and «не зачтено» in the five-point system [3]. Results A, B, C and P are positive and are not subject to a repeat sitting aimed at improving the result [38].

### Transfer to the next course (later regulation)

Aspirants who have fully fulfilled the requirements of the individual curriculum and of the individual research plan of the current year of study and have passed all passes and exams are transferred to the next course by order of the director or an authorised person on the presentation of the vice-rector — head of the academic policy and educational activity management, of the deputy of that head or of the head of the responsible structural unit, one week before the start of the next academic year [39]. Aspirants with academic debt on the results of interim assessment are transferred to the next course conditionally [40].

### Aspirants with health limitations (later regulation)

Adapted assessment materials are created that allow evaluating the achievement of the learning results planned in the programme and the level of all the competences it declares [41]. The form of the assessment is set taking account of individual psycho-physical features — orally, in writing on paper, in writing on a computer, as testing etc. [42]. For visual impairment the University ensures the presence of an assistant who helps the aspirant at passes and exams, dictates written tasks to the assistant and provides a magnifying device where needed; the aspirant's own magnifying devices may also be used [43]. For severe speech disorders, deaf and hard-of-hearing aspirants, assessment tests held orally are held in writing [44]. On written application an aspirant with ОВЗ is given additional time to prepare an answer, the total duration of the assessment test being increased by not more than 1.5 hours [45], and such aspirants may use the technical means they need because of their individual features [46].

The regulation is approved by order of the director of the University or an authorised person and enters into force from the moment of its approval [47]; changes or additions are made by order of the director or an authorised person [48].

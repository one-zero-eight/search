# Admission to PhD aspirantura 2026/2027 — places, competitions, applications, documents and dates

## The document

The Rules of admission to higher education programmes — programmes for the training of scientific and scientific-pedagogical staff in aspirantura at ANO VO «Innopolis University» for the 2026/2027 academic year were approved by the University's order No. ОД/УД-01/2026/01/19 of 19 January 2026 and put into force from 20 January 2026 [1]. They were developed on the basis of Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ and of the order of the Ministry of Science and Higher Education No. 366 of 18.04.2025 on the procedure of admission to aspirantura programmes [2]. The page is a scanned document read by OCR, and the wiki warns that its numbers and dates should be checked against the PDF before relying on them [1].

## Who may apply and to which places

- Admission is announced on the basis of the licence for educational activity No. Л035-00115-77/00642712 issued by the Federal Service for Supervision in Education and Science on 25 December 2015 for an indefinite term [3].
- Admission is to the first course [4].
- Persons with education not lower than higher (specialist or master) are admitted; it is confirmed by a document of education and qualification of the established format, a state-format document obtained before 1 January 2014, documents of Moscow State University or Saint Petersburg State University, documents of organisations of the Skolkovo innovation centre or of an innovative scientific-technological centre, a certificate of completion of aspirantura, or a foreign document of education recognised in Russia [5].
- The University admits to places within the control figures of admission — the target quota and the main budget places left after it — and to paid places under contracts at the expense of persons and (or) legal entities and of the University's own funds [6].
- If no main budget places remain after the target quota is allocated, admission to main budget places is announced with a number of places equal to zero [1].

## Competitions, lists and commissions

A separate competition is held for each set of conditions of admission: full-time form; separately by direction (profile) of the aspirantura programme, i.e. by scientific specialty; separately within the control figures and for paid places; separately for the target quota and for the main budget places [7]. For all competitions within the full-time condition the University sets the same list of entrance examinations and the same minimum and maximum points [8]. For each competition group it forms the lists of persons who submitted applications and the ranked competitive lists [9].

If places remain after enrollment, the University may hold an additional admission; its dates are set by an act of the director or an authorised person not later than 2 working days after the completion of enrollment under clause 80, and information about it is published on the official site [10].

The organisational support of admission is provided by the admission committee created by the University; its chair is the director of the University, and the chair appoints a responsible secretary who organises the work of the committee and the personal reception of applicants and their legal representatives [11]. For the entrance examinations the University creates an examination and an appeal commission [11]. The powers and procedure of the admission committee and of the examination and appeal commissions are determined by regulations approved by the director [11].

## Information published on the site

Not later than 20 January of the year of admission the University publishes the list of scientific specialties, the admission rules, the dates of admission, the list of entrance examinations with their maximum and minimum points, priority, form, languages and programme, information on holding them in person or with distance technologies and on special conditions for disabled persons and persons with health limitations, the procedure and list of general individual achievements, whether a preliminary medical examination is required, the places for submitting documents and the postal and electronic addresses, the availability of dormitories and the number of places within the control figures without the target quota [12]. Not later than 10 April it publishes the number of places within the control figures by competition group, including the target quota [12]. Not later than 2 months before the start of enrollment to paid places it publishes the number of paid places and the sample contract [12]. Not later than 1 June it publishes the number of places in dormitories [12]. Not later than 14 calendar days before the start of the examinations it publishes the schedule of the examinations [12]. The information stays available from its placement until the end of admission, and telephone lines and a section of the site answer questions about admission [13]. Information about applicants is placed without surnames, names and patronymics, with the unique code of the applicant [14].

## Applications and documents

An applicant submits one application for places within the control figures (if he wants them), one application for paid places (if he wants them) and the necessary documents [15]. They may be brought in person, sent through a postal operator or submitted through the EPGU portal, and the University ensures all these ways; documents are accepted at 420500, Republic of Tatarstan, Verkhneuslonsky municipal district, Innopolis, Universitetskaya st., 1, room 100 (admission committee), and a receipt is issued for documents brought in person [16].

In the application the applicant indicates the competition groups and the priorities of admission, numbered with whole numbers starting from one, a lower number meaning a higher priority; the sequences for the control figures and for paid places are separate [17]. By his signature he confirms acquaintance with the requirement to give reliable information and genuine documents and with the admission rules and the documents listed in clause 25, and, for places within the control figures, that he receives higher education for the first time (no aspirantura diploma, certificate of completion of aspirantura or candidate of sciences diploma, except the cases established by law) [18]. The need for special conditions at the examinations is also indicated in the application [18]. The application is submitted in Russian [19]. The applicant may change it, including the competition groups and the priorities; changes of priorities on EPGU are made not more than once in 2 hours, and all changes are made not later than the day of completion of acceptance of applications [20].

The documents submitted are: an identity and citizenship document, together with the application; the document of education, not later than the day of completion of acceptance of consent to enrollment (a foreign document together with a certificate of recognition of foreign education, per clause 81); a document confirming registration in the individual (personalised) accounting system, if any; an application of consent to the processing of personal data; a document confirming disability or health limitations, if special conditions are needed, not later than 10 days before the examination; documents confirming individual achievements, at the applicant's discretion; the documents named in clauses 124–126; other documents at the applicant's discretion; photographs [21]. Documents may be submitted as originals or as copies (electronic images) without certification [22]. Documents in a foreign language need a notarised translation into Russian [23], and documents obtained abroad must be legalised unless an international treaty or Russian law provides otherwise [24]. The University checks the accuracy of the information and the authenticity of the documents, including through state information systems [25], forms the applicant's personal file [26] and, on the results of the acceptance and the examinations, decides on admitting the applicants to the competition [27].

## Dates of the 2026 campaign

Acceptance of applications and documents for places within the control figures (full-time) runs from 20 June 2026 to 14 August 2026; for paid places (full-time) from 20 June 2026 to 19 August 2026 [28]. Entrance examinations for places within the control figures run from 17 August 2026 to 21 August 2026, and for paid places from 20 August 2026 to 24 August 2026 [29].

## The applicant's calendar of the 2026/2027 admission

The applicant's calendar («Календарь абитуриента») of the University gives information on the terms of admission to aspirantura programmes in the 2026/2027 academic year [30]. It sets out the terms for full-time study within the control figures in one table and the terms for full-time study on places under contracts for paid educational services in another table [30].

Places within the control figures, full-time:

- acceptance of applications and documents starts on 20 June and ends on 14 August [30];
- entrance examinations are held from 17 August to 21 August [30];
- the competitive lists are published on 24 August [30];
- the presentation of consent to enrolment ends on 25 August until 18:00 Moscow time [30];
- the order on enrolment is issued on 26 August [30].

Places under contracts for paid educational services, full-time:

- acceptance of applications and documents starts on 20 June and ends on 19 August [30];
- entrance examinations are held from 20 August to 24 August [30];
- the competitive lists are published on 26 August [30];
- the conclusion of contracts on the provision of paid educational services ends on 28 August [30];
- the order on enrolment is issued on 29 August [30].

## What admission is based on

Admission is based on the results of entrance examinations, whose list and form are given in the annex to the rules [31]; each examination is assessed on a 100-point scale with the minimum confirming success at 60 points and the maximum at 100 points [32]. The University sets the list and the priority of the examinations [33], and the sum of competitive points is the sum of the points for the examinations and for individual achievements [34]. Individual achievements are counted as points added to the competitive score and as a criterion where applicants are equal [35], and not more than 20 points may be awarded for general achievements [36].

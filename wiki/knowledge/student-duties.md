# Duties of students under the Rules of internal order for students

## The document

The duties below are those of section 3 of the Rules of internal order for students (Правила внутреннего распорядка обучающихся) of the University, approved by its director [1]. They apply to all learners covered by the Rules — students of bachelor's and master's programmes, aspirants, listeners of additional professional programmes, pupils and externs [2]. Students are made acquainted with the Rules against signature when submitting an application for admission [3]. The wiki page is an OCR reading of a scanned document marked draft, with a warning to check numbers and dates against the PDF [4].

## Duties in study and in the University

A student of the University is obliged [1]:

- to master the educational programme conscientiously, carry out the individual curriculum, including attending the classes provided by the curriculum or the individual curriculum, to prepare for classes independently and to do the tasks given by teaching staff within the programme [5];
- to comply with the requirements of the University's charter, of these Rules — including the requirements on discipline in classes and the rules of conduct in the University — of the rules of residence in the residential complex and of the University's other local acts on the organisation and carrying out of educational activity [6];
- to take care of preserving and strengthening their health and to strive for moral, spiritual and physical development and self-improvement [7];
- to respect the honour and dignity of other students, teaching staff and employees of the University and not to create obstacles for other students' education [8];
- to refrain from immoral acts, including attempts to harm themselves or those around them, and not to take part in actions that may cause physical or psychological damage to other students, teaching staff or employees; in situations connected with a threat to health or safety, students must immediately inform the University's administration [9];
- to treat the University's property and that of third parties held by it carefully, not to bring in or take out material values belonging to the University, and not to move them from the University's premises, without agreement with the administration [10];
- to read on their own the content of the University's student information channels and of the corporate mailbox allocated to each student, in order to keep timely track of local acts, changes in the study process and information about meetings and other events, and to take part in the procedures of regular evaluation of the quality of programme delivery in the order and within the deadlines approved by the University's local acts [11];
- not to allow violations of intellectual (copyright and related, inventive, patent) rights during study, including appropriation of authorship (plagiarism), as well as copying (списывание), double submission, fraud, copying, and fabrication of data and results of work [12];
- to inform in writing in advance the responsible structural unit of the University — the one appointed by order of the director to control that type of activity — about the need to be absent from classes, including during interim and (or) state final attestation, and, when absent for a valid reason, to submit to that unit on the first day of appearance at the University documents confirming the valid reason [13];
- to arrange admission to the study process in time after academic and other leaves granted under Russian legislation, submitting an application for admission to the study process to the responsible structural unit at the end of the academic leave [14];
- to undergo medical examinations regularly and to take part in monitoring of psychological comfort and well-being and in other testing organised by the University; when refusing to receive help, the student must submit a written application of refusal [15];
- to complete all questionnaires connected with educational activity — such as questionnaires on satisfaction with study conditions and other similar surveys — and testing [16].

## Duties about the University's name and public statements

- Not to use the University's name, symbols or trademark without agreement with the responsible structural unit, including for commercial and (or) political purposes [17].
- Not to speak publicly and not to make statements about the activity of the University and its units (faculty, institute, other structural unit) without prior agreement with the responsible structural unit, in the order set by the University's local acts [18].
- Not to make political statements or state positions on behalf of the University or on behalf of an indefinite circle of students; when taking part in political or other publicly resonant activity, the student must take measures to exclude the affiliation of that activity with the University [19].
- Not to conduct political activity within the University's walls and not to allow actions or statements containing motives of political, ideological, racial, national or religious hatred or enmity, or motives of hatred or enmity towards a social group, including discriminatory actions or statements on the grounds of sex, race, skin colour, nationality, language, origin, property, family, social or official position, age, place of residence, attitude to religion, political convictions or membership of public associations [20].

## Duties on the campus and in the buildings

- When living in the University's residential complex or visiting it, to observe the rules of residence and internal order in the residential building [21].
- When entering the University's buildings and (or) being in its premises, to carry an identity document (student card) or electronic pass and to keep the student card and electronic pass carefully [22].
- Strictly to observe the fire and public safety rules on the University's territory and in its buildings [23].
- Not to use roller skates or other sports equipment for moving around the University's premises [24].
- To observe the rules of using the library and to return borrowed literature in time [25].
- Not to use the University's premises for purposes not connected with education and the extracurricular activity of the University's student clubs and communities, or for events not held and (or) not agreed by the University's administration [26].
- To submit to the responsible structural unit documents on a change of personal or passport data within 7 (seven) working days from the date of receiving the corresponding document [27].

## Military registration (male citizens of Russia)

Male citizens of Russia must carry out the instructions concerning military registration [28]:

- to submit to the responsible structural unit the documents needed for registration with the military commissariat of the Verkhneuslonsky district of the Republic of Tatarstan (at the place of actual residence in Innopolis) within 2 (two) weeks from notification [29];
- to collect from the responsible structural unit the call-up papers (повестки) sent to the University by the military commissariat where the student is registered, within 3 (three) working days from notification [30];
- to undergo medical examinations at the appointed time according to call-up papers from the military commissariat, and, if they cannot be attended because of illness, to submit a medical certificate [31];
- to sign the card of a citizen subject to military registration [32];
- to submit, at the request of the responsible structural unit, the military registration documents — the certificate of a citizen subject to military registration or the military record book [33];
- to notify the responsible structural unit within 2 (two) weeks of a change of military registration documents, of removal from registration and of registration with another military commissariat (different from the one indicated on admission) [34].

## Migration duties (foreign students)

Foreign students must observe the rules of Russian migration legislation, including [35]:

- to submit the corresponding set of documents to the responsible structural unit within 5 (five) working days after entering Russia [36];
- to submit the corresponding set of documents to the responsible structural unit 25 (twenty-five) working days before the registration expires; the set includes copies of all passport pages, a copy of the migration card and a copy of the tenancy agreement (for students living in the University's residential complex) [37];
- to submit the corresponding set of documents to the responsible structural unit not earlier than 3 (three) months and not later than 30 (thirty) working days before the study visa expires [38];
- to undergo the obligatory medical examination, fingerprint registration and photographing within 90 (ninety) working days from the date of arrival in Russia [39];
- to undergo the repeated medical examination once every year of stay in Russia [40];
- to submit the corresponding package of documents for a transit visa to leave Russia, in case of expulsion from the University, 7 (seven) working days before the date of expulsion [41];
- to leave Russia within 3 (three) working days from the date of expulsion on completion of study, on expulsion from the University, or on going on academic leave because of a reduction of the period of temporary stay [42];
- to provide a copy of the national passport to the responsible structural unit within 7 (seven) working days after receiving a new national passport — the page numbers this item 3.1.23.8 [4];
- to report the loss of an identity document to the responsible structural unit [43].

Other duties of learners not provided by clause 3.1 are established by the contract on education [44].

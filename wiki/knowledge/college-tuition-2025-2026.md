# College tuition cost for 2025/2026 (secondary vocational education)

## What the order sets

Order No. ОД/УД-01/2025/01/28 of 28 January 2025 of ANO VO "Innopolis University" sets the tuition cost for secondary vocational education programmes — programmes for training mid-level specialists implemented by Innopolis College (Колледж), a structural unit of the university, for the 2025/2026 academic year [1]. It was issued under Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29 December 2012 and the Rules for the provision of paid educational services approved by Government Resolution No. 706 of 15 August 2013, on the basis of a decision of the university's Operational Council (protocol No. 1/2025 of 27 January 2025) [1]. Control over execution is assigned to the College director I.N. Merkulova [1].

The prices apply to citizens of the Russian Federation, foreign citizens and stateless persons studying at places under contracts for the provision of paid educational services and admitted for study in the 2025/2026 academic year [1].

## Programmes and full cost for the whole period of study

The full cost of educational services for the whole period of study is set by Annex 1 to the order, for all of the following programmes full-time with a duration of 3 years 10 months [1]:

- specialty 09.02.07 Information systems and programming, qualification Программист — 1 150 000 rubles [1];
- specialty 09.02.07 Information systems and programming, qualification Разработчик веб и мультимедийных приложений — 1 150 000 rubles [1];
- specialty 09.02.07 Information systems and programming, qualification Администратор баз данных — 1 150 000 rubles [1];
- specialty 15.02.10 Mechatronics and robotics (by industry), qualification Специалист по мехатронике и робототехнике — 1 150 000 rubles [1].

## Cost of the first year in 2025/2026

For admission to the first course in the 2025/2026 academic year, Annex 2 to the order sets the cost at 300 000 rubles for each of the programmes listed above (course 1, full-time) [1].

## Reduced cost

The order approves the grounds for reducing the cost of paid educational services (Annex 3) and sets the cost with the reduction applied for the first course of 2025/2026 (Annex 4) [1]:

- the reduced full cost for the whole period of study is 805 000 rubles for each of the programmes listed above (full-time, 3 years 10 months) [1];
- the reduced cost for the first course of 2025/2026 is 210 000 rubles for each of the same programmes [1];
- the ground for the reduction is stated as "для детей сотрудников АНО ВО «Университет Иннополис»" — for children of employees of ANO VO "Innopolis University" [1].

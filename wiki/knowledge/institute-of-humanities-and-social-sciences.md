# Institute of Humanities and Social Sciences (Институт гуманитарных и социальных наук) — place, tasks, functions and rights

## The document

The Regulation on the Institute of Humanities and Social Sciences of ANO VO «Innopolis University» regulates the Institute's activity and determines its tasks, functions, rights and duties [1].

Its approval block reads «УТВЕРЖДЕНО Приказом АНО ВО «Университет Иннополис» от «9» января 2019 г.» and carries the signature of the deputy for development and HR policy A.M. Samatova; the number of the approving order is not legible in the page text [2]. The document is dated «г. Иннополис» [2]. The page is a scan read by OCR and warns that numbers and dates should be checked against the PDF before relying on them [2].

## Place in the structure

- The Institute is a structural subdivision of the Faculty of Humanities and Social Sciences and includes the Laboratory of Humanities, the Laboratory of Social Sciences and the School of Physical Culture and Healthy Lifestyle (Школа физической культуры и ЗОЖ) [3].
- The Institute reports directly to the director of the institute; in the director's absence — to the dean of the faculty [4].
- In its activity the Institute follows the Constitution of the Russian Federation, the Labour Code of the Russian Federation, the Federal Law «Об образовании в Российской Федерации» (No. 273-ФЗ), other legislative and normative legal acts regulating the activity of higher education institutions, the University's Charter, decisions of the Academic, Supervisory and Operations councils of the University, orders and instructions of the University's director, other local normative acts of the University and this Regulation [2].
- The structure, the number of staff and the staffing table of the Institute, and changes to them, are approved by the University's director [5].
- The Institute is headed by a director of the institute, who is hired and dismissed by order of the University's director [6].
- The working conditions of the Institute's employees are determined by the employment contracts concluded with each employee and by the University's internal labour regulations [2]; their rights, duties and degree of responsibility are established by job descriptions [7].
- The Regulation and additions and changes to it are approved by the University's director or by another person authorised by him [8].

## Tasks

- Ensuring a proper level of preparation of students in the disciplines belonging to the Institute's profile [9].
- Carrying on research activity at a competitive level [10].

## Functions

- Developing and delivering educational courses and higher education programmes in the subjects belonging to the Institute's profile [11].
- Conducting research work with the participation of the Institute's teaching staff and researchers [12].
- Organising and holding the University's scientific and sports events [13].
- Taking part in developing and delivering programmes of professional development of the University's teaching staff [14].
- Methodological support of the process of selecting applicants in the subjects belonging to the Institute's profile [15].

## Rights of the Institute's employees

In their activity the Institute's employees have the right to:

- request from the University's structural subdivisions the information and materials needed for the work within the Institute's competence [16];
- engage, with the consent of the heads of the University's structural subdivisions, the employees of those subdivisions to prepare drafts of local acts and other documents and to develop and carry out the measures conducted by the Institute [17];
- take part in the meetings held by the University's management when questions whose solution falls within the Institute's competence are discussed [18];
- put to the University's management proposals to repeal, amend or supplement the Institute's local acts [19];
- interact with bodies of state authority and administration, legal entities and the University's subdivisions on questions within the Institute's competence [20].

## Interaction with other subdivisions

The Institute's interaction with the University's other structural subdivisions is determined by the tasks and functions imposed on it by this Regulation [21].

Clause 5.2 names the following counterparts and questions (the page text is an OCR reading, so some names come out distorted):

- with the study department — on questions of the class schedule, of the learning management system (its name is rendered illegibly, «Геагтпе, Мападетет бузет»), of reporting on students' academic performance and of the quality of course delivery [22];
- with the international department — on questions of the conformity of partner universities' educational programmes and of confirming the level of English of students going on exchange [22];
- with the department for work with the teaching staff — on questions of registering employment relations, visas and work permits, and of settlement and help during the adaptation of newly hired employees [22];
- with the IT department (the page renders the name as «[22];
- with the department of admission and attraction of students — on questions of conducting the entrance testing in English [22].

Interaction with the other structural subdivisions takes place on questions of preparing drafts of local acts, orders, instructions, contracts and other documents and of preparing conclusions and written and oral certificates on questions within the Institute's competence that arise in practical activity [23].

## Reporting

Within its activity the Institute is responsible for preparing and keeping up to date the following information [24]:

- descriptions of the study programmes in the subjects belonging to the Institute's profile;
- course materials in the learning management system (rendered in the page text as «Геагтпе, Мападетет бузет»);
- the results of assessing students' work in the subjects belonging to the Institute's profile;
- the orders connected with the delivery of the curricula of the School of Physical Culture and Healthy Lifestyle (ЗОЖ);
- up-to-date descriptions of the teachers' profiles on the University's website.

# Department for Work with Dissertation Councils and Preparation for Dissertation Defence — creation and the order approving its Regulation

## What the order does

The order of 16 February 2026 № ОД/АД-02/2026/02/16, issued at Innopolis, approves the Regulation on the Department for Work with Dissertation Councils and Preparation for Dissertation Defence (Отдел по работе с диссертационными советами и подготовке к защите диссертаций) of ANO VO «Innopolis University» [1]. The order was issued in order to raise the efficiency of the activity of the University's structural unit [1].

Under point 1 of the order the Regulation annexed to the order is approved [1]. The wiki page contains the text of the order only; the Regulation itself is not reproduced on it [1].

## Familiarisation of the workers

The head of the Department for Work with Dissertation Councils and Preparation for Dissertation Defence, L.S. Shulyndin, must ensure timely familiarisation, against signature, with the Regulation approved by the order — both of the workers of the unit and of workers newly hired and transferred (moved) into the unit from other units of the University [1].

## What the order repeals and who controls it

The Regulation on the Department for Work with Dissertation Councils and Preparation for Dissertation Defence approved by the University's order of 15 November 2024 № ОД/АД-01/2024/11/15 is recognised as no longer in force [1]. Control over the execution of the order and of the Regulation approved by it is retained by the signer, the Director for Development and HR Policy R.F. Valiev [1].

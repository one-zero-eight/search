# Laboratory of Intelligent Robotic Systems (Лаборатория интеллектуальных робототехнических систем) — place, tasks, functions and rights

## Document and place in the structure

The Regulation on the Laboratory of Intelligent Robotic Systems regulates the activity of the Laboratory of Intelligent Robotic Systems of the autonomous non-commercial organisation of higher education «Innopolis University» and determines its tasks, functions, rights and duties [1]. The page gives no number or date of the order approving the Regulation [2].

The Laboratory is a structural subdivision of the University [3]. It reports directly to the head (руководитель) of the Laboratory; in the absence of the head it reports to the director of the Institute of Robotics [2].

In its activity the Laboratory follows the Constitution of the Russian Federation, the Labour Code of the Russian Federation, Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29.12.2012 «Об образовании в Российской Федерации» and other legislative and normative legal acts of the Russian Federation regulating the activity of higher education institutions, the University's Charter, decisions of the Academic and Supervisory councils of the University, orders and instructions of the Director of the University, other local normative acts of the University and this Regulation [2].

The structure, the number of staff and the staffing table of the Laboratory, and changes to them, are approved by the Director of the University [4]. The Laboratory is headed by a head of the Laboratory, appointed and dismissed by an order of the Director of the University in accordance with the Regulation on the procedure for filling positions of scientific-pedagogical workers in a higher education institution of the Russian Federation [5]. The working conditions of the Laboratory's employees are determined by the employment contracts concluded with each employee and by the University's internal labour regulations [2]. The rights, duties and degree of responsibility of the Laboratory's employees are established by job descriptions [6]. The Regulation is approved by the Director of the University, and additions and changes to it are made by an order of the Director of the University [7].

## Tasks

- creating a scientific-methodological database for fundamental and applied research in accordance with the University's research plans [8]
- opening experimental sites for scientific-theoretical and practical research and for testing innovative technologies [9]
- helping to form the research skills of the University's students, aspirants and teachers [10]
- expanding and strengthening scientific ties with institutions and organisations of a similar kind of activity [11]
- promoting scientific knowledge among the public [12]
- carrying out research and development work (НИР, НИОКР), industrial projects and grants in the Laboratory's profile, by its employees on their own initiative and in accordance with grant support, budget and non-budget contracts [13]
- preparing for publication and publishing annotated and abstracted materials on the newest experience and the own materials and developments of the University's scientific-pedagogical workers and students [14]

## Functions

- developing research programmes in the Laboratory's field of activity [15]
- stage-by-stage planning and conduct of research and development work, industrial projects and grants [16]
- improving the methods, technologies, techniques and instruments of research work [17]
- creating a database for the research work of students in the course of their coursework and final qualification works [18]
- developing and publishing scientific-methodological manuals based on the results of the Laboratory's activity [19]
- publishing scientific articles in journals, including those in international citation bases such as Web of Science (WOS) and Scopus [20]
- holding thematic seminars and practical sessions and consultations for the University's teachers, aspirants and students [21]
- preparing practical (experimental) material for inclusion in candidate and doctoral dissertations [22]
- drawing the University's students into research activity [23]
- revealing the scientific potential of young specialists [24]

## Rights

- request from the University's structural subdivisions the information and materials needed for the work within the Laboratory's competence [25]
- with the consent of the heads of the University's structural subdivisions, involve the employees of those subdivisions in preparing draft local acts and other documents and in developing and carrying out measures conducted by the Laboratory [26]
- take part in meetings held by the University's management when discussing questions whose solution falls within the Laboratory's competence [27]
- submit to the University's management proposals to cancel, amend or supplement the University's local acts [28]
- use the University's classroom fund, facilities and equipment of its material-technical base [29]
- use the University's logo in all presentation materials [30]
- carry out publishing activity in the established procedure, observing the rules in force at the University [31]

## Interaction and reporting

The Laboratory interacts with the Department for Attracting Grants on questions of participation in research and development work, industrial projects and grants [32], and with the Department for Support and Quality Control of Projects on questions of reporting under grants, budget and non-budget contracts [33]. Interaction with other structural subdivisions is determined by the tasks and functions assigned to the Laboratory by this Regulation, in accordance with the University's structure, the organisational-administrative and normative documents of the University's management and the University's Charter [34].

Within its activity the Laboratory is responsible for preparing reports on research and development work, industrial projects and grants and for passing them in time to the University's internal structural subdivisions [35]. It submits reporting in accordance with the requirements of the Ministry of Education of the Russian Federation and of other state statistics bodies, as needed for management and financial decisions [36].

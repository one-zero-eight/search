# Time norms for study load of learners on additional professional programmes (ДПО) — order of 23 August 2021

## The document

The page is the order of ANO VO «Innopolis University» of 23 August 2021 № ОД/ДПО-03/2021/08/23, issued in Innopolis, «О нормировании учебной нагрузки обучающихся по дополнительным профессиональным программам, реализуемым АНО ВО "Университет Иннополис"» [1]. It was issued in accordance with Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29 December 2012 «Об образовании в Российской Федерации» and the order of the Ministry of Education and Science of Russia of 01.07.2013 No. 499 approving the Procedure for organising and carrying out educational activity under additional professional programmes [1]. It is signed by the vice-rector — head of the department for project and research activity I.I. Bariev, who retained control over its execution [1]. The wiki copy is a scan read by OCR, so its numbers should be checked against the PDF [1].

The order establishes norms of time for mastering study materials and preparing for classes in the additional professional programmes implemented by the University, in accordance with the annex to the order [1].

## The norms in the annex

The annex is headed «Нормы времени на освоение учебных материалов и подготовку к занятиям по дополнительным профессиональным программам»; the norm is given per unit of study work, and the time norm is calculated per 1 hour of a classroom class [1]:

- participation in an online webinar — 1 hour — 1 [1];
- watching a video lecture, with preparation for it and study of the lecture materials — 7–30 minutes — 1 [1];
- study of presentations and visual materials — 1 sheet (л.) — 0.1–0.25 [1];
- performing a laboratory work — 1 work — 0.5–2 [1];
- work with textbooks (study manuals) — 1 printer's sheet (п.л.) — 2 [1];
- performing a control work (контрольная работа) — 1 task — 0.5–2 [1];
- writing an abstract or essay — 1 abstract — 4–12 [1];
- performing a calculation-graphic work — 1 work — 4–12 [1];
- performing a project, developing professional documents (a business plan, a marketing plan, a technical specification, a standard, a work programme and so on) — 1 project — 6–36 [1];
- preparing visual materials and presentations on a topic — 1 presentation — 2–12 [1];
- preparing a report — 1 report — 2 [1];
- preparing for an examination — 1 examination — 8 [1];
- preparing for a pass (зачёт) — 1 pass — 6 [1].

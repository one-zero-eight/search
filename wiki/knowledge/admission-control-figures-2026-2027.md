# Control figures for admission (КЦП) to higher education programmes for 2026/27 — budget places by area of study and by profile

## The document

The page publishes the "number of places for admission to study under various conditions of admission within the control figures" (контрольные цифры приёма, КЦП) for ANO VO «Innopolis University» [1]. It consists of Appendix No. 1.465 and Appendix No. 2.104 to the order of the Ministry of Science and Higher Education of the Russian Federation of 30 December 2025 No. 1100 — the admission control figures for the 2026/27 academic year [1].

Both appendices give places financed from the budget appropriations of the federal budget: Appendix No. 1.465 for bachelor's, specialist's and master's programmes having state (including temporary state) accreditation, Appendix No. 2.104 for programmes not having state accreditation [1].

## Bachelor's programmes (Appendix No. 1.465)

The places are shown by enlarged groups of areas of training (укрупнённые группы направлений подготовки) and by form of study [1]:

- total: 171 places, of them 171 full-time (очная), 0 part-time (очно-заочная), 0 extramural (заочная) [1]
- 09.00.00 Informatics and computer engineering: 159 places, all full-time, 0 part-time, 0 extramural [1]
- 15.00.00 Mechanical engineering: 12 places, all full-time, 0 part-time, 0 extramural [1]

## Master's programmes having state accreditation (Appendix No. 1.465)

- total: 60 places, of them 60 full-time, 0 part-time, 0 extramural [1]
- 09.00.00 Informatics and computer engineering: 60 places, all full-time, 0 part-time, 0 extramural [1]

## Master's programmes without state accreditation (Appendix No. 2.104)

- total: 7 places, of them 7 full-time, 0 part-time, 0 extramural [1]
- 15.00.00 Mechanical engineering: 7 places, all full-time, 0 part-time, 0 extramural [1]

## Places by profile (the page «Контрольные цифры приема по профилям»)

A separate page publishes the number of places for admission "under various conditions of admission within the control figures by profiles" (по профилям) of bachelor's programmes and, separately, of master's programmes [2]. The page names no academic year and no approving document or order, so which admission campaign its figures belong to cannot be read from it [2].

Bachelor's programmes [2]:

- 09.03.01 Informatics and computer engineering, with the profiles «Анализ данных и искусственный интеллект» and «Математические основы искусственного интеллекта» — 60 places [2]
- «Инженерия информационных систем» — 55 places, given in a row of its own in which the area of training is not repeated [2]
- 15.03.06 Mechatronics and robotics, profile «Робототехника» — 12 places [2]

Master's programmes [2]:

- 09.04.01 Informatics and computer engineering, with the profiles «Искусственный интеллект и инженерия данных», «Инженерия безопасности систем и сетей» and «Программная инженерия» — listed without a number of places in the page's text [2]
- 15.04.06 Mechatronics and robotics, profile «Робототехника и компьютерное зрение» — 7 places [2]

How reliable the reading is: in the extracted table the profile names of a row stand before the area of training and the figure 60 is printed in the 09.03.01 row, so which of the profiles of that row the 60 belongs to cannot be settled from the text layer [2]. The page gives no subtotals and no total for bachelor's or for master's programmes, and states no form of study [2].

## Places within the special, separate and target quotas, by programme

A further page, «Количество мест в рамках особой квоты, отдельной квоты, целевой квоты», gives the number of places for admission within the control figures under the various conditions of admission: for bachelor's programmes within the target quota, the special quota and the separate quota, and for master's programmes within the target quota only [3]. The page names no academic year and no approving document or order, so its admission campaign cannot be read from it [3].

Bachelor's programmes — the table has the columns area of training, educational programme, target quota, special quota, separate quota [3]:

- the programme «Анализ данных и искусственный интеллект»: a dash for the target quota, 4 places in the special quota, 4 places in the separate quota; the row gives no area of training [3]
- 09.03.01 «Информатика и вычислительная техника», programme «Математические основы искусственного интеллекта»: a dash for the target quota, 6 places in the special quota, 6 places in the separate quota [3]
- the programme «Инженерия информационных систем» in a row of its own: a dash for the target quota, 6 places in the special quota, 6 places in the separate quota [3]
- 15.03.06 «Мехатроника и робототехника», programme «Робототехника»: a dash for the target quota, 2 places in the special quota, 2 places in the separate quota [3]

Master's programmes — the table has a single column, the number of places within the target quota [3]:

- 09.04.01 «Информатика и вычислительная техника», programmes «Искусственный интеллект и инженерия данных», «Инженерия безопасности систем и сетей» and «Программная инженерия» — a dash instead of a number for each of them [3]
- 15.04.06 «Мехатроника и робототехника», programme «Робототехника и компьютерное зрение» — a dash instead of a number [3]

How reliable the reading is: in the extracted text the rows of these tables are split and the programme names stand apart from their figures, and the page gives neither subtotals nor a total [3].

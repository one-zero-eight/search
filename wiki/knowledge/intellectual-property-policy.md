# Intellectual property and technology transfer policy (IP-Policy) — scope, aims and bodies

## The document and its scope

The Policy in the field of management of intellectual property and technology transfer of ANO VO «Университет Иннополис» (the IP-Policy) was approved by an order of the University of 9 August 2022 and is dated Innopolis, 2022 [1]. The wiki page is a scan read by OCR, in which the abbreviation «IP» often appears as «1Р» or «!Р», and it warns that numbers and dates should be checked against the PDF before relying on them [1].

The Policy determines the basic principles of distributing rights to results of intellectual activity (РИД) created at the University, of exercising, disposing of and protecting the University's rights to such results, of the effective use and commercialisation of results whose rights belong to the University, and of material incentives for employees and students to create new results [2]. It rests on the Russian legal regulation of intellectual property — the Constitution of the Russian Federation, the generally recognised principles and norms of international law and international treaties, the Civil Code and other laws and legal acts on intellectual rights [3].

The Policy is binding on the University's employees — including teaching staff, researchers, administrative and managerial personnel, persons taking part in research and educational projects, and other persons in labour relations with the University under employment contracts — and on the University's students [4]. It is mandatory for all employees and students [5] and applies to all structural subdivisions without exception, both those existing when it was approved and those created later [6]. Changes and additions are approved by order of the University Director [7], and the Policy is published in the «Локальные нормативные акты» section of the Bitrix corporate portal and on the University's official website [8].

## Key concepts

- Intellectual property (ИС) — results of intellectual activity and means of individualisation equated to them that enjoy legal protection: works of science, literature and art, computer programmes, databases, performances, phonograms, broadcasts, inventions, utility models, industrial designs, breeding achievements, topologies of integrated circuits, production secrets (know-how), firm names, trademarks and service marks, geographical indications, appellations of origin of goods, commercial designations [9].
- Result of intellectual activity (РИД) — a result of a person's creative labour that enjoys legal protection, including works of science, literature and art, computer programmes, databases, inventions, utility models, industrial designs, topologies of integrated circuits, production secrets (know-how), means of individualisation and other results provided for by art. 1225 of the Civil Code [10].
- Technology transfer (ТТ) — the process of transferring technologies and the corresponding rights to them from the transferring to the receiving party for their subsequent introduction and use [11].
- Author — a citizen (including an employee or a student of the University) by whose creative labour the result was created; in the Policy an author is by default an employee of the University unless the Policy directly provides otherwise or it follows from the nature of the relations [12].
- Service result — a result recognised as service under arts. 1295, 1320, 1370, 1430, 1461, 1470 of the Civil Code [13]; initiative result — a result created by the author using the employer's money, technical or other material means, including equipment, premises and telecommunication facilities, but not in connection with performing labour duties or a specific (service) assignment [14].
- Intellectual rights — the exclusive right, which is a property right, personal non-property rights (authorship, name, integrity of the work, publication, withdrawal, integrity of performance) and other rights, including the right to remuneration for a service result and the right to obtain a patent [15].
- A contract on disposal of the exclusive right (assignment or licence) is subject to the general provisions on obligations (arts. 307–419) and on contract (arts. 420–453) of the Civil Code, with the specifics of part four of the Civil Code [16], [17]; an intangible asset (НМА) is a unit of accounting — the aggregate of exclusive rights to one result [18]; НИОКР are research and development works aimed at new knowledge or a new product or technology [19].
- The Bitrix corporate portal is the University's internal system for organising staff work, combining management automation, an information system and document flow [20]. Concepts not defined in the Policy take their meaning from Russian legislation [21].

## Aims, principles and tasks

The strategic aims of the Policy are: accelerating the transformation of the University's intellectual property into products, services and processes; bringing the results of scientific and technical activity to the level of launching a new product on the market; developing communication with the scientific and technological community and strengthening the University's reputation as an industry competence centre; building partnerships with industry for research, development and commercialisation of technologies created in the academic environment; creating an innovation infrastructure that supports commercialisation and entrepreneurship; creating and commercialising breakthrough technologies to bring the Russian information technology industry to a qualitatively new level; and creating favourable conditions for student technological entrepreneurship in information technology [22].

Its basic principles are: timely identification and securing of rights to the University's intellectual property; support of the effective transfer of technologies created through fundamental research and development into the real sectors of the economy; stimulation of employees and students to create results; support of the creation of new legal entities contributing to the economy and innovation ecosystems of Russia; and maximum favouring of the securing of rights for the University to initiative results and to results created by students and third parties where such a need and expediency are identified [23].

The tasks set for the University are: forming and carrying out a single policy in the field of intellectual property management and technology transfer; organising timely identification, registration and accounting of results belonging to the University; forming the University's set of property rights to results by classifying them as intangible assets; creating legal and economic conditions for the effective use of results and technology transfer; and organising and stimulating inventive activity [24].

## The Commission on intellectual property and the МЦТТ

A Commission on intellectual property is created at the University as a permanent collegial body, so that the basic principles of the Policy are observed [25]; its legal status and competence are determined by a local normative act of the University [26]. Its main task is to take objective decisions on questions of implementing the Policy: choosing the model of securing rights to results belonging to the University, timely securing of those rights, choosing a legal protection strategy including the expediency of registering a result abroad, and disposing of exclusive rights in full accordance with the University's interests [27].

The Inter-branch Centre of Technology Transfer (МЦТТ) is a structural subdivision of the University that carries out measures to achieve the aims and tasks of the Policy [28] and was created, together with the Commission, for that purpose [29]. The main tasks of the МЦТТ are [30]:

- to introduce and implement a procedure, mandatory for employees and students, for the legal protection and use of results created in НИОКР performed on the University's assignment and under contracts with industrial partners, state (municipal) contracts, grants and other agreements [31];
- to develop model forms of documents unifying the securing of rights to service results [32], and to give authors initial consultations on patent search and on preparing application materials [33];
- to identify protectable solutions and organise the analysis of the patent purity of a development, including the patent search and the assessment of the expediency of patenting [34];
- to organise legal protection: preparing, drawing up and filing sets of application documents with the patent offices of Russia and of foreign states, maintaining protective documents in force [35], checking that authors have filled in and assembled the materials correctly [36], and keeping document flow on service assignments, notifying authors and management of letters, requests and notices and observing the deadlines for replies and payment of fees [37];
- to consider written notifications of authors, approved by their managers, about protectable results created in НИОКР and take measures to secure the exclusive rights for the University [38];
- to give expert support to the University's contracts and state (municipal) contracts that provide for or envisage the creation of results [39], and to take part in work under contracts as executor or co-executor, in forming and fulfilling the terms on the distribution of the parties' rights to created results, their protection and use (including licence and assignment contracts) and the payment of remuneration to authors [40];
- to inventory rights to results and keep records of documents connected with their creation and registration [41], and to take part in negotiations with employees, students and third parties on the distribution of rights to protectable results, recording that distribution in a contract [42];
- to inform the University's management about unlawful actions of authors or outsiders aimed at filing patent applications or registering results that are the University's intellectual property, and about revealed infringements of the University's exclusive rights [43].

The work of the Commission and of the МЦТТ aims at effective management of the University's intellectual property, support of technology transfer and profit from commercialising the University's developments, including helping structural subdivisions to identify protectable results in time and giving authors methodological support in preparing materials for registration [44]. The legal status, competence and powers of the Commission and the МЦТТ are regulated by separate local acts with which any interested person may acquaint himself on the University's official website [45].

## Measuring effectiveness

The key performance indicators in the field of intellectual property and technology transfer are the number of applications filed for registration of results in Russia and with foreign international, regional and national offices per year, the number of protective documents obtained per year, the volume of income from managing intellectual rights and disposing of exclusive rights per year, and the number of results for which contracts on disposal of exclusive rights were concluded per year [46]. Every year, not later than 15 (fifteenth) December of the current year, the МЦТТ must submit the planned indicators for the next year for approval to the Commission [47], and not later than 30 (thirtieth) January of the year following the reporting year it must submit the indicators achieved for the previous year [48]. On the basis of the indicators the Commission and the МЦТТ jointly develop, where necessary, a plan to improve measures in the field of intellectual property and technology transfer, aimed at raising patent activity and increasing income from commercialisation [49].

## Remaining questions

Questions connected with the management of intellectual property and technology transfer that the Policy does not regulate must be decided on the basis of the need to observe its principles, aims and tasks [50]. If the University's structure changes, including the abolition of a structural subdivision named in the Policy, its functions are performed by the successor subdivision without amending the Policy [51].

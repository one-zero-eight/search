# Staff duties, conduct and communications (Правила внутреннего трудового распорядка)

## General duty

The employee must conscientiously perform the labour duties imposed by the employment contract, job description and other documents regulating the employee's activity, observe the Rules, labour discipline and the established labour norms, observe labour protection and safety requirements, treat the employer's property (including third parties' property for whose safety the employer is responsible) and other employees' property carefully, spend materials, energy, inventory and other material resources economically and rationally and keep the workplace tidy, and immediately inform the employer or the direct head of any situation threatening people's life and health or the safety of property [1].

The employee must also perform the instructions, orders, tasks and directions of the direct head with quality and in time, observe commercial (official) secrecy whose disclosure could harm the employer, other employees and/or the employer's counterparties, take measures to remove the causes and conditions preventing normal work (accidents, idle time) and immediately report the occurrence, observe the employer's procedure for storing documents and material and monetary values, and strive to raise professional level by systematic independent study of specialised literature and periodicals on the position, profession, speciality and work performed [1].

Where the employee starts work directly serving or using monetary, commodity valuables or other property, the employee concludes a contract on full material liability in the cases and procedure established by law [1]. The employee must pass training in safe methods and first aid, labour protection instruction, on-the-job training and knowledge testing, and must pass mandatory preliminary (on hiring) and periodic medical examinations, as well as extraordinary examinations on the employer's direction where the law provides [1]. The employee must help create a favourable business atmosphere in the team [1].

## Illness and personal data

In case of illness the employee must immediately inform the direct head and the Department for Personnel Records and Movement and present the sick list on the first day of return to work, or send a scanned copy of the sick list formed by the medical organisation as an electronic document [1].

When personal data change, the employee informs the Department for Personnel Records and Movement within five working days after receiving the documents confirming the change and presents copies of them: the passport and a document on the change of surname, name or patronymic; the passport with a mark of the new address of residence; the passport with a mark of children and the child's birth certificate; a diploma, certificate or other document on education of any level or advanced training obtained after employment [1]. If the actual place of residence differs from the registered address, the employee reports the actual place of residence [1].

## Use of working time, equipment and premises

The employee must not use the employer's tools, devices, machinery and equipment for personal purposes and must not use working time for questions not connected with the labour relationship with the employer [1]. During working time the employee must not make personal telephone calls, read books, newspapers or other literature unrelated to the work, use the internet or corporate e-mail for personal purposes, or play computer games; where abuse of the technical resources provided for the labour function is found, the employer, at the initiative of the employee's head, may initiate control and analysis of the history of internet site visits and of the dates, time and duration of visits and use of corporate mail [1].

Smoking is prohibited in the office premises and outside the equipped zones; it is prohibited to consume alcohol, narcotic and toxic substances during working time or to come to work in a state of alcohol, drug or toxic intoxication; cooking food within the office is prohibited except for heating food in a specially equipped room; sound notifications of incoming calls on the employee's personal telephone must be switched off during working time where more than two workplaces are located in one room [1]. The employee must observe the norms of the Ethical Code of ANO VO «Innopolis University» [1].

## Name, trademark and contacts with the media

In order to maintain a favourable image of the employer in the information environment, the employee must agree with the employer's Department for External Communications and Branding any use of the official name of the organisation, the trademark and the symbols of the employer — including transfer of them to third parties for further use — and any interaction with representatives of information resources (journalists, editors, bloggers, editorial staff of the media and opinion leaders) on questions directly or indirectly connected with the activity of the employer, the Innopolis special economic zone or the city of Innopolis, in the procedure provided by the employer's local acts [2]. Non-observance of this duty is a disciplinary offence and entails the consequences provided by section 12 of the Rules and by the current labour legislation [2].

## Electronic communication and documents

For performing the labour function and for interaction between the employer and the employee (including a remote worker) the corporate portal, the corporate e-mail and the applications Telegram, Webinar.ru, Zoom, MTS Link, Yandex Telemost and other electronic channels of communication directly agreed with the employer's authorised representatives are used [3]. The employee must be in contact with the direct head and higher management and other management subdivisions during working time, and the time of interaction (talks, correspondence, telephone calls, video conferences) is included in the employee's working time [3].

The rules of interaction are determined by the employment contract, the job description, the regulation on the subdivision and the local acts on electronic document flow and use of electronic documents [3]. The employee must familiarise themselves with documents received through the corporate portal and corporate e-mail at least twice a working day [3]. If electronic HR document flow does not apply to a remote employee, on receiving documents from the Department for Personnel Records and Movement the employee must sign the document and return the original not later than three working days from receipt to the address 420500, Republic of Tatarstan, Verkhneuslonsky district, Innopolis, Universitetskaya st. 1, marking the envelope «Отдел учета и движения персонала АНО ВО «Университет Иннополис» [3].

During working time the employee must daily (not less than twice a day) use the electronic document flow system (corporate portal) to perform labour duties promptly and check and answer e-mails arriving at the corporate e-mail in time; if failure to observe this duty led to non-performance or late performance of duties or instructions, the employee may be held disciplinary liable [1].

Where necessary a corporate e-mail address is created for the employee, and it is communicated by the Department for Support and Development of Infrastructure to the personal e-mail address given on hiring on the first working day [4]. From receiving that message the employee must interact on work matters using the corporate e-mail; if the employee has no corporate e-mail address, the employee's personal e-mail is used for interaction [4].

Remote employees (including those concluding a remote work contract) may be acquainted with the employer's local acts by exchanging electronic documents: from receiving the local act at the corporate or personal e-mail the employee must send an electronic notice of acquaintance (where necessary a scanned copy with the acquaintance visa) in the procedure and to the address stated in the message and within the time stated in the message [5].

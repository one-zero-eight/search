# Entrance examination «Математика» for bachelor programmes (22 July 2024) — published results

## What the page is

The page is the published protocol of the entrance examination «Математика» for bachelor programmes held by Innopolis University on its own on 22.07.2024, under the heading «Вступительное испытание на программам бакалавриата "Математика" 22.07.2024» [1].

Its table is headed «Результаты вступительного испытания проводимого АНО ВО "Университет Иннополис" самостоятельно» and gives, for each participant, the number, the СНИЛС or unique code, the score (оценка) and the same score written out in words (оценка прописью) [1]. Participants are identified by an identifier rather than by name [1].

## The twenty-two results

The protocol records twenty-two results, in the order of the protocol: 78, 62, 64, 60, 82, 74, 62, 79, 79, 79, 71, 61, 62, 71, 68, 64, 63, 60, 64, 62, 65, 80 [1].

- The lowest score is 60, recorded twice — against the codes [скрыто] and [скрыто] [1].
- The highest score is 82, recorded against the code [скрыто] [1].
- The scores 79 and 62 each appear three times [1].

Each score is repeated in words next to the figure, for example «Семьдесят восемь» for 78 and «Шестьдесят» for 60 [1].

## The identifiers

The participants are shown as six-digit codes (for example [скрыто], [скрыто]), once as the eleven-digit code [скрыто], and in the СНИЛС form with dashes and a space, for example [скрыто], [скрыто], [скрыто], [скрыто] and [скрыто] [1]. The page gives no names and does not explain the different forms of the identifier [1].

## What the page does not say

The page states no pass mark, no minimum score and no decision on admission [1]. It does not say on which programme or for which places the examination was taken [1].

## How reliable the reading is

The text layer of the page runs the heading table into the results table and repeats the heading fragments «Матем» / «атика» and «22.07» / «.2024» in separate small tables below the results, so the name and date of the examination have to be read from the merged heading row and the results from the rows of the results table itself [1].

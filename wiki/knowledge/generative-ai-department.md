# Generative AI Department (Отдел генеративного искусственного интеллекта) — creation and the order approving its regulation

## The order and the unit it creates

By order of 15 July 2025 № ОД/АД-01/2025/07/15 the University approved the Положение об Отделе генеративного искусственного интеллекта Исследовательского центра Института искусственного интеллекта [1]. The order states that it is issued in connection with the creation of a new structural unit, in order to optimise the activity of the University [1].

The department is therefore a structural unit of the Research Center of the Institute of Artificial Intelligence of the University, and the regulation approved by the order is attached to it [2]. This page reproduces the order only: the tasks, functions and rights of the department are set out in the regulation itself, whose text is not given here [1].

## Duties under the order

The director of the research center, R.F. Kuleev, had to ensure timely familiarisation, against signature, with the approved regulation of the workers of the unit, as well as of workers newly hired and transferred (moved) into the unit from other units of the University [3]. Control over the execution of the order and of the regulation approved by it is retained by the signatory, R.F. Valiev, director for development and HR policy [4].

# Final state certification for secondary vocational education (College)

## Document and scope

The Положение о порядке проведения государственной итоговой аттестации по образовательным программам среднего профессионального образования was approved by order of the University of 28 May 2025 № ОД/АД-05/2025/05/28, signed by the director I.I. Bariev [1]. It establishes the procedure of organising and conducting the state final certification (ГИА) of students completing state-accredited secondary vocational education programmes (ОП СПО): its forms, the requirements to the use of means of learning and of means of communication, the requirements to the persons involved, the lodging and consideration of appeals, the change and annulment of results, and the specifics for students with health limitations, disabled children and invalids [2]. It was developed in accordance with the education law of 29 December 2012 № 273-ФЗ, the orders of the Ministry of Education of 8 November 2021 № 800, of 24 August 2022 № 762 and of 14 October 2022 № 906, the распоряжение of 1 April 2020 № Р-36, the federal state educational standards of СПО (ФГОС СПО), the University Charter and the Regulation on the College [3].

## Admission and conduct

ГИА is conducted by state examination commissions (ГЭК) created to determine whether the results of mastering an ОП СПО meet the requirements of ФГОС СПО [4]. Its dates are set by order of the University director or an authorised person under the academic process schedule [5], and its volume, structure and content are set by the University for each ОП СПО under ФГОС СПО [6]. Students with no academic debt who have fully completed the curriculum or individual curriculum are admitted [7]. Persons who studied an ОП СПО by self-education or studied a programme without state accreditation may pass ГИА externally (экстерном) on a state-accredited ОП СПО [8], and a graduate who passes all the established forms receives a document of education and qualification of the established form [9].

Students and the persons involved may not have or use means of communication during ГИА, except in the cases of clause 7.12 [10], and no fee is charged from students for passing ГИА [11].

## Forms

ГИА is held in the form of a demonstration exam (ДЭ) and of a defence of a diploma project or diploma work (ДП/ДР) [12]. The ДЭ determines the level of mastery of the programme material and the degree to which professional skills have been formed, through an independent expert assessment of practical tasks performed in real or simulated production processes [13]. It has two levels: the basic level rests on the requirements of ФГОС СПО, and the profile level is held by decision of the University on the students' applications on the basis of requirements that include the qualification requirements declared by employers and partner organisations [14]. Both levels use unified assessment materials — sets of assessment documentation, task variants and evaluation criteria — developed by the Operator, the organisation designated by the Ministry of Education from among its subordinate organisations [15]. The College itself chooses the level of the ДЭ, the competence and the sets of assessment documentation [16]. The ДП/ДР systematises and consolidates the student's knowledge of the specialty and determines readiness for independent professional activity [17].

## The ГИА programme

The programme is signed by the rector after discussion at a meeting of the Academic Council with the chairs of the ГЭК, in absentia or remotely [18]; it is approved by the Academic Council and brought to the graduates' attention no later than 6 (six) months before the start of ГИА by posting it in the e-learning system [19], and its electronic version is placed on the University's website among the documents on the ОП СПО [20]. The programme is part of the ОП СПО [21]; it is drawn up for each programme under ФГОС СПО, including the ДЭ programme, the requirements for the ДП/ДР and the order of their fulfilment, the evaluation criteria and the topics, is developed by the College and recommended for approval by the Academic Council with the chairs of the ГЭК [22]. The list of ДП/ДР topics is formed by the College and approved annually as part of the programme [23]. The programme, the requirements for the ДП/ДР and the evaluation criteria are brought to the students no later than 6 months before the start of ГИА through the e-learning system [24].

## The commissions

A ГЭК is created for each ОП СПО and, where necessary, for each enlarged group of СПО specialties [25]. Its composition — chair, deputy chair and members — is approved by order of the University and is valid for 1 (one) calendar year [26], and the chair organises and controls the commission's activity and ensures a unity of requirements towards graduates [27]. The chair is approved no later than 20 December of the current year for the next calendar year (1 January to 31 December) by the Ministry of Education and Science of the Republic of Tatarstan on the University's representation, from persons who do not work at the University — heads or deputy heads of educational organisations, representatives of employers or their associations, partner organisations or experts [28]. The composition, formed from the College's teaching staff and from persons invited from third-party organisations, is approved by order of the University director no later than 1 (one) month before the start of ГИА on the representation of the College director [29], [30].

For the ДЭ an expert group is created inside the ГЭК from persons invited from third-party organisations who have professional knowledge and experience in the specialty, one group for each specialty or type of activity, with its composition approved no later than 1 month before the ДЭ [31], [32]. It is headed by a chief expert appointed from the experts included in the ГЭК, who does not take part in evaluating the results [33], [34]. The appeal commission is approved by order of the University director simultaneously with the ГЭК, on the representation of the College director [35], and consists of a chair, not fewer than 5 (five) members and a secretary from the University's teaching staff who are not members of the ГЭК in that academic year [36].

## Schedule

The ГИА schedule with the dates, times and places of the tests and of the pre-exam consultations is approved by order of the University director no later than 30 (thirty) calendar days before the first test [37]. It is brought to the graduates by posting on the College's information stand and in the e-learning system, and to the commissions, secretaries and supervisors by corporate email, no later than 30 calendar days before the first test [38]. Admission to ГИА is approved by order of the University director on the representation of the College director no later than 3 (three) working days before the start of ГИА [39].

## Grades, honours diploma, documents

Results are graded «отлично», «хорошо», «удовлетворительно» or «неудовлетворительно» and announced on the same day after the ГЭК protocols are drawn up [40]. The status of winner or prize-winner of the final of the «Профессионалы» championship or of the high technologies championship in the profile of the ОП СПО counts as «отлично» for the ДЭ [41]. A diploma with honours is issued when all grades in the diploma annex (except «зачтено») are «отлично» and «хорошо», all ГИА results are «отлично», and «отлично» makes up not less than 75 % of all grades in the annex, excluding «зачтено» [42]. On positive results the ГЭК decides to award the qualification and to issue the document of education, and the director's order on the student's выпуск is issued [43]. Students are expelled from the day following the last day of ГИА [44], and the documents of education are issued no later than 10 (ten) days after the expulsion order [45].

## Failing and repeat participation

If ГИА is completed early for reasons beyond the student's control, the results are assessed on the work actually done, or on the graduate's application the ГЭК annuls the results and recognises the graduate as not having passed for a valid reason [46]. A student who has not passed one test for a valid reason is admitted to the next test, if there is one [47].

Those who did not pass for a valid reason — temporary disability, performance of public or state duties, a court summons and other cases — including those who did not appear for a valid reason, may pass ГИА without being expelled [48]; additional ГЭК meetings are organised no later than 4 (four) months after the graduate's application, on the form of Appendix 4 [49].

A student is not admitted to the defence of the ДП/ДР if he did not appear for the ДЭ or received «неудовлетворительно» for it; those who did not pass for no valid reason and those with unsatisfactory results may be admitted to repeat participation no more than 2 (two) times [50]. They are expelled and pass ГИА no earlier than 6 (six) months after the first attempt [51], are reinstated for the period set by the calendar academic schedule for ГИА [52], and on repeat participation a different ДП/ДР topic may be set by order of the University at the student's wish [53].

## The demonstration exam

The place, date and start time of the ДЭ, the schedule of the examination groups, the planned duration and the technical breaks are fixed by the ДЭ plan, approved by the ГЭК together with the University no later than 20 (twenty) calendar days before the ДЭ, and the College familiarises the students with it no later than 5 (five) working days before the ДЭ [54]. On the day of the ДЭ at least 1 (one) member of the ГЭК not counting the expert group, the chief expert, the experts, the students and a technical expert are present, and students are admitted by the chief expert on the basis of identity documents [55]. Students may use the centre's equipment and the materials allowed by the assessment documentation and obtain a copy of the task on paper [56]; they may not have or use means of communication and storage media unless the assessment documentation expressly provides it, and may not interact with other students or experts during the tasks [57]. If a student is removed from the centre, the ГЭК annuls the results and recognises the student as not having passed for no valid reason [58]. The experts evaluate the tasks on a 100-point scale, the points being entered in a protocol signed by each expert and approved by the chief expert [59], [60]. By decision of the ГЭК, ДЭ results obtained with the Operator's participation within the interim assessment of a professional module may, on the student's application, be counted for the ГИА grade [61]. The ДЭ and the defence are held at open meetings with not less than two thirds of the ГЭК [62].

## The diploma project or work

The student may choose the topic and may propose his own with a justification, provided it corresponds to one or more professional modules of the programme [63]. Topics and supervisors are assigned by order of the College director no later than 2 (two) weeks before the start of the production (pre-diploma) practice [64], the student applies for them no later than 1 (one) month before that practice [65], and the task is issued no later than 1 (one) week before it [66]. No more than 8 (eight) students may be attached to one supervisor at the same time [67]. The work is subject to obligatory review, the reviewers being appointed no later than 1 (one) month before the defence, and the review is communicated to the student no later than 1 (one) working day before the defence, its opinion counting for the final grade [68], [69]. Defences are held at open meetings with not less than two thirds of the ГЭК, except works on closed topics [70], and usually include a report of not more than 10–15 minutes, the reading of the отзыв and the review, and questions and answers [71].

## Appeals

A student may submit a written appeal about a violation of the procedure and/or disagreement with the results [72]. It is submitted in person by the student or by the parents of a minor student: about a violation — on the day of ГИА, including before leaving the ДЭ centre; about disagreement — no later than the next working day after the results are announced [73]. It is considered no later than 3 (three) working days after receipt [74], and consideration is not a retake [75].

If a violation is confirmed and affected the result, the results are annulled and the student may pass ГИА on additional dates without expulsion, within not more than 4 (four) months after the appeal [76]. On disagreement the commission either keeps the result or sets another one, which is the basis for annulling the earlier results [77]. The decision is taken by simple majority, the chair's vote being decisive when votes are equal [78], is communicated within 3 (three) working days [79] and is final and not subject to review [80].

## Students with health limitations, disabled children and invalids

ГИА is conducted taking account of their psychophysical development, individual capabilities and state of health [81]. The general requirements include the same room as other students where this creates no difficulties, tutors (assistants), the necessary technical means, and unhindered access — ramps, handrails, widened doorways, lifts, a ground-floor room where there is no lift [82]. For blind students tasks and instructions are prepared in Braille, as an electronic document for specialised software or read by an assistant, and written tasks are done in Braille, on a computer or dictated; for visually impaired students lighting of not less than 300 lux, a magnifying device and enlarged print are provided; for deaf and hard-of-hearing students and those with severe speech disorders sound-amplifying equipment is provided and the state exam may be written at their wish; for students with musculoskeletal disorders written tasks are done on a computer or dictated and the state exam may be oral at their wish; other conditions follow the recommendations of the psychological-medical-pedagogical commission and the certificate of disability [83]. The application for special conditions is submitted no later than 3 (three) months before the start of ГИА, with the commission's recommendations and, for disabled children and invalids, the original or a certified copy of the certificate [84].

## Protocols and reporting

The protocols are sewn into books per College within 15 (fifteen) working days after the end of ГИА, certified by a person authorised by the College director and kept in the archive [85], [86]. The chair of the ГЭК prepares a report on the commission's work with recommendations, which the secretary submits to the College in 2 (two) copies within 15 (fifteen) working days after the end of ГИА [87]. Control over the Положение is exercised by the College director [88]; it comes into force from the moment of approval and is valid until changed or repealed [89].

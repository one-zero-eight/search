# Increased state academic scholarship (ПГАС) — competition, deadlines, scoring and appeal

## What the scholarship is for

The increased state academic scholarship (повышенная государственная академическая стипендия, ПГАС) is appointed for special achievements in one or several areas of activity — academic, research, social, cultural-creative and sports [1]. The procedure for appointing it is set out in Appendix 1 to the Regulation on scholarships [2].

## Who may take part

- The scholarship may be received by students of the University studying full-time at the expense of federal budget allocations [3].
- It is appointed to students who have no grades «удовлетворительно» and no academic debt [3].
- The achievements of the students of each course of the bachelor's and master's programmes form a separate rating and must meet one or more of the criteria given for the five areas of activity [3].

## Limits on the number of recipients

- The number of students receiving the ПГАС may not be more than 10% of the total number of students receiving the state academic scholarship [3].
- The number of students receiving the ПГАС for achievements in academic activity under the criterion «Оценки «Отлично» за два и более периода» may not be more than 10% of the total number of students receiving the ПГАС [3].
- A student cannot receive the ПГАС and the increased scholarship of the University at the same time; if a student receives the ПГАС, the application for the increased scholarship of the University is annulled [3].

## Deadlines, scoring and appeal

- To take part in the competition the student must upload the documents confirming his achievements for the year not later than 10 working days after the end of the interim assessment on the results of the period of study [3].
- The department responsible for checking and scoring the achievements submitted by the student is the Department of support and development of students, within 14 working days after the end of that interim assessment; in disputable situations the Department may consult other subdivisions of the University or invite the student for an individual interview [3].
- After the preliminary results of the competition are published on the portal my.innopolis.university the student has 1 day to file an appeal in the way indicated by the Department on that portal [3].
- The Department considers appeals within 2 working days and takes the final decision to agree or refuse the application, notifying the student; the decision must contain reasons, which must be brought to the student [3].
- On the results of the competition the Department forms a rating of the students for each area of activity and a list of students recommended for the ПГАС, which is presented to the Scholarship Commission for consideration [3].
- If in the year preceding the appointment the student retook an exam (a pass) for an unjustified reason, the ПГАС for achievements in academic activity under the criterion «Оценки «Отлично» за два и более периода обучения» is not appointed [3].

## What is scored

The appendix sets out a points table for each area of activity; the main lines are:

- academic activity: all grades «отлично» for the last 2 periods of study — 20 points, for 3 periods — 25, for 4 periods — 30, for 5 periods — 35, for 6 periods — 40, for 7 periods — 45; development and implementation of an IT product for the University's services without payment — 25 points [3]
- academic activity, olympiads and similar events: the points depend on the level (departmental or regional, all-Russian, international) and on the place, with separate lines for informatics events and for ICPC, RuCTF, «Робокубок» and «Евробот» [3]
- research activity: a publication in journals indexed by WOS/SCOPUS starts from a base of 40 points, a publication in Russian journals from 20 points, an award for research work at an international conference from 40 points and at an all-Russian or regional conference from 4 points, an exclusive right to a scientific result from 20 points, a report at a scientific conference from 10 points, a grant for research work from 4 points; the number of points is the base number multiplied by coefficients for the current status of the work (published, accepted for publication, presented), for the category, quartile or ВАК category of the venue and, where there are co-authors taking part in the competition, divided by 2 [3]
- social activity: participation in the organisation of student self-government — 20 points, its chairman — 25, a member of the top-5 of the aktiv — 15, starosta and participation in student clubs — from 0 to 25, head of a student club of the top-3 — 25, of the top-10 — 10, of the top-20 — 5, participation in programmes organised at the University — 5, official ambassador — from 0 to 15, participation in the organisation of events — from 0 to 20; organisers and volunteers of events with more than 25 participants and coaches of teams receive points depending on the level of the event and the time worked [3]
- cultural-creative activity: winner or prize-winner of a creative event — 10 points at regional level, 15 at all-Russian level, 20 at international level; creation of a work of literature or art — 5 points; host of an event — 3 points [3]
- sports activity: winner or prize-winner of a sports event — 10 points at regional level, 15 at all-Russian level, 20 at international level; fulfilment of the standards of the gold badge of the «Готов к труду и обороне» complex — 15 points [3]

## General rules of the competition

- Achievements obtained while studying at the previous level of training are not taken into account in the section «учебная деятельность» [3].
- If an application is submitted under the indicator «Победитель» or «Призер», an application under the indicator «Участник» is not considered [3].
- An achievement is taken into account under only one criterion; the other applications are not considered [3].
- In case of equality of points in one area of activity the Scholarship Commission decides in favour of the student on the basis of academic performance and of the totality of the achievements submitted, the GPA level being taken into account first [3].
- Events organised by the University's students through the portal my.innopolis.university are given the level «Внутривузовский» unless the organisers provide otherwise; the level of an event is set by its organisers, and if it is not indicated the Department may assign it on the basis of information in the media or at its own discretion [3].
- For a team achievement a document confirming the composition of the team must be attached if the confirming document does not list the winners [3].
- The confirming document from the organisers of an event is a letter on the organiser's official letterhead (if there is one) with the name, level and date of the event, the surnames and initials of the organiser and of the executor, and the organiser's signature and seal [3].

# Organisation of the study process and the group leader (староста) — the Internal Rules for students

## The document

Section 5 of the Rules of internal order for students (Правила внутреннего распорядка обучающихся) of the University, approved by its director, is called «Организация учебного процесса» and sets out what the University may deliver, what regulates the study process, how the timetable and the start of the academic year are fixed, the forms and terms of additional programmes, and the role of the group leader (староста) [1]. ## What the University may deliver

The University has the right to deliver higher education programmes — bachelor's programmes, master's programmes and programmes of training of scientific and scientific-pedagogical staff in aspirantura — additional educational programmes, namely additional general education programmes (additional general development programmes and additional pre-professional programmes) and additional professional programmes (professional development and professional retraining programmes), and main general education programmes (primary general, basic general and secondary general education) [1].

## How the study process is regulated

The organisation of the educational process on higher education programmes at the University is regulated by the regime of classes of students, the curricula, the calendar study schedules for the academic year and the timetable of classes [3].

In accordance with the curriculum and the calendar study schedule, before the start of the period of study on all types of higher education programmes, the University approves the timetable of classes for the corresponding period of study [4]. When drawing up the timetable the University excludes irrational expenditure of students' time so that the continuous sequence of their classes is not broken and long breaks between classes do not arise [4].

The date of the start of the academic year at the University for students of higher education programmes is approved every year by an order establishing the dates of the start of the academic year [5].

## Forms and terms of additional programmes

The forms of study and the terms of mastering additional professional programmes are determined by the educational programme and (or) the contract on education; the term of mastering an additional professional programme must make it possible to achieve the planned results and to obtain the new competence (qualification) declared in the programme [6].

The forms of study and the terms of mastering additional general education programmes are determined by the educational programme and (or) the contract on education; the term of mastering an additional general education programme must make it possible to achieve the planned results and the aims declared in the programme [7].

The content of additional general development programmes and the terms of study under them are determined by the educational programme [8]. The content of additional pre-professional programmes is determined by the educational programme in accordance with the federal state requirements [8]. Additional general education programmes may be delivered throughout the whole calendar year, including vacation time [8].

## The group leader (староста)

In each study group a group leader (староста) may be elected from among the students — an example, a role model for all students of the University, who represents the study group on questions connected with study and extracurricular activity [9].

The group leader ensures internal and external communication within the study group and is the link between the teaching staff and the group, and between the University's administration and the group [9].

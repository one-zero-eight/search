# State Funding Department (Отдел привлечения государственного финансирования) — place, tasks, functions and rights

## The document

The Regulation on the State Funding Department (Отдел привлечения государственного финансирования) of ANO VO «Innopolis University» was approved by order of 12 September 2023 No. ОД/АД-05/2023/09/12, signed by R.F. Valiev, Director for Development and HR Policy [1]. The same order instructs the head of the Department, S.A. Veselova, to ensure that the workers of the subdivision, and workers newly hired or transferred into it from other subdivisions, are acquainted with the Regulation under signature [1], and repeals the Regulation on the State Funding Department approved by order of 10 January 2022 No. 0ОД/АД-04/2022/01/10 [1].

The page is a scan read by OCR, and its front matter warns that numbers and dates should be checked against the PDF before relying on them [1].

## Place in the structure

- The Department is a structural subdivision of the Centre for Digital Economy Project Development (Центр развития проектов цифровой экономики) and belongs to the category «иные структурные подразделения» [2].
- It reports directly to the Director for Digital Economy Project Development [3].
- It is guided by the Constitution of the Russian Federation, the Labour Code, Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29.12.2012, other legislative and normative acts on higher education, the University's Charter, decisions of the Academic, Supervisory and Operations Councils, orders and instructions of the director, other local acts and the Regulation [4].
- The structure, number and staff list of the Department, and changes to them, are approved by the University director [1].
- The Department is headed by its head, hired and released from the position by order of the University director [5].
- Working conditions of the Department's workers are set by the employment contracts concluded with each of them and by the internal labour regulations [1]; the rights, duties and degree of responsibility of the workers are set by job descriptions [6].
- Within their competence the staff may be involved in work on grants, projects, subsidies and other programmes of the University [7]. The Regulation and its amendments are approved by order of the director or another person authorised by him [8].

## Tasks

- Searching for and analysing grants, submitting and formalising applications, and interacting with other universities and scholars in this field [9].
- Attracting organisations for joint work on research and development (НИР, НИОКР) and consulting in the field of information technologies and robotics [10].

## Functions

- Organising information and analytical search of competitions for grants in research and innovation [11], and the primary examination and selection of open programmes and competitions in order to decide on participation [12].
- Organising and controlling the process of submitting applications by coordinating the actions of all participants [13], negotiating with all participants [14], and collecting and processing materials on the subjects of competition projects [15].
- Preliminary planning of work on projects [16], consulting on preparing the substantive part of grant applications [17], preparing presentations of projects [18], formatting applications [19] and preparing printed copies of applications [20].
- Monitoring the results of competitions and informing participants about them [21]; organising and controlling the signing of agreements with the grantor, the industrial partner and co-executors [22] and negotiating with the parties to grant agreements [23].
- Developing proposals on the University's participation in grants and on improving the organisation of research activity [24]; providing consulting and methodological help to the University's staff in formatting grant applications [25]; long-term and current planning of staff participation in grants [26].
- Organising interaction of the University with scientific funds and other grant-making organisations [27], and information work in the University to draw structural subdivisions, staff, teachers and students into grant programmes and competitions [28].
- Generalising the experience of subdivisions and staff in preparing and promoting research projects to grant-making organisations [29] and compiling selective and final reports on staff and subdivisions' participation in scientific competitions and programmes of Russian and foreign funds, organisations and agencies [30].
- Keeping the necessary records [31]; carrying out the instructions of the Director for Digital Economy Project Development, the First Vice-Rector — Deputy Director and the Director of the University [32]; developing methodological recommendations and other documents to order activity in the field of grants [33].

## Rights

- Requesting from the University's structural subdivisions the information and materials needed for the work within the Department's competence [34].
- Involving, with the consent of the heads of subdivisions, the workers of those subdivisions in preparing draft local acts and other documents and in developing and carrying out the Department's measures [35].
- Taking part in meetings held by the University's management when matters within the Department's competence are discussed [36].
- Proposing to the University's management the cancellation of, or amendments and additions to, local acts [37].
- Interacting with state authorities and management bodies, legal entities and the University's subdivisions on matters within its competence [38].

## Interaction and reporting

- Interaction with other subdivisions is determined by the tasks and functions entrusted to the Department [39].
- With heads of subdivisions on the participation of staff in grants [40]; with the head of the department of accounting and movement of personnel on hiring staff for the Department [41]; with the financial-economic and analytical departments and the legal office on preparing agreements on won grants [42]; with other subdivisions on preparing grant applications [43].
- A structural subdivision may be the base for the practical training of the University's students [44].
- On the basis of the relevant local acts, orders and instructions the Department may be responsible, within its activity, for preparing information, references and reports and keeping them up to date [45].

# Prevention of diseases, health improvement of students and conditions for physical culture and sports (2019 Regulation and 2019–2024 plan)

## The document and its basis

The Regulation «О профилактике заболеваний и оздоровлении обучающихся, о создании условий для занятия ими физической культурой и спортом» in ANO VO «Innopolis University» was approved by order № АД-01/2019/07/03 of 3 July 2019 signed by the director V. Semenikhin [1]. The same order approved the plan of measures for the prevention of diseases and the health improvement of students for 2019–2024 (Appendix 1) and put control over its execution on the rector A.G. Tormasov [1]. The order sets out its purpose: regulating the relations arising in the sphere of health protection and organising a system of conditions that preserve and strengthen the physical and psychological health of the University's students, in accordance with the requirements of Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29 December 2012 «Об образовании в Российской Федерации» [1]. The Regulation is developed on the basis of art. 41 of Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29 December 2012, Federal Law No. 329-ФЗ of 4 December 2007 «О физической культуре и спорте в Российской Федерации», the Strategy for the development of physical culture and sport in the Russian Federation for the period up to 2020 approved by order of the Government of the Russian Federation No. 1760-р of 18 December 2006, the sanitary-epidemiological requirements to the arrangement, maintenance and regime of work of educational organisations (СанПиН 2.4.4.3172-14 of 4 July 2014 No. 41), the University's Charter and the University's local normative acts [2].

## What the University undertakes

The University creates conditions guaranteeing the protection and strengthening of students' health: it takes into account the social, economic and ecological conditions of the surrounding reality; it takes into account the risk factors present in the educational institution that lead to a deterioration of students' health; it takes into account the factor of a negative population shift in students' health; and it relies on the system of knowledge, attitudes and habits formed in students in the course of study and on rules of behaviour [3].

## Aim

The aim of the Regulation is to preserve and strengthen the physical and psychological health of students by forming in the University's collective an attitude towards a healthy way of life, by creating conditions for strengthening and preserving health in the process of study and work, and by forming health-saving competence and a conscious, active attitude to physical culture and sport in the University's future graduates [4].

Because the main factors determining the state of health are connected with the way of life and the environment, the Regulation names three means of reducing the prevalence of negative risk factors: forming and maintaining people's aspiration for positive changes in their way of life by giving them reliable medical and hygienic knowledge; creating the corresponding motivation and attitude to health; and developing skills and habits of a healthy way of life and of preventing diseases [1].

## Tasks

- providing conditions of study and work that meet sanitary-hygienic requirements and an optimal distribution of the study and work load [5]
- creating a system of comprehensive monitoring of the level of mental and somatic health and of the social adaptation of students, with an analysis of the factors of negative influence [5]
- creating conditions for the adaptation of students to the many times increased psycho-emotional loads [5]
- introducing a system of measures of a psycho-prophylactic and social-adapting character connected with better organisation of catering, healthy leisure and rest, treatment and prophylactic measures, and psychological help and support for students [5]
- introducing a complex of educational and awareness programmes aimed at students' acquiring knowledge, skills and habits of preserving and strengthening health and at forming a culture of health [5]
- improving the procedure for providing medical and consultative help to students [5]

## Principles

- the priority of health-improving and prophylactic measures [6]
- timely reaction to trends in the state of health of the University's students [6]
- accessibility of health-improving measures for the University's students [6]
- continuity of health-improving and prophylactic measures throughout the period of study at the University [6]
- physical education at the University is not only an academic discipline but also the most important basic component of forming a culture of health among students [6]

## Conditions of implementation

- creating organisational-pedagogical, material-technical, sanitary-hygienic and other conditions for saving health that take account of the individual indicators of the state of the participants of the educational process [7]
- developing the content and material base of health improvement of the participants of the educational process by means of physical culture [7]
- creating material-technical, content and information support for agitation and propaganda work aimed at introducing the participants of the educational process to a healthy way of life [7]
- developing and introducing a complex of measures to maintain the health of students at the University [7]
- ensuring the continuous advanced training of teachers of physical culture in developing their readiness to create organisational-pedagogical conditions for saving the health of the participants of the educational process [7]
- preventing deviant behaviour, alcoholism and drug addiction among students [7]
- creating a single space for forming and educating a harmoniously developed personality and physical and mental comfort at the University [7]

## Forms of implementation

- sports work built on a differentiated approach, with an initiative group of student-athletes singled out as bearers of the rules and norms of a healthy way of life [8]
- holding competitions at the University in team sports [8]
- maintaining a permanently operating information and motivational system aimed at the prevention of diseases [8]
- popularising independent classes of students [8]
- forming among students the values of a healthy way of life and of physical-culture and sports classes as the key instrument of maintaining health [8]

## Who delivers it and which clubs exist

Qualified specialists ensure health-improving work with students — teachers of physical culture and teachers in the sphere of psychology and pedagogy [9]. Besides the inclusion of the direction of upbringing of physical culture and of readiness for an independent choice of a healthy way of life in the main general education programme, classes are held in sports clubs and communities [9]:

- the University football club «Иннополис» [9]
- «Боевая Дичь» — communication united under common values and exercises in the physical, spiritual and cultural dimensions, including folk games and combat training [9]
- the volleyball club of Innopolis University [9]
- the cheerleading club of Innopolis University, which offers the basics of cheerleading combined with acrobatic training [9]
- the Innopolis Kendo Club [9]
- the Innopolis University Badminton Club [9]
- SAMBO / JUDO [9]
- the climbing association of Innopolis University [9]
- the chess club of Innopolis University [9]
- the basketball club of Innopolis University [9]
- the «Иннополис» fan club, a club of supporters of the University's sports teams [9]
- the Ping Pong Club [9]

When the study process is organised the sanitary norms for its organisation are observed: the volume of the load in hours, the time for independent work, the time for rest, and the satisfaction of the need for motor activity [9].

## Directions of work

- information and educational support: seminars and trainings on preserving and strengthening health, forming a culture of health, raising the psychological-pedagogical mastery of teachers and curators of academic groups, and using modern technologies in promoting a healthy way of life and popularising sports [10]
- security and optimisation of study and work: conditions of the study process and work that are safe for the health and life of students, with study and work places brought into line with sanitary-hygienic norms and the class schedule drawn up with an optimal combination of study and rest [11]
- organisation of leisure and organised rest: monitoring students' rest and holding cultural, sports and physical-culture health-improving events, forming a conscious need for physical exercise and involving students in sports sections and mass sports events [12]
- treatment and prophylactic observation: a system of research and assessment of the state of health of students, development of measures for the prevention of diseases, correction of unfavourable states and improvement of prophylactic work [13]
- psychological support of students: social-psychological help to students and a cycle of adaptation classes for first-year students [14]

## The plan of measures for 2019–2024

- medical examination of students — every year in September–October, by the Department of support and development of students together with the City clinical hospital No. 7 of Kazan [1]
- monitoring of psychological comfort — every year in November–December, by the Department of support and development of students [1]
- the questionnaire «Самооценка здоровья» — every year in September, by the Department of support and development of students [1]
- the championship of the city of Innopolis in football — every year in February, by the Department of support and development of students and the University football club «Иннополис» [1]
- open competitions in lapta among the residents of Innopolis — every year in May, by the Department of support and development of students and the community «Боевая Дичь» [1]
- open football classes for the children of Innopolis — every year in January–February, by the Department of support and development of students and the University football club «Иннополис» [1]
- open dance classes for the students of Innopolis University — every year in March–April, by the Department of support and development of students and the dance community «Social Dance» [1]
- organisation of the work of the University's medical point for primary medical-sanitary help in the procedure established by the legislation on health protection, for conditions of prevention of diseases and health improvement and for sanitary-antiepidemic and prophylactic measures — regularly, by the Development Department (Управление развития) [1]

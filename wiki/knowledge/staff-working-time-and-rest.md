# Staff working time and rest time (Правила внутреннего трудового распорядка)

## Working time: administrative staff

For administrative staff the working time is 40 hours a week, and the week has two days off, Saturday and Sunday [1]. From Monday to Thursday work lasts 8 hours 15 minutes, from 9.00 to 18.00; on Friday it lasts 7 hours, from 9.00 to 16.45 [1].

The rest and meal break is 45 minutes, from 13.00 to 13.45; it is not included in working time and is not paid [1]. Its start and end may be set individually with each employee and fixed in the employment contract, and no break is given where the daily work (shift) set for the employee does not exceed four hours [1]. Where necessary, the end of the last working day of the month is adjusted to bring the monthly norm of working time to the norm of the production calendar [1].

## Working time: teaching staff

For teaching staff a reduced working time of 36 hours a week is established, with one day off (Sunday) [2]. The start and end of work and the time of the rest and meal break of teaching staff follow the timetable of classes, and the break lasts from 30 minutes to 1 hour, is not included in working time and is not paid [2].

The regime of working time and rest time of teaching staff is set taking into account the length of working time or the norms of teaching hours per wage rate established by the legislation of the Russian Federation, and the time needed for the other duties provided by the qualification characteristics — educational work, individual work with students, scientific, creative and research work, and methodological, preparatory, organisational, diagnostic and monitoring work and work under the plans of educational, sports, creative and other events held with students [3]. Teaching staff perform the pedagogical, scientific, research, organisational and methodological work provided by the employment contract and the individual plan of educational-methodological work within the 36-hour working week [3].

- The class timetable is drawn up and approved by the employer in agreement with the elected trade union body (if any), with account of pedagogical expediency, sanitary and hygienic norms and maximum economy of the teacher's time [4].
- The teaching load is set from the number of hours in the curriculum and programmes, the availability of staff and other conditions of work; a load larger or smaller than the norm of hours per wage rate is set only with the employee's written consent [5].
- The volume of the teaching load set at the beginning of the academic year may not be reduced during the academic year on the employer's initiative, except in cases of reduction of the number of hours in the curricula and programmes [6]; the load may be different during the academic year depending on the hours in the curriculum [7].
- When the load is set for a new academic year, teachers and other teaching staff for whom work at the University is the main place of work as a rule keep its volume and the continuity of teaching subjects in the groups [8].
- Teaching staff are given one day a week free from classes for methodological work and advanced training, if this is possible given their teaching load and the hours in the curriculum for the discipline they teach [9].

## Other regimes

Other regimes of work may be set for some employees out of official necessity [10]:

- a working week with days off on a sliding 2/2 schedule [10]
- two-shift work, with the alternation of shifts and the distribution of working time and rest by days of the week and dates set by a shift schedule brought to the employee's knowledge not later than one month before it takes effect [10]
- a 40-hour working week with two days off, in which four days a week last 8 hours 15 minutes from 9.00 to 18.00 and one day lasts 7 hours from 9.00 to 16.45, with a 45-minute break from 13.00 to 13.45, the days off being given on a sliding schedule approved by the employer and brought to the employee's knowledge not later than one month before it takes effect [10]

If a different regime of working time and rest time is set on hiring or during the employment relationship, those conditions are included in the employment contract as mandatory [11].

## Reduced working time

On hiring a reduced working time is established: not more than 24 hours a week for employees under 16 (not more than 12 hours a week if they study in educational organisations); not more than 35 hours a week for employees from 16 to 18 (not more than 17.5 hours a week if they study); not more than 35 hours a week for employees who are disabled persons of group I or II; not more than 36 hours a week where the workplaces are classified by the special assessment of working conditions as harmful of degree 3 or 4 or as dangerous [12].

The maximum duration of daily work is set: five hours for employees from 15 to 16; seven hours from 16 to 18; two and a half hours for students from 14 to 16 combining study with work and four hours from 16 to 18; in accordance with the medical conclusion for disabled employees [13].

## Part-time work and combined work

Part-time work (part-time day, part-time week) may be established on hiring or during the employment relationship by agreement of the employer and the employee, and the part-time day may be divided into parts taking account of the employee's wishes and official needs [14].

The employer must establish part-time work on the request of: pregnant women; one of the parents (guardian, trustee) having a child under 14 (a disabled child under 18); a person caring for a sick family member in accordance with a medical conclusion issued in the established procedure; and a woman on parental leave until the child reaches three, the child's father, grandmother, grandfather, other relative or guardian who actually cares for the child and wishes to work part-time while keeping the right to the benefit [15].

For employees working in combined employment (совместительство) the working day must not exceed four hours [16]. If the employee is free from duties at the main place of work, they may work full time in combined employment, and the working time within one month (other accounting period) must not exceed half of the monthly norm established for the corresponding category [17].

For external combined employment (except in the employer's subsidiary companies), in order to exclude a conflict of interests and comply with anti-corruption legislation, the employee notifies the employer by sending a notice (Annex 1) to HRsupport@innopolis.ru not later than three working days before the start of the external combined employment [17]. These limits of working time do not apply where the employee suspended work at the main place because of delayed payment of wages or was suspended from work in accordance with a medical conclusion [18].

## Overtime, irregular hours and time accounting

For all categories of employees the working day directly preceding a non-working holiday is reduced by one hour [19]. Where there is a production need, on the basis of a memo of the head of the department (subdivision) agreed with the employee, summarised accounting of working time may be applied so that the working time for the accounting period does not exceed the normal number of working hours; the accounting period is half a year, and pay follows the current legislation [20].

The employer may involve an employee in work beyond the working time set for them in cases of necessary overtime work and where the employee works on an irregular working day [21].

Overtime work is work performed on the employer's initiative beyond the working time set for the employee — the daily work (shift) or, under summarised accounting, beyond the normal number of working hours for the accounting period [22]. The employer must obtain the employee's written consent to overtime [22].

Without consent the employer may involve the employee in overtime: in work needed to prevent a catastrophe, industrial accident or to eliminate their consequences or the consequences of a natural disaster; in socially necessary work to eliminate unforeseen circumstances disrupting the normal functioning of water, gas, heating, lighting, sewerage, transport and communication; and in work caused by the introduction of a state of emergency or martial law and urgent work in emergency circumstances (fires, floods, famine, earthquakes, epidemics or epizootics) and other cases threatening the life or normal living conditions of the population or part of it [22].

The irregular working day is a special regime under which individual employees may, by the employer's instruction, be involved from time to time in performing their duties beyond the working time set for them; the list of positions is Annex 2 to the Rules, but the condition applies to an employee only if it is included in the employment contract [23]. An employee working part-time may have an irregular working day only where the agreement of the parties establishes a part-time week with a full working day [24].

The employer records the time actually worked by each employee in the timesheet [25].

## Rest time: breaks, days off and holidays

Rest time is the time during which the employee is free from performing labour duties and may use it at their own discretion [26]. Its types are breaks during the working day (shift), daily (between-shift) rest, days off, non-working holidays and leaves [27].

Employees other than teaching staff are given a rest and meal break of 45 minutes from 13.00 to 13.45, two days off (Saturday and Sunday), the non-working holidays listed below, and annual leaves with retention of the place of work and average earnings [28]:

- 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 and 8 January — New Year holidays [28]
- 7 January — Christmas [28]
- 23 February — Defender of the Fatherland Day [28]
- 8 March — International Women's Day [28]
- 1 May — Spring and Labour Day [28]
- 9 May — Victory Day [28]
- 12 June — Russia Day [28]
- 30 August — Republic of Tatarstan Day [28]
- 4 November — National Unity Day [28]
- 6 November — Constitution Day of the Republic of Tatarstan [28]
- Uraza-bairam and Kurban-bairam, the days being determined by decree of the President of the Republic of Tatarstan [28]

Other days off and another time for the rest and meal break may be set for an employee by the employment contract [29]. The days off of a teacher are determined taking account of the class timetable, and non-working holidays follow the list of point 8.3 [30].

## Annual leave

Employees are given an annual basic paid leave of 28 calendar days; teaching staff are given an extended leave of 56 calendar days [31]. The leave must be used not later than 12 months after the end of the working year for which it is given [31]. Non-working holidays falling in the leave period are not counted in its calendar days, nor is a period of temporary incapacity confirmed by a sick list [32].

- Recall from leave is allowed only with the employee's consent and by order of the management; the unused part must be given at a time convenient to the employee during the current working year or added to the leave for the next working year [33].
- By agreement the annual paid leave may be divided into parts, at least one of which must be not less than 14 calendar days [34].
- The right to leave for the first year of work arises after six months of continuous work, though by agreement leave may be given earlier [35].
- Before six months the employer must grant leave on application to women before or immediately after maternity leave, employees under 18, employees who adopted a child (children) under three months, and combined workers at the same time as the annual paid leave at the main place of work, and in other cases provided by federal laws [36].
- Leave for the second and later years may be given at any time of the working year according to the order of leaves set by the leave schedule, approved by the employer with account of the opinion of the elected body of the primary trade union organisation (if any) not later than two weeks before the calendar year begins [37].
- Not later than 1 December of each year the employee must tell the direct head or the Department for Personnel Records and Movement about wishes for leave in the next calendar year, indicating the month and length of each part, for the drawing up of the leave schedule [38].
- Certain categories of employees are given annual paid leave at a time convenient to them in the cases provided by the Labour Code and other federal laws [39].

The employee is notified of the leave start through a task in the Bitrix corporate portal not later than 21 calendar days before it begins, and a reminder is sent to the corporate e-mail not later than 14 calendar days before it begins; not reading the notification in Bitrix or in the e-mail is no ground for cancelling leave according to the schedule [40]. If the employee wishes to use the leave at another time, they must warn the employer in writing not later than two weeks before the proposed leave, and the dates are changed by agreement of the parties [41].

On dismissal the employee receives money compensation for unused leave or, on written application, the unused leave may be granted with subsequent dismissal [42].

## Other leaves and release from work

For family circumstances and other valid reasons an unpaid leave may be granted on the employee's written application, its length being determined by agreement of the employee and the employer [43]; the employer must grant unpaid leave on written application to the persons named in article 128 of the Labour Code [44].

Teaching staff are given a long leave of up to one year every ten years of continuous pedagogical work, in the procedure determined by the employer [45]. Employees working on an irregular working day are given an annual additional paid leave of three calendar days [46].

For medical examination (dispanserisation) employees are released from work for one working day once in three years with retention of the place of work (position) and average earnings [47]. Employees who have not reached the age giving the right to an old-age pension (including early) within five years of that age, and employees receiving an old-age or long-service pension, are released for two working days once a year with retention of the place of work and average earnings [48]. The employee is released on the basis of a written application agreed by the employer, sent not later than three working days before the examination, and within three working days after it provides a certificate of the examination to the Department for Personnel Records and Movement [49].

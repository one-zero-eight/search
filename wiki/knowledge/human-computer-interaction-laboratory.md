# Laboratory of Human-Computer Interaction — place, tasks, functions and rights

## Document and place in the structure

The Regulation on the Laboratory of Human-Computer Interaction of ANO VO «Университет Иннополис» regulates the Laboratory's activity and determines its tasks, functions, rights and duties [1]. It was approved by an order of the University of 15 November 2022, whose number is given on the page in a garbled form as «№ О, -04/2022/11/15», and is signed by the director for development and HR policy; the place is «г. Иннополис» [2]. The page is a scan read by OCR and warns that numbers and dates should be checked against the PDF before relying on them [2].

The Laboratory of Human-Computer Interaction is a structural subdivision of the Institute of Humanities and Social Sciences of the University and belongs to the category «лаборатории» [3]. It reports directly to the director of the Institute of Humanities and Social Sciences and, in his absence, to the head of the department of academic policy and organisation of educational activity [4].

In its activity the Laboratory follows the Constitution of the Russian Federation, the Labour Code of the Russian Federation, Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29.12.2012 «Об образовании в Российской Федерации» and other legislative and normative legal acts regulating the activity of higher education institutions, the University's Charter, decisions of the Academic, Supervisory and Operational councils of the University, orders and instructions of the Director of the University, other local normative acts of the University and this Regulation [5].

The structure, the number of staff and the staffing table of the Laboratory, and changes to them, are approved by the Director of the University [2]. The Laboratory is headed by a head of the Laboratory, who is hired and dismissed by an order of the Director of the University [6]. The working conditions of its employees are determined by the employment contracts concluded with each employee and by the University's internal labour regulations [7], and their rights, duties and degree of responsibility are established by job descriptions [8]. The Laboratory's employees may be involved, within their competence, in work under grants, projects, subsidies and other programmes carried out by the University [9]. The Regulation and additions and changes to it are approved by the Director of the University or by a person authorised by him [10].

## Tasks

The Laboratory's activity is aimed at making a scientific contribution to the development of the scientific field of human-computer interaction and at developing the research potential of students at the level of bachelor's, master's and aspirantura programmes in the fields of study approved by the University and meeting the needs of the state and society, and its main task is the management of the research and administrative activity of the Laboratory's staff [2].

## Functions

In the field of teaching-methodological activity the Laboratory supports the research activity of students within its expert examination [11].

In the field of research activity it [12]:

- plans and carries out research activity in the field of human-computer interaction [12];
- organises the Laboratory's scientific cooperation with Russian and foreign partners [12];
- organises the participation of the Laboratory's employees in competitions for external financing and in patenting the results of research activity [12];
- helps the Laboratory's research and teaching staff (НПР) take part in scientific conferences, forums, symposiums and congresses [12].

Its administrative functions are [13]:

- planning and forming the Laboratory's staff in accordance with the University's needs and policies, and taking part in the procedures of competitive replacement of vacant positions of research and teaching staff [13];
- conducting measures for the regular attestation of research and teaching staff [13];
- helping to ensure the successful passing of the procedures of state accreditation and licensing [13];
- taking part in preparing and conducting the admission campaign [13];
- taking part in the work of initiative inter-faculty working groups [13].

## Rights

In carrying out its activity the Laboratory's employees may exercise the following rights [2]:

- request from the University's structural subdivisions the information and materials needed for the work within the Laboratory's competence [14];
- with the consent of the heads of the University's structural subdivisions, involve the employees of those subdivisions in preparing draft local acts and other documents and in developing and carrying out measures conducted by the Laboratory [15];
- take part in meetings held by the University's management when questions within the Laboratory's competence are discussed [16];
- submit to the University's management proposals to cancel, amend or supplement the University's local acts [17];
- interact with bodies of state authority and administration, legal entities and the University's subdivisions on questions within the Laboratory's competence [18].

## Interaction and reporting

The Laboratory's interaction with the University's other structural subdivisions is determined by the tasks and functions assigned to it by the Regulation [19], and the subdivision may serve as a base for the practical training of the University's students [20]. Interaction is organised as follows [2]:

- with the financial and economic department — on the application of legislation and local acts regulating remuneration at the University and on preparing draft local acts on the University's financial activity [21];
- with the legal department — on concluding contracts and agreements with external suppliers and partners, on settling intellectual property rights and on preparing grant documentation [22];
- with the Development Department of the University — on staffing the Laboratory and on regulating labour relations [23];
- with the Centre for the Organisation of Educational Programmes — on preparing and implementing educational programmes and on state accreditation and licensing [24];
- with the Centre for Preparation, Admission and Development of Students — on preparing and implementing the admission campaign and on organising events [25].

On the basis of the relevant local acts, orders and instructions, the Laboratory may be responsible within its activity for preparing and keeping up to date information, certificates and reports [26].

# Department for the Organisation of Academic Activities (Отдел организации учебной деятельности) — place, tasks, functions and rights

## The document and the Department's place in the structure

The page is the Regulation on the Department for the Organisation of Academic Activities (Положение об отделе организации учебной деятельности) of ANO VO «Innopolis University», approved by order of 31 October 2024 № ОД/АД-03/2024/10/31, issued in Innopolis and signed by the Director for Development and HR Policy R.F. Valiev [1]. The wiki copy is a scan read by OCR, so its numbers and dates should be checked against the PDF [1].

- The Regulation regulates the activity of the Department and determines its tasks, functions, rights and the duties of its employees [2].
- The Department is a structural subdivision of the centre of organisation of educational programmes of the department (управление) of academic policy and higher education and belongs to the category of educational-methodological subdivisions (учебно-методические подразделения) [3].
- It reports directly to the head of the centre of organisation of educational programmes [4].
- In its activity the Department follows the Constitution of the Russian Federation, the Labour Code of the Russian Federation, Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29 December 2012 «Об образовании в Российской Федерации» and other legislative and normative legal acts on higher education institutions, the University's Charter, decisions of the Academic, Supervisory and Operational councils, orders and instructions of the University's director, other local normative acts and this Regulation [5].
- The structure, the number of staff and the staffing table of the Department, and changes to them, are approved by the director of the University or another person authorised by him [6]; the Department is headed by its head, hired and dismissed by order of the director [7].
- The working conditions of the Department's employees are determined by their employment contracts and by the University's internal labour regulations [8]; their rights, duties and degree of responsibility are established by job descriptions [9].
- Within their competences the Department's employees may be involved in work under grants, projects, subsidies and other programmes implemented by the University [10]; the Regulation and additions and changes to it are approved by order of the director or another person authorised by him [11].

The order that approved the Regulation instructed the head of the Department E.A. Kazakova to ensure timely acquaintance, against signature, with the Regulation of the Department's employees and of employees newly hired or transferred (moved) into the Department from other subdivisions, and the signatory retained control over the execution of the order and of the Regulation [1].

## Tasks

- Organising and supporting the educational process in the main professional educational programmes of higher education — bachelor's, master's and programmes for the training of scientific and scientific-pedagogical personnel in aspirantura [12].
- Forming, recording and issuing documents on education and on qualification for students of those programmes, and the annexes to them [13].

## Functions

- Drawing up the class schedule (график учебных занятий) for the higher education programmes [14].
- Forming study groups for seminar-type classes in bachelor's and master's programmes together with the faculties and institutes, the Department for Support and Development of Students and the Department for the Organisation of Bachelor's and Master's Programmes [15].
- Developing and updating students' individual curricula (индивидуальные учебные планы) in bachelor's, master's and aspirantura programmes [16].
- Supporting the choice and appointment of research supervisors for aspirantura students [17].
- Supporting the recording of attendance, including absences from classes, interim and repeat interim assessments, candidate examinations, state final attestation and final attestation for valid reasons [18].
- Organising interim assessments and repeat interim assessments for bachelor's, master's and aspirantura students [19]:
  - drawing up their schedule and extending their terms for students who did not appear for a valid reason [19];
  - forming the membership of the commissions for second repeat interim assessments in bachelor's and master's programmes [19];
  - forming and checking the completion of the record sheets (ведомости) by discipline (module), recording their results and forming summary information, information and reports on students' performance [19];
  - organising the archival storage of the record sheets [19].
- Organising candidate examinations in aspirantura programmes, supporting the work of the commissions that admit them and forming and checking the completion of the candidate examination protocols [20].
- Controlling the fulfilment of the curriculum or individual curriculum by students and by the University's teaching staff [21] and supporting the formation, approval and fulfilment of aspirantura students' individual plans of scientific activity [22].
- Controlling the use of the University's classroom fund according to the schedules of classes, interim and repeat interim assessments [23].
- Transferring students to the next course and to the next course conditionally, and establishing the fact that an academic debt has been cleared [24].
- Supporting the processes of transfer, reinstatement, return from academic leave, academic exchange and expulsion, and of credit for results of study [25], and agreeing students' participation in an academic mobility (exchange) programme and their individual curricula for that period [26].
- Forming, printing and issuing extracts from record books, certificates of the average mark, certificates of the language of instruction, academic certificates in English and other certificates on educational activity [27], and providing information for certificates of the period of study [28].
- Organising the state final attestation of bachelor's, master's and aspirantura students together with the faculties, institutes, laboratories and the Department for the Organisation of Bachelor's and Master's Programmes [29]:
  - forming the approximate list of topics of the final qualification works and drafting the orders approving the topics and assigning supervisors and sending the works for review [29];
  - drafting the orders approving the schedule of state attestation tests, approving the composition of the state examination and appeal commissions and appointing their secretaries, and admitting students to the state final attestation [29];
  - collecting and storing the final qualification works and the documents relating to them (annotations, reviews, supervisors' opinions, acknowledgement sheets, transfer acts, certificates of the check for borrowing) and, for aspirantura, the scientific reports and scientific-qualification works (dissertations) with their documents [29];
  - supporting the work of the state examination commission, forming and checking its protocols and the record sheets, and supporting the preparation of reports on the results [29];
  - forming the lists of students applying for diplomas with honours and drafting the orders conferring the qualification and issuing documents on education, and on expulsion in connection with the completion of education [29].
- Organising the final attestation of aspirants in aspirantura programmes together with the faculties, institutes and laboratories [30]:
  - drafting the orders approving the schedule of meetings of the expert-attestation commission and the composition of the expert-attestation and appeal commissions [30];
  - collecting and organising the dissertations and the documents relating to them, including the supervisor's opinion, copies of publications, patent certificates, reviews, the conclusion on the check for incorrect borrowing and the conclusion on compliance with the criteria set under Federal Law No. 127-ФЗ of 23 August 1996 «О науке и государственной научно-технической политике» [30];
  - supporting the work of the expert-attestation commission and forming and checking the protocols of the final attestation of aspirants [30].
- Supporting the internal and external assessment of the quality of educational activity and of the preparation of students in higher education programmes [31], providing expert and consultative support to the University's structural subdivisions [32], taking part in round tables, conferences, seminars, webinars and other events [33] and interacting with external organisations, employers and subdivisions on the organisation of the educational process [34].

## Rights

In carrying out their activity the Department's employees have the right to:

- request from the University's structural subdivisions the information and materials needed for work within the Department's competence [35];
- with the consent of the heads of those subdivisions, involve their employees in preparing drafts of local normative acts and other documents and in developing and carrying out measures conducted by the Department [36];
- take part in meetings held by the University's management where matters within the Department's competence are discussed [37];
- put before the University's management proposals to cancel, amend or supplement the University's local normative acts [38];
- control the fulfilment of local normative acts, orders, instructions and decisions of the Academic council on the organisation of the educational process in higher education programmes [39];
- interact with bodies of state authority and administration, legal entities and the University's subdivisions on matters within the Department's competence [40].

## Interaction with other subdivisions

The Department's interaction with other structural subdivisions is determined by the tasks and functions assigned to it by the Regulation [41]. The Regulation names these counterparts and matters:

- the Department for the Organisation of Bachelor's and Master's Programmes — on the implementation of higher education programmes, planning and organising the educational process, classes, interim and repeat interim assessments, state final and final attestation, and recording the results in the electronic information-educational environment [42];
- the Methodological Department — on the implementation of the programmes and the organisation of classes, interim and repeat interim assessments and the attestations [43];
- the faculties, institutes and laboratories — on the implementation of programmes, classes, the class schedule, assessments, attestations, recording results and entering information into the electronic information-educational environment [44];
- the Department for Control of Educational Activity — on normative-legal support of educational activity and on assessing its quality and the preparation of students [45];
- the Centre of Pre-University Preparation and Admission of Applicants — on the enrolment of students, including by transfer from other educational organisations, their reinstatement and their transfer from one form of study to another [46];
- the Department for Support and Development of Students — on the movement of the student body, expulsion, admission to study after an academic leave, students' personal files and educational work with bachelor's and master's students [47];
- the Department for Relations with Industry and Alumni — on internships, practical preparation, their results and interim and repeat interim assessments on internships [48];
- the Department of International Cooperation — on students' participation in academic mobility (exchange) programmes [49], and the Academic Cooperation Department — on organising the educational process within academic cooperation with other organisations, including programmes in the network form [50];
- the Documentation Department of Managerial Activity — on document flow, local normative acts, the Department's files under the consolidated nomenclature and transfer to the archive [51];
- the Organisational and Technical Department — on planning, distributing and using the classroom fund and booking rooms [52];
- the IT Department — on the information systems, technical support of the educational process and the electronic information-educational environment [53];
- the Department for the Organisation of Programmes for Highly Qualified Personnel — on drafts of local normative acts, support of the educational process and documents on education [54];
- other structural subdivisions — in preparing drafts of local acts, orders, instructions, contracts and other documents and in preparing conclusions and references [55].

The Department may be a base for the practical preparation of the University's students [56].

## Reporting

- On the basis of the relevant local normative acts, orders and instructions the Department may, within its activity, be responsible for preparing information, references and reports and keeping them up to date [57].
- Within its activity the Department is responsible for preparing and storing the documents that record the results of students' mastering of the higher education programmes [58], and for storing, recording, completing and issuing documents on education and on qualification and their annexes [59].

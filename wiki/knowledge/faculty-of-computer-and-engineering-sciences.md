# Faculty of Computer and Engineering Sciences — place, tasks, functions and rights

## The document

The page is the Regulation (Положение) on the Faculty of Computer and Engineering Sciences of ANO VO «Innopolis University»; it regulates the Faculty's activity and defines its tasks, functions, rights and duties [1]. The approval stamp on the page names A.M. Samatova, but the number and date of the approving order are not legible in the page's text [1]. The page is a scanned document read by OCR, so its text and clause numbering are partly garbled and should be checked against the PDF [1].

## Place in the structure

The Faculty of Computer and Engineering Sciences is a structural subdivision of the University and includes the following structural subdivisions [2]:

- Institute of Software Development and Software Engineering [2];
- Institute of Information Security and Cyberphysical Systems [2];
- Institute of Data Analysis and Artificial Intelligence [2];
- Institute of Robotics and Computer Vision [2].

The Faculty reports directly to its dean, and in the dean's absence to the vice-rector — head of the department of academic policy and organisation of educational activity [3]. In its activity the Faculty is guided by the Constitution of the Russian Federation, the Labour Code of the Russian Federation, the Federal Law «Об образовании в Российской Федерации», other normative legal acts on the activity of higher education institutions, the University's Charter, decisions of the Academic, Supervisory and Operational councils of the University, orders and instructions of the director of the University, other local normative acts of the University and this Regulation [1].

The Faculty's structure, number of staff and staffing table, and changes to them, are approved by the director of the University [4]. The Faculty is headed by its dean, who is hired and dismissed by order of the director of the University; the conditions of work of the Faculty's employees are determined by the employment contracts concluded with each employee and by the University's internal labour regulations [5]. Their rights, duties and degree of responsibility are established by job descriptions [6]. The Regulation and additions and changes to it are approved by the director of the University or by another person authorised by him [7].

## Tasks

The Faculty's tasks are [8]:

- recruitment of the Faculty's students [8];
- multi-level training of students — training of bachelors, masters and scientific-pedagogical cadres of the highest qualification [9];
- integration of education, science and production by using the results of research in the study process and establishing mutually beneficial links between educational, scientific, experimental-production and research-production institutions, enterprises and innovative organisations as a single collective system for obtaining and using new scientific knowledge and technologies in education, the economy and the social sphere [10];
- raising the professional level of the Faculty's academic and research-pedagogical staff through research, the implementation of qualification improvement programmes and creative activity, and the use of the scientific results obtained in the educational process [11];
- organising, coordinating and controlling the academic, methodological and research work of the institutes that are part of the Faculty [12].

## Functions

The Faculty performs the following functions [13]:

- planning and organising the educational process at the Faculty in accordance with the approved curricula [13];
- organising and controlling the development of curricula and teaching-methodological complexes in accordance with the requirements of the federal state educational standards [14];
- forming the staff of academic, research and educational-support personnel and selecting the heads of the Faculty's structural subdivisions [15];
- general guidance and control of students' academic and industrial practice [16];
- control over the qualification improvement of the academic staff [17];
- other functions provided by the legislation of the Russian Federation, the Charter and other local normative acts of the University [18].

## Rights

In carrying out their activity the Faculty's employees have the right to [19]:

- request from the University's structural subdivisions the information and materials needed for the work within the Faculty's competence [19];
- with the consent of the heads of the University's structural subdivisions, involve the employees of those subdivisions in preparing draft local acts and other documents and in developing and carrying out measures conducted by the Faculty [20];
- take part in meetings held by the University's management when questions whose solution falls within the Faculty's competence are discussed [21];
- put before the University's management proposals to cancel, amend or supplement the University's local acts [22];
- interact with bodies of state authority and administration, legal entities and the University's subdivisions on questions within the Faculty's competence [23].

## Interaction with other subdivisions and reporting

The Faculty's interaction with the University's other structural subdivisions is determined by the tasks and functions assigned to it by the Regulation [24].

The Faculty interacts with public organisations in order to organise its work as effectively as possible, raise the level of public events of the Faculty and the University, help those in need and defend the interests of employees and students [25].

The Faculty interacts with the University's Academic council, the University's scientific-methodological council and the department for the organisation of educational programmes on all questions of academic, research and educational work and of the competitive replacement of academic staff positions [26].

The page gives a further clause under the same number: interaction with other structural subdivisions takes place on preparing draft local acts, orders, instructions, contracts and other documents and on preparing conclusions and written or oral information on questions relating to the Faculty's competence [1].

Within its activity the Faculty is responsible for preparing and keeping up to date the following information: the number of students, graduates and academic staff, the curricula, and the reports on students' academic and industrial practice [27]. The Faculty is responsible for providing statistical and other information established by legislation to the authorised bodies [28].

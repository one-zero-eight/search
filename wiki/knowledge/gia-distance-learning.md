# Final state certification with distance learning technologies (bachelor's and master's programmes)

## The document

The procedure for conducting the state final certification with the use of distance learning technologies for bachelor's and master's programmes was approved by order of the director of the University of 2 August 2022 № ОД/УД-05/2022/08/02 [1]. It determines the procedure for applying distance learning technologies when conducting the state final certification (ГИА) in bachelor's and master's programmes of the University [2].

It was developed in accordance with the Regulation on the procedure for conducting the state final certification in bachelor's and master's programmes of the University and the Regulation on the procedure for applying e-learning and distance learning technologies in bachelor's, master's and scientific-pedagogical personnel (aspirantura) programmes [3]. The procedure enters into force on 1 September 2022; changes and additions are made by order of the director of the University or of a person authorised by the director [4], [5].

## Form of the certification and of the defence

The state final certification of students is conducted in the form of a defence of a final qualification work (ВКР); for the bachelor's level the ВКР is a bachelor's work, for the master's level a master's dissertation [6].

The defence is held orally, with mandatory control identification of the student's personality and constant control by the state examination commission (ГЭК) over compliance with the procedure and order of the defence [7].

## What must be submitted and when

Students and staff send materials to the electronic address of the Methodological Department of the Centre for the Organisation of Educational Programmes, education@innopolis.ru [8]. No later than 10 calendar days before the day of the defence, to that address [8]:

- the student sends electronic versions (in .doc or .pdf format) of the ВКР and of the annotation to it [8];
- the student provides information about a current telephone number for prompt contact during the preparation and conduct of the defence [8];
- the supervisor of the ВКР submits a scan-copy of the written review on the student's work during the preparation of the ВКР [8];
- the reviewer of a master's dissertation (the reviewers, if the ВКР is of an interdisciplinary character) submits a scan-copy of the written review (рецензия) on that dissertation [8].

The secretary of the ГЭК controls the collection by the Methodological Department's staff of the students' electronic ВКР, taking into account the withdrawal, by decision of the rights holder, of production, technical, economic, organisational and other information of actual or potential commercial value because it is unknown to third parties, including information on results of intellectual activity in the scientific and technical sphere and on ways of carrying out professional activity [9]. No later than 5 calendar days before the defence the secretary of the corresponding ГЭК, by e-mail [9]:

- sends all the students' ВКР, the reviews and the рецензии to the members of the ГЭК so that participants get acquainted with the materials in time [9];
- sends the reviews and рецензии on the ВКР to the students for their acquaintance [9];
- sends the electronic versions of the ВКР to the Department for Control of Educational Activity for checking the texts for the amount of borrowing [9].

No later than 1 calendar day before the day of the defence, students send electronic presentations prepared for the defence to the electronic address of the Department for the Organisation of Educational Programmes, education@innopolis.ru; the secretary of the corresponding ГЭК sends the presentations by e-mail to all members of the ГЭК [10].

## Equipment and connection

For a defence with distance learning technologies a student needs computing equipment (a desktop computer, a laptop, a tablet), communication equipment providing access to the software and information of the University's electronic information-educational environment (ЭИОС) through local networks and the Internet, and the corresponding software [11].

For a defence by videoconference with the necessary number of broadcast users the following are recommended [12]:

- a web camera with resolution not lower than standard (640×480 (0.3 MP)) [12]
- a monitor/screen with resolution not lower than 1280×1024 [12]
- a microphone with speakers or a computer headset for communication [12]
- configured video conferencing software (Microsoft Teams, Zoom or a similar one allowing remote audio-visual contact) [12]
- Internet connection speed not lower than 2 Mbps (or 1024 Kbps per workplace) [12]
- the Google Chrome Internet browser (version not lower than 81.0) [12]

Interaction between the participants of the educational process (ГЭК members, staff of the Department for the Organisation of Educational Programmes and students) is carried out by means of Microsoft Teams, Zoom or a similar product in videoconference mode [13].

## Requirements to the room and workplace

The room in which the student is located must meet the following requirements [14]:

- the room must have walls and a closed door [14]
- no outside persons may be in the room during the attestation test [14]
- additional computers and other monitors must be switched off [14]
- the working surface of the desk with the student's computer must be free of all objects, including palmtop computers or other computer devices, watches, notebooks, books, notepads, sticky notes, notes or papers with printed text [14]
- the web camera must not be placed opposite a light source and must provide a view of the room within a radius of 2–3 metres from the student [14]
- documents identifying the student's personality, clean sheets of paper and a pen are allowed on the desk [14]

The chair and/or members of the ГЭК have the right to ask the student to demonstrate the room and/or the workplace for compliance with clause 2.6.3; refusal or non-compliance is a ground for the commission to recognise a violation of the procedure of the state final certification by the student [15].

## Before the defence and identification

On the day of the ВКР defences the student's preparation for the defence procedure, including checking the web camera, microphone and speakers (headphones), must begin not less than 15 minutes before the approved starting time of the defence [16].

The student must log in to the personal account of the University's ЭИОС and follow the link to join the videoconference, and, if necessary, authenticate with the personal University account in the videoconference platform in order to establish a connection with the ГЭК members [17].

A responsible technical specialist controls the students' connection to the videoconference and, where individual students are not connected, establishes contact with them by telephone at the numbers provided by the students in advance and gives them consultative help to eliminate connection problems [18].

Before the start of the state final certification the secretary of the ГЭК carries out the control identification of the student's personality: a visual check of the student against the data of the electronic pass shown by the student before the web camera to the ГЭК members; the student must state in full the surname, name and patronymic (if any); the procedure must be recorded; the results are entered by the secretary in the minutes of the ГЭК meeting [19].

## The defence

After identification the student, or the students if the ВКР was done jointly, gives the report on the ВКР orally with a presentation; 10 minutes are allotted for the report at a bachelor's work defence and 20 minutes at a master's dissertation defence; after the report the ГЭК members may ask questions on the topic of the ВКР, to which the student(s) must give detailed answers; the secretary records the course of the defence in the minutes of the ГЭК meeting; a video recording must be made during the whole time of the defence [20].

At the end of the students' answers the ГЭК members discuss the results; the students disconnect from the videoconference for an agreed time so that they cannot hear the discussion, and after it is over they are given the opportunity to re-enter the conference to get acquainted with the grades, after which the defence procedure is considered completed [21].

The minutes of the ГЭК meetings are drawn up in accordance with the requirements of clause 5.9 of the Regulation on the procedure for conducting the state final certification in bachelor's and master's programmes [22]. The secretary of the ГЭК sends the electronic versions of the ВКР to the corporate e-mail address of the responsible employee of the University Library for placement in the University's electronic library system no later than 5 calendar days after the day of the defence, except for ВКР containing state secrets and with account of the withdrawal of the information described above by decision of the rights holder [23].

## Connection failures

If a connection problem arises and is not eliminated within 20 minutes, the student(s) is/are offered by telephone and/or e-mail to take the state attestation test on another day in accordance with the approved schedule of state attestation tests [24].

If the connection breaks during the defence and a repeated connection is impossible within 30 minutes, the student(s) is/are offered by telephone and/or e-mail to take the state attestation test on another day in accordance with the approved schedule [25].

If no contact with the student was established either before the start or during the state certification, clauses 3.10 and 3.11 of the Regulation on the procedure for conducting the state final certification in bachelor's and master's programmes apply [26].

## Appeal

On the results of the ВКР defence the student has the right to appeal; the student may submit to the appeal commission a written appeal about a violation, in the student's opinion, of the established procedure of the state attestation test [27], [28].

- No later than the next working day after the announcement of the ГИА results the student sends a scan-copy of the appeal application with signature and full name to education@innopolis.ru [29]
- The secretary of the ГЭК sends to the appeal commission by e-mail a scan-copy of the minutes of the ГЭК meeting, a scan-copy of the chair's conclusion on compliance with procedural matters, the video recording of the defence, and the ВКР, the review and the рецензия (рецензии) if necessary [30]
- No later than 2 working days after the student's application the chair of the appeal commission holds an online meeting on the appeal by videoconference (Microsoft Teams, Zoom or similar); the secretary keeps the minutes and the meeting is recorded [31]
- On the day of the meeting the chair informs the Methodological Department of its results by e-mail to education@innopolis.ru with a scan-copy of the minutes [32]
- No later than the next working day after the meeting the Methodological Department informs the student by e-mail to the corporate address about the results of the appeal [33]
- The decision of the appeal commission is final and not subject to review [34]
- If necessary a repeated defence is carried out by the ГЭК in the presence of one of the members of the appeal commission according to the requirements of section 2 [35]
- An appeal against a repeated state attestation test is not accepted [36]

# Psychologist's work plan for students 2023/2024 (order of 15 August 2023)

## The order

The order of the University of 15 August 2023 № ОД/УД-06/2023/08/15 (Innopolis) approves the План работы психолога с обучающимися АНО ВО «Университет Иннополис» на 2023/2024 учебный год annexed to it, issued in order to organise effective work on the psychological support of the University's students; control over the execution of the order the signer leaves with himself [1]. The order is signed by the vice-rector — head of the management of academic policy and organisation of educational activity E.S. Bobrov [1].

The page is OCR of a scanned document, so its dates and figures should be checked against the PDF before relying on them [1].

## The plan

Every item of the plan names E.G. Ilyina as the person responsible; the activities and their dates are [1]:

- conducting consultations — during the year [1];
- conducting trainings for the participants of the tutor programme — August–December 2023 [1];
- conducting diagnostic methods to determine adaptation — September 2023 [1];
- conducting trainings for students within the «Student Escape» event — November 2023 [1];
- conducting socio-psychological testing to reveal the risk group — November–December 2023 [1];
- psycho-preventive and enlightenment classes aimed at prevention («я выбираю жизнь, как безопасную среду») — during the year [1];
- conducting meetings with students within the art-therapy project — during the year [1];
- conducting meetings with students lagging behind on the results of midterms — October 2023, March 2024 [1];
- work with students of the risk group — during the year [1];
- research of the psychological atmosphere of collectives — groups, in order to study socio-psychological adaptation — during the year [1];
- conducting meetings for underage students in order to prevent neglect — during the year [1];
- conducting a group meeting with campus administrators on questions of helping students in crisis situations — during the year [1];
- conducting monitoring of psychological comfort and identification of the risk group — November 2023 [1];
- conducting thematic meetings with students (self-confidence etc.) — during the year [1];
- support of students with mental illnesses to an appointment with a psychiatrist — during the year [1].

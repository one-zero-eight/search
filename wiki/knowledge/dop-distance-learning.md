# Distance learning in additional general education programmes (ДОП with ДОТ) — the Regulation of 18 September 2019

## The document

The Regulation on the procedure of organising studies under additional general education programmes with the use of distance educational technologies was approved by the University's order No. Д/Уд-01/2019/09/18 of 18 September 2019, signed by Director K.V. Semenikhin [1]. It regulates the delivery of additional general education programmes (ДОП) with the use of distance educational technologies (ДОТ) [2] and was drawn up under Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29 December 2012 and order No. 816 of the Ministry of Education and Science of 23 August 2017 on the procedure of using e-learning and distance educational technologies by organisations carrying out educational activity [3]. The wiki copy is a draft made by OCR, so numbers and dates should be checked against the PDF [1].

## What ДОТ are and why they are used

- ДОТ are educational technologies implemented mainly with information and telecommunication technologies, with interaction between the learner and the pedagogical worker mediated (at a distance) or not fully mediated (article 16 of Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ) [4].
- The purpose is to increase the accessibility of professional education by giving learners the possibility of mastering programmes at their place of residence or of temporary stay [5].
- Distance learning is meant to strengthen the personal orientation of the learning process and intensify the learner's independent work, reduce the cost of training, raise the quality of training and widen the geography of listeners through modern information and communication technologies, give open access to information resources at any convenient time, spread knowledge among the population and raise its general cultural level, and create conditions for quality control of electronic methodological resources [6].
- Its basis is the purposeful and controlled intensive independent work of listeners, who may study in a convenient place, having a set of special learning tools and an agreed possibility of contact with the teacher through information and telecommunication technologies in mediated interaction [7].
- After enrolment the listener is given a personal login and password allowing entry into the University's electronic information and educational environment, including the Moodle content management system [8].
- Learning and methodological materials are transferred to the listener without a right to reproduce them or transfer them to third persons and organisations [9].
- The place of educational activity is the place determined by the licence for educational activity (Innopolis, Universitetskaya street), regardless of the listener's location [10].

## Organisation of the process

- Enrolment of listeners and acceptance of documents are carried out by the structural unit that delivers ДОП at the University, under the Procedure of admission, reinstatement, transfer and expulsion of listeners under additional general education programmes approved by order No. ОД/УД-02/2018/12/11 of 11 December 2018 [11].
- The educational process with ДОТ is conducted under the approved ДОП, the University's acts regulating the educational process and Russian legislation [12]; questions about its organisation are decided by the unit that delivers ДОП [13].
- The process with ДОТ may be organised in full or for separate disciplines of the study plan where there is appropriate methodological help to learners, including individual consultations at a distance, an appropriate level of preparation of the pedagogical, research, auxiliary and administrative staff, and where classroom classes with direct interaction may be absent [14].
- The unit that delivers ДОП prepares the methodological documentation, and specialists of organisations and enterprises may be involved in its development by agreement [15].
- Interaction of listeners and pedagogical workers includes lectures and other classes under a teacher's guidance (online), practical, seminar and laboratory classes (online/offline) and interviews in chat, individual and group consultations by e-mail, chat conferences and forums, independent work with textbooks, manuals and methodological materials and performance of tasks (offline), and current and interim assessment in the form of testing and other assessment measures (online/offline) [16].
- The main interaction of listeners and teachers takes place in the University's electronic information and educational environment [17].
- Moodle provides theoretical materials (files, links to lecture recordings and external sites), communication between listeners (forum, chat, messaging), tasks answered in electronic form, knowledge checks (tests, tasks), polls of listeners and a database of articles, books and hyperlinks [18].
- On successful completion of a ДОП the listener may be issued a document of additional education; the register of issued documents on advanced training and professional retraining is kept at the University [19].

## Technologies, materials and records

- The information technologies used are the case technology, the television-satellite technology based on interactive television (tele- and radio lectures, video conferences, virtual practical classes) and Internet (network) learning [20].
- Learners, pedagogical workers and auxiliary staff are given access to a set of documents: the educational programme with the study plan, academic calendar, work programmes of subjects, courses, disciplines (modules) and other components, together with assessment and methodological materials, and educational resources (notes, textbooks, manuals, problem books, simulators, control tasks, glossaries, reference and further literature) [21].
- The rights and duties of learners mastering a programme with ДОТ are determined by the University's Charter, the rules of the University's study order and Russian legislation [22].
- After the contract on education is concluded, the learner receives by e-mail methodological materials: work programmes of disciplines, methodological developments, glossaries, learning literature, interactive presentations and a list of individual tasks [23].
- Recording and storage of the results of the educational process (interim assessment) and internal document flow are kept on paper and/or in electronic form in accordance with the law on state secrets, the law on personal data and the law on archiving [24].

## Staff, technical support and control

- The teaching staff carrying out distance learning is formed from the University's pedagogical workers and staff and, where necessary, with the involvement of staff of outside enterprises and organisations paid under a contract for paid educational services; persons obtaining higher or secondary vocational education in the enlarged group «Образование и педагогические науки» may be involved on the recommendation of the attestation commission and subject to the qualification handbooks [25].
- All managerial and pedagogical workers and auxiliary staff involved in organising, conducting and supporting the process with ДОТ must have appropriate training and regularly improve their qualification under federal legislation on education; coordination and control of distance learning are assigned to the unit that delivers ДОП [25].
- The teacher/curator has the right to control the learning process by sending control works and test tasks envisaged by the programme, to organise forums and online consultations, and not to admit a learner who has not mastered the previous module to the next module in sequential study [26].
- The teacher/curator must help with processing information and advise learners where necessary on the presentation of the results of independent work, give feedback on tasks (clarifying the degree of completion, answering questions, diagnosing difficulties, giving recommendations), design and deliver quality teaching of disciplines taught at a distance, promptly update the methodological complexes, answer learners' questions in good time and systematically improve his qualification, including in the specific tools of distance learning [27].
- Teachers/curators using ДОТ also perform the duties of a tutor: organising individual work with learners, helping them overcome difficulties of self-education and master new technologies including those of distance learning, monitoring all the learners' actions, supporting the learner's cognitive interest and organising individual and group consultations [28].
- Technical support includes computing equipment and other equipment needed to operate, develop and store the software and information of the distance learning system and to give teachers and listeners access to electronic information resources and communication over the Internet, and communication equipment giving access to the software and information through local networks and the Internet [29].
- The software includes the Moodle content management system, which gives access to study plans, work programmes of disciplines (modules) and practices, to electronic library editions and to the electronic educational resources named in the work programmes, allows all types of classes and assessment procedures envisaged with e-learning and ДОТ, and provides webinars and other ways of communication between teachers and listeners over the Internet [30].

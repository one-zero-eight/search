# Department for Work with Partners (Отдел по работе с партнерами) of the Center of Educational Content — place, tasks, functions and rights

## The document and the Department's place in the structure

The page is the Regulation on the Department for Work with Partners (Положение об отделе по работе с партнерами) of the Center of Educational Content of ANO VO «Innopolis University», approved by the Director for Development and HR Policy R.F. Valiev on 4 August 2025, Innopolis 2025 [1]. In the wiki copy the approving header is partly garbled by the text extraction (the order number reads as «№ ОД/АД‐03/2025/08/04»), so it should be checked against the PDF [1].

- The Regulation regulates the activity of the Department for Work with Partners of the Center of Educational Content and determines its tasks, functions, rights and duties [2].
- The Department is a structural subdivision of the Center of Educational Content and belongs to the category «иные структурные подразделения» (other structural subdivisions) [3].
- It reports directly to the head of the Center of Educational Content [4].
- In its activity the Department follows the Constitution of the Russian Federation, the Labour Code of the Russian Federation, Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29 December 2012 «Об образовании в Российской Федерации» and other legislative and normative legal acts on higher education institutions, the University's Charter, decisions of the Academic, Supervisory and Operational councils, orders and instructions of the University's director, other local normative acts and this Regulation [5].
- The structure, the number of staff and the staffing table of the Department, and changes to them, are approved by the director of the University [6]; the Department is headed by its head, hired and dismissed by order of the director [7].
- The working conditions of the Department's employees are determined by their employment contracts and by the University's internal labour regulations [8]; their rights, duties and degree of responsibility are established by job descriptions [9].
- Within their competences the Department's employees may be involved in work under grants, projects, subsidies and other programmes implemented by the University [10].
- The Regulation and additions and changes to it are approved by the director of the University or another person authorised by him [11].

## Tasks

- Coordinating interaction between the providers of electronic educational resources (ЭОР), the adjacent subdivisions of the Center of Educational Content and the organisations subordinate to the Ministry of Digital Development, Communications and Mass Media of the Russian Federation, in supporting the measures implementing the project «Моя школа» (the Project), in order to provide students and teaching staff of educational organisations with access to electronic educational content and digital educational services from external providers [12].
- Taking part in procurement procedures for the right to conclude contracts between providers and the University [13].
- Supporting the contractual relations with external providers [14].

## Functions

- Interacting with the ЭОР providers on questions of implementing the Project within the Department's competence [15].
- Monitoring the information resources of the ЭОР providers as regards tariff plans, availability of content and other information connected with providing access to ЭОР [16].
- Processing requests to take part in the Project coming from external providers [17].
- Analysing the consumption of ЭОР by users on the basis of statistical data from the organisations subordinate to the Ministry of Digital Development, Communications and Mass Media [18].
- Forming and checking reporting and financial documents as regards the volumes of ЭОР consumed by users for the reporting period [19].
- Settling disputed questions and disagreements in the course of supporting the contracts with external providers [20].

## Rights

In carrying out their activity the Department's employees have the right to:

- request from the University's structural subdivisions the information and materials needed for the work within the Department's competence [21];
- with the consent of the heads of the University's structural subdivisions, involve the employees of those subdivisions in preparing drafts of local acts and other documents and in developing and carrying out measures conducted by the Department [22];
- take part in meetings held by the University's management when discussing questions whose solution falls within the Department's competence [23];
- put before the University's management proposals to cancel, amend or supplement the University's local acts [24];
- interact with bodies of state authority and administration, legal entities and the University's subdivisions on questions within the Department's competence [25].

## Interaction with other subdivisions

The Department's interaction with other structural subdivisions is determined by the tasks and functions assigned to it by the Regulation [26], and the Department may be a base for the practical preparation of the University's students [27]. The Regulation names these counterparts and matters:

- the department of records and movement of personnel — on the Department's personnel questions [28];
- the occupational health and fire safety service — on the safe organisation of the workplace of the Department's employees [29];
- the centre of staff development and corporate culture — on selecting new employees for the Department, their adaptation and the development of their professional competences [30];
- the marketing department — on forming the marketing and PR policy and promoting the Project in the regions of the Russian Federation, and on working out a single media plan for promoting the Project [31];
- the department of documentation support of managerial activity — on organising the Department's document flow [32];
- the department for organising and holding events — on organising events connected with the Department's activity [33];
- the centre for software development — on the use of LMS systems in organising information and methodological events for educational organisations and teachers of the Russian Federation on the use of ЭОР in the educational process [34];
- the department of information technologies — on organising access to and work with the portal https://portal.university.innopolis.ru/, on technical support for work with the University's internal infrastructure and services, and on consultations on computer and server equipment [35];
- the organisational and technical department — on organising workplaces for the Department's employees [36];
- the department for organising accommodation — on accommodating guests and the Department's employees on the University's campus [37];
- the department of records and movement of material valuables — on additionally equipping the workplaces of the Department's employees [38];
- the accounting department — on payment of wages to the Department's employees (including holiday pay, travel allowances and other payments), on paying the ЭОР providers for the use of educational content within the Project and on other interaction connected with paying invoices within the Department's activity [39];
- the procurement department — on organising the selection of suppliers of digital educational content, on procurement of material valuables, equipment, services and other kinds of procurement within the Department's activity on the Project [40];
- the legal department — on legal support of the Department's activity on the Project [41];
- the financial and economic department — on spending the subsidies from the Ministry of Digital Development, Communications and Mass Media [42];
- other structural subdivisions — on other questions of organising work on the Project and the Department [43].

## Reporting

On the basis of the relevant local acts, orders and instructions the Department may, within its activity, be responsible for preparing information, references and reports and for keeping them up to date [44].

# Forms of interim assessment in the College: pass, exam, module, demonstration and qualification exams

## Where these forms come from

For College students on secondary vocational education programmes the forms of interim assessment are set by the Положение о формах, периодичности и порядке текущего контроля успеваемости и промежуточной аттестации обучающихся, осваивающих образовательные программы среднего профессионального образования, approved by order of the University of 17 April 2025 № ОД/АД-01/2025/04/17 [1]. Its main forms are a pass (including a graded pass), an exam, an exam on a professional module, a qualification exam on a professional module with the award of a qualification, and another form of control such as a control work, defence of a course work (project), defence of an individual project or an accumulative system of grades; a complex exam or complex pass over two disciplines, МДК or practices is allowed [2]. An exam or pass may be held orally or in writing, as testing including with computers and hardware, as defence of a referat or project, in combined form or otherwise; as a rule the teacher chooses the form and sets it in the work programme, and the assessment materials must correspond to it [3].

## The pass

A pass is a form of control of the student's performance of the programme of a discipline, МДК or practice [4]; it may be taken in one element or in several as a complex pass [5]. The assessment materials for the sections and topics put to a pass are brought to the students' attention not later than a month before it [6]. In a graded pass the level of preparation is characterised by grades of the «отлично»/«хорошо»/«удовлетворительно»/«неудовлетворительно» scale [7].

A pass is entered as «зачтено» or as a grade of that scale in the assessment sheet (Appendix 3), the journal and the student's record book [8]; «не зачтено» or «неудовлетворительно» is entered in the assessment sheet and the journal, but is not entered in the record book [9]. A pass on a practice is held on the results of the works provided by the practice programme [10], and a pass on a discipline or МДК with a course project on the results of its performance and defence [11].

## The exam

An exam on a discipline or МДК evaluates the results of studying it over a course (semester): the volume and strength of the theoretical knowledge, the development of thinking, the acquisition of skills of independent work and the ability to synthesize knowledge and apply it to practical tasks [12]. An exam may be held in one discipline or МДК or in several as a complex exam [13].

In the schedule of interim assessment exams are held in the examination period set by the calendar study schedule; not more than one exam a day is planned for a group; the interval between exams is as a rule not less than 2 (two) calendar days; the first exam may be held on the first day of the examination period; and a consultation for the group is planned before the exam [14]. The assessment materials for the sections and topics put to an exam are brought to the students not later than a month before it [15]. For an oral exam the teacher draws up exam tickets on the basis of the assessment materials, their content is not brought to the students, and the number of tickets must exceed the number of students in the group [16].

The exam is held in specially prepared rooms at the times of the schedule [17]. By its start the teacher must have provided the work programme of the discipline, МДК or ПМ, the assessment materials with tickets, tasks, tests or other tasks and criteria, the allowed visual aids, information and reference materials, normative documents and samples of equipment, the journal and the examination sheet [18]. Not more than 8 (eight) students may be in the room during an oral exam, written exams are held with the whole study group at once, and written papers are done on paper with the College's stamp [19]. The teacher and the members of the commission may ask additional theoretical questions and give additional practical tasks from the recommended list [20].

The grade obtained at the exam is entered by the teacher in the student's record book (except an unsatisfactory one), in the examination sheet and in the journal (including an unsatisfactory one) [21]. A student's non-appearance is marked «не явился» in the examination sheet, and non-appearance without a valid reason is equated to receiving an unsatisfactory grade [22].

## Complex exam and complex pass

A complex exam or complex pass may be held in not more than 2 (two) disciplines, МДК, study and production practices [23]. The complex exam is held at the expense of the hours planned for interim assessment, the complex pass at the expense of the hours allotted to the МДК, disciplines and practices concerned [24]. They are provided for disciplines or МДК with interdisciplinary links that are delivered in parallel and end in one semester (МДК from different ПМ may be included), and for practices that form part of one ПМ and end in one semester [25].

Assessment materials are developed on the basis of the ФОС for interim assessment of the elements included, together with criteria for evaluating the results [26]. A grade is given for each discipline or МДК included [27]; the results are entered in the interim assessment sheet for the complex exam or pass (Appendix 5) and signed by the teachers who hold it [28], and the grades for each element are entered in the journal and the record books [29]. For practices included in a complex pass the grade for each practice is entered in the sheet (Appendix 5), signed by the teacher or the practice supervisor from the University, and the grades are entered in the journal and the record books [30].

## Exam on a professional module

An exam on a professional module is an obligatory form of interim assessment: a set of regulated procedures in which examiners, with the participation of representatives of employers, evaluate the professional qualification or its part (a set of competences) of students who have completed the module, and its purpose is to evaluate how the achieved results meet the requirements of ФГОС СПО and the ОП СПО and the students' preparedness for work in the type of activity of the module [31].

The purpose of the module exam is to check the mastery of the main type of activity of the ПМ and the professional competences ensuring it, as well as the development of the general competences provided by the ОП СПО [32]. Its forms are a combined exam with a check of theoretical knowledge and a practical task, a demonstration and defence of a completed production task, a defence of a portfolio, a defence of a course project (work) or a demonstration exam [33]; the form is determined by the developers of the ОП СПО and set in the work programme of the ПМ, and the chosen format applies to all students of the study group [34].

An examination commission is appointed by order of the University director or another authorised person, its number being from 3 to 5 people, from the teachers leading the МДК of the ПМ, the persons organising and supervising the study and production practices and representatives of profile organisations that are potential employers for the graduates; the chair is as a rule appointed from among the representatives of employers [35]. The forms and procedure are brought to the students' attention within the first two months from the start of the academic year in which the exam is held [36]. The exam is held after the students have mastered all structural units of the module and the types of work of the curriculum and the work programme [37], at the expense of the time allotted to interim assessment on the ПМ [38].

By the start of the module exam the College must provide the order on the composition of the commission, the interim assessment sheet for the module exam (Appendix 6), the assessment materials with tasks, criteria for the formation of competences and a guide to evaluation for the members of the commission, instructions on safety when working with equipment and computers, the attestation sheets (or summary results) for the МДК and practices of the ПМ and the journal [39]. Visual aids, samples of equipment and other information and reference materials may be used, their list being regulated in advance [40]. The level and quality of preparation is graded on the «отлично»/«хорошо»/«удовлетворительно»/«неудовлетворительно» scale, and the sheet records the grade and the commission's decision whether the type of activity of the ПМ is mastered [41].

## Demonstration exam within interim assessment

Interim assessment on the results of mastering a ПМ may be held in the form of a demonstration exam [42]. Information about it as a form of interim assessment is brought to the students at the start of the academic year in which the procedures are planned [43]. The developers of the ОП СПО choose the competences and the sets of assessment documentation themselves, on the basis of an analysis of how their content corresponds to the task of evaluating the mastery of the ПМ [44]. The procedure of the interim assessment in this form is set by the Положение and the normative documents of the demonstration exam operator designated by the Ministry of Education of Russia [45].

## Qualification exam

Where the ФГОС СПО provides, within an ОП СПО, for mastering a working profession or office position, the student receives a certificate of that profession or position on the results of mastering the type of activity [46], and interim assessment on the ПМ concerned is held as a qualification exam [47]. The qualification exam may be held with the use of the mechanism of the demonstration exam [48]; it is a form of external independent evaluation of the educational results (competences) on the ПМ, and representatives of employers and their associations are involved [49].

The condition of admission is the successful mastery of all elements of the ПМ programme, admitted by order of the University director or another authorised person [50]. The exam includes a practical qualification work and a check of theoretical knowledge within the qualification requirements of the qualification reference books and/or professional standards for the professions and positions concerned [51], and must include one or several types of attestation tests of a practical part aimed at evaluating readiness for the type of activity [52].

An attestation commission is appointed by order of the University director or another authorised person, its number being from 3 to 5 people; the chair is appointed from among the heads or deputy heads of the employers' structural units depending on the profession (position) being obtained; the deputy chair may be the College director, their deputy or another pedagogical worker of the College, and a secretary from the College's pedagogical or other workers is appointed to keep the documentation [53]. The forms and procedure are brought to the students within the first two months from the start of the semester in which the exam is held [54], and the exam is held after the ПМ has been mastered, at the expense of the time allotted to interim assessment [55]. The form is determined by the developers of the ОП СПО and set in the work programme of the ПМ [56], and the exam tickets are approved by the College director [57].

By the start of the exam the College must provide the order on the composition of the attestation commission, the interim assessment sheet (Appendix 6), the assessment materials with tasks, criteria and a guide to evaluation, safety instructions and the journal [58]. The preparation is graded on the «отлично»/«хорошо»/«удовлетворительно»/«неудовлетворительно» scale and fixed in the sheet (Appendix 6); if the student does not appear, «не явился» is entered [59]. The results and the commission's decision to issue the certificate of the working profession (office position) are also entered in the protocol of the attestation commission (Appendix 7) [60], which is signed by the chair, the secretary and the members and is kept for 5 (five) years [61]. Students who did not appear or received «неудовлетворительно» may repeat the qualification exam on the basis of an order of the University director or another authorised person in the established time, but not earlier than after 1 (one) month [62].

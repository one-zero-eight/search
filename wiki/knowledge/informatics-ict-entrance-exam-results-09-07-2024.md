# Entrance examination «Информатика и ИКТ» for bachelor programmes (9 July 2024) — published results

## What the document is

The page is the published protocol of the entrance examination «Информатика и ИКТ» (informatics and ICT) for bachelor programmes, held by Innopolis University on its own on 09.07.2024; its heading reads «Вступительное испытание на программам бакалавриата "Информатика и ИКТ" 09.07.2024» [1].

The table is headed «Результаты вступительного испытания проводимого АНО ВО "Университет Иннополис" самостоятельно» — the results of an entrance examination conducted by the University itself [1].

## What the protocol lists

The results table has the columns №, СНИЛС/Уникальный код, оценка and оценка (прописью): a serial number, the participant's СНИЛС or unique code, the score as a figure and the same score written out in words [1].

Scores are published against a СНИЛС or an identifier rather than a name [1].

## The results of 9 July 2024

The rows of the table, in its order [1]:

- row 1: identifier [скрыто], score 62 (Шестьдесят два) [1];
- row 2: identifier [скрыто], score 75 (Семьдесят пять) [1];
- row 3: identifier [скрыто], score 98 (Девяносто восемь) [1];
- row 4: identifier «[скрыто]», score 32 (Тридцать два) [1].

The identifier in row 1, row 2 and row 3 is printed without dashes, while the identifier in row 4 is printed in the dashed form «[скрыто]» [1].

## What the page does not say

The page states no pass mark and no minimum score, and it records no decision on admission [1].

The name of the examination and the date appear a second time below the results, split into two further blocks («Наименование вступительного испытания | Информат | ика и ИКТ» and «Дата проведения вступительного испытания … | 09.07 | .2024»), so the scores have to be taken from the rows of the results table itself [1].

## Another examination of the same date

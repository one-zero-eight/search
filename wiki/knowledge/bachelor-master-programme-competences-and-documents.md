# Competences and the documents of a bachelor's or master's programme

## Where these rules come from

The competences, the working programmes of disciplines, the internship programmes and the state final certification programme are components of a bachelor's or master's programme under the Regulation on the procedure of development and approval of bachelor's and master's programmes, approved by order of 2 August 2022 No. ОД/АД-02/2022/08/02 and in force from 1 September 2022 [1], [2].

## Competences of the programme

On completing the programme the graduate must have all the competences established by the programme [3]. The set of competences includes the competences defined by the federal state educational standard and the competences determined by the University itself on the basis of the focus (profile) of the programme [4], and it must enable the graduate to carry out professional activity in not less than one area and sphere of professional activity established in accordance with the federal state educational standard and to solve the tasks of not less than one type of professional activity established in accordance with the standard [5]. The list of competences formed by the programme is established in accordance with the federal state educational standard [6].

Professional competences established by the programme are formed on the basis of professional standards corresponding to the graduates' professional activity, where such standards exist, and, where necessary, on the basis of an analysis of the requirements to professional competences on the labour market, of Russian and foreign experience, of consultations with leading employers and employers' associations of the industry where graduates are in demand, and of other sources [7]. When the professional competences of the programme are determined, they include [8]:

- all obligatory professional competences, where such exist [8];
- one or several recommended professional competences, where such exist [8];
- one or several professional competences determined by the University itself on the basis of the profile of the programme, on the basis of professional standards corresponding to the graduates' professional activity, where such standards exist, and, where necessary, on the basis of an analysis of other requirements to graduates [8].

When professional competences are determined on the basis of professional standards, the University chooses the professional standards corresponding to the graduates' professional activity from those named in the annex to the federal educational standard and/or other corresponding professional standards from the register of professional standards placed on the website of the Ministry of Labour and Social Protection «Профессиональные стандарты» [9]. From each chosen professional standard the University identifies one or several generalised labour functions corresponding to the graduates' professional activity, on the basis of the qualification level and the requirements of the section on requirements to education and training; generalised labour functions may be identified fully or partly [10].

The University establishes indicators of achievement of the competences; for the indicators of professional competences it determines itself, the necessary skills and knowledge stated in the description of the labour functions of the professional standard are taken into account [11]. The University plans the learning outcomes in disciplines (modules) and internships itself, and they must correspond to the indicators of achievement of the competences [12]. The list of professional competences determined by the University on the basis of the profile of the programme and the indicators of their achievement are approved by an order of the University director or a person authorised by the director [13].

The set of planned learning outcomes in disciplines (modules) and internships must ensure the formation of all the competences established by the programme [14], and the set of competences established by the programme is approved by the University's Academic Council as part of the programme [15]. The planned results of mastering the programme by field of study are given in the matrix of competences [16], which shows the correspondence between the competences established by the programme and the disciplines (modules) forming them, and their distribution by courses and semesters [17]; a model form is given in annex 3 to the model form of the programme [18].

## Working programmes of disciplines (modules)

The working programme of a discipline (module) is part of the programme and determines the content, structure, sequence, forms, methods and results of mastering the discipline (module) [1].

The working programme includes the following parts [1]:

- the name of the discipline (module) [19] and a short characteristic of it [20];
- the list of planned learning outcomes: the aim of mastering the discipline, the competences formed in classes in the form of contact work and in the form of independent work, the indicators of achievement of the planned results, and the methods and technologies of teaching that support the formation of the competences [21];
- the place of the discipline (module) in the structure of the programme [22];
- the volume, structure and content of the discipline: its volume in credit units and academic hours with the hours of contact work, its structure with the hours of contact and independent work, and its content by sections and topics with the competences formed [23];
- the methodological support needed to master the discipline: the main and additional literature and information on distance support of the discipline [24];
- the description of the material and technical support: the material and technical base, the Internet and electronic resources including electronic library systems, professional databases and reference systems, access to them including for disabled persons and persons with health limitations, the set of licensed and freely distributed software including domestic, and access to information and communication networks [25];
- methodical and assessment materials: tasks for practical classes, materials for ongoing assessment, for preparation for and conduct of interim assessment, criteria for assessing the formation of competences, the form of interim assessment, the grading system and methodological instructions for students on mastering the discipline including the organisation of their activity by type of class [26];
- other necessary additional information [27].

Working programmes are developed for all disciplines (modules) of the curriculum, including elective and optional disciplines (modules), and in accordance with the federal state educational standard [1]. They are developed by the academic staff member of the University to whom the discipline (module) is assigned, by other academic staff, by staff of institutes, laboratories, methodological departments and other units whose functions include the development and actualisation of educational programmes, and by methodologists and programme managers [1]. A model form is given in annex 4 to the model form of the programme [28].

## Internship programmes

Internship programmes are part of the programme and determine the content of the internship, its sequence and the methods of mastering the practical skills needed by students in further study and professional activity, as well as the competences formed by the internship [1]. They are developed for each type of internship of the curriculum of the programme [29].

The concrete types of internships for each programme are established by the federal state educational standard for the corresponding field of study, and the University chooses the types of internships in accordance with the standard [1]. Internship programmes are developed in accordance with the order of the Ministry of Education and Science and the Ministry of Education of 5 August 2020 No. 885/390 «О практической подготовке обучающихся», the federal state educational standard and the local acts of the University [30]; they are developed separately for each programme and approved by the University's Academic Council as part of the programme [1].

The internship programme includes [31]:

- the type of internship, its type and, where applicable, the method and form or forms of conducting it [32];
- the volume of the internship in credit units and its duration in weeks or in academic or astronomical hours [33];
- a short characteristic of the internship [34];
- the planned learning outcomes correlated with the planned results of the programme: the aims and tasks of the internship, the list of indicators and the list of planned results [35];
- the place of the internship in the structure of the programme [36];
- the content of the internship: the sections with the types of study load, their labour intensity and the competences formed, and the list of individual tasks of the internship supervisor with their approximate content and requirements to the result [37];
- the forms of reporting on the internship [38];
- the assessment fund for the interim assessment on the internship: the form of interim assessment, the criteria for assessing the results, the system for assessing the formation of competences on the example of model individual tasks and model criteria [39];
- the list of educational literature and Internet resources needed for the internship [40];
- the list of information technologies used, including software and reference systems, and the electronic resources including those adapted for disabled persons, with information on distance support [41];
- the description of the material and technical base needed for the internship, including the set of licensed and freely distributed software including domestic software [42];
- other necessary additional information [43].

A model form is given in annex 5 to the model form of the programme [44].

## State final certification programme

The state final certification programme is part of the programme and regulates the order and rules of organising and conducting the state final certification of the University's students [1]. Its aim is to determine the correspondence of the level of development of students' personal qualities and of the formation of universal, general professional and professional competences to the requirements of the federal state educational standard for the corresponding field of study, the readiness to apply knowledge, skills and personal qualities in the corresponding professional area, and the formation of competences supporting the social mobility, stability and competitiveness of graduates [1]. Its theoretical content is based on the results of mastering the disciplines of the obligatory part and of the part formed by the participants of educational relations included in the curriculum [1], and the practical skills needed for it are acquired during the study and production internships [1].

The programme includes [1]:

- the area of application [45];
- general provisions with the aim of the state final certification and the conditions of admission to it [46];
- the place of the state final certification in the structure of the programme [47];
- the list of planned results of the state final certification and of the indicators of their achievement [48];
- the order and form of conducting the state final certification [49];
- the structure and content: the form of conduct, the stages of completing and defending the final qualification work, the grading system and information on the use of distance educational technologies [50];
- methodological recommendations for students: general requirements to the final qualification work, requirements to its topic and the procedure of its approval, model topics, requirements to supervision and consulting, and the procedure of defence [51];
- the assessment fund: the competences and indicators, the tasks for ongoing assessment, the criteria for assessing the final qualification works and the quality of the works, with model criteria [52];
- the features of conducting the state final certification for disabled students and students with health limitations [53];
- the list of main and additional literature needed for the state final certification [54];
- the description of the material and technical base [55];
- the list of information technologies and Internet resources used, including electronic library systems, professional databases and reference systems, means of training and upbringing adapted for disabled persons, and access to information systems and communication networks [56].

The state final certification programme is developed in accordance with the order of the Ministry of Education and Science of 29 June 2015 No. 636 on the procedure of the state final certification, the federal state educational standard and the local acts of the University [1]. It is developed by staff of the department of academic policy and organisation of educational activity together with academic staff of the University, and is approved by the University's Academic Council as part of the programme [1]; a model form is given in annex 6 to the model form of the programme [57].

# Enrollment contract and the emergence of educational relations

## The document

The emergence, suspension and termination of educational relations at the University are governed by the Порядок оформления возникновения, приостановления и прекращения отношений between the University and students and (or) the parents (legal representatives) of minor students, approved in a new edition by an order of the University signed by the director K.V. Semenikhin [1]. It was developed on the basis of the Federal Law of 29.12.2012 No. 273-ФЗ, the University's Charter, Постановление of the Government of the Russian Federation of 15.08.2013 No. 706 «Об утверждении правил оказания платных образовательных услуг», the rules on paid educational services approved at the University and other local normative acts [2], and it regulates the formalisation of the emergence, suspension and termination of relations between the University and students and (or) the parents (legal representatives) of minor students [3].

## How educational relations arise

The basis for the emergence of educational relations is an administrative act — an order of the University on admitting a person to study, or to pass interim assessment and/or state final assessment [4]. Where a person is admitted to study at the expense of individuals and/or legal entities, the conclusion of an education contract precedes the issue of the admission order [4].

The rights and duties of the student provided for by the legislation on education and the University's local normative acts arise from the date specified in the admission order [5].

## What the contract must specify

The enrollment contract is concluded between the University (represented by the Director) and the person enrolled for study (or their parents/legal representatives for a minor student), or another individual and/or legal entity intending to order paid educational services [6].

The contract must include the following information [7]:

- Type of the educational program [7].
- Level of the educational program (or part of a program of a specific level, type, and/or direction) [7].
- Direction of the educational program [7].
- Main characteristics of the provided education (educational service) [7].
- Form of study [7].
- Terms for mastering the educational program (duration of study) [7].
- Type of document issued to the student after successful completion of the educational program (or part of it) [7].
- Procedure for modifying and terminating the contract [7].
- Any other necessary information related to the specifics of the paid educational services provided [7].

The information in the contract must correspond to the information published on the University's official website at the time the contract is concluded [8].

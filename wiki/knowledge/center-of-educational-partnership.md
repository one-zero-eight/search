# Center of Educational Partnership (Institute of Additional Education) — place, tasks, functions and rights

## Document and place in the structure

The Regulation on the Center of Educational Partnership of the Institute of Additional Education of ANO VO «Innopolis University» was approved by the University's order No. ОД/АД-03/2025/02/03 of 3 February 2025, issued in connection with the creation of a new structural subdivision in order to optimise the University's activity and signed by the Director for Development and HR Policy R.F. Valiev [1]. The order approved the attached Regulation and repealed the Regulation on the unified teaching-methodological centre of the Institute of Additional Education approved by the University's order No. ОД/АД-11/2021/07/01 of 01.07.2021 [1]. Under the order the head of the Center (М.А. Дегтярь) had to ensure that the subdivision's employees, as well as employees newly hired or transferred into it from other subdivisions, are familiarised with the Regulation against signature, and control over the order's execution was retained by the signatory [1].

The page is a scanned document read by OCR, and the wiki notes that numbers and dates should be checked against the PDF before relying on them [1].

The Regulation regulates the activity of the Center of Educational Partnership of the Institute of Additional Education and defines its tasks, functions, rights and duties [2]. The Center is a structural subdivision of the Institute of Additional Education, with the Sector for support of educational projects and the Sector of educational content as its sectors, and it belongs to the category of teaching-methodological subdivisions [3]. The Center reports directly to the director of the Institute of Additional Education [1].

The Sector for support of educational projects works under its own Regulation, approved by the University's order No. ОД/АД-01/2025/02/03 of 3 February 2025, is headed by V.N. Bulgakova and is a structural subdivision of the Center that reports directly to the head of the Center; it supports listeners of educational projects and additional professional education programmes [4].

In its activity the Center follows the Constitution of the Russian Federation, the Labour Code of the Russian Federation, Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29 December 2012 «Об образовании в Российской Федерации» and other legislative and normative legal acts regulating the activity of higher education institutions, the University's Charter, decisions of the Academic, Supervisory and Operational councils of the University, orders and instructions of the University's director, other local normative acts of the University and this Regulation [5].

The Center's structure, number of staff and staffing table, and changes to them, are approved by the director of the University [6]. The Center is headed by its head, who is hired and dismissed by order of the director of the University [7]. Working conditions of the Center's employees are determined by the employment contracts concluded with each employee and by the University's internal labour regulations [8]; their rights, duties and degree of responsibility are established by job descriptions [9]. Employees of the Center may be involved, within their competences, in work under grants, projects, subsidies and other programmes implemented by the University [10]. The Regulation and additions and changes to it are approved by order of the director of the University or of another person authorised by him [11].

## Tasks

The tasks of the Center are:

- developing the direction of certification and vendor training [12]
- organising and coordinating educational partnership with universities and vendors [13]
- ensuring the implementation of educational projects in the field of robotics [14]
- carrying out tasks and taking part in measures aimed at implementing educational projects and additional professional education programmes and at achieving the goals of the University's Institute of Additional Education [15]

## Functions

- attracting industrial and academic partners for the purposes of educational partnership with the University [16]
- developing and implementing joint programmes with partners in the network form [17]
- taking part in creating the national system of certification of IT specialists and in the measures of the Council on certification of IT specialists at APKIT [18]
- creating systems of specialist certification and organising certification exams on the University's platform on requests from vendors and manufacturers of robotic complexes [19]
- general coordination and implementation of the educational direction in the field of robotics [20]
- organising the activity of the authorised training centres of Red Soft and Astra Linux on the basis of the University [21]
- obtaining the status of new authorised training centres for the University [22]
- developing courses for vendors on newly created Russian software and hardware-software complexes [23]
- taking part in specialised forums and exhibitions in order to publicise the University's projects whose implementation is assigned to the Center [24]

## Rights

In carrying out its activity the Center's employees have the right to:

- request from the University's structural subdivisions the information and materials needed for the work within the Center's competence [25]
- with the consent of the heads of the University's structural subdivisions, involve the employees of those subdivisions in preparing draft local acts and other documents and in developing and carrying out measures conducted by the Center [26]
- take part in meetings held by the University's management when questions whose solution falls within the Center's competence are discussed [27]
- put before the University's management proposals to cancel, amend or supplement the University's local acts [28]
- interact with bodies of state authority and administration, legal entities and the University's subdivisions on questions within the Center's competence [29]

## Interaction and reporting

Interaction of the Center with the University's other structural subdivisions is determined by the tasks and functions assigned to it by the Regulation [30]. The Center may be a base for the practical training of the University's students [31]. Interaction with the finance and economics department covers accounting for material valuables, writing them off and transferring funds under contracts [32]. Interaction with the director of the Institute of Additional Education covers legal questions in the part of developing and agreeing projects, contracts and other legal documents [33]. Interaction with other structural subdivisions takes place when preparing draft local acts, orders, instructions, contracts and other documents and when preparing conclusions and written or oral information on questions relating to the Center's competence [34].

On the basis of the relevant local acts, orders and instructions the Center may, within its activity, be responsible for preparing and keeping information, certificates and reports up to date [35].

# Scholarships and other forms of material support — the Regulation of 28 May 2025: types, scholarship fund, Commission and payment rules

## The document

The Regulation on scholarships and other forms of material support for students and aspirants of ANO VO «Innopolis University» is a local normative act of the University that determines the procedure for appointing and paying scholarships and other forms of material support to the University's students [1]. It was approved by an order of 28 May 2025 № ОД/АД-01/2025/05/28, the approval block naming the director I.I. Bariyev [2]. The Regulation is approved by an order of the director of the University and enters into force from the moment of its approval [3], and changes or additions to it are approved by orders of the director or of a person authorised by him [4].

## What a scholarship is

A scholarship is a measure of material encouragement of students in monetary form [5]. It is appointed to full-time students of bachelor's and master's programmes (students) and to students of programmes of training of scientific and scientific-pedagogical personnel in aspirantura (aspirants) of the University in order to encourage them depending on their success in studies and in the social life of the University, and for support [5].

## Types of scholarship support

- state scholarships: the state academic scholarship, the state scholarship for aspirants and the increased state academic scholarship [6]
- the state social scholarship, including the state social scholarship in an increased amount [7]
- scholarships of the President of the Russian Federation, of the Government of the Russian Federation and of other state bodies [8]
- scholarships appointed by legal or physical persons and paid from the funds of those persons [9]
- University scholarships: the academic scholarship of the University, the increased scholarship and the increased academic scholarship of the University, and named scholarships of the University [10]
- material assistance [11]

## The scholarship fund

- the fund is financed from federal budget allocations, targeted subsidies for the scholarship provision of students, targeted gratuitous donations of legal and physical persons given to the University to encourage and address scholarships, means from the University's income-generating activity and other sources [12]
- the size of the fund for the University scholarships and the increased University scholarships is determined from the number of students on higher education programmes who have concluded with the University a grant agreement providing for the payment of scholarships established by the University, with account of the total number of students, and is formed from the University's own funds [13]
- the size of the fund for all types of state scholarships is determined from the federal budget allocations allocated for that purpose [14]

## The Scholarship Commission

The Scholarship Commission (Стипендиальная комиссия) is created in order to coordinate scholarship provision, to raise the efficiency of the use of the money used for payments to students of all types of scholarships and for granting material assistance; it is a collegial body acting on the organisation of the appointment and payment of scholarships and the provision of other forms of material support to the University's students [15].

The Commission performs the following functions [16]:

- forming criteria and recommendations on the sizes of scholarships and other forms of support for appointment to students and aspirants [16]
- considering and approving the lists of students who are candidates for scholarships and other forms of material support [16]
- considering questions of reducing the sizes of, and/or depriving students of, previously appointed University scholarships [16]
- forming the Commission's decision for the director of the University on these questions [16]

In performing its functions the Commission must [17]:

- observe the norms of the legislation in force in the sphere of education and the local normative acts of the University [17]
- take decisions on the basis of the local normative acts of the University and of a comprehensive assessment of the data given on each student's success in studies, research work, participation in the social life of the University, observance of academic discipline and social and material situation [17]
- keep minutes of the meetings, draw up the decisions taken in writing and pass them in time for implementation to the subdivisions concerned [17]
- coordinate its work with the University's subdivisions and with administrative and scientific staff [17]
- carry out, by order of the director of the University or of a person authorised by him, assignments on the material provision of students and on the appointment and distribution of the scholarship fund [17]

The Commission has the right [18]:

- to request additional information on a candidate's success in studies, research work, participation in the social life of the University, observance of academic discipline and social and material situation [18]
- to request submissions from the heads of the University's structural subdivisions when candidates for a scholarship and/or material assistance are considered [18]
- to request information on the size of the University's scholarship fund and to put forward proposals to change it [18]

## Composition and decisions of the Commission

- The Commission includes not fewer than 5 persons, among them the chair of the Commission [19].
- The composition is approved every year for one academic year by an order of the director of the University or of a person authorised by him; it includes representatives of the administrative and managerial staff of the University and a representative of the students' council, and other employees may also be involved [19].
- If the chair or the secretary of a meeting is absent, they are elected from among the members of the Commission by a majority of votes [19].
- Decisions are taken by a simple majority of votes with 2/3 of the total number of its members present [20].
- Decisions are drawn up as minutes of meetings signed by the chair and the secretary of the Commission and are the grounds for issuing the corresponding orders; the minutes are kept in the Department of support and development of students of the University [20].
- Meetings are held not less than 2 times per academic year, with additional meetings during the academic year when necessary; the Commission may be convened on the initiative of the director of the University, of the rector of the University or of any member of the Commission when a decision within its competence must be taken urgently [21]

## Sizes of scholarships

- State scholarships, state social scholarships, University scholarships and increased University scholarships are paid in the sizes determined by an order of the director of the University or of a person authorised by him, within the funds allocated to the University for the scholarship provision of students [22].
- The state academic scholarship to students, the state social scholarships, the state scholarship to aspirants and the increased state academic scholarship may not be less than the norms established by the Government of the Russian Federation for each level of education for the categories of students, with account of the level of inflation [22].
- Foreign citizens and stateless persons studying the main professional educational programmes full-time receive the state academic scholarships to students and the state scholarships to aspirants on the conditions established by the Regulation if they study at the expense of federal budget allocations, including within the quota set by the Government of the Russian Federation, or if this is provided by international treaties of the Russian Federation under which they were admitted [23].
- The sizes of the scholarships set by the President of the Russian Federation or by the Government of the Russian Federation and the procedure for their payment are determined by the President or by the Government [24].
- The sizes of named scholarships are determined by the federal state bodies of the subjects of the Russian Federation, local self-government bodies, legal and physical persons and structural subdivisions of the University that established them [25].

## Calculation and payment

- All scholarships for new students are calculated from the first study day according to the calendar study schedule of the corresponding educational programme; if the first month of a student's study is incomplete, all types of scholarships for that month are calculated in proportion to the number of remaining calendar days of the month that fall within the academic year according to the calendar study schedule [26].
- For students on academic exchange programmes or on individual curricula the terms of the scholarship payments may be changed according to the individual terms of interim assessment, and after that period the University scholarship is appointed according to the results of the last interim assessment [2].
- The term of the scholarship payments is set not earlier than the 25th of the current month and not later than the 10th of the following month; if it falls on a non-working day, the last working day before that day counts; if a student's study begins after the 10th of the current month, the payments for the first incomplete month may be transferred in the following month [27].
- University scholarships and state scholarships are appointed to students depending on their success in studies on the basis of the results of interim assessment according to the calendar study schedule, from the first day of the month following the month of its end, not less than twice a year, by an order of the director of the University or of a person authorised by him on the basis of the corresponding minutes of the Commission [28].
- For students who have three interim assessments in the academic year, the terms of appointment are set twice a year: first on the results of the autumn interim assessment, second on the results of the spring and summer interim assessment (the period of study) [29].
- Scholarships are paid in monetary form in a non-cash procedure to the student's account opened on the territory of the Russian Federation in the currency of the Russian Federation [30], and payments at the expense of federal budget allocations are made within the national payment system established by federal legislation [31].
- The payment of scholarships stops from the moment a student is expelled from the University or from the moment the grounds on which the scholarship was appointed cease, and in other cases established by the legislation of the Russian Federation [32].

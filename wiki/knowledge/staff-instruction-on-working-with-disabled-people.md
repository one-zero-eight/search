# Instruction for staff on working with disabled people — status, staff training and accessibility of education

## The document and its status

The "Instruction for employees of ANO VO «Innopolis University» on working with disabled people on questions connected with ensuring accessibility for them of the object and of the services provided on it" was approved by an order of the University of 16 December 2025 (No. ОД/АД‐02/2025/12/16) and carries the place «Иннополис», 2025 [1]. The Instruction is approved by the director of the University and is subject to publication on the University's official website [2]. Changes to it are approved by the director of the University, and it comes into force from the moment of its approval by the director's order [3]. It is binding on all employees of the University [4].

It was developed in accordance with the Convention on the Rights of Persons with Disabilities, Federal Law No. 181-ФЗ of 24 November 1995, Federal Law No. 323-ФЗ of 21 November 2011, Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29 December 2012, Federal Law No. 419-ФЗ of 1 December 2014, the orders of the Ministry of Science and Higher Education No. 384 of 25.04.2025 and of the Ministry of Education No. 253 of 31.03.2025 on the procedure for ensuring accessibility, the roadmap order No. 1399 of 02.12.2015, the federal state educational standards of higher, secondary vocational and secondary general education and the orders on the organisation of educational activity under the respective programmes [5].

## Purpose and tasks

The purpose is to teach the University's employees to give disabled people the necessary help correctly, depending on the character of the impairment and the state of health, when services are provided and when the person moves about the University's objects and territory [6]. Its tasks are [7]:

- training and instructing the University's employees on ensuring accessibility of services and objects for disabled people and on giving the necessary help [7]
- preventing discrimination on the ground of disability, that is any distinction, exclusion or restriction on the ground of disability whose purpose or result is the diminution or denial of the recognition, realisation or exercise, on an equal basis with others, of all rights and freedoms guaranteed in the Russian Federation [7]
- realising the right of students with health limitations (ОВЗ) to education and social adaptation in the University's conditions [7]

The Instruction sets out the federal acts needed in the work on creating an accessible environment, both binding and recommendatory, and also questions of the culture of communication with disabled people [8].

## Instruction and training of employees

Under the Instruction, employees are instructed and trained on questions of ensuring accessibility of the University's objects and services for disabled people and persons with health limitations, including with the participation of personnel (helping to overcome barriers and accompanying the person on the objects) [9]. The instruction and training are conducted by a responsible person from among the University's employees, appointed by a corresponding order of the director of the University [10].

A journal of instruction in the form of Annex 1 is kept for recording this work [11]. It records the date and topic of the instruction with the full name and position of the employees who underwent it and of the employee(s) who conducted it, and the signatures of the instructed and the instructors are obligatory; the journal is kept in the nomenclature of files of the department of personnel records (отдел учёта и движения персонала) [11].

The instruction is conducted with all newly hired employees of the University regardless of their education or length of service in the profession or position [12], and newly hired employees are admitted to work after passing the instruction [13].

## Terms the Instruction uses

- A disabled person (инвалид) is a person who has an impairment of health with a persistent disorder of the body's functions caused by diseases, the consequences of injuries or defects, leading to a limitation of life activity and making social protection necessary [14].
- Limitation of life activity is the full or partial loss of the ability or possibility to serve oneself, move independently, orient oneself, communicate, control one's behaviour, learn and work [15].
- The conditions for recognising a person as disabled are a health impairment with a persistent disorder of functions, a limitation of life activity, and the need for social protection measures including rehabilitation; the presence of only one of them is not enough [16]; [17].
- Disability group I, II or III is established according to the degree of disorder, group I for the most severe and group III for the lightest; a person under 18 is given the category "child – disabled"; the person receives a certificate and an individual rehabilitation programme [18].
- Alongside "disabled person", the term "low-mobility groups of the population" (МГН) is used — people who have difficulties in moving independently, receiving a service, obtaining necessary information or orienting themselves in space, including disabled people, people with a temporary health disorder, pregnant women, older people and people with prams; МГН is the broader category including disabled people [19].
- Accessibility is created by removing barriers, with two approaches: "universal design" — the design of objects, environments, programmes and services making them as usable as possible for all people, which does not exclude assistive devices where needed [20]; and "reasonable accommodation" — necessary and suitable modifications and adjustments, not becoming a disproportionate or unjustified burden, best suited to existing objects and services [21].
- Barriers are physical (in the external environment, above all on social infrastructure objects) and informational (arising under the influence of the form and content of information) [22].

## Accessibility of education

The content of professional education and the conditions of organising the study of students with health limitations are determined by an adapted educational programme, and for disabled people also in accordance with the individual rehabilitation programme [23]. The education of students with health limitations is carried out on the basis of programmes adapted where necessary for them [24], and may be organised both together with other students and in separate groups or organisations [25]. Students with health limitations receive special textbooks, study guides and other educational literature free of charge, as well as the services of sign-language and typhlo-sign-language interpreters [26].

Training under educational programmes of disabled people is carried out taking into account the peculiarities of their psychophysical development, individual possibilities and state of health [27]. For the accessibility of education the following is ensured [28]:

- for people with visual impairments: an alternative version of the official website for the partially sighted; information about the class schedule placed in accessible places in adapted form, in large relief-contrast type (on a white or yellow background) and duplicated in Braille; the presence of an assistant; alternative formats of printed materials (large print or audio files); access to the building for a blind student using a guide dog [28]
- for people with hearing impairments: duplication of sound information about the class schedule visually (monitors able to broadcast subtitles, their size and number determined by the size of the room); provision of proper sound means of reproducing information [28]
- for people with disorders of the musculoskeletal system: material and technical conditions ensuring unimpeded access to study rooms, canteens, toilets and other rooms and stay in them (ramps, handrails, widened doorways, lifts, local lowering of barrier posts, special chairs and other devices) [28]

Distance learning technologies play an important role: organisations may implement programmes or their parts with e-learning and distance technologies in classes, practices, ongoing assessment, interim, final and (or) state final attestation [29]. The methodological recommendations approved by order No. АК-44/05вн of 08.04.2014 are addressed to higher education organisations and are not obligatory, but may be used by other organisations delivering professional education programmes [30].

## Liability and the parking rule

Failure to comply with the legislation on ensuring an accessible environment entails administrative liability by way of a fine: for evasion of the requirements of accessibility of social, transport and engineering infrastructure objects — from 2 to 3 thousand rubles for officials and from 20 to 30 thousand rubles for legal entities [31]. Federal bodies and organisations providing services to the population must, within their powers, instruct or train specialists working with disabled people on ensuring accessibility of objects and services [32]. At least 10 per cent of parking places (but not less than one place) are allocated for parking special vehicles of disabled people, and disabled people use them free of charge [33].

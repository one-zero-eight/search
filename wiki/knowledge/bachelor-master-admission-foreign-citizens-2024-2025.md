# Admission of foreign citizens and stateless persons to bachelor's and master's programmes 2024/2025

## On what ground a foreign citizen may study

- Foreign citizens and stateless persons may receive higher education at the expense of the federal budget under international treaties of the Russian Federation, federal laws or the quota for the education of foreign citizens and stateless persons set by the Government of the Russian Federation, and at the expense of physical and legal persons under contracts on paid educational services (clause 117) [1].
- Admission within the Government quota for the education of foreign citizens is conducted according to the directions of the federal executive body in charge of higher education, and enrolment within that quota is formalised by a separate order of the University [1].

## Documents

- Foreign citizens applying on the basis of international treaties present, in addition to the general documents, documents confirming that they belong to the persons named in the treaties concerned (clause 118) [1].
- Compatriots living abroad present, in addition to the general documents, originals or copies of the documents named in article 17 of Federal Law No. 99-ФЗ of 24 May 1999 on the state policy towards compatriots abroad; the special rights in admission to bachelor's programmes granted by Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ do not extend to compatriots unless an international treaty provides otherwise (clause 119) [1].
- A foreign citizen or stateless person presents an identity and citizenship document, or a document proving the identity of a foreign citizen or a stateless person in the Russian Federation under article 10 of Federal Law No. 115-ФЗ of 25 July 2002 (clause 121) [1].
- A document of a foreign state on education is presented with a certificate of recognition of the foreign education where recognition is required, and documents in a foreign language are presented with a translation into Russian, while documents obtained abroad must be legalised unless an international treaty or Russian legislation provides otherwise [1].

## Entrance examinations

- Foreign citizens may take the general education entrance examinations held by the University itself regardless of whether they took the ЕГЭ, and may use ЕГЭ results alongside those examinations (clause 14) [1].
- On bachelor's paid places the University sets its own list of entrance examinations for foreign citizens in section 4 of annex 1 to the rules; foreign citizens and stateless persons may choose to apply either on the results of the examinations set for foreign citizens or on the results of the examinations set by clauses 12–15 of the rules (clause 120) [1].
- Under section 4 of annex 1, foreign citizens applying to bachelor's paid places take mathematics, informatics and ICT or physics, and the Russian language, as internal examinations, with a minimum of 60 and a maximum of 100 points for each [1].
- Under section 5 of annex 1 and section 2 of annex 2, foreign citizens applying to master's paid places take one entrance examination for the programme concerned in the form of computer testing, with a minimum of 40 and a maximum of 100 points [1].
- Entrance examinations are held in Russian or English; on master's programmes with a foreign language of instruction the University establishes that the examination is held in English, and where several languages are offered the applicant chooses one [1].

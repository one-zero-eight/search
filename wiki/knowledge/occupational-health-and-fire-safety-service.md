# Occupational Health and Fire Safety Service (Служба охраны труда и пожарной безопасности) — place, tasks, functions and rights

## The order and the Service's place

The Regulation on the Occupational Health and Fire Safety Service of ANO VO «Innopolis University» (Положение о Службе охраны труда и пожарной безопасности, СОТиПБ) was approved by the University's order No. ОД/АД-01/2024/11/07 of 7 November 2024 [1]. The order was issued in order to raise the efficiency of the activity of the University's structural subdivision and approved the Regulation attached to it [1]. It repealed the Regulation on the Service approved by order No. ОД/АД-02/2021/11/01 of 1 November 2021 [1]. The head of the Service, A.A. Gorokhov, was ordered to ensure timely familiarisation, against signature, with the approved Regulation of the subdivision's employees and of employees newly hired and transferred (moved) into the subdivision from other subdivisions of the University [1]; control over the execution of the order and of the Regulation was retained by the Director for Development and HR Policy R.F. Valiev, who signed it [1].

The Regulation regulates the Service's activity and determines its tasks, functions, rights and duties [2].

The Service is a structural subdivision of the University's Development Department (Управление развития) and belongs to the category «иные структурные подразделения» [3]. It is directly subordinate to the Director for Development and HR Policy [4]. In its activity it is guided by the Constitution of the Russian Federation, the Labour Code of the Russian Federation, Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29 December 2012 «Об образовании в Российской Федерации» and other legislative and normative legal acts on higher education institutions, the University's Charter, decisions of the Academic, Supervisory and Operational councils, orders and instructions of the director, other local normative acts, normative legal acts on occupational, fire, industrial and ecological safety and sanitary-hygienic legislation, and by this Regulation [5].

## Structure and staff

- The structure, number of staff and staffing table of the Service, and changes to them, are approved by the director of the University [1].
- The Service is headed by the head of the occupational health and fire safety service, who is hired and dismissed by order of the director of the University [1].
- The working conditions of the Service's employees are determined by the employment contracts concluded with each employee and by the University's internal labour regulations [1].
- The rights, duties and degree of responsibility of the Service's employees are established by job descriptions [6].
- The Service's employees may, within their competences, be involved in work on grants, projects, subsidies and other programmes implemented by the University [7].
- The Regulation and additions and changes to it are approved by the director of the University or by another person authorised by him [8].

## Tasks

- organising control and preventive work on occupational, fire, industrial and ecological safety and civil defence, aimed at preventing industrial injuries and accidents at the University's production facilities and minimising their consequences, and measures aimed at complying with sanitary-hygienic, radiation and ecological legislation of the Russian Federation [9]
- organising work so that the University's employees comply with occupational, fire, industrial and ecological safety requirements [10]
- controlling compliance by the University's employees with those requirements [11]
- informing and advising the University's employees on occupational, fire, industrial and ecological safety, civil defence and sanitary-hygienic legislation [12]
- studying and spreading good practice on occupational safety and promoting occupational health and fire safety [13]

## Functions

- organising control and preventive work on occupational, fire, industrial and ecological safety, civil defence, radiation control and sanitary-hygienic requirements [14]
- recording and analysing the state and causes of industrial injuries [15]
- organising and coordinating the special assessment of working conditions [16]
- agreeing documentation developed at the University as regards occupational, fire, industrial and ecological safety, civil defence and sanitary-hygienic requirements, where necessary [17]
- ensuring compliance with state fire supervision requirements concerning its activity and with the fire safety norms in force [18]
- drawing up lists of professions and positions whose holders must undergo compulsory preliminary and periodic medical examinations [19]
- organising the investigation of accidents at work, taking part in the work of the accident investigation commission, and drawing up and keeping occupational safety documents (form N-1 acts and other accident investigation documents) within the established periods [20]
- organising the investigation of fires and conflagrations at the University [21]
- preparing organisational and administrative documents on ensuring the fire-prevention regime on the University's territory [22]
- compiling reports on occupational safety and working conditions in the established forms [23]
- organising, together with the heads of the University's subdivisions, the development of occupational safety training programmes and conducting the induction briefing on occupational safety, fire safety and civil defence with all persons joining work (including temporary and posted workers) and with pupils and students arriving for industrial training or practice [24]
- organising timely training on occupational and fire safety and taking part in the work of commissions checking knowledge of occupational safety requirements [25]
- conducting, together with representatives of the subdivisions, checks and inspections of the condition of buildings, structures, equipment, machines and mechanisms, devices, collective and individual protection means, sanitary-technical devices and ventilation systems for compliance with safety and occupational safety requirements [26]
- drawing up, together with the heads of the subdivisions, lists of professions and types of work for which occupational safety instructions must be developed [27]
- giving methodological help to the heads of the University's subdivisions in developing and revising occupational safety instructions [28]
- providing the subdivisions with local normative acts (rules, norms, occupational safety instructions), visual aids and training materials on occupational health and fire safety [29]
- informing the University's employees about the current requirements of occupational, fire, industrial and ecological safety, civil defence and sanitary-hygienic requirements [30]
- considering letters, applications and complaints of the University's employees on questions of working conditions and occupational safety and preparing proposals to the University's management on remedying the deficiencies found [31]
- conducting contractual work in the Service's field of activity under the procedure established at the University [32]
- organising fire safety at the University [33]
- controlling the working condition of fire-extinguishing and fire-alarm systems, compliance by employees with occupational, fire, industrial and ecological safety, civil defence and sanitary-hygienic requirements, the provision and correct use of individual and collective protection means, the investigation and recording of accidents at work, the fulfilment of measures under improvement programmes and plans and of state supervision instructions, the availability and timely revision of occupational safety instructions, the special assessment of working conditions, timely testing and technical examination of equipment, the efficiency of aspiration and ventilation systems, the condition of safety and protective devices, timely training and knowledge checks and all types of briefings, the storage and issue of special clothing, footwear and other protection means, the sanitary-hygienic condition of production and auxiliary premises, and the organisation of workplaces in accordance with occupational safety requirements [34]
- organising the preparation and transfer to control and supervision bodies of the declaration on the negative impact on the environment [35]
- organising compliance with industrial and ecological safety at the University's production facilities [36]
- organising compliance with radiation safety at the University [37]

## Rights

In carrying out their activity the Service's employees have the right to:

- request from the University's structural subdivisions the information and materials needed for the work within the Service's competence [38]
- with the consent of the heads of the structural subdivisions, involve the employees of those subdivisions in preparing draft local acts and other documents and in developing and carrying out measures conducted by the Service [39]
- take part in meetings held by the University's management when questions within the Service's competence are discussed [40]
- put before the University's management proposals to cancel, amend or supplement the University's local acts [41]
- interact with bodies of state authority and administration, legal entities and the University's subdivisions on questions within the Service's competence [42]
- plan and request the amount of resources needed to perform the tasks set [43]
- at any time of day freely enter and inspect office and household premises and the University's production facilities, and, within its competence, examine documents on questions of the Service [44]
- issue to the heads of the University's subdivisions and other officials binding acts (instructions) on removing violations of occupational, fire, industrial and ecological safety requirements and of sanitary-hygienic legislation found in checks, and control their fulfilment [45]
- require the heads of the structural subdivisions to suspend from work persons who have no admission to perform the work, have not undergone medical examinations, occupational safety briefings or knowledge checks on occupational and fire safety in the established procedure, do not use individual protection means at work, or violate occupational, fire, industrial and ecological safety requirements and sanitary-hygienic legislation [46]
- request and receive from the heads and specialists of the subdivisions the necessary information, data and documents, and require written explanations from employees who have violated occupational, fire, industrial and ecological safety requirements or sanitary-hygienic legislation [47]

## Interaction and reporting

- Interaction with the University's other structural subdivisions is determined by the tasks and functions assigned to the Service by the Regulation [48].
- Interaction with the other structural subdivisions takes place on obtaining information about compliance with occupational, fire, industrial and ecological safety and sanitary-hygienic legislation and data on industrial injuries [49].
- On the basis of local acts, orders and instructions the Service may be responsible, within its activity, for preparing information, certificates and reports and keeping them up to date [50].

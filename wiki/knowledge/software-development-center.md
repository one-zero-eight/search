# Software Development Centre (Центр разработки программного обеспечения) — place, tasks, functions and rights

## The document

The Regulation on the Software Development Centre (Центр разработки программного обеспечения) of ANO VO «Innopolis University» was approved by order of 21 May 2021 № ОД/АД-06/2021/05/21, signed by the Director for Development and HR Policy R.F. Valiev, and dated Innopolis [1]. The wiki copy is a draft made by OCR of a scanned document, so numbers and dates should be checked against the PDF [1].

The Regulation governs the activity of the Centre and determines its tasks, functions, rights and duties [2].

## Place in the structure

- The Centre is a structural subdivision of the department for project and research activity (Управление по проектной и научно-исследовательской деятельности), belongs to the category of research subdivisions (научно-исследовательские подразделения) and comprises three departments: the Department of software development and maintenance (Отдел разработки и сопровождения программного обеспечения), the Department of platform solutions development (Отдел развития платформенных решений) and the Department of IT infrastructure development (Отдел развития ИТ-инфраструктуры) [3].
- The Centre reports directly to the Vice-Rector — head of the department for project and research activity [4].
- In its activity the Centre is guided by the Constitution of the Russian Federation, the Labour Code of the Russian Federation, Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ «Об образовании в Российской Федерации» and other legislative and normative legal acts regulating the activity of higher education institutions, the University's Charter, decisions of the University's Academic, Supervisory and Operational councils, orders and instructions of the University director, other local normative acts and this Regulation [1].
- The structure, headcount and staffing table of the Centre, and changes to them, are approved by the University director [5].
- The Centre is headed by a head of the Centre, hired and dismissed by order of the University director [6].
- The working conditions of the Centre's employees are determined by the employment contracts concluded with each employee and by the University's internal labour regulations [1]; the employees' rights, duties and degree of responsibility are established by job descriptions [7].
- Within their competence, the Centre's employees may be involved in work under grants, projects, subsidies and other programmes implemented by the University [8].
- The Regulation and additions and changes to it are approved by the University director or another person authorised by him [9].

## Tasks

- Creating a scientific and methodological database for fundamental and applied research in accordance with the University's research plans [10].
- Developing a set of scientific and technical solutions in the field of process automation [11].
- Carrying out research and development work (НИР, НИОКР), industrial projects and grants in the Centre's profile by its employees on their own initiative and under grant support, budget and non-budget contracts [12].
- Preparing for publication and publishing annotated, abstracted materials on the latest experience and the own materials and developments of the University's research and teaching staff and students [13].

## Functions

The Centre performs the following functions: preparing and promoting the Centre's solutions, and successful implementation of projects in the field of software development and maintenance [14].

Functions of the Department of software development and maintenance:

- software development [15];
- software testing [15];
- development of electronic document flow [15];
- automation of the solution of clients' tasks [15].

Functions of the Department of platform solutions development:

- developing platforms for further scaling for clients [16].

Functions of the Department of IT infrastructure development:

- raising the fault tolerance of the IT environment for development [17];
- developing and supporting the University's systems [17].

## Rights

In carrying out their activity the Centre's employees have the right to:

- request from the University's structural subdivisions the information and materials needed for the work within the Centre's competence [18];
- involve, with the consent of the heads of the University's structural subdivisions, employees of those subdivisions for preparing drafts of local acts and other documents and for developing and carrying out measures conducted by the Centre [19];
- take part in meetings held by the University's management where matters within the Centre's competence are discussed [20];
- put before the University's management proposals to repeal, amend or supplement the University's local acts [21];
- interact with bodies of state authority and administration, legal entities and the University's subdivisions on matters within the Centre's competence [22].

## Interaction with other subdivisions

- Interaction with the University's other structural subdivisions is determined by the tasks and functions assigned to the Centre by the Regulation [23].
- Interaction with the Financial and Economic Department concerns the application of legislation and of the University's local acts regulating remuneration, the drafting of local acts regulating the University's financial activity, legal examination of situations of bringing the University's employees to material liability, and the preparation and transfer of materials to judicial bodies for recovering receivables from organisations and on tax disputes [24].
- Interaction with the Department of support and quality control of projects concerns reporting under grants and budget and non-budget contracts [24]. The OCR text numbers this paragraph and the previous one both as 5.2 [1].
- Interaction with the other structural subdivisions takes place when preparing drafts of local acts, orders, instructions, contracts and other documents and when preparing opinions and written and oral references on matters within the Centre's competence [25].
- The subdivision may be a base for the practical training of the University's students [26].

## Reporting

On the basis of the relevant local acts, orders and instructions the Centre may, within its activity, be responsible for preparing and keeping up to date information, references and reports [27].

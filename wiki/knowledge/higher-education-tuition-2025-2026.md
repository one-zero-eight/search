# Higher education tuition cost for 2025/2026 (first and later courses)

## What the order sets

The order № ОД/АД‐02/2025/05/30 of 30 May 2025 (г. Иннополис) «Об утверждении стоимости обучения по образовательным программам АНО ВО «Университет Иннополис» на 2025/2026 учебный год» approves the tuition cost for the University's higher education programmes for 2025/2026 [1]. It was issued in accordance with Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29 December 2012 «Об образовании в Российской Федерации», the Rules for the provision of paid educational services approved by Government Resolution No. 706 of 15.08.2013, on the basis of a decision of the Operational Council of the University of 30.04.2025 № П/1/2025, and taking into account the features of implementing educational programmes by types of training and forms of study [1].

Point 1 sets the cost of study on the first course of bachelor programmes, master programmes and programmes for the training of scientific and scientific-pedagogical personnel in aspirantura implemented in 2025/2026, for citizens of the Russian Federation, foreign citizens and stateless persons studying at places under contracts for the provision of paid educational services, according to Annex 1 [2]. Point 2 sets the cost on the second and subsequent courses of the same kinds of programmes for the same categories of students, according to Annex 2 [3]. Control over execution of the order is assigned to the vice-rector — head of the management of academic policy and higher education Bobrov E.S., and the order is signed by Director Bariev I.I. [4].

## Where the two annexes differ

- «Кибербезопасность» appears twice in Annex 1 — under 09.04.01 at 390 000 and under 10.04.01 Информационная безопасность at «800000» — but in Annex 2 only once, under 09.04.01 at 390 000 [1].
- «Информационное технологическое предпринимательство» is listed in Annex 1 under 38.04.05 Бизнес-информатика and in Annex 2 under 09.04.01 Информатика и вычислительная техника, at 800 000 in both [1].
- «Управление на основе данных» and «Управление цифровым продуктом» are listed in Annex 1 under 38.04.05 and in Annex 2 under 09.04.01, at 390 000 in both [1].
- «Технологическое предпринимательство» (09.04.01, course 2, 800 000) appears only in Annex 2 [1].
- «Информатика и вычислительная техника» (09.03.01, course 4, 800 000) appears as a row only in Annex 2, and the aspirantura row for 09.06.01 appears only there as well [1].
- «Робототехника» is listed in Annex 2 twice, under 15.03.06 and under 15.04.06, both course 2 at 800 000 [1].

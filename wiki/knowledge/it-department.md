# IT Department — place, tasks, functions and rights

## The order and the department's place in the structure

The Regulation on the IT Department (Положение о Департаменте информационных технологий) was approved by order of 1 September 2023 № ОД/АД-02/2023/09/01 of ANO VO «Innopolis University» [1]. The order states that it was issued in connection with the creation of a new structural subdivision, for the purpose of optimising the University's activity, and it approves the Regulation attached to it [1]. The Regulation itself regulates the activity of the IT Department and determines its tasks, functions, rights and duties [2].

The IT Department is a structural subdivision and belongs to the category «иные структурные подразделения» (other structural subdivisions) [3]. It is directly subordinate to the Deputy Director for Project Management (заместитель директора по управлению проектами) [4].

In its activity the Department follows the Constitution of the Russian Federation, the Labour Code of the Russian Federation, Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29.12.2012 «Об образовании в Российской Федерации» and other legislative and normative legal acts of the Russian Federation regulating the activity of higher education institutions, the University's Charter, decisions of the Academic, Supervisory and Operational councils of the University, orders and instructions of the University's director, other local normative acts of the University and this Regulation [5].

## Structure and staff

- The structure, the number of staff and the staffing table of the Department, and changes to them, are approved by the director of the University [5].
- The Department is headed by the Director for Information Technologies, who is hired and dismissed by order of the Director for Development and HR Policy of the University [6].
- The working conditions of the Department's employees are determined by the employment contracts concluded with each employee and by the University's internal labour regulations [6].
- The rights, duties and degree of responsibility of the Department's employees are established by job descriptions [7].
- Employees of the Department may, within their competences, be involved in work under grants, projects, subsidies and other programmes implemented by the University [8].
- The Regulation and additions and changes to it are approved by order of the director of the University or of another person authorised by him [9].

Under the order, the Director for Information Technologies (A.M. Ermolchev) must ensure timely familiarisation with the approved Regulation of the employees of the subdivision, and also of employees newly hired and transferred (moved) into the subdivision from other subdivisions of the University [1]. Control over the execution of the order and of the Regulation approved by it is retained by the Director for Development and HR Policy [1].

## Tasks

- ensuring the reliability and continuity of the functioning of the University's information systems and information-telecommunications infrastructure [10]
- providing technical support to the University's employees [11]
- ensuring compliance with information-protection requirements when information systems and the information-telecommunications infrastructure are created and operated [12]
- creating and developing information systems and the information-telecommunications infrastructure [13]
- preparing and presenting to the University's management information and analytical materials on the state and development prospects of software and hardware and of the material and technical condition of the University [14]

## Functions

- developing, coordinating and managing the University's digital transformation strategy [15]
- supporting and developing the University's IT infrastructure, that is the set of information channels and storages, information technologies and other methods and means ensuring information activity [16]
- servicing computers, office equipment, peripheral and communication equipment and repairing them by its own forces or with the involvement of contractor organisations [17]
- automating the University's operational activity in accounting, finance, personnel, project and production records [18]
- managing the access of the University's subdivisions and of individual employees to information systems [19]
- developing methodological materials on the use and operation of information systems [20]
- taking part in organising and conducting procurement of goods, works and services for the creation, development and operation of information systems and of the information-telecommunications infrastructure [21]
- developing technical specifications for the supply of goods, performance of works and rendering of services for creating, developing and ensuring the functioning of information systems and the infrastructure, and for implementing digital transformation projects [22]
- organising acceptance of the results of works on the creation and development of information systems and the infrastructure and of services for their operation [23]
- monitoring and ensuring the proper performance of the University's information systems [24]
- preparing and agreeing the Department's budget [25]
- organising technical support during scientific conferences, forums and olympiads [26]
- studying and introducing Russian and foreign experience of applying information technologies in the educational process of higher education institutions [27]
- drafting local normative acts regulating the procedure for processing and protecting information in the University [28]
- raising the awareness of the University's employees in the field of information technologies and information security [29]
- analysing the security of the University's information systems, networks and critical resources [30]
- organising record keeping (делопроизводство) in the Department [31]

## Rights

In carrying out their activity the Department's employees have the right to:

- request from the University's structural subdivisions the information and materials needed for the work within the Department's competence [32]
- with the consent of the heads of the University's structural subdivisions, involve the employees of those subdivisions in preparing draft local acts and other documents and in developing and carrying out measures conducted by the Department [33]
- take part in meetings held by the University's management when discussing questions whose solution falls within the Department's competence [34]
- put before the University's management proposals to cancel, amend or supplement the University's local acts [35]
- interact with bodies of state authority and administration, legal entities and the University's subdivisions on questions within the Department's competence [36]
- within their competence, check and coordinate the activity of the University's structural subdivisions on questions of the use of software and hardware and report the results of such checks to the University's management [37]
- submit proposals on improving the forms and methods of work of the Department and of the University as a whole [38]
- submit to the University's management proposals on raising qualifications, on rewarding and on imposing penalties on employees of the Department and of other structural subdivisions in the Department's field of activity [39]

## Interaction with other subdivisions

The Department's interaction with the University's other structural subdivisions is determined by the tasks and functions assigned to it by the Regulation [40]. Concrete counterparts named in the Regulation:

- with the Centre for Staff Development and Corporate Culture — on the selection of new employees [41]
- with the Department for Personnel Records and Movement — on employment of employees, amendment of the terms of employment contracts and dismissal of employees [42]
- with the Financial and Economic Department — on the application of legislation and local acts regulating remuneration, on drafting local acts regulating the University's financial activity, on legal examination of situations of material liability of employees, and on preparing and transferring materials to judicial bodies for recovery of receivables from organisations and on tax disputes [43]
- with the Centre for Software Development — on granting rights to the University's information resources and on refining the software used by the University [44]
- with other structural subdivisions — in preparing draft local acts, orders, instructions, contracts and other documents and in preparing opinions, written and oral references on questions within the Department's competence [45]

The Department may be a base for the practical training of the University's students [46].

## Reporting

On the basis of the relevant local acts, orders and instructions, the Department may, within its activity, be responsible for preparing information, certificates and reports and for keeping them up to date [47].

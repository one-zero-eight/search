# Candidate examinations (PhD aspirantura)

## What the candidate examinations are

The procedure (Порядок проведения кандидатских экзаменов), approved by order of the university of 1 October 2025 № ОД/УД-02/2025/10/01, sets the rules for conducting candidate examinations at Innopolis University for aspirants — persons studying in programmes for training scientific and scientific-pedagogical staff — and for persons attached to the university to take candidate examinations without mastering an aspirantura programme (прикрепленные лица), the requirements to the examination commissions and the timing of the examinations [1].

A candidate examination is a form of evaluating how prepared the applicant for the degree of candidate of sciences is to conduct research in the specific scientific specialty and branch of science for which the dissertation is prepared, and an aspirant's passing of candidate examinations belongs to the assessment of the results of mastering disciplines (modules) carried out within interim assessment [2].

The list of candidate examinations is [3]:

- history and philosophy of science [3];
- a foreign language [3];
- the special discipline corresponding to the topic of the dissertation (специальная дисциплина) [3].

## Certificate of passing

A certificate of passing the candidate examinations (form in annex 1) is issued by the structural unit responsible for training scientific and scientific-pedagogical staff in aspirantura not later than 10 working days from the date of the last candidate examination; an aspirant or attached person may also obtain it after that period, not later than 10 working days from the date of the request [4].

## At the examination

To identify themselves at candidate examinations, aspirants and attached persons present an identity document; the presence of outsiders and the use of means of communication at candidate examinations are prohibited [5]. Peculiarities of conducting candidate examinations with the use of distance educational technologies follow a local normative act of the university [6].

## Timing and schedule

Candidate examinations for aspirants and attached persons are held within the period of the interim assessment of aspirants in accordance with the calendar study schedule [7]. Announcements about planned candidate examinations and about the deadlines for accepting documents from persons attached to the university for taking them are published on the official site of the university not later than 6 months before the start of the examinations [8].

The schedule of candidate examinations (form of the order and of the schedule in annex 2) is drawn up by the structural unit responsible for training scientific and scientific-pedagogical staff in aspirantura, approved by an order of the director of the university or another person authorised by them, and brought to the notice of scientific-pedagogical workers by corporate e-mail and to attached persons by e-mail to the address given in their application for attachment not later than 7 calendar days before the date of the first candidate examination [9].

## Examination commissions

Examination commissions are created to receive candidate examinations; their composition is approved by an order of the director of the university or an authorised person on the presentation of the responsible structural unit every year, not less than 10 working days before the period of interim assessment of aspirants, for the current academic year (form of the order in annex 3) [10].

The commission is formed from scientific-pedagogical workers of the university (including those working part-time) in a number of not more than 5 persons and includes a chairperson, a deputy chairperson and members; scientific-pedagogical workers of other organisations may be included, including participating in the work in an interactive form [11].

A commission is competent to receive a candidate examination only with the following participants [12]:

- special discipline: not less than 3 specialists holding the degree of candidate or doctor of sciences in the scientific specialty corresponding to the special discipline, including 1 doctor of sciences [12];
- history and philosophy of science: not less than 3 specialists holding the degree of candidate or doctor of philosophical sciences, including 1 doctor of philosophical, historical, political or sociological sciences [13];
- foreign language: not less than 2 specialists with a higher education in linguistics confirmed by a specialist or master diploma and command of that foreign language, including 1 candidate of philological sciences, and 1 specialist in the problems of the scientific specialty of the dissertation holding the degree of candidate or doctor of sciences and commanding that foreign language [14].

The chairperson is approved from among the members holding the degree of doctor of sciences, including a degree obtained in a foreign state and recognised in the Russian Federation; where the foreign-language commission has no doctor of sciences, a candidate of sciences may be approved as chairperson [15].

If one member of the commission is absent for valid reason on the set dates, the candidate examination may be postponed by not more than 3 working days; if that member cannot attend on the postponed dates, the member is replaced by an order of the director or an authorised person [16]. A secretary is appointed for the period of the examinations from scientific-pedagogical or administrative workers by an order of the director or an authorised person; the secretary is not a member of the commission, and the structure, content and criteria of assessment of candidate examinations are set by the work programmes of the corresponding disciplines (modules) [17].

Candidate examinations in history and philosophy of science and in the special discipline are held orally by tickets, which the responsible unit forms every year on the basis of the work programmes of the disciplines (form of the ticket in annex 4) [18]. The responsible unit gives the commissions copies of the orders approving the commissions and on the attachment of persons for taking candidate examinations, and the forms of the examination protocols, through the secretaries [19].

## Results, retakes and absence

A repeated passing of a candidate examination by an aspirant or attached person within one period of interim assessment is not allowed [20], and a retake from a positive grade to a higher positive grade is not allowed [21].

If an attached person fails to appear at a candidate examination for a valid reason, they may be admitted by personal application (form in annex 5) to take it during the period of interim assessment [22]. An aspirant who fails to appear for a valid reason may likewise be admitted by personal application during that period in accordance with a local normative act of the university [23].

Unsatisfactory results of a candidate examination or non-appearance without valid reason are recognised as an academic debt of the aspirant, and aspirants clear academic debt in accordance with a local normative act of the university [23].

The decisions of the commissions are recorded in a protocol (form in annex 6), a separate protocol being drawn up for each aspirant or attached person [24]. The protocols are transferred to the responsible structural unit within 2 working days from the date of the examination and are kept there [25].

## Appeal

Appeal commissions are created to consider appeals about the procedure of conducting candidate examination(s) and/or disagreement with their result [26].

Not later than 2 working days after an aspirant or attached person files an appeal, the composition of the appeal commission(s) is approved by an order of the director or an authorised person on the presentation of the responsible unit (form of the order in annex 7); the chairperson is the director or another authorised person, the commission consists of the chairperson and not less than 3 members, formed from full-time scientific and scientific-pedagogical workers or administrative staff of the university holding the degree of doctor or candidate of sciences (or a recognised foreign degree) and not sitting on the examination commission, and a session is competent if not less than two thirds of the members take part [27].

Decisions are taken by a simple majority of the members taking part in the session, and where the votes are equal the chairperson has the deciding vote [28]; they are recorded in a protocol (form in annex 8) [29].

An aspirant or attached person has the right to file a written appeal to the examination commission about an alleged violation of the procedure and/or disagreement with the result [30]; the appeal is filed personally with the chairperson of the examination commission not later than the next working day after the result of the candidate examination is announced (form in annex 9) [31]. It is considered at a session of the appeal commission not later than 3 working days from filing [32], for which the secretary of the examination commission passes to the chairperson of the appeal commission the appeal, the protocol of the examination commission and the examination sheets of the aspirant or attached person [33]. The decision is brought to the notice of the person who filed the appeal within 3 working days from the session, and familiarisation is confirmed by that person's signature [34].

On an appeal about a violation of the procedure the appeal commission either rejects the appeal, if the alleged violations are not confirmed and/or did not affect the result, or upholds it, if the violations are confirmed and affected the result; in the latter case the result is annulled, the protocol on the appeal is passed to the chairperson of the examination commission not later than the next working day, and the person may take the candidate examination again in the presence of one member of the appeal commission not later than 2 working days after the decision [35]. On an appeal about disagreement with the result the commission either rejects it and keeps the result or upholds it and sets another result, the decision being grounds for annulling the earlier result and issuing a new one [36]. The decision of the appeal commission is final and is not subject to review [37], and an appeal against a repeat candidate examination is not accepted [38].

## Aspirants and attached persons with disabilities

For aspirants and attached persons from among persons with disabilities (инвалиды), candidate examinations are conducted with regard to the peculiarities of their psychophysical development, their individual possibilities and state of health [39]. The following requirements are ensured [40]:

- the examinations may be held in one room together with aspirants and attached persons who are not disabled, if this does not create difficulties for them [40];
- an assistant is present in the room to give the necessary technical help (to take a workplace, to move about, to communicate with the members of the examination commissions) [40];
- the necessary technical means are used with regard to individual peculiarities [40];
- unhindered access to the rooms, toilets and other premises is ensured (ramps, handrails, widened doorways, lifts; where there are no lifts the room must be on the first floor; special chairs and other devices) [40].

Local normative acts of the university on candidate examinations are brought to the notice of such aspirants and attached persons in a form accessible to them [41].

Aspirants with disabilities file a written application on the need for special conditions, indicating their individual peculiarities, not later than 3 months before the start of the candidate examinations; attached persons file it when submitting documents for attachment, and documents confirming the individual peculiarities are attached if the university does not already have them; the application states whether an assistant is needed at the examinations [42].

Depending on the individual peculiarities the university ensures [43]:

- for the blind: if necessary, a set of writing tools and paper for writing in relief-dot Braille script and a computer with specialised software for the blind [43];
- for the visually impaired: individual even lighting of not less than 300 lux, and if necessary a magnifying device, devices of the aspirant or attached person themselves being allowed [43];
- for the deaf and hard of hearing and those with severe speech disorders: collective sound-amplifying equipment, and if necessary individually used sound-amplifying equipment [43].

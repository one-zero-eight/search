# PhD targeted admission (целевая квота) 2026/2027 — offers, detailed quotas and contracts

## Where this comes from

Section IX of the Rules of admission to aspirantura programmes of ANO VO «Innopolis University» for the 2026/2027 academic year, approved by order No. ОД/УД-01/2026/01/19 of 19 January 2026 [1]. The page is a scanned document read by OCR, and the wiki warns that its numbers and dates should be checked against the PDF before relying on them [1].

## On what basis the target quota is filled

Admission to places within the target quota is carried out in accordance with the regulation on targeted training and the standard form of the contract on targeted training approved by Decree of the Government of the Russian Federation No. 555 of 27 April 2024 «On targeted training under educational programmes of secondary vocational and higher education» [1]. The University carries out admission to these places on the basis of applications for the conclusion of a contract on targeted training submitted by applicants in accordance with offers of conclusion of such contracts formed by federal state bodies, state authorities of the subjects of the Russian Federation, local self-government bodies and legal entities named in part 1 of article 71.1 of Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ (offers, customers) and placed on the digital platform «Работа в России» or submitted by the customers to the University where the offers are not placed on that platform [1]. The University assigns unique identification numbers to the customers that submitted offers to it where the offers are not placed on the digital platform [1].

## The application of a target applicant

- The applicant submits an application for admission in accordance with an offer [1].
- In the application the applicant indicates, where the offer is placed on «Работа в России», the sign that the offer is placed there and the number of the offer formed on the platform; where the offer is not placed there, the sign that it is not placed and the number of the offer formed by the customer [1].
- If the applicant submits a new application, the previous one is considered withdrawn [1].
- The University checks that the application corresponds to the offer placed on the platform or submitted by the customer; if the offer named in the application is absent from the platform and was not submitted to the University, and (or) the competition group named in the application does not correspond to the offer named in it, the University refuses the applicant admission to places within the target quota [1].

## Quotas, competition and places

In the lists of persons who submitted applications and in the competitive lists for places within the target quota the University indicates the sign of placement (non-placement) of the offer on the platform, the number of the offer and the identification number of the customer where the offer is not placed on the platform [1].

The University allocates the target quota by scientific specialty in accordance with the quota of admission to targeted training established under the procedure approved by the Government of the Russian Federation and by the state authority of the subject of the Federation or the local self-government body, and the quota is either undetailed or detailed into several quotas in the interests of particular customers (detailed target quotas) [1]. Where the quota is detailed in the interests of particular customers, the University holds a separate competition for each detailed target quota and uses the places for which no customers are indicated (if such places exist) for a competition in the interests of customers of targeted training not indicated under other detailed target quotas; an applicant participates in the competition for one detailed target quota for the given scientific specialty [1]. Unfilled (vacated) places of detailed target quotas are added to the main budget places [1].

An applicant who took part in career-guidance events indicates in the application that he is a participant of career-guidance events, and the University includes points for targeted individual achievements in the sum of competitive points if the applicant is present in the list of participants of career-guidance events submitted by the customer to the University before the day of the start of acceptance of applications, with the individual insurance account number and signed by an authorised official on paper or in electronic form [1].

The University enrolls applicants to places within the target quota in a number not exceeding the number of contracts indicated by the customers in the offers, for each offer [1].

## Admission in the interests of state security

For admission in the interests of state security, admission is carried out if the University has information about the applicants received from the customers that formed the offers in the interests of state security; information about the applicants is not indicated in the information placed on the official site and (or) published in other ways on the EPGU, including in the lists of persons who submitted applications, the information on the results of entrance examinations, the competitive lists and the information on enrollment; and enrollment is formalised by a separate order (orders) [1].

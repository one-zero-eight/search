# Education contract for additional professional programmes (ДПО) — the form of the договор присоединения with a legal person

## What the document is

The page carries the University's standard form of a contract on education under additional professional educational programmes — a contract with a legal person which presupposes the accession of the students, that is a contract of accession [1]. The University is «Исполнитель»: it acts on the basis of a licence for educational activity whose registration number the page gives as Л035-00115-26/00096117 of 25 December 2015, issued by the Federal Service for Supervision in Education and Science without a term [1]. The other side is a legal person, «Заказчик», and the students named in annex 1 who have joined the contract are «Обучающиеся», all together «Стороны» [1].

The form is a template: its footnotes and subline text are informational, are to be deleted once the form is filled, and the гриф of the annex to the order is also to be removed [2]. The wiki copy is an OCR reading of a scan, so numbers and dates should be checked against the PDF [1].

## The subject and the term

- The University provides an educational service under an additional professional programme of a kind to be filled in (повышение квалификации or professional retraining) in a form of study to be filled in (full-time, part-time or part-time evening), or with e-learning and distance technologies, in accordance with its curricula, to the students whose number and list are determined by annex 1, and the customer undertakes to pay [3].
- The term of mastering the programme (duration of study) at the moment of signing is given in academic hours and with the dates of the beginning and the end [2].
- The services are provided at an address to be filled in [4]; the University's legal address in the form's requisites is 420500, г. Иннополис, ул. Университетская, д. 1 [1].

## The document on completion

- After mastering the programme and passing the final attestation the students receive a document on qualification of the sample established by the University; the notes on the form name the удостоверение о повышении квалификации for a повышение квалификации programme and the диплом о профессиональной переподготовке for a retraining programme [5].
- A student who did not pass the final attestation or received unsatisfactory results there, and a student who mastered part of the programme and (or) was expelled, receive a certificate of study or of the period of study of the sample established by the University [6].

## How a student joins the contract

- The contract is a contract of accession: each student named in annex 1 joins it by sending the University a consent to accession in the form of annex 2, and each student must join it [7].
- The consent may be sent on paper or handed to the University's authorised representative, or sent by e-mail or fax as a scanned copy signed by hand, from the student's address given in annex 1, in the procedure of section 9 [8].
- Sending the consent is, under art. 438 of the Civil Code, full and unconditional acceptance of the contract; it is equivalent to filing an application for enrolment and confirms the conclusion of the contract on its terms [8].
- The customer must acquaint the students with the contract, inform them in time that they must join and ensure that they join; a student who does not join within the term of п. 1.6 is not provided with the services, and the University notifies the customer by e-mail not later than 1 working day before the services begin [9].

## Rights

- The University may run the educational process independently and establish the systems of marks and the forms, procedure and frequency of the interim attestation [10], and apply measures of encouragement and disciplinary measures under Russian legislation, its constituent documents, the contract and its local acts [1].
- Students are given the academic rights of part 1 of article 34 of the Federal Law of 29 December 2012 No. 273-ФЗ [11]; they may use the University's property needed to master the programme, take part in the social, cultural, health-improving and other events organised by the University, and receive full and reliable information about the assessment of their knowledge, skills and competences and its criteria [1].
- The customer may receive information on the provision of the services, address the University on the educational process, and receive full and reliable information about the assessment of the students' knowledge and its criteria [1].

## Duties

- The University must enrol as listeners the students who have fulfilled the admission conditions [12]; bring to the customer and the students the information on the paid educational services in the scope set by the law of 7 February 1992 No. 2300-1 «О защите прав потребителей» and the Federal Law of 29 December 2012 No. 273-ФЗ and acquaint them with its charter, accreditation certificate, licence, programmes, internal rules for students and the documents regulating educational activity [13]; organise and ensure proper training [14]; ensure the conditions for mastering the programme [15]; keep the students' place where they miss classes for valid reasons, account being taken of the payment [16]; accept the payment [17]; and ensure respect for human dignity, protection from violence and insult, and protection of life and health [18].
- The customer must pay in time in the amount and procedure of the contract and provide the payment documents within 3 calendar days from the date of payment, and guarantees that the University receives the students' consents to the processing of their personal data [19].
- Students must comply with the requirements of article 43 of the Federal Law of 29 December 2012 No. 273-ФЗ [20]. In particular they must do the tasks for the classes of the curriculum [21], inform the University of the reasons for absence [22], study according to the curriculum [23], comply with the constituent documents, the internal rules, the Rules of accommodation in the residential complex and other local acts [24], and provide the necessary documents in time, reporting changes in their data within a week [25].

## Cost, act and settlements

- The total cost for the whole period of study is filled in in roubles, VAT not being charged on the basis of пп. 14 п. 2 ст. 149 of the Tax Code; the cost for one student taking account of the grounds and procedure for its reduction is given separately [26]. An increase of the cost after conclusion is not allowed, except by the level of inflation envisaged by the main characteristics of the federal budget for the next financial year and the planning period [26].
- The services are paid by transferring money to the University's settlement account; the form notes that the payment period and time are to be indicated — at once or in another payment period, a payment schedule table being allowed [27].
- Delivery and acceptance are formalised by an act of services in two copies, in the form of annex 3 [1]: the University gives the originals not later than 5 calendar days after the services end [28], the customer considers the act within 3 working days and, where there are no defects, signs it and returns one copy [29], and where defects are found it gives a motivated refusal and the University removes them and sends a new act [30].
- The parties reconcile their settlements quarterly: the University sends an act of reconciliation not later than the 5th day of the month following the reporting quarter, and the customer returns the signed act or a motivated refusal within the term set by the clause [31].

## Amendment and termination

- The terms may be changed by agreement of the parties or under Russian legislation and the contract [32], and the contract may be terminated by agreement [27].
- The University may terminate the contract wholly or partly unilaterally where a breach of the admission procedure through the students' fault led to an unlawful enrolment, where the payment is delayed, where proper performance is impossible through the students' actions or inaction, and in other cases provided by Russian legislation [33].
- The contract is terminated early in respect of one student on the customer's or that student's initiative, including on transfer to another organisation, and on circumstances independent of the will of the parties, including the liquidation of the University [34].
- The University may refuse to perform, provided it fully compensates the customer's losses [35], and the customer may refuse, provided it pays the University's actual expenses [36].

## Liability and penalties

- The parties are liable for non-performance or improper performance under Russian legislation and the contract [37].
- On discovering a defect of the service, including provision not in the full volume of the programme, the customer may at its choice demand free provision, a commensurate reduction of the cost, or reimbursement of the expenses of removing the defects [38]; it may refuse the contract and demand full compensation of losses if the University has not removed the defects in the term set for that, or if a substantial defect or other substantial deviation from the contract is found [39].
- If the University breaches the terms of providing the service, or it becomes obvious that it will not be provided in time, the customer may set a new term, entrust the service to third persons at a reasonable price and demand reimbursement, demand a reduction of the cost, or terminate the contract [40], and may demand full compensation of the losses caused by the breach of the terms and by defects [41].
- For delay in the beginning or the end of the services the customer may demand penalties of 0,02 % of the price for each day of delay [42], and for delay in payment the University may demand penalties of 0,02 % of the amount of the debt for each day of delay [43].

## Force majeure and disputes

- The parties are released from performance wholly or partly where the non-performance follows from force majeure events that arose after the contract and could not be foreseen or prevented by reasonable measures — an earthquake, flood, fire, disability, strikes or acts of state bodies making performance impossible; outbreaks of viral infection, mass diseases (epidemics, pandemics, epizootics), an unfavourable epidemiological situation and the restrictive measures taken because of them are also force majeure [1].
- A party relying on force majeure must inform the others in writing within a term which the page gives as 20 working days, with a confirming document, and after the events end notify without delay; losses caused by failure to notify or by late notification are compensated by the party that caused them [1].
- The facts must be confirmed by certificates of the Chamber of Commerce and Industry of Russia, documents of the Ministry of Internal Affairs, the Ministry of Emergency Situations, the meteorological (seismological) service and other competent bodies, while for the epidemiological facts a decision of a state authority, including acts of the Government of Russia or of Rospotrebnadzor, is sufficient [1].
- Disputes are resolved by negotiation or by a claim to the other party, the claim procedure being mandatory, with the term for considering a claim given as 15 working days from its receipt [1].
- All disputes are resolved by arbitration administered by the Arbitration Centre at the autonomous non-profit organisation «National Institute for the Development of Arbitration in the Fuel and Energy Complex» (ANO NIRA TEK); arbitrators are chosen only from its recommended list, and the award is final [44].

## Term, correspondence and annexes

- The contract enters into force from signing and runs through the year to be filled in, and in the part of settlements, services and liability for delay until the parties fully perform; the parties agreed to extend it to relations that arose from the year to be filled in [45]. The period of study runs from the student's enrolment by an order of the University to the expulsion [46].
- The parties keep in constant contact by correspondence and e-mail, the addresses of the University, the customer and the student being filled in [47], [48]; the information in the contract corresponds to the University's official site at the date of conclusion [49].
- The contract is made in two copies, one for each party, and changes must be in writing and signed by the authorised representatives [50].
- The contract and its annexes may be concluded by exchanging scanned copies by post, fax or e-mail [51]; such copies have the force of the original until the originals are exchanged, and the conditions of the electronic exchange do not extend to the act of services [52]; the second party must send back a signed scanned copy within 10 working days, failing which the document is considered not accepted [53].
- The contract contains an anti-corruption clause: the parties, their workers and affiliated persons do not commit corruption offences, including giving or receiving a bribe, mediation in bribery, abuse of office or commercial bribery, and do not pay or offer money or valuables to influence decisions [54].
- Annex 1 (the list of students), annex 2 (the consent to accession) and annex 3 (the form of the act of services) are integral parts of the contract [55].

## Personal data

- Each party is an operator of the personal data processed under the contract, meaning the data of the parties' workers and other representatives who take part in performing it [56].
- Each party keeps the personal data of the other party's representatives confidential, complies with Federal Law No. 152-ФЗ of 27.07.2006 «О персональных данных» and the acts adopted under it, and takes the legal, organisational and technical measures of protection, being liable for them [57].
- A party receiving personal data may involve third persons only where the other party confirms that the subject's consent has been obtained, and must give information about those third persons on request [58].
- The customer's details to be filled in are its name or surname, name and patronymic, telephone, place of location or residence, ИНН, passport data for an individual and bank details; the University's details include the address 420500, Республика Татарстан, г. Иннополис, ул. Университетская, д. 1, ИНН 1655258235, КПП 161501001 and its bank account [1].

## The consent to accession (annex 2)

- The student joins the contract unconditionally under article 428 of the Civil Code and confirms that he is an adult person who has or is obtaining secondary vocational and (or) higher education [1].
- The consent covers the processing of the student's personal data for the purposes of the contract and the education, including the analysis of his interests, surveys, the infrastructure, corporate e-mail accounts and online education systems and attestation with distance control systems, and lists the data to be processed, among them the passport data, education documents, place of work, address, e-mail, telephones, insurance policy number and photographs, video images, voice and sounds during the studies [1].
- It allows any actions needed for those purposes, including transfer to the Ministry of Education and Science of the Republic of Tatarstan, the Ministry of Science and Higher Education of Russia, the Ministry of Education of Russia and «Почта России» solely for the delivery of the document on education, and runs from the conclusion of the contract until it is withdrawn in writing [1].
- The student consents to the publication and use of photographs and video images with his appearance, voice and sounds during the studies under п. 1 ст. 152.1 of the Civil Code [1].
- The printed consent names «ООО "Газпром трансгаз Ухта"» as the customer and is dated 2022 — a remainder of a filled example [1].

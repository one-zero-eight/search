# College admission 2026/2027 — ranked lists, the certificate competition, individual achievements and enrolment

## Ranked lists and submission of originals

Ranked lists of applicants are placed on the University's official website not later than 18 August 2026 [1]. For enrolment the applicant submits the original document of education and, if any, the document confirming the preemptive or first-priority right under part 4 of article 68 of the Federal Law «Об образовании в Российской Федерации», by 21 August 2026 [2]. An applicant who filed through the public services portal confirms consent to enrolment and submits the original document of education through that portal by 21 August 2026, and, if free places remain, by 25 November 2026 [3].

After the deadlines for submitting originals an order is issued on the enrolment of persons recommended by the admission committee from among those who submitted the originals and, where the application was filed through the portal, those who confirmed consent through it, on the basis of an electronic duplicate of the document of education; a list of names is annexed to the order, and the order with its annex is published on the next working day on the information stand and the website [4]. The enrolment order is issued after the originals of the education document are presented, the contract for paid educational services is concluded and tuition is paid (the receipt is to be provided in person to the University or by e-mail to college@innopolis.ru), but not later than 25 August 2026 [5].

If free places remain after enrolment, enrolment continues until 25 November of the current year [6]. A student enrolled on the basis of an electronic duplicate of the education document or of a preemptive-right document when filing through the portal submits the original documents and 4 (four) photographs within one month from the enrolment order [7].

## Rights of priority and the quota

The conditions of admission guarantee the right to education and the enrolment of the most capable and best prepared applicants with the corresponding level of education [8]. Persons listed in part 5.1 of article 71 of the Federal Law «Об образовании в Российской Федерации» have the right to be enrolled in the first place (в первоочередном порядке) irrespective of the results of mastering the basic general or secondary general education programme shown in their education documents [9]. Persons listed in point 3 of part 5 and points 1–13 of part 7 of the same article have a preemptive right (преимущественное право) of enrolment, other things being equal [10].

The University grants the preemptive right of enrolment to those persons within the quota it sets under Annex 4 — 1 place for each of the two specialities, full-time [11]. For them a separate competitive selection is held within the quota, using the same average mark of the education document calculated by adding all marks and dividing the sum by their number with mathematical rounding to two decimal places, and with the same tie-breaking order as in п. 8.11 [12].

## The competition: average mark and tie-breakers

If the number of applicants exceeds the total number of places, selection is based on the results of mastering the basic general education programme shown in the education documents (a competition of certificates); a competitive rating is compiled and ranked in descending order of the average mark [13]. The average mark of the education document (and (or) of education and qualification) is calculated by adding all marks and dividing the sum by their number, rounded mathematically to two decimal places [14].

If average marks are equal, applicants are enrolled in the order of these criteria [15]:

- having more points for individual achievements — the applicant may present the results of individual achievements on admission and they must be confirmed by documents [15]
- having a higher mark in the profile subjects of the basic general education programme, in the order set in Annex 1 to the Rules [15]
- having a document confirming the right to preemptive or first-priority admission under part 4 of article 68 of the Federal Law «Об образовании в Российской Федерации» [15]

## Individual achievements: what counts

The University takes into account these results of individual achievements [16]:

- winner or prize-winner status in olympiads and other intellectual competitions and events developing intellectual abilities and interest in scientific, research, engineering, technical and inventive activity and promoting scientific knowledge in the profile subjects — including mathematics, informatics, physics, robotics, computer and information technologies, informatics and computer engineering and computer security; the list of counted olympiads is in Annex 2 [16]
- winner or prize-winner status in the championship of professional mastery among disabled people and persons with health limitations «Абилимпикс» in competences matching the profile of the chosen speciality [16]
- winner or prize-winner status in the qualifying stage or the final of the professional mastery championship «Профессионалы» in competences matching the profile of the chosen speciality [16]
- champion or prize-winner status at the Olympic, Paralympic and Surdlympic Games, world or European champion, or first place at a world or European championship in sports included in the programmes of those Games [16]
- world or European champion, or first place at a world or European championship, in sports not included in those Games [16]
- military service by conscription, under contract or by mobilisation in the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation, or presence in volunteer formations under a contract of voluntary assistance with the tasks of the Armed Forces or the national guard during the special military operation in the listed territories [16]
- experience of voluntary (volunteer) activity confirmed in the unified information system for the development of volunteering [16]

From 1 to 10 points may be awarded for each result according to its priority; the criteria for distributing the points and the priority of the achievements are in Annex 3 to the Rules [17].

## Individual achievements: the points

Annex 3 sets the following points [18]:

- olympiads and other intellectual competitions: level 1 — 10 points, level 2 — 9 points, level 3 — 8 points, level 4 — 7 points [18]
- «Абилимпикс» championship — 4 points [18]
- qualifying stage or final of the «Профессионалы» championship — 6 points [18]
- Olympic, Paralympic and Surdlympic Games, and the European championship in sports included in them — 5 points [18]
- world and European championships and first places in them in sports not included in those Games — 2 points [18]
- military service (conscription, contract, mobilisation) and presence in volunteer formations — 3 points [18]
- voluntary (volunteer) activity confirmed in the unified information system — 1 point [18]

Annex 2 lists the counted olympiads and competitions in accordance with the order of the Ministry of Education of Russia No. 639 of 31 August 2025, requiring a document confirming winner or prize-winner status [18]: at level 1 the All-Russian olympiad for schoolchildren in informatics, the All-Russian olympiad in artificial intelligence, the All-Russian olympiad for schoolchildren in mathematics and in physics, the International Mathematical Olympiad, the International Olympiad in Informatics and the International Physics Olympiad; at level 2 the international olympiads «Innopolis Open» in information security, informatics, mathematics, robotics and artificial intelligence; at level 3 the «Большие вызовы» programme and competition and the talent track and profiles of the National Technology Olympiad; at level 4 other olympiads, competitions and events approved by that order and matching the profile of the speciality [18]. The profile subjects to which these results must relate are, in Annex 1, Informatics and Mathematics for 09.02.11 and Physics, Informatics and Mathematics for 15.02.10 [18].

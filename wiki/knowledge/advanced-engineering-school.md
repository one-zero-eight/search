# Advanced Engineering School (Передовая инженерная школа) — place, tasks, functions and rights

## The document and its approval

The Regulation on the Advanced Engineering School (Передовая инженерная школа, ПИШ) of the University was approved by the University's order No. ОД/АД-02/2022/07/20 of 20 July 2022, issued in connection with the creation of a new structural subdivision in order to optimise the University's activity [1]. The order instructs the head of the Advanced Engineering School, I.I. Bariev, to ensure that the unit's employees — and employees newly hired or transferred (moved) into the unit from other University units — are familiarised with the approved Regulation against their signature [1]. Control over the execution of the order and of the Regulation is retained by the signatory, the Director for Development and HR Policy R.F. Valiev [1].

The page is OCR of a scanned document, so its numbers, clause readings and dates should be checked against the PDF before being relied on [1].

## Status and subordination

The Regulation governs the activity of the Advanced Engineering School and determines its tasks, functions, rights and duties [2]. The School is a structural subdivision of the University [3] and reports directly to the director of the University [4].

In its activity the School is guided by the Constitution of the Russian Federation, the Labour Code of the Russian Federation, Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29 December 2012 «Об образовании в Российской Федерации» and other legislative and normative acts on higher education institutions, the University's Устав, decisions of the Academic, Supervisory and Operations councils of the University, orders and instructions of the director, other local normative acts and this Regulation [5].

## Head, structure and staff

The School's structure, number of staff and staffing table (штатное расписание), and changes to them, are approved by the director of the University [6]. The School is headed by its руководитель, who is hired and released from the post by order of the director [7].

Employees' working conditions are determined by the employment contract concluded with each employee and by the University's internal labour rules [8]; their rights, duties and degree of responsibility are established by job descriptions [9]. Within their competence, employees may be engaged in work on grants, projects, subsidies and other programmes implemented by the University [10]. The Regulation and additions or changes to it are approved by the director of the University or a person authorised by the director [11].

## Tasks

The tasks of the School are [12]:

- creating and/or improving the conditions for training highly qualified personnel for the high-productivity sector of the Russian economy [12];
- developing and introducing new higher education programmes for the advanced training of engineering personnel and additional professional programmes in current scientific-technological areas and «end-to-end» digital technologies, supported by interactive complexes for advanced training [13];
- attracting practising engineers to teach in advanced engineering schools [14];
- creating, on the basis of advanced engineering schools, special educational spaces (laboratories, pilot production, interactive complexes for advanced training, digital, «smart», virtual (cyber-physical) factories) equipped with modern high-technology equipment, high-performance computing systems and specialised applied software [15];
- carrying out breakthrough developments and research aimed at tasks of world-level relevance and significance in the priority area(s) of technological development of the Russian Federation [16];
- commercialising intellectual activity results and transferring technologies [17];
- disseminating the best practices of advanced engineering schools for training and retraining engineers [18];
- professional development and/or professional retraining, including internships at high-technology companies, of management teams and teaching staff of advanced engineering schools and of higher education institutions delivering engineering programmes [19];
- organising internships outside the educational process, including mentoring, for the best students of master's programmes of a technological profile delivered on the basis of research and development projects in «end-to-end» digital economy technologies and priority areas of technology, with the support of high-technology companies; such students are selected by the advanced engineering schools annually, and grant funds for these internships are directed to reimbursing the students' related expenses [20];
- organising schoolchildren's participation in the School's activity for early professional orientation [21];
- holding other events provided by the School's development programme [22].

## Functions

The School performs the following functions [23]:

- with high-technology companies, ensuring conditions for a new type of engineering training and for breakthrough developments and research, including the events of the School's development programme [23];
- coordinating the University units involved in implementing the School's development programme [24];
- participating in events aimed at achieving the grant results and the indicators of the federal project [25] and in preparing reports on the development programme in the forms set by the operators of the federal project «Передовые инженерные школы» [26];
- sending the project operators the documents and information needed to control the procedure, purposes and conditions of the grant [27];
- interacting with the School's industrial partners and high-technology companies on cooperation and on implementing the development programme [28];
- delivering the School's higher education and additional professional education programmes in cooperation with the University's units [29];
- organising professional development and retraining, including internships at high-technology companies, for management teams, teaching staff and students of the School and of higher education institutions delivering engineering programmes [30];
- organising internships outside the educational process, including mentoring, for the best master's students of the School [31];
- developing applied software for breakthrough developments and research within the development programme [32];
- carrying out scientific research in the School's main scientific areas [33];
- participating in creating special educational spaces of the School, including equipping them with modern high-technology equipment, high-performance computing systems and specialised applied software [34];
- cooperating with higher education institutions of Russia and other countries in the School's scientific and educational activity [35];
- ensuring interaction with representatives of the executive authorities of the Russian Federation and of the Republic of Tatarstan and other interested parties, preparing answers to official requests and conducting business correspondence [36];
- maintaining a database (register) of documents on the School's activity, on paper and electronically [37];
- organising the work of the School's Expert Council with the participation of state executive authorities of Russia and Tatarstan, the School's industrial partners, development institutes and high-technology companies [38];
- holding hackathons, conferences, business events, foresight sessions, strategy sessions, round tables and other events within the School's activity [39].

## Rights

In their activity the School's employees may [40]:

- request from the University's structural units the information and materials needed for the School's work [40];
- with the consent of the heads of the University's structural units, engage those units' employees to prepare draft local acts and other documents and to develop and carry out the School's events [41];
- take part in meetings held by the University's management when discussing matters within the School's competence [42];
- propose to the University's management the repeal of, changes to and additions to the University's local acts [43];
- interact with state authorities and administration, legal entities and the University's units on matters within the School's competence [44];
- use the University's classroom fund, facilities and equipment [45];
- propose to the director of the University and to the head of the School incentives for individual employees for active work on the development programme indicators and measures against those guilty of not performing job duties or of violating higher education legislation [46].

## Interaction with other units and reporting

Interaction with other structural units is determined by the tasks and functions assigned by the Regulation [47]. The School may be a base for the practical training of the University's students [48]. It interacts with the centre of IT infrastructure on the School's technical equipment [49], with the procurement department on placing orders for goods, works and services for the School's needs [50], with the financial-economic department on the application of legislation and local acts on remuneration, on draft local acts on the University's financial activity, on legal expertise of situations of material liability and on preparing materials for courts [51], and with the Department for Grant and Project Support on reporting under grants, budget and non-budget contracts [52]. Interaction with other units takes place when preparing draft local acts, orders, instructions, contracts and other documents and when preparing conclusions and written or oral reports on the School's competence [53].

On the basis of the relevant local acts, orders and instructions, the School may be responsible for preparing and keeping up to date information, reports and accounts within its activity [54].

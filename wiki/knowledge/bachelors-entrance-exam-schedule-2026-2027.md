# Bachelor's entrance examination schedule 2026/2027 — consultation, examination and reserve dates by examination

## The document

The page is the schedule of entrance examinations for admission to higher education programmes — bachelor's programmes at ANO VO «Innopolis University» for the 2026/2027 academic year [1].

Its table gives the name of the educational programme, the date of the consultation, the date of the entrance examination and the reserve days [1].

The schedule covers admission «на обучение в рамках контрольных цифр» (places within the control figures, that is budget places) and, separately, admission «на места по договорам об оказании платных образовательных услуг» (paid places) [1].

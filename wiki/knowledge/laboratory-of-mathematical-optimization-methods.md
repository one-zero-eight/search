# Laboratory of Mathematical Optimization Methods (Лаборатория математических методов оптимизации) — creation and the order approving its Regulation

## The document

The wiki page reproduces the University's order «Об утверждении Положения о Лаборатории математических методов оптимизации Центра научных исследований Исследовательского центра Института искусственного интеллекта автономной некоммерческой организации высшего образования «Университет Иннополис»», issued in г. Иннополис on 15 September 2025 under No. ОД/АД-01/2025/09/15 [1].

The order was issued in connection with the creation of a new structural subdivision and with the aim of optimising the University's activity [1].

## What the order decides

- The Regulation on the Laboratory of Mathematical Optimization Methods of the Center for Scientific Research of the Research Center of the Institute of Artificial Intelligence of ANO VO «Innopolis University», attached to the order, is approved [2].
- The director of the research center R.F. Kuleev is to ensure timely familiarisation, against signature, with the Regulation approved by this order — both of the employees of the subdivision and of employees newly hired and transferred (moved) into the subdivision from other subdivisions of the University [3].
- Control over the execution of the order, and over the Regulation approved by it, is retained by the signatory [4].
- The order is signed by the Director for Development and HR Policy R.F. Valiev [4].

## What the page does not contain

The page carries the order only; the text of the Regulation itself (its sections on the Laboratory's place in the structure, tasks, functions, rights and interaction with other subdivisions) is not reproduced on it, so nothing about those is stated here [1]. The heading area also contains unexplained garbled marks («МДРЕГ МНРЕГ») around the place name [1].

# Material assistance from the university's own funds

## What the procedure covers

The Порядок предоставления материальной помощи обучающимся за счет собственных средств sets out the mechanism of granting material assistance from the university's own funds to students (обучающиеся) who find themselves in a difficult life situation, within the autonomous non-profit organisation of higher education «Университет Иннополис» [1].

The procedure was approved by an order of ANO VO «Университет Иннополис» signed by K.V. Semenikhin, the number in the scanned text reading -01/2020/12/11 [2]. It is developed in accordance with the university's Charter and other local normative acts of the university [3].

## Who may receive it

To qualify for material assistance, a student must meet both criteria [4]:

- the GPA received for the previous semester must be less than 4.33 for students from Russia and 4.37 for foreign students [5];
- income per one family member must be below the subsistence minimum in the region of residence for students from Russia and below the subsistence minimum in the student's country of residence for foreign students [6];
- the family counted per member includes the parents and minor brothers and sisters of the student [7].

## Documents and how to apply

- The student submits to the Department of support and development of students (отдел поддержки и развития студентов) a certificate of the family composition and a certificate of the parents' income [8].
- Foreign students may submit the certificate in English or in the language of their country; the Department may help with translating the documents submitted, but the student bears the responsibility for the translation [9].
- The documents for material assistance and the application for it may be submitted by the student on any working day, including during holidays [10].

## Forms the assistance may take

The student may receive material assistance in the form of [11]:

- financial aid [11];
- payment by the university of the student's expenses for extending a visa (for foreign students) [12];
- payment by the university of the student's expenses for compulsory medical insurance (for foreign students) [13];
- provision of free accommodation in the university campus [14];
- payment by the university for treatment or medicines [15];
- payment by the university for the student's lunches in the university canteens [16];
- other forms [11].

## Frequency and timing of payment

- Assistance may be received once or monthly throughout the whole semester, until the end of the student's next interim assessment according to the calendar study schedule [17].
- Payment is made on the nearest scholarship payment day if the application was submitted no later than a week before that day, and on the scholarship payment day of the following month if the application was submitted less than a week before that day [18].

## Decision

The final decision on granting material assistance is taken by the director of the university or another person authorised by him, on the basis of a petition of the university's scholarship commission [19]. The decision taken is approved by an order of the director of the university or of the person authorised by him [20].

## Keeping the support and refusal

- To keep material support throughout the whole semester, the student must attend at least 70% of classes; if the student does not meet this requirement, the provision of material assistance stops [21].
- If the student's average grade for the semester in which material assistance was received does not improve, the university refuses that student material support in the next semester [22].

# Use of the University's name, marks and designations

## Why the rules exist

Section 8 of Innopolis University's IP-Policy (approved by an order of the University of 9 August 2022) governs the use of the University's name and of the names of its structural subdivisions, its commercial designations, trademarks and service marks (together — means of individualisation) [1], [2]. Such use has a direct effect on the University's reputation as a leading scientific and educational centre and on its competitive position on the market of intellectual products and services [1]. ## Duties of those who use them

Structural subdivisions, employees and students who use means of individualisation or who give permission for their use under this section must [1]:

- use them in strict accordance with this section, the other local acts governing the use of means of individualisation, the decisions of authorised officials and the terms of contracts granting rights to use them [1];
- use them in accordance with the University's main tasks and directions of activity [1];
- use them reasonably and in good faith, so that a true impression is given of the University's connection with the events, projects, publications, products, works and services concerned [1];
- ensure that the events, projects, publications, products, works and services designated with the means of individualisation meet quality standards allowing the University to remain competitive on the market of intellectual products and services [1];
- refrain from any use of the means of individualisation capable of damaging the University's reputation [1].

## When permission is needed

Employees and students may use the means of individualisation to designate the connection between an event, project, publication, product, work or service and the University only with the prior written consent of the official authorised to take decisions in the field of the University's intellectual property; the request for that permission is sent to the МЦТТ [3].

No special permission is needed [4]:

- when using the University's official document forms in the manner established by its local acts [4];
- when using them in the University's official and periodical editions, on the Bitrix corporate portal (an internet site as a composite work) and on other University internet sites [4];
- to designate events, publications, products, works and services conducted (performed, provided) within the University's projects and/or in accordance with the University's obligations under contracts [4].

Use of the means of individualisation in mass media or in audiovisual works, including when requests come from mass media or producers of audiovisual works, is agreed with the University's public relations department in the manner provided by the University's administrative acts [5].

## Names of structural subdivisions

The name of a structural subdivision may be used to designate the connection between an event, project, publication, product, work or service and that subdivision only with the prior written consent of the head of the subdivision or of another person authorised by the head; in addition, the prior written consent of the official authorised to take decisions in the field of the University's intellectual property is required where the name is used in paid relations with third parties or in the interests of third parties, including in advertising their events, projects, goods, works or services [6]. No special permission is needed for the use of the subdivision's official document forms where the regulation on the subdivision provides that it has such forms, for use in the University's official and periodical editions, on the corporate portal (an internet site as a composite work) and on other University internet sites, and in other cases provided by decisions of the subdivision's head [7].

## Affiliation and references to supported projects

Employees and students may use the name of the University or of its structural subdivision, observing the internal regulations and other local acts establishing rules for indicating the connection with the University (affiliation), to designate the position they hold or the educational organisation where they study — provided that such use does not allow the conclusion that the University or its subdivision is responsible for the events, projects, products, works or services of the employee or student [8]. The same names may also be used when including in publications references to a research project supported by the University, the content of the reference being determined by the University's local acts and by its contracts with the author regulating the project [8].

The persons authorised under this section to decide on the use by employees and students of the name of the University or of its structural subdivisions may give permission for their use in relation to a defined category of cases (publications, editions, projects and the like) [9].

## Copyright notice on published works

When preparing and publishing works whose rights belong to the University, employees and students must include in the work a copyright notice in the form «© АНО ВО «Университет Иннополис», год издания» or «© Innopolis University, year of publication» [10]. The notice is placed on the title page or the second page of the publication, on the cover and the physical media of a computer programme or database and on the visual image appearing on the computer screen when the programme or database is launched or an internet site is accessed, and on the cover, the physical media and in the credits of audiovisual works [10].

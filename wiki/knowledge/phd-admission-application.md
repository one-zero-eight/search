# Application for PhD aspirantura admission (budget places)

## What the form is

The page is the sample application (образец заявления) for admission to study in aspirantura programmes — programmes for training scientific and scientific-pedagogical staff — at the expense of federal budget allocations; it is addressed to the director of ANO VO "Innopolis University", K.V. Semenikhin [1]. The university's address in the form is 420500, Innopolis, Universitetskaya st., 1 [1]. The form is dated "«___» __________________ 2024 г." [1].

## Personal data the applicant gives

The application contains the applicant's surname, name and patronymic (if any), sex, date and place of birth, registered address, citizenship, identity document (type, series, number, by whom issued, department code), insurance number of the individual personal account (SNILS, if any) and the applicant identifier [1].

## Education and programme chosen

- On education: the name of the completed educational institution, the year of graduation, and the document of education and qualification with its series and number [1].
- On the programme: scientific specialty, form of study, basis of study and grounds of admission; in the sample these are filled in as scientific specialty 1.2.2 Mathematical modelling, numerical methods and software complexes, full-time, places within the admission quotas (контрольные цифры приема) at the expense of federal budget allocations [1].

## Entrance exams

The applicant asks to be admitted to the entrance exams conducted by Innopolis University itself, giving the subject, the language, the form and the place of each exam; in the sample these are computer testing on scientific specialty 1.2.2 and an interview on scientific specialty 1.2.2, both with the use of distance technologies at the address of residence, with the language to be chosen (Russian or English) [1].

## Special conditions at entrance exams

An applicant who needs special conditions at entrance exams because of limited health possibilities or disability marks this in the application, lists the entrance exams and gives the details of the document confirming the disability or health limitation that requires the special conditions [1]. The form offers to mark the needed conditions [1]:

- written assignments in relief-dot (Braille) script, or as an electronic document readable with a computer with specialised software for the blind, or read aloud by an assistant [1];
- a set of writing tools and paper for writing in Braille, or a computer with specialised software for the blind, or an assistant for dictation [1];
- individual even lighting of not less than 300 lux [1];
- a magnifying device for taking internal entrance exams [1];
- assignments and instructions on the procedure of the entrance exams in enlarged font [1];
- collective-use sound-amplifying equipment [1];
- individually used sound-amplifying equipment [1];
- the services of a sign language interpreter [1];
- the services of a tactile sign language interpreter [1];
- a computer with specialised software for persons with musculoskeletal disorders, impaired motor functions of the upper limbs or absence of the upper limbs, or dictation to an assistant [1].

## Individual achievements, dormitory and return of originals

- The applicant may mark individual achievements; in the sample the options are scientific publications, in the profile (focus) of the master's programme, in the international database Web of Science over the last 3 years and in the international database Scopus over the last 3 years [1].
- The applicant may state the need for a place to live in a dormitory [1].
- The applicant chooses the way the submitted originals are returned if not enrolled — in person, by Russian Post or by courier service — and gives a postal address; this is not indicated when the documents are submitted in electronic form [1].

## What the applicant confirms and undertakes

The form contains acknowledgements, each confirmed by the applicant's signature [1]:

- acquaintance with the admission rules approved by the university itself, including the rules for filing an appeal on the results of entrance exams conducted by the university itself, with the charter, with the date of issue and registration number of the licence for educational activity, with the date of issue and registration number of state accreditation for the implemented educational programmes, with the educational programmes and other documents governing the organisation and implementation of educational activity and the rights and duties of students [1];
- acquaintance with the dates of completion of admission of the document of the established form [1];
- acquaintance with the requirement to give reliable information in the application and to submit genuine documents [1];
- when applying to places within the control figures, confirmation of the absence of an aspirantura (adjunctura) diploma, of a certificate of completion of aspirantura (adjunctura) or of a candidate of sciences diploma [1];
- an undertaking to provide the certificate of recognition of foreign education and confirmation of legalisation no later than the day of submitting the document of the established form, filled in by applicants who submitted a document of a foreign state on education or on education and qualification when these procedures are needed [1].

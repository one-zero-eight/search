# Integration Department (Отдел интеграции) — place, tasks, functions and rights

## The document

The Regulation on the Integration Department (Отдел интеграции) of ANO VO «Innopolis University» was approved by order No. ОД/АД-06/2024/10/01 of 1 October 2024, issued in connection with the creation of a new structural subdivision in order to optimise the University's activity [1]. The order was signed by the Director for Development and HR Policy R.F. Valiev, who retained control over execution of the order and of the Regulation [1]. The head of the Department, V.A. Bekmansurov, is to ensure timely familiarisation (against signature) with the approved Regulation of the employees of the subdivision and of employees newly hired and transferred (moved) into the subdivision from other subdivisions of the University [1].

The Regulation governs the activity of the Integration Department of the Centre for the Development of Industrial Robotics of the Centre for Automation and Robotics and determines its tasks, functions, rights and duties [2].

## Place in the structure

- The Department is a structural subdivision of the Centre for the Development of Industrial Robotics of the University and belongs to the category of research subdivisions [3].
- The Department reports directly to the director of the Centre for the Development of Industrial Robotics [4].
- In its activity the Department is guided by the Constitution of the Russian Federation, the Labour Code of the Russian Federation, Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29 December 2012 «Об образовании в Российской Федерации», other laws and normative legal acts on the activity of higher education institutions, the University's Charter, decisions of the University's Academic, Supervisory and Operational councils, orders and instructions of the University director, other local acts and this Regulation [5].
- The structure, headcount and staffing table of the Department, and changes to them, are approved by the University director [6].
- The Department is headed by a head of the Department, hired and dismissed by order of the University director [7].
- The working conditions of the Department's employees are determined by the employment contracts concluded with each employee and by the University's internal labour regulations [8]; their rights, duties and degree of responsibility are set by job descriptions [9].
- Within their competence the Department's employees may be involved in work under grants, projects, subsidies and other programmes carried out by the University [10].
- The Regulation and amendments to it are approved by the University director or another person authorised by the director [11].

## Tasks

The tasks of the Department are:

- participation in the commercialisation of the results of the activity of the Department and of the structural subdivisions included in it; commercialisation means activity bringing the results of research, developments and technologies of the Department and of the Centre for the Development of Industrial Robotics into economic circulation by transferring rights to them on a paid basis, carrying out developments on third-party orders or in other ways providing the Department with income [12];
- implementation of commercial projects in the field of robotics [13];
- creating a scientific and methodological database for fundamental and applied research in industrial robotics [14];
- developing a set of scientific and technical solutions in industrial robotics [15];
- carrying out research, development and technological work (НИР, ОКР, НИОКТР), industrial projects and grants in the profile of the Centre for the Development of Industrial Robotics, on its own initiative and under grant support, from budget and non-budget funds [16];
- participating in the implementation of state programmes, federal and national projects in industrial robotics and other related fields [17];
- popularising robotisation and automation of industrial enterprises [18];
- identifying the potential and directions of robotisation and automation of industrial enterprises [19].

## Functions

- Preparing, together with other structural subdivisions of the University, drafts of decisions of the University's governing bodies [20].
- Step-by-step planning and conduct of НИР, ОКР, НИОКТР, projects and grants [21].
- Developing scientific research programmes in the field of activity of the Centre for the Development of Industrial Robotics [22].
- Improving methodologies, technologies, methods and tools of work [23].
- Developing and updating the methodology and conducting technical and technological audits of industrial enterprises in order to identify their robotisation potential [24].

## Rights

In carrying out their activity the Department's employees have the right to:

- request from the University's structural subdivisions the information and materials needed for the work within the Department's competence [25];
- involve, with the consent of the heads of the University's structural subdivisions, employees of those subdivisions for preparing drafts of local acts and other documents and for developing and carrying out activities conducted by the Department [26];
- take part in meetings held by the University's management where matters within the Department's competence are discussed [27];
- propose to the University's management the repeal, amendment or supplementation of the University's local acts [28];
- interact with state authorities and bodies of administration, legal entities and subdivisions of the University on matters within the Department's competence [29].

## Interaction with other subdivisions

- Interaction with the other structural subdivisions of the University is determined by the tasks and functions assigned to the Department by the Regulation [30].
- The Department may be a base for the practical training of the University's students [31].
- Interaction with the Department for Attracting State Financing concerns participation in НИР, ОКР, НИОКТР, industrial projects and grants [32]; with the Department for Support of Grants and Projects it concerns reporting under grants, budget and non-budget contracts [33].
- Interaction with the Cross-industry Centre for Technology Transfer concerns the legal protection of the results of intellectual activity created at the Centre for the Development of Industrial Robotics, the exercise, disposal and protection of the University's rights to such results, their effective use and commercialisation [34].
- With other structural subdivisions, interaction takes place in the course of preparing drafts of local acts, orders, instructions, contracts and other documents and of preparing opinions and written and oral reports on matters within the Department's competence [35].

## Reporting

- On the basis of the relevant local acts, orders and instructions the Department may be responsible, within its activity, for preparing and keeping up to date information, accounts and reports [36].
- The Department is responsible for preparing reports on НИР, ОКР, НИОКТР, industrial projects and grants and for passing them in time to the University's internal structural subdivisions [37].
- The Department reports as required by federal executive authorities, executive authorities of the subjects of the Russian Federation and other bodies, agencies and organisations, in accordance with obligations under grants (subsidies) provided from the budgets of the budget system of the Russian Federation, and in other cases provided by law; in other cases reporting is determined by the contracts concluded with third parties [38].

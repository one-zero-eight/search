# Intersectoral Technology Transfer Center (МЦТТ) — place, tasks, functions and rights

## Document and place in the structure

The Regulation on the Intersectoral Technology Transfer Center (Положение о Межотраслевом центре трансфера технологий) of the autonomous non-commercial organisation of higher education «Innopolis University» regulates the activity of the centre and determines its tasks, functions, rights and duties [1].

The Regulation was approved by order No. ОД/АД-05/2022/01/10 of 10 January 2022 and put into effect on 10 January 2022; from the same date the previous Regulation, approved by order No. ОД/АД-01/2021/11/09 of 09.11.2021, ceased to be in force [2]. The order was signed by the director for development and personnel policy R.F. Valiev and countersigned by the head of the centre M.V. Abdrakhmanova; it obliges the head of the centre to ensure that the subdivision's employees, and employees newly hired or moved into the subdivision from other subdivisions, are familiarised with the Regulation against signature, and leaves control over the order and the Regulation with the signatory [2].

The page is a scan read by OCR and its front matter warns that numbers and dates should be checked against the PDF before relying on them [2].

The centre (МЦТТ) is a structural subdivision of the University's Department for Project and Research Activity (Управление по проектной и научно-исследовательской деятельности) and belongs to the category «иные структурные подразделения» [3]. It reports directly to the vice-rector — head of that department [4].

In its activity the centre follows the Constitution of the Russian Federation, the Labour Code of the Russian Federation, Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29 December 2012 «Об образовании в Российской Федерации», GOST R 57194.1-2016 «Национальный стандарт Российской Федерации. Трансфер технологий. Общие положения», other legislative and normative legal acts of the Russian Federation regulating the activity of higher education institutions, the University's Charter, decisions of the University's Intellectual Property Commission, orders and instructions of the University's director, other local normative acts of the University and this Regulation [5].

The structure, the number of staff and the staffing table of the centre, and changes to them, are approved by the director of the University [6]. The centre is headed by its head, who is hired and dismissed by an order of the director of the University or of another person authorised by him [7]. The working conditions of the centre's employees are determined by the employment contracts concluded with each employee and by the University's internal labour regulations [8]; their rights, duties and degree of responsibility are established by job descriptions [9]. Employees of the centre may, within their competences, be involved in work on grants, projects, subsidies and other projects and programmes implemented by the University [10]. The Regulation and additions and changes to it are approved by an order of the director of the University or of another person authorised by him [11].

The IP-Policy of the University defines the centre as the structural subdivision of the University that carries out the measures aimed at achieving the aims and performing the tasks of the IP-Policy [12].

## Tasks

The main tasks of the centre are [13]:

- elaborating and carrying out a single patent and licensing policy of the University in respect of results of intellectual activity (РИД) [14]
- securing the exclusive rights to results created in the University's educational, scientific, production, international and other activity for the University [15]
- information and analytical support of development work in the performance of research and development [16]
- protecting the University's exclusive rights to intellectual property and protecting the exclusive and personal non-property rights of the authors of intellectual property [17]
- helping to enlarge the portfolio of РИД whose rights belong to the University [18]
- helping the authors of the University's scientific, technical and technological developments to realise their project initiatives and to commercialise innovative developments [19]
- purposefully searching for and selecting ideas, projects and results of scientific, technical and technological developments with high commercial potential [20]
- expert evaluation of the commercial potential of РИД created by the creative work of the authors of the University's developments [21]
- helping to carry out innovative projects based on the University's РИД [22]
- helping to commercialise the University's РИД, including preparing and concluding contracts on the disposal of the exclusive right to РИД [23]
- helping to organise and hold pre-sale events with the University's potential customers in order to conclude research and development contracts and agreements, including legal support in formalising such contracts as regards the distribution of rights to РИД and the use of the University's prior intellectual property [24]
- consulting services to external organisations on registering РИД and on formalising and registering the contracts connected with them with the Federal Service for Intellectual Property of the Russian Federation, in the Unified Register of Russian Programs for Electronic Computing Machines and Databases and in other relevant organisations and foreign patent offices [25]
- assessing the level of technology readiness under its own methodology of assessing the level of technology readiness for the University's scientific, technical and technological developments and those of other scientific organisations and higher education institutions [26]
- coordination work on the certification of the University's products, services, production and/or quality systems [27]
- developing and implementing mechanisms of interaction with Russian and foreign educational organisations and companies that are world leaders in technology transfer and intellectual property management, for scientific and technical cooperation and for solving fundamental problems of intellectual property management, and taking part in developing the bringing of Russian products to the world market [28]
- attracting, to the centre's work, leading Russian specialists in intellectual property, technology transfer and management of innovation activity [29]
- holding events — educational seminars and round tables — on problems of technology transfer and on organising an effective system of intellectual property management and technology transfer in educational and scientific organisations [30]
- developing educational programmes (or separate components of educational programmes) of professional development and professional retraining in intellectual property, including technology transfer and management of intellectual rights [31]
- creating an information database of projects, developments and РИД suitable for commercialisation and supporting innovative developments, and maintaining a database of applications for commercialisation of РИД and technology transfer [32]

The centre may be a base for the practical training of the University's students [33].

## Functions

The centre [34]:

- analyses the results of the University's research and innovation activity in order to form a database of its scientific, technical and technological developments [35]
- initiates marketing research and financial and economic evaluation of the University's priority developments [36]
- organises a technological audit of the University's projects with high commercial potential [37]
- assesses the level of technology readiness of the University's scientific, technical and technological developments [38]
- carries out coordination work on the certification of the University's products, services, production and/or quality systems [39]
- conducts patent research and patent analytics [40]
- develops a strategy of commercialisation and technology transfer for the University's projects on the results of the technological audit, marketing research, financial calculations and degree of protection, and takes part in preparing business plans [41]
- takes part in preparing, monitoring and supporting licence agreements and other forms of commercialisation of the University's intellectual property [42]
- takes part in preparing research and development contracts and agreements as regards fixing the conditions of distribution of rights to РИД and the use of the University's prior intellectual property [43]
- interacts and cooperates with industrial enterprises and small and medium business in order to promote and introduce scientific, technical and technological developments and to commercialise РИД and transfer technology [44]
- organises participation in and support of projects in regional and federal innovation programmes and interaction with funds financing investment, innovation and venture projects and with development institutions as regards commercialisation and technology transfer [45]
- organises and holds thematic and other events with the involvement of industry experts [46]
- advises the employees of the University's subdivisions on intellectual property management, technology transfer and commercialisation of scientific and technical developments [47]
- monitors data on completed developments, technologies and scientific and technical products [48]
- develops educational programmes (or separate components of educational programmes) of professional development and professional retraining in intellectual property, including technology transfer and management of intellectual rights [49]
- develops national and international cooperation in technology transfer, scientific implementation and production activity [50]

## Rights

In carrying out its activity the employees of the centre have the right to [51]:

- request and receive, in the established procedure, from the University's employees and structural subdivisions the documents, materials and information certificates needed for the subdivision's tasks [52]
- interact with the University's other subdivisions [53]
- interact with federal, regional and municipal institutions, industrial enterprises and small and medium business on questions within the subdivision's competences [54]
- initiate and hold meetings with potential buyers of developments and take part in official negotiations on concluding licence agreements and other forms of commercialisation of the University's intellectual property [55]

## Duties

The centre is obliged [56]:

- to be guided in its activity by the University's Charter, orders and instructions of the director and of the vice-rector — head of the Department for Project and Research Activity, this Regulation and the normative legal acts of the Russian Federation [57]
- to perform all the tasks and functions of the centre fixed in the Regulation with quality, on time and in full [58]
- to keep confidential the information contained in the centre's documents [59]

## Interaction and reporting

The centre interacts with all structural subdivisions of the University and receives from them the information needed for its effective functioning [60]. Interaction with other structural subdivisions takes place in the course of formalising documents relating to applications for РИД, preparing drafts of local acts, orders, instructions, contracts and other documents, and preparing conclusions and written and oral certificates on questions within the centre's competence [61].

On the basis of the relevant local normative acts, orders and instructions, the centre may, within its activity, be responsible for preparing and keeping up to date information, including certificates and reports — among them the income received from commercialisation of results of intellectual activity, the number of filed applications for РИД and the number of РИД registered and commercialised with the centre's assistance, and others [62].

# College (secondary vocational education): academic debt, repeat assessment and retakes

## What counts as an academic debt

For College students on secondary vocational education programmes, unsatisfactory results of interim assessment in one or more elements of the programme (a discipline, МДК, practice, ПМ), or the failure to undergo interim assessment in the absence of valid reasons, are recognised as an academic debt [1]. Failure to appear for interim assessment without a valid reason is equated to an academic debt [2], and at an exam non-appearance without a valid reason is equated to an unsatisfactory grade [3]. These rules are set by the Положение о формах, периодичности и порядке текущего контроля успеваемости и промежуточной аттестации обучающихся, осваивающих образовательные программы среднего профессионального образования, approved by order of the University of 17 April 2025 № ОД/АД-01/2025/04/17 [4].

## Two attempts within a year

Students with an academic debt may undergo interim assessment on the element concerned not more than 2 (two) times within the time determined by the University, within 1 (one) year from the moment the debt arose; the time of the student's illness, of academic leave or of maternity leave is not included in that period [5].

The College sets, for students with an academic debt, the dates of the repeat interim assessment for each discipline, МДК, ПМ and practice; a student who has not cleared the debt at the first repeat interim assessment is given the opportunity to undergo the second repeat interim assessment, held by a commission created by the College [6].

- First repeat: it is held by the same teacher who held the interim assessment; if that teacher cannot hold it in the established time, the College director may appoint another teacher [7].
- Second repeat: a commission is created from the pedagogical workers of the College, as a rule of 3 (three) people, and its composition is approved, together with the schedule of the second repeat, by order of the University director or another authorised person [8].
- Attendance: a second repeat in written form is held in the presence of at least 1 (one) member of the commission, in oral form in the presence of all its members [9].
- Result: it is determined by the decision of all members of the commission, taken by simple majority, is recorded in the sheet of the second repeat interim assessment, is final and is not subject to revision [10].

## Recording of results

The results of the first and of the second repeat are entered by the teacher in the students' record books and in the sheets of the repeat interim assessment (appendices 8, 9, 10) or of the second repeat (appendices 11, 12, 13), which are handed to the College on the day of the repeat if the exam or pass is oral, and within 2 (two) working days after it if it is written, but not later than the end of the period for clearing the debt; the results of students of graduating courses are submitted before the order admitting them to the state final assessment is approved [11]. College staff transfer the results from the sheet of the repeat interim assessment into the journal and the University's electronic information-educational environment [12].

## When repeat assessment may be held

Students of graduating groups pass the repeat interim assessment in disciplines, МДК and ПМ before the order admitting them to the state final assessment is approved, and those of non-graduating groups within 6 (six) weeks after the start of the next semester [13]. The College may hold the first and/or the second repeat during vacations, setting several dates for it both during vacations and during the period when the disciplines (modules) are delivered [14]. The repeat may not be held during the period of a practice or during the period of interim assessment [15], and its time must not coincide with classes held as contact work [16]. The form of the repeat in a discipline, МДК, ПМ or practice must correspond to the form of the interim assessment: the exam or pass of the repeat is held in the same form as the exam or pass of the interim assessment [17]. The schedule of the repeat is brought to the students' attention against signature, and to the parents (legal representatives) of minor students [18].

## Practice and expulsion

Students who have not completed the practice programme or received an unsatisfactory grade are sent to the practice again outside class time, the repeat being formalised by order of the University director or another authorised person; if the unsatisfactory grade was caused by the absence of the required reporting documents on the practice, the student is not sent to the practice again but completes the documents [19].

On the results of the second repeat an order of the University director or another authorised person is prepared on the transfer of the students to the next course, their admission to the state final assessment or their expulsion [20]. Students who have not cleared the academic debt within the established time are expelled from the University as not having fulfilled the duties of conscientiously mastering the educational programme and completing the curriculum [21].

## Retaking to raise a positive grade

A retake of study results to raise the grade in a discipline or МДК may be granted to students of graduating courses after they have successfully mastered the ОП СПО in full and only if the student can qualify for a diploma with honours on the results of the state final assessment [22]. The possibility of retaking not more than 2 (two) grades «хорошо» is granted on a written application of the student (Appendix 14) agreed with the College director and addressed to the University director, not later than 14 (fourteen) calendar days before the day of the defence of the diploma project (work) [23].

The retake is held by the teacher to whom the discipline or МДК is assigned [24]. The student has only 1 (one) attempt at the retake, its results are formalised in accordance with п. 4.18 of the Положение, and if the student shows a result lower than the original one the grade in the discipline or МДК remains unchanged [25]. All retakes for a higher grade must be held before the order admitting students to the state final assessment is approved [26].

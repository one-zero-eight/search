# PhD aspirantura admission quotas for 2026/27 (budget places)

## What the document is

The page reproduces Appendix No. 3.750 to the order of the Ministry of Science and Higher Education of the Russian Federation No. 1098 of 30 December 2025 [1]. The appendix sets the admission quotas (контрольные цифры приёма) by groups of scientific specialties and (or) by scientific specialties for study in aspirantura programmes — programmes for training scientific and scientific-pedagogical personnel (aspirantura, adjunctura) — at the expense of federal budget allocations for the 2026/27 academic year [1]. The university named in it is ANO VO «Университет Иннополис» [1].

## The figures for Innopolis University

- In total, 10 places are set for the University [1].
- The group of scientific specialties «Компьютерные науки и информатика», group code 1.2., has 10 places [1].

So the whole quota for 2026/27 — 10 budget places — is allocated to the group of scientific specialties 1.2. «Компьютерные науки и информатика» [1]. The table of the appendix lists no other group and gives no breakdown of the 10 places by individual scientific specialty inside the group [1].

# Admission of foreign citizens and stateless persons to bachelor's and master's programmes 2026/2027

## The rules these statements come from

- The admission of foreign citizens and stateless persons is dealt with by section XVI of the University's admission rules for bachelor's and master's programmes of 2026/2027, headed «Особенности приема на обучение иностранных граждан и лиц без гражданства» (clauses 153–159) [1].
- Those rules — «ПРАВИЛА ПРИЕМА НА ОБУЧЕНИЕ ПО ОБРАЗОВАТЕЛЬНЫМ ПРОГРАММАМ ВЫСШЕГО ОБРАЗОВАНИЯ – ПРОГРАММАМ БАКАЛАВРИАТА И ПРОГРАММАМ МАГИСТРАТУРЫ ... НА 2026/2027 УЧЕБНЫЙ ГОД» — were approved by the University's order of 10 April 2026 № ОД/УД‐01/2026/04/10, in a new redaction [1].
- Under clause 1, the rules regulate the admission of citizens of the Russian Federation, foreign citizens and stateless persons alike (all of them called поступающие) to bachelor's and master's programmes of the University [1].

## On what ground a foreign citizen studies

- Under clause 153, foreign citizens and stateless persons have the right to receive higher education at the expense of budget allocations under international treaties of the Russian Federation, federal laws or the quota for the education of foreign citizens and stateless persons established by the Government of the Russian Federation (the quota for the education of foreign citizens), and also at the expense of physical and legal persons under contracts on education [1].
- Under clause 154, admission within that quota is conducted according to the directions of the federal executive body that elaborates state policy and normative regulation in higher education; enrolment within the quota is formalised by a separate order (orders) of the University [1].
- Under clause 158, admission of foreign citizens and stateless persons to programmes containing information constituting a state secret is conducted within the quota for the education of foreign citizens with observance of the requirements of the legislation of Russia on state secrets [1].

## Documents presented by foreign citizens and stateless persons

Besides the documents necessary for admission that clause 74 requires of every applicant, the rules provide the following [1]:

- under clause 155, a foreign citizen applying on the basis of international treaties presents, in addition to the documents named in the rules, documents confirming that he belongs to the persons named in the relevant international treaties [1];
- under clause 156, foreign citizens and stateless persons who are compatriots living abroad present, in addition to the documents named in clause 74, originals or copies of the documents provided for by article 17 of Federal Law No. 99-ФЗ of 24 May 1999 «О государственной политике Российской Федерации в отношении соотечественников за рубежом» [1];
- under clause 156, the special rights in admission to state-accredited bachelor's programmes granted to particular categories of citizens of the Russian Federation do not extend to compatriots who are not citizens of the Russian Federation, except the special right under п. 1 of part 4 of article 71 of Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ, unless otherwise provided by an international treaty of Russia [1];
- under clause 157, a foreign citizen or stateless person presents, in accordance with п. 1 of clause 74, the original or a copy of the document proving identity and citizenship, or the document proving the identity of a foreign citizen in Russia or the identity of a stateless person in Russia under article 10 of Federal Law No. 115-ФЗ of 25 July 2002 [1];
- under п. 2 of clause 74, a document of a foreign state on education is presented with a certificate of recognition of the foreign education, except the cases in which recognition is not required under the legislation of Russia and/or an international treaty; the certificate of recognition is presented not later than the term of completion of the presentation of the consent to enrolment (for places within the control figures) or not later than the day of completion of the conclusion of contracts (for paid places) [1];
- under clause 77, documents made in a foreign language are presented with a translation into Russian certified by a notary (including a consular official) [1];
- under clause 78, documents obtained in a foreign state must be legalised unless otherwise provided by an international treaty of Russia or by the legislation of Russia [1].

## Entrance examinations on bachelor's programmes

- Under clause 29, at admission to bachelor's programmes foreign citizens have the right to take internal general education entrance examinations in the subjects in which they have no ЕГЭ results obtained in the year of admission and/or within 4 (four) years before the year of admission, except results of the ЕГЭ in basic-level mathematics; that right is given for places within the special quota, the target quota, the main budget places and the paid places [1].
- Under clause 159, at admission to paid places on bachelor's programmes the University itself sets the list of entrance examinations for foreign citizens and stateless persons (вступительные испытания для иностранных граждан) and the minimum number of points for those examinations, in accordance with section 5 of Appendix 1; foreign citizens and stateless persons may, at their choice, apply on the results of those examinations or on the results of the entrance examinations set by clauses 21 and 27 of the rules [1].
- Under clause 159, where bachelor's programmes are delivered in Russian from the first year, the list of entrance examinations for foreign citizens includes the entrance examination in Russian as a foreign language; the University may count as its result a certificate of passing the state testing in Russian as a foreign language that confirms command of Russian at not lower than the first certification level, and the accounting of the certificate is: ТРКИ-1 (first certification level, B1) — 60 (sixty) points, ТРКИ-2 (second certification level, B2) — 80 (eighty) points, ТРКИ-3 and ТРКИ-4 — 100 (one hundred) points [1].
- Section 5 of Appendix 1 (bachelor's programmes for foreign citizens and stateless persons entering on the basis of secondary general and professional education, under contracts for paid educational services) gives for each profile, in order of priority, «Математика» (ЕГЭ/ВВИ) or «Основы математики» (ВВИ), «Информатика/Физика» (ЕГЭ/ВВИ) or «Основы информатики /Основы физики» (ВВИ), and «Русский язык» (ЕГЭ/ВВИ), with a minimum of 60 and a maximum of 100 points for every examination; the table covers the profiles of areas 09.03.01 (Инженерия информационных систем, Анализ данных и искусственный интеллект, Математические основы искусственного интеллекта, AI360: Инженерия данных) and 15.03.06 (Робототехника) [1].
- Section 6 of Appendix 1 (master's programmes for foreign citizens and stateless persons under contracts for paid educational services) gives one entrance examination for each programme, named after its profile, in the form of computer testing, with a minimum of 40 and a maximum of 100 points; it covers the profiles of areas 09.04.01, 38.04.05 and 15.04.06 [1].

## Additional admission to paid places

- Under clause 133, the University conducts additional admission to paid places on bachelor's and master's programmes in the full-time form only for persons entering on the basis of a document of a foreign state on education; that additional admission is held once and ends, for the full-time form, not later than 31 October of the year of admission [1].
- Under clause 149, additional admission to paid places is held once by decision of the University, for persons entering on the basis of a document of a foreign state on education; additional admission on bachelor's programmes is held independently of additional admission on master's programmes, and no additional enrolment is held during it [1].

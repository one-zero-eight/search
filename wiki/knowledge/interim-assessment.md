# Interim assessment

## Which document applies

The rules on ongoing and interim assessment for bachelor's and master's students are set by the Regulation on the forms, periodicity and procedure of ongoing assessment and interim assessment, approved by order of 30 June 2025 No. ОД/УД-01/2025/06/30 [1]. By the same order the orders of 5 August 2020 № ОД/УД-05/2020/08/05, of 2 March 2021 № ОД/УД-03/2021/03/02 and of 26 April 2021 № ОД/АД-03/2021/04/26 were repealed [1]. The Regulation was developed in accordance with the Federal law of 29 December 2012 № 273-ФЗ «Об образовании в Российской Федерации», the order of the Ministry of Science and Higher Education of 6 April 2021 № 245 on the procedure of organising and carrying out educational activity, the federal state educational standards, the University's Charter and other normative and local acts [2].

An earlier version of the same Regulation was approved by order of 5 August 2020 No. ОД/УД-05/2020/08/05 [3]; that page is a scan read by OCR, and its numbers and dates should be checked against the PDF [3].

## Scope and purpose

The regulation on ongoing and interim assessment applies to students of the bachelor's and master's programmes [4]. Interim assessment evaluates the results of learning in disciplines (modules), other components of the educational programme and internships by identifying how the student's level of competence development corresponds to the requirements of the educational programme [5]. The forms, frequency and procedure of interim assessment, as well as the procedure and deadlines for clearing academic failure, are established by this regulation [6].

The aim of ongoing and interim assessment is to evaluate the quality of students' mastering of disciplines (modules) and other components of the educational programme, including internships, the degree of formation of competences in the course of study, the acquisition and development of independent work skills and the raising of students' academic activity [7].

## Forms of interim assessment

Interim assessment takes the form of a pass/fail exam (including a graded pass/fail exam) or an exam [8]. Each semester or trimester of the academic year ends with the forms of interim assessment provided by the curriculum [9]. Exams and pass/fail exams may be conducted orally, in writing, via tests (including computer-based), as essay or project defence, in combined form, or in other forms [10]. The specific format for each discipline is determined by the teaching staff member and communicated to students [10].

Interim assessment is conducted after completing a discipline (module), other component of the educational program, or internship, in the form and scope specified in the curriculum, discipline syllabus, or internship program [11]. It is not charged [12].

The schedule of interim assessment for all forms of education and all fields of study, including room assignments, is shared with students and teaching staff via the University's corporate email [13]; the dates of interim assessment, including the dates of the first and second retakes, are communicated to students through corporate email [14].

At the discretion of the teaching staff member, a student may be allowed during interim assessment to use reference books, textbooks and other sources of information [15].

## Grading system

The University uses a graded system (A, B, C, D) and a non-graded system (P, F) for interim assessment [16]. Results are evaluated with this grading system and with the criteria for assigning grades set out in the discipline (module) syllabuses, internship programmes, assessment and methodological materials and other components of the educational programme [17], and they reflect the level of development of the competences that the given discipline (module), other component or internship forms according to the curriculum [18].

The letter grades translate to the following [19]:

- A — Excellent [19].
- B — Good [19].
- C — Satisfactory [19].
- D — Fail [19].
- P — Passed [19].
- F — Fail [19].

Results A, B, C, and P are positive and cannot be retaken for improvement, except as specified for final-year students seeking an honors diploma [20]. Results D and F are unsatisfactory [21].

Teaching staff may use an alternative grading system during instruction; in that case, the grading system, criteria, and conversion rules to the University's system are specified in the discipline syllabus and communicated to students in the University's electronic educational environment [22].

When assigning final grades, teaching staff may consider ongoing assessment results, mastery of practical skills, attendance, class participation, and exam performance [23].

## Results and recording

Results of interim assessment must be submitted within 3 working days after the assessment and no later than the end of the interim assessment period [24]. Results are recorded in the examination sheet and in the University's electronic information-educational environment [25].

Results are recorded in one of two ways: either the teaching staff member enters them in the examination sheet, signs it and submits it to the responsible structural unit on paper or as an electronic document, and the unit's staff transfer them to the electronic environment; or the teaching staff member enters them in the electronic environment, they are transferred to the examination sheet, checked for correctness and confirmed by the signature of the teaching staff member or of the faculty dean [26].

Results are final and cannot be revised [27]. Students check results independently via the electronic environment [28].

## Transfers and attendance

Students who have no academic failures and have fully completed the curriculum or individual study plan are transferred to the next year by order of the University Director [29]. Students who did not undergo interim assessment due to a valid excuse or who have academic failures are conditionally transferred [29].

Absence from interim assessment without a valid reason is equivalent to an unsatisfactory result [30]. Students who cannot complete interim assessment within established deadlines due to documented valid reasons (temporary disability, public or state duties, court summons, or other circumstances recognized by the University) are granted individualized timelines approved by the University Director [31]. A student who submits documents proving a valid reason for missing interim assessment may be granted an extension no later than 3 working days after receiving such documentation [32]. Students participating in an academic exchange program who miss interim assessment are granted individual deadlines after the exchange period ends [33].

## Externs

Persons mastering an educational programme through self-study (where the educational standard allows higher education in that programme in this form), as well as persons who studied in a programme without state accreditation, may be enrolled at the University as externs to undergo interim and state final assessment [34]. After the extern is enrolled, within a period set by the University but no later than 1 month from enrolment, the University approves the extern's individual study plan for interim and state final assessment [34]. The conditions and procedure of enrolment of externs and the deadlines for their interim and state final assessment are set by the University [34].

## Academic misconduct

Use of unauthorized materials, cheating, use of mobile devices or other technical means not permitted by the teaching staff, attempting to communicate with other students, or any breach of the University's Regulations during exams or pass/fail exams are grounds for immediate removal from the examination room and assignment of a grade D or F [35]. Third-party observers are prohibited without written authorization from the responsible University department [36].

## Assessment for students with disabilities

Students with disabilities (ОВЗ) may request adapted evaluation materials upon submitting a written application [37]. The form of assessment is set taking into account individual psycho-physical characteristics (oral, written on paper or computer, testing, etc.) [38]. Students with disabilities may be provided with additional time for answer preparation, extending the assessment session by up to 1.5 hours [39]. Students with visual impairment are provided with an assistant and assistive devices [40]. For students with speech disorders, deafness, or hearing impairments, oral assessments are conducted in written form [41]. Students with disabilities may use assistive devices during assessment sessions [42].

## Aspirantura

Interim assessment of aspirants is governed by separate aspirantura documents — the 2018 regulation and the 2022 regulation; see the card on interim assessment in aspirantura [43], [44].

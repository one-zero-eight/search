# Tender Department (Тендерный отдел) — place, tasks, functions and rights

## The document and the Department's place in the structure

The Regulation on the Tender Department (Положение о Тендерном отделе) of ANO VO «Innopolis University» was approved by order of 10 January 2022 No. ОД/ ХД/03/2022/01/10, signed by R.F. Valiev as Director for Development and HR Policy, in Innopolis [1]. It regulates the activity of the Tender Department of the Centre for the Development of Digital Economy Projects and determines its tasks, functions, rights and duties [2].

- The Tender Department (the Department) is a structural subdivision of the Centre for the Development of Digital Economy Projects of the Directorate for Project and Research Activity and belongs to the category of other structural subdivisions (the page runs the names of the Centre and of the Directorate together) [3]. It reports directly to the director of the Centre for the Development of Digital Economy Projects [4].
- In its activity the Department is guided by the Constitution of the Russian Federation, the Labour Code of the Russian Federation, the Federal Law «Об образовании в Российской Федерации», other legislative and normative legal acts regulating the activity of higher education institutions, the University's Charter, decisions of the University's Academic, Supervisory and Operational councils, orders and instructions of the University director, other local normative acts and this Regulation [5].
- The structure, headcount and staffing table of the Department, and changes to them, are approved by the University director [6]. The Department is headed by a head of the Department, hired and dismissed by order of the University director [7].
- The working conditions of the Department's employees are determined by the employment contracts concluded with each of them and by the University's internal labour regulations [8]; their rights, duties and degree of responsibility are established by job descriptions [9].
- Within their competence the Department's employees may be involved in work under grants, projects, subsidies and other programmes implemented by the University [10]. The Regulation and the additions and changes to it are approved by the University director or by another person authorised by him [11].

## Tasks

The tasks of the Department are [12]–[13]:

- searching for and analysing tenders, submitting and drawing up applications, and interacting with other organisations in this area [12];
- supporting the procurement activity of the subdivisions of the Directorate for Project and Research Activity [13].

## Functions

Tenders [14]–[15]:

- organising information and analytical search for tenders in the field of research and innovation activity [14];
- primary examination and selection of announced tenders in order to decide whether participation is possible [16];
- organising and controlling the process of submitting applications by coordinating the actions of all participants in the process of writing and submitting an application [17];
- negotiating with all participants in the process of writing and submitting applications [18];
- collecting and processing the materials needed for submitting applications [19];
- consulting on the preparation of the substantive part of tender applications [20];
- drawing up tender applications [21];
- preparing printed copies of tender applications [22];
- monitoring the results of competitions and informing the participants about the results [23];
- developing proposals on the University's participation in tenders [24];
- providing consulting and methodological help to the University's employees in drawing up tender applications [25];
- long-term and current planning of the participation of the University's employees in tenders [26];
- organising information work at the University to attract structural subdivisions to participation in tenders [15].

Procurements of the Directorate [27]–[28]:

- organising and controlling the process of conducting procurements by the subdivisions of the Directorate [27];
- providing consulting and methodological help to the employees of the Directorate when carrying out procurements [29];
- placing information about the Directorate's procurements on electronic trading platforms [28].

Other functions [30]–[31]:

- keeping the necessary record keeping (делопроизводство) [30];
- carrying out the instructions of the director of the Centre for the Development of Digital Economy Projects, of the first vice-rector – deputy director and of the University director [32];
- developing methodological recommendations and other documents intended to order the activity in the field of tenders and procurements at the University [31].

## Rights

In carrying out their activity the Department's employees have the right to [33]:

- request from the University's structural subdivisions the information and materials needed for the work within the Department's competence [33];
- with the consent of the heads of the University's structural subdivisions, engage the employees of those subdivisions to prepare draft local acts and other documents and to develop and carry out the measures conducted by the Department [34];
- with the consent of the University's management, place information about the procurement procedures being conducted [35];
- take part in the meetings held by the University's management when questions within the Department's competence are discussed — the Regulation numbers this right as п. 4.3 as well, repeating the number [35];
- submit to the University's management proposals to cancel, amend or supplement the University's local acts [36];
- interact with state and government bodies, legal entities and the University's subdivisions on questions within the Department's competence [37].

## Interaction with other structural subdivisions

Interaction with other structural subdivisions is determined by the tasks and functions assigned to the Department by this Regulation [38].

- With the heads of structural subdivisions — on the participation of the University's employees in tenders and procurements [39].
- With the head of the personnel department — on the hiring of employees into the Department [40].
- With the Financial and Economic and the Analytical departments and the Legal Directorate — on preparing contracts and agreements on won tenders and completed procurements [41].
- With other structural subdivisions — on preparing tender applications and placing the procurements of the Directorate's subdivisions [42].
- The Department may be a base for the practical training of the University's students [43].

## Reporting

On the basis of the relevant local acts, orders and instructions, the Department may be responsible, within its activity, for preparing and keeping up to date information, references and reports [44].

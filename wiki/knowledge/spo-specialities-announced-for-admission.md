# Specialities (professions) for which the University announces admission under its licence — the published list (СПО)

## What the document is

The page is the list of specialities (professions) for which the University announces admission in accordance with the licence for carrying out educational activity, indicating the forms of study (full-time, part-time and correspondence) [1].

It states that the University announces admission to educational programmes of secondary vocational education on the basis of licence No. Л035-00115-77/00642712, issued by распоряжение No. 2418 of 25.12.2015 of the Federal Service for Supervision in Education and Science, without time limit, for carrying out educational activity under secondary vocational education programmes [1].

## The specialities listed

The University announces admission to programmes for the training of mid-level specialists (программы подготовки специалистов среднего звена) in [1]:

- 09.02.11 Разработка и управление программным обеспечением [1]
- 15.02.10 Мехатроника и робототехника (по отраслям) [1]

## What the page does not say

The title and the introductory sentence name the forms of study in general terms (очная, очно-заочная, заочная), but the list itself gives no form of study for either speciality [1]. The page also gives no number of places, no deadlines, no entrance examinations and no tuition: it only lists the specialities covered by the licence [1].

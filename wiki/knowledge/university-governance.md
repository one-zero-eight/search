# Governance of Innopolis University — Founders, Supervisory Council, Operations Council, Director and Academic council

## The scheme of management

The activity of the University is managed by its Founders in the order established by the charter; the highest collegial management body is the Supervisory Council, the permanently acting collegial body is the Operations Council, and the sole executive body is the Director [1]. The charter also provides for a General meeting of employees and students, an Academic council, deans of faculties and a possible Board of Trustees [2], [3], [4], [5]. ## The Founders

The Founders are the Russian Federation, represented by the Ministry of Digital Development, Communications and Mass Media of the Russian Federation (ОГРН 1047702026701), and the Republic of Tatarstan, represented by the Ministry of Land and Property Relations of the Republic of Tatarstan (ОГРН 1021602851753) [7].

The competence of the Founders includes admitting new persons to the Founders, approving the charter in a new edition, taking decisions on appointing members of the Supervisory Council or terminating their powers early, appointing the sole executive body of the University and terminating its powers early, deciding on reorganisation in the form of transformation into a fund, deciding on reorganisation (except transformation into a fund) and liquidation, appointing the liquidation commission (liquidator) and approving the liquidation balance sheet, deciding on creating the Board of Trustees, appointing its members or terminating their powers early and liquidating it, approving a candidate for the post of rector if such a post is established, and determining the order of management of the University [8]. The Founders take decisions as needed, and their decisions are taken unanimously [8]. Decisions of the Founders, the Supervisory Council and the Operations Council are binding on the Director [9].

## The Supervisory Council

The Supervisory Council is formed by the Founders with no fewer than 7 and no more than 11 members [10]. It may include representatives of executive bodies of state power or of local self-government and representatives of the public, including persons with merits and achievements in education and science, as well as representatives of other state bodies and representatives of the University's employees; the number of representatives of the University's employees may not exceed one third of the total number of members [10]. The term of powers of a member of the Supervisory Council is 6 years [11]. The exercise of its functions is unpaid [12].

Its exclusive competence includes determining the priority directions of the University's activity, determining the principles of forming and using the University's property, approving the financial plan (budget) and amendments to it, approving the annual report and accounting (financial) statements, deciding on creating branches and opening representative offices, deciding on the creation of other legal entities by the University and its participation in other legal entities, approving the auditor, determining the conditions and concluding the employment contract with the Director, determining the conditions of the employment contract with a rector if such a post exists, determining measures and the order of encouraging the Director and a rector, forming the Operations Council and terminating its powers early, and giving preliminary consent to a major transaction when there are unused budget funds on the University's accounts [13]. A major transaction is a transaction or several connected transactions involving the disposal of money, the alienation of other property or the transfer of such property for use or as a pledge, where the price or the value of the property exceeds 10 percent of the book value of the University's assets on the last reporting date [13]. The decision on preliminary consent must name the parties, the beneficiary, the price, the subject and other essential conditions of the transaction, and unless a term is stated it is valid for one year from the date of adoption [13].

Members elect the Chairman from among themselves by a majority of votes [14]. The Supervisory Council is convened by the Chairman as needed but at least once a year [15]. A meeting is competent if more than half of the members are present [16], and decisions are taken by a majority of the members present, with decisions on matters of exclusive competence requiring a different majority as stated in that clause [17].

## The Operations Council

The Operations Council is formed by the Supervisory Council with 5 to 10 members [18]. Its members elect a Chairman from among themselves by a majority of votes, and the term of powers of a member is 6 years [18]. Its functions are also unpaid [19]. A meeting is competent if more than half of its members are present [20]. Its powers include deciding on conducting an audit of the University's activity [21], and decisions are taken by a majority of the members present [22]. By decision of the Founders and/or the Supervisory Council a partial transfer of the Operations Council's powers to the Director is allowed [23].

## The Director

The Director is the sole executive body and manages the current activity of the University, except for matters within the competence of the Founders, the Supervisory Council and the Operations Council [24]. The Director is appointed and dismissed by a decision of the Founders and is appointed for a term of 5 years [25]. The Director takes part in meetings of the Supervisory Council and the Operations Council on a permanent basis [26]. Separate powers of the Director may be transferred to other employees of the University by a power of attorney issued by him [27].

## The General meeting and the Academic council

A General meeting of the University's employees and students is formed as a collegial management body [2]. Its competence includes proposing amendments to the charter for further submission to the Supervisory Council, electing the initial composition of the Academic council, and considering matters put to it for discussion by the Director or the Academic council [2].

The Academic council is a collegial management body [3]. Its competence includes deciding to convene the General meeting of employees and students and other matters connected with holding it, taking decisions on educational and scientific activity on the Director's proposal, approving the thematic plan and reports on the University's research work, considering candidates for the conferment of academic titles, conducting the competitive selection of persons applying for posts of scientific-pedagogical workers, nominating employees for the conferment of academic titles, considering the nomination of employees for state awards of the Russian Federation and honorary titles, conferring honorary titles of the University on the basis of regulations approved by the Academic council, nominating students and aspirants for scholarships of the President of the Russian Federation, special state scholarships of the Government of the Russian Federation and named scholarships, taking decisions on other matters within its competence under legislation, the charter and local acts, and approving local normative acts on matters within its competence [3].

## Deans and the Board of Trustees

A faculty is headed by a dean elected by the Academic council by secret ballot for a term of up to 5 years from among the most qualified and authoritative specialists meeting the qualification requirements of the legislation of the Russian Federation, and approved in the post by an order of the Director; the procedure for electing a dean is determined by a local normative act approved by the Director [4].

By decision of the Founders a collegial advisory body, the Board of Trustees (Попечительский совет), may be created in the University [5].

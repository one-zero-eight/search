# Network form of implementing educational programmes (сетевая форма) — the Regulation of 21 March 2025

## The document and what it governs

The Regulation on the procedure of organising and carrying out educational activity when educational programmes are implemented in the network form at ANO VO «Innopolis University» was approved by order of the University of 21 March 2025 and signed by the Director I.I. Bariev, Innopolis, 2025 [1].

It establishes the procedure of organising and carrying out educational activity at the University — including the development and approval of educational programmes, the conclusion of contracts, the admission of applicants, the enrolment of students by transfer, the issue of documents of education and (or) of qualification, and the payment of scholarships and other money payments — when programmes are implemented in the network form [2].

It was drawn up in accordance with Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29 December 2012, the Government decree No. 2122 of 30.11.2021 on the training of scientific and scientific-pedagogical personnel in aspirantura, the orders of the Ministry of Science and Higher Education and of the Ministry of Education on the procedure of organising educational activity in additional professional programmes, in aspirantura, in the network form, in bachelor's, specialist and master's programmes, in additional general education programmes and in secondary vocational education, the federal state educational standards and federal state requirements, the University's Charter and other acts [3].

## What the network form is

The network form allows students to master an educational programme and (or) its separate subjects, courses, disciplines (modules), practice and other components — including components of different type, level and (or) orientation — using the resources of several organisations carrying out educational activity, including foreign ones, and, where necessary, the resources of other organisations [4]. It also allows mastering a programme that includes competences relating to one or several professions, specialities and fields of training at the corresponding levels of professional education, or to enlarged groups of them, and to an area (areas) and type (types) of professional activity, including the possibility of obtaining several qualifications at the same time [4].

A network educational programme may include parts provided for by educational programmes of different types, levels and (or) orientations [5]. Educational activity under it is carried out through interaction between the organisations under a contract on the network form of implementing the educational programme (the contract on the network form) [6].

## Parties to the contract on the network form

- The base organisation is the organisation carrying out educational activity in which the student is admitted for study in accordance with article 55 of Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ; it is responsible for implementing the network educational programme and controls the participation of the participating organisations in it [7].
- A participating organisation is an educational organisation taking part in implementing the network educational programme, and (or) a scientific, medical, cultural, sports or other organisation possessing resources for carrying out educational activity under it [7].
- Several participating organisations may be parties to the contract, and the University may act both as the base organisation and as a participating organisation [7].

## The programme and its approval

The network educational programme is approved by the base organisation alone or jointly with the educational participating organisation or organisations [8]. Where it is approved by the base organisation alone, the participating organisation develops, approves and sends to the base organisation the working programmes of the parts it implements, together with the necessary assessment and methodological materials [8]. Working programmes of parts implemented by a foreign educational organisation are included in the language of instruction of the network educational programme [8].

The use of the network form is provided for by the educational programme to which students are admitted, or the switch to the network form is made during implementation with amendments to the programme in the procedure set by the local acts of the base organisation [9]. Where the University is the base organisation, the amendments are made in the procedure set by the University's local acts governing the development of educational programmes [9].

## Admission, enrolment and transfer into the University

On admission to a network educational programme the student is enrolled in the base organisation [10]. Enrolment in the participating organisation for basic and additional educational programmes is made by transfer to that organisation without expulsion from the base organisation, in the procedure determined by the local acts of the participating organisation [11]. Students are not enrolled in an organisation that possesses resources [11].

Where the University is the participating organisation, the procedure is as follows:

- the base organisation sends the University a named list of the students and copies of their personal files [12];
- transfer enrolment to a higher education or a secondary vocational education programme is made by the University's structural unit responsible for the admission of applicants, on the basis of that named list and the copies of personal files [13];
- transfer enrolment to an additional educational programme is made by the University's structural unit responsible for that additional educational programme [14];
- transfer enrolment in the University is made before the start of study at the University as the participating organisation, by an order of the Director of the University or of a person authorised by him, whose form is given in the annex to the Regulation [15].

## Status of students, scholarships and assessment

Students of a network educational programme are students of the base organisation and, during the part of the programme implemented in the participating organisation, also students of that organisation; for that period they are not expelled from the base organisation [16].

Scholarships, other money payments and other measures of social support provided by the legislation on education are paid and provided by the base organisation for the whole term of the network educational programme [17]. By decision of the participating organisation students may be awarded an additional scholarship and other money payments and given additional measures of social support in the procedure determined by that organisation; this is not a ground for cancelling or suspending the payments and measures of the base organisation [17].

Mastering a part of the network educational programme in the participating organisation is accompanied by current control and interim attestation in the forms determined by the curriculum of the network educational programme and in the procedure established by the participating organisation [18]. Where the University is the participating organisation, current control and interim attestation are conducted under the University's local acts on current control and interim attestation of students [18]. The results of interim attestation held by the participating organisation are the results of interim attestation of the network educational programme and do not require recognition (зачёт) in the base organisation [18].

## Completion, documents and financing

Once a part of the network educational programme has been mastered in full, students are expelled from the participating organisation on completion of study [19]. Where the network educational programme provides for a final (state final) attestation held jointly by the base and the participating organisation, students are expelled after that attestation [20].

Graduates who have mastered the network educational programme in full and passed the final (state final) attestation are issued documents of education and (or) of qualification by the base organisation [21]. Where the contract on the network form so provides, they also receive the documents of education and (or) of qualification of the participating organisation [21]. For network educational programmes that do not provide for a final (state final) attestation, documents of study are issued in the cases and procedure provided by the contract [21].

Financial support of the network educational programme is provided by reimbursing the costs of implementing the part of the programme according to the list of the participating organisation's costs, or in other ways in accordance with Russian legislation [22].

## If a participating organisation cannot continue

Where a participating organisation cannot take part in implementing the network educational programme — including on termination of its activity or suspension or annulment of its educational licence — the contract on the network form must be amended and (or) terminated, and the remaining parts of the programme are implemented by the base organisation without the network form, after amendments to the programme in the procedure set by its local acts [23]. Amendments to the University's educational programme are made under the local acts governing the development of educational programmes [23].

Where there are students who have not completed the network educational programme within the set term — including because of academic leave, maternity leave or leave to care for a child until the age of three — then after the term of the contract on the network form expires the contract may be extended, or the remaining parts of the programme are implemented by the base organisation without the network form [24]. With the consent of those students, or of the parents (legal representatives) of minor students who do not have basic general education, they may transfer to another network educational programme implemented under another contract on the network form [24].

## The annex: form of the order on enrolment by transfer

The annex to the Regulation gives the form of the University's order on enrolment by transfer within an educational programme implemented in the network form; the order names the base organisation and the date from which the persons are enrolled, states the programme (secondary vocational, bachelor's, master's, aspirantura, additional general development, advanced training or professional retraining) with its code and name, and refers to a schedule to the order listing the persons by number, surname, name and patronymic (the last where present) [1].

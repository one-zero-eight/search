# Rights to intellectual results: service, initiative and student results

## Three kinds of results

Under Innopolis University's IP-Policy (approved by order of the University of 9 August 2022), results of intellectual activity created at the University are divided into service results, initiative results and results created by students [1]. ## Service results

Service results are created within and in connection with the employee's performance of labour duties or labour function or a specific (service) assignment of the employer [3]. They include, in particular [4]:

- the service work — a work of science, literature or art created within the employee's labour duties, including scientific and educational works such as textbooks, teaching aids and lecture courses, computer programmes and databases [4];
- the service performance, including a joint performance created in that order [4];
- a service invention, utility model or industrial design [4];
- a service production secret (know-how), protected by the confidentiality regime in force for it, including a commercial secrecy regime [4];
- a service topology of an integrated circuit [4].

Service results may be created within projects (works or services) performed under contracts concluded by the University for the creation and/or introduction of research, scientific-technical or scientific-methodological products, contracts on transfer or assignment of the exclusive right, including industrial projects, grant projects financed from the budget or by industrial partners' grants, consulting projects, and also within the University's own initiative projects, including research, НИОКР and work on new educational programmes, methodological materials and software carried out at its own expense [5].

A service assignment is an instruction of the person under whose direct subordination the employee works and whose powers include setting tasks in accordance with the position, the employment contract or a power of attorney, imposing on the employee work relating to the creation (or the possibility of creating) of a defined protectable result where such duties do not follow in the ordinary course from labour legislation or the employment contract [6]. Service assignments may be given in writing against the employee's signature and/or through the Bitrix corporate portal or in another form provided by local acts, and the recommended form of the service assignment is approved by order of the University Director [6].

The exclusive right to service results and the right to obtain a patent belong to the University unless an employment or civil-law contract between the University and the author provides otherwise [7]. The service character determines the relations between the employee and the employer but not the legal regime of the result as a whole; where a service result is created in co-authorship with a person who is not an employee of the University, the head of the structural subdivision involved must ensure that a contract on disposal of the exclusive right, providing for the assignment of the right to or its belonging to the University, is concluded with that co-author — for example an author's order contract, an assignment contract, work or service contracts, or contracts for research, development and technological work [8].

The exclusive right to service results may belong to the University jointly with other organisations and persons, the Russian Federation, its subjects or municipal formations, or only to third parties if the contract so provides [9]. Other persons may use service results whose exclusive right belongs to the University only under a contract on disposal of the exclusive right concluded with the University, unless the law provides otherwise [10].

## Initiative results

The exclusive right to initiative results and the right to obtain a patent belong to the author; the University may at its choice require a free simple (non-exclusive) licence to use the result for its own needs for the whole term of the exclusive right or reimbursement of the expenses it incurred in creating the result (arts. 1370, 1461 of the Civil Code) [11]. The University seeks to favour as far as possible the securing of rights to initiative results for itself [12], and the author may at his discretion transfer to the University the exclusive right and the right to obtain a patent, including the right to file for state registration of the result [13].

If the author decides to transfer the rights, he notifies the University by filing a notification of the creation of an initiative result with the МЦТТ in the form used for notifying service results; the Commission on intellectual property decides on the expediency of securing the rights for the University and the decision is communicated to the author through the МЦТТ; and the University and the author conclude an assignment contract whose remuneration may not exceed the amounts recommended at the University for service results [14].

## Results created by students

When a student creates a result, the rights are distributed in accordance with the contract between the student and the University that provides for or envisages the creation of that result [15]. A condition on the University acquiring the exclusive right and the right to obtain a patent is included in such contracts where [16]:

- the result is created in performance of the University's obligations to third parties and the transfer of the right to the customer is required by Russian legislation or by the contract [16];
- the result in the scientific and technical sphere is one whose information is to be protected in a confidentiality regime, including by introducing a commercial secrecy regime [16];
- the name or content of the result includes designations directly or indirectly associated with the University — its full or abbreviated name, its means of individualisation, other identifying designations, names of projects, structural subdivisions (including branches), educational programmes and courses [16];
- the result is a complex (art. 1240 of the Civil Code) or composite (p. 2 of art. 1260) object, or its creation is organised by the University and requires significant financial, material, organisational or other costs, or the participation of a significant (more than 10) number of employees, or by its nature requires periodic updating [16].

The inclusion of such conditions in contracts between a student and the University is ensured by the head of the relevant works [17]. The exclusive rights to course works, final qualification works and dissertations created by a student belong to the student [18].

## Rights of authors and remuneration

Whatever the distribution of the exclusive right, the author keeps the right of authorship and, in the cases provided by the Civil Code, the right to a name and other personal non-property rights [19]; these rights are inalienable and non-transferable [20].

Where the exclusive right to a result is secured for the University, the employee or student who created it may be given the opportunity to receive methodological help from the University in protecting personal non-property and other rights, to take part in determining the directions and ways of using the result, including where that use is commercial, and to create jointly with the University and/or participate in the capital of legal entities whose activity is the practical application (introduction) of such results [21].

In the cases and manner provided by part four of the Civil Code, an employee who created a service result acquires the right to remuneration, whose amount, conditions and procedure are determined by the contract between the University and the employee and, in case of a dispute, by a court; the recommended amounts of remuneration to authors of service results and the procedure for calculating them are established by the Commission on intellectual property [22].

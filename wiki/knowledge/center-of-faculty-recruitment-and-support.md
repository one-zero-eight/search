# Center for Faculty Recruitment and Support (Центр привлечения и поддержки ППС) — place, tasks, functions and rights

## The order and the document it approved

The Regulation on the Center for Faculty Recruitment and Support (Центр привлечения и поддержки ППС) of the Development Department (Управление развития университета) of ANO VO «Университет Иннополис» was approved in a new edition by an order of the University of 27 March 2026 No. ОД/АД‐02/2026/03/27, issued in «г. Иннополис» and signed by the Director for Development and HR Policy R.F. Valiev [1]. The order explains that it is issued to bring the Regulation of the structural subdivision up to date and into line with the current functions of the subdivision [1]. The page prints the order number with a non-breaking hyphen and repeats parts of the signature line in garbled characters [1].

The order requires the head of the Center, Tsyunchuk T.R., to ensure timely familiarisation, against signature, with the approved Regulation of the employees of the subdivision as well as of employees newly hired or transferred (moved) into the subdivision from other subdivisions of the University [1]. It declares invalid the Regulation on the Отдел привлечения и поддержки ППС approved by order of ANO VO «Университет Иннополис» of 26 May 2021 No. ОД/АД-03/2021/05/26, and the signer keeps control over the execution of the order and of the Regulation [1].

The Regulation itself regulates the Center's activity and determines its tasks, functions, rights and duties [2].

## Place in the structure

The Center is a structural subdivision of the Development Department of the University and belongs to the category «иные структурные подразделения» (other structural subdivisions) [3]. It reports directly to the Director for Development and HR Policy [4].

In its work the Center follows the Constitution of the Russian Federation, the Labour Code of the Russian Federation, Federal Law «Об образовании в Российской Федерации», other legislative and normative legal acts on higher education institutions, the University's Charter, decisions of the Academic and Supervisory councils, orders and instructions of the Director of the University, other local acts and this Regulation [5].

The structure, the number of staff and the staffing table of the Center, and changes to them, are approved by the Director of the University [6]. The Center is headed by its head, who is hired and dismissed by an order of the Director of the University [7]. The working conditions of the Center's employees are determined by the employment contracts concluded with each of them and by the University's internal labour regulations [8]. The rights, duties and degree of responsibility of the Center's employees are established by job descriptions [9].

Employees of the Center may be involved, within their competences, in work under grants, projects, subsidies and other programmes carried out by the University [10]. The Regulation and additions and changes to it are approved by order of the Director of the University or of another person authorised by him [11].

The earlier Regulation on the Development Department of 4 April 2021 lists among the units of that department the Faculty Recruitment and Support Office (Отдел привлечения и поддержки ППС) [12], while the order of 27 March 2026 declares the Regulation on that Office invalid and approves a Regulation on the Center instead [1].

## Tasks

- ensuring the full cycle of attracting the teaching staff (профессорско-преподавательский состав, ППС): from organising the competitive selection to concluding employment contracts or civil-law contracts and to personnel administration [1]
- organising comprehensive support of the teaching staff during their work at the University and their stay on the territory of the Russian Federation, including migration, logistic, everyday and administrative questions, on a «single window» model [1]
- ensuring the processes of adaptation, attestation and documentary support of the teaching staff's activity, including recognition of foreign qualifications and academic degrees, conferment of the academic title, and paperwork for business trips, vacations and reporting [1]
- helping the publication activity of the teaching staff by organising payment of publication fees and registration fees for participation in conferences [1]
- coordinating the interaction of the University's structural subdivisions on questions within the Center's competence [1]
- protecting the rights and lawful interests of the University and observing legality in the activity connected with the teaching staff [1]

## Functions

In the sphere of recruitment and hiring of the teaching staff [1]:

- organising and conducting competitive procedures to fill vacant positions of the teaching staff [1]
- organising the conclusion of employment contracts and civil-law contracts with the teaching staff [1]
- organising recognition of foreign qualifications and academic degrees and the conferment of the academic title [1]
- coordinating the processes of adaptation and integration of new members of the teaching staff [1]

In the sphere of documentary and administrative support [1]:

- documentary support of personnel changes (business trips, vacations, memoranda) [1]
- organising and controlling the passing of attestation by the teaching staff [1]
- preparing reports on the activity of the teaching staff at the request of the management [1]
- keeping up to date the section of the University's website with information about the teaching staff in Russian and English, and the internal database on the teaching staff (number, personal data, education and other data) [1]

In the sphere of migration and logistic support [1]:

- arranging invitations and work permits (patents, residence permits) for foreign members of the teaching staff [1]
- organising medical insurance, accommodation (housing), preparation of workplaces and passes [1]
- coordinating the logistics of the arrival and settling in of local, foreign and out-of-town teachers [1]

In the sphere of operational support of the teaching staff («single window») [1]:

- organising and controlling the work on resolving administrative, everyday, medical and housing questions of the teaching staff [1]
- providing consultational (language, logistic, socio-cultural and other) support to members of the teaching staff, their families and close relatives [1]

In the sphere of publication activity and events [1]:

- helping with payment of publication fees in scientific journals [1]
- helping with payment of registration fees for the participation of the teaching staff in conferences [1]
- organisational support of events with the participation of the teaching staff (meetings, sessions, seminars), including booking rooms, technical support and preparing materials [1]

In the sphere of interaction with the University's subdivisions the Center coordinates work with the Personnel Records and Movement Department, the Accounting Department, the Financial Service, the Legal Directorate, the Academic Department, the Methodological Department, the Center for Interaction with Students and Graduates, the Organisational and Technical Department, the User Technical Support Department and the Labour Protection and Fire Safety Service on questions of the Center's competence [1].

## Rights

In its activity the Center has the right to [13]:

- request from the University's structural subdivisions the information and materials needed for the work within the Center's competence [13]
- with the consent of the heads of the University's structural subdivisions, involve the employees of those subdivisions in preparing draft local acts and other documents and in developing and carrying out measures conducted by the Center [14]
- take part in meetings held by the University's management when discussing questions whose solution falls within the Center's competence [15]
- submit to the University's management proposals to cancel, amend or supplement the University's local acts [16]
- interact with bodies of state authority and administration, legal entities and the University's subdivisions on questions within the Center's competence [17]

## Interaction and reporting

Interaction of the Center with the University's other structural subdivisions is determined by the tasks and functions assigned to it by this Regulation [18]. Interaction with other structural subdivisions takes place on questions of preparing draft local acts, orders, instructions, contracts and other documents and of preparing conclusions and written and oral certificates on questions within the Center's competence arising in operational activity [19]. The Center may be a base for the practical training of the University's students [20].

Within its activity the Center prepares and keeps up to date the section of the University's website with information about the teaching staff in Russian and English, the internal database on the teaching staff (number, personal data, data on education, qualifications and other data), reporting on the course of competitive procedures, attestation, migration records and other indicators of the Center's activity at the request of the management, and documentation on personnel changes of the teaching staff (business trips, vacations and the like) [1].

The Center is responsible for compliance with the legislation on personal data when processing information about the teaching staff [1].

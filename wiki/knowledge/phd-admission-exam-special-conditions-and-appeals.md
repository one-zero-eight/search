# Special conditions at PhD entrance examinations and appeals (2026/2027 admission rules)

## Where this comes from

Section V and section VI of the Rules of admission to aspirantura programmes of ANO VO «Innopolis University» for the 2026/2027 academic year, approved by order No. ОД/УД-01/2026/01/19 of 19 January 2026 [1]. The page is a scanned document read by OCR, and the wiki warns that its numbers and dates should be checked against the PDF [1].

## Special conditions at the entrance examinations

At the entrance examinations for applicants from among disabled persons and persons with health limitations the University creates conditions that take account of the applicants' psycho-physical development, individual possibilities and state of health, and gives them information about the procedure of the examinations in a form accessible to them [2]. The buildings provide unhindered access to classrooms, toilets and other premises (ramps, lifts, handrails, widened doorways, lifts), and where there are no lifts the examinations are held in classrooms on the first floor [1]. The duration of an entrance examination for such applicants is increased by 1 hour [3].

The examinations are held in a separate classroom, and the number of such applicants in one classroom must not exceed 12 persons in written form and 6 persons in oral form; a larger number, or holding the examinations together with other applicants, is allowed if it creates no difficulties, and an assistant from among the University's staff or engaged persons may be present to give technical help (to take a workplace, move about, read and fill in the task, communicate with the persons conducting the examination) [4]. Applicants may use technical means they need because of their individual characteristics [5].

Additional requirements apply depending on the individual characteristics [6]:

- for blind applicants the tasks are prepared in Braille or as an electronic document readable with a computer with special software for the blind, or are read out by an assistant, and written tasks are done on Braille paper, on a computer with special software for the blind or dictated to an assistant, with writing materials and Braille paper or such a computer provided where needed (for in-person examinations) [6];
- for visually impaired applicants individual even lighting of not less than 300 lux is provided, a magnifying device is provided where needed (own devices may also be used) and tasks and instructions are printed in enlarged type, for in-person examinations [6];
- for deaf and hard-of-hearing applicants sound-amplifying equipment for collective use is provided, individual sound-amplifying equipment is provided where needed and sign language interpreting services are provided [6];
- for deaf-blind applicants tactile sign language interpreting services are provided, in addition to the requirements for blind and deaf applicants [6];
- for applicants with severe speech disorders, deaf and hard-of-hearing applicants, examinations held in oral form are held in written form by decision of the University [6];
- for applicants with disorders of the musculoskeletal system, disorders of the motor functions of the upper limbs or absence of the upper limbs, written tasks are done on a computer with special software or dictated to an assistant, and written examinations are held in oral form by decision of the University [6].

These conditions are provided on the basis of the application for admission, which contains information on the need to create the corresponding special conditions [7]. A document confirming disability or health limitations is submitted not later than 10 days before the examination [8].

## Appeal against the procedure or the result of an examination

An applicant has the right to file with the appeal commission an appeal about a violation, in his opinion, of the established procedure of holding an entrance examination and (or) about disagreement with the assessment received for an entrance examination [9]. The appeal is filed in person or by sending it to the e-mail address admissions@innopolis.ru [10]. In the consideration of the appeal the observance of the established procedure of the examination and the results of the checking and assessment of the work done are verified [11].

- The appeal is filed on the day the results of the entrance examination are announced or during the next working day, and an appeal about a violation of the procedure may also be filed on the day of the examination [12].
- The appeal is considered not later than the next working day after the day it was filed [13].
- The applicant has the right to be present at the consideration of the appeal [14].
- After the consideration the appeal commission decides to change the assessment or to leave it unchanged; the decision, formalised by a protocol, is communicated to the applicant, and the fact that the applicant has been made acquainted with it is confirmed by his signature at a personal appearance or by a letter sent to his personal e-mail address from admissions@innopolis.ru [15].

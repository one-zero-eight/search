# Individual curriculum, accelerated study and credit for previous study in additional general education programmes (ДОП)

## Where these rules come from

The rules below are section 6 of the Regulation on the additional general education programme (ДОП) of ANO VO «Innopolis University», approved by order of 22 February 2023 [1]. They concern learners of additional general education programmes (ДОП), not students of the University's main professional programmes [2].

Learners are granted the academic right to study under an individual curriculum, including accelerated study, within the ДОП being mastered [2]. An individual curriculum is a curriculum that ensures mastery of the educational programme on the basis of individualisation of its content with account of the characteristics and educational needs of the particular learner [3].

## When a learner may be transferred to an individual curriculum

Transfer to an individual curriculum is possible in the following cases:

- transfer to accelerated study [4];
- mastering the ДОП by a disabled person, a disabled child or a person with health limitations [5];
- ensuring the learner's academic right to combine education with work without detriment to mastering the educational programme [6];
- other cases by decision of the University [7].

The decision on transfer is taken by the University director or another person authorised by the director, on the proposal of the head of the structural subdivision delivering the ДОП, on the basis of an application of the learner or of the parents (legal representatives) of a minor learner, with documents attached if available [8]. The application forms are annex 2 (application of the learner) and annex 3 (application of the parent or legal representative) to the Regulation; both are addressed to University Director K.V. Semenikhin and agreed with the head of the responsible structural subdivision [1].

The application may be submitted at any period of mastering the ДОП [9]. An individual curriculum is developed for one learner or for a group of learners; learners are combined into a group where their choice coincides as to content, place, terms and (or) other conditions of mastering the ДОП [10].

## Accelerated study

Accelerated study is study under an individual curriculum within the educational programme being mastered in a shorter period compared with the period of obtaining education set by the educational programme [11]. Accelerated study under a ДОП is implemented by crediting (зачёт) the results of mastering subjects, courses, disciplines (modules), practice and additional educational programmes [12].

## How credit (зачёт) is applied for

Credit is given on the application of the learner or of the parents (legal representatives) of a minor learner — annex 4 and annex 5 to the Regulation respectively, both addressed to University Director K.V. Semenikhin and agreed with the head of the responsible structural subdivision [13], [1].

The application rests on documents confirming the results of the study completed [13]:

- a document of education and (or) qualification, including one obtained in a foreign state [13];
- a document of study, including a certificate of study or of a period of study, or a document issued by foreign organisations (a certificate, an academic certificate or another document) [13].

The application is submitted in person on paper at the University, or as an electronic document via the Internet — a paper document converted into electronic form by scanning or photographing with machine-readable recognition of its details [14].

No credit is made for results confirmed by documents of education and (or) qualification obtained in a foreign state that do not meet the conditions provided by part 3 of article 107 of Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29 December 2012, or confirmed by documents of study issued by foreign organisations [15].

## How correspondence is established

Credit is made by comparing the planned results for the corresponding part of the ДОП being mastered (a subject, course, discipline (module)) with the results of the study completed earlier [16]. The University credits the results where correspondence of the results is established [17]; correspondence is established exclusively on the documents submitted by the learner or the parents [18].

The results of the study completed earlier are taken to be the name of the educational programme (its part), the number of academic hours allotted to it and the grade from interim assessment in it [19].

Correspondence is established where all of the following conditions hold together [20]:

- the name of the earlier programme (its part) coincides with the name of the part of the ДОП being mastered; the names may differ textually but be equivalent, or include the corresponding name [20];
- the number of academic hours allotted to the earlier programme (its part) is not less than 75 % of the volume of the part of the ДОП being mastered [20];
- the grading system of the interim assessment in the earlier programme (its part) coincides with the grading system of the part being mastered [20].

Credited results are counted as the results of interim assessment for the corresponding part of the ДОП being mastered [21].

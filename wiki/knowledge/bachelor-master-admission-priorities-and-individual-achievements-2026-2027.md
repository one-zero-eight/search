# Special rights and individual achievements in bachelor's and master's admission 2026/2027 — the competitive score

## What the competitive score is made of

The sum of competitive points is calculated as the sum of points for the entrance examinations and for the individual achievements [1]. Individual achievements are taken into account in two ways: the University adds points to the sum of competitive points, and it takes the achievements into account where applicants are equal on the other criteria of ranking in the competitive lists [1].

- On bachelor's programmes the number of points for general individual achievements is not more than 10 (ten), and points for target individual achievements — 5 (five) — are added on top of them for admission to places within the target quota [1].
- On master's programmes not more than 8 (eight) points in total may be awarded for individual achievements [1].
- Where applicants are equal on the other criteria of ranking on bachelor's programmes, the achievements are taken into account in this order: 1) the score in mathematics; 2) the average score of the document of education; this list may be extended during the admission, and the procedure of account is set by the University [1].
- Where several documents confirm a special right under part 4 or part 12 of article 71 of Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ, the University determines for each whether it is used to obtain the special right or points for an individual achievement, counting the greater number of points [1].

## Special rights in bachelor's admission

- Persons named in part 4 of article 71 of Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ have the right to admission without entrance examinations [1].
- Persons named in part 12 of article 71 of Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ have the right to admission without entrance examinations and the right to be equated to persons who have scored the maximum number of ЕГЭ points in the subject corresponding to the profile of the pupil olympiad, or to persons who have successfully passed additional entrance examinations of profile, creative and/or professional orientation; in that case the applicant is given the highest result of the entrance examination — 100 (one hundred) points (the right to 100 points); both rights may be granted to the same applicant [1].
- Persons having the right to admission without entrance examinations under part 4 of article 71 are also given a special advantage — they are equated to persons having 100 (one hundred) points in the general education entrance examination or examinations, for the term of that right [1].
- The right to admission without entrance examinations under parts 4 and/or 12 of article 71 is used as a single right: it is used to file an application to only one organisation and only for one educational programme at the applicant's choice, regardless of the number of grounds; within one organisation and one programme it may be used in different competition groups, and where the competitive profile covers an area or several areas of study or several educational programmes it is used for the whole competitive profile [1].
- The University establishes the correspondence between the competitive profiles and the profiles of the final stage of the All-Russian olympiad of pupils and of the international olympiads named in part 4 of article 71, and the special advantage in the general education subjects that coincide with those profiles [1].
- For the rights under part 12 of article 71 the University sets its own list of pupil olympiads from the lists approved by the federal body, and for each olympiad the correspondence between competitive profiles and olympiad profiles for the right to admission without examinations and one or several general education examinations for the right to 100 points [1].
- For each special right the University establishes that the right is granted to winners and prizewinners of the olympiad, that the results are counted for the 9–11 grades, and one or several subjects in which the applicant needs results of the ЕГЭ or of an internal general education examination confirming the right, with a number of points of not less than 75 (seventy-five), in one subject of the applicant's choice from those established by the University [1].
- For one ground giving the right to 100 points the applicant receives 100 points within one competition in one general education entrance examination (where several examinations correspond to the olympiad, in the one he chooses); he may use several grounds at once, and in several competitions he may use the same ground in one and the same examination or in different ones [1].
- Further special rights are: admission to places within the special quota under part 5 of article 71, admission to places within the separate quota under parts 5.1 and 5.2 of article 71, the preferential right of enrolment under part 9 of article 71 and the preferential right of enrolment under part 10 of article 71 [1].

## Bachelor's individual achievements (Appendix 3)

Appendix 3 to the 2026/2027 admission rules is also published as a separate extract from the rules, which reproduces the list of achievements, the status or document confirming each of them, and the points [2]. The list and points are [2]:

- a document of education or of education and qualification with honours obtained in an educational organisation of the Russian Federation — 5 points; the documents named are an attestat of secondary general education with honours, an attestat of secondary (complete) general education with honours, an attestat of secondary (complete) general education for those awarded a gold (silver) medal, a diploma of secondary vocational education with honours, a diploma of primary vocational education with honours and a diploma of primary vocational education for those awarded a gold (silver) medal [2]
- stay in volunteer formations under a contract on voluntary assistance in carrying out the tasks assigned to the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation during the special military operation in the territories of Ukraine, the Donetsk People's Republic, the Lugansk People's Republic, the Zaporozhye region and the Kherson region — 2 points [2]
- the status of winner (prizewinner) of the national and/or international championship of professional skill among disabled persons and persons with health limitations «Абилимпикс» — 2 points [2]
- volunteering of not less than 60 hours, confirmed by a volunteer book or an electronic volunteer book, if not more than 2 years have passed from the end of the period of that activity to the day of completion of the acceptance of documents — 1 point [2]
- the All-Russian olympiad of pupils (final stage) in mathematics, informatics, physics, «Труд (технология)» with the profiles «Робототехника» and «Информационная безопасность» (9–11 grades): a diploma of participant — 8 points [2]
- the regional stage of the same olympiad and profiles (9–11 grades): a diploma of winner — 6 points, of prizewinner — 4 points, of participant — 2 points [2]
- the Russian championship (Первенство России) in sports programming, profile «Спортивное программирование» (9–11 grades): a diploma of winner — 6 points, of prizewinner — 4 points, of participant of the final stage — 2 points [2]
- the international olympiad «Innopolis Open» (9–11 grades): a diploma of winner — 6 points, of prizewinner — 4 points, of participant of the final stage — 2 points [2]
- the All-Russian olympiad in artificial intelligence (open also to foreign learners), profile «Искусственный интеллект» (9–11 grades): a diploma of winner (prizewinner) — 2 points [2]
- the Russian robotics olympiad in the profiles «Интеллектуальные робототехнические системы», «Мехатроника и робототехника», «Проекты интеллектуальных робототехнических систем» and «Робототехника» (9–11 grades): a diploma of prizewinner of the 1st degree — 10 points, of the 2nd degree — 8 points, of the 3rd degree — 6 points [2]
- the all-Russian tournament of pupils «InnoCTF Junior» (9–11 grades): a diploma of prizewinner of the 1st degree — 10 points, of the 2nd degree — 8 points, of the 3rd degree — 6 points [2]
- the All-Russian team olympiad of pupils in programming (9–11 grades): a diploma of prizewinner of the 1st degree — 10 points, of the 2nd degree — 8 points, of the 3rd degree — 6 points [2]
- the Russian movement of children and youth «Движение первых»: a diploma, certificate or other confirming document — 2 points, if not more than 1 year has passed from the participation to the day of completion of the acceptance of documents [2]
- for the volunteer formations the confirming documents are documents from the military unit (certificates, orders, extracts) and a record in the military ID; for the olympiads and tournaments, the corresponding diploma [2]
- the points for pupil olympiads apply if the results of participation are not used for special rights and/or the special advantage in admission to the given competition groups [1]
- in the wiki copies of the appendix — both in the rules and in the separate extract — some rows of the table are scrambled by extraction, so for those rows the pairing of the event with its status and points cannot be read with certainty, and some figures appear in more than one row [1], [2]

## Master's individual achievements (Appendix 4)

Appendix 4 to the 2026/2027 admission rules is published in the same extract from the rules and lists the achievements taken into account on master's programmes [2]:

- a document of higher education and qualification with honours (diploma with honours) — 5 points [2]
- the InnoGlobalHack hackathon: a certificate of winner — 6 points, a certificate of prizewinner — 4 points [2]
- participation in the Winter and Summer school of bachelors of the University — a certificate of participant — 5 points [2]
- a scientific publication in Scopus, Web of Science or ВАК (the white list) published not earlier than 2023 — 4 points, confirmed by a copy of the article from the published journal (collection) showing the title of the article, the list of authors, the number of the journal or the name of the collection, the year of publication, the elibrary id of the publication or its DOI, and other information where there is any [2]

# Citizens' appeals to the University (обращения граждан) — the Regulation of 18 October 2024

## The document

The Regulation on the procedure for considering citizens' appeals in ANO VO «Innopolis University» (Регламент) establishes the procedure for considering appeals, taking decisions on them and sending answers, and determines the terms and sequence of administrative procedures [1]. The copy on the wiki carries an approval block of a University order of 18 October 2024 signed by I.I. Bariev; the page is an OCR of a scanned document and its review asks that dates and numbers be checked against the PDF [1].

When considering appeals the University is guided by the requirements of Federal Law No. 59-ФЗ of 2 May 2006, which is not obligatory for it but is used as a recommendatory document by the University's voluntary decision [1].

The Regulation applies to written (including electronic), individual and collective appeals of citizens and does not apply to oral appeals [1].

For its purposes an appeal of a citizen is a written proposal, application (заявление) or complaint (жалоба) addressed to the University, and an official (должностное лицо) is a University employee performing organisational-administrative and administrative-economic functions [1].

## What an appeal must contain

The University accepts for consideration written appeals containing: the name of the University or the surname, name and patronymic of the official whose competence includes the matters raised; the citizen's surname, name and patronymic (if available); the postal or electronic address to which the answer must be sent; the essence of the proposal, application or complaint; and a personal signature and date [2]. Where necessary the citizen may attach documents and materials supporting the arguments [2].

## Where to send an appeal

- A citizen sends a written appeal directly to the University or to the officials whose competence includes the matters raised [3].
- An appeal sent by post or by courier goes to the postal address 420500, Republic of Tatarstan, Innopolis, ul. Universitetskaya, d. 1 [3].
- An appeal may also be sent by e-mail, the page giving a separate address for each category of addressee: a citizen who is a student, aspirant or master's student of the University; a citizen who is a listener of additional professional and additional general education programmes, or a learner or legal representative of a learner under additional general education programmes implemented by the Institute of Additional Education; a citizen who is a learner or legal representative of a learner under additional general education programmes implemented by the IT School; and other categories of citizens — the e-mail addresses themselves are garbled in the page's OCR and should be read from the PDF [4] [1].
- If an appeal arrives at a structural subdivision not authorised to consider it, the head of that subdivision must, no later than two working days, forward it to the subdivision responsible for considering appeals under the Regulation or to the Sector for Registration and Control of Documents of the Documentation Department [4].

## Registration

- Appeals are accepted in written form through postal correspondence, the University's electronic systems for receiving appeals, including electronic appeals sent to the University's e-mail addresses, and in person when submitted to an authorised University employee [5].
- Where necessary, several structural subdivisions of the University may be involved in considering an appeal [6].
- Written appeals are registered in the procedure established by the relevant local act no later than 2 working days from the date of receipt by the University [7].
- Registration and placing under control are done by the responsible subdivisions in the journals of registration of citizens' appeals according to the forms in appendices 1 and 2, with the abbreviation ОБР and the conditional designation corresponding to the subdivision's index in the approved nomenclature of files [8].
- On registration the full information about the applicant, the date of receipt and the information about the responsible executor are recorded; where a task is given to two or more employees, the responsible executor is the employee named first in the resolution [9].
- If an appeal does not meet the requirements of section 2, the citizen is sent a refusal to consider it with the corresponding reasons, within the terms established for considering an appeal [10].

## Which subdivision handles an appeal

The responsible subdivisions are assigned according to the addressee of the appeal [11]:

- for a citizen who is a student, aspirant or master's student of the University — the Department for Support and Development of Students of the Centre for Interaction with Students and Alumni [11];
- for a citizen who is a listener of additional professional and additional general education programmes, or a learner or legal representative of a learner under additional general education programmes implemented by the Institute of Additional Education — the Sector of the Support Service for Automation of Processes of the Institute of Additional Education [11];
- for a citizen who is a learner or legal representative of a learner under additional general education programmes implemented by the IT School — the IT School of the Centre for Pre-University Training and Admission of Applicants [11];
- for other categories of citizens — the Sector for Registration and Control of Documents of the Documentation Department [11].

Appeals received by the Sector for Registration and Control of Documents of the Documentation Department are registered and distributed among the University's structural subdivisions depending on their competence [11].

The heads of those subdivisions are responsible for checking an appeal against the requirements of section 2 and issuing a reasoned refusal where it does not comply, for registering it within the established term, for sending electronic copies of written appeals to the executors in the corporate portal «Битрикс», for the responsible executor's consideration of the appeal, the decision on it and the sending of the answer, and for control over the sending of the answer within the established term [12].

## Particular cases

- If an appeal contains information about a wrongful act being prepared, committed or already committed, or about the person preparing, committing or having committed it, the appeal is to be sent to the state body according to its competence [13].
- If a written appeal repeats a question on which the citizen has repeatedly received written answers on the merits in connection with earlier appeals, and the appeal brings no new arguments or circumstances, the authorised official may decide that the next appeal is unfounded and stop correspondence with the citizen on that question, notifying the citizen of the decision [14].
- If a written appeal contains obscene or offensive expressions or threats to the life, health or property of an official or of members of his family, the citizen who sent the appeal is informed of the inadmissibility of abuse of right [15].
- If the text of a written appeal cannot be read, no answer is given to it and it is not forwarded for consideration to a person according to his competence [16].
- In the cases listed in the Regulation the citizen is sent a corresponding notification within 10 working days from the date of registration of the appeal [1].

## Terms of consideration

A written appeal received by the University is considered within 30 days from the date of its registration, including the preparation and sending of the answer [17]. Where the appeal is complex or an additional request connected with its consideration has to be sent, the term may be extended by no more than 30 days, with notification of the citizen about the extension [18].

## Consideration and answer

- University staff ensure an objective, comprehensive and timely consideration of the appeal; request the documents and materials needed from the University's structural subdivisions and from the citizen himself; take measures aimed at restoring or protecting the citizen's violated rights, freedoms and lawful interests; and give a written answer on the merits of the questions raised [19].
- The answer to the appeal is signed by the University director or by another authorised official whose competence includes the matter [20].
- The answer to an appeal received by the University or by a certain employee through other channels of communication is sent to the postal address or e-mail address indicated in the appeal [21].

## Control

- Control over the consideration of an appeal includes checking compliance with the terms, the correctness and completeness of execution, and the timeliness of preparing and sending the answer to the applicant [22].
- Appeals are removed from control if the questions raised in them have been considered, the necessary measures taken and written answers given, in particular where a written answer is sent to the citizen on the questions raised or explanations are given about the procedure for protecting his rights and lawful interests, and where the appeal is forwarded to another employee whose competence includes the matters raised [23].
- The heads of the subdivisions named in the Regulation and the responsible executors for citizens' appeals appointed by the University's management bear personal responsibility for compliance with the terms and the procedure of performing the Regulation's functions [24].
- Following checks, where violations of citizens' rights are found, the guilty persons are held liable in accordance with the legislation of the Russian Federation [25].

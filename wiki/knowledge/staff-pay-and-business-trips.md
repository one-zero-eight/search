# Staff pay and business trips (Правила внутреннего трудового распорядка)

## What wages consist of

Under the employer's pay system fixed in its internal normative acts, an employee's wage includes remuneration for work depending on the employee's qualification and on the complexity, quantity, quality and conditions of the work performed, compensation payments (compensatory supplements and allowances) and incentive payments (incentive supplements and allowances, bonuses and other incentive payments) [1]. The amount of the salary (должностной оклад) is set on the basis of the University's staffing table [2]. Incentive payments, supplements and bonuses are established in accordance with the internal normative acts approved by the employer [3].

## Pay for reduced and part-time work

Employees for whom a reduced working time is established are paid in the amount provided for the normal working time, except employees under 18 [4]; the work of employees under 18 is paid taking the reduced working time into account [5]. Where part-time work is established, pay is made in proportion to the time worked [6].

## Travel expenses for work of a travelling nature

Employees whose employment contract contains a condition on the travelling nature of the work have their transport expenses compensated in the procedure and on the conditions of the employer's internal normative acts (Annex 3 to the Rules) [7]. Where the employee bought a travel ticket with their own funds, its cost is reimbursed after the ticket is presented to the Financial and Economic Department with a memo and the ticket attached, not later than three working days after the end of the month in which it was bought [7].

## When and how wages are paid

Wages are paid every half month in the following order [8]:

- the payment for the time actually worked from the 1st to the 15th of the accounting month is made on the 29th of the accounting month (the advance); in determining it the employee's salary (tariff rate) for the time worked is taken into account, as well as allowances for the time worked whose calculation does not depend on the assessment of the month's results or on fulfilment of the monthly norm of working time and labour norms [8]
- the payment for the time actually worked from the 16th to the last day of the accounting month is made on the 14th of the month following the accounting month and includes, among other things, compensation payments whose calculation depends on fulfilment of the monthly norm of working time; if the advance was overpaid or underpaid in the first half of the month, a recalculation is made and taken into account in this payment [8]

Where the payment day coincides with a day off or a non-working holiday, wages are paid before those days; the payment for leave time is made not later than three days before the leave begins [9].

Wages are paid in the currency of the Russian Federation (roubles), and the form of the pay slip is given in Annex 4 to the Rules [10]. Payments under the pay system are made by bank transfer to the employee's salary account opened under one of the employer's salary projects, chosen by the employee, or to the employee's account in another credit organisation named in a written application [11]. Where wages are paid to a salary account under a salary project, the employer bears the cost of servicing the bank card [12]. If the card is lost, stolen or blocked, the employee may, by agreement with the employer, receive wages in cash at the cash desk at the place of work or by transfer to the bank account of a third person, on a written application explaining the reasons and stating the period and the account details [13].

The employee may change the credit organisation to which wages are transferred by sending the employer an application to change the details not later than 14 calendar days before the day of payment [14]. The employer transfers taxes from the employee's wages in the amounts and procedure of the current legislation [15].

## Pay during suspension and idle time

During suspension from work (non-admission to work) no wages are accrued, except in the cases provided by the Labour Code or other federal laws [16]. Where the suspension is connected with failure to pass training and knowledge testing in labour protection or a medical examination not through the employee's fault, the whole period of suspension is paid as idle time [16]. Idle time through the employee's fault is not paid [17].

## Indexation

Ensuring an increase in the real content of wages, including indexation of wages in connection with the growth of consumer prices for goods and services (article 134 of the Labour Code), is carried out by paying bonuses in accordance with the Procedure for Bonuses of Employees of ANO VO «Innopolis University», and may also be carried out by revising salaries, paying allowances and other incentive payments provided by the employer's local acts and the current legislation [18].

## Business trips

An employee is sent on a business trip on the basis of an order of the Director for a certain period to carry out an official task outside the place of permanent work [19]. The trip of an employee who works outside the employer's location (remotely) and is sent by order of the Director to the employer's location at the address: Verkhneuslonsky district, Innopolis, Universitetskaya st. 1, is also recognised as a business trip [19].

When an employee is sent on a business trip, the place of work and average earnings are retained [20]. Where the average earnings for the period of the trip turn out to be less than the wages accrued from the salary and monthly allowances, the employee is guaranteed a supplement up to the earnings calculated from the salary with account of the monthly allowances [20].

The procedure and conditions of sending the University's employees on business trips are established in the «Regulation on the procedure and conditions of sending employees of ANO VO «Innopolis University» on business trips», which determines the main rules and procedure for trips both within the Russian Federation and to foreign states [21].

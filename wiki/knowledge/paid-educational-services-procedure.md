# Paid educational services — the procedure (Порядок оказания платных образовательных услуг) and its entry into force

## The document and its approval

The Порядок оказания платных образовательных услуг в АНО ВО «Университет Иннополис» was approved by order of 30 December 2020 № ОД/УД-02/2020/12/30, signed by the director K.V. Semenikhin [1]. The order puts the procedure into effect on 1 January 2021 [1].

The order was issued in connection with the entry into force, on 1 January 2021, of Постановление Правительства РФ of 15.09.2020 № 1441 «Об утверждении Правил оказания платных образовательных услуг», and in order to organise work on providing paid educational services in the University in accordance with the requirements of the Federal Law of 29.12.2012 № 273-ФЗ «Об образовании в Российской Федерации» [1].

The procedure is approved as an annex to the order; the page reproduces the order itself, not the text of the procedure [1].

## What it replaced

The order repeals, from 1 January 2021, the previous Порядок оказания платных образовательных услуг of the University, approved by order of 16 May 2018 № ОД/УД-01/2018/05/16 [1].

## What the procedure itself opens with

The procedure was developed in accordance with the education law of 29 December 2012 № 273-ФЗ, the law of 29 December 2006 № 256-ФЗ «О дополнительных мерах государственной поддержки семей, имеющих детей» and Постановление of the Government of the Russian Federation of 15 September 2020 № 1441, and it determines the procedure for providing paid educational services in the University [2].

## The terms it uses (п. 2)

- «Университет», «исполнитель» — the autonomous non-profit organisation of higher education «Университет Иннополис», which carries on educational activity and provides paid educational services to a student [4].
- «Заказчик» — a physical and/or legal person intending to order or ordering paid educational services for itself or for other persons under a contract [4].
- «Обучающийся» — a physical person mastering an educational programme [4].
- «Платные образовательные услуги» — educational activity carried out on the order and at the expense of physical and/or legal persons under education contracts concluded on admission [4].
- «Недостаток платных образовательных услуг» — non-compliance with the mandatory requirements of law, with the terms of the contract (or, in their absence or incompleteness, with the usual requirements), with the purposes for which such services are usually used or with the purposes of which the University was informed by the customer, including provision not in the full volume envisaged by the educational programme (part of it) [4].
- «Существенный недостаток платных образовательных услуг» — an irremovable defect, or one that cannot be removed without disproportionate expense or loss of time, or one revealed repeatedly, or one appearing anew after removal [4].

## Paid services and education financed from the budget

- Paid educational services may not be provided instead of educational activity whose financial provision comes from budget appropriations of the federal budget, budgets of the subjects of the Russian Federation or local budgets; the clause carries the number «Зь» in the OCR of the page [3].
- Organisations financed from those budgets may provide, at the expense of physical and/or legal persons, paid services not envisaged by the state or municipal assignment or by an agreement on a subsidy, on identical conditions for the same services [5].
- The customer's refusal of additional paid services not envisaged by the contract already concluded cannot be a ground for changing the volume and conditions of the educational services already provided under that contract [6].

## The University's duty and right as to the services

- The University must ensure that the customer and the student receive the paid educational services in full, in accordance with the educational programmes (part of the programme) and the terms of the contract [7].
- The University may reduce the cost of the paid educational services under the contract, covering the missing part from its own funds, including funds from income-generating activity, voluntary donations and targeted contributions of physical and/or legal persons; the grounds and procedure for the reduction are set by a local normative act and are brought to the knowledge of the customer and the student [8].

## Cost

- The cost may be paid with the means (part of the means) of maternity (family) capital under article 11 of the Federal Law of 29.12.2006 № 256-ФЗ [9].
- An increase of the cost after the contract is concluded is not allowed, except an increase by the level of inflation envisaged by the main characteristics of the federal budget for the next financial year and the planning period [10].

## Rights of students

Students of the University have the right to free use of the library and information resources and of the University's educational, production and scientific base, and to publish their works in the University's publications free of charge [11].

## Information before the contract

- The University must provide the customer with reliable information about itself and about the paid educational services offered before the contract is concluded and throughout its term, so that the customer can choose correctly [12].
- The University must bring to the customer the information in the scope and manner envisaged by the law of the Russian Federation of 07.02.1992 № 2300-1 «О защите прав потребителей» and the Federal Law of 29.12.2012 № 273-ФЗ [13].
- That information is provided at the place where the University actually carries on educational activity under the higher education programmes: 420500, Russian Federation, Republic of Tatarstan, city of Innopolis, ул. Университетская, д. 1 [14].

## The contract

- The contract is concluded in simple written form and must contain: the full name of the University; its place of location; the full name of the customer (or their surname, first name, patronymic), telephone; the place of location or residence of the customer and/or their legal representative; the name of the representative of the University and/or the customer and the details of the document confirming their powers to conclude the contract; the name, place of residence and telephone of the student, where the services are provided in favour of a student who is not the customer; the rights, duties and liability of the University, the customer and the student; the full cost of the services and the payment procedure; the details of the licence for educational activity (the licensing body, the number and date of registration), unless otherwise provided by law; the type, level and/or focus of the educational programme (part of it); the form of study; the terms of mastering the programme (duration of study); the type of document issued after successful completion; the procedure for amending and terminating the contract; other necessary information connected with the specifics of the services [15].
- The contract may not contain conditions limiting the rights of applicants and students or lowering the level of the guarantees set by the legislation on education; such conditions are not subject to application [16].
- The University develops the forms of the contracts for paid educational services in accordance with the model forms approved by the Ministry of Science and Higher Education (higher and additional professional education) and by the Ministry of Education (basic general education, secondary vocational education and additional general education programmes) [17].
- The information in the contract must correspond to the information published on the University's official site on the date the contract is concluded; the contract may also be concluded in the manner of article 428 of the Civil Code (a contract of accession) where the form published on the official site provides for it [18].

## Liability, defects and breach of the deadlines

- For non-performance or improper performance of the obligations under the contract the University and the customer bear the liability envisaged by the contract and by the legislation of the Russian Federation [19].
- On discovering a defect of the paid educational services, including provision not in full volume, the customer may at their choice demand: free provision of the educational services; a commensurate reduction of their cost; or reimbursement of the expenses incurred in removing the defects by their own efforts or by third persons [20].
- The customer may refuse to perform the contract and demand full compensation of losses if the defects are not removed by the University within the term set by the contract, and also if a substantial defect or other substantial deviation from the terms of the contract is discovered [21].
- If the University breaches the terms of providing the services (the beginning and/or the end, and/or intermediate terms) or it becomes obvious that they will not be provided in time, the customer may at their choice: set the University a new term; entrust the services to third persons at a reasonable price and demand compensation of the expenses; demand a reduction of the cost; or terminate the contract [22].
- The customer may demand full compensation of the losses caused by the breach of the terms of the beginning and/or end of the services and by defects of the services [23].

## Termination of the contract by the University

On the University's initiative the contract may be terminated unilaterally in the following cases [24]:

- expulsion of a student who has reached the age of 15 as a disciplinary measure [24];
- failure by a student under a professional educational programme to perform the duty of conscientiously mastering that programme and fulfilling the curriculum [24];
- establishment of a breach of the admission procedure which, through the student's fault, led to unlawful enrolment in the University [24];
- delay in paying the cost of the paid educational services [24];
- impossibility of proper performance of the obligations because of the student's actions or inaction [24].

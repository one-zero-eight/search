# Procurement Management Sector (Сектор управления закупками) — place, tasks, functions and rights

## The document and the order that approved it

The Regulation on the Procurement Management Sector (Положение о секторе управления закупками) of ANO VO «Innopolis University» was approved by order of 1 August 2024 № ОД/АД-03/2024/08/01, signed in Innopolis by the Director for Development and HR Policy R.F. Valiev [1]. The order was issued in connection with the creation of a new structural subdivision for the purpose of optimising the University's activity, and it approves the Regulation attached to it [1]. The head of the Procurement Management Sector, G.F. Kalimullina, must ensure timely familiarisation (against signature) with the approved Regulation of the employees of the subdivision and also of employees newly hired and transferred (moved) into the subdivision from other subdivisions of the University [1]. Control over execution of the order and of the Regulation approved by it is retained by the Director for Development and HR Policy [1].

The Regulation governs the activity of the Procurement Management Sector of the Centre for the Development of Industrial Robotics of the Centre for Automation and Robotisation of the University and determines its tasks, functions, rights and duties [2]. The wiki copy is a draft made by OCR of a scanned document, so numbers and dates should be checked against the PDF [1].

## Place in the structure

- The Sector is a structural subdivision of the Centre for the Development of Industrial Robotics (Центр развития промышленной робототехники) and belongs to the category of organisational and technical support of project activity [3].
- The Sector reports directly to the director of the Centre [4].
- In its activity the Sector is guided by the Constitution of the Russian Federation, the Labour Code of the Russian Federation, Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ «Об образовании в Российской Федерации» and other legislative and normative legal acts regulating the activity of higher education institutions, the University's Charter, decisions of the University's Academic, Supervisory and Operational councils, orders and instructions of the University director, other local normative acts and this Regulation [5].
- The structure, headcount and staffing table of the Sector, and changes to them, are approved by the University director [6].
- The Sector is headed by a head of the Sector, hired and dismissed by order of the University director [7].
- The working conditions of the Sector's employees are determined by the employment contracts concluded with each employee and by the University's internal labour regulations [1]; their rights, duties and degree of responsibility are established by job descriptions [8].
- Within their competence the Sector's employees may be involved in work under grants, projects, subsidies and other programmes implemented by the University [9].
- The Sector may be a base for the practical training of the University's students [10].
- The Regulation and additions and changes to it are approved by the University director or another person authorised by him [11].

## Task

The Sector's task is documentary support of procurement activity in accordance with the Unified Procurement Standard of ANO VO «Innopolis University» (Единый стандарт закупок), approved by order of the University, within the projects of the Centre [12].

## Functions

- Carrying out procurement independently in accordance with the Unified Standard [13].
- Searching for products according to the technical specification provided by the initiator of the procurement request [14].
- Collecting commercial offers and negotiating with counterparties on reducing the cost of products [15].
- Preparing the set of documentation for approval of the procurement by the University's Operational Council [16].
- Organising agreement of the contract terms with the counterparty [17].
- Checking technical specifications for compliance with the Unified Standard [18].
- Filing the procurement request on the «Битрикс» corporate portal [19].
- Organising agreement of contracts and expenditure documents by the internal services before payment and receipt of the products [20].
- Controlling the processing of documents confirming delivery of goods, performance of works or rendering of services, in accordance with the requirements of the budget [21].
- Keeping an analytical table of the products procured at the expense of the Centre's budget [22].
- Filing requests to find a transport vehicle and preparing transport waybills [23].
- Informing the initiators of a procurement about the terms and dates of delivery of the products [24].
- Preparing information and guarantee letters [25].

## Rights

In carrying out their activity the Sector's employees have the right to:

- request from the University's structural subdivisions the information and materials needed for the work within the Sector's competence [26];
- involve, with the consent of the heads of the University's structural subdivisions, employees of those subdivisions for preparing drafts of local acts and other documents and for developing and carrying out measures conducted by the Sector [27];
- take part in meetings held by the University's management where matters within the Sector's competence are discussed [28];
- put before the University's management proposals to repeal, amend or supplement the University's local acts [29];
- interact with bodies of state authority and administration, legal entities and the University's subdivisions on matters within the Sector's competence [30].

## Interaction with other subdivisions

- Interaction with the University's other structural subdivisions is determined by the tasks and functions assigned to the Sector by the Regulation [31].
- With the procurement department — on the part of the procurement procedure carried out through the electronic trading platform [32].
- With the analytical department — on payment of invoices and submission of signed contracts, specifications, additional agreements and protocols of disagreements [33].
- With the Financial and Economic Department — on processing and submitting closing documents [34].
- With the organisational and technical department — on finding a transport vehicle and sending correspondence [35].
- With the other structural subdivisions — when preparing drafts of local acts, orders, instructions, contracts and other documents and when preparing opinions and written and oral references on matters within the Sector's competence [36].

## Reporting

On the basis of the relevant local acts, orders and instructions the Sector may, within its activity, be responsible for preparing and keeping up to date information, references and reports [37].

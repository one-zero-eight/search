# Educational and Methodological Center of the Institute of Additional Education — place, tasks, functions and rights

## The document and the Centre's place in the structure

The Regulation on the Educational and Methodological Center of the Institute of Additional Education of ANO VO «Innopolis University» (Положение об Учебно-методическом центре Института дополнительного образования) was approved by order of 11 December 2025 № ОД/АД-01/2025/12/11, signed by the Director for Development and HR Policy R.F. Valiev [1]. The Regulation regulates the activity of the Centre and determines its tasks, functions, rights and duties [2].

The Educational and Methodological Center of the Institute of Additional Education (Центр) is a structural subdivision of the University and belongs to the category of educational-methodological subdivisions [3]. It reports directly to the director of the Institute of Additional Education [4].

In its activity the Centre follows the Constitution of the Russian Federation, the Labour Code of the Russian Federation, Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29 December 2012 «Об образовании в Российской Федерации» and other legislative and normative legal acts regulating the activity of higher education institutions, the University's Charter, decisions of the Academic, Supervisory and Operational councils, orders and instructions of the University's director, other local normative acts and this Regulation [5].

- The structure, the number of staff and the staffing table of the Centre, and changes to them, are approved by the director of the University [6].
- The Centre is headed by the head of the Educational and Methodological Center, hired and dismissed by order of the director of the University [7].
- The working conditions of the Centre's employees are determined by employment contracts concluded with each employee and by the University's internal labour regulations [8].
- The rights, duties and degree of responsibility of the Centre's employees are established by job descriptions [9].
- Employees of the Centre may, within their competences, be involved in work under grants, projects, subsidies and other programmes implemented by the University [10].
- The Regulation and additions and changes to it are approved by the director of the University or by another person authorised by him [11].

## Tasks

The Centre's tasks are [12], [13], [14], [15]:

- methodological support of the educational and project activity of the University's structural subdivisions [12];
- coordination of the educational-methodological activity of the University's structural subdivisions [13];
- developing normative, administrative and methodological documentation regulating the University's educational activity that falls within the Centre's competence [14];
- keeping records and reporting on additional professional education and secondary vocational education [15].

## Functions

On programmes and their methodological support the Centre:

- provides methodological support for the development and updating of additional professional education programmes in accordance with the requirements of normative documents [16]
- provides methodological support for the development and updating of the main professional educational programmes of secondary vocational education in accordance with the requirements of normative documents [17]
- organises work on the development and updating of methodological materials on the development, updating and expertise of main professional educational programmes and additional education programmes, within work under grants, projects, subsidies and other programmes implemented by the University [18]
- carries out expertise of educational programmes for compliance with the requirements of legislation, federal state educational standards and professional standards [19]
- develops local normative acts regulating educational activity under secondary vocational education programmes and additional professional programmes [20]
- develops recommendations on the content of programmes of повышение квалификации and professional retraining [21]

On information, analysis and monitoring the Centre:

- prepares information and analytical information on educational activity [22]
- compiles analytical reports and references on questions connected with promising directions of the development of education [23]
- provides consultative and methodological support to the University's structural subdivisions on questions within the Centre's competence [24]
- keeps the register of additional professional programmes developed and implemented by the structural subdivisions of the Institute of Additional Education [25]
- monitors the carrying out of the educational activity of the Institute of Additional Education and of the College [26]
- prepares documents and reporting in accordance with the Centre's functions [27]
- collects and processes statistical reporting on additional professional education and secondary vocational education [28]
- prepares answers to requests of executive authorities on questions within the Centre's competence [29]

On listeners, documents of education and events the Centre:

- checks the information and incoming documents of potential listeners of additional professional programmes on the University's Unified Educational Platform [30]
- forms documents of education and/or of qualification and documents of study — certificates of повышение квалификации, diplomas of professional retraining, certificates, diplomas of secondary vocational education, references of study and/or of the period of study [31]
- organises the issue and sending to the University's graduates of documents of education and/or of qualification [32]
- forms and transmits to the Federal register of information on documents of education and (or) of qualification and documents of study information on the issued documents [33]
- keeps records of strict reporting forms [34]
- confirms documents of education and/or of qualification on request [35]
- plans, organises and holds meetings, seminars, conferences, trainings and other events in the directions of the Centre's activity [36]
- carries out activity in other directions in accordance with the Centre's tasks [37]

## Rights

In carrying out their activity the Centre's employees have the right to:

- request from the University's structural subdivisions the information and materials needed for the work within the Centre's competence [38]
- with the consent of the heads of the University's structural subdivisions, involve the employees of those subdivisions in preparing drafts of local acts and other documents and in developing and carrying out measures conducted by the Centre [39]
- take part in meetings held by the University's management when discussing questions whose solution falls within the Centre's competence [40]
- put before the University's management proposals to cancel, amend or supplement the University's local acts [41]
- interact with bodies of state authority and administration, legal entities and the University's subdivisions on questions within the Centre's competence [42]

## Interaction with other subdivisions

The Centre's interaction with other structural subdivisions is determined by the tasks and functions assigned to it by the Regulation [43]. The Regulation names as counterparts the structural subdivisions of the Institute of Additional Education and the College, on the organisation of educational activity, the formation of educational programmes, quality control of the educational process and educational-methodological documentation [44], and the other structural subdivisions, on preparing drafts of local normative acts and preparing conclusions and references on questions within the Centre's competence [45]. The Centre may be a base for the practical training of the University's students [46].

## Reporting

On the basis of the relevant local normative acts, orders and instructions the Centre may, within its activity, be responsible for preparing and keeping up to date information, references and reports [47].

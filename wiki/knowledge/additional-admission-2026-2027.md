# Additional admission to bachelor's and master's programmes for 2026/2027 (order of 31 August 2026)

## The order

The additional admission (дополнительный приём) to bachelor's and master's programmes for the 2026/2027 academic year was announced by order № ОД/УД‐01/2026/08/31 of 31 August 2026, issued under the admission rules for bachelor's and master's programmes for 2026/2027 approved by order of 10.04.2026 № ОД/УД‐01/2026/04/10 [1].

## Who it is for and on what terms

The additional admission is held for persons entering on the basis of a document of a foreign state on education, to places under contracts for the provision of paid educational services (договор об образовании) [2].

## Dates of the campaign

- Applications and documents are accepted from 1 September 2026 [2].
- The day of completion of the acceptance of applications and documents is 23 October 2026 [2].
- Entrance examinations are held from 7 September to 27 October 2026 inclusive [2].
- The competitive lists are published on 28 October 2026 [2].
- The day of completion of the conclusion of contracts on education is 29 October 2026, and on that day contracts are concluded until 17:00 Moscow time [2].
- The order on enrolment is issued on 30 October 2026 [2].

## How reliable the reading is

The text layer of Annex 1 garbles the rows that carry the totals — «Итого: | Робототехника | 282» and «Программы магистратуры | Робототехника | 282» — with the programme column repeated in place of the row label; the figures 282 and 312 are nevertheless the sums of the bachelor's and master's rows above them [1]. The string «МНРЕГ» appears after the order number and before each annex heading, and is not part of the order's text [1].

# Documents on additional education (ДПО and ДОП) — types, blank forms, filling, issue and accounting

## The document

The Положение об установлении образцов документов и порядка их выдачи по результатам освоения дополнительных образовательных программ в АНО ВО «Университет Иннополис» is dated Innopolis, 2017, and is Приложение № 1 to an order of 2017 whose number and date are unreadable in the OCR text [1].

It determines the types of documents of the sample established by the University that are issued on the results of the successful mastering of additional professional education programmes and additional general education programmes [2], and it establishes the samples of the blank forms of documents on additional education, the requirements to their filling, and the procedure for issuing, storing and accounting for such documents [3].

It was developed in accordance with Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29.12.2012, the order of the Ministry of Education and Science of Russia of 01.07.2013 No. 499 (organisation of educational activity under additional educational programmes), the order of 29.08.2013 No. 1008 (organisation of educational activity under additional general education programmes), the University's Charter and other local acts [4]. The wiki copy is an OCR reading of a scan, so numbers and dates should be checked against the PDF [1].

## Types of documents

The University has approved the following types of documents issued on the results of mastering additional educational programmes [5]:

- documents on qualification: an удостоверение о повышении квалификации and a диплом о профессиональной переподготовке [6];
- documents on study: a сертификат о прохождении обучения under an additional educational programme, and a справка об обучении или о периоде обучения (in short, справка об обучении) [7].

## Who receives which document

- A диплом о профессиональной переподготовке is issued to listeners who have completed study under additional professional education programmes and have successfully passed the final attestation, on the basis of the decision of the attestation commission [8].
- The kind of the diploma — certifying the right to perform a new type of professional activity or the acquisition of a new qualification — is determined by the aims, tasks, content and results of study approved in the study-methodological documentation of the programme [8].
- Where a programme is mastered in parallel with obtaining secondary vocational and (or) higher education, the удостоверение and (or) the диплом are issued simultaneously with the corresponding document on education and qualification [8].
- An удостоверение о повышении квалификации is issued to listeners who have completed study under additional professional education programmes and have successfully passed the final attestation [9].
- A сертификат о прохождении обучения is issued to listeners who studied under additional educational programmes which do not provide for a final attestation [10].
- A справка об обучении is issued to listeners of additional professional programmes who did not pass the final attestation or received unsatisfactory results at it, and also to persons who mastered part of a programme and (or) were expelled from the University [11].

## Blank forms

- Blank forms of documents on qualification are developed by manufacturing organisations on the University's order, while blanks of documents on study are made at the University; the samples are approved by this Regulation and by an order of the director [12].
- Blanks of documents on qualification are polygraphic products protected against forgery, made on the University's order in the procedure established by Russian legislation, in accordance with the technical requirements approved by order of the Ministry of Finance of the Russian Federation of 07 February 2003 No. 14н, in organisations holding the corresponding licence of the Federal Tax Service of Russia [13].
- The диплом о профессиональной переподготовке consists of the diploma and an annex to it; the samples of the diploma certifying the right to perform a new type of professional activity and of the diploma certifying the acquisition of a new qualification are in annex No. 1, the sample of the annex to the diploma in annex No. 2 [14].
- A hard cover may be made for the diploma; it is not a protected polygraphic product and is made in a printing house on the University's order (sample in annex No. 3) [15].
- The blank of the удостоверение may be made without a cover (sample in annex No. 4); an annex to the удостоверение may be made, containing the list of results by disciplines (modules), practices and internships and the results of the final attestation (sample in annex No. 5), and there is a sample of a hard cover for the удостоверение (annex No. 6) [16].
- The blanks of documents on study — certificates and certificates of study — are not protected against forgery [17].
- Blanks of certificates of study under additional general education programmes are made at the University in accordance with the established forms (sample in annex No. 7) [18].
- Certificates of study are made on the University's official blanks in its structural units in accordance with the established form (sample in annex No. 8); a certificate may reflect the results of mastering the programme by disciplines (modules), practices and internships and the results of the final attestation (sample in annex No. 9) [19].

## How the blanks are filled

The blanks are filled in the state language of the Russian Federation with a printer, in black-and-white printing; blanks of documents on study may be filled in a foreign language (English) where the educational programme and the University's local normative acts provide for it [20].

- In the diploma: the full name of the University at the top in the centre, the registration number from the book of registration, the name of the city (Innopolis) and the date of issue; on the right side the listener's full name as in the passport, the name of the programme and the qualification (depending on the kind of the diploma), and the date of the decision of the attestation commission; signed by the chair of the attestation commission, the director or a person authorised by them, and the secretary, with the University's гербовая печать [21].
- In the annex to the diploma: the listener's full name, the data of the document on the previous higher or secondary vocational education, the dates of beginning and end of study, the names of the University and of the programme; for an internship — the organisation, its duration in weeks and (or) workload and the mark or «зачет»; the disciplines with their workload and marks in words or «зачет», the practices, the form of the final attestation and the marks; signed by the director or a person authorised by them and by the secretary, with the гербовая печать [22].
- In the удостоверение: the full name of the University, the registration number, the city and the year; the listener's full name, the period of study, the name of the University and the name of the programme according to the approved documentation, and the volume (workload) of the programme in hours and (or) credits in Arabic figures according to the approved curriculum; signed by the director or a person authorised by them and by the secretary, with the University's seal [23].
- The samples of filling the blanks of diplomas and of their annexes are endorsed by the head of the structural unit delivering the programme and agreed with the department of control of educational activity [24]; those of the удостоверения and their annexes are endorsed and agreed in the same way [25].
- In the certificate: the registration number from the journal, the listener's full name, the period of study in the form «с 00 месяца 0000 г. по 00 месяца 0000 г.», the full name of the University, the type and name of the programme according to the approved documentation and its volume in hours and (or) credits according to the approved curriculum, the position of the head signing it (the director or a vice-rector for a direction of activity) with a place for the personal signature and its decipherment, the University's seal, and the city (Innopolis) and the year of issue [26].
- In the справка об обучении: the listener's full name, the periods of study, the full name of the University, the type and name of the programme, the date and number of the order of expulsion with its ground (own wish, non-fulfilment of the terms of the contract, not having passed the final attestation, unsatisfactory results and so on); where the programme provides for them, the disciplines with workload and marks, the practices, the internships and the form of the final attestation with the marks; signed by the director or a vice-rector for a direction of activity, with the University's seal [27].

## Issue, storage and accounting

- Blanks of documents on qualification are purchased by the University on a general request of the structural units delivering the programmes, in the quantity necessary for carrying on educational activity [28].
- No fee is charged from listeners and students for the issue of documents on qualification, documents on study and duplicates of those documents [29].
- Blanks of documents on qualification are kept in the structural unit appointed responsible for their storage and accounting by an order of the director, as strict reporting forms; they are accounted in a single register by types, series and numbers, with the dates of receipt and of issue, drawn up on form No. ф.0504045 «Книга учета бланков строгой отчетности» [30].
- The blanks are issued to the units on a request signed by its head and agreed with the vice-rector for the direction of activity and the vice-rector in charge of the unit responsible for storage; an inventory is carried out within the terms set by an order of the director, at least once a year [30].
- Blanks of certificates under additional educational programmes are made and kept in the structural units that deliver the programmes [31].
- The head of the structural unit delivering the programmes is responsible for the correctness of drawing up, registration and issue of documents on additional professional education [32].
- For registering documents on qualification registration books record the serial registration number, the listener's name, the number of the blank, the name of the programme, the date of issue, the signature of the director (rector, vice-rector), the signature of the person who received the document and, for a diploma or an удостоверение, the date and number of the protocol of the attestation commission [33].
- A document on qualification is issued personally to the listener or to their legal representative by power of attorney, or sent to the listener by post on their personal application [34].
- Registration books for certificates record the serial registration number, the listener's name, the number of the blank, the name of the programme, the date of issue, the signature of the director or of the vice-rector for the direction of activity and the signature of the person who received the document [35].
- The registration books are laced, numbered, fastened with the signature of the director and kept by the head of the structural unit delivering the programme [36].
- Blanks spoiled in filling, of documents on qualification and of certificates, are subject to destruction in the procedure established at the University [37].
- A document with errors found after it has been received is replaced by one without errors, and the erroneous document is withdrawn and destroyed in the established procedure [38].
- A duplicate of a document on qualification or of a certificate is issued on a personal application, with the word «дубликат» on the blank [39].
- Documents on qualification and certificates must be ready for issue not later than 10 days from the date of the order on the expulsion of the listener (student) [40].

## Certificates of study (справки об обучении)

- Certificates of study under additional professional programmes are made and kept by the structural units that deliver the programmes [41].
- No fee is charged from listeners for a certificate of study or for its duplicate [42].
- Responsibility for the correctness of drawing up, registration and issue of certificates lies with the head of the structural unit delivering the programmes [43].
- Registration books for certificates record the serial number, the listener's name, the name of the programme, the date of issue and the signature of the person who received the document [44].
- The certificate is issued personally to the listener or to their legal representative by power of attorney, or sent by post on the listener's personal application [45].
- The registration books are laced, numbered, fastened with the signature of the authorised vice-rector and kept by the head of the structural unit [46].
- Blanks spoiled in filling are destroyed in the established procedure [47], and a certificate with errors found later is replaced, the erroneous document being withdrawn and destroyed [48].
- A duplicate of a certificate of study is issued on a personal application, with the word «дубликат» on the blank [49].
- A certificate of study is issued not later than 10 days from the date of the order on the expulsion of the listener [50].

## Amendments and other acts

The Regulation is amended in the procedure established at the University [51], and other local acts regulating the University's activity in the field of additional professional education are developed and adopted in accordance with it [52].

## What the regulations on the programmes add

A listener who has successfully mastered an additional professional programme and passed the final attestation receives an удостоверение о повышении квалификации and (or) a диплом о профессиональной переподготовке, issued on blanks protected against forgery or on other forms approved by an order of the director; those who did not pass the final attestation, received unsatisfactory results, mastered part of a programme or were expelled receive a certificate of study or of the period of study on a form established by the University [53], [54]. A listener who completed a retraining programme of not less than 250 hours receives a diploma of professional retraining, and one who completed a повышение квалификации programme of 16 hours or more receives an удостоверение [55]; after an unsatisfactory defence of the final attestation work a certificate of study listing the disciplines and the results of the passes and examinations is issued and is exchanged for the diploma after a successful repeat attestation [56].

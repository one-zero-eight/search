# Industrial Software Development Laboratory (Лаборатория промышленной разработки ПО) — place, tasks, functions and rights

## The document and the place of the Laboratory

The Regulation on the Industrial Software Development Laboratory (Положение о Лаборатории промышленной разработки ПО) regulates the activity of the Laboratory of ANO VO «Innopolis University» and determines its tasks, functions, rights and duties [1].

The page carries the place «г. Иннополис» and an approval block «УТВЕРЖДЕНО Приказом» dated 9 January 2019 with the number АД-14/2019/01/09 and signed by the director for development and HR policy A.M. Samatova [2]. It is a scan read by OCR, and it warns in its front matter that numbers and dates should be checked against the PDF before relying on them [2].

The Laboratory of Industrial Software Development is a structural subdivision of the Institute of Software Development and Software Engineering of the Faculty of Computer and Engineering Sciences of the University [3]. It reports directly to the head (chief) of the Laboratory; in the absence of the head of the Laboratory it reports to the director of the institute and the dean of the faculty [4].

In its activity the Laboratory follows the Constitution of the Russian Federation, the Labour Code of the Russian Federation, the federal law «Об образовании в Российской Федерации» and other legislative and normative legal acts of the Russian Federation regulating the activity of higher education institutions, the University's Charter, decisions of the Academic, Supervisory and Operational councils of the University, orders and instructions of the director of the University, other local normative acts of the University and this Regulation [5].

The structure, the number of staff and the staffing table of the Laboratory, and changes to them, are approved by the director of the University [6]. The Laboratory is headed by its head (chief), who is hired and dismissed by an order of the director of the University [7]. The working conditions of the Laboratory's employees are determined by the employment contracts concluded with each employee and by the University's internal labour regulations [2]. Their rights, duties and degree of responsibility are established by job descriptions [8]. The Regulation and additions and changes to it are approved by the director of the University or by another person authorised by him [9].

## Tasks

- the main goal of the Laboratory is research activity of the University in the field of industrial software production and the creation of new and innovative (advanced) models for evaluating, improving and modernising software [10]
- attracting the University's teachers, aspirants and students to research work and helping them form research skills, and serving as a reference point for the future generation of managers, engineers and software developers with an expected significant international impact on the world of research and production [11]
- disseminating the results of the research carried out by the Laboratory in the form of abstracts and reports at Russian and international conferences and of publications in thematic journals [12]
- developing the University's scientific cooperation and organising joint research activity with Russian and foreign researchers and organisations [13]
- carrying out research work, industrial projects and grants in the Laboratory's profile by its employees on their own initiative and in accordance with grant support, budgetary and extra-budgetary contracts [14]

## Functions

- conducting fundamental and applied research in the field of industrial software production [15]
- attracting and bringing out the research potential of the University's young specialists [16]
- publishing scientific articles in journals, including those indexed in international citation databases such as Web of Science, Scopus and others [17]
- presenting the results of the work at scientific and practical conferences in Russia and abroad [18]
- stage-by-stage planning and carrying out research work, industrial projects and grants [19]

## Rights

- request from the University's structural subdivisions the information and materials needed for the work within the Laboratory's competence [20]
- with the consent of the heads of the University's structural subdivisions, involve the employees of those subdivisions in preparing draft local acts and other documents and in developing and carrying out measures conducted by the Laboratory [21]
- take part in meetings held by the University's management when discussing questions whose solution falls within the Laboratory's competence [22]
- put before the University's management proposals to cancel, amend or supplement the University's local acts [23]
- interact with bodies of state authority and administration, legal entities and the University's subdivisions on questions within the Laboratory's competence [24]
- use the University's logo in all presentation materials, in agreement with the public relations department [25]
- carry out publishing activity in the established procedure, observing the rules in force at the University [26]

## Interaction and reporting

Interaction of the Laboratory with the University's other structural subdivisions is determined by the tasks and functions assigned to it by this Regulation [27].

- with the project management department on participation in research work, industrial projects and grants and on reporting under grants [28]
- with other structural subdivisions on preparing draft local acts, orders, instructions, contracts and other documents and on preparing conclusions and written and oral certificates on questions within the Laboratory's competence [29]

Within its activity the Laboratory is responsible for preparing the following information and for keeping it up to date [30]:

- reports on research work, industrial projects and grants [30]
- current information about the members of the Laboratory (professors, aspirants, students) [30]
- current information about the quantity of equipment [30]

The Laboratory is responsible for providing statistical and other information established by legislation to the authorised bodies [31].

# Application for master's admission on federal budget places — sample form

## What the form is

The page is the sample application form (образец заявления) for admission to study in master's programmes at the expense of the federal budget allocations at ANO VO «Innopolis University» [1]. It is addressed to the Director of the University K.V. Semenikhin at the University's address 420500, Innopolis, Universitetskaya street, house 1, and the form is dated «___» __________ 2024 [1].

## Applicant's personal data and education

The form asks for the surname, name and patronymic (if any); sex; date and place of birth; registered address; citizenship; the identity document (type, series, number, by whom issued, subdivision code); the insurance number of the individual personal account (СНИЛС) if any, or the unique code assigned to the applicant in the absence of a СНИЛС [1].

It asks for information about education and the document of the established form: the name of the educational organisation, the year of completion, the document of education and qualification, its series and number [1].

## Programmes and priorities named in the form

The applicant asks to be admitted to the competition for the first course of master's programmes, naming the code and name of the field of study, the form of study, the basis of study and the priority of enrolment [1]. The sample lists, all full-time (очная), at the expense of the budget allocations of the federal budget and on the main places within the control figures of admission (КЦП), in this order of priority [1]:

1. 09.04.01 Informatics and Computer Engineering, profile «Программная инженерия» [1].
2. 09.04.01 Informatics and Computer Engineering, profile «Робототехника и компьютерное зрение» [1].
3. 09.04.01 Informatics and Computer Engineering, profile «Инженерия безопасности систем и сетей» [1].
4. 09.04.01 Informatics and Computer Engineering, profile «Искусственный интеллект и инженерия данных» [1].
5. 09.04.01 Informatics and Computer Engineering, profile «Технологическое предпринимательство» [1].

## Entrance examinations

A separate checkbox asks to be admitted to the entrance examinations held by Innopolis University itself [1]. The table asks for the number, the name of the subject, the language of the examination (Russian or English), the form of taking it and the place where it is taken [1]. The sample row names the entrance examination for the master's programme in field of study 09.04.01 Informatics and Computer Engineering and lists all five profiles above as the alternatives it relates to; the form is computer testing and the place is the address of residence [1]. The form does not offer a choice between the results of the ЕГЭ and the University's own examinations [1].

## Special conditions at entrance examinations

An applicant who needs special conditions at the entrance examinations because of health limitations or disability marks this, lists the entrance examinations, gives the details of the document confirming the disability or health limitation that requires the conditions and ticks the conditions needed [1]. The conditions offered are [1]:

- written tasks in relief-dot Braille or as an electronic document accessible with specialised software for the blind, or read by an assistant [1];
- a set of writing accessories and paper for writing in Braille, or a computer with specialised software for the blind, or an assistant to dictate to [1];
- individual even lighting of not less than 300 lux [1];
- a magnifying device for the internal entrance examinations [1];
- tasks and the instructions on the procedure of the entrance examinations in enlarged print [1];
- sound-amplifying equipment for collective use, or for individual use [1];
- the services of a sign-language interpreter, or of a tactile sign-language interpreter [1];
- a computer with specialised software for persons with musculoskeletal disorders, impaired motor functions of the upper limbs or absence of the upper limbs, or dictation to an assistant [1].

## Individual achievements

The block of checkboxes lists a document of higher education and qualification with distinction (с отличием), scientific publications in the profile (focus) of the master's programme in the international database Web of Science over the last 3 years, and likewise in the international database Scopus over the last 3 years [1].

## Accommodation, return of originals and confirmations

- A checkbox states the need for a place of residence in the residential complex (жилой комплекс) [1].
- The form asks for the way the submitted originals are to be returned if the applicant is not enrolled — in person, by Russian Post or by courier service — with the applicant's postal address; this is not indicated where the documents are submitted electronically [1].
- The applicant signs confirmation of familiarisation with the admission rules approved by Innopolis University itself, including the rules for lodging an appeal against the results of the entrance examinations held by the University, with the charter, with the information on the date of grant and the registration number of the licence for educational activity, with the information on the date of grant and the registration number of the state accreditation of educational activity in the programmes implemented, and with the educational programmes and other documents regulating the organisation and conduct of educational activity and the rights and duties of students [1].
- The applicant confirms familiarisation with the information that, where admission is on a competitive basis, the applicant is given information about the competition being held and its results [1], with the dates of completion of admission of the document of the established form [1], and with the need to state reliable information in the application and to submit genuine documents [1].
- On applying to places within the control figures the applicant confirms the absence of a specialist's diploma and of a master's diploma, except applicants who have higher professional education confirmed by the qualification «дипломированный специалист» [1].
- Applicants who submitted a document (documents) of a foreign state on education or on education and qualification undertake to provide the certificate of recognition of the foreign education and confirmation of completion of the legalisation no later than the day of submission of the document of the established form, where those procedures are necessary [1].
- The application ends with the applicant's signature and full name [1].

# Assessment, practice and state final attestation with e-learning and distance technologies (bachelor's, master's, aspirantura) — the Regulation of 2 August 2022

## The document

This card rests on the Regulation on the procedure for applying e-learning and distance educational technologies in higher education programmes — bachelor's, master's and aspirantura — approved by order of the University of 2 August 2022 [1]. The Regulation's own rules on the forms, periodicity and procedure of ongoing assessment, interim attestation, grading and the elimination of academic debt are set by the University's separate local acts on ongoing assessment and interim attestation and on interim attestation with distance technologies [2]. ## Identification during assessment

- During ongoing assessment and interim attestation, alongside the ordinary level, the control level of identification may be used — reconciliation of online data with personal data, including data from the personal file, by streamed video from a camera showing the person's face or biometric data [3].
- The control level is obligatory when students pass the final (state final) attestation, and the University then ensures identification of students' identity and control over compliance with the requirements of the local acts [4] [5].
- A pedagogical worker may monitor attendance at online classes, where necessary, using ordinary or control identification data, single sign-on (SSO) data and other technologies [6].

## Ongoing assessment

- The list of forms of ongoing assessment, their procedure and periodicity, the tools and technologies used (including ЭО and ДОТ), the criteria for individual forms and the grading system are developed by the pedagogical worker responsible for the discipline (module) and reflected in its work programme [7].
- With ЭО and ДОТ ongoing assessment may be held as: writing separate parts of the code of a software product (online); independent (home) work (online); practical and laboratory work (online/offline); projects (online/offline); tests (online/offline); colloquia (online/offline); testing (online); reports, essays, review papers, surveys (online/offline); oral questioning (online/offline); discussions, trainings, round tables (online/offline); and other forms set by the pedagogical worker in accordance with the curriculum and the schedule of the educational process (online/offline) [8].
- Information on the results of ongoing assessment is entered by the pedagogical worker in the electronic journal of classes in the Moodle content management system deployed on the University's portal within five working days after the assessment [9].

## Interim attestation

- Interim attestation is held in the form determined by the curriculum; its forms are a pass (зачёт, including a differentiated pass) and an examination [10].
- The format of the interim attestation is determined by the pedagogical worker responsible for the discipline (module), practice or research and shown in the corresponding programme (work programme of the discipline, practice programme, research programme) [11].
- With ЭО and ДОТ interim attestation may be held as testing, oral questioning, a written work, defence of a review paper, defence of a project or other forms determined by the pedagogical worker, each online/offline as indicated [11].
- Results of interim attestation in a discipline (module), another component, including practice and research work, are fixed in the ЭИОС — in Moodle, in the «1С: Университет» educational process management system, in the student's portfolio and in the personal account at my.university.innopolis.ru — within 7 working days after the attestation, but not later than the end of the interim attestation period [12].
- In bachelor's and master's programmes the pedagogical worker enters the results in the interim attestation record sheet and submits it on paper, as an electronic document or as a document converted to electronic form by scanning or photographing with machine-readable recognition of its details, to the responsible structural subdivision, whose staff transfer the results into «1С: Университет» and, where necessary, certify them with the signature of the dean of the faculty [12].
- In aspirantura programmes the staff of the responsible structural subdivision transfer the results from the interim attestation record sheet filled in by the pedagogical workers into Moodle and into «1С: Университет» [12].

## Academic debt

- The University allows ЭО and ДОТ to be used to eliminate the academic debt of students who received unsatisfactory interim attestation results in one or several disciplines (modules), another component, including practice and research work, or did not take the interim attestation because of absence for an unjustified reason [13].
- The schedule of the repeat interim attestation is brought to the notice of students and pedagogical workers through corporate e-mail [14].
- The procedure of eliminating academic debt follows the rules on the forms and procedure of ongoing assessment and interim attestation, on the possible use of the control level of identification, and on the forms, formats and recording of the results [15].

## Practice

- The procedure of organising and conducting practice, its forms and methods, and its types, are determined by the University's local acts on the practice of students [16].
- The practice programme determines the use of ЭО and ДОТ, the list of information technologies used (including software and information reference systems), the description of the material and technical base and the list of Internet resources [17].
- The University's practice supervisor may use ЭО and ДОТ provided students are informed of the tasks and content of the practice (an introductory conference), when developing individual tasks to be done during the practice, giving students methodological help with those tasks, checking that the content of the practice meets the requirements, and evaluating the results of the practice [18].
- The supervisor from the profile organisation may use ЭО and ДОТ when conducting the preliminary attestation of the students' fulfilment of the practice programme [19].
- For the period of the practice the University's supervisor may propose a consultant from the University, who may use ЭО and ДОТ for the measures connected with organising and conducting the practice, for checking students' attendance at the places of practice, and for checking fulfilment of the practice programme and the correct completion of the student's practice diary (including the individual task) and report on the practice [20].
- The results of the practice are evaluated by interim attestation in the form of a pass (зачёт), including with ЭО and ДОТ [21].

## State final attestation

- The conduct of the state final attestation is determined by the University's local acts on the state final attestation of higher education programmes and on the state final attestation with distance technologies [22].
- In bachelor's and master's programmes the state final attestation is held in the form of defence of the final qualifying work: a bachelor's work for the bachelor's level and a master's dissertation for the master's level [23].
- Applying ЭО and ДОТ in preparing and defending the final qualifying work covers approval of the topic, preparation of the work, pre-defence before its supervisor, obtaining the supervisor's review, obtaining a review (рецензия) of the master's dissertation, placement in the electronic library system and checking the volume of borrowing (online), and the defence before the state examination commission (online/offline) [23].
- In aspirantura programmes the state final attestation is held as a written state examination and an oral scientific report on the main results of the prepared scientific qualification work (dissertation); the conduct of the state final tests with ЭО and ДОТ is determined by the aspirantura programme [24].
- Applying ЭО and ДОТ in preparing for and taking the state examination covers familiarisation with the list of examination questions by placing them in the ЭИОС (online), a pre-examination consultation, the choice of the ticket and a written answer to the questions of the ticket (online/offline) [24].
- Applying ЭО and ДОТ in preparing and presenting the scientific report covers approval of the dissertation topic, preparation of the dissertation, a preliminary check of the volume of borrowing including substantive detection of improper borrowing, preparation of the report, presentation of the report on paper as a manuscript and in electronic form in Russian and/or English for the preliminary borrowing check, obtaining the research supervisor's review, obtaining a review (рецензия), placement of the report's text in the electronic library system (online) and the oral presentation of the report (online/offline) [24].
- Where necessary, a meeting of the state examination commission may be held using video conferencing systems (Zoom, Skype for Business, Google Hangouts or another) or video conference communication systems, if technically possible; the commission's secretary is responsible for organising the commission's work online [25].
- A student who has fully mastered a programme implemented with ЭО and ДОТ in full is admitted to the state final attestation, which is governed by the University's local acts on that attestation [26].

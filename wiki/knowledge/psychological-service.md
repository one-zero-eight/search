# Psychological Service (Психологическая служба) — place, tasks, functions and rights

## The order and the document

The Regulation on the Psychological Service of ANO VO «Innopolis University» was approved by order of the University of 2 September 2024 No. ОД/АД-06/2024/09/02, signed by the director for development and HR policy R.F. Valiev, and was issued in connection with the creation of a new structural subdivision in order to optimise the University's activity [1].

The order obliges the head of the Psychological Service E.G. Ilyina to ensure that the workers of the subdivision, and the workers newly hired or transferred into it from other subdivisions, are familiarised with the Regulation against signature [1], and leaves control over the execution of the order and of the Regulation with the signer [1].

The Regulation governs the activity of the Psychological Service of the Center for Interaction with Students and Graduates of the Department of Academic Policy and Organisation of Educational Activity and determines its tasks, functions, rights and duties [2].

## Place in the structure

- The Service is a structural subdivision of the Center for Interaction with Students and Graduates and belongs to the category «иные структурные подразделения» [3].
- It is directly subordinate to the head of the Center for Interaction with Students and Graduates [4].
- In its activity the Service is guided by the Constitution of the Russian Federation, the Labour Code, Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29 December 2012, other legislative and normative legal acts regulating the activity of higher education institutions, the University's Charter, decisions of the Academic, Supervisory and Operations councils, orders and instructions of the director, other local normative acts and this Regulation [5].
- The structure, the number and the staffing table of the Service, and changes to them, are approved by the director of the University [5]; the Service is headed by a head who is hired and dismissed by order of the director [6].
- The working conditions of the Service's workers are determined by the employment contract concluded with each worker and by the University's internal labour regulations [7], and their rights, duties and degree of responsibility are established by job descriptions [8].
- The Service's workers may, within their competences, be involved in carrying out work under grants, projects, subsidies and other programmes implemented by the University [9].
- The Regulation and additions and changes to it are approved by order of the director of the University or of a person authorised by him [10].
- Methodological support of the Service as a component of the federal network of psychological services in higher education institutions is provided by the Federal Resource Centre of the Psychological Service in the Higher Education System of the Russian Academy of Education, and all methodological developments of that centre are approved by the Interdepartmental Working Group on the development of psychological services in higher education institutions of the Ministry of Science and Higher Education [11].
- In the interest of the student the Service establishes relations with public organisations, guardianship bodies, healthcare institutions and other subjects of psychological-medical-social support and social partnership that help the University in the positive socialisation of students [12].

## Purpose and tasks

The Service was created in order to organise accessible qualified psychological help and support of the University's students in difficult life situations, and its activity is to contribute to strengthening mental health and to forming psychological well-being and positive socialisation of the University's students [13].

Its tasks are [14]:

- ensuring the principles of psychological help stated in the Concept of development of the network of psychological services in higher education institutions [15];
- ensuring the accessibility of qualified psychological help to all categories of students, including students with special educational needs [16];
- ensuring high quality and completeness of the range of psychological-social help for all categories of students, including those experiencing difficulties in educational activity, development and social adaptation [17];
- carrying out measures to assess risk, prevent and correct aggressive and autoaggressive behaviour of the University's students [18];
- strengthening the personal, spiritual and moral sphere of students and the internal position of the personality, including the development of intercultural tolerance and traditional family values and the prevention of xenophobia and extremism [19];
- forming resilience and constructive skills of interpersonal interaction of the University's students [20];
- creating conditions for an independent conscious choice of professional area within the chosen profession and for the conscious building of personal professional plans [21];
- participating in monitoring of the current state of psychological services and in research for the development of psychological support of higher education conducted by the Ministry of Science and Higher Education and by the Russian Academy of Education [22].

## Functions

- Psychological diagnostics: participation in research, including monitoring of the psychological well-being of students; psychological analysis of the social situation at the University, identification of acute problem points in the interaction of all participants of educational relations and of signs of aggressive and autoaggressive behaviour, and determination of ways and means of resolving them; help to students in determining the priority professional area within the chosen profession and in building personal professional plans; participation in socio-psychological testing to reveal latent and evident riskiness of the socio-psychological conditions that form psychological readiness for dependent behaviour in young people [23].
- Consultative activity: group psychological counselling of the University's students on current questions of study, development and interaction [24], and individual counselling of the University's students on the same questions [24].
- Psychological prevention and correction: help in the adaptation of students, above all of the junior courses, to the conditions of study at the University, and counselling of the teaching staff, academic-pedagogical workers and curators on helping students in the adaptation period; organisation of measures to prevent and correct aggressive and autoaggressive behaviour of students; provision of psychological support to students in a state of stress, conflict, strong emotional experience or a difficult life situation [25].
- Psychological enlightenment: raising the psychological competence of the University's students [26] and popularising psychological knowledge at the University in order to lower the students' psychological barriers that prevent them from seeking professional psychological help [26].
- Organisational-methodical activity: preparation of analytical materials on the results of research, including monitoring of the psychological well-being of the University's students; preparation of proposals to lower the risks of aggressive and autoaggressive behaviour of students; participation in procedures of evaluating the effectiveness of methods of psychodiagnostics, prevention and correction throughout the whole period of study [27].

## Rights

In carrying out its activity the workers of the Service may request from the University's structural subdivisions the information and materials needed for the work within the Service's competence [28]; involve, with the consent of the heads of those subdivisions, the workers of the University's structural subdivisions in preparing drafts of local acts and other documents and in developing and carrying out the measures held by the Service [29]; take part in meetings held by the University's management when questions whose solution falls within the Service's competence are discussed [30]; submit to the University's management proposals to repeal, change or supplement local acts [31]; and interact with state authorities and administration, legal entities and the University's subdivisions on questions within the Service's competence [32].

## Interaction and reporting

Interaction of the Service with other structural subdivisions of the University is determined by the tasks and functions assigned to it by the Regulation [33], and the Service may be a base for carrying out the practical training of the University's students [34]. On the basis of the relevant local acts, orders and instructions the Service may be responsible, within its activity, for preparing and keeping up to date information, certificates and reports [35].

# Admission features under Article 5 of Federal Law No. 19-ФЗ — persons from the new regions: the Особенности of 14 April 2023 and section XVI of the 2024/2025 rules

## The documents

The 2024/2025 page is an extract from the rules of admission to bachelor's and master's programmes of ANO VO «Innopolis University» for the 2024/2025 academic year — its section XVI «Особенности приема на обучение по программам бакалавриата, программам магистратуры, предусмотренные статьей 5 Федерального закона от 17 февраля 2023 г. № 19-ФЗ», points 122–129 [1]. The features were developed on the basis of Federal Law No. 19-ФЗ of 17 February 2023 on the specifics of legal regulation in education and science in connection with the accession to the Russian Federation of the Donetsk and Lugansk People's Republics and the Zaporozhye and Kherson regions [1], the decree of the Government of the Russian Federation of 26.01.2024 No. 67 on the specifics of the state final certification and admission in 2024 [1], the order of the Ministry of Science and Higher Education of Russia of 01.03.2023 No. 231 [1] and the order of the Ministry of Education of Russia of 03.04.2024 No. 219 approving the list of educational organisations to whose pupils the 2024 features apply [1].

On the same subject the University issued its own «Особенности приема на обучение по программам бакалавриата, программам магистратуры и программам подготовки научных и научно-педагогических кадров в аспирантуре в АНО ВО «Университет Иннополис»», approved by order of 14 April 2023 No. ОД/АД-01/2023/04/14 in connection with the entry into force of the order of the Ministry of Science and Higher Education of Russia of 1 March 2023 No. 231 on the features of admission under parts 7 and 8 of article 5 of Federal Law No. 19-ФЗ [2]. The order approved the Особенности for admission in 2023, placed control over execution on the vice-rector — head of the department of academic policy and organisation of educational activity E.S. Bobrov, and was signed by Director K.V. Semenikhin [2]. The Особенности themselves say that they regulate admission in the 2023/24, 2024/25, 2025/26 and 2026/27 academic years, so they outlast the year named in the operative part [2].

## Who the features apply to

The 2024/2025 features regulate admission to places within the control figures and under contracts on education concluded at the expense of physical and/or legal persons for two groups of persons [1]:

- persons who completed secondary general education programmes and successfully passed the state final certification on the territories of the Donetsk and Lugansk People's Republics and the Zaporozhye and Kherson regions before their accession to the Russian Federation, and persons who passed the state final certification of secondary general education with the features under part 1 of article 5 of Federal Law No. 19-ФЗ («лица, принимаемые в соответствии с настоящими Особенностями») [1];
- persons who studied secondary general education programmes in educational organisations on the territory of the Belgorod, Bryansk and Kursk oblasts and the city of federal significance Sevastopol that are included in the list approved by the Ministry of Education of Russia («лица, обучавшиеся на приграничных территориях») [1].

The Особенности of 14 April 2023 name only the first of these groups — the persons who passed the state final certification in the four territories before accession and those who passed it with the features under part 1 of article 5 of Federal Law No. 19-ФЗ — and regulate their admission to places within the control figures and under contracts at the expense of physical and/or legal persons [2].

## Documents of education and consent to enrolment

- Such persons may present a document of the established form obtained in the Donetsk or Lugansk People's Republic or in Ukraine before the day of accession without presenting a certificate of recognition of foreign education; that education is recognised according to the correspondence of educational and educational-qualification levels established by article 2 of Federal Law No. 19-ФЗ [1].
- Where they cannot present the original of the document of the established form, they are enrolled to places within the control figures on the basis of an application of consent to enrolment stating the reason why the original cannot be presented; the application is filed not later than the day and time set by Innopolis University for the end of acceptance of the original, is used instead of the original for all conditions of admission stated in the application(s) for admission, and the presence or absence of the application is shown in the competitive lists [1].
- The person who filed the consent application may file an application withdrawing the consent; to file a consent application for places within the control figures in another organisation he must first withdraw the consent at Innopolis University; a person included among those enrolled who withdraws the consent is excluded from among those enrolled, while a person who withdrew the consent is not excluded from the lists of persons who submitted documents and from the competitive lists [1].

Points 3 and 4 of the Особенности of 14 April 2023 set the same two arrangements — the document of education obtained in those territories or in Ukraine before accession may be presented without a certificate of recognition, and where the original cannot be presented the applicant is enrolled to places within the control figures on the basis of an application of consent to enrolment stating the reason, filed within the time set by the University and usable for all conditions of admission, with the consent open to withdrawal [2].

## Entrance examinations on bachelor's programmes

- When such persons and persons who studied in the border territories are admitted to bachelor's programmes under Annex 3 to the rules, the University itself conducts general education entrance examinations in the subjects in which the ЕГЭ is held for persons applying on the basis of secondary general education [1].
- The general education entrance examination in Russian, and the entrance examination in Russian on the basis of professional education, are conducted by the University in the form of an interview at the wish of the applicant [1]; the general education entrance examinations in mathematics, informatics and information and communication technologies (ICT) and physics are conducted in the form of an interview at the wish of applicants from educational organisations on the territory of the Belgorod oblast [1].
- The results of the entrance examinations held by the University itself are assessed on a hundred-point scale regardless of the form in which they are held [1].
- Applicants on the basis of secondary general education may take the general education entrance examinations held by the University itself and/or use ЕГЭ results as the results of those examinations [1]; applicants on the basis of professional education may take entrance examinations on the basis of professional education and/or general education entrance examinations held by the University itself and/or use ЕГЭ results as the results of general education entrance examinations [1].
- For winners and prizewinners of school olympiads held in the procedure set by the Ministry of Science and Higher Education the University has set the number of ЕГЭ points or of points of a general education entrance examination held by the University itself that confirms special rights at 75 points [1].
- Annex 3 sets, for area 09.03.01 «Информатика и вычислительная техника» with the profiles «Инженерия информационных систем», «Анализ данных и искусственный интеллект» and «Математические основы искусственного интеллекта», and for area 15.03.06 «Мехатроника и робототехника» with the profile «Робототехника», the entrance examinations mathematics, informatics and ICT or physics, and the Russian language, in the form of computer testing with a minimum of 60 and a maximum of 100 points for each examination, and for each examination also an interview at the wish of the applicant with the same minimum of 60 and maximum of 100 points [1].
- Applicants on the basis of secondary general education and on the basis of professional education may use ЕГЭ results as the results of the general education entrance examinations held by the University itself [1].

The Особенности of 14 April 2023 (point 5, referring to Annex 1) repeat the same scheme: the University itself conducts general education entrance examinations in the subjects of the ЕГЭ for applicants on the basis of secondary general education; the examinations in Russian are held as an interview at the applicant's wish; the results are assessed on a hundred-point scale regardless of the form; applicants on the basis of secondary general education may take the University's examinations and/or use ЕГЭ results, and applicants on the basis of professional education may take examinations on the basis of professional education and/or the University's general education examinations and/or use ЕГЭ results; the number of points confirming the special rights of school olympiad winners and prizewinners is 75 [2].

## National olympiads of Ukraine

- Winners and prizewinners of the IV stage of the all-Ukrainian pupil olympiads and of the republican olympiad of schoolchildren (national olympiads), and members of the national teams of Ukraine formed in the procedure set by the legislation of Ukraine that took part in international olympiads in general education subjects, who are recognised as citizens of the Russian Federation or who permanently resided on the territory of the Donetsk or Lugansk People's Republic, the Zaporozhye or Kherson region on the day of their accession as citizens of the Russian Federation, get the right to admission without entrance examinations as winners and prizewinners of the final stage of the All-Russian olympiad of schoolchildren and members of the national teams of the Russian Federation that took part in international olympiads [1].
- When admitting them the University, under Annex 4 to the rules, establishes the correspondence of the areas of study to the profiles of the national and international olympiads for granting the right to admission without entrance examinations, and establishes that there are no entrance examinations corresponding to the profiles of the national and international olympiads [1].
- Annex 4 provides that the right to admission without entrance examinations for the subjects of the all-Ukrainian pupil olympiad — mathematics, informatics and physics — corresponds to area 09.03.01 «Информатика и вычислительная техника» [1]; the correspondence of the areas of study to the profiles of international olympiads and the general education entrance examinations corresponding to those profiles for granting the special advantage are set by order of Innopolis University of 30.11.2023 № ОД/УД-05/2023/11/30 [1].

The Особенности of 14 April 2023 grant the same right to admission without entrance examinations to the winners and prizewinners of the IV stage of the all-Ukrainian pupil olympiads and of the republican olympiad of schoolchildren and to the members of the national teams of Ukraine, where they are recognised as citizens of the Russian Federation or permanently resided in one of those four territories on the day of accession (point 6) [2]. For the period during which that right is granted they are equated to persons having 100 points in a general education entrance examination (100 ЕГЭ points or 100 points for the University's own examination) where the examination corresponds to the profile of the national or international olympiad (point 7) [2]. Under point 8 and Annex 2 the correspondence of areas of study to olympiad profiles and of the entrance examinations for the right and for the 100 points is set out separately for the all-Ukrainian pupil olympiads and for the republican olympiad of schoolchildren — mathematics, informatics and ICT, and physics, all to area 09.03.01 «Информатика и вычислительная техника» [2]. For international olympiads that correspondence for admission to 09.03.01 in 2023 is set by the order of the University of 05.12.2022 No. ОД/УД-04/2022/12/05 [2].

## Entrance examinations on master's and aspirantura programmes

- When such persons are admitted to master's programmes under section 3 of Annex 1 and section 2 of Annex 2 to the 2024/2025 rules, the University itself sets the list and forms of entrance examinations, the minimum number of points of the entrance examinations, the list of individual achievements taken into account and the procedure for taking them into account [1].
- Individual achievements obtained in the Donetsk or Lugansk People's Republic or in Ukraine before the day of accession are credited to the applicants in accordance with the rules [1].
- The annexes provide, for each master's programme, one entrance examination in the form of computer testing with a minimum of 40 and a maximum of 100 points: for the profiles «Искусственный интеллект и инженерия данных», «Инженерия безопасности систем и сетей», «Программная инженерия», «Робототехника и компьютерное зрение», «Управление разработкой компьютерных игр», «Кибербезопасность», «Технологическое предпринимательство», «Управление цифровым продуктом» и «Управление на основе данных» of area 09.04.01, and for the profile «Робототехника» of area 15.04.06 [1].

Annex 3 to the Особенности of 14 April 2023 covers master's admission for 2023/2024: computer testing for area 09.04.01 «Информатика и вычислительная техника» with the profiles «Искусственный интеллект и инженерия данных», «Инженерия безопасности систем и сетей», «Программная инженерия» and «Робототехника и компьютерное зрение», and for area 38.04.05 «Бизнес-информатика» with the profile «Информационное технологическое предпринимательство» [2]. For master's programmes points are awarded for a document of higher education with honours (3 points) and for a publication in Scopus or Web of Science (3 points for one publication), no more than 6 points in total, included in the sum of competitive points, and the applicant presents the documents confirming the achievements [2].

For aspirantura Annex 3, part 3, provides for scientific specialty 1.2.2 «Математическое моделирование, численные методы и комплексы программ» an entrance examination by computer testing in the scientific specialty (written) with a minimum of 60 and a maximum of 100 points and an interview in the scientific specialty (oral) with a minimum of 60 and a maximum of 100 points [2]. Individual achievements there are publications on the profile of the programme in Scopus over the last 3 years (5 points each, no more than 20 in total), the same for Web of Science, an article published in both counted once at 5 points, evidenced by the output data of the published article, and no more than 40 points in total [2].

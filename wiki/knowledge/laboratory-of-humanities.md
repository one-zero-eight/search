# Laboratory of Humanities (Лаборатория гуманитарных наук) — place, tasks, functions and rights

## Document and place in the structure

The Regulation on the Laboratory of Humanities (Положение о Лаборатории гуманитарных наук) regulates the activity of the Laboratory of Humanities of the autonomous non-commercial organisation of higher education «Innopolis University» and determines its tasks, functions, rights and duties [1]. It was approved by an order of ANO VO «Университет Иннополис» of «9» January 2019, whose number is given on the page in a garbled form as «№ &Д-17/2019/01/09», and carries the place «г. Иннополис» [2]. The page is a scan read by OCR and warns that numbers and dates should be checked against the PDF before relying on them [2].

The Laboratory is a structural subdivision of the Institute of Humanities and Social Sciences of the Faculty of Humanities and Social Sciences of the University [3]. It reports directly to the head (начальник) of the Laboratory; in the absence of the head it reports to the director of the institute and the dean of the faculty [4].

In its activity the Laboratory follows the Constitution of the Russian Federation, the Labour Code of the Russian Federation, Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29.12.2012 «Об образовании в Российской Федерации» and other legislative and normative legal acts of the Russian Federation regulating the activity of higher education institutions, the University's Charter, decisions of the Academic, Supervisory and Operational councils of the University, orders and instructions of the Director of the University, other local normative acts of the University and this Regulation [2].

The structure, the number of staff and the staffing table of the Laboratory, and changes to them, are approved by the Director of the University [5]. The Laboratory is headed by a head (начальник) of the Laboratory, who is hired and dismissed by an order of the Director of the University [6]. The working conditions of the Laboratory's employees are determined by the employment contracts concluded with each employee and by the University's internal labour regulations [2]. The rights, duties and degree of responsibility of the Laboratory's employees are established by job descriptions [7]. The Regulation and additions and changes to it are approved by the Director of the University or by a person authorised by him [8].

## Tasks

- creating a scientific-methodological basis for fundamental and applied research in accordance with the University's research plans [9]
- helping to form the research skills of the University's students, aspirants and teachers [10]
- expanding and strengthening the University's scientific ties with organisations carrying on similar educational and research activity [11]
- promoting scientific knowledge among the public [12]
- carrying out research work within grants in the Laboratory's profile in accordance with grant support, budget and non-budget contracts concluded by the University [13]
- preparing for publication and publishing annotated and abstracted materials on the newest experience and the own materials and developments of the University's scientific-pedagogical workers and students [14]

## Functions

- developing research programmes in the Laboratory's field of activity [15]
- improving the methods, technologies, techniques and instruments of research work [16]
- developing and publishing scientific-methodological manuals based on the Laboratory's results [17]
- publishing scientific articles in journals, including those indexed in international citation bases such as Web of Science (WoS) and Scopus [18]
- holding thematic seminars and practical sessions and consultations for the University's teachers, aspirants and students [19]
- preparing practical (experimental) material for inclusion in candidate and doctoral dissertations [20]
- drawing the University's students into research activity [21]
- revealing the scientific potential of the University's young specialists [22]

## Rights

- request from the University's structural subdivisions the information and materials needed for the work within the Laboratory's competence [23]
- with the consent of the heads of the University's structural subdivisions, involve the employees of those subdivisions in preparing draft local acts and other documents and in developing and carrying out measures conducted by the Laboratory [24]
- take part in meetings held by the University's management when discussing questions whose solution falls within the Laboratory's competence [25]
- submit to the University's management proposals to cancel, amend or supplement the University's local acts [26]
- interact with bodies of state authority and administration, legal entities and the University's subdivisions on questions within the Laboratory's competence [27]

## Interaction and reporting

Interaction of the Laboratory with the University's other structural subdivisions is determined by the tasks and functions assigned to it by this Regulation [28]. The Laboratory interacts with the Department for Attracting Grants on questions of participation in research work, industrial projects and grants [29]. Interaction with other structural subdivisions takes place on questions of preparing draft local acts, orders, instructions, contracts and other documents and of preparing conclusions and written and oral certificates on questions within the Laboratory's competence arising in practical activity [30].

Within its activity the Laboratory is responsible for preparing reports on research work, industrial projects and grants and for passing them in time to the University's internal structural subdivisions [31]. It submits reporting in accordance with the requirements of the Ministry of Education of the Russian Federation and of other state statistics bodies, as needed for management and financial decisions [32].

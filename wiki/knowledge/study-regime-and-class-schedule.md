# Study regime and class schedule

## What the regulation governs

The Regulation on the study regime of students determines the duration, periodicity and conditions of classes and regulates the regime of classes and rest of students [1]. Its requirements apply to teaching staff and to students of bachelor's programmes, master's programmes and programmes of training of research and research-pedagogical staff in aspirantura [2].

It was approved by order of 2 August 2022 № ОД/УД-06/2022/08/02, put into force from 1 September 2022, and replaced the Regulation approved by order of 20 July 2020 № ОД/УД-05/2020/07/20 [3]. ## Periods of study

Training at the University is conducted in full-time and part-time (заочная) forms [4]. The educational process is organised by periods of study [5]:

- by academic years (courses) [5];
- within courses, by semesters and (or) trimesters: two semesters per course in bachelor's and master's programmes — autumn and spring; two semesters per course in aspirantura — first and second; three trimesters per course — autumn, spring and summer [5].

The duration of a course in bachelor's and master's programmes includes study time and vacation time and cannot exceed 366 calendar days [6]. Periods of study within courses are allocated in accordance with the calendar academic schedules; the academic year consists of two semesters or three trimesters, each of which ends with the forms of interim assessment provided by the curriculum; in bachelor's and master's programmes, a course lasting less than 300 calendar days may have one semester or one or two trimesters [7].

## Vacations and non-working days

The total duration of vacations during the academic year in bachelor's and master's programmes, unless otherwise established by the federal state educational standard (ФГОС ВО), is [8]:

- not less than 49 and not more than 70 calendar days when study during the academic year lasts more than 300 calendar days [8];
- not less than 21 and not more than 49 calendar days when it lasts not less than 100 and not more than 300 calendar days [8];
- not more than 14 calendar days when it lasts less than 100 calendar days [8].

Educational activity is not carried out on non-working holiday days [9]. For aspirantura students, vacations in the academic year are set at not less than 6 weeks under ФГОС ВО, and at not less than 6 and not more than 8 weeks under the federal state requirements (ФГТ) [10].

## Start and end of the academic year

The start dates of the academic year are established by orders of the University Director or of a person authorised by him [11]. The academic year for students of full-time and part-time forms ends in accordance with the approved calendar academic schedules for the specific field of study and profile of the programme or for the specific scientific specialities [12].

## Study groups and streams

For lecture-type classes in bachelor's and master's programmes, study groups may be combined into streams; if necessary, groups of different fields of study may be combined into one stream [13]. For seminar-type classes, study groups are formed with, as a rule, no more than 30 students from one field of study; seminars are held for one study group, and if necessary students of different fields of study may be combined into one group; for laboratory work and other types of practical classes the study group may be divided into subgroups [14]. In bachelor's programmes, groups for practical classes in physical culture and sport are formed with, as a rule, no more than 20 students, taking into account the students' health, physical development and physical fitness [15].

## Schedule

Classes in the form of contact work are held according to a schedule drawn up on the basis of the curricula (individual curricula) and calendar academic schedules; the schedule is approved by order of the University Director or of a person authorised by him, and contact work may be conducted using e-learning and distance learning technologies [16].

The schedule for a semester is formed before the start of the study period and communicated to students via corporate email; the dates of interim assessment (including the dates of the first and second retakes) are communicated to students via corporate email [17]. When schedules of contact-work classes are drawn up, irrational expenditure of students' time is excluded [18], and current sanitary-hygienic norms are taken into account [19].

## Length of classes and breaks

An academic hour for all types of classes is set at 45 minutes; the duration of a contact-work class cannot exceed 90 minutes (two academic hours — one pair); breaks between classes are set at not less than 5 minutes; a 30-minute break is set for students for rest and food [20]. The timetable of classes for full-time and part-time students is set by order of the University Director or of a person authorised by him [21].

## Conduct during classes

A student must come to the start of classes [22]. After classes begin, silence and order must be observed in all classrooms and adjoining rooms; entrance to and exit from a classroom during a class is possible only with the permission of the person conducting the class [23]. Interrupting classes and entering or leaving classrooms during them is prohibited [24].

## Changes

The Regulation is approved by order of the University Director or of a person authorised by him and came into force on 1 September 2022 [25]; changes or additions are made by order of the University Director or of a person authorised by him [26].

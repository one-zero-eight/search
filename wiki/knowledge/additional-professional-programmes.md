# Additional professional programmes (ДПП) — the University Regulation: types, structure, duration, documents and how a programme is designed and delivered

## The document

The Regulation on the additional professional programme of ANO VO «Innopolis University» (Положение о дополнительной профессиональной программе) in its new edition was approved by order of the University of 10 December 2018 No. ОД/УД-03/2018/12/10 and is dated Innopolis, 2018 [1]. Compliance with it is obligatory when additional professional programmes (ДПП) are developed and delivered at the University [2].

It was drawn up in accordance with Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29 December 2012 «Об образовании в Российской Федерации», the order of the Ministry of Education and Science of Russia of 01.07.2013 No. 499 on the organisation of educational activity under additional professional programmes, the order of 23.08.2017 No. 816 on the use of e-learning and distance educational technologies, the University's Charter and other normative and local acts [3]. ## What ДПП are for and what kinds exist

Additional professional education serves the educational and professional needs of a person, their professional development and the correspondence of their qualification to changing conditions of professional activity and of the social environment, and is carried out through additional professional programmes — повышение квалификации programmes and professional retraining programmes [2].

- A повышение квалификации programme aims at improving and (or) acquiring a new competence needed for professional activity and (or) at raising the professional level within the existing qualification [2].
- A professional retraining programme aims at acquiring the competence needed for a new type of professional activity, and at acquiring a new qualification [2].
- ДПП are developed by the University with account of the needs of the person or organisation on whose initiative the additional professional education is provided (the Заказчик) [2], and their delivery may be arranged jointly with other organisations and institutions [2].
- Studies are provided on the basis of a contract of education concluded with the listener and (or) with a natural or legal person who undertakes to pay for the education of the person being enrolled [2].

## Who may be admitted

Persons who have secondary vocational and (or) higher education, and persons who are receiving secondary vocational and (or) higher education, are admitted to master ДПП [2].

## Mandatory structure of a programme

The structure of a ДПП (the model form is given in the annex to the Regulation) includes: general provisions, normative documents and a general characteristic of the programme defining its aims, terms of study, total workload and requirements to the listener; the aim; the characteristic of the new qualification and of the related types of professional activity, labour functions and (or) qualification levels; the characteristic of the competences to be improved and (or) the list of new competences; the planned learning outcomes; the organisational-pedagogical conditions of delivery; the curriculum, which determines the list, workload, sequence and distribution of subjects, courses, disciplines (modules) and other types of learning activity and the forms of attestation; the calendar study schedule; the work programmes of subjects, courses, disciplines (modules); the forms of attestation; the assessment materials; and other necessary materials [4]. The workload of a ДПП is set in academic hours [4].

## Requirements to the content

The content of a ДПП is determined by the educational programme developed and approved by the University, unless Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ and other normative legal acts provide otherwise [5]. It must:

- take account of professional standards and of the qualification requirements stated in the qualification handbooks for the relevant positions, professions and specialties, or of the qualification requirements to professional knowledge and skills necessary for performing duties established in accordance with federal laws and other normative legal acts [5];
- correspond to the requirements of the federal state educational standards of secondary vocational and (or) higher education to the results of mastering educational programmes [5];
- correspond to the requirements of the Заказчик [5];
- be oriented to modern educational technologies and means of teaching [5];
- correspond to the accepted rules of formatting of programmes [5];
- correspond to the sub-types of additional professional education [5].

The content of the programme and of its separate components (disciplines, modules, practices, internships) must be directed at achieving its aims and planned results [5]. ДПП are developed in a competence format: the list of competences is built on the federal state educational standards of secondary vocational and (or) higher education, and (or) the requirements of professional standards, and (or) the qualification requirements of the qualification handbooks, and (or) other requirements established in accordance with federal laws and other normative legal acts [5]. Depending on the aims and terms of study, the ratio between theoretical preparation and practical training may vary [5].

## Повышение квалификации programmes

- The structure of a повышение квалификации programme must describe the list of professional competences within the existing qualification whose qualitative change the training achieves [1].
- Its aim is mastering innovations in specific matters of professional activity, as well as a deeper study of problems in the professional field in order to update knowledge on questions arising at the level of an industry, a region or an organisation [1].
- The term of study is set in the programme with account of the needs of the Заказчик, but not less than 16 hours [1].
- Such a programme ends with a final attestation [1].

## Professional retraining programmes

- The learning results must correspond to the results of mastering the main professional educational programmes and must be aimed at acquiring a new qualification requiring a change of the orientation (profile) or specialisation within the field of study (specialty) of the professional education obtained earlier; they are determined on the basis of the professional competences of the corresponding federal state educational standards [6].
- Its structure must present the characteristic of the new qualification and of the related types of professional activity, labour functions and (or) qualification levels, and the characteristic of the competences to be improved and (or) the list of new competences; it is developed on the basis of the established qualification requirements, professional standards and the requirements of the corresponding federal state educational standards of secondary vocational and (or) higher education [6].
- The term of study is determined by the structural subdivision of the University that delivers the programme, independently and with account of the needs of the Заказчик, but not less than 250 hours [6].
- Mastering a retraining programme ends with a compulsory final attestation — a final attestation work or a final qualification work, or an interdisciplinary final examination [6].

## Internship (стажировка)

A ДПП may be delivered fully or partly as an internship, which serves to study advanced experience, including foreign experience, to consolidate the theoretical knowledge obtained in a повышение квалификации or retraining programme and to acquire practical skills for use in performing one's duties; its content is determined with account of the proposals of the organisations that send specialists and of the content of the ДПП, and its duration follows from the aims of the training and is agreed with the head of the organisation where it takes place [6].

An internship is individual or group and may include independent work with study publications; acquiring professional and organisational skills; studying the organisation and technology of production and work; participation in the organisation's work planning; work with technical, normative and other documentation; performing the duties of officials (as acting officer or deputy); and participation in meetings and business meetings [6]. On its results the listener is issued a document on qualification depending on the programme delivered [6].

## Documents on qualification

Persons who have successfully mastered the ДПП and passed the final attestation receive an удостоверение о повышении квалификации and (or) a диплом о профессиональной переподготовке [7]. The qualification stated in the document entitles the holder to engage in a certain professional activity and (or) perform concrete labour functions for which the legislation establishes obligatory requirements to a qualification obtained through additional professional education, unless legislation provides otherwise [7].

Where a ДПП is mastered in parallel with obtaining secondary vocational and (or) higher education, these documents are issued simultaneously with the corresponding document on education and qualification [7]. Documents on qualification are issued on blank forms protected against forgery, or on other forms approved by order of the University's director or a person authorised by the director [7]. Persons who did not pass the final attestation or received unsatisfactory results at it, as well as those who mastered part of a ДПП and (or) were expelled, receive a certificate of study or of the period of study (справка об обучении или о периоде обучения) on a form established by the University [8].

## How a programme is designed, approved, published and stored

- A ДПП is developed by the structural subdivision of the University that delivers it [9] and is formatted as a package of documents in accordance with clause 3 and the annex to the Regulation [9].
- It is prepared on paper and in electronic form (MS Word, .doc) meeting the requirements: line spacing 1.5; text margins 20 mm on all sides; paragraph indent 10 mm; Times New Roman (Cyr) font, size 14 [9].
- The programme is approved by order of the director of the University or of another person authorised by the director, and is then placed on the University's official website [9]. The original is kept together with the approval order in the structural subdivision responsible for records keeping and control, and a copy in the subdivision delivering the programme [9].

## How studies are organised

- The forms of study and the terms of mastering a ДПП are determined by the educational programme and (or) the contract of education; the term must make it possible to achieve the planned results and to obtain the new competence (qualification) declared in the programme [8].
- ДПП are delivered independently or through network forms of delivery, and the organisation of educational activity may rest on the modular principle and on various educational technologies, including distance educational technologies and e-learning [8]. A ДПП is delivered as a rule in Russian; educational activity may be carried on in a foreign language in accordance with the educational programme [8].
- The organisation of study for persons with health limitations follows the Regulation on the conditions of study of disabled persons and persons with health limitations at the University [8].
- The educational process, including the regime of classes, is regulated by the class schedule for each study group and programme, and the weekly volume of classroom load depends on the form of study and is determined on the basis of the approved programme and the calendar study schedule [8]. The process runs throughout the whole calendar year, and the academic year lasts 12 calendar months [8].
- Depending on the content and technologies used, the types of classes may include lectures, practical and seminar classes, laboratory work, round tables, master classes, workshops, business and role games, trainings, experience-exchange seminars, off-site classes, consultations, performance of an attestation, qualification or project work and other types defined by the curriculum [8]. For all types of classroom classes the academic hour is 45 minutes, and one class as a rule includes two academic hours [8].
- The quality of mastering is checked through current monitoring of performance and the final attestation, intermediate attestation being conducted as a rule for professional retraining programmes [8]. Mastering a retraining programme is accompanied by intermediate attestation in the forms set by the curriculum, in accordance with the Regulation on the organisation of current monitoring and intermediate attestation of listeners under additional professional programmes [8]; mastering a ДПП ends with the final attestation in the form determined by the educational programme, conducted in accordance with the Regulation on the final attestation of listeners under additional professional programmes [8].
- A graduate is considered to have completed studies on the basis of the order of the director (or a person authorised by the director) on their expulsion; the specialist of the subdivision delivering the programme prepares the draft order on the graduation and issue of documents, passes information to the authorised department for drawing up strict-reporting documents and receives the completed blank forms [8]. The quality of mastering is evaluated in accordance with the Regulation on the types and forms of internal evaluation of the quality of delivery of additional professional programmes and their results [8].

## Individual curriculum

Within a ДПП, study under an individual curriculum is possible; it is undertaken on the listener's personal application, is developed on the basis of the programme's curriculum within 10 working days from the submission of the application and is approved by the head of the structural subdivision delivering the programme [1].

The individual curriculum ensures mastering the programme by individualising its content with account of the educational needs of the particular listener and by choosing the pace and terms of study; it determines the list, workload and sequence of subjects, courses, disciplines (modules), practice and other types of learning activity and the forms of the listener's intermediate attestation [1]. A modular principle may be used, various educational technologies including distance ones may be applied, study may be organised in a network form, and the terms may change through a change in the intensity of study and (or) credit for separate disciplines [1].

## Credit for previous study

Credit for subjects, courses, disciplines (modules) mastered earlier under main professional and (or) additional professional programmes is given when professional retraining programmes are mastered, under the conditions that their name should as a rule coincide with the names in the curriculum of the corresponding additional professional programme, and that the volume of academic hours and the forms of attestation of the credited items should as a rule correspond to or exceed those in that curriculum [10].

The basis for credit is the documents on the basic education or a certificate of study; the decision on the possibility of credit is taken by the attestation commission and frees the listener from repeated study of the relevant items, and when a diploma of professional retraining is drawn up the credited items are entered in the annex to the diploma [10].

## Responsibility and control

The vice-rector — head of the department that includes the structural subdivision delivering the programme is responsible for the development, delivery, provision and storage of the programme, for its content, for placing it on the University's official website and for the timely issue of documents on education and qualification, and exercises control over the delivery of the programme [11], [12].

# Ongoing assessment and interim attestation of listeners of additional professional programmes (ДПП) — the Regulation of 5 September 2016

## The document

The Regulation on the organisation of ongoing assessment of academic performance and of interim attestation of listeners (слушатели) of additional professional programmes of ANO VO «Innopolis University» was approved by the University's order of 5 September 2016 (the number of the order is illegible in the OCR of the scan) together with the order's preamble, and the control over its execution was retained by the director [1]. The order was issued under Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29.12.2012 «Об образовании в Российской Федерации» and the order of the Ministry of Education and Science of Russia of 01.07.2013 No. 499 on the organisation and carrying out of educational activity under additional professional programmes, in order to organise the educational process under additional professional programmes [1].

The Regulation governs the forms and procedure of ongoing assessment and interim attestation of learners under additional professional programmes (ДПП) [2]. It is obligatory for all structural subdivisions of the University that carry on educational activity in the field of additional professional education [3]. It was drawn up on the basis of Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ «Об образовании в Российской Федерации», the order of the Ministry of Education and Science of Russia on additional professional programmes and the University's Charter [4].

## Two forms of control of learning results

Control of the quality of mastering an additional educational programme includes ongoing assessment of academic performance, interim attestation and final attestation [5].

- Interim attestation is the assessment of the intermediate and final results of study in disciplines (modules), including the results of course design (course works and projects) and of practical training (practices); it is the main form of control of the learners' academic work, and its forms and procedure are determined by the structural subdivisions of additional professional education independently [6].
- Ongoing assessment is the system of evaluating the course of mastering the disciplines (modules) and practical training provided by the ДПП (submission of current tasks, control works, colloquia, abstracts); it is an additional form of control of academic work, conducted throughout the whole period of study or during practical training; its forms are determined by the ДПП, and its procedure and frequency are set by the teacher of the discipline [7].

## Ongoing assessment

- Ongoing assessment is intended to check the quality of mastering the study material, to raise the learners' motivation to active systematic work during the period of study, to activate their independent work and to improve the methods of conducting classes [8].
- It is carried out in all disciplines (modules) of the curriculum [9].
- Its measures are organised by the teacher responsible for the delivery of the discipline (module) [10].
- They may be held during classroom work in the teacher's presence; during the learners' independent work without the teacher's presence, with the results checked afterwards by the teacher; and with the tools of the information educational environment remotely, both during contact work with the teacher and during the learners' independent work, including with automatic evaluation of the results [11].
- The forms of ongoing assessment may be home tasks, control and independent works, abstracts (рефераты), essays (эссе), colloquia, laboratory works and other forms of ongoing assessment, by decision of the teacher responsible for the delivery of the discipline (module) [12].
- The list of the forms of ongoing assessment, the procedure for holding them, the tools and technologies used, the criteria for grading the separate forms and the grading system used are indicated in the ДПП [13].
- Ongoing assessment is an element of the internal system of control of the quality of the learners' preparation [14].
- It must take into account the learners' performance of all the types of classes provided by the ДПП, attendance of classes, independent and research work and others [15].
- The teacher conducting ongoing assessment must bring to the learners' knowledge the information about the procedure of ongoing assessment in the discipline (module) and the content and types of work, and must acquaint the learners with the grading criteria; the teacher ensures the development and formation of the fund of tasks used for the control of the quality of learning [16].
- Every teacher must bring the results of ongoing assessment to the learners' knowledge, explaining the reasons for a negative result [17].
- The results of ongoing assessment are summed up before the interim attestation in the discipline begins [18].
- The results of ongoing assessment may be taken into account by the teacher when setting the grade during interim attestation [19].

## Interim attestation

- Interim attestation is meant to evaluate the competences obtained by the learners in the course of study and to ensure control of the quality of mastering the educational programmes [20].
- It is conducted on completion of the study of a discipline or of its part (module) and is recorded in an examination/pass record sheet (Приложение 1) [21].
- Its forms are a pass (зачёт) or an examination (экзамен) [22], and the forms for each academic discipline are determined by the ДПП [23].
- A pass is the form of interim attestation aimed at checking the learner's successful mastering of the material of lecture courses, practical and seminar classes, performance of laboratory works and course projects (works) and practical training; the type of pass (ordinary or differentiated) is established in the ДПП [24].
- An examination is the form of interim attestation based on the results of mastering theoretical knowledge and acquiring practical skills and competences, whose aim is to control the results of the learners' mastering of the study material in a concrete discipline and to check and evaluate the knowledge obtained, the development of creative thinking, the acquired skills of independent work and the ability to apply theoretical knowledge in solving practical tasks [25].
- An examination may be held in oral form, in the form of a written work or in the form of testing; the list of sample questions and tasks and the grading criteria are brought to the learners' knowledge [26].
- Interim attestation held with the use of distance educational technologies may be conducted by videoconference, by computer testing, by exchange of files (with the distance learning system or e-mail) or by exchange of messages in forums or chats [27].
- Examinations are held according to the approved schedule, which determines the time and place of holding them [28].
- At an oral examination the learner receives an examination ticket; the examiner may ask additional questions within the examination programme in order to evaluate the level of the competences formed; during the examination the learner may use technical means for calculation and graphic tasks, calculators, maps, reference books, tables and other reference literature only with the examiner's permission [29].
- An examination in the form of a written work is performed under the supervision of the teacher-examiner or of the teacher conducting the practical or laboratory classes [30].
- An examination in the form of testing (an examination in written form) includes questions and (or) tasks on the whole course [31].
- Written works and tests are checked by the teacher-examiner, the date of the check and the teacher's signature are put on the last page of the written work and test, and the results must be announced within 24 hours after the examination ends [32].
- A failure to appear at an examination is marked in the examination record sheet with the words «неявка» and certified by the teacher's signature [33].
- Learners who have not passed interim attestation for valid reasons (medical indications and other cases confirmed by documents) or who have received unsatisfactory results at interim attestation are given the opportunity to pass interim attestation in the corresponding subject, course or discipline (module) not more than two times, within the deadlines determined by the structural subdivision of additional professional education [34].
- An appeal against the results of interim tests is not allowed [35].
- The assessment materials for interim attestation in a discipline (module) or practical training, which form part of the ДПП, include the list of competences; the description of the indicators and criteria of evaluating the competences and the description of the grading scales; typical control tasks or other materials needed to evaluate the knowledge, skills, abilities and (or) experience of activity characterising the stages of formation of the competences in the course of mastering the educational programme; and methodological materials determining the procedures of evaluating the knowledge, skills, abilities and (or) experience of activity [36].

## Grading

- The learners' competences, knowledge, skills and abilities are evaluated with the marks «отлично», «хорошо», «удовлетворительно», «неудовлетворительно», «зачтено» and «не зачтено» [37].
- Passes are evaluated with the mark «зачтено» or «не зачтено» [38]; differentiated passes are evaluated on the four-point system «отлично», «хорошо», «удовлетворительно» and «неудовлетворительно» [39]; examinations are evaluated on the same four-point system [40].
- The main criteria of evaluation are set out in the Regulation [41]:
  - «зачтено» is given to a learner who studied the discipline successfully in the semester and has no debts on the results of ongoing assessments; «не зачтено» is given to a learner who has debts on the results of ongoing assessments in the discipline [41];
  - «отлично» is given where the learner has mastered the programme material deeply and firmly, expounds it exhaustively, consistently, clearly and logically, can closely link theory with practice, copes freely with tasks, questions and other types of application of knowledge, is not hindered when tasks are modified, uses monographic literature in the answer, justifies the decision correctly and has versatile skills and techniques of performing practical tasks [41];
  - «хорошо» is given where the learner knows the material firmly, expounds it competently and to the point without substantial inaccuracies in the answer, applies theoretical positions correctly in solving practical questions and tasks and has the necessary skills and techniques of performing them [41];
  - «удовлетворительно» is given where the learner has knowledge of the main material only, has not mastered its details, makes inaccuracies and insufficiently correct formulations and breaches of logical sequence in expounding the programme material, and has difficulties in performing practical works [41];
  - «неудовлетворительно» is given to a learner who does not know a significant part of the programme material, makes substantial mistakes and performs practical works uncertainly and with great difficulty, and also to a learner who refused to take the examination after it had begun [41].
- The criteria for evaluating the competences, knowledge, skills and abilities in a discipline are established in the ДПП [42].

## The record sheet

The form of the examination/pass record sheet (Приложение 1) contains the name of the programme (of a повышение квалификации programme), the name of the discipline (module), the group, the volume of the programme in hours, the period of study, the type of interim attestation (examination, pass and others), the list of learners with their marks, the teacher's signature, and the signature of the head (or of a person acting under a power of attorney) [1].

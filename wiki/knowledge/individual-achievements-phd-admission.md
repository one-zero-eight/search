# Individual achievements in PhD aspirantura admission

## How individual achievements are taken into account

The university accounts for individual achievements of applicants in two ways [1]:

- it awards points that are included in the applicant's total competitive score — for general individual achievements, whose list the university itself establishes, and for targeted individual achievements, i.e. participation in career-guidance events held by the customer of targeted training, which are taken into account under clause 119 of the admission rules for admission to places within the targeted quota in addition to the points for general achievements [1];
- it takes individual achievements into account where applicants are equal on other ranking criteria in the competitive lists [1].

The university establishes the list of general individual achievements and how they are counted, and sets the number of points for targeted individual achievements at 2 (two) points, the same for all applicants who took part in career-guidance events, for all customers of targeted training that held such events [2].

As an individual achievement taken into account where applicants are equal on other ranking criteria, the university establishes the average score of the document of education; if applicants are also equal on that, the list of such achievements may be supplemented during the admission campaign [3].

## Points for general individual achievements

For general individual achievements the university awards [4]:

- 3 (three) points for a document of higher education and qualification with honours (с отличием) [4];
- 5 (five) points for each scientific publication in the field (profile) of the aspirantura programme in the Scopus database published not earlier than 2023, but not more than 20 (twenty) points in total [4];
- 5 (five) points for each such publication in the Web of Science database published not earlier than 2023, but not more than 20 (twenty) points in total [4];
- 5 (five) points for each such publication in scientific journals included in the list of the Higher Attestation Commission (ВАК, the White list) published not earlier than 2023, but not more than 20 (twenty) points in total [4].

Where the same article was published both in Scopus and in Web of Science, it is counted as one individual achievement worth 5 (five) points [4]. In total no more than 20 (twenty) points may be awarded for general individual achievements [5].

## What the applicant submits

The applicant submits copies of documents confirming that the individual achievements were obtained [6]. For publications, these are copies of the articles from the published journals (collections) showing the titles of the articles, the list of authors, the numbers of the journals or the names of the collections, the year of publication, the elibrary id of the publication or its DOI, and other information if available [4].

## The same rules in the 2026/2027 admission rules

The current rules of admission to aspirantura programmes contain the same provisions [7]:

- individual achievements are counted by awarding points included in the sum of competitive points — for general achievements and for targeted achievements, i.e. participation in career-guidance events held by the customer of targeted training, counted under clause 119 in addition to the points for general achievements — and by taking achievements into account where applicants are equal on other ranking criteria [8];
- the number of points for targeted individual achievements is 2 (two) points, the same for all applicants who took part in the career-guidance events of all customers that held them [9];
- the achievement taken into account where applicants are equal is the average score of the document of education, and where applicants are equal on it the list may be supplemented during the admission campaign [7];
- the applicant submits copies of documents confirming the achievements [10];
- points are awarded for a document of higher education and qualification with honours — 3 (three) points — and for scientific publications in the field (profile) of the aspirantura programme in Scopus, in Web of Science and in journals of the list of the Higher Attestation Commission (the White list) published not earlier than 2023 — 5 (five) points for each publication, but not more than 20 (twenty) points in total for each of them, and the same article published in Scopus and in Web of Science is counted as one achievement worth 5 (five) points [11];
- no more than 20 (twenty) points in total may be awarded for general individual achievements [12].

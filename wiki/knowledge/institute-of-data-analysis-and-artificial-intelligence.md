# Institute of Data Analysis and Artificial Intelligence (Институт анализа данных и искусственного интеллекта) — place, tasks, functions and rights

## The document

The Regulation on the Institute of Data Analysis and Artificial Intelligence of ANO VO «Innopolis University» regulates the Institute's activity and determines its tasks, functions, rights and duties [1].

The approval block of the wiki copy is only partly legible: it shows January 2019 and the designation АД-05/2019/01/09 [2]. The copy is a scan read by OCR, so numbers and dates should be checked against the PDF [2].

## Place in the structure

- The Institute is a structural subdivision of the Faculty of Computer and Engineering Sciences [3].
- It includes the Laboratory of data analysis and bioinformatics, the Laboratory of data analysis and machine learning in the oil and gas industry, the Laboratory of data analysis and financial technologies, the Laboratory of artificial intelligence in game development, the Laboratory of machine learning and data representation and the master's programme «Анализ данных и машинное обучение» [3].
- The Institute and its subdivisions report to the director of the Institute and, in the director's absence, to the dean of the faculty [4].
- The structure, the number of staff and the staffing table of the Institute, and changes to them, are approved by the University's director [5].
- The Institute is headed by a director, who is hired and dismissed by order of the University's director [6].
- The Regulation and additions and changes to it are approved by the University's director or by another person authorised by him [7].
- The working conditions of the Institute's employees are determined by the employment contracts concluded with each employee and by the University's internal labour regulations [8]; their rights, duties and degree of responsibility are established by job descriptions [9].
- In its activity the Institute follows the Constitution of the Russian Federation, the Labour Code of the Russian Federation, Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29 December 2012 «Об образовании в Российской Федерации», other legislative and normative legal acts on the activity of higher education institutions, the University's Charter, decisions of the Academic, Supervisory and Operations councils of the University, orders and instructions of the University's director, other local normative acts of the University and this Regulation [10].

The Regulation on the Laboratory of Machine Learning and Data Representation, for its part, describes that laboratory as a structural subdivision of the University reporting to its head and, in the head's absence, to the director of the Institute of Robotics [11], whereas this Regulation lists it among the subdivisions of the Institute of Data Analysis and Artificial Intelligence [3].

## Tasks

- Research, expert-analytical and consulting activity in the field of analysis of large-volume and complexly structured data, including computational logic, machine learning, data mining, structural analysis, formal concept analysis, computational linguistics and automatic text processing, ontological modelling and knowledge discovery, for governmental and non-governmental organisations, legal entities and natural persons [12].
- Development of software for data analysis, computational logic and other intelligent systems of research and applied purpose [13].
- Studying and generalising Russian and world experience in the directions of the Institute's research [14].
- Integrating the professional community in data analysis (including complexly structured data in textual, numerical, relational and structural form) and intelligent information systems, and helping to attract beginning researchers to it [15].
- Involving the University's teachers, students and aspirants in the Institute's research work and using the results of the Institute's research in the University's educational process [16].
- Disseminating the results of the Institute's research [17].
- Organising joint research and educational activity with Russian, foreign and international organisations [18].
- Preparing for publication and publishing annotated and abstracted materials on the latest experience and the Institute's own materials and developments of the University's academic staff and students [19].

## Functions

- Conducting fundamental and applied research in the field of analysis of large-volume and complexly structured data, including computational logic, machine learning, data mining, structural analysis, formal concept analysis, computational linguistics and automatic text processing, ontological modelling and knowledge discovery, including on the order of enterprises and organisations of all forms of ownership and of international organisations [20].
- Attracting, on a competitive basis, the University's teachers and researchers as well as outside specialists and experts to conduct research on the basis of the Institute [21].
- Performing expert work, including ensuring the participation of the Institute's employees in state, non-governmental and international working groups and commissions on questions of the Institute's activity [22].
- Taking part in preparing and holding the University's scientific conferences, seminars and other events on the Institute's activity, and maintaining and extending profile scientific links, including international ones [23].
- Preparing scientific literature for publication and issuing scientific periodicals (monographs, articles, reports, presentations, papers and others), including those containing the results of the Institute's scientific activity [24].
- Disseminating information about the results of the Institute's research through the Internet and the mass media [25].
- Taking part in organising the students' practice and the writing of course works and final qualification works of the University's students [26].
- Preparing study and methodological materials for publication — textbooks, study and methodological guides, problem books and others within the Institute's specialisation [27].
- Developing programmes of academic disciplines, educational-methodological complexes and curricula in the field of the Institute's specialisation [28].
- Publishing scientific articles in journals, including those in the international citation databases Web of Science (WOS), Scopus and others [29].

## Rights of the Institute's employees

In their activity the Institute's employees have the right to:

- request from the University's structural subdivisions the information and materials needed for the work within the Institute's competence [30];
- engage, with the consent of the heads of the University's structural subdivisions, the employees of those subdivisions to prepare drafts of local acts and other documents and to develop and carry out the measures conducted by the Institute [31];
- take part in the meetings held by the University's management when questions falling within the Institute's competence are discussed [32];
- put proposals to the University's management on repealing, amending and supplementing the University's local acts [33];
- use the auditorium fund, the objects and the equipment of the University's material and technical base [34];
- use the University's logo in all presentation materials [35];
- carry on publishing activity in the established order in compliance with the rules in force at the University [36].

## Interaction with other subdivisions

- With the Department for attracting grants on questions of participation in research work, research and development and industrial projects and grants [37].
- With the Department for work with students on questions of study and of reporting on academic performance [38].
- With the Department for support and quality control of projects on questions of reporting on grants and on budget and non-budget contracts [39].
- The Institute's interaction with the University's other structural subdivisions is determined by the tasks and functions imposed on it by this Regulation in accordance with the University's structure and the organisational-managerial and normative documents of the University's management and the University's Charter [40].

## Reporting

- Within its activity the Institute is responsible for preparing reports on research work, research and development, industrial projects and grants and for passing them to the University's internal structural subdivisions in good time [41].
- The Institute submits reporting in accordance with the requirements of the Ministry of Science and Higher Education of the Russian Federation and of other state statistics bodies, as needed for management and financial decisions [42].

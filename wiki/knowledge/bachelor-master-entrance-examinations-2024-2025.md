# Entrance examinations for bachelor's and master's admission 2024/2025 — lists, points, procedure and appeals

## Which examinations are set

- For persons applying on the basis of secondary general education the University sets general education entrance examinations in the subjects in which the ЕГЭ is held, according to the list approved by order No. 722 of the Ministry of Science and Higher Education of 6 August 2021: one examination under section 1 of that list, one examination in one subject under column 1 of section 2, and one examination under column 2 of section 2 [1].
- For each examination under column 2 of section 2 the University sets two subjects to choose from: «Информатика и информационно-коммуникационные технологии (ИКТ)» or «Физика»; the applicant chooses one of them, and within one competition one subject corresponds to only one general education entrance examination [1]. The results of the general education examinations are the results of the ЕГЭ, assessed on a 100-point scale [1].
- For persons applying on the basis of secondary vocational or higher education the University sets, for each general education examination, a corresponding internal entrance examination and conducts it; for persons from secondary vocational education the examination in the Russian language is the general education examination in Russian, while the other examinations are set according to the content of secondary vocational programmes in the field «Инженерное дело, технологии и технические науки»; for persons from higher education the examinations are set in the same subjects as the general education examinations [1].
- The University prepares several variants of the internal examination differing in content; an applicant takes each such examination once; persons applying on the basis of professional education take the internal examinations regardless of whether they took the ЕГЭ, and/or use the results of the ЕГЭ [1].
- General education examinations conducted by the University itself may be taken, regardless of participation in the ЕГЭ, by disabled persons (including disabled children) and foreign citizens, and, in the subjects in which the applicant did not take the ЕГЭ in the current calendar year, by a person who obtained the document of secondary general education in a foreign organisation; such persons may also use ЕГЭ results; a person from secondary vocational education who has the right to take the Russian language examination takes it once (clauses 14 and 14.1) [1].
- Citizens of the Republic of Belarus may use the results of the centralised testing and/or centralised examination held in Belarus in the current or the preceding calendar year; the results are recognised as results of general education entrance examinations if the applicant did not take the ЕГЭ in the corresponding subject in the year of the certificate, and they are presented not later than the day of completion of the acceptance of documents [1].

## Points

- The result credited is the highest of the applicant's results that is not less than the established minimum number of points [1].
- The maximum number of points for each entrance examination on bachelor's programmes is 100 [1].
- The minimum number of points for a general education examination held by the University itself corresponds to the minimum number of points of the ЕГЭ set by the University under part 3 of article 70 of Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ; for an additional examination and for an examination on the basis of professional education the minimum is set by the University; for a general education examination the minimum is the minimum of the ЕГЭ set in section 1 of annex 2 to the rules [1].
- Master's admission is conducted on the results of entrance examinations whose list and form are in section 3 of annex 1 to the rules, and the maximum and minimum points for each examination are in section 2 of annex 2 (clause 34) [1].
- According to annexes 1 and 2, on bachelor's programmes the examinations are mathematics, informatics and ICT or physics, and the Russian language, taken in the form of the ЕГЭ for persons from secondary general education and in the form of the ЕГЭ or an internal examination for persons from professional education, with a minimum of 60 and a maximum of 100 points for each; for foreign citizens applying to paid places the same subjects are taken as internal examinations with a minimum of 60 and a maximum of 100 points [1].
- On master's programmes each programme has one entrance examination in the form of computer testing with a minimum of 40 and a maximum of 100 points, both for places within the control figures and for foreign citizens applying to paid places [1].

## Examinations held by the University itself

- The University itself conducts examinations on the basis of professional education, general education examinations for the persons named in clause 14, examinations on master's programmes and general education examinations on places within the separate quota (clause 55) [1].
- The results of these examinations are valid for admission in the next academic year; an applicant takes each examination once, and where several subjects are set for one general education examination he may take the examination once in each subject [1].
- Examinations are held in Russian or English; on master's programmes with a foreign language of instruction the examination is held in English, and where several languages are offered the applicant chooses one [1].

## How examinations with distance technologies are held

- Examinations are held with distance technologies through the University's electronic information system; the applicant's identity is identified by entering a personal identifier and password received at registration; examinations are held in the form of computer testing [1].
- During the examination participants and the persons involved may not carry or use means of communication, reference materials or computing equipment other than what is needed for the examination [1].
- The applicant needs a computer (desktop, laptop or tablet), communication equipment for access through local networks and the Internet, and software: the Google Chrome browser of version not lower than 72, an operating system not lower than Windows 7, macOS 10.12 «Sierra» or Linux, and an Internet connection speed of not less than 512 Kbps [1].
- The room must have walls and a closed door, no outsiders may be present, additional computers and monitors must be switched off, the desk must be free of all objects (pocket computers, watches, notebooks, books, blocks, sticky notes, printed papers), and the web camera must not face a light source and must give a view of the room within 2–3 metres of the applicant; identity documents, clean sheets of paper and a pen are allowed on the desk [1].
- Authorised officials may ask the applicant to show the room and workplace; on refusal or non-compliance they may remove him from the examination with an act of removal [1].
- The applicant must enter his personal account in the University's electronic information system not later than 5 minutes before the examination and follow the link to the computer testing page; auxiliary means may be allowed according to the examination programmes [1].
- Where the applicant breaks the admission rules, an act of breach is drawn up, its electronic copy is sent to him, and he is recognised as not having passed the examination without a valid reason; he may not record the materials of the examination or pass them to third persons [1].
- One examination is held simultaneously for all applicants or at different times for different groups; for each group one examination is held per day, and at the applicant's wish he may be given the possibility of taking more than one examination in a day [1].
- Persons who did not pass the examination for a valid reason (illness or other documented circumstances) are allowed to take it in another group or on a reserve day; where the rules are broken an act is drawn up and the person is removed [1].
- The results are announced on the official site not later than the third working day after the examination [1].
- After the results of a written examination are announced, the applicant may see the results of the checking and assessment of his work on the day of announcement or during the next working day [1].

## Appeals

- The applicant (or his authorised representative) may appeal to the appeal commission about a breach of the established procedure of the examination and/or about disagreement with the assessment [1].
- The appeal is filed with distance technologies through the University's electronic information system, and the University ensures that appeals are considered with distance technologies [1].
- In considering the appeal the observance of the procedure and the correctness of the assessment are checked [1].
- The appeal is filed on the day the results are announced or during the next working day; an appeal about a breach of the procedure may also be filed on the day of the examination [1].
- The appeal is considered not later than the next working day after it is filed [1].
- The applicant (his representative) may attend the announcement of the results of the appeal remotely; a parent or legal representative may attend with a minor applicant under 18, except minors recognised as fully capable before coming of age [1].
- The commission decides to change the assessment or to leave it unchanged, and its decision, drawn up in a protocol, is communicated to the applicant (his representative) by sending the protocol in electronic form [1].

## Special conditions for disabled persons and persons with health limitations

- The University creates conditions taking account of the applicants' psychophysical development, individual possibilities and state of health [1].
- At examinations held in person the University ensures unimpeded access to auditoriums, toilets and other rooms and stay there (ramps, lifts, handrails, widened doorways, and the auditorium on the ground floor) [1].
- Examinations in person for such applicants are held in a separate auditorium; the number of applicants in one auditorium must not exceed 12 in a written examination and 6 in an oral examination; a larger number is allowed, and joint examinations with other applicants if this creates no difficulties, as is the presence of an assistant from the University's staff or engaged persons [1].
- The duration of the examination is increased by decision of the University by not more than 1.5 hours, information on the procedure is given in an accessible form, and technical means needed because of individual features may be used [1].
- For blind applicants tasks are prepared in relief-dot Braille or as an electronic document readable with special software, or read by an assistant; written tasks are done in Braille or on a computer with special software or dictated to an assistant, and a set of writing accessories, Braille paper and a computer are provided [1].
- For visually impaired applicants individual even lighting of not less than 300 lux is provided, magnifying devices are provided (own devices may also be used), and tasks and instructions are printed in enlarged type [1].
- For deaf and hearing-impaired applicants sound-amplifying equipment for collective use is provided, individual equipment if needed, and sign-language interpreting services; for deafblind applicants tactile sign-language interpreting services are provided [1].
- For persons with severe speech disorders, deaf and hearing-impaired persons oral examinations are held in written form (on master's programmes and for additional creative or professional examinations — by decision of the University); for persons with disorders of the locomotor system or of the upper limbs written tasks are done on a computer with special software or dictated to an assistant, and written examinations are held orally on the same conditions [1].
- These conditions are granted on the basis of an application stating the need for special conditions because of disability or health limitations and a document confirming disability or health limitations requiring those conditions [1].

# College tuition cost for 2026/2027 (secondary vocational education)

## The order

The order of 16 February 2026 № ОД/СПО‐01/2026/02/16, issued in Innopolis, sets the tuition cost for secondary vocational education programmes — programmes for training mid-level specialists implemented by Innopolis College (Колледж), a structural unit of the University, for the 2026/2027 academic year [1]. It was issued under Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29 December 2012 «Об образовании в Российской Федерации» and the Rules for the provision of paid educational services approved by Government Resolution No. 706 of 15 August 2013, on the basis of a decision of the University's Operational Council (protocol of 13 February 2026 № П/2/2026) [1]. Control over execution is assigned to the College director I.N. Merkulova [1].

The full cost for the whole period of study is set for citizens of the Russian Federation, foreign citizens and stateless persons studying at places under contracts for the provision of paid educational services and admitted for study in the 2026/2027 academic year [2].

## Full cost for the whole period of study (Annex 1)

For the following programmes the full cost of educational services for the whole period of study is 1 245 833 rubles each, full-time, with a duration of 3 years 10 months under the federal state educational standard [1]:

- specialty 09.02.11, programme «Разработка и управление программным обеспечением», qualification Программист — 1 245 833 rubles [1];
- specialty 15.02.10 «Мехатроника и робототехника (по отраслям)», qualification Специалист по мехатронике и робототехнике — 1 245 833 rubles [1].

## Cost of the first course in 2026/2027 (Annex 2)

On admission to the first course in 2026/2027 the cost is 325 000 rubles for each of the two programmes named above (course 1, full-time) [1].

## Grounds for reducing the full cost (Annex 3)

The order approves the grounds for reducing the full cost of paid educational services on admission to the first course in 2026/2027 [1]:

- for children of employees of ANO VO «Университет Иннополис» the reduced full cost is 996 666 rubles for each of the two programmes (full-time, 3 years 10 months) [1];
- for winners who took 1st place in olympiads of levels 1, 2 and 3 — 996 666 rubles [1];
- for prize-winners who took 2nd place in those olympiads — 1 054 167 rubles [1];
- for prize-winners who took 3rd place in those olympiads — 1 150 000 rubles [1].

The table with the olympiad grounds refers for the list of olympiads to «приложение 3 к настоящему приказу» (the annex in which the table itself stands), while the corresponding table of Annex 4 refers to Annex 7; the list of olympiads is Annex 7 [1].

## Reduced cost of the first course in 2026/2027 (Annex 4)

- For children of employees of ANO VO «Университет Иннополис» the cost of the first course is 260 000 rubles for each of the two programmes [1].
- For winners who took 1st place in olympiads of levels 1, 2 and 3 — 260 000 rubles [1]; for prize-winners who took 2nd place — 275 000 rubles [1]; for prize-winners who took 3rd place — 300 000 rubles [1].
- The column heading of the olympiad table of Annex 4 reads «на 2025/2026 уч. год», although the annex and the order are for 2026/2027 [1].

## Second course (Annexes 5 and 6)

- Annex 5, titled as the cost on the second course in 2026/2027, gives 300 000 rubles for specialty 09.02.07 «Информационные системы и программирование» with the qualifications Программист, Разработчик веб и мультимедийных приложений and Администратор баз данных, and 300 000 rubles for specialty 15.02.10 «Мехатроника и робототехника (по отраслям)»; the course column of these rows shows 1 [1].
- Annex 6, titled as the grounds for reducing the cost on the second course in 2026/2027, gives the reduced cost of 210 000 rubles for the same programmes and qualifications, with the ground stated as «для детей сотрудников АНО ВО «Университет Иннополис»»; its course column also shows 1 [1].

## Only one ground applies

If an applicant has several grounds for reducing the cost of paid educational services, only one ground is applied — the one providing for the maximum amount of reduction; summing the benefits is not allowed [1].

## The olympiads behind the olympiad ground (Annex 7)

Annex 7 lists the olympiads and other intellectual competitions, measures aimed at developing intellectual abilities and interest in scientific, engineering and inventive activity, in accordance with the order of the Ministry of Education of the Russian Federation of 31 August 2025 No. 639, for which points are awarded as results of individual achievements; a document confirming the status of winner or prize-winner must be provided [1]. The list is arranged by level [1]:

- level 1 — the All-Russian olympiad for schoolchildren in informatics, in mathematics and in physics, the All-Russian olympiad on artificial intelligence, the International Mathematical Olympiad, the International Olympiad in Informatics (IOI) and the International Physics Olympiad (IPhO) [1];
- level 2 — the international «Innopolis Open» olympiads in information security, in informatics, in mathematics, in robotics and in artificial intelligence [1];
- level 3 — the «Большие вызовы» programme in the direction «Передовые производственные технологии» (profiles mathematics, physics, informatics) and the «Большие вызовы» competition in the direction of big data, artificial intelligence, automated systems and security, the competence track «Талант НТО» (profiles Engineering, Programming, development of business applications) and the National Technology Olympiad (profiles big data and machine learning, intelligent robotic systems, information security, artificial intelligence) [1];
- level 4 — other olympiads, competitions and measures approved by the order of the Ministry of Education of Russia of 31.08.2025 No. 639 that correspond to the profile of the specialty for which the application is submitted [1].

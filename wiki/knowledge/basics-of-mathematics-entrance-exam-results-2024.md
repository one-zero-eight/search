# Entrance examination «Основы математики» for bachelor programmes (July 2024) — published results

## What these documents are

Each page is the published protocol of the entrance examination «Основы математики» (basics of mathematics) for bachelor programmes, conducted by Innopolis University on its own, under the heading «Результаты вступительного испытания проводимого АНО ВО "Университет Иннополис" самостоятельно» [1] [2].

A protocol gives the name of the entrance examination, the date on which it was held, and then a table with the number, the СНИЛС or unique code of the participant, the score (оценка) and the same score written out in words (оценка прописью) [2] [1]. Scores are published against a СНИЛС or an identifier rather than a name — on these pages in the form [скрыто] or [скрыто] [2] [1].

## Sitting of 3 July 2024

The examination was held on 03.07.2024, and the protocol records four results, in the order of the protocol: 64 (Шестьдесят четыре), 39 (Тридцать девять), 20 (Двадцать) and 5 (Пять) [1].

## Sitting of 22 July 2024

The examination was held on 22.07.2024, and the protocol records seventeen results, in the order of the protocol: 70 (Семьдесят), 60 (Шестьдесят), 60, 60, 60, 64 (Шестьдесят четыре), 63 (Шестьдесят три), 63, 63, 62 (Шестьдесят два), 62, 60, 66 (Шестьдесят шесть), 61 (Шестьдесят один), 64, 80 (Восемьдесят) and 62 [2]. The highest score in this protocol is 80 and the lowest is 60 [2].

Two consecutive rows (numbers 10 and 11) carry the same identifier, [скрыто], with the score 62 each [2].

## How reliable the reading is

The 3 July page has a text layer produced by pdfplumber that runs the heading table into the results table and repeats the heading fragments «Основы м» / «атематики» and «03.07» / «.2024» below the results, so the four results have to be read from the rows of the results table itself [1].

Neither page states a pass mark, a minimum score or any decision on admission [2] [1].

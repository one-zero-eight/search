# Sector for Salary Calculation and Contracts with Individuals (Сектор по расчету заработной платы и договорам с физическими лицами) — place, tasks, functions and rights

## Creation and place in the structure

The Sector for Salary Calculation and Contracts with Individuals (Сектор по расчету заработной платы и договорам с физическими лицами) was set up in connection with the creation of a new structural unit, for the optimisation of the University's activity, by the order of 16 December 2024 No. ОД/АД-04/2024/12/16, which approved its Regulation; the order was signed by the Director for Development and HR Policy [1].

The Regulation governs the Sector's activity and determines its tasks, functions, rights and duties [2]. The Sector is a structural unit of the University's Accounting Department (Бухгалтерия) and belongs to the category "other structural units" [3]; it reports directly to the University's chief accountant [4].

The Sector is guided by the Constitution of the Russian Federation, the Labour Code, Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29 December 2012 "On Education in the Russian Federation", other legislative and regulatory acts on higher education institutions, the University's Устав, decisions of the Academic, Supervisory and Operations Councils, orders and instructions of the University Director, other local acts and the Regulation itself [5].

## Staff and subordination

The Director of the University approves the Sector's structure, number of staff and staffing table, and changes to them [1]. The Sector is headed by a head of the Sector, hired and dismissed by order of the Director on the proposal of the chief accountant [6]. Working conditions of the Sector's employees are set by the employment contracts concluded with each of them and by the University's internal labour regulations [7]; their rights, duties and degree of responsibility are set by job descriptions [8]. Employees of the Sector may be involved, within their competence, in work under grants, projects, subsidies and other programmes carried out by the University [9]. The Regulation and amendments to it are approved by the Director or another person authorised by him [10].

## Task

The Sector has one task: the organisation of accounting and tax accounting of the University's economic and financial activity [11].

## Functions

The Sector performs the following functions [1]:

- accounting and tax accounting for operations with individuals who concluded civil-law contracts (договоры ГПХ), and control over compliance with Russian law, completeness and correct execution of the document set when such contracts are concluded [12];
- accounting and tax accounting for operations with individuals registered as payers of the professional income tax (self-employed, договоры с СЗ), with the same control over the documents [13];
- control over the conditions under which a self-employed person loses the special tax regime, reconciliation of the income limit with the self-employed person, notification of the chief accountant not later than one working day from the date the limit is exceeded, and, in that case, withholding of the personal income tax from the cost of the services and transfer of that tax to the budget [14];
- checking the advance reports submitted by the University's employees against the University's local acts [15];
- control of the reports on accrued scholarships, processing of accruals by banks and formation of payment sheets for scholarship payment, taking deductions into account [16];
- control over reimbursement of expenses to the University's students according to orders and supporting documents [17];
- reconciliation of debts and signing of clearance sheets (обходные листы) for students and employees before their expulsion or dismissal [18];
- control over the timely receipt of documents needed for payroll calculation — timesheets, orders, sick leaves, writs of execution, memoranda on bonuses, applications for deductions from wages [19];
- calculation of wages, taking into account the timely submission of employees' loading by projects and funding sources by the grants and projects support department [20];
- formation of payment sheets for wages, vacation pay, temporary disability benefits and wages of dismissed employees, broken down by banks and funding items, and for tax transfers [21];
- formation of settlement and payment sheets for the grants and projects support department by funding sources [22];
- formation and issue of certificates on application of employees [23];
- preparation and sending of letters to the Federal Bailiff Service on withheld amounts [24];
- preparation of primary accounting documents for transfer to the archive [25];
- preparation of explanations and selection of documents for internal control, internal and external audit, documentary revisions, tax and other inspections [26];
- formation, verification and submission of reporting to supervisory bodies: ЕФС-1 information on insurance contributions for accidents at work and occupational diseases, the insurance contributions calculation, personalised information on individuals, 6-НДФЛ (from 2021), confirmation of the type of activity, statistical reports (П-4, 2-наука and others) [27];
- non-disclosure of information relating to personal data of the University's employees and other individuals that became known in the performance of duties [28], and compliance with the University's documents on personal data security [29];
- correct calculation and transfer of taxes and fees to the budgets, insurance contributions to state extra-budgetary social funds and payments to banking institutions [30];
- measures to prevent shortages, unlawful spending of money and material values and violations of financial and economic legislation [31];
- strict observance of staff, financial and cash discipline, safekeeping of accounting documents and their execution and transfer to the archive in the established order [32];
- control over the correct spending of the wage fund and strict observance of staff, financial and cash discipline [33];
- strict conduct, within the deadlines set by Russian law, of the established rules of inventory of settlements on accounts 70, 71, 73, 76, 68 and 69 of the chart of accounts approved by order of the Ministry of Finance No. 94н of 31 October 2000 [34].

## Rights

In their activity the employees of the Sector have the right to [1]:

- request from the University's structural units the information and materials needed for the Sector's work [35], and receive methodological, legal and consultative help from them [36];
- involve, with the consent of the heads of structural units, their employees for preparing draft local acts and other documents and for developing and carrying out the Sector's measures [37];
- take part in meetings held by the University's management when issues within the Sector's competence are discussed [38], [39];
- propose to the University's management the cancellation of, changes and additions to local acts [40];
- interact with state authorities and administration, legal entities and the University's units on issues within the Sector's competence [41];
- require the structural units to observe the procedure for processing operations and to submit documents and information in the established deadlines [42], and require heads of units and individual specialists to take measures to improve the efficiency of use of the University's funds, ensure the safekeeping of its property and correct accounting and control [43];
- conduct correspondence on accounting and reporting and on other issues within the Sector's competence that need no approval by the management [44];
- give instructions to the structural units on issues within the Sector's competence and arising from its functions [45];
- give explanations and recommendations on issues within the Sector's competence [46];
- get acquainted with draft decisions of the University's management concerning the Sector's work [47];
- visa documents developed at the University within the Sector's competence [48];
- propose improvements of the Sector's work and proposals on rewards, penalties and material liability of the University's employees [49].

## Interaction with other units

Interaction with other structural units is determined by the tasks and functions assigned to the Sector [50]. The Regulation names interaction with: the Director on orders, instructions, protocols, letters and other approved documents, tasks and assignments connected with the Sector's functions [51]; the legal department on documents for pre-trial recovery of receivables and for defending the University's position in court disputes [52]; the personnel records and movement department on selection and training of the Sector's employees, labour discipline, rewards and penalties, material liability, motivation, and orders on bonuses and one-off payments [53]; the grants and projects support department on the timely submission of employees' loading by projects and funding sources [54]; and the Academic Policy and Higher Education Management on the timely submission of employees' loading by projects and funding sources [55].

## Reporting

On the basis of the relevant local acts, orders and instructions the Sector may be responsible, within its activity, for preparing information, certificates and reports and keeping them up to date [56].

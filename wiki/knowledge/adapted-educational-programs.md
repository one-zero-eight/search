# Adapted educational programs for students with disabilities and health limitations

## Purpose and scope

An adapted educational program is a set of educational characteristics (scope, content, planned outcomes), organizational and pedagogical conditions, and assessment forms, presented as a study plan, academic calendar, discipline work programs, practice programs, and other components, as well as evaluative and methodological materials for training students with disabilities (инвалиды) and students with health limitations (ОВЗ), taking into account their psycho-physical development features, individual capabilities, and, where necessary, providing correction of developmental disorders and social adaptation of these students [1].

The regulation applies to employees of structural subdivisions of Innopolis University that ensure implementation of higher education programs [2].

The content of education and the conditions of organizing the training and upbringing of students with health limitations are determined by the adapted educational program, and for disabled persons also in accordance with the individual rehabilitation program of the disabled person [3]. Professional education of students with health limitations is carried out on the basis of educational programs adapted where necessary for the training of such students [4].

## Normative basis

The Regulation was developed in accordance with the Federal Law of 29 December 2012 № 273-ФЗ «Об образовании в Российской Федерации», the Federal Law of 24 November 1995 № 181-ФЗ «О социальной защите инвалидов в Российской Федерации», the orders of the Ministry of Education and Science of Russia on the organisation and implementation of educational activity under higher education programmes, the methodological recommendations on organising the educational process for disabled persons and persons with health limitations, the state program «Доступная среда», the federal state educational standards of higher education (ФГОС ВО), and the Charter and other local acts of the University [5].

## Terms used

- an adaptive discipline (module) is an element of the adapted educational program aimed at minimizing and removing the influence of health limitations in forming the necessary competencies of a disabled student and a student with health limitations, and at individual correction of learning and communication skills, contributing to the mastery of the educational program and to the social and professional adaptation of such students [6]
- inclusive education is the provision of equal access to education for all students, taking into account the diversity of special educational needs and individual capabilities [7]
- a student with health limitations is a natural person with defects in physical and (or) psychological development confirmed by a psycho-medical-pedagogical commission and preventing the receipt of education without the creation of special conditions [8]
- special conditions for receiving education are the conditions of training, upbringing and development of students with health limitations and disabled students, including the use of special educational programs and methods, special textbooks, teaching aids and didactic materials, special technical means of training for collective and individual use, the provision of the services of an assistant giving the necessary technical help, group and individual correctional classes, access to the buildings of the organizations carrying out educational activity, and other conditions without which mastering the educational programs is impossible or difficult [9]
- an individual curriculum is a curriculum ensuring the mastery of an educational program on the basis of individualization of its content, taking into account the features and educational needs of a particular student [10]

## Goals and tasks

The main goals of an adapted educational program are to ensure the right of disabled persons and persons with health limitations to receive higher education, develop their personality, individual abilities and capabilities and their socio-cultural adaptation in society, and to detail the features of implementing the educational process for a specific direction of training and profile, taking into account their psycho-physical development, individual capabilities, including the habilitation of disabled persons [11]. The development and implementation of adapted programs aim at raising the level of accessibility of higher education for disabled persons and persons with health limitations, raising its quality, creating the special conditions necessary for them to receive higher education and for their adaptation and socialisation, and forming a tolerant socio-cultural environment at the University [12].

## Criteria of an adapted educational program

Criteria include:

- specialized adaptive disciplines (modules) in the variable part of higher education programs, aimed at additional individualized correction of learning and communication skills, and professional and social adaptation [13]
- teaching methods conditioned by the features of information perception by students with disabilities and students with OВЗ [13]
- provision of students with printed and electronic educational resources in formats adapted to their health limitations [13]
- current performance monitoring, interim and final (state final) assessment taking into account the nosologies of students with disabilities and students with OВЗ [13]
- comprehensive support of the educational process for students with OВЗ and students with disabilities in accordance with recommendations of the medical-social expertise or psycho-medical-pedagogical commission [13]
- development, where necessary, of individual study plans and individual schedules allowing study in various formats: at the University (in a study group and individually) or at home using e-learning and distance education technologies [13]
- special procedures for mastering disciplines (modules) in physical education and sports based on health preservation and adaptive physical culture principles [13]
- creation of a tolerant socio-cultural environment and volunteer assistance for students with disabilities and students with OВЗ [13]

## Development of adapted programs

An adapted educational program is developed on the basis of the main higher education program (ООП ВО) of the corresponding direction and specialization (profile), with additions reflecting the specifics of conditions for students with disabilities and students with OВЗ [14].

No differentiation or restrictions may be introduced in the ООП ВО regarding competencies and professional activities of graduates, students with disabilities, and students with OВЗ. All competencies stated in the ООП ВО must be formed [15].

Adaptive disciplines (modules) are included in the variable part of the adapted program, depending on specific circumstances (number of students with disabilities and students with OВЗ, their distribution by types and degrees of health limitations) [16].

Disciplines (modules) in the basic (mandatory) part of the study plan and practices are mandatory for all students, including students with disabilities and students with OВЗ. Removal of any disciplines (modules), practices, and final assessment procedures from the basic (mandatory) part with respect to students with disabilities and students with OВЗ is not permitted [16].

The set of adaptive disciplines (modules) is determined from the specific situation and the individual needs of disabled students and students with OВЗ [17]. They «support» the study of the basic (mandatory) part and of the variable part of the educational program and are aimed at the socialisation, professionalisation and adaptation of students, contributing to their adequate professional self-determination and the possibility of independently building an individual educational trajectory [17].

Employees of the structural subdivisions responsible for implementing the higher education programs at the University give qualified assistance in the adequate choice of adaptive disciplines (modules) to students with OВЗ and disabled students, taking into account an assessment of the features of their psycho-physical development and individual educational needs [18]. Within the adaptive disciplines (modules) the preparation of disabled graduates and graduates with OВЗ for employment and for the next stage of socialisation, connected with the full disclosure and practical application of the competencies obtained during study, must be provided for [19].

## Study formats and individual plans

Education for students with OВЗ may be organized both together with other students and in separate study groups or in separate organizations [20].

After enrollment in the main program, transfer to an adapted educational program is carried out upon the student's personal application based on recommendations from medical-social expertise or psycho-medical-pedagogical commission. Transfer is possible throughout the entire period of study [21].

Students with OВЗ may study under an individual study plan and individual schedule. The duration of study under an individual plan for students with OВЗ may be extended by up to 1 year at their request compared to the standard duration for the corresponding form of study [22].

## Assessment and examination accommodations

For current performance monitoring and interim assessment, special conditions must be created to evaluate the results of competency development for students with disabilities and students with OВЗ [23].

For final (state final) assessment, the University provides all necessary technical means and, where necessary, technical assistance. The format of a state exam for students with disabilities and students with OВЗ is set taking into account individual psycho-physical features (oral, written on paper, written on a computer, testing, etc.). Additional time for preparing answers may be provided [24].

Specific forms and procedures for current control and interim assessment are set by the University independently, taking into account health limitations, and are communicated to students [25].

Assessment materials must be provided in formats adapted to health limitations [25]:

- for students with visual impairments: enlarged print, electronic document, audio file, Braille [25]
- for students with hearing impairments: printed form or electronic document [25]
- for students with musculoskeletal disorders: printed form, electronic document, audio file [25]

## Independent work and material support

Specific types and forms of independent work of disabled students and students with OВЗ are set by the teacher, taking into account individual psycho-physical features (orally, in writing on paper or on a computer, in the form of testing, electronic simulators etc.); where necessary students are given additional time for consultations and for completing tasks [26].

The material-technical and educational-methodological support of the adapted educational program must be supplemented for disabled students and students with OВЗ, taking into account their individual capabilities [27].

## Practical training

When determining practice locations for students with OВЗ and students with disabilities, recommendations from psycho-medical-pedagogical commission conclusions or individual rehabilitation programs (ИПРА) regarding recommended conditions and types of work are taken into account [28].

Special workstations may be created for practice, and practice formats are set taking into account individual capabilities and health [28].

## Physical education

A special procedure for mastering physical education and sports disciplines is established for students with disabilities and students with OВЗ, taking into account their health, including adherence to health preservation and adaptive physical culture principles [29].

## Approval, updates and entry into force

An adapted educational program is developed on the basis of the ФГОС ВО, the ООП ВО for the same direction of training and profile, this Regulation, taking into account model ООП ВО and professional standards [30].

Where students present the conclusion of a psycho-medical-pedagogical commission containing information on the necessary special conditions of training (students with OВЗ), or the individual program of rehabilitation and habilitation of a disabled person containing such information and information on recommended conditions and types of work (disabled persons), these recommendations are taken into account in developing the adapted educational program [31].

An adapted educational program is approved by a decision of the University's Academic Council [32].

It is updated at least once a year, taking into account developments in science, culture, economy, technology, and the social sphere [33].

Structural subdivisions responsible for implementing higher education programs are responsible for development, approval, implementation, and updating of adapted educational programs [34].

The Regulation enters into force from the moment of signing of the order by the director of the University or another person authorized by the director and is valid until the corresponding order is cancelled [35]. Changes and additions to the Regulation are introduced on the basis of an order of the director of the University or another person authorized by the director, on the presentation of the head of the structural subdivision responsible for implementing the adapted educational program [36].

## Support services

Support for students with OВЗ and students with disabilities should be continuous and comprehensive, including:

- organizational and pedagogical support [25]
- psycho-pedagogical support [25]
- preventive and health-oriented support [25]
- social support (housing, transport, scholarships, material assistance, leisure, student self-government, volunteer movement) [25]

Volunteer movement among students is recommended for individualized social support [25].

Teaching staff involved in implementing adapted programs must be familiar with psycho-physical features of students with disabilities and students with OВЗ and must master inclusive learning technologies. Tutors, psychologists, social educators, and specialists in special technical and software means are recommended for involvement; where necessary, sign language interpreters, tiflopedagogues, and speech therapists [37].

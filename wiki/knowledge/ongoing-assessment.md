# Ongoing assessment (current academic performance monitoring)

## What ongoing assessment is

The regulation on the forms, periodicity and procedure of ongoing and interim assessment applies to students of the bachelor's and master's programmes [1]. Ongoing assessment (текущий контроль успеваемости) evaluates the progress in mastering disciplines (modules) and other components of the educational programme, including internships [2]. It covers all types of contact and independent work provided for by the discipline (module) syllabuses and internship programmes [3], and is carried out by a teaching staff member of the University or by a person engaged by the University to deliver educational programmes on other terms [3].

Disciplines (modules) are, as a rule, mastered during the study period set by the curriculum; it is also possible to master a discipline (module) within a specific period inside the study period according to the class schedule [4].

## Objectives

The main objectives of ongoing assessment are [5]:

- raising students' motivation for active and even academic work throughout the whole period of study [5];
- improving students' individual and independent work [5];
- correcting or changing, where necessary, the forms and methods of teaching and the volume of material so that the educational process is as effective as possible [5];
- preventing expulsion from the University on the results of study in a discipline (module), other component of the programme, including an internship [5].

## Forms of ongoing assessment

The forms of ongoing assessment are [6]:

- developing separate parts of the code of a software product [6];
- completing assignments: independent (homework) work, group projects, practical and laboratory work [6];
- colloquiums [6];
- tests, written or computer-based [6];
- reports, essays, summaries (обзоры) [6];
- oral quizzes [6];
- discussions, trainings, round tables [6];
- other forms established by the discipline (module) syllabuses and internship programmes [6].

## Who sets the rules

The types and forms of ongoing assessment, the procedure and frequency of its conduct, the tools and technologies used, the criteria for grading its individual forms, the assessment materials and the grading system are developed by the teaching staff separately for each discipline (module), other component of the programme, including an internship, and are set out in the discipline (module) syllabuses, internship programmes and the University's electronic information-educational environment [7].

Students must go through all forms of ongoing assessment diligently [8]. The teaching staff member records the stages and results of students' mastering of a discipline (module) within ongoing assessment in the University's electronic information-educational environment [9].

## Midterm progress analysis

As part of the quality evaluation of the educational process, teaching staff and staff of the responsible structural units may, in the middle of the study period and on the results of ongoing assessment, analyse students' progress (midterm); its main aim is to determine how well each student has mastered the disciplines (modules) they study [10]. Students with unsatisfactory results in several disciplines (modules) on the results of this analysis may have special meetings with staff of the responsible structural units, including a psychologist, to find out why they have not fulfilled the requirements of the discipline (module) syllabuses in full [10].

## Effect on interim assessment and retakes

The results of ongoing assessment are taken into account when the results of mastering a discipline (module), other component of the programme or internship are graded in interim assessment [11]. Whether the results of ongoing assessment may be retaken is decided by the teaching staff member independently for each discipline (module), other component of the programme or internship [12].

Ongoing assessment, interim assessment and retakes may be conducted with the use of e-learning and distance educational technologies in the procedure established by the University [13].

## In aspirantura programmes (2018 regulation)

For aspirants — students of higher education programmes that train highly qualified personnel in aspirantura — a separate document applies: the Regulation on ongoing assessment and interim assessment, approved by order No. ОД/УД-04/2018/06/04 of 4 June 2018 [14]. It determines the form, periodicity and procedure of ongoing and interim assessment, the grading system and criteria for grades, and the procedure and deadlines for clearing academic debt [15]. Control of the quality of mastering the aspirantura programme includes ongoing assessment, interim assessment and final (state final) assessment [14]. Ongoing assessment evaluates the progress in mastering disciplines (modules) and passing internships, while interim assessment evaluates the intermediate and final results of learning in disciplines (modules), internships and research work [16].

Ongoing assessment is carried out for all types of contact and independent work provided for by the work programmes of disciplines (modules) and the programmes of internships and research work, by the teacher giving lecture and/or seminar classes and by the supervisor of the internship and research work [17]. Its objectives are raising aspirants' motivation for active and even work throughout the whole semester, improving individual and independent work, ensuring the maximum efficiency of the educational process and preventing expulsion during the whole period of study [18].

Its forms are independent work, practical and laboratory work, projects, testing (written or computer-based), reports, essays, referats and reviews, discussions, trainings and round tables; other forms may be established under the work programme of the discipline [19]. The list of forms, the procedure and frequency of their conduct, the tools and technologies, the criteria for grading individual forms and the grading system are developed by the teacher responsible for the discipline (module) and for the supervision of internships and research independently; the number and procedures of the forms cannot change during the semester [20]. Aspirants must pass all forms of ongoing assessment diligently [21]. During their office hours teachers may organize individual or group consultations on the discipline (module), internship and research assigned to them [22]. The results are entered in the electronic journal of classes for the discipline (module), internships and research in the Moodle content management system [23].

## In aspirantura programmes (2022 regulation)

A later aspirantura regulation was approved by order No. ОД/АД-12/2022/03/01 of 1 March 2022 and put into force on 1 March 2022 [24]. Like the earlier one, it determines the forms, periodicity and procedure of ongoing assessment, the grading system and criteria, and the procedure and deadlines for clearing academic debt [25], and the page does not say that it repeals the earlier regulation [24]. The page is OCR of a scanned document, so its figures and dates should be checked against the PDF [24].

Ongoing assessment evaluates the progress of the stages of research (научная деятельность), of mastering disciplines (modules) and of internships in accordance with the individual research plan and the individual curriculum [26]. It is carried out for all types of contact and independent work provided by the work programmes of disciplines (modules), the internship programmes and the individual research plan, by the teacher giving lecture and/or seminar classes, by the supervisor of the internship and by the scientific supervisor [27]. The part covering the stages of research is held with the participation of the scientific supervisor, who ensures control over the aspirant's timely fulfilment of the individual research plan [28].

Its objectives are raising aspirants' motivation for active and even study and research activity throughout the whole semester, improving individual and independent work, ensuring the maximum efficiency of the educational process and preventing expulsion during the whole period of study [29]. Its forms are [30]:

- fulfilment of the stages of research activity [30];
- independent work, practical and laboratory work, projects [30];
- testing, written or computer-based [30];
- reports, essays, referats, reviews [30];
- participation in discussions, trainings, round tables, scientific seminars [30];
- other forms established in accordance with the discipline work programme, the internship programme and the individual research plan [30].

The list of forms, their procedure and frequency, the tools and technologies used, the criteria for grading individual forms and the grading system are developed by the teacher responsible for the discipline (module) and for the supervision of the internship and research independently; the number and procedures of the forms cannot change during the semester [31]. Aspirants must pass all forms of ongoing assessment diligently [32]. During their office hours teachers may organise individual or group consultations for aspirants on the discipline (module), internship and research assigned to them [33]. The results are entered in the electronic journal of classes for the discipline (module), internships and research in Moodle [34].

# A bachelor's or master's programme and its components — the Regulation of 2 August 2022

## What the Regulation governs

The Regulation on the procedure of development and approval of higher education programmes — bachelor's and master's programmes — was approved by order of the University of 2 August 2022 No. ОД/АД-02/2022/08/02 [1], enters into force on 1 September 2022 [2] and is amended by order of the University director or a person authorised by the director [3]. It sets requirements to the structure and content of bachelor's and master's programmes, to the procedure of their development and to the procedure of their approval and actualisation [4].

It was developed in accordance with [5]:

- Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29 December 2012 [5];
- the order of the Ministry of Science and Higher Education of 6 April 2021 No. 245 on the procedure of organising educational activity in bachelor's, specialist and master's programmes [5];
- the order of 5 August 2020 No. 885/390 on practical training of students [5];
- the order of 29 June 2015 No. 636 on the procedure of the state final certification [5];
- the order of 23 August 2017 No. 816 on the use of e-learning and distance educational technologies [5];
- the order of Rosobrnadzor of 14 August 2020 No. 831 on the structure of the official website and the Government decree of 20 October 2021 No. 1802 on placing information on the official website [5];
- the federal state educational standards of higher education, professional standards, the University Charter and other local acts [5].

## What the programme is

An educational programme is a complex of the main characteristics of education (volume, content, planned results) and of the organisational and pedagogical conditions, presented as the curriculum, the calendar study schedule, the working programmes of disciplines (modules) and other components, assessment and methodological materials, the upbringing programme and the calendar plan of upbringing work, and the forms of attestation; by decision of the University other materials may be included [6].

The programme is developed by the University in accordance with the federal state educational standard, taking into account the level of education, the field of study, the focus (profile) of the programme and the corresponding model basic educational programme where one exists [7]. When developing it, the University sets the focus (profile) of the programme, which concretises its content within the field of study by orienting it to the areas and/or spheres of professional activity of graduates, to the types and tasks of professional activity and, where necessary, to the objects of professional activity or areas of knowledge [8]. The focus (profile) is the orientation of the programme to specific areas of knowledge and/or types of activity that determines its subject content, the prevailing types of study activity of the student and the requirements to the learning outcomes [9].

Various educational technologies are used in delivering the programme, including distance educational technologies and e-learning, in the procedure set by the local acts of the University [10]. Adapted educational programmes are developed in accordance with the local acts of the University [11]. A model form of the programme is given in the annex to the Regulation [12].

## The components of the programme

The programme includes the following components [13]:

- a sheet of updates of the programme [14];
- information on the developers of the programme [15];
- general information, including the normative documents that form the basis of the programme [16];
- the general characteristics of the programme [17];
- an annotation of the programme [18];
- requirements to the level of preparation needed to master the programme, including the features of its implementation [19];
- the features of implementation: network form, e-learning, distance technologies, the modular principle of presenting content and building curricula, joint delivery of the programme [20];
- the characteristics of the professional activity of graduates [21];
- the structure of the programme and the documents regulating the educational process [22];
- the learning outcomes [23];
- system-wide requirements to the implementation of the programme, including access to and the functioning of the electronic information-educational environment [24];
- the material, technical and methodological support of the programme [25];
- the staffing conditions of the implementation [26];
- the financial conditions of the implementation [27];
- the mechanisms for evaluating the quality of mastering the programme [28];
- the conditions of implementation of the programme for disabled persons and persons with health limitations [29].

The general characteristics of the programme state the level of education, the form or forms of study, the language of implementation, the qualification awarded to graduates, the term of obtaining education including study under an individual curriculum for disabled persons and persons with health limitations, the volume of the programme, the volume implemented in one academic year (also under an accelerated individual curriculum), the state final certification, the state accreditation of the programme and the validity of that accreditation, the graduating unit and the actualisation of the programme [17]. The annotation contains the mission of the programme, its aim and its tasks in the field of upbringing and in the field of teaching [18].

The characteristics of the professional activity of graduates cover the areas and spheres of professional activity in which graduates may work, the types and tasks of professional activity, the list of the main objects (or areas of knowledge) of professional activity, the list of professional standards correlated with the federal state educational standard, the list of generalised labour functions and labour functions relating to the graduates' professional activity, and the characteristic of the professional activity connected with the types of activity and labour functions in accordance with professional standards [21].

The structure of the programme and the documents regulating the educational process comprise the structure and volume of the programme and of its blocks in credit units, the calendar study schedule, the curriculum, the competency matrix, the working programmes of disciplines (modules), the internship programmes, the state final certification programme, the assessment materials and methodological materials, the annotations to the working programmes of disciplines (modules) and internships, the upbringing programme and the calendar plan of upbringing work [22]. The learning outcomes component contains the list of competences that graduates must develop and the indicators of their achievement [23].

The material, technical and methodological support covers the characteristics of the rooms for study sessions and for students' independent work, of the set of licensed and freely distributed software including domestic software, and the requirements to the library stock of printed publications [25]. For a master's programme the staffing conditions also state the conditions of the general supervision of the scientific content of the programme [26]. The quality mechanisms cover the internal system of evaluating the quality of educational activity, its methods (forms), the main local acts used in it and information on the external evaluation [28].

## Language of education

The language or languages of education are determined by the local acts of the University in accordance with the legislation of the Russian Federation; educational activity under the programme is conducted in the state language of the Russian Federation unless the local acts establish otherwise, teaching and studying the state language within programmes with state accreditation follow the educational standards, and higher education may be obtained in a foreign language in accordance with the programme and the procedure established by the legislation on education and the local acts [30]. The procedure of obtaining education in the language of education depending on the structural components of the programme is established by an order of the University director or a person authorised by the director [31].

## Credit units and the volume of the programme

A system of credit units may be applied to determine the structure of the programme and the labour intensity of mastering it; a credit unit is a unified unit of measurement of the student's load that includes all types of study activity provided by the curriculum, including class work and independent work, and internship [32]. The labour intensity of the programme (or of its part) in credit units characterises its volume, and the volume of a part of the programme must be a whole number of credit units [33].

The University sets the value of a credit unit itself in astronomical or academic hours (with an academic hour of 40 or 45 minutes) within 24 to 30 astronomical hours, unless the federal state educational standard establishes otherwise; the value set is the same within one curriculum [34]. The duration of the academic hour and the value of the credit unit are established by a local act of the University [35]. The volume of the programme and the annual volume of the programme are established by the corresponding federal state educational standard [36].

The number of credit units of the programme does not depend on the form of study, on the use of e-learning or distance educational technologies, or on study under an individual curriculum including an accelerated one [37]. Optional (факультативные) disciplines (modules) are not included in the volume (annual volume) of the programme [38]. Under an individual curriculum, including an accelerated one, the annual volume of the programme — without the volume of the individual disciplines (modules) and/or internships whose learning outcomes were credited — cannot exceed the volume established by the standard [39].

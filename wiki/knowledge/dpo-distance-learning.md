# Distance learning in additional professional education programmes (ДПО with ДОТ)

## What the regulation governs

The regulation on the procedure for organizing studies under additional professional education programmes with the use of distance educational technologies (ДОТ) of ANO VO «Innopolis University» was approved by order No. ОД/ДПО-01/2017/09/25 of 25 September 2017 [1]. The order was issued under Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29.12.2012 «Об образовании в Российской Федерации» and the order of the Ministry of Education and Science of Russia of 23.08.2017 No. 816 approving the procedure for the use of e-learning and distance educational technologies (registered in the Ministry of Justice on 18.09.2017 No. 48226) [1].

The regulation regulates the implementation of additional professional education programmes with the use of distance educational technologies [2]. It was drafted in accordance with Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ «Об образовании в Российской Федерации» and the order of the Ministry of Education and Science of Russia No. 816 [1]. It defines the organization of the educational process of повышение квалификации and professional retraining in distance education (ДО) [3].

Distance educational technologies are educational technologies implemented mainly with information and telecommunication technologies, with mediated (at a distance) or not fully mediated interaction between the student and the teacher (Article 16 of Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ) [4].

## Purpose and tasks

The purpose is to increase the accessibility of professional education by giving students the opportunity to master programmes directly at their place of residence or temporary stay [5]. Distance education is meant to strengthen the personal orientation of learning and intensify students' independent work, reduce the cost of training, improve quality and widen the geography of listeners, give open access to information resources at a time convenient for the student, ensure the advancing character of education, and create conditions for quality control of methodological electronic resources [6].

The basis of distance learning is the purposeful and controlled intensive independent work of listeners, who may study at a place convenient for them, having a set of special learning means and an agreed possibility of contact with the teacher through information and telecommunication technologies [7].

## Environment and materials

After enrolment the listener receives a personal login and password giving access to the University's electronic information and educational environment, including the Moodle content management system [8]. Learning and methodological materials are given to the listener without the right to reproduce them or transfer them to third parties and organizations [9]. The place where educational activity is carried out is the one determined by the educational licence (Innopolis, Universitetskaya st., 1), regardless of where the listener is [10].

After the contract on education is concluded, each student receives by e-mail the teaching and methodological materials: discipline work programmes, methodological developments, glossaries, educational literature, interactive presentations and the list of individual tasks [11].

Moodle allows the use of theoretical materials for study (files, links to lecture recordings, external sites), organization of communication between listeners (forum, chat, messaging), tasks whose answer is submitted electronically as one or several files, knowledge checks (tests, tasks), polls with one or several answer options, and a database of articles, books and hyperlinks [12]. The main interaction between listeners and teachers takes place in the University's electronic information and educational environment [13].

## Admission and organization of studies

Enrolment of listeners and acceptance of documents is done by specialists of the structural subdivision implementing the additional professional programme, according to the procedure for admission, reinstatement, transfer and expulsion of listeners under additional professional programmes, approved by order No. ОД/ДПО-01/2016/09/05 of 05 September 2016 [14]. The educational process is conducted in accordance with the University's approved additional professional programmes and the normative documents governing the educational process, as well as applicable legislation [15]. Questions connected with the organization of the educational process in distance learning are decided by the subdivision implementing the additional professional programme [16].

Studies with the use of distance technologies, in full or in individual disciplines of the curriculum, may be organized if there is appropriate teaching and methodological help for students, including individual consultations given remotely with information and telecommunication technologies; an appropriate level of training of pedagogical, research, support, administrative and economic staff for the technologies used; and the absence of classes held by direct interaction of the teacher with students in a classroom is allowed [17]. The subdivision implementing the programme prepares the teaching and methodological documentation, and specialists of organizations and enterprises may be involved in its development by agreement [18].

The forms of interaction between listeners and teachers include lectures and other classes under the teacher's guidance (online), practical, seminar and laboratory classes (online or offline) and chat interviews, individual and group consultations by e-mail, chat conferences and forums, independent work with textbooks, study guides and methodological materials and completion of tasks (offline), and current and intermediate assessment in the form of testing and other assessment events (online or offline) [19].

Studies use case technology, television-satellite technology (interactive television, tele- and radio lectures, video conferences, virtual practical classes) and Internet learning, or network technology [20]. Access is provided to the set of documents including the education programme (curriculum, academic calendar, work programmes of subjects, courses, disciplines and modules, other components, assessment and methodological materials) and educational resources (notes, textbooks, study guides, problem books, simulators, control tasks, glossaries, reference and further literature) [21]. The rights and duties of students mastering the programme with distance technologies are determined by the University's Charter, its internal regulations and applicable legislation [22].

## Assessment and completion

After successful intermediate assessment events, conducted with distance educational technologies and e-learning, and completion of the programme in full, the listener is admitted to final attestation, which is governed by the regulation on final attestation of listeners under additional professional programmes of the University; the forms and types of final attestation are fixed in the additional professional programme [23]. After the final attestation the subdivision implementing the programme prepares a draft order on completing studies under the professional retraining or повышение квалификации programme, which includes students who have met all curriculum requirements, successfully passed the final attestation and have no financial debt [24]. The document on professional retraining or повышение квалификации is received by the listener in person or by post; the register book of issued documents is kept at the University [25].

Records of the educational process (current control, intermediate and final attestation) and internal document flow are kept on paper and/or in electronic-digital form in accordance with the requirements of the law on state secrets, Federal Law No. 152-ФЗ of 27 July 2006 on personal data and Federal Law No. 25-ФЗ of 22 October 2004 on archiving [26].

## Staff, technical support and control

Teaching staff delivering distance learning is formed from the University's pedagogical workers and employees, and, where necessary, employees of third-party enterprises and organizations may be invited with payment under a contract for paid educational services; all management and pedagogical workers and support staff involved in organizing, conducting and supporting the educational process with distance learning must have appropriate training and regularly improve their qualifications in accordance with federal legislation on education; coordination and control of distance learning are assigned to the subdivision implementing the additional professional programme [27].

Technical support includes computer equipment and other equipment needed to operate, develop and store the software and information support of the distance learning system and to give teachers and listeners access to electronic information resources, and communication equipment providing access through local networks and the Internet [28]. The software includes the Moodle content management system, which provides access to curricula, discipline and practice work programmes, electronic library systems and electronic educational resources named in the work programmes and allows all types of classes and assessment procedures provided for by e-learning and distance technologies, as well as software for webinars and other ways of communication between teachers and listeners over the Internet [29].

## Teacher, curator and tutor

A teacher or curator has the right to control the learning process by assigning control works and test tasks provided for by the education programme, to organize forums and online consultations, and not to admit a student who has not mastered the previous module to the next module in sequential learning [30].

A teacher or curator must help with processing information and consult students where necessary, including on documenting the results of independent work through the Internet or in any convenient way; give feedback during tasks (clarifying the degree of completion, answering questions, diagnosing causes of difficulties, giving recommendations and helping to find solutions); design and deliver quality teaching of disciplines taught at a distance; timely replenish and edit the teaching and methodological complexes; answer students' questions in good time; and systematically improve professional qualifications, including in using the specific tools of distance learning [31].

Teachers and curators using distance technologies also perform the duties of a tutor [32]. Tutor duties are: organizing the process of individual work with students; helping students overcome difficulties of self-education and master new technologies, including those related to distance learning; monitoring all actions of students; supporting the student's cognitive interest; and organizing individual and group consultations [33].

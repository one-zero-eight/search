# Methodological Department — place, tasks, functions and rights

## The document and the Department's place in the structure

The Regulation on the Methodological Department (Положение о методическом отделе) was approved by order of 31 October 2024 № ОД/АД-06/2024/10/31, signed by the Director for Development and HR Policy R.F. Valiev [1]. The Regulation regulates the activity of the Methodological Department of ANO VO «Innopolis University» and determines its tasks, functions, rights and the duties of its employees [2].

The Methodological Department (Отдел) is a structural subdivision of the Centre for the Organisation of Educational Programmes of the Department (управление) of Academic Policy and Higher Education and belongs to the category of methodological and educational-methodological subdivisions [3]. It reports directly to the head of the Centre for the Organisation of Educational Programmes [4].

In its activity the Department follows the Constitution of the Russian Federation, the Labour Code of the Russian Federation, Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29 December 2012 «Об образовании в Российской Федерации» and other legislative and normative legal acts regulating the activity of higher education institutions, the University's Charter, decisions of the Academic, Supervisory and Operational councils, orders and instructions of the University's director, other local normative acts and this Regulation [5].

- The structure, the number of staff and the staffing table of the Department, and changes to them, are approved by the director of the University or another person authorised by him [6].
- The Department is headed by its head, hired and dismissed by order of the director of the University [7].
- The working conditions of the Department's employees are determined by employment contracts concluded with each employee and by the University's internal labour regulations [8].
- The rights, duties and degree of responsibility of the Department's employees are established by job descriptions [9].
- Employees of the Department may, within their competences, be involved in work under grants, projects, subsidies and other programmes implemented by the University [10].
- The Regulation and additions and changes to it are approved by order of the director of the University or of another person authorised by him [11].

The order approving the Regulation instructed the head of the Methodological Department T.V. Bikusova to ensure timely acquaintance, against signature, with the Regulation of the Department's employees and of employees newly hired or transferred (moved) into the Department from other subdivisions [1]. It also declared void the Regulation on the Methodological Department of the Centre for the Organisation of Educational Programmes approved by order of 26 May 2021 № ОД/АД-01/2021/05/26 [1].

## The order of 1 September 2025: the Methodological Department of the Center of Educational Content

A later page carries the order of 1 September 2025 № ОД/АД-02/2025/09/01, issued in Innopolis and signed by the Director for Development and HR Policy R.F. Valiev, under the heading «Методический отдел 1 — Положение» [12]. It is issued in connection with the creation of a new structural subdivision, in order to optimise the University's activity, and it approves the attached Regulation on the Methodological Department of the Center of Educational Content of ANO VO «Innopolis University» [12].

- The head of the Methodological Department R.R. Gabidullova is to ensure timely acquaintance, against signature, with the approved Regulation of the Department's employees, and also of employees newly hired and transferred (moved) into the Department from other subdivisions of the University [12].
- Control over the execution of the order and of the Regulation approved by it is retained by the signatory [12].

The two pages differ: the Regulation of 31 October 2024 describes the Methodological Department of the Centre for the Organisation of Educational Programmes of the Department of Academic Policy and Higher Education, headed by T.V. Bikusova [3], [1], while the page of 1 September 2025 places the Methodological Department in the Center of Educational Content and names R.R. Gabidullova as its head [12]. The page of 1 September 2025 contains only the order: it does not give the tasks, functions or rights of the Department, and it does not say that the Regulation of 31 October 2024 is repealed [12].

## Tasks

The Department's tasks are [13], [14]:

- organising and coordinating the work on the development and updating of the main professional educational programmes of higher education — bachelor's programmes, master's programmes and programmes for the training of scientific and scientific-pedagogical personnel in aspirantura [13];
- methodological support of the educational process in higher education programmes [14].

## Functions

The Department carries out the following functions:

- drawing up the calendar study schedule for higher education programmes [15]
- developing, together with staff of the Department for the Organisation of Bachelor's and Master's Programmes, of the faculties, institutes and laboratories that are part of the University's structure, and with the teaching staff, the set of competences formed by bachelor's and master's programmes [16]
- organising the process of forming and updating curricula and other components of higher education programmes on the basis of the list of disciplines (modules), internships and other components prepared by the teaching staff and the staff of those subdivisions [17]
- methodological support of the development and updating of components of higher education programmes [18]
- forming the set of documents on higher education programmes [19]
- expert and consultative support of the preparation/updating of syllabuses for the disciplines (modules) of the curriculum of higher education programmes [20]
- preparing drafts of local normative acts regulating educational activity in higher education programmes [21]:
  - the regulation on the procedure for developing and approving the main professional educational programmes of bachelor's and master's programmes [21]
  - the regulation on the procedure for developing and approving programmes for the training of scientific and scientific-pedagogical personnel in aspirantura [21]
  - the order approving the calendar study schedule for the main professional educational programmes of higher education [21]
  - the order approving the professional competences determined independently on the basis of the focus (profile) of the programme and the indicators of their achievement for bachelor's and master's programmes [21]
  - the order approving the list of persons who carry out the general supervision of the scientific content of master's programmes [21]
  - the order approving the register of the main professional educational programmes of higher education being implemented [21]
  - other local normative acts within the Department's tasks [21]
- taking part in calculating the teaching load of teaching staff and in forming individual work plans of teaching staff together with the faculties, institutes and laboratories subordinate to the vice-rector — head of the Department of Academic Policy and Higher Education [22]
- forming information on the staffing provision of higher education programmes [23]
- ensuring compliance with the requirements of Russian legislation and local normative acts on the storage of the package of documents on higher education programmes [24]
- providing information and preparing documents on higher education programmes at the request of the University's structural subdivisions and of state authorities and administration [25]
- supporting the process of internal and external assessment of the quality of educational activity and of the preparation of students in higher education programmes [26]
- expert and consultative support of the activity of the University's structural subdivisions [27]
- taking part in round tables, conferences, seminars, webinars and other events on matters touching the Department's tasks and functions [28]

## Rights

In carrying out their activity the Department's employees have the right to:

- request from the University's structural subdivisions the information and materials needed for the work within the Department's competence [29]
- with the consent of the heads of the University's structural subdivisions, involve the employees of those subdivisions in preparing drafts of local normative acts and other documents and in developing and carrying out measures conducted by the Department [30]
- take part in meetings held by the University's management when discussing questions whose solution falls within the Department's competence [31]
- put before the University's management proposals to cancel, amend or supplement the University's local normative acts [32]
- interact with bodies of state authority and administration, legal entities and the University's subdivisions on questions within the Department's competence [33]

## Interaction with other subdivisions

The Department's interaction with other structural subdivisions is determined by the tasks and functions assigned to it by the Regulation [34]. The Regulation names these counterparts and matters:

- the Department for the Organisation of Bachelor's and Master's Programmes — on the development and updating of higher education programmes [35]
- the Department for the Organisation of Educational Activity — on preparing drafts of local normative acts regulating educational activity in bachelor's, master's and aspirantura programmes [36]
- the Faculty of Computer and Engineering Sciences — on the development and updating of higher education programmes [37]
- the Faculty of Humanities and Social Sciences — on the development and updating of higher education programmes [38]
- the Department for Control of Educational Activity — on normative-legal support of educational activity and on assessing the quality of educational activity and of the preparation of students in higher education programmes [39]
- the Department for Relations with Industry and Alumni — on the development and updating of internship programmes in higher education programmes [40]
- the Department for Personnel Records and Movement and the Department for Recruitment and Support of Teaching Staff — on the staffing provision of higher education programmes [41]
- the Department of Documentation Support of Management Activity — on organising and keeping document flow, drawing up local normative acts and other documents, keeping the Department's files according to the University's consolidated nomenclature and transferring documents to the University's archive [42]
- the Organisational and Technical Department — on the material and technical provision of the implementation of higher education programmes [43]
- the IT Department (Департамент информационных технологий) — on the operation of the University's information systems, the functioning of the electronic information-educational environment, access to professional databases and information reference systems, and providing higher education programmes with licensed and freely distributed software, including that of domestic production [44]
- the Library — on providing access to electronic library systems and on educational-methodological support of the implementation of higher education programmes [45]
- other structural subdivisions — in the course of developing and updating higher education programmes, preparing drafts of local normative acts, orders, instructions, contracts and other documents, and preparing conclusions and written and oral references on questions within the Department's competence [46]

The Department may be a base for the practical training of the University's students [47].

## Reporting

On the basis of the relevant local normative acts, orders and instructions the Department may, within its activity, be responsible for preparing information, references and reports and keeping them up to date [48]. Within its activity the Department is responsible for preparing and keeping up to date the components of higher education programmes [49].

## What other documents assign to the Methodological Department

- Staff of the Methodological Department of the Centre for the Organisation of Educational Programmes, of the Department for the Organisation of Bachelor's and Master's Programmes and of the IT Department may give methodological, organisational and technical support in preparing and conducting interim assessment, while the teaching staff member remains responsible for it [50].
- The head of the Methodological Department of the Centre for the Organisation of Educational Programmes may appoint another teaching staff member to conduct the first retake if the teacher who held the assessment cannot do it within the set deadline [51].
- In the state final certification with distance learning technologies, students send the electronic ВКР and annotation to the Methodological Department at education@innopolis.ru no later than 10 calendar days before the defence; the appeal commission's chair informs the Methodological Department of the results of its meeting, and the Department notifies the student of the results of the appeal by e-mail to the corporate address [52], [53], [54].

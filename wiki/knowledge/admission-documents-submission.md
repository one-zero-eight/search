# Submitting application documents for admission — ways, postal address and receipt (the sveden pages of the 2026/2027 admission rules for bachelor's, master's and aspirantura programmes)

## What the pages are

Two pages of the same sveden section («apply») describe how and where an applicant submits the application and the documents for admission [1], [2].

- One is an extract from the admission rules for higher education programmes — bachelor's and master's programmes — of ANO VO «Innopolis University» for the 2026/2027 academic year: it reproduces section VII «Прием заявлений и документов», clause 66 [1], [3]. Its text contains no postal index and no electronic address, and states no deadlines of its own: it refers for the deadlines to section VII of the admission rules [3].
- The other is the page «Информация о местах приема заявлений о приеме на обучение и прилагаемых к ним документов, о почтовых адресах для направления документов, необходимых для поступления, об электронных адресах для направления документов, необходимых для поступления в эл. форме», which gives one clause, numbered 19; its source file in the sveden section is named «Информация_о_местах_приема_документов_асп.docx», i.e. it is the information page on the places for submitting documents for the aspirantura programmes [2], [4].

The two pages differ in the number of the clause, in the wording of what is submitted and in the office where documents are accepted [3], [4].

## Ways of submitting an application and documents

Under the bachelor's and master's extract an applicant submits the applications for admission and the documents needed for admission in one of the following ways [3]:

- by presenting them to Innopolis University in person [3];
- by sending them to Innopolis University through a general-purpose postal operator (оператор почтовой связи) [3];
- by presenting them through the federal state information system «Единый портал государственных и муниципальных услуг (функций)» (ЕПГУ) [3].

Under the aspirantura information page the applicant submits the applications for admission and (or) the documents needed for admission by the same three ways, at his own decision: in person at Innopolis University, through a general-purpose postal operator, or through ЕПГУ [4].

In both cases Innopolis University ensures the possibility of presenting (sending) the applications and the documents needed for admission by all the listed ways [3], [4].

## Where documents are accepted

- Under the bachelor's and master's extract the University accepts applications and documents at: Republic of Tatarstan, Verkhneuslonsky municipal district, town of Innopolis, ul. Universitetskaya, house 1, cabinet 100 (Приемная комиссия — the admission committee), within the deadlines established in section VII of the admission rules [3].
- Under the aspirantura information page the University accepts documents at: 420500, Republic of Tatarstan, Verkhneuslonsky municipal district, town of Innopolis, ul. Universitetskaya, house 1, cabinet 106а (Приемная комиссия), within the deadlines established by the admission rules it refers to [4].

## Receipt for the documents

Under the bachelor's and master's extract, if the documents needed for admission are presented to the University in person by the applicant, the applicant is issued a receipt (расписка) for the acceptance of the application and documents [3]. The aspirantura information page states no receipt [4].

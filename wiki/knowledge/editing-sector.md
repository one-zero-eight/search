# Editing Sector (Сектор редактирования) of the Documentation Department — place, tasks, functions and rights

## The document and the Sector's place in the structure

The Regulation on the Editing Sector (Положение о Секторе редактирования) of the Department for Documentary Support of Managerial Activity of ANO VO «Innopolis University» was approved by order of 27 March 2026 No. ОД/АД‐03/2026/03/27, signed by R.F. Valiev as Director for Development and HR Policy, in Innopolis [1]. It regulates the Sector's activity and determines its tasks, functions, rights and duties [2].

- The Editing Sector (the Sector) is a structural subdivision of the Department for Documentary Support of Managerial Activity and belongs to the category of «иные структурные подразделения» (other structural subdivisions) [3]. It reports directly to the head of the Department for Documentary Support of Managerial Activity [4].
- In its activity the Sector is guided by the Constitution of the Russian Federation, the Labour Code of the Russian Federation, the Federal Law of 29 December 2012 No. 273-ФЗ «Об образовании в Российской Федерации», other legislative and normative legal acts regulating the activity of higher education institutions, the University's Charter, decisions of the University's Academic, Supervisory and Operational councils, orders and instructions of the University director, other local normative acts and this Regulation [5].
- The structure, headcount and staffing table of the Sector, and changes to them, are approved by the University director [6]. The Sector is headed by a head of the editing sector, hired and dismissed by order of the University director [7].
- The working conditions of the Sector's employees are determined by the employment contracts concluded with each of them, by the University's internal labour regulations and by other local normative acts [8]; their rights, duties and degree of responsibility are established by job descriptions [9].
- Within their competence the Sector's employees may be involved in work under grants, projects, subsidies and other programmes implemented by the University [10]. The Regulation and the additions and changes to it are approved by the University director or by another person authorised by him [11].

## Tasks

The tasks of the Sector are [12]–[13]:

- ensuring the quality of preparation of the University's organisational, administrative, local normative and information-reference documents drawn up for the signature of the University director and of persons authorised by him, by editorial and technical proofreading, checking compliance with uniform formatting requirements and removing spelling, punctuation, stylistic and substantive defects [12];
- unifying the forms of documents arising in the activity of the University's structural subdivisions and ensuring uniformity of formatting, terminology, designations and abbreviations in accordance with the normative requirements in force [14];
- ensuring that draft documents comply with the requirements of the legislation in force, state standards and the University's local normative acts as regards the rules of drafting and formatting documents [15];
- taking part in improving the University's document flow processes as regards the development and updating of standard forms, instructions and other documents regulating the rules of formatting documents [13].

## Functions

- Proofreading and editing draft organisational, administrative, local normative and information-reference documents of the University drawn up for the signature of the University director and of the officials authorised by him (the Documents) [16].
- Checking compliance with the rules of graphical and lexical uniformity of text elements, removing spelling, punctuation, stylistic and substantive defects and, where necessary, agreeing the changes made with the authors (executors) of the Documents [17].
- Determining whether the Documents' formatting meets the general drafting and formatting requirements under the legislation in force, state standards and the University's local normative acts, including compliance with the technical rules of typing; uniformity of designations, symbols, units of measurement and conventional abbreviations; correct formatting of tables, footnotes and annexes [18].
- Checking the completeness of the Documents, including whether the number of annexes and additional materials actually submitted corresponds to the information stated in the covering letters [19].
- Returning the Documents to the authors (executors) for revision when non-compliance with the established requirements is found, stating the reasons for the return [20].
- Taking part in developing and updating standard forms of documents arising in the activity of the University's structural subdivisions [21].
- Taking part in developing and updating local normative acts, methodological documents and instructions that establish requirements, rules and recommendations on formatting documents at the University [22], and the University's document blanks [23].
- Taking part in optimising the University's document flow business processes as regards the formatting of documents and the passage of editorial and technical processing [24].
- Consulting the employees of the University's structural subdivisions on formatting documents, observing the rules of editorial and technical preparation and applying standard forms and unified requirements [25].

## Rights

In carrying out their activity the Sector's employees have the right to [26]:

- request from the University's structural subdivisions the information and materials needed for the work within the Sector's competence [26];
- with the consent of the heads of the University's structural subdivisions, engage the employees of those subdivisions to prepare draft local acts and other documents and to develop and carry out the measures conducted by the Sector [27];
- take part in the meetings held by the University's management when questions within the Sector's competence are discussed [28];
- submit to the University's management proposals to cancel, amend or supplement the University's local acts [29];
- interact with state and government bodies, legal entities and the University's subdivisions on questions within the Sector's competence [30];
- require the University's employees to observe the established rules of working with documents and to format the University's documents (outgoing letters, orders and so on) properly, and not to accept from executors documents formatted improperly [31].

## Interaction with other structural subdivisions

Interaction with other structural subdivisions is determined by the tasks and functions assigned to the Sector by this Regulation [32].

- With all structural subdivisions — on preparing draft local acts, orders, instructions, contracts and other documents; on preparing conclusions, written and oral references on questions within the Sector's competence arising in practical activity; and on electronic document flow using the «Битрикс» corporate portal and МЭДО [33].
- The Sector may be a base for the practical training of the University's students [34].

## Reporting

On the basis of the relevant local acts, orders and instructions, the Sector may be responsible, within its activity, for preparing and keeping up to date information, references and reports [35].

# Electronic information and educational environment (ЭИОС) — the Regulation of 29 December 2025: purpose, components, access, marks and portfolio

## The document

The Regulation on the electronic information and educational environment (электронная информационно-образовательная среда, ЭИОС) of ANO VO «Innopolis University» was approved by order of 29 December 2025 № ОД/АД-02/2025/12/29, signed by Director D.V. Vandyukov, and dated Innopolis 2025 [1]. It determines the purpose, structure and content of the University's ЭИОС [2].

The Regulation was developed in accordance with federal laws on information, information technologies and information protection, on personal data and on education in the Russian Federation, government decrees on the training of research and research-pedagogical staff in aspirantura and on the rules for the use of e-learning and distance educational technologies by organisations carrying out educational activity, orders of the federal ministries on the organisation of educational activity under the different types of programmes, accreditation indicators and accreditation monitoring, federal state educational standards and federal state requirements, the University's Charter and other acts [3] [4] [5].

The same Regulation is on the wiki twice, under different site sections («Сведения об образовании» and «Сведения об объектах»); the two copies carry the same text and clause numbers [1] [6].

## What the ЭИОС is

The ЭИОС comprises electronic information resources, electronic educational resources, a set of information and telecommunication technologies and the corresponding technical means [7].

It provides:

- access to curricula, work programmes of subjects, courses, disciplines (modules), practices, electronic educational publications and educational resources listed in those work programmes, to the resources accompanying the research and educational process of aspirantura training, and to electronic library systems [8];
- recording of the course of the educational process and of the results of interim assessment, including the results of interim attestations together with the results of an aspirant's individual research plan and marks for the individual work plan, and access to the electronic timetable [9];
- holding all types of classes and assessment procedures where e-learning and distance educational technologies are used [10];
- formation of the student's electronic portfolio, including storage of the student's works, reviews and marks for those works from any participant of the educational process [11];
- interaction between participants of the educational process, synchronous and/or asynchronous, over the Internet [12];
- access to other components in accordance with Russian legislation on education [13].

## Who the users are and what the ЭИОС is for

Users of the ЭИОС are: the University's students; its research and teaching staff (научно-педагогические работники); its staff; employers (within the access rights granted to them); and other natural and legal persons, representatives of local self-government bodies and state authorities — within the access rights granted to them in cases provided by legislation [14].

Its goals, tasks and functions are information openness of the University, a single educational space built on modern information technologies, information support of the educational process to the requirements of federal state educational standards and federal state requirements, access to electronic information and educational resources, a communication platform for users, access to curricula and work programmes, recording of the educational process and of interim assessment, holding classes and assessment procedures with e-learning and distance technologies, the electronic portfolio, interaction between participants, mechanisms and procedures for monitoring the quality of the educational process, and access for students who are disabled or have health limitations to the components of the ЭИОС with regard to their psychophysical development, individual capabilities and state of health [15] [16].

The principles of creating and operating the ЭИОС are accessibility and openness; systematicity, integrativity and multifunctionality; and user orientation [17].

Its operation is ensured by information and communication technologies and by the qualification of the staff who use and support it, in conformity with Russian legislation, and information about the existence of the ЭИОС and of Internet access, together with this Regulation, is published on the University's official site in the section «Сведения об образовательной организации», subsection «Материально-техническое обеспечение и оснащенность образовательного процесса. Доступная среда» [1].

## Components (where students and staff work)

- the University's official site, https://innopolis.university/ [18];
- the University's information portal, https://my.university.innopolis.ru / https://my.innopolis.university [19];
- the University's channel in the national messenger MAX, https://max.ru/id1655258235_biz [20];
- the LMS Moodle system, https://moodle.innopolis.university/ [21];
- the electronic catalogue of the University's scientific and technical library, https://portal.university.innopolis.ru/reading_hall/ [22];
- the collection of educational literature on the EBSCOHost platform, https://search.ebscohost.com/ [23];
- the IPR Books electronic library system, https://www.iprbookshop.ru/ [24];
- the «Лань» electronic library system, https://e.lanbook.com/ [25];
- ConsultantPlus, the legal reference system, accessible from the University's local network [26];
- other software, hardware and software complexes, means of synchronous and/or asynchronous interaction, electronic library systems and other components integrated into or listed in the ЭИОС [27].

## Technical requirements

The ЭИОС must have fault-tolerant storage and recovery of data and certified hardware and software means of information security [28] [29]. Its software, including that of domestic production, must have the corresponding licences and certificates and/or be freely distributed software [30]. The data transmission network must provide a throughput of at least 100 Mbit/s, the platform must give access on a 365/24/7 scheme, and on the University's territory users' devices must be connectable to the Internet by Wi-Fi [31] [32] [33]. The security of the ЭИОС's data is ensured by each component's own hardware and software [34].

## Registration, accounts and loss of access

These rules stand in the part of the Regulation on the procedure for registering users [1]:

- by level of access to the information placed in the ЭИОС users are authorised and unauthorised; authorised users have access to all components, unauthorised users only to the University's official site [1];
- access to all components except those containing publicly available information is through identification and authentication by login and password (credentials); credentials are created automatically, and control over the creation of the login and password is assigned to the structural subdivision responsible for the creation and functioning of the University's IT environment [1];
- a staff member's credentials are created automatically on the basis of the order on hiring and are brought to the employee's notice by a message to the University's corporate mail; such users have access to the personalised part of the ЭИОС, and their level of access is determined by their job descriptions [1];
- a student's credentials are created automatically on the basis of the order on enrolment and are brought to the student's notice by a message to the University's corporate mail [1];
- throughout the whole period of study each student is provided with individual unlimited access to the structural elements of the ЭИОС in accordance with the requirements of federal state educational standards [1];
- if credentials are lost, they are to be changed by sending a free-form request to the structural subdivision responsible for the creation and functioning of the University's IT environment [1];
- students' and staff's credentials are blocked on the basis of the corresponding orders on expulsion and dismissal [1];
- the information content of the ЭИОС is determined by the needs of users, the requirements of legal acts and the University's local acts, and information is placed by users who have the corresponding level of access [1].

## Curricula, work programmes and library resources

- The ЭИОС gives users access to curricula, work programmes of subjects, courses, disciplines (modules), practice programmes, programmes of the final (state final) attestation and other components of educational programmes, and to electronic educational publications, electronic library systems and electronic educational resources listed in the work programmes, for all educational programmes implemented at the University [1].
- Educational programmes — their general characteristics, curricula, academic calendars, work programmes, practice programmes as electronic documents, final (state final) attestation programmes, work programmes of upbringing and calendar plans of upbringing work — are in open access in the corresponding part of the section «Сведения об образовательной организации» of the official site; access may also be obtained by following links placed on the University's information portal [1].
- Information about the electronic library system editions and electronic educational resources used is placed on the official site in the section «Сведения об образовательной организации», and access to them is possible regardless of the point of connection to the Internet by following the links in the section «Электронные учебные издания и электронные образовательные ресурсы» on the information portal [1].

## Records of progress, marks and the timetable

- The course of the educational process is recorded in LMS Moodle by reflecting in real time how students master the subjects, courses and disciplines (modules) provided by the curriculum [1].
- That record reflects all types of work performed by the student and provided by the work programme, the marks for those works given by the academic staff and other participants of educational relations, and the results of ongoing assessment [1].
- Results of interim assessment are recorded in paper and/or electronic form; recording in the ЭИОС takes place on the University's information portal in the subsections «Фиксация результатов промежуточной аттестации» and/or «Зачетная книжка» [1].
- Results of interim assessment are placed in students' personal accounts on the information portal, stating the name, the workload (трудоемкость), the academic year of study, the period of study, the form and the result of the interim assessment as given in the University's grading order, the teacher's full name and the details of the record sheet (ведомость) [1].
- The mastering of the main professional educational programme is recorded on the information portal in the special subsection «Фиксация результатов освоения программы» by showing the status of mastering of each competence provided by the programme in correlation with the subjects, courses, disciplines (modules) and practices provided by the curriculum [1].
- The ЭИОС gives students access to the electronic timetable — a service by which each student can find their current timetable of classes and of interim assessment, placed on the information portal in the subsection «Электронное расписание» [1].

## Electronic portfolio

- The ЭИОС provides for the formation of students' electronic portfolio, including storage of students' works and of reviews by any participants of the educational process; the portfolio is formed on the University's information portal [1].
- The portfolio is a personalised electronic resource of an established form reflecting the totality of information about the individual achievements of a University student, and its purpose is to record and store information about significant results of the student's professional and personal development [1].
- Students fill in themselves: the section «Мои работы и рецензии», where a work is described by the fields Название, Тип (practice, research work, project or other work), Описание and Файл [35]; a section allowing any participant of educational relations to be evaluated by finding that person's work by their e-mail address [36]; and the section «Достижения», for achievements in academic, research, socially significant, cultural and creative and sporting activity [37].
- A student obtains access to their portfolio after receiving the login and password for the ЭИОС, while participants of the educational process (research and teaching staff and staff whose activity is connected with organising the educational process, students' research, and extracurricular and upbringing work) obtain access to view the additional part of the portfolio and access to form and review its obligatory part [1].

## Interaction and e-learning

- The ЭИОС provides synchronous and/or asynchronous interaction between participants of the educational process over the Internet [1].
- Asynchronous interaction is provided through the personal accounts on the information portal and LMS Moodle, where a message can be sent to any registered user, and through the University's e-mail and other communication means and software listed in the ЭИОС [1].
- For synchronous interaction the systems for webinars, video calls and other communication means listed in the sections «Взаимодействие между участниками образовательного процесса» and «Сервисы» of the ЭИОС are used [1].
- Participants are informed about the most important events through the news system placed on the official site, by e-mail and through other sources of information [1].
- The ЭИОС ensures the holding of all types of classes and assessment procedures whose implementation is provided with e-learning and distance educational technologies, and educational programmes at the University are implemented, among other things, with e-learning and distance educational technologies [1].
- For that purpose LMS Moodle has been created, giving access to educational materials on the subjects, courses and disciplines studied, allowing students' works to be placed and the results of their checking to be received, and enabling synchronous and/or asynchronous interaction between participants of educational relations [1].

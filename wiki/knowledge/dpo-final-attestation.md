# Final attestation of listeners of additional professional programmes (ДПП) — forms, commissions, procedure and documents

## The document and whom it covers

The Regulation on the final attestation of listeners of additional professional programmes of ANO VO «Innopolis University» was approved by order of the University of 17 September 2021 and is dated Innopolis, 2021 [1]. It determines a single procedure for passing the final attestation by listeners irrespective of the form and terms of study [2] and was drawn up on the basis of the Federal Law of 29 December 2012 No. 273-ФЗ, the order of the Ministry of Education and Science of 1 July 2013 No. 499 on the organisation of educational activity under additional professional programmes, the methodological recommendations on the organisation of the final attestation sent by letter of 30 March 2015 No. АК-821/06, and the University's Charter [3].

It applies to listeners who have completed in full the study under professional retraining and повышение квалификации programmes developed by the structural unit of the University that delivers the programme [4]. The final attestation of listeners completing study under additional professional programmes is obligatory [5] and is held as a rule on the University's premises [6]; its purpose is to determine the quality and the conformity of the competences formed in the listeners to the direction of professional activity [7].

The Regulation on the additional professional programme of the University refers to this Regulation for the final attestation that concludes the mastering of a programme [8].

## Forms of attestation tests

The final attestation may consist of one or several attestation tests depending on the terms of study and the types of programme, and its forms and types are fixed in the programme [9]. At the University the following attestation tests may be held:

- for listeners of professional retraining programmes: a final interdisciplinary examination, and the preparation and defence of a final attestation (qualification) work [10];
- for listeners of повышение квалификации programmes: an examination, the preparation and defence of a final attestation (qualification) work, a pass (зачёт) under the programme, and other types provided by the programme [11].

The final interdisciplinary examination under a retraining programme must establish the conformity of the level of the listeners' mastered competences to the qualification requirements for concrete professions or positions [12]. The final attestation (qualification) work of a retraining listener is an indicator of the quality of his practical and theoretical preparation and is intended to determine his meta-subject, subject and research competences in professional activity; the ВАР is provided in retraining programmes aimed at obtaining the competence needed for a new type of professional activity, and the ВКР in retraining programmes aimed at acquiring a new qualification [13]. The pass under a повышение квалификации programme must reflect the level of the listener's mastery of competences in concrete questions of professional activity and of a deeper study of problems in the profile of the activity, and may be held as testing or in other forms [14].

## The attestation commissions

The final attestation is carried out by attestation commissions, whose creation and activity are regulated by this Regulation, other University documents and federal normative legal acts [15]. Commissions are created for the final attestation under each professional retraining programme, and several commissions may be formed within one programme [16].

- The commission includes the teachers who delivered the programme, the head of the structural unit that delivered it and teachers of the University; teachers of other educational institutions and specialists of enterprises and organisations in the profile of the programme may be invited from outside, and the commission must have a chair and at least two members [17].
- The commission is headed by a chair who organises and controls its activity and ensures a unity of the requirements towards the listeners [18].
- The composition of the commissions is approved by order on the representation of the head of the structural unit before the start of the final attestation [19].
- Their functions are to evaluate the level of the listeners' competences with account of the aims of study and the requirements to the content and results of the programme, to confirm the right to conduct a new type of professional activity and (or) to award a qualification — under professional retraining programmes — and to consider questions of awarding a qualification, this too under professional retraining programmes [20].
- The final attestation under повышение квалификации programmes is held as a rule without forming an attestation commission [21].

## Admission and conditions

- The forms and conditions of the attestation tests are brought to the listeners' notice not later than one month before the end of study under a retraining programme and on the first day of study under a повышение квалификации programme; the listeners receive methodological recommendations on preparing and defending the ВАР/ВКР, are made acquainted with the topics and are given conditions for preparation, including consultations [22].
- A listener who has no debt and has completed in full the curriculum (individual curriculum) of the programme is admitted to the final attestation [23].
- Admission to the final attestation under повышение квалификации programmes is formalised by order [24], and admission to the final interdisciplinary examination is formalised by order on condition that the listener has completed in full the mastering of the programme [25].
- The list of listeners admitted to the defence of final attestation (qualification) works is approved by order [26].
- The examination (pass) and the defence are held at open meetings of the attestation commission with the participation of not less than two thirds of its members [27].

## The pass (зачёт) under повышение квалификации programmes

The pass is held as testing or in other forms in accordance with the programme and may be oral or written [28]. Its questions and tasks are developed in full accordance with the programme by the teachers who delivered it and correspond to its topic, and include questions and tasks showing a change in the listeners' professional competence within the topic studied [29]. The time for preparation, the length of the answer and the duration of a written pass may be determined in accordance with the programme [30].

A written pass is considered completed with 60 % of correct answers, and an oral pass is considered passed if the answer receives a positive grade of the teacher [31]. The grade includes the level of mastery of all the competences formed during the study of the programme; the grade «зачёт» corresponds to the improvement and (or) acquisition of a new competence needed for professional activity and (or) to a rise in the professional level within the existing qualification [32]. The results are graded «зачтено» or «не зачтено» [33]; a sheet of the final attestation with the full list of admitted listeners is prepared by the specialists of the structural unit delivering the programme [34].

## The final interdisciplinary examination

The examination papers are formed by the teachers who delivered the programme [35] and must be approved by the chair of the attestation commission before the start of the final attestation; they are drawn up on the approved form, contain at least two questions and are presented in two variants — a printed cut version and a printed uncut version [36].

The grade is integrated and includes the level of mastery of all competences formed during the programme; it corresponds to the threshold, advanced or high level of mastery and is determined as «отлично», «хорошо», «удовлетворительно» or «неудовлетворительно» [37]. Grades are entered by the secretary of the attestation commission in the attestation sheet and in the protocol of the commission's meeting on the day of the final attestation, and also in the annex to the diploma of professional retraining [38]. Where the answer is unsatisfactory or the listener does not appear for the final interdisciplinary examination, the listener is given the opportunity of an official retake within 3 (three) days from the day of the examination [39].

## The final attestation (qualification) work

- The defence is held within the terms set by the schedule of the study process [40].
- The approximate list of topics is developed by the teachers who delivered the programme and is a component of the programme; the topics may also be formed by the heads of enterprises and organisations that send listeners to study, and a listener may choose a topic from the list or propose his own with a justification of its expediency; the topic must correspond to the content of the programme [41].
- The topics are determined not later than one month before the end of study [42]; the assignment of a topic to a listener and the wording of the topic are approved by order [43], and a clarification or, in exceptional cases, a change of the approved topic is also formalised by order, at the initiative of the supervisor, considered by the head of the structural unit and issued as a rule not later than a week before the start of the final attestation by a распоряжение of the vice-rector — head of the department that includes the unit delivering the programme [44].
- The work is performed under the direct control of its supervisor and is an independent and logically completed study determining the level of assimilation and practical use of the knowledge and skills obtained in the course of study [45].
- The supervisor is appointed from among the teachers who delivered the programme, and a practical worker — a specialist in the given branch — may also be appointed [46]; the supervisor helps to draw up the plan of work and sets the calendar dates of its parts, holds consultations systematically, controls the progress of the work, checks its content in parts and as a whole and prepares the listener for the defence [47].
- The general requirements to the structure, presentation and formatting of the work are developed by the structural unit delivering the programme [48].
- As a rule, the original of the work is presented for the defence two weeks before it [49].
- The results of the defence are discussed at a closed meeting of the attestation commission and assessed by simple majority of the members participating, the opinion of the chair being decisive when the votes are equal [50]; the grade is announced to the listener on the same day after the protocol of the meeting is drawn up [51].
- The grade for the defence is integrated, includes the level of mastery of all competences formed during the programme and depends on the competences shown during the defence and on the supervisor's отзыв; the commission pays attention to the content and quality of the research, the formatting, the level of the answers to questions and remarks and the level of the competences demonstrated, and grades are «отлично», «хорошо», «удовлетворительно», «неудовлетворительно» [52].
- After the defence the work is kept in the structural unit delivering the programme for 5 (five) years in printed and (or) electronic form [53].

## Results, protocols and reports

- The decision of the attestation commission is taken at closed meetings by simple majority of the members participating, the chair's vote being decisive when the votes are equal, and is taken and communicated to the listener on the day of the attestation tests [54].
- The chair and the members sign the protocol of the meeting and (or) the attestation sheet, and the questions asked to the listener are recorded in the protocol [55].
- The protocols of the final attestation are approved by the chair and kept in a separate folder in the structural unit that delivered the programme for 10 (ten) years [56]; the sheets with the results of the attestation tests and the attestation (qualification) works are kept in that unit for 5 (five) years [57].
- On the results of the commission's meeting the chair prepares a report on its work with recommendations for improving the quality of the delivery of professional retraining programmes [58].

## Documents issued, failure and repeat

- A listener who has successfully completed a professional retraining programme of not less than 250 hours receives a diploma of professional retraining, and a listener who has successfully completed a повышение квалификации programme of 16 hours or more receives an удостоверение о повышении квалификации [59].
- Where an additional professional programme is mastered in parallel with obtaining secondary vocational and (or) higher education, the удостоверение and (or) diploma are issued simultaneously with the corresponding document on education and qualification [60].
- Listeners who did not pass the final attestation or received unsatisfactory results may pass it again within 3 (three) days [61]; in the case of an unsatisfactory defence of the work the listener is issued a certificate of study listing the disciplines studied and the results of the passes and examinations passed, which is exchanged for the diploma on a successful repeat attestation [62].
- Listeners who did not pass the final attestation for a valid reason (medical indications or other exceptional cases confirmed by documents) must be given the opportunity to pass it without expulsion, in accordance with the medical conclusion or another document presented, or with reinstatement on the date of the attestation; where the listener was sent to study by an enterprise (organisation), the question is agreed with that enterprise [63].
- Listeners who did not pass the final attestation or received unsatisfactory results are issued a certificate of study or of the period of study on a form established by the University [64].
- No appeal is provided for the results of the final attestation under повышение квалификации and professional retraining programmes [65].

## Quality of the programme

The requirements to the internal evaluation of the quality of mastering additional professional programmes are determined on the basis of the University's Regulation on the types and forms of internal evaluation of the quality of the delivery of additional professional programmes and of their results [66].

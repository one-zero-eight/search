# Material assistance under the Regulation on scholarships — grounds, documents and frequency (Appendix 2)

## What the Regulation provides

- The University may establish, at the expense of the funds received from income-generating activity, various types of material support for its students [1].
- For material assistance to students studying full-time at the expense of federal budget allocations, funds are allocated in accordance with the clause of the Regulation on the scholarship fund for state scholarships [2].
- The one-time size of material assistance is determined by the decision of the Scholarship Commission, and the maximum amount of material assistance may not exceed 20 state academic scholarships [2].
- The decision on one-time material assistance is taken by the Commission on the basis of the student's application and of documents confirming the student's need for this assistance, in accordance with Appendix 2 to the Regulation [2].
- Material assistance is given to needy students according to the criteria and the confirming documents of that appendix [2].

## Maternity benefit

The maternity benefit (пособие по беременности и родам) is paid to students studying full-time at the expense of federal budget allocations for the period of the maternity leave: 70 calendar days before the birth, 84 calendar days in the case of a multiple pregnancy, and after the birth 70 calendar days, in the case of a complicated birth 86 calendar days, and in the case of the birth of two or more children 100 calendar days [2].

## Grounds, frequency and documents (Appendix 2)

- Students on academic leave for pregnancy and childbirth or for the care of a child under three years of age, where one or both spouses study full-time at a higher education institution — once: the student's personal application, the conclusion of a medical commission (leave for pregnancy and childbirth) or a certificate from a medical institution on incapacity for work with the period of the leave, and certificates of study at a higher education institution from both parents dated not earlier than 1 month before the application [2].
- Birth of a child — once: the application, a copy of the child's birth certificate and a copy of the student's marriage certificate, with the documents submitted not more than 6 months from the date of the child's birth [2].
- Death (loss) of a parent (legal representative), spouse or child of the student — once, by decision of the University commission: the application, copies of the birth certificate and of the death certificate of that person and, where a spouse died, a copy of the marriage certificate, with the documents submitted not more than 6 months from the date of death [2].
- Treatment and/or restoration of health in connection with a serious illness or an accident — by decision of the University commission: the application and, for students who suffered serious illnesses, injuries or surgery and were treated in a hospital, the referral for treatment or operation or a certificate naming the illness that requires the expenses, the confirming documents (original receipts) and a certificate of the student's income [2].
- Loss of vital personal property as a result of theft, fire or other natural disasters — by decision of the University commission: the application and a copy of the act of the internal affairs bodies or of the Ministry for Emergency Situations in the student's name [2].
- Disabled children, persons disabled of groups I and II and persons disabled since childhood — not more than 2 times a year on submission of the confirming documents: the application and a certificate of disability dated not earlier than 1 month before the application or an indefinite one [2].
- Orphans, children left without parental care and persons from among them — not more than 2 times a year: the application, a document confirming the status of an orphan and, for children left without parental care, the birth certificate and the death certificates of the parents [2].
- A difficult life situation and/or a difficult material situation where the average per capita income of the student's family is below the subsistence minimum set in the corresponding subject of the Russian Federation — once, but not more than 1 time a year: the application, a certificate of the family composition, a certificate of the family income and of the student's own income, a certificate from the employment service on the status of unemployed and a certificate from the place of work of all members of the family [2].
- Children of participants of the special military operation and students who took part in it — once: the application and, for a participant, the document of the participant of the special military operation, and for children a copy of the document confirming participation and a copy of the birth certificate [2].
- Other reasons not provided for by the Regulation — by decision of the University commission: the application and the documents confirming financial difficulties [2].

# Robotics Sector (Сектор робототехники) — place, tasks, functions and rights

## The document

The Regulation on the Robotics Sector of the Integration Department of the Centre for the Development of Industrial Robotics of the Centre for Automation and Robotisation of ANO VO «Innopolis University» regulates the activity of that sector and determines its tasks, functions, rights and duties [1]. The copy on the wiki carries an approval block «УТВЕРЖДЕНО приказом АНО ВО «Университет Иннополис»» with the date and order number left blank, signed by the Director for Development and HR Policy R.F. Valiev [2].

The Sector and its parent bodies are named in full as: the Robotics Sector — the Integration Department — the Centre for the Development of Industrial Robotics — the Centre for Automation and Robotisation [1].

## Place in the structure

- The Sector is a structural subdivision of the Integration Department and belongs to the category of research subdivisions [3].
- It reports directly to the head of the Integration Department [4].
- In its work the Sector is guided by the Constitution of the Russian Federation, the Labour Code of the Russian Federation, Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29 December 2012 «Об образовании в Российской Федерации» and other legal acts regulating the activity of higher education institutions, the University's Charter, decisions of the University's Academic, Supervisory and Operational councils, orders and instructions of the University director, other local acts and this Regulation [5].
- The Sector's structure, headcount and staffing table, and changes to them, are approved by the University director; on the page this rule is numbered 1.5 and printed inside clause 1.4 [2].
- The Sector is headed by a head of the Robotics Sector (руководитель сектора робототехники), hired and dismissed by order of the University director [6].
- Working conditions of the Sector's employees are set by the employment contract concluded with each employee and by the University's internal labour rules [7].
- The rights, duties and degree of responsibility of the Sector's employees are laid down in job descriptions [8].
- Within their competence, the Sector's employees may be involved in work under grants, projects, subsidies and other programmes carried out by the University [9].
- The Regulation and amendments to it are approved by the University director or another person authorised by the director [10].

## Tasks

The Sector's tasks are [2]:

- participation in the commercialisation of the results of the Sector's activity — bringing the results of research, development and technologies of the Centre for the Development of Industrial Robotics into economic circulation by transferring rights on a paid basis, carrying out development on third-party orders or in other ways that generate income [11];
- development and issue of working robotics documentation, both for whole solutions and for individual units and parts, for commercial projects in the field of industrial robotics [12];
- carrying out the robotics part of applied scientific research in the field of industrial robotics [13];
- development of the robotics parts of complexes of scientific and technical solutions in the sphere of industrial robotics [14];
- carrying out research, development and technological work (НИР, ОКР, НИОКТР), industrial projects and grants in the profile of the Centre for the Development of Industrial Robotics, on the Sector's own initiative and under grant support, from budget and non-budget funds [15];
- participation, as part of the Integration Department, in the implementation of state programmes, federal and national projects in the field of industrial robotics and related fields [16];
- popularisation of robotisation and automation of industrial enterprises [17];
- identification of the potential and directions of robotisation and automation of industrial enterprises [18].

## Functions

The Sector performs the following functions [2]:

- step-by-step planning and conduct of robotics development within НИР, ОКР, НИОКТР, projects and grants [19];
- development of scientific research programmes in the field of activity of the Centre for the Development of Industrial Robotics, in the part on robotics and project documentation [20];
- technical and technological audits of industrial enterprises in order to identify their robotisation potential and propose technical solutions [21].

## Rights

In their activity the Sector's employees have the right to [2]:

- request from the University's structural subdivisions the information and materials needed for the work within the Sector's competence [22];
- involve, with the consent of the heads of the University's structural subdivisions, employees of those subdivisions for preparing drafts of local acts and other documents and for developing and carrying out projects of the Sector [23];
- take part in meetings held by the University's management when matters within the Sector's competence are discussed [24];
- put proposals to the University's management to repeal, amend or supplement the University's local acts [25];
- interact with state authorities and bodies of administration, legal entities and subdivisions of the University on matters within the Sector's competence [26].

## Interaction with other subdivisions

- Interaction is determined by the tasks and functions assigned to the Sector by the Regulation [27].
- The Sector has to agree its decisions with the other subdivisions of the Integration Department where those decisions touch their competence and activity [28].
- The Sector may serve as a base for the practical training of the University's students [29].
- Interaction with the Cross-industry Centre for Technology Transfer concerns the legal protection of the results of intellectual activity created on the basis of the Centre for the Development of Industrial Robotics, the exercise, disposal and protection of the University's rights to such results and their effective use and commercialisation [30].
- Interaction with other subdivisions also takes place when preparing drafts of local acts, orders, instructions, contracts and other documents and when preparing opinions and written and oral reports on matters of the Sector's competence [31].

## Reporting

- On the basis of the relevant local acts, orders and instructions the Sector may be responsible, within its activity, for preparing and keeping up to date information, reports and accounts [32].
- The Sector is responsible for preparing reports on НИР, ОКР, НИОКТР, industrial projects and grants and for passing them in time to the University's internal subdivisions [33].
- The Sector reports as required by federal executive authorities, executive authorities of the subjects of the Russian Federation and other bodies, agencies and organisations — in line with obligations under grants (subsidies) provided from the budgets of the budget system of the Russian Federation, and in other cases provided by legislation; in other cases reporting is determined by the contracts concluded with third parties [34].

# Adapted additional general education programmes (ДОП) for learners with health limitations

## Where the rules stand

Learners of the University's additional general education programmes (ДОП) who have health limitations are covered by separate rules in section 7 of the Regulation on the additional general education programme [1].

For such learners the University organises the educational process under an adapted ДОП, taking account of the characteristics of their psycho-physical development [2], and must create special conditions in accordance with the conclusion of a psycho-medical-pedagogical commission and (or) the individual programme of rehabilitation (habilitation) of a disabled person or disabled child [3].

## What special conditions mean

Special conditions mean the conditions of training, upbringing and development of such learners, including the use of special educational programmes and methods, special textbooks, teaching aids and didactic materials, special technical means of teaching for collective and individual use, the services of an assistant (помощник) giving the necessary technical help, group and individual correctional classes, access to the buildings and other conditions without which mastering the programmes is impossible or difficult [4]. The rules of accessibility of organisations delivering adapted general education programmes are determined by the procedure for ensuring accessibility of objects and services in the field of education for disabled persons and for providing them with the necessary help [5].

## Aims of work under an adapted ДОП

Work under an adapted ДОП must take account of the special educational needs of learners of different nosological groups and be aimed at psychological-pedagogical help and rehabilitation (habilitation); at differentiated help, including technical help by an assistant; at the possibility of verbal and non-verbal communication for learners with pronounced communication problems, including by means of alternative or additional communication; at fostering independence in mastering accessible activities; and at forming interest in a particular activity within the ДОП [6].

The content of education and the conditions of organising training and upbringing are determined by the adapted educational programme, and for disabled persons also by the individual rehabilitation programme [7]. The activity may be based on a ДОП adapted where necessary, with specialists in correctional pedagogy and teachers who have completed a corresponding programme of professional retraining or advanced training [8].

## Conditions for particular groups of learners

- visual impairments: tactile information boards with room numbers, names and reference information about the timetable, in enlarged type and relief-linear or Braille type; access to the building for a blind learner using a guide dog, with a place for the dog during the learner's classes; sound beacons to help find the entrance; alternative formats of printed materials (large print or audio files); contrasting marking of the outer steps as anti-slip strips and of transparent door leaves and partitions; special teaching methods connected with showing and demonstrating movements and practical actions; special textbooks, aids and visual didactic means [9];
- hearing impairments: visual duplication of audio reference information about the timetable (equipment able to broadcast subtitles); perception of instructions and speech material on a hearing-visual basis and use of it in independent speech; use of oral, written and dactyl forms of verbal speech according to the learners' speech development [10];
- musculoskeletal disorders: unimpeded access to classrooms, canteens, toilets and other premises and stay in them (ramps, handrails, widened doorways, lifts, local lowering of barrier posts to no more than 0,8 m, special chairs and other devices); a barrier-free architectural and planning environment; the possibility of verbal and non-verbal communication [11];
- severe speech disorders: adaptation of theoretical material in text/audio/video format to the learners' speech possibilities; alternative communication means, including communicators, special tablets, buttons, communication programmes and boards; prevalence of demonstration and visual-sample methods at the initial period; stimulation of speech activity; clear instructions; normative speech samples; expansion of vocabulary [12];
- autism spectrum disorders: use of visual schedules [13];
- delayed mental development: additional visual support in the form of semantic reference points, a special structure of the lesson preventing overload, special methods, differentiation of requirements, and an optimal regime of physical load with a flexible approach to its kinds [14];
- intellectual disabilities: specially equipped «rest zones» for relieving sensory and emotional overload, tutor support for learners with severe multiple developmental disorders, account of the learners' characteristics and a combination of teaching methods with prevalence of practical methods and repeated repetition [15].

## Groups and study materials

Classes with learners with health limitations may be organised both together with other learners and in separate groups, including under an individual curriculum [16]. The numerical composition of a group may be reduced when such learners are included in it [17]. Under an adapted ДОП such learners receive free of charge special textbooks, teaching aids and other educational literature, as well as the services of sign-language interpreters and tactile sign-language interpreters [18].

# Cost of accommodation in the residential complex from 1 September 2025 (order of 29 August 2025), as amended in January 2026

## The order and what it approves

The order of 29 August 2025 № ОД/АД-01/2025/08/29 approves, from 1 September 2025, the cost of accommodation services in the residential complex of the University (Жилой комплекс АНО ВО «Университет Иннополис») situated in the Republic of Tatarstan, Verkhneuslonsky municipal district, the city of Innopolis, Universitetskaya street 1, buildings 1–7 — under Annex 1 for the general rates and under Annex 2 for particular categories of persons [1]. The order repeals, from 1 September 2025, the order of 31 January 2025 № ОД/АД-04/2025/01/31 on the same subject, and places control over execution on the deputy director for infrastructure A.M. Ermolchev; it is signed by the director I.I. Bariev [1]. The stated purpose is to update the document that determines how accommodation services in the residential complex are priced [1].

## Room rates (Annex 1)

The price of a room per day, in roubles [1]:

- buildings 1–4: two-bed one-room — 3 600; two-bed one-room comfort — 4 200; two-bed two-room comfort — 5 000; four-bed two-room — 7 200; five-bed three-room — 9 000; an additional bed of any category (folding bed) — 1 200 [1]
- buildings 5–7: two-bed one-room — 4 200; two-bed two-room comfort with an additional place — 5 800; four-bed two-room — 8 000; two-bed two-room for persons with limited mobility (МГН) — 4 200; an additional bed of any category (folding bed) — 1 200 [1]

The check-in time (расчетный час) is 14:00 and the check-out time 12:00 local time [1].

The price includes maintenance cleaning of the rooms, use of the refrigerator, television, microwave oven and kettle where they are in the room, of the air conditioner in summer, of the Wi-Fi zone, the car park and the round-the-clock laundry room, and the provision of a set of bed linen, towels and a hygiene kit [1].

## Bed-place rates for particular categories (Annex 2)

The price of a bed-place (койко-место) per day, in roubles [1]:

- buildings 1–4: two-bed one-room — 1 800; two-bed two-room — 2 200; four-bed two-room — 1 800; five-bed three-room — 1 800 [1]
- buildings 5–7: two-bed one-room — 2 100; four-bed two-room — 2 000 [1]
- the figure 1 800 for a bed-place in a five-bed three-room room stands in the table's heading row for buildings 5–7 in the wiki copy, so what it applies to cannot be read with certainty [1]

What the bed-place price includes is the same, except that only a set of bed linen and towels is mentioned, without the hygiene kit [1].

## Common rules of Annexes 1 and 2

- A child aged up to and including 7 (seven) years is accommodated free of charge when staying together with a parent without a separate place being provided [1]
- Where accommodation starts before the check-in time, the surcharge is 25 % of the price for one day between 06:00 and the check-in time, and 100 % between 00:00 and 06:00, according to the chosen category and type of accommodation [1]
- Where the room is left after the check-out time, the surcharge is 50 % of the price for one day between the check-out time and 19:00, and 100 % between 19:00 and 00:00 [1]
- Where a room is taken for 20 (twenty) or more days without interruption, a discount of 30 % is given [1]

## Discounts of Annex 2

The following categories of persons are given a discount on accommodation services [1]:

- students of the University — a discount of 90 % of the accommodation price for the "bed-place" type of accommodation for the period of study [1]
- persons who have completed their studies at the University (graduates) — a discount of 72 % of the price when accommodated by the "bed-place" type during the first year after the end of their studies [1]
- employees of the University — in a two-bed one-room room, a discount for each bed-place of 90 % of the accommodation price, subject to the conditions stated in the Procedure for agreeing, on the corporate portal «Битрикс24», requests for temporary accommodation of University employees while they await rented housing or within the framework of official necessity, as well as of invited persons [1]
- students and employees accommodated together with a husband or wife — a discount for the second bed-place, for the spouse, of 80 % of the accommodation price [1]
- students and employees accommodated together with a husband or wife and children — a discount for the spouse's bed-place of 70 %, minor children being accommodated together with their parents on additional places in a room of the corresponding capacity [1]
- employees of АО «Иннополис» — in a two-bed one-room room, a discount for each bed-place of 90 % of the accommodation price [1]

During an academic leave (академический отпуск) no discount on accommodation services is given to the students of the University named in clause 4 of Annex 2 [1].

## Amendments made by the order of 21 January 2026

The order of 21 January 2026 № ОД/АД-01/2026/01/21 amends the order of 29 August 2025 «Об утверждении стоимости услуг размещения в жилом комплексе»; it is signed by the director D.V. Vandyukov, and control over its execution is placed on the deputy director for project activity and infrastructure E.V. Danilov [2]. The stated purpose is to bring up to date the document that determines how accommodation services in the residential complex at Universitetskaya street 1, buildings 1–7 are priced [2].

- The order of 24 January 2024 № ОД/АД-02/2024/01/24 on the cost of accommodation services in the residential complex for the University's profile shifts (профильные смены) is repealed from 1 February 2026 [2].
- Clause 8 is added to Annex 1 with categories of persons who are given a differentiated cost: for learners under the University's additional general education programmes (participants of the University's profile shifts) and for teaching staff invited for the period of the profile shifts, in a two-bed room of any category the room costs 1 780 roubles per day and a bed-place 890 roubles per day [2].
- The same clause 8 sets, for learners of secondary vocational education programmes of the University, a bed-place price of 190 roubles per day in a room of any category [2].
- The second paragraph of clause 4 of Annex 2 is restated: the discount of 90 % of the accommodation price for the "bed-place" type for the period of study is given to learners of higher education programmes — bachelor's programmes, master's programmes and programmes of training of scientific-pedagogical staff in aspirantura [2]; the 2025 order stated this discount for "students of the University" without enumerating those programmes [1].
- The third paragraph of clause 4 of Annex 2 is restated: graduates are given a discount of 72 % of the price for accommodation of the "bed-place" type during the first three months after the end of their studies [2]; the 2025 order gave the 72 % discount during the first year after the end of studies [1].
- The changes of clauses 2.1 (the new clause 8) and 2.2 (the restated 90 % discount) take effect on 1 February 2026, and the change of clause 2.3 (the graduates' discount) takes effect on 1 June 2026 [2].

The amending order changes only these points; the other rates and discounts of Annexes 1 and 2 of the 2025 order are not mentioned in it [2].

# Sector for Registration and Control of Documents (Сектор регистрации и контроля документов) — place, tasks, functions and rights

## The document and the Sector's place in the structure

The Regulation on the Sector for Registration and Control of Documents (Положение о Секторе регистрации и контроля документов) was approved by order of 27 March 2026 № ОД/АД-03/2026/03/27, signed by the Director for Development and HR Policy R.F. Valiev [1]. The Regulation regulates the activity of the Sector for Registration and Control of Documents of the Documentation Department (Департамент документационного обеспечения управленческой деятельности) of ANO VO «Innopolis University» and determines its tasks, functions, rights and duties [2].

- The Sector is a structural subdivision of the Documentation Department and belongs to the category «иные структурные подразделения» [3].
- The Sector reports directly to the head of the Documentation Department [4].
- In its activity the Sector follows the Constitution of the Russian Federation, the Labour Code of the Russian Federation, Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29 December 2012 «Об образовании в Российской Федерации» and other legislative and normative legal acts regulating the activity of higher education institutions, the University's Charter, decisions of the Academic, Supervisory and Operational councils, orders and instructions of the University's director, other local normative acts and this Regulation [5].
- The structure, the number of staff and the staffing table of the Sector, and changes to them, are approved by the director of the University [1].
- The Sector is headed by the head of the Sector for Registration and Control of Documents, hired and dismissed by order of the director of the University [6].
- The working conditions of the Sector's employees are determined by employment contracts concluded with each employee, by the University's internal labour regulations and by other local normative acts [7].
- The rights, duties and degree of responsibility of the Sector's employees are established by job descriptions [8].
- Employees of the Sector may, within their competences, be involved in work under grants, projects, subsidies and other programmes implemented by the University [9].
- The Regulation and additions and changes to it are approved by order of the director of the University or of another person authorised by him [10].

## Tasks

The Sector's tasks are [11], [12], [13], [14], [15]:

- prompt provision of the acceptance, registration, recording and distribution of incoming and outgoing correspondence and of the University's internal documents on paper and electronic carriers, using the corporate portal «Битрикс», the Unified interdepartmental system of electronic document flow of the Republic of Tatarstan (МЭДО), Почта России, express mail and e-mail [11];
- centralised control of the execution of documents and of the instructions of the University's management with the use of the corporate portal «Битрикс», including monitoring of deadlines and of the quality of execution and the preparation of analytical reporting [12];
- organising the recording and storage of the documents of current office work, including documents of restricted access (ДСП, КТ/КИ), and their preparation for transfer to the archive in accordance with the established requirements [13];
- ensuring uniformity of the procedures of registration, recording and control of documents in the University's structural subdivisions and giving them methodological and practical help, including on work in the corporate portal «Битрикс» [14];
- taking part in the automation of documentation processes as regards the introduction and development of the corporate portal «Битрикс» system, the testing of new modules and the optimisation of business processes [15].

## Functions

The Sector carries out the following functions:

- accepting, processing, registering and recording outgoing and incoming correspondence received through МЭДО, the corporate e-mail university@innopolis.ru, express mail and Почта России, and also organisational and administrative documents (orders, instructions), including those marked «Для служебного пользования» (ДСП) and «Коммерческая тайна/Конфиденциальная информация» (КТ/КИ), on the corporate portal «Битрикс» and on paper [16]
- sending registered documents for execution to structural subdivisions and responsible officials in accordance with the University's document routes and the management's resolutions [17]
- keeping the journals (registers) of registration of the documents named in п. 3.1, ensuring completeness, accuracy and timeliness of the data entered [18]
- putting documents and instructions under control with the use of the corporate portal «Битрикс», including setting control deadlines for execution [19]
- monitoring compliance with the deadlines for the execution of documents with the tools of the corporate portal «Битрикс» and of МЭДО, and reminding executors of expiring deadlines [20]
- checking the quality of the execution of documents for completeness and compliance with the instruction, and where necessary returning documents for revision [21]
- forming summary reports on the execution of documents and control instructions to be provided to the University's management [22]
- analysing the results of executive discipline and preparing proposals to improve it [23]
- forming the files of the Documentation Department in accordance with the approved nomenclature of files, ensuring the correct grouping, systematisation and execution of documents [24]
- ensuring the storage of materials of current office work, including documents of restricted access, with observance of the established access regime [25]
- transferring files formed and executed in the established order to the University's archive within the established deadlines [26]
- ensuring the storage of the University's main round seal and its use in the established order: putting the seal impression on the originals of documents drawn up for signature by the director of the University and by officials authorised by him, certifying copies of documents, and sealing multi-page documents (including bound documents and files) in accordance with the requirements of legislation and the University's local normative acts [27]
- carrying out information and reference work on documents in office work and providing information on the status, deadlines and results of the consideration of documents at the requests of structural subdivisions and outside organisations, within the Sector's competence [28]
- advising the University's employees on the registration of documents, on work in the corporate portal «Битрикс» and in МЭДО, on compliance with execution deadlines and on the rules for drawing up documents [29]
- taking part in the optimisation of the University's document-flow business processes and developing proposals to improve the procedures of registration, control and recording of documents [30]
- taking part in testing new modules and functions of the corporate portal «Битрикс» and of МЭДО and preparing proposals for their revision [31]
- taking part in preparing drafts of local normative acts and methodological documents establishing rules and recommendations on the organisation of office work and electronic document flow [32]
- ensuring compliance with the established procedure for working with documents containing personal data and with documents marked «Для служебного пользования» (ДСП) and «Коммерческая тайна/Конфиденциальная информация» (КТ/КИ) [33]

## Rights

In carrying out their activity the Sector's employees have the right to:

- request from the University's structural subdivisions the information and materials needed for the work within the Sector's competence [34]
- with the consent of the heads of the University's structural subdivisions, involve the employees of those subdivisions in preparing drafts of local acts and other documents and in developing and carrying out measures conducted by the Sector [35]
- take part in meetings held by the University's management when discussing questions whose solution falls within the Sector's competence [36]
- put before the University's management proposals to cancel, amend or supplement the University's local acts [37]
- interact with bodies of state authority and administration, legal entities and the University's subdivisions on questions within the Sector's competence [38]
- check the organisation and conduct of office work in all the University's structural subdivisions, report the results of the checks to the deputy director for business-process optimisation, internal control and audit and take measures to eliminate the violations found [39]
- require officials presenting documents for the seal impression to observe the established procedure for drawing up documents and to have the necessary signatures, visas and requisites confirming the lawfulness of certifying the document with the seal [40]
- return without execution documents presented for the seal impression where they do not meet the established drafting requirements, where the necessary signatures are absent or where the person asking for certification exceeds his powers [41]
- require the University's employees to comply with the established rules for working with documents and for drawing up the University's documents properly (outgoing letters, orders etc.) and not to accept from executors documents drawn up improperly [42]
- have access to all electronic catalogues of the corporate portal «Битрикс» [43]
- recommend restricting the rights and the level of access to the information base of the automated documentation system of the corporate portal «Битрикс» for various categories of employees — users of the system [44]

## Interaction with other subdivisions

- The Sector interacts with the University's management and with all structural subdivisions on the organisation and conduct of document flow, on keeping the department's files in accordance with the University's consolidated nomenclature of files and on transferring documents to the University's archive [45].
- Interaction with all structural subdivisions is carried out on preparing drafts of local acts, orders, instructions, contracts and other documents; on preparing conclusions and written and oral references on questions within the Sector's competence arising in practical activity; and on electronic document flow with the use of the corporate portal «Битрикс» and МЭДО [46].
- The Sector may be a base for the practical training of the University's students [47].

## Reporting

On the basis of the relevant local acts, orders and instructions the Sector may, within its activity, be responsible for preparing and keeping up to date information, references and reports [48].

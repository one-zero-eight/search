# Attending events not provided by the curriculum (Порядок посещения мероприятий, 2021)

## The document and its basis

The Порядок посещения обучающимися АНО ВО «Университет Иннополис» по своему выбору мероприятий, проводимых в Университете и не предусмотренных учебным планом was approved by order № ОД/АД-03/2021/03/02 signed by K.V. Semenikhin (Иннополис, 2021) [1]. It establishes the rules for holding events conducted at the University and not provided by the curriculum, and the rights, duties and responsibility of the University and of learners — pupils, students, aspirants, listeners and other learners [1]. In accordance with part 4 of article 34 of Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29.12.2012 «Об образовании в Российской Федерации», the University ensures that learners can exercise their right to attend, by their own choice, events held at the University that the curriculum does not provide [1].

## What counts as such an event, and why it is held

Events not provided by the curriculum are holidays, contests, theme evenings, festivals, sports competitions, exhibitions and other events whose procedure is not established by other local normative acts of the University [1].

They are held for [2]:

- the personal development of learners, the formation of the professional culture of future specialists, their civic position, worldview and social activity [3];
- civil-patriotic, aesthetic, spiritual and physical upbringing and the professional orientation of learners [4];
- organising the active rest of learners and creating conditions for the fullest disclosure of their creative potential [5];
- satisfying the cognitive interests of learners that go beyond the educational programme they have chosen [6].

Their tasks are [1]:

- creating conditions for the realisation of learners' abilities, inclinations and interests [1];
- promoting scientific knowledge, developing professional interest, self-determination and professional orientation [1];
- forming learners' professional and general competences [1];
- individualising learners' educational trajectories [1].

Involving learners in such events without their consent, and minor learners without the consent of their parents (legal representatives), is not allowed; participation is a right, not a duty of learners [7].

## Who organises an event

The organiser is the structural subdivision of the University that initiates the event; an event may be held on the initiative of several structural subdivisions [1]. The responsible person is an employee of the organising subdivision appointed by its head to control compliance with the procedure for holding the event [1]. An order of the University may be issued to establish the procedure for holding an event [1]. The organiser determines the procedure for deciding which learners are admitted to the event, the event programme, its start and end time and any special requirements, and brings them to learners' knowledge in advance [1].

The presence of persons who are not learners is allowed in accordance with the University's local normative acts [1].

## Rules during events

- Participation while in a state of alcoholic or narcotic intoxication is not allowed; smoking, including electronic cigarettes, on the University's territory is not allowed [1].
- During events learners must observe the rules of safety engineering and fire safety, the norms of the Rules of internal order for students, this Procedure and other local normative acts [1].
- Learners are forbidden to disrupt the order of an event by their actions or to contribute to its disruption [1].
- Organisers ensure compliance with age restrictions in accordance with Russian legislation and the University's local normative acts [1].

## Duties of learners attending

Learners who are visitors of events must [8]:

- observe the legislation of the Russian Federation, the Charter and the University's local normative acts [9];
- respect the honour and dignity of other visitors and participants [10];
- treat with care the premises, property and equipment of the organisers and of the University [11];
- maintain cleanliness and order in the premises and on the University's territory [12];
- comply with the lawful demands of the University's responsible persons [13];
- immediately inform the responsible persons about smoke or fires and about suspicious or abandoned objects [14];
- on receiving information about evacuation, act according to the instructions of the responsible persons, staying calm and not creating panic [15].

## What learners are forbidden to do

Learners are forbidden [16]:

- to attend events in dirty, unkempt or beach clothing and footwear [17];
- to bring and (or) consume alcoholic drinks and narcotic substances, to smoke, including electronic cigarettes [18];
- to bring weapons, flammable, explosive, pyrotechnic, poisonous and odorous substances, piercing and cutting objects, glassware, plastic bottles and gas canisters [19];
- to bring bulky things, large bags and briefcases into the room where the event is held [20];
- to bring animals and birds [21];
- to damage the decoration elements and equipment of the event [22];
- to make inscriptions on buildings and on the University's adjacent territory [23];
- to use the University's territory for commercial, advertising or other similar activity [24];
- to conduct political or other agitation, to incite racial, religious or national hatred, to insult visitors and participants of the event [25].

Learners and other visitors who have caused damage to the University must compensate it and bear the liability provided by Russian legislation [25].

## The University's rights and duties

When holding events the University may [1]:

- set age restrictions on attendance and take administrative and organisational measures and technical, software and hardware means of protecting children from information harmful to their health and (or) development [26];
- set restrictions on photo, video and audio recording [27];
- set restrictions on the use of mobile communication [28];
- remove from events persons who have violated the requirements of the Procedure [29];
- suspend or stop an event in case of unlawful actions by learners, including non-observance or violation of the Procedure [30].

The University's responsible persons must be personally present at the event, control compliance by the learners attending it and take other necessary measures [31].

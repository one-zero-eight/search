# Internal system of quality assessment of education (ВСОКО) — the Regulation of 24 December 2025

## The document

The Положение о внутренней системе оценки качества образования АНО ВО «Университет Иннополис» was approved by order of the University of 24 December 2025 № ОД/АД‐01/2025/12/24, signed by the Director D.V. Vandyukov, and is dated Innopolis, 2025 [1]. It determines the work of the internal system of quality assessment of education at the University [2], and its requirements are obligatory for all structural units that take part in organising educational activity [3].

It was developed in accordance with Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29 December 2012 [4].

It also rests on the Government's Decree No. 662 of 05.08.2013 on monitoring the education system [5]; on Ministry of Science and Higher Education orders No. 462 of 14.06.2013 on the procedure of self-assessment [6] and No. 1324 of 10.12.2013 on the indicators of an organisation subject to self-assessment [7]; on the orders on the indicators of the quality of conditions of educational activity for general, secondary vocational, higher and additional professional education [8], [9], [10]; on the orders on accreditation indicators for secondary vocational [11] and higher education [12]; on the joint order on accreditation monitoring of 24.04.2023 [13]; on the federal state educational standards and federal state requirements [14]; on the Устав [15]; and on other acts [16].

The University ensures the development and implementation of the system, and the recording and further use of the results obtained [17].

## Terms

- The quality of education is the complex characteristic of educational activity and of the preparation of a student, expressing the degree of their correspondence to the standards and (or) to the needs of the person in whose interests the activity is carried out, including the degree of achievement of the planned results of the educational programme [18].
- The assessment of the quality of education is the determination, by diagnostic and assessment procedures, of the degree of correspondence of educational activity and of the results of preparing students to normative requirements and to social and personal expectations [19].
- The internal system of quality assessment of education is the set of elements regulated by the University that allow it to assess and analyse the processes and results of its work in the educational, research and upbringing spheres and to develop recommendations for improving quality [20].

## Who coordinates and who controls

The coordinating function in the internal system of quality assessment is performed by the Deputy Director of the University [21]; the controlling function is performed by the Centre for Control of Educational Activity [22].

## Aims

The aims of the system are [23]:

- to form a single system reflecting the current state of the educational process, identifying the factors influencing the quality of education and controlling their state [24];
- to obtain objective information on the functioning and development of the University and on the trends and causes of changes in that information [25];
- to improve the quality of preparing students and the conditions of implementing educational programmes, including their resourcing and content [26];
- to prepare, generalise and give the University's management the information needed for justified and timely management decisions on improving education [27];
- to raise the effectiveness of managing the educational process [28];
- to give all participants of relations in education and the public reliable information on the quality of education at the University [29].

## Tasks

The tasks of the system are [30]:

- analysing how the requirements of Russian legislation on education and of the University's local acts are fulfilled [31];
- developing and introducing an effective system of control, analysis and assessment of the quality of preparing students and of the education the University provides [32];
- analysing and examining educational programmes [33];
- assessing the staffing of the educational process [34];
- analysing the conditions of organising the educational process and the effectiveness of the programmes, methods and technologies used [35];
- assessing the quality of preparing students on the basis of ongoing assessment, interim assessment and final (state final) attestation [36];
- analysing the state and use of material and technical support and controlling its development [37];
- analysing the assessments given by students, scientific-pedagogical workers and employers of the content, organisation and quality of the educational process [38];
- analysing the employment of graduates and their demand on the labour market [39];
- determining the degree of correspondence of the conditions of the educational process to the standards, accreditation indicators, accreditation monitoring indicators and educational programmes, taking account of the requests of students and employers [40].

## Principles

The system rests on objectivity, reliability, completeness and systemness of information about the quality of education; openness and transparency of the assessment procedures; availability of information about the state and quality of education to participants of relations in education and to the public; and orientation towards improving and developing educational activity [41].

## What the system includes and what it assesses

The system includes the assessment of results and the quality of the conditions of education: the assessment of results fixes the resultant side of education and assesses whether the graduate's competences meet the needs and expectations of society and the employer, and the quality of the conditions fixes the conditions (factors) of the educational process that allow the planned result to be achieved [42].

The objects of the system are [43]:

- the academic performance and achievements of students [44];
- the quality of the educational services provided [45];
- the quality of the admission of applicants [46];
- the quality of the conditions of implementing the educational process [47];
- the quality of the University's activity in the direction corresponding to the assessment procedure [48].

The main subjects taking part in the system are the Rector, the Centre for Control of Educational Activity, the structural units and officials taking part in educational activity, applicants, students and graduates, the scientific-pedagogical workers, and employers and their associations and social partners [49]. Other structural units, officials and physical or legal persons may be involved under civil-law contracts [50]. Assessment is carried out at all levels and types of education implemented at the University [51].

## Types of assessment

The system may use the following types of assessment [52]:

- a complex assessment, which analyses and assesses the work of all structural units involved in educational activity and is carried out according to an order of the Director [53];
- a particular assessment, which analyses specific types of educational activity or separate conditions of its implementation; for it an order of the Director approves the plan-schedule of measures for the calendar year, determining the forms of assessment procedures, the deadlines and the responsible structural units [54];
- an operative assessment, which is joint work with structural units in the directions where non-compliance was found in the fulfilment of the normative documents regulating the organisation of educational activity [55];
- self-assessment (самообследование), carried out annually according to an order of the Director in all directions of the University's activity and drawn up as a self-assessment report [56].

## Data sources and the steps of an assessment

The data sources are educational statistics (monitoring results, university rankings); the results of ongoing assessment, interim and final (state final) attestation; information on educational activity given by structural units for assessment procedures; the results of surveys of students, of pedagogical and scientific workers and of employers on satisfaction; data from the analysis of material-technical, methodological, informational, staffing and financial provision; and other data needed for assessment procedures [57].

An assessment procedure consists of determining the object, type, deadlines and responsible executors; developing the instruments (unless approved by this Regulation or other local acts); collecting data; processing, analysing and interpreting the data; preparing information and analytical materials; and using the results to improve the educational process [58].

The data are interpreted by educational programme where the form of the procedure allows separate analysis by programme [59].

## Forms of assessment procedures

The assessment is carried out by monitoring the admission campaign; assessing the academic performance and achievements of students; assessing the quality of the staffing of the educational process; conducting surveys; and assessing the organisation of the educational process [60].

Monitoring the admission campaign includes an audit of the results of the unified state examination — combining the results of the state final attestation of school graduates with entrance examinations at the University in order to form a contingent able to master the programmes [61]. It also includes an audit of the organisation of the admission of applicants, assessing the fulfilment of the Admission Rules, the timely publication of admission information on the official site and in the federal information system and the procedure of forming applicants' personal files [62].

Assessing the academic performance and achievements of students includes [63]:

- an audit of the results of ongoing assessment, assessing the stage-by-stage mastering of disciplines (modules) on the results placed in the LMS Moodle [64];
- an audit of the results of interim assessment, establishing the dynamics of the students' performance by programme, course or discipline (module) and practice [65];
- an audit of the fulfilment by aspirants of the individual plan of research activity, assessing the stage-by-stage mastering of the aspirantura programme, including the preparation of the dissertation for defence [66];
- an audit of the results of final (state final) attestation, establishing the correspondence of the graduates' preparation to the standards and the formation of all the competences provided by the programmes [67];
- an audit of students' personal achievements and of the statistics of wins in olympiads and of participation in research-practical and research conferences, competitions and contests [68].

Assessing the quality of staffing includes an audit of the staffing conditions against the standards and qualification requirements — the qualification of pedagogical workers, the share of workers carrying out scientific, methodological or practical work corresponding to the profile of the discipline, the share of workers who are heads or employees of other organisations in the professional sphere for which the graduates are prepared, and the share of workers with a degree and (or) academic rank [69]. It also includes an audit of the completion by pedagogical workers of advanced training and (or) professional retraining programmes [70].

Conducting surveys includes the following procedures [71]:

- a survey of pedagogical and scientific workers about their satisfaction with the conditions and organisation of educational activity within the implementation of educational programmes, under appendix 1 [72];
- a survey of students (their legal representatives) about their satisfaction with the conditions, content, organisation and quality of the educational process as a whole and of individual subjects, courses, disciplines (modules) and practices, under appendix 2 [73];
- a survey of employers assessing the quality of the preparation of students and graduates, under appendix 3, which also makes it possible to determine the degree of the employers' satisfaction with the level of professional competences, practical skills and abilities and to update the curricula, the work programmes of disciplines (modules) and the practice programmes on the results [74].

Assessing the organisation of the educational process includes audits of educational programmes against legislation and local acts; of the procedure of organising educational activity under the programmes; of the organisation and implementation of students' practice; of the procedure of final (state final) attestation and of issuing documents of education and (or) qualification; of the procedure of providing paid educational services; of the organisation of awarding scholarships; of the process of transfer of students to and from the University; of compliance with accreditation indicators; and of compliance with licensing indicators, as well as self-assessment, the collection and analysis of data for statistical reporting and the collection and analysis of information for preparing for licensing, state accreditation, professional-public accreditation and independent assessment of quality [75].

By decision of the Director, the Rector or the head of the Centre for Control of Educational Activity the list of assessment procedures may be supplemented, changed or refined when the plan-schedule of measures for the calendar year is drafted [76].

## Publication and use of results

The results are systematically brought to the attention of the University's management, employees and students, as well as to the Supervisory Council and the public, by publication on the official site in the section «Сведения об образовательной организации», subsection «Внутренняя система оценки качества образования» [77]. They may be used for preparing information and analytical materials, improving the educational process, correcting the plan-schedule of measures for the calendar year and rewarding the University's employees [78].

## Questionnaires

The Regulation contains questionnaires for pedagogical and scientific workers (appendix 1), for students (appendix 2) and for employers (appendix 3) [1]. The employer questionnaire asks for the full name of the enterprise, the level of education of the specialists who studied at the University and the programmes they studied on, scores from 1 to 10 of the level of professional general-theoretical preparation, of basic knowledge and skills, of practical knowledge and skills, of command of a foreign language, of computer skills and knowledge of the programmes needed at work, of ability to work in a team, of ability to present oneself and the results of one's work, of focus on career growth and professional development, of personnel management skills, of readiness and ability for further learning, of ability to perceive and analyse new information and develop new ideas, of erudition and general culture and of awareness in related fields of the speciality, and an overall assessment of the quality of the preparation of students and graduates [1].

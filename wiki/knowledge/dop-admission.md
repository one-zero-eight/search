# Admission to additional general education programmes (ДОП) — the Rules of 21 May 2025

## The document and whom it covers

The Rules of admission to studies under additional general education programmes (Правила приема на обучение по дополнительным общеобразовательным программам) of ANO VO «Innopolis University» were approved by order of the University of 21 May 2025 № ОД/АД-02/2025/05/21 and signed by the deputy director M.N. Obraztsova [1].

They regulate the admission of citizens of the Russian Federation, foreign citizens and stateless persons — including persons who are disabled and persons with health limitations (Поступающие) — to studies under additional general education programmes at the University [2]. The wiki copy is a draft made from the text layer of the PDF [1].

Additional general education programmes are divided into general development (общеразвивающие) and pre-professional (предпрофессиональные) programmes; general development programmes are delivered both for children and for adults, and pre-professional programmes in the field of art, physical culture and sport are delivered for children [3].

Admission within programmes and projects approved by the President of the Russian Federation and the Government of the Russian Federation is carried out in accordance with legislation on education with the particularities provided for by those programmes and projects [4].

## The particulars of a programme (Специфика образовательной программы)

The specifics of the programmes being delivered and the particularities provided for by programmes and projects that differ from the requirements of these Rules (together — the Specifics of the educational programme) are reflected in the text of the additional general education programme, and (or) in the texts of programmes and projects, and (or) in the text of the contract on education [5]. Several rules below apply "unless otherwise provided by the Specifics of the educational programme".

## Who may be admitted and on what basis

- Any persons are admitted to mastering additional general education programmes without requirements being imposed on their level of education, unless otherwise provided by the Specifics of the educational programme [6].
- Admission is carried out without entrance examinations and without a competition, on the basis of an application of the person entering on study, or of a parent (legal representative) of a minor person entering on study (the Legal representative), unless otherwise provided by the Specifics of the educational programme [7].
- The number of places for admission is not limited, unless otherwise provided by the Specifics of the educational programme [8].
- Admission is conducted on the principles of equal conditions of admission for all Поступающие [9].
- Admission is carried out throughout the whole calendar year [10].
- Studies under additional general education programmes are provided on the basis of a contract on education concluded with the Поступающий (the Legal representative) and (or) with a natural or legal person that undertakes to pay for the training of the person being admitted [11].
- The organisation of admission to a particular additional general education programme is carried out by the structural unit or official of the University responsible for the delivery of that programme [12].
- Personal data of the Поступающие and (or) Legal representatives obtained in connection with admission are transferred, processed, stored and provided in accordance with the requirements of Russian legislation on personal data [13].

## Informing about admission

Information about admission is placed on information and telecommunications networks, including the official website of the University on the Internet, or in any other accessible way that does not contradict the current legislation of the Russian Federation [14].

The University familiarises the Поступающий and (or) the Legal representatives with its Charter, with the information on the date of issue and the registration number of the licence for educational activity, with the information on the date of issue and the registration number of the state accreditation of educational activity under the programmes being delivered, with the educational programmes and other documents regulating the organisation and carrying out of educational activity and the rights and duties of learners [15]. This familiarisation is done by placing those documents on the official website of the University, and the fact of familiarisation is recorded in the application for admission or in other documents provided for by the Specifics of the educational programme [16].

## The application and the documents

The Поступающий (the Legal representative) submits an application for admission together with the necessary documents (the Documents necessary for admission), in the forms given in annex 1 and annex 2 to the Rules [17]. Annex 1 is the form of the application from the person entering on study and annex 2 the form of the application from the parent (legal representative); annex 3 and annex 4 are the corresponding forms of the application to withdraw the submitted documents [1].

The application certifies by signature (including electronic signature) that the applicant is aware of the need to state reliable information in the application and to submit documents within the deadlines set by the Rules [18], and that the Поступающий (the Legal representative) has been familiarised with the rules of admission approved by the University itself and with the documents and information named in part 2 of article 55 of Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29 December 2012 «Об образовании в Российской Федерации» [19].

Where the Specifics of the educational programme so provide, the application for admission may be replaced by another document or action of the Поступающий (the Legal representative) expressing the intention to enter on study and containing information different from that named in clauses 3.2.1–3.2.2 [20].

On submitting the application the Поступающий (the Legal representative) presents [21]:

- a document certifying identity and citizenship — including a passport of a citizen of the Russian Federation certifying identity outside the territory of Russia — or a birth certificate (for citizens of the Russian Federation under 14 years of age) [22];
- where special conditions have to be created — a document confirming disability or health limitations requiring the creation of such conditions [23];
- other documents, at the discretion of the Поступающий (the Legal representative) [24].

Where the Specifics of the educational programme so provide, the list of attached documents may differ from the list in clauses 3.4.1–3.4.3 [25].

## How the documents are submitted

The Documents necessary for admission are submitted to the University in one of the following ways [26]:

- in person by the Поступающий (a trusted person) and (or) the Legal representative [27];
- sent through operators of general postal communication [28];
- sent through the services of the electronic information and educational environment of the University [29];
- sent by electronic mail [30];
- in other ways provided for by the Specifics of the educational programme [31].

The documents must be submitted to the University before the start of delivery of the additional general education programme, unless otherwise provided by the Specifics of the educational programme; where they are sent by post, the date of their sending through the postal operators is taken as the date of submission [32].

If the Documents necessary for admission are not submitted within the established deadlines, or if unreliable information is stated in them, the student is expelled from the University because a violation of the admission procedure has been established which, through the fault of the student, led to his or her unlawful enrolment [33].

## Language, translation, legalisation and verification

- The application is submitted in Russian, and may also be submitted in a foreign language where this is provided for by the Specifics of the educational programme [34].
- Documents made in a foreign language must be translated into Russian, unless otherwise provided by an international treaty of the Russian Federation [35].
- Documents obtained in a foreign state must be legalised, unless otherwise provided by an international treaty of the Russian Federation or by the legislation of the Russian Federation [36].
- The University may verify the reliability of the information stated in the application and the authenticity of the documents submitted, including by applying to the relevant state information systems, state (municipal) bodies and organisations [37].

## Withdrawal of documents

Before the start of delivery of the additional general education programme the Поступающий (the Legal representative) has the right to submit an application to withdraw the submitted documents, in the forms of annex 3 and annex 4 to the Rules [38]. On withdrawal the Поступающий is excluded from the lists of persons who have submitted documents and from the lists of Поступающие and is not subject to enrolment (is excluded from among those enrolled) [38].

## Enrolment

- Enrolment is preceded by the conclusion of a contract on education with the Поступающий (the Legal representative) and (or) with a natural or legal person that undertakes to pay for the training; the form of the contract is approved by order of the director of the University or of another person authorised by the director, and the contract may also be concluded in the procedure provided by article 428 of the Civil Code of the Russian Federation where the form of the contract placed on the University's websites expressly provides that possibility [39].
- Enrolment is formalised by order of the director of the University or of another person authorised by the director, issued not later than the date of the start of studies under the additional general education programme [40].
- The learner is considered enrolled from the date of issue of the enrolment order [41].

## Accessibility of admission for disabled persons and persons with health limitations

For the accessibility of admission of persons with health limitations, disabled children and disabled persons, the University ensures the following [42]:

- for persons with visual impairments: adaptation of the official website to the special needs of visually impaired persons; placement, in places accessible to blind or partially sighted persons and in an adapted form, of reference information in large relief-contrast type on a white or yellow background, duplicated in Braille; the presence of an assistant giving the necessary help; the issue of alternative formats of printed materials; and access for a blind person using a guide dog to the University building, which has a place for the guide dog [43];
- for persons with hearing impairments: duplication of sound reference information (installation of monitors able to broadcast subtitles) and provision of proper sound means of reproducing information [44];
- for persons with disorders of the musculoskeletal system: material and technical conditions providing the possibility of unimpeded access to classrooms, canteens, toilets and other premises of the University and of staying in them [45].

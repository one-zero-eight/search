# Remote interim assessment

## The document

The Порядок determines the application of distance educational technologies in the interim assessment of higher education programmes — bachelor's and master's programmes at ANO VO «Innopolis University» [1]. It was developed in accordance with the Regulation on the forms, periodicity and procedure of ongoing assessment and interim assessment on bachelor's and master's programmes, and with the Regulation on the use of e-learning and distance educational technologies in bachelor's, master's and aspirantura programmes [2].

The same text is published in the sveden section of the site as the document approved by the order of the University of 2 August 2022 № ОД/УД-05/2022/08/02 [3].

## Forms of remote assessment

Interim assessment is conducted in the form determined by the curriculum of the educational programme, the discipline (module) syllabus and the internship programme; its forms are a pass/fail exam (including a graded pass/fail exam) and an exam [4]. With e-learning and distance learning technologies it may be conducted in the following formats: testing (online/offline), oral examination (online/offline), written work (online/offline), defence of a term paper (online/offline), defence of a project (online/offline), or any other format determined by the teaching staff member [5].

The teaching staff member is responsible for preparing and conducting the assessment; staff of the Methodological Department of the Centre for Organisation of Educational Programmes, of the Department for Organisation of Bachelor's and Master's Programmes and of the IT Department may provide methodological, organisational and technical support [6].

## Technical requirements

For assessment with distance learning technologies a student needs computing equipment (a desktop computer, laptop or tablet), communication equipment providing access to the software and information of the University's electronic information-educational environment (ЭИОС) via local networks and the Internet, and appropriate software [7].

### Video conferencing mode

Students need the following for assessment via Microsoft Teams, Zoom, or similar video conferencing software:

- a webcam with resolution of at least 640×480 (0.3 MP) [8]
- a monitor/screen with resolution of at least 1280×1024 [8]
- a microphone with speakers or a computer headset [8]
- configured video conferencing software (Microsoft Teams, Zoom, or equivalent) [8]
- internet connection speed of at least 2 Mbps (or 1024 Kbps per workstation) [8]
- Google Chrome browser version 81.0 or higher [8]

### Proctoring system

For assessment using a proctoring system:

- Google Chrome browser version 72 or higher [9]
- operating system at least Windows 7, macOS 10.12 "Sierra", or Linux [9]
- webcam with resolution of at least 640×480 (0.3 MP) [9]
- microphone with speakers or a computer headset [9]
- internet connection speed of at least 512 Kbps [9]
- a browser plugin if required [9]

### Online testing

For online testing:

- Google Chrome browser version 72 or higher [10]
- operating system at least Windows 7, macOS 10.12 "Sierra", or Linux [10]
- internet connection speed of at least 512 Kbps [10]

### Other formats

For any other format chosen by the teaching staff member, technical and software requirements must be communicated to students no later than 3 working days before the assessment via corporate email or other ICT means [11].

## Room and workspace requirements

The room and workspace where the student takes the assessment must meet these requirements:

- the room must have walls and a closed door [12]
- no third parties may be present during the assessment [12]
- additional computers and monitors must be disconnected [12]
- the desk surface must be free of all items except ID documents, blank paper, and a pen; no computers, clocks, notebooks, books, sticky notes, or printed text allowed [12]
- the webcam must not face a light source and must provide a view of the room within a 2–3 meter radius from the student [12]
- the teaching staff member may request the student to demonstrate the room and workspace; refusal or non-compliance is grounds for a grade of D (Fail) or F (Fail) [12]

## Procedures for video conferencing mode

Students must:

- log in to the University's electronic information-educational environment (ЭИОС) at least 5 minutes before the assessment using their personal ID and password, unless otherwise agreed with the teaching staff member [13]
- test the webcam and microphone [13]
- join the video conference via the link in their personal account [13]
- follow the teaching staff member's instructions [13]
- keep the microphone muted when not speaking [13]
- observe business communication etiquette [13]
- close other programs using the webcam [13]
- not record the assessment materials in any way or pass them to third parties [13]
- not use any audio, visual, or other hints unless permitted by the teaching staff member [13]
- the teaching staff member may record the video conference [13]
- academic misconduct during the session is grounds for a grade of D or F and disciplinary measures [13]

## Procedures for proctoring mode

Students must:

- review the system instructions no later than one day before the assessment [14]
- test the webcam and microphone [14]
- review the rules and consent to the conditions [14]
- position the webcam directly in front of the face; the head must fully fit in the frame, the face must be centered and occupy at least 10% of the camera's field of view throughout the session, with even lighting and no obstructions (hair, clothing, hands, etc.) [14]
- sunglasses are not allowed; clear-lens glasses are permitted [14]
- no third parties may be present [14]
- complete identification in the system per the specific proctoring system's requirements [14]
- a mobile camera may be connected at the teaching staff member's discretion to show the surrounding space and workspace [14]
- the teaching staff member may record the assessment at their discretion [14]
- leaving the workspace is prohibited during the assessment [14]
- there should be no voices or noise in the background; silence is ideal [14]
- the assessment must be conducted in a browser window displayed full-screen; switching to other applications, minimizing the browser, or opening external tabs is prohibited [14]
- all programs using the webcam must be closed except the browser [14]
- recording assessment materials or passing them to third parties is prohibited [14]
- using hints is prohibited [14]
- attempting to deceive the proctoring system or proctor is grounds for a grade of D or F and disciplinary measures [14]

## Procedures for online testing

Students must:

- log in to the University's ЭИОС at least 5 minutes before the assessment using their personal ID and password, unless otherwise agreed with the teaching staff member [15]
- navigate to the online testing resource [15]
- the teaching staff member may grant permission to use educational programs, reference materials, and other information sources, with the list set in advance by the teaching staff member [15]
- using materials not on the permitted list, cheating, or communicating with others is grounds for a grade of D or F and disciplinary measures [15]
- recording assessment materials or passing them to third parties is prohibited [15]

## Procedures for other formats

For any other format chosen by the teaching staff member, including identification procedures, the rules must be communicated to students no later than 3 working days before the assessment via corporate email or other ICT means [16].

## Recording results

Results of remote interim assessment are recorded in the University's electronic information-educational environment (Moodle, "1C: University", student portfolio, my.university.innopolis.ru):

- within 1 working day after the assessment for oral exams or pass/fail exams [17]
- within 7 working days for written exams or pass/fail exams [17]
- no later than the end of the interim assessment period [17]

Results are entered into the assessment sheet by the teaching staff member and submitted to the Methodological Department (for disciplines) or the Industry and Alumni Relations Department (for internships) in paper or electronic form. Staff from these departments transfer results to "1C: University" and may affix the dean's signature [17].

## Retakes via remote assessment

### Academic failure

Unsatisfactory results (D or F) or failure to take the assessment without a valid reason constitute academic failure and must be eliminated [18]. The University sets the deadlines of the retake for each discipline (module) and other component of the programmes, including internships [19].

### First retake

The first retake is conducted by the same teaching staff member who conducted the original assessment [20]. If that teaching staff member is unavailable, the head of the Methodological Department may assign another instructor [20]. The first retake follows the same technical and procedural rules as the original assessment (sections 2.4–2.6) [21].

### Second retake

If the first retake is also unsatisfactory, the student is given the opportunity of a second retake before a University committee, typically consisting of three people [19], [21]. The committee composition is approved by the University Director (or an authorized person) together with the retake schedule [21]. The second retake is conducted via video conference following sections 2.4.1, 2.5, and 2.6.1 [22]. If a connection problem arises and is not resolved within 20 minutes, the student may take the second retake on another day, notified by phone and corporate email [22]. The result is decided by agreement among all committee members; the student may be disconnected during deliberations [22]. Results are recorded per section 2.8 [23].

### Expulsion

Students who fail to eliminate academic failure within the established deadlines are expelled from the University [24].

## Effective date

The Order enters into force from 1 September 2022; it is approved by order of the Director of the University or of a person authorised by the Director, and amendments to it are made by the same order [25], [26], [27].

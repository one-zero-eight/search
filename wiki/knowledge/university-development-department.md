# Development Department (Управление развития) — place, tasks, functions and rights

## Document and place in the structure

The Regulation on the Development Department (Управление развития) of ANO VO «Innopolis University» was approved by an order of the University of 4 April 2021, signed by R.F. Valiev [1]. It regulates the department's activity and defines its tasks, functions, rights and duties [2].

The Development Department is a structural subdivision of the University and belongs to the category «иные структурные подразделения» (other structural subdivisions) [3]. It reports directly to the director of the University [4].

In its work it follows the Constitution of the Russian Federation, the Labour Code of the Russian Federation, Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ «Об образовании в Российской Федерации» and other legal acts regulating the activity of higher education institutions, the University's Charter, decisions of the Academic, Supervisory and Operational councils, orders and instructions of the director, other local acts and this regulation [1].

The department's structure, number and staffing table, and changes to them, are approved by the director of the University [5]. It is headed by its head, who is hired and dismissed by order of the director [6]. Working conditions of its employees are set by their employment contracts and the University's internal labour regulations [1]; their rights, duties and degree of responsibility are set by job descriptions [7]. Employees may be involved, within their competences, in work under grants, projects, subsidies and other programmes of the University [8]. The regulation and its amendments are approved by the director of the University or another person authorized by the director [9].

The following units are part of the Development Department as structural subdivisions [10]:

- HR Department (Отдел по работе с персоналом) [10]
- Career Development Centre (Центр развития карьеры) [10]
- Protocol and Records Office (Отдел протокола и делопроизводства) [10]
- Faculty Recruitment and Support Office (Отдел привлечения и поддержки ППС) [10]
- Events Office (Отдел организации мероприятий) [10]

The Regulation approved by the order of 27 March 2026 makes the Center for Faculty Recruitment and Support (Центр привлечения и поддержки ППС) a structural subdivision of the Development Department that belongs to the category «иные структурные подразделения» and reports directly to the Director for Development and HR Policy [11] [12]. That order declares invalid the Regulation on the Отдел привлечения и поддержки ППС approved by order of 26 May 2021 No. ОД/АД-03/2021/05/26, and approves the Regulation on the Center in a new edition in order to bring it up to date and into line with the subdivision's current functions [13].

## Tasks: personnel and corporate culture

- personnel records (кадровый учет), military records and migration records [14], [15], [16]
- statistical reporting on personnel matters, including to statistics bodies [17]
- ensuring the rights, benefits and guarantees of the University's employees [18]
- developing and introducing a system of adaptation of the University's employees [19]
- forming and developing a system of personnel evaluation [20]
- developing and implementing a system of training and development of the University's personnel [21]
- developing and maintaining a comprehensive system of social programmes and other motivational measures in line with the University's strategy and goals [22]
- developing the University's corporate culture and carrying out internal communications [23], [24]
- ensuring that the University is fully staffed, and staff recruitment [25]

## Tasks: records management, archives and documents

- organizing, maintaining and improving the documentation support of the University's management and directing the records management process [26]
- coordinating and controlling records management in the University's structural subdivisions in accordance with the legislation on records management and archives, the University's records management instruction and other normative and methodological documents [27]
- improving the technological processes of working with documents and document information, including with modern information technologies and office equipment [28]
- ordering the composition of management documents and unifying their forms to reduce their number and establish uniformity [29]
- controlling the correct drafting of organizational-administrative documents and their timely passage and execution [30]
- ensuring the selection, accounting and preservation of documents created by the University and their archival storage [31]
- organizing the work of the University's archive and its acquisition [32]
- organizing the work of the permanent expert commission (ЭК) [33]
- giving structural subdivisions methodological help with documents and advising employees on drafting documents and records management [34]
- ensuring the regime of access to and use of the University's document information [35]
- establishing uniform documentation norms and rules for working with documents in all structural subdivisions [36]

## Tasks: teaching staff (ППС) and events

- representing the interests of the professorial and teaching staff in their interaction with state bodies, institutions, service providers and other legal and natural persons on a wide range of everyday matters [37]
- organizing the stay and accommodation on the University's territory of teaching staff working under fixed-term civil-law contracts [38]
- organizing the selection and subsequent hiring of new employees for vacant teaching staff positions [39]
- providing consultational support (language, logistics, socio-cultural and other) to members of the teaching staff [40]
- organizing and holding educational, corporate and business events, including participation in external events (local, regional, federal, international) [41]
- ensuring a comfortable stay of event participants during the events [42]
- providing events with the necessary handouts and equipment [43]
- organizing the reception of delegations of various levels (local, regional, federal, international) [44]

## Tasks: information security

- ensuring comprehensive protection of information protected by law (personal data, commercial, state and other secrets) by software, hardware and organizational means on the basis of developed programmes and methods [45]
- analysing the existing methods and means of control and protection of information and developing proposals to improve them and increase the effectiveness of protection [46]
- analysing the University's normative documentation for compliance with Russian legislation on information protection and updating that documentation [46]
- developing the normative documentation required by Russian legislation on information protection [47]
- running measures to raise employees' awareness in information protection and advising them on the relevant issues [48]
- taking part in scheduled and unscheduled inspections by bodies controlling information security, analysing and eliminating the remarks made [49]
- controlling the work of the Information Technology Department for compliance with normative acts and recommendations on information protection in the University's infrastructure [50]
- controlling critical updates of software functioning at the University and notifying the Information Technology Department of the need to install them [51]
- interacting with supervisory bodies on providing the necessary normative documentation on protection of restricted-distribution information [52]

## Tasks: labour protection

- controlling compliance with labour protection norms in departments and on the University's territory according to Russian legislation requirements [53]
- comprehensive and targeted inspections of the state of labour protection at the University [54]
- controlling the preparation and certification of employees in labour protection [55]
- taking part in inspections and surveys of the technical state of buildings, constructions, equipment, machines and mechanisms, of the efficiency of ventilation systems, sanitary installations and premises, collective and individual protection means, determining their compliance with labour protection requirements and taking measures to stop the operation of machines, equipment and work at workplaces that have not had timely repair or are faulty [56]
- carrying out the special assessment of workplaces and production equipment for compliance with labour protection requirements [57]
- determining the contingent of persons and professions subject to preliminary and periodic medical examinations, drawing up the lists of professions and positions whose holders must undergo mandatory medical examinations and the lists of those who, under current legislation, receive compensations and guarantees for heavy, harmful or dangerous working conditions [58]
- giving subdivisions methodological help in developing and revising labour protection instructions and enterprise standards of the labour safety standards system, and in organizing instruction, training and knowledge testing of employees on labour protection [59]
- conducting introductory labour protection briefings with all newly hired persons, persons on business trips, pupils and students who arrived for production training or an internship, employees of contractor organizations and persons working under agreements on attracted labour [60]
- taking part in the investigation of industrial injuries, occupational diseases and the causes of accidents and incidents, and analysing the effectiveness of measures to prevent them [61]
- organizing the work of the labour protection committee and of the first aid group [62]
- controlling the organization of storage, issue, washing, dry cleaning of special clothing, special footwear and other individual protection means, the state of safety devices and protective equipment, and ordering the necessary quantity of individual protection means [63]
- planning the costs of labour protection [64]

## Functions

The department ensures, coordinates and controls the performance of functional tasks by the structural subdivisions that are part of it, according to their regulations [65]; it ensures the fulfilment of the University's development programmes within its area of responsibility [66]; it determines ways of improving the University's activity in the areas within its competence and supports and monitors the implementation of initiatives aimed at introducing new and improved practices into the University's activity [67].

## Rights

In their activity the department's employees have the right to [68]:

- request from the University's structural subdivisions the information and materials needed for the work within the department's competence [68]
- with the consent of the heads of structural subdivisions, involve their employees in preparing draft local acts and other documents and in developing and carrying out measures held by the department [69]
- take part in meetings held by the University's management when questions within the department's competence are discussed [70]
- submit to the University's management proposals to cancel or amend local acts of the University [71]
- interact with state authorities and administration, legal entities and the University's subdivisions on matters within the department's competence [72]

## Interaction and reporting

Interaction with other structural subdivisions is determined by the tasks and functions assigned to the department by this regulation [73]. Interaction with the Financial and Economic Department covers the application of legislation and local acts on remuneration at the University, the preparation of draft local acts regulating the University's financial activity, legal expertise of situations where employees are held materially liable, the preparation and transfer of materials to courts for recovering receivables from organizations, and tax disputes [74]. The department may be a base for the practical training of the University's students [75]. Interaction with other structural subdivisions takes place when preparing draft local acts, orders, instructions, contracts and other documents and when preparing conclusions and written or oral information on matters within the department's competence [76].

On the basis of the relevant local acts, orders and instructions, the department may be responsible, within its activity, for preparing and keeping information, certificates and reports up to date [77].

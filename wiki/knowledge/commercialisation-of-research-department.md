# Department for the Commercialisation of Research Works (Отдел по коммерциализации научно-исследовательских работ) — place, tasks, functions and rights

## The document and the Department's place in the structure

The page is the Regulation on the Department for the Commercialisation of Research Works (Положение об Отделе по коммерциализации научно-исследовательских работ) of ANO VO «Innopolis University», presented as approved by an order of the University № ОД/АД-03/2021/07/07 and signed by R.F. Valiev [1]. The page gives the order number without a legible date, and it is a scan read by OCR, so numbers and dates should be checked against the PDF [1]. The same order number ОД/АД-03/2021/07/07 appears on the Regulation on the Digital Solutions Promotion Center, the Centre this Department belongs to [2].

- The Regulation regulates the Department's activity and determines its tasks, functions, rights and duties [3].
- The Department is a structural subdivision of the Centre for the Promotion of Digital Solutions (Центр продвижения цифровых решений) of the Department for Project and Research Activity (управление по проектной и научно-исследовательской деятельности) and belongs to the category of other structural subdivisions (иные структурные подразделения) [4].
- It reports directly to the director of the Centre for the Promotion of Digital Solutions [5].
- In its activity the Department follows the Constitution of the Russian Federation, the Labour Code of the Russian Federation, Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29.12.2012 «Об образовании в Российской Федерации» and other legislative and normative legal acts on higher education institutions, the University's Charter, decisions of the Academic, Supervisory and Operational councils, orders and instructions of the director, other local normative acts and this Regulation [6].
- The structure, the number of staff and the staffing table of the Department, and changes to them, are approved by the director of the University [1], and the Department is headed by its head, hired and dismissed by order of the director [7].
- The working conditions of the Department's employees are determined by their employment contracts and by the University's internal labour regulations [1]; their rights, duties and degree of responsibility are established by job descriptions [8].
- Within their competences the Department's employees may be involved in work under grants, projects, subsidies and other programmes implemented by the University [9]; the Regulation and additions and changes to it are approved by order of the director or another person authorised by him [10].

## Tasks

The Department's tasks are [11], [12]:

- identifying competitive and commercially significant results of the University's intellectual activity and considering the commercial use of the University's intellectual property and scientific-technical products created by its subdivisions [11];
- selecting competitive, commercially promising scientific developments and promoting them on the market by concluding contracts and agreements [13];
- analysing the results of scientific and research works to determine the possibility and expediency of commercialisation [14];
- providing consulting services on commercialising the University's intellectual property and scientific-technical products: developing a commercialisation strategy, preparing a commercialisation plan and so on [15];
- conducting marketing research to determine the market potential and to analyse the sales markets of the University's intellectual property and scientific-technical products [16];
- preparing advertising and information products to promote the results of the University's scientific-technical activity at exhibitions, round tables, meetings and so on in the interests of industrial partners [17];
- preparing and holding negotiations with potential investors, licensees and buyers of intellectual property and scientific-technical products, in order to help the University's subdivisions and the authors of results of intellectual activity to realise intellectual property objects commercially [18];
- consulting the University's employees and students on managing its intellectual property and scientific-technical products [19];
- developing cooperation with various legal entities to promote the University's intellectual property and scientific-technical products [20];
- taking part in preparing innovative projects connected with introducing the University's intellectual property, for presenting them to investment committees, industrial enterprises and corporations to obtain financing [21];
- forming and supporting a database of competitive and commercially significant results of the University's intellectual activity and scientific-technical products [12].

## Functions

- Conducting explanatory work on the commercialisation and introduction into production of the results of scientific and innovation activity [22].
- Initiating and organising marketing events connected with the University's activity [23].
- Meeting the key performance indicators set by the management [24].
- Analysing the internal business processes of customer companies [25].

## Rights

In carrying out their activity the Department's employees have the right to:

- request from the University's structural subdivisions the information and materials needed for work within the Department's competence [26];
- with the consent of the heads of those subdivisions, involve their employees in preparing drafts of local acts and other documents and in developing and carrying out measures conducted by the Department [27];
- take part in meetings held by the University's management where matters within the Department's competence are discussed [28];
- put before the University's management proposals to cancel, amend or supplement the University's local acts [29];
- interact with bodies of state authority and administration, legal entities and the University's subdivisions on matters within the Department's competence [30].

## Interaction with other subdivisions and reporting

- Interaction with the other structural subdivisions is determined by the tasks and functions assigned to the Department by the Regulation [31].
- Interaction with the Personnel Selection Sector concerns hiring into project and product teams [32], and with the Finance and Economics Department it concerns attracting third persons to support the Department's activity within that department's competence [33].
- The Department may be a base for the practical preparation of the University's students [34].
- Interaction with the other subdivisions also concerns preparing drafts of local acts, orders, instructions, contracts and other documents and preparing conclusions and written and oral references on matters within the Department's competence [35].
- On the basis of the relevant local acts, orders and instructions the Department may be responsible, within its activity, for preparing and keeping up to date information, references and reports [36].

# Admission to secondary vocational education (College) programmes 2026/2027 — specialities, places, documents and deadlines

## The rules and what they cover

The "Rules of admission to study under educational programmes of secondary vocational education at ANO VO «Innopolis University» for the 2026/2027 academic year" regulate the admission of citizens of the Russian Federation, foreign citizens and stateless persons, including compatriots living abroad, to secondary vocational education (СПО) programmes in the specialities of СПО under education contracts concluded at the expense of natural and (or) legal persons (a contract for the provision of paid educational services) [1].

They are drafted on the basis of Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29 December 2012, the order of the Ministry of Education of Russia No. 457 of 2 September 2020 approving the Procedure for admission to СПО programmes, the University's Charter and other acts of the University [2]. Admission to places under paid education contracts is carried out on the applications of persons who have basic general education (основное общее образование) [3]. The University processes the personal data received in connection with admission as required by the legislation on personal data [4]. The conditions of admission guarantee observance of the right to education and the enrolment, from among the applicants with the corresponding level of education, of those most capable and best prepared to master СПО programmes [5].

## Specialities, places and length of study

The University announces admission on the basis of licence No. Л035-00115-77/00642712, issued by a распоряжение No. 2418 of 25.12.2015 of the Federal Service for Supervision in Education and Science, without time limit, for СПО programmes — programmes for the training of mid-level specialists [6]:

- 09.02.11 Разработка и управление программным обеспечением [6]
- 15.02.10 Мехатроника и робототехника (по отраслям) [6]

The normative length of study is 3 (three) years 10 (ten) months [6].

For full-time study, Annex 1 to the Rules gives [7]:

- 09.02.11 Разработка и управление программным обеспечением: 25 places under paid education contracts, 0 places financed from budget appropriations; profile subjects — 1. Informatics, 2. Mathematics [7]
- 15.02.10 Мехатроника и робототехника (по отраслям): 25 places under paid education contracts, 0 places financed from budget appropriations; profile subjects — 1. Physics, 2. Informatics, 3. Mathematics [7]

Annex 4 sets the quota for preemptive enrolment at 1 place for each of the two specialities, full-time [7].

Enrolled students, including those from other cities, may be accommodated in the University's residential campus under a contract for the provision of accommodation services, governed by the residence rules and the cost of accommodation approved by an order of the University; the total number of places in the hostel is 50 [8].

## Who organises admission

Admission is organised by the University's admission committee (приёмная комиссия) [9]. Its chair is the director of the University [10]; its composition, powers and procedure are governed by the Regulation on the admission committee approved by the director [11]. The work of the committee and its records, as well as personal reception of applicants and their parents (legal representatives), are organised by the responsible secretary, appointed by the director [12]. Observance of citizens' rights in education, publicity and openness of the committee's work are ensured [13]; to confirm the reliability of submitted documents the committee may apply to state (municipal) bodies and organisations [14].

## Information for applicants

The University familiarises the applicant and his parents (legal representatives) with the Charter, the licence, the certificate of state accreditation, the educational programmes and other documents governing the organisation of educational activity and the rights and duties of students [15]. Information about admission is placed on the official website and on the information stand, with free access to it in the building [16].

Not later than 1 March 2026, before the start of the acceptance of documents, the admission committee places on the website and the information stand: the admission rules; the conditions of admission under paid contracts; the list of specialities with forms of study; the requirements to the level of education (basic general); the list of entrance examinations and their forms; information on the need (absence of need) for a compulsory preliminary medical examination; the total number of places for each speciality by forms of study; the number of places financed from budget appropriations and the number under paid contracts; the rules for filing and considering appeals; information on the hostel and the places in it for out-of-town applicants; and a sample contract for paid educational services [17].

During the acceptance period the committee daily publishes the number of applications submitted for each speciality, indicating the forms of study [18]. A special telephone line and a section on the website answer admission enquiries; the committee is reachable at +7 843 203-92-53 (ext. 113) and admissions@innopolis.ru [19].

## Documents and deadlines

Admission is carried out for the first course on the personal application of citizens [20]. Acceptance of documents for full-time study starts on 20 June 2026 and continues until 15 August 2026; if free places remain, it is extended until 25 November 2026 [21].

The application is submitted in Russian [22]. Citizens of the Russian Federation present [22]:

- the original or a copy of identity and citizenship documents, except where the application is filed through the public services portal [22]
- the original or a copy of the document of education (and (or) of education and qualification), with the same exception [22]
- the original or a copy of a document confirming the right to preemptive or first-priority admission under part 4 of article 68 of the Federal Law «Об образовании в Российской Федерации», with the same exception [22]
- when filing through the public services portal: a copy of the document of education or its electronic duplicate made and certified by an authorised official of the multifunctional centre, and a copy of the document confirming the preemptive or first-priority right, except documents obtainable through the unified system of interdepartmental electronic interaction [22]
- 4 (four) photographs, except when filing through the public services portal [22]

Foreign citizens and stateless persons, including compatriots living abroad, present a copy of the identity document (or an identity document of a foreign citizen in Russia); the original document(s) of a foreign state on education where that education is recognised in Russia on the level of the corresponding education in accordance with article 107 of the Federal Law «Об образовании в Российской Федерации» (and, where that law requires, a certificate of recognition of the foreign education); the original or a copy of the document confirming the preemptive or first-priority right; a notarised translation of the foreign education document and its annex into Russian; copies of documents or other evidence of belonging to the groups listed in point 6 of article 17 of Federal Law No. 99-ФЗ of 24 May 1999; and 4 photographs [22]. The surname, name and patronymic in the translations must correspond to those in the identity document of the foreign citizen in Russia [22].

Applicants may additionally present documents confirming results of individual achievements and a copy of a targeted-training contract certified by the customer (or uncertified with presentation of the original); copies may be certified by the University when originals are presented in person [23]. The application must contain the applicant's surname, name and patronymic, date of birth, identity document details, insurance account number (if any), information on the previous level of education and the document confirming it, on belonging to persons with a preemptive or first-priority right, on the speciality (specialities) chosen with conditions and form of study, and on the need for a hostel; the applicant's signature certifies familiarisation with the licence and accreditation documents, consent to personal data processing, the fact of receiving СПО for the first time, familiarity with the Charter, licence, accreditation, programmes and other documents, and familiarity with the date for submitting the original document of education; if the application lacks required or contains untrue information, the University returns the documents [24].

For both specialities a compulsory medical examination is not obligatory [25]. Documents may be submitted in person, by post as a registered letter with notification of delivery, or in electronic form through the functional of the federal public services portal, with verification of the reliability of the information and documents, for which the University may apply to state information systems and bodies; documents are accepted no later than the deadlines of п. 4.2 [26].

No fee is charged from applicants on submission of documents [27]. A personal file is opened for each applicant with all submitted documents [28]; on personal submission a receipt is issued [29]. On written application the applicant may withdraw the original document of education and other documents; they are returned within the next working day after the application [30].

## No entrance examinations

No entrance examinations are held for admission to the specialities 09.02.11 Разработка и управление программным обеспечением and 15.02.10 Мехатроника и робототехника (по отраслям) [31]. Because there are no entrance examinations, no special conditions for disabled applicants and applicants with health limitations are established [32], and the general rules for filing and considering appeals against entrance examination results are not established either [33].

# Grant agreement for foreign citizens from far-abroad countries — study and work duties, return and reduction of the grant

## The form and when it applies

- The grant agreement (Договор о предоставлении гранта на обучение) is the model form in the annex to the Regulation on the competition for a grant for foreign citizens from far-abroad countries; it is concluded between the University as grantor (Грантодатель) and the winner as grant recipient (Грантополучатель), taking into account the education contract (Соглашение) and that Regulation [1].
- The agreement is concluded under a suspensive condition (art. 157 of the Civil Code): the rights and duties under it arise only if the recipient is actually enrolled on the higher education programme named in the agreement not later than the start of the academic year [1].
- It takes effect on signature and runs until study is completed and, as to unperformed duties and breaches, until both parties have fully performed their obligations [1].
- It ends if the recipient dies, is recognised as unable to work, or has contraindications for the work under the agreement confirmed by a medical conclusion [1].
- During its term the agreement may be changed only by a written agreement of both parties, including when the education contract is changed, and it may be terminated by mutual consent [1].
- The agreement is drawn up in English and Russian in 4 originals (2 in each language), and where the versions differ the Russian version prevails [2].

## Amount of the grant

- The grant consists of the stated percentage of the cost of study determined in the education contract, expressed in roubles [1].
- It is not paid to the recipient in money; it is counted towards his payment for study, and the part it does not cover is paid by the recipient himself under the education contract [1].
- The amount may be reduced by the University unilaterally on the grounds of section 5 of the agreement [1].
- The University's claim for payment of study and the recipient's claim for the grant are homogeneous, so the rules on set-off under art. 410 of the Civil Code apply [1].
- Sums received against a debt are applied, whatever purpose the payment order states, first to court and collection costs, then to a penalty, then to the return of the grant, and the University may change this order unilaterally [1].

## Duties during study

- The recipient must complete the programme under the education contract, observe production and study discipline, study the chosen profession in good faith, master the programme in the required volume, and attend all classes and practical work, go through all practices and pass all final assessments within the curriculum's deadlines [3], [4], [5], [6], [7].
- He must undergo an annual medical examination and the monitoring of psychological comfort and well-being organised by the University, no more than three times in one year [1].
- A recipient on a programme of the federal project «Передовые инженерные школы» (ПИШ) must, during study, take part in the University's competitive selections for grants for practices and internships outside the educational process, including work with mentors («Отбор») [1], and complete at least one internship outside the educational process in a partner company under the University's regulation on such internships [1].
- To obtain the employment contract the recipient must take part in interviews and selection procedures, attend the University's events for potential employers and give the employer the documents needed for the contract [8].
- Within 5 calendar days of employment, or of re-employment with a new employer, he must notify the University, attaching supporting documents and giving the employer's full name, his position and the start date [1]; for breach of this duty the University may claim a fine of 500 (five hundred) roubles for each day of delay [9].
- If his duties are not performed, the grant is returned within 3 working days of the event [10].

## Work obligation after graduation

- The recipient must find employment within 3 months after completing study and receiving the education document in an organisation meeting the conditions of section 1 of the agreement [11].
- He must work under an employment contract at normal working hours in the specialty obtained for at least the number of months stated in the agreement (left blank in the form) from the date of employment, with a monthly salary of not less than three times the minimum wage established in the Republic of Tatarstan [1].
- The following periods do not count in that period: unpaid leave (except where art. 128 of the Labour Code makes it mandatory), maternity leave, childcare leave (including when the child's father uses it), absence from work without valid reason including suspension from work under art. 76 of the Labour Code, the period of employment when the employer is changed, and military service or training camps [12].
- The recipient may change employer, but the periods without labour relations with an employer may not exceed 3 (three) months in total [13].
- The University may count work for the employer during the study practice provided by the programme (introductory, industrial, pre-diploma) as part of the work period [14].
- For ПИШ programmes the employment must be with a partner company; if the recipient proves that within those 3 months all partner companies refused him, he must find employment in another organisation meeting section 1 conditions within 6 months [1].
- For the «Информационное технологическое предпринимательство» programme the employment must be in the recipient's own technological startup; if he proves that he made all attempts to register it but it does not meet the criteria of a technological startup, he has 6 months to find another organisation meeting section 1 conditions [1].

## Return of the whole grant

- The whole grant must be returned if the recipient does not start work within the term set by the employment contract [15].
- It must also be returned if he evades employment and does not conclude (refuses to conclude) the employment contract with the employer within the term set by the agreement [16].

## Return in proportion to the period worked

- The grant is returned in an amount inversely proportional to the period of actual performance of the employment duties if the employment relation ends without subsequent employment with another employer as the agreement provides — including because of a move, a spouse's transfer to work in another place or dismissal on any ground of the Labour Code, also at the employer's initiative [1].
- The same applies if the recipient works for employers for less than the number of months stated in the agreement in total [17].
- The sum is the grant divided by D1 and multiplied by D2, where D1 is the number of working days the recipient had to work and D2 the number of working days he had to work but did not [1].

## Return in proportion to the period of study

- The grant is returned in proportion to the actual period of study, counted from the enrolment order to the expulsion order, if the education contract ends at the recipient's initiative because of a transfer to another educational organisation, or at the University's initiative where expulsion is applied as a disciplinary measure to a recipient who has reached fifteen years [1].
- If the education contract ends early on other grounds, the recipient returns 20 % of the sum equal to the cost of study computed for the actual period of study [18].
- If the recipient is expelled before the end of the first year (for bachelor's students) or the first trimester (for master's students), the University may decide not to require the return, recording this in an additional agreement, on the basis of his current performance and attendance and other information showing good-faith study [19].

## Valid reasons

- Illnesses that arose after the agreement was concluded and prevent study or employment, confirmed by a conclusion of a medical commission issued under Russian law, and impossibility of performance because of force majeure are valid grounds releasing the recipient from the study and employment duties [20].

## Reduction of the grant for academic results

- For master's programmes the University may reduce the grant unilaterally if the recipient has more than two «satisfactory» (C) grades after the first (autumn) trimester and/or more than four «satisfactory» grades after the spring and/or summer trimester [21].
- It may also reduce it if he has one or more «unsatisfactory» (F) grades after the corresponding trimester [22]; the results of repeat interim assessments in those disciplines are not counted [21], [22].
- For bachelor's programmes the form provides a single version of the clause: the grant may be reduced if there is one or more «unsatisfactory» grades after the interim assessment of the first semester of the first year and/or later semesters of the second, third and fourth years, repeat assessment results not being counted [1].
- The decision rests on the interim assessment record, and the new amount may not exceed the reduction table: 100 % becomes 70 %, 70 % becomes 60 %, 60 % becomes 50 %, 50 % becomes 25 %, 25 % becomes 0 % [23].
- The University notifies the recipient of the reduction within the number of working days left blank in the form after the interim assessment, and the student must pay the resulting debt within 15 (fifteen) working days of the notification [24].

## Force majeure and disputes

- A party invoking force majeure must inform the other in writing within 20 working days, with a supporting document from a competent state organisation, and after the circumstances end must notify it without delay, stating the expected period of performance; losses caused by a missing or late notification are compensated by the party that caused them [1], [25].
- Disputes are settled by negotiations and claims, a claim being considered within 20 days, and if the parties do not agree the dispute goes to the Verkhneuslonsky district court of the Republic of Tatarstan, Russian law being applicable [26].
- The parties correspond by post and e-mail; correspondence from the addresses named in the agreement is recognised as signed with a simple electronic signature; scanned pages sent by e-mail have the force of the original until the originals are exchanged; the date of transmission is the day the e-mail is sent, and the receiving party bears the risk of receiving messages [1].

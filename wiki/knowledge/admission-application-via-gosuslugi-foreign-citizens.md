# Submitting an admission application through Госуслуги — the instruction for foreign citizens

## The document

The page is the University's «Инструкция по подаче заявления на поступление для иностранных граждан» — a step-by-step instruction, illustrated with screenshots of the Госуслуги application, on submitting an application for admission through that application [1].

The service it walks through is «Поступление в вуз онлайн»: it lets an applicant submit an application to universities without visiting the admission committee, for programmes of bachelor's, specialist and basic higher education, for master's programmes including specialised higher education, and for aspiratancy programmes [1].

Because the page reproduces screenshots, part of its text is not legible — for example the minimum score for «Русский язык» in the list of examinations shown in the application is missing from the page [1].

## Registration and the profile in the application

- The applicant downloads the application to a smartphone from an app store [1].
- He registers and must state correctly the data of the foreign passport or of the other document with which he plans to cross the border of the Russian Federation [1].
- In the section «Профиль» he fills in the surname, name and patronymic in Russian in accordance with the certified translation, the date and place of birth, passport data, citizenship and the address of actual residence [1].
- To confirm identity the application may request photographs with a turn of the head and, in some cases, a voice recording [1].
- The questionnaire asks whether the applicant has one of the listed documents (a temporary residence permit, a residence permit and others), whether he has the status of a compatriot and with what education he is applying; the level of education determines the conditions of admission [1].
- A foreign citizen has the right to enter budget places if this is provided by an international treaty of the Russian Federation or if he is a compatriot permanently residing abroad; the status of a compatriot and the address of residence have to be confirmed [1].
- Foreign citizens may apply on the results of the university's internal entrance examinations if they did not take the ЕГЭ in those subjects [1].

## Universities, areas of study and competitive groups

- An application may be submitted to not more than 5 universities — for any forms of study and of financing; the head university and its branches count as one university [1].
- Not more than 2 areas of study (направления подготовки) and any number of competitive groups; areas of study are counted across the university and its branches [1].
- In the search line the applicant types the name of the university; the application found 2 areas of study at «Университет Иннополис»: 09.03.01 «Информатика и вычислительная техника» (full-time, bachelor's, 7 competitive groups) and 15.03.06 «Мехатроника и робототехника» (full-time, bachelor's, 2 competitive groups) [1].
- For a competitive group the page shows the educational programmes with their length of study and the places: «Анализ данных и искусственный интеллект», 4 years, full-time, bachelor's — 140 paid places and 36 main places; «Математические основы искусственного интеллекта», 4 years, full-time, bachelor's — 48 main places and 210 paid places [1].
- The entrance examinations of a competitive group are shown with the minimum points: «Математика» 60, «Информатика / Физика» 60/60, «Русский язык»; beside them the page has a separate list «Вступительные испытания на базе среднего профессионального образования» [1].
- The application asks whether the applicant needs a dormitory (общежитие); only universities that have dormitories with places for admitted students are offered, and a place is not guaranteed: a dormitory is given if the university has housing and free places, which are distributed under the university's rules taking into account privileges and the results of the competition [1].
- Programmes are given priorities in order of preference: the numbering starts from 1, goes without gaps, and priorities cannot repeat [1].
- If the applicant has valid ЕГЭ results obtained in the last 5 years, he cannot take the university's internal entrance examinations in those subjects; «Нет» is chosen by those who plan to take only the additional entrance examinations (ДВИ) or who apply exclusively on the results of the ЕГЭ; the rule does not concern basic-level mathematics [1].
- The internal examinations offered in the list are «Основы информатики», «Основы математики» and «Основы физики» [1].

## Documents uploaded

- A certificate of the state testing in Russian as a foreign language: it must confirm knowledge of Russian at a level not lower than ТРКИ-1/В1, and its form was approved by order of the Ministry of Education and Science of 25.04.2014 № 412 [1].
- The document of secondary general education; for some foreign documents of education legalisation is required, or recognition of the foreign education with a certificate of recognition, or both [1].
- Photographs or scans of all completed pages of a document must be uploaded unfolded; the accepted file formats are pdf, jpg and png, and up to 1 file can be attached in a field [1].
- The passport and its translation into Russian: the pages of the passport with personal data are uploaded and, where the information is not duplicated in Russian, the translation; the translation must be certified by a notary, by a consular official or in another way established by an international treaty or by the university [1].
- A photograph of the face, full face, for the applicant's personal file and the entrance examinations [1].
- For entry into Russia a foreign passport or another document proving identity on the territory of Russia is needed; a study visa is issued on an invitation after enrolment in the university, and the identity document must be valid «ещё минимум 1,5 года с даты начала действия визы» [1].
- If a visa is needed, the applicant states the country and city where he will obtain it and must be lawfully present there at the moment of obtaining the visa; a migration card is issued when crossing the border with Russia; the questionnaire also asks whether the applicant completed a preparatory faculty or Russian language courses at a Russian university and whether he works with a recruitment agency [1].

## What the applicant confirms and what happens next

- Before sending, the applicant confirms: fulfilment of the conditions listed in п. 73 of the Порядок приёма в вузы; acquaintance with the charters of the universities he applies to; the filing of applications to not more than 5 universities counting all ways of submitting; consent to the processing of data for the organisation of admission, including processing by the universities, for the term stated in the instruction; consent to the processing of data for obtaining an electronic student card; acquaintance with the rules of stay of foreign citizens on the territory of Russia [1].
- The consideration of the application may last during the whole period of acceptance of documents, and universities may request additional information through the personal account [1].
- If the applicant is going to take entrance examinations at the university, he looks at the schedule in his personal account and signs up for the needed examinations; the universities, competitive groups and priorities chosen can be changed by editing the application until the end of the acceptance of applications [1].
- The applicant follows his place in the competitive lists from his personal account and checks the results of the competition after the orders of enrolment are published [1].
- The «код поступающего» helps to find oneself in the list of applicants and in the competitive list; for a consultation on the status of the application the operator needs the surname, name and patronymic, the «код поступающего» and the number of the application [1].
- On the schedule screen for «Университет Иннополис» the page shows «Основы математики» with 1 competitive group, the dates 14 июля 10:00, 14 июля 15:00 and 22 июля 12:00, «Дистанционный экзамен» and the last day for signing up for 22 июля — 20 июля до 23:59; «Основы физики» on 9 июля 10:00–11:00 and «Русский язык» on 10 июля 10:00–11:00 [1].
- The consultation for foreign applicants is available by telephone +7 (843) 203-92-53 доб. 102 [1].

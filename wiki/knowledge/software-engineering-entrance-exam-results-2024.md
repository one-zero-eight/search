# Entrance examination for the master's programme «Программная инженерия» (5–6 August 2024) — published results

## What the document is

The page is the published protocol of the entrance examination for the master's programme «Программная инженерия» (software engineering) conducted by Innopolis University on its own, under the heading «Результаты вступительного испытания проводимого АНО ВО "Университет Иннополис" самостоятельно» [1].

The document title gives the period 05.08.2024–06.08.2024, and the heading table of the examination names 05.08.2024 as the date on which it was held [1]. The table then gives, for each participant, the number, the СНИЛС/уникальный код, the score (оценка) and the same score written out in words (оценка прописью) [1].

Scores are published against an identifier rather than a name: most identifiers are 11-digit numbers, and a few are short codes (for example [скрыто], [скрыто], [скрыто], 78960) [1].

## Results recorded

The protocol lists 30 numbered results; in the order of the protocol the scores are 60 (Шестьдесят), 60, 75, 65, 50, 55, 70, 60, 60, 40, 70, 45, 65, 50, 65, 60, 70, 50, 55, 80, 60, 65, 40, 65, 70, 65, 45, 55, 65 and 65 [1].

The scores in words in the protocol are «Шестьдесят», «Семьдесят пять», «Шестьдесят пять», «Пятьдесят», «Пятьдесят пять», «Семьдесят», «Сорок», «Сорок пять» and «Восемьдесят», matching the figures [1]. The highest score in the protocol is 80 and the lowest is 40 [1].

## How reliable the reading is

The text layer of the page runs the heading table and the first rows of the results table together, so the pairing of a number with its identifier and score has to be read row by row, and the first three rows carry only a short numeric identifier [1]. The page states no pass mark and no decision on admission [1].

# Increased scholarship of the University — criteria, deadlines for documents and appeal, and scoring

## What the scholarship is for

The increased scholarship of the University (повышенная стипендия АНО ВО «Университет Иннополис») is appointed for special achievements in one or several areas of activity — academic, research, social, cultural-creative and sports [1]. The procedure for appointing it is set out in Appendix 3 to the Regulation on scholarships [2], and it may be appointed to all students of the University on higher education programmes [3].

## Which criteria apply in which period

- From the beginning of the academic year to the month in which the first interim assessment ends, the increased scholarship is appointed according to the criteria given for the achievements a student has after enrolment in the University [4].
- From the first day of the month following the month in which the first interim assessment ends, it is appointed according to the criteria for the five areas of activity [4].
- The achievements of the students of each course of the bachelor's and master's programmes form a separate rating and must meet one or more of the criteria applying to the period of appointment [4].

## Limits and combination with other scholarships

- The number of students receiving the increased scholarship of the University may not be more than 10% of the total number of students receiving the academic scholarship of the University [4].
- A student cannot receive the increased state academic scholarship and the increased scholarship of the University at the same time [4].
- In the case of an academic debt or a grade «неудовлетворительно» during the year preceding the appointment, the increased scholarship of the University is not appointed [4].

## Deadlines, scoring and appeal

- To take part in the competition the student must upload the documents confirming his achievements for the year preceding the appointment on the portal my.innopolis.university, not later than 14 working days after the end of the interim assessment on the results of the period of study [4].
- The department responsible for checking and scoring the submitted achievements is the Department of support and development of students, within 14 working days after the end of that interim assessment; in disputable situations it may consult other subdivisions of the University or invite the student for an individual interview [4].
- After the preliminary results are published on the portal my.innopolis.university the student has 1 day to file an appeal in the way indicated by the Department on that portal [4].
- The Department considers the appeals within 2 working days and takes the final decision to agree or refuse the application, notifying the student; the decision must contain reasons, which must be brought to the student [4].
- On the results of the competition the Department forms a rating of the students by the total sum of points and a list of students recommended for the increased scholarship, which is presented to the Scholarship Commission for consideration [4].

## What is scored

The appendix sets out a points table for each area of activity; the main lines are [4]:

- academic activity: all grades «отлично» for the last periods of study — from 20 points for the last 2 periods to 45 points for the last 7 periods; development and implementation of an IT product for the University's services without payment — 25 points; olympiads, contests and similar events — points for a winner, a prize-winner and also a participant, depending on the level (departmental or regional, all-Russian, international) and with separate lines for informatics events and for ICPC, RuCTF, «Робокубок» and «Евробот» [4]
- research activity: the same criteria as for the increased state academic scholarship — awards for research work, an exclusive right to a scientific result, publications indexed by WOS/SCOPUS and in Russian journals, reports at scientific conferences and a grant for research work, with a base number of points multiplied by coefficients for the current status of the work, for the category, quartile or ВАК category of the venue and, where there are co-authors taking part in the competition, divided by 2 [4]
- social activity: participation in the organisation of student self-government — 20 points, its chairman — 25, a member of the top-5 of the aktiv — 15, starosta and participation in student clubs — from 0 to 25, head of a student club of the top-3 — 25, of the top-10 — 10, of the top-20 — 5, participation in programmes organised at the University — 5, official ambassador — from 0 to 15, participation in the organisation of events — from 0 to 20, with points for organisers, volunteers and team coaches depending on the level of the event and the time worked [4]
- cultural-creative activity: winner or prize-winner of a creative event — 5 points at inside-university or city level, 10 at regional level, 15 at all-Russian level, 20 at international level; a participant — 3, 5, 8 and 10 points at the same levels; creation of a work of literature or art — 5 points; host of an event — 3 points [4]
- sports activity: winner or prize-winner of a sports event — 3 points at inside-university or city level, 10 at regional level, 15 at all-Russian level, 20 at international level; a participant — 1, 3, 5 and 7 points at the same levels; fulfilment of the standards of the silver badge of the «Готов к труду и обороне» complex — 10 points and of the gold badge — 15 points [4]
- the increased scholarship for achievements in sports activity is appointed to students who have no grade «неудовлетворительно» for the period of study preceding the appointment [4]

## General rules of the competition

- Achievements obtained while studying at the previous level of training are not taken into account in the section «учебная деятельность» [4].
- If an application is submitted under the indicator «Победитель» or «Призер», an application under the indicator «Участник» is not considered [4].
- An achievement is taken into account under only one criterion; the other applications are not considered [4].
- Achievements obtained within the work duties of students who are at the same time employees of the University are not accepted for the competition [4].
- In case of equality of points in one area of activity the Scholarship Commission decides in favour of the student on the basis of academic performance and of the totality of the achievements submitted, the GPA level being taken into account first [4].
- Events organised by the University's students through the portal my.innopolis.university are given the inside-university level unless the organisers provide otherwise; the level of an event is set by its organisers, and if it is not indicated the Department may assign it on the basis of information in the media or at its own discretion [4].
- For a team achievement a document confirming the composition of the team must be attached if the confirming document does not list the winners [4].

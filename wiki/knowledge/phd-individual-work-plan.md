# Individual work plan of a PhD student (aspirant) — the Regulation of 22 September 2025: formation, approval, changes, control and storage

## The document

The Regulation on the individual work plan of a PhD student (aspirant) in ANO VO «Innopolis University» was approved by order of the University of 22 September 2025 № ОД/УД‐03/2025/09/22 [1]. It establishes the procedure for forming, approving, controlling the fulfilment and storing the individual work plan of an aspirant [2]. It was developed in accordance with Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29 December 2012, Government Decree No. 2122 of 30 November 2021, Ministry of Science and Higher Education order No. 951 of 20 October 2021, order No. 466 of 3 June 2025 amending the federal state requirements approved by that order No. 951, the University's Charter and other acts [3]. The Regulation is approved, and amended or supplemented, by order of the Director of the University or an authorised person, and it enters into force from the moment of approval [4], [5].

## What the plan consists of

- The individual work plan of the aspirant includes the individual plan of research activity and the individual curriculum [6].
- It is drawn up for the whole period of mastering the aspirantura programme, broken down by semesters, in the form given in annexes 1–2 to the Regulation [7].
- The individual plan of research activity provides for the aspirant's scientific (research) activity aimed at preparing the dissertation in accordance with the programme [8]; it is formed by the aspirant together with the research supervisor [9] and in Russian or in English at the aspirant's choice [10].
- The individual curriculum provides for mastering the educational component of the programme on the basis of individualising its content with account of the features and educational needs of the particular aspirant [11]; it is formed on the basis of the curriculum of the programme for the corresponding scientific specialty, form of study and year of admission [12] and includes the calendar study schedule and the plan distributing the scientific and educational components and the final attestation by semesters [13].

## Approval and changes

Not later than 30 calendar days from the date the aspirantura programme starts, after agreement with the department responsible for organising study on aspirantura programmes, the individual work plan is approved by the Director of the University or an authorised person [14]. The plan together with changes in the individual plan of research activity and/or in the individual curriculum is approved in the same way [15].

Changes to the individual plan of research activity are made [16]:

- when the curriculum of the aspirantura programme for the corresponding scientific specialty, form of study and year of admission is updated [16];
- at the aspirant's initiative on his personal application agreed with the research supervisor, submitted not later than 14 calendar days before the start of the semester (the form is annex 3) [16];
- at the research supervisor's initiative (the form is annex 4) [16].

Changes to the individual curriculum are made [17]:

- when the curriculum of the aspirantura programme for the corresponding scientific specialty, form of study and year of admission is updated [17];
- at the aspirant's initiative on his personal application, submitted not later than 14 calendar days before the start of the semester (the form is annex 5); changes made at the aspirant's initiative may concern only the moving of the terms of mastering disciplines (modules) and practice from one semester to another [17].

The Regulation on individual curricula was approved on 2 August 2022 [18]. It provides that the individual curriculum of aspirantura students is approved not later than 3 months after enrolment in the aspirantura programme, after the research supervisor is assigned and the topic of the research work is approved [19]. This Regulation instead requires the whole individual work plan to be approved within 30 calendar days from the start of the programme [14].

## Control of fulfilment

- The aspirant must conscientiously master the aspirantura programme and fulfil the individual work plan [20].
- The aspirant masters the programme and passes interim assessment within the terms set by the calendar study schedule included in the individual work plan [21].
- During interim assessment the aspirant forms a report on the fulfilment of the individual plan of research activity and gives it to the research supervisor (the form is annexes 6–7) [22].
- On the basis of the report the research supervisor forms a review of the quality, timeliness and success of the aspirant's stages of scientific (research) activity for the semester (the form is annexes 8–9) [23].
- After the research supervisor evaluates the results of the scientific (research) activity for the semester, the aspirant's report is heard at a research seminar [24].
- The research supervisor ensures control over the aspirant's timely fulfilment of the individual plan of research activity [25].
- Non-fulfilment of the individual plan of research activity established during interim assessment is recognised as unfair performance of the aspirant's duties of mastering the programme and is a ground for expelling the aspirant from the University [26].

## Grades for the semester

The research supervisor's review grades the fulfilment of the individual plan of research activity for the semester [1]:

- A «отлично» (excellent): the aspirant fulfilled the plan for the current semester — Blocks 1, 2 and 3 completed [1];
- B «хорошо» (good): Blocks 1 and 2 completed [1];
- C «удовлетворительно» (satisfactory): Block 1 completed [1];
- D «неудовлетворительно» (unsatisfactory): Block 1 not completed [1].

The forms of the plan and of the report list the mandatory conditions for fulfilling the plan of research activity [1]:

- only scientific (research) activity on the dissertation topic is taken into account [1];
- only published articles are taken into account [1];
- articles are taken into account in an edition from the List of Russian peer-reviewed scientific journals recommended by the Higher Attestation Commission in which the main scientific results of a Candidate of Sciences dissertation must be published, for the scientific specialty in which the aspirant studies [1];
- not more than one participation or publication within one conference, edition or event is taken into account — for a conference, either the report or the publication following it [1];
- in-person participation in a conference must be confirmed by a document naming the aspirant and the title of his report (a certificate, diploma, programme and so on), and the types of work in Blocks 1, 2 and 3 cannot be identical [1].

The plan forms are laid out by semester, each semester with Block 1 «достаточный уровень», Block 2 «компетентный уровень» and Block 3 «высокий уровень», the preparation of a report and presentation on the stages of the research for that semester, and the interim assessment by speaking at a research seminar with a report [1].

## Storage

- After approval the individual work plan is kept in the department responsible for organising study on aspirantura programmes [27].
- An electronic copy of the plan is sent to the aspirant and his research supervisor at their corporate electronic mail [28].
- After passing interim assessment the aspirant hands the report on the fulfilment of the individual plan of research activity and the research supervisor's review to that department [29].
- After the aspirant's expulsion, including in connection with mastering the programme, the plan, the reports and the reviews are transferred to the structural subdivision responsible for forming the students' personal files, for storage in the aspirant's personal file [30].

## Annexes

Annex 1 is the approximate form of the individual work plan in Russian and annex 2 the same form in English; annex 3 is the aspirant's application to change the individual plan of research activity, annex 4 the research supervisor's application for such changes and annex 5 the aspirant's application to change the individual curriculum; annexes 6–7 are the report on the fulfilment of the individual plan of research activity in Russian and in English, and annexes 8–9 the research supervisor's review in Russian and in English [1].

# Project Sector (Проектный сектор) of the Center for Corporate Training Programmes — place, tasks, functions and rights

## The order and the Sector's place in the structure

The Regulation (Положение) on the Project Sector of the Center for Corporate Training Programmes of the Institute of Additional Education of ANO VO «Innopolis University» was approved by the University's order No. ОД/АД-10/2024/09/02 of 2 September 2024; the order was issued in connection with the creation of a new structural subdivision and in order to optimise the activity of the University's structural subdivision, and it was signed by the Director for Development and HR Policy R.F. Valiev [1]. The order approved the attached Regulation, and the head of the project sector M.V. Sichenkova had to ensure that the subdivision's employees, as well as employees newly hired or transferred (moved) into the subdivision from other subdivisions, are familiarised with the Regulation against signature; control over the execution of the order and of the Regulation approved by it was retained by the signatory [1]. The Regulation regulates the Sector's activity and defines its tasks, functions, rights and duties [2]. The Sector is a structural subdivision of the Center for Corporate Training Programmes of the Institute of Additional Education and belongs to the category of training and demonstration centres (учебно-демонстрационные центры) [3]. The Sector reports directly to the head of the Center for Corporate Training Programmes [4].

In its activity the Sector is guided by the Constitution of the Russian Federation, the Labour Code of the Russian Federation, Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29 December 2012 «Об образовании в Российской Федерации» and other legislative and normative legal acts regulating the activity of higher education institutions, the University's Charter, decisions of the Academic, Supervisory and Operational councils of the University, orders and instructions of the University's director, other local normative acts of the University and this Regulation [5].

The Sector's structure, number of staff and staffing table, and changes to them, are approved by the director of the University [6]. The Sector is headed by its head, who is hired and dismissed by order of the director of the University [7]. Working conditions of the Sector's employees are determined by the employment contracts concluded with each employee and by the University's internal labour regulations [8]; their rights, duties and degree of responsibility are established by job descriptions [9]. Employees of the Sector may be involved, within their competences, in work under grants, projects, subsidies and other programmes implemented by the University [10]. The Regulation and additions and changes to it are approved by the director of the University or by another person authorised by him [11].

## Tasks

- the comprehensive system for implementing the University's additional professional education programmes [12]
- developing tools for the control and implementation of educational programmes [13]
- carrying out activity aimed at implementing the strategy of development and positioning of the University's educational projects and additional professional education programmes [14]
- joint coordination of the activity of the structural subdivisions that take part in creating and implementing educational projects of additional professional education of the University's Institute of Additional Education [15]
- developing proposals and implementing a set of measures to form a favourable environment for meeting the Sector's planned indicators [16]
- organising the necessary work processes and spaces for the successful conduct of educational projects and additional professional education programmes of the University's Institute of Additional Education [17]

## Functions

- developing and introducing plans (road maps) for the implementation of educational programmes with subsequent tracking of their fulfilment [18]
- creating all necessary conditions for the successful study of listeners and clients of the Center for Corporate Training Programmes [19]
- forming commercial offers, presentations and technical tasks for the landing page in order to promote and position the University's additional professional education programmes on the market [20]
- working with the project team, including the collection and distribution of tasks and duties and the building of the necessary communication [21]
- correcting the situation and testing hypotheses in order to improve the indicators of projects, products and services [22]
- working with clients and carrying out all necessary procedures to clarify the request [23]
- carrying out the functions assigned to the Sector by other local normative documents of the University and by instructions of the director of the University's Institute of Additional Education [24]
- controlling the fulfilment of tasks concerning the Sphere of the Sector's activity and presenting reports [25]

## Rights

In carrying out its activity the Sector's employees have the right to:

- request from the University's structural subdivisions the information and materials needed for the work within the Sector's competence [26]
- with the consent of the heads of the University's structural subdivisions, involve the employees of those subdivisions in preparing draft local acts and other documents and in developing and carrying out measures conducted by the Sector [27]
- take part in meetings held by the University's management when questions whose solution falls within the Sector's competences are discussed [28]
- put before the University's management proposals to cancel, amend or supplement the University's local acts [29]
- interact with bodies of state authority and administration, legal entities and the University's subdivisions on questions within the Sector's competence [30]

## Responsibility, interaction and reporting

The head of the Sector is responsible before the University's management for achieving the set goals and fulfilling the Sector's plans in the part of carrying out the Sector's main function, in accordance with the Labour Code of the Russian Federation and the legislation of the Russian Federation [31]. Interaction of the Sector with the University's other structural subdivisions is determined by the tasks and functions assigned to it by the Regulation [32], and interaction with other structural subdivisions takes place in the course of preparing contracts and other documents [33].

On the basis of the relevant local acts, orders and instructions the Sector may be responsible, within its activity, for preparing and keeping up to date information, certificates and reports [34].

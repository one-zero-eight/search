# PhD aspirantura appeal procedure

## Right to appeal

A student has the right to appeal the final certification procedure [1]. A written appeal alleging a procedural violation must be submitted in person to the Appeal Commission no later than the next working day after the Expert-Attestation Commission (ЭАК) result is announced [2], [3].

## Appeal documents

For consideration of the appeal, the ЭАK Chair forwards to the Appeal Commission the ЭАK protocol, the Chair's report, the thesis, supervisor reviews, and reviewer opinions [4].

## Appeal Commission review

The Appeal Commission reviews the appeal within 2 working days at a session attended by the ЭАK Chair and the appealing student (who must bring a passport); the session may proceed in the student's absence if they do not appear [5].

The Appeal Commission either rejects the appeal (if violations were not confirmed or did not affect the result) or upholds it (if violations were confirmed and affected the result) [6]. If upheld, the result is annulled; the protocol is forwarded to the ЭАK Chair the next working day; the student is given a chance to retake the final certification in the presence of the Chair or a member of the Appeal Commission, no later than the end of studies per the academic calendar [6]. The Appeal Commission's decision is final and not subject to review [7]. No appeal is accepted for a retake after a successful appeal [8]. The student is informed of the decision within 3 working days [9].

## Appeal Commission composition

The Appeal Commission operates for one calendar year, possibly by specialty [10], [11]. The Commission Chair is the University Director or authorized representative; the Commission includes the Chair and at least three members from University teaching staff with a Doctor of Sciences or Candidate of Sciences degree who are not on the ЭАK [12]. A Secretary is appointed but does not vote [12]. Compositions are approved no later than 30 calendar days before the start [12]. Sessions require at least two-thirds of members; decisions are by simple majority with the Chair having a casting vote [13], [14]. Sessions may use distance learning technologies [15]. Protocols are stored in the responsible structural unit and transferred to the archive [16], [17].

# Admission, reinstatement, transfer and expulsion of additional professional education (ДПО) listeners

## What the procedure governs

The Procedure for the admission, reinstatement (восстановление), transfer and expulsion of listeners (слушатели) under additional professional education (ДПО) programmes at ANO VO «Innopolis University» sets the general requirements for the procedure and conditions of admission, reinstatement, transfer and expulsion of students of additional professional programmes [1]. It was approved by order of the University of 18 September 2023 No. ОД/АД-10/2023/09/18 and signed by K.V. Semenikhin, Director [2].

It is based on Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29 December 2012 «Об образовании в Российской Федерации», the Government Decree of 15.09.2020 No. 1441 approving the Rules for the provision of paid educational services, the order of the Ministry of Education and Science of Russia of 01.07.2013 No. 499 on the organisation of educational activity under additional professional programmes, the University's Charter and other local acts [3], [4], [5], [6], [7], [8].

The procedure is binding on all officials and structural subdivisions of the University that deal with the admission, transfer, reinstatement and expulsion of students of additional professional programmes [9].

## Who may study and on what basis

- Citizens of the Russian Federation, stateless persons and foreign citizens, both those living in Russia and those who have come to Russia to study, are admitted to additional professional programmes [10].
- Persons who have secondary vocational and (or) higher education, and persons who are receiving secondary vocational and (or) higher education, are admitted to master additional professional programmes [11].
- Studies under additional professional programmes are provided on the basis of a contract concluded with the applicant and (or) with a natural or legal person that undertakes to pay for the education of the person to be enrolled [12].

## Admission rules

- Admission is conducted on the principles of equal conditions for all applicants [13].
- Admission to additional professional programmes is carried out throughout the whole calendar year [14].
- For educational programmes within programmes and projects approved by the President and the Government of the Russian Federation, admission follows education legislation with the specifics provided for by those programmes and projects [15].
- The number of places for admission to additional professional programmes is determined by the University [16].

## Informing the applicant

- Information about admission is provided through information and telecommunication networks, including the University's official website, or in any other accessible way that does not contradict the law [17].
- The University familiarises the applicant with its Charter, with the dates and registration numbers of the licence to carry out educational activity and of the state accreditation for the programmes it implements, and with the educational programmes and other documents governing the organisation of educational activity and the rights and duties of students [18].
- This is done by placing the documents on the University's official website, and the fact of familiarisation is recorded in the application for admission (annex 1 to the procedure) or in other documents required by the specifics of the programme [19].

## Application and documents

The applicant submits an application for admission addressed to the Director of the University [20]. Together with the application the applicant provides [21]:

- an identity and citizenship document (if any) and, for foreign citizens, a notarised translation of it [22];
- a diploma of secondary vocational and (or) higher education, a document on the recognition and equivalence of foreign education or qualification in Russia and (or) a notarised Russian translation of the foreign education document, with a copy; persons who are still studying must provide a certificate of study [23];
- a document confirming a change of personal data if they differ from the documents submitted, with a copy [24];
- consent to the processing of personal data (annex 2 to the procedure) [25];
- where special conditions have to be created, a document confirming disability or health limitations that require those conditions [26];
- an application on the declension of the surname (annex 3 to the procedure) [27];
- other documents at the applicant's discretion [28].

The documents may be submitted in person (or by an authorised representative), through postal operators, through the services of the University's electronic information and educational environment, by e-mail, or in other ways required by the specifics of the programme [29], [30], [31], [32], [33], [34].

- Unless the conditions of the programme provide otherwise, documents are submitted before the start of studies [35].
- Documents are accepted by the official or structural subdivision responsible for implementing the additional professional programme [36].
- The University transfers, processes, stores and provides the personal data received in connection with admission in accordance with the legislation on personal data [37].
- Applicants who provide knowingly false documents bear the liability established by the legislation of the Russian Federation [38].

## Accessibility of admission for people with disabilities

To make admission accessible for persons with health limitations, disabled children and disabled persons, the University provides the following [39]:

- for persons with visual impairments: adaptation of the official website to their special needs; placement of reference information in places accessible to blind and partially sighted persons and in an adapted form (large relief-contrast type with a capital letter height of at least 7,5 cm, on white or yellow background, duplicated in Braille); the presence of an assistant; the issue of alternative formats of printed materials; and access for a blind person using a guide dog to the University building, which has a place for the guide dog [40];
- for persons with hearing impairments: duplication of audio reference information (monitors able to broadcast subtitles) and provision of adequate audio means of reproducing information; and, for persons with disorders of the musculoskeletal system, material and technical conditions allowing unimpeded access to classrooms, canteens, toilets and other premises of the University and stay in them [41].

## Enrolment

- Enrolment is made by order of the Director of the University or another person authorised by the director [42].
- The order on enrolment is preceded by the conclusion of a contract on education [43].
- Admission takes place without entrance examinations, on the results of reviewing the documents submitted by the applicant, unless the educational programme provides otherwise [44].
- The University may refuse admission if the level of education obtained or being obtained does not meet the requirements of the law or of the additional professional programme [45]; if the documents submitted do not meet the requirements of the procedure [46]; or if there is no complete group under the programme and no free places in it [47], [48].
- A person enrolled in an additional professional programme acquires the status of «слушатель» [49].

## Reinstatement (восстановление)

- A person expelled on their own initiative or on the University's initiative before completing an additional professional programme may be reinstated within a year after expulsion, provided that the University still runs the corresponding programme, that the tuition fee under the contract on education is paid and that there is a complete group under the programme with free places in it [50].
- Reinstatement is made on the basis of an application addressed to the Director of the University (annex 4 to the procedure) [51].
- The decision on reinstatement is formalised by an order of the Director of the University or a person authorised by the director [52].

## Transfer (перевод)

- The transfer of listeners from one additional professional programme to another, and the transfer of listeners within one programme to another period of delivery of the programme (to another stream, поток), are made by order of the director on the basis of the listener's personal application (annex 5 and annex 6 to the procedure) [53].
- No fee is charged for a transfer; the parties conclude an additional agreement or, where necessary, a new contract establishing a new cost of educational services and (or) a new payment period and (or) a new name of the additional professional programme [53].
- The form of application for transfer to another stream (annex 6) records the listener's acknowledgement that studies under the programme begin anew after a transfer to another stream [2].
- A transfer from one programme to another is made subject to observing the period of study of the programme and to closing the difference in curricula that has arisen after crediting the results of previously studied subjects, courses, disciplines (modules) and other types of learning activity, within the established deadlines [54].
- The deadlines for closing the difference in curricula are set by the head of the structural subdivision that implements the programme, on the basis of the individual curriculum; studies under an individual curriculum follow the Regulation on the additional professional programme of the University [54].
- On a transfer from one programme to another, previously studied subjects, courses, disciplines (modules) may be credited; credit is given in accordance with the Regulation on the additional professional programme of the University [55].

## Termination of educational relations and expulsion

Educational relations are terminated on the following grounds [56]:

- on completion of education (completion of studies) under the additional professional programme [57];
- early, namely:
  - on the listener's initiative, on the basis of a personal application (annex 7 to the procedure) [58];
  - on the University's initiative where expulsion is applied to the listener as a disciplinary sanction, and where the listener fails to fulfil the duty to conscientiously master the educational programme and complete the curriculum — for an academic debt not cleared within the established deadlines, for failing the final attestation, for not appearing at the final attestation without a valid reason or for receiving the grade «неудовлетворительно» after passing the final attestation again [59];
  - where a violation of the admission procedure is established that led, through the listener's fault, to unlawful enrolment in the University [60];
  - for breach of the terms of the contract on education (late payment of the cost of paid educational services) [61];
  - where the University cannot properly fulfil its obligations to provide paid educational services owing to the listener's actions (inaction) [62];
  - for circumstances independent of the will of the listener and the University, including the liquidation of the University [63].

The expulsion of a listener from an additional professional programme is formalised by an order of the Director of the University or a person authorised by the director [64].

## Note on other pages

The regulation on distance learning under additional professional programmes says that the enrolment of listeners and acceptance of documents are handled according to the procedure for admission, reinstatement, transfer and expulsion approved by order No. ОД/ДПО-01/2016/09/05 of 05 September 2016 [65], while the procedure on this page was approved by an order of 18 September 2023 [2]; the pages therefore name different versions of the admission procedure.

# Protection of underage students from information harmful to their health and development (Regulation of 2 March 2021)

## The document

The page is the Regulation on the protection of underage students of ANO VO «Innopolis University» from information harmful to their health and development [1]. The page presents it as approved by order of the University of 2 March 2021 № ОД/АД-06/2021/03/02, signed by K.V. Semenikhin [1]. It was developed on the basis of Federal Law No. 436-ФЗ of 29.12.2010 «О защите детей от информации, причиняющей вред их здоровью и развитию», Federal Law No. 149-ФЗ of 27.07.2006, Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29.12.2012, the order of the Ministry of Communications of Russia of 16.06.2014 No. 161, other legal acts, the University's Charter, the Internal rules of procedure for students and other local acts [2]. The Regulation is a local act applied so that students who have not reached the age of eighteen and have not been declared fully capable («обучающиеся», «дети») do not receive information products whose distribution among children is prohibited by Federal Law No. 436 [3]. The wiki copy was read by OCR, so its numbers and dates should be checked against the PDF [1].

## What counts as harmful information

- Information harmful to the health and/or development of children is information prohibited for distribution among children [4] and information whose distribution among children of certain age categories is restricted [5].
- Prohibited for distribution among children is information: prompting children to actions threatening their life and/or health, including causing harm to their health or suicide [6]; capable of evoking a wish to use narcotic, psychotropic and/or intoxicating substances, tobacco products, alcohol and alcohol-containing products, beer and drinks made on its basis, to take part in gambling, to engage in prostitution, vagrancy or begging [7]; justifying or excusing the admissibility of violence and/or cruelty or prompting violent actions against people or animals, except the cases provided by Federal Law No. 436 [8]; denying family values, promoting non-traditional sexual relations and forming disrespect for parents and/or other family members [9]; justifying unlawful behaviour [10]; containing obscene language [11]; containing pornographic information [12]; about a minor who suffered from unlawful actions or inaction, including his surname, name, patronymic, photo and video images of him, his parents and legal representatives, his date of birth, an audio recording of his voice, his place of residence or temporary stay, place of study or work and other information allowing his identity to be established directly or indirectly [13].
- Restricted for distribution among children of certain age categories is information: presented as an image or description of cruelty, physical and/or mental violence, a crime or another anti-social action [14]; evoking fear, horror or panic in children, including an image or description in a form degrading human dignity of non-violent death, illness, suicide, an accident, a crash or a catastrophe and/or their consequences [15]; presented as an image or description of sexual relations between a man and a woman [16]; containing swear words and expressions that are not obscene language [17].

## Terms used

- Access of students to information is the possibility of obtaining and using freely distributed information [18]; an information product sign is a graphic and/or textual designation of the information product according to the classification under part 3 of article 6 of Federal Law No. 436 [19]; a spectacular event (зрелищное мероприятие) is a demonstration of an information product in a place accessible to students and where a significant number of persons not belonging to the usual family circle are present, including through theatrical, cultural-educational and entertainment events [20].
- Information security of students is a state of protection in which there is no risk of information harming their health and/or physical, mental, spiritual or moral development [21]; an information product is media production, printed, audiovisual production on any media, computer programmes and databases, and information distributed through spectacular events and through telecommunication networks, including the Internet, and mobile radiotelephone networks [22]; classification is the distribution of information products by age categories of students in the procedure set by Federal Law No. 436 [23].
- Places accessible to students are public places where access to and/or presence of a student is not prohibited, including public places where a student has access to media products and/or information products placed in telecommunication networks [24].

## Turnover of information products and age marks

- Turnover of information products containing the information prohibited for children under sub-clause 1.3.1 is not allowed, except in the cases provided by the Regulation [25]; in places accessible to students such turnover is not allowed without administrative and organisational measures and technical and software-hardware means of protecting students from that information [26].
- Turnover of information products containing the information under clause 1.3 without an information product sign is not allowed, except textbooks and study guides recommended or admitted for use in the educational process under the legislation on education [27], television programmes and broadcasts transmitted live without preliminary recording [28], information products distributed by radio broadcasting [29], information products demonstrated through spectacular events [30], periodicals specialising in socio-political or production-practical information [31], information distributed through telecommunication networks, including the Internet, except online publications [32], and comments and/or messages placed by readers of an online publication on its site in the procedure set by the editorial office [33].
- The classification of information products is made by the categories established by Federal Law No. 436 [34]. The University's structural subdivisions designate the category by a sign and/or a textual warning: for children who have not reached the age of six — a digit and a plus sign [35]; for children who have reached the age of six — a digit and a plus sign and/or the warning «для детей старше шести лет» [36]; «12» and a plus sign and/or «для детей старше 12 лет» [37]; «16» and a plus sign and/or «для детей старше 16 лет» [38]; «18» and a plus sign and/or «запрещено для детей» for information products prohibited for children [39].
- Before a film is shown in film and video servicing the sign and/or textual warning is placed in the procedure set by the federal body authorised by the Government, and the sign must be not less than five per cent of the screen area [40]; the sign must be not less than five per cent of the area of a poster or other announcement of a spectacular event, of an announcement of a film or video showing, and of an entrance ticket, invitation or other document giving the right to attend the event [41].
- The sign is placed in information products placed in telecommunication networks in the cases provided by Federal Law No. 436 [42]; the textual warning is made in Russian and, in the cases set by Federal Law No. 53-ФЗ of 01.06.2005 on the state language, in the state languages of the republics within the Russian Federation, other languages of the peoples of the Russian Federation or foreign languages [43].

## Events held at the University

- Events held in the buildings and on the territory of the University must not contain information classified by the legislation on protecting children as prohibited for children and/or assume its distribution [44].
- By order of the director or of the head coordinating the relevant line of activity or structural subdivision, an event containing information prohibited for children may be permitted, with appointment of a person ensuring control over observance of the legislation on protecting children [45]. The organisers must then provide a plan of the event with a minute-by-minute schedule for each point of the plan, and the appointed person, where children are present, must ensure their organised withdrawal well before the demonstration of the prohibited information so that children cannot perceive it [45].

## Internet, printed products and the Library

- Access to information distributed through telecommunication networks, including the Internet, in places accessible to students is given by employees of the structural subdivisions only on condition that administrative and organisational measures and technical and software-hardware means of protecting students from harmful information are applied [46].
- When organising information and technological support of the educational process the structural subdivisions carry out a set of organisational-administrative measures aimed at restricting access of students of different age categories to internet information harmful to health and/or development and not corresponding to educational tasks, and classify information products [47].
- Pages of the University's corporate site (portal) may contain an information product sign (including in machine-readable form) and/or a textual warning corresponding to one of the categories under Federal Law No. 436, and the classification is made by the structural subdivisions themselves under the Regulation; announcements inviting students to take part in creating information products harmful to their health and/or development may not be placed on the corporate site (portal) [48].
- Control over observance of Federal Law No. 436 and the Regulation when employees of the structural subdivisions place information on the corporate portal (site) is exercised by the person heading the University's activity in the field of functioning and development of the corporate portal (site) [49].
- The first and last pages of a newspaper and the cover of a printed or other polygraphic product prohibited for children must not contain information harmful to the health and/or development of students when it is distributed to an indefinite circle of persons [50]; printed information products prohibited for children may be distributed at the University only in sealed packaging [51].
- In the premises of the University's Library conditions are created making information products marked «18+» or bearing the warning «запрещено для детей» inaccessible to children: they are placed in a room separated from the common hall and locked with a key kept by a Library employee, and such products are issued from the library fund only to persons who have reached eighteen [52].

## Protection measures and who is responsible

- The director of the University's center for support of students and graduates is appointed the responsible person for applying the administrative and organisational measures of protecting students from harmful information, taking account of the specifics of the turnover of information products prohibited for children, and for checking how they are applied [53]. The responsible person ensures that all necessary measures for legality in the turnover of information products are taken and, where necessary, that local acts determining the conditions of that turnover in the relevant structural subdivisions are drafted [53].
- The measures also include: familiarising employees whose labour duties include organising and carrying out the turnover of information products prohibited for students with the legislation on protecting children and with the University's local acts [54]; placing on information stands in places accessible to students, and otherwise bringing to third persons, information about the University's local acts issued on the basis of Federal Law No. 436 [55]; placing those local acts on the corporate site (portal) together with information about the application of administrative and organisational measures and ensuring free access to them [56].
- Control over the correspondence of the content and artistic design of printed publications and polygraphic products (including exercise books, book covers, bookmarks), including the fiction, scientific and popular-science literature issued to students in the Library, to the age characteristics of students is carried out by Library employees and by the structural subdivisions using those publications and products [57]. When ordered publications arrive in the Library fund the Library employees check for the information product sign set by the manufacturer, and where the sign is absent the classification is carried out in the procedure set by Federal Law No. 436 [57].
- Control over the correspondence of the content of scripts, theme evenings and other spectacular mass events and of the film and television episodes used in them to the requirements for information products for the relevant age group is also a protection measure [58].
- During a class within the curriculum the teacher conducting the class controls the students' use of the Internet [59]: he observes the purposeful use of the computer and the Internet by students [60], forbids the student's further work on the Internet where the student breaks the requirements of the Regulation [61], and takes measures to stop access to resources unrelated to the educational process [62].

## Internal control and liability

- Internal control over observance of the legislation on protecting children, over the correspondence of the protection measures to the Regulation and other local acts, over the turnover of information products prohibited for students, over the use of internet resources during the educational process and over observance of the Regulation is exercised by the structural subdivisions that use, apply, distribute and produce information products, under the guidance of the responsible person [63].
- The responsible person sends to the person appointed responsible for ensuring safe internet access information about identified resources containing information prohibited by the legislation of the Russian Federation and information incompatible with the tasks of education and upbringing, so that access to them is restricted [64].
- Where situations that may lead students to receive prohibited information products are forecast or identified, the structural subdivisions take measures to eliminate them [65].
- Appeals, complaints and claims about violations of that legislation, including about the non-correspondence of the protection measures to the Regulation and other local acts and about students' access to prohibited information, are considered by the responsible persons [1].
- Violation of that legislation and of the Regulation entails liability under the legislation of the Russian Federation [66].

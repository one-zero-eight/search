# Grant competition for foreign citizens from far-abroad countries — bachelor's and master's study (Regulation of 19 February 2024)

## The document

The Regulation on the procedure for holding the competition for a grant for study under higher education programmes — bachelor's and master's programmes — for foreign citizens from the countries of the far abroad (Положение о порядке проведения конкурса на получение гранта на обучение по образовательным программам высшего образования — программам бакалавриата и программам магистратуры для иностранных граждан из стран дальнего зарубежья) was approved by the University's order No. ОД/АД-01/2024/02/19 of 19 February 2024 and put into effect from 1 December 2023 [1]. The order was signed by the Vice-Rector — head of the academic policy and organisation of educational activity management, E.S. Bobrov [1]. The Regulation determines the procedure for organising and holding the competition, the criteria for selecting participants and the conditions of participation in it [2].

## Purpose and openness

- The competition aims to support talented graduates of schools and of institutions of secondary vocational education who are not citizens of the Russian Federation and wish to develop their knowledge in information technology, to involve students in new forms of organising the study process and to spread the best educational practices [3].
- The University organises the competition, which is open, and information about it is published on the Organizer's Official Website, in social networks and in other public sources [4], [5].

## What the grant is

- A grant is a sum set by the Organizer that covers part or all of the cost of educational services for study during the standard period of study (starting from the first course) on the University's bachelor's and master's programmes [6].
- The Organizer determines by a separate decision the categories of winners and the volume of the grant for each category [7].
- The grant fund is formed from the Organizer's funds, and the size of a grant for each category of winners is set within 25 % to 100 % of the cost of study [8].
- Grants cannot be replaced by other kinds of rewards and are not paid in a money equivalent [9].
- The cost of educational services is determined annually before the academic year, by 31 May of the year of admission [10].
- On a transfer of a grant recipient from one University programme to another the grant is not withdrawn, though the terms of the grant agreement and of the education contract may change because of the specifics of the programme [11].
- The recipient's duty to pay for study and the University's duty to provide the grant are homogeneous monetary claims, to which the rules on set-off under art. 410 of the Civil Code may be applied [12], [13].

## Who may take part

- Citizens of states that do not belong to the Commonwealth of Independent States (as listed in the resolution of the Supreme Soviet of the Russian Federation No. 2014-1 of 12.12.1991) may take part if they study in the final class of an institution of secondary (full) general education or have graduated from one, if they are graduates of institutions of secondary vocational education (for bachelor's programmes), or if they are students or graduates of higher education institutions (for master's programmes) [14].
- Whether a foreign educational institution corresponds to these conditions is determined from the candidate's documents on education, which must be legalised in the procedure established by Russian legislation and translated into Russian, unless an international treaty provides otherwise [14].
- Persons who are or have been students on the University's higher education programmes may not take part, except where participation is envisaged within reinstatement after expulsion from the University [14].
- A participant may take part in the competition no more than once in each year of its holding [15].

## Dates

- The competition is held annually from 1 October of the year preceding admission to 10 August of the year of admission [16].
- Grants pay for study from the beginning of the academic year that starts after the end of the current year's competition [16].

## Stage 1 — registration and online testing

- The first stage consists of registration and primary testing of the participants, held online on the Official Website within the periods set by the Organizer [17].
- A potential participant must register on the Official Website, pass online testing and provide a questionnaire, a motivation letter in English, a portfolio in English, copies of the school certificate or of the diploma of secondary vocational or higher education, a certificate of knowledge of English if available, a signed consent to personal data processing and other documents requested by the Organizer [18].
- The passing score of the tests is 45 % of correct answers in the English test for bachelor's programmes and 50 % for master's programmes, and 45 % of correct answers in the profile test [19].
- Participants who meet all the conditions and reach the passing score are invited to the second stage by a message to the e-mail address given at registration, and must confirm their participation within 7 (seven) calendar days, failing which they are considered to have withdrawn [20].

## Stage 2 — selection

- The second stage is an in-person selection; it is held remotely by electronic means of communication if the participant is outside the Russian Federation, and/or at regional venues and/or on the Organizer's territory in Innopolis if the participant is in Russia [21].
- It is held in a distance (online) format using a system of cyberproctoring [22].
- It may be held repeatedly according to a schedule that the Organizer draws up itself, between 1 December of the year preceding admission and 1 August of the year of admission; a participant who has passed the first stage may take part in it only once, within the periods set in the invitation, and it lasts up to thirty days at the Organizer's discretion [23].
- It includes an interview with a representative of the University's academic staff, solving tasks from the field of information technology, and participation in measures assessing communicative effectiveness, ability to work in a team, leadership qualities, stress resistance and conflict tolerance [24].
- Participants who wish to study on a programme taught in English also take an English language test at the second stage [25].
- Participants seeking a grant for programmes of the federal project «Передовые инженерные школы» also have an interview with representatives of the University's partner companies [26].

## Winners and grant recipients

- Winners are determined by a decision of the Competition Commission taking into account the results of the second stage [27].
- The composition and number of members of the Competition Commission are approved by the Organizer; they include the Vice-Rector — head of the academic policy and organisation of educational activity management, the Director for Development and HR Policy, the head of the centre for preparation of admission and development of students, or persons replacing them, and other persons determined by the Organizer [28].
- The results of the competition and the list of winners are approved by the Competition Commission within four months after the end of each selection held within the second stage [29].
- The Commission's decision is final and not subject to appeal [30].
- The Organizer determines annually and independently the possible number of winners [31].
- The third stage — the determination of the grant recipients from among the winners — runs until 10 August of the year of admission inclusive [32].
- By 26 August of the year of admission the Organizer forms the list of grant recipients on the basis of the Commission's decisions, the categories of winners and the requirements for grant recipients, and communicates the results to them at the e-mail addresses given at registration [33].
- A winner receives the grant if he acquires the status of grant recipient by passing the admission procedure and submitting the documents established by the University's admission rules and by Russian legislation [34].
- To receive the grant the recipient must conclude an education contract with the University before the date of the enrolment order, conclude a grant agreement with the University before the start of the academic year that follows the current competition, and take part in the Summer School organised by the University if it is held, free of charge [35].
- Failure to meet the requirements deprives the winner of the right to the grant, and grants not claimed before the start of the academic year are not issued and are not carried over to the next year [36].

## Obligation to work

- A grant is provided on condition that the recipient accepts obligations to work [37].
- A recipient on a programme of the federal project «Передовые инженерные школы» undertakes to work for a partner company or partner of the University or, in cases directly set by the grant agreement, at a main workplace in the city of Innopolis with a legal entity that is a resident of the Innopolis special economic zone and/or is registered or has a subdivision in Innopolis, in the profession obtained, with an employment contract [38].
- A recipient on the master's programme «Информационное технологическое предпринимательство» undertakes to work at a main workplace in his profession in a technological startup or, in cases set by the grant agreement, for a partner of the University or an entity registered or having a subdivision in Innopolis that is a resident of the special economic zone [39].
- In other cases the recipient undertakes to work at a main workplace in his profession for a partner of the University or for a legal entity in Innopolis that is a resident of the special economic zone and/or is registered or has a subdivision there [40].
- A technological startup is a legal entity founded (co-founded) by the grant recipient, registered under Russian law in any legal form, a resident of the Innopolis special economic zone, whose purpose is to bring technological products or services to the market [41].
- A partner company is a high-technology company or organisation implementing projects on «end-to-end» technologies of the digital economy and priority directions of technology development that is the University's partner within «Передовые инженерные школы» [42].
- A partner of the University is a Russian company or organisation with a cooperation agreement with the University and/or designated as such by an order of its director [43].
- The period of the work obligation at normal working hours from the moment of employment depends on the size of the grant [44], [45]:
- 12 months — 100 % of the cost of study [44]
- 12 months — 70 % of the cost of study [44]
- 7 months — 60 % of the cost of study [44]
- 6 months — 50 % of the cost of study [44]
- 3 months — 25 % of the cost of study [44]
- If the grant is reduced unilaterally during study in the procedure set by the grant agreement, the work period is determined by the smallest grant size that applied during study [45].
- For certain categories of winners the Organizer may by a separate decision increase or reduce the work obligation or exclude the duty to work [46].
- The special conditions, organisation and terms of employment are determined in the grant agreement concluded under the form set in the annex to the Regulation [47].

## Repeat (partial) grant after expulsion

- Reinstatement for study is an academic right of the student; reinstatement of persons expelled before completing the programme is governed by the Regulation on transfer, expulsion and reinstatement and on academic leave [48].
- A decision on a repeat (partial) grant is taken on the personal application of the expelled grant recipient, which is considered by the Competition Commission [49].
- A repeat grant may be given to persons who studied at the University at the expense of a grant covering part or all of the cost of study and were expelled for failing to master the programme and to fulfil the curriculum; on reinstatement to paid places they may receive it within the following 5 years after expulsion, no more than once, and it is used to pay for study after reinstatement [50].
- The volume of the repeat grant is set by the Competition Commission and may not exceed the grant held before expulsion: a grant covering 71–100 % gives a repeat grant of no more than 70 %, 61–70 % gives no more than 60 %, 51–60 % gives no more than 50 %, and 26–50 % gives no more than 25 % [51].
- Persons whose grant covered 25 % or less of the cost of study receive no repeat grant covering part of the cost [52].
- The repeat grant is issued on condition that the reinstated recipient pays, in the semester (for master's students, the trimester) to which he is reinstated, the part of the cost that the grant does not cover; the rest is paid as the education contract provides [53].
- That payment must be made within 14 (fourteen) working days after the order of the University's director on reinstatement [1].
- The Competition Commission may hold separate selection tests for persons reinstated from among former students who applied for a repeat grant [54].
- The cost of study, taking account of the grounds and procedure for its reduction and of the individual curriculum formed on reinstatement, is determined in the education contract concluded with the grant recipient on reinstatement [55].

## Rights and duties of participants; the Organizer's rights

- A participant may receive information about the dates and rules of the competition [56] and must perform all actions connected with participation and with obtaining a grant within the periods set by the Regulation [57].
- By taking part a participant agrees to give advertising interviews, including on radio and television and in other mass media, and to take part in making graphic advertising materials without remuneration, with all copyright belonging to the Organizer and the free use of his name and of his photo and video images [58].
- Taking part confirms that the participant has read and accepts all the rules; failure to comply with any rule or condition deprives him of the right to a grant in that competition period [59].
- The Organizer may at any time change the Regulation, including the dates, or cancel the competition, keeping its rights and duties on grants for winners already determined, and notifies participants by a notice on the Official Website [60].
- The Organizer may ban further participation of a person who forges documents or benefits from forged documents, violates the Regulation, acts destructively or breaches the rules of conduct at the venues of the competition [61].
- The Organizer may refuse a grant to a participant found to have committed fraud or deception, to have violated the Regulation or to have provided false information [62].
- The Organizer is not liable where a participant does not receive notifications because of outdated or incorrect information, or does not perform his duties or the conditions of participation [63].

## Final provisions

- The Regulation enters into force on publication, is not limited in time and applies until it is cancelled or a new edition is adopted, and it extends to grant recipients who took part in the competition for programmes whose delivery starts in the 2024/2025 academic year [64].
- Requests for clarifications go to the University's admissions and recruitment department at the e-mail address given in the document or by phone at +7 (843) 203-92-53 (extension 254) [65].

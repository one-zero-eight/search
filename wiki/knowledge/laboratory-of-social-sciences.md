# Laboratory of Social Sciences (Лаборатория социальных наук) — place, tasks, functions and rights

## The document

The Regulation on the Laboratory of Social Sciences (Положение о Лаборатории социальных наук) of Innopolis University was approved by a University order of 9 January 2019 № 12/2019/01/09; the wiki copy is a scanned document read by OCR, so numbers and dates should be checked against the PDF [1]. The Regulation governs the Laboratory's activity and determines its tasks, functions, rights and duties [1].

## Place in the structure

- The Laboratory of Social Sciences is a structural subdivision of the Institute of Humanities and Social Sciences of the Faculty of Humanities and Social Sciences of the University [1].
- It reports directly to its own head (руководитель/начальник); in the absence of the head — to the director of the institute and the dean of the faculty [1].
- In its work the Laboratory is guided by the Constitution of the Russian Federation, the Labour Code of the Russian Federation, Federal Law of 29 December 2012 № 273-ФЗ «Об образовании в Российской Федерации», other legislative and normative acts on higher education institutions, the University's Charter, the decisions of the Academic, Supervisory and Operations Councils, the orders and instructions of the director, other local acts and this Regulation [1].
- The structure, number of staff and staffing table of the Laboratory, and changes to them, are approved by the director of the University [1].
- The Laboratory is headed by a head who is hired and dismissed by order of the director of the University [1].
- The working conditions of the Laboratory's workers are determined by the employment contracts concluded with each worker and by the University's internal labour regulations; their rights, duties and degree of responsibility are set by job descriptions [1].
- The Regulation and the additions and changes to it are approved by the director of the University or by a person authorised by him [1].

## Tasks

The tasks of the Laboratory are [1]:

- creating a scientific-methodical base for fundamental and applied research in accordance with the University's research plans [1]
- helping to form the skills of research activity in the University's students, aspirants and teachers [1]
- widening and strengthening the University's scientific links with institutions and organisations close in the nature of their activity [1]
- propagating scientific knowledge among the public [1]
- carrying out research within grants in the Laboratory's profile by its workers on their own initiative, subject to written agreement with the Laboratory's management, and under contracts paid from grants received by the University and from budget and off-budget funds [1]
- preparing for publication and publishing annotated and abstracted materials on the latest experience and the University's teachers and students' own materials and developments [1]

## Functions

The Laboratory performs the following functions [1]:

- developing research programmes in the Laboratory's field of activity [1]
- improving the methods, technologies, techniques and instruments of research work [1]
- developing and publishing scientific-methodical aids on the results of the Laboratory's activity [1]
- publishing scientific articles in journals, including those in the international citation databases Web of Science (WoS), Scopus and others [1]
- holding thematic seminars, practicums and consultations for the University's teachers, aspirants and students [1]
- preparing practical (experimental) material for inclusion in candidate and doctoral dissertations [1]
- drawing the University's students into research activity [1]
- revealing the scientific potential of the University's young specialists [1]

## Rights

In their activity the Laboratory's workers may [1]:

- request from the University's structural subdivisions the information and materials needed for the work within the Laboratory's competence [1]
- with the consent of the heads of those subdivisions, involve their workers in preparing drafts of local acts and other documents and in developing and carrying out the Laboratory's events [1]
- take part in meetings held by the University's management where matters within the Laboratory's competence are discussed [1]
- submit to the University's management proposals to repeal, amend or supplement local acts [1]
- interact with state authorities and administration, legal entities and the University's subdivisions on matters of the Laboratory's competence [1]

## Interaction and reporting

- Interaction with other structural subdivisions is determined by the tasks and functions placed on the Laboratory by the Regulation; interaction with the department for attracting grants concerns participation in research, industrial projects and grants; with other subdivisions it concerns drafts of local acts, orders, instructions, contracts and other documents, and conclusions, written and oral references on matters of the Laboratory's competence [1].
- The Laboratory is responsible for preparing reports on research, industrial projects and grants and for passing them in time to the University's internal structural subdivisions [1].
- It submits reports according to the requirements of the Ministry of Education of the Russian Federation and other state statistics bodies, as needed for managerial and financial decisions [1].

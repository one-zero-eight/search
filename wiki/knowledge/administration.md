# Administration of the university — place, tasks, functions and rights

## Document and place in the structure

The page holds the Regulation on the Administration of the autonomous non-commercial organisation of higher education «Innopolis University» (Положение об Администрации); the Regulation regulates the activity of the Administration and determines its tasks, functions, rights and duties [1]. The front matter carries the place «г. Иннополис» and an approval block «УТВЕРЖДАЮ» with the director of the University K.V. Semenikhin [2]. The text of the page is OCR output with garbled characters — the approval line reads «Директор?іуУниверситет Иннополис» К.В. Семенихин ^, and the clause on the category reads «относится К категории» [2].

The wiki holds this Regulation in several copies that read alike clause by clause: this page [1] and the copies [3], [4], [5], [6], [7], [8], [9] and [10]; what is said below therefore rests on any of them [1].

- The Administration is a structural subdivision of the University and belongs to the category «иные структурные подразделения» [11].
- Its employees report directly to the director of the University, and in his absence to the person replacing him [12].
- In its activity the Administration follows the Constitution of the Russian Federation, the Labour Code of the Russian Federation, Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29 December 2012 «Об образовании в Российской Федерации» and other legislative and normative legal acts of the Russian Federation regulating the activity of higher education institutions, the University's Charter, decisions of the Academic, Supervisory and Operational councils of the University, orders and instructions of the University's director, other local normative acts of the University and this Regulation [13].
- The structure, the number of staff and the staffing table of the Administration, and changes to them, are approved by the director of the University [14].
- The Administration is headed by the director of the University, who is appointed and dismissed by a decision of the University's founders [15].
- The working conditions of the Administration's employees are determined by the employment contracts concluded with each employee and by the University's internal labour regulations [2].
- The rights, duties and degree of responsibility of the Administration's employees are established by job descriptions [16].
- Employees of the Administration may be involved, within their competences, in carrying out work under grants, projects, subsidies and other programmes implemented by the University [17].
- The Regulation and the additions and changes to it are approved by the director of the University or by another person authorised by him [18].

## Tasks

The Regulation's section on tasks lists the following [2]:

- implementing the development strategy in accordance with the directions of the University's activity defined in the Charter and other documents [19]
- managing and organising the University's current activity and improving the existing management processes [20]
- forming a highly qualified team of the University by organising the proper selection and placement of staff, raising their qualification and distributing labour rationally [21]
- providing an effective and efficient mechanism for delimiting the functions, powers and responsibility of employees and establishing uniform approaches in defining their job duties and the qualification requirements imposed on them [22]
- directing and coordinating the activity of the University's subordinate subdivisions [23]
- external interaction with third-party organisations, state and municipal bodies and public organisations between the counterparty and the University [24]

## Functions

The Regulation's section on functions states [2]:

- in terms of administrative and managerial functions, general management is exercised by the director [25]
- the Administration performs the functions of operational management of the University [26]

## Rights

In carrying out its activity the employees of the Administration have the right to [2]:

- request from the University's structural subdivisions the information and materials needed for the work within the Administration's competence [27]
- with the consent of the heads of the University's structural subdivisions, involve the employees of those subdivisions in preparing draft local acts and other documents and in developing and carrying out measures conducted by the Administration [28]
- take part in meetings held by the University's management when discussing questions whose solution falls within the Administration's competence [29]
- put before the University's management proposals to cancel, amend or supplement the University's local acts [30]
- interact with bodies of state (municipal) authority and administration, legal entities and the University's subdivisions on questions within the Administration's competence [31]
- submit proposals on raising efficiency and improving the activity of all subdivisions of the University in the part concerning the competence of the subdivision's employees [32]

The section on rights also states, under a separate number, that the employees are obliged to observe the norms of the legislation in force [33]; the Regulation has no separate section on duties [2].

## Interaction and reporting

Interaction of the Administration with the University's other structural subdivisions is determined by the tasks and functions assigned to it by this Regulation [34]. A second provision appears on the page under the same clause number: in carrying out the functions assigned to it the subdivision interacts with all structural subdivisions in accordance with the questions within the Administration's competence [34].

On the basis of the relevant local normative acts of the University and relevant assignments, the Administration may, within its activity, be responsible for preparing information, certificates and reports and for keeping them up to date [35].

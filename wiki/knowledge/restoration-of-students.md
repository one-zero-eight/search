# Restoration (reinstatement) of students at Innopolis University — Regulation of 12 May 2026

## The document

Restoration (восстановление) is regulated by the Regulation on the procedure and grounds for the transfer, expulsion and restoration of students at ANO VO «Innopolis University», approved by order of 12 May 2026 № ОД/АД-01/2026/05/12 and signed by the Director D.V. Vandyukov [1]. The wiki copy is a scan read by OCR, so its numbers and dates should be checked against the PDF [1].

## Who may be restored

- Restoration for obtaining education is an academic right of the student [2]. It is made on the basis of a personal application of the person earlier expelled from the University (annex 6 to the Regulation) [2].
- The Regulation governs restoration of persons expelled before completing the main professional educational programme: those earlier expelled at their own initiative and those earlier expelled at the University's initiative [3].
- Restoration of persons expelled from other educational organisations is not allowed [4].

## Time limits and conditions

- A person expelled at their own initiative before completing the programme may be restored within 5 (five) years after expulsion, where there are free places and with the previous conditions of study preserved (subject to clause 4.13), but not earlier than the end of the academic year (semester) in which the person was expelled [5].
- A person expelled at the University's initiative before completing the programme may be restored within 5 (five) years after expulsion, where there are free places, but not earlier than the end of the academic year (semester) in which the person was expelled [6].
- Restoration of persons expelled before passing the first interim assessment is not allowed [7].
- If the field of training (or the profile of the programme, or the scientific specialty) in which the person studied is no longer offered, restoration is made to another field, profile or scientific specialty by agreement with the person being restored [8].

## Grounds for refusal

A refusal to restore may rest on expulsion applied as a disciplinary sanction, or on an established volume of academic difference that would mean restoration with a downgrade of the course by more than one year [9].

## On which course and group restoration is made

- A student expelled for academic debt is restored from the start of the semester of the course in which the academic debts are held, except for the students mentioned in clause 4.7 [10].
- A student expelled as not having started classes may be restored from the start of the semester in which the student was expelled, except for those admitted to the first course [11].
- A student expelled for breach of the education contract (late payment for study) may be restored to the same group from which the student was expelled if the financial debt is cleared: for full-time students within a month of expulsion, and for part-time students within the semester before the start of the next interim assessment [12].

## Repeat state final attestation

A person who did not pass the state final attestation and was expelled as not having fulfilled the duties to master the programme and fulfil the curriculum may pass the state final attestation again not earlier than 10 (ten) months and not later than 5 (five) years after the term of the state final attestation that was not passed; on application the person is restored for a period set by the University, but not less than the period provided by the calendar study schedule for the state final attestation of the programme, and restoration for passing the state final attestation is made without charge [13].

## Application and decision

- The application for restoration is submitted not later than 30 (thirty) calendar days before the start of the semester in which restoration is planned [14].
- The question of the possibility of restoration is considered not later than 10 (ten) working days before the start of that semester [15].
- The attestation commission establishes the difference between the part of the curriculum studied earlier and the current curriculum under the University's local act on credit; on the commission's decision the unit responsible for organising educational activity develops the individual curriculum under a local act, and the volume of the programme and the maximum annual volume are set by the federal state educational standards, federal state requirements and local acts [15].
- The fact of consent to the conditions of restoration is recorded by the student in the application for restoration [16].
- Restoration is made by order of the director or another official authorised by him on the basis of the personal application and the protocol of the attestation commission; where restoration is under an education contract, the contract is concluded before the order [17].
- Where the document of previous education was given out of the personal file on expulsion, the student must present it for keeping in the personal file until the end of study [18].
- A repeated provision of a grant for study on restoration, including its volume and the procedure of issue, is determined by the University's local acts on the competitions for a grant for study in higher education programmes [19].
- On restoration of a foreign citizen or stateless person the University notifies other organisations in the list, procedure and terms set by the corresponding local act [20].

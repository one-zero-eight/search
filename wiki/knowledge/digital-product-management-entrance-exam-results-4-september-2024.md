# Entrance examination for the master's programme «Управление Цифровым продуктом» (4 September 2024) — published results

## What the document is

The page is the published protocol of the entrance examination for the master's programme «Управление Цифровым продуктом» (digital product management) conducted by Innopolis University on its own, under the heading «Результаты вступительного испытания проводимого АНО ВО "Университет Иннополис" самостоятельно» [1].

The date of the entrance examination is 04.09.2024, given both in the title of the document and in the heading table of the protocol [1]. For each participant the table gives the number, the СНИЛС or unique code, the score (оценка) and the same score written out in words (оценка прописью) [1].

## Results recorded

The protocol records four results [1]:

- number 1 — identifier [скрыто] — 63 (Шестьдесят три) [1]
- number 2 — identifier [скрыто] — 44 (Сорок четыре) [1]
- number 3 — identifier [скрыто] — 40 (Сорок) [1]
- number 4 — identifier [скрыто] — 42 (Сорок два) [1]

The highest score in the protocol is 63 and the lowest is 40 [1].

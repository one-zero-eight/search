# Admission to bachelor's and master's programmes 2024/2025 — programmes, places and quotas (rules of 15 January 2024)

## The document

The rules of admission to bachelor's and master's programmes of ANO VO «Innopolis University» for the 2024/2025 academic year were approved by the University's order No. ОД/АД-02/2024/01/15 of 15 January 2024, signed by Rector A.V. Gasnikov [1]. They regulate admission of citizens of the Russian Federation, foreign citizens and stateless persons (поступающие) to bachelor's programmes and to master's programmes (clauses 1 and 3 of the rules) [1]. They were developed on the basis of Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29 December 2012 as amended by Federal Law No. 264-ФЗ of 24 June 2023, Decree of the President No. 268 of 9 May 2022, orders of the Ministry of Science and Higher Education No. 1076 of 21 August 2020 and No. 722 of 6 August 2021, the University's Charter and other legal acts [1].

Admission is announced on the basis of a licence for educational activity issued by the Federal Service for Supervision in Education and Science on 25 December 2015 without a term, registration number Л035-00115-26/00096117 [1]. Admission is to the first course [1].

## Programmes on offer

- Bachelor's programmes of area 09.03.01 «Информатика и вычислительная техника» with the profiles «Анализ данных и искусственный интеллект» and «Математические основы искусственного интеллекта», and of area 15.03.06 «Мехатроника и робототехника» with the profile «Робототехника»; the qualification is bachelor, the full-time programme lasts 4 years [1].
- A bachelor's programme within the federal project «Передовые инженерные школы»: area 09.03.01, profile «Инженерия информационных систем», qualification bachelor, 4 years full-time [1].
- Master's programmes of area 09.04.01 «Информатика и вычислительная техника» with the profiles «Робототехника и компьютерное зрение», «Технологическое предпринимательство», «Управление цифровым продуктом», «Управление на основе данных», «Кибербезопасность», «Управление разработкой компьютерных игр», and of area 15.04.06 «Мехатроника и робототехника» with the profile «Робототехника»; qualification master, 2 years full-time [1].
- Master's programmes within «Передовые инженерные школы»: area 09.04.01 with the profiles «Искусственный интеллект и инженерия данных», «Инженерия безопасности систем и сетей», «Программная инженерия»; qualification master, 2 years full-time [1].

## Who may apply

- To bachelor's programmes — a person who has a document of secondary general education, or of secondary vocational education and qualification, or of higher education and qualification [1].
- To master's programmes — a person who has a document of higher education and qualification [1].
- The applicant presents a document of the established form: a document of the form set by the authorised federal body in the field of general, higher, health care or culture; a state-format document on the level of education obtained before 1 January 2014 (documents on initial vocational education are equated to a document on secondary vocational education and qualification); a document of the form set by Moscow State University or St Petersburg State University, or of the form set by decision of the collegial body of an educational organisation for a person who has passed the state final certification; a document issued by a private organisation operating in the Skolkovo innovation centre or by an organisation operating in an innovative research and technology centre; or a document of a foreign state on education recognised in the Russian Federation at the level of the corresponding education [1].

## Competition and quotas

Admission is conducted on a competitive basis (clause 5) [1].

- On bachelor's programmes admission is conducted within the control figures at the expense of the federal budget — to places within the quota for admission to targeted training (целевая квота), to places within the special quota (особая квота), which the University sets at not less than 10 % of the control figures for each area of study, to places within the separate quota (отдельная квота), also not less than 10 % of the control figures for each area of study, and to the main places within the control figures (the control figures less the target, special and separate quotas) — and outside the control figures, to places under contracts on paid educational services concluded at the expense of physical and/or legal persons (clause 5.1) [1].
- Bachelor's places within the target, special and main places are filled on the results of the ЕГЭ and/or internal entrance examinations, or without entrance examinations under part 4 and/or part 12 of article 71 of Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ; places within the separate quota on internal entrance examinations and/or ЕГЭ results, or without entrance examinations under part 5.1 of article 71 (clause 5.2) [1].
- Master's admission is conducted on the results of internal entrance examinations, the establishment of the list and the conduct of which is carried out by the University itself (clause 5.3) [1].
- The University sets the priority of entrance examinations for ranking the lists and, for each examination, the maximum number of points and the minimum number of points confirming successful passing (clause 5.4) [1].

The University holds a separate competition for each combination of conditions of admission (clause 6) [1]:

- full-time form of study [1];
- separately by direction (profile) of the programmes — a single-profile competition within an area of study, held for one or several programmes of that area [1];
- separately within the control figures and under contracts on paid educational services [1];
- within the control figures separately for the target quota, the special quota, the separate quota and the main places [1].

Where there are too few places within the control figures to allocate the necessary quotas in full, the University allocates the quotas itself, including one or several combined quotas whose places belong to two or three quotas; a combined quota is allocated where all places within the control figures are used as quota places, and a separate competition is held for persons who have the right to admission within each of the quotas concerned [1]. If no main places remain after the quotas are allocated, enrolment to them is conducted if the quota places are not filled [1].

For all competitions within one condition of admission by profile the same list of entrance examinations, the same minimum and maximum points, the same special rights under parts 4 and 12 of article 71 and the same special advantages are set; the minimum number of points under the various conditions of admission is set according to annex 2 to the rules (clause 8) [1].

## Target admission

- The University sets the target quota in accordance with the quota for admission to targeted training set by the Government of the Russian Federation, state authorities of the subjects of the Federation, local authorities, or the number of places for targeted training set by the founder (clause 99) [1].
- Admission to targeted training is conducted where there is a contract on targeted training concluded between the applicant and the body or organisation named in part 1 of article 71.1 of Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ (the customer), in accordance with the regulation on targeted training and the standard form set by the Government [1].
- The applicant presents the contract on targeted training in addition to the other documents — the original, or a copy certified by the customer, or an uncertified copy with the original shown; where admission to targeted training is in the interests of state security, the University acts on information about the concluded contract received from the federal state body that is the customer, and the applicant does not present the contract [1].
- Where a federal state body has detailed the target quota by naming the customers (a detailed target quota), the University holds a separate competition for each detailed quota, the applicant takes part in the competition for one detailed quota for the area of study, places within the target quota for which no customers are named form a detailed quota for applicants who concluded a contract with customers not named in other detailed quotas, and unfilled places of detailed quotas are used for the main places [1].
- The lists of persons who submitted documents and of persons applying to the target quota do not show information relating to admission to targeted training in the interests of state security [1]; enrolment of such persons is formalised by a separate order that is not placed on the official site [1].

## Additional admission

The University may hold additional admission to vacant places; additional admission to bachelor's places within the control figures ends not later than 28 August 2024, and additional admission to bachelor's paid places and to master's programmes ends not later than 4 December 2024 (clause 11) [1].

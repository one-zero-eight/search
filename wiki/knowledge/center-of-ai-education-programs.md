# Center of Top-Level Educational Programs in Artificial Intelligence — place, tasks, functions and rights

## The document and the Centre's place in the structure

The Regulation on the Center of Top-Level Educational Programs in the field of artificial intelligence (центр образовательных программ топ-уровня в сфере искусственного интеллекта) was approved by order of 11 June 2025 № ОД/АД-02/2025/06/11, signed by the Director for Development and HR Policy R.F. Valiev, with the aim of ensuring that students of the University are trained under higher education programmes for top-level specialists in artificial intelligence [1]. The Regulation governs the Centre's activity and determines its tasks, functions, rights and duties [2]. By the same order the Vice-Rector – Head of the Department of Academic Policy and Higher Education E.S. Bobrov is to ensure that the employees of the subdivision are acquainted, against signature, with the approved Regulation [1].

The Centre is a structural subdivision of the Institute of Data Analysis and Artificial Intelligence (Институт анализа данных и искусственного интеллекта) of the Faculty of Computer and Engineering Sciences of the University, which carries out educational and research activity [3].

The Centre was created in accordance with agreements of 29.05.2025 № 70-2025-000758 and № 70-2025-000720 on the provision of funds to a legal entity or individual entrepreneur on a gratuitous and non-repayable basis in the form of a grant, financed in whole or in part by a subsidy from the federal budget (the grant agreements), concluded between the University and the autonomous non-commercial organisation «Analytical Centre under the Government of the Russian Federation», within the implementation of individual measures of the federal project «Artificial Intelligence» of the national project «Data Economy and Digital Transformation of the State», in order to ensure training of students under higher education programmes for top-level specialists in artificial intelligence [4].

- The Centre reports directly to the Vice-Rector – Head of the Department of Academic Policy and Higher Education of the University [5].
- In its activity the Centre follows the Constitution of the Russian Federation, the Labour Code of the Russian Federation, Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29 December 2012 «Об образовании в Российской Федерации» and other legislative and normative legal acts regulating the activity of higher education institutions, the University's Charter, decisions of the Academic, Supervisory and Operational councils, orders and instructions of the University's director, other local normative acts and this Regulation [6].
- The structure, the number of staff and the staffing table of the Centre, and changes to them, are approved by the director of the University or a person authorised by him [7].
- The working conditions of the Centre's employees are determined by employment contracts concluded with each employee and by the University's internal labour regulations [8].
- The activity of the Centre's employees is regulated by employment contracts, job descriptions and the individual work plan of the teacher; when the functions and tasks of the employees change, the job descriptions are revised [9].
- Employees of the Centre may, within their competences, be involved in work under grants, projects, subsidies and other programmes implemented by the University [10].
- The Regulation and additions and changes to it are approved by the director of the University or another person authorised by him [11].

## Tasks

The tasks of the Centre are:

- developing and implementing new higher education programmes in the field of artificial intelligence [12]
- developing the Centre's staff potential and improving the qualifications of the head of the educational programme and of the University's academic staff [13]
- interacting with industrial partners and bringing the industrial partners' own employees into the educational process [14]
- developing and introducing educational modules for industry specialists in the field of artificial intelligence [15]
- involving students and teachers in research activity and projects in the field of artificial intelligence [16]
- organising and taking part with students and teachers in scientific conferences, seminars, workshops and hackathons [17]
- monitoring and controlling the effectiveness of higher education programmes in the field of artificial intelligence [18]

## Functions

The Centre carries out the following functions:

- concluding and performing civil-law contracts with industrial partners [19]
- selecting students, carried out with the participation of industrial partners [20]
- organising the practical training of students, including practicals, internships and project tasks set by industrial partners [21]
- assessing the knowledge level of students under higher education programmes for top-level specialists in artificial intelligence [22]
- bringing into the educational process academic staff who have publications at A*/A level conferences in the field of artificial intelligence or on artificial intelligence topics published in journals of level 1 and 2 of the White List of scientific journals of the Ministry of Science and Higher Education of the Russian Federation [23]
- monitoring the effectiveness of educational programmes and adjusting their content on the basis of feedback from students and industrial partners [24]

## Rights

In carrying out their activity the Centre's employees have the right to:

- request from the University's structural subdivisions the information and materials needed for the work within the Centre's competence [25]
- with the consent of the heads of the University's structural subdivisions, involve the employees of those subdivisions in preparing draft local acts and other documents and in developing and carrying out measures conducted by the Centre [26]
- take part in meetings held by the University's management when discussing questions whose solution falls within the Centre's competence [27]
- put before the University's management proposals to cancel, amend or supplement the University's local acts [28]
- interact with bodies of state authority and administration, legal entities and the University's subdivisions on questions within the Centre's competence [29]

## Interaction with other subdivisions and reporting

The Regulation names these counterparts and matters:

- the Department for Attracting State Financing — on participation in research (НИР), development (ОКР), НИОКТР, industrial projects and grants [30]
- the Department for Support of Grants and Projects — on reporting under grants, budget and non-budget contracts [31]
- the Department of Academic Policy and Higher Education, the academic department and the Department for International Cooperation — on educational activity [32]

Interaction with other structural subdivisions is determined by the tasks and functions assigned to the Centre by the Regulation [33] and takes place in the course of preparing draft local acts, orders, instructions, contracts and other documents, and of preparing conclusions and written and oral references on questions within the Centre's competence [34]. The Centre may be a base for the practical training of the University's students [35].

The Centre is responsible for preparing information and reports within its activity [36] and submits quarterly and annual reporting in accordance with its obligations under the grant agreements and in other cases provided by law [37].

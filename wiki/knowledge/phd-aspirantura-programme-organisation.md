# Organisation of PhD aspirantura programmes — the Regulation of 2022: structure, research supervisor, individual plan, terms and control

## What the Regulation is

The Regulation on the preparation of scientific and scientific-pedagogical staff in aspirantura sets the procedure for organising and carrying out educational activity on aspirantura programmes, for controlling the preparation of such staff, for conducting the final attestation and for supporting those who passed it when they present the dissertation for the degree of Candidate of Sciences [1]; the edition on the wiki is dated Innopolis, 2022 [2]. It rests on Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29 December 2012, Government Decree No. 2122 of 30 November 2021, Ministry order No. 951 of 20 October 2021 and the University's Устав [1]. It is approved, and amended, by order of the Director or an authorised person [3], [4].

## Who may study and on what

Preparation is carried out within aspirantura programmes, mastered in the scientific specialties of the nomenclature approved by the Ministry of Science and Higher Education, in the groups of specialties in which the University carries out research and has scientific potential [1]. Persons with education not lower than higher education (specialist or master's), including persons whose foreign education is recognised in Russia, are admitted [2].

## Research work, rights of the aspirant and the supervisor

Under the guidance of a research supervisor the aspirant carries out research activity aimed at preparing the dissertation, which includes fulfilling the individual plan of research activity and writing, formatting and presenting the dissertation for the final attestation [5]; the aspirant solves a scientific task significant for the relevant field of science or develops new scientifically substantiated technical, technological or other solutions significant for the country [6].

The aspirant has the right to submit applications for participation in scientific discussions, conferences, symposia and other collective discussions and for scientific and scientific-technical cooperation (internships, business trips, "academic mobility" programmes); to take part in competitions for funding of research from budgets, funds and other sources not prohibited by law; to have access to information on scientific and scientific-technical results and to publish such results in the open press, where they contain no state or other secrets protected by law [7].

The research supervisor must hold a doctor's degree, or in individual cases by decision of the University a candidate's degree, or a foreign degree recognised in Russia; must have carried out research in the corresponding direction within the scientific specialty over the last 3 years; must have publications on the results in refereed Russian and/or foreign journals; and must have approved the results, including with reports at Russian and/or international conferences, over the last 3 years [8]. The involvement of candidates' degree holders in supervision and the requirements to publications are set by the local act on appointing a research supervisor [9].

The supervisor helps choose the dissertation topic and compose the individual plan of research activity; guides the research, including during experiments, technical developments, observations and measurements; consults on preparing the dissertation; carries out the primary review of the dissertation text and of the aspirant's articles and reports; and controls the fulfilment of the individual plan of research activity [10].

## What the University does for the aspirant

The University assists the aspirant, in the order it establishes, in being sent to scientific events (conferences, forums, symposia), including with a report on the dissertation topic, and to events of scientific and scientific-technical cooperation (internships, business trips, "academic mobility") [11]. It may involve the aspirant in its research activity, including scientific-technical and innovative projects financed from budgets, grants and other sources [12], and may hire the aspirant according to its staffing table, including for research support, educational support, engineering-technical, professorial-teaching and research positions; under a targeted training contract the customer organisation may employ the aspirant [13].

## Language, form and structure of the programme

Programmes are mastered in the state language of the Russian Federation and may be implemented in a foreign language according to the programme and the procedure set by legislation and local acts, which also determine the language or languages of study [14]. The process is divided into courses, and courses into semesters; study is full-time; the start date is set by the University and approved by order of the Director or an authorised person [14].

A programme includes a scientific component, an educational component and the final attestation [15]; programmes are developed according to the federal state requirements of the Ministry of Science and Higher Education [16], with observance of legislation on information technologies [17] and on state and other secrets protected by law [18]. The University may establish a focus (profile) of a programme in the order set by local acts; it does not establish a focus — the name of the programme corresponds to the name of the scientific specialty [19].

A programme is a set of documents defining the requirements to its results: the plan of scientific activity, the curriculum, the academic calendar and the work programmes of disciplines (modules) and practice [20]. The plan of scientific activity includes an approximate plan of the research, a plan of preparing the dissertation and its publications, the list of stages of the scientific component and the distribution of those stages and of the final attestation [20]; the curriculum determines the stages of the educational component and the distribution of disciplines (modules) and practice, and the programme defines the planned results of research, of disciplines and of practice [21]. The procedure for developing programmes is set by a local act [22], and information about a programme, except programmes with state or other protected secrets, is published on the University's official website [23].

Admission to aspirantura programmes is competitive; the admission procedure, the list of entrance examinations, the features of entrance examinations for persons with health limitations, the list of additional entrance examinations and the categories of citizens admitted by their results are established by the Ministry of Science and Higher Education [24].

## What the University must provide

The University provides conditions for the aspirant's research, including access to information on scientific and scientific-technical results in the specialty and to the research and experimental base needed for the dissertation; conditions for preparing for candidate examinations; classes in the forms it establishes; conditions for practice; and control of the quality of mastering the programme through ongoing assessment, interim assessment and final attestation [25].

## Supervisor, individual plan and topic

Not later than 30 calendar days from the date the programme starts, the aspirant is appointed a research supervisor, the individual work plan — the individual plan of research activity plus the individual curriculum — is approved, and so is the dissertation topic within the programme and the University's main directions of research [26]; the procedures are set by local acts, and appointment is made on the candidate supervisor's written consent [27].

For interdisciplinary research or a network form the aspirant may have two supervisors, or a supervisor and a scientific consultant, including one from another organisation; the consultant must hold a candidate's or doctor's degree, or a foreign degree recognised in Russia [28]. The number of aspirants supervised by one supervisor at a time is set by a local act [29].

The individual plan of research activity provides for research aimed at preparing the dissertation and is formed by the aspirant together with the supervisor [30]; the individual curriculum provides for mastering the educational component with individualisation of its content, taking account of the aspirant's features and educational needs [31]. The aspirant must conscientiously master the programme and fulfil the individual work plan [32].

## Credit for previous study and adapted programme

The aspirant may have the results of research activity, of disciplines (modules), practice and additional educational programmes credited, including those mastered in other organisations [33]: results from other educational organisations are credited in the order set by the Ministry of Science and Higher Education together with the Ministry of Education [34], and results obtained at the University, as well as research results, in the order set by a local act [35].

For aspirants with disabilities and health limitations the University implements an adapted programme taking account of their psycho-physical development, individual possibilities and state of health [36], and increases the period of study within the limits set by the federal state requirements or by its own requirements, on the aspirant's written application [37]. The choice of methods, educational technologies and teaching materials is made by the University, also taking account of the individual possibilities of such aspirants [38].

## Technologies, terms and holidays

The University may use various educational technologies, including distance technologies and e-learning [39], and a modular organisation of content and curricula [40]; programmes are implemented independently or in a network form using the resources of several organisations, including foreign ones [41], whose organisation is set by local acts [42]. Programmes are mastered within the terms set by the federal state requirements regardless of the technologies used [43].

The aspirant is given holidays in the academic year of at least six and not more than eight weeks in total [44]; after passing the final attestation the aspirant is given additional holidays on application within the term of the programme, after which he is expelled in connection with completing the programme [45]. The term of the programme does not include the time of academic leave, maternity leave or childcare leave until the child reaches 3 years [46].

## Early final attestation and restoration

Where the aspirant has fulfilled early the duties of mastering the programme and the individual work plan, has finished the dissertation and has no academic debt, he is given an early final attestation on a personal application agreed with the supervisor, in the order set by a local act [47].

An aspirant expelled at his own initiative before completing the programme may be restored to it within 5 years after expulsion if there are free places and with the previous conditions of study, but not earlier than after completing the course in which he was expelled [48]; for an aspirant expelled at the University's initiative the procedure and conditions are set by a local act [49].

## Control of preparation

Quality control comprises ongoing assessment, interim assessment and final attestation [50]. Interim assessment evaluates the results of the stages of research, of disciplines (modules) and of practice [51]; passing candidate examinations belongs to the assessment of disciplines within it, their procedure and list being approved by the Ministry [52]. Failure to fulfil the individual plan of research activity found during interim assessment is unfair performance of the duties of mastering the programme and a ground for expulsion [53]; unsatisfactory results in one or more disciplines of the educational component, or failure to pass interim assessment without valid reasons, are academic debt [54], and an aspirant who does not clear it in time is expelled [55]. The forms and procedures of interim assessment other than candidate examinations, and of ongoing assessment, are set by local acts [56].

## Final attestation and termination under this Regulation

The final attestation is an evaluation of the dissertation against the criteria established under the Federal Law "On Science and State Scientific-Technical Policy" [57]; it is obligatory [58], and no payment is charged from aspirants for it [59]. Educational relations end with expulsion in connection with completing the programme, or early at the aspirant's initiative, including on transfer, or early at the University's initiative where expulsion is a disciplinary measure for failure to conscientiously master the programme and fulfil the individual curriculum or the plan of research activity, on establishment of a violation of the admission procedure leading to unlawful enrolment, and on circumstances independent of the parties, including liquidation of the University [60]; expulsion is carried out in the order set by a local act [61].

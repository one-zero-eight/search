# Organisation of educational activity under secondary vocational education (СПО) programmes — the Regulation of 27 January 2025 (ППССЗ)

## The document and what it governs

The Regulation on the procedure of organising and carrying out educational activity under secondary vocational education programmes — programmes of training of mid-level specialists (ППССЗ) — of ANO VO «Innopolis University» was approved by order of the University of 27 January 2025 No. ОД/УД-04/2025/01/27, signed by the director I.I. Bariev and issued in Innopolis, and was put into force from 1 September 2025; control over its execution was entrusted to the College director I.N. Merkulova [1].

It regulates the organisation and carrying out of educational activity under secondary vocational education (СПО) programmes — programmes of training of mid-level specialists — including the features of that organisation for students with health limitations [2], and is obligatory for educational activity under СПО programmes at the University [3].

It was drawn up in accordance with the Federal Law of 29 December 2012 No. 273-ФЗ, the Government decrees of 11 October 2023 No. 1678 (e-learning and distance technologies) and of 20 October 2021 No. 1802 (information on the official site), the orders of the Ministry of Education of 24 August 2022 No. 762 and of 8 November 2021 No. 800, the order of 17 May 2012 No. 413 (the federal state educational standard of secondary general education), the joint order of 5 August 2020 No. 885/390 on practical training, the Rosobrnadzor order of 4 August 2023 No. 1493, the federal state educational standards of СПО (ФГОС СПО), professional standards and the University's Charter [4].

## Who may study

- Persons with education not lower than basic general or secondary general education are admitted to mastering СПО programmes [5].
- Obtaining СПО under a ППССЗ for the first time by persons who hold a diploma of СПО with the award of the qualification of qualified worker or employee is not obtaining a second or subsequent СПО repeatedly [6].
- Persons recognised as invalids may, after receiving СПО, receive СПО again in another profession or speciality at the expense of the federal budget, the budgets of the subjects of the Russian Federation and local budgets, in the procedure established by Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ for persons receiving СПО for the first time [7].
- Obtaining СПО on the basis of basic general education takes place with the simultaneous obtaining by the students of secondary general education within the corresponding СПО programme [8].

## How programmes are delivered

- СПО programmes are delivered by the University's academic structural units in accordance with the requirements of ФГОС СПО and the curricula and with the use of various technologies, including distance educational technologies and e-learning; a network form may be used, which allows the resources of several educational organisations and of other organisations (scientific, physical-culture and sports and others) needed for study and for educational and industrial practice to be used [9].
- The requirements to the structure, volume, conditions of delivery, results of mastering, forms of study and terms of obtaining СПО are set by ФГОС СПО [10]; the content of СПО in each speciality is determined by the СПО programme and must ensure the obtaining of the qualification under the ППССЗ [11].
- The structural units develop the programmes in accordance with ФГОС СПО for the corresponding СПО specialities, taking the corresponding model basic educational programmes into account where they exist; the programmes are agreed and approved in the procedure established at the University and are updated annually with account of the development of science, technology, culture, economy, technologies and the social sphere [12].
- A programme delivered on the basis of basic general education is developed on the basis of the requirements of the corresponding federal state educational standards of secondary general and secondary vocational education, taking the speciality being obtained into account [12].
- An СПО programme is the complex of the main characteristics of education (volume, content, planned results) and of organisational-pedagogical conditions, comprising the curriculum, the calendar study schedule, the work programmes of disciplines (modules) and of other components, assessment and methodological materials, the work programme of upbringing, the calendar plan of upbringing work and the forms of attestation [12].
- A form of organising educational activity based on the modular principle of presenting the content of the programme and of building the curricula, with the use of the corresponding educational technologies, may be applied [13]; a combination of different forms of obtaining education and forms of study is allowed [14].
- The use of methods and means of teaching and of educational technologies that harm the physical or mental health of students is prohibited [15], and mastering an СПО programme provides for the students' practice, educational activity in mastering the programme or its separate components being organised in the form of practical training [15].

## Language of instruction

Educational activity under СПО programmes is carried out in the state language of the Russian Federation [16]. Teaching and study of the Tatar language may be introduced in the University in accordance with the legislation of the Russian Federation and of the Republic of Tatarstan, and must not be carried out to the detriment of the teaching and study of the state language [17]. СПО may be obtained in a foreign language in accordance with the educational programme and in the procedure established by the legislation on education and the University's local normative acts [18].

## The academic year, vacations and classes

- The study process under an СПО programme is organised in accordance with the approved curricula, calendar study schedules, work programmes of upbringing and calendar plans of upbringing work, on the basis of which the class schedules for each speciality are drawn up [19].
- The academic year in the academic structural units begins on 1 September and ends in accordance with the curriculum of the corresponding programme; the start of the academic year may be moved by not more than one month where the programme is delivered in the part-time-evening (очно-заочная) form and by not more than three months in the part-time (заочная) form [20].
- Vacations are given to students in the course of mastering the ППССЗ and last from eight to eleven weeks in the academic year, including not less than two weeks in the winter period [21].
- The study activity of students comprises classes of the following kinds — a lesson, a practical class, a laboratory class, a consultation, a lecture and a seminar — independent work, the performance of a course project (work), practice and other types of study activity provided by the curriculum and the calendar plan of upbringing work [22].
- For all kinds of classes the academic hour is 45 minutes, and the volume of classes and practice must not exceed 36 academic hours a week; where classes are cancelled in a regulated way (coincidence of classes with all-Russian or regional holidays), the programme is delivered in full by the teachers through the adjustment of the calendar-thematic planning of the study material and of the class schedule [23].
- The number of students in a study group is not more than 25 people; classes and practice may be held with groups of students or with the group divided into subgroups, the division being possible in laboratory and practical classes in certain disciplines [24].
- The academic structural unit may join groups of students into streams for classes held as lectures [25].

## The curriculum

The curriculum determines the list, the workload, the sequence and the distribution over study periods of study subjects, disciplines (modules), practice and other types of study activity of students and the forms of interim assessment; its variable part is distributed by the University's academic structural units independently, on the basis of the employers' requirements and with account of the requirements of the digital economy, which makes it possible to develop general and professional competences further, including by extending the main types of activity, introducing additional types of activity and the professional competences needed for the graduate's competitiveness on the regional labour market [26].

## Current monitoring and interim assessment

Mastering an СПО programme, including a separate part or the whole volume of a study subject, discipline (module), is accompanied by current monitoring of academic performance and by interim assessment of students, and the forms, periodicity and procedure of both and the system of grades are determined by the University's local normative acts [27]. The same Regulation also limits the number of examinations and passes in an academic year [28], and that limit is given in the card on ongoing and interim assessment in the College.

## Accelerated study

Persons who have a qualification in an СПО profession and are admitted to study under a ППССЗ in an СПО speciality corresponding to the profession they have have the right to accelerated study under such programmes in accordance with individual curricula (ИУП); study under an ИУП, including accelerated study under an СПО programme, follows the procedure established by the University's local normative acts [29].

## Entry into force and changes

The Regulation is approved by order of the director of the University or of another person authorised by the director [30] and is valid until the date on which a new local normative act regulating the matters indicated in clause 1.1 is adopted [31]; changes and additions to it are made by order of the director or of a person authorised by him [32].

# Admission committee (приёмная комиссия) — status, composition, powers and work

## The document

Order No. ОД/УД-03/2019/03/26 of 26 March 2019 (г. Иннополис) approved the Regulation on the admission committee of ANO VO «Innopolis University» in a new version, with the Regulation as an annex to the order; the Regulation approved by order No. ОД/УД-03/2017/12/28 of 28 December 2017 was declared no longer in force, and the director kept control over execution of the order for himself; the order is signed by director V. Semenikhin [1]. The order was issued in connection with the need to update the local normative document defining the procedure of formation, activity, composition and powers of the admission committee, and in accordance with Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29 December 2012 and the orders of the Ministry of Education and Science No. 1147 of 14 October 2015 and No. 13 of 12 January 2017 [1]. ## What the committee is

- The Regulation governs the procedure of formation, the composition, the powers and the procedure of activity of the admission committee of the University for higher education programmes — bachelor, master and programmes of training of scientific-pedagogical staff in aspirantura [1].
- The committee is a collegial body created in order to receive the documents of applicants, organise the entrance examinations and enrol students on bachelor, master and aspirantura programmes [2].
- Its main task is to ensure observance of the rights of citizens to education established by the Constitution of the Russian Federation and the legislation of the Russian Federation, and the publicity and openness of all admission procedures [3].
- It is guided by the Constitution of the Russian Federation, Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29.12.2012, the orders of the Ministry of Education and Science No. 1147 of 14.10.2015 and No. 13 of 12.01.2017, the University's admission rules for bachelor and master programmes and for aspirantura, the University's Charter and other local acts [4].
- The term of its powers is one calendar year, and the committee works all year round [5].

## Composition

- The composition of the committee is approved by an order of the director of the University; it includes the chair, the deputy chair, the responsible secretary, the deputy responsible secretary and other employees from among the academic staff and the administrative-economic and educational-support personnel [6].
- The chair of the committee is the director of the University [7].
- The responsible secretary is appointed by an order of the director of the University [8].

## Competences of the committee

The section on competences re-uses item numbers 2.2–2.4, which also occur in the section on composition [1].

- On the basis of the documents submitted and in accordance with art. 55 of Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ, the committee admits applicants to the University (clause 3.1) [1].
- It receives the documents necessary for enrolment as a student or aspirant, advises applicants and their legal representatives on admission, organises the internal entrance examinations held by the University itself, carries out the enrolment procedure, transfers admission data to the federal information system ФИС ГИА и Приема and submits all statistical reports connected with the admission of applicants (clause 2.2) [1].
- It may verify other documents of education or of education and qualification submitted by applicants and, in order to confirm their authenticity, may address the relevant state (municipal) bodies and organisations (clause 2.3) [1].
- Its decisions are drawn up as a protocol and are binding on all participants of educational relations in the University (clause 2.4) [1].
- Its meetings are documented by protocols signed by the chair (by the deputy chair when the chair is absent) and by the responsible secretary; decisions are taken by simple majority of votes when at least 2/3 of the approved composition is present [9].

## The chair, the deputy chair and the secretaries

The section on rights, duties and responsibility is also numbered 3, and it contains two clauses numbered 3.3 [1].

- The chair heads the work of the committee; approves the admission rules for bachelor and master programmes, the admission rules for aspirantura, the composition of the committee and the regulations governing the powers and procedure of the examination and appeal commissions; and conducts the meetings of the committee (clause 3.1) [1].
- The deputy chair determines the schedule and the mode of work of the committee; approves the composition of the examination and appeal commissions, the programmes of the entrance examinations, the materials of the entrance examinations and the schedule of the entrance examinations; and conducts the meetings of the committee in the absence of the chair (clause 3.2) [1].
- The responsible secretary organises the work of the committee and keeps the admission information on the official site up to date; organises the personal reception of applicants, their parents (legal representatives) and authorised persons; organises and controls the preparation and the copying of the materials of the entrance examinations; determines the list of rooms and equipment for the examinations; prepares draft orders on the enrolment of applicants; organises control over the accuracy of data on participation in and results of the ЕГЭ and checks other education documents against formal criteria; may verify education documents and address state (municipal) bodies; may certify a copy of a document of education of the established form when the original is produced; controls the transfer of admission data to ФИС ГИА и Приема and the submission of statistical reports; organises and controls the encryption of examination papers; prepares other documents connected with the committee's activity (clause 3.3) [1].
- The deputy responsible secretary receives citizens on questions of admission and answers written and oral enquiries in time; observes the established document flow procedure; receives and registers documents; returns documents when the applicant or his representative withdraws them, when the applicant is removed from an entrance examination or leaves the competition; draws up the applicants' personal files; ensures the safekeeping of the applicants' documents; helps to fill in documents (application for admission, consent to enrolment and similar); enters admission data into ФИС ГИА и Приема (the second clause numbered 3.3) [1].
- For non-performance or improper performance of the duties, breach of confidentiality and information security requirements or abuse of the powers granted, the chair and the members of the committee bear responsibility under the legislation of the Russian Federation [10].

## How applications are handled

- The responsible secretary organises the work of the committee, its paperwork and the personal reception of applicants and their parents (legal representatives); this organisation must ensure observance of personal rights and fulfilment of the state requirements for admission to higher education institutions [11].
- Before the start of the acceptance of documents the committee places on the official site and in the electronic information system, within the deadlines set by legislation, the information required by the admission procedure approved by the Ministry of Education and Science [12].
- During the period of acceptance of documents the committee informs applicants daily about the number of applications submitted and the competition for each area, and runs special telephone lines and a section of the official site for answers to enquiries connected with admission [13]; it also ensures the functioning of a special telephone line and of a section of the University's site for answers to enquiries about admission [14].
- The application and the documents necessary for admission are accepted from the applicant or his representative within the deadlines set by the University's admission rules [15], and the submitted documents are registered with the help of computer equipment and special software in the «1С Университет» database [16].
- The committee enters data about applicants into the federal information system ГИА и приема and must control the accuracy of data about their participation in and results of the ЕГЭ by sending data to that system [17].
- The applicant or his representative is given a receipt for the accepted documents [18].
- On the basis of the received documents the committee decides on admitting the applicant to the entrance examinations, on the conditions of participation in the competition, or on refusing to accept the documents with an indication of the reasons (the clause carrying the number 4.8 in the scan) [1].
- The committee informs the applicant (his representative) of the decision by entering the data into the list of persons who submitted documents, which is placed on the official site of the University [19].
- A personal file is opened for each applicant, holding all documents submitted and the materials of the entrance examinations [20].
- The responsible secretary prepares information materials and forms of the necessary documentation in advance, forms and trains the technical staff to work with applicants, equips the rooms and provides conditions for storing documents [21].
- The personal files of the students enrolled are passed to the Department of support and development of students not later than 14 (fourteen) calendar days from the moment of the issue of the enrolment order [22].

## Powers in organising admission

The committee approves and announces:

- the admission plan by areas of training in which the University announces admission, with the conditions of admission [23];
- the list of entrance examinations for each separate competition, the minimum number of points for each entrance examination in each competition, the priority of the entrance examinations when applicants are ranked and the forms of the entrance examinations held by the University itself [24];
- the programmes of the entrance examinations held by the University itself [25];
- the number of places for admission for each set of conditions of admission [26];
- the possibility of taking entrance examinations held by the University in a foreign language [27];
- the special rights and advantages granted to applicants to bachelor programmes, except those determined by the levels of school olympiads [28], and the special rights and advantages determined by the levels of school olympiads [29];
- the procedure for counting the results of applicants' individual achievements [30];
- the possibility of submitting documents for admission in electronic form [31];
- whether entrance examinations are conducted with distance technologies [32];
- whether applicants must pass a compulsory preliminary medical examination [33];
- the places for submitting documents [34], the postal addresses [35] and the electronic addresses for sending documents in electronic form [36];
- whether there is a dormitory [37] and the number of places in dormitories for applicants from other cities [38];
- the deadlines of the admission for each set of conditions of admission, including the deadlines for submitting the necessary documents, for the entrance examinations and for the final submission of the original document of the established form (application of consent to enrolment) [39];
- the schedule of the entrance examinations [40].

From the day the acceptance of documents begins, the committee announces on the official site and in the electronic information system the number of applications for admission and the lists of persons who submitted the necessary documents, distinguishing the lists of persons entitled to admission without entrance examinations and of persons admitted on the results of the ЕГЭ and/or on the results of the entrance examinations held by the University itself; these lists and the number of applications are updated daily [41]. It controls the accuracy of data about participation in and results of the ЕГЭ [42], verifies the documents submitted by applicants [43] and exercises other powers on organising admission to the main higher education programmes provided by federal normative acts and the University's local acts [44].

## Entrance examinations held by the University itself

- The committee ensures the organisation of the entrance examinations; their schedule (subject, date, time and place of the examination, consultations, reserve days, date of the announcement of the results) is approved by the chair of the committee and communicated to applicants not later than the deadlines set by the University's admission rules [45].
- Consultations are held for applicants before each entrance examination [46].
- The programmes and materials of the entrance examinations for applicants to the first course are drawn up annually on the basis of the federal state educational standards [47].
- Written examination works are done on special forms on which no conventional marks revealing the authorship of the work are allowed; after the examination all written works are passed to the responsible secretary and encrypted with an alphanumeric code, the title sheets are kept in the secretary's safe while the answer forms are passed for checking, the checked works are decrypted and the results are entered in the examination sheets [48].
- The materials of the entrance examinations of persons enrolled are kept in their personal files, and those of persons not enrolled — in the committee for six months [49].
- The committee must acquaint applicants with the results of the entrance examinations; the results are placed in the electronic information system and on the official site in the section «Абитуриентам» [50].
- An applicant may submit an appeal — a reasoned written statement of disagreement with the results of an entrance examination; the procedure for considering it is regulated by the University's admission rules and the Regulation on the appeal commission [51].

## Enrolment

- The decision of the committee on enrolment as a student is drawn up as a protocol of the committee's meeting indicating the grounds of enrolment (without entrance examinations and by general competition) [52].
- On the basis of the committee's decision, orders on enrolment are issued within the established deadlines and placed in the electronic information system and on the official site of the University [53].
- The committee passes the documents of those enrolled to the department for development and support of students; unclaimed originals of documents of education or of education and qualification and other submitted documents of applicants who did not pass the competition are returned to the applicants in the order they indicated in the application for admission [54].
- Unclaimed copies of documents of education or of education and qualification and other submitted documents of applicants not enrolled are kept in the committee for six calendar months and then destroyed [55].

## Reporting and entry into force

- On the results of the committee's work the responsible secretary draws up a report on the results of the admission campaign [56]; the reporting documents are the admission rules, the committee's protocols, the applicants' personal files, the examination sheets, the lists of applicants and the orders on enrolment as students [57]; the committee prepares statistical reports on admission to the first course for all forms of study [58].
- The Regulation comes into force on the day it is approved by the head of the University and ceases to be in force from the moment a new regulation is adopted [59].

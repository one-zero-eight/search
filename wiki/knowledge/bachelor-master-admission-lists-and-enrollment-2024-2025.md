# Lists and enrolment in bachelor's and master's admission 2024/2025 — stages and deadlines

## Lists of applicants and competitive lists

- On the results of the acceptance of documents and of the entrance examinations (where they are held) the University forms a separate ranked list of applicants for each competition (the competitive list); the lists are published on the official site and on ЕПГУ and updated daily while there are changes, until the day of the enrolment order for the competition inclusive, not less than 5 times a day between 09:00 and 18:00 local time [1].
- The competitive list (except the list for places within the separate quota) includes the list of persons admitted without entrance examinations under part 4 and/or part 12 of article 71 (bachelor's programmes) and the list of persons admitted on the results of the ЕГЭ and/or internal examinations who have not less than the minimum number of points [1].
- On bachelor's programmes persons admitted on the results of the examinations are enrolled to the places remaining after the enrolment of persons admitted without entrance examinations within the corresponding competitive list [1].
- The competitive list does not show the surnames, first names and patronymics of the applicants; it shows the insurance number of the individual personal account or the unique code given to the applicant, and, depending on the ground of admission, the ground of admission without examinations, the points for individual achievements, the preferential rights, the sum of competitive points, the sum of points for the examinations, the points for each examination, the presence of the original of the document of education (or of the mark about it on ЕПГУ), the presence of a concluded contract on paid educational services and the priority of enrolment [1].

## How the competitive lists are ranked

- The list of persons admitted without entrance examinations is ranked: by the status of the persons having the right — members of the national teams at international olympiads, winners of the final stage of the All-Russian olympiad, prizewinners of that stage, champions and prizewinners of the Olympic, Paralympic and Deaflympic games and world and European champions, winners of pupil olympiads, prizewinners of pupil olympiads; then by decreasing points for individual achievements; then by the preferential right under part 9 of article 71; then by the preferential right under part 10 of article 71; then by the individually taken account achievements (clauses 83 and 111) [1].
- On bachelor's programmes applicants admitted on the results of the examinations are ranked by decreasing sum of competitive points (points for each examination and for individual achievements); where the sums are equal — by decreasing sum of points for the examinations and/or by decreasing points for individual examinations according to the priority of the examinations; then by the preferential rights under parts 9 and 10 of article 71; then by the achievements taken into account where applicants are equal (clause 84) [1].
- On master's programmes applicants are ranked by decreasing sum of competitive points, then by the sum of points for the examinations and/or by individual examinations according to their priority, then by the achievements taken into account where applicants are equal (clause 85) [1].

## Conditions of enrolment

- Enrolment is conducted according to the priorities stated in the application and according to the competitive lists until the established number of places is filled; it is conducted in one or several stages by decision of the University, and at each stage the University sets the day of completion of the marks about the original on ЕПГУ, of the acceptance of the original and of the conclusion of paid contracts [1].
- An applicant to places within the control figures is enrolled according to the highest priority of enrolment at which he passes the competition; an applicant to paid places is enrolled according to one or several priorities, and enrolment to paid places is conducted regardless of enrolment to places within the control figures [1].
- An applicant to places within the control figures is subject to enrolment if by the day of completion of the marks about the original and of the acceptance of the original the information about his document of education is confirmed by the federal register and there is a mark on ЕПГУ about the original, or the University has the original presented by him (where no consent to the processing of personal data was filed), or, on master's programmes, the University has the original [1].
- An applicant to paid places is subject to enrolment if the information about his document is confirmed and the University has a concluded contract on paid educational services, or the University has a certified copy of the document of education and a concluded contract, or, on master's programmes, a concluded contract [1].
- On the day of completion of the marks about the original and of the acceptance of the original the applicant may place the mark on ЕПГУ, present the original or a copy, or conclude the contract before the time set by the University; where he presented the original to the University the mark on ЕПГУ is considered invalid, and the original (the mark) applies to all conditions of admission stated in the application [1].
- Enrolment to places within the control figures is made on condition that by the day of the enrolment order the applicant has not withdrawn the original presented to the University (the mark about the original on ЕПГУ) [1].
- Enrolment is formalised by an order (orders) of the University [1].
- The marks about the original on ЕПГУ (acceptance of originals) are completed at 12:00 Moscow time on the days of completion at the priority and the main stages [1].

## Stages and calendar of the 2024 admission

Bachelor's programmes, places within the control figures [1]:

- acceptance of documents starts on 20 June 2024 and ends on 25 July 2024 for persons applying on the results of the ЕГЭ; internal examinations end on 25 July 2024; the competitive lists are published on 27 July 2024 [1];
- at the priority stage the marks about the original and the acceptance of the original are completed on 28 July 2024 and the order is issued on 29 July 2024 for persons applying on the special right, to targeted training, to places within the separate quota and without entrance examinations; at the main stage they are completed on 3 August 2024 and the order is issued on 4 August 2024 [1];
- at each stage the University determines the highest priority of enrolment: where it is the priority of the target quota the applicant is enrolled to the target quota, and where it is the priority of other places — to the main places without entrance examinations, otherwise to the separate quota, otherwise to the special quota [1];
- persons enrolled to the special and separate quotas and to a combined quota of those quotas are excluded from the competitive lists for the main places for the conditions under which they are enrolled [1];
- a person enrolled at the priority stage who wants to be enrolled at the main stage to the main places files a refusal of the enrolment by the day of completion of the acceptance of originals at the main stage, otherwise he is not enrolled at the main stage; a person who wants to be enrolled at the main stage in another organisation files a withdrawal of the original together with a refusal of enrolment or a withdrawal of the documents [1];
- unfilled places within a combined quota are used by decision of the University as places of one or several of the quotas concerned, and places freed because persons enrolled at the priority stage are excluded from among the enrolled are added to the main places [1];
- clause 9 of the rules sets the same start and completion dates and names 27 July 2024 as the term of publication of the competitive lists and of enrolment [1].

Bachelor's programmes, paid places: acceptance of documents from 20 June 2024, completion on 12 August 2024 for persons applying on the results of the ЕГЭ and internal examinations, publication of the competitive lists on 16 August 2024, completion of the conclusion of contracts on 19 August 2024, order on 20 August 2024 [1].

Master's programmes, places within the control figures: acceptance of documents from 20 June 2024, completion on 25 July 2024, completion of the acceptance of originals at the main stage on 3 August 2024, end of the entrance examinations on 6 August 2024, publication of the competitive lists on 7 August 2024, order on 9 August 2024 [1].

Master's programmes, paid places — one calendar for the profiles «Искусственный интеллект и инженерия данных», «Инженерия безопасности систем и сетей», «Программная инженерия», «Робототехника и компьютерное зрение», «Технологическое предпринимательство» of area 09.04.01 and «Робототехника» of area 15.04.06: documents from 20 June 2024, completion on 16 August 2024, examinations end on 17 August 2024, lists on 18 August 2024, contracts on 19 August 2024, order on 20 August 2024 [1]. Another calendar applies to the profiles «Управление цифровым продуктом», «Управление на основе данных», «Кибербезопасность» and «Управление разработкой компьютерных игр» of area 09.04.01: documents from 20 June 2024, completion on 20 September 2024, examinations end on 23 September 2024, lists on 24 September 2024, contracts on 25 September 2024, order on 26 September 2024 [1].

- Unfilled places within the quotas are used for the enrolment of persons applying to the main places without entrance examinations and on the results of examinations [1].
- Where unfilled places remain after the completion of enrolment, the University may hold an additional enrolment on the basis of the competitive lists; at the additional enrolment to bachelor's places within the control figures the acceptance of originals (marks on ЕПГУ) starts on 10 August 2024 and the orders are issued not later than 14 August 2024, while additional enrolment to bachelor's paid places and to master's programmes is held within the terms set by the University [1].
- On paid places the established number of places may be exceeded by decision of the University; in that case the University sets the sum of competitive points needed for enrolment and enrols applicants with not less than the minimum points and a sum of competitive points not less than the established sum [1].
- The University forms information on the enrolment as separate lists for each competition without surnames, first names and patronymics, showing the insurance number or unique code, the sum of competitive points, the points for the examinations and for individual achievements and the grounds of admission without entrance examinations; the information is placed on the official site on the day of the order and remains available for 6 months [1].

# Internal system of quality assessment (ВСОКО) — the plan-schedule of measures for 2026

## The document

The План-график мероприятий по внутренней системе оценки качества образования в АНО ВО «Университет Иннополис» на 2026 год was approved by the University's order of 24 December 2025 № ОД/АД‐01/2025/12/24, signed by the Director D.V. Vandyukov [1].

For every measure the plan-schedule gives the form of the assessment procedure, the object of the internal quality assessment system, the deadlines and the responsible structural unit [1]. The measures are set out by directions of activity, and nearly every one of them is carried out by the Centre for Control of Educational Activity together with the units named for it [1].

## Directions of activity

The plan-schedule is divided into the following directions [1]:

- ensuring the quality of implementation of educational programmes of higher and secondary vocational education [1];
- ensuring the quality of implementation of additional educational programmes [1];
- ensuring the timeliness of monitoring the quality of the University's processes within the collection of federal statistical reporting [1];
- ensuring compliance with licensing requirements, accreditation indicators, accreditation monitoring indicators, the requirements of the legislation on education and local normative acts [1].

## Higher and secondary vocational education: audits, surveys and their dates

- Audit of students' personal achievements and analysis of the statistics of wins in olympiads, participation in research and practical and research conferences, competitions and contests, including on the basis of the information placed in the students' portfolio — January; the Centre for Control of Educational Activity and the Department for Support and Development of Students [2].
- Audit of the data of interim assessment of students — February and August; the Centre, the Department of Organisation of Academic Activity, the Department of Bachelor's and Master's Programmes and the College [3].
- Audit of the fulfilment by aspirants of the individual plan of research activity — February and August; the Centre and the Department of Organisation of Academic Activity [4].
- Audit of the data of ongoing assessment of academic performance — April and November; the Centre, the Department of Bachelor's and Master's Programmes and the College [5].
- Survey of students about their satisfaction with the conditions, content, organisation and quality of the educational process as a whole and of individual disciplines (modules) and practices — June and December; the Centre [6].
- Survey of pedagogical and scientific workers about their satisfaction with the conditions and organisation of educational activity — June and December; the Centre [7].
- Survey of employers and/or their associations and of other legal and/or physical persons about their satisfaction with the quality of education — June and December; the Centre [8].
- Audit of the conditions of implementation of higher and secondary vocational education programmes, including the analysis of the assessment tools, of the material, technical and information support and of the staffing conditions, including the completion by pedagogical workers of training under programmes of advanced training and/or professional retraining — July; the Centre, the Methodological Department, the Department of Bachelor's and Master's Programmes and the College [9].
- Audit of the results of the final (state final) attestation of students — August; the Centre, the Department of Organisation of Academic Activity and the Department of Bachelor's and Master's Programmes [10].
- Audit of the results of the unified state examination of the students admitted in the current year (quality of admission) — October; the Centre and the Admission Department [11].

## Additional educational programmes

- Audit of the conditions of implementation of additional professional programmes — April and October; the Centre, the Institute of Additional Education and the Centre for Pre-University Training [12].
- Audit of the conditions of implementation of additional general education programmes — April and October; the Centre, the Institute of Additional Education and the Department of Pre-University Training [13].

## Federal statistical reporting and monitoring

All of these are carried out by the Centre for Control of Educational Activity [14]:

- data for the form № 1-технология on the development and/or use of advanced production technologies — January [14];
- data for the form № 2-наука (ИНВ) (short) on the organisation of the research and development sector — April [15];
- data for the form № 3-информ on the use of digital technologies and the production of related goods and services — March [16];
- data for the form № 2-наука on the performance of research and development — March [17];
- data for the form № ВПО-2 on the material, technical and information base and the financial and economic activity of the higher education organisation — April [18];
- data for the quarterly form № 1-ПК on the number of listeners aged 15 and older trained under additional professional programmes — April, July and October [19];
- data for the monitoring of the effectiveness of higher education organisations — in accordance with a request of the Ministry of Science and Higher Education of Russia [20];
- data for the form № 1-инжиниринг on the engineering activity of educational, scientific and small innovative organisations — in accordance with a request of the Ministry [21];
- data for the monitoring of the scholarship provision of students of higher education organisations — in accordance with a request of the Ministry [22];
- data for the form № ВПО-1 on the organisation carrying on educational activity under bachelor's, specialist's and master's programmes — September [23];
- data for the form № СПО-1 on the organisation carrying on educational activity under secondary vocational education programmes — September [24];
- monitoring of the international activity of higher education organisations (form № 1-Мониторинг-МД) — in accordance with a request of the Ministry [25];
- data for the form № 1-ИИ (ВО) on the organisation teaching artificial intelligence technologies within higher education and additional professional programmes — in accordance with a request of the Ministry [26];
- monitoring of the activity of higher education organisations on teaching disabled persons and persons with health limitations — November [27];
- data for the form № 1-НК on the organisation carrying on educational activity under aspirantura, ordinatura and assistantura-stazhirovka programmes and doctoral training — December [28];
- data for the form № 1-ПК on the organisation carrying on educational activity under additional professional programmes — December [29];
- data for the form № 1-ДОД on the organisation carrying on activity under additional general education programmes for children — December [30].

## Licensing, accreditation and compliance audits

- Audit of compliance with the legislation on protecting children from information harmful to their health and/or development — January; the Centre [31].
- Self-assessment (самообследование) of the University — March; the Centre [32].
- Audit of the organisation of the process of transfer to and from the University — May; the Centre and the Admission Department [33].
- Audit of the compliance of the section «Сведения об образовательной организации» of the University's official site with the requirements of the legislation on education — May; the Centre [34].
- Audit of the organisation and implementation of students' practice — July; the Centre and the Department for Relations with Industry and Graduates [35].
- Audit of the procedure for organising the final (state final) attestation and issuing documents of education and of qualification — August; the Centre [36].
- Audit of the procedure for organising educational activity under higher education programmes — September; the Centre, the Methodological Department and the Department of Organisation of Academic Activity [37].
- Audit of the procedure for organising educational activity under secondary vocational education programmes — September; the Centre and the College [38].
- Audit of the organisation of admission to educational programmes of higher education — September; the Centre and the Admission Department [39].
- Audit of the procedure for organising educational activity under additional professional programmes — September; the Centre [40].
- Audit of the procedure for organising educational activity under additional general education programmes — September; the Centre [41].
- Audit of the procedure for providing paid educational services — November; the Centre [42].
- Audit of compliance with the licensing indicators — November; the Centre [43].
- Audit of the organisation of the award of scholarships to students — November; the Centre and the Department for Support and Development of Students [44].
- Audit of compliance with the accreditation indicators and the indicators of accreditation monitoring — December; the Centre [45].

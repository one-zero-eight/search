# Institute of Software Development and Software Engineering — place, tasks, functions and rights

## The document and the Institute's place in the structure

The page is the Regulation (Положение) on the Institute of Software Development and Software Engineering of ANO VO «Innopolis University», approved by order of the University; the line naming the approving order is partly illegible on the page, so no readable date or number for the order is given there [1]. The page is a scanned document read by OCR and the wiki warns that numbers and dates should be checked against the PDF before relying on them [1].

The Regulation regulates the Institute's activity and defines its tasks, functions, rights and duties [1]. The Institute is a structural subdivision of the Faculty of Computer and Engineering Sciences [1], and the Faculty's own Regulation also lists it among the Faculty's structural subdivisions [2]. The Institute includes the Laboratory of Software Engineering, the Laboratory of Industrial Software Development, the Laboratory of Operating Systems, Programming Languages and Compilers (as read by OCR) and the master's programme «Управление разработкой ПО» [1].

The Institute reports directly to the director of the institute, and in the director's absence to the dean of the faculty [3]. In its activity the Institute is guided by the Constitution of the Russian Federation, the Labour Code of the Russian Federation, Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29 December 2012 «Об образовании в Российской Федерации» and other legislative and normative legal acts regulating the activity of higher education institutions, the University's Charter, decisions of the Academic, Supervisory and Operational councils of the University, orders and instructions of the University's director, other local normative acts of the University and this Regulation [1].

The Institute's structure, number of staff and staffing table, and changes to them, are approved by the director of the University [4]. The Institute is headed by its director, who is hired and dismissed by order of the director of the University [5]. Working conditions of the Institute's employees are determined by the employment contracts concluded with each employee and by the University's internal labour regulations [6]; their rights, duties and degree of responsibility are established by job descriptions [7]. The Regulation and additions and changes to it are approved by the director of the University or by another person authorised by him [8].

## Tasks

In the field of academic, methodological and educational work the Institute's tasks are:

- training of cadres (bachelors, masters and aspirants) at various levels of education in the profile of the Institute's educational activity, with deep professional knowledge and a high level of general culture [1]
- satisfying the individual's need for professional, intellectual, cultural and moral development on the basis of higher education obtained by combining the learning process with the University's research activity [1]
- satisfying the material, spiritual and intellectual needs of the Institute's employees [1]
- planning, organising and continuously improving the academic and teaching-methodological activity of the Institute's laboratories [1]
- coordinating the work of the laboratories and other structural subdivisions of the University that ensure the educational process at the Institute [1]
- organising and conducting measures aimed at raising the effectiveness of the Institute's integrated quality management system [1]
- planning and organising the educational activity of students in accordance with the working curricula [1]
- raising the professional level of the Institute's academic and research-pedagogical staff through participation in research and through the implementation of qualification improvement and retraining programmes [1]

In the field of research work the Institute's tasks are:

- forming and developing scientific schools [1]
- organising and conducting fundamental and applied research, research and development work and innovation activity of academic staff and students, increasing the volume and effectiveness of research and widening its subject matter [1]
- planning, organising and continuously improving the research work of the laboratories and scientific structural subdivisions of the Institute [1]
- planning and organising the research work of the University's students [1]
- performing research and development, industrial projects and grants in the Institute's profile by the Institute's employees on their own initiative with the written agreement of the Institute's leadership, and under contracts paid from grants received by the University from budget and extra-budgetary funds [1]

## Functions

- developing research programmes in the directions of the Institute's activity [1]
- staged planning and conduct of research, research and development work, industrial projects and grants [9]
- improving the methods, technologies, techniques and instruments of research work [10]
- creating a database for the conduct of students' research work in the course of their coursework and final qualification works [11]
- developing and publishing scientific and methodological manuals on the results of the Institute's activity [12]
- publishing scientific articles in journals, including those included in international citation databases Web of Science (WoS), Scopus and others, of the University's teachers, aspirants and students [13]
- conducting thematic seminars and practical workshops and consultations for teachers, aspirants and students [14]
- preparing practical (experimental) material for inclusion in candidate and doctoral dissertations [15]
- involving the University's students in research activity [16]
- revealing the scientific potential of the University's young specialists [17]

## Rights

In carrying out its activity the Institute's employees have the right to:

- request from the University's structural subdivisions the information and materials needed for the work within the Institute's competence [18]
- with the consent of the heads of the University's structural subdivisions, involve the employees of those subdivisions in preparing draft local acts and other documents and in developing and carrying out measures conducted by the Institute [19]
- take part in meetings held by the University's management when questions whose solution falls within the Institute's competence are discussed [20]
- put before the University's management proposals to cancel, amend or supplement the Institute's local acts [21]
- interact with bodies of state authority and administration, legal entities and the University's subdivisions on questions within the Institute's competence [22]

## Interaction with other subdivisions and reporting

Interaction of the Institute with the University's other structural subdivisions is determined by the tasks and functions assigned to it by the Regulation [23]. The Institute interacts with the Department for Attraction of Grants on questions of participation in research, development, industrial projects and grants [24], and with the Department for Support and Quality Control of Projects on questions of reporting on grants and on contracts paid from grants received by the University from budget and extra-budgetary funds [25]. The Institute executes all orders of the University in the part concerning its activity [26] and the decisions of the University's Academic Council in the part concerning its activity [27]. Interaction with other structural subdivisions takes place on preparing draft local acts, orders, instructions, contracts and other documents and on preparing conclusions and written or oral information on questions relating to the Institute's competence [28].

Within its activity the Institute is responsible for preparing and keeping up to date the following information: the number of graduates, the academic staff belonging to the Institute's laboratories, the number of scientific publications, and the number of qualification works with their titles [29]. The Institute submits reports in accordance with the requirements of legislation and of the contracts and agreements concluded by the University [30].

# Entrance examination for the master's programme «Кибербезопасность» (28 August 2024) — published results

## What the document is

The page is the published protocol of the entrance examination for the master's programme «Кибербезопасность» (Cybersecurity), held by Innopolis University on 28.08.2024 under the heading «Результаты вступительного испытания проводимого АНО ВО "Университет Иннополис" самостоятельно» [1].

The heading of the page gives 28.08.2024 as the date of the entrance examination and names the entrance examination as the one for the master's programme «Кибербезопасность» [1]. For each participant the table gives the number, the СНИЛС/уникальный код, the score (оценка) and the same score written out in words (оценка прописью) [1].

Scores are published against an identifier rather than a name, and every identifier on the page is a numeric code entered in the column «СНИЛС/Уникальный код» [1]. The parts of the heading that repeat the name of the examination are broken up in the text layer of the page («прогр магист Кибербез опасность») [1].

## Results recorded

The protocol lists three numbered results; in the order of the protocol the scores are 65, 75 and 43, written in words as «Шестьдесят пять», «Семьдесят пять» and «Сорок три» [1]. The highest score in the protocol is 75 and the lowest is 43 [1].

## What the protocol does not say

The page states no pass mark, no ranking of the participants and no decision on admission [1].

# Sector for Support of Educational Projects (Сектор по сопровождению образовательных проектов) — place, tasks, functions and rights

## The document

The Regulation on the Sector for support of educational projects (Сектор по сопровождению образовательных проектов) of the Center of Educational Partnership of the Institute of Additional Education of ANO VO «Innopolis University» was approved by the University's order No. ОД/АД-01/2025/02/03 of 3 February 2025 [1]. The order was issued in connection with the creation of a new structural subdivision in order to optimise the University's activity, and it is signed by the Director for Development and HR Policy R.F. Valiev [1]. The order repealed the Regulation on the Sector for support of educational projects of the Department for work with listeners of the Institute of Additional Education, approved by the University's order No. ОД/АД-01/2024/09/04 of 04.09.2024 [1]. The head of the Sector V.N. Bulgakova had to ensure that the Sector's employees, as well as employees newly hired or transferred into it from other subdivisions, are familiarised with the Regulation against signature, and control over the order's execution was retained by the signatory [1]. ## Place in the structure

- The Regulation regulates the activity of the Sector and defines its tasks, functions, rights and duties [2].
- The Sector is a structural subdivision of the Center of Educational Partnership of the Institute of Additional Education of the Directorate for project and research activity, and it belongs to the category «учебно-демонстрационные центры» [3].
- The Sector reports directly to the head of the Center of Educational Partnership [4].
- In its activity the Sector follows the Constitution of the Russian Federation, the Labour Code of the Russian Federation, Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29 December 2012 «Об образовании в Российской Федерации», other legislative and normative legal acts regulating the activity of higher education institutions, the University's Charter, decisions of the Academic, Supervisory and Operational councils, orders and instructions of the University's director, other local normative acts and this Regulation [5].
- The Sector's structure, number of staff and staffing table, and changes to them, are approved by the director of the University [6]. The Sector is headed by its head, hired and dismissed by order of the director [7]. The working conditions of its employees are determined by the employment contracts concluded with each employee and by the University's internal labour regulations [8], and their rights, duties and degree of responsibility are established by job descriptions [9].
- Employees of the Sector may be involved, within their competences, in work under grants, projects, subsidies and other programmes implemented by the University [10]. The Regulation and additions and changes to it are approved by order of the director of the University or of another person authorised by him [11].

## Tasks

The tasks of the Sector are [12]:

- supporting the listeners (слушатели) of the University's educational projects and additional professional education programmes [12];
- organising the training of listeners with the use of digital platforms and distance educational technologies of the University [13];
- providing information support for the educational process of the listeners of the University's educational projects and additional professional education programmes [14];
- interacting with the structural subdivisions taking part in organising and supporting the educational projects and additional professional education programmes of the Institute of Additional Education [15];
- carrying out tasks and taking part in measures aimed at implementing educational projects and additional professional education programmes and at achieving the goals of the Institute of Additional Education [16].

## Functions

The Sector performs the following functions:

- helping with registration and admission to the University's educational projects and additional professional education programmes [17];
- helping listeners to build their individual educational route and to complete attestation works of different types, and checking and assessing them [18];
- providing psychological and pedagogical support to listeners, helping them with academic and other problems connected with study [19];
- supporting distance learning and work with digital educational resources [20];
- carrying out selective checks of the work of specialists [21];
- forming a personnel reserve of specialists [22];
- keeping a database of specialists to record the employees' workload and to form project groups that support the University's educational projects and additional professional education programmes [23];
- forming and applying rating indicators of the employees' efficiency within the distribution of functional duties in the Sector [24];
- forming a base of methodological materials for supporting listeners, taking into account the features of the educational process in the University's additional professional programmes [25];
- studying the experience of other higher education institutions in organising the educational process and developing proposals for improving the support of the Institute of Additional Education's listeners [26];
- performing the functions assigned to the Sector by job descriptions and other local normative documents of the University and by instructions of the director of the Institute of Additional Education [27];
- controlling the fulfilment of tasks relating to the Sector's sphere of activity and presenting reports [28].

## Rights

In carrying out their activity the Sector's employees have the right to:

- request from the University's structural subdivisions the information and materials needed for the work within the Sector's competence [29];
- with the consent of the heads of the structural subdivisions, involve the employees of those subdivisions in preparing draft local acts and other documents and in developing and carrying out measures conducted by the Sector [30];
- take part in meetings held by the University's management when questions whose solution falls within the Sector's competence are discussed [31];
- put before the University's management proposals to cancel, amend or supplement the University's local acts [32];
- interact with bodies of state authority and administration, legal entities and the University's subdivisions on questions within the Sector's competence [33].

## Interaction and reporting

The interaction of the Sector with the University's other structural subdivisions is determined by the tasks and functions assigned to it by the Regulation [34]. The Sector may be a base for the practical training of the University's students [35]. On the basis of the relevant local acts, orders and instructions the Sector may, within its activity, be responsible for preparing and keeping up to date information, certificates and reports [36].

# Additional general education programmes (ДОП) — purpose, design and delivery

## The document

The Regulation on the additional general education programme of ANO VO «Innopolis University» was approved by order No. ОД/АД-01/2023/02/22 of 22 February 2023 and put into effect on 1 March 2023; from that date the earlier order of 27 December 2018 No. ОДд/УД-02/2018/12/11 on the previous version of the Regulation was repealed [1]. Control over execution of the order was assigned to the vice-rector for quality of education E.A. Khodyreva, and the order was signed by Director K.V. Semenikhin [1].

The Regulation is binding for the development and implementation of additional general education programmes (ДОП) [2]. It was drawn up under Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29 December 2012 «Об образовании в Российской Федерации», the order of the Ministry of Education of Russia No. 629 of 27 July 2022 on the organisation of educational activity under additional general education programmes, the University's Charter and other local acts [3]. The approved original is kept in the University's protocol and records department and the electronic version is on the University's website [4]. The wiki copy is a draft made by OCR, so numbers and dates should be checked against the PDF [1].

## What additional general education programmes are for

Training under ДОП at the University is aimed at: spiritual, moral and civic-patriotic upbringing of learners; forming and developing their creative abilities; meeting learners' individual needs for intellectual, moral, artistic-aesthetic development and physical improvement; forming a culture of a healthy and safe way of life, strengthening health and organising learners' free time; adapting learners to life in society; career guidance of learners; identifying, developing and supporting learners who have shown outstanding abilities; and meeting other educational needs and interests of learners that do not contradict Russian legislation and lie outside federal state educational standards and federal state requirements [5].

A ДОП takes account of the age and individual characteristics of learners [6].

## Designing, approving and updating a programme

- The content of a ДОП and the term of study are determined by the educational programme developed and approved by the University [7]; the term of mastering must make it possible to achieve the planned results [8].
- A ДОП is a set of the main characteristics of education (volume, content, planned results), organisational-pedagogical conditions and forms of attestation, presented as a study plan, an academic calendar, work programmes of subjects, disciplines (modules), and assessment and methodological materials [9].
- The University creates conditions for the implementation of ДОП that take account of Russian legislation on the sanitary-epidemiological welfare of the population [10].
- The name of a ДОП must contain information indicating the type of the programme [11].
- The structure of a ДОП consists of general provisions; aims and tasks; planned learning outcomes; organisational-pedagogical conditions; the study plan; the academic calendar; work programmes of subjects, courses, disciplines (modules); assessment tools; methodological materials and material-technical support; and conditions of implementation for disabled persons and persons with health limitations — a model programme is given in annex 1 [12].
- When a ДОП is designed, the balance between theoretical preparation and practical training may vary depending on the aims and the terms of study [13].
- A ДОП is approved by order of the University director or of a person authorised by the director [14] and is updated to take account of developments in science, technology, culture, economy, technologies and the social sphere [15].

## How studies are organised

- Educational activity under ДОП is organised in the state language of the Russian Federation — Russian; additional education may be obtained in a foreign language in accordance with the ДОП and in the procedure established by Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ and the University's local acts [16].
- Admission of learners to a ДОП is carried out in accordance with the Rules of admission to studies under additional educational programmes [17].
- The educational process is regulated by the class schedule and the educational programme; the schedule is drawn up to create the most favourable regime of work and rest, on the proposal of the University's teaching staff and taking account of the wishes of learners, of parents (legal representatives) of minor learners and of the learners' age characteristics [18].
- Educational activity under ДОП is organised throughout the whole calendar year, including holiday time [19].
- Study under an individual curriculum, including accelerated study, within the ДОП being mastered is carried out in the procedure established by the Regulation [20].
- Studies under a ДОП are carried out in full-time (очная) and part-time (заочная) forms [21]; the clause on contact work nevertheless also names the очно-заочная form: contact work with the University's teaching staff must be at least 60 % in the full-time form, at least 40 % in the очно-заочная form and at least 20 % in the part-time form of the total time allotted to the subjects, disciplines (modules) [22], and the annex 1 template also offers очная/очно-заочная/заочная [1].
- The number of learners in groups is no more than 20 [23]; lessons last no more than 4 hours a day for learners from 3 to 18 years old and no more than 8 hours a day for learners from 18 years old [24].
- A ДОП is delivered both independently and through network forms of implementation, whose organisation is regulated by the University's local acts [25]; the University may, on a contractual basis, provide services for delivering the ДОП of other organisations carrying out educational activity [26].
- Various educational technologies may be used, including distance educational technologies and e-learning [27]; both classroom and out-of-class (independent) classes may be envisaged, held in groups or individually [28].
- The University determines the forms of classroom classes and the forms, procedure and periodicity of interim assessment of learners [29].
- A modular principle of presenting the content and building the curricula may be applied [30], and combinations of different forms of obtaining education and forms of study are allowed [31].
- The use of methods and means of teaching and upbringing and of educational technologies that harm the physical or mental health of learners is prohibited [32]; mass events may be organised and conditions created for joint activity of learners and parents (legal representatives) of minor learners [33].
- Teaching under ДОП is carried out by persons with secondary vocational or higher education, including in fields corresponding to the direction of the ДОП, who meet the qualification requirements of the qualification handbooks and (or) professional standards [34]; the University may involve in teaching persons studying under higher education programmes in relevant specialties and directions who have successfully passed interim assessment for at least two years of study [35].

## Academic debt, retakes and expulsion

Unsatisfactory results of interim assessment in one or more subjects, courses, disciplines (modules), or failure to pass interim assessment in the absence of valid reasons, are recognised as academic debt [36].

Learners with academic debt may pass interim assessment in the relevant subject, course, discipline (module) no more than twice, within the deadlines set by the University and within one year from the emergence of the debt; that period does not include the learner's time of illness, stay on academic leave or maternity leave [37].

Learners who have not cleared the academic debt within the established deadlines are expelled from the University [38].

## Current control and evaluation of quality

The quality of mastering a ДОП is checked through current monitoring of learners' performance [39]. It is carried out during the study period to control the level of achievement of the results envisaged by the ДОП, to let learners assess themselves and the teacher assess their work with a view to improving the educational process, and for systematic checking of individual assignments, preparation for classes, independent study of particular questions, projects, essays and so on [40].

Current control is carried out in oral form (oral questioning), written form (written questioning, performance of set tasks and so on) and test form (oral, written, computer/electronic testing) [41]. Its results are used by the teacher to inform learners and other interested persons of the degree of mastery of the ДОП, to identify lagging learners in good time and help them with the material, and to analyse the quality of the ДОП and improve the methods of teaching it [42].

Evaluation of the quality of mastering a ДОП concerns the compliance of the procedure of organising and delivering the ДОП with the established requirements to structure, procedure and conditions of implementation, the ability of the organisation to perform effectively and efficiently in providing educational services, and support of the proper level of learners' results, their motivation and their results of participation in further activities [43].

## Annex 1 and closing provisions

Annex 1 is the form (template) of a ДОП, including its structure, the direction, aims and tasks, planned results, organisational-pedagogical conditions, study plan, academic calendar, work programmes, interim assessment, assessment materials, methodological materials and material-technical support, and the conditions of implementation for disabled persons and persons with health limitations; it also records that the programme is updated annually taking account of developments in science, technology, culture, economy, technologies and the social sphere [1].

The Regulation is approved and put into effect, and the decision to terminate it is also taken, by order of the University director [44]; its period of validity is determined by changes in education legislation and the University's local acts [45]; other local acts on ДОП are developed and adopted in accordance with it [46].

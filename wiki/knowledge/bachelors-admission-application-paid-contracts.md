# Application for bachelor's admission under paid education contracts — sample form

## What the form is

The wiki holds a sample application form (образец заявления) for admission to bachelor's programmes under contracts for the provision of paid educational services at ANO VO «Innopolis University» [1]. The application is addressed to the Director of the University, K.V. Semenikhin, at the University's address 420500, Innopolis, Universitetskaya street, house 1, and the form is dated «___» __________ 2024 [1].

## Applicant's personal data

The form asks for the applicant's surname, name and patronymic (if any); sex; date and place of birth; registered address; citizenship; the identity document (type, series, number, by whom issued, subdivision code); the insurance number of the individual personal account (СНИЛС) if any, or the unique code assigned to the applicant in the absence of a СНИЛС [1]. It also asks for information about education and the document of the established form: the name of the educational organisation, the year of completion, the document of education or of education and qualification, its series and number [1].

## Programmes listed in the form

The applicant asks to be admitted to the competition for the first course, naming the code and name of the field of study, the form of study, the basis of admission and the priority of enrolment [1]. The sample lists, all full-time (очная), on non-budget (paid) places under contracts for paid educational services and under the general competition [1]:

1. 09.03.01 Informatics and Computer Engineering, profile «Инженерия информационных систем» [1].
2. 09.03.01 Informatics and Computer Engineering, profile «Анализ данных и искусственный интеллект» [1].
3. 09.03.01 Informatics and Computer Engineering, profile «Математические основы искусственного интеллекта» [1].
4. 15.03.06 Mechatronics and Robotics, profile «Робототехника» [1].

## Special rights and entrance examinations

A block of the form concerns special rights at admission: the applicant ticks «без вступительных испытаний» (without entrance examinations) or «преимущественное право» (preferential right) and lists the documents confirming them, including a diploma of a winner or prize-winner of school olympiads with the name and details of the olympiad [1].

The applicant then chooses between using the results of the ЕГЭ as entrance examinations and being admitted to the entrance examinations held by Innopolis University itself, naming the subject, the ground for holding the examination, the language of the examination (Russian or English, by reference to the admission rules), the form (computer testing) and the place — at the address of residence [1]:

- for applicants on the basis of secondary general education: mathematics; informatics and ICT or physics; Russian language [1];
- for applicants on the basis of professional education (secondary vocational education, higher education): foundations of mathematics; foundations of informatics or foundations of physics; Russian language [1].

## Special conditions at entrance examinations

Applicants who need special conditions at the entrance examinations because of health limitations or disability fill in a separate block: the list of entrance examinations, the details of the document confirming the disability or health limitation that requires the conditions, and tick the conditions needed [1]. The conditions offered are [1]:

- written tasks in relief-dot Braille or as an electronic document accessible with specialised software for the blind, or read by an assistant [1];
- a set of writing accessories and Braille paper, or a computer with specialised software for the blind, or an assistant to dictate to [1];
- individual even lighting of not less than 300 lux [1];
- a magnifying device for the internal entrance examinations [1];
- tasks and the instructions on the procedure of the entrance examinations in enlarged print [1];
- sound-amplifying equipment for collective use, or for individual use [1];
- the services of a sign-language interpreter, or of a tactile sign-language interpreter [1];
- a computer with specialised software for persons with musculoskeletal disorders, impaired motor functions of the upper limbs or absence of the upper limbs, or dictation to an assistant [1].

## Individual achievements listed

A block of checkboxes lists the achievements that may be declared [1]:

- a certificate (аттестат) of secondary general education with distinction, and likewise the certificates of secondary (complete) general education with distinction for those awarded a gold or a silver medal [1];
- a diploma of secondary vocational education with distinction, a diploma of primary vocational education with distinction, and the diplomas of primary vocational education for those awarded a gold or a silver medal [1];
- military service by conscription, by contract or by mobilisation in the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation, or presence in volunteer formations under a contract of voluntary assistance in the tasks assigned to the Armed Forces during the special military operation in the territories of Ukraine, the Donetsk and Lugansk People's Republics, the Zaporozhye and Kherson regions [1];
- the status of winner (prize-winner) of the national and (or) international championship of professional skills among disabled persons and persons with health limitations «Абилимпикс» [1].

## Accommodation, return of originals and confirmations

- A checkbox states the need for a place of residence in the residential complex (жилой комплекс) [1].
- The form asks for the way the submitted originals are to be returned if the applicant is not enrolled — in person, by Russian Post or by courier service — with a postal address; this is not indicated where the documents are submitted in electronic form [1].
- The applicant signs confirmation of familiarisation with the admission rules approved by Innopolis University itself, including the rules for lodging an appeal against the results of the entrance examinations held by the University, with the charter, with the information on the date of grant and the registration number of the licence for educational activity and of the state accreditation of the educational activity on the programmes implemented, and with the educational programmes and other documents regulating the organisation of educational activity and the rights and duties of students [1].
- The applicant also confirms familiarisation with the information that, where admission is on a competitive basis, the applicant is given information about the competition and its results, with the information about the special rights and advantages at admission to bachelor's programmes, with the dates of concluding the contract for the provision of paid educational services, and with the need to state reliable information and submit genuine documents [1].
- The applicant confirms that no applications for admission are submitted simultaneously to more than 5 higher education institutions, including Innopolis University, and that no more than 5 fields of study are chosen at Innopolis University [1], and agrees to the transfer of the information of the application to the federal state information system «Единый портал государственных и муниципальных услуг (функций)» [1].
- The applicant confirms that the application on the basis of a special right is submitted only to Innopolis University, and only for the educational programme indicated [1].
- Applicants who submitted a document (documents) of a foreign state on education or on education and qualification undertake to provide a certificate of recognition of the foreign education and confirmation of completion of legalisation not later than the day of submission of the document of the established form, where those procedures are necessary [1].
- The application ends with the applicant's signature and full name [1].

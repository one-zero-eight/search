# Admission within the separate quota (отдельная квота) 2024/2025 — who may apply, the two lists and enrolment

## Who may apply

- The right to admission to bachelor's programmes at the expense of the federal budget within the separate quota (отдельная квота) belongs to the persons named in part 5 of article 71 of Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ (clause 105) [1].
- The separate quota is set by the University at not less than 10 % of the volume of the control figures for each area of study [1].

## Without examinations and on results

Admission to places within the separate quota is conducted in two ways (clause 106) [1]:

- without entrance examinations — for the persons named in points 1 and 7 of part 5 of article 71, and for the children of the persons named in points 2–4 of part 5 of article 71 and the children of the servicemen and employees named in point 6 of part 5 of article 71, if those persons or servicemen died or received an injury (wound, trauma, contusion) or a disease while performing the duties of military or other service during the special military operation (combat actions in the territories of foreign states) or were awarded the title of Hero of the Russian Federation or decorated with three Orders of Courage [1];
- on the results of the ЕГЭ or of the entrance examinations conducted by the University, at the applicant's choice — for the other persons named in part 5 of article 71 [1].

## Which results are used

- Applicants to the separate quota who apply on the results of the ЕГЭ or of internal examinations may take the general education entrance examinations regardless of whether they took the ЕГЭ, use the results of examinations on the basis of professional education where they have the right to take them, and use the results of the ЕГЭ [1].
- The results of the general education examinations taken on that ground are not counted for admission to places within the special quota, within the target quota, to the main places within the control figures or to paid places, except in the case of clause 108 [1].
- Where such applicants also belong to the persons named in clause 14 of the rules (for example disabled persons or foreign citizens who may take general education examinations held by the University), the results of the examinations taken under clause 14 are used both for the separate quota and for the other places [1].

## Publication of information

Information about the persons applying to the separate quota that is placed on the official site and/or on ЕПГУ — the lists of persons who submitted documents, the results of entrance examinations, the competitive lists, the information on enrolment and other information — is formed with the unique code given to the applicant, without the surname, first name and patronymic and without the insurance number of the individual personal account (clause 109) [1].

## Two competitive lists

- Enrolment to places within the separate quota is conducted at the priority stage of enrolment (clause 110) [1].
- The competitive list for the separate quota includes a list of persons applying without entrance examinations (competitive list No. 1) and a list of persons applying on the results of the ЕГЭ and/or internal examinations who have not less than the minimum number of points (competitive list No. 2) [1].
- Competitive list No. 1 is ranked by decreasing points for individual achievements; where those are equal — by the preferential right under part 9 of article 71; then by the preferential right under part 10 of article 71; then by the achievements taken into account where applicants are equal (clause 111) [1].
- Competitive list No. 2 is ranked by the criteria of clause 84 — by decreasing sum of competitive points, then by the points for the examinations according to their priority, then by the preferential rights under parts 9 and 10 of article 71 and by the achievements taken into account where applicants are equal (clause 112) [1].
- The persons of competitive list No. 2 are enrolled to the places remaining after the enrolment of the persons of competitive list No. 1 (clause 113) [1].
- Competitive list No. 1 shows the unique code, the points for individual achievements, the preferential rights and the information about the original of the document of education (or the mark on ЕПГУ) and about the priority of enrolment (clauses 114 and 86) [1]; competitive list No. 2 shows the unique code, the sum of competitive points and points for the examinations, the points for individual achievements, the information about the original and the priority of enrolment (clauses 115 and 86) [1].

## What the applicant must state and present

The applicant to places within the separate quota states in the application to which category of the persons named in clause 106 he belongs, and at the priority stage, not later than the day of completion of the marks about the original and of the acceptance of the original, presents to the University the original of the document confirming that he belongs to that category (clause 116) [1].

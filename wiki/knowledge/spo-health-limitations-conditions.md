# Special conditions for students with health limitations in secondary vocational education (СПО) — section 4 of the Regulation of 27 January 2025

## Where these rules come from

Section 4 of the Regulation on the procedure of organising and carrying out educational activity under secondary vocational education programmes of ANO VO «Innopolis University» (order of 27 January 2025 No. ОД/УД-04/2025/01/27, in force from 1 September 2025) sets out the features of organising educational activity for persons with health limitations [1]. The Regulation as a whole regulates the organisation and carrying out of educational activity under СПО programmes, including those features [2].

## Training under adapted programmes

- The training of persons with health limitations is carried out on the basis of adapted СПО programmes where necessary, and for invalids in accordance with the individual rehabilitation programme of the invalid [1].
- Training under such programmes is carried out with account of the psychophysical development, individual capabilities and state of health of the students [1].

## Special conditions

In the University's academic structural units special conditions of training, upbringing and development of such students are created, comprising the use of special educational programmes, methods of teaching and upbringing, special textbooks, teaching aids and didactic materials, special technical means of training for collective and individual use, the provision of the services of an assistant (helper) giving the students the necessary technical help, group and individual correctional classes, access to the buildings, and other conditions without which mastering the educational programmes is difficult or impossible [3].

## Accessibility of study

For the accessibility of СПО for students with health limitations the academic structural units ensure the following [4]:

- for students with health limitations in vision: adaptation of the official sites in the Internet to the international standard of accessibility of web content and web services (WCAG) with account of the special needs of visually impaired people; placement in places accessible to blind or partially sighted students of reference information about the class schedule in an adapted form, in large relief-contrast print of not less than 7.5 cm letter height on a white or yellow background and duplicated in Braille; the presence of an assistant giving help; the issue of alternative formats of printed materials (large print or audio files); and a place for a guide dog during the study hours of the student using it [4];
- for students with health limitations in hearing: duplication of the sound reference information about the class schedule in visual form (installation of monitors able to broadcast subtitles) and provision of adequate sound means of reproducing information [4];
- for students with disorders of the musculoskeletal system: material-technical conditions ensuring unhindered access to study rooms, canteens, toilet rooms and other premises and their stay in those premises [4].

## Joint or separate study, group size, interpreters

- The study process of students with health limitations may be organised both jointly with other students and in separate rooms, laboratories or in separate educational organisations [1].
- The number of students with health limitations in a study group is set at up to 15 people [1].
- With account of the special needs of such students they are given study and lecture materials in electronic form and the services of sign language interpreters and tactile sign language interpreters [1].

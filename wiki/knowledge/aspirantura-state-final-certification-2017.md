# State final certification of aspirantura programmes — the Regulation of 4 September 2017 (state examination and scientific report)

## The document

The Regulation on conducting the state final certification (ГИА) on higher education programmes — programmes of training scientific and pedagogical staff in aspirantura — was approved by order No. ОД/УД-05/2017/09/04 of 4 September 2017, signed by Director V. Semenikhin; control of its execution was entrusted to Vice-Rector S.V. Masyagin [1]. The page is an OCR scan of the PDF and parts of its text are corrupted, so numbers and wording should be checked against the PDF [1].

The Regulation governs the ГИА of aspirants completing state-accredited programmes: its forms, the requirements on the use of means of education and means of communication, the requirements on the persons involved in it, the procedure for filing and considering appeals, for changing and (or) annulling the results, and the features of the ГИА for students with health limitations [2]. It is based on Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29 December 2012, Government Decree No. 842 of 24 September 2013, orders of the Ministry of Education and Science, the Charter and other local acts [3].

The Regulation concerns the same subject as the later Regulation on the final certification of aspirants, approved by order No. ОД/УД-01/2025/02/25 of 25.02.2025 [4], under which the thesis is evaluated by an Expert-Attestation Commission (ЭАК) against the criteria of Federal Law No. 127-FZ [5], [6]; this Regulation instead sets a ГИА with a state examination and a scientific report before a state examination commission [7].

## General rules and admission

- The ГИА is conducted by state examination commissions to determine the compliance of the results of mastering the programmes with the requirements of the federal state educational standards of higher education (ФГОС ВО) [8]; the University ensures its conduct [9].
- A student who has no academic debt and has fully completed the curriculum or the individual curriculum of the programme is admitted [10]. Persons mastering a programme by self-education, or who studied on a programme without state accreditation, may pass the ГИА externally (экстерном) in the University on an accredited programme under this Regulation [11].
- No fee is charged from students for passing the ГИА [12], and students and the persons involved are forbidden to have on them and to use means of communication during the state attestation tests [13].
- The volume in credit units, the structure and the content of the ГИА are set by the University in accordance with ФГОС ВО [14]; the dates are set by the University independently [15]; the language is determined by the ГИА programme [16]; and the conduct of the tests with e-learning and distance educational technologies is determined by the educational programme [17].
- Results are graded «отлично», «хорошо», «удовлетворительно», «неудовлетворительно», and the grades «отлично», «хорошо» and «удовлетворительно» mean a successful pass [18]; a successful pass is the basis for issuing the student the document of higher education and qualification of the form established by the Ministry of Education and Science, and graduates are also issued the conclusion under paragraph 16 of Decree No. 842 [19].

## Forms, programme and schedule

- The ГИА is held in the form of a state examination and in the form of a scientific report on the main results of the prepared scientific qualification work (thesis), together the state attestation tests; the examination is held in written form and the report is presented orally [7].
- The examination covers one or several disciplines and (or) modules whose results are decisive for the professional activity of graduates [20]; the requirements on the report, the procedure for its preparation and presentation and the criteria for its assessment are set by the ГИА programme [21].
- The programme is developed by the responsible structural unit on the model of Annex 1 and approved by the Academic Council [22]; it, including the examination questions and the appeal procedure, is communicated to students not later than six months before the start by placing it in the electronic information and educational environment [23]. The examination follows the approved programme with the questions and recommendations for preparation, and a pre-examination consultation is held before it [24].
- In the model programme of Annex 1 the ticket of the state examination may be chosen once and not more than 120 minutes are given for the written answer; the oral presentation of the report lasts not more than 30 minutes [1].
- The schedule of the tests, with the dates, times and places and the pre-examination consultations, is approved by an administrative act not later than 30 calendar days before the first test and communicated to the student, the chairs and members of the commissions, the secretaries and the supervisors and consultants of reports; a break of not less than 7 calendar days is set between tests and the schedule is placed in the electronic information and educational environment [25].
- The results of the written examination are announced on the next working day after it, those of the report on the day of its presentation [26].

## The scientific report, the supervisor and the reviewers

- The report is drawn up under the requirements for an author's abstract of a Candidate of Sciences thesis in paragraph 25 of Government Decree No. 842 of 24.09.2013 and is submitted with the thesis to the research supervisor on paper as a manuscript and in electronic form not later than one month before the ГИА; the title page forms are in Annexes 2–3 [27]. The texts of reports, except those with state secrets, are placed in the University's electronic library system and checked for borrowing, access being given under legislation with exclusions of commercial information by decision of the rights holder [27].
- In the model programme the report's volume is 25–30 pages of typescript without appendices, in Times New Roman 14 pt with 1.5 line spacing, and the main results of the research must be published in not less than 2 articles in the reviewed scientific editions included in the list [1].
- The supervisor submits to the state examination commission a review on the report (Annex 4) and a statement on originality after the check for improper borrowings not later than 7 working days before the ГИА [27].
- The report is subject to review by two reviewers: one appointed from among the University's workers and the second from among persons who are not University workers, are not co-authors of works published on the thesis topic and cannot have a conflict of interest in assessing the report; a reviewer holds an academic degree (including a recognised foreign one) and scientific publications on the topic; the reviews (Annex 5) go to the commission not later than 7 working days before the start [28].
- The student is acquainted with the supervisor's review and the reviewer's review(s) not later than 5 calendar days before the presentation of the report [29].
- The supervisor's review form of Annex 4 weights the criteria: research activity 60 %, general characteristics of the research 30 % and the results of the research 10 %, with the scale 85–100 «отлично», 65–84 «хорошо», 50–64 «удовлетворительно» and 0–49 «неудовлетворительно» [1].

## Absence, unsatisfactory results and retaking

- A student who did not appear for a test for a valid reason (temporary incapacity, public or state duties, court summons, transport problems such as a cancelled flight or absent tickets, weather conditions or other cases recognised by the University as valid) may pass it within 6 months after the ГИА ends on submitting a document confirming the reason; a student who missed one test for a valid reason is admitted to the next test if there is one [30].
- Students who did not appear without a valid reason or received «неудовлетворительно», including those mentioned in clause 3.12 who did not pass within the term set for them, are expelled with a certificate of study (справка об обучении) as not having fulfilled the duties of conscientiously mastering the programme and completing the curriculum [31].
- A person who did not pass the ГИА may pass it again not earlier than one year and not later than five years after the date of the failed ГИА; on the person's application the person is reinstated for a period set by the University but not less than the period of the calendar study schedule for the ГИА on the programme [32].

## State examination and appeal commissions

- State examination commissions (chair, secretary and members) and appeal commissions (chair and members) are created for the ГИА and act for one calendar year [33]; they are created for each field of study, each programme, several fields or several programmes [34].
- The chair of the state examination commission is approved not later than 31 December of the year preceding the ГИА by the Ministry of Education and Science on the University's representation [35] from among persons not working at the University who hold a Doctor of Sciences degree (including a recognised foreign one) in the scientific specialty corresponding to the field of training; the chair of the appeal commission is the Director or another person authorised by him by an administrative act [36]. The compositions are approved not later than one month before the start [37]; the chairs organise and control the commissions' activity and ensure unity of the requirements set for students [38].
- The state examination commission consists of not less than 5 persons, of whom not less than 50 per cent are leading specialists — representatives of employers and (or) their associations in the field and (or) representatives of state, regional and municipal authorities — the rest being teaching staff and (or) research workers of the University and (or) other organisations holding a rank, degree or state honorary title or being laureates of state prizes; the appeal commission includes not less than 4 teaching staff or research workers who are not members of the state examination commissions [39].
- For the period of the ГИА the chair appoints a secretary of the state examination commission from among teaching staff, research workers or administrative workers; the secretary is not a member, keeps the minutes and submits the necessary materials to the appeal commission [40].
- A meeting is competent with not less than two thirds of the commission; meetings are conducted by the chairs, decisions are taken by a simple majority of the members taking part and the chair has a casting vote when the votes are equal [41]. Decisions are recorded in minutes in Russian and English, the minute on a test recording the questions put, the characteristics of the answers and the members' opinions on the student's preparedness and shortcomings; minutes are signed by the chair (the examination commission's also by its secretary), bound into books and kept in the University archive [42].

## Appeals

- A student has the right to appeal [43]: a written appeal about a violation, in the student's opinion, of the established procedure of the test and (or) disagreement with the results of the state examination [44], filed personally not later than the next working day after the results are announced [45]. For its consideration the secretary of the examination commission sends the minute, the chair's conclusion on procedural compliance and the student's written answers where they exist [46].
- The appeal is considered not later than 2 working days after it is filed at a meeting to which the chair of the examination commission and the student are invited, and the decision is communicated to the student within 3 working days, the student's acquaintance being certified by signature [47].
- On a procedural appeal the commission rejects it if the facts are not confirmed and (or) did not influence the result, or grants it if they are confirmed and influenced the result; in the latter case the result is annulled, the minute is passed to the examination commission not later than the next working day and the student may pass the test within the dates set by the University [48]. On disagreement with the results of the examination the commission rejects the appeal and keeps the result or grants it and sets another result, which is the basis for annulling the earlier result and setting a new one [49].
- The decision of the appeal commission is final and not subject to review [50]; a repeat test is held in the presence of one member of the appeal commission not later than the end of the student's studies, and an appeal against the repeat test is not accepted [51], [52].

## Students with disabilities and health limitations

- The ГИА for disabled students is conducted with account of the features of their psycho-physical development, individual possibilities and state of health [53]: it may be held in one room together with students without such limitations if this creates no difficulties; an assistant gives the necessary technical help (workplace, movement, reading and formatting the task, communication with the members of the commission); the necessary technical means may be used; and unhindered access to the rooms and toilets is ensured (ramps, handrails, widened doorways, lifts, the room on the first floor where there are no lifts, special chairs and other devices) [54]. All local acts on the ГИА are communicated to such students in an accessible form [55].
- On the student's written application the duration may be increased by not more than 90 minutes for a written examination, not more than 20 minutes for preparing an answer at an oral examination and not more than 15 minutes for presenting the report [56].
- Depending on the features: for the blind — tasks in Braille or as an electronic document readable with specialised software or read by an assistant, written tasks in Braille, on such a computer or dictated, and where needed writing accessories, Braille paper and the computer; for the visually impaired — enlarged print, individual even lighting of not less than 300 lux and, where needed, a magnifying device (own devices allowed); for the deaf, the hard of hearing and those with severe speech disorders — collective sound-amplifying apparatus and, where needed, individual apparatus, and at their wish a written form; for disorders of the musculoskeletal system — work on a computer with specialised software or dictation, and at their wish an oral form [57].
- Not later than 3 months before the start the student submits a written application on the special conditions needed, stating the individual features, whether an assistant is needed and whether the duration of each test must be increased, with documents confirming the features where the University does not hold them [58].
- In the model programme of Annex 1 an adapted ГИА programme is developed for such students, the form of the tests (oral, written on paper, written on a computer, testing and so on) is chosen with account of their individual psychophysical features, assessment tools with enlarged print are used for the visually impaired in testing, an accompanying person provides technical support and additional time for preparing the answer may be given [1].

## Final provisions and annexes

The Regulation is approved by order of the Director or another authorised person and enters into force on approval [59]; changes and additions are made in accordance with changes in Russian legislation on the submission of the department of educational activity by order of the Director or another authorised person [60]. Its annexes contain the model ГИА programme (Annex 1), the title pages of the thesis and of the report (Annexes 2–3), the forms of the supervisor's review and of the reviewer's review (Annexes 4–5) and the forms of the commissions' minutes on the state examination and on the report (Annexes 6–7) [1].

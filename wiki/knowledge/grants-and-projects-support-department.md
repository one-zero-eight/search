# Grants and Projects Support Department (Отдел поддержки грантов и проектов) — place, tasks, functions and rights

## The document and the place of the Department

The Regulation on the Grants and Projects Support Department (Положение об Отделе поддержки грантов и проектов) regulates the activity of the Department of ANO VO «Innopolis University» and determines its tasks, functions, rights and duties [1].

The page carries the place «г. Иннополис» and an approval block «УТВЕРЖДЕНО приказом» with the number ОД/АД-09/2021/05/31 and the director for development and HR policy R.F. Valiev [2]. It is a scan read by OCR, and it warns in its front matter that numbers and dates should be checked against the PDF before relying on them [2].

The Grants and Projects Support Department is a structural subdivision of the Centre for Digital Economy Project Development of the University and belongs to the category «иные структурные подразделения» (other structural subdivisions) [3]. The Department reports directly to the director of the Centre for Digital Economy Project Development [4]. On its own page the Centre is a structural subdivision of the Department for project and research activity and reports directly to the vice-rector — head of that department [5] [6].

In its activity the Department follows the Constitution of the Russian Federation, the Labour Code of the Russian Federation, the federal law «Об образовании в Российской Федерации» and other legislative and normative legal acts of the Russian Federation regulating the activity of higher education institutions, the University's Charter, decisions of the Academic, Supervisory and Operational councils of the University, orders and instructions of the director of the University, other local normative acts of the University and this Regulation [7].

The structure, the number of staff and the staffing table of the Department, and changes to them, are approved by the director of the University [8]. The Department is headed by its head, who is hired and dismissed by an order of the director of the University [9]. The working conditions of the Department's employees are determined by the employment contracts concluded with each employee and by the University's internal labour regulations [2]. Their rights, duties and degree of responsibility are established by job descriptions [10]. Employees of the Department may be involved, within their competences, in work under grants, projects, subsidies and other programmes implemented by the University [11]. The Regulation and additions and changes to it are approved by the director of the University or by another person authorised by him [12].

## Tasks

- systematic control over the course of project implementation and over the preparation of reporting [13]
- observance of normative requirements, completeness and good-quality execution of documentation, and observance of the established procedure for its approval [14]
- organising the provision of timely and good-quality reporting documentation on grants and projects [15]

## Functions

- interaction with representatives of grant-giving organisations and with the responsible staff of monitoring organisations and customer organisations on the implementation of projects and the fulfilment of result indicators [16]
- ensuring state accounting of the results of a project's implementation in ФГАНУ ЦИТиС (ЕГИСУ НИОКТР), where the contract or agreement provides for it [17]
- holding kick-off working meetings with the responsible executors of a project to communicate the grant-giver's or customer's requirements to the composition of the reporting documentation, the main requirements and the procedure for submitting it, the project's stages, the fulfilment of result indicators and the deadlines, the project estimate, the requirements for its execution and the possibility of making changes [18]
- organising and moderating working meetings with project heads and the main executors to communicate the current state of the project's implementation, the fulfilment of result indicators and the grant-giver's or customer's remarks, if any [19]
- documentary support of a project: preparing and agreeing orders, service notes, reference materials and other documents on the project and providing reporting-documentation templates, if any [20]
- consulting the persons responsible for a project on preparing the substantive part of the reporting documentation in accordance with the requirements of the grant-giver, monitor or customer and with methodological instructions, if any [21]
- controlling the process of carrying out work and preparing reporting on the projects being implemented as regards observance of deadlines and conformity with the requirements of the grant-giver or customer [22]
- controlling the same process as regards the fulfilment of the formal values of the project's result indicators [23]
- forming a complete set of reporting documentation ready for transfer to the grant-giver or customer according to the requirements and methodological instructions, if any [24]
- ensuring observance of normative requirements, completeness of documentation and the established procedure for its approval [25]
- forming the package of documents confirming the fulfilment of result indicators [26]
- analysing the execution of the expenditure estimate and the accounting reporting [27]
- accounting for and controlling expenses, correcting estimates and periodically reconciling planned and actual expenses with the financial and economic department [28]
- presenting analytical, statistical and similar information on the Department's activity [29]

## Rights

- request from the University's structural subdivisions the information and materials needed for the work within the Department's competence [30]
- with the consent of the heads of the University's structural subdivisions, involve the employees of those subdivisions in preparing draft local acts and other documents and in developing and carrying out measures conducted by the Department [31]
- take part in meetings held by the University's management when discussing questions whose solution falls within the Department's competence [32]
- put before the University's management proposals to cancel, amend or supplement the University's local acts [33]
- interact with bodies of state authority and administration, legal entities and the University's subdivisions on questions within the Department's competence [34]

## Interaction with other subdivisions

Interaction of the Department with the University's other structural subdivisions is determined by the tasks and functions assigned to it by this Regulation [35].

- with the financial and economic department on separate accounting for a project: communicating information about a new project, the conditions of spending and the amount of overhead costs, monthly reconciliation of the project's expenses (bringing out the remainder), and preparing financial documents for forming the reporting documentation and presenting it for the grant-giver's or customer's check [36]
- with the department of technical control, metrology and standardisation when preparing a project's reporting documentation and working through remarks [37]
- with the purchasing department for carrying out the necessary procurement procedures [38]
- with other structural subdivisions on preparing draft local acts, orders, instructions, contracts and other documents and on preparing conclusions and written and oral certificates on questions within the Department's competence [39]

The Department may be a base for practical training of the University's students [40].

## Reporting

On the basis of the relevant local acts, orders and instructions, the Department may, within its activity, be responsible for preparing information, certificates and reports and for keeping them up to date [41].

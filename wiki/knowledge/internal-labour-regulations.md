# Internal labour regulations (Правила внутреннего трудового распорядка) — document, hiring, transfer and dismissal

## The document and whom it covers

The Rules of the internal labour regulations (Правила внутреннего трудового распорядка) of ANO VO «Innopolis University» were approved by order of 21 October 2024 № ОД/АД-01/2024/10/21 [1]. They determine the labour routine in the University (the employer) and regulate the procedure of hiring, transfer and dismissal of employees, the basic rights, duties and responsibility of the parties to the employment contract, the working time regime, rest time, measures of reward and penalties, and other questions of labour relations [2].

They are a local normative act developed under Russian labour legislation, other normative legal acts containing labour law norms, including legislation on education, and the University's Charter, in order to strengthen labour discipline, organise work efficiently, use working time rationally and ensure high quality and productivity of work [3].

- The Rules apply to all employees of the University [4].
- Changes and additions are developed and approved by the employer taking into account the opinion of the employees' representative body (where one exists) [5].
- The official representative of the employer is the Director, who approves local acts on internal labour routine and may delegate their approval [6].
- Labour duties and rights of employees are specified in employment contracts and/or job descriptions [7], [8].

The Rules define the corporate portal Bitrix as the internal system for organising the work of staff (portal.university.innopolis.ru), corporate e-mail as the employer's information service with all employee addresses under one domain, the employee's account on the portal as the digital tools accessible with the login and password of the corporate e-mail, electronic channels of communication as the software through which the employee and employer interact, and electronic HR document flow (ЭКДО) as the creation, signing, use, storage and provision of access to employment-related documents in electronic form without obligatory paper duplication [9].

Under ЭКДО employees recognise the equivalence of their electronic signature and their handwritten signature, and the employer recognises electronic documents signed with an electronic signature as equal in legal force to paper documents [10]. Interaction with an employee performing work remotely is determined by agreements between the participants of electronic interaction, by the procedure for organising electronic document flow and use of electronic documents, and by other local acts [11].

## Hiring

An employment contract is concluded in writing, on paper or in electronic format [12]. Before signing it the employer must acquaint the employee, against signature, with the Rules, the collective agreement (if any) and other local acts directly connected with the employee's work; acquaintance may take place through ЭКДО [13].

The person entering work presents a passport or other identity document, the labour book and/or information on labour activity (except on a first contract or on combined work), a document confirming registration in the individual (personalised) accounting system, military registration documents, an education and/or qualification document where the work requires special knowledge, a criminal record certificate, the minors' commission decision on admission to pedagogical activity for the persons named in the Labour Code, and other documents where the law requires them; foreign citizens and stateless persons also present a work permit or patent, a temporary residence permit or a residence permit depending on their status [14].

- On a first-ever contract the employer issues an electronic labour book and no paper labour book is issued [15]; if the labour book is lost or damaged, a new one is issued on the person's written application stating the reason [16].
- The contract is drawn up in two copies, one for each party; it enters into force from the day of signature or from the day of actual admission to work with the employer's knowledge or instruction; the employee must start on the day set by the contract, or on the next working day after it enters into force if no day is set; if the employee does not start, the employer may annul the contract [17]. Contracts and additional agreements may be concluded electronically with an enhanced qualified electronic signature of the employer and a qualified or unqualified enhanced signature of the employee [17].
- A contract not drawn up in writing is considered concluded if the employee started work with the employer's knowledge or instruction; the employer must then put it in writing no later than three working days from the admission [18]. Admitting a person to work without the employer's knowledge or instruction is prohibited, and the person who did so may be held liable, including materially [19].
- Contracts may be for an indefinite term or for a fixed term of not more than five years [20]; a contract with no term and no ground for a fixed term is deemed concluded for an indefinite term [21].
- A probation condition may be agreed in the contract [22]; without it the employee is hired without probation [23]; the procedure, results and criteria of the probation period are set by a separate Regulation on the probation period [24].
- A hiring order is issued on the basis of the contract and must correspond to its conditions [25]. Before starting work the employer conducts instruction on safety rules at the workplace, training in safe methods and first aid and labour protection instruction, and an employee who has not passed it is not admitted to work [26].

The employer keeps labour books for each employee who has worked more than five days where the work is the main one, unless the law provides otherwise [27]. It notifies the last place of service of a contract with a person who in the previous two years held state or municipal service positions [28]. It notifies the migration authority of the conclusion and termination of a contract with a foreign citizen [29]. It forms electronic information on labour activity and length of service and transmits it for storage in the Pension Fund resources [30].

On written application the employee receives that information from the employer within three working days during employment and on the day of termination on dismissal, in paper or electronic form, including by e-mail to university@innopolis.ru [31]; incorrect or incomplete information is corrected on written application [32].

## Corporate accounts and equipment

The corporate e-mail address is sent to the employee's personal e-mail by the Department for Support and Development of Infrastructure on the first working day [33]. Domain accounts and corporate mail are created for staff on a request from the Personnel Selection Sector through the Bitrix corporate portal (https://portal.university.innopolis.ru) and for academic staff on a request to it@innopolis.ru from the Department for Attraction and Support of Academic Staff [34]; the login, password and connection link are sent to the personal e-mail at 9.00 of the first working day [35], and the account and e-mail are blocked at the end of the last working day [36].

Equipment is handed over by an acceptance act [37]. The employee must return it in the state received (allowing for normal wear) not later than 17:00 of the last working day by a return act at the employer's location, and at any time on the employer's demand within one working day of receiving the demand [38].

## Transfer

A transfer is a permanent or temporary change of the employee's labour function and/or of the structural subdivision in which the employee works (if named in the contract) while continuing work for the same employer, as well as a transfer to another locality together with the employer [39]. A transfer is possible only to work not contraindicated for the employee's health and only with the employee's written consent [40].

A temporary transfer for up to one month to work not provided by the contract, without the employee's written consent, is allowed in the following cases: to prevent a natural or technogenic catastrophe, industrial accident, accident at work, fire, flood, famine, earthquake, epidemic or epizootic and in any exceptional circumstances threatening the life or normal living conditions of the population or part of it; and in cases of idle time (temporary stoppage for economic, technological, technical or organisational reasons), prevention of destruction or damage to property or replacement of a temporarily absent employee, where these are caused by the emergency circumstances of part 2 of article 72.2 of the Labour Code [41].

A transfer is formalised by a written additional agreement in two copies, one for each party, and confirmed by the employee's signature on the employer's copy [42], and by an order issued on the basis of that agreement [43].

## Dismissal

The contract may be terminated on the grounds and in the procedure of the Labour Code and other federal laws [44]. Termination is formalised by an order of the employer, with which the employee must be acquainted and a certified copy of which is issued on request; if the order cannot be brought to the employee's knowledge or the employee refuses to sign, a record is made on the order [45].

- The day of termination is in all cases the last day of work, except where the employee did not actually work but the place of work (position) was retained by law [46].
- On dismissal the employee returns, not later than the day of termination, all documents, equipment, tools and other material values handed over by the employer and documents created in performing the duties [47].
- On the day of termination the employer issues the labour book or provides information on labour activity and settles with the employee; if the employee did not work that day, the sums are paid not later than the day following the employee's demand for settlement, and on written application certified copies of work-related documents are also issued [48].
- The record of the ground and reason of termination is made in exact accordance with the wording of the Labour Code or other federal law and with a reference to the article, part and paragraph [49].
- Where the labour book cannot be issued or the information provided on the day of termination because of absence or refusal, the employer sends a notice to appear or to consent to posting, or sends the information by registered letter; from that moment it is released from liability for delay, but must issue the labour book or information within three working days of a written request of the former employee [50].
- Where the amounts due on dismissal are disputed, the employer must pay the undisputed sum within the period of point 4.5; if the last day of the period falls on a non-working day, the period ends on the next working day [51].

## Final provisions

The Rules enter into force from the moment of their approval by the director; changes and additions may be introduced on the initiative of the employer or the employees in the procedure established by labour legislation [52]. Questions not settled by the Rules are governed by the Labour Code, other normative legal acts of the Russian Federation and the employer's local acts [53]. If the employer's structure changes, including abolition of a subdivision named in the Rules, its functions are carried out by the successor subdivision without amending the Rules [54].

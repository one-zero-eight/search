# Enrolment order for bachelor's paid places 2026 (order of 25 August 2026)

## What this document is

The University's order of 25 August 2026 № УД/ЛС-03/2026/08/25 (г. Иннополис) «О зачислении для обучения по образовательным программам бакалавриата на места по договорам об оказании платных образовательных услуг» [1].

It was issued in accordance with the Rules of admission to the University's higher education programmes — bachelor's and master's programmes — for the 2026/2027 academic year, approved by order of 10.04.2026 № ОД/УД-01/2026/04/10, and with a decision of the University's Admissions Committee (minutes of the meeting of 25.08.2026 № 8) [1].

The page is a scan read by OCR and warns that numbers and dates should be checked against the PDF before relying on them [1].

## What the order decides

- To admit from 1 September 2026 into the student body of ANO VO «Innopolis University», to the first year of full-time (очная) study in bachelor's programmes, to places under contracts for the provision of paid educational services, the persons who successfully passed the entrance examinations and passed the competition, in accordance with the appendix to the order [2].
- Control over the execution of the order is assigned to the Vice-Rector — head of the department of academic policy and higher education E.S. Bobrov; the order is signed by Director D.V. Vandyukov [3].
- The appendix is titled «Список лиц, зачисляемых для обучения в АНО ВО «Университет Иннополис»» [3].

## How the appendix is built

The appendix has numbered sections, one per area of training and profile of the educational programme; the sections for the four bachelor's profiles list, for every admitted person, the number of points and the number of the education contract (numbers of the form ОФ/Б229/2026), in descending order of the competitive score; the column headed «Фамилия Имя Отчество» shows only a numeric code, not a name [1].

## The lists by profile

- 09.03.01 Информатика и вычислительная техника, profile «Инженерия информационных систем» — the rows of the list are numbered up to 61 [1].
- 09.03.01 Информатика и вычислительная техника, profile «Математические основы искусственного интеллекта» — the rows are numbered up to 72 [1].
- 09.03.01 Информатика и вычислительная техника, profile «Анализ данных и искусственный интеллект» — the rows are numbered up to 55 [1].
- 15.03.06 Мехатроника и робототехника, profile «Робототехника» — the rows are numbered up to 9 [1].

The rows follow the scores in descending order, with isolated rows out of sequence in the OCR text (a row with a score of 228 after a row with a score of 224 in the first list) [1].

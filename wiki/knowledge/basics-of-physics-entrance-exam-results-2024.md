# Entrance examination «Основы физики» for bachelor programmes (22 July 2024) — published results

## What the document is

The page is the published protocol of the entrance examination «Основы физики» (basics of physics) for bachelor programmes, held by Innopolis University on its own on 22.07.2024 [1].

It states the date of the examination, 22.07.2024, the name of the examination, Основы физики, and carries the heading «Результаты вступительного испытания проводимого АНО ВО "Университет Иннополис" самостоятельно» — the results of an entrance examination conducted by the University itself [1]. The examination is named in the heading as one for bachelor programmes («Вступительное испытание на программам бакалавриата»), so the page is about a bachelor admission examination [1].

## What the protocol lists

The results table has the columns №, СНИЛС/Уникальный код, оценка and оценка (прописью): a serial number, the participant's СНИЛС or unique code, the score as a figure and the same score written out in words [1]. Scores are published against a СНИЛС or an identifier rather than a name — here in the form [скрыто] and [скрыто] [1].

## The results of 22 July 2024

The protocol contains three rows, in the order of the table [1]:

- row 1: identifier [скрыто], score 82 (Восемьдесят два) [1];
- row 2: the same identifier, [скрыто], and the same score, 82 (Восемьдесят два), so the page does not distinguish these two entries [1];
- row 3: identifier [скрыто], score 68 (Шестьдесят восемь) [1].

## What the page does not say

The page states no pass mark and no minimum score, and it records no decision on admission [1].

The heading of the table is repeated below the results as two further blocks («Наименование вступительного испытания | Основы | физики» and «22.07 | .2024»), so the name of the examination and the date appear on the page twice; the scores have to be taken from the rows of the results table itself [1].

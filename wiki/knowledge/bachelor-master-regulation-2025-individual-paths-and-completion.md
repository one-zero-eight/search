# Individual curricula, credit for previous study, assessment and completion — the Regulation of 22 September 2025

## Where these rules come from

These rules are sections 2 and 3 of the Regulation on the procedure of organising and carrying out educational activity in bachelor's and master's programmes of ANO VO «Innopolis University», approved by order of the University of 22 September 2025 [1]. The Regulation is binding for such activity at the University [2].

## Individual curriculum, accelerated study and credit for previous study

The organisation of the educational process under an individual curriculum, including accelerated study, follows the Regulation and the University's local acts [3].

A student who has secondary vocational, higher or additional education, and (or) is or was studying in such a programme, and (or) has abilities and (or) a level of development allowing to master the programme in a shorter term than the term set by the University according to the educational standard, may be taught in accelerated form under an individual curriculum by decision of the University in the procedure of its local acts; the decision is taken on the student's personal application [4]. In accelerated study the term is shortened by crediting the learning results of separate disciplines, modules and (or) practical trainings mastered during secondary vocational and (or) higher education and additional education (if any), and (or) by increasing the pace of mastering the programme [5].

A student has the right to credit the learning results of separate disciplines (modules) and (or) other components, including practical trainings, mastered during secondary vocational and (or) higher education and additional education (if any); crediting of results mastered in other organisations is done under point 7 of part 1 of article 34 of Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ, crediting of results mastered within the University under the procedure and in the forms of the University's local acts; credited results are taken into account as interim assessment results [6]. When studying under an individual curriculum, including accelerated study, the annual volume of the programme is calculated without the volume of those components whose learning results were credited and may not exceed the volume set by the educational standard [7].

## Ongoing and interim assessment

The University carries out ongoing assessment of academic performance and interim assessment; mastering a programme, including a part or the whole of a discipline (module), other component or practical training, is accompanied by interim assessment; the forms of interim assessment, their frequency and procedure, and the procedure and deadlines for clearing academic debt are set by the University's local acts [8].

The procedure of interim assessment includes the system of grading and the criteria for marks; if that system differs from the marks «отлично», «хорошо», «удовлетворительно», «неудовлетворительно», «зачтено», «не зачтено», the University establishes rules for bringing the grades of its own scale to that system [9].

## Academic debt and repeat interim assessment

Unsatisfactory results of interim assessment in one or several disciplines (modules) or other components, including practical trainings, or failure to undergo interim assessment without valid reasons are recognised as academic debt, and students must clear it [10].

The University sets for students with academic debt the deadlines of the repeat interim assessment for each discipline, module or other component including practical training; a student who has not cleared the debt at the first repeat interim assessment is given the opportunity of a second repeat interim assessment held by a commission created by the University; the repeat interim assessment is held not later than the expiry of one year after the debt arose, and the time of the student's illness, academic leave or maternity leave is not included in that period [11]. The University may hold the first and (or) the second repeat interim assessment during vacations, setting several dates for it both during vacations and during the period of mastering the programme [12].

## Externs

Persons mastering a programme in the form of self-education (where the educational standard allows it) and persons who studied in a programme without state accreditation may be enrolled as externs to undergo interim and state final attestation in the corresponding state-accredited programme; the extern's individual curriculum providing for that attestation is approved within a term set by the University but not later than one month from enrolment; the conditions and procedure of enrolling externs and the deadlines of their attestation are set by the University itself [13].

## Final attestation and documents

A student who has no academic debt and has fulfilled the curriculum or individual curriculum in full is admitted to state final attestation, unless the procedure of state final attestation provides otherwise [14]. Persons who successfully passed final (state final) attestation receive documents of education and qualification; persons who did not pass it or received unsatisfactory results, and persons who mastered part of a programme and (or) were expelled, receive a certificate of study or of the period of study in the format set by the University itself [15]. After passing final attestation students are given vacations on their application within the term of the programme, and on their expiry they are expelled in connection with obtaining education [16]. The document of education submitted on admission is given out of the personal file to a person who completed or left the University before completing the programme, and also to a student on his application, while a copy certified by the University remains in the personal file [17].

## Students with health limitations

Study by students with health limitations is organised with account of the features of their psychophysical development, individual possibilities and state of health, and may be organised both together with other students and in separate groups [18]. The University creates special conditions for them: special educational programmes and methods, special textbooks, manuals and didactic materials, special technical means of collective and individual use, the services of an assistant, group and individual correctional classes, access to the buildings and other conditions without which mastering programmes is impossible or difficult; special textbooks, manuals and other literature, and the services of sign-language and typhlo-sign-language interpreters, are provided free of charge [19].

For persons with health limitations affecting sight the University ensures [20]:

- an alternative version of the official site for the visually impaired [20];
- reference information about the class schedule placed in accessible places and in adapted form — in large relief-contrast font (on white or yellow background) and duplicated in Braille [20];
- the presence of an assistant giving the student the necessary help [20];
- alternative formats of printed materials (large font or audio files) [20];
- access to the University's building for a blind student using a guide dog [20].

For persons with health limitations affecting hearing the University ensures the duplication of sound reference information about the schedule by visual means (monitors able to broadcast subtitles, their size and number depending on the size of the room) and the provision of adequate sound and visual means of reproducing information; for persons with disorders of the musculoskeletal system the material and technical conditions must allow unimpeded access to studies, canteens, toilets and other premises and stay there (ramps, handrails, widened doorways, lifts, local lowering of barrier posts, special chairs and other devices) [21].

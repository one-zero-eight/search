# Dates of the 2026/27 admission — the applicant's calendar «Сроки проведения приема» and the terms set by the admission rules

## What the dates are and where they come from

The terms of admission are set by the University in accordance with chapter XII «Сроки приема на обучение» of its admission rules (пункт 126) [1]; the admission rules for bachelor's and master's programmes for the 2026/2027 academic year were approved by order of 10 April 2026 № ОД/УД‐01/2026/04/10 [1]. The rules give the terms separately for each case — bachelor's places within the control figures (пункт 127), additional admission to bachelor's places within the control figures (пункт 128), bachelor's paid places (пункт 129), master's places within the control figures (пункт 130), additional admission to master's places within the control figures (пункт 131) and master's paid places (пункт 132), each of them for the full-time form [1].

The University also publishes the applicant's calendar («Календарь абитуриента») — information on the terms of admission to bachelor's and master's programmes in the 2026/27 academic year [2]. It is laid out as tables, one table for each case — bachelor's places within the control figures, bachelor's paid places, master's places within the control figures and master's paid places — and each table is marked as the full-time form («очно») [2]. The calendar gives no clause numbers and is shorter than the rules: it shows the dates up to and including the main stage of enrolment, and it does not show the additional admission described in пункты 128, 131 and 133 of the rules [2] [1].

## Bachelor's programmes, places within the control figures, full-time form

- The acceptance of applications and documents starts on 20 June of the year of admission; it ends on 20 July for those taking entrance examinations and on 25 July for those not taking them, and on that day applications and documents are accepted until 17:00 Moscow time [1].
- Entrance examinations are held from 1 July to 25 July of the year of admission [1].
- The competitive lists are published on 27 July [1].
- At the priority stage the presentation of consent ends on 1 August (until 12:00 Moscow time) and the order on enrolment is issued on 3 August [1].
- At the main stage the presentation of consent ends on 5 August (until 12:00 Moscow time) and the order on enrolment is issued on 7 August [1].
- At the additional stage the presentation of consent ends on 9 August (until 12:00 Moscow time) and the orders on enrolment are issued on 11 August [1].
- The University sets the number of places for additional admission not later than 12 August of the year of admission [1].
- The applicant's calendar gives these dates up to the main stage: start on 20 June; 20 July and 25 July (until 17:00 Moscow time); examinations from 1 July to 25 July; lists on 27 July; consent on 1 August (until 12:00 Moscow time) and the order on 3 August; consent on 5 August (until 12:00 Moscow time) and the order on 7 August [2].
- The applicant's calendar does not give the additional stage of enrolment or the date by which the number of places for additional admission is set [2].

## Additional admission to bachelor's places within the control figures, full-time form

- The acceptance of applications and documents starts on 12 August; it ends on 20 August for those taking entrance examinations and on 21 August for those not taking them, and on 21 August applications and documents are accepted until 17:00 Moscow time [1].
- Entrance examinations are held from 13 August to 21 August [1].
- The competitive lists are published on 22 August [1].
- At the priority stage the presentation of consent ends on 25 August (until 12:00 Moscow time) and the orders on enrolment are issued on 26 August [1].
- At the main stage the presentation of consent ends on 28 August (until 12:00 Moscow time) and the orders on enrolment are issued on 29 August [1].
- The applicant's calendar does not give the dates of additional admission [2].

## Bachelor's programmes, paid places, full-time form

- The acceptance of applications and documents runs from 20 June to 11 August of the year of admission [1].
- Entrance examinations are held from 1 July to 18 August [1].
- The competitive lists are published on 20 August [1].
- The conclusion of contracts on the provision of paid educational services ends on 24 August and the order on enrolment is issued on 25 August [1].
- The applicant's calendar gives the same dates for this case [2].

## Master's programmes, places within the control figures, full-time form

- The acceptance of applications and documents runs from 20 June to 14 August of the year of admission [1].
- Entrance examinations are held from 1 July to 20 August [1].
- At the main stage the competitive lists are published on 21 August, the presentation of consent ends on 24 August (until 12:00 Moscow time) and the order on enrolment is issued on 25 August [1].
- At the additional stage the presentation of consent ends on 26 August (until 12:00 Moscow time) and the orders on enrolment are issued on 27 August [1].
- The University sets the number of places for additional admission not later than 30 August of the year of admission [1].
- The applicant's calendar gives the dates of the main stage: acceptance from 20 June to 14 August, examinations from 1 July to 20 August, lists on 21 August, consent on 24 August (until 12:00 Moscow time) and the order on 25 August [2].
- The applicant's calendar does not give the additional stage of enrolment or the date by which the number of places for additional admission is set [2].

## Additional admission to master's places within the control figures, full-time form

- The acceptance of applications and documents starts on 31 August and ends not later than 10 September of the year of admission [1].
- The presentation of consent ends on 15 September (until 12:00 Moscow time), and enrolment is completed not later than 20 September [1].
- The applicant's calendar does not give the dates of additional admission [2].

## Master's programmes, paid places, full-time form

- The acceptance of applications and documents runs from 20 June to 18 August of the year of admission [1].
- Entrance examinations are held from 1 July to 21 August [1].
- The competitive lists are published on 22 August, the conclusion of contracts on paid educational services ends on 27 August and the order on enrolment is issued on 28 August [1].
- The applicant's calendar gives the same dates for this case [2].

## Paid places: additional admission and the sameness of dates

- Additional admission to paid places on bachelor's and master's programmes, full-time form, is held only for persons applying on the basis of a document of a foreign state on education; it is held once and ends for the full-time form not later than 31 October of the year of admission [1].
- Enrolment to paid places is conducted on the same dates for all conditions of admission — for bachelor's programmes, and separately for master's programmes [1].
- The applicant's calendar does not give the dates of additional admission to paid places [2].

## How the applicant's calendar page is laid out

- The page presents the terms as tables, one table for each case, and each table's heading is marked as the full-time form [2].
- The heading row of the table on bachelor's paid places carries the line «Издание приказа о зачислении на основном этапе зачисления» [2].
- The heading rows of the table on master's places within the control figures and of the table on master's paid places carry «Издание приказа о зачислении» [2].

# Master's grant competition — eligibility, stages, winners and conditions

## What the grant covers

A grant covers either (partially or fully) the cost of educational services for master's programs during the standard period of study, or material support in the form of university scholarships [1], [2]. The grant fund is formed from the Organizer's funds and consists of the total sum of grants, set for each category of winners within 25 % to 100 % of the cost of study, and of the total sum of university scholarships [3], [4], [5]. The cost of educational services is determined annually before the start of the academic year, by 31 May of the year of admission [6].

A grant cannot be replaced by other rewards or paid in cash, except for the university scholarship component [7]. Transferring a grant to another person is not allowed [7].

## Purpose and openness of the competition

The competition aims to support talented young specialists in information technology, to involve students in new forms of organising the study process and to spread the best educational practices [8]. It is organised by the University, is open, and information about it is published on the Official Website, in social networks and in other public sources [9], [10].

## Eligibility

Any citizen of the Russian Federation or CIS countries, stateless person over 18 who has a higher education degree at any level, or is completing higher education at the start of the academic year for which the grant is provided, may participate [11]. Persons who are or have been students at the University's master's programs are not eligible, except when applying for reinstatement after expulsion [11].

## Contest period

The competition is held annually from 1 December of the year preceding admission to 16 August of the admission year [12]. Grants pay for study from the beginning of the academic year that starts after the end of the current year's competition [12]. A participant may enter no more than once per contest period [13]. The competition is not a lottery or another risk-based game, and participants bear no property risks connected with taking part [14].

## Three stages

**Stage 1 — registration and online testing.** Registration and a profile online test and an English language test (for those applying to English-taught programs) are held online on the Official Website [15], [16]. Passing scores: 45 % correct answers for the profile test and 45 % for the English test [17]. Participants who pass are invited to the next stage by email and must confirm participation within 7 calendar days, or they are considered withdrawn [18]. The Organizer may allow certain categories of participants to skip the online tests [19], and may admit participants who did not reach the passing score on the condition of receiving a grant of no more than 50 % of tuition cost [20].

**Stage 2 — profile testing and interviews.** Conducted online with cyberproctoring, lasting up to 30 days [21], [22]. It may be held repeatedly according to a schedule the Organizer makes itself, between 1 December of the year preceding admission and 1 August of the admission year, and a participant who passed stage 1 may take part in it only once [22]. Includes an interview with a teaching representative, an English test (for English-taught programs), IT problem-solving, and assessment of communication, teamwork, leadership, stress resistance, and conflict tolerance [23]. For applicants to "Software Engineering," "Security Systems and Network Engineering," or "Artificial Intelligence and Data Engineering" (Advanced Engineering Schools programs), an additional online interview with partner company representatives may be required [23]. The Organizer may allow a shortened stage for certain categories [24], [25].

**Final stage — determining grant recipients.** The Organizer forms the list of grant recipients from the winners after the Contest Commission decisions [26]; this third stage runs until 5 August of the admission year inclusive [15]. The list is formed by 10 August of the admission year, and the results are communicated to the recipients at the e-mail addresses they gave at registration [27].

## Documents for stage 1

- Completed online questionnaire (name, date of birth, citizenship, city, education details, desired program) [28].
- Motivation letter in English [28].
- CV in English [28].
- Copy of higher education diploma with appendices (or a study certificate for final-year students) [28].
- English language certificate (if available) [28].
- Consent to personal data processing [28].
- Any other documents requested by the Organizer [28].

## Winner determination

Winners are determined by the Contest Commission based on the second stage results [29]. The Commission's composition and size are approved by the Organizer and include, among others, the vice-rector — head of the academic policy and educational activity management, the director for development and HR policy, the head of the centre for pre-university preparation and admission of applicants, or persons replacing them, and other persons determined by the Organizer [30]. Results and the winner list are approved within four months after each selection [31]; the decision names the programs for which the grant may be used, and enrolment on other programs requires a special Commission decision after additional tests [31]. The Commission's decision is final and not subject to appeal [32]. The possible number of winners is determined by the Organizer annually [33].

## Becoming a grant recipient

Winners must complete the admission process and submit documents per the University's admission rules, including an application for a place funded by federal, regional, or local budgets where applicable [34]. A winner who does not meet these requirements loses the right to the grant [35]. Winners must then:

- Conclude an education contract with the University before the admission order date [36].
- Conclude a grant agreement with the University before the start of the academic year following the competition [36].
- Participate in the University's Summer School (if held), on a voluntary basis [36].

Grants not claimed by the start of the academic year are not issued and do not carry over to the next year [37].

## Categories and grant sizes

The Organizer annually determines categories of winners, the grant amount (from 25 % to 100 % of tuition cost), and the grant composition for each category [38], [4]. If a winner qualifies for multiple categories, priority is given to the funded-place category and, among paid-place categories, to the one covering the largest share of tuition cost [39]. Participants admitted without the passing score form a separate category [20].

## Obligation to work in Innopolis

Grants are provided on condition that the recipient undertakes to work in the city of Innopolis [40]. A recipient enrolled on a program run within the federal project "Advanced Engineering Schools" undertakes to work for a partner company or partner of the University or, in the cases directly set by the grant agreement, at a main workplace in Innopolis with a legal entity that is a resident of the Innopolis special economic zone and/or is registered or has a subdivision in Innopolis, in the profession (qualification) obtained, with an employment contract [41]. A recipient on the "Information Technology Entrepreneurship" program undertakes to work in his technological startup or, in the cases set by the grant agreement, for a partner of the University or for a legal entity registered or having a subdivision in Innopolis that is a resident of the special economic zone [42]. In other cases the recipient undertakes to work at a main workplace in the profession obtained, for a partner of the University or for a legal entity in Innopolis that is a resident of the special economic zone [43].

A technological startup is a legal entity founded by the grant recipient, registered under Russian law in any legal form, resident of the Innopolis special economic zone, whose purpose is to bring technological products or services to the market [44]. A partner company is a high-technology company or organisation implementing projects on "end-to-end" digital economy technologies and priority directions of technology development that is a partner of the University within the "Advanced Engineering Schools" federal project [45]. A partner of the University is a Russian company or organisation with a cooperation agreement with the University and/or designated as such by an order of the University's director [46].

The length of the work obligation, counted from employment at normal working hours, depends on the size of the grant [47]:

- 12 months — a grant consisting only of university scholarships, for winners admitted to a place funded from budget allocations [47]
- 12 months — 100 % of the cost of study [47]
- 9 months — 70 % of the cost of study [47]
- 7.5 months — 60 % of the cost of study [47]
- 6 months — 50 % of the cost of study [47]
- 3 months — 25 % of the cost of study [47]

If the grant is reduced unilaterally during study in the manner set by the grant agreement, the work period is determined by the smallest grant size that applied during the study [47]. For certain categories of winners the Organizer may, by a separate decision, lengthen or shorten the work period or exclude the employment obligation [48]. The conditions, organisation and terms of employment are set in the grant agreement concluded under the forms of Appendices 1 and 2 [49].

By concluding the education agreement the winner takes on the duty to pay for the educational services, while the University acquires the right to demand payment [50]; under the grant agreement the University must provide the grant and the winner may demand it [51]. These monetary claims are homogeneous, so the rules on set-off under art. 410 of the Civil Code may be applied to them [52].

## Repeat (partial) grant after expulsion

Reinstatement is an academic right of the student; reinstatement of persons expelled before completing the program is governed by the Regulation on transfer, expulsion and reinstatement and academic leave [53]. A repeat (partial) grant is decided on the personal application of the expelled grant recipient, considered by the Contest Commission [54]. It may be given to persons who studied at the University at the expense of a grant covering the cost of study in full or in part, or to persons who studied at budget expense with a grant paying a university scholarship and were expelled for failing to master the program and fulfil the curriculum [55]. On reinstatement to a paid place such persons may receive the repeat grant within 5 years after expulsion, no more than once, and it is used to pay for study after reinstatement [55].

The repeat grant may not exceed, in percentage terms, the grant held before expulsion: 71–100 % held gives no more than 70 %; 61–75 % gives no more than 60 %; 51–60 % gives no more than 50 %; 26–50 % gives no more than 25 % [56]. Persons whose grant covered 25 % or less receive no repeat grant covering part of the cost [57]. Persons who studied at budget expense with a scholarship grant and were expelled for failing to master the program may receive a repeat grant of no more than 75 % of the cost within 5 years after expulsion, no more than once [58]; by a separate decision of the University a scholarship grant may also be given to persons admitted to budget-funded places on reinstatement [58]. Persons who studied under a targeted-training agreement within the admission quota and were expelled for failing to master the program may compete for a partial grant within 5 years after expulsion, no more than once, to pay for study after reinstatement [59].

The cost of study, taking account of the grounds and procedure for reducing it and of the individual curriculum formed on reinstatement, is determined in the education agreement concluded with the grant recipient on reinstatement [60]. A repeat grant may be issued on condition that the reinstated recipient pays, in the trimester to which he is reinstated, the part of the cost of study that the repeat grant does not cover; the remaining sum is paid as the education agreement provides [27]. Payment of the part of the cost that the repeat grant does not cover must be made within 5 working days after the order of the University's director on reinstatement [61]. The Contest Commission may hold separate selection tests for persons reinstated from among former students who applied for a repeat grant [62].

## Rights and duties of a participant

A participant may receive information about the dates and rules of the competition [63] and must perform all actions connected with participation and with obtaining the grant within the deadlines set by the Regulation [64]. By taking part he agrees to give advertising interviews, including on radio and television and in other media, and to take part in making graphic advertising materials without remuneration, with all copyright belonging to the Organizer, and to the free use of his name and of his photo and video images [65]. Taking part confirms that he has read and accepts all the rules, and failure to comply with any rule or condition deprives him of the right to a grant in that contest period [66].

## Transfers between programs

If a grant recipient transfers from one University program to another, the grant is not withdrawn; however, the grant agreement terms may change to reflect the specifics of the new program [67].

## Organizer's rights

The Organizer may change or cancel the competition at any time, with notice posted on the Official Website [68]. The Organizer may ban any participant who submits forged documents, acts disruptively, or violates the Regulation [69]. The Organizer may refuse to issue a grant to a participant caught in fraud or who provided false information [70]. The Organizer is not liable where a participant does not receive notifications because his contact details are outdated or incorrect, or where the participant does not perform his duties or the conditions of participation [71].

## Final provisions

The Regulation applies to grant recipients who took part in the competition for master's programs whose delivery starts in the 2024/2025 academic year [72]. The Organizer may amend or supplement the Regulation at any moment, including during the contest period, updating the text on the Official Website [73]. Materials submitted for participation are not returned [74].

Requests for clarifications should be sent to the Admissions and Recruitment Department at the email address listed in the document or by phone at +7 (843) 203-92-53 (extension 183) [75].

# Academic failure and retakes

## What is an academic failure

Unsatisfactory results in interim assessment — grades D and F [1] — or failure of interim assessment without a valid reason constitute an academic failure [2]. Students must eliminate academic failure [3].

## Retake deadlines

Students with academic failure may retake the interim assessment no more than 2 times within one year from the date the failure occurred [4]. Periods of illness, academic leave, or maternity leave are excluded from this one-year window [4]. The University sets specific deadlines for each retake [5]. The retake schedule is communicated to students via corporate email [6]. The University may conduct retakes during holiday periods, setting multiple retake windows both during holidays and during the regular study period [7]. For graduating students, academic failure elimination is allowed before the approval of the order admitting students to state final certification [8].

## First retake

The first retake is conducted by the same teaching staff member who conducted the original assessment [9]. If that teaching staff member is unavailable, the University may assign another instructor [9]. The form of the first retake corresponds to the form of the original interim assessment [10]. Results are submitted within 7 working days after the retake [11]. Results are final and cannot be revised [12].

## Second retake

If a student fails the first retake, they are given a second retake conducted by a University-assigned committee, typically consisting of three members [5]. The committee composition is approved along with the retake schedule [13]. A written second retake requires at least one committee member present; an oral second retake requires all members [14]. The result is decided by simple majority of the committee, recorded in the examination sheet, and is final [14]. Results are submitted within 7 working days [11]. Results are final and cannot be revised [12].

## Absence from retakes

Absence from a retake without a valid reason is considered an unsatisfactory result [15]. Extensions for valid reasons follow the same procedure as for regular interim assessment: the student must notify the responsible department in writing and submit supporting documents (e.g., medical certificate) within 3 working days [16], [17].

## Expulsion

Students who fail to eliminate academic failure within the established deadlines are expelled from the University [18].

## Retaking to improve a positive grade

Retaking a positive grade (A, B, C, or P) to improve it is available only to final-year students who have fully completed the educational program and can potentially receive an honors diploma [19]. A student may apply to retake only one discipline, by submitting a written application to the University Director no later than 7 working days before the start of state final certification [20]. The retake is conducted by a committee of typically three teaching staff members [21]. The committee may leave the grade unchanged or raise it, decided by simple majority [22]. The student has only one attempt [23]. If a student misses the retake for a valid reason, a new date may be set no later than 3 calendar days before state final certification [24]. All such retakes must be completed before the order admitting students to state final certification is approved [25].

## In aspirantura programmes (2018 regulation)

Academic failure and retakes of aspirants are governed by a separate document — the Regulation on ongoing assessment and interim assessment of students on higher education programmes training highly qualified personnel in aspirantura, approved by order No. ОД/УД-04/2018/06/04 of 4 June 2018 [26]. Unsatisfactory results of interim assessment in one or more disciplines (modules), internships or research work of the programme, or failure to undergo interim assessment without valid reasons, are recognized as academic debt [27]; aspirants must clear it [28].

The University sets, for aspirants with academic debt, the deadlines of the repeat interim assessment in each discipline (module), internship and research work; an aspirant who has not cleared the debt at the first repeat interim assessment is given the opportunity of a second repeat interim assessment held by a commission created by the University [29]. The schedule of repeat interim assessments is approved by an order of the director of the University and communicated to teachers and aspirants through corporate e-mail [30].

- First repeat: it is held by the same teacher to whom the aspirant sat the exam (pass) the first time; if the teacher cannot hold it on the appointed date, the head of the responsible structural unit appoints another teacher [31].
- Second repeat: the commission's composition is formed by the dean of the faculty from teachers who are leading specialists in the field of the discipline concerned and approved by an order of the director of the University or another person authorized by the director [32].
- Deadline: the repeat interim assessment is held not later than the expiry of a period of one year after the academic debt arose; the time of the aspirant's illness, academic leave or maternity leave is not included in that period [33].
- Timing: the University may hold the first and/or the second repeat interim assessment during vacations, setting several dates for it both during vacations and during the period when disciplines (modules) are delivered [34]. It may not be held during the period of an internship, or during the period of interim assessment, except for the interim assessment period when the programme is delivered part-time (заочно) [35], and the time of the repeat may not coincide with classes held as contact work [36].
- Graduating groups: elimination of academic debt by aspirants of graduating groups is allowed before the order admitting them to final (state final) assessment is approved [37].
- Result of the second repeat: it is awarded by agreement of all commission members; the commission's decision is taken by simple majority, is final and not subject to revision; the results of the repeat and of the second repeat are recorded in Moodle [38].

Aspirants who have not cleared academic debt within the established deadlines are expelled from the University on the presentation of the vice-rector, as they have not fulfilled the duties of conscientiously mastering the programme and fulfilling the curriculum [39]. Aspirants who have cleared the debt are transferred, on the presentation of the vice-rector, by an order of the director or another authorized person to the course to which they had been transferred conditionally [40].

## In aspirantura programmes (2022 regulation)

A later aspirantura regulation was approved by order No. ОД/АД-12/2022/03/01 of 1 March 2022 and put into force on 1 March 2022 [41]; the page does not say that it repeals the earlier regulation [41]. It is OCR of a scanned document, so its figures and dates should be checked against the PDF [41].

Under it, unsatisfactory results of interim assessment in one or more disciplines (modules) of the educational component of the programme and (or) an internship, or failure to undergo such interim assessment without valid reasons, are recognized as academic debt [42], and aspirants must clear it [43]. Non-fulfilment of the individual research plan established during interim assessment is treated separately: it is recognized as unfair performance of the duties of mastering the aspirantura programme and is a ground for expelling the aspirant [44], whereas the earlier page also counted unsatisfactory results in research work as academic debt [27].

- Deadlines and two attempts: the University sets the deadlines of the repeat interim assessment for each discipline (module) and internship; an aspirant who has not cleared the debt at the first repeat interim assessment is given a second repeat interim assessment held by a commission created by the University [45].
- Schedule: the schedule of repeat interim assessments is approved by order of the director or an authorised person and communicated to teachers and aspirants by corporate e-mail [46].
- First repeat: it is held by the same teacher to whom the aspirant sat the exam (pass) the first time; if that teacher cannot hold it on the appointed date, the head of the responsible structural unit appoints another teacher [47].
- Second repeat: the commission is formed by the dean of the faculty or by the head of the responsible structural unit from teachers who are leading specialists in the field of the discipline concerned and is approved by order of the director or an authorised person [48]; its result is awarded by agreement of all commission members, the decision is taken by simple majority, is final and not subject to revision [49].
- One-year limit: the repeat is held not later than the expiry of one year after the academic debt arose; the time of the aspirant's illness, academic leave or maternity leave is not included [50].
- Timing: the University may hold the first and (or) the second repeat interim assessment during vacations [51]; a repeat may not be held during an internship or during the interim assessment period, except the interim assessment period when the programme is delivered part-time (заочно) [52], and the time of the repeat may not coincide with classes held as contact work [53].
- Graduating course: clearing academic debt is allowed until the order admitting the aspirant to итоговая аттестация is approved [54].
- Expulsion and transfer: aspirants who have not cleared the debt within the established deadlines are expelled on the presentation of the vice-rector, as not having fulfilled the duties of conscientiously mastering the programme and fulfilling the curriculum [55]; aspirants who have cleared it are transferred, on the vice-rector's presentation, by order of the director or an authorised person to the course to which they had been transferred conditionally [56].

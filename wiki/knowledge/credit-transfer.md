# Credit transfer

## What can be credited

The University credits academic results obtained at other educational organizations or within the University for students enrolled in main professional educational programs [1]. Results of final (state final) assessment are not subject to credit [2]. No fees are charged for establishing correspondence between previously mastered results and the current program, or for credit [3].

Credit is carried out by the University's Attestation Commission; the procedure for forming and working of the commission is established by the University's local normative acts [4]. The University makes the credit when correspondence is established [5].

## Supporting documents

Credit is processed upon the student's application (or the application of parents/legal representatives of a minor student) based on documents confirming the results [6]:

- A document of education and/or qualification, including from a foreign country [7].
- A document of study, such as a certificate of enrollment or period of study, or a document issued by a foreign organization [8].

Results mastered within the University at the same education level may be credited based on results entered in the student's electronic grade book [9].

## Deadlines for applying

- General rule: the application and supporting documents must be submitted to the University's Attestation Commission no later than 14 calendar days before the start of the semester (trimester) [10].
- First-year students: within 14 calendar days after the start of studies [11].
- Students who participated in an academic mobility program: within 3 working days after completing the program [12].

The application forms are given in annex 1 (application by the student) and annex 2 (application by the parent or legal representative of a minor student) to the regulation [13].

Applications and documents may be submitted in person to the secretary of the Attestation Commission, by registered mail with inventory of enclosure, or electronically via the Internet (with an electronic signature or as a scanned copy) [14].

## Procedure

The Attestation Commission establishes correspondence between the previously mastered results and the current program by comparing the supporting documents with the planned learning outcomes for the relevant part of the program [15], [16]. The Commission may use information about the program posted on the issuing organization's website or request information directly from that organization [16]. For results from foreign educational organizations, the Commission accounts for differences in credit unit systems and may compare credit units with the volume of contact and independent work [17].

The Commission may credit one mastered part of a program to several parts of the current program, or several mastered parts to one part of the current program [18].

If correspondence cannot be established through document comparison, the Commission conducts an assessment of the student's actual achievement of the planned outcomes [19], [20]. Cases that may require assessment include: mismatch of names of the previously mastered program part and the current program part, mismatch of volumes, or mismatch of assessment forms [20]. In such cases, the assessment is conducted as an interview using assessment tools from the syllabus of the relevant part of the current program [20].

If the previously mastered results do not meet the requirements for the planned outcomes, the Commission refuses credit and issues a reasoned decision recorded in a protocol [21], [22]. The decision (approval or refusal) is communicated to the student in writing or electronically within 3 working days of the Commission's decision [22].

Credited results are recorded as interim assessment results for the corresponding part of the program and entered into the University's electronic information-educational environment [23]. A student who receives credit is transferred to an individual study plan, including accelerated study, in accordance with the University's regulations [24].

## Foreign documents

Documents from foreign organizations must be submitted with a Russian translation [25]. The University may send requests to the issuing organizations to verify the authenticity of documents [25]. Education and qualification documents must be submitted with a Russian translation in a legalized form, along with a certificate recognizing foreign education, unless Russian law or an international treaty exempts recognition [26]. Credit based on foreign documents is conducted through assessment [27].

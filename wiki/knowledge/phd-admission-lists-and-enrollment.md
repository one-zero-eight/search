# Lists, consent and enrollment in PhD aspirantura admission 2026/2027

## Where this comes from

These are the provisions on lists, consent and enrollment of the Rules of admission to aspirantura programmes of ANO VO «Innopolis University» for the 2026/2027 academic year, approved by order No. ОД/УД-01/2026/01/19 of 19 January 2026 [1]. The page is a scanned document read by OCR, and the wiki warns that its numbers and dates should be checked against the PDF [1].

## Lists of applicants and competitive lists

The lists of persons who submitted applications are formed during the acceptance of applications and documents and the entrance examinations and published on the official site from the start of acceptance, and on EPGU from the start of acceptance through EPGU [2]. The competitive lists are formed on the results of the acceptance and the examinations and published on the official site and on EPGU, and are updated daily until the order on enrollment [3]. Only applicants with at least the minimum points for the entrance examinations are included in a competitive list [4].

A competitive list shows the unique code of the applicant, the sum of competitive points, the sum of points for the entrance examinations and the points for each examination, the points for general individual achievements, the points for targeted individual achievements (for the target quota), the individual achievements used where applicants are equal, the presence of consent to enrollment (for places within the control figures), the presence of a contract of education (for paid places), the priority indicated by the applicant in this group, and the highest priorities — the main highest priority and the highest passing priority [5]. The list of applicants shows the same data without the equality achievements and the highest priorities, plus information on the consideration of the application, including admission to the competition [6]. Applicants in the list of applicants are ordered by decreasing sum of competitive points and, on equality, by unique code [7].

A competitive list is ranked successively by decreasing sum of competitive points, by decreasing sum of points for the entrance examinations, by decreasing points for the individual examinations according to their priority, by decreasing points for individual achievements (for the target quota as the sum of general and targeted achievements), and by the individual achievements used where applicants are equal [8].

## Consent to enrollment and the contract

For places within the control figures the applicant submits consent to enrollment, either as an electronic mark on EPGU or as a paper application brought in person or through a postal operator; electronic submission is made not more than once in 2 hours [9]. The University sets the day of completion of submission of consent at 25 August 2026 until 18:00 Moscow time, and consent may be submitted from the start of acceptance of applications until that time [9]. Consent applies to all competition groups within the control figures, and an applicant who wants to give consent to another organisation must withdraw the consent given here [9].

For paid places the applicant concludes a contract of education; the University sets the day of completion of conclusion of contracts, and contracts are concluded from the start of acceptance of applications until that day inclusive [10]. Where the application was submitted through EPGU, consent may be given through EPGU, in person or by post; where it was submitted in person or by post, consent may be given in person, by post or through EPGU if the applicant provided his insurance number and agreed to the transfer of data to EPGU [11]. When consent is given, the University enters this information into the competitive list (before its publication — into the list of applicants) [12].

## Highest priorities and the conditions of enrollment

For enrollment the University determines the highest priorities on the basis of the priorities indicated in the applications: the main highest priority is the highest priority by which the applicant passes the competition, determined regardless of consent, and the highest passing priority is the highest priority by which the applicant passes among those who gave consent; both are determined on the basis of all competitive lists in which the applicant passes and are shown in the particular competitive list [13].

An applicant is subject to enrollment within the control figures by the highest passing priority if he passes the competition within the established number of places, consent is present at the day of completion of its submission and he has not withdrawn it before the day of the order inclusive [14]. An applicant is subject to enrollment to paid places if he passes within the established number of places, a contract of education is concluded at the day of completion of contracts and he informed the University of the need to enroll him under the contract; enrollment to paid places is made according to one or several priorities and regardless of enrollment within the control figures [15]. Enrollment follows the competitive lists and the priorities indicated in the applications until the established number of places is filled [16].

## Withdrawal and refusal

An applicant may at any stage withdraw the consent to enrollment on EPGU or by an application brought in person or by post [17]; the University then enters the withdrawal into the competitive list (before its publication — into the list of applicants) [18]. An applicant may also withdraw the application for admission [19], and is then excluded from the lists of applicants, the competitive lists and the number of enrolled persons [20]. An enrolled applicant may refuse enrollment without withdrawing consent, on EPGU or by an application [21], and is then excluded from the enrolled persons and the competitive lists are changed [22]. An applicant enrolled within the control figures who wants to withdraw consent must refuse enrollment together with the withdrawal [23].

Changes to the competitive lists and the lists of applicants are made within 2 hours after information or an application received not less than 2 hours before the end of the working day, within the first 2 hours of the next working day when received later, and not later than the next working day for applications received by post [24]. After the end of admission the originals of documents are returned within 1 working day after an application to withdraw the application for admission, and remain in storage if return is impossible [25]. Enrollment is formalised by an order (orders), which by decision of the University is published with the unique code of the applicant [26]. The results of enrollment for each competition, with the unique code, the sum of competitive points and the points for examinations and achievements, are placed on the site on the day of the order and available for 6 months [27].

## Dates of the 2026 campaign

For places within the control figures (full-time) the competitive lists are published on 24 August 2026 and the order on enrollment is issued on 26 August 2026; for paid places (full-time) the lists are published on 26 August 2026, the conclusion of contracts is completed on 28 August 2026 and the order is issued on 29 August 2026 [28].

## The two stages of enrollment within the control figures

Enrollment within the control figures is held in 2 stages, the main stage and the additional stage [29]. At each stage the University determines the main highest priority and the highest passing priority [29]. At the main stage it enrolls to the target quota and to the main budget places: if the highest passing priority is the priority of the target quota, the applicant is enrolled to the target quota, and if it is the priority of the main budget places, to the main budget places [29]. At the additional stage it enrolls to the unfilled main budget places according to the priority of the main budget places [29]. Unfilled places of the target quota are added to the main budget places [29].

An applicant enrolled at the main stage who wants to take part in the additional stage at Innopolis University refuses the enrollment obtained; those who refused before the deadline for consent at the additional stage are considered there, and those who did not refuse are not enrolled at the additional stage [29]. An applicant enrolled at the main stage who wants to take part in the additional stage in another organisation refuses the enrollment and withdraws the consent before the deadline for consent at the additional stage [29].

## Additional admission

Additional admission is held once to places within the control figures — if fewer than 10 unfilled places remain in a particular competition profile and form of study, by decision of the University — and once to paid places for persons applying on the basis of a foreign document of education; no additional enrollment is held within it [29]. It includes the acceptance of applications and documents, the entrance examinations and enrollment, and by decision of the University the results of examinations taken during the main admission may be counted [29]. Additional admission within the control figures is held to the main budget places [29]. Before the start of acceptance the University may redistribute places within a group of scientific specialties, only between the scientific specialties, aspirantura programmes (profiles) in which admission was announced [29].

## The enrollment order of 26 August 2026 for the budget places

The order of 26 August 2026 on enrollment for study under programmes for the training of scientific and scientific-pedagogical staff in aspirantura within the control figures of admission financed from the federal budget enrolled, from 1 September 2026, as students of the University on the first course of the full-time form of study, the applicants who had successfully passed the entrance examinations and passed the competition, in accordance with the annex to the order [30], [31].

The order rests on the Rules of admission to aspirantura programmes of the University for the academic year, approved by an order of the University in June 2026, and on a decision of the University's Admission committee [31]. Control over the execution of the order was assigned to the vice-rector for general education projects T.S. Kamaletdinova (named on the page as «Т.С. Камалетдино»), and the order was signed by the director D.V. Vandyukov [31]. The date of the order is the date set for the order on enrollment within the control figures for full-time study in the campaign described above [28].

The annex is headed by the direction of training 1.2.2 «Математическое моделирование, численные методы и комплексы программ» and lists, for that direction, the unique code of each person enrolled and the number of points he scored [31].

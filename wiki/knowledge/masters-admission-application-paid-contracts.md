# Application for master's admission under paid education contracts — sample form

## What the form is

The wiki holds a sample application form (образец заявления) for admission to master's programmes under contracts for the provision of paid educational services at ANO VO «Innopolis University» [1]. The application is addressed to the Director of the University, K.V. Semenikhin, at the University's address 420500, Innopolis, Universitetskaya street, house 1 [1]. The form is dated «___» __________ 2024 [1].

## Applicant's personal data

The form asks for the applicant's surname, name and patronymic (if any); sex; date and place of birth; registered address; citizenship; the identity document (type, series, number, by whom issued, subdivision code); the insurance number of the individual personal account (СНИЛС) if any, or the unique code assigned to the applicant in the absence of a СНИЛС [1].

It also asks for information about education and the document of the established form: the name of the educational organisation, the year of completion, the document of education and qualification, its series and number [1].

## Programmes and priorities named in the form

The applicant asks to be admitted to the competition for the first course of master's programmes, naming the code and name of the field of study, the form of study, the basis of admission and the priority of enrolment [1]. The sample lists, all full-time and on non-budget (paid) places under the general competition, in this order of priority [1]:

1. 09.04.01 Informatics and Computer Engineering, profile «Программная инженерия» [1].
2. 09.04.01 Informatics and Computer Engineering, profile «Робототехника и компьютерное зрение» [1].
3. 09.04.01 Informatics and Computer Engineering, profile «Инженерия безопасности систем и сетей» [1].
4. 09.04.01 Informatics and Computer Engineering, profile «Искусственный интеллект и инженерия данных» [1].
5. 09.04.01 Informatics and Computer Engineering, profile «Технологическое предпринимательство» [1].
6. 09.04.01 Informatics and Computer Engineering, profile «Управление цифровым продуктом» [1].
7. 09.04.01 Informatics and Computer Engineering, profile «Управление на основе данных» [1].
8. 09.04.01 Informatics and Computer Engineering, profile «Кибербезопасность» [1].
9. 09.04.01 Informatics and Computer Engineering, profile «Управление разработкой компьютерных игр» [1].
10. 15.04.06 Mechatronics and Robotics, profile «Робототехника» [1].

## Entrance examinations

A separate checkbox asks to be admitted to the entrance examinations held by Innopolis University itself, by the subject or subjects of the examinations, with the language of the examination (Russian or English), the form (computer testing) and the place of the examination — at the address of residence [1]. The sample names one entrance examination for the chosen master's programme, listing all ten programmes and profiles above as the alternatives to which it relates [1].

## Special conditions for entrance examinations

Another block is filled by applicants who need special conditions at the entrance examinations because of health limitations or disability: it asks for the list of entrance examinations, the details of the document confirming the disability or health limitation requiring the conditions, and ticking of the conditions needed [1]. The conditions offered are [1]:

- written tasks in relief-dot Braille or as an electronic document accessible with specialised software for the blind, or read by an assistant [1];
- a set of writing accessories and Braille paper, or a computer with specialised software for the blind, or an assistant to dictate to [1];
- individual even lighting of not less than 300 lux [1];
- a magnifying device for the University's internal entrance examinations [1];
- tasks and the instructions on the procedure of the entrance examinations in enlarged print [1];
- sound-amplifying equipment for collective use, or for individual use [1];
- the services of a sign-language interpreter, or of a tactile sign-language interpreter [1];
- a computer with specialised software for persons with musculoskeletal disorders, impaired motor functions of the upper limbs or absence of the upper limbs, or dictation to an assistant [1].

## Individual achievements

The form has a block on individual achievements, with checkboxes for a document of higher education and qualification with distinction (с отличием) and for scientific publications in the profile (direction) of the master's programme in the international database Web of Science over the last 3 years, and likewise in the international database Scopus over the last 3 years [1].

## Accommodation, return of originals and confirmations

- A checkbox states the need for a place of residence in the residential complex (жилой комплекс) [1].
- The form asks for the way the submitted originals are to be returned if the applicant is not enrolled — in person, by Russian Post or by courier service — with the applicant's postal address; this is not indicated where the documents are submitted electronically [1].
- The applicant signs confirmation of familiarisation with the admission rules approved by Innopolis University itself, including the rules for lodging an appeal against the results of the entrance examinations held by the University, with the charter, with the information on the date of grant and the registration number of the licence for educational activity, with the information on the date of grant and the registration number of the state accreditation of the educational activity on the programmes implemented, and with the educational programmes and other documents regulating the organisation and conduct of educational activity, the rights and duties of students [1].
- The applicant also signs confirmation of familiarisation with the information that, where admission is on a competitive basis, the applicant is provided with information about the competition being held and its results, with the dates of concluding the contract for the provision of paid educational services, and with the need to state reliable information in the application and to submit genuine documents [1].
- Applicants who submitted a document (documents) of a foreign state on education or on education and qualification undertake to provide a certificate of recognition of the foreign education and confirmation of completion of legalisation no later than the day of submission of the document of the established form, where those procedures are necessary [1].
- The application ends with the applicant's signature and full name [1].

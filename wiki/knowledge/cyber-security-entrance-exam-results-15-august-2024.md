# Entrance examination for the master's programme «Кибербезопасность» (15 August 2024) — published results

## What the document is

The page is the published protocol of the entrance examination for the master's programme «Кибербезопасность» (Cybersecurity), held by Innopolis University on 15.08.2024 under the heading «Результаты вступительного испытания проводимого АНО ВО "Университет Иннополис" самостоятельно» [1].

The heading of the page gives 15.08.2024 as the date of the entrance examination and names the entrance examination as the one for the master's programme «Кибербезопасность» [1]. For each participant the table gives the number, the СНИЛС/уникальный код, the score (оценка) and the same score written out in words (оценка прописью) [1].

Scores are published against an identifier rather than a name, in the column «СНИЛС/Уникальный код» [1]. The parts of the heading that repeat the name of the programme and of the examination are broken up in the text layer of the page («прогр магист Кибербез опасность») [1].

## Results recorded

The protocol lists one numbered result: the score 40, written in words as «Сорок», against the identifier [скрыто] [1].

## What the protocol does not say

The page states no pass mark, no ranking of the participants and no decision on admission [1].

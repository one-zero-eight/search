# Academic Cooperation Department (Отдел академического сотрудничества) — place, tasks, functions and rights

## The order that approved the Regulation

The Regulation on the Academic Cooperation Department (Положение об отделе академического сотрудничества) of the centre for international and academic cooperation of the department (управление) of academic policy and higher education of ANO VO «Innopolis University» was approved by the University's order of 31 October 2024 № ОД/АД-08/2024/10/31, signed by the Director for Development and HR Policy R.F. Valiev [1]. The order was issued in order to raise the efficiency of the activity of the University's structural subdivision [1]. The page is a scanned document read by OCR, and the wiki warns that its numbers and dates should be checked against the PDF before relying on them [1].

- By point 2 of the order the head of the Academic Cooperation Department A.F. Nagimova was to ensure the timely familiarisation, against signature, with the approved Regulation of the employees of the subdivision and of employees newly hired or transferred (moved) into the subdivision from other University subdivisions [1].
- By point 3 the Regulation on the Academic Cooperation Department of the centre for international and academic cooperation of the department of academic policy and organisation of educational activity, approved by the University's order of 28.11.2022 № ОД/АД-01/2022/11/28, was recognised as no longer in force [1].
- By point 4 the control of the execution of the order and of the Regulation approved by it was left with the signatory [1].

## The Department's place in the structure

The Regulation regulates the activity of the Academic Cooperation Department and determines its tasks, functions, rights and duties [1].

- The Department is a structural subdivision of the centre for international and academic cooperation of the department of academic policy and higher education and belongs to the category of other structural subdivisions (иные структурные подразделения) [2].
- The Department reports directly to the head of the centre for international and academic cooperation [3].
- In its activity the Department follows the Constitution of the Russian Federation, the Labour Code of the Russian Federation, Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29 December 2012 «Об образовании в Российской Федерации» and other legislative and normative legal acts regulating the activity of higher education institutions, the University's Charter, decisions of the Academic, Supervisory and Operational councils, orders and instructions of the University's director, other local normative acts and this Regulation [4].
- The structure, the number of staff and the staffing table of the Department, and changes to them, are approved by the director of the University [5].
- The Department is headed by the head of the Department, hired and dismissed by order of the director of the University [6].
- The working conditions of the Department's employees are determined by employment contracts concluded with each employee and by the University's internal labour regulations [1].
- The rights, duties and degree of responsibility of the Department's employees are established by job descriptions [7].
- Employees of the Department may, within their competences, be involved in work under grants, projects, subsidies and other programmes implemented by the University [8].
- The Regulation and additions and changes to it are approved by order of the director of the University or of another person authorised by him [9].

## Tasks

The Department's tasks are [10], [11]:

- developing and maintaining interaction with the University's partners within higher education programmes [10];
- strengthening the University's position as a higher education organisation [11].

## Functions

The Department carries out the following functions:

- searching for and selecting partners among Russian educational, research and industrial organisations in order to establish interaction within higher education programmes [12];
- establishing interaction, organising support and carrying out communications with Russian educational, research and industrial organisations for the mutual coordination of actions and the implementation of joint higher education programmes [13];
- coordinating and aligning the work of the University's structural subdivisions in order to organise domestic academic cooperation and implement it in accordance with the agreements reached [14];
- supporting the University's network educational programmes of higher education [15];
- advising the University's employees and students on questions concerning the Department's tasks and functions [16].

## Rights

In carrying out their activity the Department's employees have the right to:

- request from the University's structural subdivisions the information and materials needed for the work within the Department's competence [17];
- with the consent of the heads of the University's structural subdivisions, involve the employees of those subdivisions in preparing drafts of local acts and other documents and in developing and carrying out measures conducted by the Department [18];
- take part in meetings held by the University's management when discussing questions whose solution falls within the Department's competence [19];
- put before the University's management proposals to cancel, amend or supplement the University's local acts [20];
- interact with bodies of state authority and administration, legal entities and the University's subdivisions on questions within the Department's competence [21].

## Interaction with other subdivisions and reporting

- The Department's interaction with the University's other structural subdivisions is determined by the tasks and functions assigned to it by this Regulation [22].
- The Department may be a base for the practical training of the University's students [23].
- On the basis of the relevant local acts, orders and instructions the Department may, within its activity, be responsible for preparing and keeping up to date information, references and reports [24].

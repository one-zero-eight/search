# Master's grant agreement — study and work duties, return and reduction of the grant

## Two model forms of the agreement

The grant agreement is concluded between the University as grantor and the winner as grant recipient in one of two model forms attached to the Regulation: Appendix 1 for winners whose grant covers part or all of the cost of study, and Appendix 2 for winners whose grant is only material support in the form of scholarships founded by the University [1]. Both forms are concluded under a suspensive condition (art. 157 of the Civil Code): rights and duties arise only if the recipient is actually enrolled on the higher education programme named in the agreement, not later than the start of the academic year [2].

## Duties during study (both forms)

The recipient must complete the programme under the agreement, observe production and study discipline, study the chosen profession in good faith, master the programme in the required volume, attend all classes and practical work under the schedule, go through all practices and pass all final assessments within the curriculum's deadlines, and undergo an annual medical examination and the monitoring of psychological comfort and well-being organised by the University, no more than three times in one year [2].

To obtain the employment contract the recipient must take part, on the invitation of the University or the employer, in interviews and selection procedures for vacancies meeting the agreement's conditions, attend the University's events for potential employers (open days, presentations), and give the employer the documents needed for the contract [2]. Within 5 calendar days of employment, or of re-employment with a new employer, he must notify the University, attaching supporting documents and giving the employer's full name, his position and the start date [2]; for breach of this duty the University may claim a fine of 500 roubles for each day of delay [2], [2]. If his duties are not performed, the grant is returned within 3 working days of the event [2].

For recipients on a programme of the federal project "Advanced Engineering Schools" (ПИШ) the agreement adds the duty to apply, during study, to the University's competitive selections for grants for practices and internships outside the educational process, including work with mentors ("Отбор"), and to complete at least one such internship in a partner company under the University's regulation on internships; these clauses are included only in the agreement of a ПИШ recipient [2].

## Work obligation after graduation

Where the grant covers the cost of study, the recipient undertakes to work at his main place of work — for a partner of the University or, in the cases set by the agreement, for a legal entity in Innopolis that is a resident of the Innopolis special economic zone and/or is registered or has a subdivision in Innopolis — in the profession obtained, with an employment contract [2]. He must find such employment within 3 months after completing study and receiving the education document [2] and work under an employment contract at normal working hours in the specialty obtained for at least the number of months stated in the agreement (left blank in the form) from the date of employment, with a monthly salary of not less than three times the minimum wage established in the Republic of Tatarstan [2].

The following do not count in that period: unpaid leave (except where art. 128 of the Labour Code makes it mandatory), maternity leave, childcare leave (including when the child's father uses it), absence from work without valid reason including suspension from work under art. 76 of the Labour Code, the period of employment (absence of labour relations) when the employer is changed, and military service or training camps [2].

The recipient may change employer, but the periods without labour relations with an employer may not exceed three months in total [2]. The University may count work for the employer during the study practice provided by the programme (introductory, industrial, pre-diploma) as part of the work period [2]; for a ПИШ recipient the same clause also covers an internship outside the educational process in a partner company, practice in a partner company and work for the employer [2].

For ПИШ programmes the employment must be with a partner company; if the recipient proves that within those 3 months all partner companies refused him, he must find employment in another organisation meeting section 1 conditions within 6 months [2]. For the "Information Technology Entrepreneurship" programme the employment must be in the recipient's own technological startup; if he proves that he made all attempts to register it but it does not meet the criteria of a technological startup (Appendix 1) or that the startup is not realised (Appendix 2), he has 6 months to find another organisation meeting section 1 conditions [2], [2].

The scholarship form of the agreement contains the same study and employment duties [2], but its grant consists of the scholarship appointed for the study period in the amount and on the conditions of the University's local acts [2].

## Amount of the grant

Under Appendix 1 the grant is the stated percentage of the cost of study determined in the education agreement, expressed in roubles; it is not paid to the recipient in money but counted towards his payment for study, and the part it does not cover is paid by the recipient himself under the education agreement [2]. The amount may be reduced by the University unilaterally on the grounds of section 5 [2]. The University's claim for payment of study and the recipient's claim for the grant are homogeneous, so the rules on set-off under art. 410 of the Civil Code apply [2]. Sums received against a debt are applied, whatever purpose the payment order states, first to court and collection costs, then to a penalty, then to the return of the grant, and the University may change this order unilaterally [2].

Under Appendix 2 the sums to be returned on the grounds of cl. 5.1–5.3 are determined by the University's local acts on appointing the scholarship, and their total may not exceed the amount stated in the agreement [2].

## When the whole grant is returned

Under Appendix 1 the whole grant must be returned if the recipient does not start work within the term set by the employment contract, or evades employment and does not conclude (refuses to conclude) the employment contract with the employer within the term set by the agreement [2].

Under Appendix 2 the whole scholarship accrued for the period from the enrolment order to the expulsion order must be returned in those two cases and also if the study ends at the recipient's initiative, including a transfer to another educational organisation to continue the programme, if the recipient who has reached fifteen years is expelled by the University as a disciplinary measure, and if he is expelled for academic debt not cleared after interim assessments or for unsatisfactory results at the final assessment [2].

## Return in proportion to the period worked

The grant is returned in an amount inversely proportional to the period of actual performance of the employment duties if the employment relation ends without subsequent employment with another employer as the agreement provides — including because of a move, a spouse's transfer to work in another place or dismissal on any ground of the Labour Code, also at the employer's initiative — if the recipient works for employers for less than the number of months stated in the agreement in total, or if he is transferred to an employer's subdivision outside Innopolis without the University's consent, in which case work from the date of the unagreed transfer is not counted in the work period [2]. The sum is the grant divided by D1 and multiplied by D2, where D1 is the number of working days the recipient had to work under cl. 3.1.3 and D2 the number of working days he had to work but did not [2]; the same rules and formula are in the scholarship form [2].

## Return in proportion to the period of study

Under Appendix 1 the grant is returned in proportion to the actual period of study, counted from the enrolment order to the expulsion order, if the education agreement ends at the recipient's initiative because of a transfer to another educational organisation, or at the University's initiative where expulsion is applied as a disciplinary measure to a recipient who has reached fifteen years [2]. If the education agreement ends early at the recipient's initiative on grounds not listed there, the recipient returns 20 % of the sum equal to the cost of study computed for the actual period of study [2]. If the recipient is expelled before the end of the first trimester, the University may decide not to require the return, recording this in an additional agreement, on the basis of his current performance and attendance and other information showing good-faith study [2], [2].

## Refusal of the internship selection (ПИШ)

If a ПИШ recipient refuses to take part in the Отбор or, having won it, does not complete the internship by the date set by the order on internships, the University may require the return of 20 % of the grant computed for the actual period of study from the enrolment order to the order sending him to the internship [2]. In the scholarship form, in that case the scholarship paid from the appointment order to the date of refusal is returned, and the University may suspend the scholarship until the recipient takes part in the next Отбор [2].

## Valid reasons and force majeure

Illnesses that arose after the agreement was concluded and prevent study or employment, confirmed by a conclusion of a medical commission issued under Russian law, and impossibility of performance because of force majeure are valid grounds releasing the recipient from the study and employment duties of cl. 3.1.1–3.1.3 [2], [2]. A party invoking force majeure must inform the other in writing within 20 working days, with a supporting document from a competent state organisation, and after the circumstances end must notify it without delay, stating the expected period of performance; losses caused by a missing or late notification are compensated by the party that caused them [2].

## Reduction of the grant for academic results (Appendix 1 only)

The University may reduce the grant unilaterally if, after the first (autumn) trimester, the recipient has more than two "satisfactory" (C) grades, and/or after the second (spring) and third (summer) trimesters more than four "satisfactory" grades, or if he has one or more "unsatisfactory" (F) grades after the corresponding trimester; the results of repeat interim assessments in those disciplines are not counted [2]. The decision rests on the interim assessment record, and the new amount may not exceed the reduction table: 100 % becomes 70 %, 70 % becomes 60 %, 60 % becomes 50 %, 50 % becomes 25 %, 25 % becomes 0 % [2]. The University notifies the recipient of the reduction within the number of working days left blank in the form after the interim assessment, and the student must pay the resulting debt within 15 working days of the notification [2].

## Term, amendment and disputes

The agreement takes effect on signature and runs until study is completed, and as to unperformed duties until they are fully performed [2]; it ends if the recipient dies, is recognised as unable to work, or has contraindications for the work under the agreement confirmed by a medical conclusion [2]. During its term it may be changed only by a written agreement of both parties — under Appendix 1 also when the education agreement is changed — and it may be terminated by mutual consent [2].

Disputes are settled by negotiations and claims, a claim being considered within 20 days, and if the parties do not agree the dispute goes to the Verkhneuslonsky district court of the Republic of Tatarstan [2]. The parties correspond by post and by e-mail; correspondence from the addresses named in the agreement is recognised as signed with a simple electronic signature, scanned pages sent by e-mail have the force of the original until the originals are exchanged, the date of transmission is the day the e-mail is sent, and the receiving party bears the risk of receiving messages [2].

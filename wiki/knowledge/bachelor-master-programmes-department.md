# Department for the Organisation of Bachelor's and Master's Programmes (Отдел организации программ бакалавриата и магистратуры) — place, tasks, functions and rights

## The document

The Regulation on the Department for the Organisation of Bachelor's and Master's Programmes (Отдел организации программ бакалавриата и магистратуры) was approved by the University's order No. ОД/АД-01/2025/02/14 of 14 February 2025, signed by the Director for development and HR policy R.F. Valiev [1]. The same order repealed the Regulation on the Department for the Organisation of Bachelor's and Master's Programmes of the Centre of Organisation of Educational Programmes approved by order No. ОД/АД-05/2021/05/26 of 26 May 2021 [1]. The head of the department A.S. Potyomkin was to ensure that the department's staff, and staff newly hired or transferred into it from other units, are acquainted with the Regulation against signature [1]. The wiki copy is a draft made by OCR, so numbers and dates should be checked against the PDF [1].

The Regulation regulates the department's activity and defines its tasks, functions, rights and the duties of its staff [2].

## Place in the structure

- The department is a structural unit of the centre of organisation of educational programmes (Центр) of the department of academic policy and higher education and belongs to the category «учебно-методические подразделения» [3].
- It reports directly to the head of the centre of organisation of educational programmes [4].
- In its work it is guided by the Constitution of the Russian Federation, the Labour Code, Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29 December 2012 «Об образовании в Российской Федерации», other legislation on higher education, the University's Charter, decisions of the Academic, Supervisory and Operations councils, orders and instructions of the University's director, other local acts and this Regulation [5].
- The structure, number of staff and staffing table of the department, and changes to them, are approved by the University's director or a person authorised by him; the department is headed by a head who is hired and dismissed by order of the director [1].
- The staff's working conditions are determined by their employment contracts and by the University's internal labour regulations [1]; their rights, duties and degree of responsibility are set by job descriptions [6].
- Staff of the department may be involved, within their competence, in work under grants, projects, subsidies and other programmes carried out by the University [7].
- The Regulation and amendments to it are approved by the University's director or a person authorised by him [8].

## Tasks

The department has the following tasks [1]:

- analysis of the implementation of the main professional educational programmes of higher education — bachelor's and master's programmes — for compliance with the competence model [9];
- automation and digitalisation of the centre's processes [10];
- support and analysis of project activity and of practical training under the educational programmes [11].

## Functions

- Analyses: the implementation of programmes and their compliance with the competence model [12]; students' academic performance, to identify problems and find solutions optimising the educational process [13]; the activity of the teaching staff in academic work [14]; syllabuses for compliance with the competence model [15].
- Digitalisation and automation of the centre's processes [16].
- Support and analysis of the effectiveness of students' project activity [17] and of students' practical training [18] under the educational programmes.
- Support of the preparation and defence of students' final qualification works [19].
- Statistics on scientific, project and other events connected with the formation of the competences of the programmes [20].
- Support of the formation of the class schedule for classes held as contact work [21] and control over the use of the University's classroom stock according to the schedules [22].
- Agreement of students' participation in academic mobility (exchange) programmes and of their individual curricula for the period of that participation [23].
- Providing information and preparing reports on academic activity at the requests of the University's units and of state authorities, on matters within the department's tasks and functions [24].
- Support of the internal and external evaluation of the quality of educational activity and of the preparation of students under the programmes [25].
- Communication on academic matters between the teaching staff, students and other participants of the educational process, including on students' academic performance, on breaches of academic integrity and on bringing the class schedule to notice [26].
- Expert and consultative support of the activity of the University's structural units [27].
- Participation in round tables, conferences, seminars, webinars and other events on matters within its tasks and functions [28].

## Rights

In carrying out its activity the department's staff have the following rights [1]:

- to request from the University's structural units information and materials needed for the work within the department's competence [29];
- to involve, with the consent of the heads of the units concerned, their staff for preparing drafts of local acts and other documents and for developing and carrying out the department's measures [30];
- to take part in meetings held by the University's management where matters within the department's competence are discussed [31];
- to propose to the University's management the repeal of, and amendments and additions to, the University's local acts [32];
- to interact with state authorities, legal persons and the University's units on matters within the department's competence [33].

## Interaction with other units

- Interaction with the Department for the Organisation of Academic Activity covers the implementation of programmes, planning and organisation of the educational process, organisation and conduct of classes, interim assessment, repeat interim assessment and state final certification, and recording and displaying the results of mastering programmes in the University's electronic information and educational environment [34].
- With the Methodological Department — on the implementation of educational programmes [35]; with the Faculty of Computer and Engineering Sciences, the Faculty of Humanities and Social Sciences, the institutes and laboratories — on the implementation of programmes [36].
- With the Department for Control of Educational Activity — on normative support of educational activity and evaluation of its quality and of the preparation of students [37]; with the Department for Support and Development of Students — on educational work with students [38].
- With the Department for Relations with Industry and Alumni — on organising practices and practical training [39]; with the Department for International Cooperation — on students' participation in academic mobility (exchange) programmes [40].
- With the Documentation Department — on records management, execution of local acts and other documents, keeping the department's files according to the consolidated nomenclature and transferring documents to the archive [41]; with the Organisational-Technical Department — on planning, distribution and use of the classroom stock and booking rooms [42].
- With the IT Department — on the University's information systems, automation and technical support of the educational process and the functioning of the electronic information environment [43]; with the Library — on access to electronic library systems and methodological support of the programmes [44].
- With other structural units — in preparing drafts of local acts, orders, instructions, contracts and other documents and in preparing conclusions, written and oral references on matters within the department's competence [45].
- The department may serve as a base for the practical training of the University's students [46].

## Reporting

On the basis of the relevant local acts, orders and instructions the department may be responsible, within its activity, for preparing and keeping up to date information, references and reports [47].

# Admission to bachelor's and master's programmes 2026/2027 — programmes, places and quotas (rules of 10 April 2026)

## The document

The admission rules for the 2026/2027 academic year were approved by the University's order No. ОД/УД‐01/2026/04/10 of 10 April 2026 in a new edition; the same order repealed the order of 19.01.2026 No. ОД/УД-02/2026/01/19 that had approved the previous edition [1].

The wiki holds these rules in two copies whose text is the same, [1] and [2]; the statements below rest on either of them [1] [2].

The rules regulate admission of citizens of the Russian Federation, foreign citizens and stateless persons (поступающие) to the bachelor's programmes and to the master's programmes of the University [1]. They were developed on the basis of Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29 December 2012, Government Decree No. 106 of 10.02.2026, orders of the Ministry of Science and Higher Education No. 821 and No. 820 of 27 November 2024, order No. 231 of 1 March 2023, order of the Ministry of Education No. 146 of 05.03.2026, the University's Charter and other local acts [1].

Admission is announced on the strength of the licence for educational activity No. Л035-00115-77/00642712, issued by the Federal Service for Supervision in Education and Science on 25 December 2015 without a term [1].

## Programmes on offer

- Bachelor's programmes of area 09.03.01 «Информатика и вычислительная техника» with the profiles «Анализ данных и искусственный интеллект», «Математические основы искусственного интеллекта» and «AI360: Инженерия данных», and of area 15.03.06 «Мехатроника и робототехника» with the profile «Робототехника»; the qualification is bachelor, the full-time programme lasts 4 (four) years [1].
- A bachelor's programme within the federal project «Передовые инженерные школы»: area 09.03.01, profile «Инженерия информационных систем», qualification bachelor, 4 years full-time [1].
- Master's programmes outside that project: area 09.04.01 «Информатика и вычислительная техника» with the profile «Управление разработкой компьютерных игр», area 38.04.05 «Бизнес-информатика» with the profile «Информационное технологическое предпринимательство», area 15.04.06 «Мехатроника и робототехника» with the profile «Робототехника и компьютерное зрение»; qualification master, 2 (two) years full-time [1].
- Master's programmes within «Передовые инженерные школы»: area 09.04.01 with the profiles «Кибербезопасность», «Искусственный интеллект и инженерия данных», «Инженерия безопасности систем и сетей» and «Программная инженерия», and area 38.04.05 with the profiles «Управление на основе данных» and «Управление цифровым продуктом»; qualification master, 2 years full-time [1].
- Admission is to the first course [1].

## Who may apply

- For bachelor's programmes — a person who has secondary general education, or secondary professional education, or higher education; for master's programmes — a person who has higher education [1].
- The education is confirmed by documents of education or of education and qualification issued to those who have passed the state final certification or the final certification [1].
- For programmes having state accreditation the education must be confirmed by the documents listed in the rules — in particular a document of the form set by the authorised federal executive bodies, a state-format document on the level of education obtained before 1 January 2014, documents of Moscow State University or St Petersburg State University, a document issued by a private organisation operating in the Skolkovo innovation centre or in an innovative research and technology centre, or a document of a foreign state recognised in the Russian Federation; for programmes without state accreditation also a document of the form set by the University [1].

## Places: control figures, quotas and paid places

- Admission is conducted to places within the control figures of admission financed from the federal budget, including places within quotas, and to places outside the quotas (the main budget places) [1].
- The quotas are: the quota for admission to targeted training (целевая квота); on bachelor's programmes the special quota (особая квота), set by the University at not less than 10% of the volume of control figures for each area of study; and on bachelor's programmes the separate quota under part 5.1 of article 71 of Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ, also set at not less than 10% of the control figures for each area of study [1].
- Admission is also conducted to paid places — places for study under contracts concluded at the expense of physical and/or legal persons and at the expense of the University's own funds [1].
- On bachelor's programmes the quotas are allocated in this order: first the separate quota, then the special quota within the remaining places (if any), then the target quota within the remaining places (if any), except in additional admission [1].
- If the special quota is not allocated, the University announces admission to places within the special quota stating a number of places equal to zero; if no main budget places remain after the quotas are allocated, the University announces admission to the main budget places stating a number equal to zero [1].
- In additional admission on bachelor's programmes the special quota and the separate quota are allocated from the volume of control figures announced for the additional admission, each at not less than 10% [1].

## Competition groups

- Admission is on a competitive basis; the University sets the list of entrance examinations, the maximum and the minimum number of points for each of them, the list of individual achievements and the procedure for taking them into account, and the procedure for granting special rights; the sum of competitive points is the sum of points for entrance examinations and for individual achievements [1].
- A separate competition is held for each combination of conditions of admission (a competition group): by form of study (full-time), by competitive profile (a single-profile competition within an area of study), by source of financing (control figures at the expense of the federal budget, or paid places) and by type of places (special quota, separate quota, target quota, main budget places) [1].
- The University may hold different single-profile competitions in different competition groups for the same areas of study [1].
- For competitions with the same competitive profile the University sets the same list of entrance examinations, the same minimum and maximum points (according to Appendix 2) and the same list and procedure of account of general individual achievements [1].
- For each competition group the University forms lists of those who have submitted an application and ranked competitive lists of those who submitted the application and the documents and have the necessary results of the unified state examination (ЕГЭ) and/or entrance examinations [1].
- If places remain unfilled after the completion of admission, the University holds an additional admission to those places [1].

## Simultaneous application and choice of areas

- The maximum number of organisations in which a person may apply simultaneously for bachelor's programmes is 5 (five) [1].
- The maximum number of areas of study in which a person may simultaneously take part in the competition for bachelor's programmes at the University is 2 [1].
- A person may simultaneously apply to different competition groups within each area of study [1].
- In the application the bachelor's applicant confirms that he submits applications to not more than 5 (five) organisations, including the University, and to not more than 5 (five) areas of study [1].

## Target admission (target quota)

- Admission to places within the target quota follows the Regulation on targeted training approved by the Government of the Russian Federation [1].
- The University admits to the target quota on the basis of applications (заявки) submitted by the applicants in accordance with offers of customers (federal bodies, regional and local authorities, legal persons) placed on the digital platform «Работа в России» or submitted by the customers to the University [1].
- The University refuses admission to the target quota if the paper application has no заявка, if the offer named in the application or in the заявка is absent from the platform and was not submitted by the customer or does not correspond to the competition group, if the paper заявка does not follow the standard form approved by Government Decree No. 555 of 27 April 2024, if the заявка shows that the applicant does not meet the requirements for citizens concluding a targeted training contract, or if the customer reports such non-compliance [1].
- The University admits to the target quota no more applicants than the number of contracts stated by the customers in their offers, for each offer [1].
- Where admission serves state security, the University acts on the information received from the customers, does not publish the applicants' data and issues a separate enrolment order [1].

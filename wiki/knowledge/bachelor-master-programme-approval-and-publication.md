# Approval, actualisation, storage and publication of a bachelor's or master's programme

## Where these rules come from

Approval and actualisation, storage and publication of a bachelor's or master's programme, as well as its assessment materials, annotations and upbringing documents, follow the Regulation on the procedure of development and approval of bachelor's and master's programmes, approved by order of 2 August 2022 No. ОД/АД-02/2022/08/02 and in force from 1 September 2022 [1], [2].

## Assessment and methodological materials

Assessment materials are developed for each discipline (module) and internship of the programme, and also for the state final certification [3]. The assessment materials and methodological materials that ensure the quality of the students' preparation and the implementation of the programme are presented in the sections of the working programmes of disciplines (modules), the internship programmes and the state final certification programme [4].

For a specific discipline (module), internship or state final certification they include the list of competences that students must develop and the indicators of their achievement; the description of the indicators and criteria for assessing the formation of competences and the assessment scales; the types of work and types of tasks for assessing the results; the criteria of assessment; the grading system and scale and the rules of converting learning outcomes into marks of the «пятибалльная» system; the tasks for ongoing assessment, for preparation for interim assessment and for conducting interim assessment; and the methodological recommendations for students on mastering the discipline. The list of recommended main and additional literature, including in foreign languages, may also be included [5].

They are developed by the developer or developers of the working programme of the discipline (module), the internship programme or the state final certification programme; academic staff of the University, staff of institutes, laboratories, methodological departments and other units whose functions include the development and actualisation of educational programmes, methodologists and programme managers may be involved [6].

## Annotations to the working programmes

Annotations to the working programmes of disciplines (modules) and internships are formed on the basis of those working programmes and internship programmes [1]. They show the total labour intensity, the form of interim assessment and a short description of the discipline (module) or internship, including its place in the structure of the programme, its content and the list of competences formed [1]. Annotations to internship programmes are an optional component of the programme and are formed where necessary [1]. A model form of the annotation to working programmes of disciplines (modules) is given in annex 7 to the model form of the programme [7].

## Upbringing programme and calendar plan of upbringing work

Upbringing of students in mastering the programme is carried out on the basis of the upbringing programme and the calendar plan of upbringing work included in the programme, which the University develops and approves itself in accordance with its local normative act [8]. Student councils, parents' councils and representative bodies of students, where they exist, have the right to take part in developing the upbringing programmes and calendar plans [9].

The upbringing programme determines the main characteristics, content, principles of organisation and conditions of implementation of upbringing work under the programme, as well as the system of managing upbringing work and the criteria for assessing the quality of its organisation, and is approved by an order of the University director or another responsible person authorised by the director before the academic year begins [10]. The calendar plan of upbringing work is developed in accordance with the upbringing programme and shows the list and dates of upbringing activities by the areas of upbringing work implemented at the University; it is drawn up as a separate document for the current academic year and approved by an order of the director or another responsible person authorised by the director before the academic year begins [11].

## Approval and annual actualisation

The developed programme is considered and approved by the University's Academic Council before its implementation begins and, as a rule, before the announcement of admission to that programme [12]. Actualisation of the programme means updating it by making additions or changes to its components [13].

Actualisation is carried out annually, taking into account the development of science, technology, culture, economy, technologies and the social sphere, and is done within a strategic session with representatives of the Russian and world academic community, of the University's top management and professors, and of industry. All elements of the structure of the programme whose content is directly or indirectly connected with professional standards are actualised taking into account the updating of those standards. Actualisation includes determining whether components need to be revised and/or new ones developed, and making changes to the existing programme so that the provisions of professional standards are taken into account [14].

All the necessary changes and additions are reflected in the corresponding structural elements of the programme (curriculum, working programmes of disciplines, internship programmes, state final certification programme) and are approved at a meeting of the Academic Council; an entry with the number and date of the Academic Council's minutes is made in the sheet of updates of the programme [15]. When the actualised programme is approved, the previous version of the updated components is considered to have lost force [16].

## Storage of the programme documents

The set of documents of a programme being implemented is kept in the structural unit responsible for implementing the programme, in accordance with the University's consolidated nomenclature; the sets are kept until they are replaced by new ones at the next actualisation [17]. The set of documents of a programme whose implementation has ended is kept in that unit for a year in accordance with the nomenclature and then transferred to the University's archive; what is kept after the end of implementation are the versions of the programme's components approved at their last actualisation [18].

## Publication on the official website

Information on the programme being implemented and its components is placed on the University's official website in the section «Сведения об образовательной организации», in the subsection «Образование» [19]. The components are placed on the website within ten working days after the approval or actualisation of the programme, and only the current version of the programme is placed on the site [20]. The information is placed in accordance with the order of Rosobrnadzor of 14 August 2020 No. 831 on the requirements to the structure of the official website, and the Government decree of 20 October 2021 No. 1802 on the rules of placing and updating information on the official website [21].

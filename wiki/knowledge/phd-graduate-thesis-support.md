# Support of aspirantura graduates in presenting the thesis for defence (сопровождение)

## What it is

The University may provide graduates who have successfully passed the final attestation on an aspirantura programme with support (сопровождение) when they present the dissertation for the degree of Candidate of Sciences for defence [1].

## How long it lasts

Support is provided for a period of not more than one calendar year after completion of the aspirantura programme [2].

## How it is obtained

Support is provided on the graduate's personal application addressed to the Director of the University and is formalised by an order of the Director or of a person authorised by him [3]. The application is submitted by the graduate to the department responsible for organising the educational process on aspirantura programmes not later than 30 calendar days after he passed the final attestation [4]. The specifics of the support in the part not regulated by Russian legislation on education are set by a local act of the University [5].

## What the graduate gets

For the period of support the University may give the graduate access to its infrastructure, including dormitories, and to the information-educational environment, teaching materials, library funds and library-reference systems [6]. The University supports the graduate in forming the package of documents provided by the list approved by the Ministry of Science and Higher Education for presenting the dissertation to a dissertation council for the degree of Candidate or Doctor of Sciences, including for preliminary consideration [7].

## Cost

No fee is charged from the graduate for the support [8].

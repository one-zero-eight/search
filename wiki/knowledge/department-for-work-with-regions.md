# Department for Work with Regions (Отдел по работе с регионами) of the Center of Educational Content — creation and the order approving its Regulation

## The order and the new subdivision

The page carries the order of 25 August 2025 № ОД/АД-02/2025/08/25, issued in Innopolis and signed by the Director for Development and HR Policy R.F. Valiev, by which the Regulation on the Department for Work with Regions (Положение об отделе по работе с регионами) of the Center of Educational Content of ANO VO «Innopolis University» was approved [1].

- The order is issued in connection with the creation of a new structural subdivision, in order to optimise the activity of the University [1].
- It approves the Regulation on the Department for Work with Regions of the Center of Educational Content of the University, attached to the order [1].
- The head of the Department for Work with Regions K.A. Fomina is to ensure timely acquaintance, against signature, with the approved Regulation of the Department's employees and also of employees newly hired and transferred (moved) into the Department from other subdivisions of the University [1].
- Control over the execution of the order and of the Regulation approved by it is retained by the signatory [1].

## What the page does not contain

The page carries only the order: it does not reproduce the text of the Regulation it approves [1]. It therefore says nothing about the tasks, functions, rights or duties of the Department, about the category of structural subdivisions to which it belongs or about its internal organisation [1].

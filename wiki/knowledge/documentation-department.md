# Documentation Department of Managerial Activity (Департамент документационного обеспечения управленческой деятельности) — place, tasks, functions and rights

## The document and the Department's place in the structure

The Regulation on the Department of Documentation Support of Managerial Activity (Положение о Департаменте документационного обеспечения управленческой деятельности) of ANO VO «Innopolis University» was approved by an order dated 20 January 2025 № ОД/АД-01/2025/01/20, signed in Innopolis by the Director for Development and HR Policy R.F. Valiev; the same order repealed the previous Regulation approved by order of 2 October 2023 № ОД/АД-8/2023/10/02 [1]. The Regulation regulates the Department's activity and determines its tasks, functions, rights and duties [2].

- The Department is a structural subdivision of direct subordination to the deputy director of the University and belongs to the category «иные структурные подразделения»; it comprises the Sector for Support of the Administration's Activity, the Sector for Registration and Control of Documents, the Editing Sector, the Sector for Work with Archival Documents and the Electronic Document Flow Sector [3].
- The Department reports directly to the deputy director of the University [4].
- In its activity it is guided by the Constitution of the Russian Federation, the Labour Code of the Russian Federation, Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29 December 2012 «Об образовании в Российской Федерации», other legislative and normative legal acts regulating the activity of higher education institutions, the University's Charter, decisions of the Academic, Supervisory and Operational councils, orders and instructions of the University director, other local normative acts and this Regulation [5].
- The structure, headcount and staffing table of the Department, and changes to them, are approved by the University director [6]. The Department is headed by a head of the department, hired and dismissed by order of the University director [7].
- The working conditions of the Department's employees are determined by the employment contracts concluded with each of them, by the University's internal labour regulations and by other local normative acts [8]; their rights, duties and degree of responsibility are established by job descriptions [9].
- Within their competence the Department's employees may be involved in work under grants, projects, subsidies and other programmes implemented by the University [10]. The Regulation and the additions and changes to it are approved by order of the University director or of another person authorised by him [11].

Under the order itself, the head of the Department T.A. Karpacheva must ensure timely familiarisation (against signature) with the approved Regulation of the unit's workers and also of workers newly hired and transferred (moved) into the unit from other units [1]; control over execution of the order and of the Regulation is retained by the signatory [1].

## Tasks

The Department's tasks are:

- organising, conducting and improving the documentary support of the University's management and directing the record-keeping (делопроизводство) process in the University [12];
- coordinating and controlling record-keeping in the University's structural subdivisions in accordance with Russian legislation and normative legal acts on record-keeping and archival affairs, the University's regulating documents and other normative and methodological documents [13];
- improving the technological processes of work with documents and documentary information, including on the basis of modern information technologies and office equipment [14];
- ordering the composition of managerial documents and unifying their forms in order to reduce the number of documents and establish their uniformity in the University [15];
- controlling the correct drafting of organisational and administrative documents, their timely agreement and execution [16];
- ensuring the selection, recording and preservation of documents arising in the University's activity and their archival storage [17];
- organising the work of the University's archive and its acquisition [18];
- organising the work of the permanent expert commission on the archive [19];
- giving methodological help and coordinating documentation processes in the University's structural subdivisions [20];
- ensuring the regime of access to and use of the University's documentary information [21];
- establishing uniform norms of documenting and rules for work with documents in all structural subdivisions [22];
- fulfilling the requirements of the Quality Policy, observing and fulfilling the requirements of the quality management system, GOSTs and other normative documents aimed at realising the goals and tasks in the field of quality [23];
- working with incoming and outgoing documentation in electronic document flow (ЭДО) systems within the University's main activity [24].

## Functions

The Department carries out the following functions:

- develops and introduces recommendations and local normative acts establishing uniform requirements for the drafting of the University's organisational and administrative documents and the procedure for work with them [25];
- takes measures to optimise document flows and document circulation as a whole [26];
- takes direct part in setting tasks for the automation of documentation systems of the corporate portal «Битрикс» and of other information systems [27];
- controls the formation of documents of the corporate portal «Битрикс» in accordance with the University's consolidated nomenclature of files [28];
- checks the correct drafting of organisational and administrative documents created in the University [29];
- keeps the originals of the University's orders and instructions and ensures their timely distribution and familiarisation with them on paper or electronically [30];
- receives, registers, records, stores, delivers and distributes correspondence (incoming and outgoing), including that transmitted by special means of communication, and carries out reference work on it [31];
- ensures the appropriate regime of access to the University's paper and electronic documents formed and kept in the Department and to the use of the information contained in them [32];
- systematically controls compliance with the deadlines for the execution of documents and, where necessary, provides management with information on the state of executive discipline [33];
- provides methodological guidance and control over the organisation of record-keeping in the University's structural subdivisions [34];
- controls the correct drafting and keeping of files in the structural subdivisions and the preparation of documents for transfer to the archive [35];
- issues copies of documents kept in the Department and archival certificates and certifies documents in the established procedure [36];
- ensures the storage of the University's main round seal [37];
- draws up the University's consolidated nomenclature of files and brings information about it to the structural subdivisions [38];
- accepts the formed files in accordance with the approved consolidated nomenclature of files and transfers them to the archive within the established deadlines [39];
- organises the preparation of the University's documents for transfer to state archival storage [40];
- takes part in developing the University's document forms [41];
- ensures control over the execution of instructions of senior managers [42];
- monitors notifications in external ЭДО information systems within the fulfilment of obligations under concluded contracts [43];
- loads documents into and unloads them from external ЭДО information systems in connection with the fulfilment of obligations under concluded contracts [44];
- loads and unloads ЭДО documents into and from ЭДО information systems integrated with the corporate portal «Битрикс» («Контур.Диадок», «Калуга.Астрал» and others) [45];
- interacts with the University's employees in order to inform them in good time about changes in document flow and record-keeping regulations, where necessary [46].

## Rights

In carrying out their activity the Department's employees have the right to [47]:

- request from the University's structural subdivisions the information and materials needed for the work within the Department's competence [47];
- with the consent of the heads of the University's structural subdivisions, engage the employees of those subdivisions to prepare drafts of local acts and other documents and to develop and carry out the measures conducted by the Department [48];
- take part in the meetings held by the University's management when questions whose solution falls within the Department's competence are discussed [49];
- submit to the University's management proposals to cancel, amend or supplement the University's local acts [50];
- interact with state authorities and administration, legal entities and the University's subdivisions on questions within the Department's competence [51];
- check the organisation and conduct of record-keeping in all the University's structural subdivisions, report the results of the checks to the deputy director of the University and take measures to eliminate the violations found [52];
- have access to all electronic catalogues of the corporate portal «Битрикс» [53];
- recommend restricting the rights and the level of access to the information base of the automated documentation system of the corporate portal «Битрикс» for various categories of employees — users of the system [54].

## Interaction with other subdivisions and reporting

- The Department interacts with the University's management and with all structural subdivisions on the organisation and conduct of document flow, the drafting of local normative acts and other documents, the keeping of the Department's files in accordance with the University's consolidated nomenclature of files, and the transfer of documents to the University's archive [55].
- Interaction with the other structural subdivisions is carried on questions of preparing drafts of local acts, orders, instructions, contracts and other documents, of preparing conclusions and written and oral references on questions within the Department's competence arising in practical activity, and of electronic document flow [56].
- The Department may be a base for the practical training of the University's students [57].
- On the basis of the relevant local acts, orders and instructions the Department may be responsible, within its activity, for preparing and keeping up to date information, references and reports [58].

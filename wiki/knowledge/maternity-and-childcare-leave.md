# Maternity and childcare leave

## Who can apply

Students of the university are granted maternity leave and childcare leave until the child reaches the age of three, in the procedure established by federal laws [1]. The leave is granted to the student on a personal application [2].

## Grounds

- Maternity leave is granted based on a personal application and a copy of a medical leave certificate [2].
- Childcare leave until the child reaches the age of three is granted based on a personal application and a copy of the child's birth certificate [2].

The form of the application for these leaves is given in annex 7 to the regulation [2].

## Decision-making

The decision is made by the Head — the director of the university, a person acting as director, or another official authorised by them [3] — no later than 10 calendar days from the day the application and supporting documents are received [4]. The leave is formalized by an order of the University [4].

## Rights and obligations during leave

While on maternity or childcare leave, a student is released from obligations related to studying and is not allowed to participate in the educational process until the leave ends [5]. No disciplinary sanctions are applied during the leave [6]. The period of leave is not included in the timeframe set by the University for clearing academic debts [7]. If the student studies under a contract, no tuition fees are charged during the leave [8].

For international students, the University notifies relevant organizations about the leave as required by law [9]. Rules for using the student residential complex and receiving scholarships during leave are determined by the University's internal regulations [10].

## Completion

Maternity or childcare leave ends at the end of the period for which it was granted, or before the end of that period on the student's personal application (form in annex 8) [11]. The student is admitted to study after the leave by an order of the Head [11].

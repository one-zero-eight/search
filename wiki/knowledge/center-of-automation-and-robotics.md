# Centre for Automation and Robotics (Центр автоматизации и роботизации) — place, tasks, functions and rights

## The document

The Regulation on the Centre for Automation and Robotics (Центр автоматизации и роботизации) of ANO VO «Innopolis University» was approved by order No. ОД/АД-04/2024/10/01 of 1 October 2024 [1]. The order was issued in connection with the creation of a new structural subdivision with a view to optimising the University's activity; it was signed by the Director for Development and HR Policy R.F. Valiev, who retained control over execution of the order and of the Regulation [1]. The head of the Centre, T.R. Satdarov, is to ensure timely familiarisation (against signature) with the approved Regulation of the employees of the subdivision moved into it from other subdivisions as well as of employees newly hired and transferred [1].

The Regulation governs the activity of the Centre and determines its tasks, functions, rights and duties [2]. The wiki copy is a draft made by OCR, so numbers and dates should be checked against the PDF [1].

## Place in the structure

- The Centre is a structural subdivision of the University and belongs to the category of research subdivisions [3].
- The Centre is the legal successor (правопреемник) of the University's Centre of Technologies of Components of Robotics and Mechatronics (ЦНТИ), which was created to implement the programme for the creation and development of ЦНТИ [4].
- That programme rests on the agreement of 17 December 2021 No. 70-2021-00308 on the provision of funds to a legal entity or individual entrepreneur on a gratuitous and non-repayable basis in the form of a grant, whose financial support comes wholly or partly from a subsidy provided from the federal budget, concluded between the University and the NTI Projects Support Fund, with all amendments [1].
- The Centre reports directly to the University director [5].
- In its activity the Centre is guided by the Constitution of the Russian Federation, the Labour Code of the Russian Federation, Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29 December 2012 «Об образовании в Российской Федерации», other laws and normative legal acts on the activity of higher education institutions, the University's Charter, decisions of the University's Academic, Supervisory and Operational councils, orders and instructions of the University director, other local acts and this Regulation [6].
- The structure, headcount and staffing table of the Centre, and changes to them, are approved by the University director [7].
- The Centre is headed by a director of the Centre, hired and dismissed by order of the University director [8].
- The working conditions of the Centre's employees are determined by the employment contracts concluded with each employee and by the University's internal labour rules [9]; their rights, duties and degree of responsibility are set by job descriptions [10].
- Within their competence, the Centre's employees may be involved in work under grants, projects, subsidies and other programmes carried out by the University [11].
- The Regulation and amendments to it are approved by the University director or another person authorised by the director [12].

Another page names the Centre for Automation and Robotisation as the body above the Centre for the Development of Industrial Robotics and its Integration Department, in whose Sector of Industrial Automation a separate regulation applies [13].

## Tasks

The tasks of the Centre are:

- commercialisation of the results of the activity of the Centre and of the structural subdivisions included in it, including the results of implementing the programme for the creation and development of ЦНТИ; commercialisation means activity bringing the results of research, developments and technologies of the Centre and ЦНТИ into economic circulation by transferring rights to them on a paid basis, carrying out developments on third-party orders or in other ways providing the Centre with income [14];
- implementation of commercial projects in robotics, unmanned aerial systems and unmanned technologies [15];
- comprehensive development of the end-to-end technologies of the National Technology Initiative according to the ЦНТИ programme, and carrying out post-monitoring measures in accordance with the obligations accepted under the grant agreement [16];
- creating a scientific and methodological database for fundamental and applied research in robotics, mechatronics, unmanned aerial systems and unmanned technologies [17];
- developing a set of scientific and technical solutions in robotics, unmanned aerial systems and unmanned technologies [18];
- carrying out research, development and technological work (НИР, ОКР, НИОКТР), industrial projects and grants in the Centre's profile on its own initiative and under grant support, from budget and non-budget funds [19];
- promoting the products developed in the Centre on the Russian and international markets [20];
- participating in the implementation of state programmes, federal and national projects in industrial robotics, unmanned technologies, unmanned aerial systems and other related fields [21];
- popularising robotisation and automation of industrial enterprises [22];
- identifying the potential and directions of robotisation and automation of industrial enterprises [23];
- reverse engineering of technological solutions in industrial equipment and creation of industrial prototypes [24].

## Functions

- Concluding civil-law contracts for the purpose of commercialisation as defined in clause 2.1 and for the subsequent implementation of commercial projects [25].
- Step-by-step planning and conduct of НИР, ОКР, НИОКТР, projects and grants [26].
- Developing scientific research programmes in the Centre's field of activity [27].
- Improving methodologies, technologies, methods and tools of work [28].
- Creating centres for the development of industrial robotics and providing them with methodological support and monitoring of their activity [29].
- Developing and updating the methodology and conducting technical and technological audits of industrial enterprises to identify their robotisation potential [30].

## Rights

In carrying out their activity the Centre's employees have the right to:

- request from the University's structural subdivisions the information and materials needed for the work within the Centre's competence [31];
- involve, with the consent of the heads of the University's structural subdivisions, employees of those subdivisions for preparing drafts of local acts and other documents and for developing and carrying out the Centre's projects [32];
- take part in meetings held by the University's management where matters within the Centre's competence are discussed [33];
- propose to the University's management the repeal, amendment or supplementation of the University's local acts [34];
- interact with state authorities and bodies of administration, legal entities and subdivisions of the University on matters within the Centre's competence [35].

## Interaction with other subdivisions

- Interaction with the Department for Attracting State Financing concerns participation in НИР, ОКР, НИОКТР, industrial projects and grants [36]; with the Department for Support of Grants and Projects it concerns reporting under grants, budget and non-budget contracts [37].
- Interaction with the Cross-industry Centre for Technology Transfer concerns the legal protection of intellectual activity results created at the Centre, the exercise, disposal and protection of the University's rights to them, their effective use and commercialisation [38].
- Interaction with the Department of Academic Policy and Higher Education, the Educational Department and the Department of International Cooperation concerns educational activity [39].
- Interaction with the other structural subdivisions is determined by the tasks and functions assigned to the Centre by the Regulation [40] and takes place when preparing drafts of local acts, orders, instructions, contracts and other documents and when preparing opinions and written and oral reports on matters of the Centre's competence [41].
- The Centre may be a base for the practical training of the University's students [42].

## Reporting

- On the basis of the relevant local acts, orders and instructions the Centre may be responsible, within its activity, for preparing and keeping up to date information, reports and accounts [43].
- The Centre is responsible for preparing reports on НИР, ОКР, НИОКТР, industrial projects and grants and for passing them in time to the University's internal subdivisions [44].
- The Centre reports as required by federal executive authorities, executive authorities of the subjects of the Russian Federation, the NTI Projects Support Fund and other bodies, agencies and organisations, in accordance with obligations under grants (subsidies) provided from the budgets of the budget system of the Russian Federation, and in other cases provided by law; in other cases reporting is determined by the contracts concluded with third parties [45].

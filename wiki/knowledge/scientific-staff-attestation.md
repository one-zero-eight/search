# Attestation of scientific workers — the Regulation of 30 December 2020: types, timing, commission, decisions and criteria

## The document

The Regulation on conducting the attestation of scientific workers at ANO VO «Innopolis University» was approved by order of 30 December 2020 No. ОД/АД-04/2020/12/3, signed by Director K.V. Semenikhin [1]. It determines the rules, main tasks and principles, the terms and specific features of attesting scientific workers in order to confirm that they correspond to the positions of scientific workers they hold, and sets criteria for assessing the results of their professional activity [2].

## Who is attested and who is not

- The Regulation applies to workers whose positions, under order of the Ministry of Education and Science of Russia No. 538 of 27.05.2015, belong to the category of scientific workers; the list of scientific worker positions approved by order No. 937 of 02.09.2015 is given in Annex 1 [3].
- Attestation does not apply to scientific workers working under fixed-term employment contracts [4], pregnant women [5], women on pregnancy and childbirth leave and workers on childcare leave until the child reaches the age named in the clause — «до достижения им возраста трех лет» [6]. Those workers may be attested not earlier than 1 year after they return from the relevant leave [7].
- Scientific workers who were not attested before the Regulation entered into force are subject to attestation taking into account the terms set by legislation and by the Regulation [8].

## Types and timing

- The University holds planned and unplanned attestations of scientific workers [9].
- Both are held not earlier than 2 years from the day the scientific worker was hired or from the day of the previous attestation [10].
- A planned attestation is held once every 5 years [11]; an unplanned one may be held at any time but not more often than once in 2 years from the day of the previous attestation [12].

## Preparing an attestation

- For a planned attestation the HR department informs the worker's immediate supervisor that the term for the planned attestation is expiring — not later than 6 months before the expiry of the five-year period counted from the previous attestation or from hiring [13], [11].
- The immediate supervisor submits a memo (служебная записка) to the authorised person in the form of Annex 2: for a planned attestation not later than 3 months before the end of the five-year term [14], [11], and for an unplanned attestation at any time, subject to the terms of section 2, where there is a need for it, including where the worker's publication activity was negatively assessed at the end of the year [15]. No memo is required if the immediate supervisor is himself the person authorised to sign the order [16].
- The attestation is held on the basis of an order of the Director of the University or of another person authorised by him, in the form of Annex 3, stating the type of attestation, its date, time and place [17]. It may be held for all workers of a structural subdivision, for part of them or for a single worker [18].
- The order is brought to the notice of the worker being attested under signature and by e-mail to the worker's address not later than 30 calendar days before the day of the attestation [19].
- The professional activity of scientific workers is assessed in the order established by the federal executive body responsible for science and scientific-technical policy for the attestation of scientific workers [20].

## The attestation commission

- A permanent attestation commission is formed for the attestation; its composition is approved by order of the Director and is formed so as to exclude a conflict of interest that could influence its decisions [21].
- The commission must include the Director of the University, representatives of non-profit organisations that are recipients of and (or) interested in the results of the University, and leading scientists invited from other organisations carrying out scientific, scientific-technical or innovative activity of a similar profile [22].
- The chair of the commission is the Director of the University; during his temporary absence (illness, leave, business trip and other valid reasons) his powers are exercised by the deputy chair or another person authorised by him [23].
- The secretary is an authorised worker of the University — a worker of the HR department or another person competent in personnel policy — who ensures that the results are entered in the information base, whose procedure and content the University determines itself, subject to the legislation on personal data and on state and other protected secrets [24].
- In accordance with paragraph 9 of order No. 538 the worker himself may enter data on the results in the information base as needed, including when new results are obtained, and the data must be open and accessible to him; the worker checks their completeness and accuracy within 20 calendar days from the day he is notified of the attestation and may ask for inaccuracies to be corrected or correct them himself [24].

## Decisions of the commission

- The commission takes one of the following decisions: that the worker corresponds to the position held (with the position named) or that he does not correspond to it (with the position and the reasons for non-correspondence named) [25].
- If the worker is recognised as not corresponding to the position held, the employment contract with him may be terminated on the grounds and in the procedure provided by labour legislation [26].
- The decision is taken by a majority of the members present at the meeting and is recorded in a minute; when a member of the commission is attested, the decision is taken in his absence in the general order; a meeting is competent if not less than two thirds of its members are present; when votes are equal the chair takes the final decision [27].
- An extract from the minute — with the worker's surname, name and patronymic, the title of his position, the date of the meeting and the result of the vote and the decision — is sent to the worker and placed by the University in the unified information system «ученые-исследователи.рф» within 10 calendar days of the decision [28].
- The worker may appeal the results of the attestation in accordance with Russian legislation [29].
- The materials of the attestation are passed by the commission to the HR department not later than 5 working days after the meeting, for storage and for decisions under the Labour Code [30].

## Criteria for the assessment

- Objectively assessed are: the results of the worker's scientific activity and (or) of the activity of the subdivisions (scientific groups) he heads in dynamics over the period preceding the attestation, including the quantitative indicators of labour productivity he achieved [31]; his personal contribution to the development of science, to the solution of scientific problems in the corresponding field and the influence of that contribution on the performance and development of the University [32]; the raising of his personal professional level and (or) of the professional level of the workers of the subdivisions (scientific groups) he heads [33].
- The indicators of the worker's scientific activity are recognised as satisfactory, and the worker as corresponding to the position held, where he receives a satisfactory assessment; the criteria are set in accordance with the approximate list of quantitative indicators of the labour productivity of scientific workers approved by order of the Ministry of Education and Science No. 538 of 27.05.2015 [34].
- The specific assessment criteria are determined by the worker's immediate supervisor, taking into account the worker's position and the direction and specifics of the subdivision's activity; the values of the quantitative indicators are established by the University not later than two years before the next attestation, taking into account the values reached by the reference group to which the organisation belongs under Government Decree No. 312 of 8 April 2009 [35].

## Final provisions and annexes

Labour disputes and disagreements of the attested worker and the members of the commission on compliance with the Regulation are settled by mutual agreement or, failing that, in the procedure established by Russian legislation [36]; in what the Regulation does not provide for, the worker and the University follow Russian legislation [37]. The Regulation is approved by the Director and enters into force on approval [38], and is placed on the University's official website [39]; changes and additions are approved by orders of the Director or of a person authorised by him [40].

Annex 1 lists the scientific worker positions subject to attestation: deputy head (director, head) for scientific work; chief (general) designer; head of a department (institute, centre or other subdivision) and his deputy; head of a scientific and (or) scientific-technical project; head of a research department (laboratory) and his deputy; head of a design department (laboratory) and his deputy; head of a centre (department) of patenting, scientific and (or) scientific-technical information, collective use of scientific equipment or commercialisation of results and his deputy; chief, leading, senior, scientific and junior scientific researcher and research engineer [1].

Annex 4 sets the criteria — quantitative indicators of the labour results of scientific workers: publications indexed in Russian and international citation systems, published monographs, design and technological documentation, expert examinations with conclusions, issues of scientific journals, results of intellectual activity entered in the state registers, master's graduates and candidate dissertations prepared under the worker's supervision, highly qualified staff recruited for projects he led, conferences with international participation, popular science publications, funds attracted to the organisation and services of collective-use centres [1].

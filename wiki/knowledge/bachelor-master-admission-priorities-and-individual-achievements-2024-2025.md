# Special rights and individual achievements in bachelor's and master's admission 2024/2025 — the competitive score

## Simultaneous application

- The maximum number of organisations of higher education in which an applicant may simultaneously apply for bachelor's programmes is 5 [1].
- The rules set the maximum number of areas of study in which an applicant may simultaneously take part in the competition for bachelor's programmes at the University at 5, and state in the same clause that an applicant may simultaneously take part in the competition for bachelor's programmes at the University in two areas of study (clause 19) [1].
- An applicant may simultaneously apply under various conditions of admission within that number of areas of study [1].

## Olympiads: admission without examinations and 100 points

- Winners and prizewinners of the final stage of the All-Russian olympiad of schoolchildren and members of the national teams of the Russian Federation that took part in international olympiads in general education subjects have the right to admission without entrance examinations under part 4 of article 71 of Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ [1].
- Winners and prizewinners of pupil olympiads held in the procedure set by the Ministry of Science and Higher Education have special rights under part 12 of article 71: the right to admission without entrance examinations and the right to be equated to persons who scored the maximum number of ЕГЭ points in the subject corresponding to the profile of the olympiad (the right to 100 points); where the right is given in respect of additional entrance examinations, the applicant is given the highest result of 100 points, and both rights may be given to the same applicant [1].
- The right to admission without entrance examinations under parts 4 and/or 12 of article 71 is used as a single right: the applicant files the application on that ground only to one organisation of higher education and only for one educational programme of his choice, whatever the number of grounds, but may use it under various conditions of admission within one organisation and one programme (clause 23) [1].
- During the periods of those rights the persons having them also receive a special advantage — they are equated to persons having 100 points in a general education entrance examination (100 points of the ЕГЭ or 100 points for an examination held by the University) where the examination corresponds to the profile of the olympiad (clause 24) [1].
- For admission without entrance examinations under part 4 of article 71 the University establishes by a local act the correspondence of the programmes (areas of study) to the profiles of the All-Russian and international olympiads and one general education entrance examination corresponding to those profiles for the special advantage (clause 25) [1].
- For the special rights from pupil olympiads the University establishes its own list of olympiads from among those included in the federal lists, and for each olympiad establishes the correspondence of the programmes to its profiles and one general education examination corresponding to the profiles for the right to 100 points and/or the special advantage (clauses 26 and 27) [1].
- The right to admission without entrance examinations is given to winners and prizewinners of olympiads of levels I, II and III in the profiles corresponding to the subjects «Математика», «Информатика и ИКТ» and «Физика» for grades 9, 10 and 11 from the federal list of pupil olympiads, and the special right is given to those persons only if their result of the ЕГЭ or of an examination held by the University in one of those subjects is not lower than 75 points (clause 27) [1].
- Within one competition, on one ground giving the right to 100 points (the special advantage), the applicant receives 100 points in one general education entrance examination (of his choice where several corresponding examinations are set); several grounds may be used at the same time, including within one competition, and the same ground may be used in several competitions (clause 28) [1].

## Who has the special quota and the preferential right

- The right to admission within the special quota belongs to disabled children, invalids of groups I and II, invalids since childhood, invalids because of a military trauma or a disease received during military service, orphans and children left without parental care and persons from among them, and combat veterans from among the persons named in the law on veterans (clause 29) [1].
- The preferential right of enrolment belongs to: orphans and children left without parental care and persons from among them; disabled children and invalids of groups I and II; citizens under 20 who have only one parent — an invalid of group I — if the average per capita income of the family is below the subsistence minimum in the subject of the Federation of their residence; persons who suffered from radiation because of the Chernobyl disaster; children of servicemen who died or died of injury or disease received in the performance of military service, including in counter-terrorist operations; children of deceased Heroes of the Soviet Union, Heroes of the Russian Federation and full holders of the Order of Glory; children of employees of the bodies of internal affairs, the national guard, the penal system, the fire service, the bodies controlling drug trafficking, customs, the Investigative Committee who died or died of injury or disease received in the performance of service, and children dependent on them; children of prosecutors who died or died of injury received in service; servicemen serving under contract with not less than three years of continuous service and citizens who served by conscription and apply on the recommendation of their commanders; invalids of war, participants of combat actions and combat veterans; participants of nuclear tests and of the liquidation of radiation accidents; servicemen and employees who performed tasks in the armed conflict in the Chechen Republic and in counter-terrorist operations in the North Caucasus region; and the persons named in points 1–7 of part 5.1 of article 71 of Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ (clause 29) [1].
- The special rights are granted under parts 5, 9 and 10 of article 71 and part 14 of article 108 of Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ [1].

## Individual achievements on bachelor's programmes

- The applicant receives 3 points for a document of education or of education and qualification with honours obtained in Russian educational organisations (an attestation of secondary general education with honours, an attestation for those awarded a gold or silver medal, a diploma of secondary vocational education with honours, a diploma of initial vocational education with honours or for those awarded a gold or silver medal) [1].
- Military service by conscription, by contract or by mobilisation in the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation, and presence in volunteer formations under a contract of voluntary assistance in tasks of the Armed Forces during the special military operation in the territories of Ukraine, the Donetsk and Lugansk People's Republics, the Zaporozhye and Kherson regions, give 2 points [1].
- The status of winner (prizewinner) of the national and/or international championship of professional mastery among disabled persons and persons with health limitations «Абилимпикс» gives 2 points [1].
- On bachelor's programmes an applicant may be awarded not more than 7 points in total for individual achievements [1].
- The applicant presents documents confirming the results of the individual achievements, and the points for them are included in the sum of competitive points [1].

## Individual achievements on master's programmes

- On master's programmes the University awards 3 points for a document of higher education and qualification with honours and 3 points for a scientific publication in Scopus or Web of Science (for one publication), with not more than 6 points in total for individual achievements [1].
- The applicant presents documents confirming the results, and the points are included in the sum of competitive points (clauses 35 and 36) [1].

## Where applicants are equal

The average mark of the document of education of the established form is included in the list of individual achievements taken into account where applicants are equal on the criteria of ranking, and where applicants are equal on those achievements the list may be supplemented during the admission (clause 33) [1].

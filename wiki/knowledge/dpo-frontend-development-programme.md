# Additional professional education programme «Frontend-разработка с применением CSS, HTML и JavaScript» (2021) — approval, content, length and tuition

## The programme and the documents about it

The additional professional programme — a повышение квалификации programme — «Frontend-разработка с применением CSS, HTML и JavaScript» was approved by order of the University of 12 January 2021 No. ОД/ДПО-02/2021/01/12, which left control over its execution with the signer and was signed by the vice-rector — head of the department for project and research activity I.I. Bariev and by the head of the Centre for Specialised IT Training Yu.M. Guseva [1]. The programme text is annexed to that order and is approved by it [1].

Its terms of delivery and the cost of the paid educational services under it were approved by a separate order of the same day, 12 January 2021 No. ОД/ДПО-04/2021/01/12 «Об утверждении сроков реализации и стоимости оказания платных образовательных услуг по программе дополнительного профессионального образования — программе повышения квалификации» [2].

Both wiki pages are scans read by OCR, with garbled programme and document names, so the numbers and dates should be checked against the PDFs [1], [2].

## Legal basis

The programme was drawn up under Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ of 29 December 2012 «Об образовании в Российской Федерации» and the order of the Ministry of Education and Science of Russia of 01.07.2013 No. 499, as amended by the order of 15.11.2013 No. 1244; the order of 23.08.2017 No. 816 on e-learning and distance educational technologies; the federal state educational standard of higher education in field of study 09.03.01 «Информатика и вычислительная техника» (bachelor level) approved by order of 19 September 2017 No. 929; and the professional standard «Программист» approved by order of the Ministry of Labour and Social Protection of 18 November 2013 No. 679н [1]. The order of 12 January 2021 approving the programme also refers to the order of the Ministry of Science and Higher Education of 14.03.2020 No. 397 on the organisation of educational activity under the new coronavirus infection restrictions [1].

The order on the terms and cost was issued under Federal Law No. 273-ФЗ, the order of the Ministry of Education and Science of Russia of 01.07.2013 No. 499, the Rules for the provision of paid educational services approved by Government Decree of 15.08.2013 No. 708 and on the basis of contracts of education for studies under additional professional programmes, in order to deliver the programme run by the Centre for Specialised IT Training [2].

## Who may study, in what form and for how long

- Physical persons who have or are receiving higher or secondary vocational education are admitted to the programme [1].
- Studies are conducted in the full-time (очная) form with the use of distance educational technologies [1]; the educational process is organised with distance technologies, innovative technologies and teaching methods that ensure that listeners obtain knowledge and skills in software development [1].
- The term of mastering the programme is 120 academic hours, which is also the volume of the programme [1].
- The programme is delivered by the University's academic staff, and highly qualified specialists — heads and leading specialists of state bodies and institutions and teachers of leading Russian and foreign educational organisations — may be involved [1].

## Aim, field of activity and competences

The programme regulates the requirements to raising (expanding) qualification in the field of software development and defines the minimum volume of knowledge and skills a web programmer must have to start working in state and commercial companies of the production sector of information technologies [1]. Its aim is improving the competences necessary for the professional activity of a web programmer [1].

The professional activity of a web programmer covers the creation, modification and support of web sites and information resources through whose work economic, managerial, social and other tasks of private business, state corporations and state administration bodies are solved [1].

The listener improves the general professional competences «ability to solve standard tasks of professional activity on the basis of information and bibliographic culture with the use of information and communication technologies and with account of the basic requirements of information security» (ОПК-3) and «ability to master the methods of using software tools for solving practical tasks» (ОПК-9), and the professional competence «ability to develop requirements and design software» (ПК-1) [1].

## How studies and assessment are organised

The curriculum provides lectures, seminars (practical classes), independent work (homework) and the final attestation [1]. Its sections run from an introduction and review of tools, through the practical use of HTML and connecting CSS and JavaScript on a page, approaches to page layout and CSS3, JavaScript, the web server, AJAX and REST API, to JavaScript modules, CSS preprocessors and project build systems, and end with the final attestation; the whole programme is 120 hours [1].

Current monitoring of performance evaluates the progress through the sections and is carried out in the form of checking homework; the final attestation completes the whole programme [1]. Each listener performs the reporting works — homework assignments for the sections, assessed against stated criteria [1].

Listeners who have successfully completed all elements of the curriculum are admitted to the final attestation, which is held in the form of a pass (зачёт) on a project work [1].

## Documents issued

Persons who have successfully mastered the programme and passed the final attestation receive an удостоверение о повышении квалификации of the established form; where the programme is mastered in parallel with obtaining secondary vocational and (or) higher education, the удостоверение is issued simultaneously with the corresponding document on education and qualification [1]. Persons who did not pass the final attestation or received unsatisfactory results at it, as well as those who mastered only part of the programme and (or) were expelled from the University, receive a certificate of study or of the period of study (справка об обучении или о периоде обучения) on a form established by the University [1].

## Terms of delivery and tuition

The order of 12 January 2021 No. ОД/ДПО-04/2021/01/12 approves the terms of delivery of the programme according to its annex 1 and sets the cost of the paid educational services under the programme according to its annex 2 [2]. In the wiki copy both annexes were scanned upside down and their OCR text cannot be read, so neither the dates nor the tuition figure can be quoted from this page: they must be read from the PDF of the order [2].

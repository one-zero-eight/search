# Getting Started with the University's Online Learning Platform

## Obtaining your credentials
After you are enrolled, the University assigns you an identifier (personal login) and provides a primary password [1][3].

## Accessing the platform
You use the standard identification level to access the University's EIS [2]. Enter your identifier (login) and password to gain access to all data and the specific modules assigned to you in the system [2].

## Available digital resources and features
The platform provides remote access to modern professional databases and information reference systems [4]. The specific composition of these databases and systems is determined in the working programs of disciplines (modules) and is updated if necessary [4]. You can access study plans, working programs of disciplines (modules), practices, electronic educational publications, and electronic educational resources that are specified in those working programs [5]. The system supports the formation of your electronic portfolio, including the saving of your works and grades for those works [5]. It records the course of the educational process, the results of intermediate certification, and the results of mastering educational programs [5]. You can use the platform to conduct educational sessions and assessment procedures that are provided for using electronic learning and distance educational technologies [5]. The system enables interaction between participants of the educational process, including synchronous and asynchronous interaction via the Internet [5].

## Gaps and caveats
The evidence does not specify how to change the primary password after first login [1][3]. It does not provide direct links, URLs, or technical support contacts for the platform [2][5]. It does not state what happens if the identifier or password is lost or compromised [1][3]. It does not list the exact professional databases or information reference systems available, only that they are defined in the working programs of disciplines (modules) [4]. It does not explain how to access the platform from outside the university network beyond stating that remote access is provided [4].

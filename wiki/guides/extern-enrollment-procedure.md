# How to Enroll as an Extern

## How to Enroll as an Extern

You must submit your application and supporting documents no later than one month before the University's established start date of the academic year [5]. You submit your personal application to the University's structural subdivision responsible for admissions, addressed to the University Director [1]. You must present a document verifying your identity when you submit the application [2].

Along with the application, you attach the following documents [1]:
- A copy of your education document or education and qualification document [1].
- A copy of a certificate (or copies of certificates) of your study period, confirming successful completion of previous intermediate assessments at other educational organizations, if you are studying in the form of self-education [1].
- A 3x4 cm photograph [1].
- Any other documents whose submission serves your interests as a person wishing to be enrolled as an extern [1].

When submitting the application, you also sign a consent for the processing of personal data in accordance with the requirements of Federal Law No. 152-FZ of 27.07.2006 "On Personal Data" [3]. Based on this consent, the University has the right to enter information about you as an extern into federal, regional, and municipal information systems [3].

The University's Attestation Commission makes the decision on your enrollment based on the documents you submit [4]. The Commission's decision is formalized in a protocol [4]. On the basis of the Attestation Commission's protocol, the University signs a contract for paid educational services with you as an extern and issues an order enrolling you to pass the intermediate and final state assessments [6].

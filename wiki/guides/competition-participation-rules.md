# How to Apply for a Master's Study Grant

## Registration steps
You must register on the Official Website as a Contest Participant [1]. You must pass a specialized online test [2]. You must provide the following information and documents:
- A completed questionnaire on the Official Website containing your full name, date of birth, citizenship, city of residence, educational organization details, course of study, educational program, and details about the desired master's program (taught in Russian or English), plus other required data [3].
- A motivation letter in English explaining your reasons for seeking the Grant and studying at the University [3].
- A CV in English [3].
- A copy of your higher education diploma with appendices, or if you are a final-year bachelor's student, you may submit it after obtaining the diploma [3].
- If you are currently studying for a higher education degree during the first stage, a study certificate issued by your educational organization instead [3].
- An English language proficiency certificate if you have one [3].
- The University's consent form for personal data processing according to the contest purposes, which may include signing via a simple electronic signature by checking a box in the corresponding field [...the clause continues; do not state what it leaves out] [3].
If you plan to study in an English-taught master's program, you must pass an online English language test [4].

## Participation limits
You may participate in the Contest no more than once during each contest period specified in clause 1.12 of the Regulation [5].

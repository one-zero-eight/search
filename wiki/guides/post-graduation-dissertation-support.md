# Post-Graduation Dissertation Defense Support

To apply for dissertation defense support, graduates must submit a personal application to the University Director [3]. Graduates must deliver this application to the department responsible for organizing the educational process for graduate programs [4]. Graduates must submit the application no later than 30 calendar days after passing final certification [4].

The university has the right to provide graduates with support when they submit their dissertation for defense [1]. The support is formalized by an order from the University Director or an authorized person [3]. The support lasts for a period of not more than one calendar year after graduates complete their graduate program [2].

# Mechatronics Program Satisfaction and Professional Development Opportunities

## Official Satisfaction Metrics
Students, postgraduates, and applicants can access the official satisfaction metrics for the 15.02.10 Mechatronics and Robotics program in the staff survey results [1][2][3]. The program reports an overall satisfaction rate of 75% with the conditions and organization of educational and labor activities in the University [3].

## Professional and Creative Development Opportunities
The program provides opportunities for the development of creative abilities and addresses 80% of students' interests [1]. It also offers opportunities for professional development in 70% of the University [2]. Survey dynamics for Block 2 recorded a result of 83% [2]. Staff performance regarding friendliness, politeness, and competence is tracked in Block 3 [2].

## Gaps and caveats
The evidence does not specify how the 70% professional development coverage or the 80% interest coverage is distributed across courses or semesters [1][2]. It does not define what specific professional or creative activities are included [1][2]. The evidence does not explain the 83% Block 2 result or how Block 3 staff metrics relate to student opportunities [2]. The evidence does not provide satisfaction metrics for postgraduate students or applicants specifically [1][2][3]. The evidence does not state deadlines, procedures, or eligibility requirements for accessing these opportunities [1][2][3]. The evidence is silent on how these metrics are updated or who is responsible for maintaining them [1][2][3].

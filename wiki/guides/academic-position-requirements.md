# Qualification Requirements for Academic Positions

Applicants, teachers, and staff, the qualification requirements for academic positions are defined in the official regulations [1][2][3][4].

- **Professor:** You must hold a Doctor of Sciences degree and have at least 5 years of scientific-pedagogical work experience, or hold the academic title of professor [1].
- **Associate Professor:** You must hold higher professional education, a Candidate or Doctor of Sciences degree, and at least 3 years of scientific-pedagogical work experience, or hold the academic title of associate professor or senior researcher [2].
- **Senior Lecturer:** You must hold higher professional education and at least 3 years of scientific-pedagogical work experience, or hold higher professional education and a Candidate of Sciences degree with at least 1 year of scientific-pedagogical work experience, and candidates with a Candidate of Sciences degree receive priority in the selection process [3].
- **Assistant:** You must hold higher professional education and at least 1 year of work experience in an educational institution, or hold higher professional education with postgraduate professional education or a Candidate of Sciences degree which removes the work experience requirement, and candidates with a Candidate of Sciences degree receive priority in the selection process [4].

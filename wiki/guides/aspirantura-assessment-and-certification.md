# Assessment and Intermediate Certification Rules

## Types of Academic Control
Quality control of the aspirantura program includes current performance monitoring, intermediate certification of aspirants, and final certification of aspirants [1].

## Intermediate Certification
Intermediate certification of aspirants ensures assessment of the results of carrying out stages of scientific (scientific-research) activity, results of mastery of disciplines (modules), and completion of practice [3]. This assessment is conducted in accordance with the individual plan of scientific activity and the individual study plan [3].

Current performance monitoring ensures assessment of the progress of stages of scientific research, mastery of disciplines (modules), and completion of practice [2]. This assessment is also conducted in accordance with the individual plan of scientific activity and the individual study plan [2].

## Gaps and caveats
The evidence does not specify the deadlines, frequency, or procedures for conducting current performance monitoring or intermediate certification [1][2][3]. It does not state the evaluation criteria, grading scales, or consequences of failing intermediate certification [3]. The evidence does not describe the format or requirements of the final certification [1]. No information is provided regarding who conducts these assessments or how the individual plans are approved [2][3]. The evidence is silent on how results are recorded or reported [1][2][3].

# Evaluation Schedule and Results Notification

The evaluation schedule is brought to your attention under signature no less than 30 (thirty) calendar days before the scheduled evaluation date [1]. The schedule is compiled according to the form in Appendix 2 to the regulation [1].

If you are directly present at the Evaluation Commission meeting, your evaluation results are communicated to you after the voting results are tallied [3]. If you are absent from the meeting, your results are communicated to you on the next working day [3]. If it is impossible to personally acquaint you with the results, a notification is sent to you via email [3].

For you after you pass the evaluation, the secretary of the Evaluation Commission draws up an extract from the protocol no later than 2 (two) working days from the day the evaluation was conducted [2]. The extract is compiled according to the form in Appendix 5 to the regulation [2]. The extract contains your surname, first name, patronymic, position name, the date of the commission meeting, the voting results, the adopted decision, and the commission's decision [2]. You are acquainted with the extract under signature within 10 (ten) working days from the day the meeting was conducted [2]. If you refuse to familiarize yourself with the extract, an act according to Appendix 4 is drawn up [2]. A copy of the extract is kept in your personal file in the HR department [2].

# Managing Your Electronic Portfolio

## Purpose
The electronic portfolio ensures the formation of your portfolio, including the preservation of your works, reviews, and grades on these works by any participants of the educational process [1].

## Sections You Fill Out
You receive access to your portfolio after you obtain a login and password for the electronic information and educational environment [3].
You fill out the "My works and reviews" section to present information about any works completed as part of your studies or outside them [2]. This section requires you to fill out the Title, Type, Description, and File fields [2].
You provide information in the "Achievements" section about your achievements in academic, research, socially significant, cultural-creative, and sports activities [3].

## Gaps and caveats
The evidence does not state which sections are mandatory or optional [1][2][3]. The evidence does not provide deadlines for filling out the sections [1][2][3]. The evidence does not define what the "additional part" or "mandatory part" of the portfolio contain [3]. The evidence does not list any other portfolio sections beyond "My works and reviews" and "Achievements" [2][3]. The evidence does not explain the procedure for obtaining the login and password [3].

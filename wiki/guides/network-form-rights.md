# Understanding Network Form Education

## Curriculum structure and components
Your curriculum can include parts from programs of different types, levels, and directions [2]. You can study the full program or individual subjects, courses, modules, practice, or other components using the resources of multiple organizations, including foreign ones [1]. The program may cover competencies from one or several professions, specialties, or training directions, and may allow you to obtain multiple qualifications simultaneously [1].

## Enrollment and student status
When you are admitted to a network program, you are enrolled in the base organization [3]. You remain a student of the base organization throughout your studies [5]. If you study a part of the program at a participating organization, you are transferred to that organization without being dismissed from the base organization [4]. During that period, you are also a student of the participating organization [5]. You are not enrolled in an organization that possesses resources [4].

## Study periods at partner institutions
You study the assigned part of the program at the participating organization according to the forms and order set by that organization's local rules [6]. The participating organization determines the specific procedures for your study period [6]. After you fully complete that part of the program at the participating organization, you are dismissed from it [7]. If the program requires joint final certification by both the base and participating organizations, your dismissal from the participating organization happens only after that certification is completed [8].

## Assessment and certification
Your study period at the participating organization includes current control and intermediate certification [6]. These assessments follow the forms defined in the network program's curriculum and the order established by the participating organization [6]. If the University acts as the participating organization, the assessments follow the University's local regulations [6]. The results of your intermediate certification at the participating organization count as your official results for the network program and do not require additional verification at the base organization [6].

## Graduation and documents
If you successfully complete the network program and pass the final certification, the base organization issues you education and/or qualification documents [9]. If the Network Form Agreement specifies it, you also receive education and/or qualification documents from the participating organization [9]. For programs that do not include final certification, documents are issued according to the conditions and order set in the Network Form Agreement [9].

## Gaps and caveats
The evidence does not specify which partner institutions are available or how many students can be transferred to them [4][5]. It does not state the duration of study periods at participating organizations or the deadlines for transfers [4][7]. The evidence does not define what happens if you fail current control or intermediate certification at a participating organization [6]. It does not explain how joint final certification is organized or what happens if you do not pass it [8]. The evidence does not list the specific types of documents issued by participating organizations or the exact conditions for receiving them [9]. The evidence does not provide course pages, so course-specific variations are not covered [1].

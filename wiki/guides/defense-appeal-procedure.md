# How to appeal your thesis defense results

## Deadline and procedure for filing an appeal

You have the right to appeal the results of your thesis defense [1]. You may file an appeal if you believe there was a violation of the established procedure for conducting the state final certification [2].

You must submit your appeal no later than the next working day after the results of the state final certification are announced [3]. To do this, send a scanned copy of the appeal application, signed and with your full name deciphered, to the Office of Educational Programs at education@innopolis.ru [3].

After you submit your appeal, the Secretary of the State Examination Commission forwards the following to the Appeal Commission via email: a scanned copy of the Commission's meeting minutes, a scanned copy of the Chairman's conclusion on compliance with procedural issues, the video recording of the defense, and the thesis, review, and reference (if necessary) [4].

The Chairman of the Appeal Commission conducts an online meeting to consider your appeal via video conference (using Microsoft Teams, Zoom, or similar software) no later than 2 working days after you submit the appeal application [5]. The Secretary of the Appeal Commission keeps a record of the meeting and records the session [5].

On the day of the Appeal Commission meeting, the Chairman notifies the Methodological Department of the results by sending an email with a scanned copy of the meeting minutes to education@innopolis.ru [6]. You will receive notification of the appeal decision via email to your corporate email address no later than the next working day after the Appeal Commission meeting [7].

The decision of the Appeal Commission is final and not subject to review [8]. If necessary, the State Examination Commission may conduct a repeat thesis defense in the presence of one member of the Appeal Commission [9]. An appeal against a repeat state final certification is not accepted [10].

## Gaps and caveats

The evidence does not define what qualifies as a violation of the established procedure [2]. The evidence does not specify the format or content requirements for the appeal application beyond the signature and name deciphering [3]. The evidence does not specify who constitutes the Appeal Commission or how its members are selected [5]. The evidence does not specify the criteria the Appeal Commission uses to make its decision [5]. The evidence does not specify what happens if the repeat defense is also failed [9].

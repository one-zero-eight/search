# Selecting a Diploma Project Topic

## Your rights when choosing a topic
You have the right to choose the topic of your diploma project or diploma work [1]. You may propose your own theme, provided you justify the feasibility of its development for practical application [1]. You may base the content of your diploma project or diploma work on the results of a coursework or project you previously completed within the relevant professional module [2]. You may also base it on the results of previously completed practical assignments [2]. You may use automated design and management systems to prepare the documentation for your diploma project or diploma work [7].

## Requirements for the topic
Your chosen topic must correspond to the content of one or more professional modules included in your secondary vocational education educational program [1]. The topic must meet modern requirements for the development of high-tech sectors of science, production, economy, culture, and education [2]. The topic must have a practice-oriented character [2]. Your work should be performed, if possible, based on proposals or orders from enterprises, organizations, innovative companies, high-tech productions, or educational organizations [2].

## Requirements for content and structure
Your diploma project or diploma work must correspond to the developed assignment [4]. It must include an analysis of sources with generalizations, conclusions, comparisons, and an evaluation of different viewpoints [4]. It must demonstrate the required level of general scientific and specialized training, and your ability to apply mastered knowledge, practical skills, and general and professional competencies in practice according to FGOS SPO [4]. The work must include a title page, an explanatory note, a theoretical part, and a practical (graphic) part [5]. The explanatory note (introduction) must justify the relevance of the chosen topic and formulate the components of the conceptual apparatus [5]. The theoretical part must provide a theoretical overview of the topic based on an analysis of existing literature [5]. The practical part may be presented as a methodology, calculations, analysis of experimental data, or a creative product according to the types of professional activity [5]. The content of the explanatory note, theoretical, and practical parts is determined depending on the specialty profile [5]. The volume of your diploma project or diploma work is determined based on the specifics of your specialty [6]. If you perform the work in the form of experimental samples of products, products, etc., or as a creative work, the number of pages in the explanatory note may be reduced without reducing the overall quality of the work [6].

Depending on the character of your work, specific content features must be reflected [3]. If your work is experimental-practical, it must be aimed at solving the chosen problem and consist of designing professional activity, describing its implementation, and evaluating its effectiveness, with a conclusion containing conclusions and recommendations regarding the possibilities of practical application of the results [3]. If your work is experimental-experimental, it must present a plan for conducting the experiment, a characterization of experimental work methods, the main stages of the experiment (constituent, formative, control), and an analysis of the experimental work results, with a conclusion containing conclusions and recommendations regarding the possibilities of practical application of the results [3]. If your work is theoretical, the theoretical part must contain the history of the issue, justification of the developed problem in theory and practice through a deep comparative analysis of literature, with a conclusion containing conclusions and recommendations regarding the possibilities of using the research materials [3]. If your work is project-based, it must present the creative products created by you in the form of finished items, drawings, schemes, graphs, diagrams, etc., and the practical part may be [...the clause continues; do not state what it leaves out] [3].

## Review and finalization
Your completed diploma project or diploma work is subject to mandatory review [8]. External review is conducted to ensure objectivity in evaluating your work [8]. The work is reviewed by a specialist from among leading specialists of enterprises, government bodies, the labor and education sphere, research institutes, or teachers of the educational organization who are knowledgeable in issues related to the topic of your diploma project or diploma work [8]. Reviewers are appointed by an order of the College Director no later than one month before the defense [8]. You cannot make changes to your diploma project or diploma work after receiving the review [9].

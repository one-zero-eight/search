# Staff Competence and University Accessibility Metrics

## Accessibility via phone, email, and electronic services
For students, applicants, and the public, the official document reports on the accessibility of interaction with the University by phone, email, and electronic services [1]. It records a 70% result for the availability of the opportunity to submit suggestions aimed at improving the University's work [1]. This metric appears in Block 1 with a 70% value and a survey dynamics marker of мар.00 [1].

## Staff politeness
The official document reports on the friendliness and politeness of staff [2]. It records a 75% result for this metric [2].

## Staff competence
The official document reports on the competence of staff [3]. It records a 100% result for this metric [3]. This metric appears in Block 3 with an 88% value and a survey dynamics marker of дек.25 [3].

## Gaps and caveats
The evidence only provides survey results for the secondary education program 15.02.10 Mechatronics and Robotics and does not state standards or results for other programs [1][2][3]. The evidence does not define official standards, targets, or passing thresholds for these metrics [1][2][3]. The evidence does not explain the survey methodology, sample size, or how the percentages were calculated [1][2][3]. The evidence does not provide separate results for phone, email, and electronic services, only grouping them under the suggestion submission metric [1]. The evidence does not clarify the meaning of the мар.00 and дек.25 markers beyond listing them alongside the survey dynamics [1][3]. The evidence does not address how these results apply to applicants or the general public beyond the surveyed staff [1][2][3].

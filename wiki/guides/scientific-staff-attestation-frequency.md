# Attestation Schedule and Intervals

Planned attestation occurs once every 5 years [2]. Unplanned attestation may occur at any time, but it cannot happen more often than once every 2 years from the date of the previous attestation [3]. The minimum interval between any evaluations is 2 years from the date of employment or from the date of the previous attestation [1]. Postgraduates, teachers, and staff must follow these intervals [1].

## Gaps and caveats
The evidence does not specify procedures for scheduling, criteria for evaluation, or consequences of missing an attestation [1][2][3]. It does not define which roles qualify as scientific staff beyond the general address to postgraduates, teachers, and staff [1]. It does not state what happens if a planned attestation is delayed beyond the 5-year mark [2]. It does not provide details on how the 2-year minimum interval is calculated or enforced [1].

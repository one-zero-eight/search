# Recognition of earlier study results

## Who can apply and what is recognized
You can apply for credit transfer if you are a student enrolled in the main professional educational programs at the university, or if you are a parent or legal representative of a minor student [1][2][5]. The university recognizes results of study, including subjects, courses, disciplines (modules), practice, and additional educational programs, that you completed at other educational organizations or within the university itself [1].

## Documents you must submit
You must submit a written application and documents that confirm your previous study results [2][6]. The required documents include an education or qualification certificate, including those issued in a foreign country [3], or a training document such as a study certificate, a certificate of study period, or a document issued by a foreign organization [4]. If you are applying for results completed inside the university, the attestation commission uses the records already entered in your electronic grade book [5]. The application forms are listed in Appendix 1 and Appendix 2 of the regulation [9]. You may submit the application and documents in the forms specified in the regulation [10]. The application form for students begins with a heading to the Chairman of the Attestation Commission, followed by your full name, study group, and study group code, and the text continues beyond the provided excerpt [18].

## Deadlines and where to submit
You must submit your application and supporting documents to the university's attestation commission [6][7][8]. The standard deadline is no later than 14 (четырнадцати) календарных дней before the start of the semester or trimester [6]. If you are a first-year student in your first semester or trimester, you must submit the application no later than 14 (четырнадцати) календарных дней after the start of your studies [7]. If you participated in an academic mobility program, you must submit the application no later than 3 (трех) рабочих дней after the completion of that program [8].

## How the commission evaluates your results
The attestation commission evaluates your application by comparing the planned learning outcomes of the relevant part of your current educational program with the study results you have already achieved [11]. The university grants credit only when it establishes a correspondence between your previous results and your current program requirements [12]. To establish this correspondence, the commission may conduct an assessment of your actual achievement of the planned learning outcomes [13]. The matching procedure involves comparing your confirmation documents with the planned outcomes for the corresponding part of your program [14]. The commission may analyze information about the educational program from the issuing organization's official website or request information directly from that organization [14].

## Requirements for foreign documents
If you submit training documents from foreign organizations, you must provide a Russian translation [16]. The university may send requests to the issuing organizations to verify the authenticity of your training documents [16]. If you submit education or qualification certificates from foreign countries, you must provide a Russian translation in a legalized form along with a certificate of recognition of foreign education, unless Russian legislation or an international treaty exempts you from this requirement [17]. When granting credit for training results from foreign countries, the commission conducts an assessment according to the regulation's procedures [18]. The commission also considers the specific credit unit calculation system of the foreign educational organization and may compare credit units with the volume of contact and independent work [15].

## Gaps and caveats
The regulation does not specify the exact submission methods (e.g., online portal, in-person, email) beyond stating that applications can be submitted in the forms indicated in the regulation [10]. The regulation does not state the outcome timeline or how you will be notified of the commission's decision. The regulation does not define what happens if the commission denies the credit transfer or if you disagree with the assessment. The regulation does not list specific courses or programs that are eligible or ineligible for credit transfer. The regulation does not specify the composition or voting rules of the attestation commission. The regulation does not state whether partial credit is possible or how grade conversions are handled beyond the credit unit comparison.

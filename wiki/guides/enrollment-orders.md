# Official enrollment documentation for first-year students

## Official orders and information sheets for first-year students

For first-year bachelor’s students enrolled in full-time education on budgetary funds, the university issues an order of admission [1]. The university also provides information on admission for these students [2]. Additionally, the university provides information on admission for first-year bachelor’s students on main places within the controlled intake numbers for budgetary funds, for applicants placed as a result of entrance examinations [3].

For first-year master’s students enrolled in full-time education on budgetary funds, the university issues an order of admission [4]. The university also provides information on admission for these students [5].

The following documents and information sheets are required for the admission process:
*   Admission Rules for higher education programs (bachelor’s and master’s) for the 2022/2023 academic year [10].
*   Specifics of admission for higher education programs and postgraduate studies in 2022 in connection with the Government Resolution No. 434 of March 21, 2022 [11].
*   Specifics of admission for places within the special quota for 2022 [12].
*   Changes to the Admission Rules in connection with the Government Resolution No. 958 of May 27, 2022 [13].
*   The maximum number of specialties and/or fields of study for simultaneous participation in the competition (for bachelor’s programs) [14].
*   Deadlines for conducting admission [15].

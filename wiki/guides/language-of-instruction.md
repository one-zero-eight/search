# Languages of Instruction

You can take courses and complete your studies in Russian, Tatar, or a foreign language, depending on your program and the university's regulations [1][2][3]. These regulations are official university documents and take precedence over any course pages that may state otherwise or go beyond them [1][2][3].

Russian is the primary language for your studies. Educational activities for secondary vocational education programs are conducted in the state language of the Russian Federation [1].

The university may also offer instruction in Tatar. Teaching and studying of the Tatar language can be introduced in accordance with the legislation of the Russian Federation and the legislation of the Republic of Tatarstan [2]. Teaching and studying of the Tatar language must not be carried out to the detriment of teaching and studying the state language of the Russian Federation [2].

You may also complete your studies in a foreign language. Secondary vocational education can be obtained in a foreign language in accordance with the educational program and in the order established by education legislation and the local regulatory acts of the University [3].

## Gaps and caveats
The evidence only covers secondary vocational education programs and does not specify languages for other degree levels [1][2][3]. It does not list which specific foreign languages are available or which programs offer them [3]. It does not state how to enroll in Tatar language courses or foreign language tracks [2][3]. It does not define what constitutes instruction carried out to the detriment of Russian [2]. It does not provide the text of the local regulatory acts or education legislation that govern foreign language instruction [3]. Course pages may specify additional language options or differ from these regulations, but the official document takes precedence [1][2][3].

# Available Student Support and Development Services

## Material and technical support
As a student or postgraduate, you benefit from material, technical, and informational support that covers 80% of the educational process [1].

## Health and sports facilities
You can access conditions established for strengthening and protecting your health [2]. You are also provided with conditions that enable 100% engagement in physical culture and sports [2].

## Professional development opportunities
You can access opportunities for professional development in 70% of the University [3].

## Gaps and caveats
The evidence does not specify which faculties, campuses, or degree programs fall within the 80% material support or the 70% professional development coverage [1][3]. It does not list the specific health clinics, sports venues, or equipment available for the 100% sports engagement [2]. The official document reports these figures as survey results from pedagogical and scientific staff rather than as a direct service catalog [1][2][3]. No procedures, eligibility criteria, costs, or deadlines for accessing these services are provided [1][2][3]. The evidence leaves open how you can request these services or verify your eligibility [1][2][3].

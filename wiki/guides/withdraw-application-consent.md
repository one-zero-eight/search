# Withdrawing Your Application or Enrollment Consent

## Withdrawing Your Application
You may withdraw your application at any stage of the admission process [2]. You can submit this withdrawal through ЕПГУ or by delivering a written application to the University in person or via a postal operator [2].

## Withdrawing Consent to Enrollment
You may withdraw your consent to enrollment at any stage of the admission process [1]. You can submit this withdrawal through ЕПГУ or by delivering a written application to the University in person or via a postal operator [1].

## Refusing Enrollment After Admission
If you have already been enrolled, you may refuse enrollment without withdrawing your consent to enrollment [3]. You can submit this refusal through ЕПГУ or by delivering a written application to the University in person or via a postal operator [3].

## Special Requirement for Quota Places
If you are enrolled under the quota for admissions and wish to withdraw your consent to enrollment, you must refuse enrollment simultaneously with withdrawing your consent [4].

## Gaps and caveats
The evidence leaves open all deadlines for withdrawing your application, withdrawing consent, or refusing enrollment [1][2][3].

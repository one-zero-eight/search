# Format of Final Certification for Additional Professional Programs

For your final certification, the required components depend on whether you are enrolled in a professional retraining program or an advanced training program [1][2].

If you are in a professional retraining program, your final assessment consists of a final interdisciplinary exam and the preparation and defense of a graduation certification (qualification) work [1].

If you are in an advanced training program, your final assessment includes an exam, the preparation and defense of a graduation certification (qualification) work, a credit test for the training program, and other types provided by the DPP [2].

Regarding the evaluation body, the final assessment for advanced training programs is, as a rule, conducted without forming an examination commission [3]. The regulations do not specify whether an examination commission is formed for professional retraining programs [1].

## Gaps and caveats
The evidence does not specify the format, duration, or grading criteria for the exams or the graduation work [1][2]. It does not state whether an examination commission is required for professional retraining programs [1]. The phrase "other types, provided by the DPP" means the exact assessment components for advanced training programs may vary depending on your specific program [2]. The regulations do not address what happens if you fail any component of the final assessment [1][2][3].

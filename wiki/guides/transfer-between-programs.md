# Перевод с одной образовательной программы на другую

## Procedure for Transfer

To transfer from one study program, specialization, or form of study to another, you must follow the procedure established by the local regulatory acts governing student transfers [1].

**Eligibility and Timing**
You may apply for a transfer no earlier than after completing the first intermediate assessment, provided you have no academic debts [2]. Your application must be submitted to the structural unit responsible for organizing educational activity no later than seven working days before the start of the semester [3].

**Application and Review**
You must submit a written application to the Director of the University using the form prescribed by the local regulatory act governing transfers [3]. Upon receiving your application, the University organizes an attestation commission to review the possibility of your transfer [3].

The attestation commission reviews the submitted documents and determines the list of disciplines (modules), other components (including internships, scientific research, and scientific and pedagogical activity) that will be credited upon transfer, in accordance with the University's local regulatory acts [5]. The commission makes a decision on whether to approve the transfer [5].

**Decision and Notification**
After making its decision, the attestation commission sets the date for your transfer to the new program, specialization, or form of study [5]. The commission's decision is communicated to you in writing under your personal signature and/or as an electronic document via the University's electronic information and educational environment within ten working days after the decision is made [5].

**Approval and Plan Approval**
If the commission establishes that the list, workload, and periods of study of disciplines (modules), other components (including internships), and the calendar academic schedule of your current program correspond to those of the target program, you may be transferred to the basic academic plan [4]. Following the commission's decision, the Director of the University (or an authorized person) issues an order transferring you to the new program and approves your individual academic plan [6].

## Gaps and caveats

The official regulation does not specify the exact form of the application mentioned in clause 3.3, stating only that it follows the form provided by the local regulatory act governing transfers [3]. The specific local regulatory act containing this form is not provided in the evidence [1].

The regulation does not specify the composition of the attestation commission or the specific criteria for "correspondence" of workload and schedule beyond the general statement in clause 3.4 [4].

The regulation does not state what happens if the attestation commission denies the transfer [5].

The regulation does not specify the deadline for submitting the application if the transfer is requested after the first intermediate assessment but before the seven-day deadline for the semester start, other than the general eligibility rule [2][3].

The regulation does not provide details on the "local regulatory acts" referenced in clauses 3.1 and 3.3, only that they regulate the transfer process [1][3].

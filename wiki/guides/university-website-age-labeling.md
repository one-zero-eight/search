# Age Restrictions on University Digital Platforms

## Age categories and signs

The university classifies information products into five age-based categories [1][2][3][4][5].

- For information products intended for children who have not reached the age of six, the sign consists of the digit «0» and the «plus» sign [1].
- For information products intended for children who have reached the age of six, the sign consists of the digit «6» and the «plus» sign and/or a text warning in the form of the phrase «for children over six years old» [2].
- For information products intended for children who have reached the age of twelve, the sign consists of the digit «12» and the «plus» sign and/or a text warning in the form of the phrase «for children over 12 years old» [3].
- For information products intended for children who have reached the age of sixteen, the sign consists of the digit «16» and the «plus» sign and/or a text warning in the form of the phrase «for children over 16 years old» [4].
- For information products prohibited for children, the sign consists of the digit «18» and the «plus» sign and/or a text warning in the form of the phrase «prohibited for children» [5].

## Display requirements

On the university’s corporate website and portal, pages may contain the information product sign (including in machine-readable form) and/or a text warning about restrictions on dissemination among children [7]. These signs and warnings must correspond to one of the categories established by Federal Law No. 436 [7].

The classification of information products is carried out independently by structural units in accordance with the requirements of the Regulation [7].

The corporate website and portal of the university are not allowed to post announcements inviting students to participate in the creation of information products that cause harm to their health and/or development [7].

# Rules for Retaining or Losing the Scholarship

## Conditions for losing the grant or disqualification

You lose the right to receive the grant if you do not meet the requirements specified in clause 3.2 of the Regulation [1]. The grant is not issued if it is not claimed by the start of the academic year following the completion of the current year's competition, including if you fail to take the actions established in clause 3.3 [1]. Grants not claimed in this period are not issued by the Organizer and are not carried over to the next year [1].

The Organizer may prohibit your further participation in the competition or refuse to issue the grant to you if you:
*   Forge documents or derive benefit from the submission of forged documents for participation [2].
*   Act in violation of the Regulation [2].
*   Act destructively or perform actions with the intent to annoy, insult, threaten, or cause trouble to any other person connected with the competition [2].
*   Violate behavior rules at the location of the competition stages, including public order [2].
*   Are caught committing fraud or deception [3].
*   Provide unreliable information [3].
*   Violate the conditions of the Regulation [3].

## Conditions for returning the grant

If your agreement is terminated early for reasons not specified in clause 5.4 of the Agreement, you are obliged to return the portion of the grant provided under this Agreement [4]. The amount to be returned is 20% of the sum equal to the cost of education, calculated proportionally to the period of your actual study [4]. This period runs from the date of the order on admission to the date of the order on dismissal [4].

## Academic performance conditions

Your grant is affected by your academic performance after specific trimesters or semesters [6][7]. The results of repeated interim assessments for the corresponding subjects are not taken into account [6][7].

You face consequences if you have:
*   More than two grades of "satisfactory" (C) by the end of the first (autumn) trimester (semester) and/or more than four grades of "satisfactory" (C) by the end of the first (spring) and/or first (summer) trimester (semester) [6].
*   One or more grades of "unsatisfactory" by the end of the corresponding trimester [7].

## Early dismissal and repayment exceptions

If you are dismissed before the end of the first year (for bachelors) or the first trimester (for masters) of study under the Agreement, the Grantor may decide not to demand the return of the provided grant amount [5]. This decision is made by concluding a supplementary agreement to the Agreement [5]. The Grantor's decision is based on information about your current academic performance and attendance of classes, as well as other information indicating your good faith in mastering the educational program [5].

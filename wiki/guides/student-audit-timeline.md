# Student Academic Audit Schedule

## Audit Schedule

You will encounter audits of student achievements, intermediate assessments, and current grade control during the academic year according to the following schedule [1][2][3].

**Audits of Student Achievements**
An audit of student achievements and an analysis of achievements and statistics of victories in olympiads, participation in scientific and practical and research conferences, competitions, etc., including based on information posted in the student portfolio, are conducted in January [1]. This audit is carried out by the Center for Control of Educational Activities and the Department of Support and Development of Students [1].

**Audits of Intermediate Assessment Data**
An audit of intermediate assessment data and student achievements is conducted in February and August [2]. This audit is carried out by the Center for Control of Educational Activities, the Department of Organization of Educational Activities, the Department of Organization of Bachelor and Master Programs, and the College [2].

**Audits of Current Grade Control Data**
An audit of current grade control data and student achievements is conducted in April and November [3]. This audit is carried out by the Center for Control of Educational Activities, the Department of Organization of Educational Activities, the Department of Organization of Bachelor and Master Programs, and the College [3].

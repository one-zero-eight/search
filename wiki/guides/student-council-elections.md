# Running for the Student Council

## Eligibility and Candidacy

You can run for the Student Council if you are an active student of the University [1]. Every student at the University has the right to be elected to the Council in accordance with the Regulation [2].

## Election Procedure

The Council is formed by holding elections at the University Student Conference at least once every two years [3]. The Student Conference decides on the number of Council members [3].

Elections for the Council Chair, members of the administrative block, the organizational block, and the development and support block take place at the first meeting of the newly elected Council [4]. These elections are conducted by simple majority vote [4]. The elections are open [4]. A quorum of at least two-thirds of the Council members must be present at the meeting for the elections to proceed [4].

## Term Limits

The Council Chair is elected for a term of two years [5].

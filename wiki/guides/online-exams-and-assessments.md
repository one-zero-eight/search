# Rules for Online Exams and Intermediate Assessments

## Identification and General Framework
The forms, periodicity, order, grading criteria, and deadlines for clearing academic debts are defined by separate local normative acts of the university [1]. During current control and intermediate certification, alongside the standard identification level, a control identification level may be used [2].

## Current Control and Online Assessments
The specific forms, periodicity, order, tools, grading criteria, and grading system for current control are developed independently by your instructor and documented in the working program of the discipline or module [3]. You may encounter the following formats when using electronic learning and distance educational technologies [4]:
- completing parts of a software product code online [4]
- completing independent or homework assignments online [4]
- completing practical and laboratory works online or offline [4]
- completing projects online or offline [4]
- taking tests online or offline [4]
- attending colloquiums online or offline [4]
- taking tests online [4]
- submitting reports, essays, references, or reviews online or offline [4]
- participating in oral exams online or offline [4]
- joining discussions, training sessions, or round tables online or offline [4]
- other formats established by your instructor according to the curriculum and academic schedule online or offline [4]
Your instructor will enter the stages and results of your actual mastery into the electronic journal in the Moodle learning management system on the university portal within five working days after the assessment [5].

## Intermediate Certification (Mid-term Evaluations)
Intermediate certification is conducted in a format defined by your academic curriculum [6]. The official forms of intermediate certification are the credit (including the differentiated credit) and the exam [6]. Your instructor, practice supervisor, or research supervisor independently determines the specific format and records it in the relevant working program, practice program, or research program [7]. You may encounter the following formats when using electronic learning and distance educational technologies [7]:
- testing online or offline [7]
- oral exams online or offline [7]
- written work online or offline [7]
- defense of a reference online or offline [7]
- defense of a project online or offline [7]
- other formats determined by your instructor online or offline [7]
The results of your intermediate certification are recorded in the university's educational information system (Moodle, 1C: University, your student portfolio, or your personal account) within seven working days after the assessment, but no later than the end of the intermediate certification period [8]. For bachelor's and master's programs, your instructor enters the results into a grade sheet on paper or as an electronic document (or a scanned/photographed document with machine-readable recognition), which is submitted to the responsible structural unit [8]. Staff of that unit then transfer the results into 1C: University and may certify them with the dean's signature [8]. For PhD programs, staff of the responsible structural unit transfer the results from the grade sheet [...the clause continues...] [8].

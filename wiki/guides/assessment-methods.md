# Current Academic Assessment Forms

Current academic control is the method used to check the quality of your progress in the Additional General Education Program [1]. This control takes place during the academic period [2]. It is conducted for the purposes of controlling the level of achievement of results provided by the program, conducting self-assessment, and evaluating your work to possibly improve the educational process [2]. It systematically checks your individual assignments, preparation for classes, independent study of specific topics, and the completion of projects or essays [2]. The control is conducted in the following forms [3]:
- Oral control, which consists of oral questioning [3].
- Written control, which consists of written questioning and the completion of specific assignments [3].
- Test-based control, which consists of oral, written, computer, or electronic testing [3].

Students, you use this process to conduct self-assessment and receive an evaluation of your work from the teacher [2]. The teacher uses this evaluation to possibly improve the educational process [2]. The control systematically checks your individual assignments, preparation for classes, independent study of specific topics, and the completion of projects or essays [2]. It also verifies your achievement of the results provided by the program [2].

## Gaps and caveats
The evidence does not specify deadlines, grading scales, or frequency for these forms of control [1][2][3]. It does not list which specific assignments, projects, or essays are required for each course [2]. It does not detail how computer or electronic testing is administered or which platforms are used [3]. The evidence does not explain what happens if a student fails to meet the program results [2].

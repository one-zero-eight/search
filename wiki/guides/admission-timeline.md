# Admission to Additional Professional Programs

## When to apply
You can apply for admission to additional professional programs throughout the entire calendar year [1]. The deadline for submitting your documents is until the start of studies, unless the implementation conditions of your specific program state otherwise [5].

## How to apply
To enroll, you must submit an application for admission addressed to the Director of the University [2]. When you submit this application, you must provide the required documents [3]. You can submit these documents to the University in one of the following ways [4]. The University official or structural unit responsible for implementing your program will receive your documents [6]. Admission is conducted without entrance exams based on a review of your submitted documents, unless your educational program provides otherwise [7].

## Gaps and caveats
The evidence does not specify the exact list of documents you must provide when submitting your application [3]. It does not list the specific ways you can submit your documents [4]. It does not state which official or structural unit will receive your documents for any given program [6]. It does not indicate which programs might require entrance exams or what those requirements would be [7].

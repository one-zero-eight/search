# Exemptions from Mandatory Attestation

## Exempt categories
If you are a postgraduate, teacher, or staff member working as a scientific researcher, the following groups are legally exempt from mandatory attestation [1][2][3].
- Scientific staff working under fixed-term employment contracts [1].
- Pregnant women [2].
- Women on maternity leave, and staff on childcare leave until the child reaches three years of age [3].

## Timing limits for leave exemptions
The exemption for women on maternity leave and staff on childcare leave until the child reaches three years of age includes a strict timing limit [3]. Attestation for these staff members is possible no earlier than one year after their return from the corresponding leaves [3].

## Gaps and caveats
The official regulation does not state whether postgraduates or teachers who are not classified as scientific staff are covered by these exemptions [1][2][3]. The regulation does not define the duration or renewal rules for fixed-term contracts [1]. The regulation does not list other medical conditions or leave types that qualify for exemption [3]. The regulation does not describe how to submit a request or provide documentation for these exemptions [3]. The regulation does not specify what happens if attestation deadlines overlap with the one-year waiting period [3]. If any course page or internal guide claims different exemption rules, the official regulation overrides them [1][2][3].

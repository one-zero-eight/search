# Accessibility measures for visually impaired applicants

## Website adaptation
The official website of the university is adapted to the special needs of visually impaired persons [1].

## Reference information
Reference information is placed in locations accessible to blind or visually impaired persons [2]. The information is provided in an adapted form that accounts for their special needs [2]. The text is executed in a large, high-contrast font with capital letters at least 7.5 cm in height, displayed on a white or yellow background [2]. The same information is duplicated in Braille [2].

## On-site assistance
An assistant is present to provide necessary assistance to applicants [3].

## Printed materials
Alternative formats of printed materials are issued [4].

## Building access and guide dogs
Blind applicants using a guide dog have access to the university building [5]. The building contains a designated place for housing the guide dog [5].

## Gaps and caveats
The evidence does not specify how applicants must request the assistant or alternative printed formats [3][4]. It does not list the specific alternative formats available for printed materials [4]. It does not identify the exact locations where reference information is placed [2]. It does not describe the technical features of the website adaptation [1]. It does not address accessibility for applicants with other disabilities [1][2][3][4][5]. It does not state deadlines, application procedures, or contact details for requesting accommodations [1][2][3][4][5]. The regulations apply only to additional general education programs [1][2][3][4][5]. No course pages are provided to compare against these regulations [1][2][3][4][5].

# Official Information and Communication Standards

## Standards for information completeness
Students, applicants, and the public should note that the completeness and relevance of information about the University and its activities in the information-telecommunication network is set at 80% "Internet" [1]. The availability of information about teaching staff is set at 90% on the official website of the University [2].

## Standards for communication channel accessibility
Students, applicants, and the public can use the following channels for interaction with the University: by phone, by email, and using electronic services [3]. The University guarantees a 90% availability of the opportunity to submit proposals aimed at improving the work of the University [3].

## Gaps and caveats
The official document does not specify deadlines for maintaining the 80% or 90% standards [1][2][3]. The official document does not define the measurement methodology for the completeness or availability percentages [1][2][3]. The official document does not list which specific electronic services are included in the accessibility standard [3]. The official document does not state consequences or procedures for falling below the 80% or 90% thresholds [1][2][3]. The official document does not address standards for other types of information or communication channels [1][2][3].

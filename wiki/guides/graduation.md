# Final state attestation (graduation)

## Forms and schedule
The Final State Certification applies to students completing state-accredited Bachelor’s Degree and Master’s Degree programs [1]. The University independently determines the schedule for conducting the Final State Certification [4]. The scope, structure, and content of the Final State Certification for each program are established by the University in accordance with the Educational Standards [5]. Specific forms of the Final State Certification are determined independently by the University for each program in accordance with the FSES HE requirements [6]. For students enrolled in these programs, the Final State Certification is conducted in the form of a defense of a Final Qualification Work [7]. The University has established a Bachelor’s Thesis for the Bachelor's level and a Master’s Thesis for the Master's level [8]. The Final State Certification Program includes the requirements for Final Qualification Works and the procedure for their completion [8]. The Final State Certification Program constitutes a part of the Educational Program and is approved by the Academic Council of the University [8]. You will receive the Final State Certification Program no later than six months prior to the commencement of the Final State Certification [8].

## Thesis requirements
The Final Qualification Work demonstrates your level of preparedness for independent professional activity [15]. The type of the Final Qualification Work, the requirements for it, the procedure for its completion, and the criteria for its assessment are established independently by the University in accordance with the Educational Standards and these Regulations [16]. You may complete the Final Qualification Work jointly with several other students within the same field of study of your educational program [17]. In a jointly completed Final Qualification Work, the individual contribution of each student must be clearly identified [17].

## Committees
State Examination Committees are established at the University to conduct the Final State Certification [12]. These committees determine whether the learning outcomes you achieved comply with the relevant requirements of the Educational Standards [3]. The committees are established for each field of study, for each program, for a group of fields of study, or for a group of programs [13]. The committees are valid for a period of one calendar year [12]. The University independently establishes the rules of procedure for the committees [12]. The Chairperson of a State Examination Committee is approved by the Ministry of Science and Higher Education of the Russian Federation upon nomination by the University [14]. The nominee must not be employed by the University, must hold a Doctor of Sciences degree and/or the academic title of Professor, or be a leading specialist representing employers or their associations in the relevant professional field [14]. The approval must occur no later than December 31 of the year preceding the year in which the Final State Certification is conducted [14]. An Appeal Committee is established at the University to consider appeals regarding Final State Certification results [12]. The Chairperson of an Appeal Committee is appointed by the Director of the University or another authorised representative [14].

## Accessibility
If you have disabilities, the Final State Certification shall be conducted taking into account your psychophysical development characteristics, individual capabilities, and state of health [9]. During the Final State Certification, you will be conducted in the same room as students without disabilities, provided this does not create difficulties for you or other students [10]. An assistant or assistants will be present in the room to provide necessary technical assistance to you, considering your individual characteristics, such as taking your seat, moving around, reading and completing assignments, and communicating with the chair and members of the State Qualifying Committee [10]. You are permitted to use the necessary technical aids during the Final State Certification, considering your individual characteristics [10]. The University will ensure unimpeded access for you to the examination rooms, restrooms, and other premises [10]. All local regulations of the University concerning the conduct of the Final State Certification shall be communicated to you in an accessible format [11].

## Appeals
You have the right to appeal the results of the Final State Certification [18]. You will be informed about the procedure for filing and considering appeals no later than 6 months before the commencement of the Final State Certification [19]. You have the right to submit a written appeal to the Appeal Committee concerning an alleged violation of the established procedure for conducting the state certification [20]. The application form for the appeal is provided in Appendix 19 to these Regulations [20].

# International Partners

## Cooperation

## International Partners

## Guidance for Partners

## 01.

Please, nominate your students by sending their full names, e-mail address, study program, year of study and duration of mobility to international@innopolis.university.

## 02.

The nominated students will receive an email with application form and instructions on how to apply within 7 days since nomination.

## 03.

The results of application review will be sent to the students within 14 days after the application deadline for particular semester.

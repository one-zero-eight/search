## Timur Fazullin

Director of External Communications and Branding,Head of theGame Development Centre

Graduated from Bashkir State University with a degree in Social Philosophy and Anthropology. He has worked for TV news media as a correspondent, lead and production editor. Was a production studio creative director.

Founded a strategic communications agency and supervised branding, marketing and PR projects for large manufacturing companies. At Innopolis University Timur is responsible for communication strategy.

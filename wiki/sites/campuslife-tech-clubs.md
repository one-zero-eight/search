Student Scientific Community

The Student Scientific Community connects students with scientific organizations for research and professional development. We offer you to participate in partner projects to practice in research and academic writing. The focus is on three areas: fundamental math and computer science research (e.g. optimization), applied math and computer science for R&D projects (e.g. AI enhancement) and interdisciplinary research (e.g. IT for neurobiology, medicine). Join us to advance your career in science!

1202 — Corporation of Bold Ideas and Projects

1202 corp. is an open community of Innopolis University students passionate about technology, creativity, and collaborative project development. We generate ideas ourselves or receive them from our community, select the most promising ones, and bring them to life. While our projects are mainly focused on technical solutions, that doesn’t mean we only work on development. We also create and organize events and initiatives that make university life brighter, more convenient, and more engaging. Everyone can contribute — by proposing an idea, joining a team, or participating in our activities, both within and beyond the university.

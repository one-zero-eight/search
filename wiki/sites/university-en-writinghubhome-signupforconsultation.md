# Sign up for consultation

## How to get

help from the

Writing Hub

The Writing Hub offers two types of consultations:

a face-to-face consultation and e-tutoring.

Learn more about the two types of consultations we offer

## TWo TYPES OF consultations:

## A face-to-face consultation

- - choose this option if you need to discuss a writing-related issue with our tutor

- - book an in-person consultation with our tutor

- - follow the procedure described in "How to sign up or cancel a consultation in IU Writing Hub"

## E-tutoring

- choose this option if you have a text to be improved

- send your text to one of our tutors

- our tutor will proofread your text in terms of clarity, coherence, and concision

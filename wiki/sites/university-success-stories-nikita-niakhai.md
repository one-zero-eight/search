## Nikita Niakhai

Belarusian State University of Informatics and Radioelectronics, Belarus

Spring Semester 2023

## Знакомство с Иннополисом

It was important for me to get a new and unique experience, make new friends and actively participate in cultural exchange not only with foreign students and teachers, but also with Russians, from whom we, Belarusians, are still different.

When I arrived, I learned even more about Innopolis through communication with students, teachers and various internal departments of Innopolis, as well as through possible volunteer programs. Basically, everybody is really responsive!

It was very interesting for me to meet and get acquainted with foreign students! Many of them came directly from Syria, Egypt, Jordan, Africa, Central Asia and Indonesia. Each of them has its own special, interesting path and history!

Also, I would say that Innopolis is a unique and special place with its own structure, program and teaching methods. Innopolis University is technological and developing, international and futuristic.

Social and student life is active here, offering a lot of events and opportunities. Special thanks to the Student Affairs Office! In addition to Innopolis, I also saw a lot in Kazan. This is a beautiful and cozy city. In addition to the standard attractions that are worth seeing, I would recommend that you, go around Kazan by bike in the warmer weather, and take a walk and swim in the Blue Lakes in winter. In general, you will have something to do in your free time.

If you are considering student mobility and have not yet found a suitable university for yourself, then I would recommend that you think about Innopolis.

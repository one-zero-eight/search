## Bekhruz Nutfilloyev

Tashkent University of Information Technologies, Uzbekistan

Fall Semester 2022

## Знакомство с Иннополисом

I participated in 3 team projects: Blink detection, Occlusion detection and RL ChatBot. We made blink detection and occlusion detection for Liveness. RL ChatBot was an alternative version of Student Affairs bot. It was an AI-powered student response service where my role was to build the core code base. My partner and I worked on it. My friend mainly wrote reports and presentations, I compiled parts of their algorithm and made them ready. The professors really liked the projects we did and gave them high marks.

I’d like to mention the accommodation as well. The rooms are very cozy and comfortable, the cleaning service keeps everything in order. The internet is very fast, and what I like the most is that no one bothers you in the room. Everyone is very polite. Also, there are enough conditions for Muslims.

Actually, I would like to study here from 1st year. Innopolis is a very nice city, small, beautiful, peaceful and quiet. It was very important to me.

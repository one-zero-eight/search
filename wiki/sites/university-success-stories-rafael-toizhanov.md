## Rafael Toizhanov

International Information Technology University, Kazakhstan

Fall Semester 2021/22

## Знакомство с Иннополисом

The professors are quite loyal to students, always ready to meet you halfway and share their valuable experience. All assignments and grades could be found on the university platform, which is very convenient. We also often used Telegram to communicate with each other. We prepared projects during some of the courses, others included a written and an oral exam. The results, I think are good for me.

Speaking of the campus, this was my first experience of living in a dorm and I want to say that this is a great first experience. I liked that each room has its own kitchen, bathroom, shower room, cleaning was done every 10 days. It's like living in a hotel.

It's my first time in Russia, very warm memories of the country and Innopolis. I also wanted to thank the International Relations Office for helping us in many things and thanks to them, I had a positive impression of studying in Innopolis.

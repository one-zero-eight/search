# Шапка

### Kiselеv Oleg

Образование:

Ufa State Aviation Technical Institute

Doctor of Sciences degree in Physics and Mathematics, specializing in "Differential Equations, Dynamical Systems, and Optimal Control." Ph.D. (1993) and D.Sc (2002) dissertations defended at the Institute of Mathematics of the Ufa Scientific Center of the Russian Academy of Sciences. Worked there from 1988 to 2022. Professor at Innopolis University since 2020. Research interests: nonlinear dynamics of wave and discrete systems, bifurcations and theory of chaotic systems, asymptotic methods. Application areas: resonances and synchronization, control theory of unstable objects, quantum computing.

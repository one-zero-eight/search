# Center for Oil and Gas Technology

technological centers

## Center for Oil and Gas Technology

The Center has been implementing knowledge-based projects for oil and gas companies since 2017. The center’s team specializes in studying and building mathematical and geologic models, as well as researching in the new methods of forecasting oil and gas deposits.

The center’s core activities include developing knowledge-based software and process technologies that solve the strategic problems of oil and gas companies.

## activity of the center

Key areas of the center: Developing information products for building digital models of oil and gas deposits, developing a complex geomechanical modeling platform, developing an intelligence system for tracking the diagnostic engineering of gas pipelines, developing algorithms for high-performance cluster computations, and developing algorithms for processing big data emerging in modeling.
